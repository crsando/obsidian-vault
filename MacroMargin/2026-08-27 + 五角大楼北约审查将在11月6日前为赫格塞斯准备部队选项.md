### 中文译文

## 五角大楼北约审查将在11月6日前为赫格塞斯准备部队选项

*项目1/2：2020年10月9日，美国弗吉尼亚州阿灵顿拍摄的五角大楼。路透社/Carlos Barria/资料图片*

**摘要**

- 审查将在11月6日前向国防部长赫格塞斯提供至少四个部队方案
- 文件显示，方案将包括兵力数量、地点、调整速度和成本
- 盟友将面临关于支出承诺、北约兵力模式缺口和准入权的评估
华盛顿，8月27日（路透社）——根据路透社看到的一份文件，五角大楼对美国在欧洲部队部署的审查将在11月6日前为国防部长皮特·赫格塞斯制定至少四套方案，远早于12月的审查完成期限。

北约盟友担心，这项于6月宣布、为期六个月的审查将导致美国在欧洲约8万兵力的大规模削减，并惩罚那些总统[唐纳德·特朗普](https://www.reuters.com/world/us/donald-trump/)认为未能支持美以对[伊朗的战争](https://www.reuters.com/world/iran/)的国家。

五角大楼的《职权范围》此前未曾详述，其中说明了内部审查将采取的步骤，以及用于评估美国在整个欧洲基地决策的部分标准。

根据该文件，其中一项重要披露是，计划让驻欧洲美军最高将领在9月18日前与五角大楼政策主管埃尔布里奇·科尔比(Elbridge Colby)参加一次决策简报会。

根据该文件，这次简报会将是敲定为赫格塞斯制定的选项的关键。

### 北约对美国审查进程的怀疑

科尔比预计将于周四在布鲁塞尔的北约总部与[北约大使们](https://www.reuters.com/world/europe/pentagon-official-brief-nato-allies-review-fuels-questions-2026-08-26/)就该审查进行交流。

当被问及职权范围时，一位五角大楼高级官员表示，将生成一系列详细选项这一事实凸显了该进程的严肃性。

这位官员对路透社表示：“我们希望能够讨论各种不同兵力规模或部署方案的利弊。”

然而，尚不清楚这些细节能否说服对审查进程持怀疑态度的欧洲官员。这些官员私下表示，他们担心华盛顿几乎已经做出决定。

特朗普政府长期暗示，它打算减少投入欧洲的军事资源，并期望北约盟国承担比历史上更大的负担份额。

特朗普还愤怒地表示，欧洲北约盟友长期以来一直依赖美国的安全保障，却对伊朗战争提供的支持不足。指责包括一些盟友未能向美国战机提供基地和飞越权限，或者动作太慢。

北约秘书长马克·吕特已[对特朗普的失望表示某种理解](https://www.reuters.com/world/americas/trump-meets-nato-chief-iran-war-strains-alliance-2026-04-08/)，但表示绝大多数欧洲人一直有帮助。

五角大楼官员表示，特朗普政府已明确表示“我们正转向提供关键的、更有限的支持，由盟友主导。”

“特朗普总统最近几个月对某些盟友的行为感到非常正确且明显的沮丧。因此，显然至少有一些需求信号，希望有选项显示我们在欧洲的兵力水平（降级），”这位官员要求匿名讨论此事时说。

尽管如此，这位官员表示，这次评估旨在“提供从我们目前所处位置到评估可能导向的任何地方的多种选项”。

### 赫格塞斯指责一些北约盟友“搭便车”

赫格塞斯在六月宣布这项评估时，斥责了一些“搭便车”的北约盟友，并表示该评估将确保美国军队的“准入、基地和飞越权得到明确界定”。

职权范围更进一步。

该文件称，除了要求在北约盟国获得可靠的美国准入权、驻军权和飞越权（简称 ABO）之外，他们还审查了太空、网络以及“支持美国全球力量投射的情报架构和基础设施”。

这位高级官员说，五角大楼扩大了 ABO 的定义，以确保美国考虑到北约盟国提供的各种类型的支持。

职权范围还涉及评估盟友的其他标准，并且似乎与美国高级官员此前的言论一致。

该文件称，在今年早些时候美国宣布削减军力之后，五角大楼将寻求确定盟友是否有可靠的途径履行其北约开支承诺，并填补北约危机部队（即北约兵力模式）的缺口。

路透社报道过一份美国向盟友发出的[调查问卷](https://www.reuters.com/world/europe/pentagon-official-brief-nato-allies-review-fuels-questions-2026-08-26/)，以帮助进行这项评估。问卷涉及的问题从国防开支到战略、采购以及对美国外交政策优先事项的支持。

美国在问卷中问道：“该国对美国准入、驻军和飞越（ABO）施加了哪些限制（如果有的话）？如果是，原因是什么？他们是否愿意减少或取消此类限制？”

另一个问题写道：“该国是否公开反对旨在抑制或减少跨大西洋国防工业合作的法规或其他措施？如果是，如何反对？如果不是，他们是否愿意这样做？”

回复截止至周五。

“我们正非常、非常努力地工作，而时间表相对紧张，”这位官员说。“大量先前的分析将为这项工作提供参考，这应能让我们这边更快地推进。”

报道：菲尔·斯图尔特；补充报道：伊德里斯·阿里；编辑：唐·德菲和威廉·马拉德

## Pentagon's NATO review preparing troop options for Hegseth by November 6

*Item 1 of 2 The Pentagon building is seen in Arlington, Virginia, U.S. October 9, 2020. REUTERS/Carlos Barria/File Photo*

**Summary**

- Review to give Defense Secretary Hegseth at least four troop options by November 6
- Options to include force numbers, locations, adjustment speed and costs, document shows
- Allies face evaluation on spending pledges, NATO force model gaps and access rights
WASHINGTON, Aug 27 (Reuters) - A Pentagon review of U.S. troop deployments in Europe will create at least four sets of options for Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth by November 6, well ahead of a December deadline for the review's completion, according to a document seen by Reuters.

NATO allies worry that the six-month review, announced in ‌June, will lead to large-scale cuts to the roughly 80,000 U.S. forces across Europe and punish countries that President [Donald Trump](https://www.reuters.com/world/us/donald-trump/) believes failed to support the U.S.-Israeli [war against Iran.](https://www.reuters.com/world/iran/)

The Pentagon's "Terms of Reference," which have not been previously detailed, explain the steps that the internal review will take and some of the criteria that will be used to evaluate U.S. basing decisions throughout Europe.

One important disclosure in the document is a plan for the top U.S. general in Europe to participate in a decision briefing with the Pentagon's policy chief, Elbridge Colby, by September 18, according to the document.

That briefing will be key, according to the document, in hashing out options to develop for Hegseth.

### NATO SKEPTICISM OF U.S. REVIEW PROCESS

Colby is expected to speak with [NATO ambassadors](https://www.reuters.com/world/europe/pentagon-official-brief-nato-allies-review-fuels-questions-2026-08-26/) about the review on Thursday at ⁠the alliance's Brussels headquarters.

Asked about the Terms of Reference, a senior Pentagon official said the fact that a spectrum of options would be generated, in detail, underscored the seriousness of the process.

"We want to be able to talk through the pros and cons of a variety of different force levels or dispositions," the official told Reuters.

It is unclear, however, whether the details will convince European officials skeptical of the review process, who say privately they are concerned Washington's decision is all but made.

The Trump administration has long signaled it intends to reduce the military resources it devotes to Europe, with the expectation that NATO allies will carry a greater share of the burden than they have historically.

Trump has also fumed that European NATO allies have long relied on U.S. security guarantees while providing inadequate support for the Iran war. Accusations include that some allies failed to grant basing and overflight access to U.S. warplanes, or did it too slowly.

NATO Secretary General Mark Rutte has [expressed some understanding](https://www.reuters.com/world/americas/trump-meets-nato-chief-iran-war-strains-alliance-2026-04-08/) of Trump's disappointment but said the large majority of Europeans have been helpful.

The Pentagon official said the Trump administration had made clear that "we are transitioning to provide critical, more limited support with allies in the lead."

"President Trump has also been quite rightly and ‌clearly frustrated with ⁠certain allies' behavior in recent months. So there's clearly at least some demand signal to have options that would show us (downgrading) our force levels in Europe," the official said on condition of anonymity to discuss the matter.

Still, the official said the review sought to "provide a spectrum of options from where we are to wherever" the review leads.

### HEGSETH ACCUSES SOME NATO ALLIES OF 'FREE-RIDING'

Announcing the review in June, Hegseth chided some "free-riding" NATO allies and said the review would ensure U.S. military "access, basing, and overflight are clearly delineated."

The Terms of Reference go further.

Beyond calling for reliable U.S. access, basing and overflight rights, called ABO, in allied NATO nations, they examine space, cyber and "intelligence architectures and infrastructure that enable ⁠U.S. global power projection," the document says.

The Pentagon expanded the definition of ABO to ensure the U.S. takes into account all the types of support that NATO allies provide, the senior official said.

The Terms of Reference also address other ways allies will be evaluated, and appear to be in line with previous U.S. remarks by senior officials.

The document says the Pentagon will seek to determine whether allies have a credible path to meet their NATO spending pledges and ⁠fill in gaps to NATO crisis forces — known as the NATO Force Model — after U.S. reductions were announced earlier this year.

Reuters has reported on a [U.S. questionnaire](https://www.reuters.com/world/europe/pentagon-official-brief-nato-allies-review-fuels-questions-2026-08-26/) sent to allies to inform the review. It covers issues ranging from defense spending to strategy, procurement and support for U.S. foreign policy priorities.

"What if any restrictions has the country placed on U.S. access, basing, and overflight (ABO)? If so, why? Are they willing to ⁠reduce or eliminate any such restrictions?" the U.S. asked in its questionnaire.

"Has the country publicly opposed regulations or other measures designed to inhibit or reduce transatlantic defense industrial cooperation? If so, how? If not, would they be willing to do so?" another question read.

The responses are due by Friday.

"We are working very, very hard, and it is a relatively tight timeline," the official said. "A lot of prior analysis will inform this, which should allow us to move quicker on our end."
