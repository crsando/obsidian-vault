### 中文译文

## 韩国产业部长：面对中国攻势，韩国须在芯片领域保持领先的竞赛中加速

韩国产业部长官金正宽表示，除非帮助芯片制造商加速投资，否则韩国可能失去在半导体领域的优势，因为中国正以前所未有的资源投入该行业，加剧了全球技术竞赛。

“让我深感担忧的是中国，”金正宽周四在由韩国资深记者协会主办的宽勋俱乐部论坛上说。“中国的速度超乎想象。这就是我感到强烈紧迫感的原因。”

金正宽表示，全球存储芯片市场预计在本十年末前扩大到约1万亿美元，使速度成为决定性的竞争优势。他补充说，由于半导体客户一旦采用产品就很少更换供应商，韩国在需求快速增长时期不能承受失去市场份额的代价。他说，韩国目前约占全球存储芯片市场的65%。

他还警告不要进行更广泛的行业利润再分配尝试。金正宽表示，政策制定者不应讨论如何分享行业的意外之财，而应专注于使企业能够再投资。

“如果我们试图分享芯片行业繁荣带来的金钱，那将是切开韩国最后一只下金蛋的鹅，”他说。“这些钱不是用来分的，而是必须再投资的钱。”

金正宽将韩国与中国进行了对比，称北京正在向该行业投入大量国家资源，中国的存储芯片制造商长鑫存储(CXMT Corp.)的投资额超过其年收入。他说，韩国芯片制造商仍然有财务能力进行扩张，但政府应通过投资电力和供水等基础设施来提供帮助。

金正宽还批评了将员工激励计划与营业利润挂钩的举措，认为股东是公司的所有者，他们的利益应该放在首位。他警告不要让这种想法在社会上更广泛地蔓延，并补充说，韩国已经过于迷恋芯片行业的成功，而应该更清醒地思考它想建立什么样的社会和经济体系。

“谁会投资或成为一家损害股东和投资者利益的公司的股东？”金正宽说。“最终，承担后果的将是公司的管理层和员工。”

### English Original

## Korea in Race to Keep Chips Edge as China Presses, Minister Says

South Korea risks losing its edge in semiconductors unless it helps chipmakers accelerate investment, as China pours unprecedented resources into the industry and intensifies the global technology race, Industry Minister Kim Jung-kwan said.

“What deeply worries me is China,” Kim said at a Kwanhun Club forum hosted by an association of senior South Korean journalists on Thursday. “China’s speed is beyond imagination. It’s why I feel a sense of intense urgency.”

The global memory-chip market is expected to expand to roughly $1 trillion before the end of the decade, making speed the defining competitive advantage, Kim said. Because semiconductor customers rarely switch suppliers once products are adopted, South Korea cannot afford to lose market share at a time when demand is growing rapidly, he added. The country currently accounts for about 65% of the global memory-memory chip market, Kim said.

He also cautioned against a broader push to redistribute the industry’s profits. Rather than discussing how to share the industry’s windfall, policymakers should focus on enabling companies to reinvest it, Kim said.

“If we try to share the money from the chip industry’s boom, we’ll be cutting open South Korea’s last goose that lays the golden egg,” he said. “This isn’t money to divide up. It’s money that has to be invested again.”

Kim contrasted South Korea with China, saying Beijing is channeling massive state resources into the industry and that Chinese memory-chip maker [CXMT Corp.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/688825%20CH%20Equity) is investing more than its annual revenue. Korean chipmakers still have the financial capacity to expand, but the government should help by investing in infrastructure such as power and water supplies, he said.

Kim also criticized moves to tie employee incentive schemes to operating profit, arguing that shareholders are the owners of companies and their interests should come first. He warned against the idea spreading more broadly across society, adding that South Korea has become too captivated by the chip sector’s success and should instead think more soberly about what kind of society and economic system it wants to build.

“Who would invest in, or become a shareholder of, a company that acts against the interests of its shareholders and investors?” Kim said. “In the end, it is the company’s management and employees who would bear the consequences.”
