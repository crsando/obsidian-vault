### 中文译文

## 中国吴清承诺进一步扩大与香港的市场互联互通

据中国证监会主席吴清称，中国正与香港当局密切合作，通过增加人民币计价股票柜台和房地产投资信托基金来扩大股市互联互通跨境交易机制。

在香港举行的中国离岸政府债券期货启动仪式上，吴清重申了北京对开放内地市场以及通过香港促进国际投资的承诺。两地监管机构正在[积极准备](https://apps.sfc.hk/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/news-and-announcements/news/corporate-news/doc?refNo=26PR122)技术框架，以便内地投资者通过南向股市互联互通交易双币种柜台和房地产投资信托基金。

吴清向投资者发出直接呼吁之际，监管机构正加大对跨境资本流动的审查力度，此前监管机构对在线券商进行了[打击](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-01-12/china-intensifies-tax-crackdown-on-vast-wealth-stashed-overseas)，并加强了对银行账户的监管。在此之前，上月底启动的对证监会前副主席方星海的高调调查也引发了广泛关注，方星海长期以来是全球金融机构在中国的重要联系人。

监管机构将继续支持中国企业通过在香港上市寻求国际扩张，同时支持符合条件的内地企业在华上市。今年香港上市活动激增，在大型中国股票销售的推动下达到六年高点。

此外，吴清表示，中国将支持两地市场在指数和ETF方面加强合作，并深化期货市场联系。他还表示，双方将在人才流动、监管协调、风险监测和可持续金融举措方面加强合作。

扩大交易机制旨在降低内地投资者的货币风险。尽管内地背景企业占香港股票市值的绝大多数，但[香港交易及结算所有限公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/388%20HK%20Equity)的交易仍主要以港元计价。

港交所于2023年6月推出双柜台模式，以便使用人民币进行平行交易，但采用率仍然有限，香港约2700家上市公司中只有几十家提供人民币柜台。

## China’s Wu Vows Further Expansion of Market Links to Hong Kong

China is working closely with Hong Kong authorities to expand the Stock Connect cross-border trading link by adding yuan-denominated stock counters and real estate investment trusts, according China Securities Regulatory Commission Chairman Wu Qing.

At an event in Hong Kong to launch Chinese offshore government bond futures, Wu reiterated Beijing’s commitment to opening mainland markets and facilitating international investment through the city. Regulators on both sides are [actively preparing](https://apps.sfc.hk/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/news-and-announcements/news/corporate-news/doc?refNo=26PR122) technical frameworks to enable mainland investors to trade dual-currency counters and REITs via Southbound Stock Connect.

Wu’s direct appeal to investors comes amid heightened scrutiny over cross-border capital flows, following a regulatory [crackdown](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-01-12/china-intensifies-tax-crackdown-on-vast-wealth-stashed-overseas) on online brokerages and tightened oversight of bank accounts. The appearance also follows a high-profile probe launched late last month into former CSRC Vice Chairman Fang Xinghai, long a key point of contact for global financial institutions in China.

The regulator will continue supporting Chinese companies seeking international expansion through Hong Kong listings while backing qualified local firms pursuing listings in China. Hong Kong listings have surged this year, reaching a six-year high on the back of major Chinese stock sales.

Additionally, Wu said China will support closer cooperation between the two markets on indexes and ETFs, and deepen links in futures market. The two sides will also strengthen cooperation on talent mobility, regulatory coordination, risk monitoring and sustainable finance initiatives, he said.

Expanding the trading link aims to reduce currency risks for mainland investors. While mainland-origin firms make up the vast majority of Hong Kong stock market capitalization, trading on [Hong Kong Exchanges & Clearing Ltd.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/388%20HK%20Equity) remains primarily denominated in Hong Kong dollars.

HKEX introduced a dual-counter model in June 2023 to enable parallel trading in Chinese yuan, but adoption has remained limited, with only several dozen of the city’s roughly 2,700 listed companies offering yuan counters.
