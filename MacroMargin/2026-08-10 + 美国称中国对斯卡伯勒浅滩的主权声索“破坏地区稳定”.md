### 中文译文

## 美国称中国对斯卡伯勒浅滩的主权声索“破坏地区稳定”

### **要点**

- 美国指责中国以“可疑”借口，通过在斯卡伯勒浅滩（黄岩岛）实施自然保护区并禁止菲律宾渔民进入争议水域，来推进其在南海的争议领土主张。
- 在中国海警与菲律宾渔船发生暴力冲突后，中国公布了该区域管理新规，未经事先批准禁止捕鱼和采矿。
- 美国国务院发言人称中国对该自然保护区的执法对更广泛的印太地区“破坏稳定”，并表示这是中国单方面试图以牺牲邻国利益为代价推进其领土和海洋主张。
美国周六指责中国以“可疑”借口，通过在斯卡伯勒浅滩（黄岩岛）实施自然保护区并禁止菲律宾渔民进入争议水域，来推进其在南海的争议领土主张。

在中国上周宣布该区域管理新规之前，中国海警与菲律宾渔船之间的暴力冲突已经[升级](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-24/china-coast-guard-reports-third-clash-with-philippines-this-week)。据新华社报道，中国发布了黄岩岛国家自然保护区管理规则，未经事先批准。

2012年，在与菲律宾围绕该浅滩发生对峙后，中国实际控制了该区域。该浅滩距离菲律宾海岸不到150英里（241.4公里）。菲律宾政府继续声称该区域为其领土。

“这是中国又一次单方面企图，利用可疑的环境和法律借口，以胁迫为后盾，以牺牲邻国利益为代价，推进其在南海的广泛领土和海洋主张，”美国国务院发言人汤米·皮戈特在一份[声明](https://www.state.gov/releases/office-of-the-spokesperson/2026/08/chinas-administrative-measures-at-scarborough-reef/)中表示。他称中国对该自然保护区的执法对更广泛的印太地区“破坏稳定”。

中国的规则是在7月菲律宾船只与中国海岸警卫队发生多次冲突后发布的。菲律宾海岸警卫队7月24日表示，中国海岸警卫队在黄岩岛使用水炮对付菲律宾海岸警卫队和渔业局船只。

自9月中国保护区设立以来，美国一直[反对](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-09-12/us-rejects-china-s-nature-reserve-plan-in-sea-near-philippines)其成立，以此支持菲律宾，试图遏制北京在该地区日益增长的影响力。北京在争议水域建造人工岛屿并修建建筑，以宣示其对南海的广泛主权主张。

## US Says China’s Scarborough Shoal Claim ‘Destabilizing’ Region

### **Takeaways**

- The US accused China of using "dubious" pretexts to press its disputed territorial claims in the South China Sea by enforcing a nature reserve at Scarborough Shoal and barring Filipino fishermen from entering the contested waters.
- China announced new rules for the area's management that prohibit fishing and mining without prior approval, following violent clashes between China's coast guard and Philippine fishing vessels.
- The US State Department spokesperson called China's enforcement of the nature reserve "destabilizing" for the broader Indo-Pacific region, saying it is a unilateral attempt by China to advance its territorial and maritime claims at the expense of its neighbors.
The US accused China on Saturday of using “dubious” pretexts to press its disputed territorial claims in the South China Sea by enforcing a nature reserve at Scarborough Shoal and barring Filipino fishermen from entering the contested waters.

Violent clashes between China’s coast guard and Philippine fishing vessels had already [escalated](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-24/china-coast-guard-reports-third-clash-with-philippines-this-week) before China announced new rules for the area’s management last week that Xinhua: China issues management rules for Huangyan Dao National Nature Reserve without prior approval, according to the Xinhua news agency.

China took effective control of the area in 2012 following a standoff with the Philippines over the shoal, located less than 150 miles (241.4 kilometers) off the Philippine coast. The Filipino government continues to claim the area as its own territory.

“This is yet another unilateral attempt by China to use dubious environmental and legal pretexts, backed by coercion, to advance its sweeping territorial and maritime claims in the South China Sea at the expense of its neighbors,” said US State Department spokesperson Tommy Pigott in a [statement](https://www.state.gov/releases/office-of-the-spokesperson/2026/08/chinas-administrative-measures-at-scarborough-reef/), calling China’s enforcement of the nature reserve “destabilizing” for the broader Indo-Pacific region.

China’s rules were published after multiple clashes between Filipino vessels and the Chinese coast guard in July. The Philippine coast guard said on July 24 that its Chinese counterpart used water cannons against Filipino coast guard and fisheries bureau vessels at Scarborough Shoal.

The US has [opposed](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-09-12/us-rejects-china-s-nature-reserve-plan-in-sea-near-philippines) the creation of the Chinese reserve since its inception in September, lending support to the Philippines in a bid to check Beijing’s growing influence in the region. Beijing has created manmade islands and put up buildings in contested waters in a bid to assert its sweeping claims over the South China Sea.
