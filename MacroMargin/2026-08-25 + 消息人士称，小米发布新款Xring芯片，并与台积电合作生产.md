## 消息人士称，小米发布新款Xring芯片，并与台积电合作生产

![Xiaomi store in Shanghai](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3c781cc90b148100a773c719abd05a13/3c781cc9-0b14-81ed-8ff8-fdf6d6a4d6d7-7fa272b7751a489c6de3d577dfd73a27.jpg)

北京，8月24日（路透社） - 中国智能手机制造商小米 [(1810.HK)，新窗口打开](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/1810.HK) 周一发布了其自研Xring手机处理器的新版本，押注对关键组件的更大控制将加强其供应链并减少对外部芯片供应商的依赖。

Xring O3的发布距离小米推出其首款自研智能手机处理器Xring O1仅一年，这标志着这家全球第三大智能手机厂商在开发自有芯片方面迈出了最新一步，加入了苹果[(AAPL.O), 打开新标签页](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/AAPL.O)、三星电子[(005930.KS), 打开新标签页](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/005930.KS)和华为的行列。

智能手机处理器又称片上系统（SoC），将计算、图形、AI处理和成像功能集成于单个组件中。

两位知情人士称，台积电[(2330.TW), 打开新标签页](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/2330.TW)将使用其3纳米生产工艺制造这款新芯片。

更多芯片自研可能让小米对产品功能拥有更大掌控力，并提升其与供应商的议价能力。

由于相关计划尚未公开，消息人士不愿具名。小米和台积电未立即回应关于该芯片制造商、生产工艺或出货目标的置评请求。

一位消息人士称，该芯片预计将用于小米即将推出的旗舰折叠屏手机，目标出货量为20万至30万部。

小米进军折叠屏手机这一价格更高的细分市场，可能加剧与华为的竞争。

研究公司Smart Analytics Global的数据显示，第二季度华为在中国出货160万部折叠屏手机，市场份额达68%，紧随其后的是荣耀（13.7%）和OPPO（8.5%）。

### 设备制造商推动自研芯片

小米的芯片布局反映了更广泛的行业趋势，随着高端智能手机竞争加剧，设备制造商寻求产品差异化并减少对高通 [(QCOM.O), 打开新标签页](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/QCOM.O) 和联发科 [(2454.TW), 打开新标签页](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/2454.TW) 等供应商的依赖。

这家总部位于北京的公司于2021年重启大型自研芯片的研发，并表示计划在未来十年内为此投入至少500亿元人民币（合70亿美元）。

小米上周在财报电话会议上表示，自Xring O1发布以来，搭载该芯片的设备（包括智能手机、平板电脑和手表）累计出货量已超过100万台。

消息人士称，自2025年5月发布以来，小米已售出约15万部基于Xring O1的智能手机。

### 内存成本推高价格

智能手机制造商正应对全球设备销售低迷，内存和零部件成本上涨推高价格并对需求造成压力。

根据标普全球旗下Visible Alpha的数据，小米在2026年上半年销售了6500万部手机，平均售价为1329元人民币（合197.74美元），而2025年同期销量为8400万部，平均售价1141元；2024年销量为8300万部，平均售价1123元。

根据研究公司国际数据公司（IDC）的数据，2026年全球智能手机出货量预计将下降14%。

### 人工智能模型与自动驾驶芯片

第三位消息人士称，小米还委托台积电制造另外两款Xring芯片：Xring O100，一款6纳米神经处理单元，将支持消费设备上的小米MiMo大语言模型；以及Xring D100，一款用于自动驾驶的3纳米芯片。

上述消息人士称，截至目前，小米在Xring芯片开发上的投资已超过200亿元人民币（30亿美元），而其半导体设计团队已从去年的2500人增至3000多人。

据小米称，O3已进入大规模生产阶段，而O100和D100已完成开发，计划于明年部署。

小米的[电动汽车业务](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/xiaomis-q2-profit-slides-426-component-cost-pressure-2026-08-18/)第二季度实现收入239亿元人民币，交付104,199辆汽车。

## Xiaomi launches new Xring chip, partners with TSMC for production, sources say

Staff members stand near the company logo at a Xiaomi store in Shanghai, China November 1, 2021. Picture taken November 1, 2021. REUTERS/Aly Song/File Photo [Purchase Licensing Rights, opens new tab](https://www.reutersconnect.com/item/xiaomi-store-in-shanghai/dGFnOnJldXRlcnMuY29tLDIwMjY6bmV3c21sX1JDMlU0TkFYVDVYUQ%3D%3D/?utm_medium=rcom-article-media&utm_campaign=rcom-rcp-lead)

- Summary
- Companies
- Smartphone maker's Xring O3 chip made using TSMC's 3-nanometre process, sources say
- Xiaomi targets 200,000 to 300,000 Xring O3 shipments, source says
- Xring O3 expected to power upcoming foldable phone, in challenge to Huawei
- Fellow smartphone makers Apple, Samsung and Huawei also developing their own chips
BEIJING, Aug 24 (Reuters) - Chinese smartphone maker Xiaomi [(1810.HK), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/1810.HK) on Monday unveiled a new version of its in-house ‌Xring handset processor, betting that greater control over key components will strengthen its supply chain and reduce reliance on external chip suppliers.

The Xring O3 comes a year after Xiaomi launched its first proprietary smartphone processor, the Xring O1, marking the latest step in the world's third-largest smartphone vendor's push to join rivals Apple [(AAPL.O), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/AAPL.O), Samsung Electronics [(005930.KS), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/005930.KS) and Huawei in developing its own chips.

Smartphone processors, also known as systems-on-chip (SoC), combine computing, graphics, AI processing and imaging functions in a single component.

TSMC [(2330.TW), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/2330.TW) will manufacture the new chip using its 3-nanometre production technology, two people familiar with the matter said.

Developing more chips in-house could give Xiaomi greater ⁠control over product features and improve its bargaining power with suppliers.

The sources declined to be identified because the plans are not public. Xiaomi and TSMC did not immediately respond to requests for comment on the chip's manufacturer, production technology or shipment targets.

One source said the chip is expected to power Xiaomi's upcoming flagship folding phone, with shipments targeted at 200,000 to 300,000 units.

Its expansion into foldable phones, a higher-priced market segment, could intensify competition with Huawei.

Huawei shipped 1.6 million foldable phones in China in the second quarter, giving it a 68% market share, followed by Honor with 13.7% and Oppo with 8.5%, according to research firm Smart Analytics Global.

### DEVICE MAKERS PUSH FOR IN-HOUSE CHIPS

Xiaomi's chip push reflects a broader industry trend as device makers seek to differentiate their products and reduce dependence on suppliers such as Qualcomm [(QCOM.O), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/QCOM.O) and MediaTek [(2454.TW), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/2454.TW) amid intensifying competition in premium smartphones.

The Beijing-based company restarted development of large, in-house chips in 2021 and has said it plans to invest at least 50 billion ‌yuan ($7.0 billion) ⁠over a decade in the effort.

Xiaomi said on an earnings call last week that cumulative shipments of devices powered by the Xring O1, including smartphones, tablets and watches, had surpassed 1 million units since its launch.

The sources said Xiaomi has sold about 150,000 smartphones based on the Xring O1 since its May 2025 launch.

### MEMORY COSTS PUSHING UP PRICES

Smartphone makers are grappling with a global downturn in device sales as rising memory and component costs drive up prices and weigh on demand.

Xiaomi sold 65 million handsets in the first half of 2026 at ⁠an average price of 1,329 yuan ($197.74), compared with 84 million units sold at an average price of 1,141 yuan in the same period of 2025 and 83 million units at 1,123 yuan in 2024, according to data from Visible Alpha by S&P Global.

Global smartphone shipments are expected to decline 14% in 2026, according to research firm International Data Corporation.

### AI MODEL AND AUTONOMOUS ⁠DRIVING CHIPS

A third source said Xiaomi had also contracted TSMC to manufacture two other Xring chips: the Xring O100, a 6-nm neural processing unit that will support Xiaomi's MiMo large language model on consumer devices, and the Xring D100, a 3-nm chip for autonomous driving.

Xiaomi has invested more than 20 billion yuan ($3 billion) in Xring ⁠chip development so far, while its semiconductor design unit has grown to more than 3,000 people from 2,500 last year, the source said.

According to Xiaomi, the O3 has already entered mass production, while the O100 and D100 have completed development and are scheduled for deployment next year.

Xiaomi's [electric-vehicle business](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/xiaomis-q2-profit-slides-426-component-cost-pressure-2026-08-18/) generated 23.9 billion yuan in revenue and delivered 104,199 vehicles in the second quarter.
