### 中文译文

## 韩国在特朗普推动与金正恩会谈之际寻求中国帮助

### **要点**

- 在总统唐纳德·特朗普推动与金正恩会面之际，韩国寻求中国支持重启与朝鲜的对话。
- 中国外交部长王毅告诉韩国外长，中国希望看到朝鲜和韩国走向和平共处。
- 当被问及北京是否会帮助调解特朗普与金正恩的峰会时，王毅表示，美国应当改变其长期以来对朝鲜的敌对政策。
尽管平壤否认有任何高层接触，但在总统唐纳德·特朗普推动与金正恩会面之际，韩国仍寻求中国支持重启与朝鲜的对话。

外交部长赵显（Cho Hyun）周三在首尔与中国外长王毅会晤时，呼吁中国发挥建设性作用，推动朝鲜迅速重返对话。王毅当天早些时候抵达韩国，这是五年来他对这一邻国的首次双边访问。

王毅告诉赵显，中国希望看到朝鲜和韩国走向和平共处。中国外交部：中国希望看到朝韩走向和平共处。王毅此次访问正值特朗普[寻求](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-19/north-korea-says-no-trump-kim-talks-still-sees-us-as-hostile)重启与朝鲜的对话，同时缩减与韩国的联合军事演习，平壤长期以来谴责这些演习是战争预演。

朝鲜领导人金正恩的妹妹金与正在通过官方媒体朝中社发表的[声明](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-19/north-korea-says-no-trump-kim-talks-still-sees-us-as-hostile)中表示，特朗普与金正恩之间没有直接沟通。

当被记者问及北京是否会帮助调解特朗普与金正恩之间的峰会时，中国外交部长对韩联社表示：（第二次更新）中国外长承诺继续为朝鲜半岛和平发挥“建设性作用”，这是双方决定的问题。他说：“美国应改变对朝鲜的长期敌对政策。”

李在明总统周四还会见了王毅，并要求他与高级助手就双边和半岛问题继续保持密切战略沟通，李的办公室表示。

平壤强化了不一定愿意与特朗普合作的信息，周四下午晚些时候向其东海岸附近发射了几枚短程弹道导弹，仅本月就已是第三次导弹发射。

最近几个月，在金正恩大幅扩充核武库并更接近莫斯科之后，北京一直在努力重新确立其对胆大妄为的朝鲜的影响力。习近平主席六月七年以来首次访问平壤，凸显了这些努力，当时他没有公开提及金正恩的核计划。

王毅的首尔之行也为北京提供了进一步稳定与韩国关系的机会，同时利用了首尔与华盛顿之间日益紧张的双边贸易关系。

韩国外交部表示，中国正考虑在11月于深圳举行的亚太经合组织峰会期间安排习近平与李在明的会晤。当被问及可能的会晤时，中国外交部没有立即回应置评请求。

在周三的会议上，王毅表示中国希望扩大与韩国在包括人工智能等领域的合作，并愿意加快两国自由贸易协定第二阶段的谈判。

## South Korea Seeks China’s Help as Trump Pushes for Kim Talks

### **Takeaways**

- South Korea sought China's support in reviving talks with North Korea as President Donald Trump pushes for a meeting with Kim Jong Un.
- China's Foreign Minister Wang Yi told his South Korean counterpart that China hopes to see the two Koreas move toward peaceful coexistence.
- Wang said the US should change its longstanding hostile policy toward North Korea when asked if Beijing might help mediate a summit between Trump and Kim.
South Korea sought China’s support in reviving talks with North Korea as President Donald Trump pushes for a meeting with Kim Jong Un despite Pyongyang’s denial of any top-level contact.

Foreign Minister Cho Hyun called for China to play a constructive role in facilitating North Korea’s swift return to dialogue during a meeting with his Chinese counterpart, Wang Yi, in Seoul on Wednesday. Wang arrived in South Korea earlier in the day for his first bilateral visit to the neighboring nation in five years.

Wang told Cho that China hopes to see the two Koreas move toward peaceful coexistence, the Chinese Foreign Ministry China Hopes to See N., S. Korea Moving to Peaceful Coexistence. Wang’s visit comes as Trump [seeks](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-19/north-korea-says-no-trump-kim-talks-still-sees-us-as-hostile) to restart dialogue with North Korea while scaling back joint military drills with South Korea that Pyongyang has long denounced as a war rehearsal.

Kim Yo Jong, the North Korean leader’s sister, said in a [statement](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-19/north-korea-says-no-trump-kim-talks-still-sees-us-as-hostile) issued through state media KCNA that there’s been no direct communication between Trump and Kim.

President Lee Jae Myung also met with Wang on Thursday and asked him to continue close strategic communication with his top aides on bilateral and peninsula issues, Lee’s office said.

Pyongyang reinforced the message that it isn’t necessarily willing to play ball with Trump, by [firing](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-20/korea-s-lee-hails-trump-s-determination-for-peninsula-peace) several short-range ballistic missiles off its east coast late Thursday afternoon, marking its third missile launch this month alone.

Beijing has been moving to reassert its influence over an emboldened North Korea in recent months after Kim drastically expanded his nuclear arsenal and drew closer to Moscow. Those efforts were highlighted by President Xi Jinping’s first visit to Pyongyang in seven years in June, when he refrained from making any public reference to Kim’s nuclear program.

Wang’s Seoul trip also offers Beijing a chance to further stabilize ties with South Korea while capitalizing on simmering tensions over trade between Seoul and Washington.

China is considering a meeting between Xi and Lee on the sidelines of the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation summit in Shenzhen in November, South Korea’s Foreign Ministry said. China’s Foreign Ministry didn’t immediately respond to a request for comment when asked on the potential meeting.

At Wednesday’s meeting, Wang said China hopes to expand cooperation with South Korea in areas including artificial intelligence and is willing to speed up negotiations on the second phase of the two countries’ free trade agreement.
