### 中文译文

## 中国钢铁工业协会称推动人民币定价将有助于铁矿石

作者：[Katharine Gemmell](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AUtYLUwJKY0/katharine-gemmell) 2026年8月6日 上午10:58 GMT+8

中国主要钢铁行业组织呼吁在铁矿石市场中更多采用人民币计价指数定价，称这一转变将更好地反映这一大宗商品最大用户国的供需情况。

中国钢铁工业协会（CISA）在其微信公众号上的声明中表示，推广使用人民币将与现有的美元计价基准形成互补，基于大型港口现货市场的交易提供额外参考。

“铁矿石市场正在发生深刻变化，一个更公平、更透明、更可持续的市场秩序正在逐步形成，”CISA在周三晚间发布的声明中表示。

中国正在努力提高其在全球铁矿石市场中的影响力，该市场据其称由澳大利亚和巴西的少数几家主要生产商主导。CISA已[支持](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-12-22/china-steel-group-urges-domestic-pricing-in-iron-ore-contracts)国有背景的买家中国矿产资源集团有限公司（CMRG）在与主要矿商的供应谈判中使用国内价格基准。

中国矿产资源集团已[确保更多使用](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-29/china-succeeds-in-adding-yuan-pricing-to-bhp-iron-ore-contracts)人民币定价于与必和必拓集团（BHP Group）的供应安排中，此类条款预计仍将是与福特斯克有限公司（Fortescue Ltd.）和力拓集团（Rio Tinto Group）谈判的关键点。福特斯克目前正与中国矿产资源集团谈判，且一直[面临压力](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-15/china-turns-on-the-australian-iron-ore-miner-it-helped-grow)。

CISA表示，包括几内亚[西芒杜项目](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-03/guinea-s-simandou-iron-exports-surge-six-months-after-first-ore)在内的新铁矿石供应的出现正有助于市场多元化。此外，该协会表示，矿商与中国钢铁制造商之间利润的更好分配将使市场更具可持续性。

## China Steel Group Says Push for Yuan-Pricing Would Help Iron Ore

By [Katharine Gemmell](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AUtYLUwJKY0/katharine-gemmell)August 6, 2026 at 10:58 AM GMT+8

China’s main steel-industry group called for greater use of yuan-denominated index pricing in the iron ore market, saying such a shift would better reflect supply and demand in the top user of the commodity.

Promoting the use of Chinese currency would complement existing US dollar-denominated benchmarks by providing an additional reference based on transactions in the large port-side spot market, the [China Iron and Steel Association](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/2096349D:CH) said in a statement on its WeChat account.

“The iron ore market is undergoing profound changes, and a fairer, more transparent, and more sustainable market order is gradually taking shape,” CISA said in the statement, which was issued late on Wednesday.

China is moving to boost its influence in the global iron ore market, which it says is dominated by a handful of major producers in Australia and Brazil. CISA has [supported](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-12-22/china-steel-group-urges-domestic-pricing-in-iron-ore-contracts) the use of domestic price benchmarks by state-backed buyer China Mineral Resources Group Co. in supply negotiations with top miners.

CMRG has already secured [greater use](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-29/china-succeeds-in-adding-yuan-pricing-to-bhp-iron-ore-contracts) of yuan-based pricing in supply arrangements with BHP Group, and such terms are expected to remain a key point in negotiations with Fortescue Ltd. and Rio Tinto Group. Fortescue is currently in talks with CMRG, and has been [facing pressure](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-15/china-turns-on-the-australian-iron-ore-miner-it-helped-grow).

The emergence of new iron ore supply, including from the [Simandou project](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-03/guinea-s-simandou-iron-exports-surge-six-months-after-first-ore) in Guinea, was helping diversify the market, according to CISA. In addition, a better distribution of profits between miners and Chinese steelmakers would make the market more sustainable, the association said.
