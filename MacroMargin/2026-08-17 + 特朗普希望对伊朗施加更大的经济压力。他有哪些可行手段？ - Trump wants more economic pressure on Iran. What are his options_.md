> 📌 **核心摘要**
>
> 特朗普政府寻求加大对伊朗经济压力，专家分析可能涉及制裁中国炼油厂和银行、陆地封锁及附加关税等手段。
> • 美国财长贝森特称准备对伊朗采取“前所未有”的措施。
> • 自伊朗战争爆发以来，美国已实施海上、能源和金融制裁，并开始海上封锁。
> • 智库专家提出多项可行选项：制裁中国独立炼油厂、制裁中国银行、继续“打地鼠”式制裁、陆地封锁和附加关税。
> • 制裁中国炼油厂和银行可能引发北京报复，并影响中美关系。
> • 陆地封锁需要伊朗邻国配合，执行难度大。
> • 参议院已通过法案赋予特朗普新关税权力，但众议院通过存在挑战。

WASHINGTON, Aug 16 (Reuters) - U.S. President Donald Trump on Friday vowed to hit Iran hard economically, a day after Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said that Washington would impose measures on Tehran that have "never been seen" as soon as next week.
华盛顿，8 月 16 日（路透社）——美国总统唐纳德·特朗普周五誓言要对伊朗实施严厉的经济制裁。此前一天，美国财政部长斯科特·贝森特表示，华盛顿将在下周立即对德黑兰采取“前所未有”的措施。

The United States, United Nations and European Union have applied sanctions, implemented trade embargoes and frozen assets since the late 1970s over Iran's nuclear program, human rights violations and support for militant groups.
自 20 世纪 70 年代末以来，由于伊朗的核计划、侵犯人权行为以及支持激进组织，美国、联合国和欧盟一直对其实施制裁、贸易禁令并冻结其资产。

Since the Iran war began in February, Washington has levied additional maritime, energy and financial sanctions ‌and started a naval blockade.
自 2 月伊朗战争爆发以来，华盛顿又实施了更多的海上、能源及金融制裁，并开始了海上封锁。

Data from the U.S. Treasury Department's Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC) shows the agency has imposed sanctions on more than 1,000 people, vessels and aircraft since Trump began his second term.
美国财政部外国资产控制办公室的数据显示，自特朗普开始第二个任期以来，该机构已对 1,000 多名个人、船只及飞机实施了制裁。

Recent measures have targeted Iran's shadow oil fleet; shipping insurers; entities and people enabling Iran's acquisition of weapons; and digital exchanges, freezing an estimated $500 billion in Iran-linked cryptocurrency.
近期的制裁措施针对的是伊朗的“影子石油船队”、航运保险公司、那些协助伊朗获取武器的机构与个人，以及各类数字交易平台，已冻结了约 5000 亿美元与伊朗相关的加密货币资产。

专家们表示，特朗普政府还可以考虑采取这些措施：

### SANCTIONS ON CHINESE 'TEAPOT' REFINERS
对中国“茶壶”炼油厂的制裁

[Chinese independent refineries](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/us-imposes-sanctions-chinese-teapot-refinery-buying-iranian-oil-2026-04-24/) known as "teapots" account for a quarter of Chinese refinery capacity. They operate with narrow and sometimes negative profit margins.
中国那些被称为“茶壶”型的独立炼油厂占了中国炼油总产能的四分之一，其运营利润空间极为狭窄，有时甚至出现亏损。

China buys more than 80% of Iran's shipped oil, according to 2025 data from analytics firm Kpler. Independent refiners absorb much of this trade, exposing them to so-called secondary measures ⁠that penalize entities helping a primary sanctions target.
根据分析机构 Kpler 在 2025 年发布的数据，中国购买了伊朗出口石油的 80%以上。这些石油大多由独立炼油厂接收，因此它们也会面临所谓的“次级制裁”措施——即针对那些协助受主要制裁对象的国家或企业的惩罚性举措。

Past U.S. sanctions have deterred larger independent refiners from buying Iranian oil. But the independent refineries are somewhat immune since they have little exposure to the U.S. financial system, sanctions experts say.
制裁专家指出，此前的美国制裁已经阻止了大型独立炼油厂购买伊朗石油。但由于这些独立炼油厂与美国金融体系的关联度较低，因此在一定程度上能够免受制裁影响。

### SANCTIONS ON CHINESE BANKS
对中国银行的制裁

OFAC has imposed secondary sanctions on smaller China- and Hong Kong-based entities accused of processing billions of dollars in Iranian oil and helping to fund weapons procurement.
美国财政部外国资产控制办公室已对一些总部位于中国和香港的较小企业实施了二次制裁，这些企业被指处理了价值数十亿美元的伊朗石油，并协助为武器采购提供资金。

![图片](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/trump-wants-more-economic-pressure-iran-what-are-his-options-2026-08-16/undefined)

Treasury has warned two larger Chinese banks they could face secondary sanctions if Iranian funds were found moving through their systems, but has stopped short of designating them.
美国财政部已警告两家规模较大的中国银行，如果发现伊朗资金通过其系统流动，这些银行可能会面临二次制裁，但尚未正式将其列入制裁名单。

Hitting those two banks, which U.S. officials have not publicly identified, or imposing other sanctions could have a chilling effect on bigger financial institutions, sanctions experts said, although they warned it could also trigger retaliatory actions by Beijing.
制裁专家表示，打击那些尚未被美国官员公开点名的两家银行或实施其他制裁措施，可能会对规模更大的金融机构产生威慑作用，但他们同时警告说，这也可能引发北京的报复行动。

Trump administration officials have sought to play down tensions between Washington and Beijing ahead of an expected meeting between Trump and President Xi Jinping later this year. They worry that China could curtail exports of critical minerals that are essential to advanced technology production at a time when the U.S. and Western allies are still trying to develop their own supplies.
在特朗普与习近平主席预计于今年晚些时候会面之际，特朗普政府官员试图缓和华盛顿与北京之间的紧张关系。他们担心，在美国及其西方盟友仍在努力建立自身供应渠道的当下，中国可能会限制那些对先进技术生产至关重要的关键矿物的出口。

### 'WHACK-A-MOLE' “打地鼠”式制裁

The United States could continue targeting Iranian individuals and ‌entities, as well ⁠as others in China and the Gulf, that are helping Tehran evade sanctions to collect revenues for its war effort.
美国可能会继续针对那些协助德黑兰规避制裁、为其战争行动筹集资金的伊朗个人与实体，以及中国和海湾地区的相关方。

Treasury recently issued sanctions against firms that are springing up to facilitate Iran's trading of oil revenue for imports. But such measures amount to a "whack-a-mole" approach that has not altered Iran's behavior, said Brett Erickson, managing principal of Obsidian Risk Advisors, noting that Tehran simply creates new entities to replace them.
美国财政部最近对那些协助伊朗将石油收入用于进口交易的公司实施了制裁。不过，Obsidian Risk Advisors 的首席分析师布雷特·埃里克森表示，这类措施不过是“打地鼠式”的做法，根本无法改变伊朗的行为，因为德黑兰只会创建新的公司来取代被制裁的那些公司。

Miad Maleki, a sanctions expert with the Foundation for Defense of Democracies, said Bessent was likely signaling a sharpened enforcement push against oil shippers, purchasers and currency exchangers who help Iran pay for its imports.
民主防御基金会的制裁问题专家米亚德·马莱基表示，贝森特此举很可能是意在加大力度打击那些协助伊朗支付进口货款的石油运输商、购买方以及货币兑换机构。

Further aviation sanctions were also possible, aimed ⁠at degrading Iran's ability to move trade now that the U.S. has blockaded shipping via the Strait of Hormuz, he added.
他补充说，鉴于美国已封锁霍尔木兹海峡的航运，还可能进一步实施航空制裁，以此削弱伊朗的贸易运输能力。

### LAND BLOCKADE 陆地封锁

Some U.S. and Israeli officials have floated the prospect of a land blockade, which would require assistance from Iran's neighbors: Iraq, Turkey, Pakistan, Afghanistan, Turkmenistan, Azerbaijan and Armenia.
一些美国和以色列官员提出了实施陆地封锁的可能性，而这需要伊朗的邻国——伊拉克、土耳其、巴基斯坦、阿富汗、土库曼斯坦、阿塞拜疆和亚美尼亚——提供协助。

The Trump administration has varying degrees of closeness with all those countries except Afghanistan, but that border is mountainous and extremely difficult to patrol anyway.
特朗普政府与除阿富汗以外的所有国家都保持着不同程度的密切关系，而阿富汗地处山区，本来就极难进行巡逻。

Trump may ⁠have leverage over Pakistan, which recently sought a $10 billion currency swap line from Treasury, and Turkey, which is seeking to rejoin the U.S. F-35 warplane program.
特朗普或许能对巴基斯坦施加压力——该国最近向美国财政部申请了 100 亿美元的货币互换额度；同时也能对土耳其施压，因为后者正试图重新加入美国的 F-35 战机项目。

A land blockade could increase pressure on the Iranian people by halting their imports of food, energy and textiles, but experts say such a move would be difficult to execute and might not result in protests or internal pressure.
实施陆地封锁会通过切断伊朗的食品、能源和纺织品进口来增加该国人民的压力，但专家认为此举很难实施，而且未必能引发抗议或内部压力。

### SECONDARY TARIFFS 附加关税

Trump has repeatedly ⁠threatened tariffs on goods from countries that do business with Iran, although the Supreme Court struck down the legal basis for such taxes.
特朗普多次威胁要对与伊朗有贸易往来的国家的产品征收关税，尽管最高法院已经否定了此类征税的法律依据。

The Senate passed a sweeping Russia sanctions bill last week that included new Iran sanctions and would give Trump new tariff powers that he could potentially use against countries that aid Iran's commerce and weapons procurement.
上周，参议院通过了一项针对俄罗斯的全面制裁法案，其中包含了新的对伊朗的制裁措施，同时也赋予了特朗普新的关税立法权，他可以借此制裁那些协助伊朗进行贸易和武器采购的国家。

That legislation must still pass the U.S. House of Representatives, which could [prove challenging](https://www.reuters.com/world/tariff-provisions-could-doom-long-awaited-russia-sanctions-bill-2026-07-31/) given widespread concerns among Democrats and some Republicans about the tariff measures.
该法案仍需通过美国众议院，但由于民主党和部分共和党人对这些关税措施存在普遍担忧，这一过程可能会面临挑战。
