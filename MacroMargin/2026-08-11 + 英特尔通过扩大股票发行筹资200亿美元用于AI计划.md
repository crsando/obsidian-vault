## 英特尔通过扩大股票发行筹资200亿美元用于AI计划

### **要点**

- 英特尔公司通过扩大股票发行筹资200亿美元，比周一上午宣布交易时的目标高出三分之一。
- 据知情人士透露，此次股票发行吸引了超过1000亿美元的需求，定价为每股95美元，较上周五收盘价折价6.5%。
- 英特尔的交易显示出投资者对人工智能供应链股票需求的韧性，该公司正在积累现金储备，以便在人工智能热潮中发挥更核心的作用。
英特尔公司通过扩大股票发行筹资200亿美元，比周一上午宣布交易时的目标高出三分之一。

该公司将此次发行定价为每股95美元，根据一份题为“英特尔宣布扩大规模并定价200亿美元普通股发行”的公司公告。据彭博计算，这较上周五收盘价折价6.5%。据知情人士透露，此次股票发行吸引了超过1000亿美元的需求。

英特尔的交易显示出投资者对人工智能供应链股票需求的韧性。今年美国规模最大的股票发行中，大多由受益于人工智能支出热潮的公司主导。[Alphabet Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/GOOGL%20US%20Equity)正通过股票发行筹集至多850亿美元，包括所谓的市价发行和股票挂钩交易。[Oracle Corp.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/ORCL%20US%20Equity)的融资计划包括200亿美元的市价发行。

韩国存储芯片制造商[SK Hynix Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/000660%20KS%20Equity)通过首次发行美国存托凭证筹资265亿美元，这是外国公司在美国交易所上市的最大规模发行。其竞争对手CXMT Corp.上个月通过接近中国纪录的首次公开募股筹资约99亿美元，并随后成为内地市值最大的上市公司。

英特尔股价周二在纽约开盘前下跌1%，此前周一收盘价为97.52美元。** **今年迄今仍上涨约164%，此前首席执行官陈立武（Lip-Bu Tan）将清理英特尔财务列为优先事项。这些努力包括吸引来自美国政府甚至芯片竞争对手如[英伟达公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/NVDA%20US%20Equity)的外部投资。

英特尔正在积累现金储备，目标是在人工智能热潮中发挥更核心的作用。数据中心的全球扩张已经提振了对其通用处理器的需求，但该公司在人工智能处理器市场上一直难以与英伟达和[超威半导体公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/AMD%20US%20Equity)直接竞争。英特尔还需要资金建设工厂网络，以实现其成为科技行业外包制造中心的目标。

根据早些时候的一份声明，[摩根大通公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/JPM%20US%20Equity)、[高盛集团](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/GS%20US%20Equity)、[摩根士丹利](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/MS%20US%20Equity)和[花旗集团](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/C%20US%20Equity)正在参与此次发行。

## Intel Raises $20 Billion in Upsized Share Sale for AI Plans

### **Takeaways**

- Intel Corp. raised $20 billion in an upsized share sale, a third more than it was targeting when it announced the deal Monday morning.
- The share sale drew more than $100 billion in demand, people familiar with the matter said, and was priced at $95 per share, representing a discount of 6.5% to Friday’s closing price.
- Intel’s deal shows the resilience of investor demand for stocks along the artificial intelligence supply chain, with the company building up cash reserves to take on a more central role in the artificial intelligence boom.
Intel Corp. raised $20 billion in an upsized share sale, a third more than it was targeting when it announced the deal Monday morning.

The chipmaker priced the offering at $95 per share, according to a company Intel Announces Upsize and Pricing of $20 Billion Common Stock Offering. That represents a discount of 6.5% to Friday’s closing price, according to Bloomberg calculations. The share sale drew more than $100 billion in demand, people familiar with the matter said.

Intel’s deal shows the resilience of investor demand for stocks along the artificial intelligence supply chain. The year’s biggest US equity offerings have been dominated by companies riding the boom in AI spending. [Alphabet Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/GOOGL%20US%20Equity) is in the process of raising as much as $85 billion through equity offerings, including so-called at-the-market share sales and equity-linked deals. And [Oracle Corp.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/ORCL%20US%20Equity)’s fundraising plans include a $20 billion at-the-market sale.

South Korean memory chipmaker [SK Hynix Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/000660%20KS%20Equity) raised $26.5 billion in its debut offering of American depositary receipts, the largest-ever listing by a foreign company on a US exchange. Its rival CXMT Corp. raised about $9.9 billion last month in a near Chinese record initial public offering and subsequently became the largest mainland-listed company.

Intel’s shares fell 1% before the market opened in New York on Tuesday, after Monday’s close of $97.52.** **They remain up roughly 164% this year, after Chief Executive Officer Lip-Bu Tan made cleaning up Intel’s finances a priority. The effort has included attracting outside investments from the US government and even chip rivals such as [Nvidia Corp.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/NVDA%20US%20Equity)

Intel is building up cash reserves with a goal of taking on a more central role in the artificial intelligence boom. The worldwide expansion of data centers has already bolstered demand for its general processors, but the company has struggled to compete directly with Nvidia and [Advanced Micro Devices Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/AMD%20US%20Equity) in the market for AI processors. Intel also needs funds to build out a factory network to meet its goal of serving as an outsourced manufacturing hub for the tech industry.

[JPMorgan Chase & Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/JPM%20US%20Equity), [Goldman Sachs Group Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/GS%20US%20Equity), [Morgan Stanley](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/MS%20US%20Equity) and [Citigroup Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/C%20US%20Equity) are working on the offering, according to a statement earlier.
