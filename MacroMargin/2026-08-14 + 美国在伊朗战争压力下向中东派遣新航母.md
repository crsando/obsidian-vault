### 中文译文

## 美国在伊朗战争压力下向中东派遣新航母

![An aircraft launching from the flight deck of the USS Abraham Lincoln last month.](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3bc81cc90b148182b44ffd1182ba673c/3bc81cc9-0b14-814f-9770-f9c9ba9b106c-d84ca02ca929d938be38df5acb6f68e9.jpg)

上个月，一架飞机从亚伯拉罕·林肯号航空母舰的飞行甲板上起飞。美国海军

美国官员表示，美军正准备将亚伯拉罕·林肯号替换为乔治·华盛顿号，作为计划中的中东部署的一部分，此举正值人们对林肯号上的生活条件以及伊朗战争期间部队承受的压力日益担忧之际。

几位议员表示，他们担心有关航母上缺乏食物和个人用品以及管道堵塞的报道，因为这艘航母的部署时间已延长至第九个月。

一位美国官员周四证实，8月初有一人从林肯号上落水，但很快获救。

参议员理查德·布卢门撒尔（康涅狄格州民主党人）表示，林肯号已部署超过250天，且已有200天未停靠港口，创下了连续在航天数纪录。他致信国防部长皮特·赫格塞斯和代理海军部长洪·曹。

布卢门撒尔写道：“有广泛报道称，基本物资短缺、水污染、管道问题、心理健康恶化、甲板安全隐患以及邮件系统中断，导致许多寄往该船的爱心包裹在运输途中丢失数月。”

![Senator Richard Blumenthal](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3bc81cc90b148182b44ffd1182ba673c/3bc81cc9-0b14-8141-9b74-e33013f345bb-e6f9d3b3d53e69b9c6f63f996672f8d8.jpg)

参议员理查德·布卢门撒尔致信军方高层，指出林肯号上条件的恶化。Graeme Sloan/Bloomberg News

众议员马林·斯图茨曼（共和党，印第安纳州）表示，他计划向五角大楼和政府寻求最新情况。“归根结底，我们知道服役人员需要得到慰藉，知道自己得到了妥善照顾，”他在周四接受CNN采访时说。

赫格塞斯周四在巴拿马巴拿马城的一艘海军舰艇上与记者交谈时表示，对林肯号的担忧被“完全曲解了”。

“我们确保每一艘军舰、每一组舰员、每一位舰长在每一时刻都能得到我们能提供的一切，”赫格塞斯说。他还补充道，美国可以无限期维持对伊朗港口的封锁，因为美国可以让军舰轮换进出战区。

美国官员表示，林肯号上的情况在战时部署中并不罕见，但一些官员承认，数月不进港可能会给部队带来压力。通常，航空母舰在标准部署期间会停靠港口补充物资，并让船员有机会休息和调整。

杰拉尔德·R·福特号航母在海上航行了11个月，直到5月才返回母港，成为自苏联解体以来[部署时间最长的航空母舰](https://www.wsj.com/livecoverage/iran-us-strait-of-hormuz-blockade-updates/card/uss-gerald-r-ford-longest-deployed-aircraft-carrier-in-post-cold-war-era-PIozQdleHLlhMQzIKD5M?mod=article_inline)。该舰于3月，在伊朗战争最激烈时，在[克罗地亚斯普利特](https://www.wsj.com/livecoverage/iran-war-news-trump/card/largest-u-s-warship-set-to-rejoin-iran-war-zpwSZSbKKbexPmMM048n?mod=article_inline)停靠了几天，进行维护并让水手们有时间休息。尽管如此，漫长的部署和艰苦的条件仍然[让服役人员不堪重负](https://www.wsj.com/us-news/missed-funerals-and-blocked-toilets-iran-deployment-takes-a-toll-on-u-s-sailors-7e230962?mod=article_inline)。

据一位官员称，林肯号轮换出中东的计划早在生活条件问题被提出之前就已制定。与此同时，特朗普总统一直试图对伊朗施加[经济压力](https://www.wsj.com/world/middle-east/trump-shifts-to-economic-pressure-again-as-iran-wont-meet-his-demands-4445c840?mod=article_inline)，包括由大量美国军舰对伊朗港口实施长期封锁。

林肯号于11月开始其预定部署，并在美国对伊朗开战前，于[1月](https://www.wsj.com/world/middle-east/us-lincoln-carrier-arrives-central-command-48977187?mod=article_inline)被改派至中东。该航母及其搭载的舰载机在美国的“史诗愤怒行动”轰炸战役中发挥了重要作用，此后该舰一直参与美国对伊朗港口的封锁。

参议员鲁本·加列戈（民主党，亚利桑那州）表示，他正寻求与一个两党参议员代表团对林肯号进行监督访问，以调查舰上情况。“这些水手理应知道他们何时能回家，”曾参加伊拉克战争的加列戈本周在 X 上表示。

一位海军官员表示，军方并未在林肯号（USS Lincoln）上经历心理健康问题的上升，也未观察到舰上自杀意念或未遂事件的增加。这位官员说，海军认真对待每位军人的福祉，并配备有宗教、医疗和心理健康专业人员，以便在问题出现时进行评估和处理。

驻日美国海军“乔治·华盛顿”号（USS George Washington）航母近几周一直在南海活动，最近还在越南进行了港口访问。该航母在该地区的部署是美国军方在太平洋地区对抗中国影响力的一个例子。据船舶追踪数据和美国官员称，该航母最近与导弹驱逐舰一起向西穿过马六甲海峡。

分析人士称，“华盛顿”号前往中东将在太平洋留下一个暂时的空白，而中国已在太平洋部署了自己的航母，并正在寻求对周边国家施加影响。

前海军军官、现为研究国家安全问题的智库哈德逊研究所高级研究员的布莱恩·克拉克（Bryan Clark）表示：“通常情况下，乔治·华盛顿号会进行巡逻，展示旗帜，并向菲律宾和日本等国表明我们的决心和支持。我们将放弃所有这些影响与当地邻国关系的战略机会。”

官员和分析人士表示，航母轮换也引发了对海军应对全球事件和长期维持行动能力的担忧。美国海军拥有11艘现役航母，它们返回母港后需要维护。

克拉克表示，由于伊朗战争，舰队可能需要三年时间才能恢复到理想的战备状态。他说：“如果每个海岸有两三艘航母处于需要维修或保养的不同阶段，它们将排队等待。”

MS Now [本月初报道](https://www.ms.now/news/conditions-safety-uss-lincoln-iran-war)称，林肯号（USS Lincoln）上的水兵面临恶劣条件，包括食物、牙膏和肥皂短缺。CNN此前报道称，一名林肯号上的人员落水。

## U.S. Sending Fresh Aircraft Carrier to Middle East Amid Iran War Strain

## U.S. Sending Fresh Aircraft Carrier to Middle East Amid Iran War Strain

An aircraft launching from the flight deck of the USS Abraham Lincoln last month. U.S. Navy

The U.S. military is preparing to replace the USS Abraham Lincoln with the USS George Washington as part of a scheduled Middle East deployment, U.S. officials said, amid growing concerns over living conditions aboard the Lincoln and the strain on troops during the [Iran](https://www.wsj.com/topics/place/iran) war.

Several lawmakers say they are concerned about reports of a lack of food and personal supplies aboard the carrier, as well as plumbing backups, as the carrier’s deployment stretches into its ninth month.

A U.S. official confirmed Thursday that a person went overboard the Lincoln in early August but was quickly rescued.

The Lincoln has been deployed for more than 250 days and it hasn’t made a port call in 200 days, setting a record for consecutive days at sea, according to Sen. Richard Blumenthal (D., Conn.), who wrote [a letter](https://www.blumenthal.senate.gov/imo/media/doc/2026-8-12_letter_to_sec_hegseth__cao_re_uss_lincoln.pdf) to Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth and acting Secretary of the Navy Hung Cao.

“There have been widespread reports of shortages of basic supplies, water contamination, plumbing issues, deteriorating mental health, deck safety concerns, and disruptions in the mail system, which have caused many care packages in route to the ship to be lost in transit for months,” Blumenthal wrote.

Sen. Richard Blumenthal wrote a letter to top military leaders citing deteriorating conditions aboard the Lincoln. Graeme Sloan/Bloomberg News

Rep. Marlin Stutzman (R., Ind.) said he plans to seek an update from the Pentagon and the administration. “At the end of the day, we know that those who are serving have to have comfort knowing that they’re getting properly taken care of,” he said in an interview with CNN on Thursday.

Hegseth said concerns about the Lincoln have been “completely misrepresented” while speaking with reporters aboard a Navy ship in Panama City, Panama, on Thursday.

“We make sure that every ship, every crew, every captain has everything we can provide them at every single moment,” Hegseth said. He added that the U.S. can maintain its blockade of Iranian ports indefinitely because the U.S. can rotate ships in and out of theater.

U.S. officials say the conditions aboard the Lincoln aren’t unusual for a wartime deployment, but some acknowledge that months without a port call can weigh on troops. Typically, aircraft carriers pull into port to resupply and give the crew a chance to rest and regroup during standard deployments.

The USS Gerald R. Ford was at sea for 11 months before it returned home in May and became [the longest-deployed aircraft carrier](https://www.wsj.com/livecoverage/iran-us-strait-of-hormuz-blockade-updates/card/uss-gerald-r-ford-longest-deployed-aircraft-carrier-in-post-cold-war-era-PIozQdleHLlhMQzIKD5M?mod=article_inline) since the collapse of the Soviet Union. The ship stopped for several days [in Split, Croatia](https://www.wsj.com/livecoverage/iran-war-news-trump/card/largest-u-s-warship-set-to-rejoin-iran-war-zpwSZSbKKbexPmMM048n?mod=article_inline), in March, during the height of the Iran war, to undergo maintenance and give sailors time off. Still, the extended deployment and challenging conditions [weighed on servicemembers](https://www.wsj.com/us-news/missed-funerals-and-blocked-toilets-iran-deployment-takes-a-toll-on-u-s-sailors-7e230962?mod=article_inline).

The Lincoln’s rotation out of the Middle East was planned before concerns were raised about the living conditions, according to one of the officials. It comes as President Trump has been trying to apply [economic pressure](https://www.wsj.com/world/middle-east/trump-shifts-to-economic-pressure-again-as-iran-wont-meet-his-demands-4445c840?mod=article_inline) on Iran, including a prolonged blockade of Iranian ports carried out by a large number of American warships.

The Lincoln began its scheduled deployment in November and was redirected to the Middle East [in January](https://www.wsj.com/world/middle-east/us-lincoln-carrier-arrives-central-command-48977187?mod=article_inline) ahead of the U.S. war against Iran. The carrier and its embarked aircraft were instrumental to the U.S.’s Operation Epic Fury bombing campaign, and the ship has since participated in the U.S. blockade of Iranian ports.

Sen. Ruben Gallego (D., Ariz.) said he is seeking an oversight visit to the Lincoln with a bipartisan Senate delegation to probe conditions on the ship. “These sailors are owed a sense of when they will go home,” Gallego, an Iraq war veteran, said this week on X.

A Navy official said the military isn’t experiencing an uptick in mental-health issues aboard the Lincoln and that it hasn’t observed an increase in suicidal ideations or attempts aboard the ship. The official said the Navy takes every servicemember’s well-being seriously and has religious, medical and mental-health professionals available to assess and address concerns as they arise.

The USS George Washington, based in Japan, has been operating in the South China Sea in recent weeks and recently made a port call in Vietnam. The carrier’s deployment in the region was an example of how the U.S. military works to counter Chinese influence in the Pacific. The carrier recently transited the Strait of Malacca moving westward alongside guided-missile destroyers, according to ship-tracking data and a U.S. official.

The Washington’s movement to the Middle East will leave a temporary void in the Pacific, where China has deployed its own aircraft carrier and is seeking to exert influence over its neighbors, analysts say.

“Normally, the George Washington would do its patrol and go and show the flag and demonstrate our resolve and our support to countries like the Philippines and Japan,” said Bryan Clark, a former naval officer who is now a senior fellow at the Hudson Institute, a think tank that studies national security issues. “We’re going to forgo all these strategic opportunities to influence the relationships with the neighbors there.”

The carrier rotation also is raising concerns about the Navy’s ability to respond to global events and sustain its operations long term, officials and analysts say. The Navy has 11 active aircraft carriers, which require maintenance when they return home.

Due to the Iran war, it could take three years to get the fleet back to its ideal readiness posture, according to Clark. “If you’ve got two or three carriers on each coast that are in various stages of needing to be repaired or maintained, they’re going to be waiting in line,” he said.

MS Now [reported earlier this month](https://www.ms.now/news/conditions-safety-uss-lincoln-iran-war) that sailors aboard the Lincoln were facing tough conditions including shortages of food, toothpaste and soap. CNN previously reported that a person from the Lincoln went overboard.
