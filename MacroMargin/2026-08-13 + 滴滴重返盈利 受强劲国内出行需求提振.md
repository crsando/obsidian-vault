### 中文译文

## 滴滴重返盈利 受强劲国内出行需求提振

滴滴全球公司在连续两个季度亏损后恢复盈利，得益于中国网约车需求强劲。

该公司在截至6月的三个月内录得净利润8.66亿元人民币（1.28亿美元），上年同期为亏损。营收增长11%至625亿元人民币，核心中国出行交易额增长9.5%，创下纪录。

滴滴在十年前将[优步科技公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/UBER%20US%20Equity)挤出中国后，成为中国最大的网约车平台。如今，它与国内竞争对手展开竞争，包括[阿里巴巴集团控股有限公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/BABA%20US%20Equity)的导航平台高德地图。这家总部位于北京的公司也在应对中国经济的[低迷](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-16/china-s-first-consumer-spending-drop-since-covid-imperils-growth)。

该公司一直在拓展中国内地以外的市场，包括香港，以寻求更高利润。滴滴在全球14个国家开展业务，并已是巴西和墨西哥领先的拼车服务商。其网约车平台还提供额外服务，包括外卖和数字钱包。

*由于美团于2025年10月进入巴西外卖市场后竞争加剧，滴滴海外部门亏损扩大，可能在第二季度继续拖累集团利润。——分析师Robert Lea和Jasmine Lyu，点击查看研究报告《滴滴海外亏损扩大拖累盈利：二季度预览》*

首席执行官程维在6月[承诺](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-02/china-s-didi-deepens-losses-after-stepping-up-global-investment)加大对滴滴自动驾驶出租车部门的投资。该公司已在中国部分城市部署自动驾驶汽车，与国内的[百度公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/BIDU%20US%20Equity)等展开竞争。

滴滴于2021年在纽约交易所首次亮相，但很快引起中国监管机构的审查。中国网信办对滴滴的数据安全实践展开调查，随后暂停了该应用。该公司最终于2022年从主板退市。

据报道，该公司正在考虑在香港上市，但尚未宣布正式时间表。

## China’s Didi Returns to Profit on Strong Domestic Ride Demand

Didi Global Inc. swung back to profit after two consecutive quarterly losses, helped by strong ride-hailing demand in China.

The company recorded net income of 866 million yuan ($128 million) for the three months ended in June, after a loss a year earlier. Revenue increased 11% to 62.5 billion yuan, with the core China mobility transaction value rising 9.5% to a record.

Didi became China’s largest ride-hailing platform after pushing out [Uber Technologies Inc. ](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/UBER%20US%20Equity)from the Asian nation a decade ago. It now competes with domestic rivals including [Alibaba Group Holding Ltd.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/BABA%20US%20Equity)’s navigation platform Amap. The Beijing-based company is also grappling with China’s economic [doldrums](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-16/china-s-first-consumer-spending-drop-since-covid-imperils-growth).

The company has been expanding outside mainland China, including in Hong Kong, in pursuit of higher profits. Didi operates in 14 countries globally, and it is already a leading ride-sharing player in Brazil and Mexico. Its ride-hailing platforms also provide additional services including food delivery and digital wallets.

*Elevated losses in Didi’s overseas division likely remained a drag on group profit during 2Q, due to heightened competition in the Brazilian food-delivery market following Meituan’ entry in October 2025. - Robert Lea and Jasmine Lyu, analysts Click Didi's Elevated Overseas Losses to Drag on Earnings: 2Q Preview for the research*

Chief Executive Officer Cheng Wei in June [pledged](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-02/china-s-didi-deepens-losses-after-stepping-up-global-investment) more investments in Didi’s robotaxi unit. The company’s already deployed self-driving vehicles in some Chinese cities, competing with the likes of [Baidu Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/BIDU%20US%20Equity) at home.

Didi made its debut on the New York Exchange in 2021 but soon drew regulatory scrutiny from Chinese officials. China’s Cyberspace Administration opened a probe into Didi’s data security practices before suspending the app. The company ended up delisting from the main board in 2022.

The company reportedly is considering a listing in Hong Kong, but has yet to announce a formal timeline.
