### 中文译文

## 贝莱德与工会就AI建设岗位签署协议

[贝莱德公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/BLK%20US%20Equity)与工会组织签署了一项协议，旨在为新的数据中心和能源项目储备建筑工人，这是大型投资者为强调人工智能技术快速发展所创造的就业机会而非取代的就业机会而做出的最新努力。

据周一宣布谅解备忘录的声明，这家资产管理公司及其AI基础设施合作伙伴——与[微软公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/MSFT%20US%20Equity)及其他大型科技公司共同成立的300亿美元合资企业——将与北美建筑业工会（NABTU）分享即将开展的具体项目细节，该工会代表超过300万名熟练技术工人。

根据声明，通过与NABTU在未来的招聘需求上合作，贝莱德的投资组合公司更有可能获得完成数据中心和能源项目所需的工人。贝莱德和NABTU还提到了“负责任承包商计划”的好处，该计划通常用于确保公平的工资和工作条件，以及项目劳工协议，这些全面的合同可以加速大型建筑项目的招聘。

贝莱德首席执行官拉里·芬克在声明中表示：“推动创新所需的基础设施建设正在创造千载难逢的投资美国工人的机会。”该协议“反映了这样一项共同信念，即投资关键基础设施可以增强美国的竞争力，同时创造就业机会、扩大经济机遇，并最终帮助更多美国人实现财务福祉。”

这一声明发布之际，数据中心项目正面临来自社区的日益强烈的反对，这些社区担心大规模开发，同时社会各界也担忧人工智能热潮将对大量就业和行业产生何种影响。

在过去一年中，金融和科技公司已承诺开展职业培训计划，他们表示这将有助于解决建设数据中心所需的熟练工人短缺问题，并缓解数百万美国人对未来就业前景的担忧。[Meta平台公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/META%20US%20Equity) [承诺](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/06/americas-workforce-academy-free-skilled-trade-training/)最初投入1.15亿美元用于一项劳动力培训计划，该计划保证所有毕业生都能获得工作。

贝莱德在3月份宣布了一项[1亿美元](https://www.blackrock.com/corporate/corporate-sustainability/blackrock-sustanability/social-impact/future-builders)的计划，旨在帮助培训5万名美国人从事熟练技术工作，包括水管工和电工，并[加入](https://www.blackrock.com/corporate/newsroom/media/press-releases/alliance-americas-skilled-trades)了与其他公司合作的另一个职业培训项目，这些公司包括[福特汽车公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/F%20US%20Equity)、[Carhartt](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/0059242D:US)和[Alphabet公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/GOOGL%20US%20Equity)。

与其他华尔街主要投资者一样，贝莱德正在积极为人工智能技术提供融资。上月，该公司与Meta达成协议，将在[德州建设一座数据中心](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-28/meta-blackrock-plan-to-invest-in-giant-texas-data-center)，耗资约140亿美元。去年晚些时候，[全球基础设施合作伙伴](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1013340Z:US)与AI基础设施合作伙伴和阿布扎比的MGX共同牵头，以400亿美元的交易[收购](https://aligneddc.com/press-release/aip-mgx-and-blackrocks-gip-close-acquisition-of-aligned-data-centers/)了[Aligned Data Centers](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/US%20Equity)。

芬克曾[表示](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-23/fink-says-ai-threatens-to-leave-masses-behind-unless-they-invest)，人工智能将颠覆劳动力市场。他在3月警告称，除非更多人投资市场并收获他所预期的可观财务收益，否则这项技术可能加剧财富不平等。

## BlackRock Signs Deal With Labor Unions for AI Construction Jobs

[BlackRock Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/BLK%20US%20Equity) signed an agreement with organized labor designed to line up construction workers for new data centers and energy projects, the latest effort by big investors to emphasize jobs created rather than replaced by rapidly growing artificial intelligence technologies.

The money manager and its AI Infrastructure Partnership — a $30 billion joint venture with [Microsoft Corp.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/MSFT%20US%20Equity) and other big technology firms — will share details about specific upcoming projects with North America’s Building Trades Unions, which represents more than 3 million skilled trade workers, according to a statement announcing the memorandum of understanding Monday.

By working with NABTU on future hiring needs, BlackRock portfolio companies are more likely to have the workers needed to complete data center and energy projects, according to the statement. BlackRock and NABTU also cited the benefits of “responsible contractor programs,” criteria often used to secure fair wages and working conditions, as well as project labor agreements, sweeping contracts that can accelerate hiring for big construction projects.

“The infrastructure buildout required to drive innovation is creating a once-in-a-generation opportunity to invest in American workers,” BlackRock Chief Executive Officer** **Larry Fink said in the statement. The agreement “reflects a shared belief that investing in critical infrastructure can strengthen America’s competitiveness while creating jobs, expanding economic opportunity and, ultimately, help more Americans experience financial well-being.”

The announcement comes as data center projects face mounting opposition from communities concerned about the massive developments and broader societal worries about what the AI boom will mean for a swath of jobs and industries.

Over the last year, finance and technology companies have committed to job training initiatives that they say will help address the shortage of skilled workers needed to build data centers, as well as fears about the future employment prospects of millions of Americans. [Meta Platforms Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/META%20US%20Equity) [committed](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/06/americas-workforce-academy-free-skilled-trade-training/) $115 million initially to a workforce training program that guarantees a job for all graduates.

BlackRock in March announced a [$100 million](https://www.blackrock.com/corporate/corporate-sustainability/blackrock-sustanability/social-impact/future-builders) initiative to help train 50,000 Americans for skilled trade jobs, including plumbers and electricians, and [joined](https://www.blackrock.com/corporate/newsroom/media/press-releases/alliance-americas-skilled-trades) with [Ford Motor Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/F%20US%20Equity), [Carhartt](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/0059242D:US) and [Alphabet Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/GOOGL%20US%20Equity) on another job training program.

Like other major Wall Street investors, BlackRock is moving aggressively to provide financing for artificial technology. The company struck a deal with Meta last month to build a [data center in Texas](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-28/meta-blackrock-plan-to-invest-in-giant-texas-data-center) that will cost about $14 billion. Late last year, [Global Infrastructure Partners](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1013340Z:US), alongside the AI Infrastructure Partnership and Abu Dhabi’s MGX, led the [acquisition](https://aligneddc.com/press-release/aip-mgx-and-blackrocks-gip-close-acquisition-of-aligned-data-centers/) of [Aligned Data Centers](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/US%20Equity) in a $40 billion deal.

Fink has [said](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-23/fink-says-ai-threatens-to-leave-masses-behind-unless-they-invest) that AI will upend the workforce, warning in March that the technology threatens to exacerbate wealth inequality unless more individuals invest in the market and reap what he expects will be considerable financial gains.
