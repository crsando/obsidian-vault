### 中文译文

## 字节跳动瞄准接近Anthropic Mythos的巨型AI模型

![ByteDance believes only independent development can result in a model that outperforms rivals © Ore Huiying/Bloomberg](https://images.ft.com/v3/image/raw/ftcms%3Ac9d30fa6-225d-46fe-9a64-5544278f23b8?source=next-article&fit=scale-down&quality=highest&width=1440&dpr=1)

字节跳动认为，只有独立开发才能打造出超越竞争对手的模型 © Ore Huiying/彭博社

字节跳动正在训练一个AI模型，其规模可能接近Anthropic最前沿的Mythos系统，与此同时，中国企业持续缩小与美国顶尖实验室的差距。

据三位知情人士透露，这家中国科技巨头正处于训练一个拥有多达10万亿参数模型的早期阶段，该规模是迄今发布的最大中国模型——[月之暗面（Moonshot）的Kimi K3](https://www.ft.com/content/c6ecd8ce-c441-4d7c-aea6-fae3e28fb6ff?syn-25a6b1a6=1)——的三倍。

其中一位人士表示，字节跳动的该模型正在进行预训练——这一阶段通常需要三到六个月——之后如果一切顺利，将进行微调并发布。确切模型规模只会在较晚阶段确定。

[Anthropic](https://www.ft.com/stream/15c0cb45-8892-46cd-a086-1d2716ae7246)不披露其模型规模，但行业估计其最先进的Mythos 5约有8万亿参数，Fable 5约有5万亿。虽然参数数量决定了模型存储信息的基本容量或记忆上限，但实际能力还取决于数据质量和训练方法等其他因素。

[字节跳动](https://www.ft.com/stream/b8a2f6ac-53a2-44e0-966f-587002e8e26f)训练全球最大AI模型之一的努力，显示出中国实验室不仅要在最先进的AI领域赶上美国同行，还要超越他们的雄心。

仅在过去几周，来自月之暗面和阿里巴巴的中国模型就在基准测试中表现出强劲性能，在某些领域仅落后于Anthropic的Fable 5。[Mythos 5](https://www.ft.com/content/4b578b5c-258a-48f5-a5e9-15eef5659573?syn-25a6b1a6=1)是Anthropic最先进的模型，由于安全担忧于6月被暂时禁用后，目前仅向获准组织开放。

业内人士表示，多家中国实验室正在训练与Fable 5规模相当的模型，而字节跳动目前是追求最大规模方面最具雄心的。

字节跳动是病毒式视频平台TikTok的母公司，在人工智能开发方面一直保持低调，因为其模型大多不公开，这与许多中国同行不同。其最新的SeeDance模型在视频生成方面位列全球最先进之列，而其面向消费者的旗舰模型豆包是中国最受欢迎的模型，月活跃用户达3.24亿。

过去三年，字节跳动在人工智能上的投资比任何其他中国科技巨头都要激进，扩建了数据中心网络并招聘研究人员。它加大了对云部门火山引擎的投入，该部门向企业销售AI解决方案。字节跳动还雄心勃勃地开发定制AI芯片。

其模型开发团队Seed由前谷歌DeepMind科学家吴永辉领导，在中国和海外约有2000名成员。团队包括核心研究员、基础设施工程师、数据标注团队和翻译人员。

据其中一位人士称，其模型开发还采用了一种更独立的方法，不涉及从其他实验室“蒸馏”现有模型。这种方法已实施一年多，一些人认为这导致其开发速度慢于竞争对手。

模型蒸馏是将大型复杂AI模型压缩为更小、更快模型的过程，方法是训练较小的模型来复制较大“教师”模型的知识和输出。

以创始人张一鸣为首的字节跳动管理层认为，只有独立开发才能打造出超越竞争对手的模型。

据这位人士称，张一鸣两周前在一次内部会议上重申了他的立场，他告诉Seed团队，长期目标是“世界领先的模型能力”，而不要过于担心短期落后。

中国媒体《晚点》和The Information首先报道了张一鸣在近期内部会议上的评论。

字节跳动未回应置评请求。

### English Original

## ByteDance targets mega AI model nearing Anthropic’s Mythos

ByteDance believes only independent development can result in a model that outperforms rivals © Ore Huiying/Bloomberg

ByteDance is training an AI model that could approach the size of Anthropic’s most cutting-edge Mythos system, as Chinese companies continue to narrow the gap with the top US labs.

The Chinese tech giant is at an early stage of training a model with as many as 10tn parameters — three times larger than [Moonshot’s Kimi K3](https://www.ft.com/content/c6ecd8ce-c441-4d7c-aea6-fae3e28fb6ff?syn-25a6b1a6=1), the biggest Chinese model released to date, according to three people with knowledge of the matter.

The ByteDance model is being pre-trained — a stage that typically takes three to six months — before it is fine-tuned and released if all goes well, one of the people said. The exact model size would only be determined at a later stage.

[Anthropic](https://www.ft.com/stream/15c0cb45-8892-46cd-a086-1d2716ae7246) doesn’t disclose the size of its models, but industry estimates say its most advanced Mythos 5 has about 8tn parameters and Fable 5 about 5tn. While parameter count sets the fundamental capacity or memory limits for the models to store information, actual capability also depends on other factors such as data quality and training methods.

[ByteDance](https://www.ft.com/stream/b8a2f6ac-53a2-44e0-966f-587002e8e26f)’s efforts to train one of the world’s largest AI models show Chinese labs’ ambition to not only catch up but outperform their US peers in the most advanced level of AI.

In the past weeks alone, Chinese models from Moonshot and Alibaba show strong performance on benchmarks, lagging behind only Anthropic’s Fable 5 in certain areas. [Mythos 5](https://www.ft.com/content/4b578b5c-258a-48f5-a5e9-15eef5659573?syn-25a6b1a6=1), Anthropic’s most advanced model, is only available to approved organisations after a temporary ban in June due to security concerns.

Industry insiders say multiple Chinese labs are in the process of training models of the size of Fable 5, while ByteDance is currently the most ambitious in pushing for the largest.

ByteDance, the parent of viral video platform TikTok, has kept a low profile in its AI development as its models are mostly closed, unlike many of its Chinese peers. Its latest SeeDance model ranks among the most advanced globally in video generation, while its flagship consumer-facing model Doubao is the most popular in China with 324mn monthly active users.

Over the past three years, ByteDance has invested in AI more aggressively than any of the other Chinese tech giants, building out its network of data centres and hiring researchers. It has doubled down on its cloud unit, Volcano Engine, which sells AI solutions to enterprises. ByteDance also has ambitions to develop custom AI chips.

Seed, its model development team led by former Google DeepMind scientist Wu Yonghui, has about 2,000 members in China and overseas. The team includes core researchers, infrastructure engineers, a data labelling team and translators.

Its model development has also implemented a more independent approach that does not involve “distilling” existing models from other labs, according to one of the people. This approach has been in place for more than a year, which some believe has led to its slower development versus rivals.

Model distillation is the process of compressing a large, complex AI model into a smaller, faster one by training the smaller model to copy the knowledge and outputs of the larger teacher.

ByteDance’s management, led by founder Zhang Yiming, believes only independent development can result in a model that outperforms rivals.

Zhang reiterated his stance in an internal meeting two weeks ago, where he told the Seed team to target “world-leading model capabilities” in the long run without getting too worried about falling behind in the near term, according to the person.

Chinese media Latepost and The Information first reported Zhang’s comments from the recent internal meeting.

ByteDance did not respond to a request for comment.
