### 中文译文

## 中尼边境洪水致数百人失踪，其中包括游客

### **要点**

- 尼泊尔与中国边境发生猛烈山洪，导致数百人失踪，其中包括291名外国人，至少72人遇难。
- 泥石流于当地时间周三上午8时30分左右发生，共有384名旅行者下落不明，其中包括美国、印度、英国、荷兰和澳大利亚公民。
- 中国国家主席习近平要求全力救援，印度总理纳伦德拉·莫迪向尼泊尔提供人道主义援助，印度也在为本国可能发生的洪水做准备。
一场猛烈的山洪冲毁了尼泊尔与中国边境的一个检查站，导致数百人失踪，其中包括地方当局确认的291名外国人。

据尼泊尔旅游局发布的初步数据，泥石流于当地时间周三上午8时30分左右发生，共有384名旅行者下落不明——其中包括美国、印度、英国、荷兰和澳大利亚公民——以及93名尼泊尔人。中国新华社援引尼泊尔警方的话报道称，至少有72人遇难。

据尼泊尔国家灾害风险减少与管理管理局在X平台上发布的一篇帖子称，对卫星图像的初步分析表明，由于尼泊尔-中国边境发生山体滑坡，伦德河（Lhende River）发生了洪水。

这场灾害袭击了西藏吉隆县，网上流传的视频显示泥浆和洪水席卷该地区。中国国家主席习近平要求在西藏全力救援，这可能会成为该国今年最致命的自然灾害之一。

作为回应，习近平强调，当务之急是全力搜寻失踪人员，并转移安置任何受到威胁的居民，根据政府网站上的一份[声明](https://www.gov.cn/yaowen/liebiao/202608/content_7079160.htm)。

中国自然资源部已启动地质灾害二级应急响应，这是国家四级应急响应体系中的第二高等级，新华社：习近平敦促在泥石流后尽量减少西藏人员伤亡（1）。

印度总理纳伦德拉·莫迪与尼泊尔总理通话并表示哀悼，根据他在X平台上的一篇帖子。他说：“印度准备向尼泊尔提供一切可能的人道主义援助。我们的团队正在密切协调救援和救济工作。”

该地区的许多印度国民正在前往西藏的冈仁波齐峰和玛旁雍错湖，参加凯拉斯-玛旁雍错朝圣之旅，这些地点被印度教徒和佛教徒视为圣地。尼泊尔山洪死亡人数升至46人：官员向印度报业托拉斯表示。

印度人口最多的邦——北方邦，与尼泊尔接壤，由于预计河流水位将上涨，也在为可能的洪水做准备。“已向地方政府发出指示，要求保持高度警惕并确保做好必要准备，”该邦首席部长在X平台上的一篇帖子中[表示](https://x.com/myogiadityanath/status/2092558463566262737?s=20)。

与尼泊尔总理巴伦德拉·沙阿通了电话，就尼泊尔洪水造成的生命损失表示哀悼。在这个困难时刻，印度人民与尼泊尔的兄弟姐妹们站在一起。

据[新京报](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/nbqhUyFJ58JAPsNiUKQEnA)报道，泥石流在西藏日喀则市管辖的吉隆县边境口岸造成了重大人员伤亡和失踪。中国外交部周三下班后未立即回复置评请求。

中国当局已公布了用于救援和恢复工作的紧急资金的部分细节。财政部和自然资源部已下拨1.2亿元人民币（合1790万美元）的中央自然灾害救灾资金，用于支持西藏的紧急救援和救灾工作。国家发展和改革委员会已安排1亿元人民币（合1490万美元）的中央预算内投资，用于支持灾后紧急恢复和重建工作。

习近平敦促地方政府和相关机构做好最坏情况准备，加强防汛和台风控制措施，并加大对潜在危险的排查力度。

## Hundreds, Including Tourists, Missing in China-Nepal Floods

### **Takeaways**

- A violent flash flood at the border between Nepal and China left hundreds missing, including 291 foreigners, and at least 72 people were killed.
- The mudslide struck at around 8:30 a.m. local time on Wednesday, with a total of 384 travelers unaccounted for, including citizens of the US, India, the UK, the Netherlands and Australia.
- Chinese President Xi Jinping called for an all-out rescue effort and Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi offered humanitarian assistance to Nepal, with India also preparing for possible flooding in its own territory.
A violent flash flood at the border between Nepal and China tore through a checkpoint and left hundreds missing, including 291 foreigners who were identified by local authorities.

The mudslide struck at around 8:30 a.m. local time on Wednesday, according to preliminary [data](https://x.com/nepaltourismb/status/2092538936929419337/photo/1) released by the Nepal Tourism Board, with a total of 384 travelers unaccounted for — including citizens of the US, India, the UK, the Netherlands and Australia — along with 93 Nepalis. At least 72 people were killed, China’s Xinhua News Agency reported, citing Nepal police.

An initial analysis of satellite images suggests that a flood occurred in the Lhende River because of a landslide on the Nepal-China border, according to a [post](https://x.com/NDRRMA_Nepal/status/2092584620248408106) on X by Nepal’s National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Authority.

The disaster hit Tibet’s Gyirong county, with [videos](https://edition.cnn.com/2026/08/26/world/video/people-flee-as-huge-wave-of-floodwater-engulfs-nepal-china-border-crossing-digvid) circulating online showing the torrent of mud and water sweep through the area. Chinese President Xi Jinping called for an all-out rescue effort in Tibet, in what could become one of the deadliest natural disasters in the country this year.

In response, Xi stressed the immediate priority was to spare no effort in searching for persons who are missing, as well as relocating and resettling any threatened residents, according to a [statement](https://www.gov.cn/yaowen/liebiao/202608/content_7079160.htm) on the government website.

China’s Ministry of Natural Resources has activated a Level-II emergency response for geological disasters, the second-highest in the country’s four-tier emergency response system, Xinhua News Agency Xi Urges Minimizing Casualties in Tibet After Mudslide (1).

Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi spoke with his Nepali counterpart and conveyed his condolences, according to a post on X. “India is ready to provide all possible humanitarian assistance to Nepal,” he said. “Our teams are coordinating closely on rescue and relief efforts.”

Many Indian nationals in the area were undertaking the Kailash Manasarovar Yatra pilgrimage to Mount Kailash and Lake Manasarovar in Tibet, sites considered sacred by Hindus and Buddhists, Death toll in Nepal flash flood rises to 46: Officials to the Press Trust of India.

Uttar Pradesh, India’s most populous state, which borders Nepal, is also preparing for possible flooding as river levels are expected to rise. “Instructions have been issued to the local administration to remain fully vigilant and ensure necessary preparations,” the state’s chief minister [said](https://x.com/myogiadityanath/status/2092558463566262737?s=20) in a post on X.

Spoke to Prime Minister Balendra Shah and conveyed condolences on the loss of lives due to the floods in Nepal. The people of India stand in solidarity with their sisters and brothers in Nepal at this difficult time.

The mudslide caused significant casualties and missing persons at the border crossing in Gyirong, a county administered by Shigatse city in Tibet, according to the [Beijing News](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/nbqhUyFJ58JAPsNiUKQEnA). China’s Foreign Ministry didn’t immediately reply to a request for comment sent after office hours Wednesday.

Chinese authorities have announced some details of emergency funding for rescue and recovery efforts. The Ministry of Finance and the Ministry of Natural Resources have allocated 120 million yuan ($17.9 million) in central natural disaster relief funds to support Tibet in emergency rescue and disaster relief work. The National Development and Reform Commission has earmarked 100 million yuan ($14.9 million) in central budget investment to support post-disaster emergency recovery and reconstruction efforts.

Xi urged local governments and relevant agencies to prepare for worst-case scenarios, strengthen flood and typhoon control measures, and step up inspections of potential hazards.
