### 中文译文

## 习特峰会前中国对美国碧根果征收54%关税

中国已下令对从美国和墨西哥进口的碧根果征收高额反倾销保证金，在唐纳德·特朗普总统与中国领导人习近平预计举行峰会的前几周，增添了又一贸易摩擦源。

商务部周一表示，根据一项调查的初步结果，来自两国的碧根果在中国以不公平的低价销售。自周二起，美国碧根果进口商需缴纳54.3%的保证金，而从墨西哥购买的进口商则面临17.8%至51.6%不等的税率。

该决定出台之际，华盛顿和北京正寻求确保习近平9月访美计划筹备工作顺利进行，尽管新一轮贸易和技术限制交流令双方脆弱的休战关系紧张。中国上周宣布了一系列反制措施，包括制裁美国实体和加强无人机相关出口管制。

![图片](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3b881cc90b148107a3ccc195f0e417c7/3b881cc9-0b14-8145-85e6-e2fbda6a0f7d-a17a4fb544559114dd365237118558bb.png)

中国从美国进口的碧根果在2024年激增后大幅下降，今年前四个月仅为690万美元，而两年前为7720万美元。相反，近年来中国增加了从南非的采购，中国海关数据显示。

在另一份声明中，商务部表示没有美国公司参与此次调查，促使当局对所有美国供应品适用54.3%的税率。

“中国在使用贸易救济措施方面一直保持谨慎和克制，”商务部补充称，并承诺在作出最终裁定前保护相关方的权利。

此次调查于去年9月根据中国《反倾销条例》启动。2025年3月，美国碧根果已受到10%关税的打击，当时中国对特朗普的关税进行了报复。

碧根果此前在特朗普第一届政府期间曾卷入贸易战，当时中国对美国碧根果征收47%的关税，使佐治亚州和亚利桑那州的农民失去了一个关键出口市场。

## China Hits US Pecans With 54% Levies Before Xi-Trump Summit

China ordered steep anti-dumping deposits on pecan imports from the US and Mexico, adding another source of trade friction weeks before an [expected summit](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-06/trump-says-he-expects-to-host-china-s-xi-in-us-around-sept-24) between President Donald Trump and Chinese leader Xi Jinping.

The Commerce Ministry said Monday pecans from both countries were being sold in China at unfairly low prices, citing the provisional result of an investigation. Starting Tuesday, importers of US pecans are required to pay a deposit rate of 54.3%, while those buying from Mexico face rates ranging from 17.8% to 51.6%.

The decision comes as Washington and Beijing seek to keep preparations on track for Xi’s planned visit to the US in September, even as a renewed exchange of trade and [technology restrictions](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-29/us-risks-broadening-china-tech-clash-with-robot-inverter-curbs) strains their fragile truce. China last week announced a barrage of [countermeasures](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-06/xi-flexes-expanding-legal-arsenal-to-warn-trump-ahead-of-summit) including sanctions on US entities and tighter controls on drone-related exports.

China’s imports of US pecans plummeted after a spike in 2024, amounting to just $6.9 million in the first four months of this year, compared with $77.2 million two years ago. Instead, it has boosted its purchase from South Africa in recent years, Chinese customs data show.

In a separate statement, the ministry said no US company participated in the probe, prompting authorities to apply the 54.3% rate on all American supplies.

“China has consistently exercised prudence and restraint in its use of trade-remedy measures,” the ministry added, pledging to protect the rights of interested parties before issuing a final ruling.

The investigation was launched last September under China’s Anti-Dumping Regulations. American pecans were already hit by 10% tariffs in March 2025, as China retaliated against Trump’s levies.

Pecans were previously [caught ](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-09-25/pecans-are-latest-trade-focus-as-china-probes-us-mexico-exports)in the trade war during the first Trump administration, when China hit back against American pecans with 47% tariffs that left farmers in Georgia and Arizona without a key export market.
