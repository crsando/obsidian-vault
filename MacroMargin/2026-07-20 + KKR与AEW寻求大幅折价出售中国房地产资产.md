## KKR与AEW寻求大幅折价出售中国房地产资产

彭博新闻\n2026年7月20日 上午9:46 GMT+8

[KKR & Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/KKR%20US%20Equity) 和 [AEW Capital Management LP](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/168894Z:US) 正寻求大幅折价出售其在中国的商业地产资产，这是在中国持续的地产低迷期间最新一批全球投资者出售资产。

总部位于纽约的KKR计划处置在中国的九个项目，包括位于北京郊区的一个高端多户[公寓小区](https://www.bing.com/ck/a?!=&p=de77db4768069b6f9cc5dede684d7a24d63a26ae427cba1df961cf1ee2c0c618JmltdHM9MTc4Mzk4NzIwMA&ptn=3&ver=2&hsh=4&fclid=27bf2fec-574a-61a8-11c9-3d97566460bc&u=a1aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cuY29sbGllcnMuY29tLmNuL2VuLWNuL25ld3MvMjAyMjA0MjJjYXBpdGFsbWFya2V0YmVpamluZw&ntb=1)和上海外滩历史风貌区的一家中档酒店，知情人士透露。

总部位于波士顿的AEW正寻求出售北京的[合信国际广场](https://www.aew.com/assets?region=asia&strategy=all-strategies)办公楼和[京印国际中心](https://www.gmp.de/cn/projects/18588/jing-in-international-center)综合体，以及上海陆家嘴金融贸易区的[浦发银行](https://www.aew.com/assets?region=asia&strategy=all-strategies)大楼，知情人士称。

据知情人士透露，这两家资产管理公司预计任何出售所得将足以偿还用于购置这些房产的银行贷款，这些贷款占最初购买价格的50%至60%。

KKR的代表拒绝置评。AEW未回应置评请求。

根据其网站，KKR仍在中国从事私募股权业务，拥有超过十几项[活跃投资](https://www.kkr.com/invest/portfolio)，包括抖音母公司字节跳动、蘑菇生产商[江苏裕灌](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1536013D:CH)和私立医院运营商康瑞医疗。去年，KKR在中国成立了首只[人民币基金](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/cbr02l6-ocrp110g0c_4gA)，从平安资本等境内投资者募集资金。

根据MSCI Real Capital Analytics的数据，过去15年，外国买家在中国投资了近1400亿美元用于办公楼、仓库、购物中心和数据中心。但随着中国经济放缓和空前供应过剩导致租金和价格暴跌，它们已基本转向卖出模式。

市场低迷对在2019年峰值附近下注的投资者尤其痛苦，当时写字楼资产的估计市场价值处于历史高位。

![图片](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3a381cc90b14819c8043e8585222d393/3a381cc9-0b14-8159-ab47-e046e3d6c669-0c35d564e6fd71de383d5b74c3b12c12.png)

2020年，AEW与本土私募股权投资者[弘毅投资](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/HONYCZ%20HK%20Equity)买下了北京中心城区的一个甲级写字楼项目，并在翻新后更名为[和夏国际广场](https://www.aew.com/assets?region=asia&strategy=all-strategies)。2021年底，KKR收购了北京郊区一个拥有3000套单元的多户住宅项目。

中国的房地产危机也让许多贷方措手不及，包括[汇丰控股](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/HSBA%20LN%20Equity)和[渣打银行](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/STAN%20LN%20Equity)，它们已拨备数亿美元以应对商业地产组合。去年，由渣打银行牵头的一组贷方在一个上海办公楼综合项目中遭受了超过[10%的损失](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-03-20/blackrock-fund-s-forfeited-china-towers-to-be-sold-at-40-loss)，该物业此前由[贝莱德](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/9903115D:US)旗下基金持有，当时以超过40%的折扣价出售，知情人士当时透露。

## KKR, AEW Seek to Sell China Property Assets at Steep Losses

By Bloomberg News
July 20, 2026 at 9:46 AM GMT+8

[KKR & Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/KKR%20US%20Equity) and [AEW Capital Management LP](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/168894Z:US) are seeking to offload commercial real estate holdings in China at sharp losses, the latest global investors to sell assets during the country’s prolonged property downturn.

New York-based KKR plans to dispose of nine projects across China, including an upmarket multi-family [apartment complex](https://www.bing.com/ck/a?!=&amp%3B=&amp%3Bp=de77db4768069b6f9cc5dede684d7a24d63a26ae427cba1df961cf1ee2c0c618JmltdHM9MTc4Mzk4NzIwMA&amp%3Bptn=3&amp%3Bver=2&amp%3Bhsh=4&amp%3Bfclid=27bf2fec-574a-61a8-11c9-3d97566460bc&amp%3Bu=a1aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cuY29sbGllcnMuY29tLmNuL2VuLWNuL25ld3MvMjAyMjA0MjJjYXBpdGFsbWFya2V0YmVpamluZw&amp%3Bntb=1) in suburban Beijing and a mid-market hotel along Shanghai’s historic [Bund waterfront](https://www.mingtiandi.com/real-estate/finance/kkr-jv-acquires-shanghai-project-via-1-5b-fund/), according to people familiar with the matter.

Boston-based AEW is seeking buyers for the [HeXa International Plaza](https://www.aew.com/assets?region=asia&strategy=all-strategies) office tower and the mixed-use [Jing IN International Center](https://www.gmp.de/cn/projects/18588/jing-in-international-center) in Beijing, as well as the [Shanghai Pudong Development Bank](https://www.aew.com/assets?region=asia&strategy=all-strategies) building in the Lujiazui business district, the people said, asking not to be identified discussing private matters.

The two asset managers are anticipating that any sales would raise enough money to cover bank loans used to purchase the properties, which make up between 50% and 60% of the original prices paid, according to the people.

Representatives for KKR declined to comment. AEW didn’t respond to a request for comment.

KKR remains engaged in private equity business in China, where it has more than a dozen [active investments](https://www.kkr.com/invest/portfolio), including TikTok owner ByteDance, mushroom producer [Jiangsu Yuguan](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1536013D:CH) and private hospital operator Kareway Health, according to its website. Last year, KKR established its first [yuan-denominated fund](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/cbr02l6-ocrp110g0c_4gA) in China, raising capital from onshore investors including Ping An Capital Co.

Foreign buyers invested close to $140 billion in office towers, warehouses, shopping malls and data centers in China over the past 15 years, according to data from MSCI Real Capital Analytics. But they have largely switched to selling mode as the country’s economic slowdown and an unprecedented supply glut cause rents and values to tumble.

The downturn is especially painful for investors who made their bets near the 2019 peak, when the estimated market value of office assets was at a historic high.

In 2020, AEW bought a Grade-A office project in central Beijing with local private equity investor [Hony Capital Ltd.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/HONYCZ%20HK%20Equity), and later renamed it [HeXa International Plaza](https://www.aew.com/assets?region=asia&strategy=all-strategies) after renovations. In late 2021, KKR purchased a 3,000-unit multi-family project in suburban Beijing.

China’s property crisis has also caught many lenders off guard, including [HSBC Holdings Plc](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/HSBA%20LN%20Equity) and [Standard Chartered Plc](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/STAN%20LN%20Equity), which have set aside hundreds of millions of dollars for provisions against commercial real estate portfolios. Last year, lenders led by Standard Chartered took a more than [10% loss](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-03-20/blackrock-fund-s-forfeited-china-towers-to-be-sold-at-40-loss) when a Shanghai office complex previously owned by a [BlackRock Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/9903115D:US) fund was sold at a discount of more than 40%, people familiar with the matter said at the time.
