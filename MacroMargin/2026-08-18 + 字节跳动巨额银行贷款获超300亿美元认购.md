### 中文译文

## 字节跳动巨额银行贷款获超300亿美元认购

字节跳动在新加坡的办公室。

*摄影：Ore Huiying/彭博*

据知情人士透露，TikTok的开发商[字节跳动有限公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1774397D:CH)已为其巨额银团贷款吸引超过300亿美元的认购订单，这表明随着这家中国科技巨头加大对人工智能的投资，贷款机构表现出浓厚兴趣。

这笔200亿美元的[贷款](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-24/bytedance-seeks-20-billion-in-its-largest-ever-offshore-loan)上月由TikTok母公司字节跳动推向银团，附带增额选择权，但公司尚未就此做出决定，知情人士称。由于讨论的是私人事务，这些人士要求匿名。字节跳动计划将这笔新增贷款主要用于一般公司用途。

该贷款将是字节跳动最大的境外借款，期限为三年，可选择延长至最长五年。

总部位于北京的字节跳动是众多竞相构建AI能力的科技公司之一。该公司正在[考虑](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-27/bytedance-weighs-capex-of-as-much-as-70-billion-in-ai-push)将今年的资本支出提高到高达700亿美元——比去年总额翻了一番多——以扩大其数据中心和其他AI基础设施。如果经济和商业条件依然有利，它也已讨论将明年的这一数字提高至1000亿美元。

在更广泛的科技领域，四家美国超大规模企业——[亚马逊公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/AMZN%20US%20Equity)、[Alphabet公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/GOOGL%20US%20Equity)、[微软公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/MSFT%20US%20Equity)和[Meta平台公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/META%20US%20Equity)——计划今年进行高达[7250亿美元](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-30/us-big-tech-ratchets-up-ai-spending-past-700-billion-this-year)的资本支出，主要聚焦AI数据中心设备。

知情人士称，字节跳动的贷款有三个不同的承诺级别。委托牵头安排行和簿记行（MLABs）需承诺至少10亿美元，而委托牵头安排行（MLAs）的最低承诺为5亿美元。牵头安排行则可以参与低于5亿美元的承诺。

据知情人士透露，花旗集团和摩根大通是该贷款的协调人。承诺截止日期为8月19日。

字节跳动发言人未予置评。花旗集团和摩根大通拒绝置评。

字节跳动上一次[涉足](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2024-09-27/bytedance-to-sign-10-8-billion-corporate-loan-in-asian-record)全球贷款市场是在2024年，当时通过20多家贷款机构（包括国际银行和中资银行）筹集了108亿美元。

花旗集团、高盛集团和摩根大通是此次借款的协调人，所得资金部分用于为2021年筹集的50亿美元双档贷款进行再融资。

## ByteDance Draws Over $30 Billion in Orders for Jumbo Bank Loan

ByteDance offices in Singapore.

*Photographer: Ore Huiying/Bloomberg*

[ByteDance Ltd.](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1774397D:CH), the developer of TikTok, has attracted more than $30 billion of orders for its jumbo syndicated loan, people familiar with the matter said, indicating strong interest from lenders as the Chinese technology giant ramps up investments in artificial intelligence.

The $20 billion [facility](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-24/bytedance-seeks-20-billion-in-its-largest-ever-offshore-loan), which ByteDance launched into TikTok Owner ByteDance Launches $20B Loan Into Syndication last month, carries an option to upsize, but the company hasn’t made a decision on that yet, said the people, who asked not to be identified discussing private matters. ByteDance plans to use the new-money loan mainly for general corporate purpose, the people added.

The facility, which would be ByteDance’s largest offshore borrowing, carries a tenor of three years with options to extend to as long as five years.

Beijing-based ByteDance is among a slew of tech companies racing to build up their AI capabilities. The company is [weighing](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-27/bytedance-weighs-capex-of-as-much-as-70-billion-in-ai-push) plans to boost capital spending to as much as $70 billion this year — more than double last year’s total — to expand its data centers and other AI infrastructure. It has also discussed increasing that figure to $100 billion next year if economic and business conditions remain favorable.

Across the broader technology sector, four US hyperscalers — [Amazon.com Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/AMZN%20US%20Equity), [Alphabet Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/GOOGL%20US%20Equity), [Microsoft Corp.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/MSFT%20US%20Equity) and [Meta Platforms Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/META%20US%20Equity) — are planning as much as [$725 billion](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-30/us-big-tech-ratchets-up-ai-spending-past-700-billion-this-year) in capital spending this year, largely focused on AI data center equipment.

ByteDance’s loan has three different commitment levels, the people said. Mandated lead arrangers and bookrunners (MLABs) are required to commit at least $1 billion, while the minimum for mandated lead arrangers (MLAs) is $500 million, they said. Lead arrangers, meanwhile, can participate with commitments of less than $500 million, they added.

Citigroup Inc. and JPMorgan Chase & Co. are the coordinators for the loan, the people said. The commitment deadline is August 19.

A ByteDance spokesperson didn’t offer a comment. Citigroup and JPMorgan declined to comment.

The last time ByteDance [tapped](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2024-09-27/bytedance-to-sign-10-8-billion-corporate-loan-in-asian-record) the global loan market was in 2024, when it raised $10.8 billion via more than 20 lenders, comprising international and Chinese banks.

Citigroup, Goldman Sachs Group Inc. and JPMorgan were the coordinators of that borrowing, the proceeds of which were partly used to refinance a $5 billion dual-tranche facility raised in 2021.
