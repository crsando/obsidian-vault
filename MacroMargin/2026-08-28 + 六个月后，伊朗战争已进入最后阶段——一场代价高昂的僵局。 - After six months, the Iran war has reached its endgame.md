BEIRUT, Aug 27 (Reuters) - The [Iran war](https://www.reuters.com/world/iran/) has reached its endgame: a costly stalemate.
贝鲁特，8 月 27 日（路透社）——伊朗战争已进入最后阶段：一场代价高昂的僵局。

Six months after U.S. and Israeli forces attacked Iran, killing its supreme leader and maiming his [son and successor, opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/after-six-months-iran-war-has-reached-its-endgame-costly-stalemate-2026-08-27/IRAN-CRISIS/TRUMP), the Iranian government is under economic siege, but it still holds power and believes it has time on its side.
在美国和以色列军队袭击伊朗、杀死了该国最高领袖并使其儿子及继任者致残六个月后，伊朗政府虽面临经济封锁，但仍掌控着政权，并认为自己还有时间可挥。

A threat by [U.S. President Donald Trump](https://www.reuters.com/world/us/donald-trump/) to impose crippling new sanctions on Iran faces a fundamental problem: Tehran is betting he will not take the final step to break the stalemate - ‌aggressively enforcing [secondary sanctions](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/trump-threatens-isolate-iran-who-are-its-trading-partners-2026-08-20/) on the countries keeping its economy afloat, foremost among them China and India.
美国总统唐纳德·特朗普威胁要对伊朗实施严厉的新制裁，但这一举措面临着一个根本性问题：德黑兰认为他不会采取打破僵局的最终手段——即对那些支撑着伊朗经济运转的国家，尤其是中国和印度，施加严厉的次级制裁。

Washington's [latest measures](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/whats-included-new-us-sanctions-campaign-pressure-irans-economy-2026-08-25/), announced on Monday, mark a new phase in the conflict that began on February 28, shifting the battlefield from missiles and airstrikes to Iran's oil revenues, banks and trading partners.
华盛顿周一宣布的最新措施标志着 2 月 28 日爆发的这场冲突进入了新阶段，战场也从导弹与空袭转向了伊朗的石油收入、银行以及贸易伙伴。

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent has said the United States will cut off the financial lifelines sustaining Iran's economy, while a U.S.-led [naval blockade](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/us-eyes-indefinite-iran-naval-blockade-oil-supply-shortfall-deepens-2026-08-13/) restricts Tehran's oil exports and access to hard currency.
美国财政部长斯科特·贝森特表示，美国将切断维持伊朗经济的金融命脉，同时由美国主导的海上封锁措施也将限制德黑兰的石油出口及其获取硬通货的能力。

"The (Trump) administration is groping in a sort of desperate fashion to try to find a pathway out of this,” said Aaron David Miller, a former U.S. diplomat and senior fellow at Carnegie Endowment.
“特朗普政府正在以近乎绝望的方式试图寻找摆脱这一困境的出路，”曾任美国外交官、现为卡内基基金会高级研究员的亚伦·大卫·米勒如此说道。

Bessent invoked the 1944 D-Day landings in France during World War Two when he unveiled the sanctions, but Miller said: "To me, this isn’t D-Day. It’s Desperation Day. I don’t think the administration is any closer to finding a way out of this.”
在宣布实施制裁时，贝森特援引了二战期间 1944 年法国诺曼底登陆战作为例证，但米勒则表示：“对我来说，这并非诺曼底登陆日，而是绝望之日。我认为政府距离找到解决此问题的办法还远着呢。”

### BLOCKADE AND SANCTIONS 封锁与制裁

Michael Knights, head of research at Horizon Engage, a New York-based strategic advisory, said the blockade may prove more consequential than the ⁠sanctions themselves because Iran's immediate challenge was no longer how to survive economic pressure but how to break out of it.
总部位于纽约的战略咨询机构 Horizon Engage 的研究主管迈克尔·奈特斯表示，这种海上封锁可能比制裁本身带来更深远的影响，因为伊朗当前面临的挑战已不再是如何抵御经济压力，而是如何摆脱这种压力。

Tehran has spent decades adapting to sanctions and endured previous "maximum pressure" campaigns without altering its behaviour, he said. Mounting economic pressure, he said, may increase Iran's incentive to raise the cost of the confrontation for its adversaries by pressuring Gulf states, particularly Saudi Arabia, to push Washington towards a diplomatic off-ramp.
他说，德黑兰数十年来一直在适应各种制裁，并在以往的“最大压力”行动中也始终没有改变自己的行为方式。他认为，日益加剧的经济压力可能会促使伊朗试图通过向海湾国家、尤其是沙特阿拉伯施压，迫使华盛顿寻求外交解决方案，从而提高让对手付出更高代价的动机。

Knights said Iran could increasingly adopt the model used by its Houthi allies in the Red Sea: periodic attacks, intermittent negotiations and a prolonged campaign of disruption short of all-out war, potentially targeting shipping, ports, energy infrastructure or other critical assets to make the conflict increasingly costly for Gulf states to tolerate.
奈特斯表示，伊朗可能会越来越多地采用其在红海地区的胡塞盟友所使用的策略：定期发动攻击、间歇性进行谈判，并长期实施破坏活动，但避免全面战争；其攻击目标可能包括船舶、港口、能源基础设施或其他关键设施，以此提高海湾国家承受这一冲突的成本。

Washington rejects that assessment and says the balance of power is in its favour.
华盛顿方面否认这一评估，称力量平衡对自身有利。

"The Iranian economy is in free fall and the regime’s military has been decimated," U.S. State Department spokesperson Tommy Pigott said in response to questions for this article. “President Trump holds all the cards, and we are cutting off every financial lifeline the regime has remaining.”
针对本文的提问，美国国务院发言人汤米·皮戈特表示：“伊朗经济正在急剧下滑，该政权的军事力量也遭到了重创。”他同时称：“特朗普总统掌握着所有主动权，我们正在切断该政权仅存的每一条财务生命线。”

### IRANIAN OFFICIALS DISMISS US STRATEGY
伊朗官员驳斥美国的战略

Alan Eyre, a former U.S. diplomat who worked on Iran, questioned whether Washington has the international support and bureaucratic machinery to make the sanctions stick.
曾负责处理伊朗事务的美国前外交官艾伦·艾尔对华盛顿是否拥有足以让制裁措施得以落实的国际支持与官僚体系表示质疑。

He said that unlike the multilateral campaign preceding the [2015 nuclear deal](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/main-provisions-2015-iran-nuclear-deal-abandoned-by-trump-2026-06-12/) between Tehran and major powers, which curbed Iran's nuclear programme in exchange for lifting sanctions, the latest measures rely on public threats rather than sustained diplomacy with allies and trading partners.
他说，与 2015 年德黑兰与各大国达成核协议之前的多边行动不同——那次是通过限制伊朗的核计划来换取制裁的解除——而最新的措施则是依靠公开威胁，而非与盟友及贸易伙伴展开持续的外交对话。

Iranian officials dismiss the U.S. strategy as a replay of a playbook that has already failed. One senior Iranian official told Reuters that countries such as China would not stop cooperating with Iran ‌simply to serve ⁠U.S. interests.
伊朗官员认为美国的策略不过是重复那些早已失败过的手段。一位伊朗高级官员对路透社表示，中国等国不会仅仅为了迎合美国的利益就停止与伊朗的合作。

Some political analysts say the strategy faces a deeper flaw: from North Korea to Russia and Iran itself, sanctions have rarely forced governments to abandon goals they view as existential for their survival.
一些政治分析人士认为，这一策略存在更根本的缺陷：从朝鲜到俄罗斯，再到伊朗本身，制裁几乎从未迫使过那些将某些目标视为生存必需的政府放弃这些目标。

The senior Iranian official said Tehran believes Washington's broader objective is to fuel domestic discontent in the hope that economic hardship could eventually translate into political unrest, but that the "maximum pressure" campaign failed during Trump's first term and would fail again.
这位伊朗高级官员表示，德黑兰认为华盛顿的真正目的在于煽动国内不满情绪，期望经济困境最终能演变为政治动荡；不过“最大压力”策略在特朗普第一任期内就已经失败了，今后也照样会失败。

"If the pressure increases, Iran's approach will change too," he said, adding that Gulf Arab governments understood that squeezing Tehran further could put them at greater risk as well.
“如果压力增大，伊朗的策略也会随之改变，”他说，并补充称海湾阿拉伯国家政府也明白，进一步施压于德黑兰可能会使其自身面临更大的风险。

### THREAT OF UNREST FOR IRAN'S LEADERS
伊朗领导层面临动乱威胁

For Iran’s rulers, the biggest threat may not be economic pain itself. It may be the risk that worsening hardship again spills into [mass unrest](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/battered-by-war-irans-rulers-wary-more-economic-pain-unrest-if-us-tightens-2026-08-17/), according to insiders and an Iranian official.
据知情人士及一名伊朗官员称，对伊朗的统治者而言，最大的威胁或许并非经济困境本身，而是局势进一步恶化可能引发大规模动荡的风险。

Annual inflation reached 66% in July, while consumer prices were 87.9% higher than a year earlier, according to ⁠Iran’s Statistical Centre. Food prices rose 128% year on year.
根据伊朗统计中心的数据，7 月的年度通胀率高达 66%，消费者物价较去年同期上涨了 87.9%，食品价格则同比上升了 128%。

President Masoud Pezeshkian acknowledged the severity of the squeeze last week, saying: “Our problems have multiplied several times over, while our income has also fallen.”
马苏德·佩泽什基安总统上周承认了当前困境的严重性，他表示：“我们的问题大幅增加，而收入却也在下降。”

Economic grievances have repeatedly erupted into nationwide protests in recent years. In January, [demonstrations over economic hardship](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/iran-protests-abate-after-deadly-crackdown-residents-rights-group-say-2026-01-16/) were crushed by the Revolutionary Guards and the Basij militia, with thousands killed.
近年来，经济不满情绪屡次引发全国性抗议活动。1 月，针对经济困境的示威活动遭革命卫队和巴斯基民兵镇压，导致数千人丧生。

The appointment this month of Hossein Taeb, a key architect of previous crackdowns, to lead the Basij was one of the clearest signs yet of official [concern about renewed unrest](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/with-war-likely-over-iranian-rulers-must-face-demands-angry-embittered-2026-06-15/), a former reformist official said. The Basij, a paramilitary force affiliated with the Revolutionary ⁠Guards, has repeatedly been deployed by Iran's clerical rulers to suppress protests.
一位前改革派官员表示，本月任命曾在此前镇压行动中发挥关键作用的人士霍赛因·塔埃布来领导巴斯基军，这是官方对再次爆发的动荡感到担忧的最明显迹象之一。作为隶属于革命卫队的准军事力量，巴斯基军屡次被伊朗的宗教统治者用来镇压抗议活动。

That points to a paradox at the heart of Washington's strategy: sanctions may deepen Iran's economic damage without forcing its rulers to change course. The leadership has historically answered domestic unrest with repression, and external pressure with threats of retaliation abroad.
这揭示了华盛顿战略中的一个矛盾：制裁虽可能进一步加剧伊朗的经济困境，却无法迫使该国统治者改变政策。伊朗领导层历来以镇压手段应对国内动荡，以在国外进行报复的威胁来应对外部压力。

### 'DANGEROUS IMPASSE' “危险的僵局”

For the Gulf states, the calculation is especially fraught.
对海湾国家而言，这种权衡尤其复杂。

They prefer economic and diplomatic pressure to another round of war, but fear an outcome that enables Iran to declare victory while retaining the ability to disrupt traffic through the [Strait of Hormuz](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/iran-oman-discuss-temporary-hormuz-corridor-impasse-with-us-drags-2026-08-25/), a vital ⁠global oil supply route that Tehran has effectively blocked, pushing up [global oil prices](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/oil-prices-extend-losses-expectations-talks-ease-middle-east-supply-woes-2026-08-27/).
他们更倾向于通过经济和外交压力来避免另一场战争，但担心出现这样的结果：伊朗虽能宣称胜利，却仍能继续封锁霍尔木兹海峡，而这条至关重要的全球石油运输通道早已被德黑兰实际控制，从而导致全球油价上涨。

Abdulaziz Sager, chairman of the Saudi-based Gulf Research Center, said Gulf governments are unconvinced that sanctions alone can rapidly alter Iran's behaviour, given the leadership's resilience and ability to maintain internal control.
总部位于沙特的海湾研究中心主席阿卜杜拉齐兹·萨格尔表示，鉴于伊朗领导层的坚韧意志以及维持国内控制的能力，海湾各国政府并不认为仅靠制裁就能迅速改变伊朗的行为。

Significant political change, or the collapse of Iran's theocratic leadership, he said, is unlikely to come quickly.
他说，伊朗的政局不会很快发生重大变化，其神权统治体系也不大可能迅速崩溃。

The result is a dangerous impasse if [mediated talks](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/qatar-pm-visit-tehran-pursue-mediation-efforts-2026-08-26/) fail to produce a breakthrough.
如果调解谈判无法取得突破，局势就将陷入危险的僵局。

Washington wants Iran to abandon its leverage over Hormuz without conceding Tehran a role in managing the waterway, Iran wants the U.S. naval blockade lifted without appearing to surrender, and Gulf states want the conflict contained without letting Iran emerge as the victor.
华盛顿希望伊朗放弃对霍尔木兹海峡的控制权，但又不愿让德黑兰在该水道的管理中拥有任何角色；伊朗则要求美国解除海上封锁，同时又不愿显得屈服；而海湾国家则希望将冲突控制在一定范围内，防止伊朗成为赢家。
