### 中文译文

## 叙利亚告知美国，愿意大幅减少俄罗斯石油进口，消息人士称

*2026年5月14日，叙利亚巴尼亚斯，叙利亚石油运输公司入口。REUTERS/Mahmoud Hassano/文件照片*

贝鲁特/华盛顿，8月4日（路透社）——大马士革已同意大幅减少俄罗斯石油进口，作为与美国正在进行的关于解除对[叙利亚](https://www.reuters.com/world/syria/)的最后一项重大制裁指定的讨论的一部分，三位熟悉对话的消息人士告诉路透社。

这样的减少将标志着叙利亚能源政策的重大转变，尽管政治转向西方，叙利亚已变得[严重依赖](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/syria-relies-russias-oil-despite-pivot-west-2026-05-01/)俄罗斯石油，这也引发了一个问题：叙利亚将如何填补这一缺口。

专家表示，这也将成为美国对俄罗斯在该国经济影响力施加压力的新途径。

据一位直接了解对话情况的叙利亚消息人士、一位美国官员和另一位获悉此事的消息人士称，近几个月来，叙利亚高级官员已告知美国，他们愿意减少俄罗斯石油进口，同时他们讨论了华盛顿将叙利亚从一份已有数十年历史的支持恐怖主义国家名单中移除的问题。

这三名消息人士均因未获授权向媒体通报而匿名发言，他们表示，美国并未明确将削减进口作为解除指定的先决条件。

叙利亚愿意减少俄罗斯石油进口这一情况此前未被报道。

“对叙利亚而言，在不冒燃料短缺或进口成本上升风险的情况下，用其他石油替换大量俄罗斯原油不可能一夜之间实现。对大马士革的长期影响取决于华盛顿是否在施加这一压力的同时提供替代方案，”阿拉伯当代研究中心叙利亚分析师纳瓦尔·萨班（Navvar Saban）表示。

萨班表示，华盛顿正“日益针对那些让俄罗斯在叙利亚失去政治地盘后仍能保持影响力的经济网络。”

### 消息人士称，叙利亚渴望多元化石油供应

叙利亚新闻部和外交部，以及俄罗斯外交部和能源部，均未立即回应路透社的提问。

一位美国国务院官员表示，华盛顿正“鼓励叙利亚在国家的恢复和重建过程中与值得信赖的企业伙伴——尤其是美国公司——接触”，并期望叙利亚遵守美国对俄罗斯的制裁。

一名美国官员在回应向白宫提出的问题时表示，叙利亚的“支持恐怖主义国家”（SST）认定被移除与减少俄罗斯石油进口之间没有关联。

自1979年起实施的“支持恐怖主义国家”（SST）认定，是在美国前总统[唐纳德·特朗普](https://www.reuters.com/world/us/donald-trump/)政府及美国国会在前领导人巴沙尔·阿萨德被赶下台后解除全面制裁以来，仍对叙利亚实施的唯一重大制裁标签。

美国官员和了解此事的消息人士称，在讨论撤销SST认定期间，华盛顿要求并得到了叙利亚大幅减少俄罗斯石油进口的承诺。

据叙利亚消息人士称，美国官员曾告诉大马士革的对应官员，停止购买俄罗斯石油将“提高快速且无波折地解除SST认定的可能性。”

叙利亚回应称，取消该指定将允许大马士革建立多元化的能源供应网络，消息人士称。

尽管大部分西方制裁已被解除，叙利亚的石油供应选择仍然严重受限。路透社发现，今年俄罗斯对叙利亚的石油运输量跃升75%，达到每天约6万桶——虽然这只是俄罗斯全球每日石油出口的一小部分，但已使莫斯科成为叙利亚的主要原油供应国。

叙利亚消息人士说：“今天，叙利亚依赖俄罗斯石油是出于无奈，而非偏好。叙利亚官员经常（对美国）回答说：‘取消SST指定，我们就会从别处购买石油。我们现在根本没有其他选择。’”

没有消息人士提供这一变化的时间表或替代原油来源的细节。

一名叙利亚能源官员告诉路透社，大马士革已经在寻找新的供应商，并预计将出现“根本性变化”。

法国石油巨头道达尔能源（TotalEnergies）<[TTEF.PA](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/TTEF.PA)> 上月与叙利亚官员[讨论了签署](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/totalenergies-ceo-syria-discuss-signing-an-exploration-contract-2026-07-07/)一份海上勘探合同；叙利亚并于六月与康菲石油（ConocoPhillips）和Novaterra[签署协议](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/syria-signs-deal-with-conocophillips-novaterra-revive-gas-production-2026-06-16/)以恢复天然气生产。

### 美国转向在该地区施压俄罗斯

叙利亚是华盛顿SST名单上仅有的四个国家之一，与古巴、伊朗和朝鲜并列。

7月8日，特朗普通知国会，他打算将叙利亚从名单中移除，这触发了45天的通知期，在此期间他必须向国会证明叙利亚在过去六个月内没有为国际恐怖主义行为提供支持，并已保证今后不会支持此类行为。

自阿萨德下台以来，叙利亚新成立的伊斯兰主义领导的政府已承诺该国绝不再威胁地区或国际安全，加入了美国领导的打击伊斯兰国的联盟，并经常打击其与伊拉克和黎巴嫩边境的武器走私者。

尽管俄罗斯并未出现在[早先美国提出的制裁减免条件](https://www.reuters.com/world/us-gave-syria-list-conditions-partial-sanctions-relief-sources-say-2025-03-25/)中，但美国最近已发出信号，希望看到叙利亚与莫斯科保持距离。

国会已指示五角大楼评估减少俄罗斯在叙利亚影响力的选项，并确保其部队从塔尔图斯的一个海港和赫梅米姆的一个空军基地撤离。

路透社7月报道称，俄罗斯海军基地的一部分预计将变成一个[商业物流枢纽](https://www.reuters.com/world/russia-eyes-mid-july-start-commercial-logistics-hub-syrian-port-2026-07-09/)。

## Syria tells US it is willing to drastically drop Russian oil imports, sources say

*The entrance of the Syrian company for oil transport, in Baniyas, Syria, May 14, 2026. REUTERS/Mahmoud Hassano/File Photo*

BEIRUT/WASHINGTON, Aug 4 (Reuters) - Damascus has agreed to drastically reduce imports of Russian oil as part of its ongoing discussions with the United States on lifting the last major sanctions designation [on Syria](https://www.reuters.com/world/syria/), three sources familiar with the conversations told Reuters.

Such a reduction would represent a major shift in energy policy in Syria, which has become [heavily reliant](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/syria-relies-russias-oil-despite-pivot-west-2026-05-01/) on Russian oil despite a political pivot to the West, and raises the question of how Syria would plug the hole left behind.

It would also be a new way for the U.S. to exert pressure on Russia's economic sway in the country, experts say.

In recent months, senior Syrian officials have told the U.S. they are willing to reduce Russian oil imports as they discussed Washington removing Syria from a decades-old list of state sponsors of terrorism, according to a Syrian source with direct knowledge of the conversations, a U.S. official and another source briefed on the matter.

All three sources, speaking anonymously as they were not authorized to brief the press, said the U.S. did not explicitly frame the reduction as a pre-condition for lifting the designation.

Syria's readiness to decrease its Russian oil imports has not been previously reported.

"Replacing volumes of Russian crude cannot happen overnight without risking fuel shortages or higher import costs for Syria. The long-term impact on Damascus depends on whether Washington is pairing this pressure with alternatives," said Navvar Saban, a Syrian analyst at the Arab Center for Contemporary Studies.

Saban said Washington was "increasingly targeting the economic network that allows Russia to preserve influence after losing political ground in Syria."

### SYRIA KEEN TO DIVERSIFY OIL SUPPLIES, SOURCE SAYS

There was no immediate response to Reuters questions from Syria's information and foreign ministries, or from Russia's foreign and energy ministries.

A U.S. State Department official said Washington was "encouraging Syria to engage trusted corporate partners — especially U.S. firms — during the country’s recovery and reconstruction process" and expected Syria to comply with U.S. sanctions on Russia.

In response to questions to the White House, a U.S. official said there was no link between Syria's designation being removed and a reduction of Russian oil imports.

The SST designation, in place since 1979, is the only major sanctions label still in place on Syria after U.S. President [Donald Trump's](https://www.reuters.com/world/us/donald-trump/) administration and the U.S. Congress lifted comprehensive sanctions following the ouster of former leader Bashar al-Assad.

During discussions on rescinding the SST designation, Washington asked for and received a commitment from Syria to drastically reduce Russian oil imports, the U.S. official and the source briefed on the matter said.

According to the Syrian source, U.S. officials had told their Damascus counterparts that ending purchases of Russian oil would "improve the chances of lifting the SST designation quickly and without complications."

Syria responded that removing the designation would allow Damascus to build a diverse energy supply network, the source said.

Despite most Western sanctions being lifted, Syria's oil supply options remain severely limited. Russian oil shipments to Syria jumped 75% to about 60,000 barrels per day this year — a tiny share of Russia’s daily global oil exports but one that has made Moscow the dominant crude supplier to Syria, Reuters found.

"Today, Syria relies on Russian oil out of necessity, not preference. Syrian officials often respond (to the U.S.) by saying: 'Lift the SST designation and we'll buy oil from somewhere else. We simply don’t have alternatives right now,'" the Syrian source said.

None of the sources had details on a timeline for the change or alternative sources of crude.

A Syrian energy official told Reuters that Damascus was already seeking new suppliers and that a "radical change" was expected.

French oil major TotalEnergies' <[TTEF.PA](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/TTEF.PA)> [discussed signing](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/totalenergies-ceo-syria-discuss-signing-an-exploration-contract-2026-07-07/) an offshore exploration contract with Syrian officials last month and Syria [signed a deal](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/syria-signs-deal-with-conocophillips-novaterra-revive-gas-production-2026-06-16/) with ConocoPhillips and Novaterra to revive gas production in June.

### U.S. SHIFTING TO PRESS RUSSIA IN THE REGION

Syria is one of only four countries on Washington's SST list, sharing the title with Cuba, Iran and North Korea.

On July 8, Trump informed Congress of his intent to remove Syria from the list, triggering a 45-day notification period during which he must certify to Congress that Syria has not provided support for acts of international terrorism during the preceding six months and has provided assurances that it will not support such acts in the future.

Since Assad was toppled, Syria's new Islamist-led government has pledged the country would never again threaten regional or international security, joined the U.S.-led coalition fighting the Islamic State and has regularly busted weapons smugglers along its borders with Iraq and Lebanon.

While Russia did not feature in [earlier American conditions](https://www.reuters.com/world/us-gave-syria-list-conditions-partial-sanctions-relief-sources-say-2025-03-25/) for sanctions relief, the U.S. has signaled more recently that it wants to see Syria distance itself from Moscow.

Congress has directed the Pentagon to assess options for reducing Russia's influence in Syria and securing its forces' departure from a seaport in Tartous and airbase in Hmeimim.

Reuters reported in July that part of Russia's naval base is expected to be turned into a [commercial logistics hub.](https://www.reuters.com/world/russia-eyes-mid-july-start-commercial-logistics-hub-syrian-port-2026-07-09/)
