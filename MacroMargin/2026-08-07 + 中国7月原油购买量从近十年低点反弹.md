### 中文译文

## 中国7月原油购买量从近十年低点反弹

中国原油进口上月从近十年低点反弹，此前霍尔木兹海峡的流量回升，炼油商增加了包括俄罗斯在内的中东以外国家的采购。

根据周五公布的海关数据，7月份管道和海运流量升至3573万吨。这比6月份增长了22%，当时出货量创下[2016年10月](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-14/china-s-crude-oil-imports-plunge-to-lowest-in-nearly-a-decade)以来的最低水平。上月的数量相当于每天845万桶，远低于去年同期的水平。

在6月美国与伊朗达成临时和平协议后，来自波斯湾的石油流量有所增加，但最近敌对行动升级再次扰乱了霍尔木兹海峡的交通。这一影响还蔓延至红海，抑制了沙特阿拉伯（该亚洲国家的主要供应国之一）的出口。北京一直在从[俄罗斯](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-24/china-rushes-to-buy-east-russian-oil-as-middle-east-risks-grow)抢购货物以填补缺口。

与此同时，7月份煤炭进口总量为4273万吨，同比增长20%，此前5月底山西省发生的一起致命事故抑制了国内产量。天然气进口同比下降0.9%，因为来自中东的液化天然气供应继续受到战争影响。

### English Original

## China’s Crude Oil Purchases Rebound in July From Near-Decade Low

China’s crude oil imports rebounded last month from a near-decade low, after flows through the Strait of Hormuz picked up and refiners boosted purchases from nations outside the Middle East including Russia.

Pipeline and seaborne flows rose to 35.73 million tons in July, according to customs data released Friday. That’s up 22% from June, when shipments hit the lowest since [October 2016](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-14/china-s-crude-oil-imports-plunge-to-lowest-in-nearly-a-decade). The volume for last month is equivalent to 8.45 million barrels a day, well below the pace in the same period last year.

Oil flows from the Persian Gulf had ramped up following an interim peace deal between the US and Iran in June, but a recent escalation of hostilities has snarled Hormuz traffic once again. There’s also been a spillover to the Red Sea, crimping exports from Saudi Arabia, one of the Asian nation’s top suppliers. Beijing has been snapping up cargoes from [Russia](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-24/china-rushes-to-buy-east-russian-oil-as-middle-east-risks-grow) to fill the gaps.

Meanwhile, coal imports totaled 42.73 million tons in July, up 20% on an annual basis, after a deadly accident in Shanxi province in late May crimped domestic production. Natural gas imports fell 0.9% on-year, as liquefied supplies from the Middle East continued to be impacted by the war.
