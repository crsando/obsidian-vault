## 伊朗毁容的最高领袖在关乎存亡的战争持续六个月后依然不见踪影

2026 年 8 月 13 日，伊朗德黑兰的一条街道上，一名女子走在印有伊朗最高领袖莫杰塔巴·哈梅内伊照片的横幅旁。Majid Asgaripour/WANA（西亚新闻社）摄，路透社/档案照片提供。[购买许可权，打开新标签页](https://www.reutersconnect.com/item/a-woman-walks-next-to-a-banner-with-a-picture-of-irans-supreme-leader-mojtaba-khamenei-in-tehran/dGFnOnJldXRlcnMuY29tLDIwMjY6bmV3c21sX1JDMlBYTUFTMlJRMw%3D%3D/?utm_medium=rcom-article-media&utm_campaign=rcom-rcp-lead)

- 摘要
- 伊朗官员及高层消息人士表示，莫杰塔巴·哈梅内伊仍对重大决策拥有最终决定权。
- 自他在战时被任命以来，尚未有任何照片、视频或音频消息发布。
- 许多伊朗人对领导人的健康状况及当前角色持怀疑态度。
迪拜，8月28日（路透社）——在导致[战争](https://www.reuters.com/world/iran/)并将重伤的莫杰塔巴·哈梅内伊推上伊朗最高领袖之位的空袭过去六个月后，他依然完全不被伊朗民众所见所闻——这是伊朗领导层核心的真空。

关于他的健康状况与领导角色的疑问日益[严重](https://www.reuters.com/world/how-absence-irans-new-supreme-leader-is-becoming-liability-islamic-republic-2026-07-10/)，伊朗正面临关乎[战争](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/six-months-war-how-middle-east-conflict-has-shaped-financial-markets-2026-08-27/)的至关重要抉择——这场战争不仅点燃了中东的战火，更进一步破坏了本已十分脆弱的国民经济。

伊朗官员及该国其他高级消息人士表示，哈梅内伊仍然掌控着实权，出于安全原因在幕后统治，他在从毁容性创伤中恢复的同时，将广泛的日常权力委托给一批核心人物。

但是，自从他在战争开始一周后被任命以来，没有发布过任何照片、视频或音频信息，伊朗人对他在这个本应拥有绝对领袖话语权的伊斯兰共和国中的地位愈发怀疑。

华盛顿中东研究所的亚历克斯·瓦坦卡表示：“问题不再是穆杰塔巴是否活着，而是伊朗是否还有一位正常运作的最高领袖。”

哈梅内伊向伊朗人民传递信息的唯一方式是通过新闻播音员朗读的寥寥几份书面声明，他甚至缺席了上个月为其父亲举行的为期一周的奢华[葬礼仪式](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/iran-bury-slain-supreme-leader-culmination-mass-funeral-2026-07-09/)。

高级官员和其他内部人士将哈梅内伊的缺席归因于暗杀担忧，并表示他经常与高层人物会面，接收所有重要报告，并就每个重大问题做出最终决定。

总统马苏德·佩泽什基安于8月10日表示，他与哈梅内伊进行了一次长达七小时的会面。

但即使是伊斯兰共和国最坚定的支持者中，疑虑也在悄然滋生。

库姆的一位巴斯基志愿民兵成员表示：“我们需要听到我们领袖的声音，或者看到任何他仍在领导国家的迹象，才能更有信心。”库姆是伊朗神权国家核心的宗教神学院城市。

尽管这位要求匿名讨论这一敏感话题的巴斯基成员表示，他相信哈梅内伊确实仍在做出关键决策，但连一张照片都没有损害了士气，且不符合伊朗的利益。

在反对统治集团或希望推行全面改革的伊朗人中，这一信息更为直白。“我们怎么知道莫杰塔巴（Mojtaba）没有被杀？”德黑兰一名支持改革的商人沙赫鲁克（Shahrokh）说，他也要求匿名。

他补充说：“没有任何迹象能证明他还活着，更不用说有证据表明他参与决策了，这是不合逻辑的。”

### 革命卫队：权力结构的关键

伊朗在与最强大敌人的六个月的战争中挺了过来，虽受重创但依然屹立。其政治体制得以维系。其军队仍具备切断[全球能源供应](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/shipping-traffic-through-strait-hormuz-rises-slightly-data-shows-2026-08-27/)的能力，并可打击美国在海湾的盟友。

神职体制已经显示出，它能够承受最高领袖和高级指挥官损失而不致崩溃，并保持忠于其神权意识形态的政治和军事指挥链。

但伊朗经济正因冲突代价和长达数月的石油出口封锁而承压，里亚尔货币汇率暴跌，这可能导致大规模抗议重新爆发。当局曾于1月以杀害数千名示威者的方式镇压了这些抗议。

与此同时，统治集团围绕一个明确的要务进行了重组：维护国家、恢复威慑，并阻止经济影响破坏国家稳定。

消息人士称，革命卫队长期以来一直是强大的力量，自战争开始以来其影响力不断增长，并且在围绕最高国家安全委员会重组后的统治体系高层中，仍然是战略思维的核心。

那个最高层包括革命卫队高级指挥官以及佩泽什基安和议长穆罕默德·巴盖尔·卡利巴夫等资深政治人物。

路透社接触到的伊朗多名高级消息人士表示，哈梅内伊实际上正在与这些高层人物会面，全面参与审议，并在所有重大问题上拥有最终决定权。

一名伊朗高级官员表示，只有少数官员能与哈梅内伊保持直接接触，并且没有公布他的任何细节，以免泄露信息让美国能够锁定他为目标。

与此同时，一名接近哈梅内伊核心圈子的人士表示，他的健康状况明显好转，并形容他思维敏锐，积极参与决策。

第二位官员表示：“尽管面临所有压力，对人民的服务并未停止。我们的外交政策路线是统一的，我们的决策是连贯的。所有这些都表明最高领袖在掌控局面。”

即使在战前，哈梅内伊也喜欢像他父亲的高级助手那样在幕后发挥作用，避开公众关注。据高级消息人士称，他在2月28日凌晨受伤，当时以色列和美国发动战争，空袭炸死了他已故的父亲，他的脸部被毁容。

但现在，他被提升到一个被视为赋予神圣指引的角色，并且在国家自1979年革命以来最严重的危机时刻，这样的考虑看起来难以令人信服。

瓦坦卡表示：“与华盛顿达成真正的协议需要穆杰塔巴明确无误的批准，任何维持与美国长期且代价高昂的冲突的决定也是如此。”

“他的长期缺席造成了一个危险的局面，各对立派系都可以声称自己知道这位领导人真正想要什么，”他补充道。

DUBAI, Aug 28 (Reuters) - Six months after the airstrikes that began [the war](https://www.reuters.com/world/iran/) and propelled a badly wounded Mojtaba Khamenei into the role of Iran's supreme leader, he remains entirely unseen and unheard by Iranians — a vacuum at the heart of his country's leadership.
迪拜，8 月 28 日（路透社）——自那场导致战争爆发、并使身受重伤的莫杰塔巴·哈梅内伊出任伊朗最高领袖以来的六个月里，伊朗民众依然看不到他、听不到他的声音，伊朗领导层核心位置出现了真空状态。

Questions over his condition and role are growing [increasingly fraught](https://www.reuters.com/world/how-absence-irans-new-supreme-leader-is-becoming-liability-islamic-republic-2026-07-10/), with Iran facing critical decisions [over a war](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/six-months-war-how-middle-east-conflict-has-shaped-financial-markets-2026-08-27/) that has set the Middle East aflame ‌while trashing the country's already fragile economy.
关于他的健康状况与领导角色的疑问日益严重，伊朗正面临关乎战争的至关重要抉择——这场战争不仅点燃了中东的战火，更进一步破坏了本已十分脆弱的国民经济。

Iranian officials and other senior sources in the country say Khamenei remains in control, ruling from the shadows for security reasons as he recovers from disfiguring wounds while delegating broad daily authority to a coterie of top figures.

But with no photograph, video or audio message released since his appointment a week into the war, Iranians are ever more sceptical about his position in an Islamic Republic in which the leader's voice is meant to be absolute.

"The question is no longer whether Mojtaba is alive, but whether Iran still has a functioning supreme leader," said Alex Vatanka of the Middle East Institute in Washington.

Khamenei's only messages to the Iranian people have come via a handful of written statements read aloud by news broadcasters and he was even missing from the extravagant week of [funeral rites](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/iran-bury-slain-supreme-leader-culmination-mass-funeral-2026-07-09/) for his father last month.

Top officials and other insiders ⁠attribute Khamenei's absence to assassination fears and say he holds frequent meetings with top figures, receives all major reports and takes the final decisions on every important issue.

President Masoud Pezeshkian said on August 10 that he had held a seven-hour meeting with Khamenei.

But even among the Islamic Republic's most hardline supporters, doubts are creeping in.

"We need to hear our leader's voice, or see any sign that he is there on top and leading the country to give us more confidence," said a member of the Basij volunteer militia in Qom, the seminary city at the heart of Iran's theocratic state.

While the Basij member, who requested anonymity to discuss such a sensitive subject, said he believed Khamenei was indeed still making key decisions, the lack of even a photograph was damaging morale and not in Iran's interest.

Among Iranians who oppose the ruling hierarchy or want broad reforms, the message is blunter. "How do we know that Mojtaba has not been killed?" said Shahrokh, a pro-reform businessman in Tehran who also requested anonymity.

"It is not logical that there is not a single sign proving he is alive, let alone evidence that he is involved in decision-making," he added.

### REVOLUTIONARY GUARDS KEY TO POWER STRUCTURE

Iran has weathered six months of war with its most powerful enemies to emerge battered but still standing. Its political system has held. Its military retains the ability to choke off [global energy supplies](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/shipping-traffic-through-strait-hormuz-rises-slightly-data-shows-2026-08-27/) and strike U.S. Gulf allies.

The clerical establishment ‌has shown it ⁠can absorb the loss of a supreme leader and senior commanders without collapsing, maintaining a political and military chain of command loyal to their theocratic ideology.

But Iran's economy is buckling under the cost of the conflict and a months-long blockade of its oil exports, which has cratered the rial currency, risking a resumption of the mass protests that authorities crushed in January by killing thousands of demonstrators.

Meanwhile the ruling establishment has reorganised around a clear imperative: preserve the state, restore deterrence and stop the economic fallout from destabilising the country.

The Revolutionary Guards, long a powerful force, grew in influence from the start of the war and remain central to strategic thinking at the top of a reconfigured ruling system around the Supreme National Security Council, the sources said.

That upper echelon includes the ⁠top Guards commanders as well as senior political figures such as Pezeshkian and parliament speaker Mohammed Baqer Qalibaf.

Several senior sources reached by Reuters in Iran said Khamenei was in fact holding meetings with those top figures, was fully involved in deliberations and has the final say on all major questions.

A senior Iranian official said only a small number of officials retained direct contact with Khamenei in person and that no details of him had been published for fear of leaking information that could allow the U.S. to target ⁠him.

His health is meanwhile improving significantly, said a person close to Khamenei's inner circle, describing him as sharp and involved in decision-making.

"Despite all the pressures, services to the people have not stopped. Our foreign policy line is unified and our decisions are coherent. All of this shows the supreme leader is in charge," a second official said.

Even before the war, Khamenei preferred a backroom role as his father's top aide, shunning public attention. His wounds in the early hours of ⁠February 28, when Israel and the United States launched their war with air strikes that killed his late father, disfigured his face according to senior sources.

But now elevated to a role seen as conferring divine guidance, and at the country's moment of greatest crisis since the 1979 revolution, such considerations look unconvincing.

"A genuine deal with Washington requires Mojtaba's unmistakable approval and so does any decision to sustain a long and costly conflict with the United States," said Vatanka.

"His prolonged absence has created a dangerous situation in which rival factions can all claim to know what the leader really wants," he added.
