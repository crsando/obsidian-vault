### 中文译文

## 澳门赌场受创，中国打击行动令市场不安

澳门的赌场正面临自新冠疫情重新开放以来最严重的挫折，6月和7月收入[降幅超预期](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-01/macau-gaming-revenue-falls-again-on-typhoon-world-cup)，令投资者不安，并引发对这个世界最大赌博中心长期放缓的担忧。

这一下滑结束了连续16个月的增长，与此同时，北京正加大力度遏制资本外流——对离岸信托[征税](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-24/china-targets-offshore-trusts-in-sweeping-tax-clampdown)、[打击](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-22/china-cracks-down-on-illegal-cross-border-securities-trading)跨境券商，并[限制](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-03/china-extends-outbound-investment-curbs-to-individual-investors)个人居民对包括香港和澳门在内的境外投资。

![Visitors take photos of the light and water show outside the Wynn Palace casino resort in Macau. Photographer: Eduardo Leal/Bloomberg](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3b581cc90b1481e18717e7ddd7e9281b/3b581cc9-0b14-81b7-a9ff-d4400d7f319a-1336ea84f1094e99d6a462483fef8327.png)

游客在澳门永利皇宫赌场度假村外的光影水秀前拍照。摄影师：Eduardo Leal/Bloomberg

与此同时，中国资本市场——豪赌客的重要晴雨表——已经转淡。[MSCI中国指数](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/MXCN%20Index)今年下跌约8%，成为全球表现最差的股指之一。随着流动性枯竭和投资组合缩水，通常传导至澳门高端博彩业的财富效应明显减弱，引发了对更长期低迷的担忧。

这与去年的博彩热潮形成鲜明对比，当时该市六家博彩运营商的收入以及澳门赌场第二季度利润预计将升至疫情后最高水平，原因是中国内地和香港市场因股票上涨和IPO活动激增而流动性充裕。

根据花旗集团的调查，赌场的贵宾和高端玩家数量及其在6月和7月的消费均出现两位数下降。虽然世界杯被认为是博彩预算的暂时拖累，但今年的收入下滑是历史上赛事期间最严重的之一。

“晨星公司分析师詹妮弗·宋（Jennifer Song）表示：‘我们预测6月和7月会很糟糕，但实际数字比我们预期的还要糟糕，这正是市场所担心的。市场最担心的是，如果博彩收入增长放缓，那么赌场如何在成本增加的情况下维持利润率？’”

利润率已经在收缩，因为赌场在酒店客房、餐饮和演唱会门票等免费赠品上大举支出，并提高佣金和回扣以吸引大赌客。随着大众市场需求疲软和高端玩家退缩，投资者担心放缓可能延续到下半年。

据彭博对券商进行的月度调查，分析师已将今年澳门博彩收入增长的预测下调至3%，低于今年年初的7%。这将是自2023年澳门从新冠疫情中重新开放以来最慢的增速，年收入仍将比2019年水平低13%。

博彩运营商的合并利润预计将在第二季度下降7%，至2023年以来的最低水平，受世界杯拖累。[拉斯维加斯金沙集团](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/LVS%20US%20Equity)和[米高梅国际酒店集团](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/MGM%20US%20Equity)均报告其澳门业务利润率大幅下降，而[永利度假村](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/WYNN%20US%20Equity)的利润率则略有改善。

彭博行业研究[指数](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/BIGAMEAC%20Index)周五上午在香港交易中下跌2.4%，创一个月来最大跌幅。其中，[美高梅中国控股有限公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/2282%20HK%20Equity)股价下跌6.1%领跌，创两个月来最大跌幅。该指数今年以来已下跌约22%，而香港基准恒生指数下跌0.4%。

Natixis高级经济学家Gary Ng表示：“这背后的原因是中国内地缺乏财富效应，消费者情绪谨慎。”“而近期在税收和跨境资本流动方面的监管变化可能带来更大压力。”

当然，澳门的博彩活动在7月下旬有所回升。Seaport Research Partners分析师Vitaly Umansky预计，8月——历来是业绩强劲的月份——将受益于世界杯结束后被压抑的需求。他表示，本月博彩业的表现对于更清楚地判断当前放缓是否会持续“至关重要”。

中信里昂证券分析师Jeffrey Kiang表示，长期来看，澳门对高端和VIP赌客的依赖将继续拖累运营商的盈利能力。

他表示：“澳门博彩业的主要问题在于，中国整体消费者信心依然低迷。”

## Macau Casinos Hit by Slump as China Crackdown Rattles Market

Macau’s casinos are facing their sharpest setback since reopening from Covid, with [steeper-than-expected](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-01/macau-gaming-revenue-falls-again-on-typhoon-world-cup) revenue declines in June and July rattling investors and raising fears of a prolonged slowdown in the world’s biggest gambling hub.

The slump ends 16 straight months of growth and comes as Beijing steps up efforts to [stem ](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2026-06-23/china-s-offshore-capital-outflow-crackdown-hits-hong-kong-wealth-boom)capital outflow — [taxing](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-24/china-targets-offshore-trusts-in-sweeping-tax-clampdown) offshore trusts, [cracking down](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-22/china-cracks-down-on-illegal-cross-border-securities-trading) on cross-border brokerages, and [restricting](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-03/china-extends-outbound-investment-curbs-to-individual-investors) outbound investments including to Hong Kong and Macau by individual residents. Those moves have dampened sentiment among high-rollers, who are particularly sensitive to financial-market volatility.

Visitors take photos of the light and water show outside the Wynn Palace casino resort in Macau. Photographer: Eduardo Leal/Bloomberg

At the same time, China’s capital markets — an important barometer for big-spending gamblers — have turned sour. The [MSCI China Index](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/MXCN%20Index) is down about 8% this year, making it one of the worst performers globally. As liquidity dries up and portfolios shrink, the wealth effect that typically feeds through to Macau’s premium gaming segment has weakened noticeably, igniting fears of a longer downturn.

That’s a stark contrast to last year’s gaming boom, when the city’s six casino operators’ revenue and Macau Casinos’ 2Q Profit Expected to Rise to Highest After Covid as Chinese and Hong Kong markets were flush with liquidity thanks to equity gains and a spike in IPO activity.

Casinos saw double-digit declines in both the number of VIP and premium players, and their spending in June and July, according to Citigroup surveys. While the World Cup has been cited as a temporary drag on gaming budgets, this year’s revenue drop ranks among the worst historically for tournament periods.

“We predicted that June and July were going to be bad, but the actual numbers are even worse than our expectations, and that’s what the market is worried about,” said Morningstar Inc. analyst Jennifer Song. “The market’s biggest concern is if gaming revenue growth has slowed — then how are casinos able to maintain their margins while costs have increased?”

Profit margins have already been shrinking as casinos have been spending heavily on freebies, such as hotel rooms, dining and concert tickets, as well as stepping up commissions and rebates to draw big bettors. With mass-market demand softening and premium players pulling back, investors fear the slowdown could extend into the second half.

Analysts have cut their forecast for Macau’s gaming income growth this year to 3%, down from 7% at the beginning of this year, according to a Bloomberg monthly survey of brokerages. That would mark the slowest pace since the city reopened from Covid in 2023 and leave annual revenue still 13% below 2019’s level.

Casino operators’ combined profits are expected to Macau Casinos’ Profit Expected to Fall in 2Q on World Cup Drag 7% in the second quarter to the lowest since 2023. Both [Las Vegas Sands Corp.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/LVS%20US%20Equity) and [MGM Resorts International](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/MGM%20US%20Equity) reported significant margin drops for their Macau operations, while [Wynn Resorts Ltd.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/WYNN%20US%20Equity)’s improved slightly.

The Bloomberg Intelligence [index](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/BIGAMEAC%20Index) of Macau casino operators fell 2.4% Friday morning in Hong Kong trading, the most in a month. The decline was led by [MGM China Holdings Ltd.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/2282%20HK%20Equity) shares falling 6.1% — its steepest drop in two months. The index has tumbled about 22% this year, compared with a 0.4% drop in Hong Kong’s benchmark Hang Seng Index.

“Underlying this are the lack of a wealth effect and cautious consumer sentiment in mainland China,” said Gary Ng, senior economist at Natixis. “And recent regulatory changes on taxes and cross-border capital flows can exert greater pressure.”

To be sure, Macau’s gaming activities have picked up in the second half of July, and Seaport Research Partners analyst Vitaly Umansky expects August — historically a strong month — to benefit from the pent-up demand following the conclusion of the World Cup. The gaming sector’s performance this month would be “crucially important” in getting more clarity on whether the current slowdown may last, he said.

In the longer term, Macau’s reliance on premium and VIP gamblers will continue to weigh on operators’ profitability, said Jeffrey Kiang, analyst at Citic CLSA.

“The major issue of Macau gaming is that the general consumer confidence in China remains depressed,” he said.
