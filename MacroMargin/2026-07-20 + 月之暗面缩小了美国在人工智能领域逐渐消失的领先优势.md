## 月之暗面缩小了美国在人工智能领域逐渐消失的领先优势

2026年1月7日，中国上海，月之暗面人工智能（Moonshot AI）的Kimi聊天机器人显示在一台电脑屏幕上。*摄影师：Raul Ariano/彭博社*

作者：[Seth Fiegerman](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AWdZXvFO0ig/seth-fiegerman)
2026年7月20日 下午6:30 GMT+8

今年至少有一件事中美双方达成了共识：前者在人工智能竞赛中远远落后于后者。

但[月之暗面人工智能（Moonshot AI）上周五发布了Kimi K3模型，颠覆了这些看法](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-18/moonshot-s-kimi-upends-conventional-wisdom-on-us-lead-over-china)。这是一种更先进的开源权重模型，该公司称其在综合能力上超越所有竞争对手，仅落后于Anthropic的Claude Fable 5和OpenAI的GPT-5.6。突然间，美国在人工智能领域的领先优势似乎缩小到了仅剩几个月，甚至更短。

中国的快速进步可能从OpenAI和Anthropic那里抢走更多客户，而这两家公司正在努力增加收入并准备上市。中美之间日益激烈的人工智能竞赛也可能促使美国重新思考其监管该技术的方法，担心在关键时刻减缓发展速度。

与去年初DeepSeek的突然成功类似，[月之暗面的发布迫使硅谷重新考虑某些长期以来的假设](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-18/moonshot-s-kimi-upends-conventional-wisdom-on-us-lead-over-china)。这些假设包括构建顶级人工智能模型究竟需要多少计算能力，以及开源系统是否必然弱于专有产品。这正在[创造人工智能交易中的新赢家和输家](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-20/moonshot-ai-is-creating-new-winners-and-losers-in-the-ai-trade)，尽管[对芯片制造商的影响可能并不明显](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-20/moonshot-s-kimi-k3-may-be-more-about-memory-than-compute)。

这也为中国注入了新的动力。在DeepSeek之后，中国科技公司向市场投放了大量廉价的人工智能替代品，给美国竞争对手带来了新的定价压力。硅谷可能需要为新一轮冲击做好准备。

### **银行与金融**

**Moonshot AI正筹备在香港IPO**，计划在六个月内完成，已向投资者分发股东决议书以寻求上市批准。这家成立三年的中国AI初创公司正在完成一轮融资，估值可能超过300亿美元，此前其年经常性收入在6月达到3亿美元。此次IPO推进是在其发布Kimi K3模型之后，该公司称该模型在整体能力上优于除Anthropic的Claude Fable 5和OpenAI的GPT-5.6以外的所有竞争对手。

**软银集团正筹集新资金以支持其日益扩大的AI野心**，计划本月晚些时候在日本向机构投资者发行约600亿日元（3.69亿美元）的债券，其中包括500亿日元的三年期和100亿日元的五年期债券，由大和、野村和SMBC日兴证券安排。此次发行之前，该公司今年已通过零售次级债券筹集了6780亿日元，并于4月进入美国和欧元市场，所有这些都旨在支持其向OpenAI承诺的近650亿美元投资，以深化其AI投资策略。

中国光模块制造商Eoptolink Technology已秘密提交赴港上市申请，可能募集40亿至50亿美元，摩根士丹利将加入中信证券和摩根大通参与该交易。该公司在深圳上市的股票在过去一年中飙升近300%，市值超过1000亿美元，受AI计算环境中连接GPU集群的光模块需求激增推动。
根据彭博的Going Private通讯，阿波罗保险支持的资本引擎使其能够超越华尔街银行，为AI驱动的大型交易提供融资。Broadcom和Anthropic上季度向阿波罗和黑石寻求350亿美元债务融资，绕过了无法匹配阿波罗旗下Athene退休服务部门永久保险资本支持私人信贷的速度或规模的传统贷款机构。

**由中国计算机科学家李开复创立的AI初创公司01.ai**正推进在2027年IPO前筹集资金的计划。这家总部位于北京的公司正寻求在上市前融资轮中筹集资金，随后在香港交易所上市。李开复在成为企业家和投资者之前曾负责谷歌中国业务，他计划在01.ai首次公布年度业绩前后完成这笔融资。李开复的公司加入了一众希望在港交所筹集资金的AI实验室行列，以资助研究和支持人工智能服务所需的高成本基础设施。

### **技术**

**台积电将其美国投资扩大至2650亿美元**，在其现有的亚利桑那州承诺基础上再增加1000亿美元，受到强劲客户需求以及来自英特尔和埃隆·马斯克的Terafab等竞争对手的推动。CFO黄仁昭表示，这笔投资被描述为美国有史以来最大的外国直接投资，是华盛顿与台北之间更广泛贸易协议的一部分。这家为英伟达和苹果提供关键芯片的制造商报告了超出预期的6月季度业绩，并提高了增长预测，称AI大趋势是一个多年的增长驱动力。

**Meta Platforms正与Anthropic进行早期谈判**，计划租赁其数据中心的计算能力，潜在协议价值高达两年100亿美元。这些谈判是Meta更广泛计划的一部分，即通过租赁容量建立云计算业务，并销售对其基础设施上托管的AI模型的访问权限。CEO马克·扎克伯格指出，计算能力的报价已经足够有利可图，以至于可以考虑出租容量，而不是仅保留用于内部使用。

**周一下午阿里巴巴股价一度上涨5.4%**，此前该公司[发布了其旗舰模型 Qwen3.8 Max 的预览版](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-19/alibaba-s-qwen-unveils-preview-of-flagship-ai-model)，称其性能仅次于 Anthropic 的 Fable 5。此次周日发布仅距初创公司月之暗面（Moonshot AI）推出一款强大的新模型数日，该模型已搅动市场并引发美国对中国缩小与 Anthropic 和 OpenAI 等全球领导者差距的担忧。Qwen3.8 Max 拥有 2.4 万亿参数，与月之暗面的 Kimi K3 同属重量级。拥有 2.8 万亿参数的 K3 与顶级产品相抗衡，而阿里巴巴也设定了类似的高期望。

**ASML 宣布将向所有全球员工** 发放[一次性 20,000 欧元股票奖励，将于 1 月 1 日授予，2030 年归属](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-17/asml-to-pay-one-time-20-000-bonus-to-staff-as-ai-propels-demand)，原因是人工智能驱动的先进半导体需求激增，推动这家荷兰芯片制造设备巨头实现创纪录销售额。此举效仿了三星电子和 SK 海力士的类似派发，而台积电承诺今年将利润分享支出提高 30% 以上，反映出受益于人工智能基础设施支出的公司面临越来越大的与员工分享收益的压力。

### **政府与经济**

**全球竞相建设人工智能基础设施**已使这项技术成为[中国经济在多年最疲弱时期之一的关键增长点](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-20/ai-is-new-engine-keeping-chinese-economy-from-harder-landing)。凯投宏观数据显示，4-6 月当季，电子和信息技术行业对经济环比增长的贡献超过一半。中金公司此前估计，仅人工智能相关出口在名义 GDP 增长中就贡献了 1.1 个百分点，几乎是 2025 年全年份额的三倍。

**特朗普政府正在考虑** 设立一个[独立的人工智能监管机构，仿照美国金融业监管局（FINRA）](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-17/us-considers-creating-finra-like-watchdog-to-vet-top-ai-models)，该机构将向美国证券交易委员会汇报，并在行业参与下审查顶级人工智能模型的安全性。财政部长斯科特·贝森特协助制定了该提案，目前正由白宫办公厅主任苏西·威尔斯审查。该计划旨在为 Anthropic 和 OpenAI 等人工智能公司提供更大的监管一致性，这两家公司近期均因影响其最新模型的临时监管措施与政府发生冲突。

## Moonshot Narrowed the US’s Evaporating Lead in AI

Moonshot AI’s Kimi chatbot on a computer in Shanghai, China, on Jan. 7, 2026.*Photographer: Raul Ariano/Bloomberg*

By [Seth Fiegerman](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AWdZXvFO0ig/seth-fiegerman)July 20, 2026 at 6:30 PM GMT+8

There’s at least one thing both China and the US have agreed on this year: the former was comfortably behind the latter in the AI race.

But [Moonshot AI upended those beliefs on Friday with the release of Kimi K3](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-18/moonshot-s-kimi-upends-conventional-wisdom-on-us-lead-over-china), a more advanced open-weight model that it said outperforms all rivals except for Anthropic’s Claude Fable 5 and OpenAI’s GPT-5.6 on overall capability. Suddenly, the US lead in AI looks to have narrowed to just a couple of months, if that.

China’s rapid gains risk siphoning off more customers from OpenAI and Anthropic as the two firms work to bolster revenue and prepare to go public. The tightening AI race between the two countries could also prompt the US to rethink its approach to regulating the technology, for fear of slowing down development at a pivotal moment.

As with DeepSeek’s sudden success early last year, [Moonshot’s release is forcing Silicon Valley to reconsider certain long-held assumptions](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-18/moonshot-s-kimi-upends-conventional-wisdom-on-us-lead-over-china). Those range from how much computing power is really needed to build top-tier AI models to whether open systems must necessarily be weaker than proprietary offerings. And it’s creating [new winners and losers in the AI trade](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-20/moonshot-ai-is-creating-new-winners-and-losers-in-the-ai-trade), though the [implications on chipmakers may not be obvious](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-20/moonshot-s-kimi-k3-may-be-more-about-memory-than-compute).

It also provides a new jolt of momentum for China. After DeepSeek, Chinese tech firms flooded the market with cheap AI alternatives, putting new pricing pressure on US rivals. Silicon Valley may need to brace for a new onslaught.

### **Banking & Finance**

**Moonshot AI is preparing for a Hong Kong IPO** within six months, having distributed shareholder resolutions to investors seeking approval for the listing. The three-year-old Chinese [AI startup is finalizing a fundraising round that could value it at more than $30 billion](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-19/china-s-moonshot-plans-ipo-in-six-months-after-ai-breakthrough), after its annual recurring revenue reached $300 million in June. The IPO push follows the release of its Kimi K3 model, which the company says outperforms all rivals except Anthropic’s Claude Fable 5 and OpenAI’s GPT-5.6 on overall capability.

**SoftBank Group is raising fresh capital to fund **its expanding AI ambitions, [planning to sell approximately ¥60 billion ($369 million) in notes to institutional investors in Japan](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-17/softbank-plans-new-60-billion-bond-after-retail-overseas-deals) later this month, including ¥50 billion in three-year and ¥10 billion in five-year notes arranged by Daiwa, Nomura, and SMBC Nikko Securities. The offering follows ¥678 billion raised through retail subordinated bonds this year and taps into US and euro markets in April, all aimed at supporting the company’s nearly $65 billion commitment to OpenAI as it deepens its AI investment strategy.

Eoptolink Technology, a Chinese maker of optical transceivers whose components power the AI data center infrastructure relied upon by global tech giants like Alphabet, has filed confidentially for a Hong Kong listing that could raise $4 billion to $5 billion, with Morgan Stanley joining Citic Securities and JPMorgan on the deal. The company’s Shenzhen-listed shares have surged nearly 300% over the past year to a market capitalization exceeding $100 billion, driven by soaring demand for transceivers that link GPU clusters in AI computing environments.
Apollo’s insurance-backed capital engine is enabling the firm to outpace Wall Street banks in financing AI-driven mega-deals, according to Bloomberg’s Going Private newsletter. Broadcom and Anthropic turned to Apollo and Blackstone for $35 billion in debt last quarter, bypassing traditional lenders who couldn’t match the speed or scale of private credit backed by permanent insurance capital from Apollo’s Athene retirement services unit.

**01.ai, the Chinese AI startup founded by computer scientist** Kai-Fu Lee, is pushing ahead with plans to raise funds before an initial public offering in 2027. The Beijing-headquartered company is seeking to raise capital in a pre-IPO financing round before pursuing a listing on Hong Kong’s stock exchange. Lee, who headed Google’s Chinese operation before becoming an entrepreneur and investor, aims to conclude that funding around the time 01.ai unveils annual results for the first time. Lee’s company joins a roster of AI labs seeking to raise capital on the city’s bourse, to [bankroll research and the costly infrastructure needed to support artificial intelligence services](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-20/ai-pioneer-kai-fu-lee-s-startup-targets-hong-kong-ipo-next-year).

### **Technology**

**TSMC is expanding its US investment to $265 billion**, adding $100 billion to its existing Arizona commitments, driven by strong customer demand and competition from rivals including Intel and Elon Musk’s Terafab. CFO Wendell Huang said the investment, [described as the largest-ever foreign direct investment in the US](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-19/tsmc-s-265-billion-us-spend-driven-by-demand-rivals-cfo-says), is part of a broader trade deal between Washington and Taipei. The chipmaker, a key supplier to Nvidia and Apple, reported better-than-expected June quarter results and raised its growth forecasts, citing the AI megatrend as a multi-year growth driver.

**Meta Platforms is in early discussions to lease** computing power from its data centers to Anthropic, with the potential arrangement valued at as much as $10 billion over two years. The talks are [part of Meta’s broader plans to build a cloud computing business by leasing capacity](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-17/meta-in-talks-to-sell-computing-power-to-anthropic-nyt-reports) and selling access to AI models hosted on its infrastructure. CEO Mark Zuckerberg noted that offers for computing power have been lucrative enough to consider renting capacity rather than reserving it solely for internal use.

**Alibaba shares rose as much as 5.4% on Monday** after the company [launched a preview version of its flagship Qwen3.8 Max model](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-19/alibaba-s-qwen-unveils-preview-of-flagship-ai-model), describing it as second only to Anthropic’s Fable 5. The Sunday release came only days after startup Moonshot AI unveiled a powerful new offering that’s roiled markets and triggered concern in the US about China closing the gap on global leaders like Anthropic and OpenAI. Qwen3.8 Max has 2.4 trillion parameters, joining Moonshot’s Kimi K3 in the heavyweight class. With 2.8 trillion parameters, K3 rivals top offerings and Alibaba is setting similarly high expectations.

**ASML announced it will award all** global employees [a one-time €20,000 share bonus on Jan. 1 that vests in 2030](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-17/asml-to-pay-one-time-20-000-bonus-to-staff-as-ai-propels-demand), as surging AI-driven demand for advanced semiconductors fuels record sales at the Dutch chipmaking equipment giant. The move follows similar payouts from Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix, while Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co. committed to boost profit-sharing payments by more than 30% this year, reflecting growing pressure on companies benefiting from AI infrastructure spending to share earnings with workers.

### **Government & Economics**

**A global rush to build out artificial intelligence infrastructure** has turned the technology into a [key pocket of growth in China’s economy](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-20/ai-is-new-engine-keeping-chinese-economy-from-harder-landing) during one of its weakest stretches in years. Electronics and information technology contributed over half of the economy’s expansion in April-June on a quarter-on-quarter basis, according to Capital Economics. Earlier estimates from China International Capital showed AI-related exports alone accounted for 1.1 percentage points in the growth of nominal gross domestic product during the first four months, nearly triple their share for the full year of 2025.

**The Trump administration is considering** establishing an[ independent AI regulatory agency, modeled after FINRA](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-17/us-considers-creating-finra-like-watchdog-to-vet-top-ai-models), that would report to the SEC and vet the safety of top AI models with industry input. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent helped develop the proposal, which is currently under review by White House Chief of Staff Susie Wiles. The plan aims to provide greater regulatory consistency for AI companies like Anthropic and OpenAI, both of which have recently clashed with the government over ad-hoc oversight measures affecting their latest models.
