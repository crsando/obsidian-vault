### 中文译文

## 中国审议新法律以扩大其海外反腐范围

中国最高立法机构正在审议一项新的法律草案，该草案将正式授予当局在海外追查跨境腐败案件的权力，这是习近平主席大规模反腐运动可能走向全球的最新迹象。

据官方新华社报道，全国人民代表大会常务委员会在本次全国人大会议期间开始对法案进行首次审议，会议为周二至周五。该法案全文尚未公开。

该法案被称为《反跨境腐败法》，将把自习近平2012年上台以来中国处理跨境腐败的现行做法编纂成法典。新华社报道称，其目的是加强北京的法律工具箱，并“解决在发现犯罪、收集证据、追回非法资金和确保定罪方面长期存在的困难”。

该法案扩展了构建中国跨境法律体系的努力，随着这个世界第二大经济体扩大其全球足迹。中国媒体财新早些时候[报道](https://china.caixin.com/2024-07-22/102218611.html)，援引学者的话说，该法律将管辖“涉及中国公司海外分支机构和外国企业在华分支机构的合规管理以及管辖权和程序问题”。

中国已经在约120个国家开展两项行动，通过与外国政府合作，遣返逃犯并从海外追回资金。在习近平2012年至2022年的前两个任期内，这些行动已从海外抓获超过10,000名犯罪分子。但这些行动也遭到了抵制，包括担心北京正在施加跨国[胁迫](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2022-01-18/china-s-global-dragnet-grinds-on-after-netting-10-000-fugitives)。

《华尔街日报》在2023年报道称，中国在最新行动中已在海外派驻反腐人员，此举引发了对域外执法的担忧。

### English Original

## China Reviews New Law to Extend Its Anti-Corruption Reach Abroad

China’s top legislature is reviewing a new draft law that would officially grant the authorities power to pursue cross-border corruption cases overseas, the latest sign that President Xi Jinping’s sweeping anti-graft campaign could go global.

The Standing Committee of the National People’s Congress started to consider the bill for its first reading during parliament’s current session, which runs from Tuesday to Friday, according to the official Xinhua News Agency. The full text of the legislation is yet to be made publicly available.

Known as the Anti-Cross-Border Corruption Law, the bill would codify China’s existing practices of tackling cross-border corruption that have been introduced since Xi came to power in 2012. The aim is to strengthen Beijing’s legal toolkit and “solve long-standing difficulties in uncovering offenses, collecting evidence, recovering illicit funding and securing convictions,” Xinhua reported.

The bill extends an effort to build up China’s body of cross-border laws as the world’s second largest economy broadens its global footprint. Chinese media outlet Caixin earlier [reported](https://china.caixin.com/2024-07-22/102218611.html), citing scholars, that the law will govern “compliance management and jurisdictional and procedural issues involving Chinese companies’ overseas branches and foreign businesses’ branches in China.”

China has already been running two operations in some 120 nations to repatriate fugitives and recover money from abroad, through cooperation with foreign governments. The campaigns have netted over 10,000 offenders from overseas during Xi’s first two terms from 2012 to 2022. But they have also been met with backlash, including concerns that Beijing is inflicting transnational [coercion](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2022-01-18/china-s-global-dragnet-grinds-on-after-netting-10-000-fugitives).

The Wall Street Journal reported in 2023 that China had China Stations Anti-Graft Personnel Abroad in New Push, WSJ Says anti-corruption officials abroad, raising concerns of extraterritorial law enforcement.
