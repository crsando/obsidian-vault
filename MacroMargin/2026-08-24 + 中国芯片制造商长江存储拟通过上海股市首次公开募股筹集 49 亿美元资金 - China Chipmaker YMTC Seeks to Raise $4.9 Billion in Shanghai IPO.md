### [Finance 金融](https://www.bloomberg.com/finance)

By [Foster Wong](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AQz-_jdi0LI/foster-wong) 作者：黄福斯特August 21, 2026 at 10:44 PM GMT+8

Chinese chipmaker [Yangtze Memory Technologies Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1577197D:CH), known as YMTC, plans to raise 33 billion yuan ($4.9 billion) in a Shanghai initial public offering, following in the footsteps of rival [CXMT Corp.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/688825%20CH%20Equity)’s near-record share sale.
总部位于武汉的中国芯片制造商长江存储科技股份公司计划通过在上海证券交易所上市来筹集 330 亿元人民币（约合 49 亿美元），此举是跟随其竞争对手中芯国际创纪录的融资规模的脚步而进行的。

YMTC’s listing application on the tech-focused Star Market has been accepted by the Shanghai Stock Exchange, according to bourse’s latest [filing ](https://www.sse.com.cn/listing/renewal/ipo/index_listing_detail.shtml?auditId=2251)on Friday. The Wuhan-based company plans to use the proceeds to upgrade production lines and boost research and development, according to the [prospectus](https://static.sse.com.cn/stock/disclosure/announcement/c/202608/002251_20260821_7075.pdf).
根据上海证券交易所周五发布的最新公告，YMTC 在专注于科技领域的星板市场的上市申请已获得批准。根据该公司的招股书，这家总部位于武汉的企业计划将筹集的资金用于升级生产线并加强研发工作。

“The large-scale adoption of AI is driving massive, long-term demand for data storage. The listing will help the company expand advanced production capacity and ensure stable supply in China and globally,” YMTC said in the prospectus.
YMTC 在招股书中表示：“人工智能的广泛应用正在带来巨大且长期的数据存储需求。此次上市将助力该公司提升先进的生产能力，从而确保在中国及全球市场的稳定供应。”

The chipmaker’s earnings have grown rapidly since it turned profitable in 2024. Net income reached 33.4 billion yuan in the first three months of 2026, more than doubling the 14.2 billion yuan net income for its whole 2025, according to the prospectus.
根据招股书显示，自 2024 年实现盈利以来，这家芯片公司的营收增长迅速。2026 年前三个月的净利润达到了 334 亿元人民币，这一数字是 2025 年全年 142 亿元人民币净利润的两倍多。

YMTC is now on track to follow CXMT’s fundraising example as China’s homegrown [chip giants look to tap capital markets](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-17/china-s-chip-industry-has-its-breakout-moment-thanks-largely-to-cxmt-huawei) to expand capacity and bolster Beijing’s drive for tech self-sufficiency. CXMT last month [raised](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-26/china-memory-champion-cxmt-set-to-debut-after-9-8-billion-ipo) 66.6 billion yuan, the second-biggest mainland debut ever.
目前，YMTC 正有望效仿中芯国际的融资模式——在国产芯片巨头们纷纷借助资本市场扩大产能、助力北京实现科技自主可控的目标背景下，中芯国际上个月已筹集了 666 亿元人民币，这一金额堪称中国大陆企业首次上市融资中的第二高。

[Citic Securities](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/600030%20CH%20Equity) and [CSC Financial](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/6066%20HK%20Equity) are joint sponsors of the YMTC IPO.
