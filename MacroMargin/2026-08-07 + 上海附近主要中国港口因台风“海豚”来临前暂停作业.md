## 上海附近主要中国港口因台风“海豚”来临前暂停作业

## 上海附近主要中国港口因台风“海豚”来临前暂停作业

8月5日，中国舟山的一个港口。*摄影：胡社友/新华社/盖蒂图片社*

彭博新闻社
2026年8月7日下午1:37（GMT+8）

上海附近的主要港口，包括中国最大的船舶加油枢纽，已在台风来临前暂停作业。该台风预计将于周日深夜前后穿越中国东海岸。

据航运业人士看到的海洋警报，舟山（加油港口）以及宁波和长江口的作业已经停止。根据通知，船只仍然可以离开港口。在气旋来临前，船只通常会在离基础设施较远的海上锚泊。

台风“海豚”目前位于日本冲绳岛附近，并向中国浙江省温州市移动。中国气象机构预测，该气旋将携带[最高持续风速](https://www.nmc.cn/publish/weather-bulletin/index.htm)每小时151公里（94英里）登陆，相当于一级飓风。

据彭博新闻社看到的通知，负责监督船舶交通安全的中国主要机构的省级实体——江苏海事局本周早些时候发布通知，从8月3日起，船舶不得进入长江。自那以来，舟山一直处于关闭状态。港口关闭通常不会由当局宣布，因为在台风期间停止作业被视为常规做法。

负责监管上海以北的日照、烟台和大连等石油进口枢纽周边交通的政府机构也敦促船舶避免向南航行。负责海事服务的中国交通运输部没有立即回复寻求评论的传真查询。

预计上海和杭州周边沿海地区从本周末起将迎来[强降雨](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-05/typhoon-dolphin-heads-for-china-bringing-heavy-rain-risk-to-east-coast)，尤其是周日。根据中国气象局的预报，浙江部分地区24小时内降雨量可能高达[550毫米](https://www.nmc.cn/publish/precipitation/1-day.html)（21.7英寸）。降雨随后将于下周初向内陆移动，波及江西和安徽等省份。

在与江苏接壤的浙江，海事部门已启动台风应急响应。据央视报道，全省162条客运轮渡航线已停运，474艘施工船舶已转移至安全避风处。

## Key Chinese Ports Near Shanghai Halt Operations Ahead of Typhoon Dolphin

## Key Chinese Ports Near Shanghai Halt Operations Ahead of Typhoon Dolphin

A port in Zhoushan, China on Aug. 5. *Photographer: Hu Sheyou/Xinhua/Getty Images*

By Bloomberg News
August 7, 2026 at 1:37 PM GMT+8

Major ports near Shanghai, including China’s largest refueling hub for ships, have suspended operations ahead of a typhoon that’s likely to cross the country’s east coast around late Sunday.

Activities have been halted at Zhoushan, the refueling port, along with Ningbo and Changjiangkou, according to maritime alerts seen by people in the shipping sector. Vessels can still leave the ports, according to the notices. Ships will usually anchor offshore away from infrastructure prior to a cyclone.

Typhoon Dolphin is currently near Japan’s Okinawa Island and tracking toward the city of Wenzhou in China’s Zhejiang province. The Chinese weather agency forecasts the cyclone will cross the coast packing [top sustained winds](https://www.nmc.cn/publish/weather-bulletin/index.htm) of up to 151 kilometers (94 miles) per hour, equivalent to a Category 1 hurricane.

The Jiangsu Maritime Safety Administration, a province-level entity of China’s main agency that oversees vessel traffic safety, issued a notice earlier this week that ships will not be allowed to enter the Yangtze River from Aug. 3, according to notices seen by Bloomberg News. Zhoushan has been closed since then. Port closures are often not announced by authorities, as it’s deemed routine to halt operations during typhoons.

The government agency that oversees traffic around oil-import hubs such as Rizhao, Yantai and Dalian, which lie north of Shanghai, also urged vessels to avoid sailing southward. China’s Ministry of Transport, which oversees maritime services, didn’t immediately reply to a faxed query seeking comment.

Coastal areas around Shanghai and Hangzhou are expected to see [heavy rainfall ](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-05/typhoon-dolphin-heads-for-china-bringing-heavy-rain-risk-to-east-coast)from the weekend, particularly on Sunday. Parts of Zhejiang may see up to [550 millimeters](https://www.nmc.cn/publish/precipitation/1-day.html) (21.7 inches) over a 24-hour period, according to forecasts from the China Meteorological Administration. Rain will then move inland early next week to provinces including to Jiangxi and Anhui.

In Zhejiang, which borders Jiangsu, maritime authorities have activated typhoon emergency responses. All 162 passenger ferry routes in the province have been suspended, while 474 construction vessels moved to safe shelter, according to CCTV.
