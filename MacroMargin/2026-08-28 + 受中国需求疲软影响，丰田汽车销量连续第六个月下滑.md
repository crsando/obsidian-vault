### 中文译文

## 受中国需求疲软影响，丰田汽车销量连续第六个月下滑

[丰田汽车公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/7203%20JP%20Equity)的汽车销量连续第六个月下滑，原因是汽油价格上涨抑制了中国对非电动车的需求。

该公司在周五的一份声明中表示，包括子公司大发汽车公司（Daihatsu Motor Co.）在内，7月份全球销量同比下降5.3%，至912,683辆。产量下降1.4%，至934,953辆。

中国国内汽车制造商之间的激烈竞争正在排挤进口商，而中东的动荡扰乱了关键供应路线，导致油价飙升。这种压力使全球制造商吃紧，迫使就连丰田也自2月份以来在中国这个世界最大的汽车市场让出份额。

在中国，丰田和雷克萨斯品牌7月销量同比下降24%，中国本土品牌正大量推出软件密集型的纯电动汽车。中东销量下降了近45%。

北美销量相对持平，日本和欧洲大部分地区销量则有所增长，这在很大程度上得益于油电混合动力汽车的再度流行。

丰田每年向中东出口约50万至60万辆汽车。该公司5月表示，预计其中近一半的销量将受到影响。

上个月，丰田[上调](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-04/toyota-unveils-6-3-billion-buyback-raises-profit-outlook-earnings-auto)了截至2027年3月的财年利润预期，至3.4万亿日元，理由是美国的混合动力车需求和汇率顺风提供了缓冲。今年早些时候，由于中东冲突导致原材料成本上升，该公司曾预测利润将罕见下滑。

丰田表示，混合动力车的销量有望在今年首次突破500万辆。替代动力系统和日元疲软一直是丰田的救命稻草，尽管日本汽车制造商在中国奋力追赶比亚迪（BYD Co.）及其他本土品牌。

[本田汽车公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/7267%20JP%20Equity)周五表示，7月销量同比增长3.4%，达到289,142辆。其中美国市场增长13%，中国销量下降44%。全球产量下降3.3%。

[日产汽车公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/7201%20JP%20Equity)上月售出219,495辆汽车，同比下降近17%。产量下降近16%。

## Toyota Car Sales Fall for Sixth Month on Weak China Demand

[Toyota Motor Corp.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/7203%20JP%20Equity)’s car sales fell for a sixth straight month as rising gasoline prices weighed on demand for non-electric vehicles in China.

Global sales in July, including those of subsidiary [Daihatsu Motor Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/7262%20JP%20Equity), fell 5.3% from a year earlier to 912,683 units, the company said in a statement Friday. Production declined 1.4% to 934,953 vehicles.

Intense competition among China’s domestic carmakers is crowding out importers, while turmoil in the Middle East have disrupted critical supply routes and sent oil prices soaring. The pressure is straining global manufacturers, forcing even Toyota to cede ground in China, the world’s largest car market, since February.

Toyota and Lexus brand sales in July were down 24% year-on-year in China, where domestic brands are churning out software-heavy, battery-powered electric vehicles. Middle East sales fell by almost 45%.

Sales were relatively flat in North America, and up in Japan and across most of Europe, thanks in large part to the resurging popularity of gas-electric hybrid cars.

Toyota exports roughly 500,000 to 600,000 vehicles annually to the Middle East. The manufacturer said in May that it expects almost half of that volume to be affected.

Last month, Toyota [raised](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-04/toyota-unveils-6-3-billion-buyback-raises-profit-outlook-earnings-auto) its profit outlook for the fiscal year ending March 2027 to ¥3.4 trillion, emboldened by US demand for hybrids and a cushion provided by currency tailwinds. The manufacturer had forecast a rare drop earlier this year as it braced for higher raw material costs stemming from the Middle East conflict.

Toyota said hybrid sales are on track to exceed 5 million units for the first time during this calendar year. Alternative powertrains and a weak yen have been a saving grace for Toyota, even as Japanese carmakers struggled to keep up with [BYD Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/1211%20HK%20Equity) and other domestic brands in China.

[Honda Motor Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/7267%20JP%20Equity)’s sales in July rose 3.4% from last year to 289,142 units, it said Friday. That included a 13% jump in the US as well as a 44% drop in China. Global production fell 3.3%.

[Nissan Motor Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/7201%20JP%20Equity) sold 219,495 automobiles last month, a drop of almost 17% from a year earlier. Production fell almost 16%.
