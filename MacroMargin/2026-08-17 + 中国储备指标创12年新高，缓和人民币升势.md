### 中文译文

## 中国储备指标创12年新高，缓和人民币升势

据分析师称，中国储备资产的关键指标创下逾12年来最大季度涨幅，因当局吸收了大量外币流入，此举有助于缓和人民币的升值步伐。

根据彭博基于国家外汇管理局数据的计算，国际收支口径的外汇储备在第二季度录得747亿美元的[流入](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CHCUFC%20%20%20Index)，为2014年第一季度以来最大增幅。这一积累恰逢在截至6月的三个月内在岸人民币连续第六个季度上涨。

储备的增加有助于中国人民银行维持汇率稳定以支持经济。这也帮助缓和了人民币的升势，尽管强劲的出口增长继续给人民币带来升值压力。

“他们一直在吸收资金流入以减缓人民币升值，”澳新银行集团亚洲研究主管Khoon Goh表示。“如果中国央行不吸收这些资金流入，人民币面临的升值压力会更大。”

![图片](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3bf81cc90b14812a9015e7a884cb39c1/3bf81cc9-0b14-81ad-a236-c28feb9952b8-496c6fcc7f3cd505db2e6d540788d207.png)

在岸人民币已逐步升至2023年以来最强水平。周一，中国央行将人民币中间价设定得弱于市场预期，这是自彭博2018年开始记录数据以来[持续时间最长的一次](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-11/yuan-s-recent-calm-may-end-on-seasonal-factors-analysts-say)。即便如此，中间价仍处于三年多来的最强水平。

“当局仍在设定更强的中间价，表明他们继续容忍货币进一步升值，但将继续管理升值步伐，”Goh表示。

然而，渣打银行大中华区策略主管Becky Liu表示，国际收支口径外汇储备的增加并不意味着央行政策转向。“调整美元流动性并非中国央行的选择，”Liu表示。“如果商业银行决定向中国央行出售过剩美元，他们就必须买入。”

她补充说，商业银行选择与央行轧平外汇头寸，是因为它们无法自行在资产负债表上吸收企业抛售的如此大量的外汇。

与月度储备不同，国际收支数据通过剔除汇率和资产价格波动来追踪实际资本流动，从而更清晰地反映跨境流动。

### English Original

## China Reserve Gauge Hits 12-Year High, Smoothing Yuan Rise

China’s key gauge of reserve assets saw its biggest quarterly rise in more than 12 years as authorities absorbed heavy foreign currency inflows, a move that helps smooth the pace of the yuan’s gains, according to analysts.

The foreign reserve under the balance of payments recorded an [inflow](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CHCUFC%20%20%20Index) of $74.7 billion in the second quarter, the biggest increase since the first quarter of 2014, according to Bloomberg calculations based on data from the State Administration of Foreign Exchange. The accumulation coincided with a sixth straight quarterly gain for the onshore yuan in the three months ended June.

The reserve build-up aided the People’s Bank of China’s efforts to maintain a stable exchange rate to support the economy. It also helped temper the yuan’s gains, even as strong export growth continues to put appreciation pressure on the yuan.

“They have been absorbing the inflows to slow the yuan’s appreciation,” said Khoon Goh, head of Asia research at Australia & New Zealand Banking Group. “If the PBOC were not absorbing those inflows, appreciation pressure on the yuan would have been greater.”

The onshore yuan has gradually inched up to its strongest level since 2023. The PBOC set the yuan fixing weaker than market estimates on Monday, [its longest such streak](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-11/yuan-s-recent-calm-may-end-on-seasonal-factors-analysts-say) since Bloomberg’s data started in 2018. Even so, the fixing was still set at the strongest level in more than three years.

“The authorities are still setting the fixings stronger, suggesting they continue to accommodate further currency appreciation, but will continue to manage the pace,” Goh said.

However, Becky Liu, head of Greater China strategy at Standard Chartered Bank, said the increase in the foreign reserves under the balance of payments does not signal a shift in central bank policy. “It is not a PBOC choice to alter dollar liquidity,” Liu said. “They have to buy if the commercial banks decide to sell excessive dollars to the PBOC.”

It is commercial banks’ choice to square their FX positions with the central bank, as they cannot absorb so much FX sold by corporates on their own balance sheets, she added.

Unlike monthly reserves, balance-of-payments data tracks actual capital flows by stripping out exchange-rate and asset-price fluctuations, giving a clearer view of cross-border flows.
