## 贝森特在国债收益率攀升后加大债务回购

华盛顿的美国财政部大楼。

*摄影师：Stefani Reynolds/彭博社*

作者：[迈克尔·麦肯齐](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AVgVy7_JF34/michael-mackenzie) 和 [格雷格·里奇](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AUJMWGi29pc/greg-ritchie)
2026年8月19日 晚上8:50 GMT+8
*更新于2026年8月19日 晚上9:26 GMT+8*

在长期国债收益率触及多年高点后，美国财政部意外宣布将加大长期政府债务的回购力度。

在发布本季度回购[计划表](https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0590)仅两周后，财政部周三表示，将“将10年期至30年期领域的流动性支持回购操作规模至少增加一倍”。最长期债券的[收益率](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/USGG30YR%20Index)下跌近10个基点至5.185%，从2007年以来的最高水平回落。

财政部长斯科特·贝森特去年曾援引该回购计划，作为财政部“可部署的庞大工具箱”的一部分，在必要时应对国债市场的失序。自上任以来，他还多次表示，他的关键金融市场基准是10年期国债收益率。去年11月，他说：“我的工作是成为国家首席债券销售员。国债收益率是衡量这项工作成功与否的有力晴雨表。”

“这届政府需要一场胜利，也许这就以试图人为抑制长期国债收益率的形式出现。”布兰迪万全球投资管理公司的投资组合经理杰克·麦金太尔表示。“他们必须尝试点办法。全球对长端国债的情绪已经处于我很久以来见过的极度看跌状态。”

官员们做出这一宣布之际，本周全球长期政府债券收益率升至显著水平——美国30年期国债交易价格创2007年以来最高。交易员们还在为160亿美元的新20年期债券拍卖做准备。上周的10年期拍卖创下该期限自2007年以来的最高融资成本，而一天后的30年期拍卖收益率则是自[2001年](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-13/us-braces-for-30-year-bond-auction-at-highest-yield-since-2001)以来的最高。

观看：美国财政部将把长期名义附息证券的流动性支持回购操作的最大规模至少提高一倍，至每次操作至少40亿美元。Michael McKee报道。来源：彭博

“如果收益率走得太远，财政部会尝试反击——现在我们知道了一些痛点在哪里，”Natixis North America美国利率策略主管John Briggs表示。

“回购操作规模的增加反映了财政部希望在长期名义板块提供更大流动性支持的意愿，这些板块有市场参与者持续强劲的参与，正如财政部在长期回购操作中常规收到的大量高质量报价所证明的那样，”财政部在声明中表示。

## Bessent Boosts Debt Buybacks After Climb in Treasury Yields

## Bessent Boosts Debt Buybacks After Climb in Treasury Yields

The US Treasury Department building in Washington.

*Photographer: Stefani Reynolds/Bloomberg*

By [Michael MacKenzie](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AVgVy7_JF34/michael-mackenzie) and [Greg Ritchie](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AUJMWGi29pc/greg-ritchie)
August 19, 2026 at 8:50 PM GMT+8
*Updated on August 19, 2026 at 9:26 PM GMT+8*

The US Treasury unexpectedly announced it’s ramping up buybacks of long-dated government debt, taking the action in the wake of yields on such securities hitting the highest levels in years.

Just two weeks after releasing its [planned schedule](https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0590) for buybacks this quarter, the Treasury Department on Wednesday said it’s “increasing, by at least double, the size of liquidity support buyback operations” for securities dated from the 10-year to the 30-year sector. [Yields](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/USGG30YR%20Index) on the longest bond dropped nearly 10 basis points to 5.185%, pulling back from their highest level since 2007.

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent invoked the buyback program last year as part of the department’s “big toolkit we can roll out” if needed to address dislocation in the Treasuries market. He’s also repeatedly said, since taking office, that his key financial-market benchmark is 10-year yields. Last November, he said, “my job is to be the nation’s top bond salesman. And Treasury yields are a strong barometer for measuring success in this endeavor.”

“This administration needs a win and maybe that comes in the form of artificially trying to keep long Treasury rates contained,” said Jack McIntyre, a portfolio manager at Brandywine Global Investment Management. “They have to try something. Sentiment around the long-end globally is about as bearish as I have seen in a very long time.”

Officials made the announcement as long-dated government bond yields around the globe rose to significant levels this week — with the US 30-year trading at its highest since 2007. Traders are also preparing for a $16 billion auction of new 20-year bonds. A 10-year auction last week drew the highest financing cost at that tenor since 2007, while a 30-year sale a day later was at the greatest yield since [2001](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-13/us-braces-for-30-year-bond-auction-at-highest-yield-since-2001).

WATCH: The US Department of the Treasury will at least double the maximum size of its liquidity support buyback operations for longer-dated nominal coupon securities to at least $4 billion per operation. Michael McKee reports. Source: Bloomberg

“If yields go to far, Treasury will try and fight it — and now we know where some pain points are,” said John Briggs, head of US rates strategy at Natixis North America.

“This increase in buyback operation sizes reflects Treasury’s desire to provide greater liquidity support in longer-dated nominal sectors where there is consistent strong sponsorship from market participants, as evidenced by the significant volume of high-quality offers Treasury routinely receives in longer-dated buyback operations,” the Treasury said in its statement.
