Philadelphia Fed President and CEO Anna Paulson shared her views on the economy and monetary policy in an essay.

费城联储主席兼首席执行官安娜·保尔森在一篇文章中分享了她对经济和货币政策的看法。

The Federal Reserve’s dual mandate of maximum employment and price stability serves as my foundation when thinking about monetary policy.[1](https://www.philadelphiafed.org/the-economy/monetary-policy/monetary-policy-perspective-keeping-an-open-mind#1) My team and I analyze data and speak with people across the Third District and beyond to evaluate economic conditions and understand the forces shaping the economy. This process and the thoughtful discussions I have with other Federal Open Market Committee (FOMC) members inform my judgment about how to deliver the best possible results for the American people.

美联储的最大就业和物价稳定双重使命是我思考货币政策的基础。[1](https://www.philadelphiafed.org/the-economy/monetary-policy/monetary-policy-perspective-keeping-an-open-mind#1) 我和我的团队分析数据，与第三联邦储备区及更广范围的人们交流，以评估经济状况并了解影响经济的力量。这一过程以及我与联邦公开市场委员会（FOMC）其他成员的深入讨论，为我如何为美国人民带来尽可能好的结果提供了判断依据。

The importance of price stability cannot be overstated. Low, predictable inflation is a bedrock of economic prosperity. Price stability allows families to budget for monthly expenses *and* make long-term plans. It helps businesses allocate resources effectively today *and* invest for the future. Low and stable inflation also helps to foster maximum employment. After more than five years of above-target inflation, it is essential that we return inflation to the 2 percent target.

物价稳定的重要性怎么强调都不为过。低且可预测的通胀是经济繁荣的基石。物价稳定使家庭能够为每月开支做预算*并*制定长期计划。它帮助企业今天就有效地配置资源*并*为未来进行投资。低而稳定的通胀也有助于促进最大就业。在经历五年多高于目标的通胀之后，我们必须让通胀回到2%的目标水平。

It is within this context that I supported last week’s FOMC decision to maintain the target range for the federal funds rate. The recent improvement in some inflation data is welcome. It is a step in the right direction, but it is only one step. I am focused on gathering more information to better understand what’s happening to underlying inflation and the impact of supply shocks from energy and tariffs. And I am keeping an open mind to ensure policy delivers price stability and maximum employment.

正是在这种背景下，我支持上周FOMC维持联邦基金利率目标区间的决定。近期一些通胀数据的改善是受欢迎的。这是朝着正确方向迈出的一步，但仅仅是一步。我专注于收集更多信息，以更好地了解潜在通胀的情况以及能源和关税带来的供应冲击的影响。我保持开放心态，以确保政策实现物价稳定和最大就业。

### Current Conditions: Much Has Happened, My Outlook Is Steady

### 当前形势：发生了很多事，我的展望依然稳定

The path to price stability starts with understanding current economic conditions.

通往物价稳定的道路始于对当前经济状况的理解。

Much has happened over the last few months. Yet when I look at the data and consider conversations with business leaders and what we're hearing from workers and consumers, I find that my overall assessment of where the economy is headed has not substantially changed. The conflict in the Middle East has repeatedly subsided and then flared up again. Old tariffs expired, new tariffs were put in place. More artificial intelligence (AI) investments were announced, and new AI models were released.

过去几个月发生了很多事。然而，当我审视数据、回顾与商业领袖的对话以及我们从工人和消费者那里听到的情况时，我发现我对经济走向的总体评估没有实质改变。中东冲突反复平息后又再度爆发。旧关税到期，新关税出台。更多人工智能（AI）投资被宣布，新的AI模型也已发布。

Despite these significant developments, the fundamental economic picture remains similar. Inflation is elevated. The labor market appears stable. Second quarter GDP increased a modest 1.5 percent, but underlying growth metrics for investment and consumption were strong, and we are on track for growth in the neighborhood of 2 percent this year. The conflict in the Middle East and tariffs are causing uncertainty and have pushed inflation up. The AI infrastructure buildout is driving growth and creating price pressures in some areas. However, AI’s long-term impact on the economy is less clear. These are all familiar themes that continue to shape my thinking.

尽管有这些重大进展，基本经济图景仍相似。通胀高企。劳动力市场似乎稳定。第二季度GDP温和增长1.5%，但投资和消费的基础增长指标强劲，我们有望实现今年约2%的增长。中东冲突和关税带来不确定性，并推高了通胀。AI基础设施的扩建正在推动增长，并在一些领域造成价格压力。然而，AI对经济的长期影响尚不明朗。这些熟悉的话题继续影响着我的思考。

So, when I look at current conditions through the lens of our dual mandate, I see a labor market that is relatively stable and near full employment and an inflation rate that is too high. The most recent inflation data were somewhat encouraging. Headline PCE inflation eased to 3.7 percent in June, thanks to lower energy prices. The June ceasefire in the Middle East provided temporary relief for consumers and businesses. Oil prices have since jumped and remain volatile, but the brief period of Middle East stability demonstrated that supply shocks can be temporary, reinforcing the case for looking through such disruptions when setting monetary policy.

因此，当我通过双重使命的视角审视当前状况时，我看到的是一个相对稳定、接近充分就业的劳动力市场，以及一个过高的通胀率。最近的通胀数据有些令人鼓舞。由于能源价格下降，6月份整体PCE通胀率放缓至3.7%。中东的6月停火为消费者和企业提供了暂时的缓解。此后油价上涨并保持波动，但中东短暂的稳定表明供应冲击可能是暂时的，这加强了在制定货币政策时忽略此类干扰的理由。

The ability to look through supply shocks depends, in part, on inflation expectations and underlying inflation. Expectations remain well-anchored. However, when I try to assess underlying inflation — what inflation would look like if we stripped out the temporary effects of tariff increases and energy prices, I estimate that underlying inflation is running somewhere between 2.4 and 2.8 percent.[2](https://www.philadelphiafed.org/the-economy/monetary-policy/monetary-policy-perspective-keeping-an-open-mind#2) Underlying inflation has been elevated for a long time, and it is what I am most focused on as I evaluate our progress toward the 2 percent target.

忽略供应冲击的能力部分取决于通胀预期和基础通胀。预期仍然保持良好锚定。然而，当我试图评估基础通胀——即剔除关税上涨和能源价格的暂时影响后的通胀情况——我估计基础通胀率在2.4%至2.8%之间。[2](https://www.philadelphiafed.org/the-economy/monetary-policy/monetary-policy-perspective-keeping-an-open-mind#2) 基础通胀率已持续偏高很长时间，这正是我在评估我们向2%目标进展时最为关注的。

Turning to the other part of our mandate, the labor market has stabilized. The unemployment rate has been remarkably steady, fluctuating only between 4 and 4.5 percent since June 2024 and currently standing at 4.2 percent, near what I consider to be full employment. Employers tell me that layoffs are infrequent. Despite this, surveys suggest that workers are worried about job security and that jobseekers are pessimistic about finding work.

转向我们使命的另一部分，劳动力市场已经稳定。失业率一直非常平稳，自2024年6月以来仅在4%至4.5%之间波动，目前为4.2%，接近我认定的充分就业水平。雇主告诉我，裁员并不频繁。尽管如此，调查显示，工人担心工作保障，求职者对找到工作感到悲观。

### The Balance of Risks: Pointing Towards Inflation

### 风险平衡：指向通胀

Given my assessment of current conditions, the key question is whether the federal funds rate is appropriately calibrated to return inflation to 2 percent in an acceptable time frame while sustaining full employment. Based on the data and my conversations, I see two possibilities.

鉴于我对当前状况的评估，关键问题是联邦基金利率是否校准得当，以便在可接受的时间范围内将通胀率恢复到2%，同时维持充分就业。根据数据和我的交流，我看到两种可能性。

One possibility is that the current setting of the federal funds rate is mildly restrictive and this will bring inflation to 2 percent in an acceptable time frame. Moderate wage growth and subdued expectations for future wage growth support this argument. Elevated mortgage rates and muted housing market activity demonstrate how higher interest rates are constraining many households. While second quarter consumption increased 3.2 percent, there are signs of weaker demand among low- and middle-income families.

一种可能性是，当前联邦基金利率的设定具有适度限制性，这将在可接受的时间范围内将通胀率降至2%。温和的工资增长和对未来工资增长的温和预期支持了这一论点。高企的抵押贷款利率和低迷的房地产市场活动表明，较高的利率正在限制许多家庭。尽管第二季度消费增长了3.2%，但有迹象显示中低收入家庭的需求减弱。

The pressures on consumers are affecting businesses as well. For example, the CEO of a large consumer goods manufacturer recently told me that, despite increased costs from multiple fronts, his firm is keeping prices fixed because shoppers are very price sensitive. Small businesses, meanwhile, are reporting more challenges, and weakness is particularly notable among small firms serving the real estate sector. While the AI buildout is creating some price pressures, these should remain contained if policy is appropriately calibrated.

消费者的压力也在影响企业。例如，一家大型消费品制造商的CEO最近告诉我，尽管来自多方面的成本增加，他的公司仍保持价格不变，因为购物者对价格非常敏感。与此同时，小企业报告了更多挑战，其中服务于房地产行业的小企业尤其疲软。虽然人工智能建设正在造成一些价格压力，但如果政策校准得当，这些压力应该可控。

All of this information points to current policy being mildly restrictive. The other possibility, however, is that current policy is not restrictive enough to deliver our target rate of 2 percent inflation. The most compelling evidence for this view is straightforward: Inflation has been above target for more than five years and even stripping out temporary factors, measures of underlying inflation have edged down only modestly over the past year or so. Persistently elevated inflation suggests more restrictive policy may be needed. Many firms have ready access to credit, and this is supporting robust investment, especially in AI. This outsized demand for AI infrastructure is creating pressure throughout the supply chain, driving up prices for key inputs and flowing through to higher consumer prices. While AI's productivity benefits may eventually help moderate inflation, those gains appear further off, while the inflationary pressures from the buildout are more immediate. Even if supply shocks fade and no new shocks hit, this information suggests policy might need to be more restrictive to achieve the Fed’s target.

所有这些信息都表明当前政策具有适度限制性。然而，另一种可能性是，当前政策不足以将通胀率降至2%的目标水平。支持这一观点的最有力证据很简单：通胀率高于目标已超过五年，即使剔除暂时因素，基础通胀指标在过去一年左右也仅略有下降。持续高企的通胀表明可能需要更具限制性的政策。许多企业可以方便地获得信贷，这支撑了强劲的投资，尤其是在人工智能领域。对人工智能基础设施的巨大需求正在给整个供应链带来压力，推高关键投入品价格，并传导至更高的消费者价格。虽然人工智能的生产率提升最终可能有助于缓和通胀，但这些收益似乎更为遥远，而建设带来的通胀压力则更为直接。即使供应冲击消退且没有新的冲击出现，这些信息也表明，政策可能需要更具限制性才能实现美联储的目标。

### Implications for Monetary Policy

### 对货币政策的影响

I am keeping an open mind about where policy goes from here. As I've outlined, I see two plausible scenarios for how current policy is affecting inflation, and the incoming evidence will clarify which path we’re on and what adjustments, if any, may be needed.

我对政策接下来走向保持开放心态。如我所概述，对于当前政策如何影响通胀，我看到了两种可能的情形，而后续证据将澄清我们处于哪条路径，以及可能需要哪些调整（如果有的话）。

How will I assess which path we're on? By watching how the evidence accumulates. If policy is appropriately calibrated, I would expect to see growing signs that inflation is coming down — more months of improving inflation data; reports from those making pricing and hiring decisions that align with a gradual return to 2 percent; signs that pressures from tariffs, energy, and AI are contained rather than intensifying; and inflation expectations that are well-anchored and consistent with 2 percent. If instead underlying inflation remains stubbornly elevated, the passage of time without progress would itself signal that more restrictive policy is needed.

我如何评估我们处于哪条路径？通过观察证据如何积累。如果政策校准得当，我预计会看到越来越多的通胀回落迹象——更多月份的通胀数据改善；那些做出定价和招聘决策的人士的报告与逐步回归2%相一致；来自关税、能源和人工智能的压力受到抑制而非加剧的迹象；以及良好锚定且与2%一致的通胀预期。相反，如果潜在通胀仍然顽固地居高不下，那么时间流逝而无进展本身便表明需要更紧缩的政策。

There will almost always be a range of interpretations about what is happening in the economy. That is why I am committed to keeping an open mind as I assess the evidence and determine the appropriate path for policy. My highest priority is delivering 2 percent inflation while sustaining full employment.

对于经济中正在发生的情况，几乎总会有多种解读。因此，在评估证据并确定政策的适当路径时，我致力于保持开放心态。我的最高优先事项是在维持充分就业的同时实现2%的通胀。
