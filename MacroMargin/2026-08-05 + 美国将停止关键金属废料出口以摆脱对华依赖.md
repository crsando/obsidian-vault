### 此举正值美国争相确保对国防及其他行业至关重要的原材料之际

美国正在禁止锂离子电池和钨废料的出口，以减少对中国的依赖，并为国防工业确保更多所需的关键材料。

根据《联邦公报》公布的商务部规定，从本月晚些时候开始，美国境内的某些电池废料和钨废料必须在国内销售。该禁令将生效一年。

此举正值美国和其他西方政府竞相确保对国防及其他行业至关重要的原材料，但这些原材料大部分由中国控制。近年来，北京[开始限制其中一些材料的出口](https://www.wsj.com/world/china/china-flexes-chokehold-on-rare-earth-magnets-as-exports-plunge-in-may-c1adac50?mod=article_inline)，引发全球警觉。

钨是一种化学元素，因其高强度和耐热性而用于坦克装甲、武器和发动机部件。根据美国地质调查局的数据，中国是世界上最大的钨生产国，并且[已将这种元素](https://www.wsj.com/livecoverage/trump-tariffs-us-trade-stock-market-02-04-2025/card/china-restricts-exports-of-critical-minerals-in-retaliatory-move-e8omEEQJLU911Z1jt4gT?mod=article_inline)列入其出口限制范围。

锂离子电池废料——通常被称为黑粉——含有锂以及其他关键材料，如钴、镍、锰和石墨。这些材料可以通过化学处理或冶炼等回收工艺从黑粉中回收。

《联邦公报》通知称，特朗普总统认定关键材料短缺正在构成日益严重的国防和安全风险。该通知称，新限制措施对于“确保这些被视为国防必需的材料的充足供应”是必要的。

在某些情况下可以给予豁免，包括如果这些材料被出口到其他地方进行加工或精炼，然后再返回美国。

美国已经在[投入巨资](https://www.wsj.com/world/europe/the-u-s-is-trampling-allies-in-the-global-hunt-for-rare-earths-82371d13?mod=article_inline)，试图启动关键原材料的非中国供应链。它已在战乱国家[谈判获得矿产资源优惠准入](https://www.wsj.com/world/a-look-at-the-ukrainian-minerals-that-trump-wants-54edb78a?mod=article_inline)，并正在为外国实体供应美国公司的关键材料项目提供融资。

另一项旨在遏制美国对中国依赖的举措是，国防部已下令承包商在明年之前将中国稀土从磁铁供应链中移除。

## **U.S. to Halt Key Metal Waste Exports in Push to Break China Reliance**

### Move comes as U.S. races to secure the raw materials that are crucial for defense and other industries

The U.S. is banning the export of lithium-ion battery and tungsten waste in an effort to curb its reliance on China and secure more of the critical materials its defense industry needs.

Beginning later this month, certain battery scrap and tungsten waste in the U.S. must be sold domestically, according to a Department of Commerce rule published on the Federal Register. The ban will be in effect for a year.

The move comes as the U.S. and other Western governments race to secure the raw materials that are crucial for defense and other industries, but are largely controlled by China. Beijing [began throttling exports of some of those materials](https://www.wsj.com/world/china/china-flexes-chokehold-on-rare-earth-magnets-as-exports-plunge-in-may-c1adac50?mod=article_inline) in recent years, raising global alarm.

Tungsten is a chemical element that is used in tank armor, weapons and engine components because of its high strength and heat resistance. China is the world’s largest producer of tungsten, according to the United States Geological Survey, and [has included the element](https://www.wsj.com/livecoverage/trump-tariffs-us-trade-stock-market-02-04-2025/card/china-restricts-exports-of-critical-minerals-in-retaliatory-move-e8omEEQJLU911Z1jt4gT?mod=article_inline) in its export restrictions.

Lithium-ion battery scrap—often referred to as black mass—contains lithium and other critical materials such as cobalt, nickel, manganese and graphite. Those materials can be recovered from black mass through recycling processes such as chemical processing or smelting.

The Federal Register notice said President Trump had determined that a shortage of critical materials posed a growing national defense and security risk. The new restrictions are necessary “to ensure an adequate supply of these materials deemed essential to the national defense,” the notice said.

Exemptions could be granted in certain cases, including if the materials are exported for processing or refining elsewhere and then returned to the U.S.

The U.S. is already [spending heavily](https://www.wsj.com/world/europe/the-u-s-is-trampling-allies-in-the-global-hunt-for-rare-earths-82371d13?mod=article_inline) in a bid to kick-start non-Chinese supply chains for critical raw materials. It has [negotiated preferential access to mineral wealth](https://www.wsj.com/world/a-look-at-the-ukrainian-minerals-that-trump-wants-54edb78a?mod=article_inline) in war-torn countries, and is offering financing for critical-materials projects in which foreign entities supply American companies.

In another move aimed at curbing U.S. reliance on China, the Defense Department has ordered contractors to remove Chinese rare earths from their supply chains for magnets by next year.
