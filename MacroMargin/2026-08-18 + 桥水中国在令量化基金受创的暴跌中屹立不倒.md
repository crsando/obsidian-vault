### 中文译文

## 桥水中国在令量化基金受创的暴跌中屹立不倒

### **要点**（彭博AI）

- 包括桥水联合公司（Bridgewater Associates LP）中国本地部门在内的中国宏观对冲基金，在7月股市大幅抛售后守住了年度收益。
- 桥水中国业务告知投资者，其本地全天候增强策略（All Weather Plus）截至7月31日的年内回报约为3%，尽管上月扣费前损失了2.8%。
- 由拍拍网（PaiPaiWang）追踪的309只宏观对冲基金上月平均下跌3.5%，但年内仍录得1.9%的涨幅，其7月跌幅不到基准沪深300指数跌幅的一半。
包括[桥水联合公司（Bridgewater Associates LP）](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/20273Z:US)中国本地部门在内的中国宏观对冲基金，在7月抛售中虽受创伤但未败下阵来，在股票市场剧烈抛售导致其他基金亏损之后，仍守住了年度收益。

据彭博社看到的一封投资者信函，桥水中国业务管理着超过600亿元人民币（89亿美元）的资产，其告知投资者，其本地全天候增强策略（All Weather Plus）截至7月31日的年内回报约为3%，尽管上月扣费前损失了2.8%。

温京资本管理（上海）公司由桥水中国校友张艾米丽·欣怡运营，今年上涨12%，尽管7月下跌4.4%。其量化基金在47%收益后募资。杭州博利尔祥资产管理公司的小红涨基金，在上月下跌1%后，年初至今回报为10.6%。数据来自深圳拍拍王投资管理公司。

这些数字表明，中国的宏观基金大军——它们将赌注分散在股票、债券、大宗商品和其他市场——上个月未能避免风险偏好急剧下滑的痛苦，因为投资者对人工智能推动的股市上涨失去了信心。

但它们也凸显了这类基金的销售卖点：在市场波动中提供缓冲，而这些波动可能会抹去其他地方的收益。

中国的量化基金今年因股市飙升而膨胀至2.6万亿元人民币（3850亿美元），上月[遭受重创](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-07/quant-crash-in-china-sends-deepseek-founder-s-funds-down-20)。由[DeepSeek](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/2544189D:CH)创始人梁文锋运营的对冲基金旗下九只基金中有八只今年以来转为亏损。国内纯多头的量化基金上月平均亏损17%。

小宏张（Xiaohongzhang）的基金经理林盈盈表示：“防御并不是完全避免损失，而是能够以相对正常的姿态迎接下一波市场走势。”她拒绝透露其基金的具体业绩。“从这个意义上说，该基金今年的表现是正常的。”

排排网跟踪的309只宏观对冲基金上月平均下跌3.5%，但今年仍录得1.9%的涨幅。它们7月份的损失不到基准指数[沪深300指数](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/SHSZ300%20Index)约7.9%跌幅的一半。中国对冲基金整体上月平均损失7.3%。

桥水公司拒绝置评。文景资本未回应置评请求。

### **风险平价**

中国一些最受欢迎的宏观基金采用一种称为风险平价（risk parity）的系统性方法，该方法侧重于在不同资产类别之间均匀分配敞口。由于桥水公司的全天候（All Weather）基金，这种方法在中国广受欢迎。

桥水中国在其投资者信中写道，其全天候增强（All Weather Plus）基金在7月股市暴跌期间“有效地缓冲了单一资产类别的剧烈波动”，并称这正是该策略的设计初衷。

信中称，该基金在7月份通过债券投资赚取了收益，同时，由于伊朗局势紧张，部分大宗商品价格上涨，但信中没有提供更多细节。信中还称，该公司的中国全天候增强（China All Weather Plus）策略自2023年7月成立以来，年化回报率为26.3%，最大回撤为9.9%。

信中称，人类决策帮助大型宏观基金在第二季度提升了回报。在截至6月30日的三个月里，桥水团队主动管理贡献了2.4%的收益，抵消了系统性全天候组合0.5%的亏损，使该时期总回报达到2.3%。

根据季度投资者信函，文景（Wenjing）的宏观配置——一个包括主动押注在内的广泛类别——在第二季度贡献了3.6%的回报，而量化部分又增加了3.5%的收益。

但纯粹依赖人类交易决策的主观宏观基金波动性更大。据排排网（PaiPaiWang）数据，上海半夏投资管理中心的平衡宏观基金上月上涨11%，但今年仍下跌14%。

据排排网数据，[上海龙泰投资有限公司（Shanghai Longlife Investment Co.）](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1527537D:CH)的宏观对冲1号基金去年以153%的回报率成为宏观基金中的最佳表现者，但在7月暴跌44%，超过了3月25%的跌幅。

## Bridgewater China Withstands Rout That Left Quants Reeling

### **Takeaways **by Bloomberg AI

- Macro hedge funds in China, including Bridgewater Associates LP’s local unit, held on to their annual gains after a wild stock market selloff in July.
- Bridgewater’s China business told investors its local All Weather Plus strategy was up around 3% for the year through July 31 despite taking a 2.8% loss before fees last month.
- The 309 macro hedge funds tracked by PaiPaiWang fell an average of 3.5% last month but still recorded a 1.9% gain for the year, with their July losses being less than half of the decline in the benchmark CSI 300 Index.
Macro hedge funds in China including [Bridgewater Associates LP](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/20273Z:US)’s local unit emerged from the July selloff bruised but not defeated, holding on to their annual gains after a wild stock market selloff that pushed other funds into the red.

Bridgewater’s China business, which manages more than 60 billion yuan ($8.9 billion), told investors its local All Weather Plus strategy was up around 3% for the year through July 31 despite taking a 2.8% loss before fees last month, according to an investor letter seen by Bloomberg.

Wenjing Capital Management (Shanghai) Co., run by Bridgewater China Alum’s Quant Fund Raises Money After 47% Gain Zhang Emily Xinyi, was up 12% this year despite a 4.4% hit in July. Hangzhou BoLiErXiang Asset Management Co.’s Xiaohongzhang fund had a 10.6% year-to-date return after losing 1% last month, according to data from Shenzhen PaiPaiWang Investment & Management Co.

The numbers show that China’s army of macro funds — which spread their bets across stocks, bonds, commodities and other markets — were unable to avoid the pain of a sharp downturn in risk appetite last month as investors lost faith in an artificial intelligence-fueled rally in stocks.

But they also underscore the sales pitch of such funds: offering a cushion against the market swings that can wipe out gains elsewhere.

China’s quant funds, whose assets swelled to 2.6 trillion yuan ($385 billion) this year as stocks zoomed higher, [suffered heavy losses](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-07/quant-crash-in-china-sends-deepseek-founder-s-funds-down-20) last month. Eight of nine funds managed by [DeepSeek](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/2544189D:CH) founder Liang Wenfeng’s hedge fund turned into a loss for the year. Long-only quants in the country suffered an average loss of 17% last month.

“Defense is not about avoiding losses entirely — it’s about being able to greet the next wave of market movement in relatively normal shape,” said Lin Yingying, fund manager of Xiaohongzhang, who declined to comment on her fund’s performance details. “In that sense, its performance this year has been normal.”

The 309 macro hedge funds tracked by[ PaiPaiWang](https://www.simuwang.com/) fell an average of 3.5% last month but still recorded a 1.9% gain for the year. Their July losses were less than half of the roughly 7.9% decline in the benchmark [CSI 300 Index](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/SHSZ300%20Index). Chinese hedge funds overall suffered an average loss of 7.3% last month.

Bridgewater declined to comment. Wenjing Capital didn’t respond to a request for comment.

### **Risk Parity**

Some of China’s most popular macro funds use a systematic approach known as risk parity, which focuses on spreading exposure evenly across different asset classes. The approach gained popularity in the country due to Bridgewater’s All Weather funds.

Bridgewater China wrote in its investor letter that its All Weather Plus fund “effectively cushioned the violent fluctuations in a single asset class” during the July stock market rout, adding that this is what the strategy was designed to do.

The fund made money from its bond investments in July while some commodities prices rose amid tensions in Iran, the letter said, without providing more details. The firm’s China All Weather Plus strategy has returned an annualized 26.3% since its inception in July 2023, with a maximum drawdown of 9.9%, the letter said.

Human decision-making helped the big macro funds bolster returns in the second quarter. Active management by Bridgewater’s team contributed a 2.4% gain for the three months ended June 30, offsetting a 0.5% loss from the systematic All Weather portfolio to deliver a combined 2.3% return for the period, according to the letter.

Wenjing’s macro allocations — a broad category which includes active bets — contributed a 3.6% return in the second quarter, while a quantitative segment brought another 3.5% gain, according to its quarterly investor letter.

But purely discretionary macro funds, which rely on humans for trading decisions, were more volatile. Shanghai Banxia Investment Management Center’s balanced macro fund jumped 11% last month and was still down 14% for the year, according to PaiPaiWang.

[Shanghai Longlife Investment Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1527537D:CH)’s Macro Hedging No. 1 fund, which was the best performer among macro funds last year with a 153% return, slumped 44% in July, outpacing its 25% loss in March, according to PaiPaiWang data.
