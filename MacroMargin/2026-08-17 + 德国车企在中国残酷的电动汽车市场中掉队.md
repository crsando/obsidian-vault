### 中文译文

## 德国车企在中国残酷的电动汽车市场中掉队

### **要点**

- 德国汽车制造商在中国正遭受下滑，梅赛德斯-奔驰、宝马、大众和保时捷报告第二季度销量至少下降30%。
- 梅赛德斯在中国最新的电动轿车CLA上半年仅售出1,153辆，该公司可能需要进一步降价以与本土品牌竞争。
- 通用汽车将出售其在印第安纳州价值35亿美元电池合资企业中49.99%的股份给合作伙伴三星SDI，同时由于燃油价格上涨，创纪录数量的德国人正在转向电动汽车。
- 德国汽车制造商在中国继续萎缩，而中国是他们曾经轻松主导的市场。梅赛德斯最新电动轿车在那里的失败表明，他们要赢回转向本土品牌的消费者还有[很长的路要走](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2026-08-11/mercedes-bmw-vw-struggle-in-china-with-new-evs-selling-poorly)。

我们还有关于通用汽车[出售](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-11/gm-said-to-plan-sale-of-indiana-battery-venture-to-samsung-sdi)电池项目股份以及创纪录数量的德国人[转向电动汽车](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-11/german-drivers-accelerate-switch-to-evs-due-to-middle-east-war)的报道。以上内容及更多，请看下文。

### **大故事**

德国汽车制造商在中国多年来一直下滑，而且似乎没有人能够[阻止这种恶性循环](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2026-08-11/mercedes-bmw-vw-struggle-in-china-with-new-evs-selling-poorly)。

以梅赛德斯-奔驰为例。这家声名显赫的德国品牌去年秋天采取了不同寻常的举措，与美国快餐连锁店麦当劳合作，在中国为其最新电动汽车造势。

在“[So Mc-Benz](https://www.instagram.com/p/DQthcOoktMn/?hl=de&img_index=5)”活动中，梅赛德斯允许一个芝士汉堡公仔取代传统的三叉星标志，出现在电动CLA上。这款轿车原本希望扭转其在全球最大汽车市场的销量下滑。这些广告本应让这款车受到中国年轻时髦人群的欢迎。

![Mercedes’ long-wheelbase CLA for China. Photographer: Ying Tang/NurPhoto /AP Photo](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3bf81cc90b14810b8143c4aad680519d/3bf81cc9-0b14-81f1-8383-fd73de59284f-e45a3e969be119a62b940a57a18b46f9.png)

梅赛德斯为中国推出的长轴距CLA。摄影：Ying Tang/NurPhoto /AP Photo

这没有奏效。梅赛德斯上半年在中国仅售出1153辆，而小米交付了超过8万辆同价位的SU7轿车，相比之下微不足道。宝马、大众和保时捷在中国也面临类似挑战，它们都报告第二季度销量下滑至少30%。与此同时，小米和比亚迪等公司正在抢走它们的客户，使德国车企失去了一个关键的增长和利润驱动力。

它们并非没有努力。大多数德国车企已与中国公司合作，以获得最新的电动汽车技术并更好地了解当地驾驶者的需求。梅赛德斯打造了CLA的长轴距版，以迎合中国消费者对更宽敞后排空间的偏好。它为这款车配备了包括AI语音控制在内的软件，起售价仅为22.9万元人民币，比欧洲版便宜约40%。

知情人士称，梅赛德斯非但没有说服客户，反而不得不承认，在中国竞争需要更大幅度的降价，这意味着几乎每售出一辆电动CLA都会亏损。该公司正在限制对这款车的推广力度，直到经济性改善。

梅赛德斯表示，该车型从未打算拉动销量，而是为了在竞争激烈的入门级细分市场展示公司的最新技术。该公司指出，上个月在中国推出的紧凑型SUV电动GLC在市场反馈和预订单方面表现良好。

部分问题在于中国消费者对品牌认知的根本转变。在中国，梅赛德斯、奥迪和宝马正在失去豪华优势，因为它们被认为在软件和电动汽车技术方面落后于本土制造商。

宝马正试图通过[新世代](https://www.bmwgroup.com/en/company/neue-klasse.html)车型来改变这一局面，这些车型本应树立新的技术标杆。其新款iX3 SUV将于8月21日在中国开启预售，不过该公司尚未公布具体售价。

![The BMW iX3. Source: BMW Group](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3bf81cc90b14810b8143c4aad680519d/3bf81cc9-0b14-8199-864c-cff498a34e16-ebe9b2b1de39acf4ae26001e4266aaaf.png)

### 宝马iX3。来源：宝马集团

为了具备竞争力，这款SUV可能需要在价格上与特斯拉Model Y相当，后者起售价约为264,000元。在德国，宝马基础版iX3的售价接近前者的两倍。Model Y L是专为中国市场设计的更宽敞版本，起售价为339,000元。

尽管宝马在中国改进后的产品表现尚无定论，但CLA销量缓慢等早期迹象对德国车企来说并不是好兆头。本土竞争对手往往更快推出同样高效的车型，并塞满了卡拉OK机、可折叠平放用于[车内露营](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-23/how-china-s-ev-boom-is-fueling-car-camping)的座椅等功能。

当来自成都的32岁旅游业从业者Summer Chen在选购新电动车时，梅赛德斯是她的首选。她的家人拥有一辆E300轿车约10年，她愿意多花一点钱继续选择这个德国品牌。但当她查看CLA时，她感到其用户界面老式、仪表盘屏幕笨拙，因此大失所望。最终，她选择了特斯拉Model 3，理由是后者设计更优。

“智能功能对梅赛德斯来说并不是优势，”陈说。“无论该公司推出什么，特斯拉、小米和华为也能做到。”

## German Carmakers Are Falling Behind in China’s Brutal EV Market

### **Takeaways**

- Germany's carmakers are suffering a decline in China, with Mercedes-Benz, BMW, Volkswagen, and Porsche reporting second-quarter sales declines of at least 30%.
- Mercedes' latest electric sedan in China, the CLA, sold only 1,153 units in the first half, and the company may need to make deeper price cuts to compete with local brands.
- General Motors is selling its 49.99% stake in a $3.5 billion battery joint venture in Indiana to partner Samsung SDI, and a record number of Germans are switching to EVs due to higher fuel prices.
German carmakers continue to shrink in China, a market they used to dominate with ease. The failure of Mercedes’ latest electric sedan there shows they have a [long way to go](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2026-08-11/mercedes-bmw-vw-struggle-in-china-with-new-evs-selling-poorly) to win back drivers shifting to local brands.

We also have stories on General Motors [selling a stake](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-11/gm-said-to-plan-sale-of-indiana-battery-venture-to-samsung-sdi) in a battery project and a record number of Germans [switching to EVs](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-11/german-drivers-accelerate-switch-to-evs-due-to-middle-east-war). All this and more, below.

### **The big story**

Germany’s carmakers have been suffering a years-long decline in China, and none of them appears able to [arrest the downward spiral](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2026-08-11/mercedes-bmw-vw-struggle-in-china-with-new-evs-selling-poorly).

Take Mercedes-Benz. The venerable German brand took an unusual step last fall to hype its latest electric car in China, partnering with US fast-food chain McDonald’s.

In the “[So Mc-Benz](https://www.instagram.com/p/DQthcOoktMn/?hl=de&img_index=5)” campaign, Mercedes allowed a cheeseburger figurine to take the spot of its traditional three-pointed star on the electric CLA, a sedan it hoped would halt a slide in sales in the world’s largest car market. The ads were supposed to make the vehicle popular with China’s young and hip.

Mercedes’ long-wheelbase CLA for China. Photographer: Ying Tang/NurPhoto /AP Photo

It didn’t work. Mercedes sold only 1,153 units in China in the first half, a fraction of the more than 80,000 similarly priced SU7 sedans Xiaomi delivered. BMW, Volkswagen and Porsche face similar challenges in China, where they all reported second-quarter sales declines of at least 30%. Meanwhile, the likes of Xiaomi and BYD are taking their customers, depriving the Germans of a key growth and profit driver.

It’s not that they’re not trying. Most of them have partnered with Chinese companies to gain access to the latest EV technology and better understand what local drivers want. Mercedes built a long-wheelbase version of the CLA to appeal to Chinese tastes for a roomier back seat. It packed the car with software including AI-powered voice control and priced it from just 229,000 yuan, roughly 40% cheaper than the European version.

Instead of convincing clients, Mercedes had to concede that competing in China would need even deeper price cuts that would see it lose money on almost every electric CLA sold, sources say. The company is limiting its push for the car until economics improve.

Mercedes said the model was never meant to drive volume but rather show off the company’s latest technology in the hotly contested entry-level segment. It pointed to the electric GLC, a compact SUV introduced in China last month, as doing well in terms of market feedback and pre-orders.

Part of the problem is a fundamental shift in brand perception in China, where Mercedes, Audi and BMW are losing their luxury edge because they’re perceived as trailing local manufacturers on software and EV tech.

BMW is trying to change that with the [Neue Klasse](https://www.bmwgroup.com/en/company/neue-klasse.html) models, which are supposed to set new tech benchmarks. Its new iX3 SUV will open for pre-sales in China on Aug. 21, though the company hasn’t yet revealed at what price.

### The BMW iX3. Source: BMW Group

To be competitive, the SUV probably would need to get a comparable sticker with Tesla’s Model Y, which starts at around 264,000 yuan. In Germany, BMW’s basic iX3 costs nearly twice that. The Model Y L, a roomier version designed specifically for the Chinese market, starts at 339,000 yuan.

While the jury is still out on BMW’s improved offering in China, early signs like the slow sales of the CLA don’t bode well for the Germans. Local competitors often introduce equally efficient models faster, brimming with features like karaoke machines and seats that fold flat for [car camping](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-23/how-china-s-ev-boom-is-fueling-car-camping).

When Summer Chen, a 32-year-old tourism worker from Chengdu, went shopping for a new EV, Mercedes was at the top of her mind. Her family had owned an E300 sedan for around 10 years and she was ready to pay slightly more to stick with the German brand. But when she checked out the CLA, she was disappointed by what she felt was an old-fashioned user interface and clunky dashboard screens. In the end, she went for Tesla’s Model 3, citing its superior design.

“Smart features aren’t an advantage for Mercedes,” Chen said. “Whatever the company comes up with, Tesla, Xiaomi and Huawei can too.”

### **Top stories**

### Geely Auto’s Li Shufu Photographer: Qilai Shen/Bloomberg

**Geely Auto announced a revamp of its executive lineup**, including the resignation of Li Shufu as chairman. Li has been appointed honorary chairman for life, allowing him to devote more time to his other businesses and helping with succession planning, the company [said today](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-17/geely-auto-profit-climbs-as-exports-counter-weak-china-demand), as it reported a surge in second-quarter profit. Li will continue to serve as chairman of parent Zhejiang Geely Holding Group, indicating he’ll retain oversight over his sprawling empire.

**General Motors will sell its 49.99% stake in a $3.5 billion battery joint venture** in Indiana to partner Samsung SDI, which will operate the New Carlisle plant as its first standalone [manufacturing hub](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-11/gm-said-to-plan-sale-of-indiana-battery-venture-to-samsung-sdi) in North America. The move is the latest retreat by GM from EVs after taking $11 billion in writedowns amid sluggish consumer demand and the Trump administration’s removal of federal sales incentives.

**Authentic Brands Group, the branding giant that owns Reebok** and holds rights to icons such as Elvis Presley and Marilyn Monroe, participated alongside HPS Investment Partners in providing Aston Martin with [a £450 million loan](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-05/aston-martin-hps-links-deepen-as-authentic-brands-circles-assets), deepening ties between the luxury-car maker and the direct lending powerhouse backed by BlackRock. HPS has also committed to provide an additional £100 million if Aston Martin transfers a 50.1% stake in a unit holding its non-automotive intellectual property to Authentic Brands.

**More German drivers than ever are switching to EVs** due to the jump in fuel prices triggered by the US-Israeli war on Iran. A [record 12%](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-11/german-drivers-accelerate-switch-to-evs-due-to-middle-east-war) replaced their combustion-engine car with an EV in the second quarter, according to a survey for insurer Huk-Coburg. That’s more than twice the percentage recorded in January and February and more consumers are considering an EV for the first time specifically because of higher fuel prices, the group said.
