> 📌 **核心摘要**
>
> 古尔斯比称近期通胀降温令人鼓舞，但希望看到更多证据以确保通胀回落至2%目标，并认为美国经济和劳动力市场基本稳定。
> • 古尔斯比表示支持7月维持利率不变，并称通胀是当前首要关注问题。
> • 他认为过去三个月的通胀表现令人鼓舞，需连续三四个月出现类似6月数据才能更确信回到2%路径。
> • 近期CPI显示关税和油价冲击正被消化，但此前五六个月方向错误且总体水平过高。
> • 历史高通胀和疫情后物价压力让他对货币政策更谨慎。
> • 近期通胀数据好转和招聘疲软使投资者削减加息预期，9月加息概率约30%。
> • 零售销售若连续数月下降将让他感到紧张。
> • 他对生产率数据放缓表示遗憾，并警告生产率提升不一定意味着央行应降息，可能推动大规模投资并导致经济过热。
> • 他对沃什设立工作组研究会议频率等议题不持强烈意见，期待听取建议。

Federal Reserve Bank of Chicago President Austan Goolsbee said he’s encouraged by a recent cooling in inflation but wants to see more of the same in coming months to be sure it’s falling back to the central bank’s 2% target.

芝加哥联邦储备银行行长奥斯坦·古尔斯比表示，他对近期通胀降温感到鼓舞，但希望未来几个月能看到更多类似迹象，以确保通胀正回落至央行2%的目标。

Goolsbee, speaking Friday in an interview with Bloomberg News, said inflation readings this summer from the consumer price index provide optimism that some price shocks from the past year, like tariffs and higher oil costs, are working themselves through the economy.

古尔斯比周五接受彭博新闻采访时表示，今年夏天消费者价格指数的通胀读数让人乐观，认为过去一年的一些价格冲击（如关税和油价上涨）正在经济中自行消化。

“Given, though, that we got five or six months going the wrong way, and the overall is way too high, I will say I’m encouraged by the last three months,” Goolsbee said. “If we get three, four months in a row like what we saw in June, I will be feeling much better that we are on path back to 2%.”

“不过，鉴于我们经历了五六个月的错误方向，而且总体水平过高，我要说的是，过去三个月的表现让我感到鼓舞，”古尔斯比表示。“如果我们能连续三四个月看到像6月那样的数据，我会更加确信我们正走在回到2%目标的道路上。”

The Chicago Fed chief, who said he supported the decision to hold interest rates steady at the July meeting, called inflation his primary concern right now. When it comes to growth and the labor market, he added, the US economy is “basically stable.”

这位芝加哥联储主席表示，他支持7月会议维持利率不变的决定，并称通胀目前是他首要关注的问题。他补充说，在增长和劳动力市场方面，美国经济“基本稳定”。

The Fed’s experience with high inflation in decades past, and the post-Covid bout with price pressures — inflation topped 7% in 2022 and hasn’t hit the target for more than five years — has made Goolsbee more cautious about how to approach the current problem.

美联储在过去几十年应对高通胀的经验，以及新冠疫情后的价格压力（2022年通胀超过7%，且已超过五年未达到目标）让古尔斯比在应对当前问题时更加谨慎。

“Both of those I do think influence my thinking now about policy, to be a little more guarded and pay a little more attention to what’s happening on the inflation side because we’ve seen in historic episodes, and in the last five years, once you get the inflation going it can be painful and hard to get rid of,” Goolsbee said.

“我确实认为这两件事都影响了我现在对政策的思考，让我更加谨慎，并更加关注通胀方面的情况，因为我们在历史事件中和过去五年里都看到，一旦通胀开始走高，它可能很痛苦且难以摆脱，”古尔斯比说。

While officials left rates unchanged for a fifth straight meeting last month, a growing number appear worried that inflation may not return to target without higher rates. Three policymakers [dissented](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-31/fed-dissenters-say-hikes-needed-to-tackle-persistent-inflation) at the July gathering in favor of raising rates by a quarter percentage point.

尽管官员们上个月连续第五次会议维持利率不变，但越来越多的官员似乎担心，如果不提高利率，通胀可能无法回到目标水平。三名政策制定者在7月会议上持异议，赞成加息25个基点。

At the same time, data over the past month has shown some signs of relief. Consumer price [growth](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CPI%20YOY%20%20Index) has slowed in the past two months as the war-driven energy price shock fades. A report released earlier Friday showed retail sales fell in July by the most in more than a year.

与此同时，过去一个月的数据显示出一些缓解迹象。随着战争引发的能源价格冲击消退，消费者价格增长在过去两个月有所放缓。周五早些时候公布的一份报告显示，7月份零售销售创下一年多来的最大降幅。

Given how central consumer demand is to overall growth in the US, Goolsbee said a continued drop in retail sales over multiple months would make him nervous.

考虑到消费者需求对美国整体增长的核心作用，古尔斯比表示，零售额连续数月下降将让他感到紧张。

The better inflation data, coupled with tepid hiring in the labor market, have driven investors to pare back expectations for rate hikes. A month ago, federal funds futures showed investors anticipated at least two rate increases this year, with the first likely in September. Now, odds for a September hike have dropped to around 30%, and traders foresee just one by year’s end.

更好的通胀数据，加上劳动力市场招聘疲软，促使投资者削减了对加息的预期。一个月前，联邦基金期货显示投资者预计今年至少加息两次，第一次可能在9月。如今，9月加息的概率已降至约30%，交易员预计到年底仅加息一次。

Goolsbee also lamented productivity data, which has slowed in recent quarters from higher readings last year, something he hopes will prove to be a blip in a data series that can be volatile. Some economists and Fed officials, including Chairman Kevin Warsh, have said new technologies like artificial intelligence may be helping companies become more efficient, which could in time lead to faster economic growth without spurring inflation.

古尔斯比还对生产率数据表示遗憾，该数据在最近几个季度从去年的较高读数放缓，他希望这将被证明是波动数据序列中的一个暂时现象。包括主席凯文·沃什在内的一些经济学家和美联储官员表示，人工智能等新技术可能正在帮助企业提高效率，这最终可能在不刺激通胀的情况下带来更快的经济增长。

If the increase in productivity growth doesn’t continue, “that would make a huge difference to all the narratives about AI and productivity growth and what it means for monetary policy and for the economy,” Goolsbee said.

古尔斯比表示，如果生产率增长的提升不能持续，“那将对所有关于人工智能和生产率增长及其对货币政策和经济的意义的叙述产生巨大影响。”

Goolsbee has warned that faster productivity might not necessarily mean central banks should lower interest rates. He has argued that increased productivity can help [fuel](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-06/fed-s-goolsbee-warns-against-front-running-productivity-growth) large investments, similar to the sums pouring into the AI build-out right now, and could lead to an overheating of the economy.

古尔斯比警告称，生产率加快不一定意味着央行应该降低利率。他认为，生产率的提高有助于[推动](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-06/fed-s-goolsbee-warns-against-front-running-productivity-growth)大规模投资，类似于目前涌入人工智能建设的资金，并可能导致经济过热。

Warsh has also said he wants to make broad changes to the Fed’s operations and recently raised the possibility of [reducing the number](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-02/warsh-raises-rethink-on-fed-s-policy-meeting-frequency-timing) of times policymakers meet each year, according to people familiar with the matter.

据知情人士透露，沃什还表示，他希望大幅改革美联储的运作方式，并最近提出了[减少](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-02/warsh-raises-rethink-on-fed-s-policy-meeting-frequency-timing)政策制定者每年开会次数的可能性。

Goolsbee said he doesn’t have a strong opinion on what number of meetings might be appropriate and is looking forward to hearing the recommendations from five task forces Warsh has convened to examine that and other topics. The Fed’s interest-rate setting body currently meets eight times a year.

古尔斯比表示，他对一年召开多少次会议合适没有强烈意见，并期待听取沃什召集的五个工作组就这一问题及其他议题提出的建议。美联储的利率制定机构目前每年开会八次。
