### 中文译文

## 中国芯片制造商长鑫存储（CXMT）上市首日暴涨466%，成为A股最大公司

CXMT Corp. 在上海上市首日股价飙升466%，成为A股市值最大的公司，投资者蜂拥买入这家中国人工智能领域的重要企业。

周一收盘价49元人民币，这家动态随机存取存储器芯片制造商的市值约为3.3万亿元人民币（合4880亿美元），超过所有其他A股公司。

此次IPO是A股史上第二大，募集资金高达666亿元人民币，其火爆首秀凸显了投资者对这家被视为中国实现半导体自主雄心关键企业的旺盛需求。长鑫存储已成为中国在DRAM芯片领域挑战外国供应商的最大希望，DRAM是从智能手机到AI服务器等各类设备的关键组件。

这家前身为长鑫存储技术有限公司的企业是全球第四大DRAM生产商，为投资者提供了押注中国在全球AI基础设施建设中角色的稀缺纯正标的。成功的上市也为其他正在排队IPO的中国芯片制造商增添了动力。

![图片](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3aa81cc90b148167859ff9b8fa945174/3aa81cc9-0b14-8156-bbe0-c65f5d8436a3-7f99acdfc977cc595f65bb133a9ddf79.png)

北京芯汉资本总经理敖飞表示：“该股开盘基本符合我的预期，但开盘后的大幅上涨令人瞩目。考虑到A股市场纯正存储标的的稀缺性，我不排除上市头几个交易日出现投机狂潮。”

周一，长鑫存储成交额达1410亿元人民币，占A股市场总成交额的近7%。

这家总部位于安徽合肥的公司现为中国市值第二大上市公司，仅次于港股上市的腾讯控股有限公司。

通过此次募资，长鑫存储将拥有与三星电子、SK海力士和美光科技竞争的资金储备。其韩国竞争对手已宣布建设新工厂的计划，韩国计划在五年内将内存芯片产量翻倍。与此同时，苹果公司正在谈判[购买](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-01/apple-seeks-to-buy-chinese-made-memory-chips-with-lobbying-push)这家中国芯片制造商的芯片，用于在中国销售的产品。

尽管首秀惊艳，这家中国芯片巨头的市值仍远低于SK海力士的8810亿美元和美光的1万亿美元。

由美国培养的芯片资深人士朱一明领导，长鑫存储的目标是今年将产量[翻倍](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2026-07-09/china-s-cxmt-chipmaker-eyes-ipo-to-challenge-samsung-sk-hynix-micron)，并建立从芯片设计到最终组装的完整供应链，知情人士曾表示，他们要求不透露身份以讨论此前未报道的目标。

![图片](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3aa81cc90b148167859ff9b8fa945174/3aa81cc9-0b14-8182-a4d9-cd32aef0a475-44479df411ac0ce03c438a463efacffd.png)

长鑫存储受到[散户投资者需求](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-16/cxmt-s-blockbuster-ipo-212-times-covered-for-retail-investors)、[有吸引力的估值](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-14/cxmt-prices-ipo-in-marquee-moment-for-china-s-chip-revolution)以及国家支持的[市场提振](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-21/china-broadens-market-rescue-with-record-inflows-into-tech-etf)新迹象的支撑。

IPO的散户部分获得了212倍的超额认购，个人投资者提交了940万份订单，认购价值7.07万亿元人民币的股票——约为SpaceX创世界纪录IPO相应订单簿的10倍。

过去一年，一批沿着AI供应链的中国公司也在首日表现惊人。赛美特半导体股份有限公司在4月飙升876%，创下中国光学热潮新高，超过了12月[MetaX集成电路上海有限公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/688802%20CH%20Equity)创下的[693%涨幅](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-12-16/china-chipmaker-metax-debuts-after-wildly-oversubscribed-ipo)。同样在备受关注的首秀中，同行芯片制造商摩尔线程科技股份有限公司在12月[上涨425%](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-12-04/leading-ai-chipmaker-moore-threads-1-1-billion-ipo-puts-china-s-tech-in-focus)。

诚然，半导体股近几周波动较大，因为一些投资者担心这个巨型上市可能标志着AI驱动涨势的顶峰。

但许多投资者仍对长鑫存储的长期吸引力持乐观态度，称即使在其他地方对芯片股的情绪恶化，买盘力量也可能为股价提供支撑。

“与之前的一些巨型IPO不同，长鑫存储的技术或市场份额都尚未达到极限，”北京诺华投资管理有限公司基金经理曾继清表示。“仍有巨大的增长空间。”

![图片](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3aa81cc90b148167859ff9b8fa945174/3aa81cc9-0b14-8149-8fbf-e2d6af2168d3-43c8337c3658148af2fde5e4a49c74cf.png)

成功上市也可能为其他排队上市的公司积攒势头，包括竞争对手长江存储科技有限责任公司和百度公司的芯片部门昆仑芯。知情人士称，深度求索（DeepSeek）可能最早于今年提交IPO申请。

卖方分析师则更为乐观，认为该公司处于当下两大投资主题——人工智能和技术自主——的交汇点。华西证券预计其市值将达到5万亿元人民币，基于2026年预期盈利的40倍市盈率，营收将从今年预计的2777亿元人民币增长一倍多，至2028年达到5727亿元人民币，净利润则攀升至2900亿元人民币。

野村控股包括邓伟志在内的分析师开始对该股进行覆盖，给予买入评级，目标价116元。该券商预计，由于市场份额增长以及中国市场更高的估值倍数，这家内存巨头的估值将为其美国主要竞争对手美光科技的两倍。

长鑫存储可能最早赶上8月底的第三季度检讨期加入股票互联互通机制，并于9月中旬生效。

## China Chipmaker CXMT Jumps 466% in Debut After Blockbuster IPO

CXMT Corp. surged 466% in its Shanghai trading debut to become China's largest onshore-listed company, as investors piled into one of the country's biggest artificial-intelligence champions.

At its closing price of 49 yuan on Monday, the maker of dynamic random-access memory chips is valued at about 3.3 trillion yuan ($488 billion), surpassing all other A-share companies.

The blockbuster debut of the second-largest initial public offering in China's history — raising as much as 66.6 billion yuan — underscores investors' voracious appetite for a company viewed as central to Beijing's ambitions to build a self-sufficient semiconductor industry. CXMT has emerged as the nation's best hope of challenging foreign suppliers in DRAM chips, a critical component used in everything from smartphones to AI servers.

The company, formerly known as ChangXin Memory Technologies, is the world's fourth-largest producer of DRAM, making it a rare pure-play for investors to bet on China's role in the global AI infrastructure buildout. A successful market listing also builds momentum for other Chinese chipmakers in the IPO pipeline.

“The stock's opening was largely in line with my expectations, but the sharp move higher after the open has been remarkable,” said Ao Fei, managing director at Beijing Xinhan Capital. “Given how scarce pure-play memory names are in the A-share market, I wouldn't rule out a period of speculative frenzy in the first few trading days,” he added, referring to China's onshore equities market.

CXMT generated 141 billion yuan in turnover on Monday, representing nearly 7% of all the transactions in China's onshore market.

The Hefei, Anhui-based company is now China's second-biggest listed firm, behind Hong Kong-traded Tencent Holdings Ltd.

With the fundraising, CXMT will have a war chest to compete with Samsung Electronics Co, SK Hynix Inc. and Micron Technology Inc. Its Korean rivals have announced plans to build new plants as the nation plans to double memory chip production within five years. Meanwhile, Apple Inc. is in negotiations to [purchase](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-01/apple-seeks-to-buy-chinese-made-memory-chips-with-lobbying-push) chips from the Chinese chipmaker for use in devices sold in China.

Despite the impressive debut, the Chinese chip behemoth remains well below SK Hynix's $881 billion market capitalization and Micron's $1 trillion.

Led by US-trained chip veteran Zhu Yiming, CXMT aims to [double](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2026-07-09/china-s-cxmt-chipmaker-eyes-ipo-to-challenge-samsung-sk-hynix-micron) output this year and build a top-to-bottom supply chain, from chip design to final assembly, people familiar with the matter had said, asking not to be identified discussing previously unreported targets.

CXMT is buttressed by [retail investor demand](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-16/cxmt-s-blockbuster-ipo-212-times-covered-for-retail-investors), [attractive valuations](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-14/cxmt-prices-ipo-in-marquee-moment-for-china-s-chip-revolution), and renewed signs of state-backed [support](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-21/china-broadens-market-rescue-with-record-inflows-into-tech-etf) for markets.

The retail portion of the IPO was 212 times oversubscribed, with individual investors submitting 9.4 million orders for 7.07 trillion yuan worth of shares — about 10 times the comparable order book of SpaceX's world record IPO.

A group of Chinese companies along the AI supply chain have also produced some spectacular first-day performances over the past year. Semight Instruments Co. soared a Semight Pops 876% as China Optical Frenzy Reaches Fever Pitch in April, surpassing the [693% mark](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-12-16/china-chipmaker-metax-debuts-after-wildly-oversubscribed-ipo) set by [MetaX Integrated Circuits Shanghai Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/688802%20CH%20Equity) in December. In a similarly watched debut, fellow chipmaker Moore Threads Technology Co. [jumped 425%](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-12-04/leading-ai-chipmaker-moore-threads-1-1-billion-ipo-puts-china-s-tech-in-focus) in December.

To be sure, semiconductor shares have been volatile in recent weeks as some investors grew concerned that the mega-listing would signal a peak in the AI-driven rally.

But many investors remain upbeat on CXMT's long-term attractiveness, saying that buying forces may put a floor under the stock even if sentiment for chip names sours elsewhere.

“Unlike some of the mega IPOs that came before it, CXMT hasn't reached the limits of either its technology or its market share,” said Zeng Jiqing, a fund manager at Beijing Nuohua Investment Management Co. “There's still enormous room for growth.”

A successful listing may also build momentum for other companies in the deal pipeline, including rival [Yangtze Memory Technologies Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1577197D:CH) and [Baidu Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/BIDU%20US%20Equity)'s chip unit Kunlunxin. DeepSeek may file as soon as this year for an IPO, people familiar with the matter have said.

Sell side analysts are even more bullish, as they see the company at the intersection of the biggest investment themes of the times: AI and tech self-reliance. Huaxi Securities [projects](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MzIwMjY3MjgxMA%3D%3D&amp%3Bmid=2247536627&amp%3Bidx=1&amp%3Bsn=bdb2016ab8aec4dc2cf1345f25b84e46&amp%3Bchksm=97ed46a324860119f6b384a7e130cf23fa4b32a6539ac9581c2ae5f843c8e39e746253be767d&amp%3Bscene=0&amp%3Bxtrack=1#rd) a market value of 5 trillion yuan at 40 times 2026 earnings, seeing revenue more than doubling to 572.7 billion yuan by 2028 from an estimated 277.7 billion yuan this year, with net profit climbing to 290 billion yuan.

Nomura Holdings Inc. analysts including Donnie Teng initiated the stock's [coverage](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-27/cxmt-can-rally-more-than-1-200-on-market-share-win-nomura-says) with a buy rating and a price target of 116 yuan. The broker expects the memory giant to trade at double the valuation of its major US competitor Micron, due to market share gains and the higher valuation multiples in China's market.

CXMT may be eligible for stock connect inclusion in time for the third quarter review in late August, taking effect in mid September, at the earliest.
