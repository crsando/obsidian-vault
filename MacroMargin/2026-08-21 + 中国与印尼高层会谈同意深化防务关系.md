### 中文译文

## 中国与印尼高层会谈同意深化防务关系

*8月21日，沙夫里·沙姆苏丁与董军海军上将在雅加达的印度尼西亚国防部总部。*

*摄影：Eko Siswono Toyudho/Anadolu/Getty Images*

作者：[钱德拉·阿斯马拉](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AWGBWKbKUm0/chandra-asmara) 2026年8月21日，下午3:26（GMT+8）

中国和印度尼西亚周五在雅加达举行的高层会谈中同意深化军事、海上和技术合作，突显了在美国对亚洲安全承诺重新出现不确定性之际，北京日益增强的地区影响力。

印度尼西亚国防部长沙夫里·沙姆苏丁在与中国国防部长董军会晤后表示，两国将扩大人员交流和国防工业合作，以增强军事能力。数小时后，印尼外交部长苏吉奥诺与中国外交部长王毅同意为印尼主要大宗商品探索更大的市场准入，并在人工智能、通信卫星、能源和矿产方面深化合作。

王毅表示，两国将重启渔业合作，推动南海共同开发，同时努力完成区域行为准则谈判。苏吉奥诺表示，双方还承诺继续推进雅加达-万隆高铁项目，采用可持续融资方式，并努力提高客流量。

此次会晤是在周五晚些时候举行的第二次外长和防长“2+2”对话之前进行的，为这一以去年约1550亿美元双边贸易为基础的关系增添了新的战略层面。这也凸显了印尼总统普拉博沃·苏比安托努力深化与北京和华盛顿的关系，而这两个大国正在该地区争夺影响力。

印度尼西亚和美国于今年四月建立了“主要防务合作伙伴关系”，涵盖军事现代化、训练、演习和作战合作。尽管如此，亚洲许多国家仍对华盛顿对该地区的承诺感到质疑，尤其是在唐纳德·特朗普总统突然缩减与韩国的联合演习之后。

印尼与中国关系趋近还建立在2025年4月在北京举行的[首次2+2对话](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-04-21/top-chinese-indonesian-officials-forge-closer-security-links)基础上，当时雅加达和北京同意深化安全与海上合作，包括情报交流、协调行动和加强海岸警卫队合作。

两国外长周五还启动了首次印尼-中国全面战略对话，并欢迎就2027-2031年五年行动计划的讨论。

中国推动这一伙伴关系发挥更广泛的战略作用。董（Dong）表示，两国应通过东南亚国家联盟框架深化安全合作，并成为全球南方国家间防务关系的典范。根据同声传译，他还呼吁双方挫败“外部势力的恶意企图”。

但在王毅和董访问前一周多，这种合作的敏感性就已显现。中国将中国和印尼军舰在[台湾以东](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-11/china-indonesia-plan-joint-exercise-in-waters-east-of-taiwan)的互动描述为一次联合“航行演习”，而印尼则称这是一艘从俄罗斯返航的护卫舰进行的例行通过演习，并强调这与作战无关。

台湾（中国宣称是其领土）谴责了这一行动。

尽管南海存在长期分歧，双方的安全合作依然在推进。2024年普拉博沃（Prabowo）与中国国家主席习近平的联合声明中提到了“在重叠主张地区进行联合开发”，引发人们担忧雅加达正在软化其对北京广泛海洋主张的反对立场。印尼后来表示，它[不承认](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2024-11-11/indonesia-refutes-charges-it-recognized-beijing-s-sea-claims)中国的九段线或任何重叠海洋主张，包括纳土纳群岛附近水域。

防务关系可能进一步扩展至武器采购领域。印尼一直在考虑[购买](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-10-16/indonesia-hints-at-coming-deal-for-chinese-j-10-fighter-jets)中国制造的成飞歼-10战斗机，但目前尚未敲定任何合同。

经济合作仍是两国关系的核心。苏吉约诺（Sugiono）表示，两国同意加强本币交易和金融合作，而王毅表示，印尼已承诺为中国企业提供公平、透明和有利的商业环境。

存在一些企业层面的摩擦。中国企业抱怨印尼收紧的镍矿政策推高成本并威胁投资，而中国大使馆警告称，这些政策变化可能会使约500亿美元的现有和计划投资面临风险。

## China and Indonesia Agree Deeper Defense Ties in High-Level Talks

*Sjafrie Sjamsoeddin and Admiral Dong at the Indonesian Ministry of Defense headquarters in Jakarta, on Aug. 21.*

*Photographer: Eko Siswono Toyudho/Anadolu/Getty Images*

By [Chandra Asmara](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AWGBWKbKUm0/chandra-asmara) August 21, 2026 at 3:26 PM GMT+8

China and Indonesia agreed to deepen their military, maritime and technology cooperation in high-level talks in Jakarta on Friday, underscoring Beijing’s growing regional reach at a time of renewed uncertainty over US security commitments in Asia.

Indonesian Defense Minister Sjafrie Sjamsoeddin, after meeting his Chinese counterpart Dong Jun, said both countries will expand personnel exchanges and defense-industry cooperation to strengthen military capabilities. Hours later, Foreign Minister Sugiono and China’s Wang Yi agreed to explore greater market access for key Indonesian commodities and deepen cooperation in artificial intelligence, communications satellites, energy and minerals.

Wang said the countries would restart fisheries cooperation and promote joint development in the South China Sea, while working toward concluding talks on a regional code of conduct. Sugiono said they also committed to continuing the Jakarta-Bandung high-speed rail project, using sustainable financing and efforts to increase passenger volumes.

The meetings came ahead of a second foreign-and-defense ministerial, or "2+2" dialogue due later Friday, and add a new strategic layer to a relationship underpinned by roughly $155 billion in two-way trade last year. They also highlight President Prabowo Subianto’s effort to deepen ties with both Beijing and Washington, which are competing for influence in the region.

Indonesia and the US in April established the Major Defense Cooperation Partnership covering military modernization, training, exercises and operational cooperation. Still, many in Asia are questioning Washington’s commitment to the region, especially after President Donald Trump abruptly curtailed exercises with South Korea.

Indonesia’s closer China ties also build on [the first 2+2 dialogue](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-04-21/top-chinese-indonesian-officials-forge-closer-security-links) in Beijing in April 2025, when Jakarta and Beijing agreed to deepen security and maritime cooperation, including intelligence exchanges, coordinated operations and stronger coast guard ties.

The foreign ministers on Friday also launched the first Indonesia-China Comprehensive Strategic Dialogue and welcomed discussions on a five-year action plan for 2027-2031.

China has pushed for a broader strategic role for the partnership. Dong said the countries should deepen security cooperation through Association of Southeast Asian Nations frameworks and serve as an example of defense ties among Global South nations. He also called on them to thwart the "malicious intentions of external forces," according to a simultaneous translation.

But the sensitivity of that cooperation was on display just over a week before Wang and Dong’s visit. China described an interaction between Chinese and Indonesian warships [east of Taiwan](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-11/china-indonesia-plan-joint-exercise-in-waters-east-of-taiwan) as a joint "navigation exercise," while Indonesia called it a routine passing exercise by a frigate returning from Russia and stressed that it wasn’t related to war-fighting.

Taiwan, which China asserts is its territory, condemned the maneuver.

The closer security cooperation also comes despite longstanding differences in the South China Sea. A 2024 joint statement between Prabowo and Chinese President Xi Jinping referred to "joint development in areas of overlapping claims," prompting concern that Jakarta was softening its opposition to Beijing’s sweeping maritime claims. Indonesia later said it [didn’t recognize](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2024-11-11/indonesia-refutes-charges-it-recognized-beijing-s-sea-claims) China’s nine-dash line or any overlapping maritime claims, including in waters near the Natuna Islands.

Defense ties could extend further into weapons procurement. Indonesia has been considering [buying](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-10-16/indonesia-hints-at-coming-deal-for-chinese-j-10-fighter-jets) Chinese-made Chengdu J-10 fighter jets, although no contracts have yet been finalized.

Economic cooperation remains central to the relationship. Sugiono said the two countries agreed to strengthen local-currency transactions and financial collaboration, while Wang said Indonesia had committed to providing a fair, transparent and conducive business environment for Chinese companies.

There have been some corporate frictions. Chinese companies have complained that tighter Indonesian nickel policies are raising costs and threatening investments, while China’s embassy warned that the policy changes could put about $50 billion of existing and planned investment at risk.
