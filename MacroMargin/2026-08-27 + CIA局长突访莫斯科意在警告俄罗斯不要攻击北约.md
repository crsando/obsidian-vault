### 中文译文

## CIA局长突访莫斯科意在警告俄罗斯不要攻击北约

这一警告是在美国新情报评估显示俄罗斯总统可能发动有限攻击之后发出的。

![CIA Director John L. Ratcliffe attends a news conference.](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3c981cc90b14815a808bca7c3d551a6d/3c981cc9-0b14-817d-bec5-f2161c7b9a7e-ec1cc760f4dd04dd09f2b4751c79b4e3.jpg)

CIA局长约翰·拉特克利夫（Anna Moneymaker/Getty Images）

据知情人士透露，中央情报局局长本周对莫斯科的[突然访问](https://www.wsj.com/world/cia-director-john-ratcliffe-makes-surprise-trip-to-moscow-c1c68fa6?mod=article_inline)旨在向[俄罗斯](https://www.wsj.com/topics/place/russia)发出警告，不要攻击北约国家。

这次行程是情报机构负责人约翰·拉特克利夫首次公开已知的访问俄罗斯首都。此前，《华尔街日报》[早些时候报道](https://www.wsj.com/world/europe/u-s-intel-finds-putin-could-test-natos-resolve-with-limited-incursion-e3b02e2c?mod=article_inline)的新美国情报评估显示，俄罗斯总统弗拉基米尔·普京可能在未来几年内通过对一个盟国发动有限攻击来测试北大西洋公约组织的决心。

据《华尔街日报》报道，美国官员担心，在[乌克兰](https://www.wsj.com/topics/place/ukraine)受困且国内面临压力的普京可能会发动从网络攻击到小规模地面入侵的袭击，目标可能是一个波罗的海国家。

伊朗战争支出导致的关键西方弹药短缺，也可能影响普京的决策。由于俄罗斯2022年入侵后向乌克兰转移武器以及伊朗冲突，美国已削减了某些武器的库存。

克里姆林宫发言人[德米特里·佩斯科夫周三在莫斯科对记者表示](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/cia-director-john-ratcliffe-russia-secret-trip/)，拉特克利夫在俄罗斯首都期间与俄罗斯情报官员举行了会谈，但没有与普京会面。佩斯科夫说，普京听取了会谈简报。

白宫对拉特克利夫此行几乎没有提供明确信息。周三在接受保守派电台主持人格伦·贝克采访时，特朗普总统将此次访问描述为“半例行”，并转而讨论俄罗斯与乌克兰的战争。“可能会有所成果，”特朗普说，“我们正在非常努力地结束那场战争，坦率地说，目前双方都希望看到它结束。”

白宫发言人请记者参考特朗普周三的评论。中央情报局发言人拒绝置评。

拉特克利夫是特朗普最亲密的助手之一，他是继美国乌克兰-俄罗斯和平谈判特使史蒂夫·维特科夫多次访问莫斯科之后，最新一位前往莫斯科的美国官员。

此次访问是美国情报机构负责人罕见的一次访问，此时俄乌战争已进入第五个年头。已知的上一次中央情报局局长访问莫斯科是在2021年11月，当时在前总统乔·拜登领导下执掌该机构的威廉·伯恩斯警告普京不要攻击乌克兰。

### English Original

## CIA Chief’s Surprise Moscow Trip Was to Warn Russia Not to Attack NATO

Warning comes after new U.S. intelligence assessments indicated Russian president could launch a limited assault

CIA Director John Ratcliffe Anna Moneymaker/Getty Images

The [surprise visit](https://www.wsj.com/world/cia-director-john-ratcliffe-makes-surprise-trip-to-moscow-c1c68fa6?mod=article_inline) by the director of the Central Intelligence Agency to Moscow this week was to deliver a warning to [Russia](https://www.wsj.com/topics/place/russia) not to attack NATO countries, according to people briefed on the visit.

The trip was spy-agency chief John Ratcliffe’s first publicly known visit to the Russian capital. It followed new U.S. intelligence assessments, [reported earlier](https://www.wsj.com/world/europe/u-s-intel-finds-putin-could-test-natos-resolve-with-limited-incursion-e3b02e2c?mod=article_inline) by The Wall Street Journal, that Russian President Vladimir Putin could try to test the resolve of the North Atlantic Treaty Organization with a limited assault on an allied country in the next few years.

U.S. officials are concerned that Putin, squeezed in [Ukraine](https://www.wsj.com/topics/place/ukraine) and under pressure at home, could launch an assault ranging from a cyberattack to a small-scale land incursion, likely on a Baltic nation, the Journal reported.

A shortfall in critical Western munitions, driven by expenditures in the Iran war, also could factor into Putin’s thinking. The U.S. has degraded its stockpile of certain weapons because of transfers to Ukraine after Russia’s 2022 invasion, as well as the Iran conflict.

Kremlin spokesman [Dmitry Peskov told reporters](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/cia-director-john-ratcliffe-russia-secret-trip/) in Moscow on Wednesday that Ratcliffe held talks with Russian intelligence officials during his time in the Russian capital, but he didn’t meet with Putin. Putin was briefed on the talks, Peskov said.

The White House has offered little clarity about the Ratcliffe trip. During an interview with conservative radio host Glenn Beck on Wednesday, President Trump described the trip as “semi-routine” and pivoted to discussing Russia’s war with Ukraine. “Something may come out of it,” Trump said. “We’re working very hard to get that war ended and frankly they both want to see it ended at this point.”

A spokeswoman for the White House referred reporters to Trump’s Wednesday comments. A spokeswoman for the CIA declined to comment.

Ratcliffe, one of Trump’s closest aides, was the latest U.S. official to travel to Moscow following several visits by Steve Witkoff, a U.S. envoy for Ukraine-Russia peace talks.

The trip was a rare visit by a U.S. spy-agency chief with the Russia-Ukraine war now in its fifth year. The last known visit by a CIA director to Moscow was in November 2021, when William Burns, who led the agency under former President Joe Biden, warned Putin not to attack Ukraine.
