## 阿里巴巴发布新Qwen模型，为中国AI突破再添新成果

作者：[Luz Ding](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AVXV62pP05M/luz-ding) 和 [Vlad Savov](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AUMmWB97hBs/vlad-savov)

2026年8月3日 10:21 AM GMT+8
*更新于2026年8月3日 12:50 PM GMT+8*

### **要点**

- 阿里巴巴集团控股有限公司发布了其有史以来最大的AI模型Qwen3.8-Max，声称性能与全球领先者Anthropic PBC相当。
- 新的Qwen3.8-Max基于2.4万亿参数构建，在多个基准测试中排名高于Moonshot的Kimi K3。
- 阿里巴巴的Qwen3.8-Max将于下周公开发布其权重供下载，允许用户定制该技术，定价为每百万输入token 2美元，每百万输出token 6美元。
[阿里巴巴集团控股有限公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/BABA%20US%20Equity)发布了其有史以来最大的AI模型，声称性能与全球领先的Anthropic PBC相当，这是中国挑战美国竞争对手的最新突破。

新款Qwen3.8-Max基于2.4万亿参数构建，在多个基准测试中的排名高于近期发布的、备受关注的[Moonshot Kimi K3](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-27/china-s-moonshot-to-release-breakthrough-ai-model-for-download)。阿里巴巴分享了[结果](https://x.com/Alibaba_Qwen/status/2084100707423289643/photo/1)，显示其得分与Anthropic的Fable 5相当，有时甚至更优。Fable 5是一款尖端人工智能模型，美国曾因其先进能力而将其置于[出口管制](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-30/us-government-lifts-restrictions-on-anthropic-s-fable-5-model)之下。

在 Moonshot 的 Kimi 在股市和硅谷引发波澜数日后，此次发布到来，表明中国开发者在计算资源相对受限的情况下，正迅速追赶 Anthropic 和 OpenAI 打造的最顶尖模型。DeepSeek 也刚刚扩大其最新模型 V4 Flash 的访问权限，而[字节跳动](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1774397D:CH)和[MiniMax Group Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/100%20HK%20Equity)于周五发布了[新的视频生成器](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-31/china-s-minimax-and-bytedance-release-dueling-ai-video-models)。

瑞士联合私人银行董事总经理 Vey-Sern Ling 表示：“许多投资者由于美国芯片限制或普遍怀疑而继续低估中国 AI 模型。实际上，差距可能已经大大缩小，而且正在迅速缩小。继 Kimi K3 之后，阿里巴巴的 Qwen 3.8 是又一个例证。”

阿里巴巴股价周一在香港一度飙升 7.3%，创近一个月最大涨幅。

这家总部位于杭州的互联网先驱将于下周公开发布 Qwen3.8-Max 权重，允许用户自定义该技术，标志着包括 Moonshot、DeepSeek、Z.ai 和字节跳动在内的中国 AI 竞争者之间日益激烈的竞争进入下一个重要阶段。尽管 DeepSeek 最新模型目前无疑是最实惠的新旗舰产品，但阿里巴巴的 Qwen 产品定价同样具有竞争力：输入每百万 token 2 美元，输出每百万 token 6 美元。每个 AI 系统处理任务所需的 token 数量不同，但与美国领先企业的最佳模型相比，阿里巴巴的模型仍显得颇具吸引力。

阿里巴巴表示，新的 Qwen3.8-Max 在自主编码和长期执行任务方面表现出色，在内部测试中能够独立完成一个耗时 16 天的软件工程项目。新系统在效率上也有所提升，使用时仅激活部分模块，以降低计算成本和延迟。

## Alibaba Adds to China AI Breakthroughs With New Qwen Model

By [Luz Ding](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AVXV62pP05M/luz-ding) and [Vlad Savov](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AUMmWB97hBs/vlad-savov)

August 3, 2026 at 10:21 AM GMT+8
*Updated on August 3, 2026 at 12:50 PM GMT+8*

### **Takeaways**

- Alibaba Group Holding Ltd. released its biggest ever AI model, Qwen3.8-Max, claiming performance on par with global leader Anthropic PBC.
- The new Qwen3.8-Max is built on 2.4 trillion parameters and ranks higher on several benchmarks than the Kimi K3 from Moonshot.
- Alibaba’s Qwen3.8-Max will have its weights released for public download next week, allowing users to customize the technology, with pricing at $2 per one million input tokens and $6 per million outputs.
[Alibaba Group Holding Ltd.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/BABA%20US%20Equity) released its biggest ever AI model, claiming performance on par with global leader Anthropic PBC in the latest Chinese breakthrough to challenge US rivals.

The new Qwen3.8-Max is built on 2.4 trillion parameters and ranks higher on several benchmarks than the headline-grabbing [Kimi K3 from Moonshot](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-27/china-s-moonshot-to-release-breakthrough-ai-model-for-download) that was recently unveiled. Alibaba shared [results](https://x.com/Alibaba_Qwen/status/2084100707423289643/photo/1) showing it delivering comparable or sometimes better scores than Anthropic’s Fable 5, a cutting-edge artificial intelligence model that was temporarily put under [export controls](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-30/us-government-lifts-restrictions-on-anthropic-s-fable-5-model) by the US due to its advanced capabilities.

The debut comes days after Moonshot’s Kimi sent ripples through stock markets and Silicon Valley as it showed Chinese developers quickly catching up with the top models crafted by Anthropic and OpenAI despite relatively constrained computing resources. [DeepSeek](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/2544189D:CH) also just [expanded access](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-31/deepseek-unveils-public-beta-api-for-flagship-ai-model) to its latest model, V4 Flash, while [ByteDance Ltd.](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1774397D:CH) and [MiniMax Group Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/100%20HK%20Equity) unveiled [new video generators](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-31/china-s-minimax-and-bytedance-release-dueling-ai-video-models) on Friday.

“Many investors continue to underestimate Chinese AI models because of US chip restrictions or general skepticism,” said Vey-Sern Ling, managing director at Union Bancaire Privée. “In reality, the gap is probably much closer, and narrowing fast. Alibaba’s Qwen 3.8 is another proof point, following Kimi K3.”

Alibaba’s shares surged by as much as 7.3%, the most in nearly a month, in Hong Kong on Monday.

The Hangzhou-headquartered internet pioneer will release the Qwen3.8-Max weights for public download next week, which will allow users to customize the technology, marking the next major move in the intensifying race among China’s AI contenders that include Moonshot, DeepSeek, Z.ai and ByteDance. While DeepSeek’s latest is by far the most affordable among new marquee releases, Alibaba’s Qwen offering is also priced aggressively at $2 per one million input tokens and $6 per million outputs. Each AI system will use a different number of tokens to handle tasks, but that still makes Alibaba’s model look attractive compared to the best from the US leaders.

The new Qwen3.8-Max performs well in autonomous coding and long-horizon execution, and was able to independently perform a software engineering project over 16 days in internal testing, Alibaba said. The new system has also improved in efficiency, activating only some parts when in use to reduce computational costs and latency.
