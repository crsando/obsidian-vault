### 中文译文

## 台湾将海巡署武器化以备战中国

台湾高雄——台湾正在赋予海巡署在国家安全中更大的角色，作为一项防御战略的一部分，该战略旨在阻止中国大陆的登陆部队抵达其海岸，并警告北京如果入侵将[遇到的困难](https://www.wsj.com/world/asia/taiwans-new-strategy-make-china-fear-the-pain-of-an-invasion-dfe28815?mod=article_inline)。

作为这一转型的一部分，周六，一艘海巡舰艇率领四艘导弹艇驶出台湾南部城市高雄的港口，约30分钟后，海军部署了机动雷达、导弹发射器、突击艇和无人机——这是一次演练，展示了台湾将如何应对中国大陆的两栖入侵。

在另一次演习中，携带反舰导弹的快速反应海巡艇“巴厘”号本周与海军和其他海巡舰艇一起参加了一次演练，演练内容是护送一艘商船穿越模拟的中国封锁，台湾海巡署周四表示。

随着中国率先行动，海巡机构正演变为在西太平洋投射力量、宣示领土和威慑对手的工具。美国也加入了这一趋势，从新加坡和菲律宾部署快速反应海巡艇，以帮助挑战中国。

中国已经通过[世界上最长的海巡巡逻船](https://www.wsj.com/world/china/china-coast-guard-pacific-sea-disputes-09f535c0?mod=article_inline)和改装海军舰艇扩充了其海巡力量。它越来越多地将海巡力量用作军事资产和向台北施压的工具，包括在台湾周边进行封锁和入侵演习。北京声称台湾是自己的领土，并威胁要武力夺取。

![Taiwan navy vessels escort the Bali cutter during a drill.](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3bb81cc90b148151a399fb9756e415e4/3bb81cc9-0b14-81ac-bb32-e8eeacb221f4-bb032d92206ff4ac148f30a1cd3dc114.jpg)

白色巴厘号海巡艇和台湾海军舰艇参加演习。台湾海巡署

中国定期派遣海巡船只连同其海军舰艇进入台湾周边海域，这些舰艇[一直持续存在](https://www.wsj.com/world/china/how-chinas-navy-is-tightening-the-noose-on-taiwan-7c8ab9bd?mod=article_inline)。中国海巡船只最近试图在台湾本岛太平洋一侧[对外国商船行使管辖权](https://www.wsj.com/world/asia/chinas-assertions-of-authority-over-foreign-ships-near-taiwan-draw-u-s-rebuke-d0546eb0?mod=article_inline)。

台湾海巡官员表示，与军方日益加强的合作既是防御战略，也是对中方海上活动的回应，台北将其描述为[“灰色地带”压力](https://www.wsj.com/world/china/china-expansion-taiwan-himalayas-146aff72?mod=article_inline)。

作为这一转变的一部分，台湾海巡署表示，在今年的年度[“汉光”军事演习](https://www.wsj.com/world/china/taiwan-tests-war-plans-to-thwart-chinese-invasion-951a2932?mod=article_inline)中参与了33次演练，演习持续10天，将于周五结束。

美国海军战争学院副教授伊恩·伊斯顿表示：“台湾海巡署的文化以海岸防御和执法任务为核心，而不是备战大型海军反封锁行动。这就是为什么加强训练和演习如此重要。”

这艘213英尺长的“八里”舰主要负责快速救援行动或驱散渔船。它通常配备火箭遥控武器和水炮，但可装载台湾的雄风二型和雄风三型反舰导弹，至少还有其他11艘同尺寸的台湾海巡署巡逻舰也具备这种能力。

![图片](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3bb81cc90b148151a399fb9756e415e4/3bb81cc9-0b14-81d2-b2d8-c7c7bb741f1b-d578dffc171fe5092a7559e33ba65ed7.jpg)

这12艘巡逻舰是2018年开始的一项为期10年计划的一部分，该计划旨在为台湾海岸警卫队新增141艘舰船。

“海岸警卫队拥有大量性能强大的巡逻舰，可在多种未来场景中用来加强海军行动。关键在于为它们进行适当装备，并培训官兵胜任战时任务，”伊斯顿表示。

根据台湾海巡署的说法，战时，“巴厘”号以及任何其他所需的海岸警卫队舰艇将归海军指挥。

台湾海军一直在训练海岸警卫队人员以增强互操作性。根据海军向台湾立法机构提交的一份报告，在去年的联合作战演习中，海岸警卫队从一艘与“巴厘”号类似的巡逻舰上试射了两枚雄风二号导弹。

海岸警卫队有时还会与海军一起部署舰艇，在台湾邻近海域附近监视中国船只。

巴厘号是一艘快速反应海岸警卫队舰艇，在台湾装载了反舰导弹。来源：台湾总统办公室。

周三在台湾东海岸海域进行的反封锁护航演习重点在于[保持海上通道畅通](https://www.wsj.com/world/asia/chinese-blockade-taiwan-d5b241c7?mod=article_inline)，以确保包括能源进口在内的关键物资供应，这是决定台湾能在中国攻击下坚持多久的关键。本周，陆军还将一架阿帕奇攻击直升机降落在海巡署总部，显示出日益增强的一体化。

“中国海上力量正在稳步提高执行日益复杂行动的能力，”台北的国家亚洲研究局非常驻研究员Tristan Tang说。“因此，台北不能只在具体突发事件发生后才作出反应。”

台湾正在建造更多更大的舰艇以应对来自中国的日益增长的威胁，指挥台湾海巡署最大舰艇之一的“台北”号巡逻舰的舰长Ger Si-an在最近一次巡逻中告诉《华尔街日报》。在美国在台协会（美国在台北的事实上的大使馆）最近公布的一张照片中，可以看到“台北”号[与美国海岸警卫队舰艇并排航行](https://www.wsj.com/world/asia/taiwan-us-military-training-china-7795c2f3?mod=article_inline)。

“我们的武器主要用于执法，”Ger说。“当我们在海上时，我们尽量避免使用武力。关键是有能力作为后备——随时准备以防万一。”

## Taiwan Weaponizes Coast Guard to Prepare for Battle With China

KAOHSIUNG, [Taiwan](https://www.wsj.com/topics/place/taiwan)—Taiwan is giving its coast guard an expanded role in national security as part of a defense strategy that is focused on keeping China’s landing forces from reaching its shores and warning Beijing of [the difficulties it will encounter](https://www.wsj.com/world/asia/taiwans-new-strategy-make-china-fear-the-pain-of-an-invasion-dfe28815?mod=article_inline) if it invades.

As part of this transformation, a coast guard cutter led four missile boats out of the port of the southern Taiwan city of Kaohsiung on Saturday, followed about 30 minutes later by a naval deployment of mobile radars, missile launchers, assault boats and drones—a drill illustrating how Taiwan would respond to a Chinese amphibious invasion.

In a separate exercise, the Bali, a fast-response coast guard cutter loaded with antiship missiles, joined the navy and other coast guard vessels this week in a drill featuring the escort of a commercial ship through a mock Chinese blockade, Taiwan’s Coast Guard Administration said on Thursday.

With China at the forefront, coast guards are evolving into tools for projecting power in the Western Pacific, marking territory and deterring rivals. The U.S. has joined the trend, operating fast-response Coast Guard cutters [out of Singapore and the Philippines](https://www.wsj.com/world/asia/china-aggressively-patrols-disputed-waters-now-the-u-s-coast-guard-is-moving-in-89d6a646?mod=article_inline) to help challenge China.

China has bulked up its coast guard with [the world’s longest coast guard patrol ships](https://www.wsj.com/world/china/china-coast-guard-pacific-sea-disputes-09f535c0?mod=article_inline) and the addition of converted naval vessels. It is using its coast guard increasingly as a military asset and a tool to pressure Taipei, including in blockade and invasion drills around Taiwan, which Beijing claims as its own and has threatened to seize by force.

The white Bali cutter and Taiwanese naval vessels take part in a drill. Taiwan’s Coast Guard Administration

China regularly sends coast guard vessels into waters around Taiwan along with its naval vessels, which [have been a persistent presence](https://www.wsj.com/world/china/how-chinas-navy-is-tightening-the-noose-on-taiwan-7c8ab9bd?mod=article_inline). Chinese coast guard vessels [recently tried to exercise authority](https://www.wsj.com/world/asia/chinas-assertions-of-authority-over-foreign-ships-near-taiwan-draw-u-s-rebuke-d0546eb0?mod=article_inline) over foreign commercial ships on the Pacific side of Taiwan’s main island.

Taiwan coast guard officials say that the increasing cooperation with the military is both a defense strategy and a response to Chinese maritime activities, which Taipei describes as [“gray-zone” pressure](https://www.wsj.com/world/china/china-expansion-taiwan-himalayas-146aff72?mod=article_inline).

As part of the shift, Taiwan’s coast guard said it has taken part in 33 drills during this year’s annual [“Han Kuang” military exercises](https://www.wsj.com/world/china/taiwan-tests-war-plans-to-thwart-chinese-invasion-951a2932?mod=article_inline), which run for 10 days and conclude on Friday.

“The Taiwanese coast guard culture is anchored on coastal defense and law enforcement missions, not preparing for big navy, counter-blockade operations,” said Ian Easton, an associate professor at the U.S. Naval War College. “That’s why enhanced training and exercises are so important.”

The 213-foot Bali is primarily responsible for rapid rescue operations or dispersing fishing boats. It typically has a rocket remote weapon and water cannon but can load Taiwan’s Hsiung Feng II and Hsiung Feng III antiship missiles, a capability shared by at least 11 other Taiwan coast guard cutters of the same size.

**Hsiung Feng II** — *Subsonic antiship cruise missile*

- Length: 16.1 ft.
- Origin: Taiwan
- In operation: 1990
- Range: 62-75 miles
- Speed: 600-650 mph
- Basing: Ship-, ground-, air-launched
- Payload: 496 lbs., Semi-armor piercing, high explosive, Fragmentation
- Propulsion: Solid-propellant booster, turbojet engine
**Hsiung Feng III** — *Supersonic antiship cruise missile*

- Length: 16.7 ft.
- Basing: Ship-, ground-launched
- Payload: 496 lbs., Semi-armor piercing, high explosive, fragmentation
- Propulsion: Solid-propellant booster, liquid-fueled ramjet engine
- Origin: Taiwan
- In operation: 2011
- Range: 75-93 miles
- Speed: 1,521-1,745 mph
Source: MissileThreat, CSIS Missile Defense Project; Roque Ruiz/WSJ

The dozen cutters are part of a 10-year plan that began in 2018 to add 141 new ships to Taiwan’s coast guard.

“The coast guard has a significant number of highly capable cutters that could be used in a variety of future scenarios to augment naval operations. The key will be outfitting them appropriately and training the officers and crews for their wartime jobs,” Easton said.

In wartime, the Bali and any other coast guard vessels needed would fall under the command of the navy, according to Taiwan’s Coast Guard Administration.

Taiwan’s navy has been training coast guard personnel to enhance interoperability. During joint combat exercises last year, the coast guard tested the live-firing of two Hsiung Feng II missiles from a cutter similar to the Bali, according to a report by the navy sent to the Taiwan legislature.

The coast guard also deploys its ships alongside the navy at times to shadow Chinese ships near Taiwan’s contiguous zone.

The Bali, a fast-response coast guard vessel, is loaded with antiship missiles in Taiwan. Taiwan’s Presidential Office

The antiblockade escort drill performed Wednesday in waters off Taiwan’s east coast was focused on [keeping sea lanes open](https://www.wsj.com/world/asia/chinese-blockade-taiwan-d5b241c7?mod=article_inline) for vital supplies including energy imports, a key to determining how long Taiwan can withstand a Chinese attack. Also this week, the army landed an Apache attack helicopter at coast guard headquarters, illustrating increased integration.

“China’s maritime forces are steadily improving their ability to conduct increasingly complex operations,” said Tristan Tang, a Taipei-based Nonresident Fellow at the National Bureau of Asian Research. “Taipei therefore cannot afford to respond only after a specific contingency occurs.”

Taiwan is building more and bigger ships to counter the growing threat from China, Capt. Ger Si-an, who commands the cutter Taipei, one of the Taiwanese coast guard’s largest vessels, told The Wall Street Journal during a recent patrol. The Taipei could be seen [sailing alongside a U.S. Coast Guard vessel](https://www.wsj.com/world/asia/taiwan-us-military-training-china-7795c2f3?mod=article_inline) in a photo publicized recently by the American Institute in Taiwan, the de facto U.S. embassy in Taipei.

“Our weapons are mainly for law enforcement,” Ger said. “When we’re out on the water, we try not to use force if we can avoid it. It’s about having the capability ready as a fallback—keeping it prepared just in case.”
