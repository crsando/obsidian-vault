## 中国进入应急状态，总理视察遭受泥石流袭击的西藏

8月26日，中国西藏自治区吉隆口岸附近一处遭泥石流袭击的地区。*来源：新华社/美联社照片*

作者：彭博新闻 2026年8月27日 上午11:50 GMT+8 *更新于2026年8月27日 下午9:13 GMT+8*

### **要点** 由彭博人工智能提供

- 国家主席习近平派遣国务院总理李强前往西藏遭受山洪袭击的地区视察，凸显了中国对这起造成数百人死亡的灾害的应对之严肃。
- 中国部署了人员、直升机、无人机和一架运输机协助紧急救援和救灾行动，并拨款支持西藏及灾后恢复工作。
- 这场灾害可能阻断中国与尼泊尔之间大部分陆路贸易，洪水成为中国社交媒体上的热门话题，引发对边境口岸设施状况及潜在次生灾害的担忧。
国家主席习近平派遣其副手前往西藏遭受山洪袭击的地区视察，凸显了中国对这起造成数百人死亡的灾害的应对之严肃。

据官方新华社报道，国务院总理李强周四下午视察了紧急救援和救灾工作，其间会见了应急响应人员和被安置到临时避难所的群众。新华社相关报道标题为《中国总理视察西藏泥石流灾区》。习近平已[要求](https://www.gov.cn/yaowen/liebiao/202608/content_7079160.htm)全力以赴搜救失踪人员，并转移安置所有受威胁居民。

据新华社报道，中国已从武装部队调派500多名人员、4架直升机、多架无人机和一架运-20运输机协助行动。

财政部和自然资源部已向西藏拨付1.2亿元人民币（约合1790万美元）中央自然灾害救灾资金，另有1亿元中央预算内投资用于灾后应急恢复和重建。中国央行表示，将增加再贷款额度，鼓励金融机构加大对受泥石流影响企业的信贷支持。

![图片](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3c981cc90b1481809e14d51fab91f064/3c981cc9-0b14-8151-8178-d8ff5791b8ea-054b9ed799f6714b9a377017d2dacd1f.png)

应对行动的规模以及灾难发生数小时后即有高层领导人抵达，表明北京正倾尽全力应对这场可能阻断中尼之间大部分陆路贸易的灾害。

周三上午，一股水流和泥浆吞没了中国通往尼泊尔的主要陆路口岸的一处边境综合体，导致超过1400人失踪，其中包括数百名外国游客。据尼泊尔警方称，至少359人遇难。

虽然中国高层领导人过去在自然灾害发生时常常迅速抵达现场，但现任领导层减少了此类行程的频率。2023年，甘肃发生6.2级地震造成151人遇难，李强总理约一周后才前往灾区。

外交部发言人林剑周四在北京对记者表示，根据地方当局提供的信息，约有100名中国公民在尼泊尔失踪。中方已确认3人死亡。

林剑表示，中国正与尼泊尔保持密切沟通，并在受影响地区协调搜救行动。

据报道，尼泊尔与中国边境发生猛烈山洪，造成数百人丧生或失踪。来源：彭博社。

视频画面捕捉到山洪冲击边境综合设施的那一刻，人们为躲避洪水拼命奔跑，洪水掀翻建筑、碎片横飞，并导致数百人下落不明。

西藏吉隆口岸的监控摄像头视频，距加德满都约100公里（62英里），随后被泥水淹没。

这一幕是尼泊尔-中国边境[灾难](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-26/americans-brits-among-hundreds-missing-after-mudslide-in-nepal)中最戏剧性的事件之一。一段[视频](https://x.com/surajit_ghosh2/status/2092557368731267194?s=46)显示，大型边境建筑被滑坡冲击，另一段视频显示，街上的人们在巨浪前奔跑，巨浪压垮了一座停车场，公交车正停在停车场顶部。

吉隆已成为西藏与尼泊尔之间最大的陆路口岸，也是中国与南亚之间的商业通道，特别是在2015年尼泊尔地震中樟木-科达里口岸严重受损之后。北京于2017年将吉隆口岸升级为国际口岸，此后陆续增加了物流园区、货运场和其他基础设施。

据中国海关部门统计，2024年通过该口岸运输的货物总价值达43亿元人民币。这相当于中尼陆路货物运输总量的60%，接近总贸易额的30%。

突发：据中国官方媒体报道，来自尼泊尔的巨大泥石流在西藏吉隆口岸造成“重大人员伤亡”，并导致人员失踪。视频画面显示人们正在拼命逃生。[pic.twitter.com/D4M0Ll1WDE](https://t.co/D4M0Ll1WDE)— Insider Paper (@TheInsiderPaper) [2026年8月26日](https://x.com/TheInsiderPaper/status/2092558482633556118?ref_src=twsrc%5Etfw)

这场洪水成为中国社交媒体上最热门的话题，一些用户担心该地区已被泥石流完全摧毁。

“这么高的建筑像蚁丘一样几秒钟就被抹平了，”一位用户在微博平台上评论道。“可怜的人们。让我们祈祷奇迹发生。”

关于该边境综合设施的状况尚未得到官方确认，但中国官方媒体报道称，附近的道路被毁，通往边境地区的电力和电信连接已被切断。

央视[报道](https://news.youth.cn/gn/202608/t20260827_16836823.htm)称，泥石流前端到达吉隆口岸下游约2.5公里处，泥沙沉积厚度达1.5米。该地区已形成堰塞湖，使救援行动更加复杂。

中国水利部表示正在监测堰塞湖的水量。它警告称，未来三天内溃决的风险正在上升，可能导致更多的次生灾害。

China deployed over 500 personnel from the armed forces, four helicopters, multiple drones and a Y-20 transport aircraft to assist in the operations, Xinhua said.

The Ministry of Finance and Ministry of Natural Resources allocated 120 million yuan ($17.9 million) in central natural disaster relief funds to support Tibet, with another 100 million yuan in central budget investment earmarked to aid post-disaster emergency recovery and reconstruction efforts. China’s central bank said it’s PBOC Boosts Relending Quota for Mudslide Relief Efforts in Tibet a relending quota to encourage financial institutions to step up credit support for businesses affected by the mudslide.

The scale of the response and the arrival of a senior leader mere hours after the catastrophe suggest Beijing is pulling out all stops to cope with the disaster that threatens to choke off the majority of overland trade between China and Nepal.

On Wednesday morning, a torrent of water and mud engulfed a border complex at China’s main overland gateway to Nepal, leaving more than 1,400 missing, including hundreds of foreign tourists. While top Chinese leaders once used to arrive quickly on the ground when natural disasters occurred, the current leadership has reduced the frequency of such trips. In 2023, it took about a week for Li to visit the quake zone in Gansu province hit by a 6.2-magnitude tremor that killed 151.

Hundreds of people have been reported killed or missing after a violent flash flood at the border between Nepal and China. Source: Bloomberg

Video footage captured the moment when the flash flood slammed into the border complex, showing people running for their lives ahead of a deluge that toppled structures, sent debris flying and left hundreds missing.

Security camera video from Gyirong Port in Tibet,** **some 100 kilometers (62 miles) away from Kathmandu**,** was then obscured by the mud and water.

The scene was one of the most dramatic incidents of the [disaster](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-26/americans-brits-among-hundreds-missing-after-mudslide-in-nepal) on the Nepal-China border. One [video](https://x.com/surajit_ghosh2/status/2092557368731267194?s=46) showed the large border structure being slammed by the landslide, and another shows people on the streets running just ahead of the wave that crushed a parking structure where buses were stopped on the top.

Gyirong has become Tibet’s largest land port with Nepal and a conduit for commerce between China and South Asia, particularly after the Zhangmu-Kodari crossing was heavily damaged in Nepal’s 2015 earthquake. Beijing upgraded Gyirong Port to an international crossing in 2017 and has since added logistics parks, freight yards and other infrastructure.

A total of 4.3 billion yuan worth of goods were transported through the port in 2024, according to Chinese custom authorities. That amounts to 60% of all goods moved between China and Nepal by land, and nearly 30% of the total trade volume.

The flood became the top-trending topic on Chinese social media, with some users expressing fear that the area had been completely destroyed by the mudslide.

“Such a tall building was wiped out in a few seconds like an anthill,” one person commented on microblogging platform Weibo. “Poor people. Let’s pray for a miracle.”

There has been no official confirmation of the status of the border complex, but Chinese state media reports said nearby roads were destroyed and power and telecommunications links to the border area had been cut off.

CCTV [reported ](https://news.youth.cn/gn/202608/t20260827_16836823.htm)that the edge of the debris flow reached about 2.5 kilometers downstream from Gyirong Port, with mud deposits up to 1.5 meters thick. A barrier lake has formed in the region, complicating rescue operations.

China’s Ministry of Water Resources said it’s monitoring the water volume of the barrier lake. It warned that the risks are rising that it could burst in the next three days, leading to more secondary damage.
