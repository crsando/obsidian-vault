### 中文译文

## 英美资源与中国国有买家达成一年期铁矿石协议

[英美资源集团](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/AAL%20LN%20Equity)已与中国国有支持的铁矿石买家达成一项为期一年的供应协议，成为继[必和必拓集团](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/BHP%20AU%20Equity)之后又一家与该集团敲定协议的矿商。

据知情人士透露，英美资源旗下的南非生产商[库博铁矿石公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/KIO%20SJ%20Equity)已与中国矿产资源集团有限公司达成协议，在今年4月1日至2027年3月31日期间向其钢厂供应铁矿石。该人士称，协议不包括英美资源位于巴西的米纳斯-里奥项目的矿石，该项目矿石不以长期合同形式销往中国。该人士因讨论机密信息而不愿具名。

包括必和必拓、如今还有[福特斯克金属集团](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/FMG%20AU%20Equity)在内的全球最大铁矿石矿商，由于矿石组合庞大，与中国买家集团达成协议面临更大困难。CMRG代表中国一半以上的钢厂与全球矿商进行采购谈判。

库博在2026年上半年业绩电话会议上向分析师表示，已与CMRG达成协议，但未透露协议期限或条款。根据公司业绩，其矿石是铁含量较高的优质产品，2025年销量约3700万吨。

英美资源全球销售与交易负责人易卜拉欣·达杜在电话会议上告诉分析师，公司约54%的产量销往中国。他还表示，公司还通过现货销售和非CMRG长期合同向中国供应铁矿石，因此CMRG合同下的数量“在我们整体组合中相当小”。

彭博计算显示，根据已确认销量、估计现货销量以及CMRG成员钢厂的数量，库博铁矿石流向CMRG的潜在数量约为800万至1000万吨。

英美资源的一位发言人拒绝进一步置评。CMRG未立即回应置评请求。

“我们与CMRG进行了非常建设性的接触，从4月1日起我们已与他们达成协议，这确实会影响我们销售给CMRG成员钢厂的产品，”达杜在7月28日的电话会议记录中表示。

必和必拓在遭遇限制并进行了数月谈判后，才得以达成一项更多采用人民币定价的一年期协议，而FMG目前正进行紧张的谈判。下一个障碍是这些协议明年续约时会发生什么，以及中国矿产资源集团是否会设法争取更多让步。

### English Original

## Anglo Strikes Yearlong Iron Ore Deal With China’s State Buyer

[Anglo American Plc](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/AAL%20LN%20Equity) has struck a yearlong supply deal with China’s state-backed iron ore buyer, joining [BHP Group Ltd.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/BHP%20AU%20Equity) among miners that have finalized an agreement with the group.

South African producer [Kumba Iron Ore Ltd.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/KIO%20SJ%20Equity), part of Anglo American, reached a deal with China Mineral Resources Group Co. to supply iron ore to its mills from April 1 this year until March 31, 2027, according to a person familiar with the matter. The agreement doesn’t include ore from Anglo’s Minas-Rio project in Brazil, which is not sold to China on a long-term contract basis, said the person, who didn’t want to be named discussing confidential information.

The world’s biggest iron ore miners, including BHP and now [Fortescue Ltd](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/FMG%20AU%20Equity)., have faced a harder time reaching a deal with the Chinese buyer group due to their large portfolios of ore. CMRG represents more than half of China’s steelmakers in procurement negotiations with global miners.

Kumba Kumba S1 2026 Earnings Call to analysts on an earnings call in late July that it had reached an agreement with CMRG, without giving details about the length or the terms. Its ore is a premium product with a higher iron content, of which around 37 million tons was sold in 2025, according to the company’s results.

Anglo’s global head of sales and trading, Ebrahim Dadoo, told analysts on the call the company sells around 54% of its output into China. It also has volumes going into the country via spot sales and non-CMRG long-term contracts, so the volumes under the CMRG contract are “fairly small on our overall portfolio,” he said.

Bloomberg calculations put the potential volume of Kumba iron ore going to CMRG at around 8 to 10 million tons based on confirmed sales, estimated spot sales, and the reported number of CMRG member mills.

A spokesperson for Anglo American declined to comment further. CMRG didn’t immediately respond to a request.

“We’ve had very constructive engagements with CMRG, we’ve got an agreement in place with them as of the first of April, and that does impact our products that we sell to CMRG member mills,” Dadoo said in a transcript of the call dated July 28.

BHP faced restrictions and months of talks before it was able to agree to a yearlong deal with more yuan pricing, while FMG is currently in the midst of tense negotiations. The next hurdle is what happens when the deals come up for renewal next year, and whether CMRG will try to eke out more concessions.
