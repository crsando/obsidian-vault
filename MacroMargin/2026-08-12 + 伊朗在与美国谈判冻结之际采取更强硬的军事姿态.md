## 伊朗在与美国谈判冻结之际采取更强硬的军事姿态

德黑兰一名全副武装的伊朗特种部队成员。*摄影师：Morteza Nikoubazl/NurPhoto/Getty Images*

伊朗已重组军队，以便在海外采取更具攻击性的行动，与此同时，关于结束与美国战争的谈判仍陷入僵局，这表明德黑兰正在为长期的地区冲突时代做准备。

伊朗最高领袖阿里·哈梅内伊任命六名高级军事指挥官的一系列举措，标志着向“进攻性学说”的转变，伊斯兰革命卫队将军、新指挥官顾问穆罕默德·礼萨·纳克迪周二晚在国营电视台采访中表示。

“只要条件有利且命令下达，我们就必须能够将行动推进到敌方领土，”他说，并将这种方式与战前“主要基于防御和维护国家”的学说进行对比。

他发表上述言论之际，伊朗与美国在霍尔木兹海峡问题上几乎没有取得突破的迹象，双方都声称控制着这条关键水道，并提出了不太可能得到满足的让步要求。尽管已进入第六个月的战争敌对行动有所缓和，但调解方巴基斯坦和卡塔尔所寻求的持久和平日益难以实现。

美国总统唐纳德·特朗普数月来一直在威胁恢复对伊朗的攻击和表达对结束战争的谈判取得进展的信心之间摇摆不定。他周三在Truth Social上发帖称：“唐纳德·特朗普：美国对霍尔木兹海峡拥有完全控制权。我认为我们会保住它！我们的海军封锁已经发布。”这一说法被伊朗一再驳斥，后者通过袭击过境该水道的船只来表明立场。

特朗普重申了伊朗军队已被摧毁，以及该国面临经济困境、没有钱的说法。纳克迪在国家电视台采访中表示，伊朗的弹道导弹生产仍在快速进行，生产速度“远高于”使用速度，但没有提供细节。

在美国和以色列于2月28日开始攻击伊朗之前，全球约五分之一的石油和液化天然气通过霍尔木兹海峡运输。目前交通仍在继续，但流量远低于战前水平，生产商正使用各种变通办法将能源供应送到全球市场。

巴基斯坦国防部长赫瓦贾·阿西夫周二在伊斯兰堡对彭博社表示，美国和伊朗“接近达成某种安排”，但没有透露细节。

周三，巴基斯坦内政部长莫赫辛·纳克维的办公室称，巴基斯坦部长向伊朗领导层传达了“重要”信息，他转达了总理夏巴兹·谢里夫和陆军参谋长阿西姆·穆尼尔元帅给伊朗领导层的口信。纳克维正在协助主持谈判的穆尼尔。

双方于6月达成一项临时和平协议，但间歇性冲突和海上交通中断仍在继续，使该协议名存实亡。其中一项条款是60天的谈判期，目前该期限已接近到期。巴基斯坦外交部发言人塔希尔·安德拉比周三表示，该期限可能会延长。

“我们肯定陷入了僵局，”美国前副国务卿温迪·舍曼对彭博电视台表示。“除非美国准备好要么允许伊朗或许还有阿曼收取自愿通行费，要么给予伊朗足够的制裁减免和冻结资产解冻，使其值得这么做，否则我认为我们无法让该海峡在任何可持续的时间内重新开放。”

“我认为现实是，在可预见的未来（如果不是永远的话），伊朗将控制霍尔木兹海峡，”她补充说。

半岛电视台此前报道称，伊朗与阿曼之间关于向部分海上航运重新开放霍尔木兹海峡的谈判已进入高级阶段，据半岛电视台援引卡塔尔外交部发言人的话说。美国没有直接参与这些讨论，尽管它恢复了对伊朗港口的海上封锁。

据伊朗国家广播电视台（IRIB）的“伊朗称霍尔木兹海峡在条件满足前将继续关闭”的报道，伊朗最高国家安全委员会新任秘书穆赫森·雷扎伊表示，与阿曼达成的任何协议都将与全面重新开放的讨论分开。伊朗可能寻求的条件仍不清楚。

据IRIB报道，雷扎伊周二表示，伊朗已通过中间人向华盛顿转达了要求，包括美国结束战争和解冻被冻结的伊朗资产。这些言论是在德黑兰会见中国驻伊朗大使丛培武之后发表的。

油价[基本持平](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-11/latest-oil-market-news-and-analysis-for-aug-12)，因交易商对中东协议前景仍不确定。周三伦敦市场全球基准布伦特原油交易价格约为每桶89美元。

美国能源部长克里斯·赖特周二在社交媒体发帖称，霍尔木兹海峡石油出口的七天平均值接近每日900万桶，高于许多交易商的估计。战前这一数字约为每日2000万桶。

美国中央司令部在X上发帖称，周二，一架美国海军直升机向一艘违反美国对伊朗封锁、试图穿越阿曼湾的巴拿马籍货船开火，并补充说该船正驶向伊朗港口，违反了美国的封锁。

尽管发生此类攻击，所谓的穿梭过境仍在继续，使[关键的石油供应阀门](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-11/a-dozen-ships-switch-oil-outside-hormuz-as-barrels-keep-flowing)保持开放。卫星图像显示，船只正在霍尔木兹海峡外进行货物转运，这表明一些油轮继续关闭应答器穿越该水道。

中东的动荡不仅限于美伊冲突。以色列继续在黎巴嫩南部与真主党发生冲突，并对加沙的哈马斯武装分子发动袭击，而伊朗支持的胡塞武装则从也门对红海船只发动袭击，最近一次发生在周二。

巴基斯坦外交部长伊沙克·达尔在X上表示，一艘商船遇袭事件中遇难的6人中有3名巴基斯坦国民。

## Iran Takes More Robust Military Stance With US Talks Frozen

An armed member of Iran's special forces in Tehran.*Photographer: Morteza Nikoubazl/NurPhoto/Getty Images*

Iran has reorganized its military to be more aggressive abroad as talks on ending the war with the US remain mired in stalemate, a sign that Tehran is preparing for a protracted era of regional conflict.

A raft of top military Iran Supreme Leader Names Six Top Military Commanders by Supreme Leader Mojtaba Khamenei marks a shift to an “offensive doctrine,” Mohammad Reza Naqdi, a general in the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps who advises the force’s new commander, said in an interview on state TV late Tuesday.

“Whenever the conditions are favorable and the order is issued, we must be able to take the operation into enemy territory,” he said, contrasting the approach with a pre-war doctrine that was “primarily based on defense and the preservation of the country.”

His comments came amid little sign of a breakthrough between Iran and the US over the Strait of Hormuz, with both sides claiming control of the critical waterway and demanding concessions that are unlikely to be met. While hostilities have eased in a war that’s into its sixth month, a durable peace sought by mediators Pakistan and Qatar looks increasingly elusive.

US President Donald Trump has vacillated for months between threatening to resume attacks on Iran and expressing confidence that talks to end the war are progressing. Trump reiterated claims that Iran’s military has been destroyed, and that the country — faced with economic hardship — has no money. Naqdi said in his state TV interview that Iran’s production of ballistic missiles continues apace and is “much greater” than the rate of their use, without providing details.

About a fifth of the world’s oil and liquefied natural gas was transported through the strait before the US and Israel started attacking Iran on Feb. 28. Traffic continues to flow, but at a much lower rate than before the war, with producers using a variety of workarounds to get energy supplies to the global market.

Pakistan Defense Minister Khawaja Asif told Bloomberg in Islamabad on Tuesday that the US and Iran are “close to some sort of arrangement,” without giving details.

Naqvi is assisting Munir, who is leading the negotiations.

An interim peace deal was agreed between the sides in June but intermittent clashes and maritime traffic disruptions continue, rendering the accord effectively obsolete. One of the terms was for a 60-day negotiating period, now close to its expiry date. Pakistan Foreign Ministry spokesperson Tahir Andrabi said Wednesday it could be extended.

“We are definitely at a stalemate,” Wendy Sherman, former US deputy secretary of state, told Bloomberg TV. “Unless the US is ready to either allow Iran and perhaps Oman to have voluntary tolls or to give Iran so much relief from sanctions and frozen assets that it’s worth their while, I don’t think we’ll get it open for any sustainable period of time.”

“I think the reality is for the foreseeable future, if not forever, Iran will control the Strait of Hormuz,” she added.

What conditions Iran may seek for that remains unclear.

Iran has conveyed demands to Washington through intermediaries, including that the US end the war and unfreeze blocked Iranian assets, IRIB cited Rezaee as saying on Tuesday. The comments followed a meeting with China’s ambassador to Iran, Cong Peiwu, in Tehran.

Oil prices [were little changed](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-11/latest-oil-market-news-and-analysis-for-aug-12) as traders remain uncertain of the prospects for a Middle East deal. Global benchmark Brent crude traded at about $89 per barrel in London on Wednesday.

US Energy Secretary Chris Wright said in a social media post Tuesday that the seven-day average for oil leaving Hormuz is almost 9 million barrels a day, a figure higher than many trader estimates. The pre-war figure was about 20 million barrels.
