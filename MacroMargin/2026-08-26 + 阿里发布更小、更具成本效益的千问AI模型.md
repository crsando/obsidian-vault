### 中文译文

## 阿里发布更小、更具成本效益的千问AI模型

[阿里巴巴集团控股有限公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/9988%20HK%20Equity)发布了其广受欢迎的千问系列下的最新模型，这是一个价格更低的平台，旨在推动其旗舰AI产品在全球的采用。

该公司于周三发布了Qwen3.8-Flash，可下载使用，并公开了其权重——这些权重是帮助AI系统做出决策的数值。阿里巴巴在一份声明中表示，新产品拥有1250亿参数——这个数字衡量模型的复杂程度——其性能与Anthropic的Opus 4.6和DeepSeek的V4-Flash等竞争对手的一些最新发布不相上下。

周三的发布采用了为下一代模型设计的新架构：Qwen 4系列。该公司表示，这一新系统显著降低了训练和推理成本。

阿里巴巴是中国在AI领域投入最大的公司，承诺三年内投入超过3800亿元人民币（570亿美元）用于AI开发和基础设施建设。为筹集这笔资金，该公司本周通过香港规模最大的增发股票融资800亿港元（102亿美元），再次彰显其积累并投入巨资以取得全球AI领导地位的决心。

这家由电商巨头转型为AI企业的公司，已加快了深化和拓展AI产品组合的步伐。阿里巴巴本月早些时候升级了其旗舰产品Qwen3.8 Max，增强了其有史以来最大的AI模型。它还于周三推出了面向企业的AI智能体工具QwenWork的国际版，希望扩大全球市场销售，重点关注亚洲、拉丁美洲和中东地区。

## Alibaba Releases Smaller, Cost-Effective Qwen AI Model

[Alibaba Group Holding Ltd.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/9988%20HK%20Equity) released the latest model under its popular Qwen series, a lower-priced platform aimed at driving adoption of its marquee AI offering globally.

The company made the Qwen3.8-Flash downloadable for use on Wednesday and revealed its weights, the values that help AI systems make decisions. The new offering sports 125 billion parameters — a measure of a model’s sophistication — and its performance is competitive to some of the latest releases by rivals such as Anthropic’s Opus 4.6 and DeepSeek’s V4-Flash, Alibaba said in a statement.

Wednesday’s release uses an architecture designed for the next generation of models: the Qwen 4 series. That new system significantly reduces training and inferencing cost, the company said.

Alibaba is China’s biggest spender on AI, pledging more than 380 billion yuan ($57 billion) over three years toward AI development and infrastructure building. To fund that effort, it [raised](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-23/alibaba-to-raise-10-billion-by-selling-shares-for-ai-expansion) HK$80 billion ($10.2 billion) this week in Hong Kong’s biggest follow-on share offering, reaffirming a determination to amass and spend vast sums to take global AI leadership.

The e-commerce giant-turned-AI player has ramped up the pace at which it’s deepening and broadening its AI portfolio. Alibaba upgraded its flagship offering Qwen3.8 Max [earlier](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-03/alibaba-drops-another-china-ai-model-with-breakthrough-performance) this month, enhancing its largest-ever AI model. It also Alibaba Launches an Overseas Version of Its Enterprise AI Agent the international version of its enterprise-targeting AI agentic tool QwenWork on Wednesday, hoping to expand sales in global markets with a focus on Asia, Latin America and the Middle East.
