> 📌 **核心摘要**
>
> 凯文·沃什坚持精简的美联储沟通风格，即使因未提供利率策略细节引发美债抛售和市场批评，仍计划在杰克逊霍尔阐述其改革框架，并可能在9月加息。
> • 上周美联储会议后，美国长期借贷成本飙升，30年期美债收益率升至2007年来最高，因投资者认为沃什未提供足够通胀指引。
> • 接近沃什的人士承认他头10周犯了错误，包括未强化物价稳定信息和改革计划造成困惑，但认为不足以逆转改革。
> • 沃什策略核心是减少政策指引，打破美联储与市场反馈循环，让市场更多关注经济数据。
> • 尽管市场批评，但通胀预期指标仍低；6月PCE为3.7%，远超2%目标。
> • 沃什准备在9月加息，如果未来几周通胀数据强劲且市场提高预期；期货市场显示55%概率加息25基点。
> • 沃什将在杰克逊霍尔发表演讲，解释其“静悄悄革命”背后的知识框架。

Kevin Warsh is set to stick to his stripped-back communications style even after the Federal Reserve chair’s decision to offer scant details of his strategy on interest rates fuelled a powerful sell-off in Treasury bonds.

凯文·沃什（Kevin Warsh）将坚持其精简的沟通风格，即使美联储主席的决定——不就其利率策略提供多少细节——引发了美国国债的大幅抛售。

Long-term US borrowing costs soared after last week’s Fed meeting as investors said Warsh failed to offer sufficient guidance on how he would contain the bout of inflation triggered by Donald Trump’s Iran war.

上周美联储会议后，美国长期借贷成本飙升，投资者称沃什未能就如何遏制唐纳德·特朗普的伊朗战争引发的通胀提供充分指引。

People close to Warsh said he acknowledged that he had made mistakes in his first 10 weeks at the helm of the world’s most important central bank, including failing to reinforce his key messages on price stability and sowing confusion over whether his longer-term plans to reform the Fed could affect near-term policy decisions.

接近沃什的人士表示，他承认自己在执掌这家全球最重要的央行的头10周里犯了错误，包括未能强化其关于物价稳定的关键信息，并在其长期改革美联储的计划是否会影响近期政策决定方面制造了困惑。

But they insist those errors do not warrant reversing course on his shake-up of the Fed, which has missed its 2 per cent inflation goal for more than five years. A pillar of Warsh’s strategy is drastically reducing policymakers’ guidance to markets, a plan he has repeatedly emphasised following last Wednesday’s Fed meeting.

但他们坚称，这些错误不足以让他逆转对美联储的改革，美联储已超过五年未能实现2%的通胀目标。沃什策略的一个支柱是大幅减少政策制定者对市场的指引，他在上周三的美联储会议后多次强调这一计划。

[US 30-year Treasury yields](https://www.ft.com/content/c4eedbe8-6345-48b6-8d44-5cc5b0bea2c7?syn-25a6b1a6=1) shot up to the highest level since 2007 last week, in a rise many investors said partly reflected concerns that Warsh’s limited communications had undercut his credibility in quelling inflation at a time when elevated energy prices risk pushing up costs more broadly.

上周，[美国30年期国债收益率](https://www.ft.com/content/c4eedbe8-6345-48b6-8d44-5cc5b0bea2c7?syn-25a6b1a6=1)飙升至2007年以来的最高水平，许多投资者表示，这一上涨部分反映了对沃什有限沟通的担忧，即在他沟通有限之际，他平抑通胀的可信度受到削弱，而此时能源价格高企可能更广泛地推高成本。

![Line chart of 30-year Treasury yield (%) showing US long-term borrowing costs hit 19-year high](https://images.ft.com/v3/image/raw/ftcms%3A02cf2b44-7935-41b2-8719-d9881325dce6?source=next-article&fit=scale-down&quality=highest&width=700&dpr=2)

However, people close to [Warsh](https://www.ft.com/stream/005e7e07-099d-4671-b5d6-6958fbe73520) noted that market-based measures of inflation expectations remain low and have fallen back in recent days, suggesting investors think the Fed remains committed to hitting its 2 per cent price-stability goal.

然而，接近[沃什](https://www.ft.com/stream/005e7e07-099d-4671-b5d6-6958fbe73520)的人士指出，基于市场的通胀预期指标仍然较低，并在最近几天有所回落，这表明投资者认为美联储仍致力于实现其2%的物价稳定目标。

Inflation swaps, which are closely monitored by the Fed, show investors expect inflation to average 2.4 per cent over a five-year period beginning in five years.

美联储密切关注的通胀互换显示，投资者预计未来五年内（从五年后开始）通胀平均为2.4%。

Warsh has maintained publicly that the “trigger pullers” in bond markets — meaning fund managers making investment decisions — understand his approach, even as commentators have become more critical.

沃什公开坚称，债券市场中“扣动扳机的人”——即做出投资决策的基金经理——理解他的做法，尽管评论人士变得更加批评。

In an environment where price pressures have been too strong for half a decade, some think Warsh’s credibility was bound to be tested, with concerns not likely to be fully alleviated until inflation is back at 2 per cent. The Fed’s preferred inflation gauge was 3.7 per cent in June.

在物价压力持续五年过强的环境下，一些人认为沃什的可信度必然受到考验，除非通胀回到2%，否则担忧不太可能完全缓解。美联储青睐的通胀指标6月份为3.7%。

President Trump’s repeated attacks on Warsh’s predecessor, Jay Powell, for not slashing borrowing costs have also complicated the new Fed chair’s efforts to convince investors that he is a committed inflation fighter.

特朗普总统多次攻击沃什的前任杰伊·鲍威尔，指责他没有大幅降低借贷成本，这也使新任美联储主席说服投资者相信他是坚定的通胀斗士的努力变得更加复杂。

Warsh would be prepared to raise interest rates at September’s meeting if inflation readings released in coming weeks are hot, and markets ratchet up their expectations for increases in borrowing costs, the people familiar with his thinking said. Futures markets currently assess a roughly 55 per cent chance of a quarter-point rise in September, CME Group data shows.

知情人士表示，如果未来几周公布的通胀数据表现强劲，且市场提高对借贷成本上升的预期，沃什将准备在9月会议上加息。芝加哥商品交易所集团数据显示，期货市场目前认为9月加息25个基点的可能性约为55%。

The two-year Treasury yield, which is sensitive to monetary policy expectations, rose 0.04 per cent to 4.22 per cent on Thursday after the FT first reported on Warsh’s openness to raising rates next month.

在英国《金融时报》首次报道沃什对下月加息持开放态度后，对货币政策预期敏感的两年期美国国债收益率周四上升0.04个百分点，至4.22%。

![Line chart of Five-year, five-year inflation swap rate (%) showing US inflation expectations remain subdued](https://images.ft.com/v3/image/raw/ftcms%3A1c07630e-319b-4678-8560-b66352078094?source=next-article&fit=scale-down&quality=highest&width=700&dpr=2)

While the Fed chair has raised the prospect of shrinking the central bank’s $6.7tn balance sheet to tighten monetary policy, interest rates remain the primary tool for now — and would be used at forthcoming meetings if needed, the people added.

知情人士补充称，尽管美联储主席提出了缩减央行6.7万亿美元资产负债表以收紧货币政策的可能性，但利率目前仍是主要工具——如有必要，将在未来会议上使用。

Any major overhaul of the monetary policymaking process is on hold until at least next year, by which time the task forces Warsh unveiled during his first press conference as Fed chair in June will have reported back to the rate-setting Federal Open Market Committee.

对货币政策制定过程的任何重大改革至少要推迟到明年，届时沃什在6月作为美联储主席首次新闻发布会上公布的工作组将向设定利率的联邦公开市场委员会汇报。

[Warsh’s pared-back communications strategy](https://www.ft.com/content/cbc7297a-647f-44be-9124-4fba662b15e5?syn-25a6b1a6=1) has been the biggest change the former Fed governor has made since rejoining the US central bank.

[沃什精简的沟通策略](https://www.ft.com/content/cbc7297a-647f-44be-9124-4fba662b15e5?syn-25a6b1a6=1)是这位前美联储理事重新加入美国央行以来做出的最大改变。

While the former financier has been tight-lipped, his predecessors Powell, Janet Yellen and Ben Bernanke had sought to give comprehensive guidance on their economic outlook and clues on policy moves.

尽管这位前金融家守口如瓶，但他的前任鲍威尔、珍妮特·耶伦和本·伯南克都曾试图就经济前景给出全面指导，并提供政策动向的线索。

Since leaving the Fed in 2011 after a five-year stint, Warsh repeatedly expressed the view that “forward guidance” by previous Fed chairs left them trapped by their own words and overpromising action.

自2011年任职五年后离开美联储以来，沃什多次表示，前任美联储主席的“前瞻性指引”让他们被自己的言辞所困，并过度承诺了行动。

Heading into last week’s decision, investors were pricing in a 33 per cent chance of a rate rise — an unusual degree of uncertainty that analysts had anticipated could lead to market volatility.

进入上周的决策前，投资者定价了33%的加息概率——这种不寻常的不确定性是分析师预期可能导致市场波动的。

By breaking the feedback loop between the Fed and investors, the new chair has said that he hopes markets will spend less time scrutinising officials’ clues and focus more on economic data.

通过打破美联储与投资者之间的反馈循环，这位新主席表示，他希望市场能减少对官员线索的审视，而更多关注经济数据。

“I don’t see where all the negativity on Warsh stems from, in terms of market pricing,” said Eric Wallerstein, chief macro strategist at Clocktower Group and a former adviser to Fed governor Stephen Miran. “The loudest critics are those without investment responsibilities, who succeeded only when everything was choreographed.”

“我不明白市场上对沃什的负面情绪从何而来，”钟塔集团首席宏观策略师、前美联储理事斯蒂芬·米兰的顾问埃里克·沃勒斯坦表示。“最激烈的批评者是那些没有投资责任的人，他们只有在所有事情都被精心安排时才能成功。”

Warsh believes this leaner [communications strategy](https://www.ft.com/content/57aac838-baee-406d-b4ac-c0ffda351aee?syn-25a6b1a6=1) will give officials a clearer read on what investors think about the health of the world’s largest economy and lead to fewer policy mistakes.

沃什认为，这种更精简的[沟通策略](https://www.ft.com/content/57aac838-baee-406d-b4ac-c0ffda351aee?syn-25a6b1a6=1)将使官员更清晰地了解投资者对世界最大经济体健康状况的看法，并减少政策失误。

“He was unfairly treated last week. There was already an emerging consensus that forward guidance was not a good idea and gave central banks too little flexibility,” said Torsten Sløk, chief economist at Apollo Global Management. But he said Warsh could do more to explain the Fed’s plan to bring inflation down.

“上周他受到了不公平对待。已经有一种新兴共识认为前瞻性指引不是一个好主意，它让央行几乎没有灵活性，”阿波罗全球管理首席经济学家托尔斯滕·斯洛克表示。但他表示，沃什可以做更多工作来解释美联储降低通胀的计划。

Warsh is expected to use his first speech at the Kansas City Fed’s Jackson Hole symposium this month — a blockbuster event for markets — to explain the intellectual framework behind his quiet revolution, including clarifying areas where he believes his own messaging has fallen short.

预计沃什将利用本月在堪萨斯城联储杰克逊霍尔研讨会上的首次演讲——这是市场的重磅事件——来阐述其静悄悄革命背后的知识框架，包括澄清他认为自己沟通不足的地方。

“The speech will take the shape of him saying he’s here to leave his mark,” said Wallerstein. “It’s been a tough period for central banking. There’s been a lot of mis-steps — including at the Fed. Warsh wants to take stock of that and try to right the ship.”

“演讲将呈现为他来此留下印记的姿态，”沃勒斯坦说。“对央行来说是一段艰难时期。有很多失误——包括美联储在内。沃什希望对此进行反思并努力纠正方向。”
