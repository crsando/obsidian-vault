### 中文译文

## 经济增长大幅放缓后，习近平的副手敦促加大对经济的支持

### **要点**

- 7月份中国经济显现疲软，工业产出、消费和投资均比预期更大幅度放缓。
- 国务院总理李强敦促官员加大支持措施，努力实现年度经济社会发展目标。
- 经济学家预计政府将优先加快现有政策的落地，同时观察经济形势；有人警告增速可能进一步低于年度目标。
7月份中国经济全面走软，增速可能进一步低于政府年度目标，引发国务院总理李强呼吁官员加大支持措施。

李强在周一召开的国务院会议上表示：“我们要锚定发展目标，充分发挥现有政策效能，及时制定务实有效的增量政策。”他敦促官员“努力实现”年度经济社会发展目标。

![Li Qiang Photographer: Qilai Shen/Bloomberg](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3c081cc90b148167a6f9df07d0b5bd7b/3c081cc9-0b14-8167-a4aa-c475d04bf9bb-0ddc97cf6b40c976bd1ecfa1f20fb5cd.png)

这一讲话由官方新华社[发布](https://www.gov.cn/yaowen/liebiao/202608/content_7078350.htm)，此前当天早些时候公布的政府数据显示，中国7月份工业产出、消费和投资均比预期[更大幅度放缓](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-17/china-s-economy-extends-slowdown-by-weakening-across-the-board)。鉴于许多经济学家估计，在第二季度仅增长4.3%后，国内生产总值增速已进一步滑落至北京4.5%-5%的年度目标下方，要求政策制定者加大刺激力度的压力正在上升。

![图片](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3c081cc90b148167a6f9df07d0b5bd7b/3c081cc9-0b14-8128-8d2c-df18ec5f97c7-1aec9ecd429a7584b642a8feceb708ca.png)

李强——习近平主席之后的中国二号人物——承诺加快中国向新增长动力的转变。他说，政府将聚焦重大项目，如[六网](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-26/how-china-plans-to-supercharge-its-ai-ambitions-with-six-networks-program)计划，以及供应链广阔、经济权重更大的领域，以释放内需。总理还表示，将加快政府资金拨付，并加大对新兴产业、新型基础设施和消费升级的投资支持。

来自法国巴黎银行、麦格理集团和潘西恩宏观经济等机构的经济学家表示，7月份的数据表明，中国的经济增长可能低于官方年度目标区间的下限。

与此同时，高盛集团和花旗集团警告称，它们对第三季度GDP增速回升的预测可能存疑。

![图片](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3c081cc90b148167a6f9df07d0b5bd7b/3c081cc9-0b14-815c-9a94-d46bbc69c0fe-6e89efda08a34123ee629ad36af47237.png)

尽管如此，大多数分析师并不预期会立即追加新的刺激措施，部分原因是官员们在7月底强大的政治局会议召开时应该已经清楚了解经济状况。在会议上，共产党的决策机构为下半年制定经济政策，但[没有宣布](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-30/china-s-top-officials-to-consider-new-stimulus-after-slowdown)重大新措施。

经济学家表示，因此政府可能会优先加快现有政策的落实，同时在未来几个月监测经济，并调整任何额外的刺激措施，以确保年度增长目标能够实现。

高盛经济学家王立升在周一的一份报告中写道：“我们继续预计政府将在未来几个月加快债券发行和资金支出速度，加快实施新的政策性金融工具，并在如果增长进一步放缓及‘4.5-5.0%’的全年增长目标面临压力时，为今年晚些时候的进一步宽松政策敞开大门。”

新的政策性金融工具指的是今年计划用于准财政政策银行融资的8000亿元人民币，以资助投资。

根据花旗经济学家余向荣领导的团队的一份报告，不利的天气条件可能是推迟新增长政策的另一个因素。大雨和大风是上月固定资产投资收缩加深的原因之一。

![图片](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3c081cc90b148167a6f9df07d0b5bd7b/3c081cc9-0b14-8126-91a6-daaca1052425-d0e98c789c8ede62c493f1d9efce766b.png)

包括胡玉玮在内的麦格理经济学家预计，中国政府将坚持他们所谓的“恰到好处”原则，“采取足够行动实现增长目标，但不会再多了。”

他们表示，得益于全球人工智能超级周期，外贸出口的韧性将让中国当局能够保持适度的刺激措施。“如果人工智能带动的出口强势持续，国内需求可能依然疲软。”

### English Original

## Xi’s No. 2 Urges More Support for Economy After Big Growth Slump

### **Takeaways**

- China's economy showed weakness in July, with industrial output, consumption, and investment softening more than expected.
- Premier Li Qiang urged officials to ramp up supportive measures and strive to achieve the annual economic and social development targets.
- Economists expect the government to prioritize a faster roll-out of existing policies while monitoring the economy, with some warning that growth may slip further below the annual target.
China’s economy showed across-the-board weakness in July and growth likely slipped further below the government’s annual target, sparking a call from Premier Li Qiang on officials to ramp up supportive measures.

“We must anchor efforts to development goals, give full play to the effectiveness of existing policies, and promptly formulate practical and effective incremental policies,” Li said at a Monday meeting of China’s cabinet, as he urged officials to “strive to achieve” the annual economic and social development targets.

The speech was [released](https://www.gov.cn/yaowen/liebiao/202608/content_7078350.htm) by the official Xinhua News Agency after government data published earlier in the day showed China’s industrial output, consumption and investment all[ softened more than expected](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-17/china-s-economy-extends-slowdown-by-weakening-across-the-board) in July. Pressure on policymakers to step up stimulus is on the rise, given many economists estimate growth of gross domestic product has slipped further below Beijing’s annual target of 4.5%-5% after reaching only 4.3% in the second quarter.

Li — China’s No. 2 official, after President Xi Jinping — pledged to accelerate China’s shift to new growth drivers. He said the government will focus on major projects, such as the [Six Networks](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-26/how-china-plans-to-supercharge-its-ai-ambitions-with-six-networks-program), and sectors with expansive supply chains and greater economic weight to unleash domestic demand. The deployment of government funding will be expedited, and more support will be given to investment in emerging industries, new types of infrastructure and consumption upgrading, the premier added.

The July data suggested China’s growth is likely running below the lower end of the official annual target range, according to economists from institutions such as BNP Paribas SA, Macquarie Group and Pantheon Macroeconomics.

Goldman Sachs Group Inc. and Citigroup Inc., meanwhile, have warned their forecasts for a pickup in GDP expansion in the third quarter could be in doubt.

Still, most analysts aren’t expecting an immediate top-up of fresh stimulus, in part because officials should have had a clear understanding of where the economy stands when the powerful Politburo met in late July. During its huddle to set economic policy for the second half of the year, the Communist Party’s decision-making body [stopped short](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-30/china-s-top-officials-to-consider-new-stimulus-after-slowdown) of announcing major new measures.

The government therefore will likely prioritize a faster roll-out of existing policies while monitoring the economy in the coming months, calibrating any extra stimulus to keep the annual growth goal within reach, economists said.

“We continue to expect the government to accelerate the pace of bond issuance and proceeds spending in coming months, speed up implementation of the new policy-based financial instrument, and keep the door open to additional easing later this year if growth slows further and the full-year growth target of ‘4.5-5.0%’ comes under pressure,” Goldman economist Lisheng Wang wrote in a Monday note.

The new policy-based financial instrument refers to 800 billion yuan planned for this year in quasi-fiscal policy bank financing to fund investment.

Adverse weather conditions could be another factor delaying new pro-growth policies, according to a report by Citigroup economists led by Xiangrong Yu. Heavy rains and strong winds were among the reasons for a deepening contraction in fixed-asset investment last month.

Macquarie economists including Larry Hu expect Beijing to stick to what they called its “Just Enough” rule, “doing just enough to achieve the growth target, but no more.”

The resilience in foreign shipments on the back of the global AI supercycle will allow Chinese authorities to keep stimulus measured, they said. “Domestic demand is likely to remain weak if AI-led export strength continues.”
