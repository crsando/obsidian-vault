### 中文译文

## CXMT缩小与SK海力士和三星在智能手机内存方面的差距

中国国有支持的[CXMT公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/688825%20CH%20Equity)即将生产与业界最先进设计同级的智能手机内存芯片，突显出北京在一个对AI部署至关重要的领域取得进展的速度。

这家被五角大楼列入黑名单的芯片制造商正准备在年底左右小批量生产先进低功耗内存芯片，知情人士称。生产将在原型测试取得满意结果后进行，这些人士因讨论内部计划而要求匿名。其中一位人士表示，大规模生产将在稍后跟进。

如果实现，这一里程碑将标志着北京多年追求技术自给自足的突破，证明中国在减少对外国零部件依赖方面取得了多大进展。移动内存芯片的大规模生产路线图将使CXMT公司能够与[美光科技](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/MU%20US%20Equity)、[SK海力士](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/000660%20KS%20Equity)和[三星电子](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/005930%20KS%20Equity)等公司竞争，并利用全球短缺的机会，这种短缺已成为包括[苹果](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/AAPL%20US%20Equity)在内的顶级消费电子品牌最大的瓶颈之一。

CXMT公司的一位发言人没有立即回复通过电子邮件发送的询问。这一进展此前已被中国新闻媒体第一财经（Yicai）[报道](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5MTM3NTMwNA%3D%3D&mid=2661779297&idx=1&sn=53b2b9f8535db75eb79a8e3c429b70c6&chksm=bc8169eabf6561362ab4e93628c2e2a92a91f8b39b440ce24d9236046df7d96945ab4162eaa0&mpshare=1&scene=1&srcid=0803UNO21gXQrWqTeKfLWjga&sharer_shareinfo=b6ac0b031981e443ec81d78aee150f51&sharer_shareinfo_first=b6ac0b031981e443ec81d78aee150f51&exportkey=n_ChQIAhIQesiftoo9KxqpY1n9jeYfRxKfAgIE97dBBAEAAAAAAJACOLSHXZQAAAAOpnltbLcz9gKNyK89dVj0xhQKHIL7jMBx7vapE0psMpCMZmBB%2BBU9arTtI6NI%2BQJ%2FJeeS9maSUP59LptSWmxtpP7tWIrOJdkGBcH1Yhf6qBIYca83gxFjp80pjEiaSOpAso05enj%2BVieJgGiqc3BN%2B21bFw1mf6DZQpfELIj%2BXE%2FdRWm3UMze32F%2FBYpAB%2FLosFE453EzYnb60jplHRB1VkNN5bNpbp9Eex%2F40sSuf4xvZ4BlPBhgvc4ZU4hh6v8oyd9OuqbPgolTrevwySc8ny4OUvB6w0HNs6qgnNp6jlD72t8zBUW3LS%2BmcJvJN8efvRImz6Hqtr7%2BDTn2iI7YS9GVqIbtNya4&acctmode=0&pass_ticket=ZKPpdsEzQ0qgeHgbc2d9E%2BLRj2kzcsV7UULlNm4CeMWkMHXyq5FcZBjH5QXClUq4&wx_header=0#rd)。

这种尖端芯片被称为第六代低功耗双倍数据速率内存（LPDDR6），其数据传输速度比前代产品大幅提升，能效也更高，可帮助智能手机和笔记本电脑本地运行AI应用。掌握此类芯片的制造工艺将提升CXMT公司的收入。CXMT公司上个月在上海完成了备受瞩目的[首次公开募股](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-26/china-memory-champion-cxmt-set-to-debut-after-9-8-billion-ipo)。

但是，尽管CXMT公司的新芯片与行业顶级厂商的产品大致属于同一产品类别，仍需要大规模生产此类芯片才有竞争力。SK海力士在[3月份](https://news.skhynix.com/en/1c-lpddr6-development-2026/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)推出了其16GB LPDDR6芯片，称其比LPDDR5X速度快33%，能效高20%。三星也表示，其产品线中已有[类似产品](https://semiconductor.samsung.com/dram/lpddr/lpddr6/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)。

内存芯片制造商正受益于AI热潮，这股热潮正推动[Meta平台](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/META%20US%20Equity)和[亚马逊](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/AMZN%20US%20Equity)等公司预订数年的产能，导致严重短缺和价格飙升。6月份，苹果提高了其所有Mac、iPad、家用设备和Vision Pro的价格。

这家总部位于加州库比蒂诺的公司的管理人员一直在[游说](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-01/apple-seeks-to-buy-chinese-made-memory-chips-with-lobbying-push)美国政策制定者，为从包括CXMT在内的中国芯片制造商采购芯片扫清道路。但立法者对此类尝试进行了[抵制](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-29/senators-warn-apple-not-to-buy-memory-chips-from-chinese-firms)，警告这家iPhone制造商可能在美国对手的关键零部件上变得依赖。

## CXMT Narrows Gap With SK Hynix and Samsung in Smartphone Memory

China’s state-backed [CXMT Corp.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/688825%20CH%20Equity) is poised to produce smartphone memory chips that are on par with the industry’s most advanced designs, highlighting the speed at which Beijing is gaining ground in a field critical for AI deployment.

The chipmaker, which is blacklisted by the Pentagon, is preparing to manufacture advanced low-power memory chips in small quantities around the end of the year, people familiar with the matter said. Production will take place once prototype testing yields satisfactory results, the people said, asking not to be named discussing internal plans. Mass production will follow at a later date, one of them said.

If realized, the milestone would mark a breakthrough in Beijing’s yearslong pursuit of technological self-sufficiency, serving as a testament of how far China has come in reducing reliance on foreign components. A roadmap to mass production of the mobile memory chips would allow CXMT to compete against the likes of [Micron Technology Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/MU%20US%20Equity), [SK Hynix Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/000660%20KS%20Equity) and [Samsung Electronics Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/005930%20KS%20Equity) and take advantage of a global shortage that has become one of the biggest bottlenecks for top consumer electronics brands including [Apple Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/AAPL%20US%20Equity)

A CXMT spokesperson didn’t immediately respond to a query sent via email. The development was [reported](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MjM5MTM3NTMwNA%3D%3D&amp%3Bmid=2661779297&amp%3Bidx=1&amp%3Bsn=53b2b9f8535db75eb79a8e3c429b70c6&amp%3Bchksm=bc8169eabf6561362ab4e93628c2e2a92a91f8b39b440ce24d9236046df7d96945ab4162eaa0&amp%3Bmpshare=1&amp%3Bscene=1&amp%3Bsrcid=0803UNO21gXQrWqTeKfLWjga&amp%3Bsharer_shareinfo=b6ac0b031981e443ec81d78aee150f51&amp%3Bsharer_shareinfo_first=b6ac0b031981e443ec81d78aee150f51&amp%3Bexportkey=n_ChQIAhIQesiftoo9KxqpY1n9jeYfRxKfAgIE97dBBAEAAAAAAJACOLSHXZQAAAAOpnltbLcz9gKNyK89dVj0xhQKHIL7jMBx7vapE0psMpCMZmBB%2BBU9arTtI6NI%2BQJ%2FJeeS9maSUP59LptSWmxtpP7tWIrOJdkGBcH1Yhf6qBIYca83gxFjp80pjEiaSOpAso05enj%2BVieJgGiqc3BN%2B21bFw1mf6DZQpfELIj%2BXE%2FdRWm3UMze32F%2FBYpAB%2FLosFE453EzYnb60jplHRB1VkNN5bNpbp9Eex%2F40sSuf4xvZ4BlPBhgvc4ZU4hh6v8oyd9OuqbPgolTrevwySc8ny4OUvB6w0HNs6qgnNp6jlD72t8zBUW3LS%2BmcJvJN8efvRImz6Hqtr7%2BDTn2iI7YS9GVqIbtNya4&amp%3Bacctmode=0&amp%3Bpass_ticket=ZKPpdsEzQ0qgeHgbc2d9E%2BLRj2kzcsV7UULlNm4CeMWkMHXyq5FcZBjH5QXClUq4&amp%3Bwx_header=0#rd) earlier by Chinese news outlet Yicai.

The cutting-edge chip, known as sixth-generation low-power double-data-rate memory, offers substantially faster data-transfer speeds and greater power efficiency than its predecessors, helping smartphones and laptops run AI applications locally. Mastering the manufacture of such chips, called LPDDR6, would boost revenues at CXMT, which completed a [blockbuster](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-26/china-memory-champion-cxmt-set-to-debut-after-9-8-billion-ipo) initial public offering in Shanghai last month.

But while CXMT’s new chip is in roughly the same product category as the industry’s top players, it would still need to produce such chips at scale to compete. SK Hynix introduced its 16-gigabyte LPDDR6 chip [in March](https://news.skhynix.com/en/1c-lpddr6-development-2026/?utm_source=chatgpt.com), saying it’s 33% faster and 20% more power-efficient than LPDDR5X. Samsung also announced it has a [similar product](https://semiconductor.samsung.com/dram/lpddr/lpddr6/?utm_source=chatgpt.com) in its pipeline.

Memory chipmakers are benefiting from an AI boom that’s pushing companies like [Meta Platforms Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/META%20US%20Equity) and [Amazon.com Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/AMZN%20US%20Equity) to reserve years’ worth of production capacity, leading to severe shortages and price surges. In June, Apple raised prices of all its Macs, iPads, home devices and the Vision Pro.

Executives at the Cupertino, California-based company have [lobbied](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-01/apple-seeks-to-buy-chinese-made-memory-chips-with-lobbying-push) US policymakers to clear the way for it to procure chips from Chinese chipmakers including CXMT. But lawmakers have [pushed back](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-29/senators-warn-apple-not-to-buy-memory-chips-from-chinese-firms) on such attempts, warning that the iPhone maker risked becoming reliant on a US adversary for crucial components.
