### 中文译文

## 中国物价涨幅低于预期，通缩风险重现

### **要点**

- 中国消费者价格增速创六个月最慢，7月居民消费价格指数（CPI）同比上涨0.5%。
- 生产者通胀从6月的4.1%放缓至3.5%，这是自3月份中国生产者价格指数转正以来的首次放缓。
- 经济学家警告称，中国持续的通缩压力可能损害经济长期增长，而整体价格指标的放缓重新引发了这种担忧。
中国消费者价格增速创六个月最慢，同时工厂出厂价格通胀自2月底伊朗战争爆发以来首次放缓，这是另一个迹象，表明随着石油冲击[消退](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-09/china-consumer-inflation-surprises-by-slowing-more-than-forecast)且国内支出低迷，再通胀动能正在减弱。

根据国家统计局周日发布的数据，7月份两项价格指标均比预期放缓。居民消费价格指数同比上涨0.5%，较上月的1%减速，为1月以来最低读数。剔除波动较大的食品和能源项目的核心CPI回落至0.9%，也是六个月低点。

[生产者通胀](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CHEFTYOY%20Index)从6月的4.1%放缓至3.5%，这是自3月份中国生产者价格指数在油价连续下跌三年多后上涨而转正以来的首次放缓。

“中国的通缩风险可能再次上升，”花旗集团包括Xinyu Ji在内的经济学家在周一发布的一份报告中表示。“CPI和PPI均不及预期，不仅受到能源价格修正的推动，还受到国内需求更明显疲软的影响。”

![图片](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3b881cc90b148112b75ced859881a26b/3b881cc9-0b14-8116-ab69-f8fee34fed5e-75375811ee51970d2b7ccb0a27daf1da.png)

中国已从一段[创纪录的通缩期](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-15/china-s-gdp-price-gauge-turns-positive-after-years-of-deflation)中走出，但物价上行势头疲软。迄今为止，国内消费者支出低迷限制了工厂将全球油价、芯片和金属价格上涨导致的不断上升的生产成本转嫁出去的程度。

因此，上游和下游行业之间的利润出现分化。服装制造等行业利润大幅下滑，而能源生产商等行业利润则飙升。

全球大宗商品价格上涨对经济的影响在中国已开始消退。尽管原油价格在6月和7月剧烈波动，但平均成本仍较今年早些时候的峰值有所回落。

美国总统唐纳德·特朗普本周表示，伊朗与阿曼就霍尔木兹海峡进行的谈判正在“[取得进展](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-07/trump-says-hormuz-talks-moving-along-even-as-iran-mulls-us-ban)”。德黑兰表示，与阿曼就海峡新海上交通路线达成协议已“[非常接近](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-08/iran-says-hormuz-deal-close-but-that-its-conditions-must-be-met)”，即便伊朗伊斯兰共和国重申了美国须在开放该水道前同意的一系列要求。

近年来，许多经济学家警告称，中国持续的通缩压力可能损害经济的长期增长，促使家庭减少支出、侵蚀企业利润、抑制投资和招聘。

整体价格指标的放缓重新点燃了此类担忧，进一步证明在油价冲击后，健康通胀的回归可能仍很遥远。

由于汽油价格回落，7月份燃料成本仅微涨0.8%，较上月15%的涨幅有所回落。[汽车](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CMCPCARY%20Index)价格跌幅加深至1.3%，对交通通胀构成又一个拖累。

![图片](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3b881cc90b148112b75ced859881a26b/3b881cc9-0b14-8116-8b38-c2e2b15ef996-8aa05c534dcc51b69e2a06ef19d17e3b.png)

猪肉（中国的主食肉类）价格同比跌幅收窄，表明在经历一年的收缩后，生猪行业供应过剩情况正在缓解。

旅游服务价格同比上涨2.2%，较6月份4.5%的涨幅有所放缓。当地媒体[报道](https://www.eeo.com.cn/2026/0731/981837.shtml)称，该行业国内支出在暑期表现不及预期，酒店房价和机票价格均较上年同期下降。

数据不佳本身可能不会改变中国政策应对的考量。但如果增长继续放缓或市场遭受更大挫折，官员们可能会在今年晚些时候被迫加大支持力度。

中国最高领导人在7月底召开的最近一次经济会议上展现出灵活态度。他们将加快财政支出，考虑新的刺激措施，并“适时调整货币政策工具”。

尽管许多经济学家不再预期央行今年会降息，但最新通胀数据为其他人呼吁降息提供了更多理由。

荷兰国际集团（ING Bank NV）大中华区首席经济学家林恩·宋（Lynn Song）周一在一份报告中表示：“通胀势头放缓，加上二季度以来国内经济活动走弱，为未来几个月降息10个基点以支持增长提供了充分理由。”

## China’s Slower-Than-Expected Price Gains Revive Deflation Risk

### **Takeaways**

- China's consumer prices grew at the slowest pace in six months, with the consumer price index rising 0.5% in July from a year earlier.
- Producer inflation moderated to 3.5% from 4.1% in June, the first slowdown since China's produce price index turned positive in March.
- Economists have warned that persistent deflationary pressures in China could harm the economy's long-term growth, and a slowdown in the headline price gauges has reignited such concerns.
China’s consumer prices grew at the slowest pace in six months while factory-gate inflation eased for the first time since the Iran war broke out in late February, in another sign that reflation momentum is fading as the oil shock [subsides](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-09/china-consumer-inflation-surprises-by-slowing-more-than-forecast) and domestic spending languishes.

Both price gauges slowed more than expected in July, based on data published by the National Bureau of Statistics on Sunday. The consumer price index rose 0.5% in July from a year earlier, decelerating from 1% in the previous month to the weakest reading since January. The core CPI, which strips out volatile food and energy items, eased to 0.9%, also a six-month low.

[Producer inflation](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CHEFTYOY%20Index) moderated to 3.5% from 4.1% in June, the first slowdown since China’s produce price index turned positive in March on the back of higher oil prices after more than three years of declines.

“Deflation risks in China could be rising again,” Citigroup economists including Xinyu Ji said in a report distributed Monday. “Both CPI and PPI missed expectations, driven not only by energy price corrections but also by more pronounced weakness in domestic demand.”

China has emerged from a [record bout](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-15/china-s-gdp-price-gauge-turns-positive-after-years-of-deflation) of deflation with weak upward momentum for prices. Sluggish domestic consumer spending has so far limited the extent to which factories can pass on their growing production expenses from higher global prices for oil, chips and metals.

As a result, a divergence in profits has opened up between upstream and downstream sectors. Industries like clothes-making are suffering a plunge in their earnings, while others such as energy producers are seeing profits soar.

The economic fallout of higher global prices for commodities is already starting to fade in China. While crude fluctuated wildly in June and July, average costs still eased from their peak earlier this year.

US President Donald Trump said this week that negotiations between Iran and Oman over the Strait of Hormuz are “[moving along](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-07/trump-says-hormuz-talks-moving-along-even-as-iran-mulls-us-ban).” Tehran said it’s “[very close](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-08/iran-says-hormuz-deal-close-but-that-its-conditions-must-be-met)” to a deal with Oman on a new maritime transit route in the strait, even as the Islamic Republic renewed a list of demands for the US to agree to before the waterway would open.

Many economists have warned in recent years that persistent deflationary pressures in China could harm the economy’s long-term growth, encouraging households to cut back on spending, eating into corporate profits and stifling investment and hiring.

A slowdown in the headline price gauges reignited such concerns by offering further evidence that a return of healthy inflation in the aftermath of the oil shock could still be a long way off.

As gasoline prices pulled back, the cost of fuels inched up just 0.8% in July, down from 15% in the previous month. The fall in [car](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CMCPCARY%20Index) prices deepened to 1.3%, another drag on transportation inflation.

The cost of pork, China’s staple meat, narrowed its year-on-year decline in a sign that an oversupply is easing in the hog industry after a year of contraction.

The cost of tourism services rose 2.2% from a year ago, slowing from the 4.5% increase in June. Domestic spending in the sector was worse than expected during the summer holidays, local media [reported](https://www.eeo.com.cn/2026/0731/981837.shtml), citing a decrease in hotel rates and flight ticket costs from a year earlier.

The data disappointment** **will probably not move the calculus of China’s policy response by itself. But if growth continues to slow or the markets suffer a bigger setback, officials may be compelled to step up their support later this year.

China’s top leaders struck a flexible tone in their latest economic meeting at the end of July. They will speed up fiscal spending, consider new stimulus and “adjust monetary policy tools at an appropriate time.”

While many economists no longer see an interest rate cut this year from the central bank, the latest inflation print is giving the rest more reasons to call for a reduction.

“The softening inflation momentum, combined with weaker domestic economic activity starting in the second quarter, provides a solid case for a 10 basis point rate cut in the coming months to support growth,” Lynn Song, chief economist for Greater China at ING Bank NV, said in a note Monday.
