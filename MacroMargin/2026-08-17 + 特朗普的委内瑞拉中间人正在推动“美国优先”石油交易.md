### 中文译文

## 特朗普的委内瑞拉中间人正在推动“美国优先”石油交易

*亚历杭德罗·贝坦库尔，2014年在加拉加斯。摄影师：Vladimir Marcano/Bloomberg*

一位通过销售发电涡轮机和开采石油而发家的委内瑞拉大亨，如今已成为特朗普政府为 favored 美国公司推动“美国优先”交易的中间人，而华盛顿正寻求在这个资源丰富的南美国家施加更大影响力。

据知情人士透露，亚历杭德罗·贝坦库尔——一位经历复杂的商人，控制着委内瑞拉领先的独立石油生产商——正在帮助美国行政当局识别有前景的能源资产、评估运营瓶颈并建立[行业联系](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2026-07-07/why-oil-wildcatters-are-pouring-into-venezuela-after-maduro-s-fall)。

贝坦库尔正在努力促进美国的一项战略，即利用较小的美国独立石油勘探公司，因为大型石油巨头大多不愿在那里投资。最近几个月，已与Lionheart Capital和[Pacific Coast Energy Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-21/venezuela-oil-deal-near-for-california-wildcatter-pacific-coast-energy)（简称PCEC）等公司达成了几项初步协议。

彭博社对贝坦库尔的报道基于对他的商业伙伴、政府官员以及熟悉其工作的顾问的采访。他们要求匿名，因为他们不想因讨论机密事务而被识别身份、担心遭到报复，或者未获授权公开谈论此事。

这位大亨成为核心人物，反映了华盛顿正在加大力度鼓励美国公司在委内瑞拉开采石油，并向这个被唐纳德·特朗普总统[描述](https://truthsocial.com/@realDonaldTrump/posts/116564063096952080)为第51个州的国家投资1000亿美元。在美国抓获尼古拉斯·马杜罗、认可其继任者并宣布委内瑞拉开放营业七个多月后，重大石油交易仍难以达成，原因在于与国有委内瑞拉石油公司的复杂谈判以及制裁限制。

在缺乏竞争性招标的情况下，进展并不透明。知情人士称，这赋予了贝坦库尔在委内瑞拉石油界巨大的影响力，尽管他多年来在欧洲、美国和委内瑞拉因腐败、洗钱和税务欺诈指控而受到调查。他否认有任何不当行为，也从未被刑事指控。

据知情人士透露，直到最近，贝坦库尔一直避免踏上美国领土，因为担心美国的调查。

贝当古本人也是委内瑞拉的顶级石油业者。据知情人士透露，他的[北美蓝色能源合伙公司](https://www.nabep.net/)（NABEP）每天从[马拉开波湖](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2023-03-15/chevron-asks-venezuela-to-dredge-lake-maracaibo-in-hope-of-doubling-oil-exports)和奥里诺科带周边的油田开采约20万桶原油。这使其成为委内瑞拉仅次于[雪佛龙公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CVX%20US%20Equity)的第二大私营生产商。

据知情人士透露，上周，与贝当古关系密切的一方同意购买哈里·萨金特三世持有的NABEP少数股权。萨金特是佛罗里达州石油大亨，此前因涉嫌支持马杜罗而受到委内瑞拉反对派人士和美国盟友的抨击。NABEP交易签署后不久，美国财政部通知萨金特的律师，已冻结其离岸控股公司的资产。

贝当古拒绝置评。记者未能通过萨金特位于佛罗里达的控股公司Global Oil Management Group以电话或电子邮件联系到他本人请其置评。NABEP未回应置评请求。

据熟悉贝当古行程的人士透露，他在加拉加斯、华盛顿和莫斯科都有有权势的朋友，最近开始频繁从伦敦的住所前往委内瑞拉。据知情人士称，他定期会见高级官员，包括美国支持的代理总统德尔茜·罗德里格斯及其外交政策顾问费利克斯·普拉森西亚，并为美国国会代表团举办了一次晚宴。

委内瑞拉坐拥全球最大已探明原油储量之一，从20世纪50年代到70年代，曾是全球最大石油出口国之一，使加拉加斯成为拉丁美洲[最富有的首都](https://www.weforum.org/stories/global-cooperation/venezuela-economic-woes-2017-explained/)之一。后来产量曾攀升至每日300万桶以上，但数十年的忽视、腐败、征收、经济崩溃和美国制裁使产量急剧下降。

在马杜罗被捕后的几天里，特朗普大力宣扬该行业的潜力。尽管美国政府将这次夜间突袭定性为打击国际毒品贩运的努力，但特朗普表示，美国公司将涌入重建基础设施，产量将飙升，汽油价格将下跌，这将在争夺国会控制权的中期选举前，让共和党在一个关键的民生问题上获胜。

但大多数美国大型石油公司，包括[埃克森美孚控股公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/9666655D:US)和[康菲石油公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/8375930Q:US)，在委内瑞拉征收其资产多年后仍心有余悸。因此，据知情人士透露，贝当古正在帮助美国政府将机会引向风险偏好较高的独立钻井商和私人投资者。

白宫称美国与委内瑞拉的关系对双方而言“非同寻常”。白宫在回应置评请求时表示：“我们与德尔茜·罗德里格斯总统及其代表合作得非常愉快。石油开始流动，多年来未见的大量资金正极大地帮助委内瑞拉人民。”但没有直接回答有关贝当古角色的问题。

*随着总统尼古拉斯·马杜罗被罢免，美国总统唐纳德·特朗普希望美国石油巨头重振委内瑞拉能源业。来源：彭博*

据知情人士透露，贝当古长期主要在幕后运作。过去一个月，随着交易开始取得成果，曾任拉丁美洲特使、后来担任委内瑞拉事务非正式顾问的毛里西奥·克拉弗-卡罗内从这一事务中抽身，贝当古的角色变得更加引人注目。

与国务卿马可·卢比奥关系密切的克拉弗-卡罗内，曾帮助召集了参与执行特朗普政府委内瑞拉战略的许多人，包括贝当古。知情人士表示，虽然克拉弗-卡罗内现在参与减少，但其中许多人仍然活跃。

贝当古在石油领域声名鹊起，将NABEP从一家小型运营商转变为本国领先的生产商之一。过去十年间，尽管许多外国公司在政治动荡中缩减了业务布局，NABEP却将产量提升了多达10倍。在执掌NABEP之前，2010年代中期，贝当古曾投资于哥伦比亚最大油田[Rubiales](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2015-08-21/man-who-spoiled-pacific-rubiales-oil-deal-has-a-plan-for-growth)的一家运营商。随后他返回委内瑞拉，与俄罗斯前官员合作成立[Petrozamora](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2020-04-15/two-ex-gazprom-execs-are-helping-venezuela-keep-its-oil-flowing)，这是马拉开波湖的一家合资企业。

![The PDVSA Bajo Grande Refinery at the Paraguana Refinery Complex on Lake Maracaibo. Source: Bloomberg](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3bf81cc90b14815cb3afd6799f6c6bab/3bf81cc9-0b14-8110-a47b-c9f8809dfc95-4d769bf11e8723fb66edbac9c5d84806.png)

知情人士称，贝当古与萨金特一起，还率先与PDVSA建立了更灵活的合同结构，该结构此后成为许多现向新投资者提供的协议的模板。

贝当古目前的工作包括与PCEC相关的努力，PCEC是一家鲜为人知的加州公司，最近签署了在马拉开波湖和奥里诺科重油带运营油田的协议。该公司表示，PCEC与NABEP达成了一项本地采购协议，这是该公司在现场建立的30多个类似关系之一，旨在帮助该公司实现开采委内瑞拉石油的目标。

该公司补充说，PCEC不披露其投资者，但它从银行和贸易公司获得融资。

尽管在委内瑞拉内部和[外部](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2016-08-09/swiss-court-paves-way-to-share-venezuela-linked-data-with-u-s)屡屡引发法律争议，贝当古的影响力仍在增长。

在委内瑞拉，贝当古被视为[bolichicos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bolibourgeoisie)之一——即在前总统乌戈·查韦斯统治期间积累财富的企业家——他联合创立了Derwick Associates，该公司自2009年起获得数十亿美元的应急电力设备合同，当时正值长期停电困扰的时期。

德威克最终受到委内瑞拉、[美国](https://www.icij.org/investigations/fincen-files/how-banks-helped-venezuelas-boligarchs-extract-billions/)和西班牙调查人员的审查，他们怀疑其与涉及PDVSA资金的洗钱计划有关。该公司和贝当古否认了这些指控。

据当时当地媒体报道，委内瑞拉当局在没有提出指控的情况下结束了调查。美国司法部未回应关于其调查现状的请求。西班牙调查人员今年，西班牙法院在委内瑞拉澄清PDVSA贷款后撤销了对贝当古的调查，但《国家报》后来[报道](https://elpais.com/espana/2026-06-02/la-audiencia-nacional-obliga-al-juez-pedraz-a-reabrir-la-causa-contra-el-venezolano-dueno-de-las-gafas-hawkers.html)称该调查已重新启动。西班牙高等法院官员未回应就贝当古案件现状发表评论的请求。

彭博社2019年[报道](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2019-12-19/giuliani-keeps-the-hustle-going-even-as-trump-is-impeached)称，律师、前纽约市长鲁迪·朱利安尼曾在与司法部的会面中帮助代理贝当古。贝当古从未在与该调查相关的公开法庭文件中被点名。

## Trump’s Venezuela Fixer Is Promoting ‘America First’ Oil Deals

*Alejandro Betancourt, center, in Caracas in 2014. Photographer: Vladimir Marcano/Bloomberg*

A Venezuelan mogul who minted a fortune selling power turbines and pumping oil has emerged as the Trump administration’s fixer for promoting “America First” deals for favored US companies, as Washington moves to exert more influence in the resource-rich South American country.

Alejandro Betancourt, an entrepreneur with a checkered history who controls Venezuela’s leading independent oil producer, is helping the US administration identify promising energy assets, assess operational bottlenecks and make [industry connections](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2026-07-07/why-oil-wildcatters-are-pouring-into-venezuela-after-maduro-s-fall), according to people familiar with his role.

Betancourt is working to facilitate a US strategy to tap smaller American wildcatters as established oil majors have largely balked at investing there. Several preliminary agreements have been [reached](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-20/lionheart-cub-nears-400-million-deal-for-venezuela-oil-stake) in recent months with companies including Lionheart Capital and [Pacific Coast Energy Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-21/venezuela-oil-deal-near-for-california-wildcatter-pacific-coast-energy), known as PCEC.

Bloomberg’s reporting on Betancourt is based on interviews with his business associates, government officials and advisers familiar with his work. They requested anonymity because they didn’t want to be identified discussing confidential matters, feared retribution or were not authorized to speak publicly on the matter.

The tycoon’s emergence as a central figure offers insight into how Washington is stepping up efforts to encourage US companies to pump Venezuelan oil and invest $100 billion in a country President Donald Trump [describes](https://truthsocial.com/@realDonaldTrump/posts/116564063096952080) as the 51st state. More than seven months after the US captured Nicolás Maduro, blessed his replacement and declared Venezuela open for business, significant oil deals remain elusive, held up by complex negotiations with state-owned Petróleos de Venezuela SA and sanctions constraints.

In the absence of competitive bidding, progress is opaque. That’s given Betancourt tremendous sway in Venezuelan oil circles, the people said, in spite of years of investigations in Europe, the US and Venezuela over allegations of corruption, money laundering and tax fraud. He has denied any wrongdoing and was never charged with a crime.

Until recently, Betancourt avoided US soil out of concern over the American probe, people familiar with the matter said.

Betancourt himself is also a top oil player in Venezuela. His [North American Blue Energy Partners](https://www.nabep.net/), or NABEP, pumps about 200,000 barrels of crude a day from fields around [Lake Maracaibo](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2023-03-15/chevron-asks-venezuela-to-dredge-lake-maracaibo-in-hope-of-doubling-oil-exports) and the Orinoco Belt, according to a person familiar with the matter. That makes it Venezuela’s second-largest private-sector producer behind [Chevron Corp.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CVX%20US%20Equity)

Last week, a party close to Betancourt agreed to buy the minority stake in NABEP held by Harry Sargeant III, according to people familiar with the transaction. Sargeant, a Florida oil magnate, had come under fire from Venezuelan opposition figures and US allies for allegedly propping up Maduro. Shortly after the NABEP deal was signed, the Treasury Department notified Sargeant’s attorney that it had blocked the assets of his offshore holding company.

Betancourt declined to comment. Sargeant couldn’t be reached by telephone or email for comment through his Florida-based holding company Global Oil Management Group. NABEP didn’t reply to a request for comment.

Betancourt, who has powerful friends in Caracas, Washington and Moscow, recently began traveling frequently from his London home to Venezuela, according to people familiar with his schedule. He regularly meets with senior officials, including US-supported acting President Delcy Rodríguez and her foreign policy adviser Félix Plasencia, and hosted a dinner for a US congressional delegation, according to people with knowledge of the meetings.

Venezuela sits atop some of the world’s largest proven crude reserves and, from the 1950s through the 1970s, was among the world’s biggest petroleum exporters, transforming Caracas into one of Latin America’s [richest capitals](https://www.weforum.org/stories/global-cooperation/venezuela-economic-woes-2017-explained/). Production later climbed above 3 million barrels a day before decades of neglect, corruption, expropriations, economic collapse and US sanctions sent output plunging.

Trump played up the industry’s potential in the days after Maduro’s capture. While the administration justified the nighttime raid as an effort to dismantle international narcotics trafficking, Trump said US companies would pour in to rebuild the infrastructure, production would soar and gasoline prices would fall, giving Republicans a win on a key checkbook issue ahead of midterm elections in which control of Congress is up for grabs.

But most of the biggest US oil companies, including [ExxonMobil Holdings Corp.](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/9666655D:US) and [ConocoPhillips](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/8375930Q:US), remain skittish years after Venezuela expropriated their assets. So Betancourt is helping the administration steer opportunities toward the wildcatters, who are less risk-averse, and private investors, according to people familiar with the matter.

The White House described US relations with Venezuela as “extraordinary” for both sides. “We are dealing very well with President Delcy Rodríguez and her representatives. Oil is starting to flow, and large amounts of money, unseen for many years, is greatly helping the people of Venezuela,” the White House said in response to a request for comment, without directly answering questions about Betancourt’s role.

*With President Nicolás Maduro deposed, President Donald Trump wants US oil giants to revive Venezuela’s energy industry. Source: Bloomberg*

Betancourt long operated largely behind the scenes. His role has come into sharper focus over the last month as deals began to come to fruition and Mauricio Claver-Carone, a former special envoy for Latin America who later served as an unofficial adviser on Venezuela, stepped back from the portfolio, according to people familiar with the matter.

Claver-Carone, who is close to Secretary of State Marco Rubio, helped assemble many of the people involved in executing the Trump administration’s Venezuela strategy, including Betancourt. While Claver-Carone is now less involved, many of those figures remain active, the people said.

Betancourt rose to prominence in the oil sphere by transforming NABEP from a small operator into one of the country’s leading producers. While many foreign companies reduced their footprint over the past decade amid the political turmoil, NABEP boosted production as much as 10-fold. Before leading NABEP, in the mid-2010s Betancourt invested in an operator in Colombia’s largest oil field, [Rubiales](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2015-08-21/man-who-spoiled-pacific-rubiales-oil-deal-has-a-plan-for-growth). He then returned to Venezuela to partner with former Russian officials in [Petrozamora](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2020-04-15/two-ex-gazprom-execs-are-helping-venezuela-keep-its-oil-flowing), a joint venture in Lake Maracaibo.

Together with Sargeant, Betancourt also helped pioneer a more flexible contractual structure with PDVSA that has since become the model for many of the agreements now on offer to new investors, the people said.

Betancourt’s current work includes efforts tied to PCEC, a little-known California-based company that recently [signed agreements](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-21/venezuela-oil-deal-near-for-california-wildcatter-pacific-coast-energy) to operate fields in Lake Maracaibo and the Orinoco Belt. PCEC has an agreement with NABEP for local procurement, one of more than 30 such relationships on the ground intended to help the firm reach its goal of pumping Venezuelan oil, the company said.

PCEC doesn’t disclose its investors, but it has financing from banks and trading houses, the company added.

Betancourt’s influence has grown despite repeated legal controversies inside and [outside](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2016-08-09/swiss-court-paves-way-to-share-venezuela-linked-data-with-u-s) Venezuela.

Known in Venezuela as one of the [bolichicos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bolibourgeoisie) — entrepreneurs who amassed fortunes during former President Hugo Chávez’s rule — Betancourt co-founded Derwick Associates, which won billions of dollars in emergency power equipment contracts beginning in 2009** **during years plagued by chronic blackouts.

Derwick ultimately came under scrutiny by investigators in Venezuela, [the US](https://www.icij.org/investigations/fincen-files/how-banks-helped-venezuelas-boligarchs-extract-billions/) and Spain who suspected ties to money laundering schemes involving PDVSA funds. The company and Betancourt denied the allegations.

Venezuelan authorities closed their probe without bringing charges, according to local media reports at the time. The Justice Department didn’t respond to a request on the current status of its investigation. Spanish investigators this year Spain court drops Betancourt probe after Venezuela clears PDVSA loan, but newspaper El Pais later [reported](https://elpais.com/espana/2026-06-02/la-audiencia-nacional-obliga-al-juez-pedraz-a-reabrir-la-causa-contra-el-venezolano-dueno-de-las-gafas-hawkers.html) it was reopened. Officials at Spain’s high court didn’t reply to a request for comment on the status of the case against Betancourt.

Bloomberg [reported](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2019-12-19/giuliani-keeps-the-hustle-going-even-as-trump-is-impeached) in 2019 that lawyer and former New York Mayor Rudy Giuliani helped represent Betancourt in a meeting with the Justice Department. Betancourt was never named in public court documents tied to the investigation.
