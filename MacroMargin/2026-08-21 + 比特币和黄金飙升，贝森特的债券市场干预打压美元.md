### 中文译文

## 比特币和黄金飙升，贝森特的债券市场干预打压美元

![US Treasury secretary Scott Bessent promised to ‘at least double’ long-term bond purchases © Reuters](https://images.ft.com/v3/image/raw/ftcms%3A74755619-732b-4795-8524-af00a006cf3d?source=next-article&fit=scale-down&quality=highest&width=1440&dpr=1)

美国财政部长斯科特·贝森特承诺将长期债券购买量“至少翻倍” © 路透社

由于美国财政部长斯科特·贝森特对债券市场的意外干预动摇了投资者对美元的信心，比特币预计将创下三年多来的最佳单周表现，黄金则有望创下本世纪最大单月涨幅。

周五，[比特币](https://www.ft.com/bitcoin)价格跃升近7%，本周累计上涨23%，至约77,380美元，为5月以来的最高水平。黄金周五上涨1.5%，至每盎司约4,585美元，8月累计上涨逾13%，有望创下1999年以来的最大单月涨幅。

周五的走势出现之前，贝森特周三曾表示，在10年期和30年期美国国债大幅抛售将美国借贷成本推至数十年高位之后，美国财政部将从下个月起“至少”将长期政府债务购买量翻倍。

贝森特周四表示，他试图为全球最重要的债券市场带来“均衡”。然而，许多投资者怀疑他是否有能力遏制长期收益率上升的势头。

自周三宣布以来，美元大幅下跌，原因是投资者担心通胀升温及美国40万亿美元的国家债务，纷纷涌向所谓的“贬值交易”，抢购包括比特币和黄金在内的替代财富储存方式。

“贝森的干预措施无助于解决根本性的脆弱性，”Satori Insights宏观策略师兼创始人马特·金表示。“难怪市场正重新转向贬值交易。”

美元在周三宣布后大幅下跌，自周二以来兑一篮子货币已下跌约1%。

Jefferies的欧洲首席经济学家莫希特·库马尔表示，自贝森特宣布以来，黄金和加密货币经历了“大幅上涨”。他说：“[这]支持了美元走弱的观点。”

贬值交易——即交易员买入他们认为能保护自己免受美元及其他法定货币走弱影响的资产——在美国今年早些时候发动[对伊朗的战争](https://www.ft.com/middle-east-war)后遭受重创，这场战争引发通胀上升，推高了短期美国国债收益率，从而帮助美元走强。

![Line chart of Dollar index (points) showing Bessent’s intervention continues to weigh on dollar](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3c381cc90b1481e39430fa592b6a250e/3c381cc9-0b14-81eb-ab17-ce43441fda05-5fee96b7f84b92cf3bed77550736c411.png)

一位大宗商品交易员表示：“大家都喜欢贬值交易——伊朗战争曾打乱了计划，但现在我们又回到了贬值主题。”

此人补充说，市场被“赐予了一场完美风暴”，通过降低对加息的预期，“再加上贝森特的声明”，得以重返贬值交易。

TwentyFour资产管理公司的投资组合经理约恩·沃尔什表示：“反复无常和不可预测通常会导致溢价上升，这在一定程度上解释了美元为何贬值。”

然而，ING全球市场研究主管克里斯·特纳表示，贝森特的干预应被视为旨在稳定债券市场并应对美国较长期债务遭抛售风险的努力。

他说：“一些评论人士认为，[美元走弱表明]美国政策制定丧失了公信力。但我认为……我们看到的是美国财政部长对国债市场长端表现出浓厚兴趣。”

其他加密代币如以太币、Solana和瑞波币（XRP）也出现上涨，过去一周分别上涨约26%、22%和37%。此前，自去年秋天以来，加密货币价格一直处于长期低迷状态，比特币价格在过去一年下跌了31%。

“这是势头催生势头，”交易平台IG的首席技术分析师阿克塞尔·鲁道夫表示。“在经历了数月的脆弱情绪后，反弹的速度和规模表明，当流动性改善且投资者竞相追逐行情时，加密货币的转变可以有多快。”

本周数字资产类股票也大幅上涨。数字交易所Coinbase上涨16%，稳定币公司Circle的股价攀升17%，而比特币囤积者Strategy的股票上涨21%。

分析师表示，比特币价格突然上涨引发了逼空行情，因为押注加密货币下跌的交易者纷纷平仓。本周，美国总统唐纳德·特朗普与美国主要监管机构和加密货币公司负责人的会晤也被视为对该行业的利好消息，此前数月，围绕一项至关重要的数字资产市场结构法律的辩论一直陷入僵局。

《清晰法案》是一项具有里程碑意义的立法，将监管加密货币市场，并让华尔街机构更放心地持有和交易代币，但该法案在参议院受阻，因为民主党人推动道德条款，以防止政府官员从该行业获利。

与此同时，美国证券交易委员会本周也发布了自身的加密货币监管提案，此举受到业界欢迎。

“现在很难不对（比特币）和加密货币都极度看涨，”加密资产管理公司Bitwise的首席投资官马特·霍根表示。

## Bitcoin and gold surge as Bessent’s bond market intervention weighs on dollar

US Treasury secretary Scott Bessent promised to ‘at least double’ long-term bond purchases © Reuters

Bitcoin is set for its best week in more than three years and gold is heading for its biggest monthly gain this century as US Treasury secretary Scott Bessent’s surprise intervention in the bond market rattled investors’ faith in the dollar.

The price of [bitcoin](https://www.ft.com/bitcoin) jumped nearly 7 per cent on Friday and is up 23 per cent this week to around $77,380, the cryptocurrency’s highest level since May. Gold gained 1.5 per cent on Friday to about $4,585 a troy ounce and is up more than 13 per cent in August, putting it on track for its biggest monthly gain since 1999.

Friday’s moves come after Bessent on Wednesday said the US Treasury would “at least” double its purchases of long-term government debt from next month following a sharp sell-off in 10-year and 30-year Treasury bonds that has pushed US borrowing costs to multi-decade highs.

Bessent on Thursday said he was attempting to “bring equilibrium” to the world’s most important bond market. Yet many investors doubt his ability to stem the tide of rising long-end yields.

The dollar has fallen sharply since Wednesday’s announcement as investors worried about simmering inflation and America’s $40tn national debt have piled into so-called debasement trades, snapping up alternative stores of wealth including bitcoin and gold.

“Bessent’s interventions do nothing to deal with fundamental vulnerabilities,” said Matt King, macro strategist and founder of Satori Insights. “No wonder the market is turning back to debasement trades.”

The US dollar fell sharply after Wednesday’s announcement and is now down about 1 per cent against a basket of currencies since Tuesday.

Mohit Kumar, chief European economist at Jefferies, said gold and cryptocurrencies had experienced a “massive performance” since Bessent’s announcement. “[This] supports the weaker dollar view,” he said.

The debasement trade — in which traders buy assets they expect will protect them from a weakening of the dollar and other fiat currencies — took a significant hit after the US launched [its war on Iran](https://www.ft.com/middle-east-war) earlier this year, triggering a rise in inflation that pushed up short-term US Treasury yields, helping to strengthen the dollar.

“Everyone liked debasement — the Iran war threw a spanner in the works but now we’re back to the debasement theme,” a commodities trader said.

The person added that the market had been “gifted a perfect storm” for a return to the debasement trade through a lowering of expectations for interest rate rises “combined with Bessent’s announcement”.

“Being erratic and unpredictable generally translates into higher premiums, which partly explains why the dollar is depreciating,” said Eoin Walsh, portfolio manager at TwentyFour Asset Management.

However, Chris Turner, head of global markets research at ING, said Bessent’s intervention should be seen as an attempt to stabilise the bond market and address the risk of a sell-off in longer-dated US debt.

“Some commentators are suggesting that [the weaker dollar demonstrates] a loss of credibility in US policymaking,” he said. “But I think . . . what we’re seeing is the US Treasury secretary taking a strong interest in the long end of the Treasury market.”

Other crypto tokens such as Ether, Solana and XRP have also rallied, up around 26, 22 and 37 per cent respectively over the past week. Crypto prices had previously been in a prolonged downturn since last autumn, with the price of bitcoin down 31 per cent over the past year.

“It’s momentum begetting momentum,” said Axel Rudolph, chief technical analyst at trading platform IG. “After months of fragile sentiment, the speed and scale of the rebound show just how quickly crypto can turn when liquidity improves and investors rush to chase the move.”

Shares in digital asset groups have also jumped this week. Digital exchange Coinbase is up 16 per cent, stablecoin company Circle’s shares have climbed 17 per cent and bitcoin hoarder Strategy’s stock has risen 21 per cent.

The sudden move higher in bitcoin’s price led to a short squeeze, analysts said, as traders betting against the cryptocurrency closed their positions. A meeting this week between President Donald Trump and the heads of the top US regulatory agencies and crypto companies was also seen as good news for the industry, which has faced months of stalled debates over a crucial digital assets market structure law.

The Clarity Act, a landmark piece of legislation that would oversee the crypto market and make Wall Street institutions more comfortable holding and trading tokens, has been stalled in the Senate as Democrats push for ethics provisions that would prevent government officials from profiting from the industry.

Meanwhile, the Securities and Exchange Commission also released its own proposals to regulate crypto this week in a step welcomed by the industry.

“Hard not to be extremely bullish both [on bitcoin] and crypto right now,” said Matt Hougan, chief investment officer at crypto asset manager Bitwise.
