## 美国回购承诺引发与日本比较，美元承压

作者：[瓦西里斯·卡拉马尼斯](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/ASDy9oG21gw/vassilis-kamanis) 和 [艾丽斯·格莱德希尔](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AUFfTWYF4c4/alice-gledhill)，2026年8月21日 下午8:04 GMT+8

### **彭博AI要点**

- 美国债券回购承诺正引发与日本政策的比较，日本为控制借贷成本所做的努力导致了长期货币疲软。
- 美国政府此举被视为“迄今最明确的迹象”，表明美国正效仿日本走向货币贬值，美元交易于三个月低点。
- 外汇交易员正等待美联储主席凯文·沃什在杰克逊霍尔研讨会上的演讲，如果他发表鹰派言论，可能给美元带来喘息之机，或引发美国国债、美元、黄金和股票的重大重新定价。
美国[债券回购](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-20/bessent-says-buybacks-could-be-more-than-4-billion-per-issue)承诺本周令市场[剧烈波动](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-21/week-of-whiplash-in-treasuries-is-closing-with-traders-on-pause)，正引发与日本政策的比较，日本为控制借贷成本所做的努力导致了长期货币疲软。

美元交易于三个月低点，并有望创下本月最差周度表现，此前美国财政部在周中意外宣布，将提前把可回购的较长期债券数量翻倍。

布鲁金斯学会高级研究员罗宾·布鲁克斯表示，美国政府的此举是“迄今最明确的迹象”，表明美国正效仿日本走向[货币贬值](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-01-30/dollar-fears-are-flaring-as-trump-rekindles-debasement-trade)。他说，政府“玩火”。

在最初因消息跳涨后，美国国债回吐涨幅。黄金和其他贵金属正在反弹。

据标准银行（Standard Bank）十国集团（G10）策略主管史蒂文·巴罗（Steven Barrow）称，通过回购来遏制债券收益率只会给美元带来更大压力，而未能解决推动收益率走高的根本预算赤字。

### [美元料将为美债压低收益率的努力付出代价](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-20/dollar-risks-becoming-biggest-loser-from-bessent-s-bond-buying)

![图片](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3c381cc90b1481b59db6cf7b977d614a/3c381cc9-0b14-81bc-9113-c0181e854c90-f0fdce97439468b4769f22b020eba5ff.png)

诚然，彭博美元指数今年仅下跌1%，与日本的比较也有其局限性。所谓的“安倍经济学”——日本前首相安倍晋三推行的经济政策——依赖激进的货币政策宽松来刺激增长。

其中包括一项大规模量化宽松计划，该计划实际上通过印钞购买债券并压低收益率，从而强力削弱日元。

美国国债回购无法与那种货币刺激相提并论，而且美国并未选择接受本币贬值作为压低收益率的代价。

据巴罗称，上月华盛顿为支撑日元进行干预即证明了这一点。由于动用的是欧元而非美元，美元得以受到保护。与此同时，由于日本无需出售美国国债来获取提振日元所需的美元，美国收益率也得以免受影响。

“问题在于，美国不可能鱼与熊掌兼得，”巴罗表示。

### **杰克逊霍尔**

外汇交易员正在等待美联储主席凯文·沃什在月底杰克逊霍尔研讨会上的演讲。

如果鹰派言论抵制对降息的预期，美元可能获得喘息之机。

### 杰克逊霍尔可能引发又一轮美元抛售：交易员谈

Capital.com高级市场分析师丹妮拉·哈索恩写道：“任何关于他如何看待持续通胀、近期长期收益率上升或美联储资产负债表未来规模和作用的迹象，都可能引发国债、美元、黄金和股票的显著重新定价。”

但如果美联储抵制加息压力，货币贬值论调可能获得支持。期权情绪显示，自2月以来对美元的看跌程度最为严重，即使现货价格回落，也表明交易员正在为未来更多损失布局。

布鲁金斯学会的布鲁克斯表示：“一旦货币进入贬值螺旋，几乎不可能稳定该货币。”

## US Buyback Pledge Draws Japan Comparisons and Pressures Dollar

By [Vassilis Karamanis](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/ASDy9oG21gw/vassilis-karamanis) and [Alice Gledhill](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AUFfTWYF4c4/alice-gledhill)August 21, 2026 at 8:04 PM GMT+8

### **Takeaways ** by Bloomberg AI

- The US bond buyback pledge is prompting comparisons with policies in Japan, where efforts to contain borrowing costs resulted in prolonged currency weakness.
- The US government's move is seen as the "clearest sign yet" that the US is following Japan toward debasing its currency, with the dollar trading at a three-month low.
- Currency traders are awaiting Federal Reserve Chairman Kevin Warsh's speech at the Jackson Hole symposium, which could give the dollar a reprieve if he makes hawkish comments, or trigger a meaningful repricing across Treasuries, the dollar, gold and equities.
The US [bond buyback](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-20/bessent-says-buybacks-could-be-more-than-4-billion-per-issue) pledge that [whipsawed](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-21/week-of-whiplash-in-treasuries-is-closing-with-traders-on-pause) markets this week is prompting comparisons with policies in Japan, where efforts to contain borrowing costs resulted in prolonged currency weakness.

The dollar is trading at a three-month low and is on track for its worst week this month after the Treasury’s surprise midweek announcement that it would double the amount of longer-dated bonds it can repurchase ahead of schedule.

The US government’s move is the “clearest sign yet” that the US is following Japan toward [debasing its currency](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-01-30/dollar-fears-are-flaring-as-trump-rekindles-debasement-trade), said Robin Brooks, a senior fellow at the Brookings Institution. The administration is “playing with fire,” he said.

After initially jumping on the news, Treasuries have unwound their advance. Gold and other precious metals are rallying.

According to Steven Barrow, Standard Bank’s head of G10 strategy, containing bond yields with buybacks will only pile pressure on the greenback, while failing to tackle the underlying budget deficit that’s pushing them higher.

### [Dollar Seen Paying the Price for Treasury Bid to Tame Yields](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-20/dollar-risks-becoming-biggest-loser-from-bessent-s-bond-buying)

To be sure, the Bloomberg dollar index is only down 1% this year and the Japan comparison has its limits. So-called Abenomics — the economic policy pursued by former Japanese Prime Minister Shinzo Abe — relied on aggressive monetary policy loosening to spur growth.

That included a massive quantitative easing program that effectively printed yen to buy bonds and keep yields low — forcefully weakening the Japanese currency.

The Treasury buybacks can’t be compared to that kind of monetary stimulus, and the US hasn’t chosen to accept a falling currency as the cost of keeping yields pinned.

According to Barrow, the proof of that was in Washington’s intervention to support the yen last month. By using euros, rather than dollars, the greenback was protected. Meanwhile, US yields were shielded because Japan didn’t have to sell Treasuries to get the dollars needed to buoy the yen.

“The problem is that the US can’t have its cake and eat it,” Barrow said.

### **Jackson Hole**

Currency traders are awaiting Federal Reserve Chairman Kevin Warsh’s speech at the Jackson Hole symposium at the end of the month.

Hawkish comments that push back against expectations for lower interest rates, could give the dollar a reprieve.

### Jackson Hole Can Trigger Another Dollar Selloff: Trader Talk

“Any indication of how he views persistent inflation, the recent rise in long-term yields or the future size and role of the Fed’s balance sheet could trigger a meaningful repricing across Treasuries, the dollar, gold and equities,” wrote Daniela Hathorn, a senior market analyst at Capital.com.

But if the Fed resists pressure for higher rates, the debasement narrative could gain traction. Options sentiment is the most bearish on the dollar since February, even as spot prices retreat, suggesting that traders are positioning for more losses ahead.

“It can be next to impossible to stabilize a currency once it enters a devaluation spiral,” Brooks at the Brookings Institution said.
