### 中文译文

## 汇丰乘中国与巴西贸易联系激增之势

巴西与中国创纪录的贸易量正成为汇丰控股有限公司的一大福音——就在十年前，该行出售了其庞大的巴西业务，随后回归专注于跨境业务，如今这一领域已成为其优势所在。

就在三年前，巴西公司在香港办理的贸易融资对汇丰而言在收入上几乎微不足道。去年，在通过汇丰在香港办理贸易融资交易的50个国家中，巴西排名第10。而根据该行巴西首席执行官Alexandre Guião的说法，现在巴西已跃居第四位。

Guião在该英国银行圣保罗办公室接受采访时表示：“巴西的大宗商品（包括硬大宗商品和软大宗商品）出口以及来自亚洲（主要是中国）的进口都增长了很多。”他说：“我们希望确保促进这些地区企业之间的联系。”

Guião表示，来自巴西客户的收入去年增长了20%，其中包括在离岸预订的交易；他补充说，今年上半年这一数字又增长了37%。

![Alexandre Guião Source: HSBC](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3c181cc90b14813b9405e562bd2be6aa/3c181cc9-0b14-81f4-a775-fe03382d97c4-0ec0d1253b39988b8db35b610fad22c3.png)

原因显而易见：据巴西政府称，今年截至7月，巴西对华出口增长了20%，达到创纪录的690亿美元。与此同时，受电动汽车浪潮推动，进口增长了8%，达到历史新高的450亿美元。据巴西-中国企业家委员会称，去年巴西接收的中国投资超过其他任何国家。

作为中国最大的外资银行，汇丰尤其处于有利位置，能够从这些更紧密的关系中获益。

10年前的情况有所不同。汇丰于2016年将其当地子公司出售给巴西布拉德斯科银行，在竞业禁止期结束后，于两年半后重返巴西，如今其业务已成为一家完整的公司和投资银行。它为该国约720家全球跨国公司提供服务，并服务约80家巴西跨国公司。

在全球范围内，汇丰正在出售业务、收缩并改革运营，这是一项重大重组的一部分，其中包括削减数十亿美元开支。其理念是简化公司，将资本集中在汇丰具有结构性优势的领域，更加积极地进军财富管理和与亚洲相关的资金流动领域，并利用人工智能降低成本和改善客户服务。

在拉丁美洲，这意味着于2025年将其乌拉圭业务以1.75亿美元出售给[Banco BTG Pactual SA](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-07-28/btg-pactual-buys-hsbc-s-uruguay-unit-in-international-expansion)，并于2024年以5.5亿美元将阿根廷子公司剥离给[Grupo Financiero Galicia](https://www.hsbc.com/-/files/hsbc/investors/results-and-announcements/stock-exchange-announcements/2024/april/sea-240409-hsbc-agrees-to-sell-its-business-in-argentina-to-grupo-financiero-galicia.pdf?download=1)。

在墨西哥，汇丰保留了一家包括零售业务在内的全功能银行，据该行墨西哥首席执行官兼总裁、拉丁美洲业务负责人Jorge Arce称，该行决定专注于更富裕的客户，并在18个月内关闭了约200家分行。

Arce表示，汇丰在智利也设有银行，智利是“亚洲与拉丁美洲走廊非常重要的国家”，而在哥伦比亚，汇丰设有一家同时覆盖秘鲁的办事处。

Arce表示：“如果你看看在全球运营的跨国公司，巴西和墨西哥会在其市场的前五或前十之列。”

![Jorge Arce Source: HSBC](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3c181cc90b14813b9405e562bd2be6aa/3c181cc9-0b14-8179-bd61-c394fae9e31f-44a5cf72c114f2b6581a250ed7161496.png)

汇丰最近的交易中，有一笔15亿美元的融资方案，由汇丰与国际金融公司（International Finance Corp.）共同牵头，为Mubadala Capital的可再生能源部门Acelen Renewables在巴西巴伊亚州建设一座绿地生物精炼厂提供资金。

但也遇到过挫折。在巴西，糖和乙醇生产商Raizen、超市连锁GPA（即Cia. Brasileira de Distribuicao）等公司的大规模债务重组拖累了汇丰的利润。但Guião表示，收入增长远远抵消了这些影响。

他表示，在这些债务重组之后，汇丰暂停了业务以审查其投资组合，并决定在提供信贷方面变得更加保守。

Arce称这些问题“是业务的一部分”，并补充说，“归根结底，我认为这些公司的债务重组对贷款方来说将是积极的。”

巴西的利率居高不下，“让人感到谨慎”，阿尔塞表示，但他补充说，巴西企业“非常有韧性”。巴西的基准利率一年多来一直保持在14%或以上。

吉昂表示，高昂的融资成本和巴西本地市场一些公司面临的“信贷紧缩”也为汇丰创造了机会，该行增加了在巴西境外记账的双边贷款规模。他说，面向巴西客户的现金管理、支付和外汇业务也在扩大。

两位高管均表示，计划继续在巴西投资，尽管10月的总统选举带来了不确定性。

阿尔塞表示：“无论谁获胜，巴西都将继续成为对我们客户极具吸引力的市场，我们将在这里为他们服务。”

## HSBC Rides Wave of China’s Surging Trade Links With Brazil

Brazil’s record trading volume with China is proving to be a boon for [HSBC Holdings Plc](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/HSBA%20LN%20Equity), a decade after the bank sold its sprawling Brazilian operation and then returned to focus on the cross-border business that has become a sweet spot.

Trade-finance booked in Hong Kong for Brazilian companies was almost immaterial for HSBC from a revenue standpoint just three years ago. Last year it ranked 10th among firms from the 50 nations that book trade-finance deals in Hong Kong using HSBC. Now it’s the fourth-biggest, according to Alexandre Guião, the bank’s chief executive officer for Brazil.

“Brazil’s exports of hard and soft commodities, as well as imports from Asia, mainly China, have been growing a lot,” Guião said in an interview at the UK bank’s Sao Paulo offices. “We want to make sure that we facilitate those connections” between companies from those regions, he said.

Revenue from Brazilian customers surged 20% last year, including deals booked offshore, Guião said, adding that the figure soared an additional 37% in the first half of this year.

The reason is clear: Brazil’s exports to China jumped 20% this year through July, to a record $69 billion, according to the Brazilian government. Meanwhile, imports increased by 8%, to an all-time high of $45 billion, fueled by a wave of electrified vehicles. Brazil received more investments from China than any other country last year, according to the Brazil-China Business Council.

As the biggest foreign bank in China, HSBC is especially well-positioned to gain from those closer relations.

The situation was different 10 years ago. HSBC sold its local subsidiary to Banco Bradesco SA in 2016, and then returned 2 1/2 years later, after a noncompete period, with an operation that’s now a complete corporate and investment bank. It serves about 720 global multinationals in the nation and about 80 Brazilian multinationals.

Globally, HSBC is selling businesses and shrinking and revamping its operations, part of a major restructuring that includes cutting billions in expenses. The idea is to simplify the company, concentrate capital where HSBC has structural advantages, push harder into wealth and Asia-linked flows, and use AI to cut costs and improve customer service.

In Latin America, that meant selling its Uruguay unit in 2025 to [Banco BTG Pactual SA](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-07-28/btg-pactual-buys-hsbc-s-uruguay-unit-in-international-expansion) for $175 million and divesting the Argentina subsidiary in 2024 to [Grupo Financiero Galicia](https://www.hsbc.com/-/files/hsbc/investors/results-and-announcements/stock-exchange-announcements/2024/april/sea-240409-hsbc-agrees-to-sell-its-business-in-argentina-to-grupo-financiero-galicia.pdf?download=1) for $550 million.

In Mexico, where HSBC kept a full bank including a retail business, the lender decided to focus on wealthier clients and closed about 200 branches in 18 months, according to Jorge Arce, the bank’s CEO and president for Mexico and head of Latin American operations.

HSBC also has a bank in Chile, “a very important nation for the Asia corridor with Latin America,” Arce said, while in Colombia it has an office that also covers Peru.

“If you look at multinationals that operate around the world, Brazil and Mexico would be in the top five or top 10 in their markets,” Arce said.

Among HSBC’s recent deals was a $1.5 billion financing package, which it lead along with International Finance Corp., for Mubadala Capital’s renewable energy arm, Acelen Renewables, to build a greenfield biorefinery in Bahia, Brazil.

But there have been hiccups. Big debt restructurings in Brazil from companies such as sugar and ethanol producer Raizen and supermarket chain GPA, known as Cia. Brasileira de Distribuicao, weighed on HSBC’s profit. But Guião said that was more than outweighed by the revenue growth.

After those debt restructurings, HSBC paused to review its portfolio and decided to become more conservative in offering credit, he said.

Arce called those issues “part of the business,” adding that “at the end of the day, I think the debt workouts from those companies are going to be positive for lenders.”

Brazil’s stubbornly high interest rates “make you feel cautious,” Arce said, but he added that Brazilian companies “are very resilient.” Brazil’s benchmark interest rate has been at or above 14% for more than a year.

The high cost of funding and “a credit crunch” for some companies in Brazil’s local market also created opportunities for HSBC, which increased the volume of bilateral loans booked outside Brazil, Guião said. Cash management, payments and foreign-exchange businesses for Brazilian clients are also expanding, he said.

The plan is to keep investing in Brazil, despite the uncertainties raised by October’s presidential elections, both executives said.

“No matter who wins, Brazil will continue to be a very attractive market for our clients, and we are going to be here serving them,” Arce said.
