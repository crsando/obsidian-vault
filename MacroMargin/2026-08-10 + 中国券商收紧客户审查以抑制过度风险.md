### 中文译文

## 中国券商收紧客户审查以抑制过度风险

在市场大幅回调后，监管机构采取措施遏制过度冒险行为，中国券商正在加强对新账户的审查，并加大对融资融券和期权交易的审核力度。

据知情人士透露，包括[中信证券](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/600030%20CH%20Equity)和[东方财富](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/300059%20CH%20Equity)在内的多家公司近期提高了对寻求杠杆或衍生品交易的客户的合规要求。这些知情人士因讨论私人事务而要求匿名。上述两家券商未回应置评请求。

知情人士称，这些措施包括在批准融资融券或期权账户前，对财务状况、交易经验和风险承受能力进行更严格的检查。在多家券商，过去六个月内新开账户或频繁收到追加保证金通知的投资者将被限制进一步借款。

此次收紧凸显出北京对股市杠杆日益增长的警惕，散户严重亏损可能引发更广泛的金融和社会不稳定。当局正关注高杠杆交易，此类交易可能触发强制平仓并加速市场下跌。

“某些领域杠杆的过度集中增加了市场不稳定的风险，”旭方投资管理有限公司合伙人王晨表示，“尽管这些风险看似有所缓解，但可能尚未完全化解，并将在未来一段时间内持续成为监管重点。”

在今年股市上涨期间，散户投资者涌入中国股市，严重依赖借入资金追涨。但据券商客户经理称，随着市场波动加剧，越来越多的散户在7月下旬被迫平仓。

根据彭博汇编的数据，中国两融余额在6月底突破3万亿元人民币后，7月底降至2.6万亿元人民币（合3620亿美元）。今年上半年，投资者新开融资融券账户960,660个，同比增长60%。仅6月份就新开179,021个账户，同比增长77%，凸显散户对杠杆交易需求的激增。

交易热潮提振了整个券商行业的盈利。[中金公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/3908%20HK%20Equity)估计，42家上市证券公司上半年合计实现净利润1425亿元人民币，同比增长50%。中信证券上半年净利润飙升69.6%，创历史新高，而国泰君安证券预计利润增长27%至30%，经常性收益创上半年峰值。

6月下旬，中国股市的日交易量一度接近4万亿元人民币，但最近几周已回落至3万亿元以下。

政策制定者正努力在培育长期资本市场发展的同时，剔除投机行为。7月底召开的政治局会议上，高层领导人承诺提振投资者信心并增强市场韧性。

基准沪深300指数在6月触及2021年末以来的最高水平后出现回落，受到区域市场动荡和人工智能行业前景变化的影响。科技股重仓指数在7月的抛售中首当其冲，创业板指数和科创50指数均下跌超过20%。

## China Brokers Tighten Client Scrutiny to Curb Excessive Risk

Chinese brokerages are tightening scrutiny on new accounts and stepping up reviews for margin financing, securities lending, and options trading as regulators move to curb overly risky bets following a sharp market pullback.

Several firms including [Citic Securities Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/600030%20CH%20Equity) and [East Money Information Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/300059%20CH%20Equity) have recently raised compliance requirements for clients seeking leverage or derivatives trading, according to people familiar with the matter, who asked not to be identified discussing private matter. The two brokers didn’t respond to requests for comment.

The measures include more stringent checks on finances, trading experience and risk tolerance before approving access to margin financing or options accounts, the people said. At several brokerages, investors who opened fresh accounts in the past six months or have received frequent margin calls will be restricted from further borrowing.

The clampdown underscores Beijing’s growing caution over equity market leverage, where severe retail losses risk spilling over into broader financial and social instability. Authorities are focusing on highly leveraged trading, which can trigger forced liquidations and accelerate market declines.

“Excessive concentrations of leverage in certain segments has increased the risk of market instability,” said Wang Chen, a partner at [XuFunds Investment Management Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/US%20Equity) “While those risks appear to have eased, they have likely not been fully unwound and will remain a regulatory focus in the coming period.”

Retail investors flooded into Chinese stocks during this year’s rally, relying heavily on borrowed money to chase gains. But as market volatility spiked, an increasing number of mom-and-pop traders were forced out of their positions in late July, according to brokerage account managers.

China’s total margin trading balance dropped to 2.6 trillion yuan ($362 billion) at the end of July after topping 3 trillion yuan in late June, according to data compiled by Bloomberg. Investors opened 960,660 new margin trading accounts in the first half of the year — a 60% jump from a year earlier. June alone saw 179,021 new accounts opened, up 77% year-on-year, highlighting the surge in retail demand for leveraged bets.

The trading boom boosted earnings across the brokerage industry. [China International Capital Corp.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/3908%20HK%20Equity) estimates that 42 listed securities firms posted a combined 142.5 billion yuan in net income in the first half, up 50% from a year earlier. Citic Securities Co. reported a 69.6% surge in first-half net income to a record high, while Guotai Junan Securities Co. forecast profit growth of 27% to 30%, with recurring earnings reaching a new first-half peak.

Daily trading volumes in Chinese equities approached 4 trillion yuan in late June before falling below 3 trillion yuan in recent weeks.

Policymakers are attempting to foster long-term capital market development while weeding out speculative behavior. At a Politburo meeting in late July, top leaders pledged to boost investor confidence and reinforce market resilience.

The benchmark CSI 300 Index retreated after touching its highest level since late 2021 in June, rattled by regional market turbulence and shifting outlooks for the artificial intelligence sector. Tech-heavy indexes took the brunt of the selloff in July, with both the ChiNext Index and the STAR 50 Index tumbling more than 20%.
