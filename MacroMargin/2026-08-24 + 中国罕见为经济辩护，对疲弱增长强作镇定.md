### 中文译文

## 中国罕见为经济辩护，对疲弱增长强作镇定

中国试图将2026年不均衡的经济表现描绘成一个韧性与技术创新的故事，这表明北京方面在为其未来发展奠定基础之际，并不因经济放缓加剧而感到不安。

执政党中国共产党机关报《人民日报》刊登的一系列不同寻常的每日评论文章提出，应该超越短期经济波动来看待问题，尤其是在中国应对海内外逆境之际。这些文章从周六开始以笔名“钟才文”发表，外界普遍认为该笔名由中央财经委员会办公室使用——这是一个由副总理何立峰领导的强大的经济决策机构。

周一发表的三篇社论中的[最新一篇](https://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202608/24/content_30176629.html)将2026年上半年4.7%的增速描述为“高质量”，并符合当前经济条件，如劳动力和资本的可用性。它称这些成果“来之不易、值得肯定”，与全年4.5%至5%的官方目标一致。

文章称，“这是一个现实的增长速度，符合经济的潜在增长率。在这一增长的背后，是中国经济增长动力向新动能转换以及经济结构的优化。”

![图片](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3c681cc90b14815fa71bfb3513e40779/3c681cc9-0b14-815d-9cf9-dba18a7e292f-62d1059702e6c7c14dd21c3817faf4ea.png)

北京的这一系列表态，很可能是官员们近期在经济脆弱之际展现信心的努力的一部分，因为中国在面临海外保护主义风险不断上升的同时，国内消费疲软因住房危机而加剧。

这一时机很重要，因为[7月份数据](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-17/china-s-economy-extends-slowdown-by-weakening-across-the-board)显示，在令人失望的第二季度之后，经济增速进一步滑落至年度目标下方。一项新增贷款指标出现[创纪录的缩减](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-14/china-s-credit-growth-exceeds-forecasts-despite-rare-loan-slump)，国内汽车购买量急剧下降，整体零售销售徘徊在近几十年来除新冠疫情封锁外的最差水平附近。

但这些评论也凸显出，由于北京将技术突破和降低金融风险方面的进展置于优先地位，它愿意牺牲一些短期经济收益。这种权衡对中国领导层来说似乎很有吸引力，因为他们认为这对确保国家的长期增长和安全至关重要。

周六的[评论](https://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202608/22/content_30176469.html)认为，官员们不应只关注增长率，或“陷入单个季度或月度指标的高低起伏”，因为经济正进入向新动能转型的关键时期。

次日发表的一篇[文章](https://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202608/23/content_30176537.html)为中国海外出口的强劲势头辩护，驳斥关于另一波“中国冲击”的警告等说法，称其为“虚假”。文章认为，中国的产品——如今年夏天销往欧洲的[空调](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-18/china-s-gree-haier-hisense-midea-tcl-see-ac-sales-surge-in-europe-heat-waves)——惠及全球消费者，而且中国市场为其他国家的企业提供机遇，并以对许多非洲国家实行零关税为例。

不过，周一这篇文章罕见地承认了经济疲软，称强劲供给与国内需求疲软之间的错配依然严重，投资下行压力“相当大”。

与此同时，文章暗示当局愿意容忍[资本支出放缓](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-21/china-scales-back-austerity-with-smaller-drop-in-budget-spending)，以换取更高质量、更高效的投资。

![图片](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3c681cc90b14815fa71bfb3513e40779/3c681cc9-0b14-818a-b8cf-e3d469022370-52ce731703d6914fb97e5b796b3a6b4a.png)

文章说，增长质量正在改善，并列举了从[科技领军企业](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-14/z-ai-aims-to-catch-anthropic-openai-in-coding-with-new-ai-model)崛起到低碳能源转型加速等一系列进展。文章还指出服务消费增加，以及中国在化解[地方债务](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-09/china-gets-chance-at-fiscal-rethink-after-debt-cleanup-milestone)、房地产和小型金融机构风险方面取得的进展。

文章还强调，北京有意避免采用“某些经济体”为支撑增长而采取的“高债务、高赤字”做法。文章补充说，尽管如此，政府仍有“充足”空间加大稳增长措施，并重申了最高领导人上个月作出的承诺，即推出“务实有效”的[额外措施](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-21/china-plans-new-fiscal-support-policies-this-year-amid-slowdown)，确保实现今年目标。

这些文章未言明的是，中国的债务负担已成为一个压力来源。彭博经济研究预测，到2035年底，中央和地方政府债务合计将升至国内生产总值的101%，高于去年底的69%。

尽管被这篇评论誉为经济亮点的服务零售额今年前七个月增长5%，但累计增速连续第三个月放缓，为2025年第一季度以来最慢。商品消费支出则更加低迷。

咨询公司Trivium China市场研究主管Dinny McMahon在分析中国官方媒体近期经济报道（包括周六这篇评论）时表示，国内外受众都应听从这些指引。

“北京是在打持久战，”他在周六的报告中写道。“围绕国内消费反弹做规划的公司需要重新设定预期。在第三市场与中国企业竞争的出口商应预期竞争将加剧。而押注中国股市因刺激措施而转机的投资者应三思。”

### English Original

## China Puts Brave Face on Weak Growth in Rare Defense of Economy

China has sought to frame its uneven economic performance in 2026 as a story of resilience and technological innovation, signaling Beijing is unfazed by a deepening slowdown as it lays the groundwork for development into the future.

An unusual series of daily commentaries carried by the People’s Daily, the ruling Communist Party’s official newspaper, made a case for looking past the economic volatility over the short term, especially as China copes with adversity overseas and at home. The articles appeared starting on Saturday under a pen name, Zhong Caiwen, widely believed to be used by the Office of the Central Commission for Financial and Economic Affairs — a powerful economic policymaking body headed by Vice Premier He Lifeng.

[The latest](https://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202608/24/content_30176629.html) of the three editorials on Monday cast the 4.7% growth pace in the first half of 2026 as “high quality” and in line with the economy’s current conditions, such as the availability of labor and capital. It described the results as “hard-won and commendable,” consistent with the official target of 4.5%-5% for the full year.

“It is a realistic pace of growth and aligns with the economy’s potential growth rate,” it said. “Behind this growth lies the transformation of China’s economic drivers towards new ones and the optimization of its economic structure.”

The messaging by Beijing is likely part of a recent effort by officials to project confidence at a moment of economic vulnerability, as China faces the rising risk of protectionism abroad against the backdrop of a domestic consumer malaise made worse by the housing crisis.

The timing is important, after [July data](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-17/china-s-economy-extends-slowdown-by-weakening-across-the-board) suggested growth slipped further below the annual target following a disappointing second quarter. A measure of new loans contracted by [a fresh record](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-14/china-s-credit-growth-exceeds-forecasts-despite-rare-loan-slump) and domestic car purchases are plunging, with broader retail sales hovering around their worst pace in recent decades outside of the Covid lockdowns.

But the commentaries also underscore Beijing’s willingness to sacrifice some short-term economic gains because of the priority it places on technological breakthroughs and making progress in reducing financial risks. The trade-off appears appealing for China’s leadership because of what it sees as crucial for ensuring the country’s long-term growth and security.

Saturday’s [commentary](https://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202608/22/content_30176469.html) argued officials shouldn’t just focus on the rate of growth or “get bogged down in the highs and lows of individual quarterly or monthly indicators,” as the economy enters a critical period of transitioning to new drivers.

And a [piece](https://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202608/23/content_30176537.html) published the following day defended the strength of China’s overseas shipments, dismissing narratives such as warnings of another “China shock” as “false.” It argued China’s products — like [air conditioners](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-18/china-s-gree-haier-hisense-midea-tcl-see-ac-sales-surge-in-europe-heat-waves) to Europe this past summer — benefit global consumers, and that its domestic market provides opportunities for companies from other nations, citing zero tariffs for many African countries.

Still, in a rare acknowledgement of weakness in the economy, the article on Monday said the mismatch between strong supply and poor domestic demand remains severe and the downward pressure on investment is “rather enormous.”

At the same time, it indicated the authorities are willing to tolerate [slower capital spending](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-21/china-scales-back-austerity-with-smaller-drop-in-budget-spending) in favor of higher-quality, more efficient investment.

The quality of growth is improving, it said, citing developments ranging from the emergence of [technology champions](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-14/z-ai-aims-to-catch-anthropic-openai-in-coding-with-new-ai-model) to an acceleration in the low-carbon energy transition. It also pointed at increases in services consumption and the headway made in China’s campaigns to reduce risks in [local debt](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-09/china-gets-chance-at-fiscal-rethink-after-debt-cleanup-milestone), real estate and at small financial institutions.

It also emphasized an intention by Beijing to avoid the “high debt, high deficit” approach adopted by “some economies” to prop up growth. Still, the government has “ample” room to strengthen pro-growth measures, it added, repeating a pledge made by top leaders last month to launch “pragmatic and effective” [additional measures](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-21/china-plans-new-fiscal-support-policies-this-year-amid-slowdown) to ensure reaching this year’s target.

Left unsaid in the articles is that China’s indebtedness is already a source of distress. Bloomberg Economics predicts the combined debt of central and local governments will climb to 101% of gross domestic product by end-2035, up from 69% at the end of last year.

While retail sales of services — which the commentary touted as a bright spot in the economy — rose 5% in the first seven months of the year, their cumulative growth slowed for a third straight month to the weakest pace since the first quarter of 2025. Consumer spending on goods has been even more sluggish.

Dinny McMahon, head of markets research at consultancy Trivium China, said in an analysis of recent publications by state-run Chinese media on the economy, including the Saturday commentary, that audiences both abroad and at home should heed the guidance.

“Beijing is playing the long game,” he wrote in a Saturday note. “Companies planning around a domestic consumption rebound need to reset their expectations. Exporters competing with Chinese firms in third markets should expect competition to sharpen. And investors betting on a stimulus-driven turn in Chinese equities should think again.”
