### 中文译文

## 中国计划加快新能源回收和废弃物规则制定：央视

中国正在加快制定风能和太阳能设备回收与再利用的规则，目标是在2030年前建立该行业废弃物的闭环系统，中国中央电视台[报道](https://content-static.cctvnews.cctv.com/snow-book/index.html?item_id=3212295774384630649&toc_style_id=feeds_default&track_id=63B3CF90-AA68-42F8-9912-62A29132DEDB_808538193762&share_to=copy_url)周日称。

国家发展和改革委员会官员表示，该体系将覆盖废弃物回收和利用的整个供应链，因为中国在2026-2030年期间推出支持循环经济发展的措施，据央视报道。

政府旨在解决该行业产生的废弃物回收方面的不足，包括电动汽车电池、风能和光伏设备产生的废弃物，央视称。

中国将严格执行电动汽车与电池一起报废的要求，并打击非法回收和拆解活动，央视报道。另外，当局将支持专注于资源回收的国家重点实验室，并提升对废旧机器、车辆和电子产品的拆解能力。

### English Original

## China Plans to Speed Up New Energy Recycling, Waste Rules: CCTV

China is accelerating the establishment of rules governing the recycling and reuse of wind and solar equipment and aims to set up a closed-loop system for waste from the industry before 2030, China Central Television [reported](https://content-static.cctvnews.cctv.com/snow-book/index.html?item_id=3212295774384630649&toc_style_id=feeds_default&track_id=63B3CF90-AA68-42F8-9912-62A29132DEDB_808538193762&share_to=copy_url) on Sunday.

National Development and Reform Commission officials said the system would cover the full supply chain of waste recovery and utilization as China rolls out measures to support circular-economy development during the 2026-2030 period, according to CCTV.

The government aims to address shortcomings in recycling waste generated by the industry, including from electric vehicle batteries, wind energy and photovoltaic equipment, it said.

China will strictly implement requirements for scrapping EVs together with their batteries and crack down on illegal recycling and dismantling activities, CCTV reported. Separately, authorities will support national key laboratories focused on resource recycling and will upgrade dismantling capacity for used machinery, vehicles and electronic products.
