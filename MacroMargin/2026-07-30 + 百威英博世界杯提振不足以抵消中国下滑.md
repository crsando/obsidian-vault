## 百威英博世界杯提振不足以抵消中国下滑

作者：[Jennifer Creery](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AWE3qNMLx8Q/jennifer-creery)
2026年7月30日 下午1:09 GMT+8
*更新于2026年7月30日 下午3:30 GMT+8*

[百威英博](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/ABI%20BB%20Equity)对足球世界杯的赞助提振了美国市场Michelob Ultra和其他墨西哥啤酒的需求，但这不足以抵消包括中国在内的地区销量疲软。

这家全球最大啤酒商周四发布的[声明](https://cdn.builder.io/o/assets%2F2e5c7fb020194c1a8ee80f743d0b923e%2Ffe05113a2095432faa6ed5871a72918b?alt=media&amp%3Btoken=c87…[redacted 82 chars])称，第二季度有机销量增长0.9%，略低于分析师预期的0.96%。包括墨西哥和加勒比地区的中美洲市场增长4.7%，而包括中国在内的亚太市场则下滑4.7%。

百威英博股价早盘一度下跌2.6%，随后略有收窄。截至周三收盘，该股今年累计上涨近35%。

在多个关键市场饮酒放缓之际，百威英博正加大对Corona、Stella Artois和Michelob Ultra等“超级品牌”的投资，以推动增长。近年来，生活成本压力以及对饮酒负面健康影响的担忧，导致欧洲和美国的啤酒消费量下降。

该啤酒商表示，其投资努力正在取得回报，例如Corona在本土市场以外的收入增长了17%。

总部位于比利时的百威英博也正试图通过罐装鸡尾酒等产品实现业务多元化，超越啤酒。今年早些时候，它完成了对即饮品牌BeatBox多数股权的收购。旗下拥有健力士的竞争对手[帝亚吉欧](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/DGE%20LN%20Equity)也正专注于即饮鸡尾酒领域，尤其是在美国。

中国仍是百威英博的薄弱环节，受恶劣天气以及消费者缩减开支导致酒吧和餐厅销售持续低迷影响，该季度销量下降9.7%。

该公司面临的关键问题包括中国市场的表现能否复苏，以及拉丁美洲的利润率能否维持，巴克莱(Barclays)分析师劳伦斯·怀亚特(Laurence Whyatt)等在给客户的一份报告中写道。

## AB InBev World Cup Boost Not Enough to Offset China Decline

By [Jennifer Creery](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AWE3qNMLx8Q/jennifer-creery)
July 30, 2026 at 1:09 PM GMT+8
*Updated on July 30, 2026 at 3:30 PM GMT+8*

[Anheuser-Busch InBev](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/ABI%20BB%20Equity)’s sponsorship of the soccer World Cup boosted demand for Michelob Ultra in the US and other beers in Mexico but it was not enough to offset weak volumes in the region that includes China.

The world’s largest brewer reported a 0.9% rise in organic volumes in the second quarter, which was slightly below analysts’ estimates of 0.96%, according to a [statement](https://cdn.builder.io/o/assets%2F2e5c7fb020194c1a8ee80f743d0b923e%2Ffe05113a2095432faa6ed5871a72918b?alt=media&amp%3Btoken=c87…[redacted 82 chars]) Thursday. Middle Americas, which includes Mexico and the Caribbean, recorded growth of 4.7%, while Asia Pacific, including China, fell 4.7%.

Shares of AB InBev fell as much as 2.6% in early trading before paring back slightly. The stock had risen nearly 35% this year through Wednesday’s close.

AB InBev is pushing investment into its “megabrands” like Corona, Stella Artois and Michelob Ultra to help drive growth at a time when there is a slowdown in drinking in several key markets. Cost-of-living pressures, along with concerns over the negative health impact of consuming alcohol, have led to beer consumption declining in Europe and the US in recent years.

The brewer said its investment efforts are paying off with Corona, for example, growing revenue 17% outside of its home market.

Belgium-based AB InBev is also trying to diversify beyond beer with products such as canned cocktails. Earlier this year, it completed the acquisition of a majority stake in ready-to-drink brand BeatBox. Rival [Diageo Plc](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/DGE%20LN%20Equity), which owns Guinness, is also focusing on the ready-to-drink cocktails segment, particularly in the US.

China remained a weak spot for AB InBev with volumes dropping 9.7% in the quarter, impacted by poor weather and persistent weak sales in bars and restaurants as the country’s consumers pull back.

Key questions for the company include whether China’s performance can recover and if margins in Latin America can be sustained, Barclays analysts including Laurence Whyatt wrote in a note to clients.
