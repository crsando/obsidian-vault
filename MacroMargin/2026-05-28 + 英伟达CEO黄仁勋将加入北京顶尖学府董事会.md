### 中文译文

## 英伟达CEO黄仁勋将加入北京顶尖学府董事会

![Jensen Huang’s move suggests he still sees value in maintaining links to China’s academic and corporate circles despite Nvidia’s advanced chips remaining barred from the country © Bridget Bennett/Bloomberg](https://archive.li/vDiL9/22f7b2536fbf609f9e44c796f75e34d61615ed90.avif)

黄仁勋已同意加入一所中国顶尖大学的顾问委员会，该委员会的现任主席是苹果公司的蒂姆·库克。这位英伟达CEO正努力保持与北京的联系。

据两位知情人士透露，黄仁勋曾陪同美国总统唐纳德·特朗普近期访华，他已接受清华大学经济管理学院（SEM）的邀请，加入其顾问委员会。

位于北京的清华大学是中国顶尖的理工科大学，中国国家主席习近平是该校校友。经管学院顾问委员会的目标包括帮助商学院加强国际联系并制定长期战略。

除了库克，该委员会还有65名成员，包括美国科技领袖埃隆·马斯克、迈克尔·戴尔、微软的萨提亚·纳德拉、Meta的马克·扎克伯格，以及摩根大通的杰米·戴蒙、贝莱德的拉里·芬克、花旗集团的简·弗雷泽等金融高管。

黄仁勋的这一举措尚未正式公布，暗示他希望保持与中国学术和企业界的联系，尽管英伟达的先进芯片仍被禁止出口到中国。

自去年4月美国禁止出口其为中国市场设计的H20芯片（该芯片是为满足早期出口规则而制造的）以来，这家全球最大芯片制造商基本被挡在中国市场门外。

今年早些时候，华盛顿批准向部分中国客户有限销售英伟达更强大的H200芯片，但北京方面为保护国内半导体产业而限制了进口。

黄仁勋上周表示，美国的出口管制意味着英伟达已将中国市场“基本让给”了华为等国内竞争对手，尽管该公司公布了亮眼的季度业绩。

虽然中国曾占英伟达全球收入的五分之一，但黄仁勋告诉投资者，短期内不应预期获得向中国销售先进芯片的批准。

“我们非常乐意服务这个市场，”黄仁勋上周对CNBC表示。“我们在那里有很多客户和合作伙伴，已经深耕30年。”

![Beijing-based Tsinghua has a strong focus on science and engineering and counts President Xi Jinping as an alumnus © Imagine China/Reuters](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/36e81cc90b1481a88368f936ce6461f7/36e81cc9-0b14-81d7-a8f0-db478dad7b86-1326bc5b59d4d2c723537c531428221f.png)

经管学院顾问委员会成立于2000年，由前中国总理朱镕基创立，是在日益加深的脱钩时代为数不多的仍将中美企业与学术领袖聚集在一起的精英论坛之一。

朱镕基与时任高盛首席执行官亨利·保尔森（后成为该委员会首任主席）合作，招募了首批全球商业领袖。

经管学院顾问委员会成员通常每年在北京会面，在中国政策制定者与全球商业领袖之间建立了一条非正式沟通渠道。

2025年，中国副总理何立峰在年会前会见了23名委员会成员。前国家副主席王岐山也会见过成员，并曾在早年举办欢迎晚宴。

苹果CEO库克自2019年起担任该委员会主席。根据去年10月更新的名单，阿里巴巴的马云、腾讯的马化腾和百度的李彦宏等中国科技领袖也是成员。

其他成员包括来自可口可乐、西门子、通用汽车、英国石油和塔塔等多家全球最大公司的高管。

根据学院网站信息，经管学院顾问委员会成员除参加年会外，还会定期为学生授课。

西方最接近的类比是哈佛商学院的院长顾问委员会，该委员会也包含西方金融和科技精英。

英伟达（Nvidia）未立即回应置评请求。清华大学拒绝置评。

### English Original

## Nvidia chief Jensen Huang to join board at prestigious Beijing university

Jensen Huang has agreed to join the advisory board of a prestigious Chinese university that counts Apple’s Tim Cook as chair, as the Nvidia chief pushes to maintain ties with Beijing.

Huang, who accompanied US President Donald Trump on his recent visit to China, has accepted an invitation from Tsinghua University School of Economics and Management (SEM) to join its advisory board, according to two people with knowledge of the matter.

Beijing-based Tsinghua is China’s top science- and engineering-focused university and counts Chinese President Xi Jinping as an alumnus. The SEM board’s stated goals include helping the business school strengthen its international ties and shaping long-term strategy.

In addition to Cook, the 65-member board includes US tech leaders Elon Musk, Michael Dell, Microsoft’s Satya Nadella and Meta’s Mark Zuckerberg, as well as finance executives such as JPMorgan’s Jamie Dimon, BlackRock’s Larry Fink and Citigroup’s Jane Fraser.

Huang’s move, which has not yet been formally announced, suggests he wants to maintain links to China’s academic and corporate circles despite Nvidia’s advanced chips remaining barred from the country.

The world’s largest chipmaker has been largely shut out of China since last April, when the US banned the export of its China-specific chip, H20, which was made to meet earlier export rules.

Earlier this year, Washington approved limited sales of Nvidia’s more powerful H200 chips to selected Chinese customers, but Beijing restricted the imports to protect its domestic semiconductor industry.

Huang said last week that the US export controls meant Nvidia had “largely conceded” the Chinese market to domestic competitors such as Huawei, even as the chipmaker posted stellar quarterly results.

While China once accounted for a fifth of Nvidia’s global revenue, Huang told investors to “expect nothing” regarding approvals to sell advanced chips in the country in the near term.

“We would be more than delighted to serve the market,” Huang told CNBC last week. “We have a lot of customers there, we have a lot of partners there, and we’ve been there for 30 years.”

The SEM board, which was established in 2000 by former Chinese premier Zhu Rongji, is one of the few elite forums still bringing together US and Chinese corporate and academic leaders in an era of deepening decoupling.

Zhu partnered with Henry Paulson, then chief executive of Goldman Sachs who served as the founding chair of the SEM board, to recruit its first cohort of global business leaders.

SEM board members usually meet annually in Beijing, creating a back channel between Chinese policymakers and global business leaders.

In 2025, China’s vice-premier He Lifeng met 23 board members ahead of the annual meeting. Former vice-president Wang Qishan has also met board members and hosted welcome dinners for members in previous years.

Apple chief Cook currently chairs the board and has held the position since 2019. Chinese tech leaders including Alibaba’s Jack Ma, Tencent’s Pony Ma and Baidu’s Robin Li are also members, according to a list updated last October.

Other members include executives from a variety of the world’s largest companies such as Coca-Cola, Siemens, General Motors, BP and Tata.

According to the school’s website, the SEM board members give regular lectures to students in addition to holding annual meetings.

The closest western comparison is Harvard Business School’s board of dean’s advisers, which also counts western financial and tech elites as members.

Nvidia did not immediately reply to a request for comment. Tsinghua University declined to comment.
