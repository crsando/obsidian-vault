## Trump Used Elaborate Ruse to Fly Out of Turkey Due to Iran Threat, Report Says

## Trump Used Elaborate Ruse to Fly Out of Turkey Due to Iran Threat, Report Says

*US President Donald Trump stands in front of Air Force One after disembarking from another aircraft at an airbase in Mildenhall, in eastern England, on July 8.*
*Photographer: Saul Loeb/AFP/Getty Images*

By Associated Press
August 11, 2026 at 10:40 AM GMT+8
*Updated on August 11, 2026 at 5:08 PM GMT+8*

President [Donald Trump](https://apnews.com/hub/donald-trump) secretly flew out of last month's [NATO summit in Ankara, Turkey](https://apnews.com/article/nato-summit-takeaways-trump-ukraine-iran-albania-4821e7c6f2ab0b8a729d0e798bfe6359), on an alternate military aircraft while the White House made it appear that the Republican president was flying on Air Force One, according to a report published by The Washington Post on Monday.

The Post said the operation was prompted by an Iranian assassination threat against Trump. The ruse was carried out as journalists and some White House staff members were led to believe they were on the same plane as the president as he began his journey back to Washington from the annual gathering of NATO country leaders.
