DUBAI, Aug 18 (Reuters) - The Strait of Hormuz will remain shut until the U.S. meets the conditions of an interim deal signed with Iran in June, the top ‌Iranian negotiator Mohammad Baqer Qalibaf said in comments published by state media on Tuesday.
迪拜，8 月 18 日（路透社）——伊朗首席谈判代表穆罕默德·巴克尔·卡利巴夫在周二由官方媒体发布的言论中表示，除非美国满足 6 月与伊朗签署的临时协议中的各项条件，否则霍尔木兹海峡将继续关闭。

These conditions include the U.S. lifting its blockade of Iranian ports, lifting oil sanctions, releasing Tehran's frozen assets, and ending threats and military ⁠operations on all fronts, Qalibaf told parliament.
卡利巴夫向议会表示，这些条件包括美国解除对伊朗港口的封锁、取消石油制裁、解冻德黑兰的被冻结资产，以及停止在各个方面的威胁与军事行动。

The memorandum of understanding, clinched on June 17, quickly unravelled over a dispute about control of the Strait of Hormuz, the narrow waterway through which a fifth of global oil and liquefied natural gas flowed before the war.
6 月 17 日达成的那份谅解备忘录，由于在霍尔木兹海峡控制权问题上的分歧而迅速破裂。战前，全球五分之一的石油和液化天然气都是通过这条狭窄的水道运输的。

U.S. President Donald Trump said the ‌deal ⁠was "over" on July 7 and a week later Iran's foreign ministry declared it "suspended".
美国总统唐纳德·特朗普于 7 月 7 日宣布该协议“已经结束”，一周后伊朗外交部则称其“暂时中止”。

Under the MoU, Iran and the U.S. had committed to negotiating a final deal — ⁠covering broader issues such as the fate of Iran's nuclear programme — in a maximum of 60 days, extendable by mutual ⁠consent.
根据该谅解备忘录，伊朗与美国承诺在最多 60 天内谈判达成最终协议——该协议将涉及伊朗核计划等更广泛的问题，且可在双方同意的情况下延长期限。

A senior Iranian official [told Reuters on Monday ](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/iran-says-it-will-escalate-if-us-does-not-honour-deal-within-weeks-2026-08-17/)that Iran would now shift to a "fully offensive" posture ⁠due to the stalled diplomatic efforts to secure a permanent end to the conflict.
一名伊朗高级官员周一向路透社表示，由于旨在永久结束冲突的外交努力陷入停滞，伊朗现在将采取“全面进攻”的姿态。
