### 中文译文

## 美国对伊朗及其贸易伙伴的新经济制裁是什么？

![U.S. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent speaks outlines further sanctions against Iran, at the Treasury Department in Washington](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3c781cc90b1481cd8a5aefa3b634b17e/3c781cc9-0b14-8133-8b06-c060ae5bf91c-41870daefaae325ab0acf0edc5746fb1.jpg)

美国财政部长斯科特·贝森特于2026年8月24日在美国华盛顿特区的财政部举行的新闻发布会上讲话，概述针对伊朗的进一步制裁。REUTERS/Evelyn Hockstein [购买许可权，打开新标签页](https://www.reutersconnect.com/item/us-treasury-secretary-scott-bessent-speaks-outlines-further-sanctions-against-iran-at-the-treasury-department-in-washington/dGFnOnJldXRlcnMuY29tLDIwMjY6bmV3c21sX1JDMjY1TkE0OFBIQQ%3D%3D/?utm_medium=rcom-article-media&utm_campaign=rcom-rcp-lead)

伦敦，8月25日 - 美国财政部长斯科特·贝森特周一宣布，其部门所称的对[伊朗](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/iran-vows-retaliate-after-us-widens-sanctions-2026-08-25/)的[“经济打击”](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/us-treasury-broaden-scope-secondary-sanctions-iran-source-says-2026-08-24/)，将新制裁与对[德黑兰](https://www.reuters.com/world/iran/)贸易伙伴的威胁相结合。

他的声明未达到一些最严厉的制裁措施，并未列出将受针对的具体国家，也未说明这些惩罚措施何时生效。

以下是他宣布的行动摘要：

### 向其他政府施压，包括威胁实施新制裁

美国将为其他国家设定停止与伊朗进行经济活动的时限，并威胁若其不遵守将采取未具体说明的行动。

为伊朗协助洗钱或规避制裁的外国实体，可能面临被切断与美国金融体系联系的风险。

现在，与伊朗进行的更多类型的经济活动，可能导致对其他国家、个人或实体的二级制裁，无论其位于何处。

这些新列出的活动包括加密货币等数字资产、技术、黄金、航空和航运的交易。

### 对特定伊朗关联实体、个人和船只的制裁

美国财政部外国资产控制办公室（OFAC）已对近60个实体、个人和船只实施特定制裁，称这些对象正在为伊朗的活动提供便利。

新制裁针对伊朗国防部的弹道导弹和核采购网络、美国称其幕后策划针对美国基础设施攻击的网络组织，以及参与伊朗石油销售的航运、贸易和金融网络。

OFAC还暂停了多项许可证，这些许可证此前允许向伊朗进行部分汇款支付，并允许伊朗人进入美国文化和学术体系。

### 军事采购

新制裁中有20多项针对中东和亚洲的实体和个人，美国财政部称这些实体和个人参与为伊朗核研究和导弹开发提供财政和后勤支持。

这些实体包括位于中国的公司，这些公司向一个此前受制裁的伊朗技术研究所提供了美国财政部视为两用物资的物品。

### 网络攻击

美国正在扩大对几名此前已受制裁个人的制裁，指控他们参与网络犯罪或威胁美国国家安全。

### 石油网络

旨在阻止伊朗石油出口的新制裁措施包括船舶经纪人、加油服务提供商和金融中介机构。这些措施点名了几家位于阿联酋的个人和实体，以及位于新加坡和香港的其他个人和实体。

美国财政部还将五艘油轮列为所谓的“影子船队”的一部分，并将其列为“冻结财产”，这意味着OFAC禁止美国国民或居住在美国的人与它们进行任何金融交易。

## What are the new US economic sanctions on Iran and its trade partners?

U.S. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent speaks during a press conference to outline further sanctions against Iran, at the Treasury Department in Washington, D.C., U.S., August 24, 2026. REUTERS/Evelyn Hockstein [Purchase Licensing Rights, opens new tab](https://www.reutersconnect.com/item/us-treasury-secretary-scott-bessent-speaks-outlines-further-sanctions-against-iran-at-the-treasury-department-in-washington/dGFnOnJldXRlcnMuY29tLDIwMjY6bmV3c21sX1JDMjY1TkE0OFBIQQ%3D%3D/?utm_medium=rcom-article-media&utm_campaign=rcom-rcp-lead)

LONDON, August 25 - U.S. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent announced what his department called ["an economic onslaught"](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/us-treasury-broaden-scope-secondary-sanctions-iran-source-says-2026-08-24/) against [Iran ](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/iran-vows-retaliate-after-us-widens-sanctions-2026-08-25/)on Monday that combined new sanctions with threats against [Tehran's](https://www.reuters.com/world/iran/)trading partners.

His statement stopped short of some of the most punishing sanctions and did not list specific countries that would be targeted, or ‌when those penalties would take effect.

Here's a summary of actions he announced:

### PRESSING OTHER GOVERNMENTS, INCLUDING WITH THREATS OF NEW SANCTIONS

The United States will set timelines for other countries to shut down economic activity with Iran and threatens unspecified actions if they do not comply.

Foreign entities that facilitate money laundering or sanctions evasion for Iran risk being cut off from the U.S. financial system.

More types ⁠of economic activity with Iran may now lead to secondary sanctions on other countries, individuals or entities regardless of where they are located.

These newly listed activities include dealings in digital assets like cryptocurrency, technology, gold, aviation and shipping.

### SANCTIONS ON SPECIFIC IRAN-LINKED ENTITIES, INDIVIDUALS AND VESSELS

The Treasury's Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC) has added specific sanctions on nearly 60 entities, individuals and vessels that it says are enabling Iranian activity.

The new sanctions target the Iranian defence ministry's ballistic and nuclear procurement network, a cyber group the U.S. says is behind attacks on its infrastructure, and the shipping, trading and financial network involved in Iranian oil ‌sales.

OFAC ⁠has also suspended several licences that previously allowed some remittance payments to Iran and Iranian access to the U.S. cultural and academic system.

### MILITARY PROCUREMENT

More than 20 of the new sanctions are targeted at entities and individuals in the Middle East and Asia that Treasury says are involved in financial and logistical support for Iranian nuclear research and ⁠missile development.

These include China-based companies that supplied what the Treasury considers dual-use items for a previously sanctioned Iranian technology institute.

### CYBERATTACKS

The U.S. is expanding sanctions on several previously sanctioned individuals, accusing them of involvement in cybercrime or threats to U.S. ⁠national security.

### OIL NETWORKS

New sanctions aimed at stopping Iran's oil exports include vessel brokers, bunkering service providers and financial intermediaries. These name several individuals and entities based in the United Arab Emirates and others in ⁠Singapore and Hong Kong.

The Treasury also identified five tankers as part of what it called a "shadow fleet" and are listed as "blocked property," meaning OFAC prohibits U.S. nationals or people based in the United States from having any financial transactions with them.
