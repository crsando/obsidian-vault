### 中文译文

## 福特将把部分林肯车型的生产从中国迁至美国

底特律，8月12日（路透社）——福特汽车首席执行官周三对路透社表示，福特汽车计划从2030年开始将部分林肯车型的生产从中国迁至美国，并称此举虽然艰难，但对于加强美国汽车制造业基础是必要的。

从中国进口的汽油动力汽车和电动汽车需缴纳高额关税。福特证实，美国对林肯航海家（Lincoln Nautilus）——福特从中国进口的主要车型——征收的关税为52.5%。

“我们一看到政府政策出台就做出了这个决定，”福特首席执行官吉姆·法利在谈到关税时表示。“我们很清楚他们想做什么，也很清楚这对福特意味着什么，”法利在接受美国商务部长霍华德·卢特尼克联合采访时说。

法利表示，作为大幅提升产量努力的一部分，美国生产的林肯车将在国内市场销售，但公司未透露具体生产地点。

卢特尼克补充说：“福特有优势。国内制造业有优势。”

同城的竞争对手通用汽车已宣布，将从2028年开始把别克昂科威的生产从中国迁至美国。

除了关税，汽车制造商还面临《联网车辆规则》的限制，该规则禁止在美国车型中使用某些中国技术和硬件。法利表示，这两项法规促使福特做出了这一决定，但他指出关税是主要驱动因素。

福特是向美国商务部请求授权继续销售可能受该规则限制的车辆的几家公司之一。被拒绝授权的汽车制造商，如电动汽车公司极星（Polestar），则面临在美国市场销售某些产品的禁令。

总部位于密歇根州迪尔伯恩的这家汽车制造商的一位发言人周三表示，在与商务部讨论后，该公司意识到林肯航海家（Lincoln Nautilus）不再需要获得在美国销售的授权。

该公司此前曾表示，航海家的软件在美国开发，但在中国安装到车辆上，因此需要政府批准才能继续在美国销售。福特去年在美国销售了约34,000辆航海家汽车。

美国立法者试图在《联网汽车规则》的基础上进一步收紧禁令。其中一项举措于7月获得美国参议院商务委员会批准，将禁止中国实体持股超过15%的公司在美国销售汽车。如果实施，这将阻止梅赛德斯-奔驰在美国销售新车。

福特表示，周三宣布的举措是基于林肯在美国的生产基地。林肯在肯塔基州路易斯维尔的工厂组装领航员（Navigator），在芝加哥装配厂组装飞行家（Aviator），并将这两款车型出口到加拿大、墨西哥和中东等市场。

报道：诺拉·埃克特；编辑：罗德·尼克尔和埃德蒙·克拉曼

### English Original

## Ford to move production of some Lincoln models from China to US

DETROIT, Aug 12 (Reuters) - Ford Motor plans to move production of some Lincoln models from China to the U.S. beginning in 2030, Ford's CEO told Reuters on Wednesday, saying the move was difficult but necessary to strengthen the U.S. auto manufacturing base.

Gasoline-powered cars and electric vehicles imported from China are subject to hefty duties. The U.S. tariff on the Lincoln Nautilus, the main vehicle Ford imports from China, is 52.5%, Ford confirmed.

“We made this decision as soon as the policy of the administration was set," Ford CEO Jim Farley said, referring to the tariffs. "We knew exactly what they wanted to do, and we knew exactly what it meant for Ford," Farley said in a joint interview with U.S. Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick.

The U.S.-made Lincolns would be sold in the domestic market as part of a significant effort to scale up output, Farley said, although the company did not disclose where they would be produced.

Lutnick added: "Ford's got an edge. Domestic manufacturing has an edge."

Crosstown rival General Motors has announced it will move production of its Buick Envision to the U.S. from China starting in 2028.

The Lincoln Nautilus SUV is displayed at the Los Angeles Auto Show in Los Angeles, California U.S. November 29, 2017. REUTERS/Lucy Nicholson

Along with tariffs, automakers have faced restrictions under the Connected Vehicle Rule, which bans some Chinese technology and hardware in U.S. models. Farley said that both regulations prompted Ford to make the decision, although he pointed to tariffs as the driving factor.

Ford was one of several companies requesting authorization from the U.S. Commerce Department to continue selling vehicles potentially restricted under the rule. Automakers denied an authorization, such as EV company Polestar, face bans from selling certain products in the U.S. market.

A spokesperson for the Dearborn, Michigan-based automaker said on Wednesday that, after discussions with the Commerce Department, it realized the Lincoln Nautilus no longer needed an authorization to sell in the U.S.

The company had previously said the Nautilus software was developed in the U.S. but installed into the vehicle in China, requiring government approval to continue selling it in the United States. Ford sold about 34,000 Nautilus vehicles in the U.S. last year.

U.S. lawmakers have sought to further tighten prohibitions beyond those introduced in the Connected Vehicle Rule. One such push, approved by the U.S. Senate Commerce Committee in July, would bar companies that are more than 15% owned by Chinese entities from selling vehicles in the United States. If implemented, it would prevent Mercedes-Benz from selling new vehicles in the U.S.

Ford said the move announced on Wednesday builds on Lincoln's U.S. production base. Lincoln assembles the Navigator at a plant in Louisville, Kentucky, and the Aviator at the Chicago Assembly Plant, and exports both models to markets including Canada, Mexico and the Middle East.
