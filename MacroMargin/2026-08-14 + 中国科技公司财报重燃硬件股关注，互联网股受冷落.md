## 中国科技公司财报重燃硬件股关注，互联网股受冷落

### **彭博AI要点**

- 中国科技公司的早期财报为硬件股注入了新的动力，而互联网平台仍难以展示全面的消费需求复苏。
- 中芯国际（Semiconductor Manufacturing International Corp.）股价攀升，此前该芯片制造商公布的业绩超出预期，毛利率展望也强于预期。
- 投资者普遍认为，中国企业在AI硬件上的支出将继续增长，因此资金正从非AI股（如京东）撤出，尽管这些股票盈利增长强劲且股价下跌。
中国科技公司的早期财报为硬件股注入了新的动力，而互联网平台仍难以展示全面的消费需求复苏。

中芯国际（[Semiconductor Manufacturing International Corp.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/981%20HK%20Equity)）股价周五在香港一度上涨6.4%，此前该芯片制造商公布的业绩超出预期，且毛利率展望强于预期。

但在线零售商[京东集团](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/9618%20HK%20Equity)下跌超过10%，此前京东经历自2014年上市以来的首次季度营收下滑。与[美团](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/3690%20HK%20Equity)一起，京东成为拖累恒生科技[指数](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/HSTECH%20Index)最大的个股之一，该指数下跌1.6%。

自6月下旬以来，随着AI热潮降温，投资者日益转向互联网和电商股，扭转了上半年的趋势。本周早些时候的财报初步显示，他们对AI股的偏好可能重新增强，随着更多财报出炉，互联网股将面临压力。

“投资者普遍认为，中国企业的人工智能硬件支出将持续增长，因此资金正从非人工智能类股票（如京东）中撤出，尽管这些公司盈利增长强劲且股价下跌，”三井住友DS资产管理公司高级投资组合经理斯坦利·唐（Stanley Tang）表示。

进一步凸显投资者对人工智能硬件股的持久兴趣，新上市的内存芯片制造商[长鑫存储（CXMT Corp.）](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/688825%20CH%20Equity)于周四[超越](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-13/cxmt-overtakes-tencent-to-become-most-valuable-chinese-company)互联网巨头[腾讯（Tencent Holdings Ltd.）](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/700%20HK%20Equity)，成为市值最高的中国公司。

中国互联网公司也承诺加大人工智能支出，但其财报发布后的股价表现在一定程度上显示出对这些投资速度的担忧。腾讯股价周四下跌，尽管其广告和手机游戏业务增长稳健，因为高盛集团分析师在《腾讯因人工智能支出引发利润率担忧而下跌：Street Wrap》报告中指出，人工智能支出高于预期。

并非所有硬件股都表现良好。[华虹半导体（Hua Hong Grace Semiconductor Ltd.）](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/1347%20HK%20Equity)周五因盈利未达预期而大跌。

美团和[百度公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/9888%20HK%20Equity)可能提供下一个重大考验，看看投资者是否会重新转向互联网股票，还是继续青睐与人工智能相关的公司，它们的财报预计分别于周五和8月18日发布。

Chinese tech firms’ early earnings are giving hardware stocks renewed momentum, while internet platforms still struggle to show a full consumer‑demand recovery.

Shares of [Semiconductor Manufacturing International Corp.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/981%20HK%20Equity) climbed as much as 6.4% in Hong Kong on Friday after the chipmaker reported SMIC Shares Rise After 2Q Beat, Gross Margin Outlook Seen Strong that beat estimates and a stronger-than-expected gross margin outlook.

But online retailer [JD.com Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/9618%20HK%20Equity) fell more than 10% after JD.com’s Shares Slump After First Revenue Decline: Street Wrap its first quarterly revenue decline since listing in 2014. Along with [Meituan](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/3690%20HK%20Equity), JD.com was among the biggest drags on the Hang Seng Tech [Index](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/HSTECH%20Index), which slid 1.6%.

Since late June, investors have increasingly shifted into internet and e-consumer stocks as the AI rally cooled, reversing first‑half trends. Early reads on this week’s earnings point to their preference toward AI stocks possibly regaining strength, pressuring internet shares as more results come due.

“Investors generally believe spending in AI hardware by Chinese firms will continue to grow, hence funds are switching out from non-AI names, e.g. JD, despite strong bottom-line growth and share price dips,” said Stanley Tang, senior portfolio manager at Sumitomo Mitsui DS Asset Management.

Further underscoring investors’ durable appetite for AI hardware stocks, newly listed memory-chip maker [CXMT Corp. ](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/688825%20CH%20Equity)on Thursday [overtook](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-13/cxmt-overtakes-tencent-to-become-most-valuable-chinese-company) internet giant [Tencent Holdings Ltd.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/700%20HK%20Equity) as the most valuable Chinese company.

Chinese internet firms also have vowed heavy AI spending, but their post‑earnings stock performance partly shows concerns about the pace of such investments. Tencent’s shares fell Thursday despite reporting solid growth for advertising and mobile games, as Goldman Sachs Group Inc. analysts Tencent Drops as AI Spending Fuels Margin Concern: Street Wrap that AI spending was higher than expected.

Not all hardware stocks did well. [Hua Hong Grace Semiconductor Ltd.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/1347%20HK%20Equity) plunged Friday after a profit miss.

Meituan and [Baidu Inc. ](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/9888%20HK%20Equity)may provide the next major test of whether investors rotate back into internet stocks, or keep favoring AI-linked players, with earnings expected on Friday and Aug. 18, respectively.
