### 中文译文

## 瑞银加入中国在岸债券市场低利率热潮

[瑞银集团](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/UBSG%20SW%20Equity)本周首次在中国境内发行债券，突显出该市场在寻求降低借贷成本的国际发行人中日益受到欢迎。

这家瑞士银行将瑞银集团20亿元人民币（2.975亿美元）的5年期熊猫债券定价为票息1.78%。瑞银在声明中表示，这是外国金融机构发行的五年期债券中有记录以来利率最低、与境内基准利差最窄的一次。熊猫债券是注册在中国境外的实体在中国境内市场发行的人民币计价债券。

这是瑞士金融机构首次发行此类债券，但此前已有类似定价区间：法国农业信贷银行(Crédit Agricole SA)和巴克莱银行(Barclays Bank Plc)近几个月发行了最高60亿元人民币的3年期、5年期和7年期熊猫债券。中国熊猫债券市场今年迄今的发行量已飙升至创纪录的2140亿元人民币（318亿美元），较2025年同期增长67%，且已经超过彭博自2005年开始汇编数据以来任何一年的总量。

尽管增长迅速，但中国对全球发行人而言仍是一个远小于美国或欧洲的市场，部分原因在于限制资本国际流动的规则。

瑞银中国区负责人胡知鸷(Janice Hu)表示：“此次发行标志着我们持续参与中国金融市场开放的重要里程碑，并展现了中国境内资本市场与国际发行人之间日益增强的连通性。”

### **降低成本**

彭博汇编的数据显示，今年发行的熊猫债券平均票息率比以美元发行的债券低约200个基点。数据显示，企业、金融机构和主权借款人迄今已累计发行约1.4万亿元人民币的熊猫债券。

从下月初（9月1日）开始，中国将削减熊猫债券和结算的费用，包括熊猫债券的发行注册费，作为更广泛降低成本计划的一部分，此举可能会带来更多活动。

澳大利亚新西兰银行集团高级中国策略师邢兆鹏表示，蓬勃发展的熊猫债券市场是人民币国际化的关键引擎，尤其是随着全球银行纷纷涌入。他补充说，这将有助于提升人民币在海外贸易结算和投资中的使用。

中国离岸债券市场也一直在吸引希望分散融资来源并降低利率成本的新发行人。2026年4月，葡萄牙成为首个在中国境外发行人民币计价债券的欧元区国家，该国曾在2019年涉足熊猫债券市场。

## UBS Joins Rush to Tap Lower Rates in China’s Onshore Bond Market

[UBS Group AG](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/UBSG%20SW%20Equity)’s debut Chinese domestic bond sale this week underscores the market’s growing popularity among international issuers looking for lower borrowing costs.

The Swiss bank PRICED: UBS AG CNY 2b 5Y Panda Bond at 1.78% 2 billion yuan ($297.5 million) of so-called panda bonds at a coupon of 1.78%. This is the lowest rate and the tightest spread over China’s onshore benchmark on record for a five-year note issued by a foreign financial institution, UBS said in a statement. Panda bonds are yuan-denominated notes sold in the onshore market by entities registered outside China.

It was the first such bond sold by a Swiss financial institution but follows similar PRICING RANGE: Crédit Agricole Up to CNY6b 3Y, 5Y, 7Y Panda Bonds by Crédit Agricole SA and Barclays Bank Plc in recent months. Issuance in China’s panda bond market has surged to a record 214 billion yuan ($31.8 billion) so far this year, up 67% from the same period in 2025 and already more than the total for any year since Bloomberg began compiling data in 2005.

Despite the rapid growth, China is still a much smaller market for global issuers than the US or Europe, partly because of rules that restrict the international flow of capital.

“The issuance marks an important milestone in our continued participation in the opening-up of China’s financial markets and demonstrates the growing connectivity between China’s onshore capital markets and international issuers,” said Janice Hu, China country head at UBS.

### **Reducing Costs**

Average coupon rates are about 200 basis points lower than those of bonds issued in dollars this year, Bloomberg-compiled data show. Corporates, financial institutions and sovereign borrowers have now issued panda bonds of around 1.4 trillion yuan in total, according to the data.

From the start of next month, China will China to Cut Fees on Panda Bonds, Settlements From Sept. 1 issuance registration fees for panda bonds as part of a broader program to reduce costs, a move that could generate even more activity.

A booming panda bond market is a vital engine for yuan internationalization, especially with global banks piling in, according to Zhaopeng Xing, senior China strategist at Australia & New Zealand Banking Group. It will help boost the use of the currency overseas in trade settlement and investment, he added.

China’s offshore bond market has also been attracting new issuers keen to diversify their funding sources and reduce interest-rate costs. In April, Portugal became the first euro-area country to [issue](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-16/portugal-sells-offshore-yuan-bonds-in-first-for-euro-area) yuan-denominated bonds outside China, having tapped the panda bond market in 2019.
