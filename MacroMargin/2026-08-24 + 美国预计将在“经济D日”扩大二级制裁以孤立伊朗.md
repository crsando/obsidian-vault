### 中文译文

## 美国预计将在“经济D日”扩大二级制裁以孤立伊朗

华盛顿/伊斯兰堡，8月24日（路透社）——一位消息人士对路透社表示，美国预计将于周一公布“有史以来最大规模的金融攻势”，届时将警告各国切断与伊朗的关系，否则其企业可能被切断与以美元为基础的金融体系的联系。

美国财政部长斯科特·贝森特将于下午1点（格林尼治标准时间17:00）概述扩大对与伊朗保持经济联系的实体和国家潜在二级制裁范围的措施，上述消息人士称，该消息人士熟悉相关计划，但未获授权公开发言。

贝森特[此前曾敦促中国合作](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/us-treasury-secretary-bessent-hold-press-conference-iran-on-monday-2026-08-20/)，中国多年来一直是伊朗石油的最大买家，尽管美国于7月中旬重启的对伊朗港口的封锁已[削减了伊朗对中国的石油流入](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/looming-us-sanctions-iran-put-china-oil-buying-spotlight-2026-08-24/)。

美国财政部已对总部位于中国和香港的较小实体实施二级制裁，这些实体被指控处理了数十亿美元的伊朗石油并帮助资助武器采购，同时还警告两家较大的银行不要处理伊朗资金。

但专家表示，在特朗普与中国国家主席习近平预计下月举行会谈之前，华盛顿对中国的报复持谨慎态度，对中国关键矿产出口的任何限制都是特别敏感的问题。

中国外交部表示，制裁和施压手段无济于事，北京将采取必要措施保护中国的利益。

### 巴基斯坦陆军参谋长就伊朗问题举行会谈

贝森特还可能加强对帮助伊朗支付进口费用的石油运输商、采购商和货币兑换商的执法力度。他还可能将航空业列为目标。特朗普已威胁要对与伊朗有业务往来的国家的商品征收关税，尽管最高法院已驳回了此类税收的法律依据。

伊朗驳斥了美国的威胁，誓言“如果经济战继续”，将切断来自海湾的所有石油出口。伊朗发布了[新的航运警告](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/iran-warns-vessels-violating-hormuz-transit-rules-fines-detention-2026-08-24/)，要求未经其允许不得通过霍尔木兹海峡，并列出了45艘其声称违反规则的船只，威胁要对与这些船进行的任何船对船转运进行报复。

贝森特在《金融时报》的评论文章中指出，周一将标志着“经济D日——有史以来针对对手集结的最大规模金融攻势”。

据伊朗媒体报道，巴基斯坦陆军参谋长阿西姆·穆尼尔（Asim Munir）周一抵达伊朗进行会谈。穆尼尔与美国总统唐纳德·特朗普建立了私人交情。巴基斯坦表示，此次访问是其“促进地区和平与稳定”努力的一部分，一名巴基斯坦消息人士称，预计穆尼尔将会见与伊朗最高领袖关系密切的人士。

*在这段视频截图中，巴基斯坦陆军参谋长阿西姆·穆尼尔和伊朗内政部长埃斯坎达尔·莫梅尼于2026年8月24日在伊朗德黑兰会面。瓦纳通讯社/路透社供图*

两位巴基斯坦消息人士称，特朗普上周曾致电穆尼尔，另一位消息人士称，主要请求是[让伊朗回到谈判桌](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/pakistans-munir-spoke-trump-ahead-tehran-visit-sources-say-2026-08-24/)。白宫没有立即回应置评请求。

### 监测机构称，沙特红海港口附近油轮遭弹体击中

美国与伊朗已有数周未对彼此军队实施空袭，但双方为结束[持续六个月的冲突](https://www.reuters.com/world/iran/)而进行的最后一次正式面对面会谈是在6月，而且对霍尔木兹海峡船只的袭击仍在继续。

英国海上贸易行动办公室的航运监测员称，周一，一枚弹体击中了红海上沙特港口城市延布以西的一艘油轮，导致主甲板起火。伊朗的胡塞盟友上月宣布将封锁为避开霍尔木兹海峡而改道红海的沙特石油出口，这些盟友的发言人表示，他们在该地区袭击了一艘船只。

自2月28日美国和以色列开始发动打击以来，已有数千人死亡，其中大多数在伊朗和黎巴嫩，这些打击削弱了伊朗大部分常规军事能力，造成经济痛苦，并杀死了[时任伊朗最高领袖阿亚图拉阿里·哈梅内伊](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/iran-bury-slain-supreme-leader-culmination-mass-funeral-2026-07-09/)。

然而，伊朗保留了足够的导弹和无人机能力，可以攻击其海湾邻国，并使霍尔木兹海峡的航运[几乎停滞](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/fewer-than-20-ships-transit-key-strait-hormuz-over-weekend-data-shows-2026-08-24/)，推高了世界燃料价格。美国和以色列旨在摧毁的伊朗核计划的确切状态仍然未知。

在连续两周上涨后，周一油价[回落](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/oil-falls-1-ahead-us-announcement-impose-further-sanctions-iran-2026-08-23/)，因投资者在美国预期公告前获利了结。

贝森特没有详细说明具体措施，但表示美国将针对那些通过与伊朗经济和金融体系往来而实行“绥靖”政策的“恐惧国家”。

伊朗数日来一直在为制裁做准备，发表一系列声明，暗示将作出重大军事回应。

伊朗最高国家安全委员会秘书穆赫森·雷扎伊周日表示，进一步的惩罚只会导致霍尔木兹海峡航运受到更严密的封锁。

伊朗带着高通胀、能源短缺和严重的结构性弱点进入战争，现在必须应对贸易中断、生产损失和重建受损基础设施的成本。里亚尔汇率下滑曾引发1月遭暴力镇压的抗议，周一触及新纪录低点，较1月水平低25%。

## US expected to broaden secondary sanctions to isolate Iran on 'economic D-Day'

WASHINGTON/ISLAMABAD, Aug 24 (Reuters) - The United States was set to warn countries to cut ties with Iran or risk their businesses being cut off from the dollar-based financial system when it unveils the "greatest financial offensive ever" on ‌Monday, a source told Reuters.

U.S. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent will outline measures at 1 p.m. (1700 GMT) to broaden the scope of potential secondary sanctions on entities and countries that maintain economic ties with Iran, said the source, who was familiar with the plans but not authorized to speak publicly.

Bessent [previously urged cooperation from China](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/us-treasury-secretary-bessent-hold-press-conference-iran-on-monday-2026-08-20/), the biggest buyer of Iranian oil for several years, although the U.S. blockade of Iran's ports, renewed in mid-July, has already [cut Iranian oil flows](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/looming-us-sanctions-iran-put-china-oil-buying-spotlight-2026-08-24/) to China.

The U.S. Treasury has imposed secondary sanctions on smaller China and Hong Kong-based entities accused of processing billions of dollars in Iranian oil and helping to fund arms purchases and warned two larger banks against handling Iranian funds.

But experts say Washington is wary of Chinese retaliation ahead of expected talks next month between Trump and Chinese President Xi Jinping, with any curbs on China's exports of ⁠critical minerals a particularly sensitive issue.

The Chinese foreign ministry said sanctions and pressure tactics do not help and that Beijing would do what was necessary to protect China's interests.

### PAKISTAN'S ARMY CHIEF HOLDS TALKS ON IRAN

Bessent could also sharpen enforcement against oil shippers, purchasers and currency exchangers who help Iran pay for its imports. He could also target aviation. Trump has threatened tariffs on goods from countries that do business with Iran, although the Supreme Court struck down the legal basis for such taxes.

Iran has dismissed the U.S. threat, vowing to shut down all oil exports from the Gulf "if the economic war continues". It issued a [new warning to shipping](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/iran-warns-vessels-violating-hormuz-transit-rules-fines-detention-2026-08-24/) not to pass through the Strait of Hormuz without its permission, listing 45 ships it said had violated its rules and threatening retaliation for any ship-to-ship transfers with them.

Bessent wrote in an opinion piece in the Financial Times that Monday would mark "an economic D-Day — the single greatest financial offensive ever marshalled against an adversary".

Pakistan's army chief Asim Munir, who has built a personal rapport with U.S. President Donald Trump, arrived in Iran on Monday for talks, Iranian media said. Pakistan said the visit was part of its efforts "to promote regional peace and stability", and a Pakistani source said Munir was expected to meet people close to Iran's supreme leader.

*Pakistan's Army Chief Asim Munir and Iranian Interior Minister Eskandar Momeni meet, in Tehran, Iran, August 24, 2026, in this screengrab from a video. Wana News Agency/Pool via REUTERS*

Two Pakistani sources said Trump had called Munir last week, with another saying the main ‌request was to [bring ⁠Iran back to negotiations](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/pakistans-munir-spoke-trump-ahead-tehran-visit-sources-say-2026-08-24/). The White House did not immediately respond to a request for comment.

### PROJECTILE HITS TANKER NEAR SAUDI RED SEA PORT, MONITORS SAY

The U.S. and Iran have not conducted air strikes on each other's militaries for weeks, but their last official face-to-face talks to end the [six-month-old conflict](https://www.reuters.com/world/iran/) took place in June and strikes on vessels in the Strait of Hormuz have continued.

A projectile struck a tanker west of the Saudi port city of Yanbu in the Red Sea on Monday, causing a fire on the main deck, shipping monitors from the United Kingdom Maritime Trade Operations said. A spokesperson for Iran's Houthi allies, who last month [announced they would block](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/yemens-houthis-declare-naval-blockade-against-saudi-arabia-statement-2026-07-20/) Saudi oil exports diverted to the Red Sea to avoid Hormuz, said they had attacked a vessel ⁠in the area.

Thousands of people have died, most of them in Iran and Lebanon, since the U.S. and Israel began strikes on February 28, degrading much of Iran's conventional military capacity and inflicting economic pain while killing [Iran's then-Supreme Leader Ayatollah Ali Khamenei](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/iran-bury-slain-supreme-leader-culmination-mass-funeral-2026-07-09/).

Yet Iran has preserved enough missile and drone capability to attack its Gulf neighbours and bring shipping in the Strait of Hormuz to [a near standstill](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/fewer-than-20-ships-transit-key-strait-hormuz-over-weekend-data-shows-2026-08-24/), pushing up world fuel prices. The exact state of Iran's nuclear program, which the Americans and Israelis aim to wipe ⁠out, remains unknown.

Oil prices [eased](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/oil-falls-1-ahead-us-announcement-impose-further-sanctions-iran-2026-08-23/) on Monday after two weeks of gains as investors took profits ahead of the expected U.S. announcement.

Without detailing specific measures, Bessent signalled the U.S. would target "fearful nations" that practice "appeasement" by engaging with Iran's economy and financial system.

Iran has been bracing for the sanctions for days, issuing a series of statements hinting at a major military response.

Mohsen Rezaei, secretary of Iran's Supreme National Security Council, said on Sunday further penalties would simply trigger a ⁠tighter squeeze on shipping through the Strait of Hormuz.

Iran entered the war with high inflation, energy shortages and deep structural weaknesses, and must now contend with disrupted trade, lost production and the cost of rebuilding damaged infrastructure. A slide in the rial currency, which prompted protests in January that were violently suppressed, reached a new record low on Monday, 25% below its January level.
