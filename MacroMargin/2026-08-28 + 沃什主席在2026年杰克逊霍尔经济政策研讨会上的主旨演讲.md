#### *In Our Time*

#### *在我们这个时代*

[Chairman Kevin Warsh](https://www.federalreserve.gov/aboutthefed/bios/board/warsh.htm)

[主席凯文·沃什](https://www.federalreserve.gov/aboutthefed/bios/board/warsh.htm)

At “Financial Innovation: Implications for Payments and Policy,” an economic policy symposium sponsored by the Federal Reserve Bank of Kansas City, Jackson Hole, Wyoming

在“金融创新：对支付和政策的启示”经济政策研讨会上，该研讨会由堪萨斯城联邦储备银行在怀俄明州杰克逊霍尔主办

Thank you. It's great to be here again and to see so many familiar faces. I've been looking forward to this weekend—what better place to mark my 100th day as Chairman?

谢谢。很高兴再次来到这里，见到这么多熟悉的面孔。我一直期待这个周末——还有什么地方比这里更适合纪念我担任主席的第100天呢？

For the fine hospitality, everyone here is in debt to President Jeff Schmid and his colleagues at the Federal Reserve Bank of Kansas City. Jeff, our thanks to you all.

对于热情的款待，在座的各位都感谢堪萨斯城联邦储备银行行长杰夫·施密德及其同事。杰夫，我们感谢你们所有人。

Jeff and the other planners have some recreation options lined up for later today. And I'd advise you to be very careful with your choices.

杰夫和其他组织者为今天晚些时候安排了一些休闲活动。我建议你们在选择时要非常谨慎。

As I learned years ago, you can take two different kinds of hikes on the trails around Jackson Hole. I can sum up my hikes with former Vice Chairman Don Kohn in two words: I survived. These steely marathon death marches revealed a side of Don I wasn't ready for.

正如我多年前学到的，在杰克逊霍尔周围的小径上，你可以进行两种不同的徒步旅行。我可以总结和前任副主席唐·科恩一起的徒步旅行，用两个词：我活下来了。这些坚毅的马拉松式死亡行军揭示了唐不为人知的一面，我还没准备好。

There's another kind of hike—one I associate with Chairman Ben Bernanke, my old colleague. With Ben, it's a much more leisurely pace, an easy stroll along the wandering trails at the Rockefeller Preserve.

还有另一种徒步——让我想起我的老同事、主席本·伯南克。和本在一起，节奏要从容得多，就像在洛克菲勒保护区蜿蜒的小径上轻松漫步。

So before setting out, do a wellness check and ask yourself: "Is this a Kohn day or a Bernanke day?"

所以出发前，先做个状态检查，问问自己：“今天是科恩日还是伯南克日？”

The best thing about this gathering is that it helps us all clear our minds and think straight about our world and our time. For me, it feels like the right place, and the right audience, for a real engagement with the ideas that matter most.

这次聚会最棒的地方在于，它帮助我们所有人理清思绪，清醒地思考我们的世界和我们的时代。对我而言，这里似乎是正确的场所、正确的听众，能与最重要的思想进行真正的交流。

Innovation is the conference theme, and I believe that the public and the markets—in their collective wisdom—understand that innovations in the conduct of policy at the Fed will help deliver price stability alongside full employment.

创新是本次会议的主题，我相信公众和市场——凭借其集体智慧——理解美联储在政策执行上的创新将有助于在实现充分就业的同时实现物价稳定。

Here is a quick overview of what I'll cover in my remarks this morning. You can call it an outline . . . you can call it a trail map . . . just don't call it forward guidance.

以下是我今天上午讲话内容的快速概述。你可以称它为提纲……也可以称它为路线图……只是别称它为前瞻性指引。

First, I'll touch on a few of the longer-term questions we're asking at the Fed about the latest general-purpose technology, artificial intelligence (AI), and where it might take the economy.

首先，我将简要提及美联储正在思考的几个更长期的问题，关于最新的通用技术——人工智能（AI）——以及它可能把经济带向何方。

Then I'll reflect a bit on the practice of forward guidance and the interaction between the central bank and financial markets.

然后，我会稍微反思一下前瞻性指引的实践，以及中央银行与金融市场之间的互动。

Next, I'll present some of the key principles that I believe should guide the conduct of monetary policy.

接下来，我将阐述一些我认为应该指导货币政策实施的关键原则。

And, finally, I'll give you my assessment of the economy.

最后，我将给出我对经济的评估。

**Preparing for Future Policy Conjunctures**

With the unchanging picture of the Tetons as our backdrop, we are here to survey an economic landscape that is anything but static.

**为未来的政策局势做好准备**

以不变的提顿山脉为背景，我们在此审视一个绝非静态的经济格局。

It wasn't so long ago—in the run-up to the crisis of 2008 and over the decade that followed—when economists and policymakers were speaking of secular stagnation and a global saving glut.[1](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/warsh20260828a.htm#fn1) It was a widely held view that an excess of capital would sit on the sidelines for a long, long time, because there just wouldn't be enough compelling investment opportunities. All the good stuff had been invented. So growth would be low and slow.[2](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/warsh20260828a.htm#fn2)

就在不久之前——在2008年危机前夕及随后的十年里——经济学家和政策制定者还在谈论长期停滞和全球储蓄过剩。[1](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/warsh20260828a.htm#fn1) 当时人们普遍认为，过剩的资本将长期处于观望状态，因为没有足够有吸引力的投资机会。所有好点子都已被发明。因此增长将低而缓慢。[2](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/warsh20260828a.htm#fn2)

Well, times sure have changed. We've come to a hinge point in history.[3](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/warsh20260828a.htm#fn3)

嗯，时代确实变了。我们已经来到历史的一个关键转折点。[3](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/warsh20260828a.htm#fn3)

To cite the clearest example, progress in artificial intelligence—the 80-year-old name for the newest technology—has been faster even than its evangelists predicted a couple of years ago.

以最明显的例子来说，人工智能——这个已有80年历史的名字所代表的最新科技——其进展甚至比几年前其布道者所预测的还要快。

The potential for substantially higher growth is on the rise. Ever-expanding pools of capital are pouring into AI-related infrastructure of all sorts. A kind of hyper–Moore's law seems to be playing out. Scaling laws, too, are changing both the method and speed of innovation.[4](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/warsh20260828a.htm#fn4)

实现大幅更高增长的潜力正在上升。不断扩大的资本池正涌入各类与AI相关的基础设施。某种超级摩尔定律似乎正在上演。规模化法则也在改变创新的方式和速度。[4](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/warsh20260828a.htm#fn4)

Capital and labor have combined to create the large language models at the heart of AI. Users buy tokens to gain access to the models. Reports put annualized token sales for the two leading labs alone at more than $100 billion—an increase of 500-plus percent from a year ago.

资本与劳动力相结合，创造了处于AI核心的大语言模型。用户购买代币以获取这些模型的使用权。据报道，仅两家领先实验室的年化代币销售额就超过1000亿美元——较一年前增长超过500%。

The Fed watches all of this attentively. We recognize that AI is a new variable—potentially a new factor of production—that will have consequences for both the economy and the conduct of monetary policy. It opens some major lines of inquiry:

美联储密切关注这一切。我们认识到，人工智能是一个新的变量——可能是一个新的生产要素——将对经济和货币政策执行产生影响。它开启了一些重大研究课题：

Will the application of AI cause a significant, sustained rise in productivity across the economy? And if so, when?

人工智能的应用将导致整个经济体生产率显著、持续地提高吗？如果是的话，何时会实现？

Will token usage be complementary or competitive to labor? Will the next generation of AI models demand even greater capital intensity, or will the models themselves help devise a capital-light solution?

代币的使用对劳动力来说是互补还是竞争？下一代人工智能模型是否需要更高的资本密集度，还是模型本身有助于设计出资本轻量化解决方案？

Among the other yet unknowns is the resulting market structure. It's not obvious where the returns on capital will land or on what timescale. Early on, how much of the surplus goes to owners of scarce assets—AI labs, chipmakers, energy producers, and cloud providers? Over time, how much of that value accrues to businesses and consumers? What are the broad implications for workers and for the employment side of the Fed's mandate?

在其他未知因素中，还有由此产生的市场结构。资本回报将落在何处或按何种时间表实现，目前尚不明朗。早期，盈余有多少流向稀缺资产的所有者——人工智能实验室、芯片制造商、能源生产商和云服务提供商？随着时间推移，这些价值有多少归属于企业和消费者？对工人以及美联储使命中的就业方面有哪些广泛影响？

Likewise, we don't yet know the equilibrium price of the tokens. Might there be a heterogeneity of tokens, such that growing sums will be paid for access to the best models at the frontier? Will token prices for older models fall to the level of their marginal cost?

同样，我们还不知道代币的均衡价格。是否可能存在代币的异质性，使得人们为获取前沿最佳模型而支付越来越多的费用？旧模型的代币价格是否会降至其边际成本的水平？

We will be thinking through these matters with the help of a task force on productivity and jobs. My early check-ins with the leaders of that task force, and the four others, have been encouraging.

我们将借助一个关于生产力和就业的工作组来思考这些问题。我与该工作组以及另外四个工作组的负责人的早期沟通令人鼓舞。

To be clear, though, their recommendations will come later and have no bearing on decisions we make in the current policy conjuncture. But I believe that for future policy challenges, this intellectual investment today will leave us far better prepared.

不过需要明确的是，他们的建议将在稍后提出，并且不会影响我们在当前政策形势下的决策。但我相信，对于未来的政策挑战，今天这种智力投资将使我们做好准备。

**Forward Guidance and Its Stand-ins**

As our task forces go about their work, I am not waiting to introduce innovations at the Fed to make us fit for purpose. To highlight one example, I have set out to change the form and function of the Fed Chairman's so-called forward guidance. You might know about my long-time discomfort with early pronouncements of future policy decisions. I much prefer another path . . . and will make the case for it.

**前瞻性指引及其替代**

随着各工作组开展相关工作，我不会坐等美联储引入创新以使我们符合目标。举一个例子，我已着手改变美联储主席所谓前瞻性指引的形式和功能。你可能知道我长期以来对过早宣布未来政策决定的不适。我更倾向于另一条路径……并且会阐述其理由。

Transparency in communications about future policy decisions is not a virtue unto itself. Communications must be in service to the Fed's paramount responsibility: getting monetary policy right.[5](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/warsh20260828a.htm#fn5)

关于未来政策决策的沟通透明度本身并不是一种美德。沟通必须服务于美联储的首要责任：正确制定货币政策。[5](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/warsh20260828a.htm#fn5)

Forward guidance as a regular practice was adopted by my colleagues and me during the Global Financial Crisis.[6](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/warsh20260828a.htm#fn6) It was essential at the time, and we introduced it with much fanfare. But, as with other legacies of crises past, I believe that the practice has overstayed its welcome.

作为常规做法的前瞻性指引是在全球金融危机期间由我和我的同事们采用的。[6](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/warsh20260828a.htm#fn6) 当时它至关重要，我们隆重推出了它。但是，正如过去危机的其他遗产一样，我认为这种做法已经不再受欢迎。

In normal times, the role of forward guidance should be limited and circumscribed. Otherwise it risks creating ambiguity in the name of clarity. Oversharing policy deliberations and overcommitting to future decisions can lead markets, businesses, and households astray.[7](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/warsh20260828a.htm#fn7) And I believe when policymakers make quasi-commitments on interest rates through the cycle, we inhibit our own freedom to make the right calls when it's time to decide.

在正常时期，前瞻性指引的作用应该受到限制和约束。否则，它可能会以清晰的名义制造模糊。过度分享政策讨论和对未来决策的过度承诺可能会误导市场、企业和家庭。[7](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/warsh20260828a.htm#fn7) 而且我认为，当政策制定者在整个周期中对利率做出准承诺时，我们在需要做出决定的时候限制了自己做出正确判断的自由。

To get policy right, we also need to get the relationship right between financial markets and the central bank. The Fed needs clear market signals, as unfiltered as possible . . . from market internals . . . the level and change in asset prices across sectors . . . the prices and trading volumes of Treasury securities. . . the foreign exchange value of the dollar . . . the cost and availability of credit . . . and the price of a broad set of commodities.

要制定正确的政策，我们还需要正确处理金融市场与中央银行之间的关系。美联储需要清晰的市场信号，尽可能不加过滤……来自市场内部……各行业资产价格的水平与变化……国债的价格和交易量……美元的外汇价值……信贷的成本和可得性……以及广泛商品的价格。

These and other indicators should inform the Fed's near-term outlook on economic activity and inflation throughout the business cycle. They should also reveal the state of broader financial conditions . . . and the risks and uncertainties in the financial cycle.

这些和其他指标应该为美联储对经济活动和整个商业周期内通胀的近期展望提供信息。它们还应揭示更广泛金融状况的状态……以及金融周期中的风险与不确定性。

At the same time, market participants themselves should be tracking real information across the economy. They should draw their own conclusions; form their own expectations of output, employment, and inflation; and stay sharply attuned to risks.

与此同时，市场参与者自己应该跟踪整个经济中的真实信息。他们应该得出自己的结论；形成自己对产出、就业和通胀的预期；并对风险保持高度警觉。

The Fed should be humble and never naïve. The Fed plays an essential role in the economy and the markets. And our tools are powerful. We determine the path of short-term interest rates. And market participants will always try to anticipate what we will do next. But we should not indulge a regime in which market participants are looking primarily to the Fed for their next trade.

美联储应该保持谦逊，绝不要天真。美联储在经济和市场中扮演着至关重要的角色。我们的工具很强大。我们决定短期利率的路径。市场参与者总是会试图预测我们接下来要做什么。但我们不应纵容一种体制，在这种体制下，市场参与者主要指望美联储来指导他们的下一笔交易。

The economic literature has long described the distorting effects: a hall-of-mirrors problem.[8](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/warsh20260828a.htm#fn8) If markets rely materially on the Fed's guidance and the Fed relies on market prices, we are all more likely to be blinded to new developments . . . more likely to be caught unprepared for a turn of events . . . and more likely to commit errors in policymaking.[9](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/warsh20260828a.htm#fn9)

经济学文献早已描述了这种扭曲效应：一个“镜子大厅”问题。[8](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/warsh20260828a.htm#fn8) 如果市场在很大程度上依赖美联储的指引，而美联储依赖市场价格，那么我们所有人都更可能对新的发展视而不见……更可能在事件转折时措手不及……更可能在政策制定中犯错。[9](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/warsh20260828a.htm#fn9)

Perversely, market participants are unlikely to bear the biggest costs of the hall-of-mirrors problem. The most serious harm is likely to befall those without financial assets. If the Fed gets inflation wrong and judges the economy wrong, who gets the worst of it? Not the financial high-fliers. Hard-working Americans are the ones left to deal with inflation that is too high or jobs that suddenly appear less secure.

反常的是，市场参与者不太可能承担“镜子大厅”问题的最重大代价。最严重的伤害很可能落在那些没有金融资产的人身上。如果美联储判断错了通胀，也判断错了经济，谁受的损失最大？不是那些金融界的高飞者。辛勤工作的美国人才是不得不应对过高通胀或突然显得不那么稳定的工作的人。

So, if forward guidance is ill-suited to normal times, then how about the new Fed chief commits—at the very least—to an explicit reaction function? Surely, he should tell us his interest rate path—if, say, the data were to come in hot or cold.

因此，如果前瞻性指引不适合正常时期，那么新美联储主席至少应该承诺一个明确反应函数？当然，他应该告诉我们利率路径——比如说，如果数据表现过热或过冷。

I wish our understanding of the economy were so precise as to provide a mechanical, tried-and-true answer—that some simple function like a Taylor rule could be rigorously relied upon. But our knowledge just doesn't extend that far—at least not yet—and the factors most relevant to the proper conduct of monetary policy change over time.

我希望我们对经济的理解如此精确，以至于能提供一个机械的、经过验证的答案——像泰勒规则这样简单的函数可以被严格依赖。但我们的知识还没有延伸到那一步——至少目前还没有——而且与货币政策适当实施最相关的因素会随时间变化。

Providing forecasts to illustrate the Fed's reaction function works better in theory than in practice, better in the lab than in the field. I'm not alone in noticing that forward guidance in 2021, to cite one example, might well have slowed the policy response to high inflation.[10](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/warsh20260828a.htm#fn10)

提供预测以说明美联储的反应函数，理论上比实践中更有效，实验室里比实际现场更有效。我并不是唯一注意到，举个例子，2021年的前瞻性指引可能延缓了政策对高通胀的回应。[10](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/warsh20260828a.htm#fn10)

In my term as Chairman, my colleagues and I will endeavor to construct more reliable models and more robust rules to guide policy decisions. We'll do this knowing that accuracy in economic forecasting is still just an aspiration. With so much changing so fast in geopolitics, global supply chains, and technology, it's wise to be modest about what we can and cannot know.

在我担任主席期间，我和同事们将努力构建更可靠的模型和更稳健的规则来指导政策决策。我们这样做时深知，经济预测的准确性仍然只是一个愿望。地缘政治、全球供应链和技术领域变化如此之快，我们必须对自己能知道和不能知道的事情保持谦逊。

In the same spirit, we should receive the full range of ideas on matters that may inform the Fed's monetary policy decisions. If the aim is optimal decisionmaking, we should not crowd out views on the economy.

同样，我们应该就那些可能影响美联储货币政策决策的问题广泛听取各种意见。如果目标是优化决策，我们就不应排斥对经济的各种观点。

How, then, to chart a better path to policy? In the balance of my remarks, I will share some key principles that guide my thinking on the appropriate conduct of monetary policy . . . then offer my promised assessment of the economy.

那么，如何规划一条更好的政策路径？在接下来的发言中，我将分享一些指导我对货币政策适当实施思考的关键原则……然后对我承诺的经济形势评估进行阐述。

**Key Principles**

Turning to principles . . .

**关键原则**

现在谈谈原则……

First, I've noticed that, in this line of work, yesterday's news has a way of getting mistaken for what is happening right now. The challenge is to know the difference. In other words, we must interrogate reality to make sure we are not setting forward-looking policy based on stale or inaccurate data. Nor should we rely on isolated data points. Trends matter most. The Fed is a decisionmaking agency. We make choices amid uncertainty, and the data upon which we draw must be as relevant, contemporaneous, accurate, and actionable as possible.[11](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/warsh20260828a.htm#fn11)

首先，我注意到，在这一行工作中，昨天的新闻很容易被误认为正在发生的事情。挑战在于知道区别。换句话说，我们必须审视现实，确保我们不是基于过时或不准确的数据来制定前瞻性政策。我们也不应依赖孤立的数据点。趋势最重要。美联储是一个决策机构。我们在不确定性中做出选择，而我们依赖的数据必须尽可能相关、及时、准确和可操作。[11](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/warsh20260828a.htm#fn11)

Second, the Federal Reserve's actions are intended to ensure that the aggregate demand side of the economy is broadly consistent with aggregate supply. However, all we observe directly is activity. We never see, and can only infer, what's really happening on the supply side. Hence, evaluating the current and expected balance between aggregate supply and demand is imprecise.[12](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/warsh20260828a.htm#fn12)

第二，美联储的行动旨在确保经济的总需求方面与总供给大致一致。然而，我们直接观察到的只是经济活动。我们从未看到，只能推断供给侧实际发生的情况。因此，评估总供给与总需求当前及预期的平衡是不精确的。[12](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/warsh20260828a.htm#fn12)

Third, there should be no misunderstanding: The Fed's price-stability objective of 2 percent, as measured by the personal consumption expenditures (PCE) price index, is a firm, fixed target. Let's be equally clear about another aspect of the objective: Price stability is not self-executing, nor is inflation necessarily mean-reverting. It is the Fed's job to deliver stable prices.

第三，不应有任何误解：美联储2%的价格稳定目标，以个人消费支出（PCE）价格指数衡量，是一个坚定、固定的目标。让我们同样明确该目标的另一个方面：价格稳定不是自动实现的，通胀也不一定均值回归。实现价格稳定是美联储的工作。

Fourth, the Fed also bears responsibility for maximum employment. Achieving both sides of our mandate over the medium term is not an either/or proposition. I do not believe that the Fed's dual mandate works at cross-purposes. After all, high inflation itself is very harmful to economic prosperity.

第四，美联储还承担着实现最大就业的责任。在中期内实现我们双重使命的两个方面并非非此即彼的选择。我不认为美联储的双重使命会相互矛盾。毕竟，高通胀本身对经济繁荣非常有害。

Fifth, short-term interest rates are the predominant tool to achieve the dual mandate. Unconventional policies to spur economic activity may suit genuine crises but should otherwise be used sparingly, if at all.

第五，短期利率是实现双重使命的主要工具。非常规政策可能适用于真正的危机，但除此之外应谨慎使用，甚至尽量不用。

Sixth, money matters. It's not fashionable these days, but my view is that money has something important to do with monetary policy.[13](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/warsh20260828a.htm#fn13) We should pay attention to money created by the central bank and money that comes from the banking and financial systems.[14](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/warsh20260828a.htm#fn14) It's true that financial innovations and other factors alter the mechanics that link the monetary base, the velocity of money, and the broader economy. But that is scarcely a reason to ignore the ultimate effects of money on financial conditions and prices.

第六，货币很重要。这些天这并不流行，但我认为货币与货币政策有着重要的关系。[13](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/warsh20260828a.htm#fn13) 我们应该关注中央银行创造的货币以及来自银行和金融体系的货币。[14](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/warsh20260828a.htm#fn14) 确实，金融创新和其他因素改变了货币基础、货币流通速度与更广泛经济之间的关联机制。但这并不意味着可以忽视货币对金融状况和价格的最终影响。

Finally, a quieter Fed, more purposeful in its communications, is better able to meet its objectives. And we can be held accountable for delivering on our remit—the only true test of our credibility. To borrow a line from General Chuck Yeager, "At the moment of truth, there are either reasons or results."[15](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/warsh20260828a.htm#fn15)

最后，一个更安静、沟通更有目的性的美联储，能更好地实现其目标。我们也可以为履行我们的职责负责——这是对我们信誉唯一真正的考验。借用查克·耶格尔将军的一句话：“在关键时刻，要么有理由，要么有结果。”[15](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/warsh20260828a.htm#fn15)

**The Economy Today**

Now, given these principles, how do I read the economy today? What's really going on outside the window?[16](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/warsh20260828a.htm#fn16)

**当今经济**

现在，基于这些原则，我如何看待当今经济？窗外究竟发生了什么？[16](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/warsh20260828a.htm#fn16)

You may have read in the July minutes the unanimous view of the FOMC:[17](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/warsh20260828a.htm#fn17) Labor markets were stable, and output was solid. But inflation remained too high. A good majority of my colleagues and I thought the wiser course was to await new information in the intermeeting period—especially given possible developments in supply chains, investment flows, and geopolitics—before deciding whether a change in interest rate policy was advisable. And we expressed our joint readiness to act as circumstances might require.

你们可能已在7月会议纪要中读到FOMC的一致观点：[17](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/warsh20260828a.htm#fn17) 劳动力市场稳定，产出稳健。但通胀仍然过高。我和我的大多数同事认为，更明智的做法是等待两次会议之间的新信息——尤其是考虑到供应链、投资流动和地缘政治可能的发展——然后再决定是否适宜调整利率政策。我们表示，如有需要，我们随时准备采取行动。

For my part, today I am impressed by the overall performance of the economy, which appears to have strengthened. One indicator of strength is how well an economy holds up to shocks. On that score, both Main Street and Wall Street have been remarkably resilient.

就我而言，今天我对经济的整体表现印象深刻，经济似乎已经走强。衡量经济强劲程度的一个指标是它抵御冲击的能力。在这一点上，主街（Main Street）和华尔街（Wall Street）都表现出非凡的韧性。

Several observations:

几点观察：

Business capital expenditures—the seed corn of future economic growth—are rising rapidly. The four-quarter change in investment in equipment and intangibles has been around 9 percent, its highest growth rate since 2021. More than half of the cap-ex growth this year can likely be ascribed to the buildout related to AI.

企业资本支出——未来经济增长的种子——正在快速增长。设备和无形资产投资的四个季度变化约为9%，是自2021年以来的最高增长率。今年资本支出增长的一半以上可能归因于与人工智能相关的建设。

For firms in the S&P 500, profits have grown by more than 20 percent over the past year. Profit margins are quite elevated, relative to history. Overall equity market volatility is low. We're staying keenly focused on market internals, watching performance across sectors.

对于标普500指数中的公司而言，过去一年利润增长超过20%。利润率相对于历史水平相当高。整体股市波动性较低。我们密切关注市场内部情况，观察各板块的表现。

Expectations for growth in both cap-ex and corporate earnings are running quite high. I will continue to watch the change in their growth rates, the second derivative. The follow-on effects on asset prices, business confidence, consumer income, and spending are equally important to gauge.

对资本支出和企业盈利增长的预期都相当高。我将继续关注其增长率的变化，即二阶导数。对资产价格、商业信心、消费者收入和支出的后续影响同样需要进行评估。

Credit spreads on corporate bonds and leveraged loans are near the low ends of their historical ranges, and issuance volumes in these markets have been quite strong this year. Looking beyond fixed-income markets to the banking business, in the July Senior Loan Officer Opinion Survey on Bank Lending Practices, banks tell us that standards for commercial and industrial loans are on the easier end of their historical range. That helps explain the growth we've seen this year in those loans. Credit and loan markets are showing few signs of policy restraint.

公司债券和杠杆贷款的信用利差接近其历史区间的低端，今年这些市场的发行量相当强劲。从固定收益市场转向银行业务，在7月份的高级贷款专员意见调查（Senior Loan Officer Opinion Survey on Bank Lending Practices）中，银行表示，商业和工业贷款的放贷标准处于其历史区间的较宽松一端。这有助于解释我们今年在这些贷款中看到的增长。信贷和贷款市场几乎没有显示政策限制的迹象。

Certain sectors—like housing and agriculture—are showing strains. But, on balance, I would be hard pressed to describe broad financial conditions as restrictive.

某些行业——如住房和农业——正显示出压力。但总体而言，我很难将广泛的金融状况描述为限制性的。

Real consumer spending has been healthy despite the shocks, increasing more than 2 percent over the past four quarters. Combining consumption with the brisk investment we've observed, private domestic final purchases (PDFP) has also risen. PDFP has increased at a pace of nearly 3 percent so far this calendar year. That's a measure that typically carries more signal than gross domestic product, and the trend here too is positive.

尽管受到冲击，实际消费者支出依然健康，过去四个季度增长超过2%。将消费与我们观察到的强劲投资相结合，私人国内最终购买（PDFP）也有所上升。今年迄今为止，PDFP以接近3%的速度增长。这一指标通常比国内生产总值包含更多信号，其趋势也是积极的。

On the employment side of the Fed's dual mandate, our country is doing well. Labor markets are quite stable. The jobless rate, at 4.1 percent, remains low by historical standards and has not changed much for a couple of years. Unemployment claims, on a four-week average—an empirically robust real-time indicator—are near their lowest level in decades.

在美联储双重使命的就业方面，我国表现良好。劳动力市场相当稳定。失业率为4.1%，按历史标准仍处于低位，并且多年来变化不大。四周平均初请失业金人数——一个实证上稳健的实时指标——接近几十年来的最低水平。

In my view, the relatively low turnover in today's labor market is partly a result of the significant rematching between employers and employees that happened at scale in the post-pandemic environment.

在我看来，当今劳动力市场流动率相对较低，部分原因是疫情后环境中雇主与雇员之间大规模重新匹配的结果。

When labor supply is barely growing, monthly job gains are naturally going to run low. There are always areas of concern in the labor market—for example, among recent graduates. In general, though, people who want to work, by and large, are holding or finding jobs. They may well be concerned about possible future labor disruptions, but as of now, I believe the labor markets are consistent with full employment.

当劳动力供应几乎不增长时，每月的就业增长自然就会偏低。劳动力市场中总会有一些令人担忧的领域——例如，在应届毕业生之中。但总体而言，想要工作的人大多都保有工作或能找到工作。他们或许担心未来可能出现的劳动力市场 disruptions，但就目前而言，我认为劳动力市场与充分就业是一致的。

But on the price-stability side of our mandate, the numbers are more concerning. The Fed's preferred measure of inflation, the 12-month change in the PCE price index, stands at 3.7 percent, while the six-month change is 4.1 percent. The comparable measures from the consumer price index (CPI) are also elevated, as are the core measures of both PCE and CPI inflation. None of these measures are perfect, but they all tell a similar story: Inflation is running above our 2 percent target. So the Fed's predominant focus right now should be on prices.

但在我们使命的价格稳定方面，数据更令人担忧。美联储偏爱的通胀指标——PCE价格指数的12个月变化——为3.7%，而6个月变化为4.1%。消费者价格指数（CPI）的可比指标也处于高位，PCE和CPI通胀的核心指标同样如此。这些指标没有一个是完美的，但它们都讲述了一个类似的故事：通胀运行在我们2%的目标之上。因此，美联储目前的主要关注点应该是价格。

The job for policymakers is to capture underlying trend inflation—that is, the generalized change in prices in the economy, unaffected by idiosyncratic factors. We want to gauge whether underlying inflation is rising, falling, or stuck in place. We also want to understand not just the direction of travel, but also the speed. Each of these broad inflation measures has fallen significantly from their 2022 heights. But progress over the past two years has been modest.

政策制定者的任务是捕捉潜在的通胀趋势——即经济中价格的普遍变化，不受特殊因素影响。我们要评估潜在通胀是在上升、下降还是停滞不前。我们不仅要了解变动的方向，还要了解变动的速度。上述每一项广义通胀指标都已从2022年的高点大幅回落。但过去两年的进展有限。

And while this summer's PCE and CPI readings were better than expected, they do not tell me that underlying trends have meaningfully improved.

尽管今年夏天的PCE和CPI读数好于预期，但这些数据并不能告诉我潜在趋势已显著改善。

The data also show moderate wage growth. But in tracking underlying inflation, wage growth has not proven a reliable indicator of future inflation for a very long time.[18](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/warsh20260828a.htm#fn18)

数据还显示工资增长温和。但在追踪潜在通胀方面，工资增长长期以来并未被证明是未来通胀的可靠指标。[18](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/warsh20260828a.htm#fn18)

To try to gauge underlying inflation, I find it instructive to disaggregate the 199 individual components of the PCE price measure. Over the past 12 months, 54 percent of goods and services in the PCE basket showed price increases above 3 percent. This is well below the post-pandemic highs of about 77 percent, but it remains well above the level of 32 percent in the two decades that preceded the pandemic.

为了试图衡量潜在通胀，我发现将PCE价格指标的199个单独组成部分进行分解很有启发性。在过去12个月中，PCE篮子中54%的商品和服务价格涨幅超过3%。这远低于疫情后约77%的高点，但仍远高于疫情前二十年32%的水平。

Looking over just the past six months, the conclusion is similar: Of goods and services in the PCE basket, 49 percent showed annualized price increases above 3 percent. Again, this is well below the post-pandemic highs but still quite elevated.

仅看过去六个月，结论类似：在PCE篮子中的商品和服务中，49%的年化价格涨幅超过3%。同样，这远低于疫情后的高点，但仍处于相当高的水平。

The recent rise in overall commodity prices also bears watching. What we need to judge is whether trends indicate upside inflation risks.

近期整体大宗商品价格的上涨也值得关注。我们需要判断的是，这些趋势是否预示着通胀上行风险。

It matters, too, whether the inflation readings of the past five-plus years have seeped into expectations. The good news is that measures of inflation expectations in the medium term, by and large, look stable. And inflation compensation measures from the swaps market send a strong and similar message.

同样重要的是，过去五年多的通胀数据是否已渗透到预期中。好消息是，中期通胀预期指标大体上看起来稳定。互换市场的通胀补偿指标也发出了强烈而类似的信息。

Especially in light of recent developments, it is a credit to the Fed as an institution—and consistent with the best of the Fed's traditions—that market prices show confidence that we will deliver price stability. And I can assure you . . . they're right.

尤其是鉴于近期的事态发展，市场价格显示出对我们实现价格稳定的信心，这是对美联储作为一个机构的赞誉，也符合美联储最优秀的传统。我可以向你们保证……它们是对的。

The thing about market measures of inflation expectations in economic history is that they tend to look strong and durable until they don't. Those expectations are not pushed around easily, and right now they are well anchored. But they must be closely minded. It's the Fed's job to make sure that inflation expectations do not get unanchored.

关于经济史上市场对通胀预期的衡量，有一点是：它们往往看起来强劲而持久，直到它们不再如此。这些预期不容易被撼动，而且目前它们锚定良好。但必须密切关注。确保通胀预期不会失锚是美联储的职责。

There is one signal nobody can miss: The responsibility for 65 months of sustained, elevated inflation sits squarely with the central bank. And that is where it belongs.

有一个信号无人能够忽视：持续65个月的高通胀的责任完全在于中央银行。而且这正是责任的归属。

Here is my standard: We must be confident that underlying inflation is moving to our objective, clearly and at sufficient speed. Otherwise, we have work to do. That's our job . . . our mandate . . . and our charge to keep.

这是我的标准：我们必须确信，潜在通胀正明确地并以足够快的速度向我们的目标移动。否则，我们就有工作要做。这是我们的工作……我们的使命……以及我们要保持的责任。

**Conclusion**

I stand here today committed to a discipline, not to a decision.

**结论**

我今天站在这里，承诺的是纪律，而不是一个决定。

My Fed colleagues and I are hardly the first to hold these positions in a time of great consequence. We are determined to redeem the time by doing our very best work.

我和我的美联储同事远非第一批在意义重大的时代担任这些职务的人。我们决心通过尽自己最大的努力来善用这段时光。

We take our responsibility seriously, with humility and with resolve. So much depends on choices we make. Sound monetary policy helps households and businesses to prosper. When carried out effectively, it broadens and deepens the momentum of our economy . . . and helps to secure America's leadership in the world. And I know that our country needs us to think carefully and act wisely.

我们以谦逊和决心认真对待我们的责任。太多事情取决于我们做出的选择。稳健的货币政策有助于家庭和企业繁荣。当有效实施时，它会扩大和深化我们经济的动力……并有助于确保美国在世界上的领导地位。我知道我们的国家需要我们深思熟虑、明智行动。

It is a tremendous honor to serve once again at the Federal Reserve. I am truly grateful for the encouragement and good counsel I've received from my colleagues . . . and from so many of you in this room. For that, and for your kind attention this morning, I thank you.

能够再次在美联储任职是我莫大的荣幸。我衷心感谢同事们以及今天在座的各位给予我的鼓励和宝贵建议。对此，以及感谢大家今天上午的专注聆听，我深表感谢。
