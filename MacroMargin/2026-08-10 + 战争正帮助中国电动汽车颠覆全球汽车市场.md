### 中文译文

## 战争正帮助中国电动汽车颠覆全球汽车市场

### 高油价正助推中国电动汽车出口

Aug. 10, 2026 5:30 am ET

![Workers inspect Xiaomi SU7 electric vehicles after rolling off the assembly line.](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3b881cc90b148187b867f39817d11199/3b881cc9-0b14-8107-94f0-d8c9020d21ec-f75f45ab7cb35e07aae1ed9b90d767d4.jpg)

在中国，超过一半的新车销售是电动汽车。Qilai Shen/Bloomberg News

中国电动汽车制造商是不希望伊朗战争很快结束的群体之一。

他们今年年初面临电动汽车库存过剩和国内需求疲软。[中东危机](https://www.wsj.com/world/middle-east/can-irans-diminished-diplomats-deliver-a-hormuz-deal-392ed258?mod=article_inline)正在帮助他们通过出口摆脱困境：中国汽车制造商将过剩供应推向海外市场，那里高昂的汽油价格正促使更多驾驶者购买电动汽车。传统汽车制造商的市场份额是最大的牺牲品。

国际能源署数据显示，2026年上半年中国电动汽车出口与去年同期相比增长120%。自战争开始以来，巴西、澳大利亚、韩国和越南的电动汽车销量大约翻了一番。根据国际能源署数据，哥伦比亚的电动汽车销量翻了两番。

该能源机构现在预计，2026年电动汽车将占全球新车销量的29%，高于去年的25%。在中国，从汽油车转向电动汽车的进程更进一步，目前超过一半的新车销售是电动汽车。

中国的汽车制造商希望看到快速的中国式电动化在其他地方实现。它们控制着新兴市场60%的电动汽车销量，而传统内燃机汽车仅为10%，因此它们最有可能从电动汽车的广泛普及中获益。

高额关税将中国汽车制造商挡在美国市场之外，同时保护了[美国汽车制造商](https://www.wsj.com/business/autos/ford-plans-a-new-four-door-version-of-mustang-023cbf28?mod=article_inline)（如[福特](https://www.wsj.com/market-data/quotes/F)）免受本土市场激烈竞争的冲击。自去年第三季度拜登时代的税收补贴到期以来，美国电动汽车销量也一直在下滑。

即使油价上涨，购买电动汽车对美国普通司机来说仍然不划算。ClearView Energy Partners的分析显示，根据当前的汽车价格和汽车贷款利率，除非汽油价格达到每加仑约4.80美元，否则电动汽车将无法与内燃机汽车竞争。

![Two men look at the BYD Denza Z9 GT electric vehicle displayed in a cold room.](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3b881cc90b148187b867f39817d11199/3b881cc9-0b14-8112-95ef-e405250b1fe7-625a4d66d82733f8fe30ed0c32ae894a.jpg)

中国汽车品牌在欧洲道路上越来越常见。沈其来/彭博新闻

但在欧洲和英国，关税不那么苛刻，包括吉利和比亚迪在内的中国汽车品牌在路上出现的频率更高。6月份，根据欧洲汽车制造商协会的数据，吉利、[上汽集团](https://www.wsj.com/market-data/quotes/CN/XSHG/600104)、比亚迪、奇瑞和零跑这五个中国品牌占欧盟和英国新车注册量的12.1%，高于去年同期的7.7%。[特斯拉](https://www.wsj.com/market-data/quotes/TSLA) [TSLA0.46%上涨；绿色向上三角](https://www.wsj.com/market-data/quotes/TSLA)的新注册量也有所上升。

欧洲品牌[大众](https://www.wsj.com/market-data/quotes/XE/XETR/VOW3) [VOW3-1.49%下跌；红色向下三角](https://www.wsj.com/market-data/quotes/XE/XETR/VOW3)和[雷诺](https://www.wsj.com/market-data/quotes/FR/XPAR/RNO) [RNO-0.03%下跌；红色向下三角](https://www.wsj.com/market-data/quotes/FR/XPAR/RNO)以及福特的新车注册份额在此期间有所下滑。大众集团在中国的业务下滑的同时，越来越需要捍卫本土市场。由于本土品牌的激烈竞争和消费支出疲软，大众第二季度向中国的汽车交付量同比下降37%。这家德国公司的股价已跌至2011年以来的最低水平。

大众和[菲亚特](https://www.wsj.com/market-data/quotes/STLA) [STLA-0.03%下跌；红色向下三角](https://www.wsj.com/market-data/quotes/STLA)的所有者Stellantis等欧洲汽车公司将很难反击。由于中国劳动力成本低、电动汽车供应链更加完善，在中国制造电动汽车的成本比欧洲低约35%。中国汽车制造商也愿意以极低的运营利润率运营，专注于抢占市场份额。

[东南亚](https://www.wsj.com/topics/place/southeast-asia)各国政府正遭受石油危机的严重打击，希望改善能源安全。它们将电动汽车视为减少对进口汽油依赖的一种方式，并正在出台政策鼓励司机购买电动汽车。

据当地新闻报道，泰国削减了进口电动汽车的消费税，该国总理最近将自己的劳斯莱斯换成了比亚迪，作为全国节能努力的一部分。老挝已全面禁止在年内剩余时间进口汽油车。柬埔寨宣布了友好型电动汽车政策，如降低关税。

这对日本品牌来说是个问题，例如[丰田](https://www.wsj.com/market-data/quotes/JP/XTKS/7203) [7203 0.03%增加；向上三角形](https://www.wsj.com/market-data/quotes/JP/XTKS/7203)，东南亚是重要市场，但即使在战前，他们在那里也在失去份额。2023年至2025年间，中国汽车制造商在马来西亚、印度尼西亚、泰国、越南、菲律宾和新加坡的新车销售中的份额从平均4%增至11%。普华永道数据显示，日本品牌的市场份额在那段时间下降了10个百分点以上，降至57%。

当今汽车行业发生的事情与十多年前欧洲太阳能电池板业务的颠覆有相似之处。北京的大规模补贴造成太阳能电池板产能过剩，导致中国国内的无序竞争和价格战。随后，中国公司将剩余产品充斥海外市场。全球消费者受益于更便宜的商品，但代价是掏空当地产业。

高油价使汽车保有率相对较低的国家的消费者更有可能将价格合理的中国电动汽车作为他们的第一辆车。一些消费者可能会完全跳过汽油车。

未来十年，新兴市场预计将占全球汽车需求的60%。传统汽车制造商要想分得一杯羹，将面临艰难得多的任务。

## War Is Helping Chinese EVs Upend the Global Car Market

## **War Is Helping Chinese EVs Upend the Global Car Market**

### High gasoline prices are giving a boost to China’s electric-vehicle exports

By

Aug. 10, 2026 5:30 am ET

In China, more than half of all new cars sold are electric. Qilai Shen/Bloomberg News

One group that doesn’t want the Iran war to end soon is China’s electric-vehicle makers.

They began the year with a glut of EVs on their hands and weak demand at home. The [crisis in the Middle East](https://www.wsj.com/world/middle-east/can-irans-diminished-diplomats-deliver-a-hormuz-deal-392ed258?mod=article_inline) is helping them export their way out of trouble: Chinese carmakers are sending excess supply to overseas markets, where high gasoline prices are prompting more drivers to buy EVs. The market share of traditional carmakers is the big casualty.

China’s EV exports rose 120% in the first half of 2026 from the same period of last year, data from the International Energy Agency shows. EV sales have roughly doubled in Brazil, Australia, Korea and Vietnam since the war began. EV sales in Colombia have quadrupled, based on IEA data.

The energy agency now expects EVs to make up 29% of new-car sales globally in 2026, an increase from 25% last year. The shift away from gasoline cars is further along in China, where more than half of all new cars sold are now electric.

China’s car manufacturers would love to see rapid, Chinese-style electrification happen elsewhere. They control 60% of EV sales across emerging markets—compared with just 10% for traditional combustion-engine cars—so they have the most to gain from widespread EV adoption.

High tariffs are keeping them out of the U.S. and protecting [American carmakers](https://www.wsj.com/business/autos/ford-plans-a-new-four-door-version-of-mustang-023cbf28?mod=article_inline) such as [Ford](https://www.wsj.com/market-data/quotes/F) from an onslaught of competition in their home market. EV sales have also been falling in the U.S. since a Biden-era tax subsidy expired in the third quarter of last year.

Buying an EV still doesn’t pencil out for a typical American driver even with higher prices at the pump. Based on current car prices and auto-loan rates, EVs won’t be competitive with internal-combustion cars unless gasoline hits roughly $4.80 a gallon, according to ClearView Energy Partners analysis.

Chinese car brands are appearing more frequently on European roads. Qilai Shen/Bloomberg News

But in Europe and the U.K., where tariffs are less punitive, Chinese car brands including Geely and BYD are appearing more frequently on the roads. In June, five Chinese brands, Geely, [SAIC Motor](https://www.wsj.com/market-data/quotes/CN/XSHG/600104), BYD, Chery and Leapmotor, accounted for 12.1% of new-car registrations in the EU and U.K., up from 7.7% the same month of last year, based on numbers from the European Automobile Manufacturers’ Association. New registrations also rose for [Tesla](https://www.wsj.com/market-data/quotes/TSLA) [TSLA ](https://www.wsj.com/market-data/quotes/TSLA)[0.46%](https://www.wsj.com/market-data/quotes/TSLA)[increase; green up pointing triangle](https://www.wsj.com/market-data/quotes/TSLA).

The share of new-car registrations for European brands [Volkswagen](https://www.wsj.com/market-data/quotes/XE/XETR/VOW3) [VOW3 ](https://www.wsj.com/market-data/quotes/XE/XETR/VOW3)[-1.49%](https://www.wsj.com/market-data/quotes/XE/XETR/VOW3)[decrease; red down pointing triangle](https://www.wsj.com/market-data/quotes/XE/XETR/VOW3) and [Renault](https://www.wsj.com/market-data/quotes/FR/XPAR/RNO) [RNO ](https://www.wsj.com/market-data/quotes/FR/XPAR/RNO)[-0.03%](https://www.wsj.com/market-data/quotes/FR/XPAR/RNO)[decrease; red down pointing triangle](https://www.wsj.com/market-data/quotes/FR/XPAR/RNO), as well as Ford, slipped over the period. Volkswagen Group increasingly has to defend its home turf at the same time as its business in China sinks. Volkswagen’s vehicle deliveries to China fell 37% from a year earlier in the second quarter because of intense competition from local brands and weak consumer spending. The German company’s shares have fallen to levels last seen in 2011.

European car companies such as Volkswagen and [Fiat](https://www.wsj.com/market-data/quotes/STLA) [STLA ](https://www.wsj.com/market-data/quotes/STLA)[-0.03%](https://www.wsj.com/market-data/quotes/STLA)[decrease; red down pointing triangle](https://www.wsj.com/market-data/quotes/STLA) owner Stellantis will find it tough to fight back. Making an EV is around 35% cheaper in China than in Europe because of the country’s low labor costs and more robust EV supply chain. China’s carmakers are also willing to operate on razor-thin operating margins while they focus on winning market share.

Governments in [Southeast Asia](https://www.wsj.com/topics/place/southeast-asia), where economies have been hit hard by the oil crisis, want to improve their energy security. They see EVs as a way to reduce their dependence on imported gasoline and are rolling out policies to encourage drivers to buy them.

Thailand cut excise taxes on imported electric cars and the country’s prime minister recently swapped his Rolls-Royce for a BYD as part of a national push to save energy, according to local news reports. Laos has banned the import of gasoline cars altogether for the rest of the year. Cambodia has announced EV-friendly policies such as lower customs duties.

This is a problem for Japanese brands such as [Toyota](https://www.wsj.com/market-data/quotes/JP/XTKS/7203) [7203 0.03%increase; up pointing triangle](https://www.wsj.com/market-data/quotes/JP/XTKS/7203), for whom Southeast Asia is an important market, but who were losing share there even before the war. Between 2023 and 2025, Chinese carmakers increased their share of new-car sales in Malaysia, Indonesia, Thailand, Vietnam, the Philippines and Singapore from 4% on average to 11%. Japanese brands have seen their share fall more than 10 percentage points to 57% over that time, data from PwC shows.

What is happening today in the car industry has echoes of the disruption of Europe’s solar-panel business more than a decade ago. Beijing’s generous subsidies created overcapacity in solar-panel production, which led to disorderly competition and price wars inside China. Chinese companies then flooded overseas markets with the surplus. Global consumers benefited from cheaper goods, but at the expense of hollowing out local industries.

High gasoline prices are making it more likely that consumers in countries that have relatively low rates of car ownership will buy affordable Chinese EV’s as their first cars. Some consumers might leapfrog gasoline cars altogether.

Emerging markets are expected to account for 60% of global car demand over the next decade. Traditional carmakers face a much tougher job getting a slice of the action.
