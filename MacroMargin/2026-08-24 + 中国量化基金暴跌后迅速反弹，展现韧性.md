### 中文译文

## 中国量化基金暴跌后迅速反弹，展现韧性

### **要点**

- 中国的量化对冲基金在7月暴跌中遭受巨额损失后正在反弹，所有最受欢迎的策略均跑赢基准。
- 截至8月7日当周，提供指数增强策略的692只量化基金平均回报率为9.1%，较股票指数平均回报率高2.9个百分点。
- 随着市场正常化，中国量化行业的反弹可能进一步延续，但复苏仍不均衡，截至8月7日，不到8%的量化纯多头基金完全修复了7月份的平均亏损。
中国的量化对冲基金在7月暴跌中遭受[巨额损失](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-07/quant-crash-in-china-sends-deepseek-founder-s-funds-down-20)后正在反弹，所有最受欢迎的策略均录得高于基准的回报。

提供所谓指数增强策略的量化基金跑赢了全市场的股票指标。截至8月7日当周，深圳拍拍网投资管理有限公司（Shenzhen PaiPaiWang Investment & Management Co.）追踪的692只此类产品的平均回报率达9.1%。

这比股票指数的平均回报率高2.9个百分点，表明量化基金为投资者提供超额回报（即alpha）的承诺在动荡的7月后终于兑现。该国许多纯多头量化基金在抛售中暴跌，平均损失17%，因全球投资者纷纷抛售与人工智能相关的股票。

截至8月14日当周，招商期货有限公司（China Merchants Futures Co.）追踪的所有七种指数增强策略均产生了alpha。该公司表示，挂钩中证A500和中证500的指数增强基金均跑赢基准1.2个百分点。

据PaiPaiWang称，中国2.6万亿元人民币（合3870亿美元）量化行业的反弹可能会随着市场正常化而进一步扩大，但复苏仍不均衡。这表明最弱的参与者面临艰难的回升。7月份的暴跌表明，拥挤的交易和不断变化的市场结构会多么迅速地[压倒](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-21/quant-hedge-fund-free-fall-spooks-wealthy-investors-in-china)原本多元化的投资组合。

跟踪对冲基金的PaiPaiWang将反弹归因于三股力量：7月情绪驱动抛售后的超卖水平技术反弹、流动性的回归，以及在此次暴跌中失效的量化因子（尤其是量价信号）的恢复。

许多量化基金青睐的小盘股指数中证1000（CSI 1000）在本月前两周上涨10%，此前在7月暴跌20%。大盘股指数沪深300（CSI 300）同期在下跌8%后也回升了2%。

总部位于上海的明汯投资管理公司（Mingshi Investment Management）提供了一个例子，说明7月损害基金的同一模型能多么迅速地从市场反弹中受益。据彭博社看到的一封致投资者的信，其全市场量化选股策略在8月第一周上涨了16.6%。

该公司将其7月的回撤部分归因于模型生成的科技股倾向，这一敞口在上半年带来了强劲回报，但上个月遭受损失。

### **不干预**

明汯表示，在暴跌期间没有覆盖模型，而是让策略继续对信号做出反应，包括在恐慌期间增加科技敞口，然后在8月情况变化时自然轮换至其他行业。

该公司在信中表示：“市场表现再次印证了量化的本质——让数据驱动的算法策略模型做决策，而不是依赖主观择时或人工干预。”

该产品在截至8月14日当周进一步上涨2.1%，将今年以来的回报率扩大至25.4%，在[国联民生证券股份有限公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/1456%20HK%20Equity)追踪的65家量化基金中位居首位。

根据该券商汇编的数据，总部位于杭州的[Everon Quant](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/OkIwvurTtb_CCG6_Rzn37Q)在沪深300、中证500和中证1000指数中，本周实现了最强劲且最持续的超额收益反弹。其超额收益跑赢沪深300达1.96个百分点。总部位于上海的Hainan Semimartingale Private Fund Management LP跑赢中证500达2.4个百分点，令其年初至今超额收益达到17个百分点，为该指数最佳表现。

“Semimartingale的模型不会根据市场状况进行人工干预，”该公司在一条短信中表示。“随着市场状况企稳，超额收益呈现自然回升。”

Mingshi和Everon的代表谢绝置评。

不过，整体复苏仍不完整。据排排网（PaiPaiWang）的数据，截至8月7日，不足8%的量化多头基金已完全修复7月份平均16%的亏损。数据显示，这一比例低于所有对冲基金中已恢复的13.5%。

中小盘股的全面上涨，而非少数科技股的集中上涨，将有利于指数增强和多元化选股策略。动量、流动性和波动率因子之间异常高的相关性进一步缓和，将有助于多因子模型恢复其在7月失去的部分分散化效应。

## China’s Quant Funds Snap Back in Sign of Resilience After Rout

### **Takeaways**

- China’s quantitative hedge funds are bouncing back after suffering steep losses during a July rout, with all of the most popular strategies posting higher returns than benchmarks.
- The average of 692 quant funds offering index enhancement strategies returned 9.1% in the week ended Aug. 7, 2.9 percentage points higher than the average return on the stock indexes.
- The rebound in China’s quant sector may extend further as markets normalize, but the recovery remains uneven, with less than 8% of quant long-only funds having fully repaired their average loss in July as of Aug. 7.
China’s quantitative hedge funds are bouncing back after suffering [steep losses](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-07/quant-crash-in-china-sends-deepseek-founder-s-funds-down-20) during a July rout, with all of the most popular strategies posting higher returns than benchmarks.

Quant funds offering so-called index enhancement strategies beat stock gauges across the market. The average of 692 such products tracked by Shenzhen PaiPaiWang Investment & Management Co. returned 9.1% in the week ended Aug. 7.

That was 2.9 percentage points higher than the average return on the stock indexes, showing that quants’ promise of offering investors excess returns — known as alpha — is coming good after a rocky July. Many of the country’s long-only quants plunged during the selloff, losing an average of 17% as investors around the world dumped shares linked to artificial intelligence.

All seven index-enhancement strategies tracked by China Merchants Futures Co. generated alpha for the week ended Aug. 14. Index-enhancement funds linked to CSI A500 and CSI 500 both beat their benchmarks by 1.2 percentage points, the firm said.

The rebound in China’s 2.6 trillion yuan ($387 billion) quant sector may extend further as markets normalize, but the recovery remains uneven, according to PaiPaiWang. That suggests the weakest players face a hard climb back. The steep losses in July showed how rapidly crowded trades and changing market structure can [overwhelm](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-21/quant-hedge-fund-free-fall-spooks-wealthy-investors-in-china) otherwise diversified portfolios.

PaiPaiWang, which tracks hedge funds, attributed the snapback to three forces: a technical rebound from oversold levels after July’s emotion-driven selloff, a return of liquidity, and the recovery of quant factors — particularly volume-price signals — that had broken down in the rout.

The CSI 1000 gauge of many quants’ favorite small-cap stocks gained 10% in the first two weeks of this month after plunging 20% in July. The large-cap CSI 300 also rallied 2% in the period after dropping 8%.

Shanghai-based Mingshi Investment Management offers one example of how quickly the same models that hurt funds in July could benefit from the market rebound. Its all-market quantitative stock-selection strategy gained 16.6% in the first week of August, according to a letter to investors seen by Bloomberg.

The firm attributed its July drawdown in part to a model-generated tilt toward technology stocks, an exposure that had contributed to strong first-half returns before suffering losses last month.

### **Left Alone**

Rather than overriding the model during the rout, Mingshi said it allowed the strategy to continue responding to its signals, including by adding technology exposure during the panic before naturally rotating toward other industries as conditions changed in August.

“Market performance once again reaffirmed the essence of quant — letting data-driven, algorithmic strategy models make decisions, rather than relying on discretionary timing or human intervention,” it said in the letter.

The product gained a further 2.1% in the week ended Aug. 14, extending this year’s return to 25.4%, ranking it at the top of the 65 quants tracked by [Guolian Minsheng Securities Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/1456%20HK%20Equity)

Based on data compiled by the broker, Hangzhou-based [Everon Quant](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/OkIwvurTtb_CCG6_Rzn37Q) delivered the strongest and most consistent bounce in excess returns across the CSI 300, CSI 500 and CSI 1000 indexes for the week. It beat the CSI 300 by 1.96 percentage points. Shanghai-based Hainan Semimartingale Private Fund Management LP bettered the CSI 500 by 2.4 percentage points, taking its year-to-date excess to 17 percentage points, the best for that index.

“Semimartingale’s model involves no manual intervention in response to market conditions,” the firm said in a text message. “With market conditions stabilizing, excess returns have shown a natural recovery.”

Representatives for Mingshi and Everon declined to comment.

Still, the broader recovery remains incomplete. Less than 8% of quant long-only funds had fully repaired their average 16% loss in July as of Aug. 7, according to PaiPaiWang. That’s lower than the 13.5% of all hedge funds that had recovered, the data showed.

A rally across small- and mid-cap stocks, rather than gains concentrated in a handful of technology names, would support index-enhancement and diversified stock-selection strategies. A further easing of the unusually high correlations among momentum, liquidity and volatility factors would help multi-factor models recover some of the diversification they lost in July.
