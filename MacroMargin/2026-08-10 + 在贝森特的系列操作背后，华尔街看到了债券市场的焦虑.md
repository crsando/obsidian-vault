## 在贝森特的系列操作背后，华尔街看到了债券市场的焦虑

斯科特·贝森特在马里兰州戴维营内阁会议期间。*摄影：Aaron Schwartz/法新社/盖蒂图片社*

### **要点**

- 美国财政部长斯科特·贝森特正发出信号，表明他在长期利率飙升至19年高点后，急于阻止债券收益率进一步飙升。
- 贝森特已采取措施，例如自1998年以来首次实施美国货币干预以支撑日元，并对其部门关于债券销售的指引进行了微妙调整。
- 这些举措表明，贝森特正试图遏制长期债券收益率的上升，而收益率攀升的原因是持续的通胀和近2万亿美元的年度预算赤字。
华尔街交易员和策略师表示，美国财政部长斯科特·贝森特正发出新的信号，表明他急于阻止债券收益率进一步飙升。

在一周的时间里，他采取了一些他们认为是旨在缓解国债市场压力的措施，此前长期[利率飙升](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-29/bond-rout-sends-warning-to-warsh-that-tough-talk-is-not-enough)至19年高位，推高了从购房者到美国广大企业的成本。

首先，他实施了美国自1998年以来[首次货币干预](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-01/bessent-and-the-fed-help-japan-reverse-months-of-yen-losses)以支撑日元，从而降低了日本抛售美国国债以自行筹集购买日元所需美元的风险。他还提到了东京未来可能利用的美联储机制。

随后，在上周的[季度债券发售公告](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-05/us-keeps-debt-sale-guidance-steady-deepening-reliance-on-bills)中，其部门指引出现的一个微妙而意外的变化被视为为[潜在削减](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-07/us-treasury-sparks-debate-over-auction-cutbacks-to-temper-yields)长期国债发售打开了大门。

贝森特还通过电视和社交媒体为美联储主席凯文·沃什(Kevin Warsh)的新沟通策略辩护。沃什在上月会议后未能解释央行可能如何或何时采取行动来降低通胀，导致收益率飙升。

观看：麦格理(Macquarie)的蒂埃里·维兹曼(Thierry Wizman)在被问及美联储加息可能性时表示：“12月看起来并非错误的时机。”来源：彭博

总体而言，这些举措表明，贝森特正试图尽其所能遏制长期债券利率的上升。长期债券利率因持续的通胀和近2万亿美元的年度预算[赤字](https://fiscaldata.treasury.gov/americas-finance-guide/national-deficit/https://fiscaldata.treasury.gov/americas-finance-guide/national-deficit/)而攀升，这些赤字导致新债供应不断增加。

“美联储和财政部肯定对长端利率水平感到担忧，”摩根大通资产管理公司(JPMorgan Asset Management)投资组合经理普里亚·米斯拉(Priya Misra)表示。“与日本联手干预、支持沃什以及可能减少长端国债供应，可能是财政部在发出信号，表明他们意识到利率市场的走势，并且毫不犹豫地使用手中的各种工具。”

![图片](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3b881cc90b1481189ec0ce366f8fe8ac/3b881cc9-0b14-8170-b71a-c893b9feea81-9c0109d821de831877970230383c26bf.png)

归根结底，考虑到更大的力量在发挥作用，贝森特的影响力有限。周一，由于结束伊朗战争缺乏明显进展，美国国债收益率与油价一起小幅走高，收窄了周五在劳工部报告显示就业市场明显疲软后出现的跌幅。

然而，美国财政部的行动被视为一个信号，表明它愿意尽其所能降低借贷成本，而总统唐纳德·特朗普曾[一再](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-07/trump-says-rates-should-drop-but-it-s-not-entirely-up-to-warsh)表示这是一个优先事项。财政部发言人没有回应置评请求。

去年年初，在特朗普重返白宫后，贝森特[表示](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-03-23/-don-t-fight-bessent-s-treasury-is-new-mantra-in-us-bond-market)，政府[主要关注点](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-02-28/trump-dials-back-fed-bashing-seeks-a-different-kind-of-rate-cut)在于降低10年期国债收益率，该收益率是抵押贷款和其他类型贷款的基准。他表示，其财政政策将通过减少政府支出、帮助缓解通胀来实现这一目标。

去年11月，在提到自己作为“国家头号债券推销员”的工作时，贝森特——前对冲基金经理——和特朗普政府正将债券市场视为可负担性议程的关键；国债收益率是“衡量成功的强有力晴雨表”。

但政府的减支措施影响甚微，其减税政策将在未来十年[显著增加](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-02-11/cbo-boosts-us-deficit-call-by-1-4-trillion-on-trump-policies)政府债务。特朗普对美联储的攻击——他[上周再次威胁](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-07/trump-again-moves-to-fire-cook-reviving-fed-independence-fight)要解雇理事丽莎·库克——危及央行独立性，令投资者担忧。此外，伊朗战争引发的油价飙升带来了新的通胀冲击，推动[10年期国债收益率](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/USGG10YR%20Index)升至约4.66%，高于特朗普第二任期开始时的水平。

“鉴于已采取的支出政策和战争，缓解长期收益率压力的难度很大，”纽约银行美国宏观策略师约翰·维利斯表示。

在此背景下，美国财政部近期的行动被视为旨在防止长期利率进一步攀升的努力。

贝森特购买日元的决定表面上是为了[帮助盟友](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-04/bessent-s-yen-rescue-to-boost-us-pressure-on-japan-trade-rates)。但这可能缓解日本债券市场的压力——近期日本收益率的上升加剧了美国国债的抛售——同时也能抑制日本为获得捍卫本币所需的美元而出售美国国债的需求。

他还主张[扩大](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-05/japanese-yen-intervention-why-bessent-wants-fed-to-expand-fima-backstop)美联储的外国和国际货币当局回购工具，该工具允许外国央行以其持有的美国国债为抵押借入美元。

“我们显然已经达到了美国国债市场如此脆弱的程度，长期利率上升促使我们鼓励外国持有者不要卖出，”Onepoint Bfg首席投资官彼得·布克瓦说道。

在汇率市场干预几天后，财政部官员在季度再融资[声明](https://home.treasury.gov/policy-issues/financing-the-government/quarterly-refunding/most-recent-quarterly-refunding-documents)中对债券市场的指引进行了意外调整，该声明用于设定未来债务发售规模的预期。

他们没有像之前那样表示正在继续评估未来“增加”息票和浮动利率票据销售的可能性，而是表示正在考虑潜在的“变化”。

债券投资者认为这表明可能削减压力最大的长期债券销售。根据[BMO资本市场](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/0247433Z:US)对客户的调查，约61%的客户现在预计下一次30年期拍卖规模将减少而非增加。

然而，这种期限调整即使实现，其效果也可能有限。最重要的是，需要证据表明通胀已重新受控——在连续五年超过美联储2%目标之后——才能说服债券持有人以较低利率放贷。

![Kevin Warsh during a news conference following an FOMC meeting in Washington on July 29. Source: Bloomberg](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3b881cc90b1481189ec0ce366f8fe8ac/3b881cc9-0b14-8180-a269-f24cab223705-a898a9e9d0881f4dadbf98980664c775.png)

7月29日，凯文·沃什在华盛顿联邦公开市场委员会会议后的新闻发布会上。来源：彭博

在沃什的会后评论引发债券抛售，令市场对其抗通胀信誉产生怀疑后，贝森特为新任美联储主席辩护。上周他在CNBC上露面时表示，市场需要从美联储的评论中“解毒”，并相信美联储“将在其增长使命和通胀使命之间取得平衡”。

[瑞银集团](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/UBSG%20SW%20Equity)美国利率策略主管菲比·怀特表示，财政部近期的措施可能影响有限。

但她说，这表明“如果财政部能采取任何措施来阻止长期收益率上升，它将使用可用的工具。”

## Behind Bessent Moves, Wall Street Sees a Bond-Market Angst

Scott Bessent during a cabinet meeting at Camp David in Maryland.*Photographer: Aaron Schwartz/AFP/Getty Images*

By [Greg Ritchie](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AUJMWGi29pc/greg-ritchie), [Michael MacKenzie](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AVgVy7_JF34/michael-mackenzie), and [Ye Xie](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AOaIfVMEEws/ye-xie)August 9, 2026 at 9:36 PM GMT+8*Updated on August 10, 2026 at 8:08 PM GMT+8*

### **Takeaways ** by Bloomberg AI

### **Takeaways**

- US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent is sending signals that he’s eager to keep bond yields from spiking higher after long-term rates surged to a 19-year high.
- Bessent has taken steps such as staging the US’s first currency intervention to prop up the yen since 1998 and making a subtle change to his department’s guidance on bond sales.
- The moves indicate that Bessent is attempting to stem the ascent of long-term bond rates, which have climbed due to persistent inflation and nearly $2 trillion annual budget deficits.
Wall Street traders and strategists say US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent is sending fresh signals that he’s eager to keep bond yields from spiking higher.

Over the course of a week, he took steps that they see as aimed at easing pressure on the Treasury market after long-term [rates surged](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-29/bond-rout-sends-warning-to-warsh-that-tough-talk-is-not-enough) to a 19-year high, pushing up costs for everyone from homebuyers to vast swaths of corporate America.

First, he staged the US’s [first currency intervention](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-01/bessent-and-the-fed-help-japan-reverse-months-of-yen-losses) to prop up the yen since 1998, mitigating the risk that Japan would dump US government bonds to raise the dollars needed to buy the currency on its own. And he pointed to a Federal Reserve facility that Tokyo could tap in the future.

Then at last week’s[ quarterly bond sales announcement](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-05/us-keeps-debt-sale-guidance-steady-deepening-reliance-on-bills), a subtle and unexpected change to his department’s guidance was seen as opening the door to [potential cuts](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-07/us-treasury-sparks-debate-over-auction-cutbacks-to-temper-yields) in long-bond sales.

Bessent has also been taking to the airwaves and social media to defend the new communications strategy of Federal Reserve Chairman Kevin Warsh, who caused yields to surge after last month’s meeting when he failed to explain how — or when — the central bank may act to bring down inflation.

WATCH: “December doesn’t look like the wrong time to do it,” says Macquarie’s Thierry Wizman when asked about the possibility of a Fed hike. Source: Bloomberg

Taken together, the moves indicate that Bessent is attempting to do what’s in his power to stem the ascent of long-term bond rates, which have climbed due to persistent inflation and nearly $2 trillion annual budget [deficits](https://fiscaldata.treasury.gov/americas-finance-guide/national-deficit/https://fiscaldata.treasury.gov/americas-finance-guide/national-deficit/) that are resulting in an ever-increasing supply of new debt.

“The Fed and the Treasury have to be getting concerned about the level of long-end rates,” said Priya Misra, portfolio manager at [JPMorgan Asset Management](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/0555934D:LX). “The intervention with Japan, support for Warsh and a possible reduction in long-end supply can be attempts for Treasury to signal that they are aware of the rate-market move and do not hesitate to use the different tools at their disposal.”

In the end, Bessent’s influence is limited, given the bigger forces at work. On Monday, Treasury yields edged up along with oil prices due to lack of apparent progress toward ending the Iran war, trimming the declines seen Friday after a Labor Department report that showed significant weakening in the job market.

Yet the Treasury’s actions were seen as a sign that it’s willing to do what it can to get borrowing costs lower, which President Donald Trump has [repeatedly ](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-07/trump-says-rates-should-drop-but-it-s-not-entirely-up-to-warsh)said is a priority. Spokespeople for the Treasury didn’t respond to a request for comment.

Early last year, after Trump’s return to the White House, Bessent [said](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-03-23/-don-t-fight-bessent-s-treasury-is-new-mantra-in-us-bond-market) the administration’s [main focus](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-02-28/trump-dials-back-fed-bashing-seeks-a-different-kind-of-rate-cut) was on lowering 10-year yields, which serve as a baseline for mortgages and other types of loans. He said its fiscal policies would help accomplish that goal by reducing government spending, helping to ease inflation.

In November, while referring to his job as “the nation’s top bond salesman,” Bessent, a former hedge fund manager, Bessent, Trump administration are pushing bond market as key to affordability agenda Treasury yields are a “strong barometer for measuring success.”

But the adminstration’s spending cuts had little impact and its tax reductions will [add significantly](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-02-11/cbo-boosts-us-deficit-call-by-1-4-trillion-on-trump-policies) to the government debt in the coming decade. The attacks on the Fed from Trump, who [last week revived](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-07/trump-again-moves-to-fire-cook-reviving-fed-independence-fight) his threat to fire Governor Lisa Cook, have worried investors by jeopardizing the central bank’s independence. And the Iran war’s oil-price spike has created a fresh inflation shock that’s helped push the [10-year yield](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/USGG10YR%20Index) up to around 4.66%, higher than it was at the start of Trump’s second term.

“With the spending policy that’s been adopted and the war, it’s going to be hard to relieve pressure on the long end,” said John Velis, US macro strategist at BNY.

Against that backdrop, the Treasury’s recent actions were seen as an effort to prevent long-term rates from climbing even more.

Bessent’s decision to buy yen was ostensibly aimed at [helping an ally](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-04/bessent-s-yen-rescue-to-boost-us-pressure-on-japan-trade-rates). But it could ease pressure on Japan’s bond market, where a rise in yields has contributed to the selloff in Treasuries recently, as well as curb Japan’s need to sell US bonds to get the dollars needed to defend its currency.

He also advocated for an [increase](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-05/japanese-yen-intervention-why-bessent-wants-fed-to-expand-fima-backstop) to the Fed’s Foreign and International Monetary Authorities Repo Facility, which allows foreign central banks to borrow dollars against their holdings of Treasuries.

“We’ve apparently reached a point of such fragility in the US Treasury market with the rise in long rates that we are encouraging foreign holders not to sell,” said Peter Boockvar, chief investment officer at Onepoint Bfg.

Days after the currency-market intervention, Treasury officials made an unanticipated tweak to their guidance to the bond market in their quarterly refunding [statement](https://home.treasury.gov/policy-issues/financing-the-government/quarterly-refunding/most-recent-quarterly-refunding-documents), which is used to set expectations about the scale of upcoming debt sales.

Instead of saying they were continuing to evaluate potential future “increases” in coupon and floating-rate note sales, as was the case previously, they said instead they were mulling potential “changes.”

Bond investors saw that as flagging the possibility it will pare sales of the long-bonds most under pressure. Some 61% of clients polled by [BMO Capital Markets](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/0247433Z:US) now anticipate that the next move in 30-year auction sizes will be a decrease instead of an increase.

Such maturity tweaks, should they materialize, can only go so far, though. More than anything, evidence that inflation is back under control — after five years of overshooting the Fed’s 2% target — will be needed to convince bondholders to lend at lower rates.

Kevin Warsh during a news conference following an FOMC meeting in Washington on July 29. Source: Bloomberg

After Warsh’s post-meeting comments triggered a bond selloff by casting doubt about his inflation-fighting credibility, Bessent defended the new Fed chairman. During an appearance on CNBC, he said last week that markets needed a “detox” from Fed commentary and that he was confident the Fed “will balance between their growth mandate and their inflation mandate.”

Phoebe White, head of US rates strategy at [UBS Group](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/UBSG%20SW%20Equity), said the Treasury’s recent steps may only have a limited impact.

But, she said, it shows that “if there’s anything the Treasury can do to keep long-term yields from moving higher, it will use the tools available to it.”
