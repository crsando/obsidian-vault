## 伊朗威胁称，若与美国的外交失败，将在霍尔木兹海峡采取进攻行动

2026年8月17日，从阿曼的穆桑达姆看到的霍尔木兹海峡附近的一艘船只。REUTERS/Stringer [购买许可权，打开新标签页](https://www.reutersconnect.com/item/a-vessel-near-the-strait-of-hormuz-as-seen-from-musandam-oman-august-17-2026-reutersstringer/dGFnOnJldXRlcnMuY29tLDIwMjY6bmV3c21sX1JDMkMwTkFRRTBWVw%3D%3D/?utm_medium=rcom-article-media&utm_campaign=rcom-rcp-lead)

- 摘要
- 官员称：若临时和平协议无法落实，伊朗将采取更激烈的行动。
- 6月达成的临时协议很快便土崩瓦解。
- 特朗普总统表示，如果阿曼“妨碍行事”，他就会对其发动轰炸。
迪拜/华盛顿，8月17日（路透社）——一名伊朗高级官员周一对路透社表示，由于在努力达成永久结束其与美国战争的协议方面陷入僵局，伊朗已决定将其政策从防御性转为“全面进攻性”。

关于和平谈判以及恢复通过战略要地霍尔木兹海峡的油轮运输的进展已陷入停滞，目前没有迹象表明交战各方正朝着结束美国和以色列于2月28日发动的[这场冲突](https://www.reuters.com/world/iran/)的方向前进。

“伊朗相关方面必须做好准备，升级霍尔木兹海峡及更广泛地区的紧张局势，因为伊朗将准备好做出决定并就艰难决定采取行动，”[这位伊朗官员表示](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/iran-says-it-will-escalate-if-us-does-not-honour-deal-within-weeks-2026-08-17/)，并补充说，如果外交失败，德黑兰将发动“及时而精准”的军事打击，以打破美国的海上封锁。

华盛顿方面对这些言论没有立即回应，但美国总统唐纳德·特朗普周一在接受福克斯新闻电话采访时表示，伊朗“应该举起白旗投降”。

在整个冲突期间，特朗普在升级威胁和宣称和平协议即将达成之间交替变换。

### 特朗普威胁轰炸阿曼

周一标志着伊朗和美国根据[六月达成的谅解备忘录](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/g7-leaders-demand-ceasefire-lebanon-welcome-iran-deal-2026-06-17/)（旨在结束战争）预计达成最终协议的日期。

该谅解备忘录于6月17日签署，规定了60天的时间框架（经双方同意可延长），华盛顿和德黑兰在此期间应就包括限制德黑兰核计划和解除美国制裁在内的问题达成更广泛的协议。

然而，这项临时协议宣布“立即永久终止所有战线上的军事行动”，但由于在霍尔木兹海峡控制权上的争端，该协议迅速瓦解。霍尔木兹海峡是战前全球五分之一的石油和液化天然气流经的狭窄水道。

德黑兰表示，谅解备忘录第5条赋予其管理该海峡的权利，该海峡由伊朗与阿曼共享，而华盛顿拒绝了这一解释。随着德黑兰开始向据称试图未经批准航线通过该水道的船只开火，敌对行动重新开始。

![图片](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/iran-threatens-go-offensive-strait-hormuz-if-diplomacy-with-us-fails-2026-08-17/undefined)

特朗普于7月7日表示该协议“已经结束”。德黑兰一再指责华盛顿持续违反承诺。

一位伊朗官员对路透社表示：“在伊朗设定的短短几周内，美国必须执行协议的所有条款。这是与美国进一步谈判的前提条件。”并补充说，调解人将把德黑兰的最后期限告知华盛顿和地区国家。

德黑兰还单独与阿曼就霍尔木兹海峡的管理进行协议谈判，这被视为更广泛协议的关键组成部分，并表示双方已接近达成协议，尽管进展缓慢。

特朗普对福克斯新闻表示：“如果阿曼碍事，我们就狠狠炸他们。”

阿曼方面没有立即发表评论。

### 胡塞武装袭击引发更广泛战争担忧

自2月底美国和以色列对伊朗发动袭击以来，已有数千人在冲突中丧生，主要集中在伊朗和黎巴嫩。伊朗已打击了包括阿曼、约旦、科威特、以色列、阿联酋和沙特阿拉伯等国境内的美国资产和基础设施。

过去一周，伊朗和美国都加强了言辞。

特朗普在周五集会的演讲中警告美国人，要为战争导致的[持续高油价](https://www.reuters.com/world/us/trump-urges-americans-accept-higher-gas-prices-he-escalates-iran-rhetoric-2026-08-14/)做好准备，同时还表示，“在我们彻底击败伊朗之后……不久我将宣布霍尔木兹海峡为美国领土”。

周一早些时候，特朗普在其“真相社交”平台上发帖称：“首要目标，而且永远都是，伊朗不能以任何方式、形式或形态拥有核武器”，重申了他对这场战争宣称的理由之一。

自二月份战争爆发以来，基准布伦特原油期货价格飙升，触及每桶126美元的峰值，比战前水平高出约75%，剧烈波动的交易反映出市场对海湾石油供应中断的担忧以及和谈信号的变化无常。

周一，布伦特原油价格小幅上涨，至每桶89美元左右。

[伊朗支持的胡塞武装](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/yemen-government-says-houthi-missiles-kill-least-four-civilians-port-city-2026-08-14/)在也门发动的袭击也再次引发了人们对地区战争扩大及其可能带来的经济影响的担忧。

在最近的事件中，胡塞武装军事发言人叶海亚·萨雷亚周一在Telegram上表示，胡塞武装[袭击了他们所称的一艘沙特军用船只](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/houthis-say-they-attacked-saudi-vessels-off-yemens-mocha-2026-08-17/)以及曼德海峡的四艘护卫舰。该海峡是连接红海和亚丁湾的战略咽喉要道，也是重要的国际航运路线。

沙特当局没有立即予以证实。

DUBAI/WASHINGTON, Aug 17 (Reuters) - Iran has decided to shift its policy from defensive to "fully offensive" due to the deadlock in efforts to agree a permanent end to its war with the United States, a senior Iranian official told Reuters on Monday.
迪拜/华盛顿，8 月 17 日（路透社）——一名伊朗高级官员周一向路透社表示，由于与美国就永久结束战争的问题陷入僵局，伊朗已决定将其战略从防御性转为“全面进攻性”。

Progress towards peace talks and a resumption of ‌oil tanker traffic through the strategic Strait of Hormuz has ground to a halt, with no sign that the warring parties are moving towards ending [the conflict](https://www.reuters.com/world/iran/) that the U.S. and Israel launched on February 28.
关于和平谈判以及恢复通过这一战略要地霍尔木兹海峡的油轮运输的进展已经完全停滞，各方似乎都没有意愿结束美国和以色列于 2 月 28 日发动的这场冲突。

"Iranian entities must be prepared to escalate tensions in the Strait of Hormuz and wider region, as Iran will be ready to make decisions and take action on difficult decisions," [the Iranian official said](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/iran-says-it-will-escalate-if-us-does-not-honour-deal-within-weeks-2026-08-17/), adding that Tehran would conduct a "timely and precise" military attack to break the U.S. naval blockade if diplomacy failed.

There was no immediate response to those comments from Washington, but U.S. President Donald Trump said in a phone interview with Fox News on Monday that Iran "should put up the white flag of surrender".

Throughout the conflict, Trump has alternated between threats of escalation and assertions that a ⁠peace deal is imminent.

### TRUMP THREATENS TO BOMB OMAN

Monday marked the day by which Iran and the United States were expected to reach a final deal under a [memorandum of understanding agreed in June](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/g7-leaders-demand-ceasefire-lebanon-welcome-iran-deal-2026-06-17/) and aimed at ending the war.

Signed on June 17, the MoU set out a 60-day timeframe, extendable by mutual consent, by which Washington and Tehran should reach a broader agreement on issues including limiting Tehran's nuclear programme and lifting U.S. sanctions.

That interim deal, which declared the "immediate and permanent termination of military operations on all fronts", quickly unravelled, however, due to a dispute over control of the Strait of Hormuz, the narrow waterway through which a fifth of global oil and liquefied natural gas flowed before the war.

Tehran said point 5 of the MoU gave it the right to manage the strait, which it shares with Oman, while Washington rejected that interpretation. Hostilities resumed as Tehran began firing on vessels it said were trying to sail through the waterway on an unapproved route.

Trump said on July 7 that the pact was "over". Tehran has repeatedly accused Washington of consistently violating its commitments.

"Within the short period of a few weeks set by Iran, all the agreement's provisions must be implemented by the U.S. This is a precondition for further negotiations with the U.S.," the Iranian ‌official told ⁠Reuters, adding that mediators would share Tehran's deadline with Washington and regional states.

Tehran has separately been negotiating an agreement with Oman over management of the strait, seen as a key component of a wider deal, and says they are close to agreement, although progress has been slow.

"If Oman gets in the way, we'll bomb the shit out of them," Trump told Fox.

There was no immediate comment from Oman.

### HOUTHI ATTACKS RAISE FEARS OF WIDER WAR

Thousands of people have been killed in the conflict, mainly in Iran and Lebanon, since the U.S. and Israel launched attacks on Iran at the end of February. Iran has struck U.S. assets and infrastructure in countries including Oman, Jordan, Kuwait, Israel, the United Arab ⁠Emirates and Saudi Arabia.

Both Iran and the United States have stepped up rhetoric over the past week.

Trump warned Americans in a speech at a rally on Friday to prepare for [continued high fuel prices](https://www.reuters.com/world/us/trump-urges-americans-accept-higher-gas-prices-he-escalates-iran-rhetoric-2026-08-14/) as a result of the war, while also saying that "after we finish defeating Iran ... pretty soon I'll be declaring the Hormuz Strait a territory of the United States".

Earlier on Monday, Trump posted on his Truth Social platform: "The number one Goal is, and always will be, that ⁠Iran cannot have, in any way, shape, or form, a Nuclear Weapon", repeating one of his stated rationales for the war.

Benchmark Brent crude oil futures have surged since the start of the war in February, hitting a peak of $126 a barrel, roughly 75% above pre-war levels, with choppy trade reflecting concerns over disruptions to Gulf oil supplies and shifting signals on peace talks.

Brent crude rose slightly on Monday, to around $89 a ⁠barrel.

Attacks by [Iran-backed Houthis](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/yemen-government-says-houthi-missiles-kill-least-four-civilians-port-city-2026-08-14/) in Yemen have also renewed concerns about a widening regional war and the economic fallout it could bring.

In the latest incident, the Houthis [attacked what they called a Saudi military ship](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/houthis-say-they-attacked-saudi-vessels-off-yemens-mocha-2026-08-17/) and four escort vessels in the Bab el-Mandeb Strait with missiles, according to the group’s military spokesperson Yahya Saree on Telegram on Monday. The strait is a strategic chokepoint connecting the Red Sea with the Gulf of Aden and an important international shipping route.

There was no immediate confirmation from Saudi authorities.
