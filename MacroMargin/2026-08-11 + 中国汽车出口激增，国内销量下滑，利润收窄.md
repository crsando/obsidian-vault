### 中文译文

## 中国汽车出口激增，国内销量下滑，利润收窄

中国汽车出口再次激增，为深陷国内低迷困境的汽车制造商提供了生命线，而这一低迷是由油价上涨和消费者信心疲软所致。

据中国乘用车市场信息联席会数据，7月海外出货量同比增长88%，达到91.8万辆。这意味着该国生产的汽车中有41%被运往海外，而一年前这一比例为21%。

强劲的出口与乘用车零售总量的低迷形成鲜明对比，7月乘用车零售总量下降21%至146万辆。其中，燃油车销量下降41%领跌，而包括混合动力和纯电动汽车在内的新能源汽车下降了3.9%。据乘联会数据，新能源汽车占销量的比例达到创纪录的65%。

这一分化凸显了全球最大汽车市场的结构性转变，汽车制造商越来越依赖海外扩张来提振利润。在国内，旷日持久的[价格战](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-12-12/china-plans-tougher-regulations-targeting-automaker-price-wars)、成本上升以及购车补贴退坡，让[汽车制造商难以吸引买家](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2026-08-11/mercedes-bmw-vw-struggle-in-china-with-new-evs-selling-poorly)。

比亚迪上个月是新能源汽车最大的出口商，出口173,721辆，是第二名奇瑞汽车的两倍多。比亚迪上月总销量增长22%，达到约42万辆。[特斯拉公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/TSLA%20US%20Equity)从其上海工厂出口了66,330辆，并在本地销售了27,249辆。

与此同时，Stellantis NV的合作伙伴浙江零跑科技股份有限公司的月销量突破10万辆。在A10等热门大众市场车型的推动下，这家初创公司报告国内零售销量为83,698辆，使其成为中国第三畅销的汽车品牌。

不过，乘联会表示利润空间正受到挤压。今年上半年，中国汽车行业的销售利润率为3.8%，低于上下游制造业6.5%的平均水平。

乘联会表示，上游原材料成本高企、供应商付款周期拉长、以及持续的零售折扣继续限制盈利能力，使汽车制造商依赖持续的出口增长来维持经营规模。

## China’s Car Exports Boom as Domestic Sales Slump, Margins Shrink

China’s car exports surged again, serving as a lifeline for automakers mired in a domestic slump driven by rising fuel costs and weak consumer confidence.

Overseas shipments jumped 88% year-on-year in July to 918,000 units, [according to](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MzkzNDY4NzQ3MQ%3D%3D&amp%3Bmid=2247555824&amp%3Bidx=1&amp%3Bsn=38d37149e84b5319921f6a1c8c5d8889&amp%3Bchksm=c3504e30f8873b5ad094a48599b98fbdda3efa94422353aabc6623000f632731037faa75e90e&amp%3Bscene=0&amp%3Bxtrack=1#rd) China’s Passenger Car Association. That means that 41% of cars produced in the country were sent abroad, compared with 21% a year ago.

Robust exports contrast with the overall slump in total passenger vehicle retail sales, which dropped 21% to 1.46 million units in July. While that was led by a 41% decline in sales of internal combustion vehicles, new-energy vehicles including both hybrids and battery-electric cars fell 3.9%. NEVs accounted for a record 65% of sales, according to the PCA.

The divergence highlights a structural shift in the world’s largest auto market, where automakers are increasingly reliant on overseas expansion to bolster their bottomline. At home, a prolonged [price war](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-12-12/china-plans-tougher-regulations-targeting-automaker-price-wars), higher costs and a rollback purchase incentives have left [carmakers struggling](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2026-08-11/mercedes-bmw-vw-struggle-in-china-with-new-evs-selling-poorly) to entice buyers.

BYD Co. was the biggest exporter of NEVs last month, shipping 173,721 vehicles — more than twice the volume of no. 2 Chery Automobile Co. BYD’s total sales rose 22% last month to around 420,000 units. [Tesla Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/TSLA%20US%20Equity) exported 66,330 vehicles from its Shanghai hub, and sold 27,249 locally.

Meanwhile, Stellantis NV partner Zhejiang Leapmotor Technology Co. saw monthly sales volume top 100,000. Driven by popular mass-market models like the A10, the startup reported 83,698 domestic retail sales, making it the third best-selling car brand in China.

Still, the PCA said that margins are being squeezed. China’s automotive industry recorded a 3.8% sales profit margin in the first half of the year, lagging behind the 6.5% average across upstream and downstream manufacturing.

High upstream raw material costs, prolonged vendor payment terms, and persistent retail discounting continue to cap profitability, leaving carmakers dependent on sustained export growth to maintain operational scale, the association said.
