### 中文译文

## 中国汽车制造商奇瑞将在英国开设研发中心

![An assembly line producing Chery Jaecoo 8 SUVs. The Chinese group is pushing for growth in Europe © Qilai Shen/Bloomberg](https://images.ft.com/v3/image/raw/ftcms%3Ac0c75919-94ff-481b-a1bd-4058c7a174d2?source=next-article&fit=scale-down&quality=highest&width=1440&dpr=1)

生产奇瑞Jaecoo 8 SUV的装配线。该中国集团正推动在欧洲的增长 © Qilai Shen/彭博社

中国汽车制造商奇瑞将在英国开设一个乘用车研发设施，该公司是Omoda和Jaecoo品牌的拥有者，正扩大其在该市场的业务。

此举正值该中国集团进军欧洲和英国之际。2月，奇瑞宣布推出专为欧洲市场设计的Lepas品牌。该集团还在6月[与日产签署协议](https://www.ft.com/content/7a80347d-93f2-4563-b42c-a1652d252ee7?syn-25a6b1a6=1)，将在这家日本汽车制造商的桑德兰工厂生产其车辆。

奇瑞国际英国公司首席执行官Gary Lan在5月举行的英国《金融时报》未来汽车峰会上表示，推出乘用车研发中心将是其业务本地化的重要一步，随后将在英国进行制造。

Lan在周三的一份声明中表示，该设施是“我们对英国长期计划的下一步”。“我们等了20多年才找到进入这个市场的合适时机，我们的雄心一直远不止于简单地将车辆带到这里。”

奇瑞表示，该设施将于深秋开放。根据一份不具约束力的谅解备忘录，日产正寻求从明年开始在桑德兰生产该中国集团的车辆。

[奇瑞](https://www.ft.com/stream/2e03562c-ec9d-45d1-9a0a-ab654a106844)还拥有同名品牌，是英国增长最快的中国汽车制造商之一。

根据英国汽车制造商和贸易商协会的数据，7月，奇瑞及其Omoda和Jaecoo品牌占据了英国市场近8%的份额，高于去年的约3%。

包括比亚迪、吉利和小米在内的其他中国汽车制造商也在迅速扩张进入欧洲汽车市场。

新的研发设施将建在UTAC Millbrook内，这是位于贝德福德郡的一个车辆测试和开发中心。该地区是工程和赛车运动的中心，也是多个汽车研发设施的所在地。

研究将重点关注如何针对英国的驾驶条件对公司的汽车进行校准，并最终聚焦于自动驾驶和人工智能。

该集团表示，其长期目标是在该研发中心招聘本地人才并创造新的高技能工作岗位。

奇瑞7月份全球销量约28万辆，出口量约20.2万辆，同比增长70%，成为首家单月出口超过20万辆的中国汽车制造商。

## Chinese automaker Chery to open UK R&D centre

An assembly line producing Chery Jaecoo 8 SUVs. The Chinese group is pushing for growth in Europe © Qilai Shen/Bloomberg

Chinese carmaker Chery will open a research and development facility for passenger vehicles in the UK as the owner of Omoda and Jaecoo brands expands its presence in the market.

The move comes as the Chinese group pushes into Europe and the UK. In February Chery announced the launch of its Lepas brand, designed specifically for European markets. The group also [signed a deal with Nissan](https://www.ft.com/content/7a80347d-93f2-4563-b42c-a1652d252ee7?syn-25a6b1a6=1) in June to manufacture its vehicles in the Japanese carmaker’s Sunderland facility.

The launch of an R&D centre for passenger cars would be an important step in localising its operations, which would be followed by manufacturing in the UK, said Gary Lan, chief executive of Chery International UK, at the FT Future of the Car Summit in May.

The facility was “the next step in our long-term plan for the UK”, said Lan in a statement on Wednesday. “We waited over 20 years for the right time to enter this market, and our ambition has always gone much further than simply bringing vehicles here.”

Chery said the facility would open in late autumn. Under a nonbinding memorandum of understanding, Nissan is looking to start producing the Chinese group’s vehicles at Sunderland from next year.

[Chery](https://www.ft.com/stream/2e03562c-ec9d-45d1-9a0a-ab654a106844), which also owns its eponymous marque, is one of the fastest-growing Chinese carmakers in the UK.

In July, Chery and its Omoda and Jaecoo brands took almost 8 per cent of the UK market share — up from about 3 per cent last year, according to the Society of Motor Manufacturers and Traders.

Other Chinese automakers including BYD, Geely and Xiaomi are also expanding rapidly into the European car market.

The new R&D facility will be built within UTAC Millbrook, a vehicle testing and development centre in Bedfordshire. The area is a hub for engineering and motorsport and home to several automotive R&D facilities.

Research will focus on how to calibrate the company’s cars for the UK’s driving conditions and, eventually, autonomous driving and artificial intelligence.

The group said it had long-term ambitions to recruit local talent and create new, high-skilled jobs at the R&D hub.

Chery sold about 280,000 vehicles globally in July, and exports reached about 202,000, up 70 per cent year on year, making it the first Chinese automaker to export more than 200,000 in a month.
