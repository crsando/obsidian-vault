### 中文译文

## 中国对Palo Alto Networks启动网络安全审查

### **要点**

- 中国对美国科技公司Palo Alto Networks Inc.销售的产品启动正式安全审查。
- 中国国家互联网信息办公室表示，依据国家安全法律，此次审查对于保护关键基础设施是必要的。
- 北京此前指责Palo Alto Networks与间谍机构有关联，并要求中国组织在2026年上半年之前用国内技术替换其网络安全产品。
中国对美国科技公司[Palo Alto Networks Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/PANW%20US%20Equity)在中国销售的产品启动正式安全审查，此前数月该公司被指控与情报机构存在关联，此举加大了对该公司的压力。

中国国家互联网信息办公室周四在一份声明中表示，根据两部国家安全法律，此次审查是保护关键基础设施所必需的。Palo Alto Networks股价一度下跌4.2%，随后在盘前交易中收窄跌幅。

今年早些时候，北京指责包括Palo Alto Networks在内的多家美国和以色列公司与间谍机构有关联，但未提供证据。当时，彭博新闻社看到的一份政府指令还点名了[Fortinet Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/FTNT%20US%20Equity)和[Check Point Software Technologies Ltd.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CHKP%20US%20Equity)，称中国企业使用美国和以色列的网络安全产品可能导致敏感数据被发送到海外。

政府要求中国组织排查是否使用指定公司的任何网络安全产品，并在2026年上半年之前用国内技术替换这些产品。

Palo Alto Networks 未立即回应置评请求。据其网站显示，该公司在中国设有多个办事处，包括北京、深圳、上海和广州的[地点](https://www.paloaltonetworks.com/about-us/locations)。

其威胁情报部门 Unit 42 已发布大量关于中国黑客活动的研究，包括去年的一份[报告](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-09-30/chinese-hackers-breach-foreign-ministers-email-servers)，该报告指控他们入侵了外交部长的电子邮件服务器，这是针对世界各地外交官通讯的持续数年行动的一部分。

## China Launches Cybersecurity Review Into Palo Alto Networks

### **Takeaways**

- China launched a formal security review of products sold by US technology firm Palo Alto Networks Inc.
- The Cyberspace Administration of China said the review was necessary to protect critical infrastructure, citing national security laws.
- Beijing had earlier accused Palo Alto Networks of being connected to spy agencies, and told Chinese organizations to replace its cybersecurity products with domestic technologies by the first half of 2026.
China launched a formal security review of products sold in the country by US technology firm [Palo Alto Networks Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/PANW%20US%20Equity), ramping up pressure on the company months after accusing it of harboring links to intelligence services.

The Cyberspace Administration of China said in a statement Thursday that the review was necessary to protect critical infrastructure, citing a pair of national security laws. Shares in Palo Alto Networks fell as much as 4.2% before later paring losses in pre-market trading.

Beijing accused a host of American and Israeli firms including Palo Alto Networks earlier this year of being connected to spy agencies, though it didn’t provide evidence of those claims. At the time, a government directive seen by Bloomberg News, which also named [Fortinet Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/FTNT%20US%20Equity) and [Check Point Software Technologies Ltd.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CHKP%20US%20Equity), said that Chinese companies’ use of US and Israeli cybersecurity products could result in sensitive data being sent overseas.

The government told Chinese organizations to identify whether they use any cybersecurity products from the designated companies and to replace any of those with domestic technologies by the first half of 2026.

Palo Alto Networks didn’t immediately respond to a request for comment. The company has multiple offices in China, including [locations](https://www.paloaltonetworks.com/about-us/locations) in Beijing, Shenzhen, Shanghai and Guangzhou, according to its website.

Its threat intelligence arm, Unit 42, has published a body of research on Chinese hacking operations, including a [report](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-09-30/chinese-hackers-breach-foreign-ministers-email-servers) last year that accused them of breaching email servers of foreign ministers as part of a years-long effort targeting the communications of diplomats around the world.
