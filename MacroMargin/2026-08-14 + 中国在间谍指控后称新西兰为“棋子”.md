## 中国在间谍指控后称新西兰为“棋子”

### **要点**：彭博AI

- 中国指责新西兰充当“反华势力的棋子”，此前新西兰情报机构称北京正大规模对该国进行间谍活动。
- 中国大使馆表示，新西兰安全情报局的报告“捏造并炒作”了“中国干预”和“中国威胁”的叙事。
- 新西兰安全情报局报告称，中国是唯一对新西兰进行大规模间谍活动的国家，并以紫金山天文台试图在新西兰安装地面空间基础设施作为案例研究。
中国指责新西兰充当“反华势力的棋子”，此前新西兰情报机构称北京正大规模对该国进行间谍活动。

中国驻惠灵顿大使馆周五表示，新西兰安全情报局本周的一份报告“捏造并炒作”了“中国干预”和“中国威胁”的叙事。

“我们坚决反对这一点，绝不接受，”[声明说](https://nz.china-embassy.gov.cn/eng/zxgxs/202608/t20260814_12004058.htm)。“充当反华势力的棋子最终会反噬。制造外交关系中的干扰不会解决新西兰自身的问题——只会适得其反。”

这场冲突加剧了惠灵顿与北京之间日益紧张的关系，双方近几个月来已就中国在太平洋的军事活动、新西兰议员与台湾的联系以及外交部长温斯顿·彼得斯的评论进行了外交交锋。

本周报道引发的紧张局势也凸显了新西兰在所谓的“五眼联盟”安全伙伴（包括澳大利亚、美国、英国和加拿大）与中国（其最大贸易伙伴）之间所扮演的微妙平衡角色。

新西兰安全情报局（NZSIS）周四发布的[报告](https://www.nzsis.govt.nz/our-work/new-zealands-security-threat-environment/security-threat-environment-2026)特别点名中国，称虽然有几个国家对新西兰进行间谍活动，但中国是唯一一个大规模这样做的国家。

报告还给出了一个案例研究，称紫金山天文台（Purple Mountain Observatory）——一个位于中国、与政府关系密切的组织——在过去一年被发现试图在新西兰安装地面空间基础设施。该南太平洋国家的战略位置使其成为安装这些设备的理想地点。这些设备旨在跟踪卫星和太空碎片，同时也吸引着寻求提升军事能力和情报活动的外国国家。

新西兰安全情报局表示：“他们与一家当地公司合作，该公司可能并不知道这些设备具备收集具有军事价值情报的能力，也不知道数据接收方是谁。”

## China Calls New Zealand a ‘Pawn’ After Espionage Accusation

### **Takeaways **by Bloomberg AI

- China accused New Zealand of acting as a "pawn for anti-China forces" after its intelligence agency said Beijing was conducting espionage against the country on a significant scale.
- The Chinese embassy said a New Zealand Security Intelligence Service report "fabricates and hypes" the "China interference" and "China threat" narratives.
- The NZSIS report said China is the only country conducting espionage against New Zealand at scale, and gave a case study of Purple Mountain Observatory attempting to install ground-based space infrastructure in New Zealand.
China accused New Zealand of acting as a “pawn for anti-China forces” after its intelligence agency said Beijing was conducting espionage against the country on a significant scale.

The Chinese embassy in Wellington said Friday that a New Zealand Security Intelligence Service report this week “fabricates and hypes” the “China interference” and “China threat” narratives.

“We firmly oppose this and will never accept it,” the [statement said](https://nz.china-embassy.gov.cn/eng/zxgxs/202608/t20260814_12004058.htm). “Serving as a pawn for anti-China forces will ultimately backfire. Manufacturing distractions in foreign relations will not solve New Zealand’s own problems — it will only prove counterproductive.”

The clash adds to growing strains between Wellington and Beijing, which have traded diplomatic barbs in recent months over China’s military activity in the Pacific, New Zealand lawmakers’ ties with Taiwan and comments by Foreign Minister Winston Peters.

The tensions over this week’s report also highlight New Zealand’s delicate balancing act between its role with the so-called Five Eyes security partners — including Australia, the US, the UK and Canada — and China, its largest trading partner.

The NZSIS [report](https://www.nzsis.govt.nz/our-work/new-zealands-security-threat-environment/security-threat-environment-2026), issued Thursday, singled out China, saying that while there are several states that conduct espionage against New Zealand, China is the only country doing it at scale.

It also gave a case study, saying Purple Mountain Observatory, an organization based in China with close links to its government, was found in the past year attempting to install ground-based space infrastructure in New Zealand. The South Pacific nation’s strategic location makes it an ideal place to install the equipment, which is designed to track satellites and space debris and is also attractive for foreign states seeking to advance military capabilities and intelligence operations, the report said.

“They worked with a local company that was likely unaware of the equipment’s capability to collect intelligence of military value and would have no idea who was receiving the data,” the NZSIS said.
