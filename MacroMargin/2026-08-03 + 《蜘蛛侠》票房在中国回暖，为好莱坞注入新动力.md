### 中文译文

## 《蜘蛛侠》票房在中国回暖，为好莱坞注入新动力

### **要点** 来自彭博AI

- 自7月29日上映以来，《蜘蛛侠：崭新一日》在中国已斩获1.21亿美元票房，推动全球总票房达到9.27亿美元。
- 该片在IMAX中国银幕上收获超过1.3亿元人民币，创下自《复仇者联盟4：终局之战》以来好莱坞影片在IMAX格式上的最佳首日票房。
- 这一成绩为华特迪士尼公司旗下漫威在中国带来亮点。在该市场，该系列的势头曾因疫情和地缘政治紧张局势升级而受阻。
*《蜘蛛侠：崭新一日》*在中国票房表现惊艳，成为今年好莱坞电影在中国最强劲的首映之一，为全球第二大电影市场带来提振。

发行方索尼影视娱乐表示，汤姆·霍兰德和赞达亚主演的最新影片自7月29日上映以来在中国已收获1.21亿美元，助力[全球票房](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-02/-spider-man-film-notches-hollywood-s-second-biggest-debut-ever)达到9.27亿美元。该公司称，该超级英雄影片在IMAX中国银幕上收获超过1.3亿元人民币（1930万美元），创下自*《复仇者联盟4：终局之战》*以来好莱坞影片在该格式上的最佳首日票房。

这一表现为[华特迪士尼公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/DIS%20US%20Equity)旗下的漫威在这个曾经最赚钱的海外市场之一带来亮点。在*复仇者联盟*时代，中国在该系列的全球成功中发挥了关键作用，*《复仇者联盟4：终局之战》*在2019年斩获超过42亿元人民币，*《复仇者联盟3：无限战争》*则突破24亿元人民币，使它们成为中国有史以来最卖座的好莱坞影片之一。

这一势头因疫情和不断升级的地缘政治紧张局势而中断，导致多部漫威影片在2020年至2022年期间无缘在中国院线上映。即便迪士尼重新获得市场准入，近年来的漫威作品也难复往日人气。

![Rollercoaster at Spider-Man-themed land at Shanghai Disneyland Source: Shanghai Disney Resort](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3b181cc90b14811c9a3ee5bf82a67eeb/3b181cc9-0b14-81df-8fdb-e56ae9b62f93-257702a1b4064a36c5ec85cbd510484e.png)

上海迪士尼乐园蜘蛛侠主题区的过山车 来源：上海迪士尼度假区

然而，*蜘蛛侠*一直是迪士尼在中国最强大的超级英雄品牌之一——该公司正押注这一人气将远远延伸到电影院之外。据迪士尼称，上海迪士尼乐园正在建造其首个以这位蜘蛛侠为中心的大型漫威主题扩建项目。

该区域将设有高速*蜘蛛侠*过山车，以及主题餐饮、零售和娱乐体验，标志着该度假区的首个大型漫威景点，并进一步将该系列延伸到迪士尼最重要的国际目的地之一。

最新上映也正值中国电影业的一个重要时刻。2026年前七个月的中国票房收入同比下降超过30%，反映出缺乏像去年*《哪吒2》*那样大规模的爆款电影，该片曾创下创纪录的票房销售。

市场在夏季档显示出复苏迹象，得益于国内热映影片，包括体育喜剧*《功夫足球》*和动画片*《愿望成真！》*，这两部影片都吸引了家庭观众。*《蜘蛛侠》*为夏季档再添一部重要影片，放映商也在期待克里斯托弗·诺兰的*《奥德赛》*，该片在[成功首映](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-20/-odyssey-sellouts-cement-nolan-as-hollywood-s-biggest-hero)后于8月中旬在中国上映，并有望提振对[IMAX中国](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/1970%20HK%20Equity)及其在中国800块[银幕](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-06-16/imax-adds-screens-in-china-with-wanda-betting-on-blockbusters)的需求。

好莱坞今年一直难以与中国观众产生共鸣。除*《疯狂动物城2》*外，该片在去年晚些时候上映后延续了大部分放映期，在*《蜘蛛侠：崭新之日》*之前，没有任何好莱坞影片闯入年度票房前十。

2026年中国票房最大的好莱坞开局影片是*《小黄人与怪物》*，票房约为3.01亿元人民币，以及*《玩具总动员5》*，票房为2.83亿元人民币，两者都远低于漫威及其他主要制片厂系列电影曾经经常达到的大片水平。

## ‘Spider-Man’ Box Office Haul Gives Hollywood Fresh Lift in China

### **Takeaways **by Bloomberg AI

- Spider-Man: Brand New Day has earned $121 million in China since its July 29 release, contributing to a global box office haul of $927 million.
- The film brought in more than 130 million yuan on IMAX China screens, marking the biggest opening day for a Hollywood release on the format since Avengers: Endgame.
- The performance offers a bright spot for Walt Disney Co.'s Marvel in China, where the franchise's momentum was disrupted by the pandemic and escalating geopolitical tensions.
*Spider-Man: Brand New Day* got off to a smashing start in China, delivering one of the strongest debuts in the country for a Hollywood film this year and providing a boost to the world’s second-largest film market.

Distributor Sony Pictures Entertainment said Tom Holland and Zendaya’s latest film has earned $121 million in the country since its July 29 release, contributing to a [global box office](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-02/-spider-man-film-notches-hollywood-s-second-biggest-debut-ever) haul of $927 million. The superhero film brought in more than 130 million yuan ($19.3 million) on IMAX China screens, marking the biggest opening day for a Hollywood release on the format since *Avengers: Endgame*, the company said.

The performance offers a bright spot for [Walt Disney Co](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/DIS%20US%20Equity).’s Marvel in what was once one of its most lucrative overseas markets. China played a pivotal role in the franchise’s global success during the *Avengers* era, with *Avengers: Endgame* collecting more than 4.2 billion yuan in 2019 and *Avengers: Infinity War* surpassing 2.4 billion yuan, making them among the country’s biggest Hollywood releases ever.

That momentum was disrupted by the pandemic and escalating geopolitical tensions, which resulted in several Marvel films missing theatrical releases in China between 2020 and 2022. Even after Disney regained market access, more recent Marvel titles have struggled to achieve their former popularity.

Rollercoaster at Spider-Man-themed land at Shanghai Disneyland Source: Shanghai Disney Resort

*Spider-Man* has remained one of Disney’s strongest superhero brands in China, however — a popularity the company is betting extends well beyond movie theaters. Shanghai Disneyland is building its first major Marvel-themed expansion centered on the web-slinger, according to Disney.

The area will feature a high-speed *Spider-Man* roller coaster alongside themed dining, retail and entertainment experiences, marking the resort’s first major Marvel attraction and further extending the franchise into one of Disney’s most important international destinations.

The latest release also comes at an important time for China’s film industry. The country’s box office revenue for the first seven months of 2026 fell by more than 30% from a year earlier, reflecting the absence of a blockbuster on the scale of last year’s *Ne Zha 2*, which drove record ticket sales.

The market has shown signs of recovering during the summer season, helped by domestic hits including sports comedy* Kung Fu Soccer* and** **animated feature ******All Wishes Come True!****, **both of which have attracted family audiences. *Spider-Man* adds another major release to the summer slate, with exhibitors also looking ahead to Christopher Nolan’s The Odyssey**, which opens in China in mid-August after a [successful debut](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-20/-odyssey-sellouts-cement-nolan-as-hollywood-s-biggest-hero) elsewhere and is expected to drive demand for [IMAX China](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/1970%20HK%20Equity) and its 800 [screens](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-06-16/imax-adds-screens-in-china-with-wanda-betting-on-blockbusters) in the country.

Hollywood has struggled to resonate with Chinese audiences this year. Excluding Zootopia 2, which carried over much of its run after opening late last year, no Hollywood release had broken into the year’s top 10 box office before *Spider-Man: Brand New Day*.

The biggest Hollywood openings of 2026 in China had been *Minions & Monsters*, with about 301 million yuan in ticket sales, and *Toy Story 5*, at 283 million yuan, both well below the blockbuster levels once routinely achieved by Marvel and other major studio franchises.
