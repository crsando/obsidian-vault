### 中文译文

## 美国准备对伊朗实施史无前例的“经济孤立”计划

作者：[Jon Herskovitz](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AT6ZMmc-MW4/jon-herskovitz)和[Sara Gharaibeh](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AXN9SkapPns/sara-gharaibeh)
2026年8月14日上午10:50 GMT+8
*更新于2026年8月14日晚上9:25 GMT+8*

美国财政部长斯科特·贝森特表示，美国即将宣布针对伊朗的前所未有的经济措施，以加大特朗普政府迫使德黑兰在近六个月的战争后屈服的力度。

贝森特周四在接受[Newsmax采访](https://www.newsmax.com/newsmax-tv/bessent-iran-economic/2026/08/13/id/1266059/)时表示：“敬请关注，下周我们将发布更多公告，因为我们打算对一个国家实施经济孤立历史上从未有过的措施。”他补充说，此举将是“组合拳”的一部分，包括继续封锁伊朗的港口。

2月28日，美国和以色列对伊朗发动空袭，战斗由此爆发。伊朗以导弹和无人机攻击美国在中东的基地和盟友作为回应。在6月达成停战协议之前，已有数千人丧生，其中大部分在伊朗。

旨在实现持久和平的谈判陷入停滞，间歇性冲突仍在继续，扰乱了该地区石油和其他关键商品的流动。

伊朗经济遭受重创，大部分工业产能受损，原油出口因美国封锁而大幅减少。但这个伊斯兰共和国经受住了一轮又一轮的制裁，这些制裁未能迫使它在核计划上让步，也未能迫使其放弃对霍尔木兹海峡的控制。

![Ships anchored in the Strait of Hormuz on Aug. 10. Photographer: Ali Saeedi/Getty Images](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3bc81cc90b148198932ef27f7bd1e236/3bc81cc9-0b14-8178-8e31-c1653c9ad8ff-84b1f373038d9b0b6399618081918543.png)

美国总统唐纳德·特朗普近日再次推动其政府向伊朗施加经济压力，因为美国面临所需关键武器的短缺——全球洞察：美国最需要的武器即将耗尽（1）——并且似乎不愿扩大一场军事行动，该行动在11月中期选举前面临日益高涨的国内反对。

特朗普上周末对 Axios 表示，他在伊朗问题上“低调处理”。“我们只是在观察伊朗，伊朗通货膨胀严重，而且他们没有钱，”他说。

然而，在经历了几十年的制裁以及包括全面海上封锁伊朗港口在内的新一轮施压运动之后，美国究竟还能采取哪些有意义的經濟措施，尚不清楚，除非对购买伊朗石油的国家（如中国）实施二级制裁。这很可能会引发北京的报复措施，并增加全球能源价格的不确定性。

与此同时，特朗普和其他高级官员经常对伊朗发出严厉威胁——但没有兑现——以此施压德黑兰进行谈判，以结束许多分析人士现在所称的僵局。

据休斯·哈伯德与里德律师事务所（Hughes Hubbard & Reed）合伙人杰里米·帕纳（Jeremy Paner）称，自2018年以来，美国已对德黑兰增加了约2200项制裁。帕纳负责追踪伊朗石油和石化行业的制裁名单。

“如果47年的制裁都未能击破德黑兰的意志，那么更多的同类措施也不太可能带来任何改变，”这是彭博经济（Bloomberg Economics）发布的《全球洞察：特朗普几乎没有好选项来加大对伊朗施压》报告中的观点，该报告由詹妮弗·韦尔奇（Jennifer Welch）牵头。她认为，最可能出现的情况是，特朗普维持此前的路径，维持制裁和封锁，同时辅以有限打击和外交努力。

伊朗方面则[已重组](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-12/iran-takes-more-aggressive-military-stance-with-us-talks-frozen)其军队，在海外采取更具进攻性的姿态，而此时结束战争的谈判仍深陷僵局，这表明德黑兰正在为可能出现的长期地区冲突时代做准备。

霍尔木兹海峡一直是一个关键症结。双方都声称控制这条水道，战前全球五分之一的石油和天然气经由此处运输，且双方都要求对方做出不可能实现的让步。

伊朗和阿曼一直在就建立一条通过霍尔木兹海峡的航运路线进行漫长谈判，但美国并未参与这些讨论。伊朗最高国家安全委员会新任秘书穆赫森·雷扎伊（Mohsen Rezaee）表示，任何协议都将与全面重新开放分开。

![图片](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3bc81cc90b148198932ef27f7bd1e236/3bc81cc9-0b14-817c-aab9-d09b22309e94-96ccfae2c72ee12275cb7fef9a45920d.png)

据阿联酋国家通讯社WAM报道，两艘阿布扎比国家石油公司的船只周四晚间在通过霍尔木兹海峡时遭到袭击。报道称，无人受伤，情况已得到控制。

阿联酋外交部谴责此次袭击，在一份声明中表示，以商业航运为目标，并将该海峡用作经济胁迫或勒索的工具，构成伊朗伊斯兰革命卫队的“海盗行为”。

同时，英国海上贸易行动办公室表示，收到军方报告称，周四一艘油轮在驶出霍尔木兹海峡时遭无人机袭击。该船受到轻微损坏，全体船员安全并已确认无恙。

美国能源部长克里斯·赖特周四对福克斯新闻表示，美国军队护航船只通过霍尔木兹海峡的能力正在增强，来自该地区的原油流量将继续增加，石油市场的“紧俏”源于炼油而非运输。

副总统JD·万斯对该电视频道表示，与伊朗战争的首要目标是让美国民众的石油和汽油价格保持低廉，并称美国“手头有很多工具可用来迫使伊朗采取行动”。

周五，布伦特原油价格上涨多达1.9%，至接近每桶88.69美元，使该全球基准油价本周涨幅扩大至超过5%。

以色列没有参与近期在波斯湾的冲突，同时继续对加沙和黎巴嫩的伊朗支持民兵组织展开行动。

以色列总理本雅明·内塔尼亚胡表示，如果美国提出要求，以色列准备恢复对伊朗的袭击，但以色列政府成员也表示，经济压力可能足以推翻伊朗政府。

“最终将推翻这个政权的是它的衰弱，主要是经济的衰弱，”财政部长贝扎莱尔·斯莫特里赫(Bezalel Smotrich)在7月21日接受特拉维夫103调频电台采访时表示，他的内阁同僚也呼应了这番话。“目前这种存在禁运、经济压力和制裁的局面，对我们来说最有利。”

## US Readies Unprecedented ‘Economic Isolation’ Plan for Iran

## US Readies Unprecedented ‘Economic Isolation’ Plan for Iran

By [Jon Herskovitz](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AT6ZMmc-MW4/jon-herskovitz) and [Sara Gharaibeh](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AXN9SkapPns/sara-gharaibeh)
August 14, 2026 at 10:50 AM GMT+8
*Updated on August 14, 2026 at 9:25 PM GMT+8*

The US will soon announce unprecedented economic measures against Iran, Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said, intensifying the Trump administration’s effort to force Tehran’s capitulation after almost six months of war.

“Watch this space for more announcements coming next week because we are going to apply measures like have never been seen in the history of economic isolation on a country,” Bessent said in [an interview](https://www.newsmax.com/newsmax-tv/bessent-iran-economic/2026/08/13/id/1266059/) with Newsmax on Thursday. The move will be part of a “one-two punch” that includes the continued blockade of Iran’s ports, he added.

Fighting erupted on Feb. 28 when the US and Israel launched airstrikes on Iran, which responded with missile and drone attacks against American bases and allies across the Middle East. Thousands of people were killed, mostly in Iran, before a truce was agreed in June.

Talks aimed at bringing about a lasting peace have stalled and intermittent clashes have continued, disrupting the flow of oil and other key commodities from the region.

Iran’s economy has taken a major hit, with much of its industrial capacity damaged and crude exports severely curtailed by the US blockade. But the Islamic Republic has weathered waves of sanctions that have failed to force it to bend on its nuclear program or relinquish control over the Strait of Hormuz.

US President Donald Trump in recent days has renewed his administration’s push for economic pressure on Iran as the US faces a shortfall of necessary GLOBAL INSIGHT: US Is Running Out of Weapons It Needs Most (1) and appears wary of expanding a military campaign that faces mounting domestic opposition heading into midterm elections in November.

Trump Trump to Axios: "We are low keying it" with Iran Axios over the weekend that he is “low-keying it” with Iran. “We are just watching Iran with its huge inflation and the fact they have no money,” he said.

However, after decades of sanctions and a renewed pressure campaign that’s included a full naval blockade of Iran’s ports, it’s unclear exactly what meaningful economic measures the US could implement without resorting to secondary sanctions on buyers of Iranian oil, such as China. That would likely spark retaliatory measures from Beijing and cause more global energy price uncertainty.

At the same time, Trump and other senior officials have often made strident threats against Iran — and not followed through — as part of an effort to pressure Tehran into talks to end what many analysts are now calling a stalemate.

The US has already added some 2,200 sanctions on Tehran since 2018, according to Jeremy Paner, a partner at Hughes Hubbard & Reed, who tracks designations on Iran’s oil and petrochemical sectors.

“If 47 years of sanctions haven’t broken Tehran’s will, more of the same is unlikely to produce any changes,” according to a Bloomberg Economics GLOBAL INSIGHT: Trump Has Few Good Options to Up Iran Pressure led by Jennifer Welch. She sees the most likely scenario as Trump staying on the same path as before, maintaining sanctions and the blockade, alongside limited strikes and diplomatic efforts.

Iran, for its part, [has reorganized](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-12/iran-takes-more-aggressive-military-stance-with-us-talks-frozen) its military to be more aggressive abroad as talks on ending the war remain mired in stalemate, a sign that Tehran is preparing for the possibility of a protracted era of regional conflict.

The Strait of Hormuz has been a key sticking point. Both sides claim control of the waterway, through which one-fifth of the world’s oil and gas transited prior to the war, and are demanding concessions that are unlikely to be met.

Iran and Oman have been locked in lengthy talks about establishing a shipping route through the strait, but the US isn’t party to those discussions. Mohsen Rezaee, the newly appointed secretary of Iran’s Supreme National Security Council, said any deal would remain separate from a full reopening.

Two Abu Dhabi National Oil Co. vessels were attacked while transiting Hormuz on Thursday evening, the United Arab Emirates’ state news agency WAM reported. No one was injured and the situation has been brought under control, it said.

The UAE’s foreign ministry condemned the strike, saying in a statement that targeting commercial shipping and using the strait as a tool of economic coercion or blackmail constituted “acts of piracy” by Iran’s Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps.

Meanwhile, the UK Maritime Trade Operations said it received a report from military authorities of a tanker being struck by a drone on Thursday while exiting Hormuz. The vessel sustained minor damage and the crew are all safe and accounted for, it said.

The US forces’ ability to escort ships through Hormuz is growing and crude flows from the region will continue to increase, with the “pinch” in the oil market stemming from refining rather than deliveries, US Energy Secretary Chris Wright told Fox News on Thursday.

Vice President JD Vance told the TV channel that the first goal in the war with Iran is keeping the price of oil and gasoline cheap for Americans, and that the US has “a lot of tools at our disposal to compel action” from Iran.

Brent crude prices rose as much as 1.9% to almost $88.69 a barrel on Friday, lifting the global benchmark’s gains for the week to more than 5%.

Israel has sat out recent clashes in the Persian Gulf, while pursuing operations against Iran-backed militias in Gaza and Lebanon.

Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu has said Israel is poised to resume attacks on the Islamic Republic if asked by the US, but members of his government have also said economic pressure could suffice to topple its government.

“What will ultimately bring down the regime is its weakening, primarily that of its economy,” Finance Minister Bezalel Smotrich said in a July 21 interview with Tel Aviv 103 FM radio station, remarks that were echoed by his cabinet colleagues. “The current situation, in which there is an embargo, economic pressure and sanctions, is best for us.”
