## 美国核心通胀表现温和，缓解美联储压力

作者：[Mark Niquette](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AQVgYOcI2go/mark-niquette)
2026年8月12日下午8:31 GMT+8
*更新于2026年8月12日下午11:02 GMT+8*

美国7月核心通胀表现温和，可能缓解美联储加息压力。

据美国劳工统计局周三公布的数据，不包括通常波动较大的食品和能源类别的消费者价格指数较[上个月](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CPUPXCHG%20Index)上涨0.2%。按[年度](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CPI%20XYOY%20Index)计算，该指数上涨2.5%，与2021年3月以来最慢增速持平。

总体而言，消费者价格较[上月](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CPI%20CHNG%20Index)上涨0.1%，较[上年同期](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CPI%20YOY%20%20Index)上涨3.4%。

![图片](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3ba81cc90b1481919e9fc9f65ee6789b/3ba81cc9-0b14-81fd-aeaa-ce289bf03ca4-578d563a2cf72029bb18813d398bacfa.png)

该报告表明，伊朗战争引发的能源价格冲击的影响在7月继续消退。这些数据可能让美联储在关于是否于9月15-16日会议上上调借贷成本的辩论中，有更多空间权衡通胀压力与近期招聘放缓。

政策制定者将在9月会议前看到更多关于就业和通胀的报告，投资者将密切关注美联储主席凯文·沃什（Kevin Warsh）预计于本月晚些时候在央行年度杰克逊霍尔研讨会上发表的讲话。

“对于希望保持耐心的美联储官员来说，这是个好消息，”道明证券美国经济主管奥斯卡·穆尼奥斯（Oscar Munoz）表示。“至少对于寻找进展和通胀放缓迹象的美联储官员来说，这是连续第二份报告。”

美国股市开盘走高，美国国债收益率下跌，因投资者减少了对9月加息的押注。

摩根资产管理首席全球策略师 David Kelly 在“彭博监控”节目中谈及该数据。来源：彭博

能源和汽油价格连续第二个月下跌。7 月美伊停火破裂、敌对行动再次爆发后，美国汽油价格再次突破每加仑 4 美元，但全月平均仍低于 6 月。

与此同时，[食品杂货价格](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CPSFHOME%20Index)自 3 月以来首次下跌，部分原因是[环孢子虫疫情](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-06/sweetgreen-cuts-outlook-on-parasite-induced-demand-drop)期间生菜价格创纪录下跌。[未煮熟的碎牛肉](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CPIQUGBS%20Index)价格下跌 1.6%，为 2020 年以来最大跌幅。

住房价格环比上涨 0.1%，受[酒店和汽车旅馆费率](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CPIQOLHS%20Index)下跌 3.3%（一年多来最大跌幅）的抑制。该报告中的租金指标均上涨 0.3%。

根据彭博汇编的数据，不包括能源和租金的[服务价格](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CSXHSPCM%20Index)在前一个月下跌后小幅上涨 0.2%。医疗服务和机票是价格上涨的服务类别之一。

### **计算机通胀**

不包括食品和能源商品在内的商品价格在连续两个月下跌后反弹。[计算机软件和配件](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CPIQCSAN%20Index)价格同比上涨 21.2%，创纪录，而计算机、外围设备和智能家居助手涨幅为四年多来最大。

经济学家正在关注[6 月宣布的](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-25/apple-raises-mac-and-ipad-prices-to-counter-memory-shortages)热门消费科技产品（如[苹果公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/AAPL%20US%20Equity)的 Mac 和 iPad）价格上涨的影响，该涨价是由在建设数据中心的竞赛中全球内存芯片短缺推动的。

> “7月份的CPI报告表现温和，足以降低9月加息的可能性，但还不足以完全排除。由于核心CPI与2月份创下的五年低点持平，且7月非农就业人数下降，很难提出紧急加息的理据。”——Anna Wong 和 Troy Durie

> 阅读完整报告，请点击“美国反应：核心CPI温和降低美联储加息可能性（1）”

周四公布的生产者价格数据将提供更多类别的洞察，这些类别直接纳入美联储青睐的通胀指标，即个人消费支出价格指数，该指数将于本月晚些时候发布。按该指标衡量的核心通胀今年总体上一直比CPI中的核心通胀上升得更快。

9月，美国经济分析局将对PCE指数中某些类别的价格计算方式实施[变更](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-09/warsh-to-get-some-inflation-relief-from-upcoming-pce-makeover)，包括法律服务、计算机软件和投资咨询。

周三发布的另一份报告将通胀数据与近期薪资数据相结合，显示7月实际平均时薪同比下跌0.2%，延续了自伊朗战争开始以来的一系列疲弱读数。

## US Core Inflation Comes in Subdued, Easing Pressure on Fed

By [Mark Niquette](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AQVgYOcI2go/mark-niquette)
August 12, 2026 at 8:31 PM GMT+8
*Updated on August 12, 2026 at 11:02 PM GMT+8*

Underlying US inflation was subdued in July, likely easing pressure on the Federal Reserve to raise interest rates.

The consumer price index, excluding often-volatile food and energy categories, increased 0.2% [from a month earlier](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CPUPXCHG%20Index), according to Bureau of Labor Statistics data out Wednesday. On an [annual basis](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CPI%20XYOY%20Index), it advanced 2.5%, matching the slowest pace since March 2021.

Overall, consumer prices rose 0.1% [from the prior month](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CPI%20CHNG%20Index) and 3.4% [from a year earlier](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CPI%20YOY%20%20Index).

The report suggests the impact of the energy-price shock from the Iran war continued to fade in July. The figures may give the Fed more room to weigh inflation pressures against a recent slowdown in hiring as it debates whether to lift borrowing costs at its Sept. 15-16 meeting.

Policymakers will see additional reports on employment and inflation before the September meeting, and investors will be listening closely to Fed Chairman Kevin Warsh’s expected remarks at the central bank’s annual Jackson Hole symposium later this month.

“It’s good news for those Fed officials who want to be patient here,” said Oscar Munoz, head of US economics at TD Securities. “At least for the Fed officials who are looking for signs of progress, disinflation, it’s a second consecutive report.”

US stocks opened higher and Treasury yields fell as investors pared bets on a September rate hike.

David Kelly, JPMorgan Asset Management’s chief global strategist, speaks about the data on “Bloomberg Surveillance.” Source: Bloomberg

Energy and gasoline prices fell for a second month. US gasoline prices rose above $4 a gallon again in July after a US-Iran ceasefire collapsed and hostilities reignited, but remained lower on average across the entire month than in June.

[Grocery prices](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CPSFHOME%20Index), meanwhile, fell for the first time since March, thanks in part to a record decline in the cost of lettuce amid the [cyclospora outbreak](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-06/sweetgreen-cuts-outlook-on-parasite-induced-demand-drop). [Uncooked ground beef](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CPIQUGBS%20Index) prices fell 1.6%, the most since 2020.

Shelter prices rose 0.1%, restrained by a 3.3% decline in [hotel and motel rates](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CPIQOLHS%20Index), the most in more than a year. The report’s measures of rents both rose 0.3%.

[Services prices](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CSXHSPCM%20Index), excluding energy and rents, rose a modest 0.2% following a decline the month before, according to data compiled by Bloomberg. Medical care and airfares were among services categories that saw rising prices.

### **Computer Inflation**

Goods prices, excluding food and energy commodities, rebounded following two months of declines. [Computer software and accessories](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CPIQCSAN%20Index) prices rose a record 21.2% from a year earlier, while computers, peripherals and smart home assistants advanced by the most in more than four years.

Economists are monitoring the impact of price increases [announced in June](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-25/apple-raises-mac-and-ipad-prices-to-counter-memory-shortages) on popular consumer tech products like [Apple Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/AAPL%20US%20Equity)’s Macs and iPads, which have been driven by a global shortage of memory chips amid a race to build data centers.

> “July’s CPI report was modest enough to lower chances of a September rate hike, but not low enough to write it off completely. With core CPI matching its five-year low from February, and July payrolls declining, it’s hard to make an urgent case to hike.” — Anna Wong and Troy Durie

Figures on producer prices due Thursday will offer insights on additional categories that feed directly into the Fed’s preferred measure of inflation, based on the price index for personal consumption expenditures, which will be released later this month. Core inflation by that measure has generally been running faster than in the CPI this year.

In September, the Bureau of Economic Analysis will [implement changes](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-09/warsh-to-get-some-inflation-relief-from-upcoming-pce-makeover) to how prices are calculated for certain categories in the PCE index, including legal services, computer software and investment advice.

A separate report Wednesday that combines the inflation figures with recent wage data showed that real average hourly earnings declined 0.2% in July from a year earlier, extending a string of weak readings since the Iran war began.
