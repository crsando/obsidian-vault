### 中文译文

## 滴露助力中国市场增长，利洁时销售提振业绩转机

利洁时集团（Reckitt Benckiser Group Plc）第二季度销售额增长超预期，主要得益于中国和印度等市场对清洁及卫生产品的需求。

利洁时周三表示，包括滴露消毒液和杜蕾斯避孕套在内的核心品牌同比销售额增长4.2%，高于分析师预期的3.7%。公司维持全年展望，并宣布未来12个月进行5亿英镑（约合6.65亿美元）的股票回购。

伦敦股价一度上涨7.4%，创一年来盘中最大涨幅。截至周二收盘，今年累计下跌17%。

首席执行官Kris Licht正试图通过出售表现不佳的业务部门，专注于包括止痛药诺洛芬在内的畅销品牌，来扭转这家跨国公司的局面。利洁时去年剥离了家庭护理部门，并正在为其美国婴儿配方奶粉业务美赞臣寻找选择方案。

英国超市中的滴露抗菌清洁剂。摄影师：Chris Ratcliffe/彭博

巴克莱分析师Warren Ackerman在一份报告中称，业绩“好于预期”，并指出公司核心品牌增长加快。摩根士丹利的Sarah Simon表示，业绩应能让投资者对公司实现指引的能力抱有信心，“尽管仍需要下半年显著加速”。

利洁时近几个季度的盈利受到非处方药销售低迷的拖累，原因是感冒和流感季节较弱。

欧洲市场表现依然疲软，但销售额和销量下降均低于预期，利洁时预计该地区将在下半年恢复增长。北美市场增长低于分析师预期。

利希特在接受采访时表示，由于针对 Mucinex 12 Hour 的需求（该产品旨在更持久地缓解感冒和流感症状），公司预计下半年在美国将实现强劲增长。

他说：“当我们推出有意义的创新产品时，就像我们对 Mucinex 所做的那样，以及之前对 Lysol 所做的，消费者肯定感兴趣，肯定愿意付钱。”

上周，利洁时退出了受欧盟制裁影响的俄罗斯卫生业务。不包括该业务，利洁时核心品牌的同比销售额在第二季度增长了4.9%。

美赞臣在上半年恢复了增长。该公司正面临持续的诉讼，指控其早产儿配方奶粉可能导致婴儿患上危及生命的肠道疾病，该公司否认这一指控。

利洁时的业绩紧随竞争对手[联合利华](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/ULVR%20LN%20Equity)之后，该公司周二[上调了展望](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-28/unilever-sales-jump-on-emerging-market-demand-for-dove-soap)，此前其第二季度销售额增幅超过预期。

## Reckitt Sales Boost Turnaround as Dettol Fuels China Growth

Reckitt Benckiser Group Plc’s sales rose more than expected in the second quarter, driven by demand for cleaning and hygiene products in markets including China and India.

Like-for-like sales of core brands including Dettol disinfectants and Durex condoms grew 4.2%, Reckitt said Wednesday, ahead of the 3.7% estimated by analysts. The company maintained its full-year outlook and announced a £500 million ($665 million) share buyback over the next 12 months.

Shares rose as much as 7.4% in London, the most in a year on an intraday basis. They were down 17% this year through Tuesday’s close.

Chief Executive Officer Kris Licht is trying to turn around the multinational by selling off underperforming units to focus on bestselling brands including Nurofen for pain relief. Reckitt offloaded its home-care unit last year and is exploring options for its US infant-formula business Mead Johnson.

Dettol antibacterial cleaner in a UK supermarket. Photographer: Chris Ratcliffe/Bloomberg

The results were “better than feared,” Barclays analyst Warren Ackerman said in a note, pointing to faster growth in the company’s core brands. Morgan Stanley’s Sarah Simon said the results should give investors confidence about the company’s ability to meet its guidance, “even if it still requires a meaningful acceleration” in the second half.

Reckitt’s earnings were dragged in recent quarters by lackluster sales of its over-the-counter medicines due to a weak cold and flu season.

Performance remained weaker in Europe, though drops in sales and volume were less than estimates and Reckitt expects the region to return to growth in the second half. North America grew less than analysts expected.

The company expects strong growth in the US in the second half, Licht said in an interview, driven by demand for Mucinex 12 Hour, which is intended to give longer relief from cold and flu symptoms.

“When we launch meaningful new innovation, like we’re doing with Mucinex and we have with Lysol, the consumer is definitely interested, definitely willing to pay,” he said.

Last week, Reckitt Reckitt Exits Russian Hygiene Business Dented by EU Sanctions its Russian hygiene business that was impacted by European sanctions. Without the unit, like-for-like sales of Reckitt’s core brands grew 4.9% in the second quarter.

Mead Johnson returned to growth in the first half. The company is dealing with ongoing litigation over allegations — which it denies — that its preterm infant formula can cause a life-threatening intestinal disease in babies.

Reckitt’s results follow rival [Unilever Plc](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/ULVR%20LN%20Equity), which [lifted its outlook](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-28/unilever-sales-jump-on-emerging-market-demand-for-dove-soap) on Tuesday after sales rose more than expected in the second quarter.
