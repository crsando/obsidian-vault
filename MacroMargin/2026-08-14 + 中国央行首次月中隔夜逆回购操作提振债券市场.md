## 中国央行首次月中隔夜逆回购操作提振债券市场

作者：[Iris Ouyang](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AWWq_orEmek/iris-ouyang) 和 [Julia Zhong](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AXeDYtoIWg0/julia-zhong) 2026年8月14日 上午9:32 GMT+8 *更新于2026年8月14日 下午12:51 GMT+8*

### **要点** by Bloomberg AI

- 中国央行周五通过隔夜逆回购操作注入流动性，这是其首次在月中进行此类操作。
- 该操作向银行体系净注入3480亿元人民币，中国10年期国债收益率降至1.68%，为2025年7月以来最低水平。
- 分析师认为此次注资是战术性调整而非政策转向，央行旨在平滑流动性，同时避免市场过度泛滥。
中国央行周五通过隔夜逆回购操作注入流动性，这是其首次在月中开展此类操作，提振了债券多头情绪。

中国人民银行在一份声明中表示，开展了3490亿元人民币（约合517亿美元）的隔夜逆回购操作。考虑到7天期合约中有10亿元到期，该操作向银行体系净注入“中国人民银行在公开市场操作中净注入3480亿元”——这是本月[首次此类加量操作](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CZINOMNF%20Index)。

受此影响，中国10年期国债收益率下降1个基点至1.68%，为2025年7月以来最低水平。强劲的投资者需求延续至周五的国债发行，推动10年期国债拍卖收益率降至“中国以1.66%发行10年期国债：交易员（1）”所显示的水平。

隔夜期限此前曾在[6月底](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-29/pboc-debuts-overnight-operation-surprises-with-no-rate-released)和7月前后使用过，这是在中国央行行长潘功胜于6月陆家嘴论坛宣布央行将扩大短期流动性工具箱之后。

中国央行加大对该工具的使用，凸显其微调短期流动性状况的努力，同时确保投资者对月中缴税期间的充裕流动性放心。该举措也在重振债市人气，此前中国央行本周稍早罕见暂停公开市场操作，抑制了债券多头。

分析师认为，此次资金投放是战术性调整，而非政策转向。

华侨银行外汇与利率策略主管Frances Cheung表示：“鉴于*过去几天中国央行连续四天未开展7天期逆回购操作*，同时存在缴税流动性需求，此次操作规模并不算特别大。” “对流动性管理的立场似乎没有改变，即中国央行旨在平滑流动性，但不会让市场流动性过度泛滥。”

在通胀不及预期和工厂活动下滑的背景下，充裕的流动性助推了近期债市上涨。市场注意力现在转向下周一的7月活动数据，以寻找有关经济的新线索。

除短期操作外，中国央行还在应对更广泛的流动性需求。中国央行周四发布声明称，将于周五（8月14日）进行1万亿元人民币6个月期买断式逆回购操作。该操作将完全对冲本周到期的同等规模合约。

法国农业信贷银行（Credit Agricole CIB）策略师Jeffrey Zhang表示：“隔夜逆回购操作应体现出中国央行进行流动性管理的灵活性和精准性，既能满足季节性短期流动性需求，同时也避免此后市场流动性过剩。”

他说：“市场回购利率锚定更稳，且未来隔夜融资成本波动可能减小，这可能会在短期内增强套利交易的信心。”

## PBOC’s First Mid-Month Overnight Reverse Repo Boosts Bonds

By [Iris Ouyang](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AWWq_orEmek/iris-ouyang) and [Julia Zhong](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AXeDYtoIWg0/julia-zhong)August 14, 2026 at 9:32 AM GMT+8*Updated on August 14, 2026 at 12:51 PM GMT+8*

### **Takeaways ** by Bloomberg AI

- China's central bank injected liquidity through overnight reverse repos on Friday, its first such operation in the middle of a month.
- The operation resulted in a net injection of 348 billion yuan into the banking system, and China's 10-year government bond yield fell to 1.68%, its lowest level since July 2025.
- Analysts view the injection as a tactical adjustment rather than a policy shift, with the central bank aiming to smooth liquidity but not overflood the market.
China’s central bank injected liquidity through overnight reverse repos on Friday, marking its first such operation in the middle of a month in a boost to bond bulls.

The People’s Bank of China conducted 349 billion yuan ($51.7 billion) of overnight reverse repos, it said in a statement. Taking into account the 1 billion yuan maturity in the seven-day contracts, the operation resulted in a net injection of PBOC Injects Net 348b Yuan in Open Market Operations into the banking system — the [first such addition this month](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CZINOMNF%20Index).

Following the move, China’s 10-year government bond yield fell one basis point to 1.68%, its lowest level since July 2025. Strong investor demand carried over into a sovereign debt sale on Friday, pushing 10-year auction yields to the China Sells 10-Year Sovereign Bonds at 1.66%: Trader (1).

The overnight tenor was previously deployed near the [end of June](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-29/pboc-debuts-overnight-operation-surprises-with-no-rate-released) and July, following PBOC Governor Pan Gongsheng’s announcement at the Lujiazui Forum in June that the central bank would expand its short-term liquidity toolkit.

The central bank’s increased use of the tool underscores its efforts to fine-tune short-term liquidity conditions while reassuring investors of ample liquidity for mid-month tax payments. The move is also reviving sentiment in the bond market after the rally PBOC’s Rare Open Market Operation Pause Restrains Bond Bulls earlier this week when the PBOC skipped open market operations.

Analysts view the injection as a tactical adjustment rather than a policy shift.

“The amount is not seen as particularly big given *PBOC CONDUCTS ZERO VOL. OF 7-DAY RR OPS FOR 4 CONSECUTIVE DAYS in 7-day reverse repos over past days, while there is liquidity demand for tax payment,” said Frances Cheung, head of foreign exchange and rates strategy at Oversea-Chinese Banking Corp. “The stance toward liquidity management appears unchanged, in that the PBOC aims to smooth liquidity but not overflood the market.”

Ample liquidity has buoyed a recent bond rally amid weaker-than-expected [inflation](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-09/china-s-inflation-cools-as-oil-shock-of-iran-war-starts-to-ease) and a [factory activity slump](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-03/china-s-factory-activity-gauge-slows-as-economy-weakens). Attention is now turning to Monday’s July activity data for fresh clues on the economy.

Beyond short-term operations, the central bank is also addressing broader liquidity needs. The central bank is set to conduct PBOC to Auction 1t Yuan Outright Reverse Repo on Aug. 14 of six-month outright reverse repos on Friday, it said in a statement on Thursday. The operation will fully offset an equal amount of maturing contracts this week.

“The overnight reverse repo operation should indicate the flexibility and precision with which the PBOC conducts liquidity management, so as to meet seasonal short-term liquidity demand while also avoiding excess liquidity in the market thereafter,” said Jeffrey Zhang, strategist at Credit Agricole CIB.

“The better-anchored market repo rates, with likely lessened volatility of overnight funding costs ahead, could lift conviction in carry trades in the near term,” he said.
