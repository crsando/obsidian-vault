## 美债收益率攀升，数据令美联储押注持续：市场综述

华尔街交易员推动债券走低，股市波动，最新经济报告几乎没有改变市场对美联储今年将加息的押注。

货币市场已完全消化美联储12月加息预期，数据显示经济仍处于稳固基础。虽然一项关键[通胀](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/PCE%20CMOM%20Index)指标符合预期，但仍远高于央行目标。短期美国国债表现落后。[标普500指数](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/SPX%20Index)波动，交易员为[英伟达公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/NVDA%20US%20Equity)的业绩做准备。油价下跌，因霍尔木兹海峡恢复的希望。

![Traders parse economic data. Source: Bloomberg](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3c881cc90b148182a80cd3bcc2dcd6c1/3c881cc9-0b14-81c1-a581-dae79ea1c672-948333d136ad7d3b1e11e4d7427bb56b.png)

摩根士丹利财富管理部门的艾伦·赞特纳表示，由于投资者对任何可能提高加息概率的因素仍然敏感，最新经济数据未必是他们希望看到的。

她表示，这些数据不足以改变9月美联储会议的平衡，但如果后续数据指向同一方向，政策制定者可能感到更有压力需要采取行动。

“挑战越来越明显：通胀仍高得令人不安，投资者将关注美联储主席凯文·沃什是否利用周五的[杰克逊霍尔](https://www.kansascityfed.org/research/jackson-hole-economic-symposium/)演讲来阐述政策制定者计划如何将其拉回美联储的长期目标，”eToro的布雷特·肯威尔表示。

TradeStation的[大卫·拉塞尔](https://tracking.us.nylas.com/l/b501027b68e448ac89c6a68deed7bc6b/0/05f7e46bd61045727296dcff7d09feee3eed464bf02d4c32401522177f53edfb?cache_buster=1787748727)表示，强劲的消费、支出和耐用品订单表明，美联储有空间在不引发衰退的情况下收紧政策。

“这些数据支持美联储委员会中的鹰派政策制定者，并增加了本周晚些时候凯文·沃什的压力，”他表示。“他越来越难以回避加息问题。”

LPL Financial的杰夫·罗奇表示，转折点可能正在临近，但目前消费者仍受益于收入增长超过通胀。

“对政策制定者而言，风险平衡仍偏向通胀，”他表示。“如果地缘政治紧张局势在短期内缓解，核心通胀可能降至3%以下，给投资者带来乐观的理由。”

除了经济前景外，华尔街正在等待[英伟达（Nvidia）](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/NVDA%20US%20Equity)的财报，与其说是关注这家芯片巨头的数字，不如说是关注这些数字对人工智能和市场本身意味着什么。据彭博汇编的数据，分析师预计营收同比近乎翻倍，这将是两年来最快的增速。

## Treasury Yields Rise as Data Keep Fed Bets in Play: Markets Wrap

Wall Street traders sent bonds lower and stocks wavered, with the latest economic reports doing little to alter bets the Federal Reserve will raise interest rates this year.

Money markets fully priced in a Fed hike by December as data showed the economy remains on solid footing. While a key [inflation](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/PCE%20CMOM%20Index) gauge matched expectations, it’s still well above the central bank’s target. Short-dated Treasuries underperformed. The [S&P 500](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/SPX%20Index) fluctuated, with traders bracing for [Nvidia Corp.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/NVDA%20US%20Equity)’s results. Oil fell on hopes for a revival of the Strait of Hormuz.

With investors remaining sensitive to anything that could boost the odds of higher rates, the latest economic numbers weren’t necessarily what they wanted to see, according to Ellen Zentner at Morgan Stanley Wealth Management.

The data wasn’t enough to shift the balance for the September Fed meeting, but if subsequent figures point in the same direction, policymakers may feel more pressure to move off the sidelines, she said.

“The challenge is becoming increasingly clear: Inflation is still too high for comfort, and investors will be watching to see whether Fed Chair Kevin Warsh uses Friday’s [Jackson Hole](https://www.kansascityfed.org/research/jackson-hole-economic-symposium/) speech to address how policymakers plan to bring it back toward the Fed’s long-term target,” said Bret Kenwell at eToro.

Strong consumption, spending and durable goods orders suggest the Fed has room to tighten without causing a recession, according to [David Russell](https://tracking.us.nylas.com/l/b501027b68e448ac89c6a68deed7bc6b/0/05f7e46bd61045727296dcff7d09feee3eed464bf02d4c32401522177f53edfb?cache_buster=1787748727) at TradeStation.

“These numbers support hawkish policymakers at the Fed’s committee and increase pressure on Kevin Warsh later this week,” he said. “It’s getting harder for him to dodge the issue of hiking rates.”

An inflection point may be approaching, but for now consumers continue to benefit from income growth that is outpacing inflation, according to Jeff Roach at LPL Financial.

“For policymakers, the balance of risks still tilts toward inflation,” he said. “If geopolitical tensions ease in the near term, core inflation could fall below 3%, giving investors a reason for optimism.”

Aside from the economic picture, Wall Street is awaiting [Nvidia](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/NVDA%20US%20Equity)’s earnings, not so much for what the numbers will say about the chip giant, but for what they mean to artificial intelligence and the market itself. Analysts project that revenue nearly doubled from a year ago, which would be the fastest pace in two years, according to data compiled by Bloomberg.
