## 中国捷途考虑在巴西采用共享工厂模式进行汽车生产

![Auto Shanghai show in Shanghai](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3b581cc90b1481fe88c7e2bc77e13871/3b581cc9-0b14-81c8-8c63-c7bc8b6109e8-d4288f2d97b304a870c3388afbf00954.jpg)

2025年4月24日，中国上海，在上海国际汽车展媒体日期间，捷途汽车展台展出了捷途T1。路透社/Go Nakamura [购买许可权利，打开新标签页](https://www.reutersconnect.com/item/auto-shanghai-show-in-shanghai/dGFnOnJldXRlcnMuY29tLDIwMjU6bmV3c21sX1JDMjE0RUFNSklDMQ%3D%3D/?utm_medium=rcom-article-media&utm_campaign=rcom-rcp-lead)

圣保罗，8月6日（路透社）——奇瑞集团旗下的中国汽车制造商捷途正评估在巴西生产的选项，包括与已在该国设厂的车企共享闲置工厂空间，一位高管告诉路透社。

- 捷途巴西营销总监恩里克·桑帕约表示，这可能通过与另一个品牌合作实现，类似于该公司与日产[(7201.T), 打开新标签页](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/7201.T)在南非的安排。
- 他拒绝透露捷途是否正在谈判入驻位于雅卡雷伊的一家自2022年起关闭的CAOA奇瑞闲置工厂。
- 捷途自3月进入巴西以来已售出4,000辆汽车，其中大部分为大型混合动力运动型多用途车。
- 该公司计划到2027年底在巴西投资4亿雷亚尔（7800万美元），不包括任何潜在的本地生产成本。
- 捷途在巴西拥有约60家经销商。巴西是全球第六大汽车市场，预计今年总销量略低于300万辆。捷途的目标是到2026年底达到100家经销商。
- 中国汽车制造商在巴西迅速扩张，凭借价格更低的电动和混合动力车型赢得市场份额，竞争日益激烈。
（1美元 = 5.1242雷亚尔）

## China's Jetour weighs shared factory arrangement for Brazil car production

Jetour T1 is displayed at the Jetour Auto booth during a media day for the Auto Shanghai show in Shanghai, China April 24, 2025. REUTERS/Go Nakamura [Purchase Licensing Rights, opens new tab](https://www.reutersconnect.com/item/auto-shanghai-show-in-shanghai/dGFnOnJldXRlcnMuY29tLDIwMjU6bmV3c21sX1JDMjE0RUFNSklDMQ%3D%3D/?utm_medium=rcom-article-media&utm_campaign=rcom-rcp-lead)

SAO PAULO, Aug 6 (Reuters) - Chinese automaker Jetour, part of the Chery group [(9973.HK), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/9973.HK), is evaluating options for production in Brazil, ‌including sharing idle factory space with manufacturers already present in the country, an executive told Reuters.

- Henrique Sampaio, Jetour Brazil's marketing director, said it could be done in partnership with ⁠another brand, similar to an arrangement the company has with Nissan [(7201.T), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/7201.T) in South Africa.
- He declined to say whether Jetour was negotiating to occupy an idle CAOA Chery factory in Jacarei, shuttered since 2022.
- Jetour has sold 4,000 vehicles since entering Brazil in March, mostly large hybrid sport-utility vehicles.
- It ‌plans ⁠to invest 400 million reais ($78 million) in the country by end-2027, not including any potential local production costs.
- Jetour has about 60 dealerships in Brazil, the world's ⁠sixth-largest vehicle market where total sales are expected at just under 3 million units this year, and aims ⁠to reach 100 by end-2026.
- Chinese automakers have expanded rapidly in Brazil, gaining market share ⁠with lower-priced electric and hybrid models and intensifying competition.
($1 = 5.1242 reais)
