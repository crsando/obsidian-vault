### 中文译文

## 荷兰间谍机构寻求扩大权力以对抗中国和俄罗斯

作者：[Patrick Van Oosterom](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AXGOZBPA_Xk/patrick-van-oosterom)
2026年8月28日 晚上9:18 GMT+8

荷兰政府正在寻求扩大其情报机构的权力，使其能够更好地应对中国和俄罗斯的网络攻击。

该法案旨在简化间谍机构的反应流程，避免陷入官僚审批的泥潭。该提案仍需在议会进行辩论和投票，才能成为法律。

“我们看到俄罗斯或中国黑客发起的大规模网络攻击有所增加，”国防部长迪兰·耶希尔格兹-泽格留斯在海牙的新闻发布会上对记者表示。“我们的对手也变得越来越老练和明显。”

“有了这项法律，我们就磨快了刀，”她说。

此举正值荷兰面临双重威胁：一方面是与乌克兰持续战争中的俄罗斯，另一方面是与中国在半导体等关键科技领域的经济竞争。荷兰是芯片设备巨头[ASML](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/ASML%20NA%20Equity)的所在地，该公司此前曾指责[中国竞争对手](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2022-02-09/asml-warns-chinese-rival-may-be-infringing-its-trade-secrets)侵犯其商业机密。

“由于我们高度发达的经济和技术，荷兰是间谍活动和破坏活动的重要目标，”首相罗布·杰滕周五表示，并特别指出中国试图获取荷兰公司数据的网络攻击威胁。

## Dutch Spy Agencies Seek Broad Power to Counter China and Russia

By [Patrick Van Oosterom](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AXGOZBPA_Xk/patrick-van-oosterom)  
August 28, 2026 at 9:18 PM GMT+8

The Dutch government is seeking to expand the powers of its intelligence services to give them the ability to better counter Chinese and Russian cyberattacks.

The legislation seeks to streamline the spy agencies’ ability to react without getting bogged down in bureaucratic approvals. The proposal still needs to be debated and voted on in parliament before it can become law.

“We are seeing an increase in large-scale cyberattacks carried out by Russian or Chinese hackers,” Defense Minister Dilan Yeşilgöz-Zegerius told reporters at a press conference in the Hague. “Our adversaries are also becoming increasingly sophisticated and apparent.”

“With this law, we sharpen our knife,” she said.

The move comes as the Netherlands faces the dual-threat of Russia amid the ongoing war in Ukraine and economic rivalry with China over crucial technology like semiconductors. The country is home to chip equipment giant [ASML, which](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/ASML%20NA%20Equity) has previously accused a [Chinese rival](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2022-02-09/asml-warns-chinese-rival-may-be-infringing-its-trade-secrets) of infringing on its trade secrets.

“The Netherlands is an important target of espionage and sabotage because of our highly advanced economy and technology,” Prime Minister Rob Jetten said Friday, singling out the threat of cyber attacks from China that try to acquire Dutch company data.
