### 中文译文

## 伊朗与美国就霍尔木兹海峡控制权各执一词

![Vessels in the Strait of Hormuz, as seen from Musandam](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3bb81cc90b148131b6e0d887bb7b659c/3bb81cc9-0b14-8171-b6b1-e43a0e2064cc-fee98a1eeabd2e452b5ee62f49630cf2.jpg)

2026年8月3日，从阿曼穆桑代姆看到的霍尔木兹海峡船只。路透社/通讯员

### 摘要

- 伊朗表示，除非对方履行承诺，否则海峡不会开放
- 该水道用于运输全球五分之一的石油和液化天然气
- 美国与伊朗在6月达成临时停火协议，但该协议已破裂
迪拜，8月13日（路透社）——伊朗巴斯基准军事部队新任负责人周四表示，霍尔木兹海峡“处于伊朗的控制和管理之下”，而一天前，美国总统唐纳德·特朗普表示美国拥有这条战略水道的[“完全控制权”](https://www.reuters.com/world/trump-says-us-has-total-control-over-strait-hormuz-2026-08-12/)。

侯赛因·塔伊布表示，美国试图通过在霍尔木兹海峡发动另一场战争来破坏他所说的伊斯兰共和国在该地区的声望，但尽管美国声称伊朗既没有空军也没有海军，它还是再次被击败。

据半官方的法尔斯通讯社报道，塔伊布表示：“今天你们看到，霍尔木兹海峡处于伊斯兰共和国的管理和控制之下。”他补充说，伊朗正继续在完全安全的情况下推进自己的道路。

战争始于2月28日美国与以色列对伊朗的打击，此后德黑兰实际上关闭了霍尔木兹海峡，此前全球五分之一的石油和液化天然气通过该海峡运输。

美国随后对伊朗航运和港口实施海上封锁，同时表示将保护往返于非伊朗港口的船只的航行自由。

伊朗联合军事指挥部“穆罕默德·先知”中央总部周四晚些时候表示，未经德黑兰许可，任何船只不得通过霍尔木兹海峡。

该指挥部在官方媒体发表的声明中称：“美国关于船只正常通过霍尔木兹海峡的毫无根据的说法……不过是谎言和虚假之辞。”

6月，两国达成了一项[临时协议](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/g7-leaders-demand-ceasefire-lebanon-welcome-iran-deal-2026-06-17/)，宣布永久停火，并呼吁迅速恢复海湾地区的航行自由。

几周后，伊朗恢复对据称违反协议安排的船只进行有限攻击，协议破裂，促使美国重新对伊朗南部省份发动打击，华盛顿称这些打击旨在削弱德黑兰在海湾地区针对船只的能力。

另外在周四，高级军事政治官员拉苏尔·萨纳伊-拉德表示，除非对方履行临时协议中的承诺，否则伊朗不会重新开放海峡，并补充说，据法尔斯通讯社报道，重新开放该水道不是美国单方面能够实现的事情。

萨纳伊-拉德还警告说，伊朗在任何未来冲突中都将更坚决地回应，他说：“在可能的未来战争中，我们将更坚定、更具进攻性地站在一起。”

报道：埃尔维利·埃尔维利和迪拜新闻编辑室；编辑：莎伦·辛格尔顿、艾丹·刘易斯

## Iran, US make competing claims over control of Strait of Hormuz

Vessels in the Strait of Hormuz, as seen from Musandam, Oman, August 3, 2026. REUTERS/Stringer

### Summary

- Iran says strait won't open unless other side meets commitments
- Waterway used to carry a fifth of global oil and LNG shipments
- US and Iran agreed interim ceasefire in June but deal unravelled
DUBAI, Aug 13 (Reuters) - The Strait of Hormuz is "under Iran's control and management", ‌the recently appointed head of Iran's Basij paramilitary unit said on Thursday, a day after U.S. President Donald Trump said the United States had ["total control"](https://www.reuters.com/world/trump-says-us-has-total-control-over-strait-hormuz-2026-08-12/) of the strategic waterway.

Hossein Taeb said the U.S. had sought to disrupt what he described as the Islamic Republic's popularity in the region by launching another war in the Strait of Hormuz, but had once more been defeated despite claiming Iran had neither an air force nor ⁠a navy.

"Today you see that the Strait of Hormuz is under the management and control of the Islamic Republic," Taeb said, according to the semi-official Fars news. He added that Iran was continuing on its course in complete security.

After the start of the war, which began with U.S.-Israeli strikes on Iran on February 28, Tehran effectively shut the strait, through which a fifth of the world's oil and liquefied natural gas was previously shipped.

The U.S. subsequently imposed a naval blockade on Iranian shipping and ports, while saying it would protect freedom of navigation for vessels travelling to and from non-Iranian ports.

Iran's joint military command, Khatam al-Anbiya Central Headquarters, said later on Thursday that no vessel could ‌transit ⁠the Strait of Hormuz without Tehran's permission.

"The baseless claims made by the United States regarding the normal passage of vessels through the Strait of Hormuz ... are nothing more than lies and falsehoods," the command said in a statement published by state media.

In June, the two countries reached an [interim agreement](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/g7-leaders-demand-ceasefire-lebanon-welcome-iran-deal-2026-06-17/) declaring a permanent ceasefire and calling for a swift return to freedom of navigation in ⁠the Gulf.

The deal unravelled a few weeks later after Iran resumed limited attacks on vessels that it said were sailing contrary to the arrangements under the agreement, prompting the U.S. to restart strikes on Iran's southern provinces, which Washington said were aimed at ⁠degrading Tehran's ability to target vessels in the Gulf.

Separately on Thursday, senior military political official Rasoul Sanaei-Rad said that Iran would not reopen the strait unless the other side fulfilled its commitments under the interim deal, adding that ⁠the reopening of the waterway was not something the U.S. can achieve unilaterally, according to Fars news.

Sanaei-Rad also warned that Iran would respond more forcefully in any future conflict, saying: "In a possible future war, we will stand more firmly and more offensively."

Reporting by Elwely Elwelly and Dubai Newsroom; Editing by Sharon Singleton, Aidan Lewis
