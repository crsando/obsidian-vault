## 中国的小马智行计划在海外部署超过4,000辆机器人出租车

![Auto Shanghai show in Shanghai](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3c081cc90b1481b992e8ee6f3c4aa8dd/3c081cc9-0b14-81d3-a83c-d571197e19a4-91db607095143c532282db5ec3d8a694.jpg)

2025年4月24日，在中国上海车展媒体日上，中国自动驾驶初创公司小马智行(Pony.ai)的展台上展示了一辆小马智行Robotaxi。路透社/Go Nakamura [获取许可授权，打开新标签页](https://www.reutersconnect.com/item/auto-shanghai-show-in-shanghai/dGFnOnJldXRlcnMuY29tLDIwMjU6bmV3c21sX1JDMjQ0RUFESUJFOA%3D%3D/?utm_medium=rcom-article-media&utm_campaign=rcom-rcp-lead)

北京，8月18日（路透社）——中国自动驾驶公司小马智行(Pony.ai)（[(PONY.O)，打开新标签页](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/PONY.O)）周二表示，其计划中和潜在的海外机器人出租车部署管道超过4,000辆，因为该公司寻求加速在中国以外的扩张。

自动驾驶公司正竞相在海外实现机器人出租车服务的商业化。

随着技术成熟和国内竞争加剧，他们正在中东、亚洲和欧洲等市场寻求新的收入来源和合作伙伴关系。

小马智行目标是到年底拥有3,500辆机器人出租车车队，但未提供所有计划中的海外部署何时完成的时间表。它也没有说明迄今已在海外部署了多少辆机器人出租车。

上周，它公开了[计划](https://www.reuters.com/technology/chinas-ponyai-uber-jointly-deploy-over-2000-robotaxis-europe-2026-08-14/)，通过扩大与优步(Uber)（[(UBER.N)，打开新标签页](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/UBER.N)）的合作，在欧洲部署超过2,000辆机器人出租车。

首席执行官彭军(James Peng)在财报后电话会议上表示，小马智行海外部署管道中的超过4,000辆汽车已经签约，推出时间取决于许可证以及其他监管和运营要求。

竞争对手文远知行(WeRide)（[(WRD.O)，打开新标签页](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/WRD.O)）也在加速全球扩张，并正在考虑将澳大利亚、韩国、日本和东南亚作为潜在市场，路透社[报道](https://www.reuters.com/business/autos-transportation/chinas-weride-eyes-australia-southeast-asia-among-potential-new-markets-after-2026-08-13/)。

根据公司声明，Pony.ai 第二季度业绩强劲，总收入同比增长 68.8% 至 3620 万美元，净亏损同比收窄 14.9% 至 4540 万美元。

机器人出租车收入首次占总收入的三分之一，猛增 691.2%。

彭军表示：“我们将继续推进全年计划，并对超额完成全年机器人出租车服务收入目标充满信心。”

公司仍有望实现全年机器人出租车收入超过 2025 年水平的三倍半。

除机器人出租车外，小马智行第二季度机器人卡车收入同比增长 40%。

小马智行机器人卡车业务负责人何星本月早些时候告诉记者，公司计划在未来两到三年内在中国部署 500 至 1000 辆自动驾驶重型卡车，并力争到 2030 年将其无人驾驶轻型卡车车队规模扩大至 10 万辆。

报道：Qiaoyi Li 和 Liz Lee；编辑：Kirsten Donovan 和 Barbara Lewis

## China's Pony.ai plans to deploy more than 4,000 robotaxis abroad

A Pony.ai Robotaxi is displayed at the booth of the Chinese autonomous driving startup during a media day for the Auto Shanghai show in Shanghai, China April 24, 2025. REUTERS/Go Nakamura [Purchase Licensing Rights, opens new tab](https://www.reutersconnect.com/item/auto-shanghai-show-in-shanghai/dGFnOnJldXRlcnMuY29tLDIwMjU6bmV3c21sX1JDMjQ0RUFESUJFOA%3D%3D/?utm_medium=rcom-article-media&utm_campaign=rcom-rcp-lead)

BEIJING, Aug 18 (Reuters) - Chinese autonomous driving company Pony.ai [(PONY.O), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/PONY.O) has a pipeline of planned and potential overseas robotaxi deployments of more than 4,000 vehicles, it said on Tuesday, as it ‌seeks to accelerate its expansion beyond China.

Autonomous driving firms are racing to commercialise robotaxi services overseas.

As the technology matures and domestic competition intensifies, they are seeking new revenue streams and partnerships in markets including the Middle East, Asia and Europe.

Pony.ai, which is targeting a robotaxi fleet of 3,500 vehicles by year-end, did not provide ⁠a timeline for when all the planned overseas deployments would be finalised. It also did not say how many robotaxis it has deployed overseas so far.

Last week, it made public [plans](https://www.reuters.com/technology/chinas-ponyai-uber-jointly-deploy-over-2000-robotaxis-europe-2026-08-14/) to deploy more than 2,000 robotaxis in Europe through an expanded partnership with Uber [(UBER.N), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/UBER.N).

The more than 4,000 vehicles in Pony.ai's overseas deployment pipeline have already been contracted, with rollout timelines dependent on permits and other regulatory and operational requirements, CEO James Peng said on a post-earnings call.

Rival WeRide [(WRD.O), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/WRD.O) is also accelerating its global expansion and is considering Australia, ‌South ⁠Korea, Japan and Southeast Asia as potential markets, Reuters [reported](https://www.reuters.com/business/autos-transportation/chinas-weride-eyes-australia-southeast-asia-among-potential-new-markets-after-2026-08-13/).

Pony.ai posted a strong second quarter, with total revenue rising 68.8% from a year earlier to $36.2 million, according to a company statement, and its net loss narrowing 14.9% year-on-year to $45.4 million.

Robotaxi revenue, which made up one-third of total revenue for ⁠the first time, surged 691.2%.

“We will continue to advance our full-year plans and are confident in our ability to exceed our full-year robotaxi services revenue target," Peng said.

The company remains on track for ⁠full-year robotaxi revenue to exceed 3-1/2 times 2025's level.

Beyond robotaxis, Pony.ai's second-quarter robotruck revenue increased 40% on year.

The company aims to deploy 500 to 1,000 autonomous heavy-duty trucks in ⁠China within two to three years and scale its driverless light-duty truck fleet to 100,000 vehicles by 2030, Pony.ai's robotruck chief He Xing told reporters earlier this month.
