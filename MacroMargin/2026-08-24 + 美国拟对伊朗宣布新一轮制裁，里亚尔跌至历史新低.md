## 美国拟对伊朗宣布新一轮制裁，里亚尔跌至历史新低

### **要点** 由彭博人工智能提供

- 美国财政部长斯科特·贝森特预计将宣布针对伊朗的新一轮经济措施，以结束这场持续近六个月的战争。
- 此举可能影响伊朗的一些最大贸易伙伴，包括中国、土耳其和印度，并将建立在已实施数十年的广泛制裁基础之上。
- 伊朗经济已承受巨大压力，年通胀率超过80%，货币兑美元汇率跌至历史新低。德黑兰已誓言将对任何支持华盛顿计划的国家进行报复。
美国财政部长斯科特·贝森特预计将宣布针对伊朗的新一轮经济措施。华盛顿正从打击威胁转向更严厉的制裁，以此结束这场持续近六个月的战争。

贝森特将于周一晚些时候公布一项更有效孤立伊朗伊斯兰共和国计划的细节，该计划将建立在已实施数十年的广泛制裁基础之上。目前尚不清楚将宣布什么内容，但此举可能影响伊朗的一些最大贸易伙伴，包括中国、土耳其和印度。

阿拉伯联合酋长国[上周](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-19/uae-cuts-economic-ties-with-iran-after-missiles-target-territory)表示，已切断与德黑兰的一切经济联系，美国的海湾盟友为华盛顿的战略提供了支持。

伊朗货币兑美元汇率跌至[历史低点](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-24/iran-s-currency-hits-new-record-low-ahead-of-us-economic-assault)，自美国总统唐纳德·特朗普[上周](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-19/trump-says-us-starting-economic-warfare-operation-against-iran)宣布计划加大对德黑兰的经济施压以来，已下跌4.5%。

“全世界应该明白，我们的目标是切断支撑这个专制政权的每一条经济命脉，直到德黑兰孤立无援，”贝森特（Scott Bessent）表示。他在《金融时报》撰文称，伊朗的经济“D-Day”即将到来。

美国正努力推动伊朗接受按其条件结束战争，此前大规模军事行动未能实现这一目标。伊朗经济已承受巨大压力，美国海军封锁[严重削减](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-24/iran-oil-cargoes-dry-up-even-before-us-acts-on-tehran-threats)了关键石油出口。

年通胀率超过80%，货币兑美元汇率跌至历史最低点，给9000万人口带来巨大压力。自美国总统唐纳德·特朗普（Donald Trump）上周[宣布](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-19/trump-says-us-starting-economic-warfare-operation-against-iran)计划加强对德黑兰的经济施压以来，里亚尔已下跌4.5%。

伊朗过往已证明善于应对制裁，美国的举措可能引发新一轮军事行动。德黑兰在2月底战争开始后，曾用数千枚导弹和无人机轰炸美国在波斯湾的盟友，并誓言将对任何支持华盛顿计划的国家进行报复。

“伊朗将把任何国家参与或支持美国对伊朗人民的经济战争视为战争行为，”伊朗最高国家安全委员会秘书穆赫森·雷扎伊（Mohsen Rezaee）周日晚在X上写道。

其结果是，“一滴石油都不会出口，无论是通过霍尔木兹海峡还是波斯湾任何地方，”他说。

英国海军表示，周一，一艘油轮在沙特阿拉伯红海延布港（Yanbu）以西约63海里处遭不明抛射物袭击，主甲板起火。所有船员安全，未造成环境影响。

伊朗对亚洲的石油出口几乎已经枯竭，此前美国对伊朗港口的封锁导致满载船只被困在波斯湾内，空船则滞留外海。据参与谈判的交易员称，这些货物的成本因此飙升至多年来的最高水平。

上周，伊朗中央银行行长阿卜杜勒纳赛尔·赫马蒂表示，伊朗的原油出口“几乎已经停止”。

中国表示，任何美国制裁只会加剧与伊朗的紧张关系，不符合任何一方的利益。中国购买伊朗石油的大部分，将“采取必要措施维护其合法权利和利益”，中国外交部发言人林剑说。

迄今为止，美国孤立伊朗的努力主要集中在石油行业中不太引人注目的参与者上，制裁规模较小的私营炼油厂、港口和代理商——同时暂缓严厉执法。其目的是在不对北京关系造成损害或推高油价的情况下施加压力。

与此同时，缓解紧张局势的外交努力仍在继续。

巴基斯坦陆军参谋长阿西姆·穆尼尔（Asim Munir）是结束战争谈判的主要对话者之一，他将于周一[访问德黑兰](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-24/pakistan-army-chief-heads-to-iran-to-boost-mideast-peace-efforts)，与当地高级官员举行会谈，军方媒体部门周一在一份声明中表示。穆尼尔由内政部长莫赫辛·纳克维（Mohsin Naqvi）陪同。

伊朗外交部发言人伊斯梅尔·巴加埃（Esmail Baghaei）表示，阿曼外交部长将于次日访问德黑兰，举行这两个濒临霍尔木兹海峡国家之间的会谈。

## US Set to Unveil Fresh Iran Sanctions as Rial Hits Record Low

US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent is expected to announce a fresh set of economic measures against Iran, as Washington pivots from strike threats to tougher sanctions as a way to end the near six-month war.

Bessent will unveil details of a plan to more effectively isolate the Islamic Republic later Monday, building on already extensive sanctions that have been in place for decades. It’s unclear what will be announced, but the move may impact some of Iran’s largest trading partners, including China, Turkey and India.

The United Arab Emirates said [last week](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-19/uae-cuts-economic-ties-with-iran-after-missiles-target-territory) it had cut all economic ties with Tehran, as US Gulf allies give Washington’s strategy a boost.

Iran’s currency weakened to an [all-time low](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-24/iran-s-currency-hits-new-record-low-ahead-of-us-economic-assault) against the dollar, having fallen 4.5% since US President Donald Trump announced [last week](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-19/trump-says-us-starting-economic-warfare-operation-against-iran) he was planning to escalate economic pressure on Tehran.

“The world should understand that our objective is to sever every economic lifeline that sustains the tyrannical regime until Tehran stands alone,” Bessent Scott Bessent: an economic D-Day is coming for Iran in the Financial Times.

The US is striving to push Iran to accept an end to the war on its terms, having failed to do so with an extensive military campaign. The Iranian economy is already under immense strain, with a US naval blockade [severely curtailing](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-24/iran-oil-cargoes-dry-up-even-before-us-acts-on-tehran-threats) crucial oil exports.

Annual inflation is above 80% and the currency has weakened to an all-time low against the dollar, piling pressure on the population of 90 million people. The rial is down 4.5% since US President Donald Trump [announced](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-19/trump-says-us-starting-economic-warfare-operation-against-iran) last week he was planning to escalate economic pressure on Tehran.

Iran has proven adept at weathering sanctions in the past and the US move risks being met with a fresh round of military action. Tehran, which bombarded US allies in the Persian Gulf with thousands of missiles and drones after the start of the war in late February, vowed to retaliate against any country that supports Washington’s plans.

“Iran will regard any country’s participation in or support for America’s economic war against the Iranian people as an act of war,” Mohsen Rezaee, secretary of Iran’s Supreme National Security Council, wrote on X late Sunday.

The result will be that “not a single drop of oil will be exported, neither through the Strait of Hormuz nor from anywhere in the Persian Gulf,” he said.

The UK Navy said a tanker was struck by an unknown projectile on Monday about 63 nautical miles west of Saudi Arabia’s Red Sea port of Yanbu, causing a fire on the main deck. All crew are safe, with no environmental impact.

Iran’s oil shipments to Asia have already all but dried up, after the US blockade of ports in the Islamic Republic left loaded vessels trapped inside the Gulf and a fleet of empty ones stuck outside. As a result, the cost of those cargoes jumped to the highest levels in years, according to traders involved in negotiations.

Last week, Iran’s Central Bank Governor, Abdolnaser Hemmati, said his country’s crude exports have “virtually stopped.”

China said any US sanctions will only worsen tensions with Iran and won’t serve any side’s interests. The nation, which buys the bulk of Iranian oil, will “take necessary measures to safeguard its legitimate rights and interests,” Chinese Foreign Ministry spokesman Lin Jian said.

To date, US efforts to isolate Iran have focused on lower profile players in the oil sector, sanctioning smaller private refineries, ports and agents — all while holding off heavy-handed enforcement. The aim is to apply pressure without hurting relations with Beijing or driving up the oil price.

Meanwhile, diplomatic efforts to ease tensions continue.

Pakistan’s army chief Asim Munir, one of the main interlocutors in talks to end the war, is [visiting Tehran](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-24/pakistan-army-chief-heads-to-iran-to-boost-mideast-peace-efforts) on Monday for talks with senior local officials, the military’s media wing said in a statement on Monday. Munir is accompanied by Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi.

Oman’s foreign minister will visit Tehran the following day for talks between the two states that border the Strait of Hormuz, Iranian Foreign Minister spokesman Esmail Baghaei said.
