特朗普总统威胁称，如果阿曼“妨碍”美国[与伊朗达成和平协议](https://www.wsj.com/business/hospitality/behind-peace-talks-iran-hard-liners-prepare-for-a-longer-costlier-u-s-war-a42357cb?mod=article_inline)的努力，将对其进行轰炸。

“如果阿曼碍事，我们就把他们炸个*稀巴烂*，”福克斯新闻的特雷·英斯特报道称，特朗普在周一电话采访中这样说。

据英斯特报道，特朗普表示，如果这个海湾国家干预对霍尔木兹海峡伊朗船只的封锁，美军可能会对其进行打击。总统表示，封锁正在给[伊朗](https://www.wsj.com/topics/place/iran)施加压力，但没有给出结束冲突的时间表，称他并不着急。

伊朗谈判代表近几周[与阿曼举行会谈](https://www.wsj.com/world/middle-east/negotiators-close-in-on-deal-with-iran-to-open-hormuz-50c0f335?mod=article_inline)，就管理霍尔木兹海峡航运的临时安排进行磋商。这些会谈仍在继续，但尚未取得任何实质性成果。

伊朗军方持续向未经其许可通过该水道的船只开火，试图维持对商业交通的控制。与冲突初期美国通过打击霍尔木兹海峡沿岸的伊朗目标来报复此类袭击不同，美国迄今一直按兵不动，这让一些海湾盟友感到沮丧。

上周，美军[向一艘悬挂巴拿马国旗的船只开火](https://www.wsj.com/world/middle-east/u-s-fires-on-ship-breaking-its-blockade-of-iran-d8b99456?mod=article_inline)，该船试图突破美国对伊朗港口的封锁，美国加大了对该航道的执法力度，因为华盛顿正[试图在经济上施压伊朗政权](https://www.wsj.com/world/middle-east/trump-shifts-to-economic-pressure-again-as-iran-wont-meet-his-demands-4445c840?mod=article_inline)。

战争初期，阿曼官员迅速与德黑兰建立了秘密沟通渠道，据阿拉伯官员称，这一渠道帮助海湾国家重新开放了空中走廊。

马斯喀特试图在海湾地区扮演中立调解人的角色，这激怒了美国官员，他们越来越将阿曼的做法解读为对美国利益怀有敌意。

自战争开始以来，伊朗对阿曼境内目标发动了一系列打击，包括对美国使用的港口发动无人机袭击。

今年6月，《华尔街日报》报道称，特朗普政府[曾威胁要制裁并轰炸阿曼](https://www.wsj.com/world/middle-east/us-oman-iran-ties-3e056744?mod=article_inline)，此前情报评估认为马斯喀特计划加入伊朗，在该战略海峡对过往船只征收通行费。阿曼多次否认此类计划。

战争已持续近六个月，[推高了石油和天然气价格](https://www.wsj.com/world/middle-east/iran-war-economic-pressure-738b52cf?mod=article_inline)，在11月中期选举前带来影响。美伊和平谈判陷入僵局，而间歇性冲突继续扰乱石油和其他大宗商品通过这一战略要道的流通。

周一，特朗普在其Truth Social平台上发帖称，他的“首要目标”仍然是确保伊朗“无论如何、以任何形式都无法拥有核武器。”

President Trump threatened to bomb Oman if the country “gets in the way” of U.S. efforts [to reach a peace deal with Iran](https://www.wsj.com/business/hospitality/behind-peace-talks-iran-hard-liners-prepare-for-a-longer-costlier-u-s-war-a42357cb?mod=article_inline).

“If Oman gets in the way, we’ll bomb the *s—* out of them,” Fox News’ Trey Yingst reported Trump saying during a phone interview Monday.

Trump, according to Yingst, said U.S. forces could strike the Gulf nation if it interfered in the blockade of Iranian ships in the Strait of Hormuz. The president said the blockade was putting pressure on [Iran](https://www.wsj.com/topics/place/iran) but offered no timeline for ending the conflict, saying he was in no hurry.

Iranian negotiators [have held talks with Oman](https://www.wsj.com/world/middle-east/negotiators-close-in-on-deal-with-iran-to-open-hormuz-50c0f335?mod=article_inline) in recent weeks over a provisional arrangement to manage shipping through the strait. Those talks are continuing, and yet to yield any tangible results.

Iran’s military has continued to fire at ships transiting the waterway without its permission, seeking to maintain its grip on commercial traffic. Unlike earlier in the conflict, when the U.S. retaliated against such attacks by striking Iranian targets along the strait, the U.S. has so far stood down, frustrating some Gulf allies.

Last week, U.S. forces [fired on a Panama-flagged ship](https://www.wsj.com/world/middle-east/u-s-fires-on-ship-breaking-its-blockade-of-iran-d8b99456?mod=article_inline) that attempted to run the American blockade of Iranian ports, stepping up its enforcement of the waterway as Washington [attempts to squeeze the regime economically](https://www.wsj.com/world/middle-east/trump-shifts-to-economic-pressure-again-as-iran-wont-meet-his-demands-4445c840?mod=article_inline).

At the outset of the war, Omani officials moved quickly to establish a back channel with Tehran that, according to Arab officials, helped Gulf states reopen flight corridors.

Muscat’s efforts to act as a neutral broker in the Gulf has rankled U.S. officials who are increasingly interpreting the Omani approach as hostile to American interests.

Iran has launched a series of strikes on Omani sites since the start of the war, including drone attacks on ports used by the U.S.

In June, The Wall Street Journal reported that the Trump administration [had threatened to sanction and bomb Oman](https://www.wsj.com/world/middle-east/us-oman-iran-ties-3e056744?mod=article_inline) after intelligence assessments concluded that Muscat was planning to join Iran in tolling vessels in the strategic strait. Oman has repeatedly denied such plans.

The war has dragged on for nearly six months, [sending oil and gas prices higher](https://www.wsj.com/world/middle-east/iran-war-economic-pressure-738b52cf?mod=article_inline) ahead of November’s midterm elections. Peace talks between the U.S. and Iran have stalled, while intermittent clashes continue to disrupt the flow of oil and other commodities through the strategically important strait.

In a post on his Truth Social platform Monday, Trump said his “number one Goal” remained ensuring that Iran “cannot have, in any way, shape, or form, a Nuclear Weapon.”
