### 中文译文

## 随着习近平访美日益临近，有关拟进行的人工智能领域会谈的具体细节仍不确定

#### 关于美国哪些机构将牵头、私营企业是否会参与以及议程安排等问题仍悬而未决

![The bilateral discussions come amid growing US-China rivalry over competing visions for global AI development. Photo: Shutterstock](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3c181cc90b1481f29f96f92353dd7efb/3c181cc9-0b14-8196-9adb-d99c86a7586d-72ffd3bbe274b8661a2c12ce19a76e67.jpg)

[**Khushboo Razdan**](https://www.scmp.com/author/khushboo-razdan)**和**[**Nayan Seth**](https://www.scmp.com/author/nayan-seth)**在华盛顿**

发布：2026年8月19日下午6:00更新：2026年8月19日下午6:09

随着中国国家主席习近平计划[访问美国](https://www.scmp.com/news/china/diplomacy/series/3364401/xis-2026-trip-us)的时间仅剩数周，华盛顿和北京仍在努力安排期望中的人工智能对话，会谈的地点、参与者和范围尚未确定。

两位熟悉讨论情况的消息人士表示，华盛顿尚未决定美国政府中哪个部门应牵头。双方也尚未就是否应纳入技术专家和私营部门代表等问题达成一致。

尽管财政部长[斯科特·贝森特](https://www.scmp.com/topics/scott-bessent)已主导围绕更广泛的美中经济关系的讨论，但一位知情人士补充说，人工智能政策涉及白宫、情报机构、科技政策办公室以及国务院、商务部和国防部。

该人士表示，实质性的对话还需要人工智能实验室的专家参与，而不仅仅是高级外交官。

目前尚不清楚人工智能行业的公司——如美国方面的OpenAI、Anthropic、Google DeepMind和Nvidia，或中国的[DeepSeek](https://www.scmp.com/topics/deepseek)、[Alibaba](https://www.scmp.com/topics/alibaba)和[Baidu](https://www.scmp.com/topics/baidu)——是否会正式出席，还是仅扮演边缘角色。

阿里巴巴拥有《南华早报》。

特朗普命令国防部长减少与韩国的联合军事演习次数。

两位消息人士表示，双方在对话范围上存在分歧，北京寻求更广泛的对话，而华盛顿希望聚焦于人工智能安全。

尽管存在分歧，熟悉此事的人士还表示，华盛顿和北京的目标是在本月底前敲定9月会议的日期。

消息人士补充说，鉴于贝森特在制定政府国内人工智能安全框架方面的核心作用，预计他仍将代表美国牵头讨论。

美国财政部表示，政府期待“利用我们作为人工智能全球领导者的地位，与全球第二分享我们的专业知识”。

该部门补充说：“在特朗普总统的领导下，美国正在制定人工智能政策的全球标准，以保护我们的国家安全，释放美国创新，加强美国的技术领先地位，并将美国人民的需求放在首位。”

贝森特在6月表示，人工智能的“最大风险”是中国在该技术上超越美国，并补充说，中国愿意讨论人工智能，这突显了美国的技术领先地位。

“我可以告诉你，中国人愿意就人工智能进行讨论，是因为我们领先，所以我们必须保持领先，”他说。

### 北京酒吧为顾客提供饮品和人工智能模型访问权限

在特朗普与习近平于5月会晤后，两国同意就人工智能治理展开正式会谈。

上月，中国驻华盛顿大使馆表示，两国“需要共同努力”，以发展和完善人工智能治理，并确保该技术更好地服务于社会进步和共同福祉。

然而，双边讨论正值美中在全球人工智能发展愿景上的竞争加剧之际，华盛顿推进其 [Pax Silica](https://www.scmp.com/news/asia/southeast-asia/article/3362161/philippines-sovereignty-and-pax-silica-industrial-hub-how-helberg-sees-it) 供应链倡议，而北京则在推广其世界人工智能合作组织。

“政治环境已经变得不那么有利了，”昆西研究所高级研究员、昆山杜克大学前常务副校长丹尼斯·西蒙表示。

他引用了路透社最近的报道，该报道称华盛顿计划敦促 Pax Silica 成员在与中国的 AI 竞赛中[选边站](https://www.scmp.com/news/china/diplomacy/article/3364019/china-urged-avoid-us-or-them-split-us-over-ai-governance)。

“这代表着围绕人工智能外交的竞争环境明显趋于强硬，”西蒙补充说。

他说，中国倾向于就人工智能风险、测试和治理进行技术性讨论，同时寻求避免将会谈变成又一场关于美国芯片管制和技术限制的辩论。华盛顿希望在不对其商业领导地位形成约束的情况下应对人工智能风险。

一位美国国务院官员对《南华早报》表示，美国“正积极与全球伙伴合作，通过双边和多边论坛推动负责任、有利于创新和增长的人工智能发展”。

该官员补充说：“例如，美国与近三十六个‘Pax Silica’伙伴签署了《人工智能机遇联合声明》，强调我们共同致力于推进可信的人工智能生态系统。”

美国负责经济事务的副国务卿雅各布·赫尔伯格（Jacob Helberg）是拥有24个成员的“Pax Silica”的设计者，他在7月份表示，尽管中美竞争日益激烈，但他希望美中人工智能治理对话有助于确定双方“建设性接触”的领域。

赫尔伯格在接受《南华早报》[独家采访时](https://www.scmp.com/news/us/diplomacy/series/3362173/exclusive-jacob-helberg-interview)表示：“我们的目标是真诚地开展这些接触，努力重申我们的观点并听取中方的意见。”

### English Original

## As Xi’s US visit approaches, basic details of planned AI talks remain uncertain

## ***Exclusive*****As Xi’s US visit approaches, basic details of planned AI talks remain uncertain**

#### Questions remain over which US agencies will take the lead, whether private firms will join and what will be on the agenda

[**Khushboo Razdan**](https://www.scmp.com/author/khushboo-razdan)**and**[**Nayan Seth**](https://www.scmp.com/author/nayan-seth)**in Washington**

Published: 6:00pm, 19 Aug 2026Updated: 6:09pm, 19 Aug 2026

With Chinese President Xi Jinping’s planned [visit to the United States](https://www.scmp.com/news/china/diplomacy/series/3364401/xis-2026-trip-us) just weeks away, Washington and Beijing are still struggling to arrange a hoped-for dialogue on artificial intelligence, with the venue, participants and scope of the talks yet to be settled.

Two sources familiar with the discussions said Washington had yet to decide which part of the US government should take the lead. The two sides had also not agreed on whether technical experts and private-sector representatives should be included, among other questions.

While Treasury Secretary [Scott Bessent](https://www.scmp.com/topics/scott-bessent) has led discussions around the broader US-China economic relationship, one person familiar with the matter added that AI policy cut across the White House, intelligence agencies, the Office of Science and Technology Policy, and the departments of state, commerce and defence.

A substantive dialogue would also require experts from AI laboratories, rather than simply senior diplomats, to take part, the person said.

It remains unclear whether companies in the AI industry – such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind and Nvidia on the US side, or China’s [DeepSeek](https://www.scmp.com/topics/deepseek), [Alibaba](https://www.scmp.com/topics/alibaba) and Baidu – will formally attend or only play a role on the margins.

Alibaba owns the South China Morning Post.

Trump orders Pentagon chief to cut number of joint military exercises with South Korea

Both sources said the two sides were divided over the scope of the dialogue, with Beijing seeking a broader conversation and Washington wanting to focus on AI safety.

Despite the differences, people familiar with the issue also said that Washington and Beijing were aiming to finalise dates for the September meeting before the end of this month.

Sources added that Bessent was still expected to lead the discussions for the US, given his central role in developing the administration’s domestic AI safety framework.

The Treasury Department said the administration was looking forward to “using our position as the global leader in AI to share our expertise with the world’s number two”.

“Under President Trump’s leadership, the United States is setting the global standard for AI policy that protects our national security, unleashes American innovation, strengthens US technological leadership, and puts the needs of the American people first,” the department added.

Bessent said in June that the “biggest risk” of AI was China surpassing the United States in the technology, adding that China’s willingness to discuss AI underscored America’s technological lead.

“I could tell you that the reason the Chinese are willing to have a discussion on AI is because we are ahead, so we have to stay ahead,” he said.

### Beijing bar serves customers drinks and access to AI models

The two countries agreed to begin formal talks on AI governance following a meeting between Trump and Xi in May.

Last month, the Chinese embassy in Washington said that the two countries “need to work together” to develop and improve AI governance and ensure the technology better served social progress and common welfare.

However, the bilateral discussions come amid growing US-China rivalry over competing visions for global AI development, with Washington advancing its [Pax Silica](https://www.scmp.com/news/asia/southeast-asia/article/3362161/philippines-sovereignty-and-pax-silica-industrial-hub-how-helberg-sees-it) supply chain initiative, while Beijing is promoting its World Artificial Intelligence Cooperation Organisation.

“The political environment has become less favourable,” said Denis Simon, senior research fellow at the Quincy Institute and former executive vice-chancellor of Duke Kunshan University.

He cited recent reporting from Reuters that said Washington was planning to urge Pax Silica members to [choose sides](https://www.scmp.com/news/china/diplomacy/article/3364019/china-urged-avoid-us-or-them-split-us-over-ai-governance) in the AI race with China.

“That represents an appreciable hardening of the competitive environment surrounding AI diplomacy,” Simon added.

China favoured a technical discussion on AI risks, testing and governance, while seeking to avoid turning the talks into another debate over US chip controls and technology restrictions, he said. Washington wanted to address AI risks without constraining its commercial leadership.

A State Department official told the SCMP that the US was “actively engaging with global partners to advance responsible, pro-innovation, pro-growth AI development, both bilaterally and through multilateral forums”.

“For example, the United States and close to three dozen Pax Silica partners signed the Joint Statement on AI Opportunity, underscoring our collective commitment to advancing trusted AI ecosystems,” the official added.

US Under Secretary of State for Economic Affairs Jacob Helberg, the architect of the 24-member Pax Silica, said in July that he hoped US-China dialogue on AI governance would help identify areas for “constructive engagement” between the two sides despite their intensifying rivalry.

“Our goal is to pursue these engagements in good faith and work to restate our views and hear Chinese views,” Helberg told the SCMP in [an exclusive interview](https://www.scmp.com/news/us/diplomacy/series/3362173/exclusive-jacob-helberg-interview).
