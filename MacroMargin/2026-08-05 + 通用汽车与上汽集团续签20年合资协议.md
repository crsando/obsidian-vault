### 中文译文

## 通用汽车与上汽集团续签20年合资协议

[通用汽车公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/GM%20US%20Equity)正在与中国的[上汽集团](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/600104%20CH%20Equity)续签长达20年的合资协议，这证明这家美国汽车制造商看到了足够的进展，能够在多年的衰退后继续留在全球最大的汽车市场。

根据该协议，通用汽车和上汽将各持有50%的股份，并继续利用中国的设计和工程业务联合开发车型。他们还将继续分享利润。

彭博新闻社9月报道称，两家公司正在进行初步谈判，以续签合资企业。

根据通用汽车的声明，作为协议的一部分，该公司计划到2030年推出至少30款新能源汽车。合资企业还将“更加聚焦”别克和凯迪拉克品牌，这两个品牌历来在中国拥有强大的影响力。

新协议是在两家公司经过数年讨论以及伴随数十亿美元亏损的业务重组之后达成的。随着来自中国国内产业的竞争导致多年下滑，该合资企业再次实现盈利。两家公司曾共同赚取数十亿美元，但在两年前陷入困境，促使他们[收敛](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2024-12-04/gm-gm-china-writedown-deals-blow-to-global-business)野心。

通用汽车和上汽将在中国销售别克和凯迪拉克品牌，并将雪佛兰用作向全球市场出口小型、廉价车型的品牌。这些汽车不会在美国、加拿大或欧洲销售。

“今天的协议反映了我们对上汽通用及其长期增长潜力的共同信心，”通用汽车高级副总裁兼通用汽车中国总裁约翰·罗斯表示。“我们致力于在中国市场取得强劲表现，我们看到了在特定国际市场（中东、非洲、南美、墨西哥和亚太地区）竞争的重要机遇。”

根据新协议，通用汽车和上汽还将保留与合作伙伴广西汽车集团的第二家合资企业，该企业生产小型五菱品牌汽车。通用汽车以雪佛兰品牌出口其中许多车型。

通用汽车曾在中国每年盈利20亿美元。该业务在2024年[下滑](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2024-12-04/gm-gm-china-writedown-deals-blow-to-global-business)并亏损，随后才[扭转](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/newsletters/2025-11-03/general-motors-and-ford-are-showing-signs-of-life-in-china)局面，但利润已远不及通用汽车曾经的水平。通用汽车报告，2026年上半年该合资企业为其带来2.48亿美元权益收益，是去年同期的两倍。

随着中国崛起的汽车制造商推出更多混合动力和电动汽车，该合资企业遭受了冲击，而政府曾大力鼓励消费者购买这类汽车。

通用汽车在重组业务时计提了50亿美元的[减记](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2024-12-04/gm-sees-5-billion-hit-to-restructure-troubled-china-business)，期间关闭工厂、裁员，并努力使产品线贴合市场。

本周，通用汽车和上汽集团在上海车展上展示了别克电动E7插电式混合动力车。

## GM Renews China Joint Venture With SAIC in 20-Year Agreement

[General Motors Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/GM%20US%20Equity) is renewing its longtime joint venture with China’s [SAIC Motor Corp.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/600104%20CH%20Equity) for 20 years, proof the US automaker sees enough progress to stay in the world’s largest auto market after years of decline.

With the deal, GM and SAIC will both own 50% and continue to jointly develop models using design and engineering operations in China. They will also continue to split profits.

Bloomberg News [reported](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-09-17/gm-is-in-talks-to-extend-china-venture-with-demand-recovering) in September that the companies were in preliminary talks to renew the venture.

The business intends to launch at least 30 new energy vehicles by 2030 as part of the deal, according to a statement from GM. The joint venture will also “sharpen its focus” on the Buick and Cadillac brands, which have historically had a strong presence in China.

The new agreement comes after several years of discussions between the two companies and a restructuring of the business that came with billions in losses. The venture is profitable again after years of decline amid competition from China’s domestic industry. Both companies made billions together before falling on hard times two years ago, leading them to** **[rein in](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2024-12-04/gm-gm-china-writedown-deals-blow-to-global-business) their ambitions.

GM and SAIC will sell the Buick and Cadillac brands in China and use Chevrolet as an export** **brand for small, inexpensive models to global markets. Those cars will not be sold in the US, Canada or Europe.

“Today’s agreement reflects our shared confidence in SAIC-GM and its long-term growth potential,” said John Roth, a GM senior vice president and president of GM China. “We are committed to strong performance in the China market, and we see meaningful opportunities to compete in select international markets: the Middle East, Africa, South America, Mexico and Asia-Pacific.”

With the new deal, GM and SAIC will also keep their second joint venture with partner Guangxi Automobile Group, which builds the small [Wuling](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2022-08-31/gm-is-selling-a-tiny-convertible-electric-car-in-china) brand vehicles. GM exports many of those models under the Chevy brand.

GM once made $2 billion a year in profit in China. The business [declined](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2024-12-04/gm-gm-china-writedown-deals-blow-to-global-business) and lost money in 2024 before [turning](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/newsletters/2025-11-03/general-motors-and-ford-are-showing-signs-of-life-in-china) things around, but its profits are a fraction of what GM once made. GM reported $248 million in equity income from the venture in the first half of 2026, which is double the same period a year ago.

The joint venture suffered when China’s surging automakers came out with more hybrid-electric and electric vehicles, which the government had strongly encouraged among consumers.

GM took $5 billion in [write downs](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2024-12-04/gm-sees-5-billion-hit-to-restructure-troubled-china-business) while restructuring the business as it closed plants, cut jobs and worked to get its product line in step with the market.

GM and SAIC are showing off the Buick Electric E7 plug-in hybrid at the Shanghai Auto Show this week.
