### 中文译文

## 特朗普命令五角大楼减少与韩国的军事演习规模

- 摘要
- 特朗普称军事演习向朝鲜发出了“不当且具有敌意”的信号
- 特朗普称韩国拒绝参与美国针对伊朗的军事行动
- 青瓦台表示，韩国正在仔细研究特朗普的言论，期望美朝能够展开对话。
- 自 8 月 6 日演习开始以来，朝鲜已发射了两枚弹道导弹。
华盛顿/首尔，8 月 16 日（路透社）——美国总统唐纳德·特朗普周日命令五角大楼“大幅减少”与长期亚洲盟友韩国的联合军事演习，其理由是这些演习成本高昂，且首尔拒绝参与针对伊朗的行动。

在演习即将开始的前几小时，特朗普在其 Truth Social 账号上表示，他对美国同意参与此次演习感到“不满”，并强调了其与朝鲜领导人金正恩之间的“非常良好的关系”——金正恩是拥有核武器且面临严厉制裁的朝鲜的领袖。

“这些演习不仅耗资巨大……而且向一个自唐纳德·J·特朗普担任总统以来一直保持非威胁性和尊重态度的国家发出了完全不当且具有敌意的信号。”特朗普写道。

一年一度的[乙支自由护盾](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/trump-seeks-cuts-us-south-korea-drills-reviving-earlier-objections-2026-08-17/)联合军事演习定于8月27日结束，约18,000名韩国军事人员参加。

韩国联合参谋本部对路透社表示，演习已于周一如期开始。

韩国总统府青瓦台表示正在审议特朗普的帖文，并称“希望美朝领导人之间的友好关系能够促成两国之间有意义的对话，开启旨在推动朝鲜半岛和平与稳定的讨论。”

青瓦台称，韩美同盟将继续就联合防御态势和军事演习进行协调。

针对特朗普声称韩国总统李在明拒绝加入美国推动伊朗无核化的努力，青瓦台表示，首尔正在讨论潜在的军事贡献，同时考虑其在朝鲜半岛的防御准备情况和国内法律程序。

五角大楼发言人将此问题转给白宫，白宫没有立即回应提供细节的请求。

### 起伏不定的关系

特朗普[长期以来一直寻求](https://www.reuters.com/article/world/trump-surprises-with-pledge-to-end-military-exercises-in-south-korea-idUSKBN1J816M/)结束或缩减与韩国的军事演习，同时与首尔在驻韩美军军费分摊问题上发生冲突，这些美军有28,500人驻扎在韩国，并[在确保更广泛地区安全方面发挥作用](https://www.reuters.com/business/aerospace-defense/hegseth-visits-south-korea-talks-us-troops-tour-dmz-border-2025-11-03/)。

朝鲜从技术上讲仍与韩国处于战争状态，经常谴责美韩联合演习是为入侵做准备。上周，平壤从其东海岸向日本海发射了一枚弹道导弹，几天前朝鲜进行了一次类似的短程[弹道导弹发射](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/north-korea-fires-unidentified-projectile-south-koreas-military-says-2026-08-06/)。

"这一决定可能是为了与平壤创造外交机会的更大努力的一部分，这是特朗普自重返白宫以来一直希望的，尽管这一举措的随意性不太可能给平壤留下什么印象，"智库史汀生中心"38 North"项目负责人珍妮·汤恩在电子邮件中表示。

特朗普与金正恩的关系多年来一直是[过山车](https://www.reuters.com/world/trumps-rollercoaster-relationship-with-north-korean-leader-kim-2024-11-07/)。

特朗普曾嘲讽朝鲜领导人是"小火箭人"，并以"火与怒"威胁平壤，而金正恩则夸口要用"火来驯服这个精神错乱的美国老糊涂"。

第1张，共4张 美国陆军士兵参加了美韩联合渡河演习，这是年度"自由之盾"联合军事训练的一部分，地点在韩国涟川郡韩朝非军事区附近，2026年3月14日。REUTERS/Kim Soo-hyeon**\[1/4\]**美国陆军士兵参加了美韩联合渡河演习，这是年度"自由之盾"联合军事训练的一部分，地点在韩国涟川郡韩朝非军事区附近，2026年3月14日。REUTERS/Kim Soo-hyeon [购买授权许可，打开新标签页](https://www.reutersconnect.com/item/us-and-south-korean-forces-participate-in-river-crossing-exercise-as-part-of-annual-freedom-shield-military-drills-in-yeoncheon/dGFnOnJldXRlcnMuY29tLDIwMjY6bmV3c21sX1JDMjI0S0FXNkpHSA%3D%3D/?utm_medium=rcom-article-media&utm_campaign=rcom-rcp-lead)

然而，他们还参加了三次峰会，特朗普在与金正恩交换信件后宣称"我们相爱了"。这些努力都没有导致朝鲜核武器或弹道导弹发展发生变化。

尽管特朗普周日对朝鲜发表了热情洋溢的讲话，但他也指出了韩国在美国-以色列与伊朗冲突上的立场。

"虽然有点无关（？），但我最近问韩国总统，他们是否愿意加入我们对伊朗伊斯兰共和国的无核化行动，他们说'不，谢谢！'"特朗普写道。

### 演习转向模拟

目前尚不清楚这些演习将如何缩减，但近年来，演习已从实弹演练转向计算机模拟的导弹和网络攻击。

华盛顿战略与国际研究中心韩国项目负责人车维德（Victor Cha）表示，由于平壤在乌克兰问题上大力支持俄罗斯，特朗普可能正在寻求对朝鲜施加更大影响。

“看待这一问题的另一种方式是，特朗普希望与金正恩接触，以此阻挠俄罗斯与朝鲜的关系。”车维德说。

美国军方官员曾表示，与俄罗斯并肩作战、参与其入侵乌克兰的朝鲜军队从冲突中获得了经验，而乌克兰上周表示，在俄罗斯对扎波罗热一家钢铁厂的弹道导弹袭击中使用了[朝鲜制造的导弹](https://www.reuters.com/world/russian-attack-kyiv-triggers-fires-officials-say-2026-08-10/)。

美国北方司令部高级官员约瑟夫·贾拉德中将上周警告说，朝鲜已成功试射洲际弹道导弹，“其推力足以将核弹头投送到北美任何地方”。

### 经济因素

尽管特朗普一再呼吁韩国承担更大的防务费用负担，但一名美国官员今年称首尔是一个[“模范盟友”](https://www.reuters.com/business/aerospace-defense/us-south-korea-agree-deepen-cooperation-nuclear-powered-subs-seoul-says-2026-01-26/)，致力于按照美国地区战略增加防务开支。

还有更广泛的经济紧张局势。

一位曾在特朗普首届政府任职的美国前官员对路透社表示，他认为特朗普对韩国政府在解决经济问题上的“顽固态度”感到“沮丧”。

“李政府一再推行对美国公司不利的政策，并且对落实与美国的里程碑式投资协议兴趣寥寥。” 这位官员说。

两国去年签署了一项贸易协议，以降低特朗普的汽车关税，换取首尔方面投资3500亿美元的承诺。韩国尚未公布其投资计划的细节。

这两个盟友因一场涉及美国上市电子商务公司Coupang的[争端](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/how-dispute-over-e-commerce-firm-coupang-is-testing-us-south-korea-ties-2026-07-17/)而关系紧张，该公司因大规模数据泄露正受到首尔方面的调查。

## Trump orders Pentagon to cut back military exercises with South Korea

- Summary 摘要
- Trump says military drills send 'inappropriate and hostile' signal to North Korea 特朗普称军事演习向朝鲜发出了“不当且具有敌意”的信号
- Trump says South Korea refused to take part in US military action against Iran 特朗普称韩国拒绝参与美国针对伊朗的军事行动
- South Korea reviewing Trump's comments, hopes for U.S.-North Korea talks, says Blue House 青瓦台表示，韩国正在仔细研究特朗普的言论，期望美朝能够展开对话。
- North Korea has fired two ballistic missiles since August 6 ahead of drills 自 8 月 6 日演习开始以来，朝鲜已发射了两枚弹道导弹。
WASHINGTON/SEOUL, Aug 16 (Reuters) - U.S. President Donald Trump on Sunday instructed the Pentagon to "substantially reduce" joint military exercises with long-term Asian ally South Korea, citing the cost of the drills and Seoul's refusal to take part in actions against Iran.
华盛顿/首尔，8 月 16 日（路透社）——美国总统唐纳德·特朗普周日命令五角大楼“大幅减少”与长期亚洲盟友韩国的联合军事演习，其理由是这些演习成本高昂，且首尔拒绝参与针对伊朗的行动。

Hours before exercises were scheduled to begin, Trump ‌said on his Truth Social site he was "not happy" that the U.S. had agreed to participate and highlighted his "very good relationship" with Kim Jong Un, the leader of nuclear-armed and heavily sanctioned North Korea.
在演习即将开始的前几小时，特朗普在其 Truth Social 账号上表示，他对美国同意参与此次演习感到“不满”，并强调了其与朝鲜领导人金正恩之间的“非常良好的关系”——金正恩是拥有核武器且面临严厉制裁的朝鲜的领袖。

"These exercises are not only costly... but send a signal that is totally inappropriate and hostile, to a Country that, as long as Donald J. Trump has been President, has been unthreatening and respectful," Trump wrote.

The annual [Ulchi Freedom Shield](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/trump-seeks-cuts-us-south-korea-drills-reviving-earlier-objections-2026-08-17/) joint military exercises are scheduled to run until August 27, with about 18,000 South Korean military personnel participating.

South Korea's Joint Chiefs of Staff told Reuters the drills started on Monday as scheduled.

South Korea's presidential Blue House said it was reviewing Trump's post and "hopes that the friendly relationship between the U.S. and North Korean leaders will lead to meaningful dialogue between the two countries, opening discussions aimed at advancing peace and stability on the Korean Peninsula," it said.

The allies would continue coordinating on their combined ⁠defense posture and military exercises, it said.

Responding to Trump's claim that President Lee Jae Myung had refused to join U.S. efforts to denuclearise Iran, it said Seoul was discussing potential military contributions, while considering its defense readiness on the Korean Peninsula and domestic legal procedures.

A Pentagon spokesperson referred questions on the matter to the White House, which did not immediately respond to a request for details.

### UP-AND-DOWN RELATIONSHIP

Trump has [long sought](https://www.reuters.com/article/world/trump-surprises-with-pledge-to-end-military-exercises-in-south-korea-idUSKBN1J816M/) to end or curtail military exercises with South Korea while clashing with Seoul over sharing the costs of stationing 28,500 U.S. troops in South Korea and [their role in securing](https://www.reuters.com/business/aerospace-defense/hegseth-visits-south-korea-talks-us-troops-tour-dmz-border-2025-11-03/) the broader region.

North Korea, still technically at war with the South, routinely denounces the U.S.-South Korean exercises as preparations for an invasion. Pyongyang last week fired a ballistic missile from its eastern coast towards the Sea of Japan, days after a similar North Korean short-range [ballistic missile launch](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/north-korea-fires-unidentified-projectile-south-koreas-military-says-2026-08-06/).

"This decision may be part of a larger effort to create a diplomatic opportunity with Pyongyang, as Trump has consistently wanted since coming back to office, although the haphazard nature of this move is unlikely to make much of an impression in Pyongyang," Jenny Town, head of the Stimson Center think tank's 38 North program, said in an email.

The relationship between Trump and Kim has been a [roller coaster](https://www.reuters.com/world/trumps-rollercoaster-relationship-with-north-korean-leader-kim-2024-11-07/) for years.

Trump once mocked the North Korean leader as "little rocket man" and threatened Pyongyang with "fire and fury," while Kim boasted that he would "tame the mentally deranged U.S. dotard with fire."

Even as Trump on Sunday spoke warmly of North Korea, he pointed to South Korea's stance on the U.S.-Israeli conflict with Iran.

"While somewhat unrelated (?), I recently asked the President of South Korea if they would like to join us in the Denuclearization of the Islamic Republic of Iran, and they said, 'No thanks!'" Trump wrote.

### EXERCISES SHIFT TO SIMULATIONS

It's unclear how the drills could be cut back, but in recent years, they have shifted away from live-fire exercises to computer-simulated missile and cyberattacks.

Victor Cha, head of the Korea program at Washington’s Center for Strategic and International Studies, said Trump may be seeking greater influence over North Korea as Pyongyang has been heavily engaged in support of Russia in Ukraine.

"The ⁠other way to look at this is that Trump wants to engage Kim as a way to impede the Russia-NK ties," said Cha.

U.S. military officials have said North Korean troops, who have fought alongside Russia in its invasion of Ukraine, have gained experience from the conflict, while Ukraine said last week that [North Korean-made missiles](https://www.reuters.com/world/russian-attack-kyiv-triggers-fires-officials-say-2026-08-10/) were used in a Russian ballistic missile attack on a steel plant in Zaporizhzhia.

A high-ranking U.S. Northern Command official, Lieutenant General Joseph Jarrard, last week warned that North Korea had successfully tested intercontinental ballistic missiles with "enough boost to deliver ⁠a nuclear payload anywhere in North America."

### ECONOMIC FACTORS

While Trump has repeatedly called for South Korea to shoulder a bigger burden of defense costs, a U.S. official this year called Seoul a ["model ally](https://www.reuters.com/business/aerospace-defense/us-south-korea-agree-deepen-cooperation-nuclear-powered-subs-seoul-says-2026-01-26/)" committed to increasing defense spending in line with U.S. regional strategy.

There are also broader economic tensions.

A former U.S. official who worked in the first Trump administration told Reuters he believed Trump is "frustrated with the intransigence" of the South Korean administration in addressing economic issues.

“The Lee government has repeatedly pursued policies antagonistic to ⁠American companies and shown little interest in following through on the historic investment deal with the United States," the official said.

The countries signed a trade deal last year to lower Trump's auto tariffs in exchange for Seoul's commitment to invest $350 billion. South Korea has not announced details of its investment plan.

The allies have been at odds over a [dispute](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/how-dispute-over-e-commerce-firm-coupang-is-testing-us-south-korea-ties-2026-07-17/) involving U.S.-listed e-commerce company Coupang, which is being investigated by Seoul over a massive data leak.
