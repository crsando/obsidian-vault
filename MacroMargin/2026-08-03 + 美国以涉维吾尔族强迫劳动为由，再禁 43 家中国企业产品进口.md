## 美国以涉维吾尔族强迫劳动为由，再禁 43 家中国企业产品进口

![Defense Secretary James Mattis welcomes Chinese Minister of National Defense Gen. Wei Fenghe to the Pentagon](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3b181cc90b148170b261f70f19837522/3b181cc9-0b14-812d-83c9-f155bc554bec-e24f0b57abe5a645b8025fa5976ffc5e.jpg)

2018年11月9日，美国弗吉尼亚州阿灵顿，美国国防部长詹姆斯·马蒂斯在五角大楼欢迎中国国防部长魏凤和上将前，可以看到美国和中国的国旗。路透社/Yuri Gripas/文件照片 [购买许可权利，打开新标签页](https://www.reutersconnect.com/item/defense-secretary-james-mattis-welcomes-chinese-minister-of-national-defense-gen-wei-fenghe-to-the-pentagon/dGFnOnJldXRlcnMuY29tLDIwMTg6bmV3c21sX1JDMTM4RUIyMjI3MA%3D%3D/?utm_medium=rcom-article-media&utm_campaign=rcom-rcp-lead)

- 摘要
- 公司
- 特朗普领导下的首次此类行动，自名单生效以来规模最大
- 一些新入列的公司已在美国销售
- 中国谴责该行动，称将保护本国公司
- 北京经常否认强迫劳动指控
华盛顿/北京，7月31日（路透社）——美国政府周五禁止从另外43家中国企业进口，理由是这些企业涉嫌侵犯维吾尔族和其他少数民族的权利，目标涉及从电子产品到食品和金属的供应链中的企业，一份政府通知显示。

这些公司被列入《维吾尔族强迫劳动预防法》实体清单，该清单推定由所列实体全部或部分生产的商品系使用强迫劳动制造，不得进入美国，除非进口商能够证明并非如此。

北京方面经常驳斥有关在新疆地区对维吾尔族及其他穆斯林少数群体实施强迫劳动和其他虐待的指控。

美国国土安全部发布的这份通知标志着特朗普政府任内首次有企业被列入该清单。这使得被提及的实体从144家增至187家——这是自2021年该清单相关法案签署成为法律以来最大规模的单次扩容。

### 中国称将采取‘必要措施’

中国商务部周六谴责美国的这一行动，称其是毫无根据的单边制裁；商务部表示，就在此次行动前一天，两国贸易官员刚刚进行了一次其所称的建设性[视频通话](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/chinas-he-lifeng-raises-trade-restriction-concerns-call-with-bessent-greer-2026-07-30/)。

该部补充说，中国将采取“必要措施”保护本国企业，但没有透露更多细节。

美国政府称，有4家企业因与新疆当局合作招募、转移或接收维吾尔族及其他受迫害群体而被列入清单。

《联邦公报》通知称，其余41家被列入是因为它们从新疆或与当地政府劳工计划有关联的实体采购材料。

美国众议院中国问题特别委员会主席约翰·穆勒纳尔在一份声明中表示：“特朗普政府今天的行动强化了美国经济，使之免受使用奴隶劳动制造的产品的影响。”

中国驻华盛顿大使馆周五称此类指控是“谎言”，并表示中国法律禁止强迫劳动，新疆工人可自由选择职业。

### 名单包括与美国销售有关联的公司

名单包括电动汽车和储能电池所用材料的供应商。

路透社联系了新增名单中的10家公司寻求置评，但均未在正常工作时间之外作出回应。

其中包括国投新疆锂业（生产碳酸锂）及其母公司国投新疆罗布泊钾盐，它们因从新疆罗布泊盐湖的卤水中采购锂和钾而被列入名单。

新疆天宏基科技（Xinjiang Tianhongji Technology）也被加入名单，该公司生产锂离子和钠离子电池材料。美国通知称，该公司从新疆采购石油焦、无烟煤和沥青以制造电池材料。

湖南艾华集团[(603989.SS)](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/603989.SS)是一家中国铝电解电容器制造商，产品用于消费电子、工业设备、车辆和可再生能源系统。该公司被列入名单，是因为美国政府称其从新疆的一个生产基地采购包括化学箔在内的材料。

据其网站介绍，艾华集团（Aihua）以“AiSHi”品牌销售电容器，并在弗吉尼亚州格伦艾伦设有北美销售办事处。其产品也由美国电子分销商DigiKey销售。

零食生产商洽洽食品（Chacha Food）[(002557.SZ), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/002557.SZ) 也被加入。美国通知称，洽洽向近50个国家和地区出口坚果和炒货等产品，并从新疆采购农产品。

洽洽食品此前已将美国确定为其最大的海外市场。在2019年的一篇报道中，官方媒体《中国日报》称，该公司已在沃尔玛和好市多（Costco）门店推出产品，主要集中在纽约和洛杉矶。路透社暂无法确认这些零售安排是否仍然有效。

### 变压器、太阳能材料、合金、黄金

路透社联系的公司未立即在其社交媒体账号上对美国列名或相关信息作出回应。

其中规模最大的是特变电工（TBEA Co）[(600089.SS), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/600089.SS)，该公司生产变压器及其他输电设备、铝产品和高纯度多晶硅（一种用于太阳能电池板的材料）。

美国通知称，特变电工从新疆采购铝及铝合金产品。通知还列出了特变电工子公司新疆天池能源（Xinjiang Tianchi Energy），称该子公司从该地区采购煤炭。

该行动还涵盖天山铝业集团（Tianshan Aluminum Group）[(002532.SZ), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/002532.SZ) 及其七家关联公司。天山铝业称，其拥有每年140万吨电解铝产能和一条250万吨氧化铝生产线。

山东黄金矿业（[600547.SS，新开窗口](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/600547.SS)）在中国、阿根廷、加纳和纳米比亚拥有资产或项目，该公司与山东黄金冶炼等子公司一同被列入名单。

美国通知称，该集团从新疆采购黄金，其一家子公司拥有并运营该地区最大的单体金矿。

## US bars imports from 43 more companies over China's alleged forced labor involving Uyghurs

U.S. and Chinese flags are seen before Defense Secretary James Mattis welcomes Chinese Minister of National Defense Gen. Wei Fenghe to the Pentagon in Arlington, Virginia, U.S., November 9, 2018. - Some newly listed companies have sold in the United States
- China condemns action, says it will protect its companies
- Beijing has regularly denied forced labor accusations
WASHINGTON/BEIJING, July 31 (Reuters) - The United States on Friday banned imports from 43 more Chinese companies over alleged rights abuses of Uyghur and other minority groups, targeting firms in supply chains ‌from electronics to food and metals, a government notice showed.

The companies were added to the Uyghur Forced Labor Prevention Act Entity List, which creates a presumption that goods made wholly or partly by listed entities are made with forced labour and cannot enter the United States unless importers can prove otherwise.

Beijing has regularly dismissed accusations of forced labour and other abuses against Uyghurs and other Muslim minorities in the region of Xinjiang.

The notice published by the U.S. Department of Homeland Security marks the first time companies have been added to the list under the Trump administration. It brings the number of mentioned entities to 187 from 144 — the largest single expansion since the act creating the list was signed into law in 2021.

### CHINA SAYS IT WILL TAKE 'NECESSARY MEASURES'

China's Commerce Ministry on Saturday condemned the ⁠U.S. action as an unfounded unilateral sanction, which it said had come just a day after trade officials from both countries had what it described as a constructive [video call](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/chinas-he-lifeng-raises-trade-restriction-concerns-call-with-bessent-greer-2026-07-30/).

China would take "necessary measures" to protect its companies, the ministry added without going into more detail.

Four companies were listed over what the U.S. government said was their work with Xinjiang authorities to recruit, transfer or receive Uyghurs and other persecuted groups.

The Federal Register notice said the other 41 were added because they source materials from Xinjiang or from entities linked to government labour programs there.

"Today's action by the Trump administration strengthens America's economy against products made with slave labor," Representative John Moolenaar, chairman of the House Select Committee on China, said in a statement.

China's embassy in Washington called such allegations "a lie" on Friday and said Chinese law prohibits forced labour and that workers in Xinjiang are free to choose their occupations.

### INCLUDES COMPANIES WITH US SALES LINKS

The listings include suppliers of materials used in electric-vehicle and energy-storage batteries.

Reuters contacted 10 of the newly listed companies for comment but none responded outside normal business hours.

Those included SDIC Xinjiang Lithium Industry, which makes lithium carbonate, and its parent, SDIC Xinjiang Luobupo Potash, which were listed over their sourcing of lithium ‌and potassium ⁠from brine at Xinjiang's Lop Nur Salt Lake.

Xinjiang Tianhongji Technology, which makes materials for lithium- and sodium-ion batteries, was also added. The U.S. notice said it sources petroleum coke, anthracite and asphalt from Xinjiang to make battery materials.

Hunan Aihua Group [(603989.SS), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/603989.SS), a Chinese maker of aluminum electrolytic capacitors used in consumer electronics, industrial equipment, vehicles and renewable-energy systems, was listed because the U.S. government said it sources materials including chemical foil from a production base in Xinjiang.

Aihua sells capacitors under the AiSHi brand and has a North American sales office in Glen Allen, Virginia, according to its website. Its products are also sold by U.S. electronics distributor DigiKey.

Chacha Food [(002557.SZ), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/002557.SZ), a snack-food producer, was also ⁠added. The U.S. notice said Chacha exports products including nuts and roasted seeds to nearly 50 countries and regions and sources agricultural products from Xinjiang.

Chacha Food has previously identified the United States as its largest overseas market. In a 2019 report, state-owned China Daily said the company had launched products in Walmart and Costco stores, mainly in New York and Los Angeles. Reuters could not immediately establish whether those retail arrangements remain in place.

### TRANSFORMERS, SOLAR MATERIALS, ALLOYS, ⁠GOLD

The companies that Reuters contacted for comment did not immediately respond on their social media accounts to the U.S. listing or the details in it.

Among the largest was TBEA Co [(600089.SS), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/600089.SS), which makes transformers and other transmission equipment, aluminum products and high-purity polysilicon, a material used in solar panels.

The U.S. notice said TBEA sources aluminum and aluminum-alloy products from Xinjiang. It also listed TBEA subsidiary Xinjiang Tianchi Energy, saying the ⁠unit sources coal from the region.

The action also covers Tianshan Aluminum Group [(002532.SZ), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/002532.SZ) and seven affiliates. Tianshan says it has 1.4 million tons of annual electrolytic-aluminum capacity and a 2.5 million-ton alumina production line.

Shandong Gold Mining [(600547.SS), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/600547.SS), which has assets or projects in China, Argentina, Ghana and Namibia, was also listed along with units including Shandong Gold Smelting.

The U.S. notice said the group sources gold from Xinjiang, and one of its subsidiaries owns and operates the region's largest single gold mine.
