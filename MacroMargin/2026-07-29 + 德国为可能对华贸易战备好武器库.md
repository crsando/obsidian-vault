### 中文译文

## 德国为可能对华贸易战备好武器库

中国国家主席习近平于2月25日在北京钓鱼台国宾馆会见德国总理弗里德里希·默茨前欢迎他。*摄影师：Michael Kappeler/AFP/Getty Images*

欢迎阅读《布鲁塞尔版》。我是彭博社布鲁塞尔分社社长Suzanne Lynch，每周工作日为您带来欧盟最新消息。请确保您已订阅。

德国官员正在幕后识别中国经济的脆弱点，这些弱点可能在与北京发生贸易战的情况下被欧盟利用，我们的柏林团队[今日报道](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-28/germany-maps-china-s-weaknesses-in-preparation-for-a-trade-war)。总理弗里德里希·默茨的政府正通过分析贸易流动、供应链和企业级数据，非正式地绘制中国的弱点。

知情人士称，初步发现指出，德国公司通快和蔡司——ASML用于制造先进半导体机器的关键技术供应商——是北京供应链中的潜在弱点。芯片公司如Siltronic、Aixtron和SUSS MicroTec也可能被特别指出。

![图片](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3ac81cc90b14815e8b2fdc029685fd14/3ac81cc9-0b14-81d9-9db0-c52661f74ae2-b8c028ef263a731c2ee1da4f3772154d.png)

这项背景工作正在进行之际，欧盟委员会正考虑针对世界第二大经济体采取新措施。欧盟领导人已责成欧盟执行机构在10月欧盟峰会前[准备好政策提案](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-18/eu-leaders-warn-on-china-trade-imbalance-but-respond-cautiously)。默茨近几个月对北京立场强硬，本月与法国总统埃马纽埃尔·马克龙会谈时讨论了中国的挑战。两国预计将在领导人会晤前提交各自的[对华路线图](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-17/germany-and-france-to-draw-up-roadmap-on-china-trade-imbalances)。

去年欧盟对华商品贸易逆差高达3600亿欧元，这鲜明地体现了两个经济体之间日益扩大的不平衡。德国在欧洲的逆差最大，为950亿欧元，其汽车行业尤其被中国进口产品抢占市场，而德国汽车品牌在中国面临汽车需求下降的困境。

在[最新一波冲突中](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-24/china-hits-back-at-eu-with-export-curbs-that-ensnare-rheinmetall)，北京对14家欧洲公司实施出口管制，包括德国国防承包商莱茵金属，称此举是针对欧盟在其最新一轮对俄制裁中对中国大陆和香港实体实施制裁的报复。

尽管如此，要在欧盟范围内达成一致的对华立场将很复杂，因为西班牙等国家更愿意与北京合作。中国对欧洲的影响并非全是负面的。

在欧洲央行首席经济学家菲利普·莱恩最近在亚洲货币政策论坛上的演讲中，他强调了对中国对欧洲GDP的积极影响，中国进口商品价格下降有助于缓和通胀并减轻欧元区企业面临的价格压力，尽管存在进口引起的生产转移和战略脆弱性问题。

最新消息：据消息人士称，欧盟计划制裁超过1600家据称帮助俄罗斯对乌克兰战争的公司，这是欧盟单批黑名单中企业数量最多的一次。梅赛德斯-奔驰报告了富有韧性的汽车制造利润，超出预期，得益于成本削减和开发支出减少，尽管在华交付量因奢侈品市场低迷而下滑。阿斯麦股价跌至6月初以来最低点，此前有报道称一家中国国有企业已开始批量生产某些芯片制造设备，可能对这家荷兰公司的销售构成威胁。英国与乌克兰分享了能够干扰俄罗斯防空系统的“石斗篷”技术，乌克兰总统泽连斯基成为首位与英国新任首相安迪·伯纳姆会面的外国领导人。OpenAI正在扩大其位于都柏林的欧洲总部，租赁8.8万平方英尺的办公空间，并创造250个新就业岗位。匈牙利成立了一个新机构，负责追回被挪用的公共资金，主要涉及前总理维克托·欧尔班16年执政期间的资金。

### **彭博所见所闻**

## Germany Readies Arsenal for Possible Trade War With China

Chinese President Xi Jinping welcomes German Chancellor Friedrich Merz prior a meeting at the Diaoyutai State Guesthouse in Beijing on Feb. 25.*Photographer: Michael Kappeler/AFP/Getty Images*

Welcome to the Brussels Edition. I’m Suzanne Lynch, Bloomberg’s Brussels bureau chief, bringing you the latest from the EU each weekday. Make sure you're signed up.

German officials are working behind the scenes to identify Chinese economic vulnerabilities that could be exploited if the EU finds itself in a trade war with Beijing, our Berlin team [reports today](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-28/germany-maps-china-s-weaknesses-in-preparation-for-a-trade-war). Chancellor Friedrich Merz’s government is informally mapping China’s weaknesses by analyzing trade flows, supply chains and company-level data.

Initial findings pinpoint German companies Trumpf and Carl Zeiss — key suppliers of technologies used by ASML to manufacture machines that make advanced semiconductors — as potential vulnerabilities in Beijing’s supply chain, sources say. Chip firms such as Siltronic, Aixtron and SUSS MicroTec may also have been singled out.

The background work is taking place as the Commission considers new measures against the world’s second-biggest economy. EU leaders have tasked the bloc’s executive arm with [readying policy proposals](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-18/eu-leaders-warn-on-china-trade-imbalance-but-respond-cautiously) in time for an October EU summit. Merz, who has hardened his position towards Beijing in recent months, discussed the China challenge with French President Emmanuel Macron during talks this month. Both countries are due to present their [own roadmap on China](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-17/germany-and-france-to-draw-up-roadmap-on-china-trade-imbalances) ahead of the leaders’ meeting.

The EU’s trade deficit in goods with China hit a whopping €360 billion last year, a stark illustration of the widening imbalance between the two economies. Germany had Europe’s biggest deficit at €95 billion, with its auto sector in particular losing ground to Chinese imports, while German car brands grapple with waning demand for their vehicles in China.

And in the [latest flareup](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-24/china-hits-back-at-eu-with-export-curbs-that-ensnare-rheinmetall), Beijing slapped export controls on 14 European companies, including German defense contractor Rheinmetall, saying it acted in retaliation against EU sanctions on mainland and Hong Kong entities in the EU’s most recent sanctions package on Russia.

Nonetheless, securing a cohesive EU-wide position on China will be complicated, with countries like Spain more open to cooperation with Beijing. China’s impact on Europe has not all been negative.

In a [recent speech](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/key/date/2026/html/ecb.sp260522~f0f11a5f05.en.html) to the Asian Monetary Policy Forum, ECB Chief Economist Philip Lane highlighted the positive impact China has had on European GDP, with lower prices for Chinese imports contributing to moderating inflation and easing price pressures faced by euro-area firms. Notwithstanding the issues around production displacement and strategic vulnerabilities posed by Chinese imports.

In news just in, sources say the EU is planning to sanction more than 1,600 companies it says are helping Russia’s war on Ukraine, the most firms it has blacklisted in one package.Mercedes-Benz reported [resilient carmaking profits](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-28/mercedes-cuts-revenue-outlook-as-china-s-luxury-slump-deepens) that beat expectations thanks to cost-cutting and lower spending on development, even as deliveries dropped due to a slump in China.ASML shares slid to the [lowest since early June](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-27/asml-slides-after-report-of-china-beginning-duv-tool-production) after a report that a Chinese state-backed company has begun mass producing certain chipmaking machines, posing a potential threat to the Dutch firm’s sales.The UK shared its “Stone Cloak” technology capable of jamming Russian air defense systems with Ukraine as President Volodymyr Zelenskyy became the [first foreign leader to meet](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-27/zelenskyy-arrives-in-uk-for-burnham-talks-before-visit-to-trump) with new British Prime Minister Andy Burnham.OpenAI is [expanding its European headquarters](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-27/openai-announces-european-expansion-to-meet-growing-ai-demand) in Dublin, leasing 88,000 sq ft office and creating 250 new jobs.Hungary set up a [new agency](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-28/hungary-creates-agency-to-recoup-misappropriated-orban-era-funds) tasked with recovering misappropriated public funds, primarily under former Prime Minister Viktor Orban’s 16-year rule.
