### 中文译文

## 中国宇树科技拟在内地首个机器人IPO中募资9.04亿美元

中国的宇树科技公司，以[Unitree Robotics](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/688836%20CH%20Equity)闻名，正寻求通过首次公开募股筹集约61亿元人民币（9.04亿美元），这将使其成为内地首家上市的人形机器人制造商。

据周四的一份交易所文件，这家总部位于杭州的公司将在上海科技型科创板以每股150.80元的价格发行股票。此次发行将包括4040万股，占其首次公开募股后扩大股本的10%。

宇树科技计划上市之际，具身人工智能正成为全球人工智能竞赛中的关键未来战场。人形机器人已成为北京的战略重点之一，而宇树科技正成为该国最突出的参与者之一。

![图片](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3b481cc90b148179a835c143db9793b9/3b481cc9-0b14-81e7-8eca-d1f82f651f3c-8f35b1e8c520b6da58679d02e337823e.png)

根据上月发布的IPO 688836 宇树科技首次公开发行股票并在科创板上市招股意向书，该公司去年交付了超过5,500台人形机器人，全球排名第一。其四足机器人累计销量超过33,000台。

宇树科技已获得中国多家大型科技公司和国有投资者的投资，其中包括与[腾讯控股有限公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/700%20HK%20Equity)、[阿里巴巴集团控股有限公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/BABA%20US%20Equity)和[中国移动有限公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/941%20HK%20Equity)有关联的机构。

该公司的首次公开募股是在一段快速增长期之后进行的。根据其招股说明书，营收从上一年的3.93亿元人民币飙升至2025年的17亿元人民币。去年净利润为2.78亿元人民币，毛利率超过60%。

这家机器人制造商表示，计划将约42亿元人民币的首次公开募股所得资金用于资助具身人工智能模型开发、人形机器人研究、新产品项目和制造扩张项目。

宇树科技创始人王兴兴在售股后仍将牢牢掌握控制权。预计首次公开募股后，王兴兴及其控制的实体将持有公司约31.29%的股权。

### **商业模式**

宇树科技的商业模式基于通过设计集成化的内部执行器而非组装多个供应商的组件，来提供显著的成本和性能优势。通过在其产品线中应用垂直整合战略——类似于汽车制造商[比亚迪](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/002594%20CH%20Equity)——公司降低了开支并推动了销量增长，但仍需将这种硬件上的成功与更强的机器人智能和软件应用结合起来。

摩根士丹利表示，尽管地缘政治不确定性日益加剧，中国机器人公司仍处于有利地位，能够抓住全球需求。

该银行在8月3日的一份报告中表示，美国近期对某些外国制造的先进机器人设备的限制，对中国实体的短期影响可能有限，但可能使该行业的长期道路更加不确定。

该公司可能面临大量竞争。中国人形机器人行业的重心正从展示优越的运动控制转向开发使机器人能够替代人力的AI模型。

宇树科技已承认，在为人形机器人开发“大脑”方面，其面临输给竞争对手的风险。从风险投资家到汽车制造商，投资者今年已向该行业投入至少1000亿元人民币，其中大部分资金流向了从事人形AI模型的初创公司，凸显了该行业从“身体”到“思维”的转变。

## China’s Unitree Seeks $904 Million in First Mainland Robotic IPO

China’s Yushu Technology Co., known as [Unitree Robotics](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/688836%20CH%20Equity), is seeking to raise about 6.1 billion yuan ($904 million) in an initial public offering that would make it the mainland’s first publicly traded humanoid robot maker.

The Hangzhou-based firm priced its offering on Shanghai’s technology-heavy STAR Market at 150.80 yuan per share, according to an exchange filing Thursday. The sale will consist of 40.4 million shares, or 10% of its enlarged share capital following the IPO.

Unitree’s planned listing comes as embodied artificial intelligence looms as a key future battleground in the global AI race. Humanoid robots have become one of Beijing’s strategic priorities, and Unitree is emerging as one of the country’s most prominent players.

The firm shipped more than 5,500 humanoid robots last year, ranking first globally, according to its IPO 688836 宇树科技首次公开发行股票并在科创板上市招股意向书 released last month. Cumulative sales of its quadruped robots exceeded 33,000 units.

Unitree has received investment from a number of China’s largest technology firms and state-backed investors, including ones affiliated with [Tencent Holdings Ltd.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/700%20HK%20Equity), [Alibaba Group Holding Ltd.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/BABA%20US%20Equity) and [China Mobile Ltd.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/941%20HK%20Equity)

The company’s IPO follows a period of rapid growth. Revenue surged to 1.7 billion yuan in 2025, from 393 million yuan the year before, according to its prospectus. Net profit was 278 million yuan last year, while its gross margin exceeded 60%.

The robot maker said it plans to use approximately 4.2 billion yuan of its IPO proceeds to fund embodied AI model development, humanoid robot research, new product programs and manufacturing expansion projects.

Unitree’s founder Wang Xingxing will remain firmly in the driving seat even after the share sale. Wang and entities under his control are expected to hold approximately 31.29% of the company’s equity interest after the IPO.

### **Business Model**

Unitree’s business model is based on delivering significant cost and performance advantages by designing integrated, in-house actuators rather than assembling components from multiple suppliers. By applying a vertical integration strategy across its product range — similar to carmaker [BYD Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/002594%20CH%20Equity) — the company reduces expenses and drives up sales volume, though it still needs to combine this hardware success with stronger robot intelligence and software applications.

Chinese robotic companies remain well positioned to capture global demand despite growing geopolitical uncertainty, according to Morgan Stanley.

Recent US restrictions on certain foreign-made advanced robotic devices are likely to have limited near-term effects on Chinese entities, though they could make the industry’s long-term path more uncertain, the bank said in an Aug. 3 report.

The company is likely to face plenty of competition. The focus of China’s humanoid robot sector is moving away from demonstrating superior motion control to developing AI models that will enable humanoid robots to replace human labor.

Unitree has acknowledged it risks losing its edge over rivals in developing the “brain” for humanoid robots. Investors from venture capitalists to carmakers have poured at least 100 billion yuan into the sector this year, with a majority of the funds directed to startups working on humanoid AI models, underscoring the industry’s body-to-mind shift.
