### 中文译文

## 长鑫存储的竞争对手长江存储更接近中国下一大型芯片IPO

[长江存储科技](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1577197D:CH) 向在中国进行首次公开募股又近了一步，紧随竞争对手长鑫存储（CXMT Corp.）近乎创纪录的股票发行之后。

该公司被称为YMTC，完成了上市前辅导工作，并被认定具有上市公司所期望的公司治理结构、会计基础和内部控制制度，根据中国证监会网站上发布的报告。

YMTC现在有望效仿CXMT的融资模式，因为中国的国产芯片巨头正寻求利用资本市场扩大产能，并推动北京方面实现技术自主的雄心。CXMT上月融资666亿元人民币（合99亿美元），成为内地有史以来第二大IPO，并因股价开盘大涨而成为该国[最大的上市公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-13/cxmt-overtakes-tencent-to-become-most-valuable-chinese-company)。

这一消息部分归咎于科创50指数一个月来最大跌幅、宇树科技在中国科技股中的上市，因为大型IPO通常被视为抽走市场流动性并拖累投资者情绪。周三，科创50指数暴跌6.9%，创一个月来最大跌幅。

“全球科技股的疲软以及IPO的步伐——长江存储的消息紧跟宇树科技——也与此有关，尽管它们不是核心因素，”上海万基资产管理有限公司基金经理牛春宝表示。

长江存储和长鑫存储是北京战略重点的核心，因为人工智能需求导致全球内存短缺和整个行业利润飙升。在美国出口管制下，它们的重要性日益增加，推动投资者押注中国国内芯片供应链，并推动相关股票大幅上涨。

长江存储[超越](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-13/ymtc-passes-kioxia-in-nand-shipments-for-first-time-report-says)日本铠侠控股公司，成为6月份季度全球第三大闪存出货商，这表明中国芯片制造商正受益于人工智能驱动的短缺。在全球NAND比特出货量方面，它也缩小了与三星电子和SK海力士的差距。

[中信证券](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/600030%20CH%20Equity)和[中信建投证券](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/6066%20HK%20Equity)负责长江存储的辅导工作。

## CXMT Rival YMTC Moves Closer to China’s Next Big Chip IPO

[Yangtze Memory Technologies Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1577197D:CH) moved closer to an initial public offering in China, following in the footsteps of rival CXMT Corp.’s near-record share sale.

The company known as YMTC completed its pre-listing tutoring work and was found to have the corporate governance structure, accounting foundation, and internal control systems expected of a listed company, according to a report published on the China Securities Regulatory Commission’s website.

YMTC is now on track to follow CXMT’s fundraising example as China’s homegrown chip giants look to tap capital markets to expand capacity and bolster Beijing’s drive for tech self-sufficiency. CXMT last month raised 66.6 billion yuan ($9.9 billion), the second-biggest mainland debut ever, and became the country’s [largest listed company](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-13/cxmt-overtakes-tencent-to-become-most-valuable-chinese-company) as its shares surged out of the gate.

The news was partly blamed for a Star 50 Index Slumps Most in a Month, Unitree Robotics Debuts in Chinese technology stocks, as large IPOs are typically seen as draining market liquidity and weighing on investor sentiment. The STAR 50 Index plunged 6.9% on Wednesday, its biggest drop in a month.

“The global weakness in tech stocks and the pace of IPOs — with news of YMTC coming on the heels of Unitree, also have something to do with it, though they are not the core factors,” said Niu Chunbao, fund manager at Shanghai Wanji Asset Management Co.

YMTC and CXMT are central to Beijing’s strategic priorities as artificial intelligence demand drives a global memory shortage and soaring profits across the industry. Their importance has increased under US export curbs, fueling investor bets on China’s domestic chip supply chain and sending related stocks sharply higher.

YMTC has [edged out](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-13/ymtc-passes-kioxia-in-nand-shipments-for-first-time-report-says) Japan’s Kioxia Holdings Corp. to become the world’s third-biggest provider of flash memory shipments during the June quarter, a sign that Chinese chipmakers are benefiting from AI-driven shortages. It also narrowed its gap with Samsung Electronics Co. and SK Hynix Inc. in terms of global NAND bit shipment volume.

[Citic Securities](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/600030%20CH%20Equity) and [CSC Financial](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/6066%20HK%20Equity) handled YMTC’s tutoring process.
