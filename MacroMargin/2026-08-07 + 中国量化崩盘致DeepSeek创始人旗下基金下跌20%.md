## 中国量化崩盘致DeepSeek创始人旗下基金下跌20%

作者：彭博新闻
2026年8月7日 下午4:40 GMT+8
*更新于2026年8月7日 下午5:08 GMT+8*

### **要点**

- 中国的量化对冲基金在7月份因与人工智能相关的股票暴跌而遭受严重亏损。
- 中国仅做多的量化基金上月平均亏损17%，在上海实天信息技术有限公司追踪的超过1300只产品中，仅4%实现正收益。
- 这些数字揭示了中国量化基金因上月全球AI股票暴跌而遭受的广泛损失，这场抛售将美国公司Situational Awareness推向悬崖边缘，并使全球对冲基金蒙受损失。
中国的量化对冲基金在7月份因与人工智能相关的股票暴跌而遭受严重亏损，导致部分基金年内转为亏损，其他基金的表现也仅略好于基准股指。

据该咨询公司称，中国仅做多的量化基金上月平均亏损17%，在[上海实天信息技术有限公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1412186D:CH)追踪的超过1300只产品中，仅4%实现正收益。

由[DeepSeek](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/7574380Z:US)创始人梁文锋的对冲基金管理、并被该数据公司追踪的9只产品上月跌幅超过20%，其中除一只外均出现年初至今亏损。剩余一只年内涨幅仅为0.04%。

这些数字揭示了中国量化基金在上月全球人工智能股票暴跌中遭受的广泛损害。这场抛售[推动](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-30/the-24-hour-race-to-salvage-situational-awareness-souring-bets)美国公司Situational Awareness走向崩溃边缘，并让全球对冲基金蒙受损失。随着投资者争相抛售股票，中国的中证1000指数暴跌近20%。

![图片](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3b581cc90b1481d0a043c900e6b82eeb/3b581cc9-0b14-8176-bad7-f3db352617d9-8e38e465e67bea5cd7adcbeb5360f28a.png)

中国量化基金在经历多年飞速增长后迎来了清算时刻，行业总资产已超过2.6万亿元人民币（3850亿美元），所谓指数增强策略已成为中国富裕投资者的必备选择。

这些策略通常以阿尔法（alpha）衡量，即基金经理超越市场指数回报的程度。但对许多中国量化基金来说，今年的阿尔法实际上已被7月的暴跌抹去——这引发了人们对该行业新投资者是否愿意在动荡期间持有的疑问。

深圳金斧子基金销售有限公司（私募基金分销商）高级销售经理李毅表示：“尽管量化基金在上半年吸纳了行业大部分资金流入，但一旦阿尔法收窄或变为负值，怀疑情绪将迅速回归。老客户因为以前见过这种情况而较为冷静，但新投资者正变得愈发焦虑。”

### **阿尔法困境**

东方证券（Orient Securities Co.）编制的数据显示，截至7月31日，跟踪中证500指数的中国增强策略平均落后基准0.85个百分点，而过去八年平均跑赢基准8.9个百分点。

量化基金需要在今年剩余时间内实现超额收益的大幅反弹，才能达到该时期的最低水平——2024年的4.3%阿尔法。

痛苦普遍存在。据该券商数据，量化基金相对中证300指数的领先优势截至7月缩小至2.3个百分点，有望创下2019年以来的最低水平，当时落后0.04个百分点。

它们年初至今跑赢小盘股中证1000指数1.9个百分点，而自2018年以来平均跑赢19个百分点，2024年最低阿尔法为7.7个百分点。

这些数字凸显了中国量化基金要避免灾难性一年还有多远的路要走。

今年上半年，随着人工智能相关股票飙升，量化基金增加了对这些股票的敞口，因为其计算机模型中的“动量”和“风格”因素权重上升。但当7月份从韩国到美国的科技股暴跌时，抛售也拖累了中国股票，将量化基金的收益驱动因素变成了亏损来源。小盘股的拥挤头寸加剧了回撤。

除由DeepSeek的梁文锋创立的[浙江九章资产管理有限公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/2544270D:CH)外，其他管理规模各超过800亿元人民币的顶级量化基金也未能毫发无损地逃脱这场溃败。据Suntime汇编的数据，上海明泓投资有限公司管理的14只产品中有9只转为亏损，而九坤投资和衍复投资至少各有一只产品出现同样情况。

数据显示，上海文博投资管理有限公司的13只产品当月平均下跌21.5%，其中表现最差的产品暴跌42.7%，并将今年以来的涨幅转为亏损近30%。

九坤投资、明泓投资和衍复投资拒绝置评。九章资产和文博投资未立即回应置评请求。

## Quant Crash in China Sends DeepSeek Founder’s Funds Down 20%

By Bloomberg News
August 7, 2026 at 4:40 PM GMT+8
*Updated on August 7, 2026 at 5:08 PM GMT+8*

### **Takeaways**

- China’s quantitative hedge funds suffered steep losses in July after a rout in stocks linked to artificial intelligence.
- Long-only quants in the country suffered an average loss of 17% last month, with only 4% of the more than 1,300 products tracked by Shanghai Suntime Information Technology Co. posting a gain.
- The numbers reveal the widespread damage to Chinese quants from last month’s plunge in global AI stocks, a selloff that pushed US firm Situational Awareness to the brink and left hedge funds around the world nursing losses.
China’s quantitative hedge funds suffered steep losses in July after a rout in stocks linked to artificial intelligence, pushing some funds into the red for the year and leaving others doing little better than benchmark stock indexes.

Long-only quants in the country suffered an average loss of 17% last month, with only 4% of the more than 1,300 products tracked by [Shanghai Suntime Information Technology Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1412186D:CH) posting a gain, according to the consultancy.

The nine products managed by [DeepSeek](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/7574380Z:US) founder Liang Wenfeng’s hedge fund and tracked by the data firm fell more than 20% last month, and all but one of them were sitting on year-to-date losses. The remaining fund was up just 0.04% for the year.

The numbers reveal the widespread damage to Chinese quants from last month’s plunge in global AI stocks, a selloff that [pushed](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-30/the-24-hour-race-to-salvage-situational-awareness-souring-bets)US firm Situational Awareness to the brink and left [hedge funds](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-05/hedge-funds-take-big-hit-in-july-after-bruising-ai-selloff) around the world nursing losses. China’s broad [CSI 1000](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CSI1000%20Index) index plunged almost 20% as investors rushed to dump stocks.

The moment of reckoning for China’s quants followed years of [breakneck growth](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-02/china-quant-funds-draw-billions-as-ai-trounces-human-traders), which pushed total assets in the industry above 2.6 trillion yuan ($385 billion) as so-called index enhanced strategies became a must-have for the country’s wealthy investors.

These strategies are often judged by their alpha, a measure of how much fund managers can beat the returns of market indexes. But for many Chinese quants, alpha for this year was effectively wiped out by the July plunge — raising questions about whether new investors in the sector will be willing to hold on during the tumult.

“While quants captured the majority of industry inflows in the first half, skepticism will quickly return as soon as alpha narrows or turns negative,” said Li Yi, a senior sales manager at Shenzhen Golden Axe Fund Sales Co., a private-fund distributor. “Veteran clients are calmer as they’ve seen this all before, but new investors are turning more anxious.”

### **Alpha Crunch**

The average Chinese enhanced strategy tracking the CSI 500 Index trailed the benchmark by 0.85 percentage point through July 31, after beating it by an average of 8.9 percentage points in the past eight years, according to data compiled by Orient Securities Co.

It would take a sharp rebound in quants’ excess returns over the rest of the year to match the lowest of that period — the 4.3% alpha in 2024.

The pain was felt across the board. Quants’ lead over the CSI 300 Index shrank to 2.3 percentage points through July, putting it on track for the lowest since 2019 when it trailed by 0.04 percentage point, according to the broker’s data.

Their 1.9 percentage points year-to-date outperformance above the small-cap CSI 1000 Index compares with an average 19 percentage points since 2018 and the lowest alpha of 7.7 percentage points in 2024.

The numbers underscore how far China’s quants need to go to avoid a dire year.

As AI-related stocks surged in the first half, quants increased exposure to such shares as the “momentum” and “style” factors in their computer models gained weight. But when technology stocks slumped from South Korea to the US in July, the selloff dragged down Chinese shares as well and turned quants’ return drivers into sources of loss. Crowded positioning in small-caps worsened the drawdowns.

Besides [Zhejiang High-Flyer Asset Management](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/2544270D:CH) — founded by DeepSeek’s Liang — other top quants managing more than 80 billion yuan each also couldn’t escape the rout unscathed. Nine out of 14 products managed by Shanghai Minghong Investment Co. turned into a loss, while at least one product at Ubiquant and Yanfu Investments LLC did the same, according to data compiled by Suntime.

Shanghai Wenbo Investment Management Co.’s 13 products fell an average 21.5% for the month, with the worst performer plunging 42.7% and turning this year’s gain into a loss of almost 30%, the data showed.

Ubiquant, Minghong and Yanfu declined to comment. High-Flyer and Wenbo didn’t immediately respond to requests for comment.
