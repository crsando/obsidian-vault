### 中文译文

## 德国、法国、西班牙在与银行会谈后对人民币债券发行望而却步

### **要点**

- 德国、法国和西班牙曾与银行家就中国点心债券市场进行探索性讨论，但均表示目前没有计划发行此类债券。
- 会谈内容包括询问香港证券交易所的发行要求和程序，并与多家中国和国际银行组成的财团进行了磋商。
- 这些国家不愿发行点心债券，凸显了中国在吸引大型借款人进入点心债券市场方面面临的挑战，该市场流动性仍不及美元和欧元债券市场。
据知情人士透露，德国、法国和西班牙曾与银行家就中国点心债券市场进行探索性讨论，但均表示目前没有计划发行此类债券。

知情人士称，这些会谈于6月举行，参与方包括[汇丰控股有限公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/HSBA%20LN%20Equity)等中外银行组成的财团，会谈内容包括询问香港证券交易所的发行要求和程序。这些知情人士要求不具名，因涉及私下讨论。

西班牙财政部发言人在回应查询时表示，尽管该部监测包括点心债券在内的所有类型发行的表现，但其战略仍专注于发行欧元计价债务，“且我们不考虑改变这一战略”。

这种犹豫凸显了中国在吸引大型借款人进入点心债券市场方面面临的挑战，尽管北京方面[推动](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-02-02/china-highlights-xi-s-old-speech-on-risks-powerful-currency)人民币国际化，但该市场流动性仍不及美元和欧元债券市场。与此同时，人民币融资成本更低的前景吸引了越来越多的企业借款人以及包括葡萄牙在内的主权发行人。

![图片](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3b581cc90b1481fa81edcfd26799e830/3b581cc9-0b14-81d1-bca0-fde217136a08-ea97a7bb69248fc91a7cc0f4b9adf499.png)

德国财政部表示，其定期评估新的融资工具，只有在经济上可行、能够分散销售机会并对其市场存在做出积极贡献的情况下，才会发行外币债券。一位部门发言人表示：“就发行人民币债券而言，我们认为这些条件并未满足。”

法国财政部的一位女发言人拒绝置评，但点心债券发行并未出现在该国2026年的计划融资[计划](https://www.aft.gouv.fr/en/indicative-state-financing-programme)中。

汇丰控股的一位代表拒绝置评。

中国融资成本较低的吸引力引发非中国借款人的浓厚兴趣，推动点心债券发行量截至7月31日达到约2300亿元人民币（341亿美元）。这超过了以往任何一年的年度总额。

![图片](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3b581cc90b1481fa81edcfd26799e830/3b581cc9-0b14-81c8-ad70-ea6dfb3e7684-073aef51128f5ae4a04882eea0b0c309.png)

对于欧洲发行人来说，较低的成本尤其具有吸引力。彭博汇编的数据显示，自2023年欧洲央行处于紧缩周期顶峰以来，欧元债券的平均票面利率已超过人民币债券，今年两者差距扩大到近1个百分点。

欧洲央行6月[警告](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-11/ecb-lifts-rates-for-first-time-since-2023-as-inflation-heats-up)通胀风险持续存在，这可能进一步推动政策紧缩，从而进一步推高成本。

[葡萄牙](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-16/portugal-sells-offshore-yuan-bonds-in-first-for-euro-area)于4月成为首个进入离岸人民币债券市场的欧元区国家，筹集了相当于约2.5亿欧元（2.88亿美元）的资金。匈牙利是欧盟中唯一另一个发行过点心债券的国家，于2016年发行。

人民币融资的扩张也与人民币在贸易中日益广泛的使用相契合。彭博汇编的官方数据显示，人民币在中国跨境货物贸易结算中的占比去年达到创纪录的34.5%，高于2017年约10%的低点。

近几个月，中国与法国和德国之间的贸易紧张局势[加剧](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-17/germany-and-france-to-draw-up-roadmap-on-china-trade-imbalances)，使双方关系复杂化。法国总统埃马纽埃尔·马克龙还呼吁就汇率问题和金融市场开放问题与北京展开对话。

## Germany, France, Spain Balk at Yuan Bond Sales After Bank Talks

### **Takeaways**

- Germany, France and Spain had exploratory discussions with bankers about China’s dim sum bond market, but none of them say they have plans to pursue such offerings at the moment.
- The talks included questions about the Hong Kong stock exchange’s requirements and procedures for issuance, and were held with a consortium of Chinese and international banks.
- The reluctance of these countries to issue dim sum bonds highlights the challenges China faces in attracting major borrowers to the dim sum bond market, which remains less liquid than its dollar and euro counterparts.
Germany, France and Spain had exploratory discussions with bankers about China’s dim sum bond market, according to people familiar with the matter, but none of them say they have plans to pursue such offerings at the moment.

The talks were held in June with a consortium of Chinese and international banks, including [HSBC Holdings Plc](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/HSBA%20LN%20Equity), and included questions about the Hong Kong stock exchange’s requirements and procedures for issuance, the people said, asking not to be identified discussing private matters.

A spokesperson for the Spanish Treasury, in response to a query, said that while it monitors the performance of all types of issuance, including dim sum bonds, its strategy remains focused on issuing euro-denominated debt, “and we are not considering changing it.”

The reluctance highlights the challenges China faces in attracting major borrowers to the dim sum bond market, which remains less liquid than its dollar and euro counterparts despite Beijing’s [push](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-02-02/china-highlights-xi-s-old-speech-on-risks-powerful-currency) to internationalize the yuan. At the same time, the prospect of cheaper financing costs in the yuan has attracted a growing number of corporate borrowers, as well as sovereign issuers, including Portugal.

For its part, Germany’s finance ministry said it regularly evaluates new financing instruments and would issue foreign-currency bonds only if it were economically viable, would diversify sales opportunities and would make a positive contribution to its market presence. “In the case of an issuance in renminbi, we do not believe these conditions are met,” a ministry spokesperson said.

A spokeswoman for France’s finance ministry declined to comment, but dim sum bond issuance doesn’t appear on the country’s planned financing [program](https://www.aft.gouv.fr/en/indicative-state-financing-programme) for 2026.

A representative for HSBC declined to comment.

The appeal of cheaper financing in China has drawn a surge of interest from non-Chinese borrowers, propelling issuance of dim sum bonds to about 230 billion yuan ($34.1 billion) as of July 31. That’s more than the annual total for any previous year.

For European issuers, the lower costs are particularly attractive. Since 2023, when the ECB was at the peak of its tightening cycle, average coupon rates on euro notes have surpassed those of bonds issued in yuan, with the gap growing to nearly 1 percentage point this year, Bloomberg-compiled data show.

The ECB in June [warned](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-11/ecb-lifts-rates-for-first-time-since-2023-as-inflation-heats-up) of continued inflation risks, which could potentially spur further policy tightening, increasing costs even more.

[Portugal](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-16/portugal-sells-offshore-yuan-bonds-in-first-for-euro-area) became the first euro zone country to tap the offshore yuan bond market in April, raising the equivalent of about €250 million ($288 million). Hungary is the only other member of the European Union to have sold dim sum bonds, with an offering in 2016.

The expansion in yuan financing also dovetails with the growing use of the currency in trade. Its share of China’s cross-border goods trade settlement hit a record 34.5% last year, up from a low of around 10% in 2017, official data [compiled](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-05/yuan-funding-hits-record-200-billion-as-china-s-ambitions-grow) by Bloomberg show.

Trade tensions between China and France and Germany have [flared](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-17/germany-and-france-to-draw-up-roadmap-on-china-trade-imbalances) in recent months, complicating relations between the two sides. French President Emmanuel Macron has also called for a dialogue with Beijing on the issues of exchange rates and the opening of financial markets.
