### 中文译文

## 梅尔茨暗示德国将在对华贸易上采取更强硬立场

### **要点**

- 德国总理弗里德里希·梅尔茨表示，由于对贸易失衡的担忧，德国可能对中国采取更强硬的立场。
- 梅尔茨的执政联盟将在10月欧盟峰会前确定其立场，预计届时欧盟将出台措施保护其产业。
- 德国财政部长拉尔斯·克林贝尔表示，德国必须保护产业免受“不公平竞争”的影响，官员们不应天真，“当我们看到别人不按规则行事时”。
随着对贸易失衡加剧可能削弱欧洲竞争力的担忧日益加剧，德国总理弗里德里希·梅尔茨暗示德国可能对中国采取更强硬的立场。

“我们承认，德国工业显然已经改变了其在全球贸易失衡问题上的立场，”梅尔茨周三在柏林以东的内阁会议后对记者表示。他说，他的执政联盟将在10月欧盟峰会前确定最终立场，预计届时欧盟将出台旨在保护其产业的措施。

德国长期以来一直是欧盟成员国中不愿扰乱与北京贸易渠道的国家之一，但梅尔茨政府已改弦更张，因为欧盟领导人得出结论：如果欧盟不对失衡增强抵御，就有在经济上失利的风险。

今年6月，各国领导人责成负责欧盟贸易事务的欧盟委员会提出一系列新的贸易防御措施。

柏林当局一直在[幕后努力](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-28/germany-maps-china-s-weaknesses-in-preparation-for-a-trade-war)，以识别中国的脆弱点，以便在欧盟与中国陷入贸易战时可以利用这些弱点。

德国财政部长拉尔斯·克林贝尔（Lars Klingbeil）对中国的措辞时常比默尔茨更强硬。他表示，德国必须保护钢铁、汽车、化工和制药等行业，免受“不公平竞争”的影响。

克林贝尔是社会民主党联合主席，他表示自己坚持公平贸易，但官员们不应天真，“当我们看到别人不遵守规则时”。他上周在[竞选活动中](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-20/china-not-playing-by-the-rules-german-finance-minister-says)也发表了类似言论。

默尔茨与法国总统埃马纽埃尔·马克龙（Emmanuel Macron）在对华更强硬立场上更加一致。在7月的[一次联席会议](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-17/germany-and-france-to-draw-up-roadmap-on-china-trade-imbalances)上，法国领导人表示，两人在这个问题上的立场“从未如此趋同”，并抨击了他所称的北京公开贸易战。新的法德战略包括一份如何应对这一挑战的联合路线图。

## Merz Signals Tougher German Stance on Trade With China

### **Takeaways**

- Chancellor Friedrich Merz signaled that Germany is likely to adopt a tougher position on China due to concerns over the trade imbalance.
- Merz's coalition will finalize its stance ahead of a European Union summit in October, when the bloc is expected to lay out measures to shield its industries.
- German Finance Minister Lars Klingbeil said the country must defend industries against "unfair competition" and that officials shouldn’t be naive "when we see that others don’t play by the rules".
Chancellor Friedrich Merz signaled that Germany is likely to adopt a tougher position on China as concerns grow that the mounting trade imbalance will exacerbate Europe’s lack of competitiveness.

“We acknowledge that German industry has apparently changed its stance with respect to global trade imbalances,” Merz told reporters after a cabinet meeting east of Berlin on Wednesday. He said his coalition will finalize its stance ahead of a European Union summit in October, when the bloc is expected to lay out measures designed to shield its industries.

Germany has long been among EU member states reluctant to disrupt trade channels with Beijing, but Merz’s government has changed course as EU leaders conclude the bloc risks losing economic ground if it doesn’t build greater resistance against imbalances.

Leaders in June tasked the European Commission, which handles trade matters for the bloc, with presenting a slate of new trade defense measures.

Authorities in Berlin have been [working behind the scenes](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-28/germany-maps-china-s-weaknesses-in-preparation-for-a-trade-war) to identify Chinese vulnerabilities that can be exploited if the European Union finds itself in a trade war with China.

German Finance Minister Lars Klingbeil, who has often adopted sharper language on China than Merz, said the country must defend industries such as steel, autos, chemicals and pharmaceuticals against “unfair competition.”

Klingbeil, who is co-leader of the Social Democrats, said he adheres to fair trade but officials shouldn’t be naive “when we see that others don’t play by the rules.” He made similar [comments last week](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-20/china-not-playing-by-the-rules-german-finance-minister-says) on the campaign trail.

Merz and French President Emmanuel Macron aligned more closely on a firmer stance with China. At a [joint meeting in July](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-17/germany-and-france-to-draw-up-roadmap-on-china-trade-imbalances), the French leader said the two had “never been so convergent” on the issue and blasted what he described as Beijing’s open trade war. A new Franco-German strategy included a joint roadmap on how to deal with the challenge.
