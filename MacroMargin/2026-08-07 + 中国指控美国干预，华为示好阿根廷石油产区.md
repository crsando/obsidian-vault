### 中文译文

## 中国指控美国干预，华为示好阿根廷石油产区

中国指控美国干预阿根廷内政，此前美国大使馆官员反对一项将扩大中国电信巨头[华为技术有限公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/40978Z:CH)业务足迹的合同。

美国官员威胁称，如果位于内乌肯省（阿根廷页岩油田所在地）的电力分销商 CALF 董事们执意推进与华为的协议，将拒绝或撤销他们的美国签证。

“这些做法充分体现了美国的傲慢与偏见，严重不尊重他国主权，损害自由市场价值观，”中国驻阿根廷大使馆在 X 平台[发文](https://x.com/ChinaEmbArg/status/2085186688138015099?s=20)称。

CALF 发表声明称，该威胁是通过短信发送给其一名经理的，并要求美国大使馆使用更正式的渠道进行对话。华为已与 CALF 签订合同，但双方一直在商谈深化合作。

声明称：“CALF 将继续评估来自世界各地供应商的提案——无论是美国、欧洲还是亚洲——使用其一直以来的标准：质量、价格和融资。”CALF 运营着一个“多供应商网络”，华为与美国和欧洲的供应商一同在其中。

美国大使彼得·拉梅拉斯驳斥了关于签证措施等同于惩罚的说法。

“每个国家都会保护自己的家园，美国也不例外，”拉梅拉斯周四下午在 X 平台[发文](https://x.com/usambassadorarg/status/2085431553853698287?s=48)称。“拒绝或撤销签证不是惩罚，而是保护国家安全和利益的决定。”

阿根廷外交部尚未立即回应置评请求。内乌肯省拒绝置评。CALF 由一家合作社所有，而非该省本身。

阿根廷总统哈维尔·米莱公开与美国总统唐纳德·特朗普结盟，但他也欢迎与中国加强贸易和投资，这与他三年前作为候选人时对北京的猛烈批评有所转变。

内乌肯油气盆地的开发在米莱领导下加速，吸引了包括雪佛龙公司和大陆资源公司在内的美国钻探公司的新投资，同时也引起了得克萨斯州二叠纪盆地更多公司对出口页岩专长的兴趣。

除了丰富的页岩资源外，内乌肯长期以来设有一个中国空间站，美国过去曾对此表示担忧。

## China Alleges US Meddling as Huawei Courts Argentina Oil Region

China alleged the US was meddling in Argentina after American embassy officials opposed a contract that would expand the footprint of Chinese telecommunications giant [Huawei Technologies Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/40978Z:CH)

US officials threatened to deny or revoke US visas for directors of power distributor CALF in Neuquen province, home to Argentina’s shale oil fields, if they went ahead with the Huawei deal.

“These practices fully reflect the arrogance and prejudices of the US, constituting a significant disrespect of the sovereignty of other countries to the detriment of free-market values,” China’s embassy in Argentina [wrote on X](https://x.com/ChinaEmbArg/status/2085186688138015099?s=20).

CALF issued a statement saying that the threat was sent to one of its managers via text message and that it had requested the US embassy use a more formal channel for talks. Huawei already has contracts with CALF, but the companies have been in talks to grow the relationship.

“CALF will continue to evaluate proposals from suppliers around the world — American, European and Asian alike — using the same criteria it has always applied: quality, price and financing,” the statement said. CALF operates a “multi-vendor network” with Huawei featuring alongside US and European providers.

US Ambassador Peter Lamelas rejected the idea that visa measures amounted to punishment.

“Every country protects its house, the United States isn’t the exception,” Lamelas [posted](https://x.com/usambassadorarg/status/2085431553853698287?s=48) on X Thursday afternoon. “Denying or revoking a visa isn’t a punishment, it’s a decision to protect the nation’s security and interests.”

Argentina’s Foreign Ministry didn’t immediately respond to a request for comment. Neuquen’s province declined to comment. CALF is owned by a cooperative, not the province itself.

Argentine President Javier Milei has openly allied with US President Donald Trump, but he’s also welcomed more trade and investment from China in a pivot from his fierce criticism of Beijing as a candidate three years ago.

Development of Neuquen’s oil and gas basin has supercharged under Milei, drawing new investments from US drillers including Chevron Corp. and Continental Resources Inc., as well as interest from a broader set of companies in the Permian Basin in Texas looking to export their shale expertise.

As well as the vast shale resources, Neuquen has long been home to a Chinese space station that the US has expressed concern over in the past.
