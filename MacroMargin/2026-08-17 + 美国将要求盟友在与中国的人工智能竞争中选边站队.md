### 中文译文

## 美国将要求盟友在与中国的人工智能竞争中选边站队

![Illustration shows U.S. and Chinese flags](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3bf81cc90b1481309202c1c8369c7030/3bf81cc9-0b14-8180-bb66-d4e75b1f004e-c3c7dd0d6283845bc6ed75c970b15857.jpg)

- 摘要
- 美国致人工智能联盟成员的函件草案警告其不要加入竞争性框架
- 该信件将寄送给《人工智能机遇声明》的 35 个签署国，旨在推动各方在人工智能模型、芯片及关键矿物领域开展合作。
- 收件国名单中将包括哈萨克斯坦，它是目前唯一同时加入美中两大人工智能联盟的国家，同时也是关键矿物的供应地。
- 中美在人工智能领域的竞争已进入关键阶段，中国开源模型正逐渐领先于 OpenAI 与 Anthropic。
华盛顿，8 月 14 日（路透社）——据一名美国官员以及路透社看到的内部草案显示，美国正准备告知数十个国家：在与中国争夺人工智能主导权的竞争中，它们必须选边站队；否则如果这些国家同时加入北京方面的竞争性框架，就会被排除在由美国领导的 AI 联盟之外。

在与北京的激烈技术竞争中，华盛顿去年发起了旨在确保人工智能模型、半导体和关键矿物供应链的“Pax Silica”倡议。

已有约二十多个国家加入，包括关键潜在关键矿产来源国哈萨克斯坦——该国也已加入中国的联盟，以及日本、澳大利亚和韩国等美国亲密盟友。

这份由美国国务院起草的信函，是写给今年6月签署美国“人工智能机会声明”的35个签署方的，该声明包括不具约束力的“Pax Silica”框架成员以及其他表示希望与美国在人工智能合作上保持一致的国家。

通过迫使各国选边站队，美国希望在制造最先进人工智能（可能用于军事或经济主导地位）的竞赛中，切断中国的资源。

今年7月，中国国家主席习近平发起了一个对立的“世界人工智能合作组织”，推广中国的开放权重技术，以挑战美国对这个快速发展的领域的影响力。

哈萨克斯坦是迄今已知唯一加入[这两项倡议](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/chinas-xi-promotes-chinas-commitment-ai-access-speech-shanghai-conference-2026-07-17/)的国家，这在美国敲响了警钟。

“什么都参与，就等于什么都没参与。签署‘Pax Silica’宣言不仅是一种入会订阅，更是一种承诺，”信中说，敦促各国在人工智能问题上“审慎选择”。

信中说：“它不能与那些期望与我们相悖的重复性倡议的成员资格并存。”信中没有具体提到中国。

路透社无法确定美国打算何时发出这封信，也无法确定在发出之前是否会进行修改。这份草稿没有注明日期。

美国国务院对路透社表示，不会对“据称泄露的内部文件”发表评论。

中国驻华盛顿大使馆表示，该国反对将贸易和技术问题政治化。一位使馆发言人表示：“此类行为只会扼杀全球人工智能的进步，不符合任何人的利益。”

哈萨克斯坦驻华盛顿大使馆未回应置评请求。

### “不能两全其美”

Pax Silica 协议旨在推动美国盟友和合作伙伴参与联合项目和出口管制，并最终减少在关键矿产、人工智能模型以及为其提供动力的半导体芯片方面对对手的依赖。

美中之间的技术领导地位竞争已经到了关键时刻，中国开放权重的人工智能模型在与美国公司（如 OpenAI 和 Anthropic）的专有系统的竞争中迅速取得进展。

该技术能力的指数级增长，包括自主黑客攻击的能力，迫使全球对其威力进行反思。

北京正在[考虑限制](https://www.reuters.com/world/beijing-is-looking-curbing-overseas-access-chinas-top-ai-models-sources-say-2026-07-07/)海外使用中国一些领先的人工智能模型，突显了其严格的国家安全议程所带来的日益加剧的紧张关系。

美国在6月将哈萨克斯坦誉为首个加入Pax Silica的中亚国家，该国拥有大量推动先进技术所需的关键矿产储备。

中国利用其当前在关键矿产上的近乎垄断地位，作为对美国去年由唐纳德·特朗普总统发起的关税战争的报复武器。特朗普已加大美国国内及从盟友处获取这些矿产的努力。

根据美国国务院网站上发布的声明，Pax Silica成员可以共享人工智能相关项目的投资机会，而签署《人工智能机会声明》的国家则在象征意义上与美国达成了“共同目标”和“共同愿景”。

美国官员起草了这封信，以明确表示“你不能两头兼顾”，这位美国官员告诉路透社。由于内部仍在讨论此事，该官员要求匿名。

该官员表示：“很难想象一个国家如何能在某一技术生态系统中可靠地将自己定位为值得信赖的伙伴，同时却签署一项由中国设计的旨在推进竞争性人工智能愿景的倡议。”

WASHINGTON, Aug 14 (Reuters) - The U.S. is preparing to ‌tell dozens of countries they must pick sides in the artificial intelligence race with China, warning they will be excluded from a U.S.-led AI coalition if they also sign up for Beijing's competing framework, according to a U.S. official and an internal draft reviewed by Reuters.
华盛顿，8 月 14 日（路透社）——据一名美国官员以及路透社看到的内部草案显示，美国正准备告知数十个国家：在与中国争夺人工智能主导权的竞争中，它们必须选边站队；否则如果这些国家同时加入北京方面的竞争性框架，就会被排除在由美国领导的 AI 联盟之外。

Washington last year launched the Pax Silica initiative aimed at securing supply chains for AI models, semiconductors and critical minerals, amid a fierce technology rivalry with Beijing.

About two dozen countries have joined, including Kazakhstan, a key potential source of critical minerals that has also joined China's coalition, as well as close U.S. allies such as Japan, Australia and South Korea.

The draft letter, prepared by the State Department, is addressed to the 35 signatories of a U.S. "AI ⁠Opportunity Statement" signed in June, which includes members of the non-binding Pax Silica framework and other countries that have expressed a desire to align cooperation on AI with Washington.

By pressing countries to choose sides the U.S. hopes to starve China of resources in a race to make the most sophisticated AI, which could be used for military or economic dominance.

In July, Chinese President Xi Jinping launched a rival "World Artificial Intelligence Cooperation Organization", promoting his country's open-weight technology as a challenge to U.S. influence over the fast-moving sector.

Kazakhstan is the only country so far known to have [joined both initiatives](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/chinas-xi-promotes-chinas-commitment-ai-access-speech-shanghai-conference-2026-07-17/), setting off alarm bells in Washington.

"To be part of everything is to be part of nothing. Signature of the Pax Silica Declaration is not merely a membership subscription, but a commitment," the letter says, urging countries to "choose deliberately" on AI.

"It cannot be held alongside membership in duplicative initiatives whose expectations conflict with our own," the letter said, without specifically mentioning China.

Reuters could not determine when the U.S. intends to send the letter or whether it might be amended before sending. The draft was undated.

The State Department told Reuters it would not comment on "purportedly leaked internal documents."

China’s ‌embassy in ⁠Washington said the country opposes politicizing trade and technology issues. “Such actions will only stifle global AI advances and serve no one’s interests,” an embassy spokesperson said.

The Kazakh embassy in Washington did not respond to a request for comment.

### 'CAN'T HAVE IT BOTH WAYS'

The Pax Silica agreement aims to push U.S. allies and partners toward joint projects and export controls, and ultimately reduce reliance on adversaries for critical minerals, AI models and the semiconductor chips that power them.

The race between the U.S. and China for technological leadership has reached a pivotal moment, as Chinese open-weight AI models have made rapid gains against proprietary systems from U.S. companies such as ⁠OpenAI and Anthropic.

The exponential growth of the technology's capabilities, including the ability to hack autonomously, has forced a global reckoning over its power.

Beijing is [weighing restrictions](https://www.reuters.com/world/beijing-is-looking-curbing-overseas-access-chinas-top-ai-models-sources-say-2026-07-07/) on overseas access to some of China's leading AI models, highlighting the growing tension with its stringent national security agenda.

The U.S. [touted Kazakhstan, opens new tab](https://www.state.gov/releases/under-secretary-for-economic-affairs/2026/06/under-secretary-helbergs-remarks-on-the-accession-of-kazakhstan-to-pax-silica) in June as the first country in Central Asia to join Pax Silica, bringing significant reserves of critical minerals that fuel advanced technologies.

China has used ⁠its current near-monopolies over critical minerals as a retaliatory weapon in a tariff war launched last year by U.S. President Donald Trump, who has ramped up U.S. efforts to source the minerals domestically and from allies.

Members of Pax Silica have access to shared investment opportunities in AI-related projects while those who sign the AI Opportunity Statement have symbolically agreed on a "common purpose" and "shared vision" with ⁠the U.S., according to the statement posted on the State Department's website.

U.S. officials drafted the letter to make clear that "you can't have it both ways," the U.S. official told Reuters, speaking on condition of anonymity given ongoing internal discussions on the issue.

"It's difficult to see how a country can credibly position themselves as trusted partners in one technology ecosystem while simultaneously signing up for an initiative designed by China to advance a competing vision for AI," the official said.
