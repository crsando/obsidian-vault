### 中文译文

## 中国缩减紧缩力度，预算支出降幅收窄

### **要点**

- 中国7月政府支出收缩速度明显放缓，广义支出同比降4.4%。
- 广义财政收入7月增长近7%，为2024年以来最大增幅，收入增长加快有助于收窄今年前七个月的预算赤字。
- 财政部副部长廖岷表示，未来几个月当局将根据一项有针对性的计划推出新措施，利用财政资源推动企业和消费者借贷。
在经历了上个月两位数下降后，中国7月政府支出收缩速度明显放缓，这表明政策制定者可能正逐步接近扭转紧缩政策，而紧缩政策已被证明对经济构成巨大拖累。

根据彭博基于财政部周五（[发布](https://gks.mof.gov.cn/tongjishuju/202608/t20260814_3995497.htm)的数据）的计算，广义支出同比降幅为4.4%。这一降幅约为6月份跌幅的三分之一，当时支出骤降11.9%。

广义财政收入7月增长近7%，为2024年以来最大增幅，增速是6月的三倍多。收入增长加快有助于将今年前七个月的预算赤字收窄逾17%，至4.6万亿元人民币（合6840亿美元）。

虽然紧缩财政政策上月拖累了经济，[增长](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-17/china-s-economy-extends-slowdown-by-weakening-across-the-board)可能进一步跌破北京年度目标区间，但支出下滑的轨迹正趋于平缓。随着高层领导人[承诺](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-18/xi-s-no-2-urges-more-support-for-economy-after-big-growth-slump)加快执行今年批准的预算，未来几个月支出可能会回升。

![图片](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3c381cc90b14813bade8f79ae367feb7/3c381cc9-0b14-81c6-80d2-f945dc546d2f-6aada085128d762f123c3f98a956dfa2.png)

周五早些时候，财政部副部长廖岷表示，当局将在未来几个月内推出新措施，通过一项有针对性的计划，利用财政资源推动企业和消费者借贷。

与此同时，他表示，中国政府今年已计划的债券额度足以确保维持“财政支出强度”，并指出未来几个月还有超过2万亿元人民币的额度可用。

他补充说：“财政部将根据今年下半年的经济运行情况，规划并推出务实有效的额外政策。”

今年，中国加强了税收征管力度，针对企业的欠税和个人的海外投资收益征税，因为当局在省级公共财政紧张的情况下寻求增强财政实力。

7月份，税收收入同比跃升近14%，为2023年5月以来最快增速。

前七个月，预算税收收入增长6.7%，接近12万亿元人民币。业绩改善主要归因于证券交易印花税和个人所得税收入实现两位数增长。

由于今年对购车者的激励措施缩减导致汽车销量下滑，中国车辆购置税收入也增长了13%。作为中国政府最大的单一收入来源，增值税收入增长了6.1%。

然而，随着房地产低迷持续，大多数房地产相关税收收入继续下降。地方政府土地出让收入在前七个月暴跌30.8%，但略低于上半年跌幅。

### English Original

## China Scales Back Austerity With Smaller Budget Spending Dip

### **Takeaways**

- China’s government spending contracted at a much slower clip in July, with a broad measure of expenditure falling 4.4% from a year earlier.
- Broad fiscal revenue gained nearly 7% in July, the most since 2024, and the faster income growth helped shrink the budget deficit in the first seven months of the year.
- Vice Finance Minister Liao Min said the authorities will launch new measures in the coming months under a targeted program tapping fiscal resources to drive business and consumer borrowing.
China’s government spending contracted at a much slower clip in July after a double-digit crash a month earlier, suggesting policymakers may be inching closer to reversing the austerity that proved a huge drag on the economy.

A broad measure of expenditure fell 4.4% from a year earlier, according to Bloomberg calculations based on Ministry of Finance data [released](https://gks.mof.gov.cn/tongjishuju/202608/t20260814_3995497.htm) Friday. The decline was almost a third of the pace seen in June, when spending plunged 11.9%.

Broad fiscal revenue gained nearly 7% in July, the most since 2024 and over three times as fast as its rise in the prior month. The faster income growth helped shrink the budget deficit in the first seven months of the year by more than 17% to 4.6 trillion yuan ($684 billion).

While tight fiscal policy weighed down the economy last month, when [growth](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-17/china-s-economy-extends-slowdown-by-weakening-across-the-board) likely slipped further below Beijing’s annual target range, the trajectory of the spending slump was getting less steep. With top leaders [pledging](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-18/xi-s-no-2-urges-more-support-for-economy-after-big-growth-slump) to accelerate the implementation of this year’s approved budget, expenditure could pick up in the coming months.

Earlier on Friday, Vice Finance Minister Liao Min [said](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-21/china-plans-new-fiscal-support-policies-this-year-amid-slowdown) the authorities will launch new measures in the coming months under a targeted program tapping fiscal resources to drive business and consumer borrowing.

Meanwhile, he suggested the Chinese government’s bond quota already planned for this year is sufficient to ensure it will maintain “fiscal spending intensity,” noting there is more than 2 trillion yuan in the allowance available in the coming months.

“The Ministry of Finance will plan and introduce pragmatic and effective additional policies according to the operation of the economy in the second half of this year,” he added.

China strengthened efforts to collect taxes this year, targeting companies for back payments and individuals for their overseas investment gains as the authorities sought to shore up fiscal strength with public finances under strain in the provinces.

For July, tax revenue jumped almost 14% on year, the fastest since May 2023.

In the first seven months, budget [income from taxes](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CHGR1C%20%20%20Index) expanded 6.7% to approach 12 trillion yuan. The improved performance came largely as a result of double-digit gains in receipts of [stamp duty on securities trading](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CHGR15CY%20Index) and individual income tax.

Proceeds from China’s levy on [vehicle purchases](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CHGR20CY%20Index) also rose 13% as incentives for car buyers were scaled back this year and caused auto sales to slump. Income from value-added tax, the single biggest source of revenue for the Chinese government, increased 6.1%.

Receipts from most real estate-related levies continued to decline, however, as the housing downturn drags on. Local government revenue from [selling land](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CHGRSLRY%20Index) tumbled 30.8% in the first seven months, though that was slightly less than its fall in the first half.
