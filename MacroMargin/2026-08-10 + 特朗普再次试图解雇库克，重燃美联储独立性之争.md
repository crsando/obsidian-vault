> 📌 **核心摘要**
>
> 特朗普再次威胁解雇美联储理事丽莎·库克，引发美联储独立性争议
> • 特朗普在信中称正在考虑解除库克职务，并给出8月26日回应期限
> • 最高法院此前以5比4的票数允许库克继续任职，批评总统未给通知和申辩机会
> • 库克的律师表示将继续挑战解雇行动，指控缺乏依据
> • 白宫经济顾问哈塞特强调库克无罪，并排除自己接替其职务的可能性
> • 若库克被免，可能开启白宫进一步罢免其他美联储理事的先例，以推动降息政策

President Donald Trump has revived his threat to fire [Federal Reserve](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/13598Z:US) Governor Lisa Cook over unproven allegations of mortgage fraud, according to a letter seen by Bloomberg, a move that could renew a legal fight over the central bank’s independence.

据彭博社看到的一封信，美国总统唐纳德·特朗普再次威胁要解雇[美联储](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/13598Z:US)理事丽莎·库克，理由是未经证实的抵押贷款欺诈指控，此举可能重新引发关于美联储独立性的法律斗争。

In a letter dated Wednesday, Cook was told that Trump is “considering removing” her and gave her until Aug. 26 to respond to allegations made by the White House.

在日期为周三的信中，库克被告知特朗普“正在考虑解除她的职务”，并给她截止到8月26日的时间来回应白宫提出的指控。

The Supreme Court, in a 5-4 decision in June, [allowed](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-29/supreme-court-lets-fed-s-lisa-cook-stay-in-job-for-now) Cook to remain in her job while she fought Trump’s bid to oust her over the mortgage fraud accusations, faulting the president for not giving her notice and a chance to be heard before trying to remove her.

最高法院在6月以5比4的投票结果[允许](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-29/supreme-court-lets-fed-s-lisa-cook-stay-in-job-for-now)库克在就抵押贷款欺诈指控与特朗普罢免她的努力抗争期间继续任职，并批评总统在试图解除她的职务前未给予她通知和申辩机会。

The letter, signed by White House personnel director Dan Scavino, acknowledged that ruling and said “this is your opportunity to respond.”

这封由白宫人事主管丹·斯卡维诺签名的信承认了该裁决，并表示“这是你回应的机会。”

ABC News reported earlier on the new attempt to oust Cook.

ABC新闻此前报道了这一新的罢免库克的尝试。

In a statement, Cook’s lawyer Abbe Lowell said they will continue to challenge the attempt to fire her.

库克的律师阿贝·洛厄尔在一份声明中表示，他们将继续质疑解雇她的企图。

“These allegations are as baseless now as they were a year ago when President Trump tried to remove Governor Cook and interfere with the independence of the Federal Reserve,” Lowell said in the statement. “No matter what President Trump tries to do next, this much is clear under the facts and Supreme Court precedent — there is no valid cause for removing Governor Cook. As we did before, we will challenge this latest pretext and preserve her position and the historic role of the Fed.”

“这些指控现在和一年前一样毫无根据，当时特朗普总统试图罢免库克理事并干预美联储的独立性，”洛厄尔在声明中表示。“无论特朗普总统接下来试图做什么，根据事实和最高法院的先例，有一点是明确的——没有正当理由罢免库克理事。就像我们之前所做的那样，我们将挑战这一最新借口，维护她的职位以及美联储的历史性角色。”

Shortly after the Supreme Court ruling, Trump signaled that he would again try to remove Cook from her position. The president and his allies have since cast about for ways to oust members of the Fed’s Board of Governors in a bid to appoint new members that are more supportive of Trump’s push for lower interest rates.

在最高法院裁决后不久，特朗普表示他将再次试图解除库克的职务。此后，总统及其盟友一直在寻找罢免美联储理事会成员的方法，以便任命更支持特朗普推动降息的新成员。

The three-page letter amounts to an attempt to restart the process of removing Cook. It accuses Cook of “gross negligence” over the mortgage-fraud allegations, which have not been tested in a court of law.

这封三页的信函旨在重启罢免库克的程序。信中指控库克在抵押贷款欺诈指控中存在“严重疏忽”，而该指控尚未在法庭上得到审理。

“Even if your conduct does not rise to the level of a felony offense, it appears to demonstrate a level of gross negligence in financial transactions that calls into question your competence and trustworthiness as a financial regulator,” the letter reads.

信中写道：“即使你的行为未达到重罪的程度，似乎也表明你在金融交易中存在一定程度的严重疏忽，这令人怀疑你作为金融监管者的能力和可信度。”

Cook was appointed by President Joe Biden in 2022 to a term set to expire in 2038.

库克于2022年由总统乔·拜登任命，任期定于2038年届满。

The administration accuses Cook of fraudulently listing homes in Michigan and Georgia as a “primary residence” to secure more favorable terms on loans when she obtained mortgages in 2021.

特朗普政府指控库克在2021年取得抵押贷款时，欺诈性地将密歇根州和佐治亚州的房产列为“主要住所”，从而获得更优惠的贷款条件。

Cook has previously said that the claims are baseless and rely on “cherry-picked, incomplete snippets” of documents. The veracity of the allegations was not before the Supreme Court, and Cook has not been charged with any wrongdoing.

库克此前表示，这些指控毫无根据，并依赖于文件中“精心挑选的、不完整的片段”。这些指控的真实性并未在最高法院得到审理，库克也未受到任何不当行为的指控。

Close observers of the Fed warn that a successful push to dismiss Cook could open the door for the White House to pursue other governors for dismissal too, allowing the president to nominate replacements that share his calls for lower interest rates.

密切关注美联储的人士警告称，成功推动罢免库克可能为白宫打开大门，使其也寻求罢免其他理事，从而允许总统提名支持其呼吁降息的继任者。

## 哈塞特希望库克无罪，并排除接替其在美联储的职务

White House National Economic Council Director Kevin Hassett said he hoped Federal Reserve Governor Lisa Cook is right in saying she’s innocent of mortgage fraud, while ruling out replacing her on the US central bank’s board.

白宫国家经济委员会主任凯文·哈塞特表示，他希望美联储理事丽莎·库克自称在抵押贷款欺诈中无罪的说法是正确的，同时排除了接替她在美联储理事会职位的可能性。

In an interview with CNBC Monday, Hassett said the issue is “a law enforcement matter” and declined to speak on the specifics of allegations against Cook.

哈塞特周一在接受CNBC采访时表示，此事是“执法事务”，并拒绝就针对库克的指控细节发表评论。

“Now I’m an economist, I can’t add anything to it,” he said.

他说：“我是经济学家，对此我无法再多说什么。”

President Donald Trump last week revived his threat to fire Cook, in a letter to the Fed governor that said he was “considering removing” her and gave her until Aug. 26 to respond to allegations made by the White House. In a statement, Cook’s lawyer Abbe Lowell said they will continue to challenge the attempt to fire her.

美国总统唐纳德·特朗普上周在致美联储理事的信中重新发出威胁要解雇库克，称他“正在考虑罢免”她，并给她截至8月26日的时间回应白宫提出的指控。库克的律师阿贝·洛威尔在一份声明中表示，他们将继续挑战解雇她的企图。

“She says that she’s 100% innocent — as as a human being, I hope that’s true,” Hassett said.

哈塞特说：“她说自己百分之百无辜——作为一个人，我希望这是真的。”

Asked if he might get the job if Cook is ultimately removed, Hassett said “no, I promise you, that’s not going to happen.”

当被问及如果库克最终被免职，他是否会得到这个职位时，哈塞特说：“不，我向你保证，那不会发生。”

Hassett also said that, if he were at the Fed, “I would either hold steady or cut rates because there’s so much supply-side momentum.” He flagged that recent inflation reports show prices coming down.

哈塞特还说，如果他在美联储，“我会要么维持利率不变，要么降息，因为供给侧动力如此强劲。”他指出，最近的通胀报告显示物价正在下降。

“If I were going to make any move going forward, I’d want to see more months like the months we’ve just seen,” the NEC director said.

这位国家经济委员会主任说：“如果我要在今后采取任何行动，我希望看到更多像我们刚刚看到的这样的月份。”
