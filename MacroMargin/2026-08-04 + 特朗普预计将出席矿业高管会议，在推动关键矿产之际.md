### 中文译文

## 特朗普预计将出席矿业高管会议，在推动关键矿产之际

![U.S. President Trump disembarks Air Force One at Joint Base Andrews](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3b281cc90b148103a2d1e9e6dfdc527f/3b281cc9-0b14-81c0-ac4a-f0b750bd59ad-32d59c05a7b297fafa86bf36f0262d1e.jpg)

美国总统唐纳德·特朗普于2026年8月2日在美国马里兰州安德鲁斯联合基地走下空军一号后，手持雨伞做手势。路透社/丹尼尔·豪尔

8月3日（路透社）——据两名知情人士透露，预计总统[唐纳德·特朗普](https://www.reuters.com/world/us/donald-trump/)将于周五出席由美国国务院主办的与顶尖矿业高管举行的圆桌会议。

该活动是特朗普政府为扩大国内及盟友所需的[关键矿产供应](https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/trump-may-need-allow-chinese-minerals-us-industry-struggles-meet-2027-deadline-2026-07-27/)（用于能源、国防和先进技术）而做出的更广泛努力的一部分。据消息人士称，议程、参与者和其他细节仍在变化中，可能有所调整。

本届政府已将确保关键矿产供应链列为优先事项，采取措施提高国内生产和加工能力，减少对[中国](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/)的依赖，并加强与盟友的伙伴关系。

该政府认为，扩大该行业不仅需要投资于矿山和加工设施，还需要更多技术工人储备，包括工程师、地质学家、矿工和技术人员，因为企业面临支持未来项目所需人才短缺的问题。

据另一位直接了解规划的人士透露，美国能源部也预计将于周五举办一场以劳动力发展为主题的活动。

该消息人士称，该活动将包括来自美国所有14所认证矿业学校的代表，旨在提高人们对采矿作为职业道路的认识，同时强调需要更多学生进入该领域。

中国[广泛的矿业学校网络](https://www.reuters.com/graphics/CHINA-RAREEARTHS/UNIVERSITIES/zgpolorrwvd/)帮助其成为全球最大的矿产生产国。

白宫、能源部和国务院未立即回应置评请求。

行业贸易组织全国矿业协会（National Mining Association）的阿什利·伯克（Ashley Burke）表示：“我们期待继续与（政府）合作，寻求解决方案，以负责任地开发我国丰富的资源、保障供应链并打造未来的矿业劳动力。”

## Trump expected to attend event with mining execs amid critical minerals push

U.S. President Donald Trump gestures as he holds an umbrella after disembarking Air Force One at Joint Base Andrews, in Maryland, U.S., August 2, 2026. REUTERS/Daniel Heuer

Aug 3 (Reuters) - President [Donald Trump](https://www.reuters.com/world/us/donald-trump/) is expected to attend a roundtable on Friday with top mining executives hosted by the U.S. State Department, according to two people familiar with the planning.

The event is part of the Trump administration's broader efforts to expand domestic and allied [supplies of critical minerals](https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/trump-may-need-allow-chinese-minerals-us-industry-struggles-meet-2027-deadline-2026-07-27/) needed for energy, defense and advanced technologies. The agenda, participants and other details remain fluid and could change, according to the sources.

The administration has made securing critical minerals supply chains a priority, taking steps to boost domestic production and processing, reduce reliance on [China](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/) and strengthen partnerships with allies.

It has argued that expanding the sector will require not only investment in mines and processing facilities but also a larger pipeline of skilled workers, including engineers, geologists, miners and technicians, as companies face a shortage of talent needed to support future projects.

The U.S. Department of Energy is also expected to host an event on Friday focused on workforce development, according to a third person with direct knowledge of the planning.

That event will include representatives from all 14 accredited U.S. mining schools, and aims to raise awareness of mining as a career path while highlighting the need for more students to enter the field, the source said.

China's [broad network of mining schools](https://www.reuters.com/graphics/CHINA-RAREEARTHS/UNIVERSITIES/zgpolorrwvd/) has helped it become the world's largest minerals producer.

The White House, Energy Department and State Department did not immediately respond to requests for comment.

"We look forward to continuing to work with (the administration) on solutions to responsibly develop our nation's vast resources, secure our supply chains and create the mining workforce of the future," said Ashley Burke of the National Mining Association, an industry trade group.
