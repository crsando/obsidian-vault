## 印度称与中国就商务签证延误问题进行磋商

苏杰生*摄影师：Ezra Acayan/Getty Images*

作者 [Mihir Mishra](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AXPrjtEgm0g/mihir-mishra) 2026年8月22日 下午7:11 GMT+8

[印度](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1504Z:IN)正与[中国](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/PRCH%20CH%20Equity)就其企业面临的签证延误问题进行讨论，外交部长苏杰生表示。

政府已向邻国提出这一问题，苏杰生周六在新德里回答关于印度企业无法获得中国商务签证的问题时表示。

[中国](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/PRCH%20CH%20Equity)已收紧了向印度高管发放商务签证的力度，以至于即使是拥有“先进合作关系”的公司也面临签证拒签，迫使它们将重要的高管会议转移到邻近国家，《经济时报》[本周报道](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/nri/work/no-easy-entry-for-india-inc-in-china-as-beijing-tightens-business-visa-rules/articleshow/133358146.cms?from=mdr)。报道称，印度的实体人工智能[初创公司](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/artificial-intelligence/chinese-visa-impact-hits-indian-physical-ai-startups/articleshow/133387338.cms?from=mdr)也受到了影响。

这两个邻国之间的关系在2020年初的加勒万河谷冲突以及随后的军事对峙和边境紧张局势后恶化。当时，新德里禁止了大量在印度运营的中国应用程序，同时根据《第3号新闻说明》（Press Note 3）收紧了针对中国企业的投资规则，该规定至今仍要求任何中国外商直接投资都必须获得多个部委的批准。

两国关系此后有所改善，新德里放宽了签证限制。它还允许拥有不超过10%的非控制性受益所有权的外国实体在印度投资。放宽针对陆地接壤国家的外国直接投资规则刺激了489.5亿卢比的投资，工业和国内贸易促进部表示。

今年3月的政策调整吸引了截至8月20日总价值490亿卢比（5.12亿美元）的离岸投资提案，印度工商部表示。放宽针对陆地接壤国家的外国直接投资规则刺激了489.5亿卢比的投资，工业和国内贸易促进部表示。

## India Says It’s in Talks With China on Business Visa Delays

S. Jaishankar*Photographer: Ezra Acayan/Getty Images*

By [Mihir Mishra](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AXPrjtEgm0g/mihir-mishra)August 22, 2026 at 7:11 PM GMT+8

[India](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1504Z:IN) is discussing with [China](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/PRCH%20CH%20Equity) about visa delays faced by its businesses, Foreign Minister S Jaishankar said.

The government has taken up the issue with its neighbor, Jaishankar said in New Delhi on Saturday, in response to a question on the inability of Indian businesses to get Chinese business visas.

[China](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/PRCH%20CH%20Equity) has tightened the issuance of business visas to Indian executives to such an extent that even companies with “advanced partnerships” are facing visa denials, forcing them to shift crucial executive meetings to nearby countries, the Economic Times [reported this week](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/nri/work/no-easy-entry-for-india-inc-in-china-as-beijing-tightens-business-visa-rules/articleshow/133358146.cms?from=mdr). Physical Indian [AI startups](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/artificial-intelligence/chinese-visa-impact-hits-indian-physical-ai-startups/articleshow/133387338.cms?from=mdr) are impacted by this, it said.

Relations between the neighbors deteriorated after the Galwan valley clash in early 2020, and the subsequent military standoff and tensions on the border. New Delhi, at the time, banned a large number of Chinese apps operating in India, while also tightening investment rules for that country’s firms under Press Note 3 regulation, which, even now, mandates multi-ministry clearances for any Chinese foreign direct investment.
