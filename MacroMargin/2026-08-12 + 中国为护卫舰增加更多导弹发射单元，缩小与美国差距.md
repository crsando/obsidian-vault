### 中文译文

## 中国为护卫舰增加更多导弹发射单元，缩小与美国差距

卫星图像显示，中国似乎正在为其最先进的护卫舰型号增加储存和发射导弹的能力，这个全球最大海军继续在火力方面缩小与美国的差距。

6月下旬的图像显示，在上海[沪东中华造船](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/HUDOGZ%20CH%20Equity)船厂建造的最新一艘、也是第三艘054B型护卫舰，其垂直发射系统拥有6个导弹发射单元模块，而前两艘054B型舰艇只有4个模块。

每个模块由6或8个导弹发射管组成，这意味着新舰最多可拥有48个发射管，用于发射导弹。

![The Type 054B frigate at the Hudong-Zhonghua Shipbuilding shipyard in Shanghai. Source: Satellite image ©2026 Vantor](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3ba81cc90b1481399c7cfd3d99fd388c/3ba81cc9-0b14-812d-be97-fc4f540d11ab-91df9a22710a1332e4b50cc132685949.png)

上海沪东中华造船厂内的054B型护卫舰。来源：卫星图像©2026 Vantor

尽管中国海军的舰艇数量多于美国，但在舰艇性能上仍落后于其主要对手。国际战略研究所2024年末[估计](https://www.iiss.org/online-analysis/military-balance/2024/12/closing-the-gap-china-homes-in-on-us-navy-vls-advantage/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)，美国水面舰艇共有约8,400个垂直发射系统单元，约为中国水面舰艇单元数量的两倍。

054B型是中国人民解放军海军迄今最先进的护卫舰。它比054A型更大、更隐身，054A型是中国数量最多的护卫舰。

随着中国与一系列国家在南海和台湾海峡的紧张局势持续，北京一直在加强海军能力。周二，中国表示，中国人民解放军海军将与印度尼西亚在台湾以东海域举行联合演习，这是其在该地区宣示主张的最新尝试。

中国似乎也在加快潜艇建造，商业卫星图像显示，今年江南造船厂和葫芦岛造船厂已下水两种新型潜艇。

## China Adds More Missile Cells to Frigate, Narrowing Gap With US

China appears to be adding more capacity to store and fire missiles on its most advanced frigate model, satellite imagery shows, as the world’s largest navy continues to close the gap with the US in firepower.

The latest, third Type-054B frigate under construction at the shipyard of [Hudong-Zhonghua Shipbuilding](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/HUDOGZ%20CH%20Equity) in Shanghai has six modules of missile cells in its vertical launching system, up from four in the previous two Type-054B vessels, the imagery from late June shows.

Each module consists of six or eight missile tubes, suggesting the new ship has as many as 48 tubes from which it can fire missiles.

The Type 054B frigate at the Hudong-Zhonghua Shipbuilding shipyard in Shanghai. Source: Satellite image ©2026 Vantor

While China has more naval vessels that the US, it continues to lag behind its primary rival in the capabilities of its ships. US surface ships had around 8,400 VLS cells in total, around double the number of cells on Chinese surface ships, the International Institute for Strategic Studies [estimated](https://www.iiss.org/online-analysis/military-balance/2024/12/closing-the-gap-china-homes-in-on-us-navy-vls-advantage/?utm_source=chatgpt.com) in late 2024.

The Type-054B is the most advanced-yet frigate of the People’s Liberation Army Navy. It’s bigger and stealthier than the Type-054A, China’s most numerous frigate.

Beijing has been ramping up its naval capabilities as tensions persist with a range of nations over the South China Sea and the Taiwan Strait. On Tuesday, China said the PLAN would conduct a joint drill with Indonesia in the waters east of Taiwan, in the latest attempt to press claims in the area.

China also appears to be speeding up submarine construction, with commercial satellite imagery indicating the launch of two new types this year in the Jiangnan and Huludao shipyards.
