Persian Gulf slicks grow 波斯湾浮油面积扩大

By [Laura Millan](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/ASzdyi2b6bE/laura-millan) 作者：劳拉·米兰

Ecologists are concerned that the war in the Middle East risks creating an environmental disaster, as satellite images show that the number of oil slicks in the Persian Gulf has [ increased significantly](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-21/satellite-images-show-persian-gulf-oil-slicks-growing-during-war) since the beginning of the conflict in the region in late February.
生态学家担忧中东战争可能引发环境灾难，卫星图像显示，自 2 月底该地区冲突爆发以来，波斯湾的浮油数量显著增加。

“If it disperses towards a coastal system, that’s going to have detrimental impacts to any bird species, fishing resources and communities that are dependent on them,” said Liz Atwood, a senior scientist at the Plymouth Marine Laboratory, a UK-based research center.
“如果油污扩散至沿海生态系统，将对依赖这些资源的鸟类、渔业资源及社区造成毁灭性影响，”英国普利茅斯海洋实验室高级科学家莉兹·阿特伍德表示。

Experts said the slicks could be the result of attacks on ships or on oil and gas infrastructure, or caused by technical problems during loading and unloading operations of fuel.
专家指出，这些浮油可能是船舶或油气基础设施遭袭所致，也可能是燃料装卸作业中的技术故障引发。

The Strait of Hormuz, the narrow passage that links the Persian Gulf to the Arabian Sea, has been effectively shut for most of the past three months, leaving hundreds of vessels trapped and unable to transit into the open ocean. Several ships have been struck with projectiles and fragments from intercepted drones and missiles since the conflict began. Onshore energy facilities have also been attacked across the region, increasing the risk of spills.
连接波斯湾与阿拉伯海的狭窄通道霍尔木兹海峡，在过去三个月的大部分时间里实际上已处于关闭状态，导致数百艘船只被困，无法驶入开阔海域。冲突爆发以来，多艘船只遭射弹及拦截无人机与导弹的碎片击中。该地区多处陆上能源设施也遭到袭击，进一步增加了漏油风险。

“When there are attacks on oil tankers or infrastructure on land, like refineries, the possibility that there is a spill is much higher,” said Juan Peña, chief executive officer at Orbital, a Spain-based company specializing in detecting oil spills through satellite images including from the European Union’s Earth observation agency Copernicus.
"当油轮或陆上基础设施（如炼油厂）遭到袭击时，发生泄漏的可能性要高得多，"总部位于西班牙的 Orbital 公司首席执行官胡安·佩尼亚表示。该公司专门通过卫星图像（包括来自欧盟地球观测机构哥白尼的图像）检测石油泄漏。

While physically verifying incidents in the region remains challenging due to the ongoing conflict, non-profits and private companies have been using satellites to track events.
由于持续冲突，在该地区实地核实事件仍具挑战性，非营利组织和私营公司一直利用卫星追踪事态发展。

The spills that have been observed so far go from a few dozen barrels of oil to larger incidents that would typically lead to cleanup operations anywhere else in the world. The largest spills detected so far originated in Iran’s Kharg Island, Iran’s main oil export facility — one on May 6, which Orbital estimated had a volume of between 300 and 3,000 barrels of oil, and another on May 16, with a volume of 200-2,000 barrels.
迄今观测到的泄漏规模从几十桶石油到更大规模的事故不等——这类事故若发生在世界其他地区，通常会引发清理行动。目前检测到的最严重泄漏源自伊朗主要石油出口设施哈尔克岛：5 月 6 日发生的一次泄漏，Orbital 估计泄漏量在 300 至 3000 桶之间；另一次发生在 5 月 16 日，泄漏量为 200 至 2000 桶。

A crew man secured oil drums on the deck of an oil tanker as it transported crude oil from Kharg Island to India in 2018. Photographer: Ali Mohammadi/Bloomberg
2018 年，一名船员正在固定油轮甲板上的油桶，该油轮正将原油从哈尔克岛运往印度。摄影师：阿里·穆罕默迪/彭博社

Orbital’s ranges are wide because it’s impossible to know the exact amount of oil released in the Persian Gulf just with satellite images, Peña said.
Peña 表示，Orbital 的监测范围很广，因为仅凭卫星图像无法确定波斯湾中泄漏的石油确切数量。

The size of the spills are not enormous compared to major disasters, but even so “it is a very large amount of oil,” Peña said. “It’s in Iranian waters and we don’t know if there are any operations on the way to clean it up — there is no evidence that there has been one.”
Peña 称，虽然与重大灾难相比，这些泄漏的规模并不算大，但即便如此，“泄漏的石油量仍然非常巨大。它位于伊朗水域，我们不知道是否有任何清理作业正在进行——目前没有证据表明有过此类行动。”

During the first Gulf war in 1991, 11 million barrels of oil were spilled into the sea, according to a [2018 research paper](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/325427939_Oil_Spills_in_the_Arabian_Gulf_A_Case_Study_and_Environmental_Review) published in the journal Environment and Natural Resources Research. The resulting oil slick extended across the Kuwaiti and northern Saudi coastlines, polluting soil and groundwater in both countries. Up to 10,000 tons of fish were found to be unsuitable for consumption and 30,000 birds died, according to the paper.
根据 2018 年发表在《环境与自然资源研究》期刊上的一篇研究论文，1991 年第一次海湾战争期间，有 1100 万桶石油泄漏入海。由此形成的浮油延伸至科威特和沙特北部海岸线，污染了两国的土壤和地下水。该论文指出，多达 1 万吨鱼类被发现不宜食用，3 万只鸟类死亡。

Marine ecosystems in the Gulf are being studied by ecologists because the corals there are able to withstand high sea temperatures, making them key to understanding the impacts of global warming in other parts of the world.
生态学家正在研究波斯湾的海洋生态系统，因为那里的珊瑚能够耐受高海水温度，这使得它们成为理解全球变暖对世界其他地区影响的关键。

“This is where the rest of the planet, or parts of it, are heading towards,” she said. “It’s an area that we should be protecting because it potentially holds the keys for how we’re going to deal with very hot, heatwave-like conditions within the next hundred years.”
“这是地球其他地区，或其中部分地区，正在走向的未来，”她说道。“这是一个我们应该保护的区域，因为它可能掌握着未来一百年内我们如何应对极端高温、热浪般气候条件的关键。”

Activist investors press on data centers
激进投资者向数据中心施压

By [Olivia Raimonde](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AUdOpjWq8Dk/olivia-raimonde) 作者：奥利维亚·雷蒙德

Activist investors are [ pushing technology companies](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-21/shareholders-press-big-tech-over-ai-energy-use-and-climate-goals) to explain how they’re reconciling surging electricity demand for AI with their climate commitments. Shareholders at Amazon.com Inc. voted on a proposal asking the firm to disclose more information. Voting is open for shareholders at Meta Platforms Inc. and Alphabet Inc. and will conclude later this month and in early June at each company’s annual meeting.
维权投资者正推动科技公司解释，如何协调人工智能激增的电力需求与自身气候承诺。亚马逊公司股东已就一项要求该公司披露更多信息的提案进行投票。Meta Platforms 公司和 Alphabet 公司的股东投票仍在进行中，将分别于本月下旬和六月初的年度股东大会结束。

Support for such initiatives has declined in recent years amid underwhelming outcomes and a broader political backlash in the US against environmental, social and governance investing. Heavyweight investment firms, including BlackRock Inc., Vanguard Group Inc. and State Street Corp., have also pulled back from these types of measures.
近年来，由于成效不佳以及美国国内对环境、社会和治理投资的政治反弹加剧，对此类倡议的支持有所下降。包括贝莱德、先锋领航集团和道富集团在内的重量级投资机构，也已从这类措施中抽身。

The new effort shows that green shareholder activism, while quieter than in its heyday in the early 2020s, persists. The proposals were filed by the nonprofit As You Sow, along with Presbyterian Life & Witness, Mercy Investment Services and Trillium Asset Management.
这一新动向表明，尽管绿色股东维权行动已不如 2020 年代初鼎盛时期那般声势浩大，但仍在持续。这些提案由非营利组织"As You Sow"与长老会生活与见证事工、慈悲投资服务公司及 Trillium 资产管理公司共同提交。

Meanwhile Canadian shareholder activist group Investors for Paris Compliance, also known as I4PC, is [ shutting down](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-20/bank-activist-group-shuts-down-citing-limits-of-climate-push) after concluding that investor pressure alone isn’t enough to meaningfully influence financial and energy firms to address climate risks.
与此同时，加拿大股东维权组织"投资者促进巴黎合规"（简称 I4PC）宣布解散，该组织认为仅靠投资者压力不足以有效影响金融和能源企业应对气候风险。

The group was created five years ago with a plan to review corporate environmental filings and use shareholder proposals to call on banks, insurers and oil and gas companies to provide more detailed disclosure on carbon emissions and outline credible net-zero transition plans.
该组织成立于五年前，计划通过审查企业环境文件并利用股东提案，要求银行、保险公司及油气公司更详细披露碳排放信息，并制定可信的净零转型计划。

This week, the group said it will [ cease operations](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-20/bank-activist-group-shuts-down-citing-limits-of-climate-push), noting that it believed “investor accountability has reached its limits.”
本周，该组织表示将停止运营，并指出其认为“投资者问责已触及极限”。

Superpollutant rules 超级污染物法规

By [Zahra Hirji](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AVypnhGtqak/zahra-hirji) and [Jennifer A Dlouhy](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/ASlQFjfALa0/jennifer-a-dlouhy)
作者：Zahra Hirji 和 Jennifer A Dlouhy

The Trump administration on Thursday intends to [ delay compliance](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-21/trump-to-delay-superpollutant-restrictions-in-affordability-push) with two rules on refrigerants as it looks to dismantle former President Joe Biden’s environmental agenda.
特朗普政府周四计划推迟两项制冷剂规定的合规期限，此举旨在逐步废除前总统乔·拜登的环境议程。

The moves, described by a White House official who requested anonymity to discuss the plan before it was made public, would affect grocers, semiconductor manufacturers and other companies that use the potent greenhouse gases known as hydroflurocarbons or HFCs. The requirement was imposed in a 2023 rule.
据一位要求匿名讨论尚未公开计划的白宫官员透露，这些举措将影响杂货商、半导体制造商及其他使用强效温室气体氢氟碳化物（HFCs）的企业。该要求源自 2023 年的一项法规。

In addition, President Donald Trump’s Environmental Protection Agency will also propose rolling back other restrictions on the superpollutants as part of separate rule affecting transporters of refrigerated goods. USA Today first reported the planned changes.
此外，总统唐纳德·特朗普领导的环境保护局还将作为另一项涉及冷藏货物运输商的规定的一部分，提议撤销对这类超级污染物的其他限制。《今日美国》率先报道了这些计划中的调整。

Trump is set to announce the decision on Thursday at a White House event alongside EPA Administrator Lee Zeldin.
特朗普将于周四在白宫的一场活动中，与环保局局长李·泽尔丁共同宣布这一决定。
