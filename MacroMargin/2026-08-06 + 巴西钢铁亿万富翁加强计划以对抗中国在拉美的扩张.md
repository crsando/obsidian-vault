### 中文译文

## 巴西钢铁亿万富翁加强计划以对抗中国在拉美的扩张

巴西钢铁王朝正加大力度应对中国进军拉丁美洲的势头，这是亚洲巨头如何在全球向竞争对手施压的一个案例研究。

最新的战场是秘鲁。总部位于巴西的 [Gerdau SA](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/GGB%20US%20Equity) 在那里拥有约三分之一的炼钢产能。该公司敦促秘鲁新任保守派政府阻止一家中国竞争对手建设钢厂，称该钢厂缺乏必要许可证，且能够以低于生产成本的价格从中国采购原材料，从而进行不公平竞争。

中国企业 Acero Lima Shenglong SAC 已告知秘鲁当局，希望建设一座年产能 70 万吨的钢厂，相当于 Gerdau 在当地的总产能，但尚未申请必要的许可证。官员们已证实，位于首都利马以南的 Shenglong 工地正在施工，但因无法确定这些工程是否为计划中的钢厂，而拒绝采取执法行动。

![Workers at a Gerdau SA steel plant in Sao Jose dos Campos, Sao Paulo.](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3b481cc90b148102b4c9ff837c7e8999/3b481cc9-0b14-81ec-bfb0-efe5104f43c6-0b6b40633a9ab952238e20ec2f037113.png)

位于圣保罗州圣若泽多斯坎普斯的 Gerdau SA 钢厂工人。

Shenglong 在给彭博社的一封电子邮件中表示，它“目前”并未在秘鲁建设任何项目，并拒绝进一步置评。

这场对峙将 Gerdau——拉丁美洲最大的钢铁制造商，在巴西、秘鲁、阿根廷、乌拉圭、墨西哥和美国都设有钢厂——与中国这个世界最大的钢铁生产国和出口国对立起来。

这也给总统藤森庆子的政府带来了早期挑战。她一直向华盛顿示好，并刚刚任命一位在美国驻利马大使馆工作15年的资深外交官为新任外交部长。然而，中国作为秘鲁最大贸易伙伴的影响力将难以忽视。

为了向秘鲁当局施压以叫停 Shenglong 的项目，Gerdau 现已搁置了 4500 万美元的计划投资。

“我们呼吁的是立即停止这个项目，”Gerdau公司秘鲁业务负责人Aldo Tapia在接受采访时表示。Tapia补充道，Gerdau在当地的投资处于“搁置”状态，因为“我们对秘鲁未来的法律和监管框架非常担忧”。

![Aldo Tapia, head of Gerdau’s Peru unit, during an interview at the company’s headquarters in Lima. Photographer: Marco Garro/Bloomberg](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3b481cc90b148102b4c9ff837c7e8999/3b481cc9-0b14-8159-b920-f670066a0b2c-20f17277033a8720f26e4da1317bfe13.png)

Gerdau秘鲁分公司负责人Aldo Tapia在利马公司总部接受采访时留影。摄影师：Marco Garro/Bloomberg

Gerdau巴西公司拒绝对此事发表评论，也未安排其董事长接受采访。

### **精明的家族**

Gerdau由亿万富翁Gerdau家族控制，这是一个在巴西具有政治影响力的家族。他们的财富高度集中于Gerdau公司，自21世纪初中国钢铁出口激增以来，他们所持股份的价值有所下降，尽管其控股公司的普通股今年已上涨。

在本土市场，总部位于阿雷格里港和圣保罗的Gerdau公司成功游说对中国钢铁征收关税。与此同时，它也承受了美国对钢铁进口征收关税的后果，放弃了以向北美出口为前提的墨西哥投资计划。

Tapia表示，秘鲁的情况有所不同，因为建设钢厂已超越中国传统的建筑钢材出口模式。如果计划中的钢厂从中国采购更便宜的原材料，最终可能违反公平贸易惯例。

Tapia表示：“问题在于中国以倾销价格进行不公平贸易行为。”他补充说，北京对出口产品的成本进行补贴，“低至我们无法竞争的水平”。

![Rolls of steel at a stockyard in Wuxi, Jiangsu province, China. Source: Bloomberg](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3b481cc90b148102b4c9ff837c7e8999/3b481cc9-0b14-81c7-a754-e3bf2e3b3105-ffced6cf1f4a3d529a999fedc026617a.png)

中国江苏省无锡市堆场的钢卷。来源：彭博社

尽管秘鲁尚未对中国钢铁征收关税，但其竞争监管机构Indecopi在7月[得出结论](https://www.gob.pe/institucion/indecopi/noticias/1419433-el-indecopi-impone-derechos-antidumping-definitivos-a-las-importaciones-de-tubos-de-acero-lac-originarios-de-china)称，部分中国钢铁产品以“人为低价”销售，损害了当地产业。

与许多拉丁美洲国家一样，秘鲁历来与华盛顿和北京都保持友好关系。特朗普政府加大了对其地区盟友的压力，要求它们选边站队。

中国在秘鲁经济中扮演着重要角色，不仅仅是通过购买其大部分铜。2024年，中国国家主席习近平为耗资13亿美元的钱凯港揭幕，该港口是南美洲最大的港口之一。

### **许可疑虑**

秘鲁环境许可机构Senace在给彭博社的一份声明中表示，盛龙已提交初步文件，表明其打算在利马以南约50英里的奇尔卡一处地点建造一座钢厂。

Senace表示，盛龙尚未提交环境影响研究，该研究在任何施工开始前都需要获得批准。根据Senace的一份报告，检查人员于3月访问了盛龙的场地，并核实该公司指定用于钢厂的同一地点正在进行“重大建筑工程”。

Gerdau在拉丁美洲的广泛业务使其在多个领域面临中国竞争，其程度超过该地区其他主要钢铁生产商，如专注于巴西的[CSN](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CSNA3%20BZ%20Equity)和[Usiminas](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/USIM5%20BZ%20Equity)。

![图片](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3b481cc90b148102b4c9ff837c7e8999/3b481cc9-0b14-8171-b4bd-c9c2ce2c2dfe-f1d01cccb035575f9991f8fe7b171f42.png)

据世界钢铁协会数据，中国的钢铁产量超过其九大竞争对手的总和。全球产量远超需求，中国将大量国内不需要的钢铁用于出口。

“中国为了促进出口、抢占全球市场和全球供应链而推行了过剩产能，”代表拉丁美洲钢铁制造商（包括Gerdau）的组织Alacero执行董事Ezequiel Tavernelli表示。

根据Alacero的数据，中国对拉丁美洲的钢铁出口在过去15年中几乎翻了两番。

北京方面已承认其产能过剩，并承诺减少钢铁产量，但效果[参差不齐](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-05/china-targets-steel-and-refining-capacity-after-mixed-success)。

Gerdau董事长André Gerdau Johannpeter——公司创始人的玄孙——去年曾表示，由于中国的竞争，巴西钢铁行业已经处于“[极限](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-08-26/brazil-steel-industry-is-facing-its-limit-gerdau-chair-warns)”。巴西是该地区最大的钢铁消费国，也是Gerdau最大的市场。

### **马与钢**

Gerdau由巴西南部德国移民João Gerdau于1901年创立。该公司在20世纪末期在其前首席执行官Jorge Gerdau的领导下迅速扩张，他在21世纪初领导了国际扩张，包括收购了当时为国有的[Siderúrgica del Peru SAA](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/EC183786%40BGN%20%20Corp)。

尽管据彭博计算，Gerdau家族仅持有这家钢铁制造商10%的股份，但该公司实行双重股权结构，使该家族能够保留控制权。

Jorge Gerdau 一直是马术爱好者，他的儿子 André 是巴西两届奥运会场地障碍赛铜牌得主。

2006年，Jorge Gerdau 出任董事长，公司任命 André 为首席执行官。2011年，Jorge 还成为左翼时任总统 Dilma Rousseff 的亲密顾问，并赢得了精明商人的声誉。当时，中国钢铁刚刚开始对拉丁美洲钢铁制造商构成挑战。

早在2004年，Gerdau 就表示中国的需求帮助公司实现了“创纪录的利润”。该公司甚至曾考虑在中国设立自己的钢厂，以服务这一蓬勃发展的市场。该项目从未实现，到2014年，André Gerdau 警告称，中国对巴西、阿根廷和墨西哥的钢铁出口增长了一倍多。“这些数字令人震惊，”他当时说。

## Brazil Steel Billionaires Ramp Up Plans to Fight China’s Latin America Push

Brazil’s steel dynasty is stepping up efforts to combat China’s drive into Latin America, a case study in how the Asian juggernaut is pressuring competitors worldwide.

The latest battlefront is Peru, where Brazil-based [Gerdau SA](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/GGB%20US%20Equity) holds about a third of steel-making capacity. The company is urging Peru’s new conservative government to stop a Chinese rival from building a steel mill, which it says lacks the necessary permits and would be positioned to engage in unfair competition by sourcing raw material from China at below production costs.

The Chinese entity, Acero Lima Shenglong SAC, has told Peruvian authorities it would like to build a mill capable of producing 700,000 tons of steel annually, equivalent to Gerdau’s local capacity, but has yet to apply for the necessary permits. Officials have verified that construction is underway at Shenglong’s site south of the capital Lima, but have declined to pursue an enforcement case after failing to determine whether the works were a planned steel mill.

Workers at a Gerdau SA steel plant in Sao Jose dos Campos, Sao Paulo.

Shenglong said in an email to Bloomberg that it was not “currently” building anything in Peru, declining further comment.

The face-off pits Gerdau — Latin America’s largest steelmaker with mills in Brazil, Peru, Argentina, Uruguay, Mexico and the US — against China, the world’s biggest steel producer and exporter.

It also poses an early challenge for the administration of President Keiko Fujimori, who has courted Washington and just appointed a 15-year veteran of the US embassy in Lima as her new foreign minister. Yet China’s influence as Peru’s top trading partner will prove hard to ignore.

In a bid to pressure Peruvian authorities to halt Shenglong’s project, Gerdau has now put $45 million in planned investment on ice.

“What we are calling for is the immediate halt of this project,” Aldo Tapia, who heads Gerdau’s Peru operations, said in an interview. Gerdau’s local investment is on “standby” because “we’re very worried about the Peruvian legal and regulatory framework in the future,” Tapia added.

Aldo Tapia, head of Gerdau’s Peru unit, during an interview at the company’s headquarters in Lima. Photographer: Marco Garro/Bloomberg

Gerdau Brazil declined to comment for this story or make its chairman available for an interview.

### **Savvy Clan**

Gerdau is controlled by the billionaire Gerdau family, a politically influential clan in Brazil. Their wealth is highly concentrated in Gerdau and the value of their stake has fallen since Chinese steel exports skyrocketed in the early 2000s, although common shares of their holding company have risen this year.

On its home turf, Porto Alegre and Sao Paulo-based Gerdau successfully lobbied for tariffs against Chinese steel. At the same time, it has faced the consequences of US tariffs on steel imports, scrapping Mexico investment plans premised on exporting north.

Tapia says the Peru case is distinct because building a mill goes beyond China’s traditional practice of exporting the construction metal. If the planned mill were to source cheaper raw materials from China, it could end up breaching fair-trade practices.

“The problem is that China engages in unfair trade practices with dumping prices,” Tapia said, adding that Beijing subsidizes costs for export products that arrive “at a level where we can’t compete.”

Rolls of steel at a stockyard in Wuxi, Jiangsu province, China. Source: Bloomberg

Although Peru has not imposed tariffs on Chinese steel, its competition regulator Indecopi [concluded](https://www.gob.pe/institucion/indecopi/noticias/1419433-el-indecopi-impone-derechos-antidumping-definitivos-a-las-importaciones-de-tubos-de-acero-lac-originarios-de-china) in July that some Chinese steel products had been sold at “artificially low prices,” harming local industry.

Like many Latin American countries, Peru has historically maintained friendly ties with both Washington and Beijing. The Trump administration has dialed up pressure on its local allies to pick sides.

China plays a prominent role in Peru’s economy, and not just by purchasing most of its copper. In 2024, Chinese President Xi Jinping inaugurated the $1.3 billion Chancay port, one of the biggest in South America.

### **Permitting Doubts**

Peru’s environmental licensing agency, Senace, said in a statement to Bloomberg that Shenglong had submitted preliminary paperwork indicating its intention to build a steel mill on a site in Chilca, some 50 miles south of Lima.

Senace said Shenglong has yet to file an environmental impact study, which would require approval before any construction could begin. Inspectors visited Shenglong’s site in March and verified that “major construction works” were being carried out in the same site that the company had designated for the steel mill**,** according to a Senace report.

Gerdau’s broad footprint in Latin America exposes it to Chinese competition on multiple fronts, more so than other top steelmakers in the region such as [CSN](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CSNA3%20BZ%20Equity) and [Usiminas](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/USIM5%20BZ%20Equity) that are Brazil-focused.

China produces more steel than its nine closest competitors combined, according to the World Steel Association. Global production far exceeds demand, with China exporting much of the steel it doesn’t need at home.

“There is excess capacity that has been promoted by China to generate exports and capture global markets and global supply chains,” said Ezequiel Tavernelli, executive director of Alacero, a group that represents Latin American steelmakers, including Gerdau.

China’s steel exports to Latin America have almost quadrupled over the past 15 years, according to Alacero data.

Beijing has acknowledged its overcapacity and pledged to reduce its steel production, with [mixed results](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-05/china-targets-steel-and-refining-capacity-after-mixed-success).

Gerdau’s chairman André Gerdau Johannpeter, a great-great-grandson of the company’s founder, said last year that Brazil’s steel industry is already at “[its limit](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-08-26/brazil-steel-industry-is-facing-its-limit-gerdau-chair-warns)” because of Chinese competition. Brazil is the region’s top steel consumer and Gerdau’s biggest market.

### **Horses and Steel**

Gerdau was founded in 1901 by João Gerdau, a German immigrant in southern Brazil. The company expanded rapidly in the late 20th century under its former chief executive officer, Jorge Gerdau, who led an international expansion through the early 2000s, including the purchase of [Siderúrgica del Peru SAA](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/EC183786%40BGN%20%20Corp), which at the time was state-owned.

Although the Gerdau family only owns 10% of the steelmaker, according to Bloomberg calculations, it operates a dual-class of shares that allows it to retain control.

Jorge Gerdau is a longtime horse enthusiast and his son André is a two-time bronze Olympic medalist for Brazil in show jumping.

In 2006, Jorge Gerdau became chairman and the company appointed André as CEO. In 2011, Jorge also became a close adviser to leftist then-president Dilma Rousseff and earned a reputation as a politically savvy businessman. At the time, Chinese steel was just emerging as a challenge for Latin American steelmakers.

Back in 2004, Gerdau said Chinese demand had helped the company achieve “record profits.” The firm had even considered installing its own Chinese mill to serve the booming market. The project never materialized, and by 2014 André Gerdau was warning that Chinese steel exports to Brazil, Argentina and Mexico had more than doubled. “These are staggering figures,” he said at the time.
