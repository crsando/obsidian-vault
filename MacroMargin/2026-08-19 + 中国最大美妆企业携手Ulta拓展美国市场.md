## 中国最大美妆企业携手Ulta拓展美国市场

作者：彭博新闻
2026年8月19日 下午4:50 GMT+8

[珀莱雅化妆品股份有限公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/603605%20CH%20Equity) 将通过与美国Ulta Beauty公司的合作拓展美国市场。作为中国最大的美妆企业，该公司正[寻求海外增长](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2025-12-04/billionaire-founder-says-china-s-proya-cosmetics-wants-to-be-a-global-brand)，以帮助其应对国内激烈的竞争。

珀莱雅将推出其旗舰产品“红宝石”（Ruby）和“原力修复”（Original Repair）护肤系列，在400家Ulta实体店及其线上平台销售。这家中国公司在其微信账号发布的一份[声明](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MzA3NjE2NjkxMA%3D%3D&mid=2650866013&idx=1&sn=be34503c45bcdba596f155ab641ed5bd&chksm=851a69856f17c423c1bc039afa89ec9ee8cfc7d0bd5290824c7b43b6aa3bcc765ce837f12089&mpshare=1&scene=1&srcid=0819xjir8gQbqynXuQUhLT2d&sharer_shareinfo=de73dd66550641d2b4e86a2877b23419&sharer_shareinfo_first=de73dd66550641d2b4e86a2877b23419&exportkey=n_ChQIAhIQMY9WGAXJHvjkPTAL5H0sAxL3AQIE97dBBAEAAAAAAO2MDCWpRBEAAAAOpnltbLcz9gKNyK89dVj0gVJaDC1LLU5bWcn9HU6WW8YHyV9DpSX6VC2nR5%2FJWFIWWoSiTknVpDxSor0ZQhmSZVL%2Bf47hEMeFUhiKWOCwttNvm8t5QlP%2FoZfeq1l9PhEKVmcqrzs1eLe%2FcXJRoUetkM11veFfNnlo1t1rW38336GGQB3m8UQ9MklzaMkTXU7Vmdq3KRfqWiI1EuYboaY2QBhawHdAROuNLt6G%2FyjW9Lr%2Bl9KEGCH4j15z4rfH07kK5QrWP%2F2yFOBx3qF%2Fza7oNLpyxpG%2FlGffhF11BORsPgc%3D&acctmode=0&pass_ticket=TIduTHIDFyQDk7BtNwKGObcsJeRd2us2e3YHsLyx6X51NKOvPj4EtlMWty%2F1Hgik&wx_header=0#rd)中表示，此次合作是珀莱雅在全球扩张中的重大突破。

此举标志着中国护肤品牌在西方市场实现主流零售分销的罕见案例。虽然包括花西子（Florasis）和花知晓（Flower Knows）在内的一批C-beauty品牌通过跨境电商以及对亚洲美妆趋势日益浓厚的兴趣获得了发展势头，但护肤品领域仍由[欧莱雅集团](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/OR%20FP%20Equity)和[雅诗兰黛集团](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/EL%20US%20Equity)等主导。

此次合作也为珀莱雅提供了重要的增长渠道，因为国内市场需求趋于平稳，消费者支出谨慎且价格战激烈。该公司年营收在经历了多年的两位数增长后，2025年下滑了约1.7%。

在与Ulta合作之前，总部位于杭州的珀莱雅已于4月与马来西亚领先的美容及个人护理零售连锁店Guardian达成一项[协议](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MzA3NjE2NjkxMA%3D%3D&mid=2650864974&idx=1&sn=050ff472ba11e1a74a0e66739caaade2&chksm=85d800ff44b891a21ea83eafdd8ebd4f63413d74204bd7f9de9a13552e806dcd932fff6e8ea5&mpshare=1&scene=1&srcid=0819jD92c8p0EzNGVNNNUqku&sharer_shareinfo=9fa5a884ef9cd78696c02d695e4e7d4a&sharer_shareinfo_first=9fa5a884ef9cd78696c02d695e4e7d4a&exportkey=n_ChQIAhIQyiTJaeOZwDhYkU1%2FAzjsXRL3AQIE97dBBAEAAAAAAPQQKvxf550AAAAOpnltbLcz9gKNyK89dVj08nG2l9La3X%2B8XJNfHVCdjZp26WFlIdsJUeQw8DyJYV%2FOwQFWCQd7sTHL91k151bRyGXMH62e%2F5QyE90lk%2FR8o2%2FP84%2BMHczQNDHtk9ROQT7h7XYvVfdas7%2B83XnZvjYgW8fGEOkuALJ4ufn4aWIg2lkOer89owovzK6zlyp%2BdOxc5tSBWggGem0peit4HUJEOH%2FXYYs38F%2F6U%2B0TXN1vy3rKOF3%2BdrTycpDbghL1NJL4Rkitp5N%2BiXkl9GcJvt6eoUjkp9DAQer%2Fk%2B8r80qszm8%3D&acctmode=0&pass_ticket=CxKqHRHtHdREfCpoeddjJmJZP04b6bfIrutVGGermX2f2xBjPrSqKPXybshvVd%2Bc&wx_header=0#rd)，将在Guardian推出其明星产品。该协议将让产品逐渐进入这个东南亚国家超过200家实体店的销售网络。

## China’s Biggest Beauty Firm to Expand in US With Ulta Deal

By Bloomberg News
August 19, 2026 at 4:50 PM GMT+8

[Proya Cosmetics Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/603605%20CH%20Equity) is set to expand in the US market through a partnership with Ulta Beauty Inc. as China’s biggest beauty company [looks abroad](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2025-12-04/billionaire-founder-says-china-s-proya-cosmetics-wants-to-be-a-global-brand) for growth to help it weather fierce competition at home.

Proya is set to launch its flagship Ruby and Original Repair skincare lines in 400 brick-and-mortar Ulta stores, as well as on its online platform, the Chinese company said in a [statement](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MzA3NjE2NjkxMA%3D%3D&mid=2650866013&idx=1&sn=be34503c45bcdba596f155ab641ed5bd&chksm=851a69856f17c423c1bc039afa89ec9ee8cfc7d0bd5290824c7b43b6aa3bcc765ce837f12089&mpshare=1&scene=1&srcid=0819xjir8gQbqynXuQUhLT2d&sharer_shareinfo=de73dd66550641d2b4e86a2877b23419&sharer_shareinfo_first=de73dd66550641d2b4e86a2877b23419&exportkey=n_ChQIAhIQMY9WGAXJHvjkPTAL5H0sAxL3AQIE97dBBAEAAAAAAO2MDCWpRBEAAAAOpnltbLcz9gKNyK89dVj0gVJaDC1LLU5bWcn9HU6WW8YHyV9DpSX6VC2nR5%2FJWFIWWoSiTknVpDxSor0ZQhmSZVL%2Bf47hEMeFUhiKWOCwttNvm8t5QlP%2FoZfeq1l9PhEKVmcqrzs1eLe%2FcXJRoUetkM11veFfNnlo1t1rW38336GGQB3m8UQ9MklzaMkTXU7Vmdq3KRfqWiI1EuYboaY2QBhawHdAROuNLt6G%2FyjW9Lr%2Bl9KEGCH4j15z4rfH07kK5QrWP%2F2yFOBx3qF%2Fza7oNLpyxpG%2FlGffhF11BORsPgc%3D&acctmode=0&pass_ticket=TIduTHIDFyQDk7BtNwKGObcsJeRd2us2e3YHsLyx6X51NKOvPj4EtlMWty%2F1Hgik&wx_header=0#rd) on its WeChat account. The collaboration represents a significant breakthrough for Proya in its global expansion, it said.

The move marks a rare instance of a Chinese skincare brand achieving mainstream retail distribution in a Western market. While a wave of C-beauty brands, including Florasis and Flower Knows, have gained traction through cross-border e-commerce and growing interest in Asian beauty trends, skincare remains dominated by the likes of [L’Oréal SA](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/OR%20FP%20Equity) and [Estée Lauder Cos.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/EL%20US%20Equity)

The tie up also gives Proya an important avenue for growth as the domestic market plateaus due to cautious consumer spending and intense price wars. The company’s annual revenue declined about 1.7% in 2025 following years of double-digit growth.

Prior to the Ulta partnership, Hangzhou-based Proya had struck an [agreement](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MzA3NjE2NjkxMA%3D%3D&mid=2650864974&idx=1&sn=050ff472ba11e1a74a0e66739caaade2&chksm=85d800ff44b891a21ea83eafdd8ebd4f63413d74204bd7f9de9a13552e806dcd932fff6e8ea5&mpshare=1&scene=1&srcid=0819jD92c8p0EzNGVNNNUqku&sharer_shareinfo=9fa5a884ef9cd78696c02d695e4e7d4a&sharer_shareinfo_first=9fa5a884ef9cd78696c02d695e4e7d4a&exportkey=n_ChQIAhIQyiTJaeOZwDhYkU1%2FAzjsXRL3AQIE97dBBAEAAAAAAPQQKvxf550AAAAOpnltbLcz9gKNyK89dVj08nG2l9La3X%2B8XJNfHVCdjZp26WFlIdsJUeQw8DyJYV%2FOwQFWCQd7sTHL91k151bRyGXMH62e%2F5QyE90lk%2FR8o2%2FP84%2BMHczQNDHtk9ROQT7h7XYvVfdas7%2B83XnZvjYgW8fGEOkuALJ4ufn4aWIg2lkOer89owovzK6zlyp%2BdOxc5tSBWggGem0peit4HUJEOH%2FXYYs38F%2F6U%2B0TXN1vy3rKOF3%2BdrTycpDbghL1NJL4Rkitp5N%2BiXkl9GcJvt6eoUjkp9DAQer%2Fk%2B8r80qszm8%3D&acctmode=0&pass_ticket=CxKqHRHtHdREfCpoeddjJmJZP04b6bfIrutVGGermX2f2xBjPrSqKPXybshvVd%2Bc&wx_header=0#rd) in April to roll out its star products at Guardian, Malaysia’s leading beauty and personal care retail chain. The deal will see products gradually available across the network of more than 200 physical stores in the Southeast Asian country.
