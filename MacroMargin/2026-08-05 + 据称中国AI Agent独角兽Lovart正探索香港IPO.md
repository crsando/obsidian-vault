### 中文译文

## 据称中国AI Agent独角兽Lovart正探索香港IPO

彭博新闻社
2026年8月5日上午10:47 GMT+8

### **要点**（彭博AI）

- 据知情人士透露，Evoken正考虑在香港进行首次公开募股（IPO），以利用投资者对人工智能工具日益高涨的需求。
- 该公司正与顾问就潜在股票发售进行早期讨论，并即将完成一轮融资，此轮融资将这家初创公司估值定为30亿美元。
- 相关讨论尚处于初步阶段，尚未就IPO做出最终决定，Evoken发言人未回应置评请求。
据知情人士透露，中国初创公司Evoken（旗下拥有AI设计智能体[Lovart](https://www.lovart.ai/?srsltid=AfmBOoprOeCrd0H6DJbna8psdiB4QM5YkXIZjRDw_yM2M6ROQxMCzzLu)）正考虑在香港进行首次公开募股（IPO），这是在Manus取得突破性成功后，最新一家寻求利用投资者对人工智能工具高涨需求的此类公司。

上述人士称，公司正与顾问就潜在股票发售进行早期讨论，由于讨论属于私下进行，这些人士要求匿名。与此同时，上述人士称，Evoken即将完成一轮融资，该交易将这家初创公司估值定为30亿美元。

他们表示，相关讨论尚处于初步阶段，尚未就IPO做出最终决定。Evoken的发言人未回应置评请求。

Evoken在Manus崛起后成为知名AI应用开发商——Manus是推广通用AI智能体的初创公司，后被[Meta Platforms Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/META%20US%20Equity)以20亿美元收购，但随后被[中国监管机构叫停](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-29/china-s-meta-backlash-renders-manus-model-officially-dead)。与Manus类似，Lovart不自主开发基础模型，而是依赖第三方产品，如OpenAI的GPT和Alphabet Inc.旗下Google的Gemini。它为创意工作者和插画师打造易用工具，挑战Canva和Adobe Inc.的Creative Cloud等传统设计软件。

6月，Evoken表示其[融资](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MzY5NDMzNzExMA%3D%3D&amp%3Bmid=2247483672&amp%3Bidx=1&amp%3Bsn=f5f515dd907239badfce610cff216f5f&amp%3Bchksm=f50854a848fcb82f318928e7fdfe216104192df3893419b0b93c5f56aeb49e21afdf578edf97&amp%3Bmpshare=1&amp%3Bscene=1&amp%3Bsrcid=0618N3qbfbhk3KGpFWqUVKWD&amp%3Bsharer_shareinfo=12a68c1cc5bcbf8a821a590c868b047e&amp%3Bsharer_shareinfo_first=4826870eb869da874d4069dd3dcd4911&amp%3Bexportkey=n_ChQIAhIQcNqeRNO9njHgpyg2FDZNShL0AQIE97dBBAEAAAAAAGcoBhgz39UAAAAOpnltbLcz9gKNyK89dVj0%2BHc0eFQCI8IlNJTtS9pDs6xtwQde9R3B6JXf%2FgrdQ6wXg9nqI1X1cGqw2KD1S2zFtK8bdPshgZpnCsJhAU%2Beu6Uyz4dDtx4iA2ri4h0vpaXvMYxzH9UxpywuYkoxgK5FDSmxI8AAiofbhBEUKdxoyLZG6Vp5rMaL2w27UWfH6RJHtyknHCiuNmZsDicP0Lt15gBY1b5rIyMBX4DTTtMD92TCw4fTeKv6KOUGR1Y8q3yVtvygxPTRSDOtwCCiHXA7Mib5tyfYJ2Z4ik1Ar%2Bg%3D&amp%3Bacctmode=0&amp%3Bpass_ticket=d4Zx5M2e1tSpdUoDyMu6ajLE%2BN%2FnzzQAj0f2vydj9Ryy6UvyGj%2FhvKHto0Bb9k%2BI&amp%3Bwx_header=0#rd)近3亿美元，B+轮融资后估值超过20亿美元。投资者包括Granite Asia和顺为资本，以及蚂蚁集团和HSG等现有支持者。这家成立三年的公司还开发视频生成工具[LibTV](https://www.liblib.tv/)，表示截至5月其年化经常性收入已超过3亿美元。

Manus收购的流产凸显了中国科技企业家在竞相打造全球首发应用时面临的地缘政治钢丝。他们设想在AI时代取得TikTok级别的成功，押注卓越的用户体验和病毒式传播能够赢得消费者——即使由第三方美国模型驱动。

然而，Evoken走了一条与Manus不同的道路。它在北京和上海设有主要办公室，为国内和国际市场运营不同的产品。其AI内容分享平台LiblibAI和LibTV专为中国打造，而Xingliu则是Lovart的本地孪生版本。创始人[Melvin Chen](https://www.linkedin.com/in/melvin-chen-0ab458141/en)是字节跳动有限公司的产品老手，曾负责其热门视频编辑应用CapCut的全球变现。

## China’s AI Agent Unicorn Lovart Is Said to Explore Hong Kong IPO

By Bloomberg News
August 5, 2026 at 10:47 AM GMT+8

### **Takeaways **by Bloomberg AI

- Evoken is considering an initial public offering in Hong Kong, people familiar with the matter said, to tap booming investor demand for artificial intelligence tools.
- The company is in early discussions with advisers on a potential share sale and is close to raising funds in a deal that would value the startup at $3 billion.
- Deliberations are preliminary and no final decisions have been made on an IPO, with a spokesperson for Evoken not responding to a request seeking comment.
Evoken, the Chinese startup behind AI design agent [Lovart](https://www.lovart.ai/?srsltid=AfmBOoprOeCrd0H6DJbna8psdiB4QM5YkXIZjRDw_yM2M6ROQxMCzzLu), is considering an initial public offering in Hong Kong, people familiar with the matter said, the latest such firm seeking to tap booming investor demand for artificial intelligence tools after the breakout success of Manus.

The company is in early discussions with advisers on a potential share sale, the people said, asking not to be identified because the deliberations are private. Meanwhile, Evoken is close to raising funds in a deal that would value the startup at $3 billion, the people said.

Deliberations are preliminary and no final decisions have been made on an IPO, they said. A spokesperson for Evoken didn’t respond to a request seeking comment.

Evoken became a well-known AI app maker following the rise of Manus — the startup that popularized general-purpose AI agents and was acquired by [Meta Platforms Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/META%20US%20Equity) in a $2 billion deal later [unwound by Chinese regulators](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-29/china-s-meta-backlash-renders-manus-model-officially-dead). Like Manus, Lovart doesn’t develop its foundation models in-house, relying instead on third-party offerings such as OpenAI’s GPT and Alphabet Inc. Google’s Gemini. It builds user-friendly tools for creative workers and illustrators, challenging traditional design software like Canva and Adobe Inc.’s Creative Cloud.

In June, Evoken said it [raised](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MzY5NDMzNzExMA%3D%3D&amp%3Bmid=2247483672&amp%3Bidx=1&amp%3Bsn=f5f515dd907239badfce610cff216f5f&amp%3Bchksm=f50854a848fcb82f318928e7fdfe216104192df3893419b0b93c5f56aeb49e21afdf578edf97&amp%3Bmpshare=1&amp%3Bscene=1&amp%3Bsrcid=0618N3qbfbhk3KGpFWqUVKWD&amp%3Bsharer_shareinfo=12a68c1cc5bcbf8a821a590c868b047e&amp%3Bsharer_shareinfo_first=4826870eb869da874d4069dd3dcd4911&amp%3Bexportkey=n_ChQIAhIQcNqeRNO9njHgpyg2FDZNShL0AQIE97dBBAEAAAAAAGcoBhgz39UAAAAOpnltbLcz9gKNyK89dVj0%2BHc0eFQCI8IlNJTtS9pDs6xtwQde9R3B6JXf%2FgrdQ6wXg9nqI1X1cGqw2KD1S2zFtK8bdPshgZpnCsJhAU%2Beu6Uyz4dDtx4iA2ri4h0vpaXvMYxzH9UxpywuYkoxgK5FDSmxI8AAiofbhBEUKdxoyLZG6Vp5rMaL2w27UWfH6RJHtyknHCiuNmZsDicP0Lt15gBY1b5rIyMBX4DTTtMD92TCw4fTeKv6KOUGR1Y8q3yVtvygxPTRSDOtwCCiHXA7Mib5tyfYJ2Z4ik1Ar%2Bg%3D&amp%3Bacctmode=0&amp%3Bpass_ticket=d4Zx5M2e1tSpdUoDyMu6ajLE%2BN%2FnzzQAj0f2vydj9Ryy6UvyGj%2FhvKHto0Bb9k%2BI&amp%3Bwx_header=0#rd) nearly $300 million in a series B+ funding round that valued the startup at more than $2 billion post investment. Investors included Granite Asia and Shunwei Capital, as well as existing backers such as Ant Group and HSG. The three-year-old company, which also develops the video generation tool [LibTV](https://www.liblib.tv/), said it had surpassed $300 million in annualized recurring revenue as of May.

Manus’s aborted takeover underscored the geopolitical tightrope faced by Chinese tech entrepreneurs racing to build global-first applications. They envision TikTok-level success in the AI era, betting that superior user experience and viral distribution can win over consumers — even when powered by third-party American models.

Still, Evoken has taken a different path from Manus. With main offices in Beijing and Shanghai, it operates distinct products for domestic and international markets. Its AI content-sharing platform LiblibAI and LibTV are built specifically for China, while Xingliu serves as the local twin of Lovart. Founder [Melvin Chen](https://www.linkedin.com/in/melvin-chen-0ab458141/en) is a ByteDance Ltd. product veteran who previously ran global monetization for its hit video-editing app CapCut.
