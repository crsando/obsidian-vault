### 中文译文

## 唐纳德·特朗普推出针对中国无人机技术的关税

![The move also authorises the US commerce department to create an onshoring programme to help companies that are making new investments in drones or their components © Bruce Bennett/Getty Images](https://images.ft.com/v3/image/raw/ftcms%3A06f45a8d-49a6-4b6a-a9f4-ffd46abd05d7?source=next-article&fit=scale-down&quality=highest&width=1440&dpr=1)

此举还授权美国商务部创建一个“回流”计划，以帮助那些对无人机或其零部件进行新投资的公司。© Bruce Bennett/Getty Images

[****Demetri Sevastopulo****](https://www.ft.com/demetri-sevastopulo) 在华盛顿

昨天发布

唐纳德·特朗普总统已对无人机及其零部件征收大规模新关税，以减少美国企业、执法部门及州和地方政府对中国无人机技术的依赖。

白宫表示，将对被认定为对国家安全“特别敏感”的[无人机](https://www.ft.com/drones)征收100%关税，包括重量超过25公斤或具有热成像能力的系统。它将对最大起飞重量为25公斤的无人机征收25%的关税。

此举是特朗普政府打击中国无人机技术的最新努力。去年12月，美国联邦通信委员会[禁止进口中国无人机](https://www.ft.com/content/48f0cc68-dfae-48d7-8950-c8c72c2ebe93?syn-25a6b1a6=1)称将停止为来自敌对国家的无人机提供无线通信批准。

美中技术竞争专家克雷格·辛格尔顿表示，白宫周四的举措是一件“大事”，因为它将重塑商业市场。他说，美国商业和政府客户将不得不立即进行“资本密集型转向”，这将使他们转向美国或盟国的供应商，即使这些供应商更昂贵。

“它仍然主要针对中国，但旨在阻止供应链洗白，”来自捍卫民主基金会的辛格尔顿表示。

他表示，这些关税堵住了美国联邦通信委员会（FCC）行动留下的“后门”，该行动允许美国公司购买“数百万个中国零部件——如电机、旋翼和机架——并在美国组装”。

白宫周四还指责40多个国家通过将产品转运至其他国家，[帮助中国规避美国关税](https://www.ft.com/content/5d5ed2dd-8326-4cad-aac3-5816448ceccf?syn-25a6b1a6=1)。

来自美国盟友日本、列支敦士登、韩国、瑞士、台湾和欧盟的无人机将面临15%的较低关税，而英国的技术将被征收10%的关税。

特朗普表示，商务部的一项调查得出结论，无人机对美国国家和经济安全“至关重要”，并且是“现代武装冲突中的关键技术，对美国当前和未来的军事行动至关重要”。

随着[乌克兰在持续抵抗俄罗斯的努力中有效使用](https://www.ft.com/content/f6cf99e0-21f7-47c0-b5b1-64b77c18d204?syn-25a6b1a6=1)无人机，无人机在战场上的重要性急剧增加。

周四的命令还授权商务部制定一项“回流”计划，以帮助那些在无人机或其零部件领域进行新投资的公司。

特朗普的这一公告虽未点名中国但针对中国，发布时机正值预计中国国家主席习近平将访问华盛顿与特朗普举行峰会的前六周。

中国本月[加强了对美国无人机出口的管制](https://www.ft.com/content/38e11309-9cb6-4cd4-a5b6-fc9a239ad6b3?syn-25a6b1a6=1)，以报复华盛顿对中国科技的限制。北京表示，对美出口的无人机、关键零部件和相关技术将进行逐案审查。

尽管特朗普政府尚未实施拜登政府对中国实施的那种严格出口管制，但其政府，尤其是美国联邦通信委员会（FCC），一直在[悄悄采取国家安全行动](https://www.ft.com/content/48f0cc68-dfae-48d7-8950-c8c72c2ebe93?syn-25a6b1a6=1)以针对北京。

中国驻华盛顿大使馆未立即回应置评请求。

## Donald Trump launches tariffs targeting Chinese drone technology

The move also authorises the US commerce department to create an onshoring programme to help companies that are making new investments in drones or their components © Bruce Bennett/Getty Images

[**](https://www.ft.com/demetri-sevastopulo)[**Demetri Sevastopulo**](https://www.ft.com/demetri-sevastopulo)[**](https://www.ft.com/demetri-sevastopulo) in Washington

Published yesterday

President Donald Trump has imposed sweeping new tariffs on drones and their components in an effort to make US companies, law enforcement and state and local governments less reliant on Chinese drone technology.

The White House said it would levy 100 per cent tariffs on [drones](https://www.ft.com/drones) that were "particularly sensitive" for national security, including systems that weigh more than 25kg or have thermal imaging capabilities. It will put a 25 per cent tariff on drones that have a maximum take-off weight of 25kg.

The move is the latest effort by the Trump administration to crack down on Chinese drone technology. In December, the Federal Communications Commission [banned imports of Chinese drones](https://www.ft.com/content/48f0cc68-dfae-48d7-8950-c8c72c2ebe93?syn-25a6b1a6=1) by saying it would stop providing wireless communications approval for ones from adversary nations.

Craig Singleton, an expert on US-China technology competition, said Thursday’s move by the White House was a "big deal" because it would reshape the commercial marketplace. He said US commercial and government customers would have to make an immediate "capital-intensive pivot" that would involve them transitioning to suppliers in the US or allied countries even if they are more expensive.

"It still primarily targets China, but it’s designed to stop supply-chain laundering," said Singleton, of the Foundation for Defense of Democracies.

He said the tariffs close a "backdoor" left by the FCC action that allowed US companies to buy "millions of Chinese components — like motors, rotors and frames — and assembl[e] them here".

The White House on Thursday separately accused more than 40 countries of [helping China to evade US tariffs](https://www.ft.com/content/5d5ed2dd-8326-4cad-aac3-5816448ceccf?syn-25a6b1a6=1) by routing products through other countries.

Drones from US allies Japan, Liechtenstein, South Korea, Switzerland, Taiwan and the EU will face a lower tariff of 15 per cent, with a 10 per cent levy applied to technology from the UK.

Trump said an investigation by the commerce department concluded that drones were "essential" to US national and economic security and a "key technology in modern armed conflict and are critical for present and future US military operations".

The importance of drones on the battlefield has dramatically increased as [Ukraine has used](https://www.ft.com/content/f6cf99e0-21f7-47c0-b5b1-64b77c18d204?syn-25a6b1a6=1) unmanned aerial vehicles effectively in its ongoing efforts to repel Russia.

Thursday’s order also authorises the commerce department to create an onshoring programme to help companies that are making new investments in drones or their components.

Trump’s proclamation, which is aimed at China without mentioning the country by name, comes six weeks before President Xi Jinping is expected to visit Washington for a summit with Trump.

China [tightened controls](https://www.ft.com/content/38e11309-9cb6-4cd4-a5b6-fc9a239ad6b3?syn-25a6b1a6=1) this month on its own drone exports to the US in retaliation for Washington’s restrictions on Chinese tech. Beijing said exports to the US of drones, key components and related technologies would be subject to review on a "case-by-case basis".

While the Trump administration has held back from imposing the kind of tough export controls the Biden administration put on China, his administration, particularly the FCC, has been [quietly taking national security actions](https://www.ft.com/content/48f0cc68-dfae-48d7-8950-c8c72c2ebe93?syn-25a6b1a6=1) that are aimed at Beijing.

The Chinese embassy in Washington did not immediately respond to a request for comment.
