### 中文译文

## 中国铜冶炼商因出售高价硫酸而获益

中国两大铜生产商在2026年上半年利润增长逾一倍，因硫酸（冶炼过程的副产品）收入飙升，再加上金属价格上涨的影响。

[Jiangxi Copper Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/358%20HK%20Equity)和[Tongling Nonferrous Metals Group Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/000630%20CH%20Equity)的合并净利润攀升至116亿元人民币（合17.3亿美元），比去年同期高出107%。中东战争导致供应中断后，硫酸价格飙升，给许多冶炼商带来了意外之财。黄金价格高企也提振了收益，黄金是在冶炼过程中经常回收的一种金属。

铜陵在周四的财报中表示：“硫酸和贵金属等副产品的价格保持强劲，成为冶炼板块利润的主要来源。”

![图片](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3ca81cc90b1481e893d4ceac12bda904/3ca81cc9-0b14-811c-aec7-cbeade810257-a82e9bcaa86213418b30e50edebefad1.png)

全球铜冶炼商在加工费转为深度负值后承压，每吨精矿转化为金属的盈利能力都受到打击。但对江西铜业和铜陵有色而言，影响并不那么严重，因为它们拥有自己的矿山，并从利润丰厚的副产品销售中获得缓解。

6月份，中国[硫酸](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/GCFPSACH%20Index)价格飙升至2013年有数据以来的最高水平。尽管这种化学品在某些形式的铜生产中用作原料，但通常是中国占主导地位的冶炼方式的产出品。

花旗集团（Citigroup Inc.）在与该公司通话后的一份研报中写道，江西铜业管理层认为，其强劲业绩——尤其是公司第二季度——主要是受硫酸价格上涨以及收购[SolGold Plc](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/SOLG%20LN%20Equity)带来的投资收益推动。

在全球贸易混乱、采矿中断以及数据中心和可再生能源需求日益乐观的背景下，全球铜市场今年表现亮眼。伦敦金属交易所的价格交投于接近纪录高位，并有望连续第九周上涨，创2020年以来最长连涨。

定价机构Fastmarkets追踪的铜加工费上周五[跌至历史低点](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/MB041406%20Index)，报负219.1美元。加工费暴跌和铜冶炼利润结构的变化，已促使安托法加斯塔（Antofagasta）在与铜冶炼厂的交易中转向现货定价，从而摆脱传统的年度矿石销售谈判，转向更加依赖现货指数。

江西和铜陵的业绩印证了整个金属行业的广泛繁荣。

中国政府周四公布的数据显示，今年前七个月中国有色金属冶炼及加工企业利润跃升92%。中国铝业股份有限公司上半年净利润创历史新高，得益于价格上涨和成本下降。

## China’s Copper Smelters Get Boost From Selling Expensive Acid

China’s two biggest copper producers more than doubled profits in the first half of 2026, as soaring income from sulfuric acid — a by-product of the smelting process — added to the impact of higher metal prices.

Combined net income for [Jiangxi Copper Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/358%20HK%20Equity) and [Tongling Nonferrous Metals Group Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/000630%20CH%20Equity) climbed to 11.6 billion yuan ($1.73 billion), 107% higher than a year earlier. Sulfuric acid prices have rocketed after the war in the Middle East choked supplies, giving a windfall to many smelters. Elevated prices of gold, a metal often recovered during smelting, also lifted earnings.

“Prices for by-products such as sulfuric acid and precious metals stayed strong, becoming the main source of profit for the smelting segment,” Tongling said in its earnings report on Thursday.

Copper smelters worldwide have come under pressure after treatment charges turned deeply negative, hammering profitability on every ton of concentrate they convert to metal. But for Jiangxi and Tongling, the impact isn’t so sharp because they own their own mines and get relief from lucrative by-product sales.

Chinese prices of [sulfuric acid](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/GCFPSACH%20Index) surged in June to their highest ever in data going back to 2013. While the chemical is used in some forms of copper production as an input, it’s typically an output of the type of smelting that dominates China’s industry.

Jiangxi Copper’s management believes its strong results — particularly in the firm’s second quarter — were mainly driven by higher prices of sulfuric acid, as well as investment gains from the takeover of [SolGold Plc](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/SOLG%20LN%20Equity), Citigroup Inc. wrote in a note after a call with the company.

The global copper market is having a striking year amid trade dislocations, mining disruptions and growing optimism for demand from data centers and renewable energy. Prices on the London Metal Exchange are trading near record levels and heading for a ninth weekly gain, the longest run since 2020.

Copper treatment charges tracked by pricing agency Fastmarkets [fell to a record low](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/MB041406%20Index) of minus $219.1 last Friday. The plunging fees and shifting profit structure of copper smelting has Antofagasta Shifts to Spot Pricing in Deal With Copper Smelter to move away from the traditional annual negotiations for selling ore to greater reliance on spot indexes.

Jiangxi and Tongling’s results echo a broader boom across the metals industry.

Profits at China’s non-ferrous metals smelting and processing companies jumped 92% in the first seven months, government data showed Thursday. Aluminum Corp. of China Ltd. posted record net income for the first half on higher prices and lower costs.
