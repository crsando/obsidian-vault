### 中文译文

## DeepSeek推出测试模型，对标Anthropic的Opus 4.8

[DeepSeek](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/2544189D:CH) 推出了一款可理解视觉提示的实验性AI模型，并表示该工具的性能接近美国竞争对手 [Anthropic PBC](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1892140D:US) 的一款先进模型。

这家总部位于杭州的公司周五表示，这一新发布是DeepSeek旗舰纯文本V4 Flash模型的实验版本，新增了多模态能力，意味着它还能分析图像和截图等视觉提示并据此采取行动。

公司 [在X上写道](https://x.com/deepseek_ai/status/2090730032574631962)：“这款实验性多模态模型在文本能力（包括智能体、推理和世界知识）上与DeepSeek-V4-Flash相当。”

公司表示，在多模态智能体能力测试中，该模型的性能“接近” [Anthropic PBC](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1892140D:US) 的先进Opus 4.8模型；这种能力指模型无需持续监督或提示即可采取行动。

DeepSeek此前已发布过多模态和视觉模型，例如DeepSeek-VL系列。但这款名为DeepSeek-V4-Flash-Vision-Exp的新产品，为其最前沿的模型系列新增了理解图像并执行任务的能力。用户可通过DeepSeek的API访问该模型。

中国公司正与美国开发商在人工智能前沿领域激烈竞争，它们的模型通常以极低的价格提供相近的性能。DeepSeek旗舰模型 [今年早些时候](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-24/deepseek-unveils-newest-flagship-a-year-after-ai-breakthrough) 的发布，重新定义了低成本开源权重模型所能达到的水准。

### English Original

## DeepSeek Unveils Test Model to Rival Anthropic’s Opus 4.8

[DeepSeek](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/2544189D:CH) unveiled an experimental AI model that can understand visual prompts, saying the tool nears the performance of an advanced model by US rival [Anthropic PBC](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1892140D:US).

The new release is an experimental version of DeepSeek’s flagship text-only V4 Flash model that adds multimodal capabilities, meaning it can also analyze and act on visual prompts like images and screenshots, the Hangzhou-based company said on Friday.

“This experimental multimodal model matches DeepSeek-V4-Flash on text capabilities—including agents, reasoning, and world knowledge,” the company [wrote on X](https://x.com/deepseek_ai/status/2090730032574631962).

The model’s performance is “close to” [Anthropic PBC](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1892140D:US)’s advanced Opus 4.8 model on tests of its multimodal agentic capabilities, where a model can take action without constant oversight or prompting, the company said.

DeepSeek has released multimodal and visual models before, such as its DeepSeek-VL family. But this new offering, called DeepSeek-V4-Flash-Vision-Exp, adds the ability to interpret images and carry out tasks to its most cutting-edge model series. Users can access the model through DeepSeek’s API.

Chinese firms are locked in a heated rivalry with US developers at the leading edge of AI, with their models often offering similar performance for a fraction of the price. The arrival of DeepSeek’s flagship model [earlier this year](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-24/deepseek-unveils-newest-flagship-a-year-after-ai-breakthrough) reset expectations for what low-cost, open-weight models could achieve.
