## 中国医疗保健股因盈利强劲和估值更低而大涨

医疗保健股正成为中国市场最大的赢家，强劲的盈利、更低的估值以及资金从高位科技股轮动出去，提升了它们的吸引力。

该板块的一个[子指数](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/MXCN0HC%20Index)自6月初今年低点以来已飙升超过30%，明显是MSCI中国指数中11个行业组中表现最好的。同期，更广泛的[基准指数](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/MXCN%20Index)上涨约1%。医疗保健指标是三个月内唯一上涨的板块。

近期的大部分热情集中在合同开发和制造组织（CDMO）领域以及创新药企。中国目前约占全球创新药管线三分之一，是临床试验的[首选目的地](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-04/lab-monkeys-for-26-000-show-china-s-biotech-quest-to-rival-us)，并与渴望获得新疗法的美国和欧洲公司达成了重磅交易。

“该行业的创新能力持续增强，中国日益确立自己作为全球新药开发来源地的地位，而不仅仅是制造基地，”富达国际研究总监莫妮卡·李表示。“在经历了长期的融资环境收紧之后，全球生物技术融资环境已显著改善。”

![图片](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3bc81cc90b14812fafafc4ed4479e832/3bc81cc9-0b14-8125-8b25-cecde911fe66-d834693ff5d53e52485fedc2ebfaf9b8.png)

CDMO龙头药明康德（WuXi AppTec Co.）是医疗保健子指数中表现最好的股票之一，该公司公布的上半年业绩超出分析师预期并上调全年指引后，本月初股价[飙升](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-03/wuxi-apptec-sees-pentagon-tag-hurting-reputation-us-business)。药企[石药创新制药股份有限公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/300765%20C2%20Equity)的股票也位列涨幅最大的股票之中。

与此同时，科技股[子指数](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/MXCN0IT%20Index)在过去三个月下跌约5%。中国市场的走势与美国相似，在美国，医疗保健是标普500指数中三个月表现最好的板块。

下周将是考验这轮涨势持久性的关键，江苏恒瑞医药股份有限公司（Jiangsu Hengrui Pharmaceuticals Co.）和石药集团等公司将公布业绩。

“我还不认为这是全面的医疗保健牛市，”Janus Henderson Group的投资组合经理兼大中华区股票主管Victoria Mio表示。“这轮反弹仍具选择性。投资者正在严格区分那些具有盈利可见性、管线质量、明确催化剂和资产负债表实力的公司。”

估值也对投资者具有吸引力，尤其是当他们试图避开拥挤的AI相关股票时。

即使在近期飙升之后，MSCI中国医疗保健指数的市盈率仍低于26倍（基于未来一年预期盈利），低于28倍的十年平均水平，而2021年的峰值估值接近68倍。

“最大的讽刺之一是，我们目前最大的板块超配是医疗保健——历来是中国增长最快、因此也是最昂贵的板块之一，”联博(AllianceBernstein)新兴市场价值股和中国股票首席投资官John Lin表示。

“几个月的AI热潮能带来多大的变化，真是令人惊叹。经过一个季度的AI狂热之后，你突然会发现一些盈利基本面非常扎实的公司，其估值看起来更像价值股。”

## China Healthcare Stocks Win Big on Earnings, Cheaper Valuations

## China Healthcare Stocks Win Big on Earnings, Cheaper Valuations

Healthcare stocks are emerging as the biggest winners in China’s market as strong earnings, cheaper valuations and a rotation out of high-flying technology shares burnishes their appeal.

A [subgauge](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/MXCN0HC%20Index) of the sector has surged more than 30% from this year’s low in early June, by far the top performance among 11 industry groups on the MSCI China Index. The broader [benchmark ](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/MXCN%20Index)is up around 1% in the period. The healthcare measure is the only one to show gains over a three-month period.

Much of the recent enthusiasm has centered on the contract development and manufacturing organization, or CDMO, space, and innovative drugmakers. China now accounts for roughly a third of the world’s innovative drug pipeline, is the [top destination](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-04/lab-monkeys-for-26-000-show-china-s-biotech-quest-to-rival-us) for clinical trials, and has struck blockbuster deals with US and European firms eager to source new treatments.

“The industry’s innovation capabilities continue to strengthen, with China increasingly establishing itself as a global source of novel drug development rather than simply a manufacturing base,” said Monica Li, director of research at Fidelity International. “The global biotech funding environment has improved materially after a prolonged period of tighter financing conditions.”

Shares of CDMO leader WuXi AppTec Co., one of the top performers on the healthcare subgauge, [soared](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-03/wuxi-apptec-sees-pentagon-tag-hurting-reputation-us-business) earlier this month as the firm posted first-half results that exceeded analyst estimates and raised its full-year guidance. Shares of drugmaker [CSPC Innovation Pharmaceutical Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/300765%20C2%20Equity) also feature among the biggest gainers.

Meanwhile, a [subgauge](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/MXCN0IT%20Index) of the tech shares has lost about 5% over the past three months. The moves in China are similar to those seen in the US, where healthcare is the top sectoral performer on the S&P 500 over three months.

A key test of the rally’s durability will come next week, with companies like [Jiangsu Hengrui Pharmaceuticals Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/1276%20HK%20Equity) and CSPC due to report earnings.

“I would not call this a broad-based healthcare bull market yet,” said Victoria Mio, a portfolio manager and head of Greater China equities at Janus Henderson Group. “The rally remains selective. Investors are differentiating sharply between companies with: earnings visibility, pipeline quality, identifiable catalysts and balance-sheet strength.”

Valuations have also been a draw for investors, especially as they sought to avoid overcrowded AI names.

Even after the recent surge, MSCI’s China healthcare gauge is trading at a multiple of less than 26 times its one-year forward earnings, below a 10-year average of 28 times, and versus a peak valuation of nearly 68 times in 2021.

“One of the great ironies is that our largest sector overweight today is healthcare — historically one of China’s highest-growth and therefore most expensive sectors,” said John Lin, chief investment officer for emerging markets value equities and China equities at AllianceBernstein.

“Amazing what a few months of AI excitement can do for you. After a quarter of AI frenzy, you can suddenly find some companies with very solid earnings fundamentals trading at what look much more like value-stock valuations.”
