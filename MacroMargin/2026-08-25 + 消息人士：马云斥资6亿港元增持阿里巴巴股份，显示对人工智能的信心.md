### 中文译文

## 消息人士：马云斥资6亿港元增持阿里巴巴股份，显示对人工智能的信心

#### 创始人个人投资6亿港元，此前阿里巴巴董事长和首席执行官也在公司自2019年以来首次发行后购入股份

![Alibaba founder Jack Ma at the Vivatech conference in Paris. Photo: Handout](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3c781cc90b1481e98aebe116d0e73540/3c781cc9-0b14-81be-b9a9-f6342048d30d-24badec9655ce48b59f863e528ac5ee7.jpg)

据知情人士透露，阿里巴巴集团控股创始人马云连续多日购入该公司超过6亿港元的香港上市股份，显示其对公司在人工智能领域长期前景的信心。

一位消息人士称，这些购买行为表明马云对“阿里巴巴实现其人工智能雄心并抓住未来长期增长机会的强烈信心”。

与此同时，根据香港股市披露文件，阿里巴巴董事长蔡崇信和首席执行官吴泳铭在过去两天累计斥资2.02亿港元购入股份。

蔡崇信周二购入约8200万港元股份，此前周一已购入8000万港元——同日吴泳铭也投入约4000万港元。

这些个人投资发生在本公司周日宣布新股份发行之后，这是中国公司致力于人工智能开发的最大规模融资活动之一。

拥有《南华早报》的阿里巴巴周二未立即回应置评请求。

阿里巴巴股价周二在香港上涨1.5%，收于114.20港元。

此次购股正值阿里巴巴持续推进其人工智能计划之际。根据公司声明，周日的股份配售是该集团自2019年在香港上市以来的首次，将提升其“全栈人工智能能力”并扩大其“全球人工智能领导地位”。

该交易吸引了强劲的投资者需求。据知情人士透露，阿里巴巴在数小时内就达到了800亿港元的基准目标，总需求接近超额认购的三倍。

上述人士称，此次发行吸引了280亿美元的需求，其中来自主权财富基金和纯多头投资者的资金达60亿美元，并补充说锚定需求来自中东、欧洲和亚洲的领先主权财富基金。

他们表示，最终分配中超过40%将流向主权财富基金和纯多头基金。

马云于1999年与17位合伙人共同创立了阿里巴巴。他于2013年卸任集团首席执行官，并于2019年卸任阿里巴巴执行董事长。他在2018年致员工的信中说，他离开的原因之一是把接力棒交给年轻一代。

2023年，马云购买了约5000万美元的公司股票，[《南华早报》此前报道](https://www.scmp.com/tech/big-tech/article/3249552/jack-ma-joe-tsai-replace-softbank-alibabas-largest-shareholders-scooping-tech-giants-tumbling-shares)。

近年来，阿里巴巴一直致力于从电子商务巨头转型为全栈人工智能参与者，业务涵盖芯片、计算基础设施、大语言模型和人工智能应用。

上周，该公司报告称，6月当季其云和人工智能业务收入同比增长45%。在财报电话会议上，吴表示，公司预计其人工智能投资将在三年内实现收支平衡。该公司在2025年初宣布了一项为期三年、总额3800亿元人民币（合565.4亿美元）的人工智能基础设施投资承诺。

阿里巴巴本月表示，其 [Qwen AI 模型](https://www.scmp.com/tech/tech-trends/article/3364404/alibabas-lightweight-qwen-model-takes-larger-ai-systems-openai-deepseek-zhipu?module=inline&pgtype=article) 家族全球下载量已超过 30 亿次，而该公司的半导体部门 T-Head 也开始将其 Zhenwu M890 AI 处理器投入商业规模部署。

周二，阿里云深化了其云基础设施与消费级 AI 生态的整合，宣布用户将可以把他们的“Token Plan”API 密钥直接关联到 Qwen 应用和 PC 界面。

据该公司称，此次更新使用户能够从单一 token 配额中调用资源——支持 [Qwen3.8-Max](https://www.scmp.com/tech/article/3362738/alibabas-ai-model-qwen38-max-made-widely-accessible-ahead-open-weights-release) 等高级模型——为电子邮件、日历和云存储等工作场所工具提供支持。

## Jack Ma buys HK$600 million of Alibaba shares, signalling AI confidence: sources

#### Founder’s HK$600 million personal investment comes as Alibaba chairman and CEO also purchased shares after company’s first issuance since 2019

Alibaba Group Holding founder Jack Ma has bought more than HK$600 million worth of the company’s Hong Kong-listed shares on consecutive days, according to people familiar with the matter, a show of confidence in the firm’s long-term prospects for artificial intelligence.

The purchases showed Ma’s "strong confidence for Alibaba to realise its AI ambitions and capture the long-term growth opportunities ahead", one source said.

Meanwhile, Alibaba chairman Joe Tsai and CEO Eddie Wu Yongming spent a cumulative HK$202 million acquiring shares over the past two days, according to Hong Kong stock market filings.

Tsai bought about HK$82 million in shares on Tuesday, following an HK$80 million purchase on Monday – the same day Wu chipped in roughly HK$40 million.

The personal investments came after a new share issuance announced by the company on Sunday, one of the biggest fundraising efforts by a Chinese firm dedicated to AI development.

Alibaba, which owns the South China Morning Post, did not immediately respond to a request for comment on Tuesday.

Alibaba shares gained 1.5 per cent to close at HK$114.20 in Hong Kong on Tuesday.

The share purchases came as Alibaba continues to advance its AI initiatives. The Sunday share placement, the firm’s first since its 2019 listing in Hong Kong, would improve its "full-stack AI capabilities" and extend its "global AI leadership", according to a company statement.

The deal has drawn strong investor demand. Alibaba met its benchmark of HK$80 billion within hours, with total demand nearly three times oversubscribed, according to people familiar with the deal.

The issuance attracted US$28 billion in demand, including US$6 billion from sovereign wealth funds and long-only investors, the people said, adding that anchor demand came from leading sovereign wealth funds across the Middle East, Europe and Asia.

More than 40 per cent of the final allocation would go to sovereign wealth and long-only funds, they said.

Ma co-founded Alibaba in 1999 with 17 partners. He relinquished his post as group CEO in 2013 and as Alibaba’s executive chairman in 2019. One of the reasons for his departure was to hand the reins to younger generations, he said in a letter to staff members in 2018.

In 2023, Ma bought around US$50 million of company stock, the [SCMP previously reported](https://www.scmp.com/tech/big-tech/article/3249552/jack-ma-joe-tsai-replace-softbank-alibabas-largest-shareholders-scooping-tech-giants-tumbling-shares).

In recent years, Alibaba has been working to pivot from an e-commerce powerhouse into a full-stack AI player with businesses spanning chips, computing infrastructure, large language models and AI applications.

Last week, the company reported a 45 per cent year-on-year revenue jump in the June quarter in its cloud and AI operations. During an earnings call, Wu said the company expected its AI investments to break even within three years. It announced a three-year commitment of 380 billion yuan (US$56.54 billion) in AI infrastructure investment in early 2025.

Alibaba said this month that its [Qwen AI model](https://www.scmp.com/tech/tech-trends/article/3364404/alibabas-lightweight-qwen-model-takes-larger-ai-systems-openai-deepseek-zhipu?module=inline&pgtype=article) family had surpassed 3 billion global downloads, while the company’s semiconductor unit, T-Head, also began commercial-scale deployment of its Zhenwu M890 AI processor.

On Tuesday, Alibaba Cloud deepened the integration of its cloud infrastructure and consumer AI ecosystem, announcing that users would be allowed to link their "Token Plan" API keys directly to the Qwen app and PC interface.

The update enables users to draw from a single token quota – supporting advanced models such as [Qwen3.8-Max](https://www.scmp.com/tech/article/3362738/alibabas-ai-model-qwen38-max-made-widely-accessible-ahead-open-weights-release) – to power workplace tools such as email, calendars and cloud storage, according to the company.
