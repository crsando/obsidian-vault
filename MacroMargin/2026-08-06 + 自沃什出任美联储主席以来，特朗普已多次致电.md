## **Trump Has Called Warsh Repeatedly Since He Became Fed Chair**

## **自沃什出任美联储主席以来，特朗普已多次致电**

### The president has sought Warsh’s counsel on issues such as how the war in Iran is affecting the economy

### 总统已就诸如伊朗战争如何影响经济等问题征求了沃什的意见

WASHINGTON—President [Trump](https://www.wsj.com/topics/person/donald-trump) has spoken repeatedly with [Kevin Warsh](https://www.wsj.com/topics/person/kevin-warsh) since he became chairman of the [Federal Reserve](https://www.wsj.com/topics/subject/federal-reserve), according to people familiar with the matter, maintaining a line of communication between a president and a central bank chief that departs from recent precedent.

华盛顿——据知情人士透露，自沃什出任美联储主席以来，[特朗普](https://www.wsj.com/topics/person/donald-trump)已多次与[凯文·沃什](https://www.wsj.com/topics/person/kevin-warsh)通话，保持了总统与央行行长之间的沟通渠道，这与近年来的先例有所不同。

The president calls Warsh in bursts, the people said—several times in a stretch of days, then quiet for longer periods. Trump has sought Warsh’s counsel on a range of matters, including how the war in [Iran](https://www.wsj.com/topics/place/iran) and the rapid rise of artificial intelligence are affecting the economy, some of the people said. Warsh has been on the job for less than three months.

知情人士称，总统会集中一段时间给沃什打电话——在几天内多次致电，然后沉寂更长时间。部分知情人士透露，特朗普就一系列问题向沃什征求了建议，包括[伊朗](https://www.wsj.com/topics/place/iran)战争和人工智能的迅速崛起如何影响经济。沃什上任还不到三个月。

It wasn’t clear if Trump and Warsh have discussed monetary policy. A person familiar with the conversations insisted the president hasn’t broached the topic of interest rates since Warsh [was confirmed by the Senate](https://www.wsj.com/economy/central-banking/kevin-warsh-fed-chair-senate-vote-9eab1277?mod=article_inline).

目前尚不清楚特朗普和沃什是否讨论了货币政策。一位熟悉对话的人士坚称，自沃什[获得参议院确认](https://www.wsj.com/economy/central-banking/kevin-warsh-fed-chair-senate-vote-9eab1277?mod=article_inline)以来，总统从未提及利率话题。

Presidents appoint Fed chairs, but the central bank sets interest rates independently, a separation meant to keep politics out of decisions that shape borrowing costs across the economy.

总统任命美联储主席，但中央银行独立设定利率，这种分离旨在让政治远离影响整个经济借贷成本的决策。

It isn’t unusual for presidents and Fed chairs to meet on occasion, but in recent decades the contacts have generally been more formal and arranged in advance, choreographed to avoid the perception the president is influencing rate decisions. Trump regularly bypasses formal communication channels, keeping up with a wide circle of contacts—including world leaders, business executives and lawmakers—through his personal cellphone.

总统与美联储主席偶尔会面并不罕见，但近几十年来，这类接触通常更为正式并提前安排，旨在避免外界认为总统在影响利率决策。特朗普经常绕过正式沟通渠道，通过个人手机与广泛的联络圈保持联系——包括世界领导人、商界高管和立法者。

Some past presidents have had closer working relationships with Fed chairs than others. Bill Clinton [liked](https://www.wsj.com/economy/central-banking/alan-greenspan-fed-chair-dies-100-eeacb8eb?mod=article_inline) Alan Greenspan, and seated him next to first lady Hillary Clinton at his first joint address to Congress. Joe Biden only had a handful of interactions with Jerome Powell during his term. Recent presidents, from Clinton through Barack Obama, avoided publicly putting pressure on the Fed.

一些前总统与美联储主席的工作关系比其他总统更为密切。比尔·克林顿[喜欢](https://www.wsj.com/economy/central-banking/alan-greenspan-fed-chair-dies-100-eeacb8eb?mod=article_inline)艾伦·格林斯潘，并在其首次向国会发表联席会议演讲时安排他坐在第一夫人希拉里·克林顿旁边。乔·拜登在任期内只与杰罗姆·鲍威尔有过几次互动。从克林顿到贝拉克·奥巴马，近几任总统都避免公开向美联储施压。

Contact between the White House and the Fed chair was more frequent in the 1960s, when the two sides met routinely. Such meetings became more limited after President Richard Nixon’s pressure campaign on Fed Chairman Arthur Burns preceded the inflation of the 1970s. By the 1980s, communications with the Fed chair were handled largely through the Treasury secretary.

白宫与美联储主席的接触在20世纪60年代更为频繁，当时双方定期会面。在理查德·尼克松总统对美联储主席亚瑟·伯恩斯进行施压之后，此类会面变得更加有限，而这一施压行动发生在20世纪70年代通货膨胀之前。到20世纪80年代，与美联储主席的沟通主要由财政部长负责。

Arthur Burns, shown seated to Richard Nixon’s left in 1968, was an adviser to Nixon before being appointed Fed chief. AP

亚瑟·伯恩斯，1968年坐在理查德·尼克松左边，在被任命为美联储主席之前曾是尼克松的顾问。美联社

Trump has [chafed at the Fed’s independence](https://www.wsj.com/politics/policy/federal-reserve-independence-trump-8890ef78?mod=article_inline) more than any recent president. In an interview with The Wall Street Journal last December, Trump said he [wanted the Fed chair to consult with him](https://www.wsj.com/economy/central-banking/trump-says-he-is-leaning-toward-warsh-or-hassett-to-lead-the-fed-34a200e5?mod=article_inline) on where to set interest rates. “I’m a smart voice and should be listened to,” he said.

特朗普比任何一位近几任总统都更[对美联储的独立性感到不满](https://www.wsj.com/politics/policy/federal-reserve-independence-trump-8890ef78?mod=article_inline)。在去年12月接受《华尔街日报》采访时，特朗普表示，他[希望美联储主席征求他的意见](https://www.wsj.com/economy/central-banking/trump-says-he-is-leaning-toward-warsh-or-hassett-to-lead-the-fed-34a200e5?mod=article_inline)，关于利率设定在哪里。“我是个聪明的发声者，应该被倾听，”他说。

At Warsh’s swearing-in ceremony less than six months later, Trump said the opposite. “I want Kevin to be totally independent,” the president said. “Don’t look at me, don’t look at anybody. Just do your own thing and do a great job.”

不到六个月后，在沃什的宣誓就职仪式上，特朗普说了相反的话。“我希望凯文完全独立，”总统说，“不要看我，不要看任何人。只管做你自己的事，把工作做好。”

In his conversations with Trump, Warsh has often expressed a positive view of the economy, according to some of the people familiar with the matter. Those sentiments echo his public statements. “The most striking feature of the economy is the strong growth of business investment,” he said at a news conference last week.

据一些知情人士透露，在与特朗普的交谈中，沃什经常对经济表达乐观看法。这些观点与他的公开言论相呼应。“经济最显著的特点是商业投资的强劲增长，”他上周在一次新闻发布会上表示。

The president has told his political allies that he sees Warsh as a valued outside economic adviser. That is a contrast with the way the president viewed Warsh’s predecessor, [Jerome Powell](https://www.wsj.com/topics/person/jerome-powell), whom Trump chose over Warsh for the job in 2017.

总统已告诉他的政治盟友，他认为沃什是一位宝贵的外部经济顾问。这与总统对沃什前任[杰罗姆·鲍威尔](https://www.wsj.com/topics/person/jerome-powell)的看法形成鲜明对比，特朗普在2017年选择鲍威尔而非沃什担任该职位。

Trump derided Powell as “too late” for declining to cut rates and for not cutting them enough when he did. He repeatedly threatened to fire him and compared conversations with him to “talking to a wall.”

特朗普嘲笑鲍威尔“太迟了”，因为鲍威尔拒绝降息，并且在降息时降幅不够。他多次威胁要解雇他，并形容与他交谈“就像对牛弹琴”。

Trump has spared Warsh that treatment. Before [last week’s Fed decision](https://www.wsj.com/economy/central-banking/fed-holds-rates-steady-but-three-officials-voted-for-increase-3a6903e0?mod=article_inline) to hold interest rates steady, Trump praised Warsh and said he knew Warsh wanted to do “the right thing” but added, “He’s got a board, and the board members are very political.” Powell [is still on the board](https://www.wsj.com/economy/central-banking/jerome-powell-federal-reserve-decision-89f4ff22?mod=article_inline), having stayed on as a governor after his term as chairman ended.

特朗普没有对沃什采取这种态度。在[上周美联储决定维持利率不变](https://www.wsj.com/economy/central-banking/fed-holds-rates-steady-but-three-officials-voted-for-increase-3a6903e0?mod=article_inline)之前，特朗普称赞了沃什，并表示他知道沃什想做“正确的事情”，但补充说，“他有一个董事会，而董事会成员非常政治化。”鲍威尔[仍在董事会](https://www.wsj.com/economy/central-banking/jerome-powell-federal-reserve-decision-89f4ff22?mod=article_inline)，在主席任期结束后以理事身份留任。

The president often raves about the Fed chair’s looks after he sees Warsh on television, according to a person who has been in the room for the president’s remarks.

据一位曾在总统发言现场的人士透露，总统在电视上看到沃什后，经常对他的外表赞不绝口。

Since returning to Washington, D.C., Warsh has become a presence on the city’s political circuit, in the manner of Greenspan, who for years was a fixture of the social scene. Warsh dined Tuesday at a popular Georgetown restaurant with Army Secretary Dan Driscoll, an ally of Vice President JD Vance. Last month, Warsh attended a dinner in Georgetown honoring Nick Luna, the incoming chief of staff to Vance, and Cliff Sims, Vance’s national security adviser, according to Politico.

自回到华盛顿特区以来，沃什已成为该市政治圈的名人，就像格林斯潘那样，多年来一直是社交场合的常客。周二，沃什与陆军部长丹·德里斯科尔在乔治城一家热门餐厅共进晚餐，德里斯科尔是副总统JD·万斯的盟友。据Politico报道，上个月，沃什出席了在乔治城举行的一场晚宴，宴请万斯即将上任的幕僚长尼克·卢纳和万斯的国家安全顾问克利夫·西姆斯。

Trump’s relationship with Warsh could animate concerns about the independence of the central bank. Democrats on the Senate Banking Committee, which oversees the Fed, have repeatedly pressed Warsh for more disclosure about his interactions with the president.

特朗普与沃什的关系可能引发对中央银行独立性的担忧。负责监督美联储的参议院银行委员会的民主党人多次敦促沃什进一步披露他与总统的互动。

The White House said the president believes in an independent Fed.

白宫表示，总统相信美联储的独立性。

“President Trump has repeatedly stressed that he is giving Chairman Warsh the space he needs to restore confidence and competence in Fed decision-making,” White House spokesman Kush Desai said in a statement. “While the President has his First Amendment right as an American citizen and duty as Commander in Chief to voice his thoughts about the Fed, he has also repeatedly reaffirmed Chairman Warsh and the Fed’s independence.”

“特朗普总统多次强调，他正在给予沃什主席所需的空间，以恢复对美联储决策的信心和能力，”白宫发言人库什·德赛在一份声明中表示。“虽然总统作为美国公民拥有第一修正案权利，并作为三军统帅有责任就美联储发表看法，但他也多次重申沃什主席和美联储的独立性。”

The Fed declined to comment.

美联储拒绝置评。

Powell met presidential pressure with stoic nonengagement. He took Trump’s calls and dined with the president at the White House in 2019. But the contacts were sparse and carefully handled: In-person meetings were disclosed immediately with a readout, and phone calls were listed in monthly appointment calendars released on a one-month lag.

鲍威尔以坚忍的不接触态度应对总统压力。他接了特朗普的电话，并于2019年在白宫与总统共进晚餐。但接触很少且处理谨慎：面对面会议立即以简报形式披露，电话则列在延迟一个月发布的月度日程安排中。

特朗普总统去年与时任主席杰罗姆·鲍威尔一起参观美联储大楼的翻新工程。肯特·西村/路透社

Critics of Powell’s approach say his detachment heightened tensions with the Trump White House, and that a chairman who gave the president a sense of being heard could have built good will without conceding anything on policy.

批评鲍威尔做法的人士表示，他的超然态度加剧了与特朗普白宫的紧张关系，而一位让总统感到被倾听的主席本可以在不放弃任何政策立场的情况下建立好感。

Warsh’s cultivation of any relationship with Trump could be seen as corrective, engaging Trump directly to head off the sense that the Fed is his adversary. Whether Warsh’s relationship is an asset or liability turns on what he does if the Fed’s judgment differs from the president’s wishes.

沃什与特朗普建立任何关系都可被视为一种纠正，直接与特朗普接触以消除美联储是其对手的感觉。沃什的关系是资产还是负债，取决于当美联储的判断与总统意愿相左时他会怎么做。

Last week the Fed held rates steady. While Warsh said at a news conference that rate increases “could well be part of” the answer if inflation stays elevated, he leaned more heavily on the Fed’s ability to reduce inflation by convincing the public that it is serious about hitting its 2% goal. Short-term Treasury yields fell and long-term yields rose—a pattern that suggests investors expected slightly looser policy than before the meeting, and a little more inflation later.

上周美联储维持利率不变。尽管沃什在新闻发布会上表示，如果通胀持续走高，加息“很可能成为”应对措施的一部分，但他更强调美联储通过让公众相信其认真致力于实现2%目标来降低通胀的能力。短期国债收益率下降，长期收益率上升——这一模式表明投资者预期政策将比会议前略微宽松，且未来通胀会略高。

Ellen Meade, a former Fed economist now at Duke University, said she had expected Warsh’s relationship with Trump to give him room to lead the Fed toward higher rates if conditions warranted. The press conference last week made her less sure of this.

艾伦·米德曾在美联储担任经济学家，现任职于杜克大学。她曾预计，沃什与特朗普的关系将使他能够在条件允许的情况下引导美联储走向更高利率。上周的新闻发布会让她对此不那么确定了。

At his confirmation hearing in April, Warsh said Fed independence is “up to the Fed” and that it isn’t threatened when elected officials “state their views on interest rates.”

在四月份的确认听证会上，沃什表示美联储的独立性“取决于美联储自身”，并表示当民选官员“就利率发表观点”时，独立性并不会受到威胁。

Asked by Sen. Chris Van Hollen (D., Md.) last month whether he would follow Powell’s practice of disclosing his appointments, including contacts with the president, Warsh said he would comply with the law. He declined to say if he had spoken to Trump.

上个月，马里兰州民主党参议员克里斯·范霍伦要求沃什说明他是否会遵循鲍威尔的做法，披露自己的会面安排，包括与总统的接触。沃什表示会遵守法律，但拒绝透露是否与特朗普交谈过。

“The president has not, before I took this office, before I raised my right hand, he has not tried to influence the conduct of monetary policy,” Warsh said. “And if he tried to, I would continue to keep my head down and do the job.”

“在我上任之前，在我举起右手之前，总统没有试图影响货币政策的执行，”沃什说，“如果他试图这么做，我会继续埋头做好本职工作。”

The cautionary example is Burns, a respected economist who Nixon appointed as Fed chairman in 1970 after years as a trusted adviser. Nixon leaned on Burns relentlessly to loosen policy before the 1972 election. Oval Office recordings captured a phone call in December 1971 in which Burns boasted of how he was pressuring his colleagues to cut rates the following week. “Great,” Nixon replied. “Just kick ‘em in the rump a little.”

警示性的例子是伯恩斯，他是一位备受尊敬的经济学家，在担任多年值得信赖的顾问后，于1970年被尼克松任命为美联储主席。尼克松在1972年大选前不断向伯恩斯施压，要求放宽货币政策。椭圆形办公室的录音记录了1971年12月的一通电话，伯恩斯在电话中吹嘘自己如何施压同事在接下来的一周降息。“太好了，”尼克松回答，“稍微踢踢他们屁股。”

Nixon was re-elected in a landslide, but inflation later soared. The experience changed how the two institutions dealt with each other.

尼克松以压倒性优势再次当选，但此后通货膨胀飙升。这一经历改变了两家机构之间的相处方式。

## 特朗普致电沃什，最新迹象显示其试图影响美联储

US President Donald Trump has periodically spoken by phone with [Federal Reserve](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/13598Z:US) Chair Kevin Warsh since he took the role, people familiar with the matter said, the latest sign of Trump’s effort to exert greater influence over the central bank.

据知情人士透露，美国总统唐纳德·特朗普自美联储主席凯文·沃什上任以来，已定期与其通电话，这是特朗普试图对央行施加更大影响力的最新迹象。

Trump and Warsh have spoken multiple times since the latter was [confirmed in May](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-13/senate-confirms-warsh-to-lead-fed-as-trump-tests-its-autonomy), with Trump inquiring about Warsh’s forecasts and opinions but not pushing him to take any specific course of action, one of the people said, speaking on condition of anonymity to discuss the private conversations. One person said they speak irregularly, and another said their calls are infrequent. It’s unclear if the two have discussed monetary policy.

自后者于[5月获得确认](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-13/senate-confirms-warsh-to-lead-fed-as-trump-tests-its-autonomy)以来，特朗普与沃什已多次交谈。其中一位知情人士表示，特朗普询问了沃什的预测和意见，但没有推动他采取任何具体行动；因讨论涉及私人对话，该人士要求匿名。一位知情人士表示他们通话不规律，另一位则表示通话并不频繁。目前尚不清楚两人是否讨论过货币政策。

“President Trump has repeatedly stressed that he is giving Chairman Warsh the space he needs to restore confidence and competence in Fed decision-making,” White House spokesman Kush Desai said in an emailed statement. “While the President has his First Amendment right as an American citizen and duty as Commander in Chief to voice his thoughts about the Fed, he has also repeatedly reaffirmed Chairman Warsh and the Fed’s independence.”

白宫发言人库什·德赛在一份电邮声明中表示：“特朗普总统多次强调，他正在给予沃什主席恢复美联储决策信心和能力所需的空间。虽然总统作为美国公民拥有第一修正案权利，并作为总司令有责任就美联储表达自己的想法，但他也多次重申沃什主席及美联储的独立性。”

The Fed declined to comment. The calls were first reported by the Wall Street Journal.

美联储拒绝置评。这些通话最早由《华尔街日报》报道。

While the full nature of their conversations remains hazy, Trump has long implored the Fed to slash interest rates. Past presidents and Fed chairs have held calls and meetings, though they have been rare, and Trump’s drive to influence monetary policy has amounted to the most forceful intervention in the central bank’s policy making in decades.

虽然他们谈话的具体内容仍不清楚，但特朗普长期以来一直恳求美联储大幅降息。过去总统和美联储主席也曾进行过通话和会面，尽管这种情况很少见，而特朗普试图影响货币政策的努力已成为数十年来对央行决策最有力的干预。

Trump routinely brushes away inflation concerns in calling for lower borrowing costs, but has taken a less combative approach since Warsh’s confirmation.

特朗普在呼吁降低借贷成本时，通常会撇开对通胀的担忧，但自沃什获得确认以来，他的态度已不那么好斗。

In testimony to lawmakers last month, Warsh said Trump hasn’t tried to interfere with the central bank but avoided directly answering questions about whether he’s communicated with Trump since taking the Fed’s helm.

在上个月向议员们作证时，沃什表示特朗普并未试图干涉央行，但回避了直接回答自执掌美联储以来是否与特朗普有过沟通的问题。

“I just don’t want to be in the business of sharing discussions that the president and I have,” Warsh said during a Senate Banking Committee hearing, while vowing to be “an independent guy.”

“我只是不想谈论我和总统之间的讨论内容，”沃什在参议院银行委员会听证会上表示，同时誓言要成为‘一个独立的人’。

“I certainly don’t feel uncomfortable receiving a call from the chairman of this committee or the president of the United States,” Warsh said on July 15.

“我当然不会因为接到本委员会主席或美国总统的电话而感到不自在，”沃什7月15日表示。

Previous Fed chairs have issued press releases after meetings with the president. One example: in May 2025, then-Fed Chair Jerome Powell issued a release to notify the public of his meeting with Trump, in which he said he did not discuss his outlook for monetary policy. Warsh meets regularly with Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent, following a longstanding precedent of interactions between leaders of the Treasury and Fed.

此前美联储主席在会见总统后会发布新闻稿。一个例子是：2025年5月，时任美联储主席杰罗姆·鲍威尔发布声明，向公众通报他与特朗普的会面，并表示他没有讨论自己对货币政策的看法。沃什定期与财政部长斯科特·贝森特会面，遵循财政部长与美联储领导人之间长期互动的先例。

Trump repeatedly criticized Powell, who remains on the Fed board, over his interest-rate decisions. The administration opened a probe into Powell over a renovation project at the bank’s headquarters. Trump has also [sought unsuccessfully to fire](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-29/supreme-court-expands-president-s-power-to-fire-top-officials) Governor Lisa Cook and is said to be [weighing similar moves](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-22/trump-allies-eye-svb-review-as-new-path-to-oust-a-fed-governor) against Governor Michael Barr.

特朗普多次就利率决定批评仍在美联储理事会的鲍威尔。政府因美联储总部翻新项目对鲍威尔展开调查。特朗普还曾[试图解雇](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-29/supreme-court-expands-president-s-power-to-fire-top-officials)理事丽莎·库克但未成功，据称正在[考虑对理事迈克尔·巴尔采取类似行动](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-22/trump-allies-eye-svb-review-as-new-path-to-oust-a-fed-governor)。

The president’s public treatment of Warsh has been different. During his swearing-in ceremony, Trump said he wants the Fed chair “to be totally independent.”

总统对沃什的公开态度则有所不同。在宣誓就职仪式上，特朗普表示他希望美联储主席“完全独立”。

“Don’t look at me, don’t look at anybody, just do your own thing and do a great job,” Trump said at the ceremony.

“不要看我，不要看任何人，做你自己的事，做好就行，”特朗普在仪式上说。

Trump repeatedly chastised Powell for being “too late” to lower rates. But after Warsh held them steady, the president didn’t blame the chair and instead said the outcome was due to a board he accused of being “very political.”

特朗普多次斥责鲍威尔降息“太晚”。但在沃什维持利率不变后，总统并未责怪这位主席，而是表示这一结果应归咎于他指责“非常政治化”的理事会。

The president’s cumulative pressure campaign has continued to blur the line between the central bank and the administration, potentially clouding its independence. It also adds another factor for Warsh as the Fed looks to navigate the fallout from Trump’s war in Iran and the fluctuations in energy prices, as well as Trump’s shifting tariff regime, lingering inflation and a hefty US budget deficit.

总统累积的施压行动持续模糊央行与政府之间的界限，可能影响美联储的独立性。这也为沃什增添了另一个考量因素，因为美联储需要应对特朗普对伊朗战争带来的后果、能源价格波动，以及特朗普不断变化的关税政策、持续的通胀和庞大的美国预算赤字。

The Journal’s Trump Has Called Warsh Repeatedly Since He Became Fed Chair -- WSJ on the calls between Trump and Warsh said the president has sought Warsh’s views on a range of issues, including the economic impact of the war in Iran and the rise of artificial intelligence. Warsh often gave Trump a positive picture of the economy, the report said, but it’s unclear if they discussed monetary policy.

《华尔街日报》报道，特朗普自沃什成为美联储主席以来曾多次致电——关于特朗普与沃什通话的报道称，总统征求了沃什对一系列问题的看法，包括伊朗战争的经济影响和人工智能的兴起。报道称，沃什经常向特朗普描绘积极的经济前景，但尚不清楚他们是否讨论了货币政策。
