## **The Fed Is Heading Into One of Its Most Unpredictable Meetings in Years**

## **美联储面临多年来最难以预测的会议之一**

### A cooler inflation report eases pressure for a rate hike next week, but Middle East conflict is rekindling jitters. A new chairman won’t tip his hand.

### 通胀报告降温缓解了下周加息的压力，但中东冲突再度引发紧张情绪。新任主席不会透露自己的立场。

Next week’s [Federal Reserve](https://www.wsj.com/topics/subject/federal-reserve) meeting is shaping up as one of the least predictable in years. A [renewed oil shock](https://www.wsj.com/business/energy-oil/global-economy-is-suddenly-fighting-an-energy-stranglehold-on-three-fronts-93f28d2b?mod=article_inline) and a hawkish faction pressing to raise rates have collided with [cooler inflation data](https://www.wsj.com/economy/cpi-inflation-report-june-2026-afb89992?mod=article_inline) that helps the case for a hold. Presiding over it is a new chairman who has revealed nothing about which way he leans.

下周的[美联储](https://www.wsj.com/topics/subject/federal-reserve)会议正成为多年来最难以预测的会议之一。[新一轮石油冲击](https://www.wsj.com/business/energy-oil/global-economy-is-suddenly-fighting-an-energy-stranglehold-on-three-fronts-93f28d2b?mod=article_inline)和鹰派加息压力与[降温的通胀数据](https://www.wsj.com/economy/cpi-inflation-report-june-2026-afb89992?mod=article_inline)（支持按兵不动）相互碰撞。主持这场会议的新任主席尚未透露他倾向于哪一方。

Warsh has vowed repeatedly over the last two months to [restore price stability](https://www.wsj.com/economy/central-banking/warsh-tells-congress-the-fed-has-no-tolerance-for-high-inflation-50c5dfa8?mod=article_inline), to disabuse investors of the idea the Fed has implicitly accepted higher [inflation](https://www.wsj.com/topics/subject/inflation). He hasn’t spelled out how current interest rates will accomplish that.

沃什在过去两个月多次承诺[恢复物价稳定](https://www.wsj.com/economy/central-banking/warsh-tells-congress-the-fed-has-no-tolerance-for-high-inflation-50c5dfa8?mod=article_inline)，以消除投资者认为美联储已默认接受更高[通胀](https://www.wsj.com/topics/subject/inflation)的想法。他没有说明目前的利率水平将如何实现这一目标。

The officials pressing to raise rates are the more insistent, and a few of them are candidates to dissent if rates stay on hold. Some influential policymakers last week suggested they were inclined to hold, but could support tighter policy later this year. A hold would thus potentially settle little by pushing the debate to September.

那些施压加息的声音更为坚定，其中几位官员可能会在维持利率不变时投反对票。几位有影响力的政策制定者上周表示倾向于按兵不动，但可能支持今年晚些时候收紧政策。因此，维持利率不变可能只是将争论推迟到9月份，而基本没有解决什么问题。

At the Fed’s meeting last month, half of Warsh’s 18 colleagues expected they would [need to raise rates this year](https://www.wsj.com/economy/central-banking/fed-officials-flagged-risks-that-would-warrant-higher-rates-6f584cba?mod=article_inline). The other half thought they wouldn’t. Warsh “could tip the balance of the scale in whichever way he wants,” said Jonathan Pingle, chief U.S. economist at UBS.

在上个月的美联储会议上，沃什的18位同事中有一半预计[今年需要加息](https://www.wsj.com/economy/central-banking/fed-officials-flagged-risks-that-would-warrant-higher-rates-6f584cba?mod=article_inline)，另一半认为不需要。瑞银(UBS)首席美国经济学家乔纳森·平格尔(Jonathan Pingle)表示，沃什“可能将天平倒向任何他想要的方向”。

On Wednesday, futures markets put the chance of a rate increase at roughly one in three when the Fed meets July 28-29, up from about one in 10 late last week, according to CME Group. The jump tracked a renewed run-up in energy prices as conflict has flared again in the Middle East.

周三，据芝加哥商业交易所集团(CME Group)数据，期货市场认为美联储在7月28-29日会议加息的概率约为三分之一，高于上周晚些时候的十分之一左右。这一跃升伴随着能源价格再度上涨，中东冲突再次升级。

“I can make a good case for either raising rates or not,” said William English, a former senior Fed economist now at Yale. The deciding factor, he said, is largely out of the Fed’s hands: whether the [Iran](https://www.wsj.com/topics/place/iran) conflict eases and oil falls back, or worsens and leaves the Fed with a more stubborn inflation problem. If the Iran situation pushes prices up, “they’ll wish they raised rates,” he said. “They’re just kind of stuck.”

“我既可以找到加息的理由，也可以找到不加息的理由，”前美联储高级经济学家、现任职于耶鲁大学的威廉·英格利希(William English)表示。他说，决定因素在很大程度上不在美联储掌控之中：伊朗局势缓和、油价回落，还是局势恶化、给美联储留下更棘手的通胀问题。如果伊朗局势推高油价，“他们会后悔没有加息，”他说。“他们有点陷入困境。”

#### **The case for waiting**

#### **等待的理由**

The case for waiting rests on recent economic data: Energy prices declined in June, and underlying prices were flat in the consumer-price index. The [June employment report](https://www.wsj.com/economy/jobs/june-jobs-report-unemployment-6c9540b6?mod=article_inline) gave no sign of a tight labor market pushing prices higher.

等待的理由基于近期经济数据：6月份能源价格下降，消费者价格指数中的核心价格持平。[6月就业报告](https://www.wsj.com/economy/jobs/june-jobs-report-unemployment-6c9540b6?mod=article_inline)未显示劳动力市场紧张推高价格的迹象。

“The data have been more favorable than what the Fed had in front of them in June,” said Dean Maki, chief economist at hedge fund Point72 Asset Management. “It would be strange for the Fed to respond to that set of data by hiking when it chose not to in June.”

“数据比美联储6月份看到的数据更有利，”对冲基金Point72资产管理公司首席经济学家Dean Maki表示。“美联储在6月选择不加息，如果现在对这些数据作出加息回应，那将很奇怪。”

Consumers have faced higher prices at the pump this year amid the U.S. war with Iran. Brandon Bell/Getty Images

今年以来，随着美国与伊朗的战争，消费者面临着更高的油价。Brandon Bell/Getty Images

Officials who are more patient argue that this year’s inflation has been driven by a series of one-off shocks—first, tariffs, and later, higher energy prices from the war with Iran—that a central bank is supposed to look through rather than fight with interest rates. Some point to signs that the impulse from tariffs on imported goods prices has finally run its course.

更有耐心的官员认为，今年的通胀是由一系列一次性冲击推动的——首先是关税，然后是美伊战争导致的能源价格上涨——央行应该忽略这些冲击，而不是通过利率来应对。一些人指出，有迹象表明，关税对进口商品价格的推动终于结束了。

“There are encouraging reasons to expect that inflation has peaked and should edge down in coming quarters,” New York Fed President John Williams said last week.

“有令人鼓舞的理由预期通胀已经见顶，未来几个季度将有所下降，”纽约联储行长John Williams上周表示。

Earlier this month, Warsh cited falling bond yields and inflation expectations as evidence that markets already grasp his resolve on inflation. A big driver of those moves was President Trump’s truce with Iran. Yields have turned higher this week as [fighting resumed](https://www.wsj.com/world/middle-east/the-u-s-and-iran-creep-toward-a-wider-war-with-escalating-attacks-7323c060?mod=article_inline) and oil prices climbed.

本月早些时候，Warsh指出债券收益率和通胀预期下降，表明市场已经理解他对通胀的决心。这些走势的主要推动因素是特朗普总统与伊朗的休战。本周收益率回升，因为[战斗重新开始](https://www.wsj.com/world/middle-east/the-u-s-and-iran-creep-toward-a-wider-war-with-escalating-attacks-7323c060?mod=article_inline)且油价上涨。

#### **The case for hiking**

#### **加息的理由**

A growing number of officials worry their interest-rate setting, at 3.5% to 3.75%, is too low for an economy that keeps defying fears of a slowdown, with inflation still above its 2% target and new price shocks in play. Officials looked through the first Iran-related energy-price surge this spring, but may find a second one harder to dismiss. They have also pointed to the AI-related price increases as driven by demand that interest rates can restrain, unlike tariffs or oil.

越来越多的官员担心，对于一个持续打破经济放缓担忧的经济体而言，其利率设定在3.5%至3.75%的水平过低，而通胀仍高于2%的目标，且新的价格冲击正在显现。官员们此前对今年春季首次与伊朗相关的能源价格飙升进行了忽略，但可能发现第二次更难忽视。他们还指出，与人工智能相关的价格上涨是由利率可以抑制的需求推动的，这与关税或石油不同。

Even after stripping out one-off shocks, they argue, underlying inflation has been stuck near 2.5% for roughly a year—a sign the current setting isn’t restrictive enough. Lofty stock markets and easy borrowing conditions for companies suggest the economy could absorb higher rates without much difficulty. “Better modest restriction now than severe restriction later,” said Dallas Fed President Lorie Logan in a speech last week.

他们辩称，即使剔除一次性冲击，核心通胀大约一年来一直停留在接近2.5%的水平——这表明当前设定限制性不足。高企的股市和公司轻松的借款条件表明，经济能够吸收更高的利率而不会遇到太大困难。达拉斯联储主席洛里·洛根上周在一次演讲中表示：“现在适度限制比以后严厉限制要好。”

A hike would let Warsh define his chairmanship on his own terms. Raising rates over a president who has demanded lower ones would dispatch the idea, pressed by Democrats during his confirmation, that he is Trump’s “sock puppet.” Delivering on his vow before the rest of the committee forces the issue would show him leading it rather than trailing it. “One hike gets you more than you might think for your reputation when you are new to the job,” said Marc Sumerlin of Evenflow Macro.

加息将让沃什以自己的方式定义他的主席任期。在一位曾要求降低利率的总统任内加息，将打消民主党人在他确认提名期间提出的他是特朗普“傀儡”的猜测。在委员会其他成员之前兑现他的承诺，将表明他领导委员会而非落后于委员会。Evenflow Macro的马克·苏默林表示：“对于刚上任的人来说，一次加息对你声誉的好处可能超出你的想象。”

Several Fed officials who last week suggested they wouldn’t push for a July hike—including governors Lisa Cook, Christopher Waller, and Philip Jefferson—have said one will become harder to postpone if the inflation outlook doesn’t improve.

几位上周表示不会推动7月加息的美联储官员——包括理事丽莎·库克、克里斯托弗·沃勒和菲利普·杰斐逊——表示，如果通胀前景没有改善，加息将变得更加难以推迟。

#### **Reading the chairman**

#### **解读主席**

Some officials willing to hold steady next week may genuinely think a hike is unneeded, or they might simply be reluctant to force the hand of a chairman who hasn’t shown it. “Right now the data’s moving in his direction, where he can probably sit tight and buy time,” said Michael Gapen, chief U.S. economist at Morgan Stanley. “But the economy doesn’t always allow you to buy time.”

一些愿意在下周保持利率不变的官员可能真的认为不需要加息，或者可能只是不愿迫使尚未表态的主席做出决定。摩根士丹利首席美国经济学家迈克尔·加彭表示：“目前数据在朝着对他有利的方向发展，他可能能够按兵不动并争取时间。但经济并不总是允许你争取时间。”

The White House has cast pressure to raise rates as coming from Warsh’s opponents, so an increase could look like a chairman overrun by his committee. Trump this month said the board is “a little bit hostile” toward Warsh.

白宫将加息压力描述为来自沃什的反对者，因此加息可能看起来像是主席被委员会牵着鼻子走。特朗普本月表示，委员会对沃什“有些敌意”。

In the absence of guidance from Warsh, investors have taken their cues from wherever they can find them. A speech by Waller this month pushed pricing toward a July increase before the June inflation report pulled it back. Subsequent remarks from Williams and Jefferson reinforced expectations of a hold.

在沃什没有给出指引的情况下，投资者从任何能找到的地方获取线索。沃勒本月的一次演讲将市场预期推向7月加息，随后6月的通胀报告又使之回落。随后威廉姆斯和杰斐逊的言论强化了维持利率不变的预期。

Warsh, Williams and Jefferson form the Fed’s policy steering group, known internally as the troika. Under Warsh’s predecessor, Jerome Powell, the two vice chairs helped build support for a policy proposal they workshopped ahead of the meeting, giving markets a read on their thinking. The troika has continued to meet under Warsh, but he has said he wants “messier meetings” whose outcomes aren’t settled in advance. Heading into the premeeting quiet period last weekend, some of Warsh’s colleagues weren’t sure where he stood.

沃什（Warsh）、威廉姆斯（Williams）和杰斐逊（Jefferson）组成了美联储的政策指导小组，内部称为“三巨头”。在沃什的前任杰罗姆·鲍威尔（Jerome Powell）领导下，两位副主席帮助为一项政策提案建立支持，他们在会议前研讨了该提案，向市场透露了他们的想法。在沃什领导下，三巨头继续开会，但他表示希望举行“更混乱的会议”，结果不会事先确定。在进入上周的会前静默期前，沃什的一些同事不确定他的立场。

Many investors tend to hear what they already believe, said Gapen. To some, Warsh is a hawk in dove’s clothing—installed by a president who wants lower rates but, in their view, an old-school monetarist at heart who won’t let inflation fester. Others see the opposite: a dove in hawk’s clothing, talking tough on price stability while waiting for an AI-driven productivity boom to bring inflation down on its own, in no rush to provoke an unnecessary fight with the White House.

加彭（Gapen）表示，许多投资者倾向于听他们已相信的东西。对一些人来说，沃什是披着鸽皮的鹰派——由一位希望降低利率的总统任命，但本质上是一个老派的货币主义者，不会让通胀恶化。其他人则看到相反：披着鹰皮的鸽派，在物价稳定问题上言辞强硬，同时等待人工智能驱动的生产力繁荣自行降低通胀，不急于与白宫挑起不必要的争斗。

“I’m not convinced he can get away with that forever,” said Gapen.

“我不相信他能永远这样下去，”加彭说。
