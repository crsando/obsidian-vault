### 中文译文

## 阿里巴巴与长江存储融资引发供应担忧，中国科技股承压

### **要点**

- 中国科技股周一再度承压，反映市场对阿里巴巴集团控股有限公司（Alibaba Group Holding Ltd.）和长江存储科技有限责任公司（Yangtze Memory Technologies Co.）融资计划的供应担忧。
- 恒生科技指数一度下跌4%，科创50指数下跌3.8%，至约三周低点，此前上海证券交易所受理了长江存储的首次公开募股申请。
- 阿里巴巴和长江存储的股票出售进一步打击了中国市场情绪，因为阿里巴巴预期收益将被稀释，且市场担忧其他科技公司可能效仿。
中国科技股周一再度承压，反映市场对[阿里巴巴集团控股有限公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/BABA%20US%20Equity)和[长江存储科技有限责任公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1577197D:CH)融资计划的供应担忧。

恒生科技指数一度下跌4%，因电商巨头阿里巴巴在香港最大规模二次股票发行中[融资](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-23/alibaba-to-raise-10-billion-by-selling-shares-for-ai-expansion)800亿港元（102亿美元）。在内地市场，以芯片股为主的科创50指数下跌3.8%，至约三周低点，此前上海证券交易所受理了芯片制造商长江存储可能规模庞大的首次公开募股申请。

此次抛售发生之际，亚洲科技股也走弱，市场在包括英伟达在内的主要公司本周晚些时候发布关键财报之前，对广受欢迎的AI交易持久性产生了新的质疑。阿里巴巴和长江存储的股票出售进一步打击了中国市场情绪，因为阿里巴巴预期收益将被稀释，且市场担忧其他科技公司可能效仿。

上海承洲投资管理有限公司董事向晓天表示：“那些投资AI的公司都在通过发行新股来筹集资金——本质上是将AI投资的成本分摊给公众和投资者。这些支出尚未产生明确回报，因此无论通过股权还是债务为AI融资，投资者都会感到些许不安。”

阿里巴巴的竞争对手腾讯控股有限公司股价一度下跌4.1%，百度公司也下跌4.5%，尽管百度发言人称公司没有新的配股计划。

“阿里巴巴此举完全出人意料，”Union Bancaire Privee董事总经理Vey-Sern Ling表示，“如此融资必将对整个中国互联网板块的情绪造成负面影响。”

## Alibaba, YMTC Fundraising Hits China Tech Stocks on Supply Woes

### **Takeaways**

- Chinese technology stocks came under renewed pressure on Monday, reflecting supply concerns over the fundraising plans of Alibaba Group Holding Ltd. and Yangtze Memory Technologies Co.
- The Hang Seng Tech Index fell as much as 4%, and the STAR 50 gauge dropped 3.8% to its lowest in about three weeks, after the Shanghai bourse accepted Yangtze Memory's initial public offering application.
- The share sales by Alibaba and Yangtze Memory soured sentiment further in China given the expected earnings dilution for Alibaba and worries that other tech firms may follow suit.
Chinese technology stocks came under renewed pressure on Monday, reflecting supply concerns over the fundraising plans of [Alibaba Group Holding Ltd.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/BABA%20US%20Equity) and [Yangtze Memory Technologies Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1577197D:CH)

The Hang Seng Tech Index fell as much as 4%, as e-commerce giant Alibaba [raised](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-23/alibaba-to-raise-10-billion-by-selling-shares-for-ai-expansion) HK$80 billion ($10.2 billion) in Hong Kong’s biggest secondary share sale. In the mainland market, the chip-heavy STAR 50 gauge dropped 3.8% to its lowest in about three weeks, after the Shanghai bourse Shanghai Bourse Accepts YMTC’s Listing Application for Review the application of chipmaker Yangtze Memory’s likely sizable initial public offering.

The selloff came as Asian tech stocks also weakened amid fresh doubts about the durability of the popular AI trade ahead of key earnings from major firms including Nvidia Corp. later this week. The share sales by Alibaba and Yangtze Memory soured sentiment further in China given the expected earnings dilution for Alibaba and worries that other tech firms may follow suit.

“Those companies investing in AI are all raising funds by issuing new shares — essentially spreading the cost of AI investment across the public and investors,” said Xiang Xiaotian, director at Shanghai Chengzhou Investment Management. “There still haven’t been clear returns on those outlays and so whether the financing is through equity or debt to fund AI, investors will remain somewhat uneasy.”

Shares of Alibaba’s rival Tencent Holdings Ltd. dropped as much as 4.1%, while Baidu Inc. also lost 4.5% even after Baidu Has No New Share Placement Plan, Spokesperson Says it has no plan for a new share placement.

“Alibaba’s move was a complete curveball that came out of nowhere,” said Vey-Sern Ling, managing director at Union Bancaire Privee. “Raising funds like this will surely be negative for sentiment across the whole China Internet sector.”
