### 中文译文

## 中国再次加强军事采购审查，呼吁举报线索

中国军方正在呼吁公众举报火箭军中的疑似违规行为，进一步扩大了对这支处于习近平主席军队[整顿](https://www.bloomberg.com/graphics/2025-xi-china-military-officials-purge/)核心的军种的审查。

中国人民解放军周一在其采购网站上发布了一份[通知](https://www.plap.mil.cn/freecms/site/juncai/hydt/info/2026/2739189.html)，征集涉及货物和服务、建设项目、食品和医疗用品潜在违规行为的线索。通知提供了一个举报热线号码，专门向供应商、投标代表、军事人员及其他相关实体征集信息。

这一[请求](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2023-07-27/china-s-military-probes-slew-of-graft-issues-going-back-to-2017)呼应了2023年解放军装备发展部对可追溯至2017年的军用硬件采购不当行为进行的调查。那次早期的审查引发了大规模清洗，罢免了一系列国防领域的高级军官和高管。

最新通知没有明确当局将追溯审查交易的时间范围。提交截止日期为11月30日。

火箭军负责管理中国的常规导弹和核武库，是此前动荡中[受冲击最严重](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2023-12-29/china-s-legislature-ousts-nine-military-cadres-as-turmoil-widens)的军种之一。这一行动触及了军队最高层，导致中央军委副主席张又侠和何卫东接受调查。此次整顿引发了人们对世界最大规模常备军[战备状态](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2024-01-06/us-intelligence-shows-flawed-china-missiles-led-xi-jinping-to-purge-military)的质疑。

此次审查正值执政的中国共产党筹备2027年下一次五年一度的全国代表大会之际，预计届时党和军队领导层将进行调整。对采购活动的审查可能有助于当局对晋升官员进行审查。

### English Original

## China Renews Scrutiny of Military Procurement With Call for Tips

China’s military is asking the public to report suspected violations in the Rocket Force, extending scrutiny of a branch at the center of President Xi Jinping’s [shake-up](https://www.bloomberg.com/graphics/2025-xi-china-military-officials-purge/) of the armed forces.

The People’s Liberation Army on Monday posted a [notice](https://www.plap.mil.cn/freecms/site/juncai/hydt/info/2026/2739189.html) on its procurement website to solicit tips covering potential breaches involving goods and services, construction projects, food and medical supplies. It provided a hotline number for tipsters, seeking information specifically from suppliers, bidding representatives, military personnel and other related entities.

The [request](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2023-07-27/china-s-military-probes-slew-of-graft-issues-going-back-to-2017) echoes a 2023 inquiry by the PLA’s Equipment Development Department into possible procurement misconduct involving military hardware dating to 2017. That earlier review led to sweeping purges that removed a series of senior officers and executives in the defense sector.

The latest notice doesn’t specify how far back authorities will examine transactions. Submissions are due by Nov. 30.

The Rocket Force, which oversees China’s conventional missile and nuclear arsenal, was among the branches [hardest hit](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2023-12-29/china-s-legislature-ousts-nine-military-cadres-as-turmoil-widens) in the earlier upheaval. The campaign reached the military’s highest ranks, leading to the investigations of Zhang Youxia and He Weidong, who were vice chairmen of the apex Central Military Commission. The overhaul raised questions about [operational readiness](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2024-01-06/us-intelligence-shows-flawed-china-missiles-led-xi-jinping-to-purge-military) of the world’s largest standing army.

The review comes as the ruling Communist Party prepares for its next five-year congress in 2027, when changes to the party and military leadership are expected. The examination of procurement activity may help authorities vet officials for promotion.
