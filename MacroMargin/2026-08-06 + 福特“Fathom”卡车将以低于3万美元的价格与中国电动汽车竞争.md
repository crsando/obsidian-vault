### 中文译文

## 福特“Fathom”卡车将以低于3万美元的价格与中国电动汽车竞争

[福特汽车公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/F%20US%20Equity)的新款平价电动皮卡——名为Fathom——明年上市时起售价将低于3万美元，这是该公司为与中国低成本电动汽车竞争而做出的初步尝试。

这家总部位于密歇根州迪尔伯恩的汽车制造商周四公布了该车型的名称和29,945美元（含运费）的起售价，证实了其长期宣称的将该卡车定价在这一水平附近的计划。该公司表示，预购将于2027年初开始，交付将于当年晚些时候开始。

Fathom是福特首款基于全新通用电动汽车平台的车型，该平台最终将支撑一系列经济型电动车型。这款下一代电动汽车由[特斯拉公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/TSLA%20US%20Equity)资深人士领导的[绝密项目](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-05/ford-s-30-000-electric-pickup-aims-to-win-over-skeptical-buyers)在加利福尼亚开发，其设计更轻、更时尚、电力效率更高，单次充电续航更长，同时起售价远低于美国新车近5万美元的平均售价。

尽管中国汽车目前实际上被排除在美国市场之外，但该车型是对福特最终能否与[比亚迪](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/002594%20CH%20Equity)等中国汽车制造商生产的科技含量高、价格低廉的电动汽车竞争的关键考验，这些中国制造商的电动汽车正迅速在全球赢得市场份额。福特表示，Fathom将能够使用福特的Blue Cruise驾驶辅助功能，并在高分辨率触摸屏上提供Apple Maps等功能。

福特致力于打造一款美国人愿意购买的、可盈利的经济型电动汽车，这是这家拥有123年历史的汽车制造商在电池动力汽车领域取得突破的最新尝试。该公司12月计入了[195亿美元](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-12-15/ford-to-repurpose-ev-battery-plant-for-energy-storage-batteries)的费用，与其亏损的电动汽车业务的大规模重组有关，包括取消其销售缓慢的F-150 Lightning全尺寸电动皮卡。

这一努力正值美国电动汽车需求不确定的时期。自共和党议员和总统唐纳德·特朗普采取措施取消支持电动汽车的政策（包括7,500美元的消费者税收抵免）以来，电动汽车销量大幅下滑。

油价上涨正促使美国消费者转向更小、更省油的车型，越来越多的汽车制造商涌入中型卡车细分市场，在该市场中，坚固耐用的特性有助于提高利润率。

福特已经在Maverick皮卡上取得了成功，该车是上个季度该国[最畅销的混合动力车型](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-01/gas-prices-drive-shoppers-to-toyota-honda-as-gm-stellantis-sit-out)之一。

Fathom 将与另一款电动卡车新秀——Slate Auto 的 [简化版电动皮卡](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-24/slate-tests-electric-pickup-market-with-price-just-under-25-000) 正面交锋，后者将于今年年底上市。这款双座车型起售价约为 25,000 美元，标准续航里程为 205 英里。

## Ford ‘Fathom’ Truck to Vie With China’s EVs at Sub-$30,000 Price

[Ford Motor Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/F%20US%20Equity)’s new budget-conscious electric pickup — dubbed the Fathom — will start at just under $30,000 when it arrives next year, the company’s opening bid to become competitive with low-cost electric vehicles from China.

The Dearborn, Michigan-based automaker on Thursday revealed vehicle’s name and a starting price of $29,945 including shipping fees, confirming its long-stated plan to price the truck around that level. Pre-orders will begin in early 2027, with deliveries starting later in the year, the company said.

The Fathom is Ford’s first vehicle based on a new universal electric vehicle platform that will eventually underpin a series of affordable electric models. Developed in California by a [top-secret operation ](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-05/ford-s-30-000-electric-pickup-aims-to-win-over-skeptical-buyers)led by [Tesla Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/TSLA%20US%20Equity) veterans, the next-generation EV was engineered to be lighter, sleeker and more electrically efficient to go farther on a charge while still starting well below the nearly $50,000 average selling price of a new car in the US.

While Chinese vehicles are effectively blocked from the US market currently, the vehicle represents a crucial test of whether Ford can eventually compete with the tech-laden, low-priced EVs from Chinese carmakers such as [BYD Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/002594%20CH%20Equity) that are rapidly gaining market share around the world. The Fathom will be able to operate Ford’s Blue Cruise driver-assist feature and offer features such as Apple Maps on a high-resolution touch screen, Ford said.

Ford’s push to make an affordable, profitable EV that Americans will buy is the 123-year-old automaker’s latest attempt to break through in battery-powered cars. The company in December took [$19.5 billion](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-12-15/ford-to-repurpose-ev-battery-plant-for-energy-storage-batteries) in charges tied to a sweeping overhaul of its money-losing EV business, including the cancellation of its slow-selling F-150 Lightning full-sized electric pickup.

The effort comes in an uncertain period for EV demand in the US. Sales have plummeted since Republican lawmakers and President Donald Trump moved to unwind policies supporting EVs, including a $7,500 consumer tax credit.

Elevated gas prices are nudging US consumers into smaller, more fuel-efficient vehicles, and more carmakers are flocking to the midsize truck segment, where rugged features help plump up margins.

Ford has already found success with the Maverick pickup truck, one of the [top-selling hybrid models](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-01/gas-prices-drive-shoppers-to-toyota-honda-as-gm-stellantis-sit-out) in the country last quarter.

The Fathom will face off with another EV truck entrant, Slate Auto’s [stripped down electric pickup](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-24/slate-tests-electric-pickup-market-with-price-just-under-25-000), due at the end of this year. The two-seater starts at about $25,000 with a standard range of 205 miles.
