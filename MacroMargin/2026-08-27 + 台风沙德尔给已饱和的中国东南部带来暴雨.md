### 中文译文

## 台风沙德尔给已饱和的中国东南部带来暴雨

8月26日，台风沙德尔经过日本那霸时，近海可见大浪。*摄影师：Hiroaki Yamashiro/AFP/Getty Images*

台风沙德尔预计将于周五早些时候穿过中国东南沿海，这是一系列强风暴中的最新一场，这些风暴给中国带来了暴雨并破坏了农作物。

沙德尔目前位于东海，并正朝浙江省沿海城市温州移动。据美国联合台风警报中心称，该[台风](https://www.metoc.navy.mil/jtwc/jtwc.html)的最大持续风速高达每小时139公里（86英里），相当于一级飓风。

[James Caron](https://www.linkedin.com/in/james-caron-3956689/)，天气情报公司Atmospheric G2负责北美和亚洲气象业务的主任，表示：“这是一个物理规模很大的风暴，对于任何考虑中断影响的人来说，这比峰值风速数字更重要。”他补充说，台风将把暴雨倾泻在已经饱和的地面上，加剧洪水和山体滑坡风险。

![图片](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3c981cc90b14816489e0ef8dea20b4da/3c981cc9-0b14-81d1-b535-e27a5d3bd086-ad507b30c50a8f07518fe545e6323deb.png)

沙德尔将袭击三周前台风海豚（Dolphin）袭击过的同一地区。当时，该风暴继续向内陆移动，在多个省份降下暴雨，这些省份种植着[玉米和大麦](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-10/tropical-cyclone-brings-heavy-rains-to-china-in-risk-to-crops)。周一，中国国家气象中心警告称，恶劣天气“可能导致一些低洼[农田](https://www.nmc.cn/publish/agro/ten-week/index.html)出现涝灾”。

“强降雨可能导致迁飞性害虫降落并危害庄稼，温暖潮湿的环境还可能引发喜湿性病虫害的发展，”该公报说。

据[中国气象局](https://www.cma.gov.cn/wmhd/2011wzbft/2011wftzb/202608/t20260826_8005998.html)预报，浙江和福建两省可能迎来200毫米至400毫米（8至16英寸）的降雨，部分地区降雨量可达500毫米。

## Typhoon Saudel Brings Heavy Rain to Saturated Southeastern China

Large waves are seen offshore as Typhoon Saudel passes in Naha, Japan on Aug. 26. *Photographer: Hiroaki Yamashiro/AFP/Getty Images*

Typhoon Saudel is expected to cross China’s southeastern coast early Friday, the latest in a series of powerful storms that have lashed the country with heavy rain and damaged crops.

Saudel is currently in the East China Sea and tracking toward the coastal city of Wenzhou in Zhejiang province. The [typhoon](https://www.metoc.navy.mil/jtwc/jtwc.html) is packing top sustained wind speeds up to 139 kilometers (86 miles) per hour, according to the US Joint Typhoon Warning Center, making it equivalent to a Category 1 hurricane.

“This is a physically big storm, and that matters more than the peak wind number for anyone thinking about disruption,” said [James Caron](https://www.linkedin.com/in/james-caron-3956689/), director of meteorological operations for North America and Asia at Atmospheric G2, a weather intelligence company. The typhoon will dump heavy rain on already saturated ground, elevating flood and landslide risks, he added.

Saudel is set to hit the same region that Typhoon Dolphin struck three weeks ago. That system then tracked inland, dropping heavy rain across several provinces where [corn and barely](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-10/tropical-cyclone-brings-heavy-rains-to-china-in-risk-to-crops) are grown. On Monday, China’s National Meteorological Center warned of severe weather that “may lead to waterlogging in some low-lying [farmland](https://www.nmc.cn/publish/agro/ten-week/index.html).”

“Heavy rainfall can cause migratory pests to descend and damage crops, and the warm and humid environment may also lead to the development of moisture-loving pests and diseases,” the bulletin said.

Zhejiang and Fujian provinces could see 200 millimeters to 400 millimeters (8 inches to 16 inches) of rainfall, the [China Meteorological Administration](https://www.cma.gov.cn/wmhd/2011wzbft/2011wftzb/202608/t20260826_8005998.html) forecast, with some areas receiving as much as 500 millimeters.
