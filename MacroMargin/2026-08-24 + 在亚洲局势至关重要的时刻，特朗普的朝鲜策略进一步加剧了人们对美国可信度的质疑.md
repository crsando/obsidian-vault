### 中文译文

## 在亚洲局势至关重要的时刻，特朗普的朝鲜策略进一步加剧了人们对美国可信度的质疑

2019年6月30日，美国总统唐纳德·特朗普在韩国板门店分隔朝韩的非军事区会见朝鲜领导人金正恩。路透社/Kevin Lamarque/资料图片

**摘要**

- 特朗普缩减韩国军演，此举长期遭朝鲜反对
- 日本官员：与平壤恢复友好关系可能适得其反
- 盟友在安全谈判与习特会期间重新评估美国的支持
东京/台北/新德里，8月24日（路透社）——唐纳德·特朗普以牺牲长期盟友为代价向朝鲜金正恩递出的橄榄枝，正值美国在亚洲安全关系的关键时刻，从日本到印度的伙伴们正在重新评估他们对华盛顿的依赖程度。

美国政府官员和安全专家表示，美国总统上周突然决定[削减](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/trump-instructs-pentagon-reduce-military-exercises-with-south-korea-2026-08-16/)与韩国的军事演习，他称这些演习对平壤怀有敌意，这加剧了人们的担忧，即特朗普的不可预测性可能使长期维持该地区和平的同盟关系紧张。

日本担心，在开始棘手的安保谈判之际，自己也可能成为特朗普的目标；台湾正警惕地关注特朗普与中国国家主席习近平即将举行的峰会；印度则对自身在华盛顿战略棋盘中的地位越来越不确定。

“这一决定必将加剧该地区对美国安全承诺日益增长的疑虑，”政治风险咨询公司欧亚集团高级分析师、曾任美国驻日本和中国外交官的杰里米·陈表示。

加剧这种焦虑的是，韩国周一表示，在华盛顿以伊朗战争导致美军受限为由后，美国取消了原定下月与韩国举行的另一场军事演习。

一位白宫官员在被问及此类担忧时表示，特朗普对美国盟友未能支持这场战争感到沮丧，并明确表示其中许多国家必须增加自身防务开支。

据一位了解美韩官员讨论情况的人士透露，他[渴望重启与朝鲜的谈判](https://www.reuters.com/business/aerospace-defense/trump-wants-new-meeting-with-kim-jong-un-north-korea-says-not-so-fast-2026-08-21/)，并在国际舞台上取得胜利，以对抗其与伊朗之间不得人心的冲突。

#### 对东京而言“极其令人不安”

日本颇具影响力的议员、前防卫大臣小野寺五典表示，特朗普寻求与金正恩会面的努力——迄今只得到平壤[冷淡回应](https://www.reuters.com/business/aerospace-defense/trump-wants-new-meeting-with-kim-jong-un-north-korea-says-not-so-fast-2026-08-21/)——可能适得其反，助长拥有核武器的朝鲜的胆量。

上周四，他在富士电视台新闻节目中表示，这一前景对东京来说“极其令人不安”。尽管特朗普频频示好，平壤当天早些时候仍在其东海岸[发射了一连串弹道导弹](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/north-korea-fires-suspected-ballistic-missile-japanese-authorities-say-2026-08-20/)，触发邻国韩国和日本的警报。

更让日本官员担忧的是，在东京与华盛顿就承担驻日美军费用（美国最大海外部署）展开谈判之际，特朗普的处理方式。

一位不愿透露姓名的日本政府高级官员表示，日本正在为特朗普可能在没有预警的情况下要求大幅增加而做准备，这一要求可能在政治上难以接受。

分析人士称，特朗普还可能利用10月即将举行的美日军演作为杠杆。华盛顿和东京的官员表示，预计演习不会被缩减，但提醒说特朗普随时可能改变主意。

由于美国将资产和人员转移至与伊朗的冲突，被称为“利剑”（Keen Sword）的演习规模可能已经比2024年那一次要小。

这一转变——最近导致美国在亚洲唯一前沿部署的航空母舰“乔治·华盛顿”号（USS George Washington）启程前往中东，以接替陷入困境的“亚伯拉罕·林肯”号（USS Abraham Lincoln）——引发了人们对美国对中国威慑力正在被[削弱](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/trumps-asian-allies-fear-iran-war-will-sap-defences-against-china-2026-03-03/)的担忧。

五角大楼政策主管埃尔布里奇·科尔比（Elbridge Colby）本月访问亚洲期间表示，与盟友合作确保从日本延伸至菲律宾的所谓“第一岛链”沿线作战准备是华盛顿的优先事项。

#### 特朗普、习近平与台湾

对于中国日益增长的军事压力，台湾的担忧最为尖锐。台湾是一个由民主治理的岛屿，北京声称对其拥有主权。

特朗普推迟批准向台北出售140亿美元武器的一揽子计划，以维持与中国国家主席习近平的友好关系和脆弱的贸易休战。

随着特朗普准备下月在华盛顿与习近平进行最新一轮峰会，台北官员表示，他们预计华盛顿至少会将军售拆分成更小的部分，以避免过度触怒北京。

台湾国防部智库“国防安全研究院”的杨太正（Domingo Yang）表示，特朗普的言论与实际美国行为之间存在区别，并指出美国与台湾的情报共享、训练和巡逻仍然相当可观。

一架美国海军飞机周五[穿越台湾海峡](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/us-navy-aircraft-transited-taiwan-strait-it-says-2026-08-22/)，美国称此举展示了其对“自由开放的印太地区”的承诺。

安全分析人士称，特朗普的最新行动也动摇了印度的信心，美国可能浪费多年努力，无法将新德里拉近以制衡俄罗斯和中国。

此前，五角大楼于6月决定将其亚洲军事总部从印太司令部更名为太平洋司令部，一些分析人士认为这表明印度对华盛顿的战略重要性正在下降。

五角大楼否认有任何此类转变。但特朗普对印度出口征收高额关税，并多次声称他调解了印巴停火，这已使新德里感到恼怒。据两位知情印度消息人士称，印度官员现在对与美国达成长期防务协议持“非常非常谨慎”的态度。

“最新决定只会强化特朗普总统对外交事务的交易性看法，并提醒人们，即使在盟友之间，具体政策上的分歧也可能对更广泛的双边关系带来代价，”冲突监测组织武装冲突地点与事件数据项目的高级分析师珀尔·潘迪亚（Pearl Pandya）表示。

## Trump's Kim gambit deepens doubts over US reliability at a key moment for Asia

U.S. President Donald Trump meets with North Korean leader Kim Jong Un at the demilitarized zone separating the two Koreas, in Panmunjom, South Korea, June 30, 2019. REUTERS/Kevin Lamarque/File Photo

**Summary**

- Trump scales back South Korea drills long opposed by North
- Rapprochement with Pyongyang could backfire, Japan official says
- Allies reassess US support amid security talks, Xi summit
TOKYO/TAIPEI/NEW DELHI, Aug 24 (Reuters) - Donald Trump's olive branch to North Korea's Kim Jong Un at the expense of a long-standing ally comes at a pivotal moment for U.S. security ties in Asia, as partners from Japan to India reassess how much they can rely on Washington.

The U.S. president's abrupt decision to [cut back](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/trump-instructs-pentagon-reduce-military-exercises-with-south-korea-2026-08-16/) military drills with South Korea last week, which he described as hostile to Pyongyang, has reinforced fears that Trump's unpredictability could strain alliances that have long maintained peace in the region, government officials and security experts said.

Japan worries it too could find itself in Trump's cross-hairs as it embarks on thorny security negotiations; Taiwan is warily watching an upcoming summit between Trump and Chinese President Xi Jinping; and India is growing less certain of its place in Washington's strategic calculus.

"The decision will certainly fuel growing doubts in the region about U.S. security commitments," said Jeremy Chan, a senior analyst with political risk consultancy Eurasia Group and former U.S. diplomat in Japan and China.

Adding to the anxiety, the United States [cancelled](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/us-cancels-joint-marine-drills-with-south-korea-over-iran-war-related-force-2026-08-24/) another military exercise with South Korea scheduled for next month, Seoul said on Monday, after Washington cited constraints on U.S. forces due to the Iran war.

Trump has been frustrated by the failure of U.S. allies to support the war and made it clear that many of them must spend more on their own defence, a White House official said when asked about such concerns.

He is [eager to restart talks](https://www.reuters.com/business/aerospace-defense/trump-wants-new-meeting-with-kim-jong-un-north-korea-says-not-so-fast-2026-08-21/) with North Korea and secure a win on the international stage to counter his unpopular conflict with Iran, according to a person briefed on discussions between U.S. and South Korean officials.

#### 'EXTREMELY TROUBLING' FOR TOKYO

Trump's push to meet Kim - which has so far drawn a [frosty response](https://www.reuters.com/business/aerospace-defense/trump-wants-new-meeting-with-kim-jong-un-north-korea-says-not-so-fast-2026-08-21/) from Pyongyang - could backfire and embolden nuclear-armed North Korea, said Itsunori Onodera, an influential Japanese lawmaker and former defence minister.

Such a prospect is "extremely troubling" for Tokyo, he said on a Fuji TV news programme last Thursday. Despite Trump's overtures, Pyongyang [fired a volley](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/north-korea-fires-suspected-ballistic-missile-japanese-authorities-say-2026-08-20/) of ballistic missiles off its east coast earlier that day, triggering alerts in neighbouring South Korea and Japan.

What worries Japanese officials more is Trump's approach as Tokyo embarks on talks with Washington over how much it should pay towards the cost of hosting U.S. forces, America's largest overseas deployment.

Japan is bracing for the possibility that Trump could, with little warning, demand a sharp hike that would be politically difficult to swallow, said a senior Japanese government official, requesting anonymity due to the sensitivity of the matter.

Trump could also wield upcoming U.S. military exercises with Japan in October as leverage, analysts say. Officials in Washington and Tokyo said they did not expect the exercises to be curtailed, though Trump could change his mind at any point, they cautioned.

The drills, known as Keen Sword, may already be smaller than their 2024 iteration because of the diversion of U.S. assets and personnel to the conflict with Iran.

That shift - which recently saw America's only forward-deployed aircraft carrier in Asia, the USS George Washington, depart for the Middle East to relieve the beleaguered USS Abraham Lincoln - has stirred concern that U.S. deterrence against China [is being eroded](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/trumps-asian-allies-fear-iran-war-will-sap-defences-against-china-2026-03-03/).

During a visit to Asia this month, Pentagon policy chief Elbridge Colby said working with allies to ensure combat readiness along the so-called first island chain that runs from Japan through the Philippines is a priority for Washington.

#### TRUMP, XI AND TAIWAN

Nowhere are concerns about China's growing military pressure more acute than in Taiwan, the democratically governed island claimed by Beijing.

Trump has held up approval of a $14 billion package of arms sales to Taipei in order to maintain warm relations and a fragile trade truce with China's Xi.

As Trump prepares for his latest round of summitry with Xi in Washington next month, officials in Taipei say they expect Washington will at the very least carve up the arms sales into smaller chunks to try and avoid upsetting Beijing too much.

Domingo Yang of the Taiwan defence ministry's think tank, the Institute for National Defence and Security Research, says there is a distinction between Trump's rhetoric and actual U.S. behaviour, noting U.S. intelligence sharing, training and patrols with Taiwan remain substantial.

A U.S. Navy aircraft [transited the Taiwan Strait](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/us-navy-aircraft-transited-taiwan-strait-it-says-2026-08-22/) on Friday in a move the United States described as demonstrating its "commitment to a free and open Indo-Pacific."

Trump's latest actions have also rocked confidence in India, where the U.S. risks squandering years of efforts to draw New Delhi closer as a counterweight to Russia and China, security analysts say.

It also comes after the Pentagon's decision in June to rename its Asia military headquarters to Pacific Command from Indo-Pacific Command, a move some analysts say is a sign India is no longer as strategically important to Washington.

The Pentagon denied any such shift. But Trump's steep tariffs on Indian exports and repeated claims that he had mediated an India-Pakistan ceasefire had already exasperated New Delhi. Officials there are now "very, very cautious" about committing to long-term defence deals with Washington, according to two Indian sources with knowledge of the matter.

"The latest decision only reinforces President Trump's transactional view of foreign affairs and serves as a reminder that differences over specific policies, even among allies, can carry costs for the broader bilateral relationship," said Pearl Pandya, senior analyst at Armed Conflict Location & Event Data, a conflict monitoring group.
