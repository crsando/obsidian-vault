### 中文译文

## 中国在紫金矿业挫折后释放采矿并购新时代信号

科瑞德黄金（Allied Gold）多数股权持有的科特迪瓦阿格鲍（Agbaou）金矿的一名工人。
*摄影：Issouf Sanogo/法新社/盖蒂图片社*

作者：[安妮·李](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AVyL-lreEmY/annie-lee) 撰稿 August 3, 2026 at 7:28 PM GMT+8

欢迎阅读我们的商品驱动全球经济指南。今天，记者安妮·李探讨了紫金矿业对一家加拿大矿企的投资为何可能预示中国并购策略的转变。

在过去近二十年里，中国的金属巨头一直是矿业界最积极的并购者之一，在从刚果（金）到澳大利亚的多个国家投入了数十亿美元的资金。

这种大手笔可能已接近尾声，而紫金黄金国际公司[放弃](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-29/allied-gold-says-china-s-zijin-gold-terminates-takeover)以39亿美元收购加拿大联合黄金公司（Allied Gold Corp.）的交易，是迄今最明确的迹象，表明中国官员正在改变策略。

一段时间以来，质疑声一直笼罩着紫金的最新计划，因为在马里等国，令人不安的头条新闻增多，这些国家的矿区已成为武装团体[冲突的爆发点](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-22/militants-target-sahel-s-gold-networks-beyond-mine-gates)。联合-紫金黄金收购截止日期已于5月延长至7月29日。

与此同时，北京当局发出信号，表示希望[加大力度](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-03/china-picks-state-firm-to-help-coordinate-mining-deals-abroad)应对风险，尤其是在地缘政治和其他紧张局势加剧之际。

最终，虽然没有官方拒绝，但中国监管机构未能及时批准。紫金黄金是大型企业紫金矿业的子公司，它改变了方向，转而宣布以2.95亿美元收购股权。

北京的决定——以及其明显的解决方案——是值得注意的信号。

![图片](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3b181cc90b1481188a77f21234e54bc7/3b181cc9-0b14-81c7-924e-dacaabf91b37-3428b32b5cb39343dd9574bf7609de40.png)

一方面，它们证实了官员们早已知道的情况。中国终于正式从其激进的收购阶段退却，这是一个漫长的支出狂潮，帮助这个世界第二大经济体牢牢掌握了全球矿产供应链。

另一方面，它们表明较低的风险偏好并不意味着零投资。虽然北京不希望看到中国矿企在危险交易上挥霍，但仍希望鼓励战略性押注。

像紫金矿业收购联合黄金（Allied Gold）约9%股份这样的入股——为未来谈判敞开大门——可能会更加频繁。

这将与国家发展和改革委员会牵头的其他努力相契合，该委员会负责中国的经济规划，并已[引入](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-13/china-expands-strategic-mineral-toolkit-with-new-investment-firm)了一家名为广研国际投资有限公司（Guangyan International Investment Co.）的国有企业，以加强监督和风险管理。

联合黄金（Allied Gold）交易有其特殊性，包括在马里遇到的困难，那里圣战组织、叛军派系、亲政府民兵等发生了冲突。资源民族主义也在抬头，即生产国要求获得更大收入份额。

中国矿企以前也遇到过此类挑战。但今后，他们可能会以不同的方式应对。

### *—Annie Lee，彭博新闻*

## China Signals New Era for Mining M&A After Zijin Gold’s Travails

A worker at Allied Gold’s majority-owned Agbaou gold mine in Ivory Coast.*Photographer: Issouf Sanogo/AFP/Getty Images*
科瑞德黄金（Allied Gold）多数股权持有的科特迪瓦阿格鲍（Agbaou）金矿的一名工人。摄影：Issouf Sanogo/法新社/盖蒂图片社

By [Annie Lee](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AVyL-lreEmY/annie-lee) 安妮·李 撰稿August 3, 2026 at 7:28 PM GMT+8

Welcome to our guide to the commodities driving the global economy. Today, reporter Annie Lee explores why Zijin Gold’s investment in a Canadian miner could herald a change in China’s approach to M&A.
欢迎阅读我们的商品驱动全球经济指南。今天，记者 Annie Lee 探讨了紫金矿业对一家加拿大矿企的投资为何可能预示中国并购策略的转变。

For the best part of two decades, China’s metals heavyweights have been among the most acquisitive players in the mining world, making billions of dollars of investments from the Democratic Republic of Congo to Australia.
在过去近二十年里，中国的金属巨头一直是矿业界最积极的并购者之一，在从刚果（金）到澳大利亚的多个国家投入了数十亿美元的资金。

That splurge may be nearing an end, and Zijin Gold International Co.’s [abandonment](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-29/allied-gold-says-china-s-zijin-gold-terminates-takeover) of a $3.9 billion takeover of Canada’s Allied Gold Corp. is the clearest indication yet that Chinese officials are changing tack.

Questions had clouded Zijin’s latest plan for some time, as troubling headlines increased in countries like Mali, where mining regions have [become flashpoints](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-22/militants-target-sahel-s-gold-networks-beyond-mine-gates) for clashes between armed groups. An acquisition deadline was Allied-Zijin Gold Acquisition Deadline Extended to July 29 in May.

At the same time, authorities in Beijing were signaling that they wanted [greater efforts](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-03/china-picks-state-firm-to-help-coordinate-mining-deals-abroad) to counter risk, especially as geopolitical and other tensions increase.

Ultimately, while there was no official rejection, Chinese regulators failed to give their blessing on time. Zijin Gold, a subsidiary of giant Zijin Mining Co., switched course and announced a $295 million stake purchase instead.

Beijing’s decision — as well as its apparent solution — are signals worth noting.

On one hand, they confirm what officials had known for some time. China is finally, and officially, pulling back from its aggressive acquisition phase, a lengthy spending spree that helped the world’s second-largest economy gain a strong grip on the global minerals supply chain.

On the other hand, they show that lower risk appetite doesn’t mean zero investment. While Beijing doesn’t wish to see Chinese miners splurge on hazardous deals, it still wants to encourage strategic bets.

Stakes like the roughly 9% of Allied to be acquired by Zijin — leaving the door open to future negotiations — could become more frequent.

This would dovetail with other efforts led by the National Development and Reform Commission, which oversees Chinese economic planning and has [introduced](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-13/china-expands-strategic-mineral-toolkit-with-new-investment-firm) a state-owned entity called Guangyan International Investment Co. to improve oversight and risk management.

The Allied Gold deal had its peculiarities, including difficulties in Mali, where jihadist groups, rebel factions, pro-government militias and others have clashed. Resource nationalism, where producer nations demand a larger share of revenue, has also grown.

Chinese miners have encountered such challenges before. But from now on, they may prepare for them differently in future.

### *—Annie Lee, Bloomberg News*
