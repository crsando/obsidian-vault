### 中文译文

## 伊朗誓言抵抗美国扩大制裁，称华盛顿寻求谈判

开罗/华盛顿，8月25日（路透社）——伊朗承诺抵抗美国旨在孤立其经济的扩大制裁，并表示相信主要贸易伙伴将抵制这一施压行动，同时称华盛顿热衷于重启谈判。

在美国努力解决冲突近六个月后，财政部长斯科特·贝森特于周一[公布了这些措施](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/us-treasury-broaden-scope-secondary-sanctions-iran-source-says-2026-08-24/)，但并未实施最严厉的制裁。

尽管他表示继续与伊朗贸易的国家可能面临被逐出基于美元的金融体系的风险，但他拒绝给出时间表或指明可能针对哪些国家，称将给予它们时间遵守新指令。

伊朗议会外交关系委员会主席阿巴斯·古尔鲁表示，巴基斯坦陆军参谋长阿西姆·穆尼尔周一已将美国的信息传达给伊朗。

“似乎主要目标是重启已停止的政治进程，”他对ISNA通讯社表示。白宫和国务院未立即发表评论。

### 美国提议撤销制裁，巴基斯坦消息人士称

伊朗和巴基斯坦官员周一会议期间，伊朗表达了恢复和平谈判的总体意愿，同时[对制裁](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/whats-included-new-us-sanctions-campaign-pressure-irans-economy-2026-08-25/)表示关切，一位巴基斯坦政府消息人士说。

“巴基斯坦告诉他们，美国将在新一轮谈判之前或期间撤销那些决定，”该官员说。

美国财政部宣布对60名个人、实体和船只实施新制裁，但名单中未包含任何涉嫌为伊朗石油贸易提供便利的中国金融机构。

多年来中国一直是伊朗石油的最大买家，尽管自华盛顿7月中旬重启封锁以来，美国对伊朗港口的封锁已[削减了伊朗对中国的石油出口](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/looming-us-sanctions-iran-put-china-oil-buying-spotlight-2026-08-24/)。

美国总统唐纳德·特朗普表示，他预计下月将接待中国国家主席习近平；华盛顿方面希望避免对中国关键矿产出口施加任何限制。

中国周二表示，其与伊朗的合作是在国际法框架内进行的，不应受到干涉或破坏。

油价[连续第二日下跌](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/oil-prices-steady-investors-weigh-impact-expanded-us-sanctions-against-iran-2026-08-25/)，交易商对制裁影响不予理会，尽管市场参与者仍对伊朗持续扰乱航运的能力保持警惕。

### 油轮在霍尔木兹海峡附近遭袭

英国海事贸易运营机构称，一艘油轮周二在阿曼阿什希沙东北约9海里（17公里）处遭不明射弹击中并失去动力，该位置位于霍尔木兹海峡入口处。

2026年8月13日，伊朗德黑兰街头，人们走过描绘伊朗已故最高领袖阿亚图拉阿里·哈梅内伊的壁画。Majid Asgaripour/WANA（西亚通讯社）经由路透社/档案照片

阿曼外交大臣周二抵达伊朗，讨论该海峡的未来安排。

在最新制裁公布后，伊朗经济部长阿里·马达尼扎德表示，[伊朗已准备作出回应](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/what-are-irans-options-escalate-further-face-us-economic-threats-2026-08-24/)。

"敌人应该等待一次攻击，"他告诉国家电视台。他补充说，中国和俄罗斯都没有"接受"美国的措施，并预测其他国家也会抵制这些措施。

数周来，美国和伊朗都没有发动重大打击，但伊朗伊斯兰革命卫队发言人侯赛因·莫赫比准将誓言，如果伊朗的基础设施受到威胁，将对美国的利益和能源咽喉要道进行沉重打击。

伊朗和美国于6月签署了一项旨在结束战争的临时协议，这场战争始于2月美国和以色列对伊朗的袭击，但该协议很快受挫，伊朗恢复了船只袭击，扼杀了海湾地区的能源出口。

### 伊朗官员承诺调解访问很快见成效

[巴基斯坦军方](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/pakistan-iran-made-significant-progress-talks-conflict-minister-says-2026-08-25/)表示，调解国巴基斯坦在与德黑兰的最新会谈中取得了"重大进展"，会谈重点是防止进一步升级和重新开放霍尔木兹海峡。

"我们进行了非常建设性的交流，"陪同穆尼尔前往德黑兰的巴基斯坦内政部长穆赫辛·纳克维在X上表示。

伊朗总统办公室官员迈赫迪·塔巴塔巴伊（Mehdi Tabatabaei）发帖称，穆尼尔对伊朗的访问“取得了极具价值的外交成果，其成果将很快揭晓”。

《纽约时报》援引美国国务院的一份文件称，战争期间撤离的美国外交官最早可能于本周开始返回中东。

目前尚不清楚这是否与调解努力有关。

路透社/益普索周一结束的一项民调显示，美国公众对战争的支持率[降至冲突初期以来的最低水平](https://www.reuters.com/world/us/trumps-approval-holds-record-low-us-support-iran-war-falls-reuters-ipsos-poll-2026-08-24/)，特朗普的支持率在 11 月国会选举前创下新低。

船舶追踪公司 Vortexa 的临时数据显示，周一通过霍尔木兹海峡的石油运输量为每天 500 万桶，低于战前每天超过 2000 万桶（约占全球消费量的五分之一）的水平。

数千人在冲突中丧生，其中大部分在伊朗和黎巴嫩，而伊朗大部分常规军事能力已被削弱，其经济陷入困境，时任[最高领袖阿亚图拉阿里·哈梅内伊](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/iran-bury-slain-supreme-leader-culmination-mass-funeral-2026-07-09/)也被杀害。

但德黑兰仍能攻击海湾邻国并威胁油轮。其核计划的确切状态仍不得而知，美国和以色列旨在摧毁该计划。

### English Original

## Iran vows to resist widened US sanctions, says Washington seeks talks

CAIRO/WASHINGTON, Aug 25 (Reuters) - Iran pledged to fight back against expanded U.S. sanctions aimed at isolating its economy, expressing confidence that major trading partners would resist the pressure campaign and saying that Washington was keen to revive talks.

Almost six months into a conflict the U.S. has struggled to resolve, Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent [unveiled the measures](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/us-treasury-broaden-scope-secondary-sanctions-iran-source-says-2026-08-24/) on Monday but stopped short of the most punishing sanctions.

While he said countries that continued trading with Iran risked being forced out of the dollar-based financial system, he declined to give a timeline or identify which may be targeted, saying he would give them time to comply with the new directive.

Abbas Golroo, head of the foreign relations committee in the Iranian parliament, said Pakistan's army chief Asim Munir had delivered a message from the U.S. to Iran on Monday.

"It seems the main goal was to revive the stopped political process," he told ISNA news agency. There was no immediate comment from the White House or the State Department.

### US OFFERS TO REVERSE SANCTIONS, PAKISTANI SOURCE SAYS

Iran expressed a general willingness to resume peace talks during meetings between Iranian and Pakistani officials on Monday, while expressing concern [about the sanctions](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/whats-included-new-us-sanctions-campaign-pressure-irans-economy-2026-08-25/), a Pakistani government source said.

"Pakistan told them the U.S. will reverse those decisions either before, or during a new round of negotiations," the official said.

The Treasury Department announced new sanctions on 60 individuals, entities and vessels, but the list did not feature any of the Chinese financial institutions suspected of facilitating Iran's oil trade.

China has been the biggest buyer of Iranian oil for several years, although the U.S. blockade of Iran's ports has [cut Iranian oil flows](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/looming-us-sanctions-iran-put-china-oil-buying-spotlight-2026-08-24/) to China since Washington renewed it in mid-July.

U.S. President Donald Trump has said he expects to host Chinese President Xi Jinping next month; Washington is keen to avoid any curbs on China's exports of critical minerals.

China said on Tuesday that its cooperation with Iran is conducted within the framework of international law and should not be interfered with or disrupted.

Oil prices [fell for a second day](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/oil-prices-steady-investors-weigh-impact-expanded-us-sanctions-against-iran-2026-08-25/) as traders brushed off the impact of the sanctions, although market participants remained wary of Iran's continued ability to disrupt shipping.

### OIL TANKER STRUCK NEAR STRAIT OF HORMUZ

An oil tanker was struck on Tuesday by an unidentified projectile and disabled about 9 nautical miles (17 km) northeast of Oman's Ash Shishah, which lies at the entrance to the Strait of Hormuz, the United Kingdom Maritime Trade Operations said.

People walk by a mural depicting Iran's late Supreme Leader Ayatollah Ali Khamenei, on a street in Tehran, Iran, August 13, 2026. Majid Asgaripour/WANA (West Asia News Agency) via REUTERS/File Photo

Oman's foreign minister arrived in Iran on Tuesday to discuss future arrangements for the strait.

After the latest sanctions were unveiled, Iranian Economy Minister Ali Madanizadeh said that [Iran was prepared to respond](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/what-are-irans-options-escalate-further-face-us-economic-threats-2026-08-24/).

"The enemies should wait for an attack," he told state television. Neither China nor Russia had "accepted" the U.S. measures, he added, predicting that other countries would resist them.

There have been no major U.S. or Iranian strikes for weeks, but Brigadier General Hossein Mohebbi, a spokesperson for Iran's Revolutionary Guards, vowed heavy blows to U.S. interests and energy chokepoints if Iran's infrastructure is threatened.

Iran and the United States signed an interim deal in June aimed at ending the war that began with U.S. and Israeli attacks on Iran in February, but it quickly faltered and Iran resumed ship attacks that have strangled energy exports from the Gulf.

### IRANIAN OFFICIAL PROMISES RESULTS OF MEDIATING VISIT SOON

Mediator Pakistan made "significant progress" in the latest talks with Tehran that focused on preventing further escalation and reopening the Strait of Hormuz, the [Pakistani military](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/pakistan-iran-made-significant-progress-talks-conflict-minister-says-2026-08-25/) said.

"We had a very constructive exchange," Pakistani Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi, who accompanied Munir to Tehran, said on X.

An official at the Iranian president's office, Mehdi Tabatabaei, posted that Munir's visit to Iran "yielded highly valuable diplomatic achievements, the results of which will soon be revealed".

The New York Times cited a State Department document saying that U.S. diplomats evacuated during the war could start returning to the Middle East as early as this week.

It was unclear if it was linked to the mediation attempt.

U.S. public approval of the war [fell to its lowest level](https://www.reuters.com/world/us/trumps-approval-holds-record-low-us-support-iran-war-falls-reuters-ipsos-poll-2026-08-24/) since the conflict's early days, with Trump's popularity at a record low ahead of congressional elections in November, a Reuters/Ipsos poll that closed on Monday showed.

Oil transits through the Strait of Hormuz were at 5 million barrels per day on Monday, provisional data from shiptracker Vortexa showed, down from more than 20 million per day before the war or about one of every five barrels consumed worldwide.

Thousands of people have died in the conflict, most of them in Iran and Lebanon, while much of Iran's conventional military capacity has been degraded, its economy is struggling and then-[Supreme Leader Ayatollah Ali Khamenei](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/iran-bury-slain-supreme-leader-culmination-mass-funeral-2026-07-09/) was killed.

But Tehran is still able to attack Gulf neighbors and threaten oil tankers. The exact state of its nuclear program, which the U.S. and Israel aim to wipe out, remains unknown.
