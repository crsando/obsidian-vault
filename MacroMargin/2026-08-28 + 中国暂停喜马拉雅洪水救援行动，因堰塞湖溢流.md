### 中文译文

## 中国暂停喜马拉雅洪水救援行动，因堰塞湖溢流

尼泊尔发生大规模山体滑坡后，西藏形成的一个堰塞湖已开始溢流 © 拉萨流动消防与救援支队/法新社/盖蒂图片社

在西藏-尼泊尔边境发生灾难性泥石流后，由于灾害形成的湖泊开始溢流，可能向下游倾泻新的泥石流，中国已暂停现场救援工作。

周五宣布的暂停行动对吉隆边境口岸及下游[尼泊尔](https://www.ft.com/stream/176b1156-b0a4-3ea1-9f39-5eff7ccbf1dc)的救援工作造成打击，工作人员正争分夺秒地搜救幸存者。

尼泊尔当局表示，周三一场[巨大山洪](https://www.ft.com/content/7fbae161-96a0-4e72-9366-0f2f3f4667c8?syn-25a6b1a6=1)席卷边境地区的定居点，摧毁了沿途的多层建筑，目前已造成553人死亡，仍有1500多人失踪。

据中国官媒央视在现场的报道，山体滑坡在吉隆口岸上游10多公里、海拔高出口岸1000米处形成了一个湖泊。

报道称：“如此巨大的堰塞湖在口岸正上方持续溢流，构成极大危险，极有可能引发新的山洪或泥石流。”“现场救援工作不得不暂停，等待进一步指令。”

尼泊尔警方周五发布警报称，边境西藏一侧的一座水坝已“溃决”。尼泊尔军方发言人表示救援行动仍在进行中，但尼泊尔国家灾害管理局呼吁博特科西河和特里苏里河沿岸居民“高度警惕”，因为存在“进一步洪水的风险”。

紧急救援部门正争分夺秒地营救尼泊尔灾难性洪水的幸存者 © 新华社/路透社

在尼泊尔救援行动的中心比杜尔，靠近特里苏里河、位于首都加德满都以北，直升机将受害者和幸存者从上游受灾最严重的地区紧急运送出来。

“我们正在争分夺秒地挽救生命，这是我们现在的工作重点，”尼泊尔内政部长苏丹·古隆（Sudhan Gurung）周五在该地区视察时对英国《金融时报》表示。

尼泊尔总理办公室报告称，士兵们已救出被困在水电站隧道中的两名工人，并正在寻找更多人。

“即使在隧道条件十分困难的情况下，军方仍在继续搜寻，”该办公室表示。

这场规模巨大且来势凶猛的泥石流[由冰川雪崩引发](https://www.ft.com/content/0d794bd6-a7ec-465a-ba97-452c9928df4e?syn-25a6b1a6=1)，促使中国最高领导层做出了异常强烈的公开回应。

中国国家主席习近平周五在主持中共最高领导机构中央政治局会议前，为遇难者默哀，会议审议了此次灾害的紧急救援工作。

他还派遣排名第二的领导人李强牵头负责救援工作。分析人士称，官方媒体强调李强是奉总统之命前往，这是一个罕见的公开姿态，意在显示习近平的高度关切。

尼泊尔军人在山洪暴发后安抚获救的婴儿…… © Prabin Ranabhat/法新社/盖蒂图片社

...冲毁了尼泊尔努瓦科特等地区的整栋建筑 © Sulav Shrestha/新华社/美联社

中国官方通讯社称，截至周四晚间，已有近500名村民和555名游客从灾区疏散。该通讯社表示，截至周五上午，紧急道路抢修作业已推进至距吉隆口岸2.8公里以内。

中国消防员和救援人员正徒步、乘橡皮艇或由无人机吊运进入该地区，无人机也用于运送物资。画面显示，救援人员利用绳索穿越湍急的水流。

四川省消防救援机动总队拉萨支队支队长邹明奇表示，他的队伍驱车至距吉隆口岸5至8公里处，随后在海拔2800米处徒步跋涉最长五小时才抵达该区域。

“我们必须携带装备和搜救仪器，与搜救犬一起徒步前进，”他说，“内部情况复杂，植被茂密。”

与此同时，中国官方电视台表示，灾害形成的堰塞湖仍在扩大，估计蓄水量高达250万立方米，足以填满1000个奥林匹克标准游泳池。

## China suspends Himalaya flood rescue efforts as lake overflows

A barrier lake that formed in Tibet after a massive landslide in Nepal has begun to overflow © Lhasa Mobile Fire and Rescue Detachment/AFP via Getty Images

China has suspended rescue efforts at the scene of a catastrophic mudslide on the Tibet-Nepal border after a lake formed by the disaster began overflowing, threatening to send a new torrent of debris downstream.

The suspension, announced on Friday, is a blow to rescue efforts at the Gyirong border crossing and downstream in [Nepal](https://www.ft.com/stream/176b1156-b0a4-3ea1-9f39-5eff7ccbf1dc) as workers race against time to rescue survivors.

Nepali authorities have said that 553 people have been killed, while more than 1,500 are still missing after a [giant flash flood](https://www.ft.com/content/7fbae161-96a0-4e72-9366-0f2f3f4667c8?syn-25a6b1a6=1) on Wednesday tore through settlements in the border area, wiping out multistorey buildings in its path.

A lake was formed by the landslide more than 10km upstream and 1,000 metres above the Gyirong Port crossing, according to a Chinese state television CCTV report near the scene.

“Such a massive barrier lake continuously overflowing directly above the port poses an extreme danger, with a high risk that it could trigger new flash floods or mudslides,” the report said. “On-site rescue efforts had to be suspended pending further orders.”

Nepal’s police force issued an alert on Friday that a water dam on the Tibetan side of the border had “burst”. A spokesperson for Nepal’s military said rescue operations were ongoing, but the country’s national disaster agency called for people along the Bhote Koshi and Trishuli rivers to “exercise high caution” due to the “risk of further flooding”.

Emergency services are racing to save survivors of the catastrophic flooding in Nepal © Xinhua News Agency / Reuters

At the centre of the Nepali rescue effort in Bidur, near the Trishuli River and north of the capital Kathmandu, helicopters rushed victims and survivors from the worst-affected areas upstream.

“We are in a race to save lives, that’s our focus now,” Nepal’s interior minister Sudhan Gurung told the FT while visiting the area on Friday.

The office of Nepal’s prime minister reported that soldiers had rescued two workers trapped in a hydropower plant tunnel and were looking for more.

“Even in the difficult conditions of the tunnel, the military is continuing the search,” it said.

The size and violence of the mudslide, which was [sparked by a glacial avalanche](https://www.ft.com/content/0d794bd6-a7ec-465a-ba97-452c9928df4e?syn-25a6b1a6=1), have prompted an unusually strong public response from China’s top leadership.

China’s President Xi Jinping on Friday held a moment of silence for the victims before presiding over a meeting of the Communist Party’s top leadership body, the Politburo, to consider emergency rescue work for the disaster.

He also dispatched second-ranked leader Li Qiang to lead rescue efforts. State media highlighted that Li went at the president’s behest, a rare public gesture intended to show Xi’s grave concern, analysts said.

Nepali military personnel comfort infants rescued in the aftermath of flash floods... © Prabin Ranabhat/AFP/Getty Images

... that wiped out whole buildings in areas such as Nuwakot, Nepal © Sulav Shrestha/Xinhua/AP

China’s state news agency said that as of Thursday evening, nearly 500 villagers and 555 tourists had been evacuated from the disaster area. By Friday morning, emergency road repair operations had reached within 2.8km of the Gyirong Port, it said.

Chinese firefighters and rescue workers were entering the area on foot, by rubber boat or being dropped by drones, which were also being used to deliver supplies. Footage showed rescue workers crossing fast-flowing water using ropes.

Zou Mingqi, the commander from the Lhasa branch of the Sichuan Fire and Rescue Mobile Corps, said his team drove to within 5-8km from the Gyirong port before trekking at an altitude of 2,800 metres for up to five hours to reach the area.

“We have to proceed on foot carrying our equipment and search instruments with our rescue dogs,” he said, adding: “The conditions inside are complex and the vegetation is dense.”

Meanwhile, Chinese state television said the barrier lake formed by the disaster was still expanding and estimated to contain as much as 2.5mn cubic metres of water, or enough to fill 1,000 Olympic swimming pools.
