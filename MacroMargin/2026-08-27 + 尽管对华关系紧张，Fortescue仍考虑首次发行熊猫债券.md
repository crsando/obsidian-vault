### 中文译文

## 尽管对华关系紧张，Fortescue仍考虑首次发行熊猫债券

据知情人士透露，澳大利亚铁矿石开采商[Fortescue Ltd.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/FMG%20AU%20Equity)正考虑通过其首次熊猫债券发行筹集约30亿元人民币（合4.46亿美元）。

计划中的债券发行正值该公司与中国国有背景的铁矿石买家发生纠纷，导致其对华部分出口中断。

知情人士称，Fortescue计划于9月初与投资者会面，并可能最早于10月推销这批人民币计价债券。由于此事未公开，这些人士要求匿名。这些人士还透露，该债券将由其子公司Fortescue Treasury (China) Pty Ltd发行，并由母公司提供担保。

中国矿产资源集团（China Mineral Resources Group Co.）是一家国有背景买家，其[设立](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2026-04-01/china-pushes-mining-giants-for-iron-ore-pricing-power-in-challenge-to-bhp)以[加强](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-15/china-turns-on-the-australian-iron-ore-miner-it-helped-grow)中国在铁矿石采购中的议价能力。在供应协议谈判破裂后，该集团加大了对Fortescue的压力，这导致其对华出口结构发生转变，并促使公司[探索](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-31/fortescue-looks-to-markets-outside-china-as-cmrg-pressure-mounts)东南亚和印度的销售机会。

中国矿产资源集团在与[必和必拓集团](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/BHP%20AU%20Equity)早先的僵局中曾使用过[类似策略](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2026-04-01/china-pushes-mining-giants-for-iron-ore-pricing-power-in-challenge-to-bhp)。

熊猫债券作为一种成本更低的融资渠道，近来吸引了越来越多的兴趣。据彭博汇编的数据，今年外国政府和全球企业发行人发行的熊猫债券平均票息为1.88%，创历史新低。这比三年期美国国债收益率低了200多个基点。

“Fortescue的一位发言人在回应置评请求时表示：‘Fortescue不断评估实现资金来源多元化和进一步优化债务资本结构的方案。’该发言人补充说：‘这可能包括进入中国的熊猫债券市场，但需获得必要的监管批准并视市场情况而定。’”

根据本月早些时候的一份公开披露，Fortescue已申请在未来两年内于中国银行间市场发行至多200亿元人民币的票据。

### English Original

## Fortescue Mulls Debut Panda Bond Sale Despite China Tensions

Australian iron ore miner [Fortescue Ltd.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/FMG%20AU%20Equity) is considering raising about 3 billion yuan ($446 million) in its first-ever panda bond offering, according to people familiar with the matter.

The planned bond sale comes as the company navigates a dispute with China’s state-backed iron ore buyer that has led to a disruption in some of its exports to the country.

Fortescue plans to meet investors in early September and may market the yuan-denominated notes as early as October, the people said, asking not to be identified as the matter is private. The bond would be issued by its subsidiary Fortescue Treasury (China) Pty Ltd and guaranteed by the parent, the people added.

China Mineral Resources Group Co., a state-backed buyer [created](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2026-04-01/china-pushes-mining-giants-for-iron-ore-pricing-power-in-challenge-to-bhp) to [strengthen](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-15/china-turns-on-the-australian-iron-ore-miner-it-helped-grow) Beijing’s bargaining power in iron ore procurement, has been ratcheting up pressure on Fortescue, after negotiations stalled over a supply agreement. That’s led to a shift in its exports to China and has prompted the company to [explore](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-31/fortescue-looks-to-markets-outside-china-as-cmrg-pressure-mounts) sales opportunities in Southeast Asia and India.

CMRG used [similar tactics](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2026-04-01/china-pushes-mining-giants-for-iron-ore-pricing-power-in-challenge-to-bhp) in an earlier standoff with [BHP Group Ltd.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/BHP%20AU%20Equity)

Panda bonds have drawn increasing interest recently as a cheaper route for funding. Foreign countries and global corporate issuers have sold panda bonds at an average coupon of 1.88% this year, a record low level, according to data compiled by Bloomberg. That’s more than 200 basis points lower than the three-year US Treasury yield.

“Fortescue continuously assesses options to diversify its funding sources and further optimise its debt capital structure,” a company spokesperson said in response to a request for comment. “This may include accessing China’s Panda Bond market, subject to the necessary regulatory approvals and market conditions,” the spokesperson added.

Fortescue has applied to issue up to 20 billion yuan of notes in China’s interbank market within the next two years, according to a public disclosure earlier this month.
