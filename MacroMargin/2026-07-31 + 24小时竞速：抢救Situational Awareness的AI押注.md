## 24小时竞速：抢救Situational Awareness的AI押注

**彭博AI要点**

- Leopold Aschenbrenner的对冲基金Situational Awareness被迫出售数十亿美元迅速贬值的科技投资，因为紧张的银行开始对他的交易要求更多抵押品。
- 在Griffin与Aschenbrenner交谈后，亿万富翁Ken Griffin的Citadel对冲基金联系了Situational Awareness，并以折扣价迅速买入了这些投资。
- Aschenbrenner的公司资产从7月初的450亿美元暴跌至约100亿美元，但他仍在运营全球最大的股票对冲基金之一，并且预计最终只会受点皮外伤。
他被誉为AI界的“诺查丹玛斯”——一位对未来有大胆预言的年轻先知。

“不久之后，世界就会觉醒，”从科技预言家转行对冲基金经理的Leopold Aschenbrenner在2024年预言。本周，世界确实觉醒了——但并非以Aschenbrenner所预见的方式。

转瞬之间，他极为成功的对冲基金[Situational Awareness](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/2551833D:US)被迫出售数十亿美元迅速贬值的科技投资，因为紧张的银行开始对他的交易要求越来越多的抵押品。随后，亿万富翁Ken Griffin出现了。

据一位因涉及私人信息而要求匿名的知情人士透露，在不到24小时内——其中包括Griffin和Aschenbrenner的一次通话——Griffin的Citadel对冲基金联系了Situational Awareness，并以折扣价抢购了这些投资。

这对Aschenbrenner来说是一个惊人的逆转，他曾是OpenAI的研究员，大约两年前创办对冲基金之前没有投资经验。他的羽翼未丰的公司资产从7月初的450亿美元暴跌至约100亿美元。

![Ken Griffin Photographer: Aaron Schwartz/Bloomberg](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3ae81cc90b1481e6a9e9ec6eec877bac/3ae81cc9-0b14-81cf-93b8-ddb0bca6460a-d55de62bfdf6ba281fc8ae5a1dd9da67.png)

这一事态使Situational Awareness——以Aschenbrenner在2024年发布的一篇关于人工智能未来的爆款文章命名——走上了一条新的且不确定的道路，并让经验丰富的金融老手Griffin获得了主要AI公司的股份。

不过，随着Aschenbrenner混乱一周尘埃落定，这位二十多岁的年轻人似乎仍将只受些皮外伤。他继续管理着全球最大的股票对冲基金之一。而就在两天后，他将在加州卡梅尔谷的一个场地与他的未婚妻——[Anthropic PBC](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1892140D:US)首席执行官的幕僚长——结婚。

根据周四晚间发出的一封信，尽管Situational Awareness在7月迄今下跌约67%，但该对冲基金今年仍上涨约80%。如此极端的波动可能意味着，该基金的投资者可能会面临巨大的账面收益或损失，具体取决于他们何时向Situational Awareness投入资金。

“这个月我们让你们失望了，”Aschenbrenner在信中写道。

在Aschenbrenner作为对冲基金巨头的短暂职业生涯中，他既看到了围绕AI的极度乐观情绪，也看到了这仅仅是最新科技泡沫的强烈担忧。

据知情人士透露，近几个月来，包括[高盛集团](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/GS%20US%20Equity)、[摩根大通](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/JPM%20US%20Equity)和[美国银行](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/BAC%20US%20Equity)在内的银行都乐于助推他的崛起，一些公司甚至提出向该基金提供相当于其资本四到五倍的贷款。然而，随着科技股在过去几周开始下滑，这些贷款机构被证明不愿撑过这段时期。

Aschenbrenner开始清算头寸以应对接二连三的追加保证金通知。这些举措近日在全球市场引起反响，让投资者质疑这场暴跌背后的原因。

周三的抛售“说实话完全没有理由”，Oraclum Capital 首席投资官 Vuk Vukovic 表示。“背后一定有其他原因。”

“现在我们看到发生了什么，”他补充道。

甚至在本周的追加保证金之前，就有迹象表明华尔街开始变得谨慎。

随着部分持仓在最近几周创下历史新高，Situational Awareness 正在寻找更多银行来为其押注提供资金。据知情人士透露，该基金已接洽包括 [巴克莱银行](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/BARC%20LN%20Equity) 在内的多家银行，希望与它们的主经纪业务部门合作。

知情人士称，巴克莱拒绝了该基金，因为担心其对单一行业的敞口水平过高。该银行发言人拒绝置评。

高盛、摩根大通、美国银行和 Situational Awareness 的代表均拒绝置评。

本周早些时候，已向少数能够一次性买下大量股份的公司发出了电话联系。[千禧管理公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/2878538Z:US) 和 [简街集团](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1087299D:US)（也投资于 Situational Awareness）也在考虑该投资组合的公司之列。

该基金甚至[考虑](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-31/aschenbrenner-tried-to-sell-private-stakes-before-citadel-deal)出售部分有价值的私人投资以争分夺秒地筹集资金，并开始谈判出售其在 Anthropic 的 50 亿美元股份中的一部分。

Citadel（城堡投资）听闻此事后，联系了阿申布伦纳，后者最终与格里芬进行了交谈。经过整夜数小时的谈判，双方在周四开盘前达成协议，救助了该公司，并允许其保留所有私营公司。

Citadel、Millennium（千禧年）和Jane Street（简街）的代表拒绝置评。

阿申布伦纳在致客户的信中表示，他对这些事件承担全部责任，但将7月股价暴跌的部分原因归咎于做空者，这些做空者瞄准了他持有的股票。他还誓言在“我们从这些事态发展中吸取教训”的同时，以无杠杆方式运营其公开股票投资组合。

他还承诺将改变其投资组合的管理方式。

他写道：“我对你的核心承诺是，我们不会浪费从这些事件中学习的机会。”

### **FTX、OpenAI**

阿申布伦纳出生于德国，来到美国时年仅15岁，就读于哥伦比亚大学。

他在2024年的播客中表示：“我跳了几级，当时我觉得15岁上大学、来到美国很正常。”他还说：“我的一个妹妹现在快15岁了，当我看着她时，我理解了我母亲为何担心。”

在哥伦比亚大学就读期间，他创立了该校的有效利他主义分会——该运动研究如何以最高效的方式做慈善。他于2021年获得经济学和数理统计学学士学位，并成为[毕业生代表](https://www.college.columbia.edu/cct/latest/take-five/valedictorian-special-times-college)。有一次去华盛顿旅行时，他凌晨3点排队观看最高法院的口头辩论。

在2023年加入OpenAI之前，他曾参与运营FTX Future Fund，这是山姆·班克曼-弗里德的加密货币帝国的慈善分支，该帝国在数十亿美元的金融欺诈中崩塌。此后不久，他加入了山姆·奥特曼的OpenAI，成为其超级对齐团队的研究人员，该团队中包括苏茨克维的盟友，于2024年因涉嫌泄密而被解雇。

![OpenAI’s Sam Altman Source: Al Drago/Bloomberg](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3ae81cc90b1481e6a9e9ec6eec877bac/3ae81cc9-0b14-81bc-9742-e5cda3442122-d29bbe1ac193e1af2fedfb773bbe66ff.png)

公司称他因泄露信息而被解雇，而他声称自己对阻止外国敌对攻击缺乏兴趣一事发出了警报。

在他名为“情境意识”（Situational Awareness）的博客上发表一系列文章后，阿申布伦纳创立了同名对冲基金，并招募了其他年轻科技爱好者加入，其中包括他在哥伦比亚的朋友。他的早期投资者包括Stripe联合创始人帕特里克·科利森和约翰·科利森，以及丹尼尔·格罗斯和纳特·弗里德曼，两人如今都参与了Meta Platforms Inc.的AI项目。

在开启他投资生涯的博客中，他对AI的危险与前景进行了哲学式的论述。他甚至打趣道，如今全力押注英伟达或许看起来很棒，* *“但历史的负担沉重。我不会选择这条路。”

“认识到超级智能的力量也意味着认识到它的危险，”阿申布伦纳当时写道。“我们绝不能搞砸了。”

## The 24-Hour Race to Salvage Situational Awareness’ AI Bets

**Takeaways by Bloomberg AI**

- Leopold Aschenbrenner's hedge fund, Situational Awareness, was forced to sell billions of dollars of technology investments that had rapidly lost value, as nervous banks began to demand more collateral for his trades.
- Billionaire Ken Griffin's Citadel hedge fund reached out to Situational Awareness and snapped up the investments at a discount, after a conversation between Griffin and Aschenbrenner.
- Aschenbrenner's firm has watched its assets plunge from $45 billion at the start of July to about $10 billion, but he continues to run one of the biggest equity hedge funds in the world and is still set to come out only bruised.
He was hailed as the “Nostradamus” of AI — a young oracle with a bold prophecy for the future.

“Before long, the world will wake up,” Leopold Aschenbrenner, tech-seer-turned-hedge-fund-manager, predicted in 2024. This week, it did — but not in the way Aschenbrenner had foreseen.

In a blink, his wildly successful hedge fund, [Situational Awareness](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/2551833D:US), was forced to sell billions of dollars of technology investments that had rapidly lost value, as nervous banks began to demand more and more collateral for his trades. Then came billionaire Ken Griffin.

In less than 24 hours — which included a conversation between Griffin and Aschenbrenner — Griffin’s Citadel hedge fund reached out to Situational Awareness and snapped up the investments at a discount, according to a person familiar with the matter who asked not to be identified citing private information.

It was a startling reversal for Aschenbrenner, a former researcher at OpenAI who — before starting his hedge fund roughly two years ago — had no previous investment experience. His fledging firm has watched its assets plunge from $45 billion at the start of July to about $10 billion.

The development puts Situational Awareness — named after a viral essay that Aschenbrenner posted in 2024 about the future of artificial intelligence — on a new and uncertain course, and hands Griffin, a seasoned finance veteran, stakes in major AI players.

Still, as the dust settles on Aschenbrenner’s chaotic week, it looks like the 20-something is still set to come out only bruised. He continues to run one of the biggest equity hedge funds in the world. And in just two days, he’ll wed his fiancee — the chief of staff to the CEO at [Anthropic PBC](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1892140D:US) — at a venue in California’s Carmel Valley.

While Situational Awareness lost about 67% so far in July, the hedge fund is still up around 80% on the year, according to a letter sent late Thursday. Such extreme swings likely mean that investors in the fund may face large paper gains or losses depending on when they gave Situational Awareness money.

“We let you down this month,” Aschenbrenner wrote in the letter.

In Aschenbrenner’s short career as a hedge fund titan, he’s seen both the extreme optimism around AI and the intense fear that this is just the latest technology bubble.

In recent months, banks including [Goldman Sachs Group Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/GS%20US%20Equity), [JPMorgan Chase & Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/JPM%20US%20Equity) and [Bank of America Corp.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/BAC%20US%20Equity) were happy to power his rise, with some firms offering to lend the fund four to five times its capital, according to people familiar with the matter. And then, as technology shares started to slide in the past few weeks, the lenders proved unwilling to ride it out.

Aschenbrenner began liquidating positions to meet the barrage of margin calls. The moves reverberated across markets all over the world in recent days, leaving investors to question what was behind the carnage.

The selloff on Wednesday was “completely unwarranted to be honest,” said Vuk Vukovic, chief investment officer at Oraclum Capital. “There had to be something else behind it.”

“Now we see what happened,” he added.

Even before this week’s margin calls, there were signs that Wall Street was starting to grow cautious.

As some of its positions reached all-time highs in recent weeks, Situational Awareness was on the hunt for more banks that could finance its bets. The fund approached some lenders, including [Barclays Plc](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/BARC%20LN%20Equity), to work with their prime brokerage units, according to people familiar with the matter.

Barclays turned away the firm because it was concerned about the level of its exposure to just one sector, the people said. A spokesperson for the bank declined to comment.

Representatives for Goldman, JPMorgan, Bank of America and Situational Awareness declined to comment.

Calls went out earlier this week to a handful of firms that could buy up a big chunk of shares in one go. [Millennium Management](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/2878538Z:US) and [Jane Street Group](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1087299D:US), which also invests in Situational Awareness, were among the firms that considered the portfolio.

The firm even [considered](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-31/aschenbrenner-tried-to-sell-private-stakes-before-citadel-deal) selling some of its valuable private investments as it raced to raise capital, and began negotiating to sell some of its $5 billion stake in Anthropic.

Citadel, hearing about the troubles, called Aschenbrenner, who eventually talked to Griffin. After hours of negotiations throughout the night, a deal was struck just before the market opened on Thursday, bailing out the firm and allowing it to keep all its private companies.

Representatives for Citadel, Millennium and Jane Street declined to comment.

Aschenbrenner said he takes full responsibility for the events, but attributed some of the reasoning for July’s plummet on short sellers, who targeted the shares he owned, he wrote in the client letter. He also vowed to run his public stock portfolio without leverage “while we draw the lessons from these developments,” he added.

And he pledged to make changes to the way he manages the portfolio.

“My core promise to you is that we will not waste the opportunity to learn from these events,” he wrote.

### **FTX, OpenAI**

Just 15 years old when he came to the US, Aschenbrenner, who was born in Germany, enrolled at Columbia University.

“I skipped a few grades, and it seemed normal to me at the time to go to college at 15 and come to America,” he said on a podcast in 2024. “One of my sisters is turning 15 now, and when I look at her, I understand why my mother was worried.”

While at Columbia, he founded its effective altruism chapter — the movement that studies the most efficient way to be philanthropic. He graduated with a B.A. in economics and mathematics statistics in 2021 and was [valedictorian](https://www.college.columbia.edu/cct/latest/take-five/valedictorian-special-times-college). Once, on a trip to Washington, he waited in line at 3 a.m. to watch oral arguments at the Supreme Court.

Before joining OpenAI in 2023, he helped run the FTX Future Fund, a philanthropic arm of Sam Bankman-Fried’s crypto empire that fell apart in a multibillion-dollar financial fraud. Soon after that, he joined Sam Altman’s OpenAI, where he was OpenAI Researchers, Including Ally of Sutskever, Fired for Alleged Leaking in 2024 from its Superalignment team.

The company said he was let go for leaking information, while he claims he raised the alarm over lack of interest in stopping foreign adversarial attacks.

After a series of posts on his blog, Situational Awareness, Aschenbrenner started a hedge fund by the same name and recruited other young tech enthusiasts to join him, including friends from Columbia. His early investors included [Stripe](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/0170016D:US) co-founders Patrick and John Collison, as well as Daniel Gross and Nat Friedman, who are both now involved in [Meta Platforms Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/META%20US%20Equity)’s AI efforts.

In the blog that launched his investing career, he waxed philosophically about both the dangers and promise of AI. He even quipped that going all-in on Nvidia might look great now,* *“but the burdens of history are heavy. I would not choose this.”

“Recognizing the power of superintelligence also means recognizing its peril,” Aschenbrenner wrote at the time. “We need to not screw it up.”
