### 中文译文

## 长鑫存储缺席令沪深300指数缺失中国最大股票

据彭博行业研究，若沪深300指数未纳入存储巨头[长鑫存储（CXMT Corp.）](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/688825%20CH%20Equity)，该指数作为中国关键基准指数的吸引力或将减弱，而这一纳入预计要到2027年底才可能发生。

沪深300指数的规则禁止在上交所科创板（STAR market）上市的公司在其上市第一年内纳入该指数。除非豁免，该规则将适用于长鑫存储，该公司上月科创板[首秀](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-26/china-memory-champion-cxmt-set-to-debut-after-9-8-billion-ipo)表现抢眼，成为中国市值最大的上市公司。

跟踪沪深300指数的交易所交易基金可能在长鑫存储被纳入之前遭遇资金流出，彭博行业研究分析师（包括Rebecca Sin）在一份报告中写道。他们补充道：“在那之前，该指数不会纳入市场上最大的股票，就像剔除英伟达的标普500指数一样。”这里指的是美国芯片巨头。

随着中国倚重人工智能和科技来推动经济和股市持续反弹，这一问题日益凸显。机构投资者可能错失一些国内最重要公司的早期上涨行情，这可能促使他们转向[科创板50指数](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/STAR50%20Index)等能更大程度覆盖新兴技术的指标。

随着其他知名科技公司（如芯片制造商[长江存储科技有限责任公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1577197D:CH)）计划进行首次公开募股（可能在上交所科创板），情况可能进一步恶化。[宇树机器人](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/688836%20CH%20Equity)已经确认[计划](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-06/china-s-unitree-seeks-904-million-in-first-mainland-robotic-ipo)在该板块上市，人工智能开发商[深度求索](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/7574380Z:US)也在考虑IPO。

“这些公司可能在纳入沪深300指数之前面临漫长且重叠的等待期，在此期间，该指数对推动中国下一阶段增长的上市公司的覆盖将相对较小。”彭博行业研究分析师表示。

鉴于长鑫存储市值约3.5万亿元人民币（合5240亿美元），其缺席该基准指数将十分显眼。据彭博行业研究，该公司最终将进入沪深300指数，权重约为7.8%，与英伟达在标普500指数中的权重相当。

彭博行业研究分析师表示，编制沪深300指数的中证指数有限公司可能会给予长鑫存储豁免以加快纳入，但这种可能性不大。

[SpaceX](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/SPCX%20US%20Equity) 在美国同样被快速推进上市，但到8月初便跌破发行价。他们写道，CXMT 若出现类似结果，可能拖累沪深300指数，并“对市场造成声誉损害”。

他们表示，与此同时，CXMT 很可能在未来几个月内以约9%的权重纳入科创50指数。

科创50指数今年已上涨28%，而沪深300指数仅微升0.7%

“中国的AI资本支出周期相当于美国2023年时的水平，而科创50指数为这场支出热潮的赢家提供了最高的敞口，”法国兴业银行分析师（包括Manish Kabra）在报告中写道。

### English Original

## CXMT’s Absence Leaves CSI 300 Missing China’s Biggest Stock

China’s CSI 300 Index risks losing its appeal as the nation’s key benchmark until it includes memory giant [CXMT Corp.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/688825%20CH%20Equity), an event that’s unlikely to happen until the end of 2027, according to Bloomberg Intelligence.

The CSI 300’s rules bar companies listed on the Shanghai Stock Exchange’s tech-heavy STAR market from joining the index during their first year. Unless waived, this rule would apply to CXMT, which had a blockbuster [debut](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-26/china-memory-champion-cxmt-set-to-debut-after-9-8-billion-ipo) last month on STAR to become China’s largest listed company.

Exchange-traded funds tracking the CSI 300 may suffer outflows until CXMT is added, Bloomberg Intelligence analysts including Rebecca Sin wrote in a note. “Until then, the index won’t include the market’s biggest stock, resembling the S&P 500 stripped of Nvidia,” they added, referring to the US chip giant.

The issue is coming to the forefront as China leans on AI and tech to power the economy and a lasting stock rebound. Institutional investors risk missing out on early gains of some of the nation’s most consequential companies, potentially pushing them toward gauges like the [Star 50 Index](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/STAR50%20Index), which offers greater exposure to emerging technology.

The situation may worsen as other high-profile tech firms, such as chipmaker [Yangtze Memory Technologies Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1577197D:CH), plan initial public offerings, potentially on STAR. [Unitree Robotics](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/688836%20CH%20Equity) has already confirmed that it [plans to list ](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-06/china-s-unitree-seeks-904-million-in-first-mainland-robotic-ipo)on that board, and AI developer [DeepSeek](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/7574380Z:US) is also eyeing an IPO.

“These companies might face extended, overlapping periods of waiting before they join the CSI 300, during which time the index would provide relatively little exposure to the listed companies driving China’s next phase of growth,” the BI analysts said.

CXMT’s absence in the benchmark, given its market value of about 3.5 trillion yuan ($524 billion), would be conspicuous. The firm will eventually enter the CSI 300 with a weighting of around 7.8%, commanding roughly the same weight as Nvidia Corp. has in the S&P 500, according to BI.

China Securities Index, which runs the CSI 300, could grant CXMT an exemption for faster entry, but that’s unlikely, BI analysts said.

[SpaceX](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/SPCX%20US%20Equity) was similarly fast-tracked in the US, only to fall below its IPO price by early August. Such an outcome for CXMT could drag the CSI 300 down and “cause reputational damage to the market,” they wrote.

Meanwhile, CXMT is likely to enter the Star 50 Index in the coming months with a weight of around 9%, they said.

The STAR 50 Index has climbed 28% this year, while the CSI 300 has edged up by 0.7%

“China’s AI capex cycle is where the US was in 2023, and the Star 50 Index provides the highest exposure to the winners of that spending boom,” Societe Generale analysts including Manish Kabra wrote in a note.
