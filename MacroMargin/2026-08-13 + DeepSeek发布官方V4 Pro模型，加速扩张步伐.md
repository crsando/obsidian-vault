### 中文译文

## DeepSeek发布官方V4 Pro模型，加速扩张步伐

2025年1月29日拍摄的插图中可以看到DeepSeek标志和“人工智能AI”字样。路透社/Dado Ruvic/插图/资料图片

北京，8月13日（路透社） - 中国人工智能初创公司DeepSeek周四正式发布其官方V4 Pro模型，旨在在扩大招聘、计算能力和融资努力的背景下，收复对阵国内快速发展的竞争对手的失地。

DeepSeek表示，V4-Pro-0813大幅增强了智能体能力，并可通过API、APP和Web渠道使用。该公司还表示，将[提高其V4 Pro和V4 Flash模型的API定价](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/deepseek-raises-api-pricing-its-v4-models-2026-08-13/)，并引入峰谷定价。

此次发布将受到密切关注，此前DeepSeek更便宜的V4 Flash模型在几项独立测试中意外优于4月份预览版的V4 Pro。这很不寻常，因为Pro被设计为该公司更强大的产品，表明DeepSeek在预览版发布和正式发布之间迅速改进了技术。

DeepSeek在2025年初其R1模型走红后成为中国最受关注的人工智能公司之一，引发了关于是否可以以远低于美国竞争对手的成本构建强大人工智能系统的问题。

其早期领先地位此后受到中国竞争对手一系列发布的挑战，包括月之暗面（Moonshot AI）、智谱AI、MiniMax、阿里巴巴和字节跳动。

DeepSeek还面临着将其知名度转化为持久业务的更艰巨任务。路透社7月报道，该公司计划以约740亿美元的估值进行新一轮融资，此前在6月完成了首轮外部融资，筹集约74亿美元。

这轮融资标志着该公司长期以来回避外部资本的转变。这也反映出在人工智能领域竞争的成本不断上升，需要大量投资于计算机芯片、数据中心和专业人才。

DeepSeek表示，其目标是在数据中心和AI代理团队等部门至少将人员编制增加一倍。路透社7月还报道称，该公司增加了对芯片设计工程师的私下招聘，以开发自己的AI芯片，此举可能减少对英伟达和华为等供应商的依赖。

报道：Eduardo Baptista；编辑：Kirsten Donovan

## DeepSeek releases official V4 Pro model as it steps up expansion

The Deepseek logo and words reading "Artificial Intelligence AI" are seen in this illustration taken on January 29, 2025. REUTERS/Dado Ruvic/Illustration/File Photo

BEIJING, Aug 13 (Reuters) - Chinese artificial intelligence startup DeepSeek on Thursday formally released its official V4 Pro model, aiming to regain ground against fast-moving domestic rivals as it expands hiring, computing capacity and fundraising efforts.

DeepSeek said the V4-Pro-0813 ‌greatly enhances agent capabilities and is available through the API, APP and Web channels. The company also said it will [raise API pricing](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/deepseek-raises-api-pricing-its-v4-models-2026-08-13/) for its V4 Pro and V4 Flash models and introduce peak and off-peak pricing.

The release will be closely watched after DeepSeek's cheaper V4 Flash model unexpectedly outperformed the April preview of ⁠V4 Pro in several independent tests. That was unusual because Pro is designed as the company's more capable product, suggesting DeepSeek had improved its technology rapidly between the preview release and the official launch.

DeepSeek became one of China's most closely watched AI companies after its R1 model went viral in early 2025, raising questions about whether powerful AI systems could be built at much lower cost than those of U.S. rivals.

Its early lead has since been challenged by a string of releases from Chinese ‌competitors ⁠including Moonshot AI, Zhipu AI, MiniMax, Alibaba and ByteDance.

DeepSeek has also faced the harder task of turning its prominence into a lasting business. Reuters reported in July that the company was planning a new fundraising round at a valuation of about $74 billion, weeks after raising about $7.4 ⁠billion in its first outside financing round in June.

The fundraising marked a change for a company that had long avoided external capital. It also reflects the growing cost of competing in AI, ⁠which requires large investments in computer chips, data centres and specialised staff.

DeepSeek has said it aims to at least double staffing across departments, including data-centre and AI-agent ⁠teams. Reuters also reported in July that it had increased private hiring of chip-design engineers to develop its own AI chip, an effort that could reduce reliance on suppliers including Nvidia and Huawei.
