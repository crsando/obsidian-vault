## 交易商和追踪机构称，中石化加大俄罗斯石油进口以弥补中东供应削减

![China's crude stocks drain deepens in June despite biggest throughput tumble on record](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3b481cc90b148146947bc29e3fcd3726/3b481cc9-0b14-8102-91c8-f329b48bb821-a2fac9e056323306773f53c74ac8211c.jpg)

- 摘要
- 公司
- 俄罗斯石油采购弥补了沙特供应削减
- 伊朗战争前，中石化一半的原油购自中东
- 消息人士称，中石化购买了7月至9月交付的30至40船俄罗斯原油
新加坡，8月6日（路透社）——据多位贸易消息人士和船舶追踪数据，中国国有企业中石化（Sinopec Corp）——全球最大炼油商——已加大购买俄罗斯远东石油，以弥补因[伊朗战争](https://www.reuters.com/world/iran/)而减少的中东供应。

中石化购买的俄罗斯石油价格低于巴西和西非等地的同类原油，这帮助其保持相对稳定的加工量，并凭借强劲的出口利润率出口剩余燃料，尽管为应对战争相关的贸易中断、保护国内供应，中国自3月起限制了燃料产品的海外销售。

中石化 [（600028.SS，打开新标签页）](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/600028.SS) 已购买总计30至40批、约每日24.1万至32万桶（bpd）的俄罗斯东西伯利亚-太平洋（ESPO）混合原油，用于7月至9月交付。据数位不愿透露姓名的消息人士称。这相当于该炼油商每日520万桶加工能力的5%至6%。

中石化一位代表表示，公司不公开讨论运营事宜。

船舶追踪机构Vortexa Analytics的首席中国分析师Emma Li表示：“中石化的原油需求在燃料出口限制放松后似乎已经触底，但复苏仍具有选择性。”

全球最大买家中国自伊朗战争爆发以来大幅削减了其整体原油进口量，[6月购买量](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/chinas-june-oil-imports-hit-near-10-year-low-amid-iran-war-2026-07-14/)较去年同期水平骤降41%。然而，中国已于7月和8月放松了[燃料出口限制](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/china-eases-controls-fuel-exports-second-month-sources-say-2026-08-05/)。

Li对路透社表示：“需求并非全面增长，而是转向交付确定性更高、运费更低的原油——主要是陆上库存和短途俄罗斯远东货物。”

根据Li的货运追踪，中石化7月获得了约740万桶ESPO原油，大部分运往山东省炼油中心日照港。

据Li和四位密切关注ESPO交易的交易商称，该炼油商为8月和9月各购买了至少10批货物。ESPO通常使用可装载74万桶的阿芙拉型油轮运输。

### 沙特大幅削减供应

自乌克兰战争爆发以来，中国和印度一直是俄罗斯石油的最大买家，但包括中国石化在内的中国大型国有炼油企业在华盛顿对俄罗斯主要生产商俄罗斯石油公司（Rosneft）[(ROSN.MM)，打开新标签页](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/ROSN.MM)和卢克石油公司（Lukoil）[(LKOH.MM)，打开新标签页](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/LKOH.MM)实施制裁后，于10月暂停了此类购买，路透社此前报道。

北京不承认其所称的单边制裁，中国独立炼油商继续购买俄罗斯石油。

在获得美国临时豁免后，中国石化于3月和4月恢复了俄罗斯石油采购，[路透社报道](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/sinopec-buys-russian-oil-replace-mideast-supply-after-us-waiver-sources-say-2026-04-14/)，购买约10船货物，并在豁免到期后因伊朗战争导致供应紧张而增加了购买量。

四位知情人士表示，其近期的ESPO采购未涉及受制裁实体作为交易对手，且均通过中介进行，但未提供更多细节。自乌克兰战争初期以来，中国石化一直使用人民币购买俄罗斯石油。

在伊朗战争之前，中国石化近一半的原油来自中东，并且是沙特阿拉伯最大的客户之一。

据贸易消息人士称，中国石化6月和7月未购买任何沙特原油，8月仅购买了200万桶。这远低于其在3月和4月各进口的2000万桶沙特原油，也低于伊朗战争开始前一年内平均每月进口1100万桶的五分之一。

交易商称，9月装载的ESPO原油较基准布伦特原油每桶折价1至2美元，比中东阿曼原油和巴西图皮原油等竞争品级每桶便宜约10美元。

在伊朗战争之前，俄罗斯ESPO原油每桶折价约10美元。

## Sinopec steps up Russian oil imports to offset Mideast supply cuts, traders and tracker say

SINGAPORE, Aug 6 (Reuters) - China's state-owned Sinopec Corp, the world's biggest refiner, has ramped up purchases of Far East Russian oil to compensate for Middle East supplies diminished by the [Iran war](https://www.reuters.com/world/iran/), according to multiple trade sources and ship tracking data.

Sinopec's purchases ‌of Russian oil, which is cheaper than rival grades from sources such as Brazil and West Africa, have helped it maintain relatively stable throughput and ship surplus fuel on strong export margins, despite China limiting overseas sales of fuel products from March to protect domestic supply amid the war-related trade disruptions.

Sinopec [(600028.SS), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/600028.SS) has bought a total of 30 to 40 shipments, or about 241,000 to 320,000 barrels per day (bpd), of Russia's Eastern Siberia-Pacific Ocean (ESPO) blend for July to September deliveries, according to several of the sources, who spoke on condition of anonymity. That equates to 5% to 6% of the refiner's processing capacity ⁠of 5.2 million bpd.

A Sinopec representative said the company does not publicly discuss operational matters.

"Sinopec's crude demand appears to have bottomed out following the easing of fuel export restrictions, but the recovery remains selective," said Emma Li, lead China analyst with ship tracker Vortexa Analytics.

Top global buyer China has cut its overall crude imports by steep levels since the start of the Iran war, with [June purchases](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/chinas-june-oil-imports-hit-near-10-year-low-amid-iran-war-2026-07-14/) plunging 41% from year-earlier levels. However, it has dialled back its [fuel export curbs](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/china-eases-controls-fuel-exports-second-month-sources-say-2026-08-05/) for July and August.

"Rather than broad-based import growth, demand is shifting towards barrels with greater delivery certainty and lower freight costs - primarily onshore inventories and short-haul Russian Far East cargoes," Li told Reuters.

Sinopec secured about 7.4 million barrels of ESPO in July, mostly delivered into Rizhao port in the refining hub of Shandong province, according to Li's tracking of shipments.

The refiner has bought at least 10 cargoes each for August and September, according to Li and four traders who closely follow ESPO trade. ESPO is typically ‌shipped in ⁠Aframax vessels capable of carrying 740,000 barrels.

### STEEP SAUDI CUTS

China and India have been the biggest Russian oil buyers since the Ukraine war started, but China's big state refiners, including Sinopec, [suspended](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/china-state-oil-majors-suspend-russian-oil-buys-due-sanctions-sources-say-2025-10-23/) such purchases in October after Washington imposed sanctions on top Russian producers Rosneft [(ROSN.MM), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/ROSN.MM) and Lukoil [(LKOH.MM), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/LKOH.MM), Reuters reported previously.

Beijing does not recognise what it calls unilateral sanctions, and independent Chinese refiners have continued buying Russian oil.

Sinopec resumed Russian oil purchases in March and April after a temporary U.S. waiver, [Reuters reported](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/sinopec-buys-russian-oil-replace-mideast-supply-after-us-waiver-sources-say-2026-04-14/), buying roughly 10 cargoes and increasing volumes after the waiver expired as ⁠the Iran war squeezed supply.

Its recent ESPO purchases have not involved sanctioned entities as counterparties and have been made via intermediaries, four people familiar with the matter said, without providing further details. Since the early days of the Ukraine war, Sinopec has bought Russian oil using Chinese yuan.

Before the Iran war, Sinopec sourced nearly half of its crude from the Middle ⁠East and was among Saudi Arabia's biggest customers.

Sinopec did not buy any Saudi crude for June and July, and took only 2 million barrels in August, according to trade sources. That's far less than the 20 million Saudi barrels it imported in both March and April, and less than a fifth of the 11 million ⁠barrels it took each month on average in the year before the Iran war started.

September-loading ESPO was pegged at discounts of $1 to $2 a barrel to benchmark Brent, about $10 cheaper than rival grades such as Middle Eastern Oman and Brazil's Tupi, traders said.

Before the Iran war, Russian ESPO crude traded at a discount of about $10 per barrel.
