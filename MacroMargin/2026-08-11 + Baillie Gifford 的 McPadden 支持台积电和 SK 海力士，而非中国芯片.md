### 中文译文

## Baillie Gifford 的 McPadden 支持台积电和 SK 海力士，而非中国芯片

Baillie Gifford & Co. 的 Paulina McPadden 表示，台湾半导体制造公司和 SK 海力士公司在先进芯片制造领域拥有近乎垄断的地位，中国将难以匹敌，即使北京正在[动员其股票和债券市场](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2026-08-09/china-bets-on-ai-stocks-as-it-races-against-us-for-chip-tech-dominance)为其人工智能雄心提供资金。

台积电、韩国的 SK 海力士和总部位于荷兰的 ASML Holding NV “几乎处于自成一档的水平”，总部位于爱丁堡的 Baillie Gifford 的国际集中增长策略联席经理 McPadden 表示。

“特别是台积电，实际上在芯片前沿领域创造了垄断，这将是很难被颠覆的，不仅仅是因为资本渠道，”McPadden 周一在接受彭博电视采访时表示。“台积电几十年来积累了大量固有工艺知识，这极难复制，即使中国公司能够获得他们所需的许多设备——实际上他们无法获得——由于出口管制。”

McPadden 是在台积电公布季度业绩后发表上述言论的，台积电是她管理的 Baillie Gifford 基金的头号重仓股。总部位于台湾新竹的台积电报告营收为 402 亿美元，与人工智能相关的需求“异常强劲”——这些结果提振了人工智能看涨者，并凸显了中国境内外芯片制造商之间的竞争。

在 SK 海力士，高带宽内存带来的收入和利润占比越来越大，McPadden 表示。她说，这些芯片需要复杂的工艺和设备，中国的竞争对手目前还无法获得。

SK 海力士正在[考虑](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-07/sk-hynix-is-said-to-mull-options-for-3-billion-chongqing-assets)包括出售其位于重庆的价值 30 亿美元的半导体封装工厂部分股权等选项，这突显出重塑内存芯片格局的战略压力，尽管该股的前瞻估值低得惊人。

除半导体外，McPadden 还强调 [Shopify Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/SHOP%20CN%20Equity) 和 [MercadoLibre Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/MELI%20US%20Equity) 是美国以外引人注目的 AI 机会。关于 Shopify，她认为，代表消费者做出自主购买决策的 AI 智能体正在增加商家目录数据的价值，而 Shopify 的结构化产品记录被证明对 AI 智能体的有效性是抓取网站数据的两倍。

McPadden 对投资者倾向于将“新”与“重要”混为一谈提出了更广泛的警告。她说，Baillie Gifford——截至 6 月管理资产达[1970 亿英镑](https://www.bailliegifford.com/en/uk/individual-investors/about-us/)（当时约合 2250 亿美元）——着眼长远。

“市场从未如此之快和喧嚣，”她说。专注于基本面特征和5至10年的投资视野——而非季度业绩——仍是识别具有持续长期增长能力企业的关键差异化因素，她说。

### English Original

## Baillie Gifford’s McPadden Backs TSMC, SK Hynix Over China Chips

Baillie Gifford & Co.’s Paulina McPadden said companies like Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co. and SK Hynix Inc. hold near-monopoly positions in advanced chip manufacturing that China will struggle to match, even as Beijing [mobilizes its stock and bond market](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2026-08-09/china-bets-on-ai-stocks-as-it-races-against-us-for-chip-tech-dominance) to fund its AI ambitions.

TSMC, South Korea’s SK Hynix and Netherlands-based ASML Holding NV are “are almost in a league of their own,” said McPadden, co-manager of international concentrated growth strategy at Edinburgh-based Baillie Gifford.

“TSMC, in particular, has created effectively a monopoly at the leading edge of chips and that’s going to be very difficult to disrupt, not just because of access to capital,” McPadden said Monday in a Bloomberg Television interview. “There is so much inherent process knowledge that TSMC has built up over decades, and that is incredibly hard to replicate even if Chinese companies had access to a lot of the equipment they need — which they do not — because of export controls.”

McPadden spoke after quarterly earnings from TSMC, a top holding in her Baillie Gifford fund. Hinschu, Taiwan-based TSMC reported revenue of $40.2 billion and “exceptionally robust” demand related to artificial intelligence — results that buoyed AI bulls and highlighted the jockeying between chipmakers outside and inside China.

At SK Hynix, a growing portion of revenue and earnings is coming from high-bandwidth memory, McPadden said. Those chips, she said, require complex processes and equipment that Chinese rivals cannot yet access.

SK Hynix is [considering](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-07/sk-hynix-is-said-to-mull-options-for-3-billion-chongqing-assets) options including a stake sale for its $3 billion semiconductor packaging plant in Chongqing, underscoring the strategic pressures reshaping the memory chip landscape even as the stock trades at a strikingly low forward valuation.

Beyond semiconductors, McPadden highlighted [Shopify Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/SHOP%20CN%20Equity) and [MercadoLibre Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/MELI%20US%20Equity) as compelling non-US AI opportunities. On Shopify, she argued that AI agents making autonomous purchasing decisions on behalf of consumers are increasing the value of merchant catalog data, with Shopify’s structured product records proving twice as effective as scraped website data for AI agents.

McPadden offered a broader cautionary note on investors’ tendency to conflate “new” with “important.” Baillie Gifford — [with £197 billion in assets](https://www.bailliegifford.com/en/uk/individual-investors/about-us/) under management as of June ($225 billion at the time) — takes the long view, she said.

“Markets have never been faster and noisier,” she said. The ability to focus on fundamental attributes and a five- to 10-year investment horizon — rather than quarterly results — remains the key differentiator for identifying businesses capable of sustained long-term growth, she said.
