>
> 俄罗斯阿穆尔州正与中国合作试点用无人机和无人驾驶卡车跨境运输，以缓解中俄贸易激增带来的物流瓶颈。
> • 俄地方官员称，无人机桥将连接布拉戈维申斯克与黑河，运送电子元件等不超过50公斤的高价值货物。
> • 俄罗斯自动驾驶公司Navio开发项目，计划2027年跨境试运行载安全员无人卡车，2028年实现全无人运营。
> • 中俄贸易前7个月同比增长26%至1590亿美元，中国驻俄大使称年底可能创新高。
> • 世界首条跨境缆车预计2027年运营，将缩短两地通行时间至约5分钟。

Aug 17 (Reuters) - Drones and self-driving trucks could soon be ferrying goods between China and Russia's Far East, a Russian regional official said on Monday, as brisk cross-border trade prompts a search for new forms of cargo transport to ‌avoid bottlenecks.
8 月 17 日（路透社）——俄罗斯一名地方官员周一表示，由于跨境贸易日益活跃，人们正在寻求新的货物运输方式以避免瓶颈，不久之后，无人机和自动驾驶卡车或许就能用于在中俄远东地区之间运送货物。

Russia's Amur region, which shares a long border with China, is developing the trial programmes in partnership with Chinese authorities, the regional government's deputy chairman, Pavel Puzanov, told a business forum in the city of Kazan.
该地区政府副主席帕维尔·普扎诺夫在喀山举行的一场商业论坛上表示，与中国有着漫长边境线的俄罗斯阿穆尔州正在与中国方面合作开展相关试点项目，以缓解贸易瓶颈。

China has thrown a major [economic lifeline](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/chinas-president-xi-meets-with-russias-prime-minister-mishustin-2025-11-04/) to Russia since it was hit by [sanctions](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/whats-eus-21st-package-sanctions-against-russia-2026-07-23/) following its invasion of [Ukraine](https://www.reuters.com/world/ukraine-russia-war/) ⁠in February 2022, buying Russian oil, coal and gas and selling goods from cars to electronics to its neighbour.
自 2022 年 2 月俄罗斯因入侵乌克兰而面临制裁以来，中国为其提供了重要的经济支持，持续购买俄罗斯的石油、煤炭和天然气，同时向该国出口从汽车到电子产品等各类商品。

The rapid growth is pressuring border checkpoints and existing logistics infrastructure.
快速的贸易增长给边境检查站及现有的物流基础设施带来了巨大压力。

### PROJECTS INCLUDE A DRONE BRIDGE ACROSS BORDER RIVER
相关项目包括一座横跨边境河流的无人机货运桥。

One of the trial programmes involves establishing a so-called drone bridge between the Russian city of Blagoveshchensk and the Chinese city of Heihe, which face each other across the Amur river. The drones would ship high-margin cargo such as electronic components weighing up to 50 kg (110 pounds), Puzanov said.
其中一项试验计划是在位于阿穆尔河对岸的俄罗斯布拉戈维申斯克市与中国黑河市之间建立所谓的“无人机桥梁”。普扎诺夫表示，这些无人机将用于运送价值较高的货物，比如重量可达 50 公斤的电子元件。

A second ‌project, ⁠developed by Russian autonomous driving firm Navio, would allow driverless Chinese trucks to transport heavier freight between the two cities. Cross-border test runs with a safety driver in the cabin are scheduled for 2027, with fully autonomous operations possible in 2028, according to a presentation at the ⁠forum.
另一个项目由俄罗斯自动驾驶公司 Navio 开发，它能够让无人驾驶的中国卡车在两座城市之间运送更重的货物。根据论坛上的介绍，计划于 2027 年进行有安全驾驶员在车内的跨境试运行，而完全无人驾驶的运营则有望在 2028 年实现。

[China-Russia trade](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/chinas-2025-trade-with-russia-posts-first-decline-5-years-2026-01-14/) rose 26% year-on-year to $159 billion in January to July and could hit a record by the end of the year, Chinese Ambassador to Russia Zhang Hanhui said at the event.
中国驻俄罗斯大使张汉晖在活动中表示，1 月至 7 月中俄贸易额同比增长 26%，达到 1590 亿美元，到今年底这一数字还可能创下新高。

In ⁠January, Chinese data [showed](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/chinas-2025-trade-with-russia-posts-first-decline-5-years-2026-01-14/) China's trade with Russia in 2025 pulled back from a record level in 2024 and dropped for the first time in five years.
1 月发布的中国数据表明，2025 年中国与俄罗斯的贸易额较 2024 年的历史最高水平有所回落，出现了五年来首次下降。

For passengers, travelling ⁠between Blagoveshchensk and Heihe should become easier in 2027 with the launch of the world's first cross-border cable car, which promises to cut transit time to around five minutes, excluding border checks.
