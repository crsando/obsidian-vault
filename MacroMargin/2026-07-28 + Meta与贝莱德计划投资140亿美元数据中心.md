### 中文译文

## Meta与贝莱德计划投资140亿美元数据中心

[Meta Platforms Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/META%20US%20Equity)与[贝莱德](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/9903115D:US)计划在得克萨斯州建设一个1吉瓦的数据中心综合体，开发成本约140亿美元，这为支撑人工智能的计算中心投资浪潮再添一笔。

该设施将于2028年投入使用，Meta将是初始的独家租户。Meta于周二宣布与贝莱德建立新的战略合资企业，在埃尔帕索开发数据中心。贝莱德基金将持有合资企业80%的权益，而Meta将保留剩余的20%。

140亿美元的开发成本不包括驱动AI模型所需的尖端芯片。一个1吉瓦的数据中心通常耗资350亿至500亿美元。

包括[Alphabet Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/GOOGL%20US%20Equity)、[亚马逊](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/AMZN%20US%20Equity)、Meta和[微软](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/MSFT%20US%20Equity)在内的科技公司，一直在积极建设数据中心，以在仍处于初期阶段的AI工具市场争夺主导地位。这种前所未有的开发规模，通过资本支出、私人基础设施基金、债务和主权财富的混合方式融资。

Meta将初步贡献价值约23亿美元的土地和其他资产，并获得10亿美元的一次性付款。

贝莱德将贡献约49亿美元的现金，其中部分资金来自约125亿美元的债券[于周一发售](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-27/blackrock-raises-12-5-billion-of-debt-for-meta-data-center)，经过了近一周的流程以及[弱于预期的投资者需求](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-24/blackrock-kicks-off-12-3-billion-bond-sale-for-meta-data-center)。这些投资级债券提供的收益率与风险更高的垃圾债更为接近，部分由于定价时的大幅让步，在早期交易中上涨。

埃尔帕索项目是Meta Compute的一部分，这是这家社交媒体公司建设AI基础设施并出售过剩算力访问权限的举措。Meta将与设施签订租赁协议，初始期限为四年，并有权选择续约。

与贝莱德的合资企业“使我们能够以更快的速度和更大的规模推进，”Meta首席执行官马克·扎克伯格在声明中表示。

今年早些时候，Meta [将全年支出预期上调至1250亿至1450亿美元](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-29/meta-raises-outlook-for-capital-spending-in-2026-shares-slide)，原因是大力投资人工智能基础设施和组件价格上涨。该公司将于周三公布第二季度财报，届时可能会更新其支出计划。

### English Original

## Meta, BlackRock Plan to Invest in $14 Billion Data Center

[Meta Platforms Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/META%20US%20Equity) and [BlackRock Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/9903115D:US) plan to build a 1-gigawatt data center complex in Texas that will cost about $14 billion to develop, adding to a wave of investment in the computing hubs that power artificial intelligence.

The facility will go online in 2028 with Meta as the initial sole tenant, the company Meta Announces New Strategic Venture with BlackRock to Develop Data Center in El Paso Tuesday. BlackRock funds will hold an 80% interest in the joint venture, while Meta will retain the remaining 20%.

The $14 billion development costs don’t include the cutting-edge chips required to power AI models. A 1GW data center typically costs $35 billion to $50 billion.

Tech firms including [Alphabet Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/GOOGL%20US%20Equity), [Amazon.com Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/AMZN%20US%20Equity), Meta and [Microsoft Corp.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/MSFT%20US%20Equity), have been aggressively building out data centers in pursuit of dominance in the still-nascent market for AI tools. The unprecedented scale of development is being financed through a mix of capital expenditure, private infrastructure funds, debt and sovereign wealth.

Meta will initially contribute land and other assets valued at about $2.3 billion and receive a one-time payment of $1 billion.

BlackRock will contribute about $4.9 billion in cash, with some of its portion funded by about $12.5 billion of bonds [that were sold Monday](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-27/blackrock-raises-12-5-billion-of-debt-for-meta-data-center) after a nearly weeklong process and [weaker-than-expected investor demand](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-24/blackrock-kicks-off-12-3-billion-bond-sale-for-meta-data-center). The investment-grade debt offered yields that were more in line with riskier junk-rated bonds, which rose in early trading in part because of the hefty concessions it priced at.

The El Paso project is part of Meta Compute, the social media company’s initiative to build out AI infrastructure and sell access to excess computing power. Meta will enter lease agreements for the facility with a four-year initial term, with options to extend.

The joint venture with BlackRock “allows us to move faster and at greater scale,” Meta Chief Executive Officer Mark Zuckerberg said in the statement.

Earlier this year, Meta [raised its spending outlook for the year](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-29/meta-raises-outlook-for-capital-spending-in-2026-shares-slide) to $125 billion to $145 billion, driven by heavy investment into AI infrastructure and higher component pricing. The company reports second-quarter earnings on Wednesday, during which it is likely to give an update on its spending plans.
