### 中文译文

## 中国大型银行利润上升 息差压力缓解

中国最大的国有银行公布上半年利润增长，净息差企稳为多年来的盈利压力提供了喘息之机，这些压力来自房地产债务风险和信贷需求疲软。

根据周五的交易所文件，中国最大银行[中国工商银行股份有限公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/601398%20CH%20Equity)的净利润同比增长3.3%，至1736.8亿元人民币（合258亿美元）。其净息差（衡量盈利能力的关键指标）仅收窄1个基点至1.29%，此前曾大幅下降。

其他大型银行报告了更大的利润增幅。[中国农业银行股份有限公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/1288%20HK%20Equity)净利润增长4.9%，[交通银行股份有限公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/3328%20HK%20Equity)报告增长4%，[中国银行股份有限公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/1398%20HK%20Equity)增长5.1%，[中国建设银行股份有限公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/939%20HK%20Equity)增长4.6%。

这些业绩提供了新的证据，表明在经过长期的息差挤压后，中国银行业正在改善。根据监管机构[数据](https://www.nfra.gov.cn/cn/view/pages/ItemDetail.html?docId=1258121&itemId=954&generaltype=0)，行业整体净息差从上一季度的1.40%升至二季度的1.41%，这是自2022年以来首次季度环比扩张。

中国银行行长张辉在业绩发布会上表示：“本币净息差环比上升2个基点，外币净息差同比上升13个基点，为集团整体息差企稳提供了有力支撑。”

改善主要源于资金成本下降，因为几年前锁定的高收益存款到期，银行以较低利率重新定价负债。尽管如此，息差仍接近历史低位。

资产质量仍是关键担忧。从债务沉重的房地产开发商到家庭，借款人尚未从经济放缓中完全恢复，这给银行的资产负债表持续带来压力。尽管多数银行报告其表面不良贷款率有所改善，但底层数据揭示出部分领域的压力。

工商银行的不良贷款率为1.29%，低于2025年6月末的1.31%。相比之下，交通银行同期的不良贷款率从1.28%升至1.30%。

银行还面临艰难的平衡。信贷需求疲软和贷款利率下降限制了贷款增长并压缩了回报，而政策制定者继续敦促银行支持实体经济。截至6月末，银行业不良贷款余额达到[3.7万亿元](https://www.nfra.gov.cn/cn/view/pages/ItemDetail.html?docId=1268038&itemId=915)元，不良贷款率为1.52%。

大型银行继续提高中期现金分红比例，工商银行将分红比例从30%提高到31%，建设银行从31%提高到32%。

“此举旨在满足投资者需求和市场预期，并得到了银行稳健盈利和经营业绩的支持，”工商银行董事会秘书田凤林表示，他补充说，分红政策将保持灵活，并根据宏观经济形势、监管指导和业务需要进行调整。

## China’s Mega Lenders Lift Profits as Margin Pressure Eases

China’s biggest state-owned lenders reported higher first-half profits as stabilizing net interest margins offered respite after years of earnings pressure from property debt risks and sluggish credit demand.

Net income at [Industrial & Commercial Bank of China Ltd.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/601398%20CH%20Equity), the nation’s largest lender, rose 3.3% from a year earlier to 173.68 billion yuan ($25.8 billion), according to an exchange filing Friday. Its net interest margin, a key gauge of profitability, narrowed just 1 basis point to 1.29%, after posting steep drops earlier.

Other major lenders reported larger profit gains. [Agricultural Bank of China Ltd.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/1288%20HK%20Equity)’s net income rose 4.9%, [Bank of Communications Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/3328%20HK%20Equity) reported a 4% gain, [Bank of China Ltd.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/1398%20HK%20Equity) posted a 5.1% increase and [China Construction Bank Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/939%20HK%20Equity) saw a rise of 4.6%.

The results offer fresh evidence of improvements in China’s banking industry after a prolonged margin squeeze. The industry-wide net interest margin rose to 1.41% in the second quarter from 1.40% in the previous three months, according to regulator [data](https://www.nfra.gov.cn/cn/view/pages/ItemDetail.html?docId=1258121&itemId=954&generaltype=0), marking the first quarterly expansion since 2022.

Local currency “NIM was up by 2 basis points quarter-on-quarter, while foreign-currency NIM rose 13 basis points year-over-year, providing strong support for the group’s overall margin stabilization,” said Zhang Hui, president of Bank of China, at an earnings briefing.

The improvements were driven largely by falling funding costs, as higher-yielding deposits locked in several years ago matured and banks repriced liabilities at lower rates. Even so, margins remain near record lows.

Asset quality remains a key concern. Borrowers ranging from heavily indebted property developers to households have yet to fully recover from the economic slowdown, keeping pressure on banks’ balance sheets. While most banks reported improvements in their headline non-performing loan ratios, the underlying data reveals pockets of stress.

ICBC’s non-performing loan ratio stood at 1.29%, down from 1.31% at the end of June 2025. In contrast, BOCOM’s NPL ratio rose to 1.30% from 1.28% over the same period.

Lenders also face a difficult balancing act. Weak credit demand and lower lending rates are limiting loan growth and compressing returns, while policymakers continue to urge banks to support the real economy. The banking sector’s non-performing loan balance hit [3.7 trillion](https://www.nfra.gov.cn/cn/view/pages/ItemDetail.html?docId=1268038&itemId=915) yuan at the end of June, while the NPL ratio stood at 1.52%.

Major lenders have continued to raise interim cash dividend payout ratios, with ICBC increasing its ratio to 31% from 30% and CCB to 32% from 31%.

“The move was intended to address investor demand and market expectations, supported by the bank’s resilient earnings and operating performance,” said Tian Fenglin, ICBC board secretary, adding that dividend policy would remain flexible and be adjusted in line with macroeconomic conditions, regulatory guidance and business needs.
