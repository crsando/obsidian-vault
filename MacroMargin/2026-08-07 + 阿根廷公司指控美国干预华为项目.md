### 中文译文

## 阿根廷公司指控美国干预华为项目

![Javier Milei has deepened ties with the US but also relies on China as a creditor and customer for Argentina’s exports © Mandel Ngan/AFP/Getty Images](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3b581cc90b14811a954bd2fc36eaccd8/3b581cc9-0b14-81ea-8757-f8c8135d85aa-15aeda3ede8081033e99865275ced96c.png)

*哈维尔·米莱加深了与美国的关系，但同时也依赖中国作为债权人和阿根廷出口的客户 © Mandel Ngan/AFP/Getty Images*

[****Ciara Nugent****](https://www.ft.com/ciara-nugent) 于布宜诺斯艾利斯

发布于昨日

阿根廷一家电网运营商指控美国官员不当施压，要求其取消与华为的基础设施项目，与此同时唐纳德·特朗普加大力度限制北京在拉丁美洲的影响力。

CALF是一家地区性电网合作社，周三表示，美国大使馆一名官员在7月询问其一名高管“是否有取消与中国科技集团项目的可能性”，并援引“美国政府限制或吊销与[华为](https://www.ft.com/stream/254cd19f-4724-4c89-9230-926e8201a823)相关项目董事签证的政策”。

中国驻布宜诺斯艾利斯大使馆发言人周三晚些时候表示，美国通过威胁吊销高管签证，“公然阻碍了[阿根廷](https://www.ft.com/argentina)与中国公司之间的正常合作”。

美国驻布宜诺斯艾利斯大使周四否认这些言论构成不当施压。

“每个国家都保护自己的家园，美国也不例外，”彼得·拉梅拉斯在X上表示。“拒绝或吊销签证不是惩罚，而是保护国家安全和利益的决定。”

美国国务院未立即回应置评。

此事凸显了右翼总统[Javier Milei](https://www.ft.com/stream/ee4cafe6-4e12-4ce8-b5b5-92887434b094)面临的双重压力，他是美国总统的坚定盟友。

尽管米莱深化了与美国的联系，并在去年从特朗普政府获得了200亿美元的信贷额度，但他也依赖中国作为阿根廷中央银行的主要债权人和其农产品出口的客户。米莱尚未对这场争端置评。

美国财政部长斯科特·贝森特（Scott Bessent）在10月表示，米莱“承诺让中国离开阿根廷”，此前米莱政府因去年中期选举前比索挤兑而获得救助。

CALF表示，已将这些信息报告给阿根廷政府，因为其“在制度渠道之外”进行，并威胁对其工作人员施加个人后果。

米莱是一位自由意志主义经济学家，在竞选期间曾称中国政府为“杀人犯”，但自2023年上任以来，他一直避免批评阿根廷第二大贸易伙伴。次年，他称中国为“极好的合作伙伴”。

周三，阿根廷官员与中国达成了190亿美元货币互换额度的五年延期，该额度占这个南美国家500亿美元外汇储备的很大一部分。

外交部长帕布洛·基尔诺（Pablo Quirno）最近在接受电台采访时表示，他预计将很快与经济部长路易斯·卡普托（Luis Caputo）一起访问中国，然后再考虑米莱可能的出访。

CALF 负责监督内乌肯省会城市的电网。内乌肯是巴塔哥尼亚一个石油资源丰富的省份，处于阿根廷页岩繁荣的中心地带。该省也是中国政府运营的一个深空测控站所在地，该测控站于2014年通过双边协议开放。

该设施遭到华盛顿的激烈批评，华盛顿警告称，中国可能将其用于全球监视活动。

米莱政府于2024年派代表团视察了该设施，但此后一直坚称无需采取进一步行动。

米莱扩大了与美国的国防合作，并且自他重返华盛顿以来，在联合国和其他国际论坛上，他在绝大多数投票中都与特朗普政府站在一边。

## Argentine company accuses US of meddling in Huawei project

*Javier Milei has deepened ties with the US but also relies on China as a creditor and customer for Argentina’s exports © Mandel Ngan/AFP/Getty Images*

[**](https://www.ft.com/ciara-nugent)[**Ciara Nugent**](https://www.ft.com/ciara-nugent)[**](https://www.ft.com/ciara-nugent) in Buenos Aires

Published yesterday

An Argentine grid operator has accused US officials of inappropriately pressuring it to cancel an infrastructure project with Huawei, as Donald Trump widens efforts to limit Beijing’s influence in Latin America.

CALF, a regional electric grid co-operative, said on Wednesday that a US embassy official in July asked one of its executives “if there was a possibility of cancelling the project” with the Chinese technology group, citing a “US government policy restricting or revoking visas” for directors of [Huawei](https://www.ft.com/stream/254cd19f-4724-4c89-9230-926e8201a823)-linked projects.

A spokesperson for the Chinese embassy in Buenos Aires said later on Wednesday that the US had “blatantly hindered normal co-operation” between the [Argentine](https://www.ft.com/argentina) and Chinese companies by threatening to revoke executives’ visas.

The US ambassador in Buenos Aires on Thursday denied that the messages represented inappropriate pressure.

“Every country protects its house, the United States isn’t the exception,” Peter Lamelas said on X. “Denying or revoking a visa isn’t a punishment, it’s a decision to protect the nation’s security and interests.”

The US state department did not immediately provide comment.

The episode underscores the duelling pressures on rightwing president [Javier Milei](https://www.ft.com/stream/ee4cafe6-4e12-4ce8-b5b5-92887434b094), a staunch ally of the US president.

While Milei has deepened ties with the US and received a $20bn credit line from the Trump administration last year, he also relies on China as a major creditor of Argentina’s central bank and customer for its agricultural exports. Milei has yet to comment on the dispute.

US Treasury secretary Scott Bessent in October said Milei was “committed to getting China out of Argentina” after bailing out his government amid a run on the peso ahead of midterm elections last year.

CALF said it reported the messages to Argentina’s government for taking place “outside of institutional channels” and threatening personal consequences against its staff.

Milei, a libertarian economist who referred to the Chinese government as “murderers” on the campaign trail, has avoided criticism of Argentina’s second-largest trading partner since taking office in 2023. He called China “a fabulous partner” the following year.

On Wednesday Argentine officials secured a five-year extension of a $19bn currency swap line with China, which constitutes a large portion of the South American country’s $50bn in foreign currency reserves.

Foreign minister Pablo Quirno said in a recent radio interview that he expects to visit China with economy minister Luis Caputo soon, ahead of a possible trip by Milei.

CALF oversees the grid in the capital city of Neuquén, an oil-rich Patagonian province at the heart of Argentina’s shale boom. The province is also home to a deep-space station run by China’s government that opened through a bilateral agreement in 2014.

The facility has drawn fierce criticism from Washington, which has warned that China could use it in global surveillance efforts.

Milei’s government sent a delegation to inspect the facility in 2024 but has since insisted that no further action is needed.

Milei has expanded defence co-operation with the US and has sided with the Trump administration in the vast majority of its votes at the UN and in other international forums since his return to Washington.
