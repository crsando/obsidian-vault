## 中国批评欧盟在京东调查中的域外管辖

### [政治](https://www.bloomberg.com/politics)亚洲

作者：[Foster Wong](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AQz-_jdi0LI/foster-wong) 2026年8月19日 下午8:39 GMT+8

### **要点** 由Bloomberg AI提供

### **要点**

- 中国批评欧盟利用其《外国补贴条例》调查京东集团股份有限公司（JD.com Inc.）计划收购德国电子零售商Ceconomy的交易，称其属于“不当域外管辖”。
- 中国司法部表示，欧盟要求提供位于中国境内的信息“严重损害国际法治”，并称任何组织或个人不得遵守或协助执行这些措施。
- 司法部敦促欧盟“立即纠正错误做法”，提供公平的市场环境，并警告称，如果欧盟继续越界，中国将采取反制措施回应。
中国批评欧盟利用其《外国补贴条例》调查[京东集团股份有限公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/JD%20US%20Equity)计划以22亿欧元收购德国电子零售商[Ceconomy](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CECV%20GY%20Equity)的交易，称其属于“不当域外管辖”，并禁止遵守这些措施。

北京表示，欧盟在调查这家中国电商公司时，不当要求获取位于中国境内的广泛且不必要的信息。中央电视台周三援引司法部的消息[报道](https://content-static.cctvnews.cctv.com/snow-book/index.html?item_id=15921693856452070371&t=1787138682308&toc_style_id=feeds_default&track_id=78BD957D-FE4F-47C4-828F-EE66A43FBC7C_808831800174&share_to=copy_url)称，这些要求“严重损害了国际法治”。

司法部表示，它与商务部等部门共同认定，欧盟的行为构成根据中国反外国制裁法规的不当域外管辖。司法部还表示，任何组织或个人不得遵守或协助执行这些措施。

司法部表示：“我们敦促欧盟立即纠正错误做法，停止滥用外国补贴调查工具，为在欧洲投资和运营的企业提供公平、公正、可预测的市场环境。” 并警告如果欧盟继续越界，中国将采取反制措施。

欧盟于5月对京东的拟议收购展开[深入审查](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-28/jd-com-s-2-2-billion-ceconomy-deal-hit-by-eu-subsidy-probe)，这是首起根据欧盟外国补贴法被调查的中国企业收购案。欧盟监管机构表示，京东可能受益于包括优惠融资、税收优惠和补贴在内的中国政府支持，这些支持可能扭曲竞争。欧盟必须在京东22亿欧元收购Ceconomy交易收到欧盟正式警告之前，对该交易予以批准、附加条件或阻止。

这一争端标志着中欧紧张关系的最新爆发。7月底，中国对14家欧洲公司实施[出口管制](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-24/china-hits-back-at-eu-with-export-curbs-that-ensnare-rheinmetall)，包括德国国防巨头莱茵金属公司，以报复欧盟对大陆和香港实体的制裁。布鲁塞尔指责中国的贸易行为损害了欧盟经济，造成贸易巨大失衡并掏空当地产业。

## China Criticizes EU Over Extraterritorial Reach in JD.com Probe

### [Politics](https://www.bloomberg.com/politics)Asia

By [Foster Wong](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AQz-_jdi0LI/foster-wong)August 19, 2026 at 8:39 PM GMT+8

### **Takeaways ** by Bloomberg AI

### **Takeaways**

- China criticized the European Union's use of its Foreign Subsidies Regulation to probe JD.com Inc.'s planned takeover of German electronics retailer Ceconomy, calling it an "improper extraterritorial jurisdiction".
- The Ministry of Justice said the EU's demands for information located within China "seriously undermine the international rule of law" and that no organization or individual may comply with or assist in carrying out the measures.
- The ministry urged the EU to "immediately correct its wrong practices" and provide a fair market environment, warning that China would respond with countermeasures if the bloc continued to overstep.
China criticized the European Union’s use of its Foreign Subsidies Regulation to probe [JD.com Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/JD%20US%20Equity)’s planned €2.2 billion takeover of German electronics retailer [Ceconomy](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CECV%20GY%20Equity), calling it an “improper extraterritorial jurisdiction” and prohibiting compliance with the measures.

Beijing said the bloc had improperly demanded broad and unnecessary information located within China during its probe of the Chinese e-commerce firm, state broadcaster China Central Television [reported](https://content-static.cctvnews.cctv.com/snow-book/index.html?item_id=15921693856452070371&t=1787138682308&toc_style_id=feeds_default&track_id=78BD957D-FE4F-47C4-828F-EE66A43FBC7C_808831800174&share_to=copy_url) on Wednesday, citing the Ministry of Justice. The demands “seriously undermine the international rule of law,” the ministry said.

The Ministry of Justice said it had determined, together with the Commerce Ministry and other agencies, that the EU’s actions constituted improper extraterritorial jurisdiction under China’s anti-foreign sanctions regulations. It also stated that no organization or individual may comply with or assist in carrying out the measures.

“We urge the EU to immediately correct its wrong practices, stop abusing the foreign subsidy investigation tool, and provide a fair, impartial and predictable market environment for companies investing and operating in Europe,” the ministry said, warning that China would respond with countermeasures if the bloc continued to overstep.

The EU opened an [in-depth review](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-28/jd-com-s-2-2-billion-ceconomy-deal-hit-by-eu-subsidy-probe) of JD.com’s proposed acquisition in May, making it the first Chinese corporate takeover to be targeted under the bloc’s foreign subsidies law. EU regulators have said the company may have benefited from Chinese state support including preferential financing, tax incentives and grants that could distort competition. The EU has until JD.com’s €2.2 Billion Ceconomy Deal Gets EU Formal Warning to clear the deal, impose conditions or block it.

The dispute marks the latest flareup of tensions between China and Europe. In late July, China slapped [export controls](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-24/china-hits-back-at-eu-with-export-curbs-that-ensnare-rheinmetall) on 14 European companies, including German defense giant Rheinmetall AG, in retaliation against the EU’s sanctions on mainland and Hong Kong entities. Brussels has accused China of trade practices that it says harm the bloc’s economy by generating huge imbalances in trade and hollowing out local industry.
