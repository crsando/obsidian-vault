### 中文译文

## 中国的煤制化学品布局以创纪录利润获得回报

彭博新闻 2026年8月13日 上午9:31 GMT+8

### **要点**（由彭博AI提供）

- 中国用煤炭替代石油生产化学品的十多年努力，正在为宁夏宝丰能源集团股份有限公司带来回报。
- 宝丰能源上半年利润为97.3亿元人民币，去年同期为57.2亿元人民币。
- 该公司第二季度净利润为61亿元人民币，为2019年上市以来单季度最高。
中国用煤替代石油生产化学品的十多年努力，正在为行业领军企业宁夏宝丰能源集团股份有限公司带来回报。由于中东战争导致原油价格飙升，该公司获得了创纪录的利润。

宝丰能源占中国煤制化学品产能约三分之一。该公司在周三的交易所公告中表示，上半年利润为97.3亿元人民币（合14亿美元），去年同期为57.2亿元人民币。

![图片](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3bb81cc90b1481a49fcacc0139aa843a/3bb81cc9-0b14-81c2-9122-c1b29089f216-a00940facea599dadd52e43f262bc441.png)

第二季度，在霍尔木兹海峡贸易中断导致油价飙升至四年高位的情况下，该公司表现尤为出色。据彭博计算，其净利润为61亿元人民币，高于2019年上市以来任何季度。

“原油价格快速上涨且波动剧烈，显著提高了油基烯烃的原料成本，”宝丰在业绩声明中表示。“国内煤炭价格涨幅温和，煤制烯烃生产的原料成本仅小幅上升。”

煤制化学品工厂将固体燃料转化为合成气、液体燃料和化学原料，用于从纺织品到建筑材料的产品。根据国际能源署的数据，煤炭约占中国甲醇和氨产量的85%。根据能源与清洁空气研究中心的数据，2024年该行业使用了约3.9亿吨煤炭。

虽然这项技术的历史可以追溯到一个多世纪以前[超过一个世纪](https://www.bloomberg.com/opinion/articles/2025-06-02/commodities-china-s-coal-industry-has-a-big-dirty-secret)，但中国对该行业的推动始于上一个十年，当时人们对国家依赖进口石油的担忧日益加剧。在本十年早些时候短暂放缓后，该行业再次处于中国煤制化学品行业瞄准730亿美元扩张的进程中，北京将供应安全置于能源战略的核心。

该行业获得政府支持，不仅因为它有助于减少石油和天然气进口，还因为它促进了内蒙古、新疆和宁夏等较贫穷但煤炭资源丰富地区的经济发展，宝丰便位于宁夏。

据摩根士丹利包括Jack Lu在内的分析师称，该公司拥有约每年520万吨煤制烯烃产能，约占中国总产能的三分之一。Lu在4月的一份报告中表示，该公司获得廉价煤炭资源意味着它不需要高昂的油价就能盈利——即使布伦特原油价格在每桶30多美元时也能产生现金流。

## China’s Coal-to-Chemicals Push Pays Off With Record Profits

By Bloomberg NewsAugust 13, 2026 at 9:31 AM GMT+8

### **Takeaways ** by Bloomberg AI

- China's decade-plus quest to replace oil with coal in chemicals production is paying off for Ningxia Baofeng Energy Group Co.
- Baofeng recorded first-half profit of 9.73 billion yuan, compared to 5.72 billion yuan in the same period last year.
- The company's net income was 6.1 billion yuan in the second quarter, higher than any previous three-month period since it listed in 2019.
China’s decade-plus quest to replace oil with coal in chemicals production is paying off for industry leader Ningxia Baofeng Energy Group Co., which pulled in record profits as crude prices soared due to the Middle East War.

Baofeng, which accounts for about a third of China’s coal-to-chemicals capacity, recorded first-half profit of 9.73 billion yuan ($1.4 billion), it said in an exchange filing Wednesday. That compares to 5.72 billion yuan in the same period last year.

The second quarter, when oil prices soared to a four-year high amid trade disruptions through the Strait of Hormuz, was particularly good for the company. Its net income was 6.1 billion yuan, according to Bloomberg calculations, higher than any previous three-month period since the company listed in 2019.

“Crude oil prices rose rapidly and were highly volatile, significantly increasing feedstock costs for oil-based olefins,” Baofeng said in its earnings statement. “Domestic coal prices increased moderately, and feedstock costs for coal-to-olefins production rose only slightly.”

Coal-to-chemicals plants convert the solid fuel into synthetic gas, liquid fuels and chemical feedstocks for products from textiles to building materials. Coal accounts for about 85% of China’s methanol and ammonia production, according to the International Energy Agency. The industry used about 390 million tons of coal in 2024, according to the Centre for Research on Energy and Clean Air.

While the technology dates back [more than a century](https://www.bloomberg.com/opinion/articles/2025-06-02/commodities-china-s-coal-industry-has-a-big-dirty-secret), China’s push into the sector began in earnest last decade amid growing concern about the nation’s dependence on imported oil. After a brief slowdown earlier this decade, the sector is once again in China’s Coal-to-Chemicals Industry Eyes a $73 Billion Expansion as Beijing puts supply security at the center of its energy strategy.

The sector enjoys government support not only because it helps cut down on oil and gas imports but also because it contributes to economic development in poorer coal-rich regions like Inner Mongolia, Xinjiang and Ningxia, where Baofeng is based.

The company has about 5.2 million tons a year of coal-to-olefins capacity, about a third of China’s total, according to Morgan Stanley analysts inlcuding Jack Lu. Its access to cheap coal resources mean it doesn’t need sky-high oil prices to be profitable — it’s cash generative even when Brent is in the $30s a barrel, Lu said in an April note.
