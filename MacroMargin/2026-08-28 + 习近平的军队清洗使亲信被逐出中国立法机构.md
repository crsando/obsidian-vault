## 习近平的军队清洗使亲信被逐出中国立法机构

中央军委副主席张又侠（中）。

*摄影：Adek Berry/AFP/Getty Images*

作者：[Josh Xiao](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AVxUV9dEiUc/josh-xiao)
2026年8月28日 21:25（GMT+8）

**彭博AI摘要**

- 习近平主席的两名亲信张又侠和钟绍军已被最高立法机构免去人大代表职务。
- 在1月宣布一项突然调查后，张又侠和刘振立也被免去国家中央军委职务。
- 包括巨乾生和赵宗岐在内的其他军方官员已被免职，这表明军队中根除腐败的行动仍在继续。
习近平主席的两名亲信被逐出中国立法机构，这位最高领导人誓言在整顿军队时绝不手软。

据官方新华社报道，前中央军委副主席张又侠和国防大学前政治委员钟绍军已被最高立法机构解除人大代表职务。

张又侠和钟绍军都被认为是习近平的忠诚者。张是习近平的家庭友人，而钟的职业生涯与现任总统有交集。这些清洗表明，这位自毛泽东以来最有权势的中国领导人正在继续推进军队反腐，即使牵涉到与他有关的人。

张又侠还与前联合参谋部参谋长刘振立一起被免去了国家中央军委职务。中国于1月宣布对这两人进行突然调查。尽管目前该调查尚未有官方结果，但中国官员很少能在调查中全身而退。张和刘的免职预示着更多细节可能会在10月即将召开的中央全会上公布。

已解散的战略支援部队前司令员巨乾生也被从立法机构中除名。此前他曾断断续续公开露面，引发外界对其命运的猜测。最新公告表明，巨乾生可能也已受到调查。

反腐败运动深化的另一个迹象是，西部战区前司令员赵宗岐于周三被免去中国政治咨询机构（政协）的职务。赵曾参加对越自卫反击战，有实战经验，并处理过中印边境冲突。

## Xi’s Military Purge Sees Loyalists Ousted From China Legislature

Zhang Youxia, center, vice chairman of the CMC.

*Photographer: Adek Berry/AFP/Getty Images*

By [Josh Xiao](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AVxUV9dEiUc/josh-xiao)
August 28, 2026 at 9:25 PM GMT+8

**Takeaways by Bloomberg AI**

- Two associates of President Xi Jinping, Zhang Youxia and Zhong Shaojun, have been removed as lawmakers by the apex legislative body.
- Zhang Youxia and Liu Zhenli have also been removed from the state Central Military Commission after a surprise investigation was announced in January.
- Other military officials, including Ju Qiansheng and Zhao Zongqi, have been removed from their positions, signaling a continued drive to root out corruption from the military.
Two associates of President Xi Jinping were ousted from China’s legislature as the paramount leader vows no mercy in his push to clean up the military.

Zhang Youxia, former vice chair of the Central Military Commission, and Zhong Shaojun, former political commissar of the National Defense University, have been removed as lawmakers by the apex legislative body, according to the official Xinhua New Agency.

Both Zhang and Zhong have been considered as Xi loyalists. Zhang is a family friend of Xi, while Zhong’s career overlapped with that of the current president. The purges show that China’s most powerful leader since Mao Zedong is continuing his drive to root out corruption from the military, even if it implicates people associated with him.

Zhang has also been removed from the state CMC, along with ex-joint chief of staff Liu Zhenli. China announced a surprise investigation into the pair in January. While so far there hasn’t been any official outcome from that probe, Chinese politicians rarely come back from an investigation unscathed. Zhang’s and Liu’s removals signal further details could come at the upcoming plenum in October.

Ju Qiansheng, former commander of the now-defunct Strategic Support Force, has also been removed from the legislature. He has appeared publicly on and off previously, sparking uncertainty over his fate. The latest announcement suggests Ju likely has been probed as well.

In another sign of the deepening anti-graft campaign, Zhao Zongqi, former commander of the Western Theater Command, was removed from China’s political advisory body on Wednesday. Zhao has combat experience in the Sino-Vietnamese war and handled China-India border conflicts.
