## 孟加拉国计划在军事改革中购买中国战斗机

一架J-10CE战斗机。*摄影师：邹伟/VCG/盖蒂图片社*

作者：[阿伦·德夫纳特](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AQeSdimdcl0/arun-devnath)
2026年8月25日 21:16 GMT+8

孟加拉国正在推进购买中国J-10CE战斗机的计划，一位政府官员指出该战机在近期印巴冲突中的表现。

政府谈判委员会已敲定采购这种多用途战机的合同草案，总理政策、战略和信息顾问扎赫德·乌尔·拉赫曼周二在达卡的一场媒体简报会上表示。

该采购计划是更广泛军事现代化举措的一部分。拉赫曼在简报会上未透露拟购战机数量及价格。

据这位顾问称，采购计划还包括为孟加拉国空军采购攻击直升机，以及要员直升机和无人机系统。

他表示：“如果本财年资金到位，计划将继续推进；否则将推迟到下一个预算周期。”

拉赫曼援引了中国战机在2025年印巴军事冲突中的“实战经验”。巴基斯坦声称在冲突中击落了印度法制的“阵风”战机。印度承认损失了飞机，但没有说明数量，并称还摧毁了巴基斯坦数架飞机，伊斯兰堡对此予以否认。

中国战斗机比同等质量的欧洲替代品(如“阵风”或“欧洲台风”战斗机)更便宜,尽管在“直接面对面作战情况下”表现“更好”,拉赫曼说。

## Bangladesh Plans to Buy Chinese Fighters in Military Overhaul

A J-10CE fighter jet. *Photographer: Zou Wei/VCG/Getty Images*

By [Arun Devnath](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AQeSdimdcl0/arun-devnath)
August 25, 2026 at 9:16 PM GMT+8

Bangladesh is advancing plans to purchase Chinese J-10CE fighter jets, with a government official pointing to the aircraft’s performance in the recent India-Pakistan conflict.

A government negotiation committee has finalized a draft contract for the procurement of the multirole combat aircraft, Zahed Ur Rahman, adviser to the prime minister for policy, strategy and information, said at a media briefing in Dhaka on Tuesday.

The purchase plan is part of a wider military modernization initiative. Rahman did not disclose the number of jets being sought or their price in his briefing.

The procurement plan also includes attack helicopters for the Bangladesh Air Force, along with VIP helicopters and unmanned aerial vehicle systems, according to the adviser.

“If funds are available this fiscal year, the plans will move forward; otherwise, they will be pushed to the next budget cycle,” he said.

Rahman cited the “real-world experience” of the Chinese fighters during the India-Pakistan military conflict in 2025. Pakistan claimed that it downed India’s French-made Rafale jets during the conflict. India acknowledged losing aircraft, without specifying a number, and said it also destroyed several Pakistani planes, which Islamabad denies.

Chinese fighter aircraft are cheaper than European alternatives of similar quality, such as the Rafale or Eurofighter Typhoon, despite performing “better in direct face-to-face combat situations,” Rahman said.
