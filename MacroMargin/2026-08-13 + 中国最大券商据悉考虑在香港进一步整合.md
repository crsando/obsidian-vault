### 中文译文

## 中国最大券商据悉考虑在香港进一步整合

上海的海通证券大厦。 *摄影：Qilai Shen/彭博*

作者：彭博新闻
2026年8月13日下午3:19 GMT+8
*更新于2026年8月13日下午3:56 GMT+8*

[国泰海通证券](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/601211%20CH%20Equity)在上周宣布计划将一家香港上市业务私有化后，正在研究合并其国际业务部门的选项。

在提出以44%溢价将[国泰君安国际控股有限公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/601211%20CH%20Equity)私有化后，这家中国资产规模最大的券商正考虑对另一家子公司[海通恒信国际融资租赁股份有限公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/1905%20HK%20Equity)进行类似交易，知情人士称，由于讨论未公开而不愿具名。

知情人士表示，退市并使得这些业务成为全资子公司，应有助于国泰海通将重叠的海外部门整合为一个上市实体。这也符合中国整合券商行业以更好地与华尔街巨头和国际公司竞争的努力。去年国泰君安证券与海通证券的合并催生了国泰海通，是这一过程中的重要里程碑。

总部位于上海的海通恒信主要从事金融租赁业务，即购买实物资产并将其出租给各行业。该公司于2019年在香港上市，周四早盘时的市值为65亿港元（约8.3亿美元）。随着股价因收购考虑的消息一度上涨5.7%，市值在当日稍后升至68亿港元。

知情人士称，讨论尚处于早期阶段。国泰海通和海通恒信的代表未回应置评请求。

国泰海通于8月7日宣布对国泰君安国际（其旗舰海外上市平台）的要约，提议每股3港元，较7月22日该股收盘价溢价44%。尽管股价在要约后飙升，但仍远低于2025年7月的峰值，当时公司获得监管[批准](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-06-26/guotai-junan-shares-set-for-best-week-on-crypto-trading-approval)在香港提供加密货币交易服务。

尽管获得了牌照且随后投资者热情高涨，但知情人士表示，由于中国对内地关联金融机构和加密货币的[限制](https://www.pbc.gov.cn/tiaofasi/144941/3581332/2026020619591971323/index.html)，国泰君安国际不太可能成为稳定币发行方。今年早些时候，香港向汇丰控股有限公司和渣打集团有限公司的合资企业发放了首批[稳定币发行人牌照](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-10/hsbc-stanchart-get-first-hong-kong-stablecoin-issuer-licenses)。

国泰君安国际的[业务包括](https://www.gtjai.com/en/aboutus)经纪和财富管理服务、企业融资、资产管理和贷款及融资。

海通证券在2023年将另一家香港上市子公司——[亏损中的](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2023-04-19/haitong-moves-to-rein-in-hong-kong-unit-after-840-million-loss)海通国际证券集团有限公司——私有化，之后与国泰君安证券合并。

## China’s Biggest Broker Is Said to Mull More Consolidation in Hong Kong

The Haitong Securities Tower in Shanghai. *Photographer: Qilai Shen/Bloomberg*

By Bloomberg News  
August 13, 2026 at 3:19 PM GMT+8  
*Updated on August 13, 2026 at 3:56 PM GMT+8*

[Guotai Haitong Securities Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/601211%20CH%20Equity) is studying options to combine its international units after announcing plans to take one of its Hong Kong-listed businesses private last week.

Having made an Guotai Haitong Offers 44% Premium to Take Hong Kong Unit Private for [Guotai Junan International Holdings Ltd.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/601211%20CH%20Equity), China’s biggest brokerage by assets is considering a similar deal for another unit, [Haitong UniTrust International Financial Leasing Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/1905%20HK%20Equity), people familiar with the matter said, asking not to be identified discussing private deliberations.

Delisting and making the businesses wholly owned should make it easier for Guotai Haitong to integrate overlapping offshore divisions into one listed entity, the people said. It would also align with China’s efforts to consolidate the country’s securities industry to better compete with Wall Street giants and international firms. Last year’s merger of Guotai Junan Securities and Haitong Securities, which created Guotai Haitong, was a key milestone in the process.

Shanghai-based Haitong UniTrust is primarily involved in financial leasing, buying physical assets and lending them out to a range of industries. It listed in Hong Kong in 2019 and had a market value of HK$6.5 billion ($830 million) as of earlier Thursday. That rose to HK$6.8 billion later in the day as the shares jumped as much as 5.7% on news of the buyout considerations.

Deliberations are at an early stage, the people said. Representatives for Guotai Haitong and Haitong UniTrust didn’t respond to requests for comment.

Guotai Haitong announced its offer for Guotai Junan International — its flagship offshore-listed vehicle — on Aug. 7, with a proposal of HK$3 per share, a 44% premium to the stock’s previous close on July 22. While the shares surged after the offer, they are still way off a July 2025 peak that came as the company got regulatory [approval](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-06-26/guotai-junan-shares-set-for-best-week-on-crypto-trading-approval) to provide cryptocurrency trading services in Hong Kong.

Despite the license and the investor enthusiasm that followed, Guotai Junan International is unlikely to become a stablecoin issuer because of China’s [restrictions ](https://www.pbc.gov.cn/tiaofasi/144941/3581332/2026020619591971323/index.html)on mainland-affiliated financial firms and cryptocurrency, the people said. Hong Kong granted its first [stablecoin issuer licenses](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-10/hsbc-stanchart-get-first-hong-kong-stablecoin-issuer-licenses) to HSBC Holdings Plc and a Standard Chartered Plc joint venture earlier this year.

Guotai Junan International’s [businesses include](https://www.gtjai.com/en/aboutus) brokerage and wealth management services, corporate finance, asset management, and loans and financing.

Haitong Securities took another Hong Kong-listed unit, the [loss-making](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2023-04-19/haitong-moves-to-rein-in-hong-kong-unit-after-840-million-loss) Haitong International Securities Group Ltd., private in 2023 prior to the merger with Guotai Junan Securities.
