### 中文译文

## 报告称中国人形机器人制造商占全球出货量97%

据新的行业数据，2026年上半年中国的人形机器人制造商占据了全球出货量的97%以上，这证实了中国在这一新兴领域相对于美国竞争对手的早期领先地位。

据[Smart Analytics Global的数据](https://smartanalyticsglobal.com/global-humanoid-robot-shipments-2026-agibot-unitree/)，2026年上半年全球人形机器人出货量总计约19,100台，是去年同期5,100台的三倍多。这家总部位于加利福尼亚的研究公司预计，今年出货量将升至约60,000台，到2030年达到50万台。

总部位于上海的[智元机器人](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/2267429D:CH)超越总部位于杭州的[宇树科技](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/688836%20CH%20Equity)，夺得市场份额第一，在六个月内以8,400台的出货量占全球出货量的44%，而宇树科技的出货量为5,900台。它们的出货量使[特斯拉公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/TSLA%20US%20Equity)、[Figure AI Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/2265591D:US)和[Agility Robotics Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1585639D:US)等美国顶级公司的出货量相形见绌，突显了中国境内的发展速度。

7月下旬，美国[禁止进口](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-29/us-risks-broadening-china-tech-clash-with-robot-inverter-curbs)新的中国人形机器人和四足机器人及部分组件，理由是这些产品对美国关键人工智能基础设施构成国家安全和网络安全风险。

尽管实际投入商业生产用途的人形机器人数量仍然很少，但中国的政策支持和各级政府资金正在帮助加速它们进入主流。上个月在上海举行的世界人工智能大会上，这种机器人的优先地位得到了充分展示，数十家机器人制造商展示了在灵活性、速度和能力方面的进展。

这些机器人的使用方式也正在发生重要转变。

“工业和商业应用占出货量的70%以上，高于一年前的约50%。”SAG创始人兼首席研究员Linda Sui表示。Sui补充说，监管不确定性和地缘政治风险可能影响该行业下一阶段的增长。

### English Original

## China Humanoid Makers Hold 97% of Global Shipments, Report Says

China’s humanoid robot makers commanded more than 97% of global shipments in the first half of 2026, according to new industry data affirming the country’s early lead against US rivals in the burgeoning field.

Global humanoid robot shipments totaled roughly 19,100 units in the first half of 2026, more than triple the 5,100 units shipped in the same period last year, according to [data from Smart Analytics Global](https://smartanalyticsglobal.com/global-humanoid-robot-shipments-2026-agibot-unitree/). The California-based research firm expects shipments to rise to around 60,000 units this year and reach half a million by 2030.

Shanghai-based [Agibot](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/2267429D:CH) overtook Hangzhou-based [Unitree Robotics](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/688836%20CH%20Equity) to claim the top spot in market share, capturing 44% of global shipments with 8,400 units in the six-month period, compared with Unitree’s 5,900 units. Their volumes dwarf shipments from top US companies such as [Tesla Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/TSLA%20US%20Equity), [Figure AI Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/2265591D:US) and [Agility Robotics Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1585639D:US), underscoring the pace of development within China.

In late July, the US [banned imports](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-29/us-risks-broadening-china-tech-clash-with-robot-inverter-curbs) of new Chinese humanoid and quadruped robots, along with certain components, citing national security and cybersecurity risks to critical US artificial intelligence infrastructure.

Although the number of humanoid robots actually deployed in commercially productive roles remains small, China’s policy support and funding from various levels of governments are helping accelerate their path into the mainstream. The prioritization of such robotics was on full display at the World AI Conference in Shanghai last month, where dozens of robot makers unveiled advances in dexterity, speed and capabilities.

An important shift is also underway in how the robots are being used.

“Industrial and commercial applications accounted for more than 70% of shipments, up from approximately 50% a year earlier,” said Linda Sui, founder and principal researcher at SAG. Regulatory uncertainty and geopolitical risks could shape the industry’s next phase of growth, Sui added.
