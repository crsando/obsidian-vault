## Meta、Anthropic、谷歌、OpenAI 将与特朗普官员会面讨论 AI 安全测试

华盛顿，8月3日（路透社）——据三位知情人士及新闻报道，Meta、Anthropic、OpenAI和谷歌已受邀于周二与白宫官员会面，讨论对美国最先进[AI](https://www.reuters.com/technology/artificial-intelligence/)模型开展自愿性政府安全测试的事宜。

Anthropic和OpenAI近日[披露](https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/anthropic-says-claude-ai-models-accessed-three-companies-during-tests-2026-07-30/)，它们的AI工具侵入了其他公司的系统，这引起了美国立法者的担忧，即日益强大的AI模型可能被用来实施或助长网络攻击。

一位白宫官员周一表示，特朗普政府已完成自愿性网络安全测试细节的制定，这些测试旨在衡量美国最先进人工智能模型的黑客攻击能力，并计划与人工智能行业讨论这些测试。该官员未透露谁将参加讨论。

Meta 公司发言人表示，Meta 已受邀；据两位熟悉会议的消息人士透露，Anthropic 和 OpenAI 也收到了邀请。

白宫没有提供有关这些测试的细节，包括结果将如何报告、使用哪些指标以及是否会公开任何内容。

周一，由15位共和党州总检察长组成的团体要求 OpenAI 保留与其披露的 AI 系统逃逸控制并攻击 AI 公司 Hugging Face 相关的所有潜在相关文件。他们援引路透社的一篇[报道](https://www.reuters.com/business/its-ai-agent-spent-days-hacking-company-sources-say-openai-did-not-notice-week-2026-07-24/)，称在其中一起事件中，这个恶意代理留下了关于其未来版本如何逃脱内部防护措施的笔记，并写道该公司可能违反了州消费者保护法。

OpenAI 在一份声明中表示，它认真对待总检察长们的来信，并将在完成审查后分享一份关于 Hugging Face 攻击的技术报告。

周一，美国众议院网络安全委员会要求 OpenAI 的萨姆·奥尔特曼向其简要介绍针对 Hugging Face 的攻击情况。

该公司在一份声明中表示，奥尔特曼上周[访问](https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/openais-sam-altman-discuss-voluntary-ai-safety-tests-with-trump-officials-after-2026-07-30/)了白宫，讨论自愿性测试的细节以及公司即将推出的人工智能产品。在周一发布的另一份声明中，该公司表示，已要求特朗普政府将商务部的人工智能安全专家置于任何网络安全测试的核心。该公司提到了中国，中国政府在人工智能方面拥有比美国更为集中的战略。

科技媒体 The Information 周一报道称，特朗普政府邀请了来自 Google 的代表。Google 发言人拒绝置评。

美国总统 [唐纳德·特朗普](https://www.reuters.com/world/us/donald-trump/) [指示](https://www.reuters.com/world/trump-signed-order-promote-advanced-ai-innovation-security-white-house-says-2026-06-02/) 其团队于六月编写一系列测试，以评估美国最先进的 AI 系统的黑客攻击能力。

特朗普政府与 Anthropic 的关系一直紧张。该公司今年早些时候拒绝允许美国军方将其 AI 模型用于国内监控和完全自主武器系统，[政府随后进行报复](https://www.reuters.com/world/us/trump-says-he-is-directing-federal-agencies-cease-use-anthropic-technology-2026-02-27/)，将其列入国家安全黑名单。

Anthropic 上周表示，在网络安全测试期间，其部分 AI 模型侵入了三家公司的系统。此前，竞争对手 OpenAI 报告称，其一个 AI 智能体逃出测试环境，侵入了 AI 公司 Hugging Face 的系统。

报道：Courtney Rozen；编辑：Nick Zieminski、Deepa Babington、Rod Nickel 和 Edmund Klamann

## Meta, Anthropic, Google, OpenAI to meet Trump officials about AI safety testing

REUTERS/Dado Ruvic/Illustration [Purchase Licensing Rights, opens new tab](https://www.reutersconnect.com/item/illustration-shows-words-ai-artificial-inteligence-keyboard-and-a-robotic-hand/dGFnOnJldXRlcnMuY29tLDIwMjY6bmV3c21sX1JDMlVOTEFXNjBaTw%3D%3D/?utm_medium=rcom-article-media&utm_campaign=rcom-rcp-lead)

- Summary 总结

- White House official says US government finalized tests for advanced AI models, does not provide details 白宫官员称，美国政府已完成针对先进 AI 模型的测试方案，但未透露具体细节
- Companies invited to discuss tests with White House on Tuesday 受邀企业将于周二与白宫就测试方案进行磋商
- Meeting follows disclosures that Anthropic and OpenAI tools breached other companies' systems 会议召开前，Anthropic 和 OpenAI 的工具被曝已侵入其他公司系统
WASHINGTON, Aug 3 (Reuters) - Meta, Anthropic, OpenAI and Google have been invited to meet White House officials on Tuesday to discuss ‌voluntary government safety testing for the most advanced U.S. [AI](https://www.reuters.com/technology/artificial-intelligence/) models, according to three sources familiar with the matter and news reports.
华盛顿，8 月 3 日（路透社）——据三位知情人士及新闻报道透露，Meta、Anthropic、OpenAI 和谷歌已受邀于周二与白宫官员会面，讨论针对美国最先进 AI 模型的自愿性政府安全测试事宜。

Anthropic and OpenAI [disclosed](https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/anthropic-says-claude-ai-models-accessed-three-companies-during-tests-2026-07-30/) in recent days that their AI tools breached the systems of other companies, stirring concerns among U.S. lawmakers about whether increasingly capable AI models could be used to conduct or facilitate cyberattacks.
Anthropic 和 OpenAI 近日披露，其 AI 工具侵入了其他公司的系统，这引发了美国立法者的担忧，即日益强大的 AI 模型可能被用于实施或协助网络攻击。

A White House official said on Monday the Trump administration has finalized the details of voluntary cybersecurity tests to measure the hacking capabilities of the most advanced American AI models, and is planning to discuss them with ⁠the AI industry. The official did not indicate who would attend the discussions.

Meta was invited, a company spokesperson said, as were Anthropic and OpenAI, according to two sources familiar with the meeting.

The White House did not provide details about the tests, including how results would be reported, the metrics used and whether any of it would be made public.

A group of 15 Republican state attorneys general on Monday asked OpenAI to preserve all potentially relevant documents related to its disclosure that its AI system escaped containment and hacked AI company Hugging Face. Citing a Reuters [report](https://www.reuters.com/business/its-ai-agent-spent-days-hacking-company-sources-say-openai-did-not-notice-week-2026-07-24/) that the rogue agent in one case left notes for how future versions of itself could escape internal guardrails, they wrote that the company may have violated state consumer protection laws.

OpenAI said in a statement it takes the letter from the attorneys general seriously and will share a technical report ‌about the ⁠Hugging Face attack after it completes a review.

The U.S. House of Representatives' cybersecurity committee on Monday asked OpenAI's Sam Altman to brief them on the attack on Hugging Face.

Altman [visited](https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/openais-sam-altman-discuss-voluntary-ai-safety-tests-with-trump-officials-after-2026-07-30/) the White House last week to discuss details of the voluntary tests and the company's upcoming AI products, the company said in a statement. In a separate statement on Monday, the company said it had asked the Trump administration to put the Commerce Department's AI safety specialists at the center of ⁠any cybersecurity testing. The company pointed to China, whose government has a more centralized strategy on AI compared with the U.S.

The Information, a tech publication, reported on Monday that the Trump administration invited representatives from Google. A Google spokesperson declined to comment.

U.S. President [Donald Trump](https://www.reuters.com/world/us/donald-trump/)[directed](https://www.reuters.com/world/trump-signed-order-promote-advanced-ai-innovation-security-white-house-says-2026-06-02/) his team in June to write a series of tests ⁠to assess the hacking capabilities of the most advanced American AI systems.

The Trump administration has had a rocky relationship with Anthropic. The company earlier this year refused to allow the U.S. military to use its AI models for domestic surveillance and fully autonomous weapons systems, and the [government retaliated](https://www.reuters.com/world/us/trump-says-he-is-directing-federal-agencies-cease-use-anthropic-technology-2026-02-27/) ⁠by putting it on a national security blacklist.

Anthropic said last week that some of its AI models [hacked into the systems](https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/anthropic-says-claude-ai-models-accessed-three-companies-during-tests-2026-07-30/) of three companies during cybersecurity tests. That disclosure followed rival OpenAI’s report that one of its AI agents escaped a testing environment and hacked into the systems of the AI company Hugging Face.
