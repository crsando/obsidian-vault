### 中文译文

## 海尔称欧洲和印度空调需求抵消中国销售疲软

员工在中国武汉的海尔工厂空调生产线上工作。*摄影师：AFP/Getty Images*

[海尔智家股份有限公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/600690:CH)表示，来自欧洲和印度的空调等制冷设备需求增加，帮助其抵消了国内需求疲软的影响。

这家总部位于山东的家电及制冷设备制造商表示，针对南亚的“极端工作条件”，该公司定制并开发了可在高达70摄氏度（158华氏度）温度下正常运行的空调。该地区上半年销售额增长了17%。

在欧洲，反复的热浪促使居民购买便携式空调设备，因为大多数家庭没有制冷设施。西班牙的暖通空调销售额增长了50%以上，而法国和英国则出现了“明显的复苏和渠道扩张”。

“暖通空调行业正迎来中长期发展机遇，”该公司在周四的600690 海尔智家股份有限公司2026年半年度报告中表示。“全球气温上升正在系统性地推高制冷需求，加速欧洲等低渗透市场的需求释放。”

同行[格力电器股份有限公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/000651%20CH%20Equity)在周三的000651 格力电器：2026年半年度报告中表示，其今年上半年在挪威和丹麦等西北欧国家的销售额跃升92%，而在西班牙、葡萄牙和法国的自有品牌收入增长了55%。

海尔超过一半的销售额来自海外——这一比例远高于格力电器和[美的集团](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/000333:CH)——这有助于限制中国业务疲软的影响，在中国，长期的房地产低迷和疲弱的消费者信心拖累了销售。

美的集团定于周五公布上半年业绩。

## Haier Sees Europe, India AC Demand Offset Weak China Sales

Employees work on an air conditioner production line at a Haier factory in Wuhan, China. *Photographer: AFP/Getty Images*

[Haier Smart Home Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/600690:CH) said it’s seeing increased demand for cooling appliances such as air conditioners from Europe and India, helping it offset sluggish demand at home.

The Shandong-based maker of home appliances and cooling equipment said it customized and developed air conditioners able to operate normally in temperatures up to 70C (158F), in response to “extreme working conditions” in South Asia. Sales in the region were up 17% in the first half of the year.

In Europe, repeated heat waves have spurred residents to buy portable air conditioner units as most homes lack cooling installations. HVAC sales in Spain rose over 50%, while France and UK saw “clear recovery and channel expansion.”

“The HVAC industry is poised for medium- to long-term development opportunities,” it said in a Thursday 600690 海尔智家股份有限公司2026年半年度报告. “Rising global temperatures are systematically driving up cooling demand, accelerating demand release in low-penetration markets such as Europe.”

Peer [Gree Electric Appliances Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/000651%20CH%20Equity) on Wednesday 000651 格力电器：2026年半年度报告 sales in Northwestern European countries such as Norway and Denmark that jumped 92% in the first six months of the year, while revenue from its proprietary brands in Spain, Portugal, and France grew 55%.

Haier generated more than half of its sales overseas — a much higher rate than Gree and [Midea Group Co](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/000333:CH). — which helped limit the impact of a sluggish performance in China, where a prolonged property downturn and weak consumer confidence weigh on sales.

Midea is due to report first-half earnings on Friday.
