### 中文译文

## 中国战斗机获难得机会在演习中与“阵风”战机过招

中国先进的歼-16战斗机将参加与“阵风”战机的联合训练，这是一个难得的机会，可以在西方平台上测试其能力。此前，在去年的印巴冲突中，中国的歼-10战机与法国制造商战机的作战表现引起了关注。

根据埃及武装部队发布的照片，歼-16和达索“阵风”战机参加了今年在埃及举行的“文明之鹰”联合空中演习。埃及武装部队在一份声明中表示，第二届演习于周六开始，将持续数天。

歼-16和歼-10是中国中国人民解放军空军装备的四代半战斗机，拥有先进的航电设备、先进雷达和电子战系统。由中航工业沈飞（AVIC Shenyang）制造的歼-16比歼-10更大更重，采用双发动机和双座设计，专为远程任务而设计。中国曾在2025年派遣歼-10参加“文明之鹰”演习。

2025年5月南亚邻国之间的冲突标志着巴基斯坦首次大规模使用歼-10C战斗机，伊斯兰堡认为该战机击落了多架印度飞机，其中包括法制“阵风”战机。尽管印度对此说法持异议，但自冲突以来，中国战机的性能一直受到密切关注。

印度空军表示，在对抗中摧毁了约十几架巴基斯坦飞机，其中包括至少五架先进战斗机，如美制F-16或中制JF-17类战机。被摧毁飞机的确切数量仍存在争议，分析人士对此前冲突中双方声称的军事胜利表示质疑。

埃及是美国的战略伙伴，其军队装备美制F-16战斗机以及CH-47“支奴干”和AH-64“阿帕奇”直升机。埃及与中国保持外交关系已有约[七十年](https://english.www.gov.cn/news/202605/30/content_WS6a1a45c4c6d00ca5f9a0b538.html)，近年来关系不断深化。

## China’s Fighter Jet Gets Rare Chance to Face Rafales in Exercise

China’s advanced J-16 fighter is set to train alongside Rafales, offering a rare opportunity to test its capabilities against a Western platform after China’s J-10 drew attention for its combat performance against the French maker’s planes in the India-Pakistan conflict last year.

The J-16 and the Dassault Rafale are taking part in this year’s Eagles of Civilization joint air exercise in Egypt, according to photos released by the Egyptian Armed Forces. The second edition of the drills began Saturday and will last several days, the [EAF](https://www.mod.gov.eg/modwebsite/NewsDetailsAr.aspx?id=45900) said in a statement.

The J-16 and J-10 are 4.5-generation fighters operated by China’s People’s Liberation Army Air Force, with modern avionics, advanced radar and electronic warfare systems. The J-16, built by [AVIC Shenyang](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/600760%20CH%20Equity), is larger and heavier than the J-10, with twin engines and two seats, and is designed for longer-range missions. China sent J-10s to Eagles of Civilization in 2025.

The May 2025 clash between the South Asian neighbors marked Pakistan’s first major use of J-10C fighters, which Islamabad credited with downing multiple Indian aircraft, including French-made Rafales. While India disputes the account, the Chinese aircraft’s performance has drawn intense scrutiny since the conflict.

India’s air force said it [destroyed](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-10-03/india-says-it-downed-us-china-made-jets-in-pakistan-conflict) about a dozen Pakistani aircraft during the confrontation, including at least five advanced fighters, such as the US-made F-16s or Chinese-made JF-17-class jets. The exact number of aircraft destroyed remains disputed, and analysts have questioned claims of military success made by both sides in previous conflicts.

Egypt is a US strategic partner whose military operates American F-16 fighters as well as CH-47 Chinook and AH-64 Apache helicopters. It has maintained diplomatic relations with China for about [seven decades](https://english.www.gov.cn/news/202605/30/content_WS6a1a45c4c6d00ca5f9a0b538.html), with ties deepening in recent years.
