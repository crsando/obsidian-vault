### 中文译文

## 中国对日本议员表示台湾问题是紧张关系核心

### **要点**

- 一名中国高级外交官告诉日本议员，历史问题和台湾问题是两国紧张关系的核心，东京需要采取“具体行动”来改善关系。
- 陆慷表示，日本政界人士应敦促本国领导人汲取历史教训，并“按照一个中国原则，诚实认真地处理台湾问题”。
- 日本代表团会见了包括陆慷在内的中国官员，认为面对面对话很有帮助，还参观了与机器人和自动驾驶相关的设施，并会见了在华日本企业的代表。
一名中国高级外交官对一群日本议员表示，历史问题和台湾问题是两国关系紧张的核心，并称东京需要采取“具体行动”来改善两国关系。

据周四的一份[声明](https://www.idcpc.org.cn/lldt/202608/t20260827_170032.html)，中共中央对外联络部副部长陆慷对来访的日本国会议员表示，“两国关系面临严重困难，根源是清楚的。”

该部门表示，陆慷希望日本政界人士能够敦促日本领导层汲取历史教训，并“按照一个中国原则，诚实认真地处理台湾问题”。该部门负责党的外交事务。

日本代表团包括执政的自民党的桥本岳（Gaku Hashimoto）和在野党“中间派改革联盟”的伊佐进一（Shinichi Isa）。桥本岳今年6月也曾访问北京，出席一场供应链活动。

自首相高市早苗11月发表言论暗示若北京入侵台湾，东京可能出兵以来，日本与其最大贸易伙伴之间的摩擦不断加剧。中国对此作出回应，指责日本对该地区构成军事威胁，并建议中国公民不要前往日本。

在其他措施中，北京加强了对军民两用产品的出口管制，即既可用于民用也可用于军用的物品。

作为善意的表示，高市早苗致信中国国家主席习近平，对中尼边境的[致命洪灾](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-28/rescuers-scour-nepal-valleys-for-survivors-as-flood-threats-rise)表示慰问，日本外务省日本的高市早苗就致命洪灾向习近平致唁电（8月27日）。

据一位因讨论私人事务而要求匿名的知情人士透露，日本议员代表团与陆（Lu）会谈了两个小时——比原定的一小时更长——讨论中包括一些激烈的交锋。

该人士称，日方认为这次面对面会谈是有用的。

该人士称，代表团在华期间的议程还包括与中国社会科学院及其日本研究所的官员会面。

该人士补充说，代表团还参观了北京亦庄科技中心与机器人和自动驾驶相关的设施，以及习近平的旗舰项目雄安新区，并与在华日本企业代表会面。

## China Tells Japanese Lawmakers Taiwan Is at Heart of Tensions

### **Takeaways**

- A senior Chinese diplomat told Japanese lawmakers that history and Taiwan are at the center of tensions between the nations and Tokyo needs to take "concrete actions" to improve relations.
- Lu Kang said Japan's politicians should urge the nation's leadership to learn from history and "handle the Taiwan issue honestly and earnestly according to the One-China principle".
- The Japanese delegation met with Chinese officials, including Lu, and considered the face-to-face dialogue useful, and also visited facilities related to robotics and autonomous driving and met with representatives of Japanese companies in China.
A senior Chinese diplomat told a group of Japanese lawmakers that history and Taiwan are at the center of tensions between the nations, saying Tokyo needed to take “concrete actions” for them to improve.

Lu Kang, a deputy head of the ruling Communist Party’s International Department, told the visiting members of the Japanese Diet that “relations are in serious difficulty, and the root cause is clear,” according to a [statement](https://www.idcpc.org.cn/lldt/202608/t20260827_170032.html) on Thursday.

Lu said he hoped that Japan’s politicians could urge the nation’s leadership to learn from history and “handle the Taiwan issue honestly and earnestly according to the One-China principle,” said the department, which handles diplomacy for the party.

The Japanese delegation includes Gaku Hashimoto, of the ruling Liberal Democratic Party, and Shinichi Isa, of the Centrist Reform Alliance, an opposition party. Hashimoto also visited Beijing in June to attend a supply chain event.

Friction has run high between Japan and its biggest trading partner since Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi’s November remarks implying that Tokyo could dispatch its military in the event Beijing invaded Taiwan. China responded by accusing Japan of posing a military threat to the region and advising its citizens not to travel there.

Among other measures, Beijing bolstered export controls on dual-use products, meaning items with both civilian and military applications.

In a show of goodwill, Takaichi sent Chinese President Xi Jinping a letter expressing condolences for the [deadly floods](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-28/rescuers-scour-nepal-valleys-for-survivors-as-flood-threats-rise) along the China-Nepal border, Japan’s Ministry of Foreign Affairs Japan’s Takaichi Sends Xi Condolences on Deadly Floods (Aug. 27).

The delegation of Japanese lawmakers met Lu for two hours — longer than the one hour that was planned — and the discussion included some tough exchanges, according to a person familiar with the matter, who asked not to be identified discussing private matters.

The Japanese side considered the face-to-face dialogue useful, the person said.

The group’s agenda while in China also included meetings with officials from the Chinese Academy of Social Sciences and its Institute of Japanese Studies, the person said.

It also visited facilities related to robotics and autonomous driving in Beijing’s Yizhuang tech hub as well as Xi’s [flagship Xiong’an New Area](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-23/xi-urges-new-flagship-city-to-lead-tech-finance-policy-trials) and met with representatives of Japanese companies in China, the person added.
