## 中国债券因与全球同行政策分化而逆势抗跌

作者：[Julia Zhong](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AXeDYtoIWg0/julia-zhong) 和 [Shulun Huang](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AWlV0NAHrz4/shulun-huang)

### **要点**

- 中国长期国债收益率大幅下跌，交易员押注经济将进一步疲软，凸显出与全球市场日益扩大的分化，全球收益率已飙升至多年高位。
- 30年期国债收益率进一步下挫，创去年11月底以来新低，与10年期国债的利差收窄至约50个基点。
- 中国收益率下滑与全球利率高企之间的差距不断扩大，反映出国内通缩风险；随着油价冲击消退和国内支出疲软，此前的再通胀动能减弱。
中国长期国债收益率大幅下跌，交易员押注经济将进一步疲软，凸显出与全球市场日益扩大的分化——全球收益率已飙升至[多年高位](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-18/bond-rout-sends-long-term-borrowing-costs-to-highest-in-decades)。

30年期国债收益率延续跌势，降至11月底以来最低，与10年期国债的利差收窄至约50个基点，接近2月以来的最窄水平。周五，[30年期国债期货](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/TBS1%20Comdty)的持仓量飙升至历史新高，凸显出对收益率进一步下跌的激进押注。

中泰证券股份有限公司（Zhongtai Securities Co.）分析师吕品（Lv Pin）将10年至30年期利差压缩称为年内“风险回报比最佳的交易”，预计30年期收益率将跌向2%。

惨淡的7月经济数据——产出、零售和投资均未达到预期——助推了中国进一步政策宽松的势头。交易员纷纷涌入长期债务，表明他们预计中国经济将持续疲软。这与世界其他地区的高利率形成鲜明对比，美国10年期国债收益率本周触及2025年以来最高水平，全球长期债券承压。

“这轮上涨主要由短期交易组合而非长期配置组合驱动，”总部位于深圳的私募基金管理公司善泽基金（Shanze Fund）研究总监宋晓飞（Song Xiaofei）表示。“如果货币宽松和流动性充裕的预期一致，10年/30年期利差仍有压缩至40个基点的空间。”

中国国债今年迄今回报率达[2.6%](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/I08273CN%20Index)（不含汇率波动），在主要债券市场中排名第三。周三，中国30年期国债收益率持稳于2.15%，接近上一交易日触及的九个月低点。债券需求依然强劲，中国周三发行20年期特别国债，中标利率为2.0991%，投标倍数最高。交易员：中国20年期特别国债加权平均中标利率2.0991%。

与此同时，全球主要经济体的长期收益率飙升：美国30年期国债触及2007年高点，法国借贷成本升至2008年以来未见水平，日本收益率接近历史峰值。

中国收益率下滑与全球利率高企之间不断扩大的差距，反映了国内[通缩风险](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-09/china-s-inflation-cools-as-oil-shock-of-iran-war-starts-to-ease)。随着油价冲击消退和国内支出疲软，此前的再通胀动能逐渐减弱。这使中国处于与主要经济体不同的宏观经济轨道上。

收益率分化使北京如履薄冰：收益率差扩大可能引发资本外流并给人民币带来压力，而过于平坦的收益率曲线压缩银行净息差。更广泛地说，债券涨势表明流动性仍被困在金融资产中，而非流入实体经济，这可能在经济增长停滞之际令私营企业信贷枯竭。

“中国经济尚未显示出由内需驱动的明确复苏迹象，因此市场继续定价降息预期，这与许多其他主要地区的加息前景形成对比，”渣打银行北亚区首席投资官Raymond Cheng表示。

“今年上半年，中国政府发行了大量债券，但支出并未跟上。未来，关键是要观察当局是否会宣布任何大规模增量刺激措施，尤其是在最近的政治局会议之后，”他表示。

在地方政府债券发行上半年[明显滞后](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-14/china-is-set-for-record-bond-issuance-this-quarter-analysts-say)之后，市场预期第三季度将出现创纪录的发行量，这可能减缓债券涨势。然而，分析师预计，一旦供应激增过去，长期收益率将继续走低。

Shanze Fund的Song表示，要让10年至30年期收益率利差降至40个基点以下并保持在该水平，债券供应需要放缓，长期机构买家需要介入。国联民生证券分析师梁旭认为，随着发行担忧消退，30年期收益率将在第四季度降至2.0%至2.05%。

另外，中国首次允许住房公积金[购买政策性银行债券](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-19/china-widens-use-of-1-6-trillion-fund-to-spur-spending-on-homes)，此举旨在提高回报。

## China Bonds Buck Global Selloff on Policy Divide With Peers

By [Julia Zhong](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AXeDYtoIWg0/julia-zhong) and [Shulun Huang](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AWlV0NAHrz4/shulun-huang)

*Updated on August 19, 2026 at 1:54 PM GMT+8*

### **Takeaways **by Bloomberg AI

### **Takeaways**

- Chinese long-end bond yields are tumbling as traders bet on further economic weakness, highlighting a growing divide with global markets where yields have soared to multi-year highs.
- Yields on 30‑year government bonds have extended declines to the lowest since late November, shrinking the spread over 10-year notes to around 50 basis points.
- The growing gap between China’s slumping yields and elevated global rates reflects the deflation risk at home as earlier reflation momentum fades with the subsiding oil shock and weak domestic spending.
Chinese long-end bond yields are tumbling as traders bet on further economic weakness, highlighting a growing divide with global markets where yields have soared to [multi-year highs](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-18/bond-rout-sends-long-term-borrowing-costs-to-highest-in-decades).

Yields on 30‑year government bonds have extended declines to the lowest since late November, shrinking the spread over 10-year notes to around 50 basis points, near its narrowest since February. Open interest in [30-year bond futures](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/TBS1%20Comdty) surged to a record high on Friday, underscoring aggressive bets on a further decline in yields.

Zhongtai Securities Co. analysts including Lv Pin call the 10- to 30-year spread compression “the trade with the best risk-reward profile” for the remainder of the year, projecting 30-year yields to fall toward 2%.

Dismal July economic data — with output, retail, and investment all missing forecasts — has fueled momentum for further policy easing in China. Traders are piling into longer-term debt in a sign that they foresee prolonged economic softness in China. That stands in sharp contrast to elevated rates elsewhere, with US 10-year yields touching their highest levels since 2025 this week as long-dated paper comes under pressure worldwide.

“This round of rally is mainly driven by short-term trading portfolios rather than long-term allocation portfolios,” said Song Xiaofei, research director of Shanze Fund, a private fund management company based in Shenzhen. “If expectations of monetary easing and loose liquidity align, there could still be room to squeeze the 10‑/30‑year spread toward 40 basis points.”

Chinese government bonds have returned [2.6%](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/I08273CN%20Index) so far this year excluding currency swings, ranking third among major debt markets. Yields on China’s 30-year bonds held steady at 2.15% on Wednesday, near a nine- month low touched in the last session. Demand for bonds remains robust, with 20-year special sovereign notes China Sells 20-Year Special Sovereign Bonds at 2.0991%: Trader at the highest bid-to-cover ratio on 交易员：中国20年期特别国债加权平均中标利率2.0991% on Wednesday.

Meanwhile, global long-term yields spiked across major economies: US 30-year Treasuries touched 2007 highs, French borrowing costs hit levels not seen since 2008 while Japanese yields neared record peaks.

The growing gap between China’s slumping yields and elevated global rates reflects the [deflation risk](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-09/china-s-inflation-cools-as-oil-shock-of-iran-war-starts-to-ease) at home as earlier reflation momentum fades with the subsiding oil shock and weak domestic spending. That leaves China on a different macroeconomic trajectory than major peers.

The yield divergence leaves Beijing walking a tightrope as a widening yield spread threatens to spur capital outflows and pressure the yuan, while an overly flat curve shrinks bank net interest margins. More broadly, the bond rally signals that liquidity is remaining trapped in financial assets rather than flowing into the real economy, threatening to starve private businesses of credit just as the economy is stalling.

“China’s economy has yet to show clear signs of domestically-led recovery, so markets continue to price in expectations of rate cuts, in contrast to rate‑hike outlooks in many other major regions,” said Raymond Cheng, Standard Chartered CIO for North Asia.

“In the first half of the year, the Chinese government issued a sizable amount of bonds, while spending didn’t keep pace. Going forward, the key to monitor is whether authorities will announce any sizable incremental stimulus, especially after the recent Politburo meeting,” he said.

After local government bond issuance [lagged significantly](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-14/china-is-set-for-record-bond-issuance-this-quarter-analysts-say) in the first half of the year, expectations are building for a record-breaking third quarter of sales, which could slow the bond rally. However, analysts expect long-end yields to keep grinding lower once that supply surge passes.

Shanze Fund’s Song said that for the 10- to 30-year yield spread to fall below 40 basis points and stay there, debt supply needs to slow down and long-term institutional buyers need to step in. Guolian Minsheng Securities analyst Liang Xu sees 30-year yields dropping toward 2.0%–2.05% in the fourth quarter as issuance fears abate.

Separately, China is allowing the housing provident fund to [purchase policy bank bonds](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-19/china-widens-use-of-1-6-trillion-fund-to-spur-spending-on-homes) for the first time, in a move aimed at boosting returns.
