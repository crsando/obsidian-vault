## 中国火箭发射通信卫星时爆炸

8月10日，一枚长征七号改运载火箭在海南省上空爆炸。*摄影：孟忠德/VCG/盖蒂图片社*

### **要点**

- 据官方媒体报道，一枚长征七号改火箭在发射通信卫星时爆炸。
- 火箭搭载的是中星4B通信卫星，火箭上无人员。

据官方媒体报道，一枚中国主力火箭在发射通信卫星时爆炸，这是该火箭家族今年第二次失败。

据[任务直播](https://www.youtube.com/live/GBj4IB6zM1s?t=2439s)，周一晚间，一枚长征七号甲火箭在海南省文昌航天发射场升空约一分半钟后疑似发生爆炸。

这枚火箭搭载的是中星4B通信卫星。火箭上没有人员。官方新华社[确认](https://english.news.cn/20260810/092535e6a2bb414082f9cd4cde22a2a2/c.html)了火箭故障，称原因正在调查中。

航天相关股票周二在卫星发射失败后下跌。中国卫星股份有限公司、湖南航天环宇通信技术有限公司和中国卫通集团股份有限公司的跌幅均至少达到6.6%，随后有所收窄。

国内媒体第一财经[援引](https://www.yicai.com/brief/103312919.html)未透露姓名的知情人士报道，原定于周二发射的商业火箭朱雀三号被推迟。

这一事件标志着中国航天计划罕见受挫，该计划正在与俄罗斯和美国争夺领先地位。长征七号甲自2020年首飞遇到问题以来，已成功执行了十多次任务。

长征系列的另一型号长征三号乙于1月[发射失败](https://english.news.cn/20260117/a9d87c637cca4a85b732b41664de98b6/c.html)。两者都被视为中国航天计划中可靠的组成部分。

另一型号长征五号预计在未来几周内发射嫦娥七号任务，该任务旨在将机器人航天器送往月球南极着陆。长征五号与长征七号甲共享硬件，但目前尚不清楚周一的故障是否会导致即将到来的任务推迟。

## China Rocket Explodes During Communications Satellite Launch

A Long March 7A carrier rocket explodes over Hainan province on Aug. 10.*Photographer: Meng Zhongde/VCG/Getty Images*

By [Loren Grush](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AV6nXO-QPVQ/loren-grush)August 11, 2026 at 12:52 AM GMT+8*Updated on August 11, 2026 at 11:03 AM GMT+8*

### **Takeaways ** by Bloomberg AI

### **Takeaways**

- A Long March-7A rocket blew up while launching a communications satellite, according to state media.
- The rocket was carrying the Zhongxing-4B communications satellite and no people were on board the rocket.
- The cause of the rocket failure is under investigation, and the incident marks a rare setback for China’s space program.
A workhorse Chinese rocket blew up while launching a communications satellite, state media reported, the second failure of the rocket family this year.

A Long March-7A rocket appeared to explode roughly a minute and a half after taking off from the Wenchang Spacecraft Launch Site in the southern province of Hainan on Monday evening, [according to a livestream of the mission](https://www.youtube.com/live/GBj4IB6zM1s?t=2439s).

The rocket was carrying the Zhongxing-4B communications satellite. No people were on board the rocket. The official Xinhua News Agency [confirmed](https://english.news.cn/20260810/092535e6a2bb414082f9cd4cde22a2a2/c.html) the rocket failure, saying that the cause was under investigation.

Chinese space-related stocks Chinese Space Stocks Slide After Satellite Launch Failure on Tuesday. China Spacesat Co., Hunan Aerospace Huanyu Communication Technology Co. and China Satellite Communications Co. all retreated at least 6.6% before paring the loss.

The planned launch on Tuesday of commercial rocket Zhuque-3 was delayed, domestic media outlet Yicai reported, [citing ](https://www.yicai.com/brief/103312919.html)people familiar with the matter whom it didn’t identify.

The incident marks a rare setback for China’s space program, which is competing with Russia and the US for supremacy. The Long March-7A has had more than a dozen successful missions since the first flight in 2020 encountered a problem.

Another variant of the Long March, the 3B, [misfired ](https://english.news.cn/20260117/a9d87c637cca4a85b732b41664de98b6/c.html)in January. Both are considered reliable parts of the Chinese program.

An additional rocket variant, the Long March 5, is slated in the coming weeks to launch the Chang’e-7 mission, which aims to send robotic spacecraft to land at the moon’s south pole. The Long March 5 shares hardware with the Long March-7A, but it’s unclear if Monday’s failure will lead to a delay of the upcoming mission.
