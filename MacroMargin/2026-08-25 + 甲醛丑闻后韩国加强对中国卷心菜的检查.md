### 中文译文

## 甲醛丑闻后韩国加强对中国卷心菜的检查

韩国加强了对来自中国卷心菜的检查，此前中国卖家被发现在使用甲醛保鲜这种十字花科蔬菜，引发北京方面的广泛打击。

卷心菜是泡菜的重要原料，泡菜在韩国饮食中无处不在。韩国食品医药品安全部表示，已加强边境对中国卷心菜的检查，并正在核查问题蔬菜是否已运抵韩国。该部周一在一则短信中表示，如有必要将采取更多措施。

在显示卷心菜被浸入工业化学品中以延长保鲜期的视频在网上广泛传播后，中国[发起了](https://www.samr.gov.cn/xw/zj/art/2026/art_c43d4419b3244191a4ed37b5ba2d59fa.html)抽查和市场检查。这种化学品也用于太平间保存尸体等用途。

“任何违反法律法规的行为，一经发现，将严厉打击，零容忍”，中国国家市场监督管理总局在一份声明中表示。

![Kimchi at a food stall in Jeonju New Joongang Market in Jeonju. Photographer: SeongJoon Cho/Bloomberg](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3c781cc90b14819cad40c693beee300b/3c781cc9-0b14-8148-905f-e0195f21634c-e17f839b1db50678925076839c4fa0a6.png)

韩国即将进入被称为“ kimjang ”的泡菜腌制季，这是一项被联合国教科文组织列入名录的传统实践，从深秋开始一直持续到年底。

虽然韩国自产大部分卷心菜，但从中国进口的卷心菜以及中国产泡菜已成为餐馆和其他餐饮服务业的重要廉价供应来源。

海关数据显示，在截至7月的12个月里，韩国进口的341,316.9吨泡菜中，99.9%来自中国。中国还几乎包揽了传统上用来制作泡菜的大白菜的所有进口。

这起食品安全丑闻的爆发，源于网上流传的视频显示，中国河北省北部康保县的工人在将卷心菜装车前将菜根浸入液体中。据中国和韩国媒体报道，当地政府确认在采购和运输过程中使用了甲醛。

## South Korea Tightens China Cabbage Inspections After Formaldehyde Scandal

South Korea has stepped up checks of cabbage arriving from China after sellers there were found to be using formaldehyde to keep the brassicas fresh, triggering a widespread crackdown by Beijing.

Cabbage is an essential ingredient in kimchi, a fermented dish that is omnipresent in the Korean diet. South Korea’s Ministry of Food and Drug Safety said it had strengthened inspections of Chinese cabbage at the border and was checking whether affected vegetables had already arrived in the country. It will take more measures if needed, it said in a text message Monday.

China [launched](https://www.samr.gov.cn/xw/zj/art/2026/art_c43d4419b3244191a4ed37b5ba2d59fa.html) spot checks and market inspections after videos circulated widely online, showing cabbages being dipped into the industrial chemical — which is used by morgues to preserve dead bodies, among other applications — to make them last longer.

“Any violations of laws or regulations, once discovered, will be severely punished with zero tolerance,” China’s State Administration for Market Regulation said in a statement.

South Korea is heading into kimchi making season known as kimjang, a UNESCO-listed heritage practice, which starts late autumn and runs through to year end.

While Korea produces the bulk of its own cabbage, imports from China, including Chinese-made kimchi, have become an important source of cheaper supply for restaurants and other food-service businesses.

Of the 341,316.9 tons of kimchi South Korea imported in the 12 months through July, 99.9% came from China, customs data show. China also accounted for nearly all imports of napa cabbage, the variety traditionally used to make kimchi.

The food safety scandal erupted after footage circulated online showing workers in China’s Kangbao county, in northern Hebei province, dipping cabbage roots into liquid before loading the vegetables onto trucks. Local authorities confirmed formaldehyde had been used during the purchasing and transportation process, according to Chinese and Korean media reports.
