Aug 18 (Reuters) - A U.S. congressional advisory body said on Tuesday that China is commercialising and monetising data as a strategic national asset to power its AI and technology goals, which could potentially give it an advantage over the U.S. in the AI race.
8 月 18 日（路透社）——美国国会一个咨询机构周二表示，中国正在将数据作为战略性国家资产加以商业化运作并从中获利，以此推动其人工智能及技术发展目标，这可能会让中国在人工智能领域的竞争中占据优势。

Beijing is "marshalling data to drive productivity ... (and) improve its intelligence collection ‌and military capabilities," the U.S.-China Economic and Security Review Commission wrote in a report released on Tuesday.
美国-中国经济与安全审查委员会在周二发布的报告中指出，北京正在“整合各类数据以提高生产力……并加强其情报收集与军事能力”。

China's National Data Administration did not immediately respond to a faxed request for comment outside of working hours.
中国国家数据管理局在非工作时间收到传真询问后，未立即予以回复。

While major U.S. AI companies have largely exhausted the open internet for language model training, China is systematically collecting domestic "enterprise, operational and physical-world data that cannot be scraped" — crucial for training AI tools designed for business use, autonomous vehicles and humanoid robots, a major ⁠symbol of Beijing's top-down innovation push.
尽管美国的大型人工智能企业已在很大程度上利用公开网络数据进行语言模型训练，但中国却在系统地收集国内的“企业数据、运营数据以及无法通过爬虫获取的物理世界数据”——这些数据对于训练用于商业场景、自动驾驶汽车以及人形机器人的人工智能工具至关重要，也是北京推行自上而下创新战略的重要体现。

The report said for example that China's advanced manufacturing and industrial robotics ecosystems provide a vast pool of high-quality data for use in embodied AI applications — AI systems that operate in the physical world, such as robots — giving it a potential advantage over the U.S. in developing robotics software for commercial and military uses.
报告指出，例如，中国先进的制造业和工业机器人生态系统为用于实体人工智能应用提供了大量高质量数据——这类人工智能系统是在物理世界中运行的，比如机器人——这使中国在开发用于商业和军事领域的机器人软件方面具备超越美国的优势。

"Over the last half decade, China has been able to consolidate data, find ways to label it and refine it, and hoover up new data and make sure it's quickly made available to entities," said Mike Kuiken, the commission's vice chair, in an interview.
该委员会副主席迈克·库伊肯在采访中表示：“在过去的五年里，中国已经能够整合各类数据，找到为数据标注和优化的方法，同时不断收集新数据，并确保这些数据能迅速被相关机构获取。”

"It has the benefit of advancing their innovation ecosystem, and it has the benefit of enhancing the control of the (Chinese Communist) Party."
“这既有助于推动他们的创新生态发展，也有利于加强（中国共产党）的掌控力。”

China ‌has tightened ⁠its regulatory oversight of cross-border data transfer in recent years, leading to widespread [compliance issues](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/european-firms-urge-china-give-more-clarity-data-transfer-laws-2023-11-15/) for foreign multinational firms operating in the country.
近年来，中国加强了对于跨境数据传输的监管力度，导致在华经营的外国跨国企业普遍面临合规问题。

In order to be in compliance with China’s cross-border data regulations, the firms had to further localise their China subsidiaries and separate their China customer data from their global operations, although Beijing has since introduced [limited exemptions](https://www.reuters.com/markets/eu-china-launch-mechanism-facilitate-industrial-data-flows-2024-08-28/) for some firms.
为遵守中国的跨境数据法规，这些企业不得不进一步将其中国子公司本地化，并将中国客户数据与全球业务数据分开管理，不过北京方面后来为部分企业提供了有限的豁免措施。

U.S. firms operating in China "risk running afoul of China's ⁠strict data and cyber governance regimes," the report said.
报告指出，在华经营的美国企业“有可能违反中国严格的数据与网络监管规定”。

China has elevated data to a core "factor of production" alongside land, labour, capital and technology, and it established the National Data Administration body in 2023 that oversees nationwide data standardisation and classification and aims to build a unified ⁠national market for data trading.
中国已将数据与土地、劳动力、资本和技术一同视为核心的“生产要素”，并于 2023 年成立了国家数据管理局，负责统筹全国的数据标准化与分类工作，旨在构建统一的国家数据交易市场。

Beijing has also pursued the commercialisation and deployment of data in its digital economy, alongside a broader push to [deploy AI across all industries](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/china-vows-accelerate-technological-self-reliance-ai-push-2026-03-05/).
北京方面也在其数字经济中推动数据的商业化与应用，同时大力推动人工智能在各个行业的普及应用。

It aims to standardise data assets across critical industries including manufacturing, transport, finance and healthcare, ⁠while Chinese firms have begun to list their proprietary datasets on regional data exchanges in cities including Shanghai, Shenzhen and Beijing.
该举措旨在实现制造业、交通业、金融业和医疗保健业等关键行业的数据资产标准化，与此同时，中国企业也已开始在上海、深圳和北京等城市的区域数据交易所上架其专属数据集。

"We recommend that U.S. Congress think about a national data strategy and how the U.S. government could treat data as an economic asset as our number one recommendation," Kuiken said.
“我们建议美国国会制定国家数据战略，并将数据视为经济资产来加以管理，这是我们最核心的建议，”库伊肯说道。
