## 中国Zbtlink暂停销售被发现含后门的路由器

![Illustration shows displayed CYBER SECURITY words and binary code](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3b581cc90b14810fa64ff236d0fdd4bf/3b581cc9-0b14-8125-8e96-d597a6fb8401-b823132f4d8b224f51487578b392f0ec.jpg)

2022年2月1日拍摄的图片插图中，可以看到在显示的 CYBER SECURITY 字样和二进制代码前，有正在使用电脑的人们和挂锁的3D打印模型。REUTERS/Dado Ruvic/插图 [购买授权许可，打开新标签页](https://www.reutersconnect.com/item/illustration-shows-displayed-cyber-security-words-and-binary-code/dGFnOnJldXRlcnMuY29tLDIwMjI6bmV3c21sX1JDMlhBUzk3V0Q0WQ%3D%3D/?utm_medium=rcom-article-media&utm_campaign=rcom-rcp-lead)

8月6日（路透社）——中国路由器制造商Zbtlink Electronics周四表示，将暂停销售被发现含有后门的路由器，并从其网站撤下受影响的软件，同时开发更新以解决该问题。

网络安全公司VulnCheck发现，总部位于深圳的Zbtlink Electronics制造的至少20款路由器存在后门，路透社周三率先[报道](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/chinese-made-zbtlink-routers-have-backdoor-researchers-say-2026-08-05/)了这一消息。Zbtlink在其网站上的一份[声明，打开新标签页](https://www.zbtlink.com/pages/zbt-router-firmware-download-announcement)中表示，已知晓该项研究，该研究发现该漏洞可能允许访问和控制设备，并可能访问和控制网络上的其他设备。

该公司表示，由VulnCheck首席技术官Jacob Baines发现并被命名为“Endlessdoors”的后门“仅作为售后技术支持工具”，并补充称，该工具旨在“仅在客户明确要求和授权的情况下”协助客户进行设备故障排除和配置。

该公司表示，该工具“从未被用于未经授权的访问”。

关于后门的披露发布于西方日益担忧中国制造的网络设备带来的网络安全和风险之际。加拿大政府周三发布了与该路由器漏洞相关的安全公告。

Baines在一份[分析报告，打开新标签页](https://www.vulncheck.com/blog/zbt-endlessdoors)中表示，该后门每35秒自动连接到一个特定的IP地址和一个中国注册的域名，任何控制这些域名或劫持它们的人都可能控制路由器，并可能利用它访问同一网络上的其他设备。

Baines周四告诉路透社：“全球部署的路由器仍然容易通过后门被恶意接管。”Baines表示，路由器用户唯一的缓解措施是将它们从网络中移除，并监控任何受损迹象。

贝恩斯补充说，公司的解释并未说明为何该工具使用的名称难以识别，除非你刻意寻找它，也未说明公司为何选择以易被劫持的不安全方式实施该工具。

Zbtlink周四未立即回应置评请求。

由底特律的AJ Vicens报道；Sanjeev Miglani编辑。

## China's Zbtlink suspends sales of routers found to contain backdoor

3D printed models of people working on computers and padlock are seen in front of a displayed CYBER SECURITY words and binary code in this picture illustration taken, February 1, 2022. REUTERS/Dado Ruvic/Illustration [Purchase Licensing Rights, opens new tab](https://www.reutersconnect.com/item/illustration-shows-displayed-cyber-security-words-and-binary-code/dGFnOnJldXRlcnMuY29tLDIwMjI6bmV3c21sX1JDMlhBUzk3V0Q0WQ%3D%3D/?utm_medium=rcom-article-media&utm_campaign=rcom-rcp-lead)

Aug 6 (Reuters) - Chinese router maker Zbtlink Electronics said on Thursday it was suspending sales of routers found to contain a backdoor and pulling the affected software from its website while it developed updates to address ‌the issue.

Cybersecurity firm VulnCheck identified a backdoor in at least 20 models of routers made by Shenzhen-based Zbtlink Electronics, as Reuters first [ reported ](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/chinese-made-zbtlink-routers-have-backdoor-researchers-say-2026-08-05/)on Wednesday. Zbtlink said in a [statement, opens new tab](https://www.zbtlink.com/pages/zbt-router-firmware-download-announcement) on its website that it was aware of the research, which found the flaw could allow access and control of the device and potentially other ⁠devices on the network.

The backdoor discovered by VulnCheck CTO Jacob Baines and dubbed “Endlessdoors” serves “solely as an after-sales technical support tool,” the company said, adding that it was intended to assist customers with device troubleshooting and configuration “only upon their explicit request and authorization.”

The tool “has never been used for unauthorized access,” the company said.

Revelations about the backdoor landed amid growing Western concerns about cybersecurity and risks posed by Chinese-made networking equipment. The Canadian government on Wednesday issued a security advisory related to the routers' vulnerability.

The backdoor ‌automatically ⁠connected to a specific IP address and Chinese-registered domain every 35 seconds, and whoever controlled those domains or hijacked them could have taken control of the router and potentially used it to access other devices on the same network, Baines said in [an analysis, opens new tab](https://www.vulncheck.com/blog/zbt-endlessdoors).

“Routers deployed around the ⁠globe are still vulnerable to hostile takeover via the backdoor,” Baines told Reuters on Thursday. The only mitigation for router users is to remove them from their networks and monitor for any ⁠signs of compromise, Baines said.

Baines added that the company’s explanation does not address why the tool used a name that was difficult to identify unless you were ⁠looking for it, and why the company chose to implement it in an insecure way that was vulnerable to hijack.

Zbtlink did not immediately respond to a request for comment on Thursday.
