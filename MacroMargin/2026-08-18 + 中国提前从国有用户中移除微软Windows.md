### 中文译文

## 中国提前从国有用户中移除微软Windows

中国正在停止使用[微软公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/MSFT%20US%20Equity)专为政府机构定制的旧版Windows，这是其在减少对外国技术依赖的更广泛努力中，向根除这款全球最流行的个人电脑软件迈出的又一步。

知情人士称，中国国家安全部最近要求部分国有单位从电脑中卸载定制版Windows 10软件。他们补充说，这使停用该操作系统的计划提前了数月，凸显出北京方面对使用美国关键技术的日益不安。

据因讨论政治敏感问题而要求匿名的知情人士称，该特定版本的Windows 10由[C&M Information Technologies Co.](https://www.cmgos.com/web/home_en/)（CMIT）开发。CMIT是微软与国有的[中国电子科技集团公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CETCGZ%20CH%20Equity)（China Electronics Technology Group Corp.）的合资企业，成立于2016年，旨在帮助确保Windows 10符合北京的安全要求。

中国软件类股上涨，[湖南麒麟信安科技股份有限公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/688152%20CH%20Equity)和[中科创达](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/300598%20CH%20Equity)触及20%的涨停限制。[中国软件与技术服务股份有限公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/600536%20CH%20Equity)上涨10%。

CMIT原本[计划](https://document.cmgos.com/lifecycle/lifecycle)在2027年2月退役该软件，这与政府限制外国技术使用的行动相一致。不过，据知情人士称，就在美国总统唐纳德·特朗普计划与中国国家主席习近平会晤的几周前，这一加速进程的新指令让一些国家机构员工感到意外。

他们补充说，北京的指示源于对数据安全的担忧，但没有具体说明中国官员担心哪些潜在漏洞。

“微软不知道有影响该产品的安全事件，该产品仍会定期收到安全更新，”这家美国公司的发言人表示。“我们没有更多信息可以分享。”

中国国务院新闻办公室没有回应传真发送的置评请求。由于国家安全部没有公开的联系方式，该请求被发送至该机构。CMIT未回应置评请求。

中国一直在处理敏感数据的领域（包括军事和国有企业）推动使用国产技术。包括[麒麟软件有限公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1854995D:CH)和[统信软件技术有限公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1766400D:CH)在内的多家中国公司，已推出了自己的计算机操作系统版本以取代Windows。北京已[下令](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2022-05-06/china-orders-government-state-firms-to-dump-foreign-pcs)中央政府部门更换外国品牌的个人电脑，而苹果公司的iPhone也已被[禁止](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2023-12-15/china-s-apple-iphone-ban-accelerates-across-state-firms-government)进入至少部分敏感的国家关联实体。

由于无法获取[英伟达公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/NVDA%20US%20Equity)的尖端AI加速器，中国也更加依赖包括[华为技术有限公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/40978Z:CH)和[寒武纪科技公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/688256%20CH%20Equity)在内的国内龙头企业，以获取构建新兴技术所必需的芯片。

CMIT的Windows 10产品线在原始软件基础上定制了中国开发的网络安全功能，并且为满足国家安全要求，某些原生功能也被关闭。微软在推出Windows 11后，于2025年底正式[结束](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/deployment/updates-lifecycle/windows-10-support-has-ended-on-october-14-2025)了对Windows 10的支持。

中国仍然是微软的关键市场。这家总部位于华盛顿州雷德蒙德市的公司已建立起一项向中国公司出售AI模型的大业务，其中包括[字节跳动有限公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1774397D:CH)和[腾讯控股有限公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/700%20HK%20Equity)。据彭博新闻社[报道](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-17/microsoft-s-china-ai-business-grows-on-openai-model-sales)，仅字节跳动一家公司每年在微软AI和云服务上的支出就有望超过10亿美元。

### English Original

## China Removes Microsoft Windows at State Users Ahead of Plan

China is pulling the plug on an older version of [Microsoft Corp.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/MSFT%20US%20Equity)’s Windows tailored for government agencies, taking another step toward rooting out the world’s most popular PC software in a broader effort to reduce its dependence on foreign technology.

The Chinese Ministry of State Security recently told some state-linked entities to uninstall customized Windows 10 software from their machines, people familiar with the matter said. That moves up the schedule to discontinue the operating system by several months, they added, underscoring Beijing’s increasing discomfort with using a key US technology.

That particular version of Windows 10 is developed by [C&M Information Technologies Co.](https://www.cmgos.com/web/home_en/), according to the people, who asked not to be named discussing a politically sensitive matter. CMIT, a joint venture between Microsoft and state-owned [China Electronics Technology Group Corp.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CETCGZ%20CH%20Equity), was founded in 2016 to help ensure Windows 10 is compliant with Beijing’s security requirements.

Chinese software stocks jumped, with [Hunan Kylinsec Technology Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/688152%20CH%20Equity) and [Archermind Technology Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/300598%20CH%20Equity) surging by the daily limit of 20%. [China National Software & Service Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/600536%20CH%20Equity) climbed 10%.

CMIT originally [planned](https://document.cmgos.com/lifecycle/lifecycle) to retire the software in February 2027, in line with a government campaign to curb the use of foreign technology. Still, the fresh directive to accelerate the schedule, just weeks before US President Donald Trump’s planned meeting with Chinese leader Xi Jinping, came as a surprise to some state agency employees, the people said.

Beijing’s instruction stemmed from data security concerns, they added, without elaborating exactly what potential vulnerabilities Chinese officials are worried about.

“Microsoft is not aware of a security incident affecting this product, which continues to receive regular security updates,” a spokesperson for the US company said. “We have nothing further to share.”

China’s State Council Information Office didn’t respond to a faxed request for comment. The request was sent to the agency in lieu of there being no publicly available contact information for the Ministry of State Security. CMIT didn’t respond to request for comment.

China has been pushing the use of homemade technologies in sectors handling sensitive data, including military and state-owned firms. Several Chinese companies, including [Kylin Software Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1854995D:CH) and [Tongxin Software Technology Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1766400D:CH), have introduced their own versions of operating systems for computers to replace Windows. Beijing has [ordered](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2022-05-06/china-orders-government-state-firms-to-dump-foreign-pcs) central government agencies to swap out foreign-branded PCs, while Apple Inc.’s iPhones have also been [banned](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2023-12-15/china-s-apple-iphone-ban-accelerates-across-state-firms-government) from at least some sensitive state-affiliated entities.

In the face of a lack of access to cutting-edge AI accelerators from [Nvidia Corp.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/NVDA%20US%20Equity), China is also leaning more on domestic champions including [Huawei Technologies Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/40978Z:CH) and [Cambricon Technologies Corp.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/688256%20CH%20Equity) for chips essential for building emerging technologies.

CMIT’s Windows 10 product line customizes the original software with some cybersecurity features developed in China, and certain native functions have also been switched off to meet national security requirements. Microsoft officially [ended](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/deployment/updates-lifecycle/windows-10-support-has-ended-on-october-14-2025) support for Windows 10 in late 2025 after the launch of Windows 11.

China remains a key market for Microsoft. The Redmond, Washington-based company has built a big business selling AI models to Chinese companies including [ByteDance Ltd.](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1774397D:CH) and [Tencent Holdings Ltd.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/700%20HK%20Equity) ByteDance alone is on track to spend more than $1 billion a year on Microsoft AI and cloud services, Bloomberg News has [reported](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-17/microsoft-s-china-ai-business-grows-on-openai-model-sales).
