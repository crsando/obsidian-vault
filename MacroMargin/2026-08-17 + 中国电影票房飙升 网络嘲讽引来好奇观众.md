### 中文译文

## 中国电影票房飙升 网络嘲讽引来好奇观众

*《牛来》*
*来源：《牛来》*

彭博新闻社
2026年8月15日 下午4:47 GMT+8

据当地媒体报道，一部中国动画电影在影院上映前10天仅获得约1000美元的票房收入，在网上遭到嘲笑后成为社交媒体热议话题，其票房收入攀升至六位数。

《牛来》是周六该平台上[讨论度最高](https://s.weibo.com/weibo?q=%E7%89%9B%E6%9D%A5%E7%A5%A8%E6%88%BF%E6%9A%B4%E6%B6%A81000%E5%80%8D)的话题之一，网友发布的片段显示其动画质量差、叙事生硬。如潮的嘲笑让这部电影意外成为票房现象。

一些社交媒体用户表示，他们买票是为了亲自评判这部电影，而随着讨论在微博和其他平台传播，影院也增加了放映场次。

据当地报道，该片在影院上映前10天票房收入约7700元（约合1140美元）。媒体称，随后该片票房收入达到六位数。

该片的相对成功表明，在中国，社交媒体关注对推动消费者行为的影响力日益增强，即使这种反应在很大程度上是负面的。

## China Film’s Sales Soar as Online Scorn Attracts Curious Viewers

*Niulai*  
*Source: Niulai*

By Bloomberg News  
August 15, 2026 at 4:47 PM GMT+8

A Chinese animated film that generated only about $1,000 in ticket sales in its first 10 days at theaters became a social-media sensation after online ridicule helped propel its box-office revenue into six figures, according to local media reports.

Niulai was one of the platform’s [most-discussed](https://s.weibo.com/weibo?q=%E7%89%9B%E6%9D%A5%E7%A5%A8%E6%88%BF%E6%9A%B4%E6%B6%A81000%E5%80%8D) topics on Saturday, with posts of clips showing what viewers described as poor animation quality and awkward storytelling. The flood of scorn helped turn the movie into an unlikely box-office phenomenon.

Some social-media users said they bought tickets to judge the film for themselves, while cinemas increased screenings as discussion spread across Weibo and other platforms.

The film generated about 7,700 yuan ($1,140) in ticket sales during its first 10 days in theaters, according to local reports. The movie’s box-office revenue subsequently increased to a six-figure amount, the media said.

The film’s relative success illustrates the growing power of social-media attention to drive consumer behavior in China, even if the reaction is largely negative.
