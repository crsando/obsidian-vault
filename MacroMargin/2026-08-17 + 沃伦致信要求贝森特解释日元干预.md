### 中文译文

## 沃伦致信要求贝森特解释日元干预

参议员伊丽莎白·沃伦敦促财政部长斯科特·贝森特解释特朗普政府干预日元汇率的理由，这延续了这位资深民主党人对特朗普政府外汇政策进行审查的一贯做法。

“迄今为止，政府尚未就其干预行为提供详细理由，也未正式披露动用多少与纳税人相关的资金购买日元，”沃伦在8月13日的信中写道。

贝森特证实了媒体关于自1998年以来美日首次联合干预以支撑日元的报道，这是在7月31日行动之后，但他并未说明使用了多少资金。他表示，此次行动动用了财政部外汇稳定基金（ESF）中的欧元。

“国会希望这些权力得到审慎行使，以促进国家利益，”沃伦在谈及ESF的使用时表示。“请提供政府使用ESF的法律分析，”她说，并要求在8月28日前作出回应。

沃伦是参议院银行委员会的资深民主党人，她还要求贝森特说明美国纳税人预计将承担的成本，以及“目前正在考虑的美国对日本金融支持的规模和附加条件”。

### **阿根廷、欧洲**

美国财政部没有立即回应就沃伦的信件提出的置评请求。

贝森特此前曾在2025年秋季动用ESF资金干预阿根廷比索市场，为总统哈维尔·米莱提供支持，沃伦也曾试图[审查](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-10-09/warren-asks-hedge-fund-group-if-it-played-argentina-bailout-role)这一举措。在她最近的这封信中，她将其描述为“由政治驱动、以纳税人资金支持的救助行动”。

财政部长曾表示，美国在对阿根廷的支持中赚了钱，但他的部门尚未公布该干预措施的具体细节。

沃伦还询问财政部是否就日元操作中使用欧元咨询了欧洲央行。本月初，贝森特在CNBC上表示，他向欧洲官员保证“这只是我们储备的重新分配”。据《金融时报》报道，欧洲央行只是在[事后](https://www.ft.com/content/d9922d0b-51a0-48be-811b-42e08f90985a?syn-25a6b1a6=1)才被告知。

这位参议员更广泛地询问贝森特，日本金融市场的动荡可能如何影响美国的就业、工资和金融稳定。日本是美国国债的最大外国持有者，分析师猜测，贝森特干预的原因之一是阻止东京方面可能推高美国收益率的抛售。

## Bessent Asked to Justify Yen Intervention in Letter from Warren

Senator Elizabeth Warren pressed Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent to explain the Trump administration’s justification for intervening in the yen, extending the senior Democrat’s pattern of scrutinizing the Trump administration’s foreign-exchange policies.

“To date, the administration has yet to provide a detailed justification for its intervention, nor has it officially disclosed how much taxpayer-linked funds were spent purchasing yen,” Warren wrote in the letter, dated Aug. 13.

Bessent confirmed media reports of the first US-Japan joint intervention to support the yen since 1998 in the wake of the July 31 actions, but hasn’t spelled out how much was used. He’s indicated that the operation featured use of euros from the Treasury’s Exchange Stabilization Fund, or ESF.

“Congress intends for these authorities to be invoked judiciously to advance the national interest,” Warren said of the ESF’s use. “Please provide the administration’s legal analysis” for using the ESF, she said, asking for a response by Aug. 28.

Warren, the top Democrat on the Senate Banking Committee, also asked Bessent to outline the expected cost to US taxpayers, and “the scale and conditionality of the US financial support currently under consideration” for Japan.

### **Argentina, Europe**

The US Treasury didn’t immediately respond to a request for comment on the Warren letter.

Bessent previously used ESF funds in the fall of 2025 to intervene in the market for Argentine pesos, offering support for President Javier Milei in an initiative that Warren also sought to [scrutinize](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-10-09/warren-asks-hedge-fund-group-if-it-played-argentina-bailout-role). In her latest letter, she described that as a “politically driven, taxpayer-backed bailout.”

The Treasury chief has said the US made money on its support for Argentina but his department hasn’t released specifics of that intervention.

Warren also asked whether the Treasury consulted with the European Central Bank on the use of euros in the yen operation. Earlier this month, Bessent said on CNBC that he assured European officals “that it’s just a reallocation of our reserves.” The Financial Times reported that the ECB was only told [after the fact](https://www.ft.com/content/d9922d0b-51a0-48be-811b-42e08f90985a?syn-25a6b1a6=1).

The senator more broadly queried Bessent on how financial market turmoil in Japan could affect US jobs, wages and financial stability. Japan is the single largest foreign holder of Treasuries, and analysts have surmised that one of Bessent’s reasons for intervening was to head off sales by Tokyo that might have sent US yields higher.
