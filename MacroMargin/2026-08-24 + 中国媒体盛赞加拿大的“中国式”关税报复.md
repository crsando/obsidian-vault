### 中文译文

## 中国媒体盛赞加拿大的“中国式”关税报复

中国民族主义小报《环球时报》[称赞](https://www.globaltimes.cn/page/202608/1368853.shtml)加拿大对美国关税的报复，认为渥太华的举动印证了北京对华盛顿保护主义贸易政策的反对。

该报在周一的一篇社论中称，加拿大的行动是“中国式反击”，加拿大政府的决定表明“更多国家正在意识到，对美国让步并不一定会带来尊重和稳定。”

加拿大在周末退出了与美国的贸易谈判，双方互相指责对方在最后一刻提出变更。美国将对约200亿美元的加拿大商品征收50%的关税，而渥太华已承诺从9月8日起进行“对等报复”。

环球时报援引美国贸易代表贾米森·格里尔（Jamieson Greer）的[比较](https://lethbridgeherald.com/news/national-news/2026/08/14/u-s-trade-rep-says-canada-must-lift-retaliatory-measures-to-avoid-new-tariffs/)指出，加拿大措施就像“中国会做的事情”。该报称，这番言论相当于承认“美国政策的失败”。

该社论认为，这一争端表明，美国的盟友无法免受华盛顿关税政策的影响。“今天对加拿大，明天可能对欧盟，后天对日本或韩国，”社论说。

北京一直试图将自己针对美国此前关税的报复行为描述为对等举措，以及对国际贸易体系的捍卫。经过几轮谈判，中美两国达成了[贸易休战](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-10-27/latest-us-china-trade-truce-leaves-fundamental-issues-unresolved)协议，尽管在技术和其他经济限制方面的[紧张局势](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-05/china-hits-back-at-us-with-tighter-drone-export-curbs-sanctions)依然存在。

然而，自去年以来，北京与华盛顿之间的关系已经趋于稳定。美国总统唐纳德·特朗普于5月访问北京，双方同意维持一种以“[战略稳定](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-15/emboldened-xi-moves-to-corner-trump-with-new-relationship-talk)”为标志的新型关系。中国国家主席习近平预计将于9月对美国进行十多年来的首次国事访问。

自总理马克·卡尼上台以来，中加关系有所升温。今年卡尼访问中国期间，两国达成了一项旨在降低贸易壁垒的广泛[协议](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-01-16/canada-s-carney-to-meet-xi-after-new-world-order-remarks)，这标志着加拿大背离了与特朗普贸易议程的结盟。

这一接触标志着与前总理贾斯廷·特鲁多执政时期的冻结状态相比出现急剧逆转，当时贸易、外国干涉和其他事务上的争端将两国关系推至几十年来的最低点。据复旦大学美国研究中心主任、曾任中国外交部顾问的吴心伯称，最近的解冻还引发了习近平回访加拿大的可能性。

吴心伯表示，他并不知晓具体计划，但预计习近平或国务院总理李强将在“今年某个时候或明年初”访问加拿大。吴心伯表示，外交部长王毅访问渥太华将是一个值得关注的信号，并补充说，习近平不太可能将加拿大之行与他下月计划中的华盛顿访问结合在一起。

## China Media Hails Canada’s ‘Chinese-Style’ Tariff Retaliation

China’s nationalist tabloid Global Times [praised](https://www.globaltimes.cn/page/202608/1368853.shtml) Canada’s retaliation against US tariffs, casting Ottawa’s move as validating Beijing’s opposition to Washington’s protectionist trade policies.

Calling Canada’s action “Chinese-style counterattack,” the newspaper said in an [editorial](https://www.globaltimes.cn/page/202608/1368853.shtml) Monday that the government’s decision shows that “more countries are realizing that concessions toward the US do not necessarily bring respect and stability.”

Canada walked away from trade negotiations with the US over the weekend, with both sides blaming the other for last-minute changes. The US will impose 50% tariffs on about $20 billion of Canadian goods, while Ottawa has pledged to retaliate “dollar-for-dollar” staring Sept. 8.

The Global Times cited US Trade Representative Jamieson Greer’s [comparison](https://lethbridgeherald.com/news/national-news/2026/08/14/u-s-trade-rep-says-canada-must-lift-retaliatory-measures-to-avoid-new-tariffs/) of Canada’s measures to “the kind of things that China would do.” That remark amounted to an acknowledgment of “the failure of US policy,” the newspaper said.

The editorial argued that the dispute demonstrated that US allies aren’t exempt from Washington’s use of tariffs. “Today it is Canada; tomorrow it could be the EU, and the day after, Japan or South Korea,” it said.

Beijing has sought to cast its own retaliation against previous US tariffs as a matter of reciprocity and a defense of the international trading system. China and the US agreed to a [trade truce](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-10-27/latest-us-china-trade-truce-leaves-fundamental-issues-unresolved) after several rounds of negotiations, though [tensions](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-05/china-hits-back-at-us-with-tighter-drone-export-curbs-sanctions) have persisted over technology and other economic restrictions.

Relations between Beijing and Washington have nevertheless stabilized since last year, with the US President Donald Trump visiting Beijing in May and the two sides agreeing to uphold a new form of relationship marked by “[strategic stability](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-15/emboldened-xi-moves-to-corner-trump-with-new-relationship-talk).” Chinese President Xi Jinping is expected to make his first state visit to the US in more than a decade in September.

Ties between China and Canada have warmed since Prime Minister Mark Carney came to power. The two countries reached a wide-ranging [agreement](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-01-16/canada-s-carney-to-meet-xi-after-new-world-order-remarks) to lower trade barriers during Carney’s visit to China this year, in a break from alignment with Trump’s trade agenda.

The engagement marks a sharp reversal from the freeze under former Prime Minister Justin Trudeau, when disputes over trade, foreign interference and other matters drove relations to their lowest point in decades. The recent thaw has also raised the prospect of a reciprocal visit to Canada by Xi, according to Wu Xinbo, director of Fudan University’s Center for American Studies and a former adviser to China’s Foreign Ministry.

Wu said he was unaware of specific plans but expected either Xi or Premier Li Qiang to visit Canada “sometime this year or early next.” A visit to Ottawa by Foreign Minister Wang Yi would be one sign to watch, Wu said, adding that Xi was unlikely to combine a Canada trip with his planned visit to Washington next month.
