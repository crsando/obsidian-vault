## DeepSeek 重启80亿美元融资轮，Monolith 参与竞逐

### **要点**（Bloomberg AI）

- 据知情人士透露，DeepSeek 已恢复第二轮融资，寻求接近80亿美元的资金，Monolith Management 正在洽谈参与投资。
- 该公司正以接近5000亿元人民币的估值融资，并计划将部分新资金用于支持其数据中心建设。
- 知情人士表示，讨论仍在进行中，融资的规模、时间或投资者名单等细节仍可能发生变化。
据知情人士透露，DeepSeek 已恢复第二轮融资，寻求接近80亿美元的资金，[Monolith Management](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/2021792D:HK) 正在洽谈参与投资。

此次重启融资之前，这家总部位于杭州的初创公司上个月在创始人对投资者的言论泄露引发不满后，[暂停](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-25/deepseek-said-to-tell-backers-of-funding-pause-after-viral-posts)了融资进程。知情人士称，DeepSeek 正以接近5000亿元人民币（合740亿美元）的估值融资，由于细节尚未公开，这些人士要求匿名。

知情人士表示，讨论仍在进行中，融资的规模、时间或投资者名单等细节仍可能发生变化。中国《财经》杂志此前报道了该轮融资的恢复。

在发布 [V4 Flash](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-31/deepseek-unveils-public-beta-api-for-flagship-ai-model) 人工智能模型后，DeepSeek 势头正盛，该模型重新设定了性价比曲线的预期，引发了对 [DeepSeek 死亡地带](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-04/china-s-ai-blitz-creates-death-zone-for-rival-us-model-makers) 的讨论，即任何竞争对手的产品要么以极低价格提供更少功能，要么为同样的服务支付更高成本。这标志着该公司在2025年初首次闻名全球的方式重演，当时它颠覆了人工智能开发成本高昂的假设。

DeepSeek和Monolith未立即回应置评请求。

由梁文锋领导的公司本身现在正涉足采购更多计算资源。部分新资金将用于支持其数据中心建设，其中一项计划中的大规模[内蒙古设施](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-30/deepseek-is-developing-massive-ai-data-center-in-inner-mongolia)可能耗资数十亿美元。DeepSeek周四通知用户，计划[提高](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-06/deepseek-plans-significant-price-increase-for-its-ai-services)其AI服务的价格。

今年夏初，DeepSeek完成首轮外部融资时，梁文锋的净资产[增长了一倍多](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-14/deepseek-s-liang-tops-amodei-and-brockman-as-richest-ai-founder)，达到约360亿美元。这家AI初创公司是从他的对冲基金High-Flyer分拆出来的，后者提供了大量初始支持。梁文锋在[500亿元融资轮](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-03/deepseek-close-to-sealing-7-billion-funding-in-historic-ai-deal)中再次出资，该轮融资对DeepSeek的估值约为3500亿元人民币。据知情人士透露，公司在本轮融资中提高了估值，同时目标是募集与首轮相当的金额。

在潜在的新投资者中，Monolith因其对Moonshot的投资而声名鹊起，Moonshot是Kimi K3背后的公司。该AI模型对全球AI领域和美国科技股产生了类似DeepSeek的颠覆性影响，展示了中国以少量预算取得快速进步。Monolith目前正在[为一支专注于中国AI的新基金募集5亿美元](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-05/google-deepmind-boss-hassabis-moves-to-chair-role-in-shakeup)。

## DeepSeek Resumes $8 Billion Round With Monolith in the Running

### **Takeaways **by Bloomberg AI

- DeepSeek has resumed its second funding round, seeking close to $8 billion, with Monolith Management in talks to contribute, according to people familiar with the matter.
- The company is raising the funds at a valuation close to 500 billion yuan and plans to use part of the new funds to support its data center buildout.
- Discussions are ongoing and details such as the size and timing of the funding or the list of investors can still change, the people said.
DeepSeek has resumed its second funding round, seeking close to $8 billion, with [Monolith Management](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/2021792D:HK) in talks to contribute, according to people familiar with the matter.

The reopening comes after the Hangzhou-based startup [paused](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-25/deepseek-said-to-tell-backers-of-funding-pause-after-viral-posts) the process last month following frustration about leaked remarks from the company’s founder to investors. DeepSeek is raising the funds at a valuation close to 500 billion yuan ($74 billion), the people said, asking not to be named as the details aren’t public.

Discussions are ongoing and details such as the size and timing of the funding or the list of investors can still change, the people said. Chinese magazine Caijing reported the resumption of the round earlier.

DeepSeek is riding high after the release of its [V4 Flash](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-31/deepseek-unveils-public-beta-api-for-flagship-ai-model) AI model, which reset expectations for the cost-performance curve, eliciting talk of a [DeepSeek death zone](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-04/china-s-ai-blitz-creates-death-zone-for-rival-us-model-makers) for any rivals whose products either deliver less for the rock-bottom price or cost more for the same service. It marked a repeat of the way the company first came to global renown in early 2025, when it upset assumptions about the heavy cost of AI development.

DeepSeek and Monolith did not immediately respond to requests for comment.

The company, led by Liang Wenfeng, is itself now venturing into procuring more computing resources. Part of the new funds will be used to support its data center buildout, led by a planned large-scale [facility in Inner Mongolia](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-30/deepseek-is-developing-massive-ai-data-center-in-inner-mongolia) likely to cost billions of dollars. DeepSeek on Thursday informed users it plans to [increase prices](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-06/deepseek-plans-significant-price-increase-for-its-ai-services) for its AI services.

Liang’s net worth [more than doubled](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-14/deepseek-s-liang-tops-amodei-and-brockman-as-richest-ai-founder) to around $36 billion when DeepSeek closed its first round of external fundraising earlier this summer. The AI startup grew as an offshoot from his hedge fund High-Flyer, which provided much of the initial support. Liang contributed again in the [50-billion-yuan round](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-03/deepseek-close-to-sealing-7-billion-funding-in-historic-ai-deal), which had valued DeepSeek at roughly 350 billion yuan. The company has lifted its valuation in the current fundraising, while aiming to raise as much as in the first round, the people said.

Among its potential new backers, Monolith has risen in prominence with its investment in Moonshot, the company behind Kimi K3. That AI model has had a DeepSeek-like disruptive impact on the global AI scene and US tech stocks by showing China making rapid advances on a small budget. Monolith is now [raising $500 million for a new fund](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-05/google-deepmind-boss-hassabis-moves-to-chair-role-in-shakeup) focused on Chinese AI.
