## 特朗普政府将拨款5亿美元支持美国矿产项目

![Western miners push for higher metals prices to ward off Chinese rivals](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3c281cc90b1481c18960c14476fa9717/3c281cc9-0b14-81e1-bf67-de59f1512641-a622f3ad8f439e653181ac800b77ae38.jpg)

- 路透见到的文件显示，Lilac Solutions、Jervois和Nth Cycle各获得1亿美元
- 拨款支持七家公司建设矿产加工、回收和电池材料项目
- 能源部表示，入选项目在所需电池生态系统领域提供最高回报
8月20日（路透）——根据路透见到的一份文件，美国能源部将向七家公司提供5亿美元拨款，用于建设国内锂、钴及其他矿产和电池项目，这是旨在加强美国采矿和加工的一系列投资中的最新一笔。

这笔资金出炉前几周，总统唐纳德·特朗普提出了使美国成为“世界矿产超级大国”并减少对市场领军者中国依赖的目标。政府已利用贷款、拨款、政府投资和其他工具鼓励新的采矿和加工项目，同时寻求为被视为对经济和国家安全至关重要的矿产建立更安全的国内供应链。

与[伊朗的战争](https://www.reuters.com/world/iran/?gad_source=1&gad_campaignid=22314562799&gbraid=0AAAAA-mwunFUsyqdaAwhV4sC_4XuLDW-u&gclid=CjwKCAjwqJXUBhBNEiwA8BgG7iQsi7rm-A-rOTylXqGw0Em5LuljxH0yDkAq7HQ0PF2qnKhwO_SrRhoCqnwQAvD_BwE)加剧了这一努力的紧迫性，突显了重大冲突对美国武器库存以及补充库存所需工业基础造成的压力。

能源部从其电池材料加工和电池制造项目中收到了数百份第三轮资助申请。该部助理部长奥黛丽·罗伯逊表示，被选中的项目“最有前景，能够在电池生态系统最急需的领域为美国人带来最高回报。”

Lilac Solutions 将获得1亿美元，用于在犹他州大盐湖建设一座[直接提锂](https://www.reuters.com/markets/commodities/inside-race-remake-lithium-extraction-ev-batteries-2023-06-16/)加工设施。这家由宝马 [(BMWG.DE), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/BMWG.DE) 支持的公司预计将于2028年前投产，年产锂金属5000公吨。

罗伯逊表示，能源部的科学家们“坚信（Lilac）将成为美国碳酸锂的有益来源。”

能源部此前已宣布为ioneer [(INR.AX), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/INR.AX)、Standard Lithium [(SLI.V), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/SLI.V) 和 Lithium Americas [(LAC.TO), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/LAC.TO) 的锂项目提供资金。

Jervois 在爱达荷州控制着一个大型[钴矿床](https://www.reuters.com/markets/commodities/western-miners-push-higher-metals-prices-ward-off-chinese-rivals-2024-07-22/)，该公司还将获得1亿美元，用于建设该国唯一一座钴精炼厂，该金属用于制造电池、电子产品和一系列武器。

该公司去年因市场价格低迷，作为[预包装破产](https://www.reuters.com/markets/commodities/cobalt-miner-jervois-rescue-deal-after-struggle-compete-with-china-2025-01-02/)的一部分被私有化。即便如此，罗伯逊表示，钴仍然受到各行业的需求，能源部的资助旨在增加国内供应，并补充说该设施未来可能处理[深海结核](https://www.reuters.com/world/trump-speed-permits-deep-sea-mining-international-waters-2026-01-21/)。

电池回收商 Nth Cycle 获得[托克集团](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/nth-cycle-signs-11-billion-lithium-offtake-deal-with-trafigura-2026-03-16/)的支持，将获得1亿美元，用于建设一座处理电池金属废料（即黑粉）的设施。

本月早些时候，特朗普政府[阻止了黑粉的出口](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/trump-admin-blocks-tungsten-battery-waste-exports-boost-us-minerals-supply-2026-08-06/)，这些黑粉富含可回收的矿物。罗伯逊表示，这笔资金与出口禁令无关，但他补充说：“美国需要并将建立完整的生态系统，在国内充分回收和处理电池和黑粉。”

能源部还将分别向Princeton NuEnergy、Arcanum Ventures和Coreshell Technologies各提供5000万美元。Princeton NuEnergy负责再加工阴极电池部件；Arcanum Ventures生产电池电解液化学品；Coreshell Technologies正在开发用硅而非石墨（长期行业标准）制造的电池阳极。

## Trump administration to back US minerals projects with $500 million in grants

A cobalt mining site operated by Jervois Global is seen west of Salmon, Idaho, U.S. May 16, 2024. REUTERS/Carlos Barria/File Photo [Purchase Licensing Rights, opens new tab](https://www.reutersconnect.com/item/western-miners-push-for-higher-metals-prices-to-ward-off-chinese-rivals/dGFnOnJldXRlcnMuY29tLDIwMjQ6bmV3c21sX1JDMlVSN0FaMlZaRg%3D%3D/?utm_medium=rcom-article-media&utm_campaign=rcom-rcp-lead)

- Summary
- Companies
- Lilac Solutions, Jervois and Nth Cycle each receive $100 million, document seen by Reuters shows
- Grants support seven companies building minerals processing, recycling and battery materials projects
- Energy Department says selected projects offer highest return in needed battery ecosystem areas
Aug 20 (Reuters) - The U.S. Department of Energy is awarding $500 million in grants to seven companies building ‌domestic lithium, cobalt and other mineral and battery projects, the latest in a string of investments aimed at bolstering American mining and processing, according to a document seen by Reuters.

The funding comes weeks after President Donald Trump outlined a goal to make the U.S. the "[minerals superpower of the world](https://www.reuters.com/business/aerospace-defense/trump-host-mining-ceos-administration-seeks-minerals-defense-supply-chains-2026-08-07/)" and curb reliance on market leader China. The administration has used loans, grants, government investments and other tools to encourage new mining and processing projects, while seeking to build a more secure ⁠domestic supply chain for minerals considered essential to the economy and national security.

The [war with Iran](https://www.reuters.com/world/iran/?gad_source=1&gad_campaignid=22314562799&gbraid=0AAAAA-mwunFUsyqdaAwhV4sC_4XuLDW-u&gclid=CjwKCAjwqJXUBhBNEiwA8BgG7iQsi7rm-A-rOTylXqGw0Em5LuljxH0yDkAq7HQ0PF2qnKhwO_SrRhoCqnwQAvD_BwE) has added urgency to the effort, underscoring the strain that a major conflict can put on U.S. weapons inventories and the industrial base needed to replenish them.

The Energy Department received hundreds of applications for this third round of funding from its Battery Materials Processing and Battery Manufacturing programs. Those chosen "were most promising and had the highest return for Americans in the most-needed areas of the battery ecosystem," said Audrey Robertson, the department's assistant secretary.

Lilac Solutions will receive $100 million for a [direct lithium extraction](https://www.reuters.com/markets/commodities/inside-race-remake-lithium-extraction-ev-batteries-2023-06-16/) processing facility on Utah's Great Salt Lake. The company, which is backed by BMW [(BMWG.DE), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/BMWG.DE), expects to open the facility by 2028 and produce 5,000 metric tons per year of the metal.

Robertson said the Energy Department's scientists "firmly believe (Lilac) will ‌be ⁠a beneficial resource of lithium carbonate to the nation."

The Energy Department had previously announced funding for lithium projects from ioneer [(INR.AX), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/INR.AX), Standard Lithium [(SLI.V), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/SLI.V) and Lithium Americas [(LAC.TO), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/LAC.TO).

Jervois, which controls a large [cobalt deposit](https://www.reuters.com/markets/commodities/western-miners-push-higher-metals-prices-ward-off-chinese-rivals-2024-07-22/) in Idaho, will also receive $100 million to build the country's only refinery for that metal, used to make batteries, electronics and a range of weapons.

The company was taken private last year as part of a [pre-packaged bankruptcy](https://www.reuters.com/markets/commodities/cobalt-miner-jervois-rescue-deal-after-struggle-compete-with-china-2025-01-02/) caused by low market prices. Even so, cobalt ⁠is in demand from various sectors and the Energy Department funding aims to boost domestic supplies, Robertson said, adding that the facility could in time process [deep-sea nodules.](https://www.reuters.com/world/trump-speed-permits-deep-sea-mining-international-waters-2026-01-21/)

Nth Cycle, a battery recycler [backed by Trafigura](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/nth-cycle-signs-11-billion-lithium-offtake-deal-with-trafigura-2026-03-16/), is receiving $100 million for a facility to process battery metal scrap known as black mass.

⁠Earlier this month, the Trump administration [blocked exports of black mass](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/trump-admin-blocks-tungsten-battery-waste-exports-boost-us-minerals-supply-2026-08-06/), which are filled with minerals that can be recycled. Robertson said the funding was not tied to the export block, but added that "the United States needs to and will build the ecosystem to fully recycle ⁠and process batteries and black mass here at home."

The Energy Department is also giving $50 million each to Princeton NuEnergy, which reprocesses cathode battery parts; Arcanum Ventures, which produces chemicals for battery electrolytes; and Coreshell Technologies, which is developing battery anodes made with silicon, rather than graphite, the longtime industry standard.
