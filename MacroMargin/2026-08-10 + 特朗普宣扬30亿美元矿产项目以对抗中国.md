### 中文译文

## 特朗普宣扬30亿美元矿产项目以对抗中国

美国总统唐纳德·特朗普周五在与顶级行业高管会晤时，宣扬了美国在关键矿产开采领域30亿美元的投资，此次会议旨在让美国摆脱由中国主导的供应链。

特朗普在国务院的活动上表示，这些项目将“创造数千个就业岗位，并促进我们的经济稳定与安全”。他补充说：“我们正在让我们的矿工重新上岗，并重新夺回美国作为世界矿产超级大国的应有地位。”

特朗普表示，这些投资包括与[Sila Nanotechnologies Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/0855210D:US)达成的14亿美元贷款协议，该协议由国防部战略资本办公室提供，用于扩大硅电池负极和锂离子电池电芯的生产。其他投资包括五角大楼向[Sunrise Energy Metals Ltd.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/SRL%20AU%20Equity)提供的4亿美元，用于扩大澳大利亚的钪生产，钪对航空航天和国防工业至关重要，以及与明尼苏达州磁体制造公司[Niron Magnetics Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1848774D:US)达成的1.5亿美元投资。

美国进出口银行也在努力为[Ivanhoe Electric Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/IE%20US%20Equity)位于亚利桑那州的圣克鲁斯铜矿项目提供超过10亿美元的融资——这是先前宣布的协议的一部分——同时还将投资2500万美元在阿拉巴马州启动一座石墨矿。

![Doug Burgum, US secretary of the interior, from left, US President Donald Trump, Marco Rubio, US secretary of state, and Howard Lutnick, US commerce secretary, during an American mining industry roundtable at the State Department in Washington, DC Photographer: Graeme Sloan/Bloomberg](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3b881cc90b1481d4ad30fc082e4c47c8/3b881cc9-0b14-8148-92ec-fcfe4b1744f7-a4cefdf3dd884c80bafffd41a5c025dc.png)

从左至右：美国内政部长道格·伯古姆、美国总统唐纳德·特朗普、美国国务卿马尔科·卢比奥和美国商务部长霍华德·卢特尼克，在华盛顿特区国务院举行的美国矿业圆桌会议上。摄影师：Graeme Sloan/Bloomberg

总统还称赞了投入超过1.8亿美元以加强行业教育项目的计划，称这将“培养新一代美国矿工”。

特朗普表示，他的努力将确保美国“不再依赖敌对国家提供我们国家未来主宰所需的资源。”

与总统同行的还有来自知名公司的高管，包括[力拓集团](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/0730100D:AU)、[必和必拓集团](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/BHP%20AU%20Equity)、[MP Materials Corp.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/MP%20US%20Equity)、[USA Rare Earth Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/USAR%20US%20Equity)、[Energy Fuels Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/EFR%20CN%20Equity)、[US Antimony Corp.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/UAMY%20US%20Equity)和[The Metals Company](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/TMC%20US%20Equity)。

该活动展示了政府在中国国家主席习近平计划于9月访问华盛顿之前，为刺激美国关键矿产开发和加工所做的努力。特朗普表示，在他的政府领导下，国内矿山正以快速步伐重新开放，包括他所说的70多年来美国首个稀土矿，以及自1980年以来首座新建铝冶炼厂的建设。

尽管贸易休战放松了对关键矿产和对众多尖端产业至关重要的磁体的出口管制，但稀土仍是世界两大经济体之间的紧张根源。美国已寻求与国内公司合作，建立一条不依赖中国的从矿山到磁体的供应链。

参加会议的[Aclara Resources Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/ARA%20CN%20Equity)首席执行官拉蒙·巴鲁阿（Ramón Barúa）称，该活动是一个信号，表明政府准备“全力支持采矿计划”。

联邦政府已[承诺](https://www.cfr.org/articles/washingtons-growing-portfolio-tracking-u-s-government-investments)超过100亿美元的投资，以启动美国新兴的稀土和永磁体市场。一年多前，五角大楼开启了这场支出狂潮，宣布将以4亿美元优先股入股MP Materials——美国唯一的稀土生产商——此后美国又宣布了其他政府支持的项目。

尽管美国关键矿产建设的规模巨大，但在发展国内产业方面面临严重挑战，进展缓慢。

为学校提供资金之际，美国采矿业正面临劳动力老龄化和工程师、地质学家、冶金学家及其他专业技能人才储备有限的问题。几十年来，矿业学校的入学率和学生对行业的兴趣一直在下降，这使得在美国寻求迅速扩大国内关键矿产生产之际，企业争相争夺相对较小的人才库。

劳动力挑战尤为严峻，因为新矿投产可能需要数年时间，这意味着公司需要在项目开始生产之前就建立人才储备。

政府还推动资助非洲和南美洲的采矿项目，以削弱北京对关键矿产的控制。上周五，美国国际开发金融公司表示，将为[Harena Rare Earths Plc](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/HREE%20LN%20Equity)提供480万美元的投资配套资金，用于在马达加斯加开发一座稀土矿，该矿将为美国制造业供应包括磁体金属在内的材料。

“我们在多届政府中一直如同梦游，”矿业亿万富翁罗伯特·弗里德兰（Robert Friedland）表示，特朗普周五强调为其旗下公司Ivanhoe Electric和Sunrise Energy Metals提供资金。“这对我们的国家安全至关重要。”

## Trump Touts $3 Billion in Minerals Projects to Counter China

President Donald Trump touted $3 billion in US investments in critical minerals mining at a meeting Friday with top industry executives aimed at weaning the US off supply chains dominated by China.

Trump said the projects would “create thousands of jobs and promote our economic stability and security,” at an event at the State Department. “We’re putting our miners back to work, and we’re reclaiming America’s rightful place as the minerals superpower of the world,” he added.

Trump said the investments included a $1.4 billion loan agreement with [Sila Nanotechnologies Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/0855210D:US) from the Defense Department’s Office of Strategic Capital to expand production of silicon battery anodes and lithium-ion battery cells. Other investments include $400 million to [Sunrise Energy Metals Ltd.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/SRL%20AU%20Equity) by the Pentagon to expand production in Australia of scandium, which is critical to aerospace and defense industries, and $150 million with [Niron Magnetics Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1848774D:US), a Minnesota magnet-making firm.

The Export-Import Bank is also working to provide more than $1 billion in financing for [Ivanhoe Electric Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/IE%20US%20Equity)’s Santa Cruz copper project in Arizona — as part of a previously announced agreement — along with a $25 million investment to launch a graphite mine in Alabama.

Doug Burgum, US secretary of the interior, from left, US President Donald Trump, Marco Rubio, US secretary of state, and Howard Lutnick, US commerce secretary, during an American mining industry roundtable at the State Department in Washington, DC Photographer: Graeme Sloan/Bloomberg

The president also hailed plans to spend over $180 million to bolster educational programs for the industry, saying it would “train the next generation of American miners.”

Trump said his efforts would ensure the US “is never again reliant on hostile foreign nations for the resources our country needs to dominate in the future.”

The president was joined by executives from prominent companies, including [Rio Tinto Group](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/0730100D:AU), [BHP Group](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/BHP%20AU%20Equity), [MP Materials Corp.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/MP%20US%20Equity), [USA Rare Earth Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/USAR%20US%20Equity), [Energy Fuels Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/EFR%20CN%20Equity), [US Antimony Corp.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/UAMY%20US%20Equity) and [The Metals Company](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/TMC%20US%20Equity).

The event showcased administration efforts to spur US critical minerals development and processing before a planned visit by Chinese President Xi Jinping to Washington in September. Trump said that under his administration domestic mines were reopening at a fast pace, including what he said was the first American rare-earths mine in more than 70 years and the construction of the first new aluminum smelter since 1980.

Despite a trade truce that eased export controls over critical minerals and magnets essential to a host of cutting edge industries, rare earths remain a source of tension between the world’s two largest economies. The US has sought to partner with domestic companies to build out a mine-to-magnet supply chain independent of China.

Ramón Barúa, the chief executive officer of [Aclara Resources Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/ARA%20CN%20Equity) and a participant in the meeting, called the event a signal that the administration is prepared to go “all out supporting mining initiatives.”

The federal government has already [pledged](https://www.cfr.org/articles/washingtons-growing-portfolio-tracking-u-s-government-investments) more than $10 billion of investments in a bid to jump-start nascent US markets for rare earths and permanent magnets. The Pentagon kicked off the spending spree more than a year ago, announcing it would take a $400 million preferred equity stake in MP Materials — the sole domestic rare earths producer — and the US has since announced other projects the government is championing.

While the scale of the US critical minerals build-out is enormous, there have been serious challenges and slow progress in developing the domestic industry.

The funding for schools comes as the US mining sector faces an aging workforce and a limited pipeline of engineers, geologists, metallurgists and other workers with specialized skills. Mining schools have seen enrollment and interest in the industry decline over decades, leaving companies competing for a relatively small pool of talent just as the US seeks to rapidly expand domestic production of critical minerals.

The workforce challenge is particularly acute because bringing new mines online can take years, meaning companies need to develop the talent pipeline well before projects begin producing.

The administration has also pushed to fund mining projects in Africa and South America in an effort to loosen Beijing’s grip on critical minerals. On Friday, the US International Development Finance Corp. said it would match a $4.8 million investment in [Harena Rare Earths Plc](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/HREE%20LN%20Equity) to develop a rare-earth mine in Madagascar, which would supply materials including magnet metals for US manufacturing.

“We’ve been sleepwalking through many administrations,” said mining billionaire Robert Friedland, whose firms, Ivanhoe Electric and Sunrise Energy Metals, Trump highlighted for funding on Friday. “This is critical to our national security.”
