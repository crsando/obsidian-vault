### 中文译文

## 受全球酒类行业低迷影响，Treasury Wine 的盈利下降 36%

### 要点

- Treasury Wine Estates Ltd. 股价大涨，因为中国市场增长的迹象抵消了全年盈利的大幅下滑。
- 中国市场的库存消耗量增长了超过34%，表明威胁其核心奔富品牌的第三方销售有所减少。
- 公司表示，截至2027年6月的财年盈利将“至少与上一时期持平”，中国库存再平衡将在未来12个月内完成。
[Treasury Wine Estates Ltd.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/TWE%20AU%20Equity)股价一度大涨6.4%，因为这家葡萄酒商关键的中国市场增长迹象抵消了全年盈利的大幅下滑。

这家生产奔富品牌的澳大利亚葡萄酒商周四报告收入大幅下滑，原因是其正应对消费者对酒类需求疲软，以及美国业务长期供应过剩和分销难题。

但出现了令投资者鼓舞的转机迹象。中国市场的库存消耗量——即从批发商或经销商流向客户的销量——增长了超过34%，表明威胁其核心奔富品牌的第三方销售有所减少。

首席执行官Sam Fischer自10月上任以来，一直将通过电商网站和第三方打击进入中国的平行进口作为其重振这家陷入困境的葡萄酒商命运战略的重要支柱。在财报发布后接受彭博采访时，他表示库存消耗的增长部分得益于打击行动，部分得益于新消费。他说：“我们认为大约是各占一半。”

Treasury Wine Estates首席执行官Sam Fisher表示，在公司公布年度盈利下降36%后，关键品牌的需求正在改善。解决美国供应链问题和遏制中国平行进口的努力开始显现成效。他在Insight with Haslinda Amin节目中独家接受了Paul Allen的采访。来源：彭博

在推进精简公司战略的过程中，Fischer表示，Treasury Wine可能在年底前向市场更新澳大利亚资产剥离计划。随着公司重新聚焦奢侈品牌——试图逆转全球酒类消费下滑的趋势——他表示，不符合高端类别的品牌可能会被出售。

“我们正在就各种资产和葡萄园进行多项洽谈，我预计将在日历年年底前向市场更新一些进展消息，”他补充道。

对Treasury Wine来说，过去几年颇为艰难。该公司受到中国宴会上[酒精限制](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-12-16/treasury-wine-unveils-overhaul-to-tackle-slowdown-in-china-us)以及美国分销困难的负面影响，本周早些时候，该公司宣布了一项5.584亿澳元（3.94亿美元）的减值，以[应对供应过剩](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-09/treasury-wine-flags-395m-writedown-to-tackle-excess-us-supply)。在2025年全年，该公司股价暴跌[超过50%](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-02-26/penfolds-billionaire-backer-sees-comeback-for-luxury-wine-maker)。

在6月的投资者日上，Fischer公布了向奢华白葡萄酒和清淡酒品的转型，以适应消费者对更健康、低酒精替代品的需求，同时还[宣布对](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-04/treasury-wine-flags-lower-earnings-and-focus-on-luxury-market)美国业务进行[审查](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-04/treasury-wine-flags-lower-earnings-and-focus-on-luxury-market)。

周四，他表示审查尚未有时间表，公司准备等待必要的时间，以从任何美国资产剥离中获得合理价值。“我们可能需要一段时间才能找到合适的买家，愿意以我们认为这些资产价值的价格购买，”他说。“因此，这不是一个明确的时间表。这需要多年时间，如果我们能更快完成，我们当然会告知市场。”

Treasury Wine报告称，其关键EBITS指标——息税前利润及一项平抑农业估值波动的衡量指标——同比下降36.1%，截至6月30日的12个月为4.923亿澳元，略高于分析师预期。

花旗集团(Citigroup Inc.)分析师Sam Teeger在一份报告中表示，Penfolds在中国的势头强劲，尽管全球酒类消费下滑对公司构成压力，但“Treasury内部有一些被低估的特殊因素，与新的高能力管理团队领导下的扭转型策略相关，这些因素正在积聚动力。”

展望未来，该公司表示，截至2027年6月的财年盈利将“至少与上一财年持平”，并将在未来12个月内完成中国库存的重新平衡。

### English Original

## Treasury Wine Earnings Drop by 36% on Global Alcohol Downturn

### Takeaways

- Treasury Wine Estates Ltd.’s shares jumped as signs of growth in the China market outweighed a steep drop in full-year earnings.
- Stock depletions in China were up by more than 34%, indicating a reduction of the third-party sales that have threatened its core Penfolds brand.
- The company flagged earnings for the financial year ending June 2027 to be “at least equivalent” to the previous period, with rebalancing of inventory in China to be completed in the next 12 months.
[Treasury Wine Estates Ltd.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/TWE%20AU%20Equity)’s shares jumped by as much as 6.4% as signs of growth in the vintner’s critical China market outweighed a steep drop in full-year earnings.

The Australian winemaker, which produces the Penfolds brand, reported a sharp downturn in revenue on Thursday as it grapples with weakening consumer demand for alcohol, as well as long-running oversupply and distribution headaches in its US business.

But there were signs of a turnaround that cheered investors. Stock depletions in China — the volume that moves from wholesalers or distributors to customers — were up by more than 34%, indicating a reduction of the third-party sales that have threatened its core Penfolds brand.

Chief Executive Officer Sam Fischer has made tackling parallel imports into China through e-commerce sites and third parties a significant pillar of his strategy to revive the fortunes of the embattled vintner since he took the helm in October. Speaking to Bloomberg after the results, he said the growth in stock depletions was partly driven by the crackdown and partly by new consumption. “We think it’s about 50-50,” he said.

Treasury Wine Estates CEO Sam Fisher says demand for key brands is improving after the company reported a 36% decline in annual earnings. Efforts to tackle US supply chain issues and curb parallel imports in China are beginning to show results. He spoke exclusively with Paul Allen on Insight with Haslinda Amin. Source: Bloomberg

Moving forward with his strategy to streamline the company, Fischer said Treasury Wine could update the market on Australian divestment plans by the end of the year. With a renewed focus on luxury brands — an attempt to buck a global downturn in alcohol consumption — he said asset sales were likely for labels that didn’t fit premium categories.

“We’ve got quite a few conversations going on in relation to various assets and vineyards, and I would expect to be updating the market with some news around progress before the end of the calendar year,” he added.

It has been a rough couple of years for Treasury Wine. The company has been adversely affected by [restrictions on alcohol](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-12-16/treasury-wine-unveils-overhaul-to-tackle-slowdown-in-china-us) at banquets in China, as well as distribution difficulties in the US, which saw it announce a A$558.4 million ($394 million) writedown to [tackle excess supply](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-09/treasury-wine-flags-395m-writedown-to-tackle-excess-us-supply) earlier this week. Over the course of 2025, the company’s share price plummeted [more than 50%](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-02-26/penfolds-billionaire-backer-sees-comeback-for-luxury-wine-maker).

Fischer unveiled the shift toward luxury white wines and lighter refreshments as consumers look to healthier, low-alcohol alternatives, as well as [a review](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-04/treasury-wine-flags-lower-earnings-and-focus-on-luxury-market) of US operations, at an investor day in June.

On Thursday, he said there was not yet a timeline for the review and the company was prepared to wait as long as necessary to get proper value for any US assets divested. “It might take us a while to find an appropriate buyer who’s willing to pay what we think these assets are worth,” he said. “So hence, it’s not a defined timeline. It’s multiyear and if we can do it faster, we’ll certainly tell the market.”

Treasury Wine reported a 36.1% year-on-year decline in its key EBITS performance metric – earnings before interest, tax and a measure to smooth the volatility of agricultural valuations — to A$492.3 million for the 12 months to June 30, slightly ahead of analyst expectations.

Citigroup Inc. analyst Sam Teeger said in a note that Penfolds’ momentum in China was strong and that while flagging alcohol consumption globally weighed on the company, “there are underappreciated idiosyncratic factors at Treasury related to the turnaround under the new highly capable management team which are gaining momentum.”

Looking ahead, the company flagged earnings for the financial year ending June 2027 to be “at least equivalent” to the previous period, with rebalancing of inventory in China to be completed in the next 12 months.
