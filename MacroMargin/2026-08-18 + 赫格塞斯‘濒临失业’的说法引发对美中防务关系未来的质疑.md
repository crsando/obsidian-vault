### 中文译文

## 赫格塞斯‘濒临失业’的说法引发对美中防务关系未来的质疑

据多位消息人士称，由于伊朗战争陷入僵局，唐纳德·特朗普对五角大楼负责人失去了耐心。

多位知情人士称，越来越多的人猜测，[五角大楼负责人皮特·赫格塞斯](https://www.scmp.com/news/world/middle-east/article/3361399/us-war-iran-has-cost-us375-billion-so-far-pentagon-chief-hegseth-says)面临丢掉职位的风险正在加大，这引发对美中关系潜在影响的质疑。

这些消息人士因无权公开讨论此事而要求匿名，他们表示，赫格塞斯“即将失去工作”。

他们援引了伊朗问题上持续的僵局以及大量负面媒体报道，其中一位消息人士指出，人们对[“亚伯拉罕·林肯”号航空母舰上船员的状况](https://www.scmp.com/news/world/middle-east/article/3364117/iran-war-trump-dismisses-concerns-over-conditions-uss-abraham-lincoln-aircraft-carrier)的担忧日益加剧。这艘航母已在海上航行九个月，据报道，人员面临食物短缺和卫生条件恶劣的问题。

一位消息人士称，[伊朗战争缺乏“实质性突破”](https://www.scmp.com/news/world/united-states-canada/article/3347215/us-has-no-end-date-iran-war-strikes-very-much-track-hegseth)尤其激怒了总统唐纳德·特朗普，而另一位消息人士则直言不讳：“[赫格塞斯的]职位岌岌可危”。

这一不确定性正值美中防务关系处于敏感时期，自特朗普与中国国家主席习近平于5月在北京会晤以来，双边关系已显示出改善迹象。

一位熟悉两国军队交往的消息人士表示，在特朗普的第二个任期内，两国几乎在各个层面都有接触，两国关系是十多年来最好的。

在北京峰会数周后，赫格塞斯在一次防务集会上表示，两国关系“比多年来都要好”，并称特朗普和习近平关于建立建设性关系的协议也延伸至军事领域。

上个月，美国高级官员在华盛顿会见了中国外交部副部长马朝旭，负责中国、台湾和蒙古事务的国防部副助理部长阿尔瓦罗·史密斯表示，[两军之间的沟通](https://www.scmp.com/news/china/military/article/3362210/pentagon-official-hails-stronger-military-ties-pla-anniversary-event)“比多年来都要强”。

据五角大楼称，史密斯表示：“在赫格塞斯部长的领导下，我们正在积极修复曾经破裂的军事沟通渠道，并取得明确而稳定的进展。”

复旦大学国际问题研究院教授赵明昊表示，特朗普可能需要有人为伊朗战争“承担责任”。

他还说，在美国中期选举前进行内阁改组也是常见做法。

他说：“如果赫格塞斯被免职，将对中国和美国军方之间的沟通产生一些影响。”

赵明昊提到赫格塞斯此前与中国同行董军的会晤——包括在马来西亚以及特朗普访问北京期间——并表示“如果有新的国防部长，肯定需要重新建立关系的过程”。

但美国消息人士表示，赫格塞斯可能的离职不一定会破坏正在发展的军事关系。

一位人士表示，[负责政策的五角大楼副部长埃尔布里奇·科尔比](https://www.scmp.com/news/china/diplomacy/article/3364144/why-us-indo-pacific-burden-sharing-risks-deepening-polarisation-and-big-power-rivalry)曾是会谈的关键人物，定期与中国国防官员会面。

科尔比是上月会见马（Ma）的官员之一，中国外交部称双方进行了“深入”的意见交流。

据一位熟悉此事的消息人士称，中国官员对科尔比印象深刻，他曾表示台湾并非美国至关重要的切身利益。

北京视台湾为中国的一部分，从未放弃使用武力将其与大陆统一的承诺。

美国与大多数国家一样，不承认这个自治岛屿独立，但反对任何以武力夺取它的企图，并有法律义务向其提供武器。

另一位消息人士称美中军事接触“目前非常好”，第三位消息人士表示，除非赫格塞斯的继任者是对华鹰派，否则新任防长可能对两军交往影响甚微。

赵表示，如果科尔比留在五角大楼，军事交流将“保持连续性”。

他补充说，美中两国军队在其他工作层面的会谈也已恢复，部分原因是特朗普与习近平同意在“战略稳定”基础上建立关系。

“这意味着恢复和加强中美防务交流，”他说。

“这将继续下去，因为这是特朗普方面非常重要的意图。随着习近平即将访问美国，我认为加强军事关系也将是议程上的一个非常重要项目。”

预计习近平下月将前往华盛顿，这将是自2015年以来他对美国的首次国事访问。

## Claims Hegseth ‘on verge of losing job’ raise questions about future US-China defence ties

## **Claims Hegseth ‘on verge of losing job’ raise questions about future US-China defence ties**

Donald Trump is losing patience with the Pentagon chief because of the stalled Iran war, according to several sources

Speculation is growing that [Pentagon chief Pete Hegseth](https://www.scmp.com/news/world/middle-east/article/3361399/us-war-iran-has-cost-us375-billion-so-far-pentagon-chief-hegseth-says) is at increasing risk of losing his job, raising questions about the possible implications for US-China relations, according to multiple sources familiar with the matter.

The sources, speaking on condition of anonymity because they were not authorised to discuss the matter publicly, said Hegseth was “on the verge of losing his job”.

They cited the ongoing deadlock over Iran and a slew of negative media reports, with one source pointing to mounting concerns about the [welfare of the crew on board the USS Abraham Lincoln](https://www.scmp.com/news/world/middle-east/article/3364117/iran-war-trump-dismisses-concerns-over-conditions-uss-abraham-lincoln-aircraft-carrier). The aircraft carrier has been at sea for nine months, with personnel reportedly facing food shortages and poor sanitary conditions.

The source said the [lack of a “solid breakthrough” on the Iran war](https://www.scmp.com/news/world/united-states-canada/article/3347215/us-has-no-end-date-iran-war-strikes-very-much-track-hegseth) had particularly angered President Donald Trump, while another put it bluntly: “[Hegseth’s] job is in danger”.

The uncertainty comes at a sensitive time for defence relations between the United States and China, which have shown signs of improvement since Trump and his Chinese counterpart Xi Jinping met in Beijing in May.

One source familiar with engagements between the two countries’ armed forces said there had been contact at almost all levels during Trump’s second term and relations were the best they had been in more than a decade.

Weeks after the Beijing summit, Hegseth told a defence gathering that relations between the two countries were “better than they’ve been in many years”, saying Trump and Xi’s agreement to build a constructive relationship extended to the militaries too.

Last month, senior US officials met Chinese vice-minister for foreign affairs Ma Zhaoxu in Washington, with Alvaro Smith, deputy assistant defence secretary for China, Taiwan and Mongolia, saying [communication between the two militaries](https://www.scmp.com/news/china/military/article/3362210/pentagon-official-hails-stronger-military-ties-pla-anniversary-event) was “stronger than it has been in years”.

“Under the leadership of Secretary Hegseth, we are actively repairing a once-broken military-to-military channel and making clear, steady progress,” Smith said, according to the Pentagon.

Zhao Minghao, a professor at Fudan University’s Institute of International Studies, said Trump might need “someone to take responsibility” for the Iran war.

Cabinet reshuffles were also typical ahead of US midterm elections, he added.

“If Hegseth is removed from his post, it would have some impact on communication between the Chinese and US militaries,” he said.

Zhao cited Hegseth’s previous meetings with his Chinese counterpart Dong Jun – including in Malaysia and during Trump’s visit to Beijing – saying that “if there’s a new secretary, there will definitely need to be a new process of [building a relationship]”.

But US sources said Hegseth’s possible exit would not necessarily derail the developing military relationship.

One said [Elbridge Colby, the Pentagon undersecretary for policy](https://www.scmp.com/news/china/diplomacy/article/3364144/why-us-indo-pacific-burden-sharing-risks-deepening-polarisation-and-big-power-rivalry), had been the point man for the talks, holding regular meetings with Chinese defence officials.

Colby was one of the officials who met Ma last month for what the Chinese foreign ministry described as an “in-depth” exchange of views.

According to one source familiar with the matter, Chinese officials have been impressed by Colby, who said Taiwan was not a vital existential interest for the US.

Beijing regards Taiwan as part of China and has never renounced the use of force to reunite it with the mainland.

The US, in common with most countries, does not recognise the self-ruled island as independent but is opposed to any attempt to seize it by force and is legally bound to supply it with weapons.

Another source described US-China military contacts as “very good right now”, with a third saying a new defence chief was likely to have little effect on military-to-military engagement unless Hegseth’s replacement was a China hawk.

Zhao said that if Colby stayed at the Pentagon, there would be “continuity going forward in military exchanges”.

Talks between the US and Chinese militaries at other working levels have also resumed, he added, in part because of Trump and Xi’s agreement to build a relationship based on “strategic stability”.

“The implication of that is restoring and strengthening China-US defence exchanges,” he said.

“This will continue because it is a very important intention on Trump’s part. With Xi’s coming visit to the US, I think strengthening military-to-military relations will also be a very important item on the agenda.”

Xi is expected to travel to Washington next month for what will be his first US state visit since 2015.
