### 中文译文

## 中国低成本人工智能正将投资者焦点转向互联网股票

### **要点**（由彭博人工智能提供）

- 投资者正押注，人工智能成本的下降将把更多行业价值转移向拥有人们日常使用的应用和服务的公司。
- 中国开发者在开放权重人工智能模型方面竞争力不断增强，在保持低价的同时缩小了与全球领先者的差距，使人工智能的使用成本更低。
- 这一转变反映出人们日益认为，人工智能的最大赢家可能正从硬件制造方扩展到向消费者和企业提供人工智能服务的公司。
市场参与者表示，中国在低成本人工智能领域的快速进展，正在强化该国互联网巨头的投资理由，这些巨头多年来一直处于芯片制造商的阴影之下。

投资者愈发押注，人工智能成本的下降将把更多行业价值转移向拥有人们日常使用的应用和服务的公司，从搜索、电商到广告和企业软件。

自6月25日低点以来，KraneShares中国互联网[ETF](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/KWEB%20US%20Equity)已上涨超过20%，约为恒生指数涨幅的两倍，而费城半导体指数下跌约11%。这一转变反映出人们日益认为，人工智能的最大赢家可能正从硬件制造方扩展到向消费者和企业提供人工智能服务的公司。

![图片](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3b481cc90b14818d835cd0dff798a1e5/3b481cc9-0b14-81a1-bdf3-deda8fcff903-b6e17bdb3765816e788514966a6e4b26.png)

“中国超大规模云服务商将大幅受益，因为它们拥有更便宜的运行token，而且更便宜的模型将显著增加它们及其客户对人工智能的使用，”对冲基金Lotus Asset Management Ltd.首席投资官Hao Hong表示。“这就是阿里巴巴和腾讯最近表现强劲的原因。更便宜的token将刺激需求。更低的服务成本可能对中国超大规模云服务商的利润率产生积极影响。”

中国开发者在开放权重AI模型方面已日益具有竞争力，在保持低价的同时缩小了与全球领先者的差距。例如，阿里巴巴集团控股有限公司最新的Qwen3.8-Max [模型](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-03/alibaba-drops-another-china-ai-model-with-breakthrough-performance)目前在Arena的基准测试中位列领先的编程模型之一，该基准测试根据性能、速度和成本对AI系统进行比较。

这些进展正在降低AI的使用成本。Silicon Data的综合推理价格[指数](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/SDLLMTK%20Index)自5月峰值以来已下跌36%，即便客户越来越多地采用功能更强大的推理模型和AI代理。更低的计算成本改善了搜索、广告、电商、客户支持和企业软件等服务的经济效益，同时让新的AI应用在商业上变得可行。

这一想法并非中国独有。在微软公司上周的财报电话会议上，首席执行官萨蒂亚·纳德拉表示，AI模型正变成可互换的投入要素，这表明，相较模型本身，拥有客户关系和软件平台的公司可能获得更多价值。

高盛集团认为，中国加速增长的token、下半年资本开支上升以及国内芯片供应增加，强化了其对云和数据中心的偏好。该行亚洲互联网研究主管Ronald Keung周三在接受彭博电视采访时表示，中国超大规模云服务商凭借强劲的现金流和扩大云业务的同时为资本开支融资的能力，处于最有利的位置。他估计阿里云在6月当季增长了45%。

![图片](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3b481cc90b14818d835cd0dff798a1e5/3b481cc9-0b14-8183-bd41-fd92caed0385-ae04b17dab4d0c95483a14fdaa5495ed.png)

诚然，谨慎是有理由的。中国的科技行业长期以来优先考虑规模而非盈利能力，AI可能也不例外。拥有自己AI模型的互联网巨头也面临着越来越大的压力，需要捍卫自己的业务免受包括DeepSeek、Z AI Co.、Moonshot和字节跳动等竞争对手的冲击。与此同时，阿里巴巴、美团和京东集团在外卖业务上仍面临价格战，且国内消费需求疲软。

![图片](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3b481cc90b14818d835cd0dff798a1e5/3b481cc9-0b14-81c6-837c-c0e78b296144-188f7f30e916371d6cf250376b34e7ce.png)

目前，投资者认为轮动可能还有进一步空间，因为中国的云和互联网股票在多年表现不佳后，仅仅是阿里巴巴因云增长和旗舰AI模型的乐观情绪而上扬，这是对AI成本下降的反映。坚挺的GPU租赁[价格](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/SDH100RT%20Index)也表明，即便token价格下降，计算需求依然持续。

## China’s Cheap AI Is Shifting Investors’ Focus to Internet Stocks

### **Takeaways ** by Bloomberg AI

- Investors are betting that falling AI costs will shift more of the industry’s value toward companies that own the applications and services people use every day.
- Chinese developers have become increasingly competitive in open-weight AI models, narrowing the gap with global leaders while keeping prices low, making AI cheaper to use.
- The shift reflects a growing belief that the biggest winners from AI may be broadening from those making the hardware to the companies delivering AI services to consumers and businesses.
China’s rapid progress in low-cost artificial intelligence is strengthening the investment case for the country’s internet giants after years in the shadow of chipmakers, market participants say.

Investors are increasingly betting that falling AI costs will shift more of the industry’s value toward companies that own the applications and services people use every day, from search and e-commerce to advertising and enterprise software.

Since a low on June 25, the KraneShares China Internet [ETF](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/KWEB%20US%20Equity) has gained more than 20%, about twice the Hang Seng Index’s advance, while the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index has fallen roughly 11%. The shift reflects a growing belief that the biggest winners from AI may be broadening from those making the hardware to the companies delivering AI services to consumers and businesses.

“Chinese hyperscalers will benefit substantially because they have cheaper currency to run on and cheaper models will increase the use of AI significantly by them and their clients,” said Hao Hong, chief investment officer at hedge fund Lotus Asset Management Ltd. “That is why BABA and Tencent are roaring lately. So cheaper tokens are going to spur demand. And cheaper serving costs can be margin positive for the Chinese hyperscalers.”

Chinese developers have become increasingly competitive in open-weight AI models, narrowing the gap with global leaders while keeping prices low. Alibaba Group Holding Ltd.’s latest Qwen3.8-Max [model](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-03/alibaba-drops-another-china-ai-model-with-breakthrough-performance), for example, now ranks among the leading coding models on Arena’s benchmark that compares AI systems on performance, speed and cost.

### Arena Source: https://arena.ai/leaderboard/code/webdev/pareto

Those advances are making AI cheaper to use. Silicon Data’s blended inference-price [index](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/SDLLMTK%20Index) has fallen 36% from its May peak even as customers increasingly adopt more capable reasoning models and AI agents. Lower computing costs improve the economics of services such as search, advertising, e-commerce, customer support and enterprise software, while making new AI applications commercially viable.

The idea isn’t unique to China. On Microsoft Corp.’s earnings call last week, Chief Executive Officer Satya Nadella said AI models are becoming interchangeable inputs, suggesting that more value could accrue to companies that own customer relationships and software platforms rather than the models themselves.

Goldman Sachs Group Inc. believes China’s accelerating token growth, rising second-half capex, and increasing domestic chip supply reinforce its preference for cloud and data centers. Ronald Keung, the broker’s head of Asia Internet research said in a Bloomberg TV interview Wednesday that Chinese hyperscalers are best positioned given their strong cash flows and ability to fund capex while scaling cloud businesses. He estimates Alibaba Cloud grew 45% in the June quarter.

To be sure, there are reasons for caution. China’s technology sector has a long history of prioritizing scale over profitability, and AI may prove no different. The internet giants that have their own AI models are also under growing pressure to defend their franchises against rivals including DeepSeek, Z AI Co., Moonshot and ByteDance. Meanwhile, Alibaba, Meituan and JD.com Inc. continue to face a price war in the food delivery business and sluggish domestic consumer demand.
