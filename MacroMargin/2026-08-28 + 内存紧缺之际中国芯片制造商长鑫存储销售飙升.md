## 内存紧缺之际中国芯片制造商长鑫存储销售飙升

位于北京亦庄科技园区的长鑫存储（CXMT Corp.）芯片制造厂。

*摄影师：沈绮来/彭博社*

彭博新闻
2026年8月28日 下午6:24（GMT+8）
*更新于2026年8月28日 下午7:45（GMT+8）*

### **彭博AI要点**

- 长鑫存储（CXMT Corp.）上半年营收扩大至1503亿元人民币，同时实现净利润776亿元人民币，而去年同期为亏损。
- 该公司将出色的增长归因于全球DRAM供应紧张，并表示这一趋势将在下半年持续；同时其下一代LPDDR6存储产品有望量产。
- 长鑫存储已成为中国市值最高的企业，被视为中国减少对外国技术依赖努力的象征；其股价自上市以来持续上涨，市值已超越腾讯控股有限公司。
[长鑫存储（CXMT Corp.）](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/688825%20CH%20Equity)上半年营收飙升近10倍，利润激增，反映出市场对AI硬件的抢购热潮使这家存储芯片制造商成为中国市值最高的企业。

这家总部位于合肥的公司在今年前六个月的收入扩大至1503亿元人民币（合224亿美元），是其2025年全年销售额的两倍多。长鑫存储上半年收入1503亿元人民币，上年同期为154.4亿元人民币（1），利润为776亿元人民币，而去年同期为亏损。

这一可观的盈利表现出现在长鑫存储于上海完成中国第二大首次公开募股（IPO）一个月之后，该公司最终通过超额配售选择权筹集了666亿元人民币。长鑫存储是全球第四大DRAM制造商，随着人工智能的兴起以及为其提供动力的对硬件需求极高的数据中心，其产品的需求和价格一路飙升。

长鑫存储将其出色的增长归因于全球DRAM供应紧张，并表示这一趋势将在下半年持续。该公司表示，其下一代LPDDR6存储产品（可用于智能手机和平板电脑）已交付客户验证，目前正按计划投入量产。

长鑫存储的股价自上市以来持续上涨，该公司现已超越[腾讯控股有限公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/700%20HK%20Equity)，成为中国市值最大的公司，标志着中国科技格局的范式转变。即便如此，据高盛称，这家芯片制造商的估值仍较全球同行有明显折让，高盛计算其当前估值约为2027年预期盈利的10倍。该倍数基于以下预测：净利润将从今年的1610亿元人民币增至2027年的3610亿元人民币，增长逾一倍。

这些业绩显示这家中国存储芯片制造商的增长急剧加速，而此前不久，该公司还在大宗商品市场竞争中承受巨额亏损。长鑫存储如今受益于同样严重的[内存短缺](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-02-15/rampant-ai-demand-for-memory-is-fueling-a-growing-chip-crisis)，这种短缺也推高了[三星电子](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/005930%20KS%20Equity)、[SK海力士](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/000660%20KS%20Equity)和[美光科技](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/MU%20US%20Equity)的估值。所有这些公司都在优先生产英伟达等公司制造AI加速器所需的高端内存。

作为中国在这些国际供应商之外的最佳替代选择，长鑫存储已成为中国减少对外国技术依赖的[象征](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2026-07-09/china-s-cxmt-chipmaker-eyes-ipo-to-challenge-samsung-sk-hynix-micron)。由美国培养的芯片老将朱一明创立的长鑫存储，还在致力于开发自己的高带宽内存，这是一种用于AI加速器的高级半导体，但该公司在周五的[业绩发布](https://static.sse.com.cn/disclosure/listedinfo/announcement/c/new/2026-08-29/688825_20260829_TZRI.pdf)中并未提及在这一领域的进展。

该公司表示将利用IPO募资来扩大产能，并努力追赶规模更大的竞争对手。事实上，它已经在今年上半年加大了支出力度。

其研发支出增长87%，达到68.6亿元人民币。公司研发团队规模也同比增长61%，达到近7500人。

作为长鑫存储（CXMT）影响力不断上升的一个迹象，据称[苹果公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/AAPL%20US%20Equity)正在洽谈采购其存储芯片，同时还有中国供应商[长江存储科技有限责任公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1577197D:CH)。彭博新闻社7月[报道](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-01/apple-seeks-to-buy-chinese-made-memory-chips-with-lobbying-push)称，这将用于苹果在中国销售的设备。这些讨论在华盛顿引起警觉，一群美国参议员敦促苹果放弃依赖中国供应商的尝试。

该公司最近被美国国防部[列入黑名单](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-08/pentagon-accuses-alibaba-baidu-byd-of-aiding-china-s-military)，长鑫存储周五表示，此举不会影响其日常运营。

## Chinese Chipmaker CXMT’s Sales Soar Amid Memory Crunch

A CXMT Corp. chip fabrication plant in the E-Town tech hub in Beijing.

### **Takeaways **by Bloomberg AI

- CXMT Corp.'s revenue expanded to 150.3 billion yuan in the first six months, while it posted a profit of 77.6 billion yuan, versus a loss a year earlier.
- The company attributed its stellar growth to a global constraint in DRAM supplies, a trend it said will continue in the second half, and its next-generation LPDDR6 memory product is on track for mass production.
- CXMT has become China's most valuable business and is seen as a symbol of the country's effort to reduce reliance on foreign technology, with its stock keeping rising since its debut and overtaking Tencent Holdings Ltd. in market capitalization.
[CXMT Corp.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/688825%20CH%20Equity)’s first-half revenue leapt almost 10-fold while profit surged, reflecting a rush for AI hardware that’s made the memory chipmaker China’s most valuable business.

The Hefei-based company’s revenue expanded to 150.3 billion yuan ($22.4 billion) in the first six months, more than double the sales it raked in across all of 2025. It CXMT 1H Revenue 150.3B Yuan Vs. 15.44B Yuan Y/y (1) a profit of 77.6 billion yuan, versus a loss a year earlier.

The impressive earnings emerged a month after CXMT pulled off China’s second biggest initial public offering in Shanghai, eventually raising 66.6 billion yuan with an overallotment option. The company is the fourth-biggest maker of DRAM in the world, and it’s seen demand and prices for its products skyrocket with the advent of artificial intelligence and the hardware-hungry data centers used to power it.

CXMT attributed its stellar growth to a global constraint in DRAM supplies, a trend it said will continue in the second half. The company said its next-generation LPDDR6 memory product, which can be used in smartphones and tablets, has been delivered to clients for validation and is now on track for mass production.

CXMT’s stock has kept rising since its debut, and the company has now overtaken [Tencent Holdings Ltd.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/700%20HK%20Equity) to become China’s largest by market capitalization, marking a paradigm shift in the country’s tech landscape. Even so, the chipmaker CXMT’s 570% Rally Raises Bar for First Earnings Since IPO (1) at a significant discount to global peers, according to Goldman Sachs, which calculates its current valuation at about 10 times estimated 2027 earnings. That multiple is based on forecasts for net income to more than double to 361 billion yuan in 2027 from an estimated 161 billion yuan this year.

The results show sharp growth acceleration for the Chinese memory chipmaker that had until recently incurred hefty losses competing in a market for commodity products. CXMT now benefits from the same acute [shortage](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-02-15/rampant-ai-demand-for-memory-is-fueling-a-growing-chip-crisis) of memory that’s fired up the valuations of [Samsung Electronics Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/005930%20KS%20Equity), [SK Hynix Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/000660%20KS%20Equity) and [Micron Technology Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/MU%20US%20Equity) All those companies are prioritizing the production of high-end memory required to make AI accelerators by the likes of [Nvidia Corp](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/NVDA%20US%20Equity).

As China’s best alternative to those international suppliers, CXMT has become a [symbol](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2026-07-09/china-s-cxmt-chipmaker-eyes-ipo-to-challenge-samsung-sk-hynix-micron) of the country’s effort to reduce reliance on foreign technology. Founded by US-trained chip veteran Zhu Yiming, CXMT is also working on developing its own high-bandwidth memory, the advanced class of semiconductor used in AI accelerators, though it made no mention of its progress in the field in the [earnings release](https://static.sse.com.cn/disclosure/listedinfo/announcement/c/new/2026-08-29/688825_20260829_TZRI.pdf) on Friday.

The company has said it will use the IPO proceeds to fund capacity expansion and try catch up with larger rivals. Already, it ramped up its spending level in the first half.

Its research spending rose 87% to 6.86 billion yuan. The company also grew its research and development team by 61% year-over-year to nearly 7,500 people.

In a sign of CXMT’s growing clout, [Apple Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/AAPL%20US%20Equity) is said to be in talks to purchase its memory chips, alongside fellow Chinese supplier [Yangtze Memory Technologies Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1577197D:CH) That would be for use in Apple devices sold in China, Bloomberg News [reported](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-01/apple-seeks-to-buy-chinese-made-memory-chips-with-lobbying-push) in July. The discussions have raised alarms in Washington, with a group of US senators urging Apple to drop its attempt to rely on Chinese suppliers.

The company was recently [blacklisted](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-08/pentagon-accuses-alibaba-baidu-byd-of-aiding-china-s-military) by the US Department of Defense, a move CXMT on Friday said won’t impact its day-to-day operation.
