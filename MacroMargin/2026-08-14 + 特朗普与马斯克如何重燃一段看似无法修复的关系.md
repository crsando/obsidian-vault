### 中文译文

## 特朗普与马斯克如何重燃一段看似无法修复的关系

> 鉴于中期选举对两人均利害攸关，马斯克计划斥资至少1亿美元援助共和党

![President Trump and a delegation including Elon Musk were greeted by Chinese officials in Beijing in May.](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3bc81cc90b148157a597f6788f8663bb/3bc81cc9-0b14-81cd-a491-db3afa749f49-5aafc297b87ab5f036802a8b58631c21.jpg)

*布伦丹·斯米亚洛夫斯基/法新社/盖蒂图片社*

正当内阁部长、商界高管和特朗普总统的家人登上空军一号飞往中国的长途航班时，埃隆·马斯克出现了。

大约一年前，马斯克曾公开与特朗普决裂。但据机上人员透露，5月份当他们飞往北京途中，一切似乎都很友好。

作为[特斯拉](https://www.wsj.com/market-data/quotes/TSLA)和[SpaceX](https://www.wsj.com/market-data/quotes/SPCX)的掌门人，当时即将成为[全球首位万亿美元富翁](https://www.wsj.com/livecoverage/spacex-ipo-stock-market-06-12-2026/card/elon-musk-is-officially-world-s-first-trillionaire-ITVmBMl5897JmIsBJLle?mod=article_inline)的马斯克，向总统讲述了在美国建造新工厂的计划。特朗普谈到了他最近观看的一段太空发射视频，并问起马斯克的一个小儿子。马斯克与白宫顾问们回忆起参与2024年总统竞选时的愉快时光。

随后，马斯克带来了特朗普团队数月来一直期待的消息：他打算在11月的中期选举中援助共和党。据熟悉该策略的人士透露，马斯克已表明计划斥资至少1亿美元用于选民动员行动，以帮助共和党维持对国会的控制。

这一场景为近一年来让特朗普与马斯克重归于好的努力画上了句号，此前两人曾经历现代美国政治史上最不寻常且最重要的政治联盟之一的[破裂](https://www.wsj.com/politics/policy/elon-musk-trump-bromance-fight-40ca745c?mod=article_inline)。

这涉及幕后电话、共同朋友的劝说、海湖庄园的邀请以及一场白宫晚会。这场重聚也因一场意外悲剧而向前推进：保守派活动人士查理·柯克遇刺，他在去世前的几个月里一直在努力修复这段关系。

据总统助手称，私下里，特朗普表示他和马斯克永远不会像从前那样亲密。但知情人士称，他们又恢复了通话，大约每月一次，讨论人工智能、中国和世界大事。

这段重燃的关系可能对两人都有利。如果民主党赢得国会控制权，他们准备对总统及其家人发起一连串调查。依赖利润丰厚的政府合同的马斯克也可能成为目标。

马斯克的代表未回应置评请求。白宫在一份声明中表示，特朗普“建立了美国政治史上最伟大的联盟，充满了像[埃隆·马斯克](https://www.wsj.com/topics/person/elon-musk)这样的爱国者，他们致力于捍卫本届政府的所有历史性成就。”

![Trump and Musk spoke at the White House in March 2025, as tensions between them mounted behind the scenes.](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3bc81cc90b148157a597f6788f8663bb/3bc81cc9-0b14-81cb-9102-e69523d7a3d5-a0d6f4a077f79ea2087854bab7dc9bf3.jpg)

*罗伯托·施密特/法新社/盖蒂图片社*

### **“重磅炸弹”**

特朗普和马斯克在2024年竞选期间迅速成为好友，在特朗普第二次就职后不久，马斯克就搬进了白宫建筑群——有时睡在那里——以监督总统缩减联邦政府规模的努力。

但马斯克非常规的做法招致负面报道，并激怒了特朗普内阁成员，他们认为自己应该对所属机构拥有更多控制权，而他削减数百万美国人依赖的项目的做法也招致批评。助手们对马斯克抱怨不断，但数周来特朗普一直坚称他们的关系牢固。

去年6月，一切都崩溃了，幕后的摩擦在网上爆发。

“是时候扔出真正的大炸弹了，”马斯克在争执最激烈时告诉他的2.4亿X粉丝。他说，特朗普被列入了杰弗里·爱泼斯坦的调查文件中。“这就是他们一直没有公开的真正原因。”

特朗普借助他的Truth Social平台，威胁要取消马斯克的政府合同，并指责他反对一项标志性的税收和支出法案，因为该法案取消了电动汽车的激励措施。马斯克称该方案（他称之为“令人作呕的怪物”）将使国家陷入更多债务。

“没有我，特朗普会输掉选举，”马斯克写道，他在2024年花费了近3亿美元帮助特朗普连任。“真是忘恩负义。”

![Musk joined Trump during an election campaign rally in Butler, Penn., in 2024.](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3bc81cc90b148157a597f6788f8663bb/3bc81cc9-0b14-819e-b4f2-d4395ba9d7e6-12b72613f73f1c71880a49b034dfc1e2.jpg)

*吉姆·沃森/法新社/盖蒂图片社*

### **和解之举**

公开场合，白宫淡化了马斯克对总统的攻击，称之为“不幸的事件”。但据知情人士透露，在幕后，高级政府官员正急于修复这段关系。

在两人公开闹翻的一天后，副总统JD·万斯和白宫办公厅主任苏西·威尔斯悄悄联系了马斯克，试图缓和紧张局势。他们觉得情况已经糟到极点，尝试一下也没什么损失。据知情人士透露，对话进行得很顺利。

据知情人士透露，次周早些时候，马斯克致电特朗普，双方也进行了积极的交谈。马斯克随后在社交媒体上发帖，对自己的一些言论表示遗憾，称这些言论过火了。

但这段关系要等到数月之后才得到更充分的修复。九月，特朗普告诉一位白宫高级官员，他还没有准备好让马斯克重返自己的核心圈子。

万斯在修复两人关系方面发挥了核心作用，他与这位商人及其盟友保持着开放的沟通渠道。据知情人士称，去年夏末，马斯克放弃了组建新政党的计划——在一些特朗普顾问看来，这是一种和平姿态——部分原因是希望与万斯保持关系，而万斯被广泛视为2028年总统大选的可能候选人。

据知情人士称，万斯认为马斯克是一位不容忽视的重要人物。考虑到SpaceX合同对国防和国家安全的重要性，与政府关系的永久破裂可能产生深远影响。事实上，在马斯克与特朗普争吵最激烈的时候，他威胁要切断对国际空间站任务的支持，这让NASA的一些人感到恐慌。

据《华尔街日报》此前报道，在6月与特朗普发生争执后，政府主导的对SpaceX与联邦政府合同的审查发现，其中大部分合同对国防部和NASA至关重要。从那以后，该公司只会更加深入地融入美国的战争机器。

据知情人士透露，与万斯一样，总统的长子小唐纳德·特朗普也与马斯克保持着友好关系。在两人关系破裂前后，他的风险投资公司1789 Capital投资了马斯克的xAI、SpaceX和Neuralink。特朗普副幕僚长斯蒂芬·米勒的妻子凯蒂·米勒也与马斯克关系密切，她曾在马斯克白宫任职期间与他共事。

### **人事变动与悲剧**

据与柯克关系密切的人士透露，拥有大量追随者的年轻保守派活动人士柯克也曾致力于让特朗普和马斯克走到一起，在争吵公开化时与马斯克沟通。柯克于9月遭枪击身亡，特朗普和马斯克出席了在亚利桑那州一座足球场为他举行的追悼会。据知情人士透露，特朗普的技术顾问、马斯克的盟友大卫·萨克斯曾向威尔斯建议，让他设法让两人走到一起。

![Trump and Musk spoke during a memorial for Charlie Kirk in Glendale, Ariz., in September.](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3bc81cc90b148157a597f6788f8663bb/3bc81cc9-0b14-8134-bc77-f2f2d9baa1a9-c6b016c1e9a98da120f570c913afa4b2.jpg)

*罗斯·D·富兰克林/美联社*

马斯克走到特朗普的体育场包厢，与总统短暂交谈；当企业家离开时，特朗普拍了拍马斯克的胳膊。后来，在空军一号上，特朗普谈到了这次交谈，称其有趣，并询问其他人的看法，据一位熟悉这次会面的人士透露。

当晚，马斯克[发布了一张他正在纪念活动上与总统交谈的照片](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/1969897602524328440?s=20)。“为了查理，”他写道。

与此同时，两个人事变动也帮助缓解了紧张关系。

在白宫，马斯克与特朗普的长期助手塞尔吉奥·戈尔多次发生冲突。在特朗普第二任期早期的一次会议上，特朗普表示，他对马斯克的DOGE在某些计划上进展不够快感到沮丧。据一位知情人士透露，马斯克指责负责监督政府任命审查的戈尔，导致总统斥责了戈尔。

去年八月，特朗普任命戈尔为美国驻印度大使——将他送到距华盛顿7500英里之外。

特朗普还在11月做出惊人逆转，重新任命马斯克的盟友贾里德·艾萨克曼领导NASA，据知情人士透露，这一举动令马斯克感激。该职位的竞争非常激烈，艾萨克曼最终胜出，击败了运输部长肖恩·达菲——达菲是在特朗普于6月因与马斯克决裂而罢免艾萨克曼后接任该职位的。

11月，作为关系缓和的迹象，马斯克回到白宫参加特朗普为沙特王储穆罕默德·本·萨勒曼举办的晚宴。近几个月来，特朗普公开赞扬马斯克。在马斯克成为万亿富翁后，总统给他发了一封祝贺信。

对于中期选举，马斯克计划向美国政治行动委员会（America PAC）捐款，该组织是他为2024年支持特朗普的努力而设立的。据一位知情人士透露，他计划投入至少1亿美元用于关键州的动员投票行动，这一细节此前由Axios和《纽约时报》报道。

America PAC将与MAGA Inc.协调，后者是主要的特朗普超级政治行动委员会，已持有超过4亿美元资金。

马斯克已表示，他不会像上次选举以及他在监督DOGE期间那样高调露面，那是一个两极分化且常常混乱的角色。马斯克上个月对《经济学人》表示：“我想我对政治介入得有点太深了。”

“坦率地说，有点得意忘形了。”

## How Trump and Musk Rekindled a Relationship That Seemed Beyond Repair

## **How Trump and Musk Rekindled a Relationship That Seemed Beyond Repair**

> With much at stake for both men in the midterms, Musk plans to spend at least $100 million to help Republicans

*BRENDAN SMIALOWSKI/AFP/GETTY IMAGES*

As cabinet secretaries, business executives and President Trump’s family members boarded Air Force One for a long-haul flight to China, in came Elon Musk.

Musk had publicly torched his relationship with Trump nearly one year earlier. But in May, as they cruised toward Beijing, all appeared friendly, according to people on the plane.

The leader of [Tesla](https://www.wsj.com/market-data/quotes/TSLA) and [SpaceX](https://www.wsj.com/market-data/quotes/SPCX), then on the brink of becoming the [world’s first trillionaire](https://www.wsj.com/livecoverage/spacex-ipo-stock-market-06-12-2026/card/elon-musk-is-officially-world-s-first-trillionaire-ITVmBMl5897JmIsBJLle?mod=article_inline), told the president about plans to build new factories in the U.S. Trump talked about a space-launch video he had recently watched and asked about one of Musk’s young sons. Musk reminisced with White House advisers about how much fun it had been to be involved in the 2024 presidential race.

Then Musk delivered news that Trump’s team had been hoping to hear for months: He intended to aid Republicans in November’s midterm elections. Musk has signaled he plans to spend at least $100 million on a voter-turnout operation to help the GOP maintain control of Congress, people familiar with the strategy said.

The scene capped a nearly yearlong effort to reunite Trump and Musk following [the breakup](https://www.wsj.com/politics/policy/elon-musk-trump-bromance-fight-40ca745c?mod=article_inline) of one of the most unusual and significant political alliances in modern American history.

It involved back-channel phone calls, coaxing from mutual friends, invites to Mar-a-Lago and a White House soiree. The reunion was also nudged forward by an unexpected tragedy: the assassination of conservative activist Charlie Kirk, who had spent the months before his death trying to repair the relationship.

Privately, Trump says he and Musk will never be as close as they once were, according to the president’s aides. But they are talking again, roughly once a month, discussing artificial intelligence, China and world events, people with knowledge of the calls said.

The rekindled relationship could help both men. If Democrats win control of Congress, they are prepared to launch a litany of investigations into the president and his family. Musk, who relies on lucrative government contracts, could be targeted as well.

Representatives for Musk didn’t respond to a request for comment. In a statement, the White House said Trump “has built the greatest coalition in the history of American politics, full of patriots like [Elon Musk](https://www.wsj.com/topics/person/elon-musk) who are deeply committed to preserving all of the historic accomplishments of this administration.”

*ROBERTO SCHMIDT/AFP/GETTY IMAGES*

### **The ‘big bomb’**

Trump and Musk became fast friends during the 2024 campaign, and soon after Trump took office for a second time, Musk moved into the White House complex—sleeping there at times—to oversee the president’s efforts to reduce the size of the federal government.

But Musk’s unorthodox approach attracted negative headlines and angered members of Trump’s cabinet, who felt they should have more control over their own agencies, while his cuts to programs relied on by millions of Americans drew criticism. Aides grumbled about Musk, but for weeks Trump insisted that their bond was strong.

Everything fell apart in June of last year, as behind-the-scenes friction erupted online.

“Time to drop the really big bomb,” Musk told his 240 million X followers at the apex of the fight. Trump, he said, was named in the Jeffrey Epstein investigative files. “That is the real reason they have not been made public.”

Trump, armed with his Truth Social platform, threatened to cancel Musk’s government contracts and accused him of opposing a signature tax-and-spending bill because it eliminated incentives for electric vehicles. Musk said the package, which he called a “disgusting abomination,” would drive the country into more debt.

“Without me, Trump would have lost the election,” wrote Musk, who spent nearly $300 million to help re-elect Trump in 2024. “Such ingratitude.”

*JIM WATSON/AFP/GETTY IMAGES*

### **Peace offering**

Publicly, the White House played down Musk’s attacks on the president as an “unfortunate episode.” But behind the scenes, senior administration officials were rushing to repair the relationship, according to people familiar with the matter.

One day after the public falling out between the two men, Vice President JD Vance and White House Chief of Staff Susie Wiles quietly reached out to Musk in an attempt to lower tensions. They figured things were so bad, they had little to lose by trying. The conversation went well, according to people familiar with the matter.

Early the next week, Musk called Trump and they also had a positive conversation, the people said. Musk followed the call with a social-media post expressing regret for some of his comments, saying they went too far.

But it would be many months before the relationship was more fully repaired. In September, Trump told a senior White House official that he wasn’t ready to bring Musk back into his inner circle.

Vance played a central role in mending ties between the two men, keeping open lines of communication with the businessman and his allies. Late last summer—in what was viewed as a peace offering by some of Trump’s advisers—Musk [abandoned plans](https://www.wsj.com/politics/policy/elon-musk-third-political-party-69bf9bd8?mod=article_inline) to start a new political party, according to people familiar with the matter, in part because he wanted to maintain a relationship with Vance, who is widely seen as a likely 2028 presidential candidate.

Vance believed that Musk was too important a figure to ignore, the people said. A permanent rupture in relations with the administration could have far-reaching consequences, given the significance of SpaceX contracts for defense and national security. Indeed, some at NASA panicked when Musk threatened to cut off [support for missions](https://www.wsj.com/livecoverage/trump-elon-musk-feud/card/spacex-to-end-dragon-spacecraft-that-nasa-needs-musk-says-l9VjE5X2bzL7fmDRyPdC?mod=article_inline) to the International Space Station during the peak of his feud with Trump.

An administration-led review of SpaceX’s contracts with the federal government that was initiated after the June spat with Trump found that most of those contracts were critical to the Defense Department and NASA, [The Wall Street Journal previously reported](https://www.wsj.com/business/trump-aides-discussed-ending-some-spacex-contracts-but-found-most-were-vital-d1cf9ab5?mod=article_inline). Since then, the company has only become [more embedded in America’s war machine](https://www.wsj.com/politics/national-security/how-spacex-became-embedded-in-americas-war-machine-ca4878e8?mod=article_inline).

Like Vance, the president’s oldest son, Donald Trump Jr., remained friendly with Musk, according to people familiar with the matter. Around the time of the breakup, his venture-capital firm, 1789 Capital, invested in Musk’s xAI, SpaceX and Neuralink. Katie Miller, the wife of Trump’s deputy chief of staff, Stephen Miller, is also close to Musk, having worked with him during his stint at the White House.

### **Personnel changes and tragedy**

Kirk, the young conservative activist with a large following, also worked to bring Trump and Musk together, according to people close to him, communicating with Musk as the feud exploded into public view. Kirk was shot and killed in September, and Trump and Musk attended a memorial for him at an Arizona football stadium. David Sacks, a technology adviser to Trump who is a Musk ally, suggested to Wiles that he try to get the two men together, according to people familiar with the events.

*ROSS D. FRANKLIN/AP*

Musk walked over to Trump’s stadium box and briefly spoke with the president, Trump patting Musk on the elbow as the entrepreneur left. Later, on Air Force One, Trump talked about the exchange, describing it as interesting and asking what others thought, according to a person familiar with the encounter.

That night, Musk [posted a photo](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/1969897602524328440?s=20) of him talking to the president at the memorial. “For Charlie,” he wrote.

Meanwhile, two personnel changes also helped ease tensions.

In the White House, Musk clashed repeatedly with Trump’s longtime aide Sergio Gor. During a meeting early in Trump’s second term, Trump said he was frustrated that Musk’s DOGE wasn’t moving fast enough on certain initiatives. Musk blamed Gor, who oversaw vetting of appointments in the administration, causing the president to berate Gor, according to a person familiar with the matter.

Last August, Trump tapped Gor to be the U.S. ambassador to India—sending him 7,500 miles away from Washington.

Trump also made an extraordinary U-turn in November, reappointing Musk ally Jared Isaacman [to lead NASA](https://www.wsj.com/politics/elections/trump-renominates-jared-isaacman-to-lead-nasa-55515142?mod=article_inline), a move that Musk appreciated, according to people familiar with the relationship. Competition for the job was intense, and Isaacman won out over Sean Duffy, the transportation secretary who had taken on the role after Trump ousted Isaacman in June amid the Musk breakup.

In November, in a sign of the detente, Musk returned to the White House for a dinner Trump hosted for Saudi Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman. In recent months, Trump has praised Musk publicly. After Musk became a trillionaire, the president sent him a congratulatory note.

For the midterms, Musk plans to donate to America PAC, the group he set up to fund pro-Trump efforts in 2024. He plans to invest at least $100 million in a get-out-the-vote operation in key states, according to a person familiar with the details, which were earlier reported by Axios and the New York Times.

Musk has signaled he won’t be as visible as he was in the last election and during his polarizing, often chaotic, role overseeing DOGE. “I think I got a little too involved in politics,” Musk told the Economist last month.
