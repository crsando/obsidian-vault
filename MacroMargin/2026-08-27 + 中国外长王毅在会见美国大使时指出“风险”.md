## 中国外长王毅在会见美国大使时指出“风险”

**要点**

- 中国外交部长王毅在与大使戴维·珀杜会晤时表示，中美关系“仍然面临各种风险挑战”。
- 王毅建议双方“聚焦积极议程，妥善管控分歧，消除干扰，克服高层交往中的障碍”。
- 美中两国正在为中国领导人习近平于9月24日前往白宫与美国总统唐纳德·特朗普会晤做准备。
中国外交部长王毅在与美国驻华大使戴维·珀杜会晤时暗示了对美关系中的复杂问题，突显出在领导人峰会举行不到一个月前，紧张局势依然存在。

虽然双方正在努力建立稳定关系，但王毅在周三的会晤中表示，“中美关系仍然面临各种风险挑战”，据中国外交部发布的《中国外交部：王毅会见美国驻华大使庞德伟》消息。他没有详细说明。

王毅补充说，双方应“聚焦积极议程，妥善管控分歧，消除干扰，克服高层交往中的障碍”。

珀杜在X平台上发帖称，本周他与包括王毅在内的官员举行了“富有成效”的会谈，为习近平主席于9月24日访问白宫与美国总统唐纳德·特朗普会晤做准备。

珀杜补充说，双方“正共同努力，以公平和互惠为基础，建立具有战略稳定性的建设性关系。”

北京和华盛顿似乎正试图在峰会前保持关系正常。最新的考验来自本周，美国因与伊朗的经济联系制裁了中国和香港的实体。

上个月，美国贸易代表贾米森·格里尔（Jamieson Greer）表示，他对习特会[期望不高](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-16/trump-xi-summit-to-take-stock-of-china-commitments-greer-says)，暗示会议将侧重于评估现有承诺，而非促成新协议。

## China’s Wang Yi Points to ‘Risks’ in Meeting With US Ambassador

**Takeaways**

- Chinese Foreign Minister Wang Yi said China-US relations "still face various risks and challenges" during a meeting with Ambassador David Perdue.
- Wang advised the two sides to "focus on positive agendas, properly manage differences, eliminate interference and overcome obstacles in high-level exchanges".
- The US and China are working to prepare for Chinese leader Xi Jinping's trip to the White House to meet President Donald Trump on Sept. 24.
Chinese Foreign Minister Wang Yi alluded to complications in relations with the US during a meeting with Ambassador David Perdue, underscoring lingering tensions less than a month before a leaders’ summit.

While the two sides were working to build stable ties, “China-US relations still face various risks and challenges,” Wang said in the sitdown on Wednesday, according to a 中国外交部: 王毅会见美国驻华大使庞德伟 from the Chinese Foreign Ministry. He didn’t go into specifics.

The two sides should “focus on positive agendas, properly manage differences, eliminate interference and overcome obstacles in high-level exchanges,” Wang added.

Perdue said in a post on X that he had “productive” meetings this week with officials, including Wang, to prepare for Chinese leader Xi Jinping’s trip to the White House to meet President Donald Trump on Sept. 24.

The sides were “working together to achieve a constructive relationship of strategic stability with fairness and reciprocity,” Perdue added.

Beijing and Washington appear to be trying to keep relations on track before the summit. The latest test came this week when the US sanctioned Chinese and Hong Kong entities over economic ties to Iran.

Beijing has responded by saying its relationship with Tehran “should not be disrupted or undermined,” and that it will take “all necessary measures” to safeguard its interests.

Last month, US Trade Representative Jamieson Greer signaled he had [modest expectations](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-16/trump-xi-summit-to-take-stock-of-china-commitments-greer-says) for the Xi-Trump meeting, suggesting it will focus on assessing current commitments rather than brokering new deals.
