### 中文译文

## 消息人士称，阿里巴巴计划向其下一代开源AI模型的大用户收费

- 摘要
- 公司
- 消息人士称，Moonshot 的 Kimi K3 许可寻求最高 30% 的收入分成
- 消息人士称，阿里巴巴也在寻求收入分成
- 根据输入和输出令牌，Kimi K3 的定价约为 Anthropic 的 Fable 模型的三分之一
8月7日（路透社） - 据两位熟悉公司计划的人士透露，中国科技巨头阿里巴巴[(9988.HK), 打开新标签页](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/9988.HK)计划要求其下一版Qwen开源AI模型的主要用户分享他们从[备受期待](https://www.reuters.com/business/retail-consumer/alibaba-unveils-its-most-capable-ai-model-date-not-far-behind-moonshots-size-2026-08-03/)的产品中获得的收入的一部分。

与上个月[中国AI初创公司Moonshot](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/chinas-moonshot-unveils-worlds-largest-open-ai-model-closing-us-rivals-2026-07-17/)发布的重磅模型类似，阿里巴巴的Qwen3.8-Max模型是一个开源、开放权重的模型，这意味着允许开发者运行或调整系统的底层学习设置可供下载。相比之下，美国开发者OpenAI、Anthropic和Alphabet的[(GOOGL.O), 打开新标签](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/GOOGL.O)谷歌拥有闭源模型。

开源常被认为是免费的，但在Moonshot的Kimi K3的[许可条款, 打开新标签](https://huggingface.co/moonshotai/Kimi-K3/blob/main/LICENSE)中夹带着一项条款，要求任何以服务形式出售该模型且年销售额超过2000万美元的人与Moonshot达成商业协议。

两位熟悉其战略的人士表示，阿里巴巴计划下周对其开源模型采取类似措施，这一战略此前未被报道。由于计划尚未公开，消息人士要求匿名。

迄今为止，阿里巴巴一直对开发者使用其托管在自家云计算平台上的模型收费，但允许大多数开源产品在客户自己的数据中心免费使用。

这些计划表明，中国AI公司——它们通过发布与OpenAI和Anthropic能力相当的开源模型令市场震惊——在努力从美国竞争对手手中夺取市场份额的同时，正在向一种商业模式靠拢。

### 收入分成协议

美国公司与中国 AI 实验室分享中国模型所产生收入的协议正在成形，尽管白宫已指控 Moonshot 从 Anthropic [窃取技术](https://www.reuters.com/world/us/us-accuses-chinas-moonshot-stealing-anthropics-fable-latest-ai-model-2026-06-30/)。中国官员否认该指控，称其毫无根据。

据这两位人士称，Kimi K3 的许可要求 Moonshot 的合作伙伴分享收入，其中一位人士表示，Moonshot 要求最高 30% 的收入分成。两位人士均表示，阿里巴巴也计划要求收入分成，但目标比例尚不明确，因为讨论仍在进行中。

Moonshot 未回应置评请求。

这张插图摄于2025年1月29日，展示了通义千问和阿里巴巴的标志。路透社/Dado Ruvic/插图/资料图 [购买许可权，打开新标签](https://www.reutersconnect.com/item/illustration-shows-qwen-and-alibaba-logos/dGFnOnJldXRlcnMuY29tLDIwMjU6bmV3c21sX1JDMkFLQ0FWSjRVOA%3D%3D/?utm_medium=rcom-article-media&utm_campaign=rcom-rcp-lead)

中软国际[(0354.HK)，打开新标签页](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/0354.HK)是一家中国IT服务提供商，上月在一份监管文件中披露了与月之暗面的收入分成协议，但未披露分成比例。

这些中国AI公司正在效仿硅谷的常见做法：初期以低价或免费提供软件，对重度商业使用和附加服务收费。

“要想优化部署，就要为与这些开放权重模型实验室的合作付费，还要为抢先获得模型下一版本的访问权付费，”云计算公司DigitalOcean Holdings [(DOCN.N)，打开新标签页](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/DOCN.N)的首席执行官帕迪·斯里尼瓦桑表示。DigitalOcean是提供Kimi K3和其他中国模型的几家美国公司之一。

他证实其公司与月之暗面签有商业协议，但拒绝讨论具体细节。

斯里尼瓦桑表示：“这是一种久经考验的开源‘免费增值’模式。”

根据两个模型列出的输入和输出令牌价格，Kimi K3 的价格约为 Anthropic 的 Fable 模型的三分之一。

Together AI 内核副总裁 Dan Fu 表示，像他这样提供 AI 软件的公司通过优化服务来赚钱，例如更好地利用令牌——AI 查询的构建模块。

Fu 表示：“在应用层面，如何利用它、如何让模型和令牌真正发挥作用，这其中蕴含着价值。”

与此同时，开源AI正在扩张，美国公司也加入了竞争。

Thinking Machines Lab是一家总部位于旧金山的AI初创公司，由OpenAI前首席技术官米拉·穆拉蒂（Mira Murati）于去年创立，上个月发布了其首个开源模型，并被普遍预期将推出更强大的模型。

“我没有看到强大的美国开源模型存在根本性障碍，”硅谷Fireworks AI的首席执行官兼联合创始人林乔（Lin Qiao）表示，他拒绝讨论该公司与Moonshot的商业安排。“我们真的在等待这一天的到来。”

由斯蒂芬·内利斯（Stephen Nellis）在旧金山和爱德华多·巴普蒂斯塔（Eduardo Baptista）在北京报道；由彼得·亨德森（Peter Henderson）和杰米·弗里德（Jamie Freed）编辑。

### English Original

## Alibaba plans to charge big users of its next open-source AI model, sources say

- Summary
- Companies
- Moonshot's Kimi K3 license seeks revenue share of up to 30%, source says
- Alibaba also seeking revenue share, sources say
- Kimi K3 is priced at roughly one-third of Anthropic's Fable model based on input and output tokens
Aug 7 (Reuters) - Chinese technology giant Alibaba [(9988.HK), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/9988.HK) plans to ask major users of the next version of its Qwen open-source AI model for a share of revenue they make from the [hotly anticipated](https://www.reuters.com/business/retail-consumer/alibaba-unveils-its-most-capable-ai-model-date-not-far-behind-moonshots-size-2026-08-03/) offering, according to two people familiar with the company's plans.

Like ‌last month's blockbuster model release from [Chinese AI startup Moonshot](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/chinas-moonshot-unveils-worlds-largest-open-ai-model-closing-us-rivals-2026-07-17/), Alibaba's Qwen3.8-Max model is an open-source, open-weight model, meaning that the underlying learned settings that allow developers to run or adapt the system are available for download. By contrast, U.S. developers OpenAI, Anthropic and Alphabet's [(GOOGL.O), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/GOOGL.O) Google have closed-source models.

Open source is often thought of as free, but tucked into the [licensing terms, opens new tab](https://huggingface.co/moonshotai/Kimi-K3/blob/main/LICENSE) of Moonshot's Kimi K3 was a provision that requires anyone offering the model for sale as a service and generating more than $20 million in annual sales to work out a commercial agreement with Moonshot.

Alibaba plans to implement a similar measure for its open-source model next week, said the two people familiar with its ⁠strategy, which has not been reported previously. The sources requested anonymity because the plans are not public.

To date, Alibaba has charged developers for use of its models when hosted on its own cloud computing platform but allowed most of its open-source offerings to be used in customers' own data centers without payment.

The plans show Chinese AI firms, which have shocked markets by releasing open-source models nearly as capable as those from OpenAI and Anthropic, are converging on a business model as they push to take market share from their U.S. rivals.

### REVENUE-SHARING DEALS

Deals under which U.S. firms share revenue generated by Chinese models with the Chinese AI labs that created them are taking shape, even as the White House has accused Moonshot of [stealing technology](https://www.reuters.com/world/us/us-accuses-chinas-moonshot-stealing-anthropics-fable-latest-ai-model-2026-06-30/) from Anthropic. Chinese officials have rejected that claim as unfounded.

The Kimi K3 license requires Moonshot's partners to share revenue, according to the two people, with Moonshot requiring up to a 30% revenue share, one of them said. Alibaba also plans to ask for a revenue share, both people said, but the rate remains unclear as the discussions are ongoing.

Moonshot ‌did not ⁠respond to a request for comment.

Chinasoft International [(0354.HK), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/0354.HK), a Chinese IT services provider, last month disclosed a revenue-sharing agreement with Moonshot in a regulatory filing, without disclosing the percentage.

The Chinese AI firms are following a common playbook from Silicon Valley: Offer software at low or zero cost to start, charging for heavy commercial use and additional services.

"You pay for collaboration with these open-weight model labs to make sure that you're optimizing your deployment. You pay for getting early access for the next revision of the model," said Paddy Srinivasan, CEO of cloud computing firm DigitalOcean Holdings [(DOCN.N), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/DOCN.N), one of several U.S. firms offering ⁠Kimi K3 and other Chinese models.

He confirmed his company has a commercial agreement with Moonshot, but declined to discuss specifics.

"This is a tried and tested open-source 'freemium' model," Srinivasan said.

Kimi K3 costs about a third of Anthropic's Fable model, according to listed prices of input and output tokens for both models.

Dan Fu, vice president of kernels at Together AI, said companies like his that offer AI software make their ⁠money by optimizing the service, such as better use of tokens, the building blocks of AI queries.

"At the application layer, there's value out there for how you use it, how you actually get the models and the tokens to do something useful," Fu said.

Open-source AI, meanwhile, is expanding, with U.S. firms joining the fray.

Thinking Machines Lab, a San Francisco-based AI startup ⁠founded last year by OpenAI’s former Chief Technology Officer Mira Murati, last month released its first open-source model, and is widely expected to follow with more powerful ones.

"I don't see a fundamental barrier" to powerful open-source U.S. models, said Lin Qiao, CEO and co-founder of Silicon Valley-based Fireworks AI, who declined to discuss the company's commercial arrangements with Moonshot. "We are really waiting for that to happen."
