### 中文译文

## 中国以创纪录低收益率发行长期离岸人民币国债

中国在香港发行长期人民币主权债券，收益率创历史新低，凸显全球投资者对离岸发行的强劲需求。

根据财政部周三的声明，每只10亿元（合1.48亿美元）的15年期和30年期债券的发行收益率分别为1.99%和2.24%。

“拍卖结果显示，投资者仍然愿意承担中国的久期风险，”法国农业信贷银行(Credit Agricole CIB)策略师Jeffrey Zhang表示。“低波动率可能也有帮助。”

![图片](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3b381cc90b1481218eaac54ef24c88e9/3b381cc9-0b14-81b5-a5af-f9568eef14b6-01fc63d4306e642263ed64e80a8a855c.png)

另外，根据财政部的声明，两年期中国债券的发行收益率为1.27%，三年期为1.30%，五年期为1.43%。彭博汇编的数据显示，总体而言，周三所有期限的认购倍数为4.67倍，为去年6月以来的最高水平。

强劲需求恰逢中国在岸债券近期上涨，基准10年期收益率周二跌至1.71%的两个月低点。与此同时，由于持续的通货膨胀担忧，[全球债券收益率](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-24/global-inflation-angst-is-back-with-100-oil-and-more-tariffs)大多在上升，美国国债类似期限的收益率7月底达到2025年1月以来的最高水平。

### English Original

## China Sells Long-Dated Offshore Yuan Bonds at Record Low Yields

China sold long-dated yuan-denominated sovereign bonds in Hong Kong at record-low yields, underscoring strong demand from global investors for the offshore issuance.

Auctions of 15-year and 30-year notes worth one billion yuan ($148 million) each were priced at 1.99% and 2.24%, respectively, according to a statement from the Ministry of Finance on Wednesday.

“The auction results indicate that investors remain comfortable taking duration risks in China,” said Jeffrey Zhang, a strategist at Credit Agricole CIB. “The low volatility also likely helped.”

Separately, two-year Chinese bonds were sold at a yield of 1.27%, three-year debt at 1.30% and five-year notes at 1.43%, according to the ministry’s statement. Overall, the bid-to-cover ratio for all the tenors on Wednesday was 4.67 times, the highest since June last year, data compiled by Bloomberg show.

The strong demand coincides with a recent advance in China’s onshore bonds, with the benchmark 10-year yield dropping to a two-month low of 1.71% on Tuesday. That’s when [global bond yields](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-24/global-inflation-angst-is-back-with-100-oil-and-more-tariffs) have largely been rising amid persistent inflation concerns, with those on similar-maturity US Treasuries reaching their highest levels since January 2025 at the end of July.
