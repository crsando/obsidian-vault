### 中文译文

## 中石化称中国石油需求“很可能”已于去年见顶

中国最大炼油商的负责人表示，中国的石油需求可能已于去年见顶，早于此前预期。

中石化董事长侯启军周一在香港的业绩简报会上表示，清洁能源发展、电气化和低碳目标意味着中国的石油需求可能已经见顶。该公司此前预测石油需求将在2027年[见顶](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2024-12-19/china-s-gasoline-demand-to-fall-quickly-from-2025-sinopec-says)，而政府的目标是在当前五年计划期间（到2030年）使石油和煤炭消费量达到上限。

侯启军表示：“明年，即使美伊冲突缓和，情况可能会恢复，但不会达到去年的水平。”他说：“因此，需求很可能已在去年见顶。”

中国是全球最大的石油进口国。更早开始减少消费将有助于抑制其全球领先的排放量，同时也会给全球最大的原油开采商带来疑问。

中石化（正式名称为中国石油化工股份有限公司）在周日发布的业绩报告中表示，由于消费者不愿接受高价并转向电动汽车，上半年道路燃料需求[暴跌](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-23/sinopec-first-half-profit-rises-12-after-surge-in-energy-prices)。该公司高级副总裁[田洪斌](http://www.sinopec.com/listco/en/000/000/070/70639.shtml)表示，在支持性经济政策下，下半年跌幅预计将略有收窄。

总裁[万涛](http://www.sinopec.com/listco/en/000/000/070/70640.shtml)在周一的简报会上表示，即使燃料需求下降，公司也确保国内供应需求得到满足。公司正在将原油来源从中东分散，同时与该地区的供应商合作，确保航运路线免受伊朗战争暴力的影响。他说，该炼油商已收到此前滞留在波斯湾的11艘油轮，共载有276万吨原油。

万涛表示，该公司通常保持约20天的原油储存用于炼油，15天的成品油储存用于销售。他说，战争期间库存水平保持稳定，中石化将继续遵循政府对其商业库存水平的指示。

## China’s Oil Demand ‘Very Likely’ Peaked Last Year, Sinopec Says

China’s oil demand probably peaked last year, earlier than previous estimates, according to the head of the nation’s top refiner.

Clean energy development, electrification and low-carbon goals mean that the country’s oil demand has probably already crested, Sinopec Chairman Hou Qijun said Monday at an earnings briefing in Hong Kong. The company had previously forecast usage to [top out](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2024-12-19/china-s-gasoline-demand-to-fall-quickly-from-2025-sinopec-says) in 2027, while the government is targeting oil and coal consumption to reach their limits during the current five-year plan period, which runs through 2030.

“Next year, even if the US-Iran conflict eases up, things might recover, but it won’t hit last year’s level,” Hou said. “So it’s very likely demand peaked last year.”

China is the world’s largest oil importer. An earlier start to reducing consumption would help rein in its world-leading emissions while raising questions for the world’s top crude drillers.

Sinopec, known officially as China Petroleum & Chemical Corp., said in its earnings report on Sunday that road fuel demand [plummeted](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-23/sinopec-first-half-profit-rises-12-after-surge-in-energy-prices) in the first half as consumers shied away from higher prices and shifted to electric vehicles. The declines are expected to narrow a bit in the second half because of supportive economic policies, said [Tian Hongbin](http://www.sinopec.com/listco/en/000/000/070/70639.shtml), a senior vice president at the company.

Even as fuel demand drops, the company is making sure domestic supply needs are met, President [Wan Tao](http://www.sinopec.com/listco/en/000/000/070/70640.shtml) said during Monday’s briefing. It’s diversifying crude sources away from the Middle East while working with its suppliers in the region on shipping routes safe from the violence of the Iran War. The refiner has received 11 oil tankers previously stuck in the Persion Gulf that were carrying a combined 2.76 million tons of crude, he said.

The company typically keeps about 20 days of crude storage for refining purposes, and 15 days of refined products for marketing, Wan said. Inventory levels have remained steady during the war, and Sinopec will continue to follow directions from the government on its commercial storage levels, he said.
