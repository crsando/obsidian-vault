## 中国AI上市热潮背后的银行

[**威廉·桑德兰**](https://www.ft.com/william-sandlund) 在香港，[**埃莉诺·奥尔科特**](https://www.ft.com/eleanor-olcott) 在北京

发布于2026年8月9日

中国顶尖投资银行已从持续多年的政府整治中脱颖而出，并挤开竞争对手，成为北京方面在人工智能领域与美国竞争的主要融资机构。

中国国际金融股份有限公司（CICC）已为国内多家科技领军企业的数十亿美元上市提供保荐，包括芯片制造商长鑫存储和数据中心零部件供应商中际旭创。

这家国有银行在中国内地和香港的首次公开发行（IPO）排行榜上名列前茅。今年迄今，其交易总额已达115亿美元，是2025年同期的两倍多，并有望创下年度纪录。

“十多年来，我们一直在投资并为这波人工智能浪潮做准备，”中金公司科技、媒体与电信投资银行部副主管娄新宇表示。

长期以来，中金公司一直是大型交易的首选银行，但也被卷入了政府针对金融业的整治行动，该行动在2021年中国房地产泡沫破裂时进一步升级。

![Column chart of Mainland China and Hong Kong equity IPO volume ($bn) showing CICC is on track for a record year for its IPO business](https://images.ft.com/v3/image/raw/ftcms%3Ae5e6f709-e53e-4b22-bd1f-38b39dfd9f5f?source=next-article&fit=scale-down&quality=highest&width=700&dpr=2)

多名高管被卷入调查，而许多银行家也遭遇大幅减薪，这是习近平主席旨在减少财富不平等的“共同富裕”运动的一部分。

由房地产崩盘引发的长期市场低迷，使中金公司的投行业务收入从2021年68亿元人民币的峰值，在2024年降至28亿元人民币（4.15亿美元）的最低点。

2024年底，政府公布了提振市场的刺激方案，市场情绪开始转变。次年，随着监管机构放宽此前为保持现有股票流动性而收紧的要求，一波中国企业开始上市。

在政府对上市进行打压之际，中金公司正在为科技股的回归奠定基础。

娄表示，该行将投行、私募股权和研究团队整合在一起的战略，在帮助其抓住人工智能机遇方面发挥了重要作用。

他将该行私募股权子公司中金资本（CICC Capital）在帮助其进入市场并“完成这些交易”方面所发挥的作用归功于它。

“那些科技公司确实信任中金品牌，因为我们拥有科技专业知识和对中国市场趋势的了解，”娄说道。“我们可以充分利用内部能力，提供投行解决方案，并为那些科技公司提供一揽子服务。”

当前这波中国科技公司上市潮，正值美国人工智能驱动的资本市场繁荣和两国间日益加剧的科技竞争之际。娄预计中国上市热潮还将持续一年。

“我们仍然认为，未来12个月存在非常明显且巨大的市场机会，而且它们受到流动性制约的影响较小，”他说道。他指的是对美国大型IPO将从香港抽走资金的担忧。

“对于人工智能和硬科技公司，只要你能证明你是中国市场上的稀缺资产，并具有长期投资价值，你仍然会受到市场的欢迎。”

中金公司成立于1995年，是中国建设银行与摩根士丹利的合资企业。2010年美国投行退出以及经过数轮股东重组后，其控股股东现在是中国的主权财富基金中央汇金。

在习近平的领导下，北京一直寻求将金融业从盈利机器重塑为国家战略目标的工具。在2024年一次呼吁中国成为“金融强国”的演讲中，这位国家主席阐述了金融业发展经济、避免“盲目”追求扩张的愿景，他表示这种扩张会导致“危机”。

“高层的指导性观点是金融应服务于实体经济，并帮助增强中国的经济实力和竞争力，”香港大学金融学教授汤勇军表示。“金融行业和金融从业者都将受到更严格的审查和监管。”

中金公司的投资银行收入去年实现部分复苏，达到49亿元人民币（合7.26亿美元），但随着其他业务（尤其是财富管理）的增长，其收入占比有所下降。集团净利润增长72%，达到98亿元人民币。

中国金融业竞争加剧，进一步压缩了投资银行的利润率。近年来，随着中国金融业摆脱本世纪初放任自流的资本主义过度行为，北京方面一直[推动行业整合](https://www.ft.com/content/fd3f5440-69a1-4e2f-8ee2-19c8f8802d89?syn-25a6b1a6=1)。

“市场竞争更加激烈，像长鑫存储这样的发行方拥有很大的议价能力，从而降低了银行家和金融家的费用，”汤勇军说。

尽管中金公司是长鑫存储上市（中国16年来最大IPO）的联席保荐人，但承销和保荐费用仅为2.266亿元人民币（合3360万美元），占交易额的0.39%，将由五家银行分摊。该费用不包括尚未行使的超额配售权。

这一数字远低于中国农业银行2010年在上海和香港两地上市时约2.67亿美元的费用，中金公司在那次上市中也发挥了关键作用。

尽管中金公司在将科技宠儿推向公开市场方面取得了成功，但投资者似乎不太愿意接受中国银行业的新常态。该行香港股价较峰值下跌接近12%，上海股价则下跌超过一半。

在上月提交给香港交易所的文件中，该行表示将报告上半年利润的大幅增长。其表态呼应了北京方面将金融部门重塑为国家工具的行动。

中金公司在公告中表示，其“紧密遵循推动资本市场改革的政策指示”。

“公司全力服务实体经济和国家科技驱动战略，”它补充道。

## The bank behind China’s AI listings bonanza

[**William Sandlund**](https://www.ft.com/william-sandlund) in Hong Kong and [**Eleanor Olcott**](https://www.ft.com/eleanor-olcott) in Beijing

PublishedAug 9 2026

China’s top investment bank has emerged from a years-long government crackdown and muscled aside rivals to become the dominant financier of Beijing’s effort to compete with the US in AI.

China International Capital Corporation has sponsored the multibillion-dollar listings of several of the country’s tech champions, including [chipmaker CXMT](https://www.ft.com/content/8e82e939-908b-42bf-a314-0bb02a3f1b07) and data centre parts supplier Zhongji Innolight.

The state-owned bank is topping league tables for initial public offerings in mainland China and Hong Kong. It has netted $11.5bn in deal volume this year, more than double the same period in 2025, and is on track for a record annual haul.

“We have been investing in and preparing for this AI wave for more than a decade,” said Lou Xinyu, deputy head of CICC’s technology, media and telecom investment banking department.

[CICC](https://www.ft.com/stream/7417037d-6366-4b3a-b201-ea4efd7ec45a) had long been the go-to bank for big deals, but it was caught up in a government campaign against the financial sector that intensified when the Chinese property bubble burst in 2021.

Senior executives were ensnared in investigations, while many bankers experienced [severe pay cuts](https://www.ft.com/content/ed6e9866-ffd7-4a14-bdb0-ccce0a3b81bf) as part of President Xi Jinping’s “common prosperity” campaign aimed at reducing wealth inequality.

A prolonged market downturn, precipitated by the property crash, sent CICC’s investment banking revenues to as low as Rmb2.8bn ($415mn) in 2024, from a peak of Rmb6.8bn in 2021.

Sentiment began to turn when the government unveiled a stimulus package in late 2024 to boost markets. The following year, a wave of Chinese companies began listing as regulators relaxed requirements that had been tightened previously to preserve liquidity in existing stocks.

While the government was putting a damper on listings, CICC was laying the groundwork for a tech comeback.

Lou said the bank’s strategy of bringing together its investment banking, private equity and research teams had been instrumental in helping it capture opportunities with AI.

He credited the bank’s private equity arm, CICC Capital, which has actively invested across the AI sector, for helping it tap into the market and “get those deals done”.

“Those tech companies do trust the CICC brand because we have the tech expertise and the knowhow about the trends in China’s market,” said Lou. “We can fully leverage our in-house capabilities to provide investment banking solutions and offer a whole package for those tech companies.”

The current wave of Chinese tech listings is happening alongside an AI-driven capital markets boom in the US and intensifying technological competition between the two countries. Lou forecast the Chinese listings surge would continue for another year.

“We still think there are very obvious and enormous market opportunities over the next 12 months, and they are less affected by liquidity constraints,” he said, referring to concerns that mega IPOs in the US would drain capital from Hong Kong.

“For AI and hard-tech companies, as long as you can show that you are a scarce asset in China’s market and have long-term investment value, you will still be welcomed by the market.”

CICC was established in 1995 as a joint venture between China Construction Bank and Morgan Stanley. After the US investment bank’s exit in 2010 and several rounds of shareholder restructuring, its controlling shareholder is now Central Huijin, China’s sovereign wealth fund.

Under Xi, Beijing has looked to reshape the financial sector from a profit-making machine into a tool for the nation’s strategic goals. In a 2024 speech calling for China to become a “financial powerhouse”, the president laid out a vision for the sector to develop the economy and avoid “blindly” pursuing expansion, which he said would lead to “crises”.

“The guiding view from the top is that finance should serve the real economy and help strengthen China’s economy and competitiveness,” said Dragon Tang, a finance professor at the University of Hong Kong. “Both the finance industry and finance practitioners will come under more scrutiny and regulation.”

CICC’s investment banking revenues staged a partial recovery last year, hitting Rmb4.9bn ($726mn), but its share of earnings has declined as other parts of the business, particularly wealth management, have grown. Group net profit rose 72 per cent to Rmb9.8bn.

Rising competition in the Chinese financial sector has further squeezed investment banking margins. In recent years, Beijing has [pushed for industry consolidation](https://www.ft.com/content/fd3f5440-69a1-4e2f-8ee2-19c8f8802d89?syn-25a6b1a6=1) as the Chinese finance sector moves away from the freewheeling capitalist excesses of the 2000s.

“The market has become more competitive and issuers like CXMT have much bargaining power, reducing fees for bankers and financiers,” said Tang.

While CICC was a joint sponsor on the CXMT listing — China’s largest IPO in 16 years — the underwriting and sponsorship fee was just Rmb226.6mn ($33.6mn), or 0.39 per cent of the deal, to be shared with five other banks. The fee does not include an overallotment option that has yet to be exercised.

This figure is far smaller than the roughly $267mn in fees for the 2010 IPO of the Agricultural Bank of China when it listed in both Shanghai and Hong Kong, in which CICC also played a key role.

Despite CICC’s success in bringing tech darlings to public markets, investors appear less ready to embrace the new norm in China’s banking sector. The bank’s Hong Kong shares are almost 12 per cent below their peak, while those in Shanghai are down more than half.

In a filing to Hong Kong’s stock exchange last month, the bank said it would report a substantial increase in first-half profits. Its messaging echoed Beijing’s campaign to rework the financial sector into an instrument of the state.

In the announcement, CICC said it “closely followed the policy directives for advancing the capital market reform”.

“The company exerted full efforts to serve the real economy and the national tech-driven strategy,” it added.
