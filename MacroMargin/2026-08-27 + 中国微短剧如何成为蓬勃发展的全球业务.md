### Big Take Asia：两分钟微短剧的崛起（播客）

微短剧——专为手机观看而设计的迷你肥皂剧——已成为世界上增长最快的娱乐形式之一。

在今天的Big Take Asia播客中，主持人K. Oanh Ha与彭博社的Yang Yang和《彭博商业周刊》的Christopher Beam谈论了数百万人如何迷上这种媒介、制片厂在试图接触新观众时面临的挑战，以及人工智能如何改变这一切。

### Big Take Asia: The Rise of the Two-Minute Microdrama (Podcast)

Microdramas — mini soap operas designed to be watched on your phone — have become one of the fastest-growing forms of entertainment in the world.

On today’s Big Take Asia podcast, host K. Oanh Ha speaks with Bloomberg’s Yang Yang and Bloomberg Businessweek’s Christopher Beam about how millions got hooked on the medium, the challenges studios face as they try to reach new audiences and how AI could transform it all.
