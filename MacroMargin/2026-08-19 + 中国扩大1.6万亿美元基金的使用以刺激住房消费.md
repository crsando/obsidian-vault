### 中文译文

## 中国扩大1.6万亿美元基金的使用以刺激住房消费

中国正在调动一笔经常被忽视的、价值10.9万亿元人民币（1.6万亿美元）的资金池，以促进广泛的住房相关消费，这是该国在7月经济放缓加深后首次出台的政策回应。

根据将于下月生效的《中国扩大公积金使用范围以满足房地产市场需求》，居民将能够提取他们在[住房公积金](https://fdichina.com/blog/housing-fund-in-china/)（一项此前用于帮助人们购房的政府计划）中的储蓄，用于包括装修在内的大额支出。监管机构还将减少居民使用公积金储蓄支付租金的条件。

中国还将首次允许公积金管理中心购买政策性银行债券，此举旨在提高其回报率。

在这些修订出台之前，中国经济在7月表现出全面疲软，消费软化[超过预期](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-17/china-s-economy-extends-slowdown-by-weakening-across-the-board)，[房价](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-17/china-new-home-prices-fall-at-faster-pace-damping-rebound-hopes)持续下跌。许多经济学家估计，经济增长进一步低于政府年度目标，引发了国务院总理李强关于加强[支持措施](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-18/xi-s-no-2-urges-more-support-for-economy-after-big-growth-slump)的呼吁。

中国的公积金制度大约三十年前从新加坡引入，要求雇员和雇主每月向一个资金池缴款，该资金池随后可发放抵押贷款，利率通常低于银行。根据最新官方数据，全国近1.8亿用人单位和职工缴存，截至2024年，该基金规模为10.9万亿元，超过未偿还的抵押贷款总额。

最新修订还允许国务院单独决定住房公积金发放的抵押贷款利率。此前，央行被指定提出此类贷款的降息建议，但须经国务院批准。

此举为该基金的“更灵活利率调整”铺平了道路，并对住房市场构成“温和利好”，[中国指数控股](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CIH%20US%20Equity)分析师周二晚些时候在一份报告中表示。

![图片](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3c181cc90b14810ca844d84cf8b9ca1a/3c181cc9-0b14-81f0-85dd-ed55186c0c26-53f57f8d8348a07b3514892987514fe5.png)

基于公积金的住房贷款利率已经比基准银行抵押贷款利率低0.9个百分点。下调公积金利率将有助于购房者减轻整体抵押贷款负担，因为他们被允许提取公积金储蓄来偿还银行抵押贷款。

自去年以来，随着银行因[利润挑战](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-27/china-s-big-banks-post-flat-2025-profit-on-margin-squeeze)而更加谨慎，公积金已成为购房者获取融资日益重要的途径。据[中国指数研究院](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CIH%20US%20Equity)称，今年已有80多个地方政府提高了由公积金支持的借款额度。

中国债券市场情绪因将公积金投资范围扩大至政策性银行债券而进一步改善。周二，[国家开发银行](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/SDBZ%20CH%20Equity)债券的10年期收益率创下两个月来最大跌幅。

### English Original

## China Widens Use of $1.6 Trillion Fund to Spur Spending on Homes

China is mobilizing an often overlooked pool of funds worth 10.9 trillion yuan ($1.6 trillion) to boost a broad range of housing-related consumption, the country’s first policy response after its economic slowdown deepened in July.

Residents will be able to withdraw their savings in the [housing provident fund](https://fdichina.com/blog/housing-fund-in-china/), a government program previously used to help people buy homes, for big-ticket spending including renovations, according to a China Widens Provident Fund Use to Meet Property Market Needs taking effect next month. Regulators will also reduce strings attached for residents using savings from the fund on rent payments.

China will also allow the provident fund’s management center to purchase policy bank bonds for the first time, in a move to increase its returns.

The revisions came after China’s economy showed across-the-board weakness in July, with consumption softening [more than expected](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-17/china-s-economy-extends-slowdown-by-weakening-across-the-board) and [home prices](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-17/china-new-home-prices-fall-at-faster-pace-damping-rebound-hopes) continuing to fall. Many economists estimate that growth slipped further below the government’s annual target, sparking a call from Premier Li Qiang to ramp up [supportive measures](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-18/xi-s-no-2-urges-more-support-for-economy-after-big-growth-slump).

China’s provident fund system, which it adopted from Singapore about three decades ago, requires employees and employers to contribute monthly into a pool that can then give out mortgages, often at a lower interest rate than banks. With contributions from almost 180 million employers and workers across the country, the fund had 10.9 trillion yuan as of 2024, more than the outstanding amount of mortgage loans, according to the latest official data.

The latest revision also allows the State Council, or China’s cabinet, to solely decide lending rates on mortgage loans given out by the housing provident fund. Previously, the central bank was designated to propose any rate cuts for such loans subject to approval by the State Council.

The move “paves the way for more flexible interest-rate adjustments” on the fund, and is “mildly positive” for the housing market, [China Index Holdings](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CIH%20US%20Equity) analysts said in a note late Tuesday.

Rates for home loans based on the provident fund are already 0.9 percentage point cheaper than benchmark bank mortgage rates. A rate cut on the fund would help homebuyers reduce the overall mortgage burden, as they are allowed to withdraw savings from the fund to repay bank mortgages.

The provident fund has become an increasingly important means for homebuyers to obtain financing since last year, as banks turn more cautious with [profit challenges](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-27/china-s-big-banks-post-flat-2025-profit-on-margin-squeeze). More than 80 local governments this year raised borrowing quotas backed by the fund, according to [China Index Holdings](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CIH%20US%20Equity).

China’s bond market sentiment improved further on the move to expand the investment scope of the provident fund to include policy bank bonds. Ten-year yields on [China Development Bank](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/SDBZ%20CH%20Equity) bonds fell the most in two months on Tuesday.
