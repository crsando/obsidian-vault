### 中文译文

## 印度力争到2029年结束关键电力系统对外国技术的依赖

印度计划为包括国家电网在内的关键电力基础设施开发本土控制系统和设备，以结束对外国技术的依赖并加强能源安全。

中央电力管理局已制定路线图，以全面实现电网实时监控和控制系统生产的本地化。

在周二发布的报告中，该电力部下属机构[概述了一条路径](https://cea.nic.in/wp-content/uploads/notification/2026/08/Report_of_Committee_for_trajectory_of_Indeginsation_of_SCADA_system.pdf)，即到2029年构建国产的监控与数据采集系统（SCADA），包括其硬件和软件。该系统收集关键数据，使印度能够实时了解电力资产状况。

这些努力是新德里更广泛的自力更生战略的一部分，旨在标准化设备、降低成本并使升级更容易。近期地缘政治动荡加剧了这一倡议的紧迫性，该倡议始于2020年，当时疫情暴露了全球供应链的脆弱性。

中央电力管理局在提交给电力部的提案中表示，建立国内能力对于减少电力系统受到供应链中断、技术限制和风险的影响至关重要。“在日益复杂的地缘政治形势下，这种依赖对国家能源安全构成潜在风险。”

电力部此前要求SCADA系统的本地化含量至少达到一半，但据该报告，国内软件和硬件的实际贡献仅为20%左右。

除了建设制造能力外，提案还包括设立测试和认证机构，并为SCADA系统开发商提供财务支持。

### English Original

## India Aims to End Foreign Reliance in Key Power Systems by 2029

India plans to develop homegrown control systems and equipment for critical power infrastructure, including the national grid, to end reliance on foreign technology and bolster energy security.

The Central Electricity Authority has laid out a road map to fully localize the production of the systems that enable real-time monitoring and control of the electricity network.

In its report published Tuesday, the power ministry body [outlined a pathway](https://cea.nic.in/wp-content/uploads/notification/2026/08/Report_of_Committee_for_trajectory_of_Indeginsation_of_SCADA_system.pdf) to build a homegrown Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition system, or SCADA, including its hardware and software, by 2029. The system collects critical data and gives India real-time visibility over power assets.

The efforts are part of New Delhi’s broader self-reliance push aimed at standardizing equipment, lowering costs and making upgrades easier. Recent geopolitical turmoil has added urgency to the initiative that began in 2020, when the pandemic exposed vulnerabilities in the global supply chain.

Building domestic capabilities is essential to reduce the power system’s exposure to supply chain disruptions, technology constraints and risks, CEA said in its proposal to the ministry of power. “In an increasingly complex geo-political landscape, such dependence poses potential risks to national energy security,” it said.

The power ministry had earlier mandated that SCADA systems have at least a half of locally made content, although the actual contribution of domestic software and hardware is just around 20%, according to the report.

Besides building manufacturing capacity, the proposals include setting up testing and certification agencies and financial support for SCADA system developers.
