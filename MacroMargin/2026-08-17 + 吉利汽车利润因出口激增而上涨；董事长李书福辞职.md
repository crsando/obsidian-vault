## 吉利汽车利润因出口激增而上涨；董事长李书福辞职

[吉利汽车控股有限公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/175%20HK%20Equity)公布第二季度利润激增，强劲的出口帮助这家汽车制造商抵御了中国市场的需求低迷，并宣布了高管阵容的调整，包括李书福辞去董事长职务。

公司周一表示，李书福已被任命为终身名誉董事长，以便将更多时间投入其他业务，并有助于继任规划。他将继续担任母公司浙江吉利控股集团的董事长，表明他将继续监督其庞大的汽车帝国。

该公告发布前，财报显示截至6月30日的三个月净利润增长36%至49亿元人民币（7.27亿美元），符合分析师预期。上半年净利润下降1.8%至90.9亿元人民币，但高于预期。

吉利汽车股价在香港收盘上涨4.8%。

随着国内增长放缓，吉利正寻求扩大海外版图。该公司表示已将2026年出口目标提高23%至92万辆。

这建立在今年强劲开局的基础上，根据早前公司声明，吉利上半年出口量增长逾一倍，达到474,228辆。中国汽车销量上半年下降20%，乘联会预测2026年全年将收缩14%。

年初对[比亚迪股份有限公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/1211%20HK%20Equity)的短暂领先优势已经削弱，这家全球最大的电动汽车制造商受益于美伊战争导致油价上涨所驱动的需求。今年上半年海外销量超过812,700辆，几乎是吉利出口量的两倍。

![图片](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3bf81cc90b1481a09997e404d00c0783/3bf81cc9-0b14-8196-ad94-e9d6a14122f1-ac866a8932b045d498ad78c3e7b9ce77.png)

尽管吉利汽车也销售纯电动汽车和混合动力汽车，但在海外建立分销网络方面一直慢于比亚迪(BYD)，目前才刚刚赶上。

不过，吉利通过旗下极氪(Zeekr)品牌推出的更高端产品也开始带来回报。9X豪华运动型多用途车(SUV)成为中国市场50万元以上最畅销车型，并将吉利的单车平均售价提高了1.5万元，达到11.2万元。

该公司于7月达成一项[协议](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-22/ford-and-geely-agree-to-share-underused-spanish-auto-factory)，共享产能，并在西班牙的[福特汽车公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/F%20US%20Equity)未充分利用的瓦伦西亚工厂生产汽车，且预计将通过与[雷诺公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/RNO%20FP%20Equity)在巴西的合资企业开始[本地生产](https://imprensa.renault.com.br/release/item/renault-geely-do-brasil-announces-production-of-the-geely-ex2-at-the-ayrton-senna-complex)其EX5紧凑型跨界车和EX2小型两厢车——后者在中国被称为[星愿](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2024-09-03/china-s-geely-unveils-compact-ev-to-challenge-byd-s-seagull)车型。

## Geely Auto Profit Jumps on Export Boom; Chairman Li Resigns

[Geely Automobile Holdings Ltd.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/175%20HK%20Equity) reported a surge in second-quarter profit as robust exports helped the carmaker weather sluggish demand in China, and announced a revamp of its executive lineup including the resignation of Li Shufu as chairman.

Li has been appointed honorary chairman for life, allowing him to devote more time to his other businesses and helping with succession planning, the company said Monday. He will continue to serve as chairman of parent Zhejiang Geely Holding Group, indicating he’ll maintain oversight over his sprawling auto empire.

The announcement followed an earnings report that showed net income rose 36% to 4.9 billion yuan ($727 million) in the three months ended June 30, meeting analyst expectations. Half-year profit fell 1.8% to 9.09 billion yuan, though topped estimates.

Geely Auto shares closed 4.8% higher in Hong Kong.

A Geely Galaxy Light 2nd Generation Concept at the Beijing auto show in April, 2026. Photographer: Qilai Shen/Bloomberg

With domestic momentum slowing, Geely is looking to expand its overseas footprint. The carmaker said it’s raised its 2026 export target 23% to 920,000 units.

That’s building on a strong start to the year, with Geely’s exports more than doubling in the first half to 474,228 vehicles, according to an earlier company statement. Chinese car sales in the first six months fell 20%, with the Passenger Car Association forecasting a 14% contraction for all of 2026.

A brief lead over [BYD Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/1211%20HK%20Equity) at the start of the year has eroded, as the world’s largest EV maker benefited from demand driven by higher oil prices due to the US-Iran war. Overseas sales reached more than 812,700 cars in the first six months of this year, nearly twice the volume of Geely’s exports.

While Geely Auto also sells fully electric vehicles and hybrids, it has been slower than BYD in setting up a distribution network overseas and is only now catching up.

Still, Geely’s more premium offerings through its Zeekr sub-brand are also paying off. The 9X luxury sport utility vehicle became China’s best-selling vehicle above 500,000 yuan and lifted Geely’s average selling price per car by 15,000 yuan to 112,000 yuan.

The company reached an [agreement](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-22/ford-and-geely-agree-to-share-underused-spanish-auto-factory) in July to share capacity and produce vehicles at [Ford Motor Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/F%20US%20Equity)’s underutilized Valencia plant in Spain, and is due to start [local production](https://imprensa.renault.com.br/release/item/renault-geely-do-brasil-announces-production-of-the-geely-ex2-at-the-ayrton-senna-complex) of its EX5 compact crossover and EX2 subcompact hatchback — which is known as the [Xingyuan](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2024-09-03/china-s-geely-unveils-compact-ev-to-challenge-byd-s-seagull) model in China — through a joint venture with [Renault SA](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/RNO%20FP%20Equity) in Brazil.
