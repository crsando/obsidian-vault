## 中国新房价格加速下跌，削弱复苏希望

上海住宅开发项目。*摄影师：Qilai Shen/彭博社*

作者：彭博新闻社 2026年8月17日 下午3:09 GMT+8

### **彭博AI要点**

- 中国新房价格7月加快下跌，较6月下降0.18%。
- 二手房价格环比下跌0.29%，较上月0.32%的跌幅略有改善。
- 房地产市场复苏迹象基本局限于大城市和部分市场领域，如地段好的旧公寓。
中国新房价格7月加速下跌，削弱了市场对于长期房地产低迷或接近底部的乐观情绪。

中国国家统计局周一公布的数据显示，70个大中城市的新建商品住宅价格（不包括保障性住房）环比下跌0.18%，而6月份为环比下跌[0.15%](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-15/china-new-home-price-slump-eases-in-sign-market-is-stabilizing)。二手房价格受政府干预较少，环比下跌0.29%，较上月0.32%的跌幅略有改善。

中国的房地产下行已拖累经济五年，阻碍了官员刺激消费者支出并减少对[出口](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-14/china-s-exports-imports-soar-faster-than-forecast-amid-ai-rush)依赖的努力。周一发布的其他[数据](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-17/china-s-economy-extends-slowdown-by-weakening-across-the-board)显示，零售销售增长低于经济学家的预期，而工业生产增速放缓。

房地产市场复苏的迹象大多局限于大城市和市场的某些细分领域，例如位于黄金地段的[老式公寓](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-08/china-demand-for-soviet-style-apartments-shows-limits-to-revival)。

尽管包括花旗集团和美国银行在内的公司分析师已经开始[断言](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-07/wall-street-dares-to-ask-if-china-s-property-turnaround-is-close)该行业终于趋稳，但其他人仍持怀疑态度。惠誉评级在6月底表示，由于复苏不均，中国房地产行业仍然疲弱；其对新房销售的预测是，低线城市的持续疲软将继续超过少数较强市场的复苏。

中国高层官员在上个月的共产党重要会议上[未](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-30/china-s-top-officials-to-consider-new-stimulus-after-slowdown)宣布新的刺激措施，尽管上季度经济增长意外放缓至北京4.5%至5%的目标区间以下。

花旗集团的格里芬·陈（Griffin Chan）写道：“政治局声明缓和了房地产政策的语气，暗示大多数城市没有迫切需要实施大规模需求侧房地产宽松政策。”

尽管如此，地方政府已推出温和措施以提振市场情绪。本月早些时候，北京[进一步放宽](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-07/beijing-follows-shanghai-in-easing-homebuying-restrictions)了非本地居民的购房规定，首都加入上海的行列，取消了长达十年的限制。此前大约八个月，北京已经采取了一项渐进式措施，放宽了对非本地居民购房者的规定。

## China New Home Prices Fall at Faster Pace, Damping Rebound Hopes

Residential housing developments in Shanghai.*Photographer: Qilai Shen/Bloomberg*

By Bloomberg NewsAugust 17, 2026 at 3:09 PM GMT+8

### **Takeaways ** by Bloomberg AI

- China’s new-home prices fell at a faster pace in July, dropping 0.18% from June.
- Resale home values decreased 0.29%, a slight improvement from the previous month’s 0.32% drop.
- Signs of a recovery in the real estate market have largely been confined to big cities and pockets of the market such as old apartments in good locations.
China’s new-home prices fell at a faster pace in July, denting optimism that the prolonged property downturn may be nearing a bottom.

New-home prices in 70 cities, excluding state-subsidized housing, dropped 0.18% from June when they slid [0.15%](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-15/china-new-home-price-slump-eases-in-sign-market-is-stabilizing), figures from the National Bureau of Statistics showed on Monday. Resale home values, which are subject to less government intervention, decreased 0.29%, a slight improvement from the previous month’s 0.32% drop.

China’s property downturn has weighed on the economy for five years, hampering efforts by officials to spur consumer spending and reduce reliance on [exports](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-14/china-s-exports-imports-soar-faster-than-forecast-amid-ai-rush). Other [figures](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-17/china-s-economy-extends-slowdown-by-weakening-across-the-board) released Monday showed retail sales rose less than economists anticipated, while industrial production expanded at a slower rate.

Signs of a recovery in the real estate market have largely been confined to big cities and pockets of the market such as [old apartments](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-08/china-demand-for-soviet-style-apartments-shows-limits-to-revival) in good locations.

While analysts at firms including Citigroup Inc. and Bank of America Corp. have begun to [assert ](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-07/wall-street-dares-to-ask-if-china-s-property-turnaround-is-close)that the sector is finally stabilizing, others are doubtful. Fitch Ratings in late June Fitch Ratings: China’s Property Sector Remains Weak on Uneven Recovery its forecast for new home sales, saying persistent weakness in lower-tier cities continues to outweigh a recovery in a small number of stronger markets.

China’s top officials [stopped short ](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-30/china-s-top-officials-to-consider-new-stimulus-after-slowdown)of announcing fresh stimulus in a key Communist Party meeting last month, even after economic growth unexpectedly weakened last quarter to below Beijing’s target range of 4.5% to 5%.

“The Politburo statement softened tone on real estate policy, implying no urgency for major demand-side property easing in most cities,” Citigroup’s Griffin Chan wrote.

Still, local governments have rolled out marginal steps to shore up sentiment. Earlier this month, Beijing [further eased](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-07/beijing-follows-shanghai-in-easing-homebuying-restrictions) homebuying rules for non-residents, as the capital city joined Shanghai in lifting decade-long restrictions. It followed another incremental step from about eight months ago, when the city relaxed rules for non-resident homebuyers.
