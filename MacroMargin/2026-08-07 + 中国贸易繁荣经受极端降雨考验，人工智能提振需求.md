### 中文译文

## 中国贸易繁荣经受极端降雨考验，人工智能提振需求

中国的出口和进口在7月份成功抵御了[极端天气](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-30/hong-kong-set-for-wettest-july-since-1997-with-more-rain-to-come)造成的干扰，因为人工智能领域的全球投资超级周期推动海外和国内对科技的需求。

根据海关总署周五发布的数据，出口增长超过预期，同比增长23.9%，连续第二个月增幅超过20%。进口增长27.5%，贸易顺差达到1125亿美元，有望超过去年的创纪录水平。

巴克莱银行分析师周英科（Yingke Zhou）及其团队在报告中表示：“尽管主要港口受到台风干扰，出口表现依然强劲。与人工智能相关的以及绿色科技出口继续受益于全球AI投资周期和能源转型，但劳动密集型产品出口疲软继续拖累就业市场和消费。”

在吸收了唐纳德·特朗普关税和中东战争的冲击后，这个全球最大的制造业国家正在经历一段强降雨期。[台风“巴威”](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-13/china-to-see-unusually-active-typhoon-season-after-bavi-passes)成为近八十年来袭击经济强省浙江的[最强](https://www.xinhuanet.com/local/20260712/bd021c3c0e204101a1521f3e9d1d710c/c.html)风暴，并导致该国一些最繁忙的港口暂时停止运营（中国宁波一码头将暂停集装箱、商品车进提作业）。

![图片](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3b581cc90b14814d8612eaf5ddda28db/3b581cc9-0b14-8196-8d3c-d262f6ecdcc3-b22f363bb5cecff1ad1f0ef6f2661ac6.png)

人工智能相关电子产品以及电动汽车等其他尖端产品的海外出货量飙升，缓解了今年经济更深度放缓的压力。

与此同时，贸易繁荣正在加剧经济内部持续存在的分化，并降低中国官员在国内需求疲软情况下加大对国内消费者支持力度的必要性。

“出口仍是主要增长引擎，”香港荷兰国际集团大中华区首席经济学家宋琳（Lynn Song）表示。“中国今年的K型分化在未来几个月料将持续，因为外部需求明显强于国内需求。”

*“中国贸易数据继续显示新旧经济之间的分化日益扩大。7月份强劲的进出口增长展示了科技相关制造业的实力，而传统产品出口则远远落后于整体增速。我们预计这种格局将持续，科技相关行业将保持强劲并支撑整体出口。”——曲大卫（David Qu），中国经济学家。点击《中国实时：科技行业主导贸易，失衡仍将持续》阅读完整报告*

中国企业在全球价值链中日益增强的主导地位，也导致与欧洲和美国的关系日趋紧张，尤其是在汽车制造以及最近的数据中心硬件等领域。中国不断扩大的贸易顺差也引发了经济学家之间关于人民币在保护中国制造业优势方面所起作用的争论。

自今年年初以来，中国官员一直承诺实现“平衡贸易”，并增加从国外的采购。

今年进口确实大幅增长，但价格因素发挥了更重要的作用。一个迹象表明，即使进口激增也难以赶上强劲的出口：中国7月份年初至今的贸易顺差自2月以来首次超过上年同期水平。

芯片和石油、金属等大宗商品价格飙升推高了中国贸易数据，出口和进口的实际数量增长较为温和。随着数万亿美元资金涌入人工智能领域，半导体和其他电子产品的短缺导致部分芯片价格在过去一年内飙升了700%。

根据最新官方数据，6月份出口价格同比上涨8%，在经历了近三年的下跌后连续第三个月上涨。6月份进口价格飙升25%，创下自2006年有数据以来的最大涨幅。按数量计算，6月份进口仅增长4%。

![图片](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3b581cc90b14814d8612eaf5ddda28db/3b581cc9-0b14-81bb-9b51-cda42c5ea5aa-302d39046003b43522098b77bf1fc0ab.png)

极端天气可能导致了上个月贸易略有放缓。在出口旺季期间，包括巴威在内的多个连续台风造成港口拥堵。一项衡量出口集装箱航运成本的指标飙升至2024年9月以来的最高水平。

“退一步看，出口仍然非常强劲，上月的小幅恶化部分归因于近期台风对港口运营的暂时干扰，”凯投宏观中国经济主管朱利安·埃文斯-普里查德表示。

最新数据进一步描绘出这样一幅图景：中国经济对高科技和人工智能驱动的出口依赖程度较高，使其不易受到恶劣天气事件的影响。

由于价格上涨，7月份芯片和电脑出口持续飙升，同比分别增长117%和67%。汽车海外销量跃升60%，船舶销量激增92%。通用机械增速从6月的15%加快至31%。

尽管与主要贸易伙伴的紧张局势升级，中国与欧盟的贸易顺差仍在攀升，7月份达到338亿美元的新纪录。

中国继续抑制原油进口，尽管其数量同比降幅收窄至24%，而6月份为41%。

## China’s Trade Boom Survives Extreme Rains as AI Lifts Demand

China’s exports and imports managed to ride out the disruptions caused by [extreme weather](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-30/hong-kong-set-for-wettest-july-since-1997-with-more-rain-to-come) in July, as a global investment supercycle in artificial intelligence powers tech demand overseas and at home.

Shipments abroad climbed more than forecast and grew 23.9%, their second straight month of gains in excess of 20%, according to data released by the General Administration of Customs on Friday. Imports advanced 27.5%, leaving a trade surplus of $112.5 billion and putting it on track to exceed last year’s record level.

“The strong outturn came despite typhoon-related disruptions at major ports,” Barclays Plc analysts led by Yingke Zhou said in a report. “AI-related and green-tech exports continue to benefit from the global AI investment cycle and energy transition, but weakness in labor-intensive exports continues to weigh on the job market and consumption.”

After absorbing the shocks of Donald Trump’s tariffs and the war in the Middle East, the world’s biggest manufacturing nation is pulling through a period of heavy rains. [Typhoon Bavi](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-13/china-to-see-unusually-active-typhoon-season-after-bavi-passes) became the [strongest](https://www.xinhuanet.com/local/20260712/bd021c3c0e204101a1521f3e9d1d710c/c.html) storm to hit the powerhouse economy of Zhejiang province in nearly eight decades and some of the country’s busiest ports 中国宁波一码头将暂停集装箱、商品车进提作业 由于台风影响 operations temporarily.

Soaring overseas shipments for AI-related electronics as well as other cutting-edge products like electric vehicles have cushioned growth from a deeper slowdown this year.

At the same time, the boom in trade is widening a persistent divide within the economy and reducing the need for Chinese officials to step up support to domestic consumers despite weak demand at home.

“Exports remain the main growth engine,” said Lynn Song, chief economist for Greater China at ING Bank NV in Hong Kong. “This year’s K-shaped divergence in China looks like it will continue in the months ahead as external demand is clearly much stronger than domestic demand.”

*“China’s trade data continue to show a widening divide between the old and new economies. Robust import and export growth in July demonstrated the strength of tech-related manufacturing, while exports of traditional products lagged well behind the headline pace. We expect that pattern to persist, with tech-related sectors staying strong and supporting overall exports.” — David Qu, China economist. The rising dominance of Chinese companies across the global value chain has also led to rising tensions with Europe and the US, especially in areas like carmaking and more recently [hardware for data centers](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-05/china-s-official-newspaper-warns-us-over-expanding-tech-curbs). China’s swelling trade surplus has also prompted a Can a Stronger Yuan Fix China’s Trade Imbalances?: China Today among economists over the role the domestic currency played in protecting the country’s manufacturing edge.

Chinese officials have been [pledging](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-22/china-premier-pledges-to-promote-balanced-trade-as-exports-surge) to achieve “balanced trade” and boost purchases from abroad since early this year.

Imports did surge this year, though price factors played a more important role. In a sign that even soaring imports are struggling to catch up with strong exports, China’s year-to-date trade surplus in July exceeded its level from the same time last year for the first time since February.

Surging prices for chips and commodities like oil and metals have inflated China’s trade figures, with exports and imports rising less in volume terms. And as trillions of dollars pour into AI, a shortage for semiconductors and other electronics has sent some chip prices soaring as much as 700% over the past year.

Export prices rose 8% from a year ago in June, according to the latest official data, increasing for the third straight month after nearly three years of declines. The prices of imports surged 25% in June, the fastest increase since data began in 2006. In [volume](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CHHQMTTL%20Index) terms, imports grew just 4% in June.

The extreme weather may have contributed to a slight slowdown in trade last month. Several consecutive typhoons including Bavi created a jam at ports during a peak season for exporters. A gauge tracking outbound container shipping costs surged to the highest level since September 2024.

“Stepping back, exports remain very strong, with the slight deterioration last month partly due to temporary disruptions to port operations from recent typhoons,” said Julian Evans-Pritchard, head of China economics at Capital Economics.

The latest data added to a picture of an economy whose reliance on high-tech and AI-driven exports makes it less vulnerable to intense weather events.

Exports of chips and computers kept soaring in July thanks to higher prices, climbing 117% and 67% from a year ago, respectively. Overseas sales of cars jumped 60% while ships surged 92%. The growth for general machinery accelerated to 31% from 15% in June.

And while tensions with major trading partners have escalated, China’s trade surplus with the European Union is still climbing, reaching a fresh record of $33.8 billion in July.

China continued to curb its imports of crude oil, though their decline in volume terms narrowed to 24% from a year earlier versus 41% in June.
