> 📌 **核心摘要**
>
> 多位中国经济学家呼吁放松资本管制，以应对美元贬值的历史机遇。
> • 著名经济学家认为，美元走弱为提升人民币全球吸引力提供了机会。
> • 经济学家苗彦良表示，开放资本账户将吸引更多资本流入。
> • 居建东教授认为，今年和明年是开放资本项目的“战略窗口”。
> • 人民币升值与地缘政治风险上升形成利好环境。
> • 政府官员暗示将优化现有跨境投资渠道，促进人民币国际化。
> • 尽管资本管制仍是障碍，但市场环境已与十年前显著不同。
> • 有效的政策改革可能帮助人民币在国际市场上获得更多流动性。

**🔑 关键词**：人民币国际化、资本管制、美元贬值

![图片](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/f3dc3066f0cb4e8da5ff6f0f71809a21/39285ada-671a-41a5-8672-01a8893f2818-641fbf00c275.jpg)

Several prominent Chinese economists have called for loosening restrictions on the movement of money in and out of the country, arguing that a weaker dollar provides a historic opportunity to boost the yuan’s global appeal by increasing its convertibility.
多位著名中国经济学家呼吁放松对资金进出国门的限制，主张美元走弱为提升人民币全球吸引力提供了历史机遇，可通过增加其可兑换性来把握这一机遇。

In what would represent a tectonic policy shift for the world’s No. 2 economy, Miao Yanliang — chief strategist at China International Capital Corp. — said in an [article](https://finance.sina.com.cn/stock/stockzmt/2026-02-10/doc-inhmhrqq4790030.shtml) Tuesday that conditions are becoming ripe to push for such reforms without necessarily provoking massive capital outflows.
中国国际资本公司首席策略师苗彦良周二在一篇文章中表示，这将代表全球第二大经济体政策的根本转变，他认为条件正逐步成熟，可以推动这样的改革，而不必然引发大规模资本外流。

Echoing that view, Ju Jiandong, a finance professor at Tsinghua University, identified this year and next as a “strategic window” for opening up the so-called capital account because the yuan’s appreciation provides a favorable environment while geopolitical risks rise.
清华大学金融学教授居建东呼应了这一观点，他认为今年和明年是开放所谓资本项目的"战略窗口"，因为人民币升值提供了有利的环境，同时地缘政治风险上升。

#### Calls Grow for China to Loosen Capital Controls

中国放松资本管制的呼声日益高涨

Economists cite yuan's current appreciation as favorable condition
经济学家引用人民币目前升值作为有利条件

Source: China Foreign Exchange Trade System
来源：中国外汇交易中心

“The more China opens its capital account and allows greater exchange rate flexibility, the more it can actually attract capital inflows,” said Miao, formerly the chief economist at China’s foreign-exchange regulator. “Especially when the dollar enters a strategic and sustained depreciation cycle and the yuan enters an appreciation cycle, that is precisely the right time to raise the level of capital account openness.”
苗彦良表示："中国越是开放资本项目，越是允许汇率更灵活地波动，就越能实际吸引资本流入。尤其是当美元进入战略性持续贬值周期，人民币进入升值周期时，正是提高资本项目开放水平的恰当时机。"苗彦良曾担任中国外汇管理机构的首席经济学家。

The remarks follow comments by President Xi Jinping — delivered in 2024 but published recently — in which he [laid out ambitions](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-02-02/china-highlights-xi-s-old-speech-on-risks-powerful-currency) to make the yuan “widely used” in global trade and finance as well as attaining reserve currency status.
这些言论紧跟习近平主席在 2024 年发表但最近才公开的评论，在评论中他阐述了使人民币在全球贸易和金融中"广泛使用"以及获得储备货币地位的雄心。

The quick succession of speeches and publications in recent weeks will add to speculation that Chinese authorities could make a much freer flow of capital the centerpiece of their efforts to promote broader international use of the yuan. Progress toward a convertible currency was disrupted by a shock devaluation in 2015, and momentum behind discussions of reforming the capital account waned in recent years.
最近几周一系列讲话和出版物的快速发布将引发人们的猜测，即中国当局可能会将更自由的资本流动作为推动人民币更广泛国际使用的核心举措。实现货币可兑换的进展被 2015 年的意外贬值打断，近年来围绕资本项目改革讨论的势头有所减弱。

But for the first time in a decade, influential voices in academia and finance are publicly broaching the possibility of ceding more control to seize the chance provided by misgivings over the dollar’s dominance that are driving diversification needs for central banks and investors alike.
但十年来首次，学术界和金融界的有影响力的声音公开提出了放松控制以抓住机遇的可能性，这一机遇由对美元主导地位的担忧所推动，这种担忧正在推动央行和投资者的多元化需求。

The dollar ended 2025 with the sharpest annual retreat in eight years after months of unpredictable policymaking during Donald Trump’s second presidency. By contrast, the yuan had its best annual performance since 2020 in nominal terms.
美元在 2025 年以八年来最大的年度跌幅收盘，此前特朗普第二个任期内数月的政策制定缺乏可预测性。相比之下，人民币以名义计以 2020 年以来最佳的年度表现收盘。

A key part of the new calculus for China also stems from the mismatch between its standing as a global manufacturing powerhouse and the limited reach of its currency abroad. China has for years maintained a “[managed float](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-01-09/here-are-the-tools-that-china-uses-to-manage-the-yuan-quicktake)” of the yuan, while existing restrictions on cross-border investments and personal remittances mean the capital account remains largely closed.
中国新局面的一个关键因素也源于其全球制造业大国地位与货币国际影响力有限之间的不匹配。中国多年来一直维持人民币的"管理浮动"，同时对跨境投资和个人汇款的现有限制意味着资本项目基本保持封闭状态。

For months, officials have been signaling a greater ambition of reforming the yuan and the capital account, which tracks investment flows.
数月来，官员们一直在暗示更大的改革野心，即改革人民币和追踪投资流动的资本账户。

The Communist Party’s October proposal for its next economic plan through 2030 included a pledge to “push forward the internationalization of the yuan and enhance the openness of the capital account.” That’s a bolder stance compared with the previous five-year plan, which called for promoting the currency’s globalization in a “stable and prudent” manner.
共产党 10 月提出的 2030 年前经济计划提案包括承诺"推进人民币国际化，提高资本账户开放度"。这相比前一个五年计划立场更加大胆，前一个计划要求以"稳妥谨慎"的方式推进货币全球化。

China has a long way to go to accomplish its goal. Despite gaining a bigger role in trade settlement and financing, the [yuan’s share](https://data.imf.org/en/news/imf%20data%20brief%20december%2019) in global international reserves is less than 2%, according to the International Monetary Fund, lagging in a distant sixth place and remaining far behind the dollar.
中国要实现这一目标还有很长的路要走。根据国际货币基金组织的数据，尽管人民币在贸易结算和融资中的作用有所增大，但其在全球国际储备中的占比不足 2%，排名在第六位，远远落后于美元。

#### Yuan's Share in International Official Reserves Is Small

人民币在国际官方外汇储备中的占比很小

Dollar and euro dominate in global foreign exchange reserves
美元和欧元在全球外汇储备中占据主导地位

Source: International Monetary Fund
来源：国际货币基金组织

Capital controls remain the biggest obstacle to Xi’s vision, even though China has taken steps in recent years to relax the restrictions. Investors and companies still need permission to move money in and out of the country or have to rely on designated channels, preventing domestic markets from being more integrated with the outside world and limiting the currency’s appeal.
尽管中国近年来采取了措施放松限制，但资本管制仍然是实现习近平愿景的最大障碍。投资者和企业仍需获得许可才能将资金进出境，或必须依赖指定渠道，这防止了国内市场与外部世界更好地融合，也限制了人民币的吸引力。

The threat of large capital outflows that risk destabilizing markets and the economy is the biggest concern for authorities as they explore bolder liberalization.
大规模资本外逃可能造成市场和经济不稳定的威胁是当局在探索更大胆自由化时最主要的关切。

China last experienced significant capital flight in 2015 after a botched move to shift to a more market-based exchange rate triggered panic among investors and forced the central bank to intervene. China’s foreign reserves plunged by around $1 trillion yuan within the span of about two years, prompting authorities to tighten capital controls.
中国上一次经历重大资本外逃是在 2015 年，当时一项旨在转向更多市场化汇率机制的操作处理不当，引发投资者恐慌，迫使央行进行干预。中国外汇储备在大约两年内下降了约 1 万亿元人民币，促使当局加强了资本管制。

But the environment both at home and overseas has changed significantly from a decade ago, according to Miao.
但根据苗圩的观点，国内外环境与十年前相比已经发生了显著变化。

He said foreign investors have a growing need to invest in Chinese assets as the appeal of the US suffers from Trump’s drastic tariffs and geopolitical maneuvering. Meanwhile, Chinese households and companies have already [purchased](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-01-16/china-s-1-2-trillion-windfall-quietly-seeps-into-global-markets) a sizable amount of assets overseas via approved channels, reducing potential outflow pressures, Miao said.
他表示，随着特朗普大幅关税和地缘政治操纵导致美国吸引力下降，外国投资者对投资中国资产的需求不断增长。与此同时，苗圩指出，中国家庭和企业已经通过批准渠道购买了大量海外资产，减少了潜在的资本外流压力。

Li Xunlei, chief economist at Zhongtai Financial International Ltd. who attended an economic seminar hosted by Premier Li Qiang in April, made a similar point in an [article](https://finance.sina.cn/zl/2026-02-06/zl-inhkupsz8768299.d.html?vt=4&cid=79615&node_id=79615) published last week.
中泰金融国际有限公司首席经济学家李迅雷在 4 月份参加了总理李强主持的经济研讨会，他在上周发表的一篇文章中提出了类似的观点。

The Chinese currency’s significant undervaluation — based on actual purchasing power — results from a lack of global liquidity, he said. A more open capital account could boost the availability of yuan internationally, a change that will likely help the currency appreciate, according to Li.
他表示，人民币的严重低估——基于实际购买力——源于全球流动性不足。根据李迅雷的观点，更开放的资本项目可以增加国际上人民币的可用性，这一变化可能会有助于人民币升值。

To be sure, after treading carefully for decades, Chinese authorities are likely to remain cautious and be in no rush to roll out any big changes.
当然，经过几十年的谨慎行事之后，中国当局很可能会保持谨慎态度，不会急于推出任何重大变革。

“There could be some room on a marginal basis to experiment with certain policy,” said Gary Ng, senior economist at Natixis SA. China could still take modest steps to promote its currency, such as pushing for some commodities to be traded in the yuan or using it to settle and finance investments in overseas projects, he said.
"在边际基础上，可能有一些空间来试验某些政策，"瑞银集团高级经济学家高沛德表示。他表示，中国仍可以采取适度措施来推动其货币，例如推动某些商品以人民币交易或使用人民币来结算和融资海外项目投资。

Xiao Sheng, an official with the State Administration of Foreign Exchange, signaled the focus will likely be on optimizing existing channels of cross-border investment.
国家外汇管理局官员肖声暗示，重点可能将放在优化现有跨境投资渠道上。

In an [article](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/terminal/TA9XDCKJH6V4) published Wednesday, Xiao said the regulator will make it more convenient for foreigners to use programs such as the Qualified Foreign Institutional Investor system. Authorities will “step up the supply of policies to expedite cross-border investment and financing,” he said.
肖声在周三发表的文章中表示，监管机构将使外国人更便利地使用合格境外机构投资者等项目。他表示，当局将"加大政策供给力度，推进跨境投融资"。
