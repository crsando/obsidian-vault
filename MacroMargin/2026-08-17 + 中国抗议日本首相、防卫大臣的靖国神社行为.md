### 中文译文

## 中国抗议日本首相、防卫大臣的靖国神社行为

在日本首相高市早苗向靖国神社献上祭祀品、防卫大臣小泉进次郎在第二次世界大战日本投降纪念日参拜靖国神社之后，中国向日本提出正式抗议。

根据中国外交部的一份[声明](https://www.fmprc.gov.cn/fyrbt_673021/202608/t20260815_12004727.shtml)，北京对这些举动“强烈谴责”。

小泉进次郎于周六参拜了靖国神社，当天是日本在战争最后阶段投降81周年纪念日。他经常参拜该神社。据共同通讯社报道，高市早苗原计划不前往参拜，而是改为献上供品。

中国官员表示，靖国神社是日本军国主义的象征和精神工具，并指出神社内供奉着14名已被定罪的甲级战犯。中国外交部表示，一些日本政客试图为战犯的行为开脱、掩盖战争暴行责任并歪曲历史事实。

中国还敦促日本正视其战时历史，并“彻底决裂”于军国主义。北京表示，正确认识历史是日本与邻国关系的政治基础，也是检验日本致力于和平发展的重要标准。

在中国外交部发表声明后，中国国防部在其官方微信公众号上表示，这些参拜行为侮辱了历史正义，伤害了遭受日本战时侵略的国家人民的感情。国防部敦促日本以史为鉴，并警告所谓的“新军国主义”，称其可能成为地区和平的威胁。

### English Original

## China Protests Japan PM, Defense Minister’s Yasukuni Actions

China lodged a formal protest with Japan after Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi sent an offering to Yasukuni shrine and Defense Minister Shinjiro Koizumi visited it on the anniversary of Japan’s surrender in World War II.

Beijing “strongly condemns” the moves, according to a [statement](https://www.fmprc.gov.cn/fyrbt_673021/202608/t20260815_12004727.shtml) from its Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

Koizumi visited the shrine on Saturday, the 81st anniversary of Japan’s surrender during the final days of the war. He is a regular visitor to the site. Takaichi was planning to skip a visit and made an offering instead, according to the Kyodo news agency.

Chinese officials said the shrine is a symbol and spiritual tool of Japanese militarism, adding that it enshrines 14 convicted Class-A war criminals. The Chinese foreign ministry said some Japanese politicians are seeking to exonerate the actions of war criminals, conceal responsibility for wartime atrocities and distort historical facts.

China also urged Tokyo to confront its wartime history and “completely break” with militarism. Beijing said a proper understanding of history is the political foundation of Japan’s relations with neighboring countries and an important test of its commitment to peaceful development.

Following the foreign ministry’s statement, China’s Defense Ministry said on its official WeChat account that the visits insulted historical justice and hurt the feelings of people in countries that suffered under Japanese wartime aggression. It urged Japan to learn from history and warned against what it called “new militarism,” saying it could become a threat to regional peace.
