> 📌 **核心摘要**
>
> 美中正通过特朗普特使戴恩斯等渠道敲定习近平9月访华盛顿的峰会日程，双方在多领域缩小分歧但仍有不确定性。
> • 戴恩斯将再访北京，为习近平与特朗普9月华盛顿峰会敲定议程。
> • 双方正在落实年初北京会晤共识清单，争取转化为具体成果。
> • 讨论议题包括AI安全合作、双边贸易投资委员会、约300亿美元免关税贸易方案等。
> • 官员还将讨论伊朗战争、乌克兰战争、台湾等敏感议题。
> • 中方对美方最新动作有不满，贸易紧张可能影响峰会，计划尚未最终确认。

**🔑 关键词**：中美峰会、习近平访美、戴恩斯

#### Montana Republican is helping narrow differences on issues expected to dominate summit planned for September in Washington, source says

消息人士称，这位蒙大拿州共和党人正协助缩小双方在预计将于 9 月在华盛顿举行的峰会议题上的分歧

![US Senator Steve Daines has emerged as a pivotal backchannel figure between Washington and Beijing. Photo: AP](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3b381cc90b148185bfd8f7848ea27bd0/3b381cc9-0b14-8115-87b8-f0b7065ead9d-bd5faf6f16e27a00f1024f16c74ea68d.jpg)

US Senator Steve Daines is expected to return to China for another round of talks ahead of President [Xi Jinping](https://www.scmp.com/topics/xi-jinping)’s planned September visit to Washington, according to people familiar with the preparations.
美国参议员史蒂夫·戴恩斯预计将再次访华，为习近平主席计划中的 9 月华盛顿之行进行新一轮会谈，据知情人士透露。

The trip was intended to help finalise the agenda for what both sides hoped would be [a high-stakes summit](https://www.scmp.com/topics/2026-xi-trump-summits) between Xi and US President Donald Trump, one of the sources said.
此次行程旨在协助敲定双方都期待的高风险峰会——中国国家主席习近平与美国总统唐纳德·特朗普会晤的议程，其中一位消息人士透露。

Daines, a Montana Republican, has emerged as an important backchannel figure between Washington and Beijing. He visited Beijing in May as the two sides sought to stabilise relations ahead of Trump’s trip to the Chinese capital later that month.
蒙大拿州共和党参议员史蒂夫·戴恩斯已成为华盛顿与北京之间的重要幕后沟通渠道。他于今年 5 月访问北京，当时双方正寻求在特朗普当月晚些时候访问中国首都之前稳定双边关系。

One person familiar with the matter said Daines had been carrying messages ahead of leader-level engagements and helping narrow differences on issues expected to dominate the coming summit.
一位知情人士表示，戴恩斯一直在领导人层级接触前传递信息，并协助缩小双方在预计将成为峰会核心议题上的分歧。

Daines’ role positions him as a pivotal intermediary as preparations enter their final stretch, according to the source.
据消息人士称，随着筹备工作进入最后阶段，戴恩斯的角色使其成为关键的中介人物。

“Both sides understand his role very well,” the source said. “He has become an effective channel for communicating President Trump’s concerns to the Chinese side and vice versa.”
“双方都非常清楚他的角色，”消息人士称。“他已成为向中方传达特朗普总统关切的有效渠道，反之亦然。”

According to multiple sources, officials are now working through a checklist of understandings reached when Trump and Xi met in Beijing earlier this year, with the goal of turning several of them into concrete deliverables for the Washington summit.
据多位消息人士透露，官员们目前正在梳理今年早些时候特朗普与习近平在北京会晤时达成的共识清单，目标是将其中多项共识转化为华盛顿峰会的具体成果。

Several items are under active discussion, including agreements on [AI](https://www.scmp.com/topics/artificial-intelligence) safety cooperation and the creation of bilateral trade and investment boards.
多项议题正在积极讨论中，包括人工智能安全合作协议以及建立双边贸易与投资委员会的方案。

Officials are also refining a roughly US$30 billion tariff-free trade package covering critical minerals, [semiconductors](https://www.scmp.com/topics/semiconductors) and chip issues, as well as holding further exchanges about citizens detained in each other’s countries.
官员们还在完善一项价值约 300 亿美元的免关税贸易方案，涵盖关键矿产、半导体及芯片相关议题，并就各自国家被拘留公民问题进一步交换意见。

Artificial intelligence (AI) is expected to feature prominently during Xi’s visit. Trump and Xi discussed the issue in Beijing in May, with officials now preparing a formal bilateral AI dialogue in September as one of the main follow-ups to that meeting.

One source said momentum had recently picked up on the proposed trade and investment boards, pointing to Secretary of State Marco Rubio’s recent public comments in the Philippines that Washington hoped to have the trade board operational by the time Xi visited.

Beyond economics, officials are also expected to discuss the [US-Iran war](https://www.scmp.com/topics/us-israel-war-iran), which has continued to choke global energy supply chains; [the Ukraine war](https://www.scmp.com/topics/ukraine-war), which has entered its fifth year; and [Taiwan](https://www.scmp.com/topics/taiwan), the most sensitive issue between Beijing and Washington.

Beijing sees Taiwan as part of China to be reunited by force if necessary.

Most countries, including the United States, do not recognise Taiwan as an independent state, but Washington is opposed to any attempt to take the self-ruled island by force and is committed to supplying it with weapons*.*

According to a person familiar with the planning, both Washington and Beijing believe understandings reached on Iran during the leaders’ previous meeting need to be revisited because regional dynamics have shifted significantly since then.

Behind the scenes, planning for Xi’s state visit to Washington has been progressing smoothly, according to two people familiar with the preparations.

“The communication channels are all open,” one source said. “Both sides talk regularly.”

Top financial officials of both countries spoke by phone on Thursday, and on Tuesday, a senior Pentagon official made a rare public speech at a Chinese embassy event in Washington, hailing stronger communications between the two militaries.

The current plan calls for Trump to host Xi for a formal state banquet.

In addition to the traditional larger bilateral meeting involving senior officials, the two leaders are expected to hold an extended small-group session reserved for the most sensitive strategic issues, the source added.

What remains far less certain is whether Xi will also travel to New York to attend the UN General Assembly.

Xi’s trip to Washington on September 24 coincides with the General Assembly’s high-level general debate, scheduled for September 22 to 28.

A source said the Chinese leader was increasingly unlikely to include New York in his itinerary and was instead expected to spend virtually all of his visit in Washington.

The reasoning, the source said, was straightforward: this year’s UN gathering is the 81st session of the General Assembly rather than a major anniversary year, reducing the diplomatic value of a New York stop.

The source also noted that Trump’s earlier visit to China was limited to Beijing rather than including multiple cities.

However, another person familiar with the discussions said plans for Xi’s US trip had not yet been finalised.

“There are many moving parts currently … Nothing is 100 per cent confirmed. Beijing is clearly not happy with some of Washington’s latest moves.

“Both sides need to manage the recent trade tensions well to avoid potential disruptions to the summit.”

The source added that China on Thursday voiced “serious concern” over recent US economic restrictions during a video call between Vice-Premier [He Lifeng](https://www.scmp.com/topics/he-lifeng), US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent and US Trade Representative Jamieson Greer.

Security planning in New York had become much more complicated – particularly following a self-immolation incident involving a Tibetan activist in early July – the source noted, making any presidential visit more challenging.
