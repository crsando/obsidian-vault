### 中文译文

## 随着人工智能需求激增，英特尔将发售150亿美元普通股

[英特尔公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/INTC%20US%20Equity)表示，将发售150亿美元普通股，以利用人工智能数据中心热潮期间市场对其商业前景重新燃起的兴趣。

英特尔在周一的一份声明中表示，这笔资金将用于一般公司用途。该公司表示：“包括物理人工智能、专用硅、先进封装和外部晶圆在内的新兴领域的进展，为英特尔带来了重大增长机遇。”“此次发售旨在进一步使英特尔能够抓住未来的增长机遇，同时保持强劲的资产负债表及其对投资级评级的承诺。”

今年以来，英特尔股价已上涨近两倍，至101.65美元，该公司正努力实现业务扭亏为盈。虽然它在AI专用芯片上并不领先，但数据中心需求增加了其中央处理器的销量。英特尔数据中心[部门上季度销售额飙升59%](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-23/intel-forecast-shatters-estimates-fueled-by-data-center-growth)，是英特尔整体营收增速的两倍以上。不过，该公司仍在努力为其工厂争取重要的外部客户。

![图片](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3b881cc90b148116ba9cd73958beb52f/3b881cc9-0b14-816a-9490-c55e17b6994e-d740cbf8d56f7b325772e58085242942.png)

彭博情报分析师罗伯特·希夫曼在报告中写道，这笔收益提供了“大量资金能力”，用于资助人工智能、代工等项目，而无需增加杠杆。“英特尔还强化了人工智能基础设施的一个更广泛主题：不断增长的投资不必完全由债券持有人承担，”他表示，并引用了[甲骨文公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/ORCL%20US%20Equity)、[SpaceX](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/SPCX%20US%20Equity)、[Alphabet Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/GOOGL%20US%20Equity)、[Meta平台公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/META%20US%20Equity)和[微软公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/MSFT%20US%20Equity)作为比较。

### English Original

## Intel Selling $15 Billion in Common Stock as AI Demand Booms

[Intel Corp.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/INTC%20US%20Equity) said it will be offering $15 billion in common stock, taking advantage of renewed interest in its business prospects during the artificial intelligence data center boom.

The money will be used for general corporate purposes, Intel said in a statement on Monday. “Progress in emerging areas including physical AI, purpose-built silicon, advanced packaging and external wafers represent significant growth opportunities for Intel,” the company said. “The offering is intended to further enable Intel to pursue the growth opportunities ahead while maintaining a strong balance sheet and its commitment to an investment-grade rating.”

Intel shares have nearly tripled in value so far this year, to $101.65, as it works on a turnaround of its business. While it doesn’t lead in AI-specific chips, data center demand has increased sales of its central processing units. Sales in Intel’s data center [segment soared 59% last quarter](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-23/intel-forecast-shatters-estimates-fueled-by-data-center-growth), more than double the pace of Intel’s overall revenue. Still, the company is working to line up significant outside customers for its factories.

Bloomberg Intelligence analyst Robert Schiffman wrote in a note that the proceeds provide “substantial capacity” to fund AI, foundry and other projects without adding leverage. “Intel also reinforces a broader theme across AI infrastructure: rising investment doesn’t have to fall entirely on bondholders,” he said, drawing comparisons to [Oracle Corp.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/ORCL%20US%20Equity), [SpaceX](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/SPCX%20US%20Equity), [Alphabet Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/GOOGL%20US%20Equity), [Meta Platforms Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/META%20US%20Equity) and [Microsoft Corp](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/MSFT%20US%20Equity).
