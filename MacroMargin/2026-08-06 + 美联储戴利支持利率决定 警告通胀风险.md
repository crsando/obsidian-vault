Federal Reserve Bank of San Francisco President Mary Daly said she supported the central bank’s decision to keep interest rates on hold last week, but warned of the possibility that high inflation is a broader problem that could require more aggressive action from policymakers.

旧金山联邦储备银行行长玛丽·戴利表示，她支持央行上周维持利率不变的决定，但警告称高通胀可能是更广泛的问题，可能需要政策制定者采取更积极的行动。

“I was completely supportive of the decision to hold rates in July,” Daly said Wednesday at an event in Tokyo.

“我完全支持7月维持利率的决定，”戴利周三在东京的一次活动中表示。

The San Francisco Fed chief said she sees two potential scenarios for inflation — one where it starts to cool and a second in which more persistent price pressures take hold — that would require different policy actions.

这位旧金山联储行长表示，她认为通胀有两种可能的情景——一种是通胀开始降温，另一种是更持续的价格压力占据主导——这两种情况需要不同的政策行动。

“The answer there is be vigilant to watch the information as it comes in, but be very prepared to take the action,” she said.

她表示：“对此的答案是，要警惕地观察陆续传来的信息，但同时做好采取行动的准备。”

Fed officials voted last week to keep interest rates unchanged, though three policymakers dissented because they wanted to raise rates by a quarter percentage point. Daly is not a voter on the rate-setting Federal Open Market Committee this year, though she participates in the policy deliberations.

美联储官员上周投票决定维持利率不变，尽管有三位政策制定者持异议，因为他们希望加息25个基点。戴利今年不是利率制定机构联邦公开市场委员会的投票委员，但她参与了政策讨论。

Fed officials have been divided over the risks from inflation pressures, and how to deal with them. Some, like Daly, still see the shocks from tariffs, the oil-price surge and the artificial intelligence boom as likely to be isolated and eventually dissipate. Others see evidence that inflation in those areas has already broadened to other parts of the economy.

美联储官员在通胀压力的风险以及如何应对这些问题上存在分歧。一些官员（如戴利）仍认为关税、油价飙升和人工智能热潮带来的冲击可能是孤立的，并最终会消散。另一些官员则认为，有证据表明这些领域的通胀已经扩大到经济其他领域。

### **Two Scenarios**

### **两种情景**

In her remarks, Daly outlined two potential scenarios for the inflation path. In the first, which Daly said she still sees as most likely, inflation is short lived and the Fed can keep holding interest rates where they are.

戴利在讲话中概述了通胀路径的两种可能情景。第一种情景（戴利表示她仍认为这种情景最有可能）是通胀短暂存在，美联储可以将利率维持在当前水平。

But a second possibility — that price increases from tariffs, higher energy costs and continued AI investment are compounded — is becoming increasingly likely, she warned. Inflation would then become more broad based and persistent, requiring more aggressive action from the Fed.

但她警告说，第二种可能性——即关税、能源成本上升和持续的人工智能投资导致的价格上涨相互叠加——正变得越来越有可能。届时通胀将变得更加广泛且持久，要求美联储采取更积极的行动。

Daly said policymakers should carefully monitor the likelihood of the second scenario, and watch incoming data carefully over the coming weeks.

戴利表示，政策制定者应仔细监测第二种情景出现的可能性，并在未来几周密切关注即将公布的数据。

“If we find that scenario two is taking hold then I think the question would be, ‘why do it incrementally?’” she said, referring to possible rate increases, adding that it might be best in that case to adjust policy as quickly as possible.

“如果我们发现第二种情景正在成为现实，那么我认为问题将是，‘为什么要逐步行动？’”她说，她指的是可能的加息，并补充说，在这种情况下，或许最好尽快调整政策。

Daly also warned that while long-term inflation expectations are well anchored right now, another move up there — following the uptick after the inflation surge of 2022 — could prove more challenging for policymakers to return to normal.

戴利还警告称，尽管目前长期通胀预期锚定良好，但在2022年通胀飙升后已经有所上升，若再度上行，政策制定者可能更难使其回归正常。
