## 消息人士称，苹果在阿里巴巴的支持下为中国市场训练自己的AI模型

8月14日（路透社）——苹果[(AAPL.O), 打开新标签页](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/AAPL.O)已针对中国市场专门训练了一个大语言模型，三位知情人士透露。此举改变了这家iPhone制造商依赖第三方模型为中国市场AI功能提供支持的战略。

消息人士称，该AI模型是与阿里巴巴集团[(9988.HK), 打开新标签页](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/9988.HK)合作开发，并在中国科技巨头的支持下训练而成。由于信息敏感且未公开，这些消息人士要求匿名。

苹果训练自有的中国专属模型此前未见报道。该公司此前倾向于采用国内合作伙伴的模型，将生成式AI引入在中国销售的iPhone和其他设备。在美国市场，苹果将Anthropic的Claude和OpenAI的ChatGPT等美国模型与自身技术结合，但这些模型在中国不可用。

苹果和阿里巴巴未回应置评请求。

消息人士称，苹果的AI工具套件Apple Intelligence预计将在未来几个月内，在iOS操作系统更新后于中国推出。

拥有专为中国定制的模型，将使苹果在其竞争最激烈的海外市场对AI体验拥有更大控制权。在中国市场，苹果已败给了华为等本土竞争对手，这些对手凭借配备AI的手机抢先一步。

苹果的推出计划代表着外国公司在中国部署AI的独特双轨战略，这将使其能够跨越许多其他美国科技公司难以逾越的严格监管障碍。

这也将使苹果成为首家获得北京批准在中国提供专有AI模型的外国公司，标志着在美中围绕AI的贸易和外交裂痕日益扩大的背景下，两国科技公司罕见合作。

### 监管障碍已清除，但不确定性依然存在

此举之前经历了漫长的监管流程，该流程于上月结束，当时中国互联网监管机构国家互联网信息办公室为苹果的生成式AI服务办理了登记，为苹果智能首次进入中国iPhone扫清了障碍。

根据该安排，阿里巴巴的通义千问模型将整合到在中国销售的兼容iPhone、iPad、Mac和Vision Pro机型所搭载的苹果智能版本中，且该服务还将整合百度的技术，路透社在7月曾报道。

上周，苹果发布了一份中文指南，说明中国大陆符合条件的Mac用户如何将通义千问连接到其Siri数字助手和写作工具功能，这是一项针对Mac的安排，可能有助于苹果在中国AI PC市场展开竞争。苹果随后删除了该指南，未作解释。

得益于7月登记的消息，阿里巴巴在美上市股票盘前上涨约4%，凸显出投资者预期这一合作可能重塑中国AI生态。

### 苹果寻求借AI提振在华表现

苹果与阿里巴巴的合作关系最初于2025年2月得到确认，当时阿里巴巴董事长蔡崇信在迪拜世界政府峰会上表示，苹果将使用这家中国公司的AI来支持其在中国的iPhone。

“他们与中国多家公司进行了洽谈。最终他们选择与我们合作，”蔡崇信当时表示。由于苹果需要根据中国法规调整其功能，该服务随后推迟上线。

中国对苹果的业绩仍至关重要，占其收入的很大一部分。中国版iPhone缺乏AI功能曾被列为拖累销售的因素之一，因为消费者纷纷转向提供内置AI助手的国产品牌。

目前尚不清楚苹果自训练的模型将与第三方中国模型共同扮演何种角色，以及它们如何协同工作。

Aug 14 (Reuters) - Apple [(AAPL.O), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/AAPL.O) has trained a large language model specifically for the China market, three people familiar with the matter said, a departure from the iPhone maker's strategy of relying on third-party models to power AI features ‌in the country.

The AI model was developed in partnership with Alibaba Group [(9988.HK), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/9988.HK) and trained with the Chinese tech giant's support, the people said, declining to be named as the information is sensitive and not public.

Apple's training of its own China-specific model has not been reported before. The company had previously leaned towards domestic partners' models to bring generative AI to iPhones and other devices sold in China, where U.S. models such as Anthropic's Claude and OpenAI's ChatGPT, which Apple pairs with its own technology in its home market, are not available.

Apple and Alibaba did not respond to requests for comment.

Apple Intelligence, the ⁠company's suite of AI tools, is expected to launch in China in the coming months after an update to its iOS operating system, the people said.

A China-tailored model of its own would give Apple greater control over the AI experience in its most competitive overseas market, where it has lost ground to local rivals including Huawei that have raced ahead with AI-equipped handsets.

Apple's planned rollout represents a unique dual-track strategy on AI deployment for a foreign firm in China, which would allow it to navigate steep regulatory hurdles that have limited access for many other U.S. technology firms.

It would also make Apple the first foreign company approved by Beijing to offer a proprietary AI model in China, marking a rare collaboration by U.S. and Chinese tech firms amid a widening trade and diplomatic rift between the two countries over AI.

### REGULATORY HURDLES CLEARED BUT UNCERTAINTY REMAINS

The move follows a [lengthy regulatory process](https://www.reuters.com/technology/apple-intelligence-ai-service-registered-with-chinas-cyberspace-regulator-2026-07-15/) that ended last month, when the Cyberspace Administration of China, the country's internet watchdog, registered ‌Apple's generative ⁠AI service, clearing the way for Apple Intelligence to reach Chinese iPhones for the first time.

Under that arrangement, Alibaba's Qwen model is to be incorporated into the version of Apple Intelligence that ships with compatible iPhone, iPad, Mac and Vision Pro models in China, and the service was also set to incorporate technology from Baidu, [Reuters reported in July](https://www.reuters.com/technology/apple-intelligence-ai-service-registered-with-chinas-cyberspace-regulator-2026-07-15/).

Last week, Apple [published a Chinese-language guide](https://www.reuters.com/business/retail-consumer/apple-says-mac-users-china-can-connect-alibabas-qwen-ai-service-2026-08-08/) explaining how eligible Mac users in mainland China could connect Qwen to its Siri digital assistant and Writing Tools feature, a Mac-specific arrangement that could help it compete ⁠in China's AI PC market. Apple has since deleted the guide, without providing an explanation.

Alibaba's U.S.-listed shares climbed about 4% in premarket trading on news of the July registration, underscoring investor expectations that the tie-up could reshape China's AI ecosystem.

### APPLE LOOKS TO AI FOR BOOST IN CHINA

Apple's partnership with Alibaba was first confirmed in February 2025, when Alibaba chairman Joe Tsai, ⁠speaking at the World Governments Summit in Dubai, said Apple would use the Chinese company's AI to power its phones in China.

"They talked to a number of companies in China. In the end they chose to do business with us," Tsai said at the time. The rollout was subsequently delayed as Apple worked to adapt ⁠its features to Chinese regulations.

China remains critical to Apple's fortunes, accounting for a significant share of its revenue. The absence of AI features on Chinese iPhones had been cited as a factor weighing on sales as consumers gravitated toward domestic brands offering built-in AI assistants.

It was not immediately clear what role Apple's self-trained model would play alongside third-party Chinese models and how those are working together.
