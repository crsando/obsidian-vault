## 中国AI芯片设计公司摩尔线程计划赴港上市

人工智能芯片制造商[摩尔线程科技公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/688795%20CH%20Equity)表示，计划在“合适的时候”在港上市，此前该公司股价自去年在上海上市以来已飙升超过420%。

据周日发布的一份[文件](https://static.sse.com.cn/disclosure/listedinfo/announcement/c/new/2026-08-10/688795_20260810_29E9.pdf)，这家总部位于北京的公司正[计划](https://static.sse.com.cn/disclosure/listedinfo/announcement/c/new/2026-08-10/688795_20260810_29E9.pdf)在香港发行上市，以深化其国际化战略并吸引优秀的研发和管理人才。根据另一份文件，摩尔线程还[报告](https://static.sse.com.cn/disclosure/listedinfo/announcement/c/new/2026-08-10/688795_20260810_PQ5Y.pdf)上半年净亏损1160万元人民币（172万美元），低于去年同期的2.709亿元人民币。1月至6月营收跃升147%至17.4亿元人民币。

摩尔线程表示：“公司将充分考虑现有股东的利益以及境内外资本市场的状况，选择合适的时机和发行窗口完成发行上市。”

摩尔线程去年底掀起了中国AI相关IPO浪潮，在一笔超额认购倍率极高的交易中筹资80亿元人民币。该公司在上海上市首日股价大涨425%，创下中国2019年改革以来大型新股上市首日最大涨幅。这为一系列上市铺平了道路，最终存储芯片制造商长鑫存储（CXMT Corp.）上月筹资66亿元人民币，成为内地史上第二大IPO，并随后成为[市值最大的中国上市公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-26/china-memory-champion-cxmt-set-to-debut-after-9-8-billion-ipo)。

中国AI供应链企业也助推了今年香港的交易热潮。2026年，香港首次公开募股筹资额已超过420亿美元，创六年新高。中际旭创股份有限公司（Zhongji Innolight Co.）是一家生产数据中心建设所需光收发模块的企业，上月筹资68亿美元，创下香港七年来最大新股上市。

摩尔线程已成为与寒武纪（Cambricon Technologies Corp.）和华为技术有限公司并列的竞争者，争夺英伟达被迫退出后留下的市场份额空白。摩尔线程由前英伟达高管张建中于2020年创立，最初依靠用于游戏和视觉渲染的图形芯片创收，后来转向用于支持大语言模型的AI加速器。

## China AI Chip Designer Moore Threads Plans Hong Kong Listing

Artificial intelligence chipmaker [Moore Threads Technology Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/688795%20CH%20Equity) said it plans to list in Hong Kong at an “appropriate time” after its shares have surged more than 420% since their Shanghai debut last year.

The Beijing-based firm is [planning](https://static.sse.com.cn/disclosure/listedinfo/announcement/c/new/2026-08-10/688795_20260810_29E9.pdf) a Hong Kong offering to deepen its internationalization strategy and attract outstanding research and development as well as management talent, according to a [filing](https://static.sse.com.cn/disclosure/listedinfo/announcement/c/new/2026-08-10/688795_20260810_29E9.pdf) released Sunday. Moore Threads also [reported](https://static.sse.com.cn/disclosure/listedinfo/announcement/c/new/2026-08-10/688795_20260810_PQ5Y.pdf) a first-half net loss of 11.6 million yuan ($1.72 million), down from 270.9 million yuan in the same period last year, according to a separate filing. Revenue jumped 147% to 1.74 billion yuan in January-June.

“The company will fully consider the interests of existing shareholders and the conditions of domestic and overseas capital markets, selecting an appropriate timing and issuance window to complete the issuance and listing,” Moore Threads said.

Moore Threads late last year kicked off a wave of Chinese AI-linked IPOs, raising 8 billion yuan in a heavily oversubscribed deal. Its shares jumped 425% in its Shanghai trading debut, at the time marking the biggest first-day pop for a major debut since China’s 2019 reforms. It paved the way for a wave of listings that culminated with memory chipmaker CXMT Corp. raising 6.6 billion yuan last month in the second-largest mainland IPO in history and subsequently becoming the [biggest China-listed company](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-26/china-memory-champion-cxmt-set-to-debut-after-9-8-billion-ipo).

Chinese companies in the AI supply chain have also fueled a Hong Kong deals boom this year. First-time share sales in the city have raised more than $42 billion in 2026, already a [six-year high](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-20/hong-kong-stock-listing-boom-rumbles-toward-six-year-high). Zhongji Innolight Co., a maker of optical transceivers essential to the data-center buildout, raised $6.8 billion last month in Hong Kong’s [biggest debut](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-28/innolight-prices-6-8-billion-hong-kong-listing-below-maximum) in seven years.

Moore Threads has emerged as a contender alongside Cambricon Technologies Corp. and Huawei Technologies Co. in the race to fill a market share void left after Nvidia Corp.’s forced exit. Founded in 2020 by former Nvidia executive Zhang Jianzhong, Moore Threads had started out earning revenue from graphics chips for gaming and visual rendering before pivoting to AI accelerators used in powering large language models.
