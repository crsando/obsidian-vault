## 中国以无人机出口管制和制裁回击美国

### [政治](https://www.bloomberg.com/politics)

彭博新闻 2026年8月5日 下午4:27 GMT+8 *更新于2026年8月5日 下午7:15 GMT+8*

### **要点**

- 针对美国近期的举动，包括禁止部分外国制造的机器人和电源逆变器以及将中国企业列入黑名单，北京正在对美国发起报复行动。
- 中国商务部宣布了反制措施，包括加强无人机出口管制和对美国实体实施制裁，并指出这些决定“严重违背了两国之间的重要共识”。
- 商务部将其反制描述为“总体克制”，并敦促美国撤销其措施，回到磋商解决分歧的轨道上，同时警告称，如果美国出台新的限制性措施，中国将采取进一步反制。
北京正利用其在全球无人机生产中的主导地位，再次与华盛顿就美国不断扩大的技术和贸易限制展开较量，并于周三发起一系列报复行动，称其反应“克制”。

中国商务部加强了对无人机出口的管制，宣布了针对美国近期举动的四套反制措施。

![US President Donald Trump shakes hands with China’s leader Xi Jinping in Beijing last May. Photographer: Evan Vucci/AFP/Getty Images](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3b381cc90b148158a903e26a1674b73c/3b381cc9-0b14-8167-b17d-df79dccff25a-1bd2ccc3a96d51ed2259e6a08bf93e8a.png)

去年五月，美国总统唐纳德·特朗普在北京与中国国家主席习近平握手。摄影师：Evan Vucci/AFP/Getty Images

如今北京与华盛顿之间的针锋相对对峙正在重新引发世界两大经济体之间的紧张关系，并考验他们脆弱的贸易休战。中国国家主席习近平计划下个月在美国与唐纳德·特朗普会晤，此前双方在五月于中国首都举行的峰会上同意建立“建设性战略稳定”的关系。

北京尤其对美国对43家中国企业实施制裁的时机感到恼怒，这些制裁是在上周副总理何立峰与美国财政部长斯科特·贝森特和贸易代表杰米森·格里尔进行[视频通话](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-30/china-shares-concern-over-restrictive-us-measures-with-bessent)仅一天后出台的。

中国商务部发言人在一份声明中表示，美方近期措施“严重违背了两国元首达成的重要共识，严重损害中国合法权益”。中国“别无选择，只能采取必要反制措施”。

但发言人同时表示北京愿保持关系正常发展，称其反制措施“总体克制”，同时敦促美方立即撤销其措施，回到协商解决分歧的轨道上来。

商务部表示，作为反制措施的一部分，向美国出口列入中国两用出口管制清单的无人机、其关键部件及相关技术将受到“严格逐案审查”。

中国是全球无人机的主要生产国，总部位于深圳的大疆科技（DJI Technologies）去年占美国商用无人机市场[约70%](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-08-07/chinese-drone-maker-dji-shut-out-of-washington-with-us-market-at-risk)的份额。

但美国已采取行动减少这种依赖。

去年12月，美国联邦通信委员会（FCC）宣布禁止大部分外国制造的无人机和无人驾驶飞机系统的关键部件。该禁令仅适用于新的无人机进口和销售，而不适用于已经售出或正在使用的无人机。

周三，中国商务部还制裁了六家美国实体，包括Applied DNA Sciences和Stratum Reservoir，称其参与了美国对新疆的制裁。此外，商务部还制裁了Compliance Testing，因其协助美国联邦通信委员会对中国实施这些措施。

商务部表示，中国还将开展对外贸易国家安全调查，针对进口的打印机、复印机和其他带有进口软件的办公影像设备。

北京的报复行动正值有报道称[美国进一步限制](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-05/china-s-official-newspaper-warns-us-over-expanding-tech-curbs)中国技术和产品。彭博社周二援引知情人士的话报道，美国联邦通信委员会正在起草一项命令，禁止中国制造的数据中心组件。路透社早些时候[报道说](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-04/us-drafting-ban-on-china-data-center-components-reuters-says)这家美国政府机构正在考虑禁止进口中国新型光收发器。

“如果美方坚持对中国采取新的限制措施，中方将采取进一步反制措施，”商务部发言人在声明中表示。

## China Retaliates Against US With Drone Export Curbs, Sanctions

### [Politics](https://www.bloomberg.com/politics)

By Bloomberg NewsAugust 5, 2026 at 4:27 PM GMT+8*Updated on August 5, 2026 at 7:15 PM GMT+8*

### **Takeaways**

- Beijing is unleashing retaliatory actions against the US in response to recent US moves, including a ban on some foreign-made robots and power inverters and the blacklisting of Chinese companies.
- China's Commerce Ministry announced countermeasures, including stiffened controls on drone exports and sanctions on US entities, citing decisions that "seriously violated the important common understandings" between the two countries.
- The ministry described its retaliation as "generally restrained" and urged the US to revoke its measures and return to consultations to resolve differences, while warning that China will take further countermeasures if the US introduces new restrictive measures.
Beijing is leveraging its dominance over global drone production as it spars again with Washington over widening US curbs on technology and trade, unleashing a barrage of retaliatory actions on Wednesday in what it described as a “restrained” response.

China’s Commerce Ministry stiffened controls on the exports of drones as it China Hits Back at US With Tighter Drone Export Curbs, Sanctions four sets of countermeasures to recent US moves. It cited decisions such as the Federal Communications Commission’s ban on some foreign-made robots and power inverters and the blacklisting of more than 40 Chinese companies accused of using forced labor.

US President Donald Trump shakes hands with China’s leader Xi Jinping in Beijing last May. Photographer: Evan Vucci/AFP/Getty Images

The tit-for-tat standoff now pitting Beijing against Washington is reviving tensions between the world’s two biggest economies and straining their fragile trade truce. President Xi Jinping is scheduled to meet Donald Trump in the US next month, after agreeing to build a relationship of “constructive strategic stability” during their summit in the Chinese capital in May.

Beijing has been particularly irked by the timing of US sanctions against the 43 Chinese firms, which came just a day after Vice Premier He Lifeng held a [video call](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-30/china-shares-concern-over-restrictive-us-measures-with-bessent) with US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent and Trade Representative Jamieson Greer last week.

The recent US measures “seriously violated the important common understandings reached by the two heads of state and severely damage China’s legitimate rights and interests,” a spokesperson for the Commerce Ministry said in a statement. “China has no choice but to take necessary countermeasures in response.”

But the spokesperson also signaled Beijing’s willingness to keep relations on track, calling its retaliation “generally restrained” while urging the US to immediately revoke its measures and return to consultations to resolve differences.

As part of the retaliation package, sales to the US of drones, their key components and related technologies listed on China’s dual-use export control list will be subject to “strict case-by-case review,” the ministry said.

China is the world’s dominant manufacturer of unmanned aerial vehicles, with Shenzhen-based DJI Technologies accounting for [about 70%](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-08-07/chinese-drone-maker-dji-shut-out-of-washington-with-us-market-at-risk) of the US commercial drone market as of last year.

But the US has moved to reduce that reliance.

Last December, the FCC announced a ban on most foreign-made drones and critical components for unmanned aircraft systems. The prohibition applied only to new drone imports and sales and not those that had already been sold or were in use.

On Wednesday, the Commerce Ministry also sanctioned six US entities, including [Applied DNA Sciences](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/BNBX%20US%20Equity) and Stratum Reservoir, for what it said was their involvement in US sanctions over Xinjiang. In addition, it sanctioned Compliance Testing for helping the FCC impose the measures against China.

China will also conduct a foreign trade national security investigation, which takes aim at imported printers, copiers, and other office imaging equipment with imported software, the ministry said.

Beijing’s retaliation comes amid reports of [further US curbs](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-05/china-s-official-newspaper-warns-us-over-expanding-tech-curbs) on Chinese technology and products. The FCC is drafting an order to ban Chinese-made data center components, Bloomberg reported Tuesday, citing a person familiar with the matter. Reuters earlier [said](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-04/us-drafting-ban-on-china-data-center-components-reuters-says) earlier the US government agency was considering barring imports of new models of Chinese optical transceivers.

“If the US side insists on introducing new restrictive measures against China, China will take further countermeasures,” the Commerce Ministry spokesperson said in the statement.
