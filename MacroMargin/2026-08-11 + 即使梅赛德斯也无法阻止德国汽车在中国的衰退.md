### 中文译文

## 即使梅赛德斯也无法阻止德国汽车在中国的衰退

![German carmakers are losing ground in China's auto market. Photographer: Pedro Pardo/AFP/Getty images](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3b981cc90b1481449a77ffa76a7d61eb/3b981cc9-0b14-8198-a566-f79524898ca0-76e00fd7481a59ad688969450b950e23.png)

### **要点**

- 梅赛德斯-奔驰集团股份公司与麦当劳合作，在中国推广其全电动CLA车型，但该活动并未成功，上半年仅售出1,153辆。
- 包括梅赛德斯、宝马和大众在内的德国汽车制造商，由于价格高企和缺乏有竞争力的电动汽车技术而在中国苦苦挣扎，小米和比亚迪等本土制造商正在抢占市场份额。
- 德国汽车制造商正试图通过与本地公司合作并推出新车型来适应中国市场，但它们面临重大挑战，包括价格战，以及需要加快从研发到上市的时间，以跟上中国快节奏的电动汽车市场。
凭借德国工程赢取中国买家数十年的芳心后，梅赛德斯-奔驰集团股份公司去年秋天采取了放下身段的一步，与快餐连锁店麦当劳合作，为其最新车型造势。

在“[So Mc-Benz](https://www.instagram.com/p/DQthcOoktMn/?hl=de&img_index=5)”活动中，梅赛德斯让一个芝士汉堡小雕像取代了其传统的三叉星标志，出现在全电动CLA上——这是一款它希望能在全球最大汽车市场阻止销量下滑的轿车。这些广告本应让这款车受到中国年轻时尚人群的欢迎。

但这并没有奏效。梅赛德斯上半年在中国仅售出1,153辆，而同期价格相近的[小米集团](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/1810%20HK%20Equity)SU7轿车交付量超过80,000辆，相形见绌。这一表现印证了[宝马集团](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/BMW%20GR%20Equity)、[大众汽车集团](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/VOW%20GR%20Equity)和[保时捷集团](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/P911%20GR%20Equity)在中国面临的挑战，它们均报告第二季度销量下滑至少30%，比整体市场跌幅更严重。与此同时，小米和[比亚迪股份有限公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/1211%20HK%20Equity)等企业正在从它们手中抢夺客户，使德国车企失去了一个关键的增长和利润驱动力。到目前为止，它们都没有找到阻止这种螺旋式下滑的方法。

并不是说它们没有尝试。大多数公司已与中国企业合作，以获取最新的电动汽车技术并更好地了解当地驾驶者的需求。梅赛德斯打造了一款长轴距版CLA，以迎合中国消费者对更宽敞后排空间的偏好。该车配备了包括人工智能语音控制在内的软件，起售价仅为229,000元人民币（约合33,943美元），比欧洲版本便宜约40%。

![图片](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3b981cc90b1481449a77ffa76a7d61eb/3b981cc9-0b14-81ec-ad7d-c61b662e162d-c005c7e2062a0a9711d0866c493ca6a6.png)

据知情人士透露，梅赛德斯非但没有赢得客户，反而不得不承认，在中国竞争需要进一步降价，而这将导致几乎每一辆电动CLA的销售都亏损。这些人士要求不具名讨论内部商业决策，表示该公司正在限制对这款车的推广，直到经济状况改善。

梅赛德斯表示，该车型从未旨在推动销量，而是要在竞争激烈的入门级细分市场展示公司的最新技术。该汽车制造商在电子邮件声明中表示，公司“专注于可持续增长，而非购买短期市场份额”。该公司指出，上个月在中国推出的紧凑型运动型多用途车电动GLC，在市场反馈和预订单方面表现良好。

与此同时，中国制造商愿意忍受巨大痛苦以赢得市场份额。由于价格战造成冲击，比亚迪第一季度利润[下滑55%](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-28/byd-posts-lowest-quarterly-profit-in-years-as-price-war-rages-on)，降至三年多来的最低水平。吉利汽车控股有限公司的同期收入也有所下降。

![Mercedes built a long-wheelbase version of the CLA to appeal to Chinese tastes. Photographer: Ying Tang/NurPhoto /AP Photo](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3b981cc90b1481449a77ffa76a7d61eb/3b981cc9-0b14-81f7-8d65-ddb8400609cf-e45a3e969be119a62b940a57a18b46f9.png)

摩根大通分析师Jose Asumendi表示，目前德国汽车制造商很大一部分产品在中国已因价格过高而无人问津。虽然中国整体市场因长期房地产危机拖累支出而下滑，但德国公司损失更大，因为它们的车型往往更贵，且以燃油车为主，而这一细分市场正在萎缩。宝马因中国市场的低迷大幅下调了利润率预期，使其有望成为今年欧洲主要的汽车制造商。包括梅赛德斯首席执行官Ola Källenius在内的管理者预计，未来几年市场竞争将依然残酷。

![Ola Källenius, CEO of Mercedes-Benz Group AG. Photographer: Qilai Shen/Bloomberg](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3b981cc90b1481449a77ffa76a7d61eb/3b981cc9-0b14-81bf-a7cc-ef3fb97b1e5a-1dc8b680682f98d2928e7d464bf27b8e.png)

问题不仅仅在于价格。在许多情况下，德国汽车制造商仍按照燃油车时代的研发到上市节奏运作，每四年或更长时间更新产品，并在消费者真正买到新车之前很久就放出预告片段。但在中国，电动汽车市场已变得类似于消费电子的疯狂节奏，品牌最快每18个月就更新一次车型，并立即准备好新车型供大众购买。

“在销售现有车型的同时，你已经在制造下一代了，”曾在德国和中国工作的AlixPartners汽车顾问[Xing Zhou](https://www.alixpartners.com/our-people/28404/xing-zhou/)表示。“这个行业不可能回到老路上去。”

这正在推动中国消费者对品牌认知的根本性转变，梅赛德斯、奥迪和宝马正失去奢华优势，因为它们被认为在软件和电动汽车技术方面落后于本土制造商。

据为中国汽车经销商协会提供咨询的Li Yanwei表示，电动CLA表现不佳的部分原因在于，以小米SU7为首的竞争对手在该价位段凭借更先进的自动驾驶功能、以手机为先的娱乐系统和更潮流的品牌形象主导了这一细分市场。他还说，CLA的运动化设计试图迎合更年轻的群体，却忽视了梅赛德斯传统的中年客户，这些人往往有家庭，更喜欢更大的车内空间。Li Yanwei表示，CLA“处境尴尬，既不是最实惠的，也不是最豪华的。”

这就是 Flora Li 和她的丈夫在购买第二辆车时的感受。他们看了一系列电动车型，包括 CLA、SU7，以及中国上汽集团与科技公司华为技术有限公司合作打造的 Z7 轿车。她说，在试驾时，CLA 的自动驾驶软件没有打动她，而且汽车对语音命令的响应很慢。

![The Xiaomi SU7 Ultra electric vehicle in Tianjin. Photographer: Nicholas Takahashi/Bloomberg](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3b981cc90b1481449a77ffa76a7d61eb/3b981cc9-0b14-8189-a358-f5265a65dc1f-0c217495b9471a30b8b17a997b5c45f3.png)

“CLA 的操控对于电动车来说不错，接近汽油车的感觉，但不如”一些中国车型，她说。最终，这对夫妇被其华为技术所吸引，购买了上汽的 Z7。

这一经历揭示了德国汽车制造商在中国的困境，这个市场曾经他们不费吹灰之力就能主导。大约在2023年，随着芯片和其他零部件短缺逐渐缓解，人为的高回报消散后，他们在中国的衰落开始显现。

正是在那时，本地制造商开始占据主导地位，他们既得到了国家的支持，也拥有更优越的电动车技术和软件。据大众汽车首席执行官奥博穆称，政府对行业的推动催生了约150个本土汽车品牌，仅这些品牌在今年上半年就推出了约500款新车型或改款车型。这就是外国汽车制造商难以匹敌的“中国速度”。

![Volkswagen AG CEO Oliver Blume. Photographer: Angel Garcia/Bloomberg](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3b981cc90b1481449a77ffa76a7d61eb/3b981cc9-0b14-8125-bccb-f9071c3510ae-0dfbd15c98a740b7c4edcb9da55d0ce6.png)

这一转变自今年年初以来加速，正在使德国汽车制造商失去回报，而这些回报有助于维持其在国内成本高昂的汽车生产。奥博穆在6月表示，大众的商业模式基本上已经破产。这番言论为他削减10万个工作岗位并关闭德国工厂的计划铺平了道路，但遭到强大工会领导人的坚决反对。

在中国，奥博穆正在押注与小鹏汽车和国有上汽集团的合作，以重振大众和奥迪品牌。大众-小鹏的首款新车型 ID.Unyx 08 刚刚上市，因此还很少有数据来评判其成功与否。中国奥迪子品牌的 E5 Sportback 已推出大约一年，尽管该车获得了[好评](https://www.audi.com/en/press-releases/audi-e5-sportback-named-2026-china-car-of-the-year-16954)，但其交付[表现平平](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-02/audi-s-no-rings-china-ev-gamble-is-falling-flat-with-drivers)。

![The Volkswagen ID.Unyx 08 electric SUV, developed in collaboration with Xpeng, on the production line at Volkswagen Anhui in Hefei, China. Source: Volkswagen AG](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3b981cc90b1481449a77ffa76a7d61eb/3b981cc9-0b14-81e8-bd33-d5ec26fd8757-fc3c89f807f4bf6d3b7cc7144c81ac83.png)

对于宝马来说，由于中国市场的下滑，该公司在6月将其预计的汽车制造利润率下调至[低至1%](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-16/bmw-cuts-profit-forecast-as-china-car-market-slump-accelerates)，而其主要考验即将到来。该公司即将在中国开展多年来最雄心勃勃的推广，推出两款重新设计的 Neue Klasse 车型：电动 i3 轿车和 iX3 SUV。后者将于8月21日在中国开启预售。这两款都是长轴距车型，迎合当地驾驶者的需求。

![图片](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3b981cc90b1481449a77ffa76a7d61eb/3b981cc9-0b14-81d5-83b7-c42ce5099f44-5a87ca07bd498dbeee8089ef740f7f4a.png)

该制造商已在 Neue Klasse 系列上投入超过100亿欧元，并在去年9月的首次亮相时辩称该技术[树立了新的标杆](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-09-05/bmw-starts-cashing-in-on-over-10-billion-investment-in-new-evs)。但近一年后，尚不清楚它能否在中国带来转机。

宝马尚未公布面向中国市场的iX3售价，这一车型被视为重新赢得中国年轻客户青睐的关键。为了具备竞争力，这款SUV的定价可能需要与特斯拉Model Y相当，后者起售价约为26.4万元人民币。在德国，宝马iX3基础版的售价几乎是这一价格的两倍。Model Y L是专为中国市场设计的更宽敞版本，起售价为33.9万元人民币。

![The BMW iX3 long-wheelbase all-electric SUV built for the Chinese market. Source: BMW Group](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3b981cc90b1481449a77ffa76a7d61eb/3b981cc9-0b14-8136-8ccc-c70785b4c5f5-ebe9b2b1de39acf4ae26001e4266aaaf.png)

虽然宝马在华改进产品的前景尚不明朗，但CLA等车型销售缓慢等早期迹象对德国品牌来说并非好兆头。即便是那些吸引欧洲驾驶者、适合长途高速巡航的高配版本，在中国也处境艰难，因为竞争对手正更快地推出同样高效的车型，并配备了迎合当地口味的丰富功能，如卡拉OK设备和可折叠平放的座椅以供车内露营。

当来自成都的32岁旅游业从业者Summer Chen选购新电动汽车时，梅赛德斯-奔驰是她的首选。她的家庭拥有一辆E300轿车约10年，她愿意多花一点钱继续选择这个历史悠久的德国品牌。但她在经销商处看CLA时，对这款车老旧的用户界面和笨拙的仪表盘屏幕感到失望。最终，她选择了特斯拉Model 3，理由是后者设计更优。

“智能功能并不是梅赛德斯的优势，”陈说。“无论这家公司推出什么，特斯拉、小米和华为也能做到。”

### English Original

## Even Mercedes Can’t Stop the Decline of German Cars in China

### **Takeaways**

- Mercedes-Benz Group AG partnered with McDonald's to promote its all-electric CLA in China, but the campaign was unsuccessful, with only 1,153 units sold in the first half.
- German carmakers, including Mercedes, BMW, and Volkswagen, are struggling in China due to high prices and a lack of competitive electric vehicle technology, with local manufacturers such as Xiaomi and BYD gaining market share.
- The German carmakers are trying to adapt to the Chinese market by partnering with local companies and introducing new models, but they face significant challenges, including a price war and a need to accelerate their development-to-market schedule to keep up with the fast-paced EV market in China.
After decades of winning over Chinese buyers with its German engineering, Mercedes-Benz Group AG took the humbling step last fall of partnering with fast-food chain McDonald’s to hype its newest car.

In the “[So Mc-Benz](https://www.instagram.com/p/DQthcOoktMn/?hl=de&img_index=5)” campaign, Mercedes allowed a cheeseburger figurine to take the spot of its traditional three-pointed star on the all-electric CLA, a sedan it hoped would stop sales from sliding in the world’s largest car market. The ads were supposed to make the vehicle popular with China’s young and hip.

It didn’t work. Mercedes sold only 1,153 units in China in the first half, a fraction of the more than 80,000 similarly priced SU7 sedans [Xiaomi Corp.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/1810%20HK%20Equity) delivered in the period. The performance echoes the challenges [BMW AG](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/BMW%20GR%20Equity), [Volkswagen AG](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/VOW%20GR%20Equity) and [Porsche AG](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/P911%20GR%20Equity) face in China, where they all reported second-quarter sales declines of at least 30%, worse than the overall market’s drop. Meanwhile, the likes of Xiaomi and [BYD Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/1211%20HK%20Equity) are taking customers from them, depriving the Germans of a key growth and profit driver. So far, none of them has found a way to arrest the downward spiral.

It’s not that they’re not trying. Most of them have partnered with Chinese companies to gain access to the latest EV technology and better understand what local drivers want. Mercedes built a long-wheelbase version of the CLA to appeal to Chinese tastes for a roomier back seat. It packed the car with software including AI-powered voice control and priced it from just 229,000 yuan ($33,943), roughly 40% cheaper than the European version.

Instead of winning clients, Mercedes had to concede that competing in China would require even further price cuts that would see it lose money on almost every electric CLA sold, according to people familiar with the matter. The company is limiting its push for the car until economics improve, the people said, asking not to be identified discussing internal commercial decisions.

Mercedes said the model was never meant to drive volume but rather show off the company’s latest technology in the hotly contested entry-level segment. The carmaker is “focusing on sustainable growth rather than purchasing short-term market share,” it said in an emailed statement. The company pointed to the electric GLC, a compact sport utility vehicle introduced in China last month, as doing well in terms of market feedback and pre-orders.

Chinese manufacturers, meanwhile, are willing to endure significant pain to win market share. BYD’s first-quarter profit [tumbled 55%](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-28/byd-posts-lowest-quarterly-profit-in-years-as-price-war-rages-on) to its lowest level in more than three years as the price war takes its toll. Geely Automobile Holdings Ltd.’s income also fell in the period.

At this point, significant parts of German carmakers’ offerings are simply priced out of China, according to JPMorgan analyst Jose Asumendi. While the overall Chinese market is down due to a protracted real estate crisis that’s weighing on spending, German companies are losing out more because their lineups tend to be pricier and feature more combustion-engine cars, a segment that’s in decline. BMW slashed its margin outlook due to the China slump, putting it on course to be the major European automaker this year. Managers including Mercedes Chief Executive Officer Ola Källenius expect the market to remain brutally competitive for years to come.

The problem isn’t only price. In many cases, German carmakers still operate on the development-to-market schedule of the gasoline era, refreshing products every four years or longer, and teasing snippets of new cars long before drivers can buy them. But in China, the EV market has come to resemble the frenzied pace of consumer electronics, with brands rejuvenating cars as fast as 18 months and having a new model ready for mass purchase right off the bat.

“And while it’s being sold, you’re already making the next iteration,” said [Xing Zhou](https://www.alixpartners.com/our-people/28404/xing-zhou/), an automotive adviser at AlixPartners who has worked in both Germany and China. “There’s no way that this industry will go back to the old way.”

That’s fueling a fundamental shift in brand perception in China, where Mercedes, Audi and BMW are losing their luxury edge because they’re perceived as trailing local manufacturers on software and EV technology.

Part of why the electric CLA is flopping is because rivals led by Xiaomi’s SU7 dominate the segment at that price point with more advanced automated-driving functionality, a mobile-phone first entertainment system and hipper branding, said Li Yanwei, who advises the China Automobile Dealers Association. The CLA’s sporty design tries to cater to a younger crowd, ignoring Mercedes’ traditional middle-aged customers who tend to have families and prefer bigger interiors, he added. The CLA “is in an awkward place where it’s not the most affordable and not the most luxurious,” Li said.

That’s kind of what Flora Li and her husband felt when they went shopping for their second car. They looked at a range of electric models including the CLA, the SU7, and the Z7 sedan China’s SAIC Motor Corp. is making in partnership with tech company Huawei Technologies Co. When testing the vehicles, the CLA’s automated-driving software didn’t convince her, and the car was slow in responding to voice commands, she said.

“The CLA’s handling was good for an EV and close to the feel of a gasoline car but just not as good as” some of the Chinese options, she said. Eventually, the couple bought SAIC’s Z7, persuaded by its Huawei-powered technology.

The experience shines a light on German carmakers’ struggles in China, a market they used to dominate with little effort. Their demise there started coming to the fore around 2023, after artificially high returns dissipated when shortages of chips and other components gradually eased.

That’s when local manufacturers, bolstered by state support but also superior EV technology and software, began to dominate. The government’s push of the industry has created some 150 domestic car brands, and they alone churned out some 500 new or updated models in the first half of the year, according to Volkswagen CEO Oliver Blume. It’s a “China Speed” foreign carmakers struggle to match.

The shift, which has accelerated since the start of this year, is starving the German carmakers of returns that help keep costly car production going in their home country. Blume in June said Volkswagen’s business model was essentially broken. The comments paved the way for his plan to cut 100,000 jobs and shutter German factories, triggering staunch opposition from powerful labor leaders.

In China, Blume is betting on partnerships with Xpeng Inc. and state-owned SAIC to rejuvenate the VW and Audi brands. The first of the new VW-Xpeng models, the ID.Unyx 08, went on sale only recently, meaning there’s little data to judge its success. Deliveries of the Chinese AUDI sub-brand’s E5 Sportback, which has been out for roughly a year, have been [underwhelming](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-02/audi-s-no-rings-china-ev-gamble-is-falling-flat-with-drivers) despite the car garnering [critical acclaim](https://www.audi.com/en/press-releases/audi-e5-sportback-named-2026-china-car-of-the-year-16954).

For BMW, which in June slashed its projected carmaking margin to [as low as 1%](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-16/bmw-cuts-profit-forecast-as-china-car-market-slump-accelerates) because of the decline in China, the main test is around the corner. The company is about to embark on its most ambitious push in the country in years with the first two of its revamped Neue Klasse models, the electric i3 sedan and the iX3 SUV. The latter will open for pre-sales in China on Aug. 21. Both are long-wheelbase models catering to local drivers.

The manufacturer has spent more than €10 billion on the Neue Klasse line, arguing at the initial unveiling last September that the technology was [setting new benchmarks](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-09-05/bmw-starts-cashing-in-on-over-10-billion-investment-in-new-evs). But nearly a year later, it’s unclear whether it can usher in a turnaround in China.

BMW hasn’t yet released a price for the Chinese iX3, seen key to win back favor with the country’s younger customers. To be competitive, the SUV probably would need to get a comparable sticker than Tesla’s Model Y, which starts at around 264,000 yuan. In Germany, BMW charges nearly twice for the iX3’s base version. The Model Y L, a roomier version designed specifically for the Chinese market, costs from 339,000 yuan.

While the jury is still out on BMW’s improved offering in China, early signs like the slow sales of the CLA don’t bode well for the Germans. Even high-end versions that appeal to European drivers looking for long Autobahn rides are struggling in China as competitors introduce equally efficient models faster, brimming with features catering to local tastes, like karaoke machines and seats that fold flat for car camping.

When Summer Chen, a 32-year-old tourism worker from Chengdu, went shopping for a new EV, Mercedes was at the top of her mind. Her family had owned an E300 sedan for around 10 years and she was ready to pay slightly more to stick with the venerable German brand. But when she checked out the CLA at a dealership, she was disappointed by what she felt was an old-fashioned user interface and clunky dashboard screens. In the end, she went for Tesla’s Model 3, citing its superior design.

“Smart features aren’t an advantage for Mercedes,” Chen said. “Whatever the company comes up with, Tesla, Xiaomi and Huawei can too.”
