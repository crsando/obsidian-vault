## 中国关键钢材价格因建筑业低迷跌至近十年低点

作者：[Katharine Gemmell](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AUtYLUwJKY0/katharine-gemmell) 2026年8月7日 14:05 GMT+8

### **要点** 由彭博AI提供

- 中国一种关键钢材产品的价格已跌至近十年来的最低点，原因是长期的房地产低迷削弱了建筑需求，并导致库存膨胀。
- 中国使用全球约一半的钢铁，但由于长期建筑业低迷拖累了最大的钢铁消费领域，需求依然疲软。
- 螺纹钢价格下跌，加上焦煤和焦炭成本上升，也导致5月底以来螺纹钢生产商的利润率收缩。
中国一种关键钢材产品的价格已跌至近十年来的最低点，原因是长期的房地产低迷削弱了建筑需求，并导致库存膨胀。

根据北京安泰科信息科技公司的地区均价，现货螺纹钢周五连续第49个交易日下跌至每吨3142元（465.63美元），为2016年11月以来最低。螺纹钢是中国传统钢铁市场的关键指标，因其在房地产和基础设施项目中广泛使用。

“中国房地产市场仍处于低迷状态，”BMI（惠誉解决方案旗下）大宗商品分析主管Sabrin Chowdhury表示。“7月政治局会议未给黑色金属市场带来任何喘息之机，因为政府表现出对较低经济增长轨迹日益容忍的迹象。”

![图片](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3b581cc90b1481159d98d6e908600d8a/3b581cc9-0b14-81b8-bff3-e0b33ccd2f34-e9144190173cd23c01e846a7aa924162.png)

中国消耗了全球约一半的钢铁，但[需求依然疲软](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-22/chinese-steel-market-enters-long-plateau-after-property-crash)，因为长期建筑低迷拖累了这一最大消费来源。全球第二大经济体的房屋销售可能继续下滑（中国楼市销量和价格三季度或继续下滑，摩根士丹利称），摩根士丹利上月表示。

中国钢铁工业协会追踪的钢企螺纹钢库存截至7月31日升至420万吨，环比增长9%，进一步印证了消费未能跟上供应的迹象。

麦格理集团大宗商品策略师弗洛伦斯·孙（Florence Sun）表示：“尽管我们多年来听说钢厂已将生产转向其他钢材产品，而非螺纹钢，但这一调整的速度似乎滞后。”

她补充称，螺纹钢价格下跌，加之炼焦煤和焦炭成本上升，也导致自5月底以来螺纹钢厂的利润率出现收缩。

Mysteel本周发布的数据显示，受调查的247家钢厂中，盈利比率连续第五周下滑，降至32%，较去年同期低36个百分点。不过，本周钢铁生产出现一些改善的迹象。

## Key China Steel Price Hits Near-Decade Low on Construction Slump

By [Katharine Gemmell](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AUtYLUwJKY0/katharine-gemmell)August 7, 2026 at 2:05 PM GMT+8

The price of a key Chinese steel product has dropped to the lowest in almost a decade as a prolonged property downturn sapped construction demand and swelled inventories.

[Spot rebar](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CDSPDRAV%20Index) fell for a 49th straight day to 3,142 yuan ($465.63) a ton on Friday, the lowest since November 2016, according to an average of regional prices from Beijing Antaike Information Technology Co. Reinforcement bar — or rebar — is a crucial gauge of China’s traditional steel market because of its widespread use in real estate and infrastructure projects.

“The Chinese property market remains in the doldrums,” said Sabrin Chowdhury, commodities analysis head at BMI — a unit of Fitch Solutions. “The July Politburo meeting offered no reprieve for ferrous metal markets as the government showed increasing signs of tolerance toward a lower economic growth trajectory.”

China uses about half of the world’s steel, but demand has [remained weak](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-22/chinese-steel-market-enters-long-plateau-after-property-crash) as a prolonged construction slump is weighing on the biggest source of consumption. Home sales in the world’s second-biggest economy may continue to China’s Home Sales, Prices May Keep Declining in 3Q, MS Says in the third quarter this year, Morgan Stanley said last month.

Rebar [inventories](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/IOCISIRR%20Index) at steelmakers tracked by the China Iron and Steel Association climbed to 4.2 million tons as of July 31, up 9% over the previous month, reinforcing signs that consumption is failing to keep pace with supply.

“While we have heard that mills’ have shifted production toward other steel products and away from rebar over the years, it appears that the speed of this adjustment is lagging,” said Florence Sun, commodities strategist at [Macquarie Group](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/MQG%20AU%20Equity).

The decline in the rebar price, along with rising costs of coking coal and coke, has also led to a contraction in profit margins for rebar mills since late-May, she added.

The profitability rate among the 247 mills surveyed declined for a fifth consecutive week, falling to 32%, or 36 percentage points below the same period last year, Mysteel data released this week showed. Still, there are signs of some improvement in steel production this week.
