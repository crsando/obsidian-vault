### 中文译文

## 据悉中国富嘉集团考虑出售和谐健康保险

据知情人士透露，中国最大的私营企业集团之一正考虑出售旗下的[和谐健康保险股份有限公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1638009D:CH)业务。

[富嘉集团股份有限公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1693049D:CH)正处于考虑出售其所持这家中国保险公司多数股权的早期阶段，上述知情人士因讨论私下事宜而要求匿名。

知情人士称，谈判尚处于早期阶段，可能不会达成任何交易。富嘉和和谐健康的代表未回应置评请求。

和谐健康在全球第二人口大国提供从疾病管理到残障护理和意外险等各项服务。根据其企业社会责任[报告](https://www.hexiehealth.com/docs/2026-06/20260629111743555684.pdf)，该公司2025年实现营业收入约750亿元人民币（合110亿美元）。其[资产](https://www.hexiehealth.com/docs/2025-06/20250630171605562302.pdf)超过4500亿元人民币。

和谐健康成立于2006年，在其前母公司[安邦保险集团股份有限公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1083236D:CH)被国家接管后进行了重组。业务涵盖石化、房地产等多个领域的富嘉，于2019年同意收购安邦计划出售的该健康保险部门51%的控股权，其余股份由其他国有及私募股权公司接手。

该保险公司的股权投资组合近期带来了丰厚收益。其最引人注目的成功之一源于2022年初对[CXMT Corp.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/688825%20CH%20Equity)（长鑫存储）的20亿元人民币投资。这家存储芯片制造商在7月的[轰动性](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-13/cxmt-overtakes-tencent-to-become-most-valuable-chinese-company)首次公开募股后成为中国市值最高的公司。和谐健康还是图形处理器制造商[摩尔线程科技有限公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/688795%20CH%20Equity)和计算基础设施提供商[超聚变数字技术有限公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/2603676D:CH)的早期支持者，彰显了其聚焦科技的投资策略。

然而，该保险公司一直在推迟披露文件。根据其4月份的最新[公告](https://www.hexiehealth.com/docs/2026-04/20260430150450778611.pdf)，自2017年以来，该公司一直未能发布强制性的年度财务报告或偿付能力声明。

根据[官网](http://www.fujiagroup.com/)，总部位于东北港口城市大连的富嘉集团拥有五个业务板块，包括物业管理和石化。

### English Original

## China’s Fujia Is Said to Consider Sale of Hexie Health Insurance

One of China’s largest privately held conglomerates is considering selling its [Hexie Health Insurance Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1638009D:CH) division, according to people familiar with the matter.

[Fujia Group Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1693049D:CH) is in the early stages of weighing a potential sale of its majority stake in the Chinese insurer, the people said, asking not to be identified discussing a private matter.

Talks are at an early stage and may not lead to any transaction, the people said. Representatives for Fujia and Hexie Health didn’t respond to requests for comment.

Hexie Health provides services ranging from disease management to disability care and accident insurance in the world’s second-most populous country. The insurer reported operating revenue of about 75 billion yuan ($11 billion) in 2025, according to its corporate social responsibility [report](https://www.hexiehealth.com/docs/2026-06/20260629111743555684.pdf). It has [assets](https://www.hexiehealth.com/docs/2025-06/20250630171605562302.pdf) exceeding 450 billion yuan.

Founded in 2006, Hexie Health underwent an overhaul after its former parent company, [Anbang Insurance Group Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1083236D:CH), was placed under state receivership. Fujia, whose businesses range from petrochemicals to real estate, agreed to Anbang Plans to Sell Health-Insurance Arm to Fujia Group, Othersa 51% controlling stake in 2019, while other state-owned and private equity firms took up the rest.

The insurer’s equity portfolio has recently yielded big windfalls. One of its most notable triumphs stems from a 2 billion yuan investment in [CXMT Corp.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/688825%20CH%20Equity) in early 2022. The memory-chip manufacturer became China’s most valuable company after its [blockbuster](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-13/cxmt-overtakes-tencent-to-become-most-valuable-chinese-company)initial public offering in July. Hexie Health was also an early backer of graphics processing unit maker [Moore Threads Technology Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/688795%20CH%20Equity) and computing infrastructure provider [Xfusion Digital Technologies Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/2603676D:CH), highlighting its tech-focused investment strategy.

However, the insurer has been postponing disclosure documents. It has failed to publish mandatory annual financial reports or solvency statements since 2017, according to its latest [announcement](https://www.hexiehealth.com/docs/2026-04/20260430150450778611.pdf) in April.

Fujia, which is based in the northeast port city of Dalian, has five business segments, including property management and petrochemicals, according to its [website](http://www.fujiagroup.com/).
