### 中文译文

## 据报道，习近平访美期间将缺席联合国会议

据Politico报道，中国领导人习近平下月访美与美国总统唐纳德·特朗普会晤期间，将不会顺访纽约在联合国大会上发表讲话。

Politico援引四位未透露姓名的知情人士的话称，习近平计划对美国进行为期三天的访问，于9月24日与特朗普会晤，其中一位[知情人士表示](https://www.politico.com/news/2026/08/17/xi-jinping-skip-unga-trump-summit-01039237)。

北京外交部发言人在一份声明中表示，中国参加联合国大会活动的安排将适时公布。

此前有猜测称，习近平将与其他世界领导人一起出席联合国会议，作为其美国之行的一部分。特朗普在5月访问北京期间邀请习近平访美。

双方利用春季峰会稳定了因贸易、科技、台湾和其他问题而恶化的关系。尽管未达成重大协议，但习近平称此次访问是双边关系中的“历史性”里程碑。

### English Original

## China’s Xi to Skip UN Meeting During Trip to US, Report Says

Chinese leader Xi Jinping will forgo a stop in New York to address the UN General Assembly during a trip to the US next month to meet President Donald Trump, Politico reported.

Politico cited four people familiar with the matter whom it didn’t identify. Xi plans to visit the US for three days, meeting Trump on Sept. 24, one of [the people said](https://www.politico.com/news/2026/08/17/xi-jinping-skip-unga-trump-summit-01039237).

Arrangements for China’s participation in the UN General Assembly event will be announced in due course, a spokesperson for the Foreign Ministry in Beijing said in a statement.

There’d been speculation that Xi would join other world leaders at the UN meeting as part of his US visit. Trump invited Xi to visit during his trip to Beijing in May.

The two sides used their spring summit to steady ties that had deteriorated over trade, tech, Taiwan and other issues. Although no major deals were reached, Xi declared the visit a “historic” landmark in bilateral ties.
