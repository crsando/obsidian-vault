## 中国将供应印度化肥招标的大部分，这是一笔重大交易

作者：[Hallie Gu](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AVfpuo_aA7w/hallie-gu) 和 [Pratik Parija](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/ACT1hUlVowE/pratik-parija) 2026年8月27日 下午4:20 GMT+8

### **要点** 由彭博AI

- 中国预计将向印度运送至少120万吨关键化肥品种，这意味着在年初北京放松出口管制后，这个全球最大生产国的销售量有所增加。
- 这些货物是印度最近一次尿素招标的一部分，尿素是一种广泛使用的氮肥，该数量至少占预订总量的三分之二。
- 这笔交易表明尿素市场的紧张状况正在改善，此前伊朗战争严重扰乱了中东的供应，这种关键作物营养素的价格在4月份飙升至四年高位。
中国预计将向印度运送至少120万吨关键化肥品种，这意味着在年初北京放松出口管制后，这个全球最大生产国的销售量有所增加。

这些货物是印度尿素招标的一部分（“化肥招标：印度购买177万吨尿素，超出预期”），尿素是一种广泛使用的氮肥。据知情人士透露，因未获授权对媒体发言，他们要求匿名。该数量至少占预订总量的三分之二，货物（“化肥招标：印度RCF寻求进口170万吨尿素”）将于9月24日前离开装货港。

印度化肥部的一位发言人拒绝置评。

印度是全球最大的尿素进口国之一，其采购动向备受关注，被视为全球基准。这笔交易进一步表明，尿素市场的紧张状况正在缓解。今年早些时候，伊朗战争严重扰乱了来自中东（主要出口地区）的供应。[关键作物养分价格](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/GCFPURGB%20Index)在4月飙升至四年高点，但此后已回吐涨幅。

这在一定程度上是因为中国的政策变化。北京在战争开始前后[收紧化肥出口管制](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-16/china-reins-in-fertilizer-exports-as-war-pushes-up-global-prices)以保障当地农民的供应。当局随后放宽了限制，发布了尿素初始配额，并于5月宣布中国化肥出口窗口开启，尿素可售。

据知情人士透露，自那以来几个月内，配额已增至约500万至550万吨，这意味着计划用于印度招标的数量将占总量的五分之一以上。他们估计，自配额开放以来，至少还有100万吨已被承诺，其中一位人士甚至认为高达200万吨。

中国海关数据显示，6月和7月约出口了41万吨尿素。

印度使用的尿素比美国和巴西的总和还多，这得益于政府补贴，使农民支付的价格远低于市场水平。这个南亚国家是全球最大的大米出口国，也是小麦、糖和棉花的主要生产国，因此化肥供应对其农业经济至关重要。

## China to Supply Bulk of India’s Fertilizer Tender in Major Sale

By [Hallie Gu](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AVfpuo_aA7w/hallie-gu) and [Pratik Parija](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/ACT1hUlVowE/pratik-parija)August 27, 2026 at 4:20 PM GMT+8

### **Takeaways ** by Bloomberg AI

- China is expected to ship at least 1.2 million tons of a key fertilizer variety to India, marking a ramp-up in sales from the world’s top producer after Beijing loosened export controls earlier this year.
- The shipments are part of India’s most recent tender for urea, a widely-used nitrogen fertilizer, with the volume accounting for at least two-thirds of the total quantity booked.
- The trade is a sign that tightness in the urea market is improving, after the war in Iran significantly disrupted flows from the Middle East and prices for the key crop nutrient surged to a four-year high in April.
China is expected to ship at least 1.2 million tons of a key fertilizer variety to India, marking a ramp-up in sales from the world’s top producer after Beijing loosened export controls earlier this year.

The shipments are part of India’s FERTILIZER TENDER: India Buys 1.77M Tons Urea, More Than Sought for urea, a widely-used nitrogen fertilizer, according to people familiar with the matter, who asked not to be identified as they are not authorized to speak to the media. The volume would account for at least two-thirds of the total quantity booked, with shipments FERTILIZER TENDER: India’s RCF Seeks to Import 1.7M Tons of Urea to leave loading ports by Sept. 24.

A spokesperson for India’s fertilizer ministry declined to comment.

India is one of the biggest urea importers, making its purchases closely watched as a global benchmark. The trade is a further sign that tightness in the urea market is improving, after the war in Iran significantly disrupted flows from the Middle East — a major export region — earlier in the year. [Prices for the key crop nutrient](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/GCFPURGB%20Index) surged to a four-year high in April, but have since erased the rally.

That’s partly due to policy changes in China. Beijing [tightened control](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-16/china-reins-in-fertilizer-exports-as-war-pushes-up-global-prices) over fertilizer exports near the start of the war to protect supplies for local farmers. Authorities later loosened the restrictions by releasing an initial quota for urea, China's Fertilizer Export Window Opens With Urea for Sale said in May.

The quota has been increased in the months since to about 5 to 5.5 million tons, meaning the volume slated for the India tender would mark more than a fifth of the total, according to people familiar. They estimated that at least another 1 million tons has already been committed since the quota was opened, with one pegging it as high as 2 million tons.

China customs data shows about 410,000 tons of urea was exported in June and July.

India uses more urea than in America and Brazil combined, helped by government subsidies that keep prices for farmers far below market rates. The South Asian nation is the world’s biggest rice exporter and a major producer of wheat, sugar and cotton, making fertilizer availability crucial to its agricultural economy.
