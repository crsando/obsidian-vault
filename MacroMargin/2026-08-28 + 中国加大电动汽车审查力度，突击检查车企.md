### 中文译文

## 中国加大电动汽车审查力度，突击检查车企

*中国电动汽车生产。*
*摄影师：Qilai Shen/彭博*

彭博新闻社
2026年8月28日下午6:00（GMT+8）

### **要点**

- 监管机构将重点检查电动汽车的耐用性、生产一致性以及驾驶辅助和人工智能功能等领域。
- 这些机构正聚焦结构设计，以评估汽车是否以对驾驶者直观易操作且能承受极端场景的方式制造。
- 被认定未达标的企业或收到大量消费者投诉的制造商，可能面临生产许可被暂停或强制召回的风险。
中国正在加大对汽车生产标准的审查力度，因北京加强了对快速开发周期的监管，这些周期支撑了国内价格战，并引发了对车辆安全的担忧。

根据中国四个最高机构（包括工业和信息化部和国家市场监督管理总局）发布的[联合指令](https://www.miit.gov.cn/zwgk/zcwj/wjfb/tz/art/2026/art_8d0f546dabb440189c0ceea0bea099fc.html)，监管机构将重点检查电动汽车的耐用性、生产一致性以及驾驶辅助和人工智能功能等领域。

上述机构表示，他们还针对结构设计进行评估，以评估汽车是否以驾驶员操作直观且能承受极端场景的方式制造。这与对电池、高级驾驶辅助技术和门把手实施[更严格标准](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-21/tesla-recalls-3-million-evs-as-china-cracks-down-on-door-handles)的做法一致。

此次整治反映出政策制定者的担忧，即中国极度压缩的开发时间（新车型在不到两年内推向市场）可能诱使制造商在安全和测试方面偷工减料。随着[中国汽车制造商](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2026-03-23/china-s-ev-boom-sees-byd-geely-leapmotor-rewriting-global-auto-industry-rules)迅速扩张至欧洲、拉丁美洲和东南亚，安全丑闻或其他质量问题可能会损害其声誉，并有可能破坏该行业主导海外市场的更广泛雄心。

新的检查制度将包括对组装厂、零部件供应商和经销商的突击抽查。第三方检测实验室也面临检查，这些实验室一直面临巨大压力以加快审批。任何被发现出具虚假报告或忽视设计风险的公司都将面临立即吊销执照。

根据新规，被认定未达到标准的公司或收到大量消费者投诉的制造商，其生产许可可能被暂停，或面临强制召回。政府还可以让当地交警暂停车辆注册，从而有效停止向客户交付车辆。

监管机构还要求汽车制造商自己对车辆的可靠性和耐久性进行检查。

## China’s EV Scrutiny Grows With Surprise Checks for Carmakers

*Electric vehicle production in China.*  
*Photographer: Qilai Shen/Bloomberg*

By Bloomberg News  
August 28, 2026 at 6:00 PM GMT+8

### **Takeaways**

- Regulators will focus inspections on areas including durability and production consistency, as well as driver-assistance and artificial intelligence features in electric vehicles.
- The agencies are targeting structural designs to assess whether cars are built in a way that’s intuitive for drivers to operate and can withstand extreme scenarios.
- Companies determined to have fallen short of standards or manufacturers that receive numerous consumer complaints risk having their production approval suspended or face mandatory recalls.
China is intensifying scrutiny of car production standards as Beijing tightens oversight of rapid development cycles that have underpinned a domestic price war and sparked concerns about vehicle safety.

Regulators will focus inspections on areas including durability and production consistency, as well as driver-assistance and artificial intelligence features in electric vehicles, according to a [joint directive](https://www.miit.gov.cn/zwgk/zcwj/wjfb/tz/art/2026/art_8d0f546dabb440189c0ceea0bea099fc.html) from four of China’s top bodies, including the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology and the State Administration for Market Regulation.

The agencies are also targeting structural designs to assess whether cars are built in a way that’s intuitive for drivers to operate and can withstand extreme scenarios, it said. That’s in line with the imposition of [tougher standards](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-21/tesla-recalls-3-million-evs-as-china-cracks-down-on-door-handles) on batteries, advanced driver-assistance technology and door handles.

The clampdown reflects concern among policymakers that the ultra-compressed development times in China, where new models are brought to market in under two years, may tempt manufacturers to cut corners on safety and testing. With [Chinese automakers](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2026-03-23/china-s-ev-boom-sees-byd-geely-leapmotor-rewriting-global-auto-industry-rules) rapidly expanding into Europe, Latin America and Southeast Asia, a safety scandal or other quality issues could tarnish their reputation and risk derailing the industry’s broader ambitions to dominate overseas markets.

The new inspection regime will include surprise spot checks of assembly plants, component suppliers and dealerships. Third-party testing laboratories, which have faced immense pressure fast-track approvals, will also be inspected. Any company caught issuing fraudulent reports or ignoring design risks faces immediate license revocation.

Under the new mandate, companies determined to have fallen short of standards or manufacturers that receive numerous consumer complaints risk having their production approval suspended or face mandatory recalls. The government can also have local traffic police suspend vehicle registrations, effectively halting customer deliveries.

Regulators also ordered carmakers to conduct their own inspections into vehicle reliability and durability.
