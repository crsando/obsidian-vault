## 创纪录空头押注瞄准中国AI模型双雄 竞争激烈

作者：[Jeanny Yu](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/ASf81kJXKek/jeanny-yu) 与 [Elaine Lai](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AYT9sIQvMns/elaine-lai)
2026年8月26日 上午7:30 GMT+8

### **要点** 来自Bloomberg AI

- 在财报公布之前，针对Z.AI Co.和MiniMax Group Inc.股票的看空押注已飙升至创纪录水平，凸显出投资者对中国AI竞争白热化的担忧日益加剧。
- 根据标普全球（S&P Global）的数据，空头头寸已升至占MiniMax自由流通股的20%，以及Z.AI的6%。
- MiniMax将于周三晚些时候发布业绩，智谱（Zhipu）则将于下周一发布，这将有助于显示两家公司迄今为止如何应对挑战。
针对Z.AI Co.和MiniMax Group Inc.股票的看空押注已飙升至创纪录水平，在财报公布前凸显出投资者对中国AI竞争白热化的担忧。

根据标普全球的数据，空头头寸已升至占MiniMax自由流通股的20%，以及Z.AI（智谱）的6%。即使在这两只港股从今年早些时候轰动上市后的历史高位回落之后，做空活动仍在加剧。

作为全球仅有的上市纯AI模型开发商，智谱和MiniMax在中国因廉价但高质量的人工智能而声名鹊起之际获得了关注。然而，随着来自阿里巴巴集团控股有限公司、DeepSeek和Moonshot AI Inc.在定价和技术上的竞争加剧，投资者的追捧已经降温。

“中国价格更低的开源模型正将前沿人工智能能力普及至全球——这可能挑战关于单一模型稀缺价值的假设，”法国巴黎银行资产管理公司大中华区股票主管 David Choa 表示。“鉴于模型开发速度和商业模式的持续演变，我们认为现在确定永久赢家还为时过早。”

智谱股价自1月首次公开募股以来仍上涨超过800%，MiniMax 上涨超过80%，但两者均较峰值水平下跌超过一半。卖出的触发因素包括 MiniMax 6月对其 M3 模型降价，以及上月 Moonshot 发布先进的 [Kimi K3](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-27/china-s-moonshot-to-release-breakthrough-ai-model-for-download) 对智谱 GLM 构成的威胁。

智谱近期推出的 [GLM-5.3](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-14/z-ai-aims-to-catch-anthropic-openai-in-coding-with-new-ai-model) 并未提振其股价，尽管杰富瑞金融集团分析师表示，其性能与 Kimi K3 相当，且每个任务成本低约19%。

内地投资者对这两家公司表现出支持，尽管他们的大量买入尚未引发反弹。香港交易所数据显示，自智谱纳入港股通以来不到三个月，内地投资者持股比例已升至12%，而 MiniMax 被纳入后仅两周，内地投资者持股比例便迅速升至8.1%。

本月早些时候，DeepSeek 出人意料地提高价格，尽管此举将如何影响行业未来的盈利能力仍有待观察。

MiniMax 将于周三晚些时候公布业绩，智谱将于下周一公布，这将有助于显示两家公司迄今如何应对挑战。根据彭博数据，智谱上半年收入预计环比增长约153%，但调整后净亏损预计扩大。

“投资者越来越担心价格战，这正在阻碍智谱提高定价的能力”及其利润率，Hedgeye Risk Management 科技行业主管 Felix Wang 表示。对于 MiniMax，其产品“陷入中间地带——既不是最智能的模型，也不是最便宜的。”

## Record Short Bets Hit China’s AI Model Duo on Fierce Competition

By [Jeanny Yu](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/ASf81kJXKek/jeanny-yu) and [Elaine Lai](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AYT9sIQvMns/elaine-lai)
August 26, 2026 at 7:30 AM GMT+8

### **Takeaways **by Bloomberg AI

- Bearish bets against shares of Z.AI Co. and MiniMax Group Inc. have soared to records ahead of their earnings reports, highlighting growing investor worry over China’s heated AI rivalry.
- Short interest has climbed to account for 20% of the free float for MiniMax and 6% for Z.AI, according to data from S&P Global.
- Results due later Wednesday from MiniMax and next Monday from Zhipu will help show how the firms have weathered the challenges so far.
Bearish bets against shares of Z.AI Co. and MiniMax Group Inc. have soared to records ahead of their earnings reports, highlighting growing investor worry over China’s heated AI rivalry.

Short interest has climbed to account for 20% of the free float for MiniMax and 6% for Z.AI, or Zhipu, according to data from S&P Global. Short selling ramped up even after the Hong Kong-listed stocks tumbled from record highs following the pair’s blockbuster listings early this year.

As the world’s only listed pure-play model developers, Zhipu and MiniMax garnered attention as China built a reputation for cheap but good-quality AI. Investor infatuation has moderated, however, as competition on pricing and technology from [Alibaba Group Holding Ltd.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/BABA%20US%20Equity), DeepSeek and Moonshot AI Inc. intensifies.

“Lower-cost, open-source models from China are democratizing frontier AI capabilities globally — that may challenge assumptions around the scarcity value of individual models,” said David Choa, head of Greater China equities at BNP Paribas Asset Management. “Given the speed of model development and the continued evolution of business models, we believe identifying permanent winners today would be premature.”

Zhipu shares are still up more than 800% and MiniMax’s are up more than 80% since their January initial public offerings, though both have more than halved from peak levels. Triggers for selling have included MiniMax’s price cuts for its M3 model in June, and the threat to Zhipu’s GLM from last month’s release of Moonshot’s advanced [Kimi K3](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-27/china-s-moonshot-to-release-breakthrough-ai-model-for-download).

Zhipu’s recent launch of [GLM-5.3](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-14/z-ai-aims-to-catch-anthropic-openai-in-coding-with-new-ai-model) hasn’t helped its shares, although Jefferies Financial Group analysts say it offers comparable performance to Kimi K3 at about 19% lower cost per task.

Mainland investors have shown support for the pair, though their heavy buying has yet to spark a rebound. Their stake in Zhipu has climbed to 12% in less than three months since joining Stock Connect, while their holding in MiniMax quickly rose to 8.1% within just two weeks of its inclusion, according to Hong Kong exchange data.

DeepSeek surprisingly moved to [hike prices](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-06/deepseek-plans-significant-price-increase-for-its-ai-services) earlier this month, though it remains to be seen how this will impact industry profitability going forward.

Results due later Wednesday from MiniMax and next Monday from Zhipu will help show how the firms have weathered the challenges so far. Zhipu’s first-half revenue is expected to have risen about 153% sequentially, but its adjusted net loss is expected to have widened, according to data from Bloomberg.

“Investors are increasingly concerned about the price war, which is hampering Zhipu’s ability to raise pricing” and its profit margins, said Felix Wang, tech sector head at Hedgeye Risk Management. For MiniMax, its products are “stuck in the middle - not the most intelligent model, nor the cheapest.”
