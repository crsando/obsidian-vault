## 中国将继续对印度单模光纤进口征收反倾销税

![Shanghai Cooperation Organisation summit in Tianjin](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3bb81cc90b1481efaba9d0f8f84b4781/3bb81cc9-0b14-81e2-a5ba-f4b5f3333c26-9e721c29d1056e47260cd69a49a3bd18.jpg)

中国国旗和印度国旗在天津梅江会展中心旁飘扬，该中心是2025年上海合作组织（SCO）峰会的举办地，摄于2025年8月30日。路透社/Maxim Shemetov [购买许可权，打开新标签页](https://www.reutersconnect.com/item/shanghai-cooperation-organisation-summit-in-tianjin/dGFnOnJldXRlcnMuY29tLDIwMjU6bmV3c21sX1JDMkpIR0EyQlVGRA%3D%3D/?utm_medium=rcom-article-media&utm_campaign=rcom-rcp-lead)

北京，8月13日（路透社）——中国商务部周四表示，自周五起，中国将继续对从印度进口的单模光纤征收反倾销税，为期五年。

该决定是在一年前启动的日落复审后做出的，复审结论认为，终止反倾销措施可能导致倾销行为持续或再次发生，并损害中国国内光纤产业。

税率与以往裁决相比保持不变，不同印度出口商的税率在7.4%至30.6%之间，其中Sterlite Technologies（[(STTE.NS)，打开新标签页](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/STTE.NS)）的税率为7.4%，Aksh Optifibre（[(AKSO.NS)，打开新标签页](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/AKSO.NS)）和Finolex Cables（[(FNXC.NS)，打开新标签页](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/FNXC.NS)）的税率均为30.6%。

中国于2014年8月首次对印度单模光纤实施反倾销措施，并于2020年将这些措施延长五年。

报道：Shi Bu和Liz Lee；编辑：Christian Schmollinger

## China to keep imposing anti-dumping duties on Indian single-mode optical fibre imports

National flags of China and India fly next to the Meijiang Convention and Exhibition Center, a venue for 2025 Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO) summit in Tianjin, China August 30, 2025. REUTERS/Maxim Shemetov [Purchase Licensing Rights, opens new tab](https://www.reutersconnect.com/item/shanghai-cooperation-organisation-summit-in-tianjin/dGFnOnJldXRlcnMuY29tLDIwMjU6bmV3c21sX1JDMkpIR0EyQlVGRA%3D%3D/?utm_medium=rcom-article-media&utm_campaign=rcom-rcp-lead)

BEIJING, Aug 13 (Reuters) - China will continue imposing anti-dumping duties on single-mode optical fibre imports from ‌India for another five years starting on Friday, the commerce ministry said on Thursday.

The decision follows a sunset review initiated ⁠a year ago, which concluded that terminating the anti-dumping measures could lead to the continuation or recurrence of dumping and harm to China's domestic optical fibre industry.

The tax rates will remain unchanged ‌from ⁠previous rulings, ranging between 7.4% and 30.6% across different Indian exporters, including Sterlite Technologies [(STTE.NS), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/STTE.NS) with 7.4% rate and ⁠Aksh Optifibre [(AKSO.NS), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/AKSO.NS) and Finolex Cables [(FNXC.NS), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/FNXC.NS) both get 30.6%.

China first implemented anti-dumping duties ⁠on Indian single-mode optical fibre in August 2014 and extended ⁠them for five years in 2020.
