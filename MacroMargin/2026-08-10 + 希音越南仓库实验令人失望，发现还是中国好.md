### 中文译文

## 希音越南仓库实验令人失望，发现还是中国好

- 摘要
- 公司
- 消息人士称，希音在租赁大型物流中心一年后大幅缩减越南业务
- 美国“最低限度”免税漏洞终结削弱越南作为出口基地的作用
- 即将上市的希音承诺投入超15亿美元建设广东供应链体系
- 随着美国需求降温，一些希音国内供应商另寻出路
河内/中国广州，8月10日（路透社）——就在一年多前，中国超快时尚零售商希音开始在胡志明市附近租赁15公顷的仓库设施——相当于21个足球场的面积——这是其将越南打造为主要出口基地的宏大实验的一部分。

在2024年底制定这些计划时，这似乎是一个虽有风险但值得一搏的赌注。

支撑其商业模式的美国对小额包裹从中国进口的关税豁免政策看似将被取消，唐纳德·特朗普刚刚赢得第二个美国总统任期，而贸易战升级的担忧很快成为现实——到 2025 年 4 月，美国对许多中国商品的关税飙升至 145%。

希音开始鼓励其最大的中国供应商在越南建立制造基地。

但事情并未按计划进行。

如今，即将进行首次公开募股（IPO）的希音，以销售5美元上衣和10美元连衣裙而闻名，正在大幅缩减其在越南的业务，六位熟悉其当地业务的人士如此表示。

这个保税[物流枢纽](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/shein-set-up-huge-vietnam-warehouse-us-tariff-hedge-sources-say-2025-05-15/)占地15公顷，是该国同类中最大的，曾雇佣数千人。据其中两位消息人士称，目前的租赁面积仅为6公顷。另一位直接知情人表示，原计划用地的三分之一正在使用。

仓库工人表示，大规模裁员从4月开始，预计还会有更多裁员，一些团队只保留了四分之一的员工，而其他团队裁员更多。路透社在7月底到访现场时，现场只有少数工人，仓库里只停着几辆卡车。而由其他租户运营的邻近仓库则一片繁忙。

希音未回应路透社的置评请求。

希音在越南的突然转向此前未被报道，反映出美国贸易政策的急剧变化，同时凸显了希音商业模式对中国供应商的依赖程度——这些供应商能够忍受其他国家的制造商不愿接受的条款。这一模式也已在[巴西](https://www.reuters.com/business/retail-consumer/shein-tried-turn-brazil-into-production-hub-local-factories-walked-away-2026-02-04/)上演。

这也凸显了希音如何更加贴近其中国根基。在未能在纽约和伦敦成功上市，并在全球扩张期间将总部迁至新加坡之后，该公司目前正在[寻求香港IPO](https://www.reuters.com/legal/transactional/shein-seeks-30-40-billion-valuation-august-hong-kong-ipo-sources-say-2026-08-04/)同时深化其对中国南方制造基地的承诺。

### 小额豁免之痛相当大

对希音越南计划的第一个也是最大的打击是美国取消了针对所有国家（不仅是中国）价值800美元以下包裹的最低限度免税豁免。特朗普于2025年7月30日下令实施该举措，一个月后生效，而中国包裹的豁免结束仅几个月后。

随后，美国对中国商品征收的高额关税逐步下调。越南服装的关税仍低于中国服装，但优势已不如从前。

例如，从越南或中国进口的针织涤纶连衣裙需缴纳16%的关税，但中国商品将受到301条款关税的打击，该关税因涉嫌不公平贸易行为而征收，可能会使实际税率升至约33.5%。

上个月，中国和越南均遭到美国新增[12.5%关税](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/top-apparel-exporter-vietnam-faces-higher-us-tariffs-than-peers-2026-07-24/)的打击，原因是据称未能阻止进口使用强迫劳动制造的商品——这一决定使越南在拥有大型服装产业的其他东南亚国家中处于不利地位，并进一步削弱了希音供应商在越南设厂的理由。

第1张，共9张 2026年7月27日，中国广东省广州市番禺区一家为希音生产服装的服装厂内，带有希音标志的服装模特模型。REUTERS/Go Nakamura**[1/9]**2026年7月27日，中国广东省广州市番禺区一家为希音生产服装的服装厂内，带有希音标志的服装模特模型。REUTERS/Go Nakamura [购买许可权，打开新标签页](https://www.reutersconnect.com/item/dress-form-mannequins-with-shein-logos-are-seen-in-guangzhou/dGFnOnJldXRlcnMuY29tLDIwMjY6bmV3c21sX1JDMjdNTUFCUk5UWA%3D%3D/?utm_medium=rcom-article-media&utm_campaign=rcom-rcp-lead)

### 本土基地在速度与低利润率方面无可匹敌

这不仅仅关乎关税。希音的中国供应商消息人士称，事实证明，很难找到愿意长时间工作且工资低廉的越南工人。

希音在中国庞大的供应商网络以小批量生产数百万种款式，每件利润低至1元（0.15美元），订单在数天内完成，如果该公司2.73亿购物者喜欢这些款式，就会迅速重新下单。

特拉华大学时尚与服装研究教授盛路（Sheng Lu）表示：“在中国以外实现采购多元化存在实际限制，尤其是对于像希音这样竞争优势依赖于速度、灵活性和极小规模生产的公司而言。”

广州番禺区一位温姓工厂经理表示，许多前往越南的供应商已经回来了。番禺是“希音村”的所在地，那里有数千家小型服装厂。

温先生补充说：“他们意识到，尽管美国对越南商品的关税税率低于中国商品，但低效率仍使其不如在中国制造可行。”他拒绝透露全名，称希音曾警告供应商不要与媒体交谈。

据一位直接知情人士透露，广州当局热衷于保护当地就业，对希音补贴中国制造商在越南开设工厂的努力也不满意，并在2025年年中警告其不要将订单大幅转移出该地区。

广州市商务局没有回应置评请求。

希音现在正进一步投资广州和更广泛的广东省，其首席执行官许仰天（Sky Xu）在2月份罕见公开露面，承诺在该地区投入超过100亿元人民币（15亿美元）建设智能供应链系统。

### 承诺并非双向

但是，尽管希音已重新承诺扎根中国，但随着需求放缓，一些国内供应商却未必愿意重新投入合作。希音的招股说明书草案显示，由于最低免税额豁免终止，其第一季度美国收入[下滑14%](https://www.reuters.com/legal/transactional/shein-reveals-key-financials-ahead-hong-kong-ipo-2026-07-26/)。

温和其他三名供应商表示，来自希音的订单要么停滞不前，要么仅有少量增长。上个月欧盟对低价[电商进口商品](https://www.reuters.com/business/retail-consumer/shein-ipo-faces-lower-valuation-e-commerce-crackdown-starts-bite-2026-07-16/)征收3欧元关税后，需求预计将进一步放缓。

曾在希音和TikTok Shop从事运营管理工作的何平表示，希音的数千家小型供应商已开始在[PDD控股公司（PDD.O）](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/PDD.O)旗下的Temu或[亚马逊（AMZN.O）](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/AMZN.O)开设店铺，以增加收入。

她说：“希音不再是镇上最帅的男孩了。现在选择多多了。”

其他供应商则回归到交付周期更长的更大订单。

位于番禺、几个月前还在与希音合作的江工服装工厂经理杨某表示：“希音的利润率实在太薄……他们的订单量很小，常常一次只有几十件，这使得生产很麻烦。所以我们决定放弃他们。”

## Shein finds there's no place like China after Vietnam warehouse experiment disappoints

- Summary 摘要
- Companies 公司
- Shein sharply scaling back operations in Vietnam a year after leasing huge logistics hub, sources say 消息人士称，希音在租赁大型物流中心一年后大幅缩减越南业务
- End of US de minimis loophole weakened Vietnam's role as an export base 美国"最低限度"免税漏洞终结削弱越南作为出口基地的作用
- IPO-bound Shein pledges more than $1.5 billion for Guangdong supply-chain system 即将上市的希音承诺投入超 15 亿美元建设广东供应链体系
- Some Shein domestic suppliers look elsewhere as US demand cools 一些希音国内供应商在美国需求降温之际另寻出路
HANOI/GUANGZHOU, China, Aug 10 (Reuters) - Just over a year ago, Chinese ultra-fast fashion retailer Shein began leasing 15 hectares of warehouse facilities — equivalent in size to 21 soccer pitches — near Ho Chi Minh City, part of a grand experiment to make Vietnam a major export base.
河内/广州，8 月 10 日（路透社）——就在一年多前，中国超快时尚零售商希音开始在胡志明市附近租赁 15 公顷的仓库设施——相当于 21 个足球场的面积——这是其将越南打造为主要出口基地宏大实验的一部分。

When it was formulating those plans in late 2024, it seemed like a bet that, while risky, was worth making.
在 2024 年底制定这些计划时，这似乎是一个虽有风险但值得一搏的赌注。

U.S. exemptions for duties on small parcels from China that underpinned ‌its business model looked as if they would be abolished, Donald Trump had just been elected U.S. president for a second term and fears of a heightened trade war were soon realised, with U.S. tariffs on many Chinese goods rocketing to 145% by April 2025.
支撑其商业模式的美国对小额包裹从中国进口的关税豁免政策看似将被取消，唐纳德·特朗普刚刚赢得第二个美国总统任期，而贸易战升级的担忧很快成为现实——到 2025 年 4 月，美国对许多中国商品的关税飙升至 145%。

Shein started encouraging its biggest Chinese suppliers to set up manufacturing bases in Vietnam.

But things did not go to plan.

Today, IPO-bound Shein, known for selling $5 tops and $10 dresses, is drastically scaling back in Vietnam, six people familiar with its operations there said.

At 15 hectares, the bonded [logistics hub](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/shein-set-up-huge-vietnam-warehouse-us-tariff-hedge-sources-say-2025-05-15/) was the largest of its kind in the country and used to employ thousands. The lease now covers 6 hectares, according to two of the sources. A separate person with direct knowledge of the matter said a third of the originally planned site is in use.

Mass layoffs began in April and more are expected, warehouse workers said, adding that some teams have retained one in four employees, while others lost even more. During a Reuters visit to the site in late July, only a handful of workers were present and just ⁠a few trucks were parked at its warehouses. Nearby warehouses operated by other tenants were bustling with activity.

Shein did not respond to Reuters requests for comment.

Its sharp U-turn in Vietnam, which has not previously been reported, reflects abrupt changes in U.S. trade policies while underscoring how dependent Shein's business model is on Chinese suppliers which put up with terms that manufacturers in other countries won't — a pattern Shein has also seen play out in [Brazil](https://www.reuters.com/business/retail-consumer/shein-tried-turn-brazil-into-production-hub-local-factories-walked-away-2026-02-04/).

It also highlights how Shein is hewing more closely to its Chinese roots. Having unsuccessfully sought listings in New York and London and moved its headquarters to Singapore as it expanded globally, the company is now [pursuing a Hong Kong IPO](https://www.reuters.com/legal/transactional/shein-seeks-30-40-billion-valuation-august-hong-kong-ipo-sources-say-2026-08-04/) while deepening its commitment to its manufacturing base in southern China.

### DE MINIMIS PAIN QUITE MAJOR

The first and biggest hit to Shein's Vietnam plans was the end of the U.S. de minimis duty-free exemption for shipments under $800 from all countries, not just China. Trump ordered the move on July 30, 2025, and it took effect a month later, only a few months after the exemption for shipments from China ended.

Then, sky-high U.S. tariffs on Chinese goods gradually came down. Vietnamese apparel is still subject to smaller tariffs than Chinese clothing, but the advantage is no longer as large as it used to be.

A knit polyester dress, for example, imported from either Vietnam or China is subject to a 16% duty, but the Chinese item would be hit with Section 301 tariffs imposed for alleged unfair trade practices that could lift the effective rate to around 33.5%.

Last month, both China and Vietnam were hit with new U.S. [tariffs of 12.5%](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/top-apparel-exporter-vietnam-faces-higher-us-tariffs-than-peers-2026-07-24/) for allegedly failing to prevent imports of goods made with forced labour — a decision that puts Vietnam at a disadvantage to ‌other Southeast Asian nations ⁠with big apparel sectors and further undermines the case for Shein's suppliers to set up shop there.

### HOME BASE UNMATCHED FOR SPEED, LOW MARGINS

It's not all about tariffs. Finding Vietnamese workers willing to work long hours for low wages has proven difficult, sources at Shein's Chinese suppliers say.

Shein's vast network of suppliers in China produces millions of styles in small batches at margins of as little as 1 yuan ($0.15) per piece, with orders fulfilled in days and quickly reordered if the company's 273 million shoppers take a liking to them.

"Sourcing diversification beyond China has practical limits, especially for companies like Shein whose competitive advantage depends on speed, flexibility, and extremely small production runs," said Sheng Lu, professor of fashion and apparel studies at the University of Delaware.

Many suppliers that went to Vietnam have come back, said a factory manager with the surname Wen in Guangzhou's Panyu district, home to "Shein villages" that comprise thousands of small garment factories.

"They realised that despite the smaller ⁠U.S. tariff rate on Vietnamese goods compared to Chinese ones, the low efficiency still makes it less viable than manufacturing in China," Wen added. He declined to give his full name, saying Shein had warned suppliers not to speak to media.

Authorities in Guangzhou, keen to protect local jobs, were also not happy with Shein's efforts to subsidise Chinese manufacturers opening plants in Vietnam, and in mid-2025 warned it against moving orders significantly away from the region, according to a source with direct knowledge of the matter.

The Guangzhou Municipal Commerce Bureau did not respond to a request for comment.

Shein is now investing further in Guangzhou and the broader Guangdong ⁠province, with CEO Sky Xu making a rare public appearance in February to pledge spending of over 10 billion yuan ($1.5 billion) on a smart supply-chain system in the region.

### COMMITMENT DOESN'T GO BOTH WAYS

But while Shein has recommitted to China, some domestic suppliers are not necessarily recommitting back as demand slows. Shein's draft prospectus showed a [14% slide in U.S. revenue](https://www.reuters.com/legal/transactional/shein-reveals-key-financials-ahead-hong-kong-ipo-2026-07-26/) during the first quarter due to the end of the de minimis exemption.

Wen and three other suppliers said orders from Shein were either stagnant or showing only a little growth. Demand is expected to further slow after the European ⁠Union last month imposed a €3 duty on low-value [e-commerce imports](https://www.reuters.com/business/retail-consumer/shein-ipo-faces-lower-valuation-e-commerce-crackdown-starts-bite-2026-07-16/).

Ping He, who has worked in operations management for Shein and TikTok Shop, said thousands of Shein's smaller suppliers have begun supplementing their income by opening stores on PDD Holdings [(PDD.O), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/PDD.O)-owned Temu or Amazon [(AMZN.O), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/AMZN.O).

"Shein is not the prettiest boy in town anymore. There are many more options now," she said.

Other suppliers are returning to larger orders with longer lead times.

"Shein's profit margins are just too thin ... their order volumes are quite small, often just dozens of pieces at a time, which makes production a hassle. So we decided to drop them," said Yang, a manager at Jiang Gong Clothes, a factory in Panyu that worked with Shein until a few months ago.
