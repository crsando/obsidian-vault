### 中文译文

## 英伟达将向OpenAI数据中心投资最高1050亿美元

[英伟达公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/NVDA%20US%20Equity)已同意投入最高1050亿美元，用于支持位于俄亥俄州的一个大规模新数据中心园区，该园区将由OpenAI租用，这是根据一份财务文件披露的，标志着推动人工智能繁荣的两大主导力量之间的最新合作。

ChatGPT的开发商周一表示，已达成协议，从派克县综合设施获得最高约8吉瓦的计算能力，其中首批800兆瓦预计将于2028年投入运营。1吉瓦的电力足以在任何时候为多达75万户美国家庭供电。

OpenAI表示，只有在产能可供租赁时才会开始付款。英伟达表示，将为“租赁和电力费用的特定部分”提供支持，从而助力这一将大规模部署其AI芯片的项目。

另外，英伟达已同意向SB Energy Corp.投资15亿美元，该公司是[软银集团](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/SOFTBK%20Corp)支持的开发商，将负责该设施的建设、拥有和运营。

按设想，俄亥俄州数据中心综合体将成为全球最大之一——象征着推动人工智能发展对计算能力的巨大需求。它也是软银创始人孙正义和[特朗普政府](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/3352Z:US)的核心项目，后者已将该投资誉为重大胜利。

据彭博新闻社报道，英伟达此前曾讨论过[提供担保](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-26/nvidia-in-talks-on-250-billion-backing-for-openai-hub-wsj-says)，为OpenAI的数据中心租赁提供高达2500亿美元的担保。

英伟达曾为其他试图快速构建AI硬件的公司提供财务支持，尤其是[CoreWeave公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CRWV%20US%20Equity)，并直接投资于OpenAI及AI同行[Anthropic PBC](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1892140D:US)。但最新这笔贷款担保是这家芯片制造商此类安排中规模最大的一次。

人们一直对某些不体现在资产负债表上的债务，以及英伟达为其他公司提供融资、这些公司再购买其芯片的循环交易感到担忧。

英伟达还表示，与OpenAI在俄亥俄州的交易并非循环融资的例子。“OpenAI将支付租赁费用，”英伟达称。

## Nvidia to Invest Up to $105 Billion for OpenAI Data Center

[Nvidia Corp. ](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/NVDA%20US%20Equity)has agreed to spend as much as $105 billion to support a massive new data center campus in Ohio set to be leased by OpenAI, according to a financial filing, marking the latest tie-up between two dominant forces driving the AI boom.

The ChatGPT maker said Monday it has entered into an agreement to secure up to roughly 8 gigawatts of computing capacity from the Pike County complex, with the first 800 megawatts expected to come online by 2028. A single gigawatt is enough to power up to 750,000 US homes at any given time.

OpenAI said it will only begin paying as capacity becomes available for lease. Nvidia said it will provide support for “defined portions of lease and power payments,” aiding a project that will lead to a large deployment of its AI chips.

Separately, Nvidia has agreed to invest $1.5 billion in SB Energy Corp., the [SoftBank Group Corp.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/SOFTBK%20Corp)-backed developer that will build, own and operate the facility.

The Ohio data center complex as envisioned would be among the biggest in the world — a symbol of the enormous demand for computing to propel AI development. It’s also a centerpiece project for SoftBank founder Masayoshi Son and the [Trump administration](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/3352Z:US), which has hailed the investment as a major win.

Nvidia previously discussed [providing a guarantee](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-26/nvidia-in-talks-on-250-billion-backing-for-openai-hub-wsj-says) of as much as $250 billion to OpenAI for the data center lease, Bloomberg News has reported.

Nvidia has provided a financial backstop to other companies trying to rapidly build out AI hardware, notably [CoreWeave Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CRWV%20US%20Equity), and it has invested directly in OpenAI and AI peer [Anthropic PBC](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1892140D:US). But the latest loan guarantee is the largest arrangement of its type for the chipmaker.

There have been some concerns about debt that doesn’t show up on their balance sheets, as well as circular deals that involve Nvidia financing other companies, which then pay for its chips.

Nvidia also said the Ohio deal with OpenAI is not an example of circular financing. “OpenAI will pay the lease,” Nvidia said.
