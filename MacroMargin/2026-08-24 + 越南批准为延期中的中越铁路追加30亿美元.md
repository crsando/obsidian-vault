### 中文译文

## 越南批准为延期中的中越铁路追加30亿美元

### **要点**

- 越南国会批准将连接该国与中国的铁路项目总投资提高逾86万亿越南盾。
- 据越南国会的一份声明，项目总成本增至289.3万亿越南盾，竣工时间不晚于2030年。
- 由于建筑和设备成本上升，以及对安置居民的额外补偿，需要追加投资。
越南国会批准将连接该国与中国的铁路项目总投资提高逾86万亿越南盾（约33亿美元），因土地清拆延误导致项目进度落后。

据越南国会周一发布的一份[声明](https://quochoi.vn/tintuc/Pages/su-kien-noi-bat.aspx?ItemID=101237)，此举使项目总成本增至289.3万亿越南盾，竣工时间不晚于2030年。

这条全长363公里的铁路将连接港口城市海防，经河内至老街。新闻网站《青年报》[报道](https://tuoitre.vn/quoc-hoi-dong-y-tang-hon-86000-ti-lam-du-an-duong-sat-lao-cai-ha-noi-hai-phong-100260824100137287.htm)称，由于建筑和设备成本上升，以及对安置居民的额外补偿，需要追加投资。

更高的造价凸显了越南当前基础设施建设浪潮的规模和复杂性，以及在该国今年力争至少10%增长的背景下，在加速时间表内交付重大项目的挑战。建设部今年6月[表示](https://thanhnien.vn/du-an-duong-sat-lao-cai-ha-noi-hai-phong-cham-tien-do-185260618013947087.htm)，这条铁路是越南北方通往中国边境的三条规划线路之一，目前进度至少滞后八个月。

该部将延误归因于该项目是越南首个此类项目，采用了先进技术并与外国伙伴协调。该铁路的部分开发资金来自中国政府的贷款。

该项目还面临土地征收和居民搬迁的挑战，这是越南大型基础设施项目中长期存在的延误和紧张局势的根源。据港口城市网站上的[声明](https://baohaiphong.vn/khan-truong-khac-phuc-cham-giai-phong-mat-bang-du-an-duong-sat-lao-cai-ha-noi-hai-phong-550782.html)，海防市副主席已下令当地官员在农历新年假期前加快场地清理，包括迁移墓地。

政府已于12月破土动工建设五个车站，这是第一阶段建设的一部分，总投资为3.3万亿越南盾。第二阶段建设原计划于今年12月开工。

该铁路线将穿越越南北部六个省份，总占地面积约2,075公顷，涉及约25,544名居民的重新安置。建设部已制定计划，选择中国承包商与合格的越南企业组建合资企业，以实施该项目。

## Vietnam Approves Extra $3 Billion for Delayed China Rail Link

### **Takeaways**

- Vietnam's parliament approved raising the total investment in a railway linking the country to China by just over 86 trillion dong.
- The overall cost increases to 289.3 trillion dong with completion due no later than 2030, according to a statement from the National Assembly.
- The extra investment is needed due to rising construction and equipment costs, as well as additional compensation for resettled residents.
Vietnam’s parliament approved raising the total investment in a railway linking the country to China by just over 86 trillion dong ($3.3 billion), as delays in land clearance push the project behind schedule.

The move increases the overall cost to 289.3 trillion dong with completion due no later than 2030, according to a [statement](https://quochoi.vn/tintuc/Pages/su-kien-noi-bat.aspx?ItemID=101237) from the National Assembly on Monday.

The extra investment for the 363-kilometer line that will link the port city of Haiphong to Lao Cai via Hanoi is needed due to rising construction and equipment costs, as well as additional compensation for resettled residents, news website Tuoi Tre [reported](https://tuoitre.vn/quoc-hoi-dong-y-tang-hon-86000-ti-lam-du-an-duong-sat-lao-cai-ha-noi-hai-phong-100260824100137287.htm).

The higher price tag highlights the scale and complexity of Vietnam’s current infrastructure drive and the challenge of delivering major projects on an accelerated timeline as it targets at least 10% growth this year. The rail line, one of three planned routes from northern Vietnam to the China border, is at least eight months behind schedule, the construction ministry [said](https://thanhnien.vn/du-an-duong-sat-lao-cai-ha-noi-hai-phong-cham-tien-do-185260618013947087.htm) in June.

The ministry attributed the delay to the project being the first of its kind in Vietnam, harnessing the use of advanced technology and coordination with foreign partners. The railway is being developed in part with loans from the Chinese government.

It’s also facing challenges in land acquisition and resettlement for residents being moved off their land, a longstanding source of delays and tensions in Vietnam’s large-scale infrastructure projects. Haiphong’s deputy chairman has ordered local officials to speed up site clearance, including the relocation of cemeteries, before the Lunar New Year holiday, according to a [statement](https://baohaiphong.vn/khan-truong-khac-phuc-cham-giai-phong-mat-bang-du-an-duong-sat-lao-cai-ha-noi-hai-phong-550782.html) on the port city’s website.

The government has already [broken ground](https://baochinhphu.vn/khoi-cong-dong-loat-5-nha-ga-tren-tuyen-duong-sat-lao-cai-ha-noi-hai-phong-102251219081744494.htm) on five stations, part of the first construction phase with total investment of 3.3 trillion dong in December. Construction of the second phase was planned to start in December this year.

The rail line will cross six northern provinces and cover a total land area of about 2,075 hectares, involving the resettlement of around 25,544 residents. The construction ministry has set out plans to select Chinese contractors to form a joint venture with qualified Vietnamese firms for the implementation of the project.
