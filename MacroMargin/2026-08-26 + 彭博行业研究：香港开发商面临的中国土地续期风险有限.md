## 彭博行业研究：香港开发商面临的中国土地续期风险有限

作者：彭博新闻社
2026年8月26日 上午10:59 GMT+8

据彭博行业研究，香港主要地产商持有的550亿美元中国商业地产即将面临土地租约到期，这看起来更像是一笔可控的资本支出，而非迫在眉睫的危机。

上海和广州对续租规则的澄清可能缓解早前的担忧，有助于限制重估下行风险，并提高开发商（包括[恒隆地产有限公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/101%20HK%20Equity)和[太古地产有限公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/1972%20HK%20Equity)）的长期盈利可见度。这个问题多年来一直困扰着房地产行业，因为到期租约像定时炸弹一样破坏了交易，使投资者面临巨额亏损的风险。

“香港开发商即将在中国内地测试一个基本未经考验的土地续租流程，因为他们最早的到期租约已进入20年倒计时，”彭博行业研究分析师Kristy Hung和Kingsley Chan在《550亿美元中国租约风险看起来比担心的更可控》报告中表示，并指出其他中国城市将效仿上海和广州的续租框架。

彭博行业研究的计算显示，续租恒隆的恒隆广场和港汇恒隆广场将使公司的净负债与权益比率增加5.8个百分点至37.4%，凸显了续租带来的财务影响有限。报告称，这两项资产贡献了该开发商近四分之一的收入。

## HK Firms See Limited Risk From China Land-Lease Renewal, BI Says

By Bloomberg News
August 26, 2026 at 10:59 AM GMT+8

The upcoming land-lease expiration across $55 billion in China commercial real estate held by leading Hong Kong landlords looks more like a manageable capital outlay rather than an impending crisis, according to Bloomberg Intelligence.

Clarification of lease renewal rules by Shanghai and Guangzhou could ease earlier fears, helping limit revaluation downside and improve long-term earnings visibility for developers including [Hang Lung Properties Ltd.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/101%20HK%20Equity) and [Swire Properties Ltd.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/1972%20HK%20Equity) The issue had plagued the real estate sector for years as the ticking time bomb of expiring leases scuttled deals and left investors facing the risk of heavy losses.

“Hong Kong landlords are set to test a largely untried land-lease renewal process in mainland China as their earliest expiries move within 20 years,” BI analysts Kristy Hung and Kingsley Chan said in the $55 Billion China Lease Risk Looks More Manageable Than Feared, noting that other Chinese cities are set to follow Shanghai and Guangzhou’s framework for lease renewals.

BI’s calculations show that renewing Hang Lung’s Plaza 66 and Grand Gateway 66 would increase the company’s net debt-to-equity ratio by 5.8 percentage points to 37.4%, underscoring the contained financial impact of lease extensions. The two assets generate almost a quarter of the developer’s revenue, according to the report.
