### 中文译文

## 沙特在阿曼附近提供石油销售，可能表明其正悄悄通过霍尔木兹海峡

沙特阿拉伯正提供从阿曼海岸附近销售石油，这标志着该国可能效仿阿联酋，通过霍尔木兹海峡运送更多桶原油。

据知情人士透露，沙特国有石油公司沙特阿美正以所谓的船对船方式，从包括阿曼湾索哈尔在内的地点提供货物，这些人士因信息未公开而要求匿名。所涉货物为阿拉伯中质原油和阿拉伯重质原油，这很可能意味着这些桶装原油来自波斯湾内部。

沙特阿美拒绝置评。

中东产油国一直在[推进](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-16/covert-mideast-oil-flows-are-keeping-global-prices-in-check)将大量原油运出波斯湾，帮助抑制油价，并缓解能源驱动通胀飙升的担忧。

沙特有能力将出口转至其红海港口延布，这使得它对这条水路的依赖程度降低，并使其通过该通道运输的原油量增加速度较慢。

过去几周，在也门胡塞武装宣称对沙特实施海上封锁后，即使是这些改道的红海运输也面临威胁。

近期有迹象显示，沙特阿拉伯在波斯湾内的设施装货活动有所增加。根据卫星图像，过去一周，至少有 900 万桶运输能力的船只在该国大型拉斯塔努拉出口设施或其附近装载。

沙特阿拉伯还在波斯湾外集结了大量超大型油轮。

沙特阿美首席执行官阿明·纳赛尔3月表示，向延布输送原油的管道主要运输阿拉伯轻质原油和超轻质原油。

他当时表示，该国大部分位于波斯湾内的海上油田主要生产中质和重质原油。

## Saudis Offer to Sell Oil Near Oman, a Possible Sign They’re Sailing Dark Through Hormuz

Saudi Arabia is offering to sell oil from off the coast of Oman, a sign that the kingdom may be following the United Arab Emirates in shuttling more barrels through the Strait of Hormuz.

State oil company Saudi Aramco is offering cargoes on a so-called ship-to-ship basis from locations including Sohar in the Gulf of Oman, people familiar with the matter said, asking not to be identified because the information isn’t public. The cargoes in question are Arab Medium and Arab Heavy, something that mean it’s highly likely the barrels came from inside the Persian Gulf.

Saudi Aramco declined to comment.

Middle Eastern oil producers have been [pressing ahead](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-16/covert-mideast-oil-flows-are-keeping-global-prices-in-check) with shuttling large volumes of crude out of the Persian Gulf, helping keep a lid on oil prices and assuaging fears of an energy-driven inflation spike.

The kingdom’s ability to divert exports to its Red Sea port of Yanbu made it less reliant on the waterway and slower to boost the amount it has shuttled through.

Over the past several weeks, even those diverted Red Sea flows have also come under threat after Yemen’s Houthi militants declared a maritime blockade on Saudi Arabia.

*Saudi oil tankers are seen gathered in the Gulf of Oman*

There have been recent signs of a pickup in cargo activity from Saudi Arabia’s facilities inside the Persian Gulf. According to satellite imagery, vessels with at least 9 million barrels of transport capacity have loaded at or near the country’s giant Ras Tanura export installations over the past week.

Saudi Arabia has also amassed a large cluster of oil supertankers just outside the Persian Gulf.

Saudi Aramco Chief Executive Officer Amin Nasser said in March that the pipeline sending barrels to Yanbu was primarily carrying Arab Light and Extra Light.

The nation’s offshore fields, which are largely inside the Persian Gulf, are made up of the country’s medium and heavy supplies, he said at the time.
