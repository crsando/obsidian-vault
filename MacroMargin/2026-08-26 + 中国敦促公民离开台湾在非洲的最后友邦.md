### 中文译文

## 中国敦促公民离开台湾在非洲的最后友邦

斯威士兰面积约为美国新泽西州大小，人口仅120万。*摄影师：Marco Longari/AFP/Getty Images*

作者：彭博新闻
2026年8月26日 下午7:07 GMT+8
*更新于2026年8月26日 下午7:53 GMT+8*

### **要点**

- 中国正在敦促本国公民撤离斯威士兰，理由是安全担忧和诈骗风险，称该国安全局势“复杂”，涉及网络诈骗和非法赌博的犯罪活动“猖獗”。
- 中国政府警告称，坚持前往或滞留斯威士兰的人员将面临“极高的安全风险”，且可能难以获得援助。
- 台湾外交部谴责中国的这一行动，称其蓄意破坏台湾与斯威士兰的历史友谊，并将其描述为“威权恐吓”。
中国正敦促本国公民撤离斯威士兰，并警告其他人不要前往这个非洲小王国，称在台湾在非洲仅存的邦交国存在安全担忧和诈骗风险。

中国外交部领事司在微信上发布的一篇[帖子](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/rcBSlfR8UUDYIA7AnRS9WA)中称，斯威士兰的安全局势“复杂”，涉及网络诈骗和非法赌博的犯罪活动“猖獗”，公共安全和社会安全风险“极高”。

“目前在该国的中国公民和机构被敦促尽快撤离或转移到相对更安全的地区——例如南非，”据微信帖子称。“坚持前往或留在该国的人员将面临极高的安全风险，获得援助的及时性可能会受到影响。”

北京官员在发布这一建议时并未提及台湾。外交部发言人林剑周三告诉记者，此类通知是国际通行做法，称中国“将采取必要措施”维护海外公民的安全、权益。政府是在对斯威士兰局势进行“审慎评估”后发布安全建议的，该国与中国没有正式外交关系。

斯威士兰将这一举措与针对外国公民（主要来自中国）的执法行动联系起来，这些外国人涉嫌与网络相关的犯罪活动。“任何暗示斯威士兰存在特殊或普遍安全风险的说法，都与当前安全局势不符，因此令人遗憾，”政府代理发言人塔比莱·姆杜卢利（Thabile Mdluli）在一份声明中表示。

台湾外交部谴责中国的这一行动，称其是蓄意破坏台湾与该非洲国家历史友谊的行为。它呼吁国际社会认清“中国威权恫吓的严重性质”。

![图片](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3c881cc90b148107bf32d6c4141f9a03/3c881cc9-0b14-81ad-b964-cee48330e0fa-46b6d6a7424d6e3baca2093b86fcf037.png)

斯威士兰位于非洲东南角，面积与新泽西州相当，人口仅120万。据《斯威士兰时报》[报道](https://times.co.sz/42044/news/two-chinese-online-gambling-kingpins-fined-total-of-e77k/)，两名中国公民因在该国经营非法网络赌博被罚款，若未缴纳罚款，可能面临监禁。中国媒体《财新》3月曾[报道](https://www.caixinglobal.com/2026-03-12/china-fugitive-in-220-million-scam-arrested-in-africas-only-taiwan-ally-102422253.html)一起类似案件。

尽管网络犯罪呈上升趋势，但该国总体上远比邻国南非安全，南非的谋杀率位居[世界最严重之列](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/VC.IHR.PSRC.P5?locations=ZA&most_recent_value_desc=true)。根据欧盟支持的 Enact 组织发布的[排名](https://africa.ocindex.net/rankings)，南非在非洲的犯罪率得分位居第二，而斯威士兰排名第45位。

尽管[诈骗](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-23/cambodia-s-leader-pledges-more-to-tackle-evolving-scam-networks)和网络犯罪在[东南亚](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-04-21/crime-syndicates-in-southeast-asia-go-global-defying-crackdowns)十分普遍，但北京很少针对该地区国家发布类似的旅行建议。北京最近一次针对东南亚国家的通知是在一年前，涉及菲律宾局势，中国外交部当时[表示](https://www.mfa.gov.cn/wjbzwfwpt/kzx/aqtx/zyaq/202508/t20250830_11698875.html)，菲律宾正处于“社会动荡时期，针对中国公民的犯罪激增”。

这不是北京首次对斯威士兰给予特别对待。去年中国宣布对非洲国家实行零关税时，将斯威士兰排除在外，因为该政策仅限于与中国有外交关系的国家。

非洲已成为中国在全球舞台上孤立台北存在的一个关键前线。

8月，在一系列护照争端之后，台湾[敦促公民](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-13/taiwan-warns-against-uganda-travel-as-passport-disputes-grow)推迟前往乌干达的旅行，这些争端中，抵达恩德培机场的台湾商旅人士及其家属的证件被移民官员没收，并被要求改用中国护照。

今年早些时候，北京说服三个非洲国家关闭其领空，以阻止台湾总统赖清德的飞机，试图扰乱其访问斯威士兰的行程。斯威士兰是全球12个承认台湾的国家之一。赖清德最终在5月访问了斯威士兰，尽管当时台湾政府没有透露他如何抵达该国的细节。

中国外交部随后发布了一份声明，标题为“赖清德乘坐斯威士兰国王私人飞机前往斯威士兰”。

台湾也在7月底暂停为摩洛哥国民办理签证，此前摩洛哥实施了类似限制；而台北方面威胁要进行报复，此前台湾抨击肯尼亚在中国压力下阻止学者，6月肯尼亚禁止台湾代表参加全球海事会议，并扣留了岛上的一名学者20小时。

虽然台湾的商业利益在该王国广泛存在，但中国大陆的利益更为有限。国有中国电力建设集团有限公司于2023年赢得了一份26亿斯威士兰里兰吉尼（1.65亿美元）的合同，在斯威士兰建造一座[大型水坝](https://www.afdb.org/en/documents/contract-awards-eswatini-construction-mpakeni-dam-and-associated-works-including-main-access-road-mnwap)。

根据[官方统计](https://www.thekingdomofeswatini.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/12-Dec-Arrivals-2025-1.pdf)，2025年斯威士兰接待了来自中国大陆的3251名游客，而来自台湾的有1333名。

周三上午，这条微信帖子在中国热搜榜上排名第一，截至中午，阅读量超过8900万，讨论量超过8000条，互动量达3.9万次。

一些评论者表示，他们并不了解这个国家。“直到被提醒之前，我甚至不知道斯威士兰在哪里，”其中一人写道。

另一位用户则关注外交层面。“请注意：中国与斯威士兰没有外交关系。拒绝离开是危险的，如果你遇到麻烦，将很难获得帮助。”

## China Urges Citizens to Leave Taiwan’s Last Friend in Africa

Eswatini is about the size of New Jersey with just 1.2 million people. *Photographer: Marco Longari/AFP/Getty Images*

By Bloomberg NewsAugust 26, 2026 at 7:07 PM GMT+8*Updated on August 26, 2026 at 7:53 PM GMT+8*

### **Takeaways**

- China is urging its citizens to evacuate Eswatini due to safety concerns and scamming risks, describing the country's security situation as "complex" with crimes involving online fraud and illegal gambling "rampant".
- The Chinese government has warned that those who insist on traveling to or remaining in Eswatini will face "extremely high security risks" and may have difficulty receiving assistance.
- Taiwan's Ministry of Foreign Affairs has denounced China's action, calling it a deliberate effort to undermine the island's historical friendship with Eswatini and describing it as "authoritarian intimidation".
China is urging its citizens to evacuate Eswatini and warning others not to visit the tiny African kingdom over what it said were safety concerns and scamming risks in Taiwan’s last remaining diplomatic partner on the continent.

In a [post](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/rcBSlfR8UUDYIA7AnRS9WA) on WeChat, the Department of Consular Affairs of China’s Ministry of Foreign Affairs described Eswatini’s security situation as “complex,” with crimes involving online fraud and illegal gambling “rampant” and public-security and safety risk “extremely high.”

“Chinese citizens and institutions currently in the country are urged to evacuate or relocate to relatively safer areas — such as South Africa — as soon as possible,” according to the WeChat post. “Those who insist on traveling to or remaining in the country will face extremely high security risks, and the timeliness of receiving assistance may be compromised.”

Officials in Beijing made no connection to Taiwan in issuing the advisory. Foreign Ministry spokesman Lin Jian told reporters on Wednesday that such notices were a common international practice, saying China “will take necessary measures” to safeguard the safety, rights and interests of its citizens overseas. The government issued its security advisory after what he called a “prudent assessment” of the situation in Eswatini, which has no official diplomatic ties with China.

Eswatini linked the move to a law enforcement operation targeting foreign nationals, mostly from China, for suspected cyber-related criminal activity. “Any suggestion that Eswatini presents an exceptional or generalized security risk is unsupported by the prevailing security situation and is therefore regrettable,” Thabile Mdluli, acting government spokeswoman, said in a statement.

Taiwan’s Ministry of Foreign Affairs denounced China’s action, describing it as a deliberate effort to undermine the island’s historical friendship with the African country. It called on the international community to recognize “the egregious nature of China’s authoritarian intimidation.”

Located in the southeast corner of Africa, Eswatini is about the size of New Jersey with just 1.2 million people. Two Chinese nationals were fined for running an illegal online gambling operation there and face the possibility of imprisonment if those charges aren’t paid, Times of Eswatini has [reported](https://times.co.sz/42044/news/two-chinese-online-gambling-kingpins-fined-total-of-e77k/). A similar case was [reported](https://www.caixinglobal.com/2026-03-12/china-fugitive-in-220-million-scam-arrested-in-africas-only-taiwan-ally-102422253.html) by Chinese publication Caixin in March.

While cyber crime has been on the rise, the kingdom is generally far safer than neighboring South Africa, which has one of the [world’s worst](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/VC.IHR.PSRC.P5?locations=ZA&most_recent_value_desc=true) murder rates. South Africa has the second-highest criminality score in Africa, according to [rankings from](https://africa.ocindex.net/rankings) the European Union-backed Enact. Eswatini, meanwhile, ranks 45th.

And although [scams](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-23/cambodia-s-leader-pledges-more-to-tackle-evolving-scam-networks) and cyber crime are widespread in [Southeast Asia](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-04-21/crime-syndicates-in-southeast-asia-go-global-defying-crackdowns), Beijing rarely issues comparable travel advisories for nations in the region. Its most recent notice for any Southeast Asian country concerned the situation in the Philippines a year ago, when China’s Foreign Ministry [said](https://www.mfa.gov.cn/wjbzwfwpt/kzx/aqtx/zyaq/202508/t20250830_11698875.html) it was experiencing “a period of social unrest, with a surge in crimes targeting Chinese citizens.”

This isn’t the first time that Eswatini was accorded special treatment by Beijing. When it announced zero tariffs for African countries last year, it excluded Eswatini by limiting the policy to countries that have diplomatic ties with China.

Africa has become a key front in China’s efforts to isolate Taipei’s presence on the world stage.

In August, Taiwan [urged citizens](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-13/taiwan-warns-against-uganda-travel-as-passport-disputes-grow) to postpone travel to Uganda after a series of passport disputes in which Taiwanese business travelers and their family members arriving at Entebbe airport had their documents seized by immigration officials, who demanded Chinese passports instead.

Earlier this year, Beijing persuaded three African countries to close their airspace to Taiwan President Lai Ching-te’s plane in a bid to derail his trip to Eswatini, which is one of 12 countries worldwide to recognize Taiwan. Lai eventually visited Eswatini in May, though at the time Taiwan’s government didn’t disclose details of how he traveled to the country.

China’s Foreign Ministry later released a statement titled, “Lai Ching-te’s travel to Eswatini on its king’s private jet.”

Taiwan also Taiwan Suspends Visa Processing for Moroccan Nationals visa processing for Moroccan nationals in late July after Morocco imposed similar restrictions, while Taipei threatened retaliation after Taiwan Blasts Kenya for Blocking Scholar Under China Pressure barred Taiwanese delegates from a global maritime conference and detained an academic from the island for 20 hours in June.

While Taiwanese business interests are widespread in the kingdom, mainland China’s are more limited. State-owned Power Construction Corp. of China in 2023 won a 2.6 billion-emalangeni ($165 million) contract to build a [major dam](https://www.afdb.org/en/documents/contract-awards-eswatini-construction-mpakeni-dam-and-associated-works-including-main-access-road-mnwap) in Eswatini.

Eswatini had 3,251 visitors from China in 2025, compared with 1,333 from Taiwan, according to [official statistics](https://www.thekingdomofeswatini.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/12-Dec-Arrivals-2025-1.pdf).

The WeChat post was trending at No. 1 in China on Wednesday morning, generating more than 89 million reads, over 8,000 discussions, and 39,000 interactions as of noon.

Some commenters said they weren’t aware of the country. “Didn’t even know where Eswatini was until I was reminded,” wrote one.

Another user focused on the diplomatic dimension. “Take note: China and Eswatini have no diplomatic ties. Refusing to leave is dangerous, and it will be difficult to get help if you run into trouble.”
