### 中文译文

## 哈萨克斯坦拟通过5亿美元交易重返熊猫债券市场

作为中亚最大石油生产国，哈萨克斯坦计划今年重返人民币债券市场，以寻求从中国获得更廉价的[融资](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-09-11/cheap-yuan-funding-is-tempting-more-borrowers-outside-of-china)。

据知情人士透露，哈萨克斯坦政府已开始与银行讨论最早于下月发行熊猫债券，筹资约5亿美元等值货币。这些人士因计划尚未公开而要求匿名，他们表示潜在交易的参数将取决于市场状况。

哈萨克斯坦财政部表示，正在考虑发行熊猫债券，以实现预算赤字融资来源多元化。该部在回答彭博提问时称，交易的最终决定将取决于中国银行间债券市场的情况以及从外部来源为赤字融资的需求。

“中国资本市场目前提供了以相对较低利率借款的机会，”总部位于阿斯塔纳的财政部表示。“在全球经济不确定性中，人民币表现出稳定性，降低了借款人的货币风险。”

今年5月，哈萨克斯坦首次发行三年期熊猫债券，筹资34亿元人民币（5亿美元），此后进一步加大了对中国资本市场的拓展力度。财政部当时表示，需求超过发行量的两倍，使得这成为在中国境内债券市场上BBB评级主权发行人所能设定的最低票息。

这笔交易凸显了阿斯塔纳在融资来源多元化和加强与北京金融联系方面的努力。该主权国家的首只熊猫债券标志着在扩大中国投资者准入、支持哈萨克斯坦政府长期目标方面迈出了一步，哈萨克斯坦央行与中国签署了货币互换协议，以促进贸易和融资。

中国已超越俄罗斯成为哈萨克斯坦最大的贸易伙伴，近年来双边贸易达到创纪录水平，这得益于能源、金属、农业以及北京“一带一路”倡议下不断扩大的跨境基础设施联系。哈萨克斯坦也已成为中国商品通过所谓“中间走廊”运往欧洲的关键陆路通道。

自去年以来，哈萨克斯坦借款人在人民币债务市场的参与度稳步提升。包括PRICED：哈萨克斯坦开发银行（JSC Development Bank of Kazakhstan）20亿离岸人民币（票息3.35%）和PRICED：哈萨克斯坦国家石油天然气公司（KazMunayGas）12.5亿离岸人民币5年期（票息3.15%）在内的国有支持发行人，已在香港离岸点心债券市场融资。

哈萨克斯坦拥有三大评级机构授予的投资级评级，其中惠誉评级（Fitch Ratings）给出的评级较垃圾级高出两档。标普全球评级（S&P Global Ratings，S&PGR）确认了哈萨克斯坦“BBB-/A-3”评级，展望正面；并于2月对哈萨克斯坦BBB-信用评分给出正面展望。

## Kazakhstan Eyes Return to Panda Bonds With $500 Million Deal

Kazakhstan plans to return to the yuan debt market this year as Central Asia’s largest oil producer seeks to tap cheaper [financing](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-09-11/cheap-yuan-funding-is-tempting-more-borrowers-outside-of-china) from China.

The government has started discussions with banks about raising the equivalent of about $500 million in a sale of panda bonds as early as next month, according to people familiar with the matter. The parameters of the potential deal will depend on market conditions, said the people, who declined to be identified as the plans aren’t public.

The Kazakh Finance Ministry said it’s considering a panda bond sale to diversify sources of funding the budget deficit. A final decision on a deal will depend on the situation on the Chinese interbank bond market and the need to finance the deficit from external sources, the ministry said in response to Bloomberg questions.

“The Chinese capital market currently offers opportunities to borrow at relatively low rates,” the Astana-based ministry said. “The Chinese yuan has demonstrated stability amid global economic uncertainty, reducing currency risks for borrowers.”

Kazakhstan deepened its push into China’s capital markets this year after raising 3.4 billion yuan ($500 million) in a debut three-year panda bond in May. Demand topping twice the amount offered enabled the lowest coupon to be set for a BBB-rated sovereign issuer in China’s onshore bond market, the [Finance Ministry](https://www.gov.kz/memleket/entities/minfin/press/news/details/1228961?lang=en) said at the time.

The transaction underscored Astana’s efforts to diversify funding sources and strengthen financial ties with Beijing. The sovereign’s inaugural panda bond marked a step in broadening access to Chinese investors and supporting the Kazakh government’s longer-term objective of Kazakh Central Bank Signs Currency Swap Agreement With China in trade and financing.

China has overtaken Russia as Kazakhstan’s largest trading partner, with bilateral trade reaching record levels in recent years, driven by energy, metals, agriculture and expanding cross-border infrastructure links under Beijing’s Belt and Road Initiative. Kazakhstan has also become a [key transit](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-26/iran-war-boosts-10-billion-kazakh-rail-plan-linking-eu-to-china) land route for Chinese goods moving to Europe through the so-called Middle Corridor.
