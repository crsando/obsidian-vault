### 中文译文

## 美债攀升，美伊和解希望推动油价下跌：市场综述

华尔街交易商买入债券，多数股票上涨，因恢复霍尔木兹海峡通航的协议希望推动油价下跌，缓解了通胀担忧。

巴基斯坦对可能达成的协议表示乐观，该协议有望缓解扰乱能源市场的紧张局势，美国原油价格跌破82美元。美国国债收益率下降之际，市场预计即将公布的数据将显示消费者价格增长放缓。尽管[标普500指数](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/SPX%20Index)基本持平，但其中超过300只成分股上涨。

![Stocks rise as oil falls. Photographer: Michael Nagle/Bloomberg](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3b981cc90b14819eb5e7e9529dcff2f7/3b981cc9-0b14-816f-a685-fb561350248a-07b7c1d925af231597673db81bd64af1.png)

股市上涨，油价下跌。摄影师：Michael Nagle/Bloomberg

巴基斯坦国防部长表示，美国和伊朗在霍尔木兹海峡问题上“接近达成某种安排”，尽管双方在长期陷入僵局的谈判中似乎都强化了立场。

在唐纳德·特朗普总统强化立场之后，这些乐观评估出现。他周一表示，德黑兰应为与伊朗伊斯兰共和国有关的袭击以及国内抗议中遇难者支付赔偿金。他的言论是对伊朗周末提出的一系列要求的回应。

布朗兄弟哈里曼公司的Elias Haddad表示：“我们看到原油价格主导着战争叙事，价格波动可能决定局势升级和降级的节奏。”

经济方面，由于企业加大招聘计划且通胀压力缓解，美国小企业乐观情绪在7月升至近一年来最高水平。

LPL Financial的Jeffrey Roach表示：“中东冲突的解决可能进一步提振市场情绪。报告摘要中一个特别令人鼓舞的观察是提醒我们‘世界有充足的石油’。经济环境有利于风险偏好。”

## Treasuries Climb as US-Iran Hopes Spur Oil Decline: Markets Wrap

Wall Street traders lifted bonds and most stocks rose as hopes for a deal to revive the Strait of Hormuz spurred a drop in oil prices, easing worries about inflation.

US crude fell below $82 after Pakistan signaled optimism about a potential agreement that could reduce tensions roiling energy markets. The decline in Treasury yields came on the eve of data that’s expected to show moderation in consumer price growth. While the [S&P 500](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/SPX%20Index) was little changed, more than 300 of its companies advanced.

Stocks rise as oil falls. Photographer: Michael Nagle/Bloomberg

Pakistan’s defense minister said the US and Iran are “close to some sort of arrangement” over the Strait of Hormuz, even after both sides appeared to harden their positions in the long-deadlocked negotiation.

The upbeat assessments came after President Donald Trump toughened his stance, saying on Monday that Tehran should pay reparations for those killed in attacks linked to the Islamic Republic as well as in domestic protests. His comments came in response to a series of demands laid out by Iran over the weekend.

“We see crude oil prices driving the war narrative, with price swings likely to dictate the pace of escalation and de-escalation,” said Elias Haddad at Brown Brothers Harriman & Co.

On the economic front, US small-business optimism rose in July to the highest level in almost a year as firms ramped up hiring plans and inflation pressures eased.

“A resolution to the Middle East conflict could provide an additional boost to sentiment,” said Jeffrey Roach at LPL Financial. “One particularly encouraging observation from the report’s summary was the reminder that ‘the world has plenty of oil.’ Economic conditions are favorable for risk appetite.”
