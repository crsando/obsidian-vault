### 中文译文

## 中国AI闪电战为美国竞争对手模型制造商创造“死亡地带”

中国AI领域密集发布的一系列模型正迅速缩小与硅谷的差距，并为那些既没有前沿技术也没有颠覆性定价的公司创造了一个所谓的“死亡地带”。

[阿里巴巴集团控股有限公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/BABA%20US%20Equity)本周凭借其迄今最先进的模型Qwen3.8-Max成为最新一家登上全球基准榜首的中国公司，该模型似乎达到或超过了[Anthropic PBC](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1892140D:US)的旗舰模型Fable 5。两周前，Moonshot AI的Kimi K3展现出与最昂贵的美国选项相当的性能，而预算却少得多，这引发了人们对美国旨在减缓中国技术崛起的芯片制裁有效性的[新质疑](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-17/what-is-moonshot-ai-why-china-s-new-model-is-roiling-markets)。

此外，[字节跳动有限公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1774397D:CH)凭借其Seedance在视频生成领域将其他竞争对手挤到一边，其2.5版本[刚刚发布](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-31/china-s-minimax-and-bytedance-release-dueling-ai-video-models)。作为中国打破美国人工智能霸权的最初颠覆者，DeepSeek今年夏天携[V4 Flash](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-31/deepseek-unveils-public-beta-api-for-flagship-ai-model)回归，这是一款在定价上具有突破性的模型。曾经孤立的冲击波如今变成了一连串超出预期的深度冲击——不仅提供了低成本替代方案，还在推理、编码和复杂任务方面提供高端能力，给[OpenAI](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1554630D:US)及其美国同行带来了真正的竞争。

“自2025年1月以来最重要的变化是，中国的进步不再像是单一公司的突破，”驻北京的技术分析师、Hello China Tech通讯稿创始人Poe Zhao表示。“第一个DeepSeek时刻看起来像是特例。最近的发布表明，中国现在拥有一个可重复的系统来生产接近全球前沿的模型。”

再加上6月份[Z.ai](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/2513%20HK%20Equity)推出的当时全球排名最高的开源模型GLM-5.2，这波在八周内推出的五款模型表明，中国开发者正在接近、有时甚至超越通常被视为前沿AI领导者的美国先驱者。其核心价值主张不仅是低价格——而是极致的效率。

根据独立评估机构Artificial Analysis的[基准测试](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models#cost-tabs)，执行一个复杂的现实工作负载，使用DeepSeek V4 Flash仅需0.03美元，而使用Claude Fable 5则需要3.15美元。当中国要价几美分而美国公司要价几美元时，两国之间争夺AI客户（尤其是世界其他地区的客户）的竞争就开始显得迥然不同。

“很多国家都在关注美国和中国之间的竞争，他们现在不想选边站，因为竞争才刚刚开始，”The Asia Group合伙人兼数字实践主席George Chen表示。美国总统唐纳德·特朗普和中国国家主席习近平都将AI领导地位列为[国家优先事项](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/newsletters/2026-07-21/china-s-world-ai-conference-and-tsmc-s-arizona-investment-mark-wins-for-leaders?srnd=undefined&amp%3Bsref=QpGTssUQ)。“当习近平9月访问白宫时，因为Kimi，因为阿里巴巴和他们昨晚刚发布的模型，习近平肯定会觉得自己用特朗普的话说，有更多牌可打。”

快速的进步正在[加剧](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-03/china-is-getting-more-anxious-about-mythos-before-trump-meets-xi)美中科技摩擦，促使华盛顿在Moonshot的Kimi取得突破后，以知识产权问题为由威胁实施制裁。特朗普已表示，他的政府正在权衡中国的威胁与对Anthropic的Fable或OpenAI的GPT系列等产品实施额外安全管控的需要。“我们必须两方面都小心，”他上周三在椭圆形办公室对记者表示。“我们不希望在限制他们之后，突然间我们落后于中国。”

为了赢得开发者，AI竞争者现在要么必须在价格上击败DeepSeek，要么在纯粹能力上超越它。在Artificial Analysis一份被广泛分享的[基准测试](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models?models=mimo-v2-5-pro%2Cgemini-3-5-flash-lite%2Cinkling%2Cclaude-sonnet-5%2Cminimax-m3%2Ccommand-a-plus%2Cqwen3-8-max%2Cgpt-5-6-luna%2Cnvidia-nemotron-3-ultra-550b-a55b%2Cqwen3-7-max%2Cgemini-3-6-flash%2Cgrok-4-5%2Cclaude-4-5-haiku-reasoning%2Cclaude-opus-5%2Cgpt-5-6-terra%2Cdeepseek-v4-pro%2Cgemma-4-31b%2Cclaude-fable-5%2Cmuse-spark-1-1%2Cgpt-5-6-sol%2Cmistral-medium-3-5%2Cgpt-5-5-pro%2Cgpt-oss-120b%2Cglm-5-2%2Ckimi-k3%2Cdeepseek-v4-flash-0731&amp%3Bcost=intelligence-vs-cost-per-task#cost-tabs)图表中，现已出现所谓的DeepSeek死亡地带。同等产品收费更高，或同等价格能力更弱，那么你不如不试。跨越这一危险地带的边界已成为长期生存的关键。中端市场竞争对手现在面临压力，要么大幅降价以匹配这家杭州初创公司，要么重金投入打造更智能的模型。更大的GLM-5.2、Kimi K3和Qwen3.8-Max模型都位于更高性能档次，在其之上。

“我认为DeepSeek V4 Flash真正改变经济模式的地方在于智能体工作负载，”位于上海的初创咨询公司ZenGen Labs创始人Dermot McGrath表示。

麦格拉思表示，在研究数百家公司时，他仍将Anthropic的Claude Code作为架构师，用于起草行动计划和摘要简报。然后，他在同一环境中将执行工作交给DeepSeek，后者部署专门的AI智能体来运行查询并完成任务。近几个月来，AI工作的成本不断上升，效率已成为更高的优先事项。这为服务创造了机会，可以增强而非必然取代美国最先进的产品。

“几个月前我不会这么做。中国模型在工具调用或长时运行的智能体工作流方面不够可靠，”麦格拉思说。阿里巴巴针对他的这一痛点，在其新的Qwen版本中改进了所谓的长程执行能力。之前的模型“更慢、更脆弱，有时甚至不完全理解自己该做什么。”

总部位于北京的Z.ai也将提升最新版本的长程性能作为优先事项。过去一年里，中国公司以惊人的速度迭代，内部竞争加速了时间表并聚焦重点。DeepSeek对国内竞争的冲击不亚于其对全球格局的冲击。

Moonshot和阿里巴巴下了更冒险的赌注，[构建超大规模模型](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-20/chinese-ai-sensation-moonshot-s-gamble-on-big-models-pays-off)，每个模型的参数超过2万亿，这表明其技术的精密程度。他们通过每次只激活模型的一部分来控制能源和计算成本。相比之下，OpenAI和Anthropic将参数数量视为机密，不披露具体数字。

参数是AI大脑的突触，帮助系统在更多信息的辅助下进行存储、处理和响应。

美国两大顶级AI实验室都在筹备首次公开募股，渴望实现[至少一万亿美元的市场估值](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-08/openai-filed-confidentially-for-ipo-as-rivals-race-to-market)，这一惊人目标依赖于高利润率的商业模式。然而，中国廉价且开源的竞争如今正威胁着它们的定价能力。

“如果没有这些中国的开源模型，OpenAI和Anthropic早就赚得盆满钵满了，”AI先驱李开复表示，他的初创公司01.ai向全球客户提供开放模型。“现在有了替代品，而且更便宜。”

当然，中国公司自身也在为了争夺用户、开发者和技术领导地位而牺牲近期可观利润的前景。“中国的AI提供商已陷入残酷的价格战，优先考虑市场份额而非盈利能力，”彭博智能高级分析师Rob Lea表示，“许多公司过于愿意为了市场份额而牺牲利润率。”

除了核心语言模型，中国的AI势头在视频生成领域最为明显。在OpenAI因运营资源密集型服务成本高昂而搁置其Sora工具后，这一市场被完全留空。[快手科技](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/1024%20HK%20Equity)的Kling AI视频工具填补了这一空白，最近在阿里巴巴和[腾讯控股有限公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/700%20HK%20Equity)的支持下完成了[28亿美元融资](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-02/china-s-kling-ai-raises-2-billion-to-expand-ai-video-operations)。中国实力的一大重要部分来源于企业和投资者愿意忽视对即时回报的担忧。

根据BI的Lea所言，没有哪家公司比字节跳动更能体现这种做法。它投入巨资使其Seedance成为AI视频创作质量的全球领导者，并加剧了价格战。有多激进？“相比市场主流价格折扣高达99%，”Lea说。

## China’s AI Blitz Creates ‘Death Zone’ for Rival US Model Makers

A flurry of model launches from China’s AI sector is rapidly narrowing the gap with Silicon Valley and creating what’s been described as a death zone for anyone without frontier-pushing technology or market-breaking pricing.

[Alibaba Group Holding Ltd.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/BABA%20US%20Equity) became the latest in a parade of Chinese debuts hitting the top of global benchmarks with its Qwen3.8-Max this week, its most advanced model to date that appeared to match or exceed [Anthropic PBC](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1892140D:US)’s flagship Fable 5. Two weeks earlier, Moonshot AI’s Kimi K3 showed performance comparable to the priciest US options built on a much humbler budget, sparking [fresh questions](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-17/what-is-moonshot-ai-why-china-s-new-model-is-roiling-markets) over the effectiveness of US chip sanctions intended to slow China’s tech ascent.

Elsewhere, [ByteDance Ltd.](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1774397D:CH) has pushed aside all rivals in video generation with its Seedance, version 2.5 of which has [just come out](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-31/china-s-minimax-and-bytedance-release-dueling-ai-video-models). DeepSeek, the original Chinese disruptor of US hegemony in artificial intelligence, returned this summer with [V4 Flash](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-31/deepseek-unveils-public-beta-api-for-flagship-ai-model), a breakthrough model in terms of pricing. What was once an isolated shockwave is now a cascade of unexpected depth — delivering not just low-cost alternatives, but high-end capabilities in reasoning, coding and complex tasks that give [OpenAI](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1554630D:US) and its US peers real competition.

“The most important change since January 2025 is that China’s progress no longer looks like a single-company breakthrough,” said Poe Zhao, a Beijing-based tech analyst and founder of the Hello China Tech newsletter. “The first DeepSeek moment looked exceptional. The recent releases suggest China now has a repeatable system for producing models close to the global frontier.”

Taken together with [Z.ai](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/2513%20HK%20Equity)’s GLM-5.2 in June, the highest-ranked open-source model globally at the time, this blitz of five models in eight weeks shows Chinese developers approaching or in some cases surpassing the US pioneers that are commonly held up as frontier AI leaders. The core value proposition isn’t just low price — it’s radical efficiency.

In [benchmark testing](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models#cost-tabs) by independent evaluator Artificial Analysis, executing a complex real-world workload costs just $0.03 with DeepSeek V4 Flash, compared to $3.15 with Claude Fable 5. When China asks for cents where American companies charge dollars, the contest between the two nations for AI customers — especially ones in the rest of the world — starts to look materially different.

“A lot of countries are looking at the competition between US and China, and they don’t want to take a side right now because the competition’s just starting,” said George Chen, partner and Digital Practice chair at The Asia Group. Both President Donald Trump and his counterpart Xi Jinping have made AI leadership a [national priority](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/newsletters/2026-07-21/china-s-world-ai-conference-and-tsmc-s-arizona-investment-mark-wins-for-leaders?srnd=undefined&amp%3Bsref=QpGTssUQ). “When Xi visits the White House in September, because of Kimi, because of Alibaba and the model they just released last night, Xi will definitely feel like he has, in Trump’s language, more cards to play.”

The rapid advances are [deepening](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-03/china-is-getting-more-anxious-about-mythos-before-trump-meets-xi) US-China tech friction, prompting Washington to threaten sanctions over intellectual property concerns after Moonshot’s Kimi breakthrough. Trump has signaled that his administration is weighing China’s threat against the need for additional safety controls on products like Anthropic’s Fable or OpenAI’s GPT series. “We have to be careful in both ways,” he told reporters in the Oval Office last Wednesday. “We don’t want to restrict them where all of a sudden, we come in second to China.”

To win over developers, AI contenders must now either beat DeepSeek on price or surpass it on pure capability. On a widely shared [benchmark](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models?models=mimo-v2-5-pro%2Cgemini-3-5-flash-lite%2Cinkling%2Cclaude-sonnet-5%2Cminimax-m3%2Ccommand-a-plus%2Cqwen3-8-max%2Cgpt-5-6-luna%2Cnvidia-nemotron-3-ultra-550b-a55b%2Cqwen3-7-max%2Cgemini-3-6-flash%2Cgrok-4-5%2Cclaude-4-5-haiku-reasoning%2Cclaude-opus-5%2Cgpt-5-6-terra%2Cdeepseek-v4-pro%2Cgemma-4-31b%2Cclaude-fable-5%2Cmuse-spark-1-1%2Cgpt-5-6-sol%2Cmistral-medium-3-5%2Cgpt-5-5-pro%2Cgpt-oss-120b%2Cglm-5-2%2Ckimi-k3%2Cdeepseek-v4-flash-0731&amp%3Bcost=intelligence-vs-cost-per-task#cost-tabs) chart from Artificial Analysis, there’s now emerged a so-called DeepSeek death zone. Charge more for the same product or be less capable for the same price and you might as well not try. Clearing the boundaries of that danger zone has become essential for long-term viability. Mid-market rivals are now under pressure to either slash prices to match the Hangzhou-based startup or spend heavily to build smarter models. The bigger GLM-5.2, Kimi K3 and Qwen3.8-Max models all sit above it in a higher performance bracket.

“Where I think DeepSeek V4 Flash has really changed the economics is agentic workloads,” said Dermot McGrath, founder of Shanghai-based startup consultancy ZenGen Labs.

When researching hundreds of companies, McGrath said he still uses Anthropic’s Claude Code as an architect to draft an action plan and summary brief. He then hands off execution to DeepSeek within the same environment, which deploys specialized AI agents to run queries and finish the task. The cost of AI work has inflated over recent months and efficiency has risen to be an even greater priority. That opens opportunity for services to augment, not necessarily replace, the most advanced US products.

“A few months ago I wouldn’t have done that. The Chinese models weren’t as reliable at tool calling or long-running agent workflows,” McGrath said. Alibaba, addressing his exact pain point, made improvements in the so-called long-horizon execution of its new Qwen release. Previous models “were slower, more brittle and sometimes didn’t fully understand what they were supposed to do.”

Beijing-based Z.ai also made a priority of enhancing its long-horizon performance with the latest release. Chinese companies have spent the past year iterating at a blistering speed, with internal competition helping accelerate timelines and sharpen focus. DeepSeek disrupted the Chinese contest as much as it did the global picture.

Moonshot and Alibaba made the riskier bet of [building very large models](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-20/chinese-ai-sensation-moonshot-s-gamble-on-big-models-pays-off), each with more than 2 trillion parameters, an indication of their sophistication. They then moderated their energy and computing costs by only activating segments of the model at a time. OpenAI and Anthropic, by contrast, treat their parameter counts as confidential and do not disclose specific figures.

Parameters are the synapses of an AI brain that help systems store, process and respond with the help of more information.

The two premier US AI labs are both planning initial public offerings with aspirations for [market valuations of at least a trillion dollars](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-08/openai-filed-confidentially-for-ipo-as-rivals-race-to-market), a staggering mark that hinges on high-margin business models. Yet China’s cheap and open-source competition now threatens their pricing power.

“If there weren’t these Chinese open-source models, OpenAI and Anthropic would be laughing all the way to the bank,” said Kai-Fu Lee, an AI pioneer whose startup 01.ai offers open models to global clients. “Now there’s an alternative, and it’s cheaper.”

To be sure, Chinese companies are themselves sacrificing the prospect for significant profit in the near term in an all-out rush for users, developers and tech leadership recognition. “China’s AI providers have become trapped in a brutal price war, prioritizing market share over profitability,” Firms Too Willing to Sacrifice Margin for Market Share Bloomberg Intelligence senior analyst Rob Lea.

Beyond core language models, China’s AI momentum is most visible in the way it’s taken over the realm of video generation. That market has been left wide open after OpenAI shelved its Sora tool, citing the high costs of operating a resource-intensive service. [Kuaishou Technology](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/1024%20HK%20Equity)’s Kling AI video tool, stepping into the void, drew backing from Alibaba and [Tencent Holdings Ltd.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/700%20HK%20Equity) recently in a [$2.8 billion financing round](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-02/china-s-kling-ai-raises-2-billion-to-expand-ai-video-operations). A significant part of China’s strength comes from the willingness of companies and investors to disregard concern about immediate returns.

No company has exemplified that approach more than ByteDance, according to BI’s Lea. It has poured money into making its Seedance the global leader on quality for AI video creation and stoked the price wars. How aggressive? “A 99% discount to prevailing market rates,” Lea said.
