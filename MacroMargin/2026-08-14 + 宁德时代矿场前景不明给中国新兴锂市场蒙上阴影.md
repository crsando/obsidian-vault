### 中文译文

## 宁德时代矿场前景不明给中国新兴锂市场蒙上阴影

在[宁德时代](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/300750%20CH%20Equity)旗舰锂矿首次[停产](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-08-10/catl-suspends-production-at-china-lithium-mine-for-three-months)一年后，围绕其命运的猜测仍在引发剧烈的价格波动，交易员和分析师日益担忧，中国新兴交易所合约的波动正在掩盖其他驱动因素。

围绕这家电池巨头枧下窝矿的传闻和未经证实的报道，导致锂价自5月以来[下跌](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-22/lithium-plunges-in-china-on-speculation-over-status-of-catl-mine)近30%，市场担心这座约占全球供应量4%的矿场恢复生产。

但在缺乏全面实际复产的情况下，一些市场参与者认为，这座引人注目的矿场正在主导市场注意力，尤其是在散户参与者中，而此时锂正受益于为快速扩张的人工智能数据中心供电的电池储能[需求](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-12-12/lithium-may-get-much-needed-demand-boost-from-battery-storage)激增。

“锂市场与实际情况脱节，”咨询公司House Mountain Partners创始人Chris Berry表示。“如今锂需求可以说比过去几个周期都要强劲，”他补充道，新的供应“要投入运营仍然困难且资本密集。”

![图片](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3bc81cc90b1481929740d586ea6aeba6/3bc81cc9-0b14-815c-a0ba-fdeff14a5398-379d2f8f8a41db7379dd079658b70663.png)

碳酸锂期货于2023年在广州期货交易所首次上市，此举当时被视为提高锂价透明度、为生产商和买家提供更有效对冲风险工具的机会。这一亮相帮助提升了芝加哥商品交易所集团（CME Group）锂期货的交易活跃度。

广州期货交易所（GFEX）的未平仓合约和成交量在过去一年飙升至创纪录水平。但价格波动屡次促使该交易所介入，限制新开仓或提高交易费用，以抑制波动。

该交易所最活跃的锂合约6月在两天内下跌9%，此前有关政府初步土地评估通知的报道[引发押注](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-22/lithium-plunges-in-china-on-speculation-over-status-of-catl-mine)该矿可能重新开放。上周五，该合约一度上涨3%，此前有报道题为“据当地政府，宁德时代关键锂矿仍处于停产状态”，该报道称该矿实际上仍处于关闭状态，并仍在等待环境审批。

“围绕复产时间和范围的持续猜测，引发了GFEX乃至CME一些最剧烈的交易日，”大宗商品经纪公司Freight Investor Services电池金属主管Anna Chadwick表示。“新闻对市场的实际影响应该不会那么剧烈，因为其中大部分已被消化。随之而来的投机资金涌入GFEX，使我们的市场更多受仓位和情绪影响，而非基本面。”

广期所和宁德时代未立即回应置评请求。

![图片](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3bc81cc90b1481929740d586ea6aeba6/3bc81cc9-0b14-8191-8126-cecd0be830b4-63d51e0f143be8aff7aafa64b15ddb04.png)

锂在2022年价格繁荣开始消退后经历了艰难的几年，当时供应激增，而电动汽车的普及并未达到看涨预期。此后，大型电池成为新的需求驱动力，改变了局面。

雅保公司（Albemarle Corp.）是一家主要的锂矿商，在上周的财报电话会议上提到了雅保称锂需求超过供应，市场趋紧（1），指出固定式储能需求和电动汽车销量的改善，而中国汽车制造商和电池制造商比亚迪公司（BYD Co.）即使在提高产量的情况下，也难以满足其第二代“刀片”电池的需求，比亚迪称电池供应紧张，限制出口。

与此同时，尽管澳大利亚产量增加，但供应依然紧张，行业参与者库存较低。在占开采产量略低于十分之一的津巴布韦自1月1日起实施锂精矿出口禁令后，市场可能进一步收紧。CRU集团分析师卡梅伦·休斯表示：“全球精矿供应中相当大的一部分可能被困在津巴布韦。”

8VantEdge Pte Ltd.旗下Arcane Green Metal Fund的基金经理YJ Lee表示：“在可预见的未来，宁德时代很可能仍是最大的电池制造商，任何这样的市场状况都不可避免地会把市场权力交给最大的参与者。”“但随着锂市场的增长，同样地，随着更多全球机构参与者的加入，广期所期货市场逐渐成熟，市场的价格发现功能应该会改善。”

## CATL Mine’s Unclear Future Clouds China’s Nascent Lithium Market

A year after [Contemporary Amperex Technology Co. Ltd.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/300750%20CH%20Equity)’s flagship lithium mine was first [shuttered](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-08-10/catl-suspends-production-at-china-lithium-mine-for-three-months), speculation over its fate is still driving wild price swings, prompting growing concerns among traders and analysts that volatility in China’s nascent exchange contract is clouding other drivers.

Rumors and unverified reports around the battery giant’s Jianxiawo operation have contributed to a nearly 30% [decline](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-22/lithium-plunges-in-china-on-speculation-over-status-of-catl-mine) in lithium prices since May, as the market frets over the return of a mine that accounts for about 4% of global supply.

But absent an actual full-blown restart, some market participants argue that one eye-catching mine is dominating attention, especially among retail participants, at a time when lithium is benefiting from a surge in [demand](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-12-12/lithium-may-get-much-needed-demand-boost-from-battery-storage) for battery storage to power fast-expanding artificial intelligence data centers.

“The lithium market is disconnected from what’s truly going on,” said Chris Berry, founder of consultancy House Mountain Partners. “Lithium demand today is arguably stronger than it was in past cycles,” he said, adding that new supply “remains difficult and capital-intensive to bring online.”

Lithium carbonate futures were first traded on the Guangzhou Futures Exchange in 2023, a move seen at the time as an opportunity to bring more transparency to lithium pricing and give producers and buyers more effective ways to hedge risk. That debut helped boost trading activity for CME Group’s lithium futures.

Open interest and trading volume on the Guangzhou bourse, known as GFEX, have surged to a record within the past year. But price swings have repeatedly prompted the bourse to step in to cap new positions or raise trading fees in an effort to rein in volatility.

The most-active lithium contract on the exchange tumbled 9% over two days in June after reports of a preliminary government land assessment notice [fueled bets](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-22/lithium-plunges-in-china-on-speculation-over-status-of-catl-mine) that the mine may reopen. It rose as much as 3% last Friday after CATL’s Key Lithium Mine Remains Shut, Says Local Govt: Report reported the mine in fact remained shut and was still awaiting environmental approvals.

“Ongoing speculation surrounding the timing and scope of the restarting of mining capabilities has driven some of the most volatile trading days on GFEX, and in turn, CME,” said Anna Chadwick, head of battery metals at commodity brokerage Freight Investor Services. “The real impact of the news on the market should be less dramatic, as much of it has already been priced in. The resultant increase in speculative money flowing into GFEX is giving us a market more influenced by positioning and sentiment than fundamentals.”

GFEX and CATL didn’t immediately respond to requests for comments.

Lithium has endured a difficult few years after a price boom started fading in 2022 when supply surged and electric-vehicle adoption did not advance in line with bullish expectations. Large-scale batteries have since emerged as a new demand driver, changing the picture.

[Albemarle Corp.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/ALB%20US%20Equity), a major lithium miner, pointed to Albemarle Says Lithium Demand Tops Supply as Market Tightens (1) demand for stationary storage and improved EV sales during an earnings call last week, while Chinese automaker and battery manufacturer [BYD Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/1211%20HK%20Equity) has struggled to meet BYD Says Battery Supply Tight, Shipping Limits Exports for its second-generation “blade” batteries even as it ramps up production.

Supply, meanwhile, remains tight with low inventories among industry players, despite an [increase in output ](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-22/australian-lithium-mine-cleared-to-double-output-as-prices-soar)from Australia. The market may tighten further after Zimbabwe — which accounts for just under a 10th of mined production — enforces its [export ban](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-17/zimbabwe-rejects-lithium-miners-request-to-delay-export-ban) on lithium concentrate from Jan. 1. “A notable portion of global concentrate supply could be trapped in Zimbabwe,” said Cameron Hughes, analyst at CRU Group.

“CATL is likely to remain the largest battery manufacturer for the foreseeable future, and any such market condition will inescapably yield market power to its largest player,” said YJ Lee, fund manager at 8VantEdge Pte Ltd.’s Arcane Green Metal Fund. “But as the lithium market grows, and similarly the GFEX futures market matures with more global institutional players, the price discovery function of the market should improve.”
