### 中文译文

## 特朗普据悉可能拒绝美国业界加快多晶硅关税的要求

![A solar cell at the Qcells North America factory in Cartersville, Georgia, U.S. June 8, 2026. REUTERS/Alyssa Pointer/File Photo](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3b581cc90b14819588c5ce47365a993c/3b581cc9-0b14-814f-a54d-d9d011ed1e47-4a5313e6f7a6978ca19e9cc963768002.jpg)

美国佐治亚州卡特斯维尔 Qcells 北美工厂的太阳能电池，2026年6月8日。路透社/Alyssa Pointer/资料照片

华盛顿，8月6日（路透社）——一位知情人士称，特朗普政府预计将拒绝一些美国[太阳能](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/solar/)制造商提出的加快实施多晶硅产品进口新底价和关税时间表的要求。

四位消息人士称，针对多晶硅衍生物的新15%[关税](https://www.reuters.com/business/tariffs/)和底价措施，[路透社报道](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/trump-administration-impose-15-tariff-polysilicon-sources-say-2026-08-05/)最早可能于周四公布，最初计划在公告后120天生效。

但业内一些人士一直在游说政府将实施时间表定为90天，他们认为多出一个月将给此类产品的最大生产国[中国](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/)太多时间向[美国](https://www.reuters.com/world/us/)销售，从而损害美国竞争对手的利益。

当被问及加快时间表的提议时，一位白宫官员表示，政府正在仔细审视国内生产能力，但不会超越总统。

商务部进行了调查，发现多晶硅产品进口威胁国家安全，从而促成了这些措施，该部门尚未立即回应置评请求。

报道：Alexandra Alper、Nichola Groom 和 Jarrett Renshaw；编辑：Mark Porter

## Trump likely to reject US industry requests for quicker polysilicon tariff, source says

A solar cell at the Qcells North America factory in Cartersville, Georgia, U.S. June 8, 2026. REUTERS/Alyssa Pointer/File Photo

WASHINGTON, Aug 6 (Reuters) - The Trump administration is expected to spurn requests from ‌some U.S. [solar](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/solar/) manufacturers for a speedier timeline to put in place a new price floor and tariff on imports of polysilicon products, a person familiar with the matter said.

A new 15% [tariff](https://www.reuters.com/business/tariffs/) ⁠for the polysilicon derivatives and price floor, which [Reuters reported](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/trump-administration-impose-15-tariff-polysilicon-sources-say-2026-08-05/) could be unveiled as soon as Thursday, was initially slated to kick in 120 days following the announcement, four sources said.

But some in the industry have been lobbying the administration for a 90-day timeline for implementation, arguing that an extra month ‌would ⁠give [China](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/), a top producer of such products, too much time to sell into [the United States](https://www.reuters.com/world/us/), hurting U.S. rivals.

When asked about the proposal for an expedited timeframe, a ⁠White House official said the administration is closely examining domestic production capabilities but would not get ahead of ⁠the president.

The Commerce Department, which conducted the investigation that found polysilicon product imports threaten national security, ⁠prompting the measures, did not immediately respond to a request for comment.

Reporting by Alexandra Alper, Nichola Groom and Jarrett Renshaw; Editing by Mark Porter
