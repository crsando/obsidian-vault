## 特朗普威胁要孤立伊朗。谁是其贸易伙伴？

![A drone view shows vessels in the Strait of Hormuz, as seen from Musandam](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3c381cc90b1481408c6bff6407947678/3c381cc9-0b14-813d-9f26-dd660b4983ed-8f1e8e015eb33a014a67b4e2a7c0d103.jpg)

无人机视角显示霍尔木兹海峡的船只，摄于阿曼穆桑代姆，2026年6月15日。路透社/自由摄影师/资料图

迪拜，8月20日 - 美国总统唐纳德·特朗普威胁要对伊朗实施前所未有的[经济战](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/trump-warns-economic-consequences-any-country-that-supports-iran-2026-08-20/)和孤立，这可能对与伊朗有贸易往来的国家产生影响。[特朗普](https://www.reuters.com/world/iran/)警告称，任何支持伊朗的国家都将面临经济后果。

以下是伊朗近年来的一些主要贸易伙伴：

### 中国

中国是伊朗石油的最大买家，如果特朗普兑现承诺，中国的石油采购可能面临来自美国的额外压力。

过去十年，中国建立了一个隔离的炼油体系，这些炼油厂主要加工伊朗石油，与美国业务往来不多。

炼油厂消息人士和参与交易的贸易商表示，运往中国的伊朗石油长期以来被标记为马来西亚原产，最近则标记为印度尼西亚原产，其交易在一个封闭的循环中进行，以人民币结算，并涉及一系列难以追踪的中间商。

美国财政部4月对一家中国独立炼油厂[实施制裁](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/us-imposes-sanctions-chinese-teapot-refinery-buying-iranian-oil-2026-04-24/)，因其购买了价值数十亿美元的伊朗石油，并警告中国银行，如果它们为伊朗石油贸易提供便利，将面临[二级制裁](https://www.reuters.com/world/americas/us-targets-irans-oil-transportation-infrastructure-with-sanctions-2026-04-15/)。

"制裁和压力解决不了问题。中国呼吁有关各方采取负责任的措施，通过政治和外交手段解决这个问题。"中国外交部发言人林剑在新闻发布会上对记者表示。

伊朗出口的石油中，超过80%运往中国，中国独立炼油商利用受美国制裁的折扣石油。Kpler估计，2025年中国平均每日购买138万桶伊朗石油。

### 阿拉伯联合酋长国

阿拉伯联合酋长国历来是伊朗最关键的经济生命线之一，迪拜的银行长期持有大量与伊朗相关的存款，其中大部分现在因美国制裁而被冻结。

根据世界贸易组织的最新数据，阿联酋曾是伊朗最大的贸易伙伴之一，2024年提供了伊朗进口的30%，总额达210亿美元。数据显示，同年阿联酋还占伊朗出口的13%。阿联酋经济部数据显示，2024年两国非石油对外贸易总额为66亿美元，其中绝大多数贸易以转口贸易为代表。

本周，阿联酋宣布暂停与伊朗的所有金融和经济交易，直至另行通知，理由是德黑兰的军事升级和导弹威胁，这重新聚焦了两国之间紧张的关系。

### 土耳其

土耳其和伊朗保持着重要的经济联系，土耳其进口伊朗天然气并向伊朗出口制成品。土耳其没有表示任何削减与伊朗贸易的意愿，双边贸易额每年约50-60亿美元，其中土耳其对伊朗出口约30亿美元。土耳其几乎全部天然气消费依赖进口，而伊朗是重要供应来源，占其全部天然气进口的13%。

### 伊拉克

据伊拉克官方贸易数据，2025年伊拉克与伊朗的贸易额超过100亿美元，这种关系主要由伊朗向伊拉克市场出口食品、消费品和其他产品推动。伊拉克贸易部熟悉与伊朗贸易活动的官员穆罕默德·卡里姆表示，2026年伊朗战争开始后，贸易有所下降，这增加了地区安全风险，并导致主要边境口岸的交通间歇性中断，以及运输成本上升。卡里姆说，这些情况，加上影响货物流动的更广泛供应链中断，降低了跨境贸易的规模和可靠性，尽管两国仍然是重要的贸易伙伴。

能源是两国关系的关键部分。据伊拉克能源官员称，伊拉克每年向伊朗支付约40亿至50亿美元购买天然气，这些天然气用于发电。两名伊拉克能源官员表示，美国对伊拉克实施任何新制裁都可能给巴格达在维持对伊朗能源付款的同时避免受到美国制裁带来重大挑战，特别是考虑到现有限制迫使伊拉克使用受限账户结算付款。

### 阿曼

阿曼长期以来一直与伊朗保持友好关系，这种关系可追溯到1979年伊斯兰革命之前，马斯喀特经常充当伊朗与其他国家（包括美国）之间的调解人。

根据阿曼国家统计和信息中心的数据，近年来两国的贸易关系有所增长，2025年货物贸易总额达15亿美元，今年前四个月达3.45亿美元。

### 巴基斯坦

分析人士称，在巴基斯坦和伊朗承诺将双边贸易额扩大到100亿美元之际，美国对参与对伊朗贸易或援助的国家采取任何行动都可能对巴基斯坦造成严重挫折。

在以往对伊朗实施制裁后，两国之间的正式贸易一度停止，直到最近才恢复。然而，据非官方统计，非正式贸易使双方的进出口总额达到约40亿美元。六月签署伊斯兰堡临时协议后，双方迅速努力扩大贸易合作。巴基斯坦和伊朗多年来一直通过非正规渠道交易石油、小麦、大米、牲畜和药品。

### 印度

印度与伊朗的贸易在2020年大幅下降，当时华盛顿对德黑兰持续施压制裁。

双边贸易额较上年的170亿美元减少逾三分之二，在2019-20财年降至近48亿美元。据印度商务部数据，多年来印伊贸易进一步下滑，在2025/26财年达到16.3亿美元。

印度出口额达13亿美元，占该贸易的绝大部分，因为新德里主要向伊朗运送谷物、茶叶、咖啡和香料。

新德里官员此前坚称，这些出口是基于人道主义理由继续进行的，应不受任何制裁范围限制。

### 亚美尼亚

根据亚美尼亚官方统计数据，2025年伊朗占邻国亚美尼亚总贸易额的7.68亿美元，即3.6%。2026年上半年，亚美尼亚与伊朗的贸易额达3.714亿美元，增长8.4%。其中出口额为4280万美元，下降6%；进口额为3.369亿美元，增长12%。

亚美尼亚约20%的对外贸易经由伊朗。亚美尼亚和伊朗之间有一项“天然气换电力”的交换协议，亚美尼亚使用伊朗供应的天然气发电，所发电量既输回伊朗，也用于亚美尼亚国内。

### 阿塞拜疆

据阿塞拜疆国家海关委员会数据，2026年1月至6月，阿塞拜疆与伊朗的贸易额比2025年同期增长4.5%，从2.991亿美元增至3.126亿美元。阿塞拜疆从伊朗的进口额增长1.5%，从2025年同期的2.927亿美元增至2.97亿美元。

阿塞拜疆对伊朗的出口额从2025年1月至6月的640万美元增长2.4倍，至1560万美元。

伊朗占阿塞拜疆总进口的3.56%，高于2025年同期的2.54%。

## Trump threatens to isolate Iran. Who are its trading partners?

A drone view shows vessels in the Strait of Hormuz, as seen from Musandam, Oman, June 15, 2026. REUTERS/Stringer/File Photo

DUBAI, August 20 - A threat by U.S. President Donald Trump to impose unprecedented [economic warfare](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/trump-warns-economic-consequences-any-country-that-supports-iran-2026-08-20/) and isolation on Iran could have implications for countries that trade with it. [Trump](https://www.reuters.com/world/iran/) warned of economic consequences for any country that supports Iran.

Here are some of Iran's main trading partners in recent years:

### CHINA

China, the biggest buyer of Iranian oil, could face additional pressures from the U.S. over its purchases if Trump follows through on his promise.

Over the past decade, China has created a ring-fenced system of refiners that process mainly Iranian oil and do not have much U.S. exposure.

Iranian oil delivered to China has long been branded as Malaysian, and more recently Indonesian, and trades in a tight loop, is settled in Chinese currency and involves a chain of difficult-to-track ‌intermediaries, say refinery sources and traders involved in the business.

The U.S. Treasury in April [imposed sanctions](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/us-imposes-sanctions-chinese-teapot-refinery-buying-iranian-oil-2026-04-24/) on a Chinese independent refinery for buying billions of dollars’ worth of Iranian oil and warned Chinese banks of [secondary sanctions](https://www.reuters.com/world/americas/us-targets-irans-oil-transportation-infrastructure-with-sanctions-2026-04-15/) if they facilitated trade of Iranian oil.

"Sanctions and pressure will not solve the problem. China calls on all parties concerned to take responsible measures to resolve the issue through political and diplomatic means," Chinese Foreign Ministry spokesperson Lin Jian told reporters in a briefing.

Over 80% of Iran’s shipped oil has been destined for China, as Chinese independent refiners take advantage of discounted U.S.-sanctioned oil. Kpler estimates that China bought an average of 1.38 million barrels per day of Iranian oil in 2025.

### UNITED ARAB EMIRATES

The United Arab Emirates has historically been one of Iran's most critical economic lifelines, and Dubai's banks have long held substantial Iranian-linked deposits, much of them now immobilised under U.S. sanctions.

The UAE was among Iran's largest trade partners before the [war](https://www.reuters.com/world/iran/), providing 30% of its imports worth an overall $21 billion in 2024, according to the World Trade Organization's latest figures. It ⁠also accounted for 13% of Iran's exports in the same year, the data showed. Non-oil foreign trade between the two countries totalled $6.6 billion in 2024, according to UAE economy ministry data, with the vast majority of trade represented by re-exports.

This week, the UAE suspended all financial and economic transactions with Iran until further notice, citing a military escalation by Tehran with a missile threat, refocusing the spotlight on fraught ties between the countries.

### TURKEY

Turkey and Iran maintain significant economic ties, with Turkey importing Iranian natural gas and exporting manufactured goods to Iran. Turkey has not signalled any willingness to curtail commerce with Iran, with bilateral trade standing at around $5-6 billion annually, with Turkey exporting to Iran around $3 billion. Turkey imports almost all its natural gas consumption and Iran is a notable source, providing 13% of all gas imports.

### IRAQ

Iraq’s trade with Iran reached more than $10 billion in 2025, according to official Iraqi trade figures, with the relationship largely driven by Iranian exports of food, consumer goods and other products to the Iraqi market. Trade declined in 2026 after the Iran war started, which increased regional security risks and led to intermittent disruptions along key border crossings, as well as higher transport costs, said Mohammed Karim, an Iraqi trade ministry official with knowledge of trade activities with Iran. These conditions, combined with broader supply chain disruptions affecting the movement of goods, reduced the volume and reliability of cross-border commerce although the countries remain important trading partners, said Karim.

Energy is a key part of the relationship. Iraq pays Iran around $4 billion to $5 billion a year for natural gas, according to Iraqi energy officials, with the gas used to generate electricity. Two Iraqi energy officials said any new U.S. sanctions on ‌Iraq could create ⁠significant challenges for Baghdad in maintaining payments for Iranian energy while avoiding exposure to U.S. sanctions, particularly given existing restrictions that have forced Iraq to use restricted accounts to settle payments.

### OMAN

Oman has long maintained cordial ties with Iran, dating back to before the 1979 Islamic revolution, with Muscat often acting as a mediator between Iran and other countries, including the U.S.

The countries' trade relationship has grown in recent years, with trade of goods totalling $1.5 billion in 2025 and $345 million in the first four months of this year, according to data from Oman's National Centre for Statistics & Information.

### PAKISTAN

For Pakistan, any U.S. action against countries involved in trade or assistance to Iran could be a serious setback, analysts said, amid Pakistan-Iran commitments to expand bilateral trade to $10 billion.

Following past sanctions on Iran, formal trade between the two countries had come to a halt until recently. However, informal trade has taken exports and imports from both sides to approximately $4 billion, according ⁠to unofficial statistics. After the Islamabad interim agreement was signed in June, efforts were quickly made to expand trade cooperation. Pakistan and Iran have for years traded oil, wheat, rice, live stocks and medicines through informal channels.

### INDIA

India's trade with Iran dropped sharply in 2020, when Washington pressed on with sanctions on Tehran.

Two-way trade was cut by more than two-thirds to nearly $4.8 billion in the fiscal year 2019-20, from $17 billion in the previous year. Over the years, trade between India and Iran has fallen further, and stood at $1.63 billion in the fiscal year 2025/26, according to data from India's commerce ministry.

Indian exports worth $1.3 billion make for the ⁠bulk of this trade as New Delhi predominantly ships cereals, tea, coffee and spices to Iran.

Officials in New Delhi have previously maintained that such exports continue on humanitarian grounds and should remain outside the scope of any sanctions.

### ARMENIA

Iran accounted for $768 million, or 3.6% of neighbouring Armenia’s total trade turnover in 2025, according to official Armenian statistics. Armenia's trade turnover with Iran reached $371.4 million in the first half of 2026, marking an 8.4% increase. Exports were recorded at $42.8 million, a 6% decrease, while imports amounted to $336.9 million, a 12% increase.

Some 20% of Armenia’s foreign trade passes through ⁠Iran. Armenia and Iran have a “gas for electricity” swap agreement, where Armenia uses gas supplied by Iran to make electricity, which is then sent back to Iran as well as being used domestically in Armenia.

### AZERBAIJAN

Trade turnover between Azerbaijan and Iran rose 4.5% in January-June 2026 compared with January-June 2025, to $312.6 million from $299.1 million, according to Azerbaijan’s State Customs Committee. Azerbaijan’s imports from Iran increased 1.5% to $297 million from $292.7 million in the same period of 2025.

Azerbaijan’s exports to Iran rose 2.4-fold to $15.6 million from $6.4 million in January-June 2025.

Iran accounted for 3.56% of Azerbaijan’s total imports, up from 2.54% in January-June 2025.
