### 中文译文

## 中国数据中心到2030年用电量将超过韩国

据[伍德麦肯兹有限公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/3251866Z:LN)称，到本十年末，中国数据中心的用电量将翻两番，届时其用电量将超过[韩国](https://www.bnef.com/countries/kor)目前的用电量。

伍德麦肯兹周二发布的报告称，人工智能工作负载的快速增长将使数据中心到2030年用电量达到774太瓦时，约占中国预计总用电量的6%。该机构表示，到2060年这一比例将升至17%。

AI热潮凸显了中国和其他国家在扩大发电量以满足激增需求方面面临的挑战。在美国，对快速建设数据中心的抵制正成为一个[选举议题](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-18/ai-election-backlash-is-shaping-wall-street-s-stock-market-views)，并成为华尔街股票推荐的一个因素。这些数据中心也消耗大量水资源。

伍德麦肯兹表示，虽然数据中心往往集中在主要城市和技术中心附近，但中国AI扩张现在正转向“计算跟随电力”的模式。该国西部地区人口稀少，拥有丰富的土地和不断增长的可再生能源发电能力，为靠近电力来源选址创造了机会。

伍德麦肯兹亚太电力和可再生能源分析师赵婉婷在报告中表示：“随着AI推动计算需求持续增长，获得可靠、具有成本竞争力且低碳的电力将在决定新数据中心产能的开发地点和方式方面发挥越来越重要的作用。”

### English Original

## China’s Data Centers to Use More Power Than South Korea by 2030

China’s data centers will quadruple their electricity consumption by the end of the decade, according to [Wood Mackenzie](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/3251866Z:LN) [Ltd.](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/3251866Z:LN), by which point they will use more power than [South Korea](https://www.bnef.com/countries/kor) does now.

Rapid growth in artificial intelligence workloads will see data centers use 774 terawatt-hours of electricity by 2030, around 6% of China’s total projected consumption, WoodMac said in a report released on Tuesday. The proportion would rise to 17% by 2060, it said.

The AI boom highlights the challenge for China and other countries in ramping up electricity generation to meet the surging demand. In the US, a backlash against the rapid buildout of data centers, which also consume a lot of water, is becoming an [election issue](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-18/ai-election-backlash-is-shaping-wall-street-s-stock-market-views) and a factor in Wall Street stock recommendations.

While data centers have tended to cluster around major cities and technology hubs, China’s AI expansion is now shifting to a “compute follows power” model, WoodMac said. Sparsely populated regions in the western part of the country offer abundant land and growing renewable generation capacity, creating opportunities to locate close to power sources, it said.

“As AI drives sustained growth in computing demand, access to reliable, cost-competitive and lower-carbon electricity will play a growing role in determining where and how new data-center capacity is developed,” Wanting Zhao, a Woodmac analyst for Asia Pacific power and renewables, said in the report.
