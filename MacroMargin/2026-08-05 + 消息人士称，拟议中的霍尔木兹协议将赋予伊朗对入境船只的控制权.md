### 中文译文

## 消息人士称，拟议中的霍尔木兹协议将赋予伊朗对入境船只的控制权

- 摘要
- 对海峡的任何控制权都将是对伊朗的重大让步
- 特朗普称谈判进展顺利
- 地区消息人士反驳协议即将达成的说法
- 胡塞武装袭击红海油轮后油价小幅上涨
迪拜/利雅得，8 月 5 日（路透社）——一名伊朗高级消息人士及两名地区官员周三向路透社透露，伊朗与阿曼拟议中的协议将赋予德黑兰对通过霍尔木兹海峡进入海湾船只的控制权，这成为迄今为止对[伊朗](https://www.reuters.com/world/iran/)做出的最大让步之一。

尽管在向伊朗的要求让步方面看似迈出了一步，但消息人士反驳了美国总统唐纳德·特朗普关于重新开放海峡的协议即将达成的说法，称仍有重要细节有待商定。

“关于对霍尔木兹海峡某种形式的控制，让步已经作出，”其中一位地区消息人士告诉路透社。

另一位地区消息人士表示，关于如何界定“控制”仍有细节有待确定，海湾地区谈判代表坚持由地区国家监督对船只的检查，并且任何费用支付都应是自愿的。

伊朗高级消息人士表示，已在谈判桌上的协议文本设想伊朗将控制通过海峡进入海湾的船只，而主要症结之一是伊朗在相反方向行驶的船只中扮演何种角色。

华盛顿方面对拟议协议的相关披露没有立即回应。

美国官员一再坚称，他们绝不会接受任何让伊朗控制世界最重要能源供应贸易通道的安排。但特朗普及其政府高级官员近日表示，即将达成结束他二月发起的战争的协议，而[民调显示](https://www.reuters.com/world/us/just-one-three-americans-back-iran-war-most-unsure-trumps-goals-reutersipsos-2026-07-27/)大多数美国选民反对这场战争。

### 红海袭击后油价小幅走高

周三油价小幅走高，此前也门与伊朗结盟的胡塞武装表示已向红海一艘悬挂沙特国旗的油轮[开火](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/yemens-houthis-say-they-attacked-saudi-oil-tanker-red-sea-2026-08-05/)，这是对中东航运的最新袭击，已扰乱全球能源供应。

但全球原油价格仍接近7月初以来的最低水平。过去两天，在特朗普以称可能结束冲突的新谈判为由取消对伊朗的新袭击后，油价大幅下跌。

虽然德黑兰和华盛顿之间没有直接会谈，但伊朗一直在与控制海峡对岸的阿曼进行谈判。上周，伊朗拒绝了早些时候阿曼提出的提议，认为该提议赋予伊朗的发言权太小。

### 特朗普称谈判“进展顺利”

特朗普隔夜对福克斯新闻表示，谈判“进展顺利”，周二进行了“全天谈判”。

特朗普补充说，霍尔木兹海峡“将很快开放”。“如果他们再次退缩，他们将受到非常严厉的打击。”

上周末，特朗普将谈判作为取消对伊朗“大规模攻击”计划的原因，这一模式在战争期间他多次重复。

这位伊朗高级消息人士淡化了协议将很快敲定的说法，指出伊朗外长阿巴斯·阿拉格齐正在休假。

这位伊朗高级消息人士说：“谈判仍在继续，但现在说与阿曼的协议已经敲定还为时过早。”

另一位伊朗消息人士说：“细节决定成败。特朗普的一条推文就可能导致整个事情崩溃。”

一项赋予伊朗对霍尔木兹海峡通行权任何控制权的协议，将意味着美国和以色列发动的战争导致地区力量平衡发生重大转变，朝着有利于伊朗的方向发展。战前，该海峡对所有船只免费自由开放。

但特朗普最初表示“史诗般愤怒行动”将以伊朗的“[无条件投降](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/trump-urges-iranian-kurds-attack-iran-war-widens-2026-03-06/)”告终，并批准了[对其领导人的选择](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/trump-tells-reuters-us-will-have-role-choosing-irans-next-leader-2026-03-05/)，但他一直承受着寻找战争出路压力，[美国选民如今以二比一的比例反对这场战争](https://www.reuters.com/world/us/just-one-three-americans-back-iran-war-most-unsure-trumps-goals-reutersipsos-2026-07-27/)。

据这位伊朗高级官员称，伊朗希望向使用该海峡的船只收取货物价格5%至7%的费用。阿曼正在讨论约3%的较低费用，而华盛顿则希望完全不收费。

至少名义上让任何费用自愿缴纳，可能是摆脱僵局的一种方式，尽管伊朗攻击的隐含威胁可能意味着托运人不太可能在不付费的情况下冒险通行。

美国数月的军事努力，包括7月为期两周的打击行动，并未打破伊朗对海峡的控制。美国指挥官7月向特朗普报告称，部分弹药库存即将耗尽。路透社周二报道，美国陆军已[几乎用尽了其全部远程精确导弹](https://www.reuters.com/world/us-has-used-virtually-all-its-long-range-precision-missiles-during-iran-war-2026-08-04/)。

美国军队的周期性升级遭到了伊朗及其代理人更强烈的回应，包括控制也门大部分地区的胡塞武装，以及红海入口处的第二个主要区域航运咽喉要道。

周三，胡塞武装表示他们再次[在红海袭击了一艘沙特油轮](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/yemens-houthis-say-they-attacked-saudi-oil-tanker-red-sea-2026-08-05/)，这是他们为执行所谓的对沙特石油运输封锁而发动的最新袭击，而这条航线是绕过霍尔木兹海峡的主要通道。

印度官员称，周二一枚射弹在也门附近[击沉了一艘悬挂印度国旗的船只](https://www.reuters.com/world/projectile-sinks-indian-ship-near-yemeni-waters-all-seafarers-safe-indian-2026-08-04/)，所有14名船员（其中13名是印度人）均获救。胡塞武装在控制也门方面的对手——一个位于也门南部的政府——将此次袭击归咎于胡塞武装。

## Proposed Hormuz deal would give Iran control of inbound traffic, sources say

- Summary 摘要
- Any control over strait would be major concession to Iran 对海峡的任何控制权都将是对伊朗的重大让步
- Trump says talks going well 特朗普称谈判进展顺利
- Regional sources push back against suggestion deal is imminent 地区消息人士反驳协议即将达成的说法
- Oil prices tick higher after Houthi attack on Red Sea tanker 胡塞武装袭击红海油轮后油价小幅上涨
DUBAI/RIYADH, Aug 5 (Reuters) - A proposed deal between Iran and Oman would give Tehran control over ships entering the Gulf through the Strait of Hormuz, a senior Iranian source and two regional officials told Reuters on Wednesday, in one of the biggest concessions yet to [Iran](https://www.reuters.com/world/iran/).
迪拜/利雅得，8 月 5 日（路透社）——一名伊朗高级消息人士及两名地区官员周三向路透社透露，伊朗与阿曼拟议中的协议将赋予德黑兰对通过霍尔木兹海峡进入海湾船只的控制权，这成为迄今为止对伊朗做出的最大让步之一。

Despite the apparent step towards yielding to Iranian demands, the sources pushed back against assertions by U.S. President Donald Trump that ‌a deal reopening the strait was imminent, saying important details still had to be agreed.
尽管在向伊朗的要求让步方面看似迈出了一步，但消息人士反驳了美国总统唐纳德·特朗普关于重新开放海峡的协议即将达成的说法，称仍有重要细节有待商定。

"The concession has already been made regarding some form of control over Hormuz," one of the regional sources told Reuters.

The other regional source said there were still details to be determined about how "control" would be defined, with Gulf negotiators insisting regional countries supervise inspections of ships, and that any payment of fees be voluntary.

The senior Iranian source said that the text of an agreement already on the table envisioned Iran having control of ships heading into the Gulf through the strait, and that one of the main sticking points was what role Iran would also have over ships heading in the opposite direction.

There was no immediate reply from Washington to the disclosures about the proposed agreement.

U.S. officials have repeatedly insisted that they would never accept any arrangement that lets Iran control access ⁠to the world's most important trade route for energy supplies. But Trump and senior officials in his administration have said in recent days that a deal is imminent to end the war he launched in February and which [polls show](https://www.reuters.com/world/us/just-one-three-americans-back-iran-war-most-unsure-trumps-goals-reutersipsos-2026-07-27/) most U.S. voters oppose.

### OIL PRICES TICK HIGHER AFTER RED SEA ATTACK

Oil prices ticked higher on Wednesday after the Iran-aligned Houthi movement in Yemen said it had [fired on a Saudi-flagged tanker in the Red Sea](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/yemens-houthis-say-they-attacked-saudi-oil-tanker-red-sea-2026-08-05/), the latest attack on Middle East shipping that has disrupted global energy supplies.

But global crude prices remain close to their lowest levels since early July, having plunged sharply over the past two days after Trump called off fresh attacks on Iran citing new talks he said could end the conflict.

While there are no direct talks between Tehran and Washington, Iran has been negotiating with Oman, which controls the opposite shore of the strait. Iran rejected an earlier Omani proposal last week as giving it too little say.

### TRUMP SAYS TALKS 'MOVING ALONG NICELY'

Trump told Fox News overnight that talks were "moving along nicely" and "an all-day negotiation" had taken place on Tuesday.

The Strait of Hormuz "is going to be open very soon," Trump added. "If they back out again, they are going to get hit really hard."

Over the weekend Trump cited the negotiations as the reason for a decision to call off plans for "massive attacks" on Iran, a pattern he has repeated numerous times throughout the war.

The senior ‌Iranian source downplayed ⁠the suggestion that a deal would be finalised quickly, noting that Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araqchi was on holiday.

"Talks are continuing, but it is too early to say that a deal with Oman has been finalised," the senior Iranian source said.

Another Iranian source said: "The devil is in the details. A single tweet from Trump could cause the whole thing to collapse."

An agreement that gives Iran any control over access to the strait would mean the war launched by the United States and Israel had resulted in a major shift in the balance of regional power in Tehran's favour. Before the war, the strait was freely open to all ships with no fees.

But Trump, who initially said "Operation Epic Fury" would end in Iran's "[unconditional surrender](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/trump-urges-iranian-kurds-attack-iran-war-widens-2026-03-06/)" with him approving the [choice of its leader](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/trump-tells-reuters-us-will-have-role-choosing-irans-next-leader-2026-03-05/), ⁠has been under pressure to find a way out of a war [U.S. voters now oppose two-to-one](https://www.reuters.com/world/us/just-one-three-americans-back-iran-war-most-unsure-trumps-goals-reutersipsos-2026-07-27/).

According to the senior Iranian official, Iran was seeking fees of between 5% and 7% of the price of cargoes from ships using the strait. Oman was discussing smaller fees of around 3%, while Washington wants no fees at all.

Making any fees voluntary, at least nominally, could be a way out of the impasse, although an implied threat of Iranian attacks could mean shippers would be unlikely to risk transit without paying.

Months ⁠of U.S. military efforts, including a two-week campaign of strikes in July, have not broken Iran's grip on the strait. U.S. commanders advised Trump in July that they were running low on supplies of some munitions. Reuters reported on Tuesday that the U.S. Army had [used up nearly all of its long-range precision missiles](https://www.reuters.com/world/us-has-used-virtually-all-its-long-range-precision-missiles-during-iran-war-2026-08-04/).

Periodic U.S. military escalations have been met by stronger responses from Iran and its proxies, including the Houthi fighters who control much of Yemen as well ⁠as a second major regional shipping chokepoint at the mouth of the Red Sea.

On Wednesday, the Houthis said they had again [struck a Saudi tanker in the Red Sea](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/yemens-houthis-say-they-attacked-saudi-oil-tanker-red-sea-2026-08-05/), their latest attack to enforce what they say is a blockade of Saudi oil shipments through the main route used to bypass the Strait of Hormuz.

On Tuesday a projectile [sank an Indian-flagged vessel](https://www.reuters.com/world/projectile-sinks-indian-ship-near-yemeni-waters-all-seafarers-safe-indian-2026-08-04/) near Yemen, Indian officials said. All 14 crew members, 13 of them Indian, were rescued. The Houthis' rivals for control of Yemen, a government based in the south of the country, blamed that attack on the Houthis.
