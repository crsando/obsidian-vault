### 中文译文

## 美中竞逐金属，铜市场供应紧张正在酝酿

铜市场正在迅速收紧，运往美国的出货量激增以及中国订单增加，为可能将全球基准价格推至历史高位的上涨行情奠定了基础。

本周伦敦期货价格突破每吨14,000美元，这一阻力位今年仅有少数几天被突破，许多交易商认为价格很快将飙升至1月底中国一波[投机性买盘](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-01-29/copper-rallies-to-record-on-lme-as-base-metals-extend-bull-run)期间短暂触及的14,500美元以上的纪录水平。

这一次的上涨更多与世界两大经济体的吸引力造成的贸易错位有关。一方面，在预期关税决定之际，美国本土的铜囤积加速，规模空前；另一方面，交易商加大了对中国的出货，以缓解那里的[供应紧张](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-20/key-china-copper-gauge-rallies-to-100-after-tax-crackdown)。

除套利交易推动货物运往美国并推动纽约Comex期货周三创下纪录外，对中国市场的发货也在增加。这一套利交易自去年以来一直存在，但随着交易商等待白宫决定是否将半成品铜产品的关税扩大到原铜，已加速至至少12年来的[最快水平](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-03/us-copper-inflows-surge-as-market-awaits-trump-tariff-call)。

目前尚无迹象表明美国总统唐纳德·特朗普计划何时或是否宣布关税决定，关税一直是他为支撑工业供应链而采取的核心政策工具。总统将于周五在华盛顿与矿业高管举行[会议](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-06/rio-tinto-bhp-summoned-to-critical-minerals-meeting-with-trump)，以展示为促进关键矿产开发和加工所做的努力，并计划公布一些交易和谅解备忘录。

与此同时，在过去两周里，几乎每天都有4000至6000吨的铜被从伦敦金属交易所的仓库网络中提取，这加速了库存的稳步下滑——自5月份以来，支撑全球流动性最强铜合约的库存已减少逾40%。

据熟悉这些动向的交易商、仓库运营商和加工商称，其中大量金属，尤其是来自台湾和韩国的设施，已陆续运往中国。美国LME仓库也出现了出库，交易商利用Comex价格更高的机会。

据知情人士透露，[Mercuria Energy Group](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/0163947D:CY)、[Trafigura Group](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1188058D:SP)、[Vitol Group](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1284513D:NA)和[Hartree Partners LP](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/0077151D:US)最近几周均从LME仓库提取铜。这些人士因讨论非公开信息而要求匿名。

Mercuria、Trafigura、Vitol和Hartree的代表拒绝置评。

![图片](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3b581cc90b148151a3e2ec53f327a055/3b581cc9-0b14-81e4-826e-c440d12ca220-39a78dd61eaf16bcb349b3551d9b30aa.png)

这种抢购潮是因为中国（同时是全球最大铜生产国和消费国）的冶炼厂因原料供应紧张而减产，而客户不得不更加依赖进口，同时上海期货交易所库存也在减少。

在伦敦，典型的逼仓危险信号正在迅速堆积。周四，近期合约相对三个月期货的溢价触及每吨153.75美元，超过了1月底的水平。这种价格结构被称为现货升水（backwardation），表明短期供应紧张。

截至伦敦时间下午2:02，LME铜期货微涨0.1%，报每吨14,120美元。

![图片](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3b581cc90b148151a3e2ec53f327a055/3b581cc9-0b14-81eb-b45f-cf29ece60024-83bf107586da323c1f81b2010ce277db.png)

“即使当前需求并非十分强劲，供应受限导致库存下降，也可能带来阶梯式变化，”[花旗集团](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/C%20US%20Equity)金属策略师汤姆·穆尔奎恩（Tom Mulqueen）表示。“除美国以外，整体库存量并不大，在这种环境下，我们看到这种情况已部分反映在定价中。”

穆尔奎恩预计，未来几个月LME仓库持续的去库存可能有助于推动铜价升至每吨15,000美元。

这一切在很大程度上将取决于特朗普。如果他决定不对精炼铜征收关税，来自美国的吸引力将消散，从而为中国提供更多金属。如果他维持当前的不确定性，交易商预计价格会走高并出现逼仓。如果他真的宣布征收关税，价格大幅飙升几乎是可以肯定的，因为交易商可能会在关税生效前加紧向美国发货。

诚然，当铜价上涨过多时，中国的铜消费商往往会抑制买盘，而本周的上涨可能已让其中一些消费者犹豫不决，这可能会限制进口势头。

但中国铜产量的制约因素仍可能使市场保持紧张。据上海有色网（Shanghai Metals Market）的一份[报告](https://news.metal.com/newscontent/104036923-chinas-copper-cathode-production-continued-to-decline-more-than-expected-in-july-smm-analysis)，中国冶炼厂7月份的铜产量降幅超过预期。他们一直疲于应对安托法加斯塔（Antofagasta）在与铜冶炼厂交易中转向现货定价的情况增多，以及矿石品位下降（即其中所含金属含量降低）的问题。

与此同时，他们正面临[铜](https://news.smm.cn/news/103701499)废料短缺的问题，这是另一种重要的原料。再生铜占中国精炼铜产量的四分之一。

国内回收行业近几个月因发票方面更严格的监管挑战而受到严重干扰，SMM：2026年7月废铜市场价格飙升、供应紧张。废品回收商通常经营小型非正规企业，常常用完开票额度而无法完成销售。

过去20年来，废铜为恢复市场平衡提供了弹性。花旗的穆尔奎恩（Mulqueen）表示，为应对矿山供应减少或终端用户需求增长，价格通常会上升，以吸引更多废铜回收。

他表示：“由于这些额外的阻力，你会失去部分弹性。”

## Copper Market Crunch Brews as US and China Compete for Metal

The copper market is tightening fast, with a surge in shipments to the US and rising orders in China setting the stage for a rally that could take global benchmark prices to all-time highs.

Futures in London this week pushed past $14,000 a ton, a ceiling that had only been breached on a handful of days this year, and many traders see prices soon surging past the record $14,500-plus level reached briefly during a bout of [speculative buying](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-01-29/copper-rallies-to-record-on-lme-as-base-metals-extend-bull-run) in China at the end of January.

This time around, the upswing has more to do with trade dislocations caused by the gravitational pull of the world’s two largest economies. While an unprecedented hoarding of copper on US shores has sped up in anticipation of a tariff decision, traders have been stepping up shipments to China to alleviate [tightness](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-20/key-china-copper-gauge-rallies-to-100-after-tax-crackdown) there.

The flows to China come on top of an arbitrage trade that’s encouraging cargoes to the US and drove futures on New York’s Comex to a record on Wednesday. That has been going on since last year but has accelerated to the [fastest pace](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-03/us-copper-inflows-surge-as-market-awaits-trump-tariff-call) in at least 12 years as traders await a White House decision on whether to extend duties on semi-finished copper products to raw metal.

There’s been no indication when or whether US President Donald Trump plans to announce a decision on tariffs, which have been a core policy tool in his effort to shore up industrial supply chains. The president will be holding a [meeting](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-06/rio-tinto-bhp-summoned-to-critical-minerals-meeting-with-trump) with mining executives on Friday in Washington, in a bid to showcase efforts to help spur critical minerals development and processing, with plans to unveil a handful of a deals and memoranda of understanding.

Meanwhile, almost every day over the past two weeks, batches of 4,000 to 6,000 tons of copper have been removed from the London Metal Exchange’s network of warehouses, quickening a steady decline that has shrunk the stockpiles backing the world’s most liquid copper contracts by more than 40% since May.

A lot of that metal, particularly from facilities in Taiwan and South Korea, has been making its way to China, according to traders, warehouse operators and fabricators familiar with the flows. Outflows have also been seen in LME warehouses in the US as traders have taken advantage of higher Comex prices.

[Mercuria Energy Group](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/0163947D:CY), [Trafigura Group](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1188058D:SP), [Vitol Group](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1284513D:NA)and [Hartree Partners LP](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/0077151D:US) have all been withdrawing copper from LME warehouses in recent weeks, according to people familiar with the matter, who asked not to be named discussing private information.

Representatives from Mercuria, Trafigura, Vitol and Hartree declined to comment.

The rush is because smelters in China — which is at the same time the world’s top copper producer and consumer — are producing less due to tight feedstock supplies, and customers are having to rely more on imports while Shanghai Futures Exchange inventories are also dwindling.

In London, the typical red flags of a squeeze are mounting quickly. The premium on nearby contracts over three-month futures hit $153.75 a ton Thursday, surpassing the levels reached at the end of January. That price structure, known as backwardation, signals short-term tightness.

Copper futures on the LME edged up 0.1% to $14,120 a ton as of 2:02 p.m. in London.

“Even if demand is not at the moment doing amazingly, you can have a bit of a step change where the constraint on the supply is causing this draw on inventory,” [Citigroup Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/C%20US%20Equity) metals strategist Tom Mulqueen said. “There’s not a huge amount of inventory there outside of the US generally, and in that kind of environment, we’re seeing that a little bit reflected in the pricing.”

Mulqueen expects that ongoing withdrawals from LME warehouses in the coming months could help fuel a rally toward $15,000 a ton.

It will all depend largely on Trump. If he decides not to impose a levy on refined copper, the pull from the US would dissipate, making more metal available for China. If he maintains the current uncertainty, traders are predicting higher prices and a squeeze. And if he actually announces tariffs, a sharp price surge is almost certain, as traders would likely ramp up shipments to the US before the levies come in.

To be sure, copper consumers in China tend to refrain from buying when prices rise too much, and this week’s rally may already be giving some of them pause, which could limit the momentum for imports.

But the constraints on Chinese copper production could keep the market tight nonetheless. Chinese smelters reduced copper production more than expected in July, according to a [report](https://news.metal.com/newscontent/104036923-chinas-copper-cathode-production-continued-to-decline-more-than-expected-in-july-smm-analysis) from Shanghai Metals Market. They have struggled with an increase in the Antofagasta Shifts to Spot Pricing in Deal With Copper Smelterof ores and a decline in grades, or the content of metal they contain.

At the same time, they are confronting a lack of [copper](https://news.smm.cn/news/103701499) scrap, another important feedstock. Secondary copper accounts for one-quarter of China’s refined copper production.

The domestic recycling industry has been hugely disrupted by stricter SMM: Copper Scrap Market Sees Price Surge and Supply Tightness in July 2026 Amid Regulatory Challenges on invoicing in recent months. Scrap collectors, which typically run small and informal businesses, often run out of invoicing quotas and cannot complete sales.

Over the past 20 years, copper scrap has provided elasticity to restore market balance. In response to lower mine supply, or growing demand from end-users, prices usually rise to attract more recovery of copper scrap, Citi’s Mulqueen said.

“You lose some of that elasticity because of these additional headwinds,” he said.
