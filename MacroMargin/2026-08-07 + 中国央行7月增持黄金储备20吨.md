### 中文译文

## 中国央行7月增持黄金储备20吨

中国央行上月加大了对黄金储备的增持力度，连续增持期逼近两年，金价在每盎司4000美元上方获得支撑。

据[中国人民银行](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/PBCZ%20CH%20Equity)上周五公布的数据，7月份黄金持有量增加64万盎司（约20吨），为连续第21个月增持。这是自2023年10月以来最大的增持量，此前6月份储备增加了约15吨。

本月金价进一步走高。美国与伊朗可能在霍尔木兹海峡达成协议、从而缓解能源推动的通胀并降低收紧货币政策可能性的迹象为价格提供了支撑。此外，中国机构投资者也一直在[增持](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-04/chinese-dip-buying-bolsters-gold-as-prices-find-floor-at-4-000)本地黄金支持的交易所交易基金。

在央行数据公布后，现货黄金一度上涨1.8%，突破每盎司4316美元，创6月中旬以来最高价格。

据知情人士透露，近几个月来，中国央行一直在增加其在香港的黄金库存，这支持了香港打造黄金交易中心的努力。知情人士还称，最新的增持正在加速一个长期趋势，即中国人民银行一直在将部分黄金储备从伦敦迁回国内。

据生产商资助的[世界黄金协会](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-30/central-banks-bought-far-less-gold-than-thought-at-start-of-year)估计，全球央行第二季度合计购买了289吨黄金。虽然官方部门购买量较第一季度回升，但全年购买总量预计仍将下降。

## China’s Central Bank Adds 20 Tons to Gold Reserves in July

China’s central bank ramped up additions to its gold reserves last month, pushing a buying streak toward the two-year mark as bullion prices built support above $4,000 an ounce.

The holdings rose by 640,000 ounces, or about 20 tons, in July, marking the 21st consecutive month of accumulation, data from the [People’s Bank of China](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/PBCZ%20CH%20Equity) showed on Friday. That was the biggest increase in holdings since October 2023, and followed the addition of about 15 tons to the reserves in June.

Gold has pushed higher this month. Prices have been supported by signs that there may be deal over the Strait of Hormuz between the US and Iran, potentially easing energy-driven inflation and curbing chances of tighter monetary policy, In addition, institutional investors in China, have also been [adding to positions](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-04/chinese-dip-buying-bolsters-gold-as-prices-find-floor-at-4-000) in local bullion-backed exchange-traded funds.

Spot gold rose as much as 1.8% to top $4,316 an ounce following the PBOC data release, hitting the highest price since mid-June.

China’s central bank has also been building up its inventories of gold in Hong Kong over the past few months, according to people familiar with the matter, supporting the city’s push to become a bullion-trading hub. The latest additions are accelerating a longer-term trend whereby the PBOC has been moving some of its gold reserves back home from London, the people added.

Collectively, central banks around the world purchased 289 tons of gold in the second quarter, according an estimate from the producer-funded [World Gold Council](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-30/central-banks-bought-far-less-gold-than-thought-at-start-of-year). While official-sector purchases rebounded from the first quarter, overall buying is still expected to decline over the full year.
