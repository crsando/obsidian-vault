### 中文译文

## 中国1190亿美元应对投资疲软的措施姗姗来迟

### **要点**

- 据财通证券股份有限公司称，中国一项旨在为项目释放8000亿元人民币的关键融资计划尚未启动。
- 这项名为“新政策性金融工具”的举措直到最近才开始接受地方政府的项目申报，财通分析师预计资金到位还需“至少一个月左右”。
- 财通分析师表示，延迟可能会削弱该计划对经济增长的提振作用，“工具推出得越晚，今年对融资需求和实体活动的拉动作用就越小”。
据财通证券股份有限公司称，中国近半年前宣布的一项旨在支撑投资的关键融资计划尚未启动，而随着[经济增长](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-23/goldman-says-chinese-easing-hopes-revive-with-economy-growing-4)放缓，这一延迟可能削弱其影响。

该举措的正式名称为“新政策性金融工具”，根据政府计划，2026年可为项目释放8000亿元人民币（1190亿美元）。财通证券以孙彬彬为首的分析师在[报告](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/Z2pBX0tulQce7GWH2MblDg)中写道，该计划最近才开始接受地方政府的项目申报。这家总部位于杭州的券商由中国东部浙江省政府[控股](https://www.ctsec.com/company/)，预计资金到位还需“至少一个月左右”。

他们表示：“工具推出得越晚，今年对融资需求和实体活动的拉动作用就越小。”

该分析让人们难得一窥李强总理3月[宣布](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-05/china-keeps-budget-deficit-target-at-record-high-to-spur-economy)的融资机制的运作情况。尽管该计划比2025年扩大了3000亿元，但仍处于休眠状态，而2025年投资出现前所未有的萎缩，促使高层领导人[承诺](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-12-11/china-vows-flexible-rate-rrr-cuts-to-boost-economy-in-2026)遏制下滑势头。

在接下来的几个月里，尽管自4月以来投资持续深度下滑拖累了全球第二大经济体的增长，但关于资金使用的官方披露却很少。虽然2025年的额度规模在当年晚些时候才公布，但全额发放这笔资金只花了大约[一个月](http://finance.people.com.cn//n1/2025/1102/c1004-40594850.html)。

![图片](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3c681cc90b1481ba8a4af274f8121e7d/3c681cc9-0b14-81d6-9462-cf1c84521a0d-0fa80974c2ba61ef822060ce5ceed775.png)

该计划作为一种准财政杠杆，将长期资本引入由国家发展和改革委员会（NDRC，中国最高经济规划机构）筛选的项目。

在现行安排下，政策性银行发行债券或利用央行的低成本融资便利筹集资金，作为合格项目的股权资本来源。这些银行可以以仅略高于国家政府的利率借款。

由官方《人民日报》监管的《证券时报》上月报道，该融资便利[尚未被启用](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-22/china-steps-up-austerity-by-cutting-spending-most-since-october)。国家发改委[表示](https://www.ndrc.gov.cn/fzggw/wld/yxh/zyhd/202608/t20260817_1407053.html)，它于8月中旬与地方官员和三家国有政策性银行召开会议，以“加快”推广。

财通证券的分析师预计，该工具将在今年内带动约2万亿元人民币的直接投资——这一数额相当于中国2025年[GDP](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/GDPOTTL%20%20Index)的约1.4%。高盛集团（Goldman Sachs Group Inc.）在7月的一份报告中表示，它认为该计划的影响集中在2026年末和2027年初。

据财通证券称，要求只有符合条件的项目才能获得资金的规定可能会对其部署施加“更大限制”。分析师表示，部分原因是该计划拟资助的行业与去年同一计划覆盖的行业“高度”相似——去年资金流向了2300多个项目——从而缩小了可供开工项目的储备池。

他们写道，该计划的核心目的是为成熟项目补充股权资本，以清除融资障碍，使建设能够尽早启动，而不是创造新的投资需求。

财通证券表示：“如果它在短期内对稳定投资产生强劲影响，但项目储备未能以类似速度扩张，那么它也可能以牺牲未来活动为代价，将投资前置。”

他们补充说，即使来自国家新五年计划中纳入的[项目](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-26/how-china-plans-to-supercharge-its-ai-ambitions-with-six-networks-program)的需求，在2026年也将受到限制，因为这些项目需要时间才能启动。

据高盛称，启动融资计划——以及加快政府债券发行——是官员们在今年剩余时间内为支持经济增长而可能考虑的首要步骤之一。

然而，挑战不在于从国库中找到资源。相反，官员们面临的问题是把资金投向何处。

“制约因素不是财政空间，而是缺乏可立即启动的项目来部署资金，”凯投宏观中国经济主管朱利安·埃文斯-普里查德表示。

### English Original

## China’s $119 Billion Answer to Sagging Investment Is Coming Late

### **Takeaways**

- A key funding program in China to unlock 800 billion yuan for projects has yet to get off the ground, according to Caitong Securities Co.
- The initiative, known as the New Policy-Backed Financing Instruments, has only been recently opened to pitches from local administrations, with Caitong analysts expecting it will take "at least another month or so" for the money to be deployed.
- The delay could blunt the program's impact on economic growth, with Caitong's analysts saying "the later the tool is rolled out, the less it will do to boost financing demand and spur activity on the ground this year"
A key funding program announced by China nearly half a year ago to shore up investment has yet to get off the ground, according to Caitong Securities Co., a delay that could blunt its impact as [economic growth](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-23/goldman-says-chinese-easing-hopes-revive-with-economy-growing-4) grinds slower.

Officially known as the New Policy-Backed Financing Instruments, which under government plans can unlock 800 billion yuan ($119 billion) for projects in 2026, the initiative has only been recently opened to pitches from local administrations, Caitong analysts led by Sun Binbin wrote in a [note](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/Z2pBX0tulQce7GWH2MblDg). The Hangzhou-based brokerage, [controlled](https://www.ctsec.com/company/) by the provincial government of Zhejiang in eastern China, expects it will take “at least another month or so” for the money to be deployed.

“The later the tool is rolled out, the less it will do to boost financing demand and spur activity on the ground this year,” they said.

The analysis offers rare visibility into the workings of a financing mechanism [announced](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-05/china-keeps-budget-deficit-target-at-record-high-to-spur-economy) by Premier Li Qiang in March. The program has been dormant despite being expanded by 300 billion yuan from 2025, when an unprecedented contraction in investment prompted a [vow](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-12-11/china-vows-flexible-rate-rrr-cuts-to-boost-economy-in-2026) from top leaders to halt the decline.

In the months that followed, there’s been little official dislosure on the use of the funds even as a deepening slump in [investment](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CNFAYOY%20%20Index)since April weighs down growth in the world’s second-largest economy. While the size of the quota in 2025 was made public much later in the year, it took only about a [month](http://finance.people.com.cn//n1/2025/1102/c1004-40594850.html) to disburse the money in full.

The program acts as a quasi-fiscal lever by channeling long-term capital into projects screened by the National Development and Reform Commission, China’s top economic-planning agency known as NDRC.

Under the current set-up, policy banks issue bonds or draw on the central bank’s low-cost financing facility to raise money for use as a source of equity capital for qualified projects. The lenders can borrow at a rate only modestly higher than those of the national government.

The Securities Times, a newspaper supervised by the official People’s Daily, reported last month the facility was [yet to be tapped](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-22/china-steps-up-austerity-by-cutting-spending-most-since-october). The NDRC [said](https://www.ndrc.gov.cn/fzggw/wld/yxh/zyhd/202608/t20260817_1407053.html) it held a meeting in mid-August with local officials and the three state-owned policy banks to “accelerate” the rollout.

Caitong’s analysts expect the tool will drive direct investment of around 2 trillion yuan within this year — an amount equivalent to around 1.4% of China’s [GDP](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/GDPOTTL%20%20Index) in 2025. In a July report, Goldman Sachs Group Inc. said it saw the program’s “ impact concentrated in late 2026 and early 2027.”

Rules requiring that only eligible projects qualify for the funding may impose “greater constraints” on its deployment, according to Caitong. That’s partly because the sectors it’s meant to finance are “highly” similar to those covered by the same initiative last year — when the money went toward more than 2,300 projects — narrowing the pool of shovel-ready initiatives, the analysts said.

The program’s core purpose is to replenish the equity capital of mature projects to remove financing roadblocks so construction can start early, rather than to create new investment demand, they wrote.

“If it has a strong impact on stabilizing investment in the short term, it could also shift investment forward at the expense of future activity if the project pipeline is not expanded at a similar rate,” Caitong said.

Even demand from [projects](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-26/how-china-plans-to-supercharge-its-ai-ambitions-with-six-networks-program) included in the country’s new five-year plan will be limited in 2026 because it takes time to get them ready for launch, they added.

Kickstarting the financing initiative — along with faster government bond issuance — is among the first steps likely to be considered by officials in the remainder of the year to support economic growth, according to Goldman.

The challenge, however, isn’t about finding the resources in the coffers. Instead, the question for officials is where to put money to work.

“The constraint is not fiscal space but rather a lack of shovel-ready projects to deploy funds on,” said Julian Evans-Pritchard, head of China economics at Capital Economics.
