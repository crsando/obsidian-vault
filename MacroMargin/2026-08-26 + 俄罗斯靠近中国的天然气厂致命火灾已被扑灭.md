### 中文译文

## 俄罗斯靠近中国的天然气厂致命火灾已被扑灭

*摄影师：米哈伊尔·梅策尔/卫星通讯社/法新社/盖蒂图片社*

彭博新闻社
2026年8月26日下午3:38 GMT+8

俄罗斯阿穆尔天然气化工厂的一场火灾已扑灭，该火灾造成7人死亡，当时该工厂即将开始生产。

运营商在 [Telegram声明](https://t.me/AmurGHK/3993) 中表示，残留工艺气体仍在受控状态下燃烧，对火灾原因的调查已启动。火灾于周二发生，当时该设施的设备正在调试。遇难者包括中国和俄罗斯公民，另有超过36人住院。

该项目最初预计于2024年投产，但因俄罗斯入侵乌克兰后实施的制裁而面临延误。据国际文传电讯社报道，该工厂原定于本月启动聚乙烯生产。

西布尔已向中国石油化工股份有限公司（Sinopec）出售阿穆尔天然气化工综合体40%的股份，其余部分由俄罗斯最大的石化生产商西布尔控股公司控制。该综合体靠近中国边境，设计用于将饱和烃加工为基础聚合物，每年生产多达230万吨聚乙烯和40万吨聚丙烯。

据西布尔称，该工厂投产将使俄罗斯基础聚合物产能提高超过40%。

## Deadly Fire Extinguished at Russian Gas Plant Near China

*Photographer: Mikhail Metzel/Sputnik/AFP/Getty Images*

By Bloomberg News
August 26, 2026 at 3:38 PM GMT+8

A fire at Russia’s Amur gas chemical facility that killed seven people has been extinguished, shortly before the plant was due to start production.

Residual process gases are still burning in a controlled manner and an investigation into the cause of the blaze has begun, the operator said in a [Telegram statement](https://t.me/AmurGHK/3993). The fire started on Tuesday as equipment at the facility was being commissioned. Those killed included Chinese and Russian citizens, while more than 36 people were hospitalized.

The project was initially expected to come online in 2024 but has faced delays due to sanctions imposed on Russia after its invasion of Ukraine. The facility was due to start polyethylene production [this month](https://www.interfax-russia.ru/far-east/news/amurskiy-ghk-protestiroval-oborudovanie-gotov-k-zapusku), according to Interfax.

China Petroleum & Chemical Corp., or Sinopec, Sibur Sold 40% in Amur Gas Chemical Complex to Sinopec 40% of the plant. The rest is controlled by Sibur Holding PJSC, Russia’s largest petrochemical producer. The complex near the border with China is designed to process saturated hydrocarbons into base polymers, producing as much as 2.3 million tons of polyethylene and 0.4 million tons of polypropylene per year.

The start of the plant will increase Russia’s capacity for basic polymers production [by more than 40%](https://nauka.sibur.ru/news/sibur-provel-modelirovanie-zapuska-amurskogo-gkhk/), according to Sibur.
