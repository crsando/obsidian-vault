### 中文译文

## 印度部长在访日期间间接抨击中国

印度贸易部长在率领其有史以来规模最大的商业代表团访问日本时，向日本企业推介印度是一个“崇尚创业精神”的地方，并敦促日本企业前往印度。印度贸易部长敦促日本企业加倍在印度的存在。

皮尤什·戈亚尔（Piyush Goyal）还在东京告诉投资者，印度对数据和知识产权有强有力的保护——并显然将中国对待企业家的方式进行了比较，一些社交媒体用户认为这是对中国的抨击。

“你不必担心，如果你在印度赚钱，如果你是一个成功的商人，你可能有一天会消失，然后以日本大学教授的身份出现，”戈亚尔说，据[当地媒体报道](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/short-videos/no-risk-of-disappearing-here-minister-goyals-subtle-jack-ma-swipe-at-china-in-tokyo/videoshow/133503224.cms?from=mdr)。

戈亚尔没有点名任何国家或个人，尽管一些印度网友认为这些言论明显是在暗指中国科技企业家、[阿里巴巴集团控股有限公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/BABA:US)联合创始人马云；马云在2020年批评中国监管机构后曾一度从公众视野中消失。

马云后来于2023年3月在中国杭州[重新露面](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2023-03-27/jack-ma-stays-abroad-as-china-seeks-trust-of-private-sector-businesses)，罕见地出现在中国大陆。同年5月，他加入东京大学担任[客座教授](https://www.tc.u-tokyo.ac.jp/en/members/9315/)，随后高调[回归](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/newsletters/2025-09-16/jack-ma-is-back-and-making-bold-bets-at-alibaba)阿里巴巴。

印度商务部没有立即回应关于戈亚尔是否在暗指这位中国企业家的置评请求。

在周二的演讲中，戈亚尔告诉投资者，他们的知识产权和数据在印度将是安全的，并称印度“可能是一个你可以放心数据绝不会被盗的最佳目的地。”

戈亚尔正率领约200人的商业代表团对日本进行为期四天的[访问](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseDetail.aspx?PRID=2302445&reg=48&lang=1)。这些言论发表之际，印度正加大力度向全球投资者[推介其增长故事](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-26/india-takes-growth-pitch-abroad-as-foreign-money-pulls-back)并吸引新资本。财政部长尼尔玛拉·西塔拉曼也在对加拿大和美国进行为期九天的访问，重点在于加强贸易、投资和经济联系。

对印度能否维持其快速经济增长速度的质疑令投资者感到担忧，特别是考虑到对经商便利性的持续担忧。印度在本月早些时候美国银行公司对基金经理的调查中[取代](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-19/india-stocks-top-indonesia-as-asia-s-least-favored-in-bofa-poll)印尼成为亚洲最不受青睐的股市，这表明人们对这个今年全球表现最差的市场之一日益谨慎。

调查显示，32%的受访者对印度净低配，而对中国净低配的比例为18%。

历经多年边界紧张后，印度与中国的关系也在逐步改善，尽管摩擦依然存在。新德里正准备下月主办金砖国家领导人会晤，中国国家主席习近平预计将出席。

周二，中国外交部长王毅与印度国家安全顾问阿吉特·多瓦尔在北京举行了“深入”会谈，[同意](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-25/china-india-agree-to-seek-fair-settlement-of-boundary-dispute)在最新一轮高层会谈中寻求解决边界争端。

## Indian Minister Takes Indirect Swipe at China During Japan Trip

India’s trade minister pitched his country to Japanese businesses as a place where “entrepreneurship is celebrated,” as he led its largest-ever business delegation to Japan and urged Japanese companies to India’s Trade Minister Urges Japanese Firms to Double Presence their presence in India.

Piyush Goyal also told investors in Tokyo that India has robust protection for data and intellectual property — and drew an apparent comparison to the treatment of entrepreneurs in China that some social media users interpreted as a swipe against the country.

“You don’t have to worry that if you make money in India, if you are a successful businessman, you may disappear one day and land up coming out as a professor in a Japanese University,” Goyal said, according to [local media reports](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/short-videos/no-risk-of-disappearing-here-minister-goyals-subtle-jack-ma-swipe-at-china-in-tokyo/videoshow/133503224.cms?from=mdr).

Goyal didn’t name any country or individual, though some Indian users online interpreted the remarks as an apparent reference to Chinese technology entrepreneur and [Alibaba Group Holding Ltd.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/BABA:US) co-founder Jack Ma, who disappeared from public view for a spell after criticizing the country’s regulators in 2020.

Ma later [resurfaced](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2023-03-27/jack-ma-stays-abroad-as-china-seeks-trust-of-private-sector-businesses) in Hangzhou, China, in March 2023, making a rare appearance on the mainland. Later in May that year, he joined the University of Tokyo as a [visiting professor](https://www.tc.u-tokyo.ac.jp/en/members/9315/) before making a splashy [return](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/newsletters/2025-09-16/jack-ma-is-back-and-making-bold-bets-at-alibaba) to Alibaba.

India’s Commerce Ministry did not immediately respond to a request for comment on whether Goyal was referring to the Chinese entrepreneur.

In his Tuesday speech, Goyal told investors their intellectual property and data would be safe in India, calling the country “probably the best destination where you can trust your data will never be stolen.”

Goyal is on a four-day [visit](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseDetail.aspx?PRID=2302445&reg=48&lang=1) to Japan with a business delegation of around 200 people. The comments come as India steps up efforts to [pitch its growth story](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-26/india-takes-growth-pitch-abroad-as-foreign-money-pulls-back) to global investors and attract fresh capital. Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman is also on a nine-day tour of Canada and the US, focused on boosting trade, investment and economic ties.

Questions over India’s ability to sustain its rapid economic growth pace have weighed on investors, particularly given persistent concerns over ease of doing business. India [replaced](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-19/india-stocks-top-indonesia-as-asia-s-least-favored-in-bofa-poll) Indonesia as Asia’s least-preferred stock market in a survey of fund managers by Bank of America Corp. earlier this month, signaling growing caution toward a market that’s among the world’s worst performers this year.

The survey showed that 32% of the respondents were net underweight on India, compared to 18% of China.

India’s relations with China are also gradually improving after years of border tensions, though strains remain. New Delhi is preparing to host BRICS leaders next month, with Chinese President Xi Jinping expected to attend.

On Tuesday, Chinese Foreign Minister Wang Yi and Indian National Security Adviser Ajit Doval held “in-depth” talks in Beijing, [agreeing](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-25/china-india-agree-to-seek-fair-settlement-of-boundary-dispute) to pursue settlement of their border dispute in the latest round of high-level talks.
