### 中文译文

## 中国抨击印度在争议边界地区为地点命名

中国谴责印度正式宣布在争议边境地区为27个地点命名，此举使争端升级，而此时双方正寻求在多年边境紧张后稳定关系。

北京外交部周一在一份声明中表示，印度“试图取代中国长期使用的名称是非法的、无效的，不能改变藏南属于中国的事实。”

藏南是中国对印度所称“阿鲁纳恰尔邦”的称呼，该地区几十年来一直是这两个亚洲大国之间紧张关系的根源。上周五，印度为这个东北邦的山口、定居点、一个湖泊和一个战争纪念碑命名，强化其主权主张，同时对中国自己的官方地名进行反击。

“在印度测量局的阿鲁纳恰尔邦地图上正式标注它们，旨在促进公众对其准确识别和更好了解，”内政部在列有这些名称的声明中表示。

这些名称包括Dzo La、Riza La、Pukur La和Thag La山口、Sambho Sarovar湖和Sher-e-Thapa纪念碑。

周日，中国官方媒体《环球时报》援引学者的话称，此举是单方面的，并警告称“可能使妥善处理边界问题的努力复杂化。”

北京还公布了该地区地点的标准化名称。印度称这些举动“恶意”且试图推进“虚假主张”。印度外交部发言人兰德希尔·贾伊斯瓦尔在4月表示，阿鲁纳恰尔邦“将永远是印度不可分割的一部分”。

中国和印度共享一条长达3488公里的未划定边界，这条边界近年来一直是紧张关系的根源。在2020年6月导致士兵死亡的边界冲突后，这两个拥核邻国之间的关系正在逐步恢复。印度总理纳伦德拉·莫迪去年8月在天津会见了中国国家主席习近平，标志着两国关系解冻。

印度和中国官员周四还在[新德里会晤](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-07/india-seeks-china-river-data-as-tibet-mega-dam-raises-concerns)，同意保持外交和军事沟通，旨在维护两国边境沿线的和平与稳定。

这一命名争端出现之际，印度内容创作者发布的声称中国在阿鲁纳恰尔邦活动增加的视频已在南亚国家的社交媒体上疯传。彭博新闻社无法独立核实视频中的说法，尽管如此，这些视频在本届议会会议期间加剧了反对党对莫迪政府边境安全问题的审视。

### English Original

## China Slams India for Naming Sites in Disputed Border Region

China condemned India for formally announcing names for 27 sites in a disputed border region, escalating a spat just as the two sides seek to stabilize ties after years of border tensions.

India “attempting to replace the names long used by China is illegal and invalid, and cannot change the fact that Zangnan belongs to China,” the Foreign Ministry in Beijing said in a statement on Monday.

Zangnan is China’s name for what India calls Arunachal Pradesh, and the region has been the source of tensions between the two Asian superpowers for decades. On Friday, India assigned names to passes, settlements, a lake and a war memorial in the northeastern state, reinforcing its sovereignty claim while pushing back against China’s own official place names.

“Identifying them formally on the Survey of India map of Arunachal Pradesh is aimed at facilitating their accurate recognition and better awareness among the public at large,” the Ministry of Home Affairs [said](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2296151&amp%3Breg=48&amp%3Blang=2) in the statement listing the names.

The names include the Dzo La, Riza La, Pukur La and Thag La mountain passes, Sambho Sarovar lake and the Sher-e-Thapa Memorial.

On Sunday, China’s state-run Global Times [quoted scholars](https://www.globaltimes.cn/page/202608/1367846.shtml) who called the move unilateral and warned it “could complicate efforts to properly handle border issues.”

Beijing has also released standardized names for sites in the region. India has called those moves “mischievous” and an attempt to advance “false claims.” Arunachal Pradesh “will always remain an integral and inalienable part of India,” Randhir Jaiswal, spokesperson for India’s External Affairs Ministry, said in April.

China and India share a 3,488-kilometer-long unmarked border that has been the source of tensions in recent years. Ties between the nuclear-armed neighbors are gradually recovering after the June 2020 border clash that killed soldiers. Prime Minister Narendra Modi met Chinese President Xi Jinping in Tianjin last August, signaling a thaw in ties.

Indian and Chinese officials also [met in New Delhi](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-07/india-seeks-china-river-data-as-tibet-mega-dam-raises-concerns) on Thursday and agreed to maintain diplomatic and military communication aimed at preserving peace and stability along their frontier.

The naming spat comes as videos by Indian content creators alleging increased Chinese activity in Arunachal Pradesh have gone viral on social media in the South Asian nation. Bloomberg News could not independently verify the claims in the videos, which have nevertheless added to opposition scrutiny of Modi’s government over border security during the current session of parliament.
