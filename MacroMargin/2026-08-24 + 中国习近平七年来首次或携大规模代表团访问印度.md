## 中国习近平七年来首次或携大规模代表团访问印度

![Chinese President Xi and India Prime Minister Modi meet in Kazan](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3c681cc90b1481bdb8fbc3f79c861e87/3c681cc9-0b14-8169-921e-e125b11e4060-01193b5f8c22306b065685ff3ba679e6.jpg)

2024年10月23日，中国国家主席习近平与印度总理纳伦德拉·莫迪在俄罗斯喀山金砖国家峰会期间会面。中国日报 via 路透社 [购买许可版权，打开新标签页](https://www.reutersconnect.com/item/chinese-president-xi-and-india-prime-minister-modi-meet-in-kazan/dGFnOnJldXRlcnMuY29tLDIwMjQ6bmV3c21sX1JDMkNRQUFIN0lVQQ%3D%3D/?utm_medium=rcom-article-media&utm_campaign=rcom-rcp-lead)

- 摘要
- 消息人士称，习近平可能出席德里金砖峰会
- 习近平七年来首次访问印度，标志着关系解冻
- 中国计划派400人代表团参加金砖峰会
- 习近平访问或考验脆弱的中印和解进程
新德里，8月24日（路透社） - 三位消息人士称，中国国家主席习近平很可能携大规模代表团出席下月在新德里举行的金砖国家峰会，这将是其七年来首次访印，也再次表明这两个对手之间的关系正在解冻。

印度将主办9月12日至13日的峰会，届时习近平的出席更受关注的不是金砖议程，而是这所传递出的北京和新德里在2020年致命边境冲突后努力稳定双边关系的信号。

“中国官员正忙于敲定酒店和安保方案，”一位直接知情的印度消息人士对路透社表示。

去年，印度[总理纳伦德拉·莫迪](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/powerful-optics-chinas-xi-welcome-putin-modi-grand-show-solidarity-2025-08-26/)七年来首次访问中国，但两国关系仅逐步改善。最近，一场新的边界争端引发双方激烈外交交锋。

另两位印度消息人士称，习近平可能会率领约400名官员的代表团，而2019年上一次访问时随行官员不足200人。

消息人士补充说，两国官员预计将利用此次峰会探讨如何扩大交往，同时处理边境和地区安全问题上长期存在的分歧。

消息人士称，习近平的最终行程安排以及任何潜在的中印双边会晤议程尚未敲定。

中国外交部表示：“中方高度重视并积极参与金砖合作，支持现任主席国印度成功举办今年金砖国家领导人会晤。”

该部称：“关于习近平的访问，我们目前没有可提供的信息。”习近平缺席了2025年在巴西举行的上一次金砖峰会。

印度外交部不予置评。

### 中印边界问题

世界上人口最多的两个国家都是核大国，在喜马拉雅山脉共享一条长约3,800公里（2,400英里）、很大程度上未划定且有争议的边界。

边境地区数十年的相对和平被2020年的冲突打破，当时有20名印度士兵和4名中国士兵丧生。这一事件导致了长达四年的军事对峙，直到2024年10月达成脱离接触协议。

但在8月12日，印度表示[边境和平对两国关系至关重要](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/india-says-border-peace-critical-ties-with-china-amid-reports-military-tensions-2026-08-12/)，此前有报道称印度和中国军队的巡逻队于7月底在一条偏远的边境地区面对面相遇。

印度国家安全顾问阿吉特·多瓦尔周一启程访问北京，与中国外交部长王毅就边界问题举行会谈。

尽管印度和中国仍是竞争对手，但美国总统唐纳德·特朗普的贸易政策创造了一个契合点，使习近平的出席成为一种信号，表明双方都不希望外部压力破坏金砖国家合作。

NEW DELHI, Aug 24 (Reuters) - Chinese President Xi Jinping is likely to attend next month's BRICS summit in New Delhi with a ‌large delegation, three sources said, in what would be his first visit in seven years and a fresh sign of a thaw between the rivals.

India is hosting the September 12 to 13 summit, where Xi's presence would be watched less for the BRICS agenda than for what it signals about Beijing and New Delhi's efforts to stabilise relations after deadly border clashes in 2020.

"Chinese officials are busy finalising hotels and security protocols," one of the ⁠Indian sources with direct knowledge told Reuters.

Last year, Indian [Prime Minister Narendra Modi](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/powerful-optics-chinas-xi-welcome-putin-modi-grand-show-solidarity-2025-08-26/) visited China for the first time in seven years, but the relationship has only improved gradually. More recently, a fresh border-related dispute triggered sharp diplomatic exchanges between the two sides.

Two other Indian sources said Xi was likely to be accompanied by a delegation of about 400 officials, compared with fewer than 200 officials the last time in 2019.

Officials from both countries are expected to use the summit to explore ways to expand engagement while managing long-standing differences over the border and regional security issues, the sources added.

Xi's final travel plans and agenda for any potential bilateral between India and China are yet to be finalised, 
the sources said.

The Chinese foreign ‌ministry ⁠said, "China attaches great importance to and actively participates in BRICS cooperation, and supports India, the current chair, in successfully hosting this year's BRICS summit."

"We currently have no information available to provide" about Xi's visit, it said. Xi missed the last BRICS summit in 2025 in Brazil.

India's foreign ministry did not comment.

### INDIA-CHINA BORDER ISSUE

The world's two most populous nations, both nuclear powers, share a roughly 3,800-km (2,400-mile), largely ⁠undemarcated and disputed border in the Himalayas.

Decades of relative peace along the border were shattered by 2020 clashes where 20 Indian and four Chinese soldiers were killed. The incident led to a four-year military standoff before reaching a disengagement pact in October 2024.

But on August ⁠12, India said [peace along the border was critical](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/india-says-border-peace-critical-ties-with-china-amid-reports-military-tensions-2026-08-12/) to ties after reports that Indian and Chinese army patrols came face-to-face in late July near a remote border.

India's National Security Advisor Ajit Doval on Monday started his visit to Beijing to ⁠meet Chinese Foreign Minister Wang Yi to hold talks on the boundary issue.

While India and China remain competitors, U.S. President Donald Trump's trade policies have created one area of convergence, making Xi's attendance a signal that neither side wants external pressure to undermine BRICS.
