### 中文译文

## 伊朗网络组织的招募行动引发黑客攻击担忧

### **要点**

- 受制裁的伊朗网络安全培训公司拉文学院（Ravin Academy）宣布了其有史以来规模最大的招生班次，招募600至1200名伊朗人参加一项为期一年的免费网络安全奖学金项目。
- 美国于2022年对拉文学院实施制裁，但自2020年学院成立以来，其业务规模不断扩大，培训学生网络安全和黑客技术，并为伊朗情报部招募学员。
- 据TRM Labs称，该学院的学员可能成为伊朗网络战力量的一部分。该学院的培训项目借鉴了美国网络安全公司和国际认证机构的专有材料。
一家受制裁的伊朗网络安全培训公司宣布了其有史以来规模最大的招生班次，加剧了对该国持续黑客攻击美国的担忧。

该组织——拉文学院——表示，它正在招募600至1200名年龄在17至30岁之间的伊朗人，参加一项为期一年的免费网络安全奖学金项目。区块链和人工智能调查公司 [TRM Labs](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1694489D:US) [周五表示](https://trmlink.info/3SBZ5Ky)，学生们可以参加技术竞赛，分析真实或模拟的工业基础设施，并制定可能的网络攻击场景。该学院早先的项目曾宣传就业安排，让学员能够履行伊朗兵役要求。

美国于[2022年](https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/jy1048)对拉文学院实施制裁，但自该学院2020年成立以来，其业务规模不断扩大。据[美国财政部](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/3061059Z:US)[称](https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/jy1048)，该学院培训学生的网络安全和黑客技术，并招募其学员进入伊朗情报部。

[普华永道国际有限公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1360069D:LN) [此前认定](https://www.pwc.com/gx/en/issues/cybersecurity/cyber-threat-intelligence/muddy-advanced-persistent-teacher.html)，涉嫌的伊朗黑客“极有可能”熟悉拉文学院的培训材料，并且有前学员参与了国家支持的攻击组织。据TRM Labs称，该学院的学员可能成为伊朗网络战力量的一部分。

Ravin Academy 未立即回应置评请求。

7月30日，联邦调查局（FBI）警告称，黑客针对至少七个美国州的水务和废水处理公司。该局在一份声明中表示，这些入侵在某些情况下破坏了水处理操作，包括导致水压下降和洪水。美国官员没有公开指责伊朗发动袭击，但网络安全专家[表示](https://www.tenable.com/blog/coordinated-cyberattack-on-minnesota-water-utilities-what-you-need-to-know)，与伊朗有关的黑客（包括一个名为 CyberAv3ngers 的组织）可能是这些入侵的幕后黑手。

没有迹象表明 Ravin Academy 与这些事件中的任何一起有关。

"这是一个为了威慑、减缓其进展并理想情况下阻止其存在而受到制裁的组织，"TRM Labs 的国家威胁情报主管 Will Doran 表示。"但我们发现，这是一个适应性极强的项目，它找到了继续存在甚至蓬勃发展的方法。"

分析师表示，培训项目的前五个月借鉴了美国网络安全公司和国际认证机构的专有材料，包括 Linux Professional Institute、[Microsoft](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/MSFT%20US%20Equity) [Corp](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/MSFT%20US%20Equity). 和 [SANS Institute](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1752314D:US)。研究人员无法确切查明 Ravin 是如何获得这些私有材料的。

在线材料推广的课程大纲包含了从这些组织盗用的材料，并将这些课程宣传为昂贵的英语课程的替代品。Ravin Academy 表示，这些课程通过不具名的伊朗企业的赞助免费提供。

该学院公开与 Positive Technologies 合作，后者是俄罗斯最大的网络安全公司之一，自 2021 年以来因涉嫌与俄罗斯情报机构有联系而受到美国制裁。

## Iran Cyber Outfit’s Recruiting Drive Sparks Hacking Concerns

### **Takeaways**

- A sanctioned Iranian cybersecurity training firm, Ravin Academy, has announced its largest-ever recruitment class, recruiting between 600 and 1200 Iranians for a free, yearlong cybersecurity scholarship.
- The US placed sanctions on Ravin Academy in 2022, but its operations have grown since the academy was founded in 2020, training students in cybersecurity and hacking, and recruiting its trainees for Iran’s Ministry of Intelligence.
- Participants in the academy may become part of Iran’s cyberwarfare efforts, according to TRM Labs, with the academy's training program drawing on proprietary material from US cybersecurity firms and international certification bodies.
A sanctioned Iranian cybersecurity training firm has announced its largest-ever recruitment class, deepening concerns about hacks from the country continuing to pummel the US.

The organization, Ravin Academy, said that it’s recruiting between 600 and 1200 Iranians ages 17 to 30 for a free, yearlong cybersecurity scholarship, [TRM Labs](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1694489D:US), a blockchain and artificial intelligence investigation company, [said Friday](https://trmlink.info/3SBZ5Ky). Students can participate in technological competitions to analyze real or simulated industrial infrastructure and produce possible cyberattack scenarios. Earlier programs offered through the academy advertised job placements that could allow trainees to fulfill Iranian military service requirements.

The US placed sanctions on Ravin Academy in [2022](https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/jy1048), but its operations have grown since the academy was founded in 2020. The academy trains students in cybersecurity and hacking, and recruits its trainees for Iran’s Ministry of Intelligence, [according to](https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/jy1048) the [US Treasury](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/3061059Z:US).

[PricewaterhouseCoopers International Ltd](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1360069D:LN). [had previously determined](https://www.pwc.com/gx/en/issues/cybersecurity/cyber-threat-intelligence/muddy-advanced-persistent-teacher.html) it’s “highly likely” that alleged Iranian hackers are familiar with training materials from Ravin Academy and that former students participate in state-sponsored attack groups. Participants in the academy may become part of Iran’s cyberwarfare efforts, according to TRM Labs.

Ravin Academy didn’t immediately respond to a request for comment.

On July 30, the FBI warned that hackers had targeted water and wastewater companies in at least seven US states. The breaches had in in some cases degraded water operations, including by causing a loss of pressure and flooding, the bureau said in a statement. American officials didn’t publicly blame Iran for the attacks, but cybersecurity experts have [said](https://www.tenable.com/blog/coordinated-cyberattack-on-minnesota-water-utilities-what-you-need-to-know) Iranian-affiliated hackers, including a group known as CyberAv3ngers, were likely behind the intrusions.

There’s no indication that Ravin Academy was tied to any of those incidents.

“This is an organization that was sanctioned in order to deter and slow its progress and ideally stop it from existing,” said Will Doran, the head of nation state threat intelligence at TRM Labs. “But what we found is, it’s an extremely adaptable program that has found a way to continue to not only exist, but thrive.”

The first five months of the training program draw on proprietary material from US cybersecurity firms and international certification bodies, including the Linux Professional Institute, [Microsoft](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/MSFT%20US%20Equity) [Corp](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/MSFT%20US%20Equity). and the [SANS Institute](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1752314D:US), analysts said. Researchers were unable to ascertain exactly how the private materials were obtained by Ravin.

Online materials promote syllabi that includes material lifted from those organizations, marketing the courses as substitutes for expensive English-language courses. Ravin Academy says the courses are offered for free through sponsorships from unnamed Iranian corporations.

The academy has publicly worked with Positive Technologies, one of Russia’s largest cybersecurity companies, which has been under US sanctions since 2021 over its alleged links to Russian intelligence services.
