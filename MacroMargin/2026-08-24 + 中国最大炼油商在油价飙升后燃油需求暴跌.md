## 中国最大炼油商在油价飙升后燃油需求暴跌

上海的一家中石化加油站。*摄影师：Qilai Shen/彭博*

彭博新闻2026年8月23日下午4:03 GMT+8*更新于2026年8月24日上午9:11 GMT+8*

### **要点** 来自彭博AI

- 中国最大的炼油商中石化表示，由于消费者不愿支付更高价格并转向电动汽车，上半年公路燃料需求暴跌。
- 中石化的财报显示，汽油消费量下降7.9%，柴油下降12%，公司预计下半年将维持目前的炼油产量水平。
- 该公司截至6月的六个月初步净利润为266亿元人民币，周一其港股股价一度上涨1.5%。
中国最大的炼油商表示，由于消费者不愿支付更高价格并转向电动汽车，上半年公路燃料需求暴跌。

[中石化](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/386%20HK%20Equity)的财报显示，汽油消费量下降7.9%，柴油下降12%。化工产品也受到打击，乙烯及其等价物的消费量较2025年同期下降9.9%。天然气和航空燃料消费量有所增长，但增幅甚微。

中石化在声明中表示：“受高油价对需求的抑制作用以及新能源加速替代的影响，国内成品油消费量同比下降8.6%。”

花旗集团（Citigroup Inc.）包括 Oscar Yee 在内的分析师在一份研究报告中表示，总体炼油产量下降5.6%至1.13亿吨，公司预计下半年将维持该水平，这表明需求回升的希望渺茫。

对该公司来说并非全是坏消息，因为油价上涨提升了公司井口生产的收入，也增加了储罐中石油的价值。截至6月份的六个月，初步[净利润](https://static.sse.com.cn/disclosure/listedinfo/announcement/c/new/2026-08-24/600028_20260824_59U2.pdf)为266亿元人民币（合40亿美元）。该公司正式名称为中国石油化工股份有限公司（China Petroleum & Chemical Corp.），在[去年上半年](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-08-21/sinopec-reports-slump-in-first-half-profit-on-lower-oil-prices)盈利238亿元人民币。

周一，中石化在香港的股价一度上涨1.5%。

1月至6月，布伦特原油均价约为每桶87美元，而2025年同期约为每桶71美元。4月底，油价触及超过126美元的四年高点，当时美国和以色列对伊朗的军事行动已开始两个月。如今，这场冲突已进入第六个月，却几乎没有结束的迹象，油价仍波动不定。

花旗的 Yee 表示，尽管如此，由于有可能获得廉价的俄罗斯原油以及中东供应溢价大幅降低，公司下半年的利润率可能会有所改善。分析师还称赞中石化将化工产品出口提高70%以获得更高海外价格的能力，不过他们警告称，这对其他亚洲炼油商来说可能是个坏消息。

中石化表示，计划下半年资本支出为829亿元至999亿元人民币。目标是生产1.418亿桶原油和7463亿立方英尺天然气。

## China’s Top Refiner Saw Fuel Demand Plunge After Oil Spike

A Sinopec gas station in Shanghai.*Photographer: Qilai Shen/Bloomberg*

By Bloomberg NewsAugust 23, 2026 at 4:03 PM GMT+8*Updated on August 24, 2026 at 9:11 AM GMT+8*

### **Takeaways ** by Bloomberg AI

- China's biggest oil refiner, Sinopec, said road fuel demand plummeted in the first half as consumers shied away from higher prices and shifted to electric vehicles.
- Sinopec's earnings report showed that gasoline consumption fell by 7.9% and diesel by 12%, and the company expects to maintain its current refining output level in the second half.
- The company's preliminary net income was 26.6 billion yuan for the six months through June, and its shares rose as much as 1.5% in Hong Kong on Monday.
China’s biggest oil refiner said road fuel demand plummeted in the first half as consumers shied away from higher prices and shifted to electric vehicles.

[Sinopec](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/386%20HK%20Equity)’s earnings report showed that gasoline consumption fell by 7.9% and diesel by 12%. Chemical products also took a hit, with consumption of ethylene and its equivalents down 9.9% from the same period in 2025. Natural gas and jet fuel consumption grew, but only barely.

“Due to the dampening effect of high oil prices on demand and accelerated substitution by new energy, domestic refined oil products consumption declined by 8.6% year on year,” Sinopec said in the statement.

Overall refining output dropped 5.6% to 113 million tons, and the company expects to maintain that level in the second half, indicating only muted hopes for a pick up in demand, Citigroup Inc. analysts including Oscar Yee said in a research note.

It wasn’t all bad news for the company, as higher oil prices boosted revenue for the company’s well-head production, and also increased the value of its oil stored in tanks. Preliminary [net income](https://static.sse.com.cn/disclosure/listedinfo/announcement/c/new/2026-08-24/600028_20260824_59U2.pdf) was 26.6 billion yuan ($4 billion) for the six months through June. The company, officially known as China Petroleum & Chemical Corp., earned 23.8 billion yuan in the [first half of last year](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-08-21/sinopec-reports-slump-in-first-half-profit-on-lower-oil-prices).

Sinopec’s shares rose as much as 1.5% in Hong Kong on Monday.

Brent crude averaged about $87 a barrel from January through June, compared with around $71 in the same period in 2025. Prices touched a four-year high above $126 in late April, two months after the US and Israel began their military campaign against Iran. Prices remain volatile as the conflict, now in its sixth month, shows little sign of ending.

Still, the company could see improved margins in the second-half of the year thanks to potential access to cheap Russian crude and sharply lower premiums for Middle East supply, Citigroup’s Yee said. The analysts also praised Sinopec’s ability to boost chemical exports by 70% to access higher overseas prices, although they cautioned that may be bad news for other Asian refiners.

Sinopec said it plans capital spending of 82.9 billion yuan to 99.9 billion yuan in the second half. It’s targeting production of 141.8 million barrels of crude oil and 746.3 billion cubic feet of natural gas.
