## 中国与印尼计划在台湾以东水域举行联合演习

中国海军将与印度尼西亚在台湾以东举行联合演习，这是北京方面在这片日益紧张的水域最新一次宣示主权。

中国国防部在周二的一份[声明](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MzA4MTkxNTQ3Mw%3D%3D&amp%3Bmid=2650092216&amp%3Bidx=1&amp%3Bsn=6aff1bbf3db01e60fb7c4cd407387680&amp%3Bchksm=865ef5220522718dd85dd3624a04ffec8e8fdf8330b38cd220120d1dee31f5f8dc694c34033f&amp%3Bmpshare=1&amp%3Bscene=1&amp%3Bsrcid=0811JGI8xyl776ySoxcTGyAt&amp%3Bsharer_shareinfo=ad3d169413be5459245812e703b2b198&amp%3Bsharer_shareinfo_first=ad3d169413be5459245812e703b2b198&amp%3Bexportkey=n_ChQIAhIQQ1GdKlWx%2FrdHFjE70c%2FYeRKcAgIE97dBBAEAAAAAALVGKRb%2FrM0AAAAOpnltbLcz9gKNyK89dVj0KdDtNnB8IF%2BxleqV8B%2FrILBPhlD3unkb36h0MwGHKMH4bV%2BHUCf4CYDXYQkrNVg0AyUXNOaGlUjiWV3CxIVr29aV7nWL7G%2FS%2FruyvzESWv%2BmNEh5UN5Csk2q4c7Ue7i5XflMZw6FfFEaRGAvZs8bSaTFEsxB18Xl4finY%2FqlOhTzAvpj3bP2Cryvn1c99ZL0wc%2B8JgyJeJpT1vv5RRwjrBHV2HDelLauPdXcWUvqEa1qeK0GfIG57VZOOlcHi6GIjgJS7gLsJ1u%2BwHB1%2F1IYsQiqYMlm02f%2FoAHJUYmpYsVJ1QXX8rYY55pJOduFYbEhZKZNKEht&amp%3Bacctmode=1&amp%3Bpass_ticket=c0AA7nQkKdYNxYsnEjxwj%2By5wgSvZPaJr6ZB71fyOJ93qV2QvhROKMSp9gdX4YpY&amp%3Bwx_header=0#rd)中宣布，054A型护卫舰“红河”号将于8月中旬与印度尼西亚海军的一艘护卫舰进行航行演习，但未说明开始和结束日期、坐标或航线。印度尼西亚国防部未立即回应置评请求。

中方声明补充说，此次演习将聚焦海上通信和补给，旨在促进联合作战能力并维护地区稳定。

两国海军此前曾进行过联合演习，但这是中国首次公开宣布在台湾以东水域举行双方部队的训练。这也标志着北京首次在这些水域与外国合作。

此举可能加剧紧张局势。为回应东京和马尼拉宣布将举行会谈以解决该地区海上争端的[公告](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-28/takaichi-to-meet-philippines-marcos-to-deepen-security-ties)，北京已加强在该地区的存在。相关讨论涉及台湾以东水域，台湾是北京声称拥有主权的自治民主实体。

此后，中国派遣了海警以及多个部委的政府船只，以宣示对这些水域的声索。北京甚至首次[盘问](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-11/china-presses-taiwan-by-querying-foreign-ships-for-first-time)外国商船，明显意在展示其主权。

北京的最新行动是在多年来中国在台湾周边活动加剧的基础上进行的。自2022年时任美国众议院议长南希·佩洛西访问台北以来，中国举行了环绕该岛的大规模军事演习，并将军舰和战机穿越台湾海峡中线的部署常态化。

台湾已演练过突破封锁，包括今年计划进行的为运载石油和天然气的船只护航演习，官员们警告称，封锁台湾周围水域将扼杀整个地区的能源供应。

红河舰于2022年入列东部战区。054A型是中国海军数量最多的护卫舰。

## China, Indonesia Plan Joint Exercise in Waters East of Taiwan

China’s navy will conduct a joint drill with Indonesia to the east of Taiwan, the latest assertion of sovereignty by Beijing in the waters that have become increasingly fraught with tensions.

Type 054A frigate Honghe will hold a navigation exercise with a frigate from Indonesia’s navy in mid-August, China’s Defense Ministry announced in a [statement](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MzA4MTkxNTQ3Mw%3D%3D&amp%3Bmid=2650092216&amp%3Bidx=1&amp%3Bsn=6aff1bbf3db01e60fb7c4cd407387680&amp%3Bchksm=865ef5220522718dd85dd3624a04ffec8e8fdf8330b38cd220120d1dee31f5f8dc694c34033f&amp%3Bmpshare=1&amp%3Bscene=1&amp%3Bsrcid=0811JGI8xyl776ySoxcTGyAt&amp%3Bsharer_shareinfo=ad3d169413be5459245812e703b2b198&amp%3Bsharer_shareinfo_first=ad3d169413be5459245812e703b2b198&amp%3Bexportkey=n_ChQIAhIQQ1GdKlWx%2FrdHFjE70c%2FYeRKcAgIE97dBBAEAAAAAALVGKRb%2FrM0AAAAOpnltbLcz9gKNyK89dVj0KdDtNnB8IF%2BxleqV8B%2FrILBPhlD3unkb36h0MwGHKMH4bV%2BHUCf4CYDXYQkrNVg0AyUXNOaGlUjiWV3CxIVr29aV7nWL7G%2FS%2FruyvzESWv%2BmNEh5UN5Csk2q4c7Ue7i5XflMZw6FfFEaRGAvZs8bSaTFEsxB18Xl4finY%2FqlOhTzAvpj3bP2Cryvn1c99ZL0wc%2B8JgyJeJpT1vv5RRwjrBHV2HDelLauPdXcWUvqEa1qeK0GfIG57VZOOlcHi6GIjgJS7gLsJ1u%2BwHB1%2F1IYsQiqYMlm02f%2FoAHJUYmpYsVJ1QXX8rYY55pJOduFYbEhZKZNKEht&amp%3Bacctmode=1&amp%3Bpass_ticket=c0AA7nQkKdYNxYsnEjxwj%2By5wgSvZPaJr6ZB71fyOJ93qV2QvhROKMSp9gdX4YpY&amp%3Bwx_header=0#rd) Tuesday without specifying the start and end dates, the coordinates or the route. Indonesia’s Defense Ministry didn’t immediately respond to a request for comment.

The drill will focus on communication and replenishment at sea, aiming to promote joint operational capability and safeguard regional stability, the Chinese statement added.

The two navies have exercised together previously but this is the first time that China publicly announced a training for both forces in waters east of Taiwan. It also marks the first time that Beijing is working with a foreign country in those waters.

The move could heighten tensions. Beijing has stepped up its presence in the area in response to an [announcement](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-28/takaichi-to-meet-philippines-marcos-to-deepen-security-ties) by Tokyo and Manila that they would hold talks to resolve their maritime disputes in that region. The discussions concern waters east of Taiwan, the self-ruled democracy that Beijing claims as its territory.

Since then, China has dispatched its coast guard as well as a number of government vessels from various ministries to press its claims in the waters. Beijing even [questioned](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-11/china-presses-taiwan-by-querying-foreign-ships-for-first-time) foreign commercial boats for the first time in an apparent effort to demonstrate its sovereignty.

Beijing’s latest operation builds on years of intensifying Chinese activity around Taiwan. Since then-US House Speaker Nancy Pelosi visited Taipei in 2022, China has staged large-scale military drills encircling the island and normalized warship and warplane deployments across the Taiwan Strait’s median line.

Taiwan has practiced breaking up blockades, including planned exercises this year to escort ships carrying oil and natural gas, and officials have warned that sealing off the waters around the island would choke energy supplies for the entire region.

The Honghe entered service in 2022 with the Eastern Theater Command. The Type-054A is the most numerous frigate of the Chinese navy.
