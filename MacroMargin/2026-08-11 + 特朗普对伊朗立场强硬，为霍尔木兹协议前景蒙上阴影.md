## 特朗普对伊朗立场强硬，为霍尔木兹协议前景蒙上阴影

巴基斯坦国防部长表示，美国和伊朗在霍尔木兹海峡问题上“接近达成某种安排”，尽管双方在长期陷入僵局的谈判中似乎都强化了各自的立场。

“事情正朝着有利于和平的方向发展，”赫瓦贾·阿西夫周二在伊斯兰堡对记者说，但没有透露任何突破的细节。巴基斯坦内政部长穆赫辛·纳克维不久将抵达德黑兰，与伊朗内政部长举行会谈，伊朗半官方的法尔斯通讯社报道称“巴基斯坦部长将在数小时内抵达伊朗进行会谈：法尔斯通讯社”。

伊朗与阿曼之间关于重新开放霍尔木兹海峡以供部分海上航运的谈判已进入高级阶段，卡塔尔方面表示。半岛电视台早些时候援引卡塔尔外交部发言人的话报道了这一消息。

阿西夫发表上述言论后，油价回吐涨幅，这是有关这条关键水道谈判状况的一系列相互矛盾声明中的最新一项。巴基斯坦与卡塔尔一直在冲突期间进行调解。

这些乐观评估出炉之前，美国总统唐纳德·特朗普周一强化了立场，称德黑兰应为与伊斯兰共和国有关的袭击以及国内抗议中丧生的人支付赔偿。他的言论是对伊朗周末提出的一系列要求的回应，其中包括要求美国赔偿其违反双方6月达成的现已失效的临时和平协议的行为。

“调解人正试图让事情重回正轨，但说起来容易做起来难，”国际危机组织项目副主任阿里·瓦埃兹表示。

伊朗几乎肯定会拒绝特朗普的最新要求，美国领导人表示这些要求将被“坚定地纳入未来所有谈判”。这种反复似乎降低了交战双方能够就这条水道达成任何立即协议的可能性，在2月28日冲突开始之前，全球约五分之一的石油和液化天然气经由该水道运输。

作为紧张局势持续的一个迹象，美国军队周二早些时候向一艘试图突破美国对伊朗港口封锁的巴拿马旗船只开火，《华尔街日报》援引一位未具名的美国官员的话报道。此前，Vanguard Tech表示，这艘集装箱船被导弹击中，失去指挥。

英国海军表示，周二收到一份关于阿曼湾一艘集装箱船与军事力量发生事件的报告，但没有提供更多细节。英国海军还获悉，也门红海沿岸附近发生一起事件，一艘货船报告被不明射弹击中，造成人员伤亡。

伦敦午后交易中，布伦特原油小幅走低，略高于每桶87美元。

欧洲柴油期货飙升超过10%，原因是利比亚扎维耶炼油厂（遭无人机摧毁燃料储罐(1)）和俄罗斯的炼油厂遭到[攻击](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-10/russia-says-13-killed-in-ukraine-attack-on-tatarstan-energy-hub)，进一步收紧了这种被视为全球经济主力燃料的市场。沙特阿拉伯能源部称，周日早些时候，沙特扑灭了吉赞炼油厂的一场火灾，数小时后也门胡塞武装声称[袭击](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-09/saudi-says-fire-at-aramco-refinery-in-jazan-was-extinguished-msl61cku)了该设施。

阿联酋总统高级外交顾问安瓦尔·加尔加什（Anwar Gargash）在[X平台上的帖子](https://x.com/AnwarGargash/status/2087068621902430597)中写道：“该地区不能无限期地处于不战不和的状态。其稳定及其人民的未来需要清晰的愿景和方向。”

特朗普对Axios表示：“我们正在对伊朗低调处理。”他周末称，他准备让对伊朗的经济压力累积，而不是发动新的军事打击以迫使霍尔木兹海峡重新开放。伊朗伊斯兰共和国的经济在战争中受到重创，大部分工业产能被摧毁，原油出口因美国封锁而[严重受限](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-10/iran-s-oil-terminals-look-idle-as-us-keeps-up-naval-blockade)。央行数据显示，最近同比通胀率达到77%。

与此同时，伊朗货币较战前水平下跌超过10%，加剧了伊朗人的经济压力。里亚尔贬值引发了全国范围的暴力抗议，抗议在今年早些时候达到顶峰，随后当局的镇压导致数千人死亡。目前没有反政府示威活动恢复的迹象。

德黑兰要求霍尔木兹海峡全面重开的条件包括：美国解除对伊朗港口的海上封锁、释放被冻结资产以及赔偿战争损失。它还呼吁永久停止对其在黎巴嫩、伊拉克、也门和加沙所支持的武装组织的攻击。此外，关于周一伊朗，伊朗应对这些国家冲突造成的损害负责。

霍尔木兹海峡的实际关闭正在对全球经济和贸易造成损害。在中东至中国的基准航线上，租用一艘超级油轮运油的成本[逼近](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-10/sinokor-deal-sends-mideast-oil-tanker-benchmark-rate-surging)每天50万美元，是战前成本的两倍多。

## Trump Hardens Stance on Iran, Clouding Hopes for Hormuz Deal

Pakistan’s defense minister said the US and Iran are “close to some sort of arrangement” over the Strait of Hormuz, even after both sides appeared to harden their positions in the long-deadlocked negotiation.

“Things are shaping up in favor of peace,” Khawaja Asif told reporters in Islamabad on Tuesday, without giving details of any breakthrough. Pakistan’s Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi will shortly arrive in Tehran to hold talks with his Iranian counterpart, the Islamic Republic’s semi-official Fars agency Pakistan Minister Due in Iran Within Hours for Talks: Fars.

Talks between Iran and Oman about reopening Hormuz to some maritime shipping have reached Qatar Says Oman-Iran Talks Reached Advanced Stage: Al Jazeera, Al Jazeera reported earlier, citing a Qatari Foreign Ministry spokesperson.

Oil prices erased gains following Asif’s comments, the latest in a long run of conflicting statements about the status of talks over the critical waterway. Pakistan has been mediating during the conflict, alongside Qatar.

The upbeat assessments came after US President Donald Trump toughened his stance on Monday, saying Tehran should pay reparations for those killed in attacks linked to the Islamic Republic as well as in domestic protests. His comments came in response to a series of demands laid out by Iran over the weekend, including compensation for US violations of a now-lapsed interim peace deal agreed by the sides in June.

“The mediators are trying to put things on the right track again, but it’s easier said than done,” said Ali Vaez, a deputy program director at the International Crisis Group.

Iran will almost certainly reject Trump’s latest demands, which the US leader said will be included “firmly into any, and all, future negotiations.” The back and forth appeared to reduce the chance that the warring parties will be able to agree to any immediate pact on the waterway, where around a fifth of the world’s oil and liquefied natural gas flowed before the conflict began on Feb. 28.

In a sign of ongoing tensions, US forces fired on a Panama-flagged ship that attempted to run the American blockade of Iranian ports early Tuesday, the Wall Street Journal reported, citing an unnamed US official. Vanguard Tech had earlier said the container ship was hit by a missile and left without a command.

The UK navy said it received a report on Tuesday of an incident involving a container ship and military forces in the Gulf of Oman, without providing further details. It was also informed of an incident off Yemen’s Red Sea coast in which a cargo vessel reported being hit by an unknown projectile, resulting in casualties.

Saudi Arabia extinguished a fire at its Jazan oil refinery early Sunday, the country’s energy ministry said, with Yemen’s Houthi militants [claiming an attack](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-09/saudi-says-fire-at-aramco-refinery-in-jazan-was-extinguished-msl61cku) on the facility hours later.

“The region cannot remain indefinitely in a state of neither war nor peace,” Anwar Gargash, senior diplomatic adviser to the president of the United Arab Emirates, wrote in [a post on X](https://x.com/AnwarGargash/status/2087068621902430597). “Its stability and the future of its peoples require clarity in vision and direction.”

Trump to Axios: "We are low-keying it" with Iran over the weekend that he was prepared to let economic pressure on Iran build rather than launch fresh military strikes to force a reopening of Hormuz. The Islamic Republic’s economy has taken a hammering from the war, with much of its industrial capacity destroyed and crude exports [severely curtailed](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-10/iran-s-oil-terminals-look-idle-as-us-keeps-up-naval-blockade) by a US blockade. The currency, meanwhile, has fallen more than 10% from its prewar level, adding to the economic pressure on Iranians. A depreciation of the rial sparked violent nationwide protests that peaked early this year, leading to a crackdown by authorities that left thousands dead. There have been no sign of the anti-government demonstrations resuming.
