### 中文译文

## 曾主导经济改革的中国总理朱镕基逝世

朱镕基，作为中国总理，推动国有企业改革，导致[数千万](http://www.stats.gov.cn/tjsj/ndsj/2005/html/E0510e.htm)工人失业，并协助促成了中国加入世界贸易组织，现已去世。享年97岁。

据官方新华社报道，这位前高级领导人在北京因病治疗无效，于周三上午去世。

在1998年出任总理之前，朱镕基作为中国最高经济规划者，帮助国家渡过了一系列冲击，包括1997年亚洲金融危机和两位数的通货膨胀。他担任总理直到2003年。

据新华社发布的官方[讣告](https://www.news.cn/politics/20260812/4c2c72e299ef4561915d2e507393a81f/c.html)，朱镕基始终强调政府工作人员必须“敢于讲真话，不怕得罪人，不搞特殊化，主动挑重担”。

2002年，朱镕基在哥本哈根。摄影：Gerard Cerles/AFP/Getty Images

他对中国许多国有企业进行了彻底改革，关闭了数以万计效率低下或业绩不佳的电话运营商、银行、石油生产商和其他公司，以应对不断上升的债务，并通过私营部门促进增长。历届政府采取了不同战略，最近的例子是习近平主席倡导“[更强、更优、更大](https://ca.news.yahoo.com/xi-jinping-calls-china-state-105210588.html)”的国有经济，以更好地服务国家战略利益。

“这是一个不怕做出艰难决定的人。”高盛集团前联席总裁约翰·桑顿这样评价朱镕基。桑顿在1990年代中期与他相识。

桑顿是常驻纽约的投资银行家，曾担任1997年10月香港股票发售的首席经理，发售的公司后来成为[中国移动有限公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/941%20HK%20Equity)。桑顿回忆说，在香港回归中国主权仅数月后，朱镕基就想向世界表明，这块前英国殖民地将保持其资本主义性质，并因与内地的新联系而繁荣。

高盛召集中国移动高管——当时称为中国电信（香港）——在海南岛举行会议，并邀请德国电信股份公司的专家演示如何设立财务和营销部门等单位。朱镕基密切关注会议，不断向高管们提问，展现出对细节的娴熟掌握。

“这些问题我们之前已经回答过很多次，但通常你不会从一个国家的总理那里听到这些问题。”桑顿说。“通常，这类问题来自公司的首席财务官或首席执行官。”

### **经济改革**

作为1993年至1995年的中国人民银行行长，朱镕基通过“中国保险市场将向外资金融公司开放（更新1）”试点计划，以及“更新：中国发售首只全球债券，但投资者持谨慎态度”中的首次全球债券发售，进一步向外国投资者开放中国。

1999年，朱镕基监督成立了资产管理公司，以便从四大中国银行购买1.4万亿元人民币（2200亿美元）的不良贷款，这些银行一直难以处理这些坏账，他并试图阻止它们向亏损的国有企业提供更多贷款。这些行动对于银行清理资产负债表、于2005年开始上市并变得更加市场化至关重要。

![Zhu Rongji shakes hands with European Union Trade Commissioner Pascal Lamy in Beijing in 2000. Source: Xinhua/AFP/Getty Images](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3ba81cc90b1481129c44c462a818c9cb/3ba81cc9-0b14-816f-a7fd-e427cb22d51a-0c74c7da81befe35f65e8e4587fe1db7.png)

中国前领导人邓小平[称](http://finance.sina.com.cn/360desktop/leadership/20140821/095520077999.shtml)他为“稀有人才”，懂经济，敢于迎接挑战、面对困难。

2000年，他——朱镕基与拉米会面弥合WTO分歧；谈判恢复（更新2）——与时任欧盟贸易专员帕斯卡尔·拉米在北京会晤，以帮助打破谈判僵局，这使中国在次年加入世贸组织。自那时以来，中国的GDP增长了11倍，成为世界第二大经济体。然而，贸易伙伴批评北京不按规则行事，采取补贴国内公司、设置非正式进口壁垒以及[知识产权盗窃](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2019-06-15/forced-or-not-why-u-s-says-china-steals-technology-quicktake)等策略。

朱镕基在担任总理期间领导了反腐败运动，并致力于精简政府机构。他削减繁文缛节、减少官方审批数量的努力为他赢得了“[朱一章](https://www.scmp.com/article/235217/one-chop-zhu-set-success)”的绰号，这指的是中国签署文件所用的“图章”或官方印章。

他流利的英语和幽默感给外国领导人留下了深刻印象。1999年，在与美国总统比尔·克林顿于华盛顿举行的联合[记者会](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wp2WwaqOkvc)上，朱镕基回应了关于中国向民主党提供政治献金的指控。

![Chinese Prime Minister Zhu Rongji, left, with US President Bill Clinton in Washington DC in 1999. Photographer: Joyce Naltchayan/AFP/Getty Images](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3ba81cc90b1481129c44c462a818c9cb/3ba81cc9-0b14-8186-b37e-e208040554f1-233b9813a3c5e61bd73d0c3faca1a706.png)

朱镕基说：“这表明一些美国人确实低估了我们。如果政治献金真的有效，我有1460亿美元的外汇储备，我至少可以拿出100亿美元用于此目的。为什么只有30万美元？”

“那也太愚蠢了！”朱镕基的话引来笑声和零星的掌声。

2003年将总理职务移交给温家宝后，朱镕基将时间用于帮助提升母校——北京[清华大学](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/TSHUNZ%20CH%20Equity)的国际地位，并用他的版税收入向农村学校进行[捐赠](http://www.chinadaily.com.cn/m/fujian/longyan/2014-07/18/content_17840815.htm)。

### **政治历程**

根据他的官方[传记](http://www.gov.cn/test/2007-11/21/content_811884.htm)，朱镕基于1928年10月出生于中国南部的湖南省。他于1947年至1951年就读于清华大学，主修电气工程，并于1949年10月加入中国共产党。就在那个月，同样来自湖南的毛泽东主席宣告了中华人民共和国的成立。

朱镕基从1952年到1969年在国家计划部门工作。在鼓励言论自由的短暂的[百花齐放运动](https://www.britannica.com/event/Hundred-Flowers-Campaign)期间，他批评了毛泽东的经济政策，称其导致不理性的高增长。朱镕基被贴上“右派”标签并被[降职](https://books.google.com.hk/books?id=Fe_WDgAAQBAJ&lpg=PT65&ots=_lMoh9HGKj&dq=zhu%20rongji%20cultural%20revolution%20raise%20pigs%20planted%20rice&hl=zh-TW&pg=PT65#v=onepage&q=zhu%20rongji%20cultural%20revolution%20raise%20pigs%20planted%20rice&f=false)。后来，在[文化大革命](https://www.bloomberg.com/view/articles/2021-01-17/a-new-book-on-china-s-cultural-revolution-reveals-lessons-obscured-by-politics)期间，他在一个农场度过了五年，养猪和种稻。

1978年平反后，朱镕基在政府机构中逐步晋升，1987年被任命为上海市市长，1991年任副总理。1992年，他成为执政党政治局精英常务委员会成员。

“我只希望在我离任后，中国人民会说‘他是个清官，不是贪官’，我就很满足了。”朱镕基在2000年全国人大会议期间如是说。“如果他们更慷慨一些，说‘朱镕基确实办成了一些实事’，那我就谢天谢地了。”

朱镕基与妻子劳安育有两个孩子。其子朱云来曾于2000年代初至2014年执掌北京投资银行中国国际金融有限公司；其女朱燕来曾在香港中银国际控股有限公司担任多个高级管理职务。

### English Original

## Zhu Rongji, Chinese Premier Who Overhauled Economy, Dies

Zhu Rongji, who as China’s premier pushed through reforms at state-owned companies that cost [tens of millions](http://www.stats.gov.cn/tjsj/ndsj/2005/html/E0510e.htm) of workers their jobs, and who helped engineer China’s entry into the World Trade Organization, has died. He was 97.

The former senior leader died in Beijing on Wednesday morning after medical treatment for an unspecified illness failed, the official Xinhua News Agency reported.

Zhu helped steer the country through a series of shocks including the 1997 Asian financial crisis and double-digit inflation as China’s top economic planner before becoming premier in 1998, a post he held until 2003.

Zhu always emphasized government workers must “dare to speak the truth, not be afraid of offending people, avoid seeking special privileges, and proactively shoulder heavy responsibilities,” according to his official [obituary](https://www.news.cn/politics/20260812/4c2c72e299ef4561915d2e507393a81f/c.html) published on Xinhua.

Zhu Rongji in Copenhagen in 2002. Photographer: Gerard Cerles/AFP/Getty Images

He overhauled many of China’s state-owned enterprises, closing down tens of thousands of inefficient or badly performing telephone operators, banks, oil producers and other companies to deal with rising debt and encourage growth through the private sector. Successive administrations pursued a different strategy, most recently with President Xi Jinping advocating a “[stronger, better, and bigger](https://ca.news.yahoo.com/xi-jinping-calls-china-state-105210588.html)” state-controlled economy to better serve national strategic interests.

“This was someone who was not afraid to make a difficult decision,” John Thornton, the former co-president of Goldman Sachs Group Inc., said of Zhu, whom he met in the mid-1990s.

Thornton, a New York-based investment banker, was the lead manager for the October 1997 Hong Kong share sale of the company that became [China Mobile Ltd](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/941%20HK%20Equity). Only months after the territory’s return to Chinese rule, Zhu wanted to show the world that the former British colony would retain its capitalist nature and prosper from its new tie to the mainland, Thornton recalled.

Goldman Sachs gathered China Mobile executives — then called China Telecom (Hong Kong) — on the southern island of Hainan and brought in Deutsche Telekom AG experts to show how to set up units such as finance and marketing departments. Zhu kept tabs on the meeting, peppering the executives with questions and displaying a mastery of detail.

“We’d answered these questions many times before, but usually you don’t get these questions from the premier of a country,” Thornton said. “Usually you get these kind of questions from the chief financial officer of a company or the chief executive.”

### **Economic reforms**

As governor of the People’s Bank of China from 1993 to 1995, Zhu further opened China to foreign investors by Chinese Insurance Market to Open Up to Foreign Firms (Update1) pilot programs for overseas financial firms and Update: China Sells First Global Bond, but Investors are Wary its first worldwide bond sale.

Zhu oversaw the creation of asset-management companies in 1999 to buy 1.4 trillion yuan ($220 billion) in bad loans from the top four Chinese banks, which had been struggling to deal with them, and sought to keep them from lending more to unprofitable state-owned enterprises. The actions were essential to allow the banks to clean up their balance sheets and go public, starting in 2005, and become more market oriented.

Deng Xiaoping, the former Chinese leader, [called](http://finance.sina.com.cn/360desktop/leadership/20140821/095520077999.shtml) him a “rare talent” who understood the economy and dared to take on challenges and face hardships.

In 2000, he Zhu, Lamy Meet to Bridge WTO Gaps; Talks to Resume (Update2) with then-European Union Trade Commissioner Pascal Lamy in Beijing to help break a deadlock in talks, which led to China joining the WTO the following year. China’s GDP has increased 11-fold since then to become the world’s second-largest economy. However, trade partners have criticized Beijing for not playing by the rules with such tactics as subsidizing domestic companies and putting up informal barriers to imports as well as [intellectual property theft](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2019-06-15/forced-or-not-why-u-s-says-china-steals-technology-quicktake).

Zhu led an anti-corruption campaign as premier and also worked to trim the government bureaucracy. His efforts to cut red tape and reduce the number of official approvals earned him the nickname “[one-chop Zhu](https://www.scmp.com/article/235217/one-chop-zhu-set-success),” a reference to the “chops,” or official seals, used in China to sign documents.

He impressed foreign leaders with his fluent English and sense of humor. In 1999, during a joint Washington [press conference](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wp2WwaqOkvc) with US President Bill Clinton, Zhu responded to allegations that China had made political contributions to the Democratic Party.

“This shows that some Americans really have underestimated us,” Zhu said. “If political contributions could really be effective, and I have $146 billion of foreign-exchange reserves, I could take out at least $10 billion for this purpose. Why just $300,000?”

“That would be too foolish!” Zhu said to laughter and scattered applause.

After handing the premiership to Wen Jiabao in 2003, Zhu spent his time helping to raise the international stature of his alma mater — Beijing’s [Tsinghua University](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/TSHUNZ%20CH%20Equity) — and made [donations](http://www.chinadaily.com.cn/m/fujian/longyan/2014-07/18/content_17840815.htm) to rural schools from his book royalties.

### **Political Journey**

Zhu was born in October 1928 in Hunan province in southern China, according to his official [biography](http://www.gov.cn/test/2007-11/21/content_811884.htm). He attended Tsinghua from 1947 to 1951, majoring in electrical engineering, and joining the Communist Party in October 1949. That was the month when Chairman Mao Zedong, who was also from Hunan, proclaimed the founding of the People’s Republic.

Zhu worked for the state planning agency from 1952 to 1969. He criticized Mao’s economic policies during the [Hundred Flowers Campaign](https://www.britannica.com/event/Hundred-Flowers-Campaign), a brief period when freedom of speech was encouraged, saying they led to irrational high growth. Zhu was labeled a “rightist” and [demoted](https://books.google.com.hk/books?id=Fe_WDgAAQBAJ&lpg=PT65&ots=_lMoh9HGKj&dq=zhu%20rongji%20cultural%20revolution%20raise%20pigs%20planted%20rice&hl=zh-TW&pg=PT65#v=onepage&q=zhu%20rongji%20cultural%20revolution%20raise%20pigs%20planted%20rice&f=false). He later spent spent five years working on a farm, raising pigs and planting rice, during the [Cultural Revolution](https://www.bloomberg.com/view/articles/2021-01-17/a-new-book-on-china-s-cultural-revolution-reveals-lessons-obscured-by-politics).

After being rehabilitated in 1978, Zhu rose through the ranks of government agencies and was named Shanghai’s mayor in 1987 and a vice premier in 1991. He became a member of the ruling Politburo’s elite Standing Committee in 1992.

“I only hope that after I leave office, the Chinese people will say that he was a clean official, not a corrupt one. I would be very satisfied,” Zhu [said](http://cpc.people.com.cn/pinglun/n/2013/0816/c241220-22593876.html) during a national legislature session in 2000. “If they are more generous and say that Zhu Rongji got some real things done, then I’d thank heaven and Earth.”

Zhu had two children with his wife, Lao An. Levin Zhu, his son, [headed](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2008-02-25/china-weatherman-zhu-loses-grip-as-citic-weakens-ciccs-perch) China International Capital Corp., a Beijing-based investment bank from the early 2000s to 2014, and his daughter, Zhu Yanlai, held a number of top management positions at Bank of China (Hong Kong).
