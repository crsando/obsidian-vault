### 中文译文

## 伊朗将霍尔木兹海峡重新开放与美国在多项要求上作出让步挂钩

迪拜/华盛顿/开罗，8月10日（路透社）——伊朗表示，即将与阿曼就通过霍尔木兹海峡的新航道达成最终协议，但重申美国必须满足其他条件，包括赔偿、结束制裁和军事威胁，之后这条战略水道才会重新开放。

该海峡在冲突前承载着全球约五分之一的石油和液化天然气运输，自2月28日[美以袭击](https://www.reuters.com/world/iran/)伊朗以来实际上已被封锁，推高了油价和通货膨胀。

伊朗外交部发言人埃斯梅尔·巴加伊周一表示，与阿曼就霍尔木兹海峡航行安排的会谈“进展顺利且富有建设性”，已就航运路线图达成协议，而联合声明的一些技术问题仍未解决。

他在德黑兰的每周新闻发布会上表示，讨论还涉及安全航行机制、环境保护、海事服务和打击犯罪，并补充说此类服务通常需要收取费用作为回报。

### 条件包括赔偿

周日，伊朗外交部长阿巴斯·阿拉格齐表示，与阿曼的协议已进入“最后阶段”，但强调德黑兰[不会重新开放](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/iran-says-deal-strait-hormuz-is-close-will-not-open-waterway-by-itself-2026-08-08/)该通道，除非美国满足德黑兰提出的某些要求。

阿拉格齐表示，伊朗与美国没有进行直接谈判，只要华盛顿违反6月签署的临时协议，德黑兰就不会开始谈判，并补充说目前正在通过中间人传递信息。

美国总统唐纳德·特朗普在接受Axios采访时表示，美国对伊朗“保持低调”。“我们只是半谈判状态。我们只是看着伊朗严重的通货膨胀以及他们没有钱的事实。”

阿拉格希表示，重新开放海峡将部分取决于美国为其对伊朗发动的大范围攻击所造成的损害支付赔偿。

伊朗最高国家安全机构即将卸任的秘书穆罕默德·巴克尔·佐勒卡德尔还列出了以下要求：停止美国对伊朗的进一步威胁；停止对伊朗及其黎巴嫩、[巴勒斯坦](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/israel-rejects-trumps-15-point-plan-gaza-pm-netanyahu-says-2026-08-09/)、[也门](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/fire-extinguished-aramco-facility-jizan-saudi-arabia-says-2026-08-09/)和伊拉克盟友的侵略；解除美国在海湾的海上封锁；取消对伊朗的制裁；以及释放被冻结的伊朗资产。

伊朗周日宣布，前革命卫队指挥官[穆赫森·雷扎伊](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/mohsen-rezaei-appointed-secretary-irans-top-security-body-2026-08-10/)将接任该国协调安全与外交政策机构的二把手，此人被认为比佐勒卡德尔更为直言不讳。

没有说明换人的原因，目前也不清楚这是否预示着政策上的任何变化。

*2026年8月3日，从阿曼穆桑代姆看到的霍尔木兹海峡中的船只。路透社/特约记者*

### 油价小幅上涨

周一，[油价小幅上涨](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/oil-rises-uncertainty-continues-over-reopening-strait-2026-08-09/)，至每桶84.22美元，因市场对开放海峡协议的乐观情绪被伊朗坚持美国必须满足其要求的立场所抵消。

4月底，全球布伦特原油基准价格触及每桶约126美元，为2022年以来最高，因航运持续中断。

特朗普曾表示，对伊朗的打击旨在阻止伊斯兰共和国发展核武器并削弱其威胁地区的能力。如今，他面临压力，要在11月中期选举前结束一场在国内极不受欢迎的战争。

华盛顿和德黑兰在6月达成停火协议，但美国在7月[重新实施封锁](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/iran-says-it-struck-us-air-base-jordan-us-military-ends-five-hours-attacks-2026-07-14/)对伊朗在海湾的航运，德黑兰称此举违反了停火协议，而当时该协议已经破裂。

“一旦宣布达成协议，恢复无障碍商业航运，美国将解除对伊朗港口的封锁。”一名美国官员周五匿名向路透社表示。

该官员表示，美国的行动将与伊朗履行承诺挂钩。

美国的评论表明，达成协议的步骤安排十分微妙。消息人士曾告诉路透社，该协议似乎将[让德黑兰控制](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/us-iran-having-very-good-discussions-trump-says-2026-08-05/)通过海峡进入海湾的船只通行，而[航运商表示](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/proposed-hormuz-passage-deal-not-feasible-shipping-industry-sources-say-2026-08-06/)这并不容易操作。

### 胡塞武装袭击

伊朗对霍尔木兹海峡航运的打击，以及其也门盟友胡塞武装增加的袭击行动同时发生。胡塞武装在阿拉伯半岛另一侧、红海与亚丁湾之间的另一个石油咽喉要道袭击船只。

胡塞武装上个月还宣布在红海对沙特阿拉伯实施海上封锁，以回应他们所称的沙特在也门对其的围困。沙特方面否认这一指控，沙特支持也门国际公认的政府。

也门胡塞武装与政府军之间的战斗已经升级，也门军方周一表示，胡塞武装对红海港口城市穆哈的袭击造成包括军人和平民在内的7人死亡。

军方称，防空部队拦截并击落了参与袭击的11架胡塞武装无人机。

## Iran ties Hormuz reopening to US concessions on several demands

DUBAI/WASHINGTON/CAIRO, Aug 10 (Reuters) - Iran said it was nearing a final pact with Oman defining new shipping lanes between them through the Strait of Hormuz but repeated that the U.S. must meet other conditions, including compensation and an end to sanctions and military threats, before the strategic waterway is reopened.

The strait, through which about a fifth of global oil and liquefied natural gas flowed prior to the conflict, has been effectively blocked since the [U.S.-Israel attacks](https://www.reuters.com/world/iran/) on Iran on February 28, pushing up oil prices and inflation.

Iranian Foreign Ministry spokesman Esmaeil Baghaei said on Monday that talks with Oman on arrangements for navigation through the strait were "progressing smoothly and constructively", with an agreement reached on a shipping route map, while some technical issues on a joint statement remained unresolved.

He said during a weekly press conference in Tehran that the discussions also covered mechanisms for safe navigation, environmental protection, maritime services and combating crime, adding that such services would normally entail receiving payment in return.

### CONDITIONS INCLUDE COMPENSATION

On Sunday, Iran's Foreign Minister Abbas Araqchi said an agreement with Oman was in its "final stages" but stressed that Tehran [would not reopen](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/iran-says-deal-strait-hormuz-is-close-will-not-open-waterway-by-itself-2026-08-08/) the channel unless the U.S. met certain demands made by Tehran.

Iran and the U.S. are not engaged in direct talks, and Tehran will not start them as long as Washington breaches an interim deal signed in June, Araqchi said, adding that messages were being exchanged through intermediaries.

U.S. President Donald Trump said in an interview with Axios that the U.S. was "low-keying" it with Iran. "We are only semi-negotiating with them. We are just watching Iran with its huge inflation and the fact they have no money."

Araqchi said reopening the strait would depend in part on the U.S. paying compensation for damage it caused in widespread attacks on Iran.

The outgoing secretary of Iran's top national security body, Mohammad Baqer Zolqadr, also listed ending further U.S. threats against Iran; halting aggression toward Iran and its Lebanese, [Palestinian](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/israel-rejects-trumps-15-point-plan-gaza-pm-netanyahu-says-2026-08-09/), [Yemeni](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/fire-extinguished-aramco-facility-jizan-saudi-arabia-says-2026-08-09/) and Iraqi allies; removing a U.S. naval blockade in the Gulf; lifting sanctions on Iran; and releasing frozen Iranian assets.

Iran on Sunday announced that former Revolutionary Guards commander [Mohsen Rezaei](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/mohsen-rezaei-appointed-secretary-irans-top-security-body-2026-08-10/), seen as a more outspoken figure than Zolqadr, would take over as second-in-command of the body that coordinates the country's security and foreign policy.

No reason was given for the change and it was not immediately clear if it would herald any change in policy.

*Vessels in the Strait of Hormuz, as seen from Musandam, Oman, August 3, 2026. REUTERS/Stringer*

### OIL EDGES HIGHER

[Oil prices edged higher](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/oil-rises-uncertainty-continues-over-reopening-strait-2026-08-09/) on Monday to $84.22 a barrel, as optimism over a deal to open the strait was tempered by Iran's insistence the U.S. must satisfy its demands.

At the end of April, the global Brent oil benchmark hit about $126 a barrel, the highest since 2022, amid ongoing disruption to shipping.

Trump, who has said the attacks on Iran were aimed at keeping the Islamic Republic from developing nuclear weapons and degrading its ability to threaten the region, is under pressure to end a war that is deeply unpopular at home ahead of midterm elections in November.

Washington and Tehran agreed to a ceasefire in June, but the U.S. [reimposed a blockade](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/iran-says-it-struck-us-air-base-jordan-us-military-ends-five-hours-attacks-2026-07-14/) on Iranian shipping in the Gulf in July, a move that Tehran said violated the truce, which by then had already broken down.

"Once the deal is announced to restore commercial shipping without impediments, the United States will lift the blockade of Iranian ports," a U.S. official told Reuters on Friday on condition of anonymity.

U.S. actions would be tied to Iran's implementation of its commitments, the official said.

The U.S. comments indicated delicate sequencing toward a deal that sources had told Reuters appeared to be set [to give Tehran control](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/us-iran-having-very-good-discussions-trump-says-2026-08-05/) over vessel traffic entering the Gulf through the strait, which [shippers have said](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/proposed-hormuz-passage-deal-not-feasible-shipping-industry-sources-say-2026-08-06/) was not easily workable.

### HOUTHI ATTACKS

Iranian strikes on Strait of Hormuz shipping have been coupled with increased attacks by its Yemen-based allies, the Houthi rebels, who have targeted ships at another oil chokepoint on the other side of the Arabian Peninsula between the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden.

The Houthis also declared a naval blockade against Saudi Arabia in the Red Sea last month in response to what they said was a Saudi siege on them in Yemen, an allegation denied by Riyadh, which backs Yemen's internationally recognised government.

Fighting has escalated between the Houthis and government forces in Yemen, with the Yemeni military on Monday saying seven people including military personnel and civilians were killed in a Houthi attack on the Red Sea port city of Mocha.

Air defences intercepted and shot down 11 Houthi drones that participated in the attack, the military said.
