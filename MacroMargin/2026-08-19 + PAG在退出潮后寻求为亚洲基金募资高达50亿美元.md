### 中文译文

## PAG在退出潮后寻求为亚洲基金募资高达50亿美元

据知情人士透露，PAG正寻求为其新基金募资高达50亿美元，此前该公司已将投资多元化，撤出中国并加快向投资者返还现金。

新基金的规模预计将略大于其前身，目标在40亿至50亿美元之间，因为该公司寻求在其庞大的中国敞口与该地区其他地区的机遇之间取得平衡。知情人士表示，基金的最终规模尚未确定，预计将在年底前完成首次关闭。由于此事属于机密，这些人士要求不具名。

过去几年，PAG扩大了在亚洲的业务版图，减少了对中国的依赖。据知情人士透露，在其第四期基金中，分配给日本的比例已赶上中国，目前两者各占约30%，这突显了该公司向更加平衡的泛亚投资组合转变。他们还表示，其余部分由澳大利亚和印度构成。

一位驻香港的发言人拒绝置评。

知情人士称，自2025年以来，该公司通过退出向投资者返还了超过70亿美元，目前正寻求更强劲的募资回归。两年前，这家泛亚另类资产管理公司仅为其第四只收购基金募集了40亿美元，不到其最初90亿美元目标的一半，原因是其沉重的中国敞口加剧了投资者在地缘政治紧张和退出匮乏背景下对该地区的整体撤退。

过去16个月，PAG的交易退出加速。其中一位知情人士表示，该公司去年向投资者返还了约50亿美元，今年迄今又返还了20亿美元。

对印度Nuvama Wealth Management Ltd.的投资按当前估值已实现近6倍回报。在澳大利亚，PAG在出售部分消费业务后，不到两年就收回了资本。知情人士称，对中国的AirPower Technologies约10亿美元的投资已产生近60亿美元的价值，这是中国有史以来最大的一笔退出。

### English Original

## PAG Seeks as Much as $5 Billion for Asia Fund After Exit Spree

PAG is seeking as much as $5 billion for a new fund after diversifying its investments outside of China and accelerating cash returns to investors, people familiar with the matter said.

The new vehicle is expected to be slightly larger than its predecessor, targeted at between $4 billion to $5 billion, as the firm seeks to balance its sizable China exposure with opportunities elsewhere in the region. The fund’s final size hasn’t been decided on yet, and first closing is expected by year-end, the people said, asking not to be identified because the matter is confidential.

PAG has broadened its footprint across Asia in the past few years, reducing its reliance on China. The portion allocated to Japan caught up with China in its Fund IV and is now roughly the same size at 30% each, underscoring the firm’s shift toward a more balanced pan-Asian portfolio, the people said. Australia and India account for the remainder, they said.

A Hong Kong-based spokeswoman declined to comment.

After returning more than $7 billion to investors through exits since 2025, the firm is seeking a stronger fundraising comeback, the people said. Two years ago, the pan-Asia alternative asset manager [raised](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2024-06-13/pag-raises-just-half-of-9-billion-goal-for-new-asia-pe-fund) just $4 billion for its fourth buyout fund, less than half its original $9 billion target, as its heavy China exposure compounded a broader investor retreat from the region amid geopolitical tensions and a dearth of exits.

PAG’s deal exits accelerated in the past 16 months. It handed about $5 billion to investors last year and a further $2 billion so far this year, one of the people said.

Investment in India’s Nuvama Wealth Management Ltd. has achieved almost 6 times return based on current valuation. In Australia, PAG recouped its capital in under two years after selling part of a consumer business. A roughly $1 billion investment in China’s AirPower Technologies** **has generated close to $6 billion in value, a largest exit ever in China, the people said.
