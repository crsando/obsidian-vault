### 中文译文

## 在竞争激烈的中国人工智能领域，MiniMax 销售额几乎翻了两番

七月在上海世界人工智能大会上的 MiniMax 集团展位。*摄影：沈绮来/彭博社*

### **彭博 AI 要点**

- MiniMax 集团股份有限公司 2026 年上半年营收飙升 283%，达 1.166 亿美元，同期亏损达 3.58 亿美元。
- 公司 8 月份的年度经常性收入达到 8 亿美元，并将加快发布新模型的步伐，以经济实惠的模型在中国人工智能领域展开竞争。
- 分析师预计 MiniMax 全年销售额将增长 360%，但也预测由于代理式 AI 成本上升以及在该领域与主导企业竞争艰难，运营亏损将增加。
[MiniMax Group Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/100%20HK%20Equity) 的营收在 2026 年上半年飙升 283%，尽管这家中国 AI 新贵在发布顶级大语言模型方面落后于 [Z.AI Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/2513%20HK%20Equity) 等竞争对手。

截至 6 月的六个月中，营收几乎翻了两番，达到 1.166 亿美元，而 2025 年全年营收增长 159%（见[此处](https://www1.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2026/0302/2026030202837.pdf)）。期间亏损达 3.58 亿美元。分析师预计全年销售额将增长 360%。

公司首席执行官严俊杰在周三的电话会议上告诉分析师，公司 8 月份的年度经常性收入达到 8 亿美元，并将加快发布新模型的步伐。

这家总部位于上海的公司选择以价格亲民的模型参与日益拥挤的中国人工智能领域竞争。这一策略与Z.ai和[Moonshot AI](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/2312319D:CH)等开放权重同行形成鲜明对比，后者正与硅谷的前沿模型正面交锋。

其做法未能赢得投资者青睐。MiniMax的股价自1月份在香港上市以来一度飙升650%，但已从3月份的峰值大幅回落。目前该公司估值约为130亿美元，市值约为Z.ai的四分之一。

![图片](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3c881cc90b1481c1847df1559af52ce8/3c881cc9-0b14-8116-b924-ec1e4e0d5338-06a20115c4693b8c6f7290a56053312d.png)

6月，MiniMax发布了其最新模型M3，但仅一周后就下调了价格。

该迭代版本不及同期推出的竞品，如Z.ai的GLM-5.2和Moonshot AI的Kimi K3。在第三方测试网站[Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/?models=muse-spark-1-2%2Cgemini-3-5-flash-lite%2Cinkling%2Ck-exaone-2-0-0803%2Cminimax-m3%2Cnemotron-3-5-lightning%2Ccommand-a-plus%2Cgpt-5-6-luna%2Cmuse-glimmer%2Cnvidia-nemotron-3-ultra-550b-a55b%2Cnvidia-nemotron-3-super-120b-a12b%2Csolar-open2-250b%2Ca-x-k2%2Cdeepseek-v4-pro%2Cqwen3-8-2-4t-a95b%2Cclaude-4-5-haiku-reasoning%2Cqwen3-8-27b%2Cmotif-3%2Cgemini-3-7-flash%2Cclaude-opus-5%2Cgpt-5-6-terra%2Cgrok-4-6%2Cclaude-fable-5%2Cglm-5-3%2Cgpt-5-6-sol%2Cmistral-medium-3-5%2Cgpt-5-5-pro%2Cgpt-oss-120b%2Ckimi-k3%2Cdeepseek-v4-flash)上，M3的智能得分为45，而数家国内竞争对手得分超过50。就单任务成本而言，M3属于最经济的选择之一，但仍比[DeepSeek](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/2544189D:CH)的V4 Flash更昂贵，后者被视为性价比的[行业基准](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-04/china-s-ai-blitz-creates-death-zone-for-rival-us-model-makers)。

MiniMax或许能在多模态和消费应用方面找到优势。其本月开源的H3视频生成模型与[字节跳动](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1774397D:CH)的Seedance展开竞争，争夺电影工作室和创意人士。与此同时，其消费级AI工具（包括伴侣应用和工作空间智能体）正在推动商业化。

*智能体AI成本上升将推动这家规模不足的公司今年的营业亏损约翻一番，达到约6亿美元，因为该公司正努力在中国分散且商品化的人工智能领域开辟防御性利基市场。MiniMax缺乏与腾讯、字节跳动和阿里巴巴竞争所需的必要竞争优势和资源，这些公司日益主导该行业。推理成本上升将导致未来三年营业亏损激增，我们预计该公司将在未来18个月内需要额外资本融资。由于短期内不太可能发生整合，中国人工智能行业将在未来三年内继续深陷亏损。- Robert Lea和Jasmine Lyu，分析师 点击查看：MiniMax的AI盈利之路可能以失败告终：股票研究展望*

上个月，MiniMax通过出售新股和可转换债券筹集了约20亿美元，以资助训练和托管其AI系统所需的昂贵算力和半导体。

### English Original

## MiniMax Sales Nearly Quadruple in Crowded China AI Race

The MiniMax Group booth at the World AI Conference in Shanghai, in July.*Photographer: Qilai Shen/Bloomberg*

### **Takeaways **by Bloomberg AI

- MiniMax Group Inc.'s revenue surged 283% in the first half of 2026 to $116.6 million, with losses for the period reaching $358 million.
- The company's annual recurring revenue hit $800 million in August, and it will accelerate the pace of releasing new models, competing in the Chinese AI field with budget-friendly models.
- Analysts expect MiniMax's full-year sales to expand 360%, but also predict rising operating losses due to increasing agentic AI costs and a struggle to compete with dominant firms in the sector.
[MiniMax Group Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/100%20HK%20Equity)’s revenue surged 283% in the first half of 2026, even as the Chinese AI upstart trailed rivals such as [Z.AI Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/2513%20HK%20Equity) in releasing top-tier large language models.

Revenue almost quadrupled to $116.6 million in the six months through June, compared with the 159% growth it [posted](https://www1.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2026/0302/2026030202837.pdf) for all of 2025. Losses for the period reached $358 million. Analysts expect full-year sales to expand 360%.

The company’s annual recurring revenue hit $800 million in August, Chief Executive Officer Yan Junjie told analysts on a call on Wednesday, and it will accelerate the pace of releasing new models.

The Shanghai-based company has opted to compete in the increasingly crowded Chinese AI field with budget-friendly models. That strategy contrasts with open-weight peers such as Z.ai and [Moonshot AI](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/2312319D:CH), which are competing head-to-head with Silicon Valley’s frontier models.

Its approach has failed to win over investors. MiniMax’s shares, which soared as much as 650% from its Hong Kong listing in January, have retreated sharply from a March peak. Now valued at about $13 billion, the company’s market cap is about a quarter of Z.ai’s.

In June, MiniMax released its latest model, M3, but cut prices just a week later.

That iteration fell short of competing offerings launched around the same time, such as Z.ai’s GLM-5.2 and Moonshot AI’s Kimi K3. On third-party testing site [Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/?models=muse-spark-1-2%2Cgemini-3-5-flash-lite%2Cinkling%2Ck-exaone-2-0-0803%2Cminimax-m3%2Cnemotron-3-5-lightning%2Ccommand-a-plus%2Cgpt-5-6-luna%2Cmuse-glimmer%2Cnvidia-nemotron-3-ultra-550b-a55b%2Cnvidia-nemotron-3-super-120b-a12b%2Csolar-open2-250b%2Ca-x-k2%2Cdeepseek-v4-pro%2Cqwen3-8-2-4t-a95b%2Cclaude-4-5-haiku-reasoning%2Cqwen3-8-27b%2Cmotif-3%2Cgemini-3-7-flash%2Cclaude-opus-5%2Cgpt-5-6-terra%2Cgrok-4-6%2Cclaude-fable-5%2Cglm-5-3%2Cgpt-5-6-sol%2Cmistral-medium-3-5%2Cgpt-5-5-pro%2Cgpt-oss-120b%2Ckimi-k3%2Cdeepseek-v4-flash), M3 scored 45 on intelligence, whereas several domestic rivals scored above 50. On a per-task cost basis, M3 ranks among the most economic options, though it remains pricier than [DeepSeek](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/2544189D:CH)’s V4 Flash, which serves as an [industry benchmark](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-04/china-s-ai-blitz-creates-death-zone-for-rival-us-model-makers) for price-to-performance.

MiniMax may find its edge in multimodality and consumer applications. Its H3 video generation model — open-sourced this month — competes with [ByteDance Ltd.](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1774397D:CH)’s Seedance for film studios and creatives. Meanwhile, its consumer AI tools, including a companion app and a workspace agent, are driving monetization.

*Rising agentic AI costs will drive an approximate doubling of operating losses at the sub-scale firm this year, to around $600 million, as it struggles to carve out a defensive niche in China’s fragmented, commoditized AI sector. MiniMax lacks the necessary competitive edge and resources to compete with Tencent, ByteDance and Alibaba, which increasingly dominate the sector. Rising inference costs will cause operating losses to balloon over the next three years, and we expect the firm will require additional capital financing within the next 18 months. With near-term consolidation unlikely, China’s AI sector is set to remain deeply unprofitable for the next three years. - Robert Lea and Jasmine Lyu, analysts Click MiniMax's AI Profit Quest Likely to End in Vain: Equity Outlook for the research.*

Last month, MiniMax [raised](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-09/minimax-seeks-1-9-billion-from-share-sale-convertible-bond) about $2 billion by selling new shares and convertible bonds to fund the costly compute power and semiconductors needed to train and host its AI systems.
