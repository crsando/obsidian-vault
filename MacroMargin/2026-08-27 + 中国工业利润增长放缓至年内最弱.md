### 中文译文

## 中国工业利润增长放缓至年内最弱

中国工业利润增速连续第三个月放缓，至年内最低水平，因经济降温拖累生产和价格涨幅。

据官方数据，[工业利润](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CNPRETLY%20Index)增速在7月再次放缓至11.2%，使今年前七个月的利润增长17.6%。累计增幅低于彭博经济研究（Bloomberg Economics）在《亚洲一周前瞻：韩国央行、菲律宾将加息；东京、澳大利亚CPI(2)》中提出的18.1%增幅。

![图片](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3c981cc90b1481dc8757e72acf7007b1/3c981cc9-0b14-8197-9644-eb09256f2ffb-0b4a154be9f3965dddc3aa01fc75141a.png)

这些数据表明，随着国内需求减弱以及早前油价飙升的影响消退，中国企业面临的压力日渐增大，这抵消了全球对人工智能相关商品需求带来的部分收益。

国家统计局分析师于卫宁在随数据发布的[声明](https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/sjjd/202608/t20260827_1965127.html)中表示：“国际环境依然复杂严峻，国内方面，供给强劲而需求偏弱的失衡较为明显。”

于卫宁表示，政府将加大力度扩大内需、“优化”供给并强化新兴产业，以帮助“确保新旧增长引擎平稳过渡”。他提到，供给这一措辞可能意味着将进一步采取措施抑制过剩工业产能。

7月份，生产、消费和投资均[未达预期](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-17/china-s-economy-extends-slowdown-by-weakening-across-the-board)，工厂出厂价格和消费者价格涨幅[走弱](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-09/china-s-inflation-cools-as-oil-shock-of-iran-war-starts-to-ease)，加剧了企业收入和利润率的压力。一些经济学家估计，在第二季度放缓之后，经济增速已进一步滑落至北京设定的4.5%-5%年度目标下方。

工业利润今年才开始持续回升，这得益于伊朗战争期间全球大宗商品价格的飙升和对人工智能相关产品需求的猛增。利润增速放缓可能使企业对经营前景和投资更加谨慎，从而给经济带来下行压力。

整体数据还掩盖了各行业日益不均衡的表现。于卫宁表示，以集成电路为首的电子行业仍是带动利润增长的关键行业，在1月至7月期间为增长贡献了9.3个百分点。原材料制造业贡献了另外7.1个百分点。

相比之下，国家统计局数据显示，[黑色金属冶炼](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CNPTFRCY%20Index)和[家具制造](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CNPTFNCY%20Index)企业的利润收缩至少51%，反映出房地产低迷和基础设施投资下降。由于汽车销量暴跌，[汽车制造商](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CNPTVHCY%20Index)的利润下降了20%。

*“中国7月份利润增长放缓表明高科技制造业领先，而其他领域盈利能力减弱。战争驱动的能源对材料行业的提振已经见顶。这些数据强化了科技蓬勃发展的信息，但其规模还不足以支撑整体经济。” — Eric Zhu。如需完整分析，请点击CHINA REACT：利润增长放缓凸显科技差距扩大*

这种放缓在不同所有制类型的企业中都有体现，但在[民营企业](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CNPRPEEY%20Index)中最为明显。其利润增长从上半年的13%放缓至前七个月的10.9%。[外商投资企业](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CNPRTPFY%20Index)的利润增长最弱，仅增长1.2%。

## China’s Industrial Profit Growth Slows to Weakest This Year

Growth in China’s industrial profits slowed for a third straight month to the weakest pace this year, as a cooling economy weighed on production and price gains.

[Industrial profit](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CNPRETLY%20Index) growth eased again in July to 11.2%, leaving earnings up 17.6% in the first seven months of the year, official data showed Thursday. The figures point to growing pressure on Chinese companies as domestic demand weakens and an earlier surge in oil prices fades, offsetting some of the benefits from global demand for artificial intelligence-related goods.

“The international environment remains complex and challenging, and domestically the imbalance between strong supply and weak demand is fairly pronounced,” NBS analyst Yu Weining said in a [statement](https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/sjjd/202608/t20260827_1965127.html) accompanying the data.

The government will step up efforts to expand domestic demand, “optimize” supply and strengthen emerging industries to help “ensure a smooth transition from old to new growth engines,” Yu said. The reference to supply may signal further efforts to curb excess industrial capacity.

Production, consumption and investment all [missed expectations](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-17/china-s-economy-extends-slowdown-by-weakening-across-the-board) in July, while factory-gate and consumer price gains [weakened](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-09/china-s-inflation-cools-as-oil-shock-of-iran-war-starts-to-ease), adding to pressure on corporate revenue and margins. Some economists estimate growth has slipped further below Beijing’s annual target of 4.5%-5% after a slowdown in the second quarter.

Industrial earnings only began a sustained rebound this year, helped by a surge in global commodity prices during the war in Iran and soaring demand for AI-related products. The deceleration in profit gains could hold companies more cautious about their business outlook and investment, adding downward pressure on the economy.

The headline figures also masked an increasingly uneven performance across industries. Electronics, led by integrated circuits, remained the key sector driving profit growth, contributing 9.3 percentage points to the expansion in the January-July period, according to Yu. Raw-material manufacturers accounted for another 7.1 percentage points.

By contrast, profits at [ferrous metals smelters](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CNPTFRCY%20Index) and [furniture manufacturers](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CNPTFNCY%20Index) contracted by at least 51%, NBS data showed, reflecting the prolonged property slump and falling infrastructure investment. Earnings at [automakers](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CNPTVHCY%20Index) fell by 20% as car sales plunged.

*“China’s slower profit growth in July shows high-tech manufacturing powering ahead, with profitability weakening elsewhere. The war-driven energy boost to material sectors has peaked. The readings reinforced the message that tech is booming, but it still isn’t big enough to carry the wider economy.” — Eric Zhu. For full analysis, click CHINA REACT: Slower Profit Gains Highlight Widening Tech Gap*

The slowdown extended across companies of different ownership types but was most pronounced among [private firms](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CNPRPEEY%20Index). Their profit growth moderated to 10.9% in the first seven months from 13% in the first half. [Foreign-invested companies](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CNPRTPFY%20Index) recorded the weakest growth, with earnings rising just 1.2%.
