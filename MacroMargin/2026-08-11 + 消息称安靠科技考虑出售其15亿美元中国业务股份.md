## 消息称安靠科技考虑出售其15亿美元中国业务股份

### [科技](https://www.bloomberg.com/technology)

作者：[Pei Li](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AVZECW3TirQ/pei-li) 与 [Dong Cao](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AUZmGbfPsvM/dong-cao) 2026年8月11日 上午9:56 GMT+8 *更新于2026年8月11日 上午11:20 GMT+8*

### **彭博AI要点摘录**

### **要点摘录**

- 据知情人士透露，安靠科技公司（Amkor Technology Inc.）正在考虑包括出售其中国业务股份在内的选项。
- 该公司正在与一家顾问合作，以帮助准备业务分拆并评估初步兴趣，且可能保留该业务的少数股权。
- 亚洲投资公司和行业参与者可能对这些资产感兴趣，但相关考虑尚处于初步阶段，尚未做出任何决定。
[安靠科技公司（Amkor Technology Inc.）](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/AMKR%20US%20Equity)，一家提供外包半导体封装和测试服务的供应商，据知情人士透露，正在考虑包括出售其中国业务股份在内的选项。

这些人士称，这家总部位于亚利桑那州坦佩的公司正在与一家顾问合作，帮助准备该子公司的分拆事宜并评估初步兴趣。由于相关商议未公开，这些人士要求不具名。这些人士表示，Amkor可能保留该业务的少数股权，该业务估值可能在10亿至15亿美元之间。

Amkor股价今年已上涨34%，使公司市值达到131亿美元。尽管如此，由于人工智能相关股票波动，该股自6月份见顶以来已大幅下跌。

这些人士称，亚洲投资公司和行业参与者可能对这些资产感兴趣。他们表示，考虑尚处于初步阶段，尚未做出任何决定，并补充说包括估值在内的细节可能会发生变化。

Amkor的一位代表拒绝置评。

Amkor将加入其他审查其在世界第二大经济体业务的全球公司之列。知情人士上周表示，SK海力士（SK Hynix Inc.）正在探索为其位于中国重庆的工厂引入投资者，以帮助加速增长。时尚品牌Abercrombie & Fitch Co.也在考虑其中国业务部门的[选项](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-04/abercrombie-is-said-to-seek-fresh-backers-for-china-business)，效仿通用磨坊（General Mills Inc.）、星巴克（Starbucks Corp.）和瑞典的Oatly Group AB。

Amkor于2001年在上海设立业务，当时在那里开设了一家封装厂。根据其[网站](https://amkor.com/company-history/#:~:text=May%201.%20May%202004.%20Amkor%20Technology%20acquires,establishes%20two%20semiconductor%20packaging%20plants%20in%20Taiwan.)，三年后，它从国际商业机器公司（International Business Machines Corp.）手中收购了这座中国城市的第二家大型半导体工厂。

7月，Amkor Technology宣布与英伟达（Nvidia Corp.）建立战略合作伙伴关系，以扩展下一代人工智能的先进封装与测试，这是与英伟达达成的价值15亿美元的多年期协议，旨在为下一代人工智能和加速计算平台开发先进的半导体封装和测试技术。此前一个月，台积电（Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co.）和Amkor Technology宣布在美国建立长期合作伙伴关系，以加速先进封装。

## Amkor Is Said to Mull Stake Sale in $1.5 Billion China Unit

### [Technology](https://www.bloomberg.com/technology)

By [Pei Li](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AVZECW3TirQ/pei-li) and [Dong Cao](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AUZmGbfPsvM/dong-cao)August 11, 2026 at 9:56 AM GMT+8*Updated on August 11, 2026 at 11:20 AM GMT+8*

### **Takeaways ** by Bloomberg AI

### **Takeaways**

- Amkor Technology Inc. is considering options including selling a stake in its China business, according to people with knowledge of the matter.
- The company is working with an adviser to help prepare the carveout of the unit and gauge initial interest, and may retain a minority stake in the business.
- Asian investment firms and industry players are likely to be interested in the assets, and considerations are preliminary with no decisions having been made.
[Amkor Technology Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/AMKR%20US%20Equity), a provider of outsourced semiconductor assembly and testing services, is considering options including selling a stake in its China business, according to people with knowledge of the matter.

The Tempe, Arizona-based company is working with an adviser to help prepare the carveout of the unit and gauge initial interest, the people said, asking not to be identified because the deliberations are private. Amkor may retain a minority stake in the business, which could be valued at $1 billion to $1.5 billion, the people said.

Shares of Amkor have gained 34% this year, giving the company a market value of $13.1 billion. Still, they have dropped sharply since their peak in June amid volatility for artificial intelligence-related stocks.

Asian investment firms and industry players are likely to be interested in the assets, the people said. Considerations are preliminary and no decisions have been made, they said, adding that details including valuation could change.

A representative for Amkor declined to comment.

Amkor would join other global firms reviewing their presence in the world’s second-largest economy. SK Hynix Inc. is exploring bringing in an investor for its Chongqing, China-based facility to help accelerate growth, people familiar with the matter [said](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-07/sk-hynix-is-said-to-mull-options-for-3-billion-chongqing-assets) last week. Fashion brand Abercrombie & Fitch Co. is also mulling [options](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-04/abercrombie-is-said-to-seek-fresh-backers-for-china-business) for its local unit, following in the footsteps of General Mills Inc., Starbucks Corp. and Sweden’s Oatly Group AB.

Amkor established its presence in Shanghai in 2001, when it opened a packaging plant there. It acquired a second major semiconductor plant in the Chinese city from [International Business Machines Corp.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/IBM%20US%20Equity) three years later, according to its [website](https://amkor.com/company-history/#:~:text=May%201.%20May%202004.%20Amkor%20Technology%20acquires,establishes%20two%20semiconductor%20packaging%20plants%20in%20Taiwan.).

In July, Amkor Amkor Technology Announces Strategic Partnership with NVIDIA to Expand Advanced Packaging and Test for Next-Generation AI a $1.5 billion multiyear agreement with [Nvidia Corp.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/NVDA%20US%20Equity) to develop advanced semiconductor packaging and test technologies for next-generation AI and accelerated computing platforms. It followed a TSMC and Amkor Technology Announce Long Term Partnership to Accelerate Advanced Packaging in the United States with [Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/2330%20TT%20Equity), which was announced a month earlier.
