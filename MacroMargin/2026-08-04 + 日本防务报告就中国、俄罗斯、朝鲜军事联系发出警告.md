### 中文译文

## 日本防务报告就中国、俄罗斯、朝鲜军事联系发出警告

日本政府在最新防务白皮书中表示，中国、俄罗斯与朝鲜之间日益加强的军事协调正在加剧日本面临的安全风险，并指出中俄轰炸机巡逻日益具有挑衅性，以及莫斯科对朝鲜军事现代化的支持。

这份年度评估日本安全环境的报告指出，去年12月中国和俄罗斯轰炸机进行了一次[巡逻](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-12-10/japan-scrambled-jets-to-watch-chinese-russian-bomber-formation)，在飞越日本南部岛屿后首次朝东京方向飞行。这些飞机在进入日本领空前掉头。

今年6月，中国和俄罗斯又派出轰炸机沿类似航线飞行。

周二发布的白皮书称：“中俄联合轰炸机飞行和海军演习显然意在向我国炫耀武力，对国家安全构成严重关切。”

中国和俄罗斯飞机在日本周边的活动

2026年6月27日（周六），日本航空自卫队发现中国和俄罗斯轰炸机在日本周边进行了远程联合飞行。pic.twitter.com/14yJOGp9EY

——日本统合幕僚监部（@JapanJointStaff）2026年7月1日

自2019年以来，中俄战略轰炸机在日本附近的联合飞行每年至少举行一次，但这些日益强硬的演习正值北京试图[迫使](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-23/china-japan-top-diplomats-hold-first-talks-since-takaichi-row)日本首相高市早苗收回她去年11月在议会就台湾问题发表的言论。

这表明，中国和俄罗斯正更紧密地协调，通过军事演习传递政治信号，东京国防研究所研究员相泽里穗（Riho Aizawa）表示。

“中国和俄罗斯现在的关系是，中国可以在符合自身政治利益的时候，借助俄罗斯的军事力量对日本施压，”相泽在最近的一份[报告](https://www.csis.org/analysis/beyond-deterrence-evolving-china-russia-military-coordination-and-us-japan-alliance)中写道。

今年，日本军方对中俄联合空中威胁的反应更加频繁。日本防卫省7月发布的数据显示，今年第一季度，日本战斗机为监视日本附近飞行的中国或俄罗斯飞机而进行的紧急起飞次数达到三年来的最高水平。

除空中威胁外，日本还对中俄在其群岛周边的海军活动保持警惕。7月，东京表示，观察到一艘中国驱逐舰在太平洋上日本岛屿的专属经济区内用机枪射击。此前，该驱逐舰被看到与一艘俄罗斯护卫舰和另一艘中国军舰在该地区航行。

日本防卫白皮书 来源：彭博

报告称，俄罗斯与朝鲜于2024年签署的军事同盟关系也为平壤的武器开发提供了支持。报告援引朝鲜在导弹开发方面“极其迅速”的进展，包括先进的高超音速导弹。

报告称：“通过与俄罗斯的军事合作，有人担心朝鲜的军事力量在中长期内将会增强。”

尽管没有明确迹象表明中国、俄罗斯和朝鲜之间存在三方军事协调，但日本防卫白皮书指出，去年9月三国领导人曾共同出现在北京的一场阅兵式上。

这份白皮书首次纳入了一个章节，介绍日本为应对日益加剧的地区威胁而建设国防工业的努力。它还强调了无人机和人工智能等技术对于现代战争日益增长的重要性。

## Japan Defense Report Warns On China, Russia, NK Military Links

Growing military coordination between China, Russia and North Korea is heightening security risks for Japan, Tokyo said in its latest defense white paper, citing increasingly provocative Chinese-Russian bomber patrols and Moscow’s support for North Korea’s military modernization.

The report, an annual review of Japan’s security environment, noted a [patrol](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-12-10/japan-scrambled-jets-to-watch-chinese-russian-bomber-formation) by Chinese and Russian bombers in December last year that for the first time traveled in the direction of Tokyo after passing over Japan’s southern islands. The aircraft turned around before reaching Japanese airspace.

China and Russia sent bombers on a similar flight path in June this year.

“Joint Chinese and Russian bomber flights and naval exercises are clearly intended as a show of force against our country and represent a serious concern for national security,” the white paper released Tuesday said.

Joint Chinese-Russian strategic bomber flights near Japan have been held at least once every year since 2019, but the increasingly assertive exercises come as Beijing tries to [force](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-23/china-japan-top-diplomats-hold-first-talks-since-takaichi-row) Japanese Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi to retract remarks she made in parliament last November about Taiwan.

That suggests China and Russia are coordinating more closely on sending a political signal through military exercises, according to Riho Aizawa, a research fellow at the National Institute for Defense Studies in Tokyo.

“China and Russia now have a relationship in which China can apply pressure on Japan with the support of Russian military power at a time that serves China’s political interests,” Aizawa wrote in a recent [report](https://www.csis.org/analysis/beyond-deterrence-evolving-china-russia-military-coordination-and-us-japan-alliance).

This year, Japan’s military has responded more frequently to combined Chinese and Russian air threats. Data released by Japan’s Defense Ministry in July showed that the number of scrambles by Japanese fighter jets to monitor either Chinese or Russian aircraft flying near Japan in the first quarter of the year reached a three-year high.

Besides air threats, Japan is wary of Chinese and Russian naval activity around its archipelago. In July, Tokyo said it observed a Chinese destroyer firing its machine gun inside the exclusive economic zone of a Japanese island in the Pacific Ocean. The destroyer was earlier seen sailing with a Russian frigate and another Chinese warship in the area.

Japan’s defense white paper Source: Bloomberg

Russia’s military alliance with North Korea, signed in 2024, is also providing support for Pyongyang’s weapons development, the report said. It cited North Korea’s “extremely rapid” progress in missile development, including advanced hypersonic missiles.

“Through military cooperation with Russia, there is a concern that North Korea’s military power will increase in the mid- to long-term,” it said.

While there are no clear indications of three-way military coordination between China, Russia and North Korea, the Japanese white paper noted a joint [appearance](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-09-03/xi-s-guest-list-is-shorter-more-authoritarian-than-a-decade-ago) by the countries’ leaders at a military parade in Beijing in September last year.

For the first time, the white paper included a section on Japan’s efforts to build up its defense industry in order to respond to rising regional threats. It also highlighted the growing importance of drones and technology such as AI for modern warfare.
