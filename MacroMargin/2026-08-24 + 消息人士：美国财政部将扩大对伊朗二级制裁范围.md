### 中文译文

## 消息人士：美国财政部将扩大对伊朗二级制裁范围

- 贝森特将于美国东部时间下午1点概述加大对伊朗经济压力的计划
- 财政部将警告各国切断与伊朗的关系，否则将面临二级制裁
- 消息人士称，财政部将使更多行业和交易受到二级制裁
华盛顿8月24日（路透社）——据一位熟悉计划的知情人士透露，美国财政部预计周一将扩大对与伊朗保持商业关系的实体和国家实施二级制裁的范围，特朗普政府正寻求加大对德黑兰的经济压力。

该知情人士表示，此举旨在向各国发出最后警告，要求其切断与伊朗的商业联系，以迫使近六个月的冲突结束，该冲突已封锁霍尔木兹海峡和海湾能源出口。由于未获授权公开谈论此事，该消息人士要求匿名。

美国财政部长斯科特·贝森特预计将于美国东部时间下午1点（格林尼治标准时间17:00）的新闻发布会上公布伊朗行动的更多细节。该消息人士称，贝森特还打算更全面地概述对伊朗的经济施压行动，他和总统唐纳德·特朗普将其描述为“经济D日”，并将向各国明确表示，它们必须站在美国一边，否则关键企业和实体可能被切断与美元金融体系的联系。

贝森特上周将对伊行动称为“史上最严厉制裁”，并表示，加上美国对伊朗港口的海军封锁，这些行动将减少对伊朗发动新的“动能”军事行动的必要性。

美国对伊朗实施制裁已有数十年，其中大部分旨在限制该国的石油收入、航空业、加密货币以及武器部件和其他军事硬件的采购，并切断对伊斯兰革命卫队控制的企业的资助，伊斯兰革命卫队在伊朗经济中占主导地位。

制裁措施将被指定的实体排除在以美元为基础的金融体系之外，但伊朗已成功迅速建立新的幌子公司、其他实体和船只注册，以规避制裁。

熟悉贝森特计划的消息人士表示，该行动可能会揭示更多未来将受到二级制裁的伊朗相关行为类别，从而更容易对代表伊朗政府促进这些交易的行为采取行动。

消息人士没有具体说明可能受到制裁的活动，但表示对于伊朗的某些行业，任何活动，即使在第三国，也可能受到二级制裁。

美国财政部目前批准了伊朗多个行业的交易许可，包括药品和医疗器械、文化艺术交流以及农业交易。

一位政府高级官员表示，预计贝森特将警告，任何剩余的金融生命线，包括通过银行和容忍某些活动的第三国，都必须被切断。

这位同样要求匿名的官员表示，美国财政部已“绘制了伊朗石油走私和规避制裁网络的地图”，并将把这些信息作为警告提供给帮助伊朗规避制裁的国家。

### 对与中国关系的影响

近几个月来，美国财政部已对购买伊朗石油的中国独立“茶壶”炼油厂实施制裁，并扩大了对其运输伊朗油的影子船队的打击范围。

一个更强大的工具是对中国及其他国家为伊朗交易提供便利的银行实施制裁，而特朗普政府迄今为止不愿采取这一步骤，因为目前与中国正处于微妙的贸易休战期。

随着美国总统唐纳德·特朗普和中国国家主席习近平计划于9月下旬在华盛顿会晤，对中国银行实施新制裁可能会破坏去年11月达成的协议的延续前景，该协议旨在维持中国稀土的供应并限制美国关税。

美国对伊朗港口的封锁已经抑制了中国买家购买伊朗原油的报价，[路透社周五报道](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/iranian-oil-offers-chinese-buyers-fall-us-blockade-bites-sources-say-2026-08-21/)，这可能会减轻二级制裁对中国的影响。

## US Treasury to broaden scope of secondary sanctions on Iran, source says

- Bessent to outline plan to increase economic pressure on Iran at 1 p.m. EDT
- Treasury to warn countries to cut ties with Iran or face secondary sanctions
- Source says Treasury to make additional sectors, transactions subject to secondary sanctions
WASHINGTON, Aug 24 (Reuters) - The U.S. Treasury is expected on Monday to broaden the scope of secondary sanctions it can impose on entities and countries that maintain business ties with Iran as the Trump administration seeks to increase economic pressure on Tehran, a source familiar with the plans told Reuters.

The action is aimed at giving a final warning to countries to sever their business ties with Iran in an effort to force an end to the nearly six-month conflict that has bottled up the Strait of Hormuz and Gulf energy exports, said the source, who spoke on condition of anonymity due to not being authorized to speak publicly about the matter.

U.S. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent is expected to announce more details of the Iran actions in a press conference at 1 p.m. EDT (1700 GMT). The source said Bessent also intended to provide a broader overview of an economic pressure campaign against Iran that he and President Donald Trump have described as an "economic D-Day", and would make it clear to countries that they must side with the U.S. or risk having key companies and entities cut off from the dollar-based financial system.

Bessent last week billed the action against Iran as the "toughest sanctions in history," saying that along with the U.S. naval blockade of Iranian ports, they would reduce the need for new "kinetic" military operations against Iran.

The U.S. has maintained sanctions against Iran for decades, most of which have been aimed at curtailing the country's oil revenues, aviation sector, cryptocurrency, procurement of weapons components and other military hardware, and cutting off funding for business enterprises controlled by the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps, a dominant force in the Iranian economy.

The sanctions bar designated entities from the dollar-based financial system, but Iran has been successful in quickly standing up new front companies, other entities and vessel registrations to evade the sanctions.

The source familiar with Bessent's plans said the action is likely to reveal additional categories of Iran-related conduct that would be subject to secondary sanctions in the future, making it easier to take action against those facilitating the transactions on behalf of the Iranian government.

The source did not specify the activities that could be subject to sanctions, but said that for certain Iranian sectors, any activity, even in a third country, could be subject to secondary sanctions.

The Treasury currently approves licenses for transactions in a number of sectors in Iran, including for medicine and medical devices, cultural and arts exchanges and agricultural transactions.

A senior administration official said Bessent is expected to warn that any remaining financial lifelines, including through banks and third countries that have tolerated certain activity, must be shut down.

The official, who also spoke on condition of anonymity, said the Treasury has "mapped Iran's oil-smuggling and sanctions-evasion network" and will present this information to countries helping Iran evade the sanctions as a warning.

### IMPACT ON RELATIONS WITH CHINA

The Treasury in recent months has sanctioned independent Chinese "teapot" refineries for purchases of Iranian oil and expanded its targeting of the shadow fleet of tankers transporting Iranian oil.

A much more powerful tool is the authority to sanction banks in China and other countries that are facilitating transactions with Iran, a step that the Trump administration has so far been unwilling to take amid a delicate trade truce with Beijing.

With President Donald Trump and Chinese President Xi Jinping scheduled to meet in Washington in late September, new sanctions on Chinese banks could sour prospects for extending a deal struck last November to keep Chinese rare earths flowing and cap U.S. tariffs.

The U.S. blockade of Iran's ports has already curbed Chinese offers to purchase Iranian crude, [Reuters reported on Friday](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/iranian-oil-offers-chinese-buyers-fall-us-blockade-bites-sources-say-2026-08-21/), which may lessen the impact of secondary sanctions on China.
