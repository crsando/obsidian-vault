### 中文译文

## 中国研制可打击数千英里外飞机的导弹

中国军方研制出了可发射空对空武器的高超音速滑翔载具，这一进展旨在威胁数千英里外的指挥控制飞机等高价值目标。

这一突破是中国利用其导弹武库——世界最大的导弹武库，拥有[超过3150枚弹道武器](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-29/a-new-dawn-of-missiles-shapes-battlefields-from-ukraine-to-iran)——执行几乎所有军事任务这一更大努力的一部分，据一位直接知情人士透露。与此同时，在与伊朗战争六个月后，美国正[面临关键防空导弹和远程打击武器供应困难](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-22/iran-war-saps-us-military-ahead-of-any-potential-china-conflict)。

该人士还表示，中国还为一些高超音速巡航导弹增加了空对空能力，并在多种导弹中内置了反舰能力。由于信息敏感，该人士要求匿名。

此类武器将对中国冲突期间的美国空中行动构成严重威胁。加油机、空中预警控制飞机，甚至像E-4B“守夜者”这样的空中指挥所，本应在远离战斗的地方运作。

从DF-17导弹上部署的高超音速滑翔载具可飞行约1500英里（2414公里）——略短于从中国到美国领土关岛（一个主要军事基地）的距离。DF-27导弹的最大射程约为5000英里，可将夏威夷纳入射程。

高超音速滑翔载具（HGV）是可以在前往目标途中在大气层内机动的弹头，可延长射程并使拦截复杂化。目前没有其他国家据知使用将HGV与空对空导弹结合起来的武器。

美国空军中国航空航天研究所今年的一份报告未提供细节地表示，中国导弹现代化努力“日益优先考虑对对手作战体系造成协调的、系统级损害的能力。”

诚然，使用本质上为弹道导弹的武器攻击远程飞机是昂贵、困难且危险的。

弹道导弹的飞行时间——最大射程时达20分钟或更长——意味着目标信息必须极其精确。将远处传感器信息传递给导弹的漫长“杀伤链”使得作战行动更容易受到干扰。

弹道导弹还可能被误认为核攻击。这种所谓的“区分问题”使武器的使用复杂化，尤其是在全面战争期间，可能无意中引发核回应。

彭博社5月报道，中国去年[增加导弹产量](http://bloomberg.com/graphics/2026-china-missiles-military-ambitions/)的幅度似乎是自习近平2013年就任国家主席以来最大的，该报道首次梳理了该行业的财务状况。

中国的远程PL-15空空导弹去年参加了实战，当时[印度和巴基斯坦发生短暂冲突](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-10-06/chinese-arms-did-exceptionally-against-india-pakistan-says)。巴基斯坦称使用这些武器击落了多架印度飞机。

五角大楼估计，截至2024年，中国拥有至少3150枚弹道导弹和300枚陆基巡航导弹。与美国2015年开始公布此类估计时相比，分别增加了147%和50%。

远程空空武器被视为未来任何冲突的关键。美国从[RTX公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/RTX%20US%20Equity)的SM-6防空导弹研制了AIM-174B；其确切射程属于机密，但陆基SM-6的射程为250英里或更远。

周六，美国海军表示正在测试RTX旗下[雷神公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/RTN%20US%20Equity)的[AIM-424“恶意”空空导弹](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-23/us-navy-unveils-advanced-air-to-air-missile-with-300-mile-range)，并称其射程超过300英里。

[洛克希德·马丁公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/LMT%20US%20Equity)的[AIM-260导弹](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-07/pentagon-seeks-2-billion-boost-for-secretive-lockheed-missile)已投入生产但尚未服役，预计也将拥有极远射程。欧洲广泛使用的[MBDA导弹系统服务公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/7431124Z:FP)的“流星”导弹采用冲压发动机，可打击100多英里外的目标。

### English Original

## China Develops Missile to Target Planes Thousands of Miles Away

The Chinese military has developed hypersonic glide vehicles that can launch air-to-air weapons, an advancement meant to threaten high-value targets such as command-and-control aircraft thousands of miles away.

The breakthrough is part of a larger effort by China to use its missile arsenal — the world’s largest at [more than 3,150 ballistic weapons](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-29/a-new-dawn-of-missiles-shapes-battlefields-from-ukraine-to-iran) — for nearly every military task, according to a person with direct knowledge of the matter. Meanwhile, the US is [struggling with supplies](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-22/iran-war-saps-us-military-ahead-of-any-potential-china-conflict) of crucial air defense missiles and long-range strike weapons after six months of war with Iran.

China also added air-to-air capabilities to some hypersonic cruise missiles and built anti-ship capabilities into several types of missiles, said the person, who asked not to be identified because of the sensitivity of the information.

Such weapons would pose a serious threat to US air operations during a conflict with China. Support aircraft such as tankers, airborne warning-and-control planes and even flying command posts such as the E-4B Nightwatch are meant to operate far from combat.

A hypersonic glide vehicle deployed from a DF-17 missile could travel about 1,500 miles (2,414 kilometers) — just short of the distance from China to the US territory of Guam, a major military site. The DF-27 missile has a maximum range of about 5,000 miles, putting Hawaii in range.

Hypersonic glide vehicles, or HGVs, are warheads that can maneuver in the atmosphere on their way to a target, extending range and complicating interception. No other country is known to operate weapons that combine an HGV with air-to-air missiles.

A report from the US Air Force’s China Aerospace Studies Institute this year said, without providing details, that China’s missile modernization efforts “increasingly prioritize the ability to inflict coordinated, system–level damage across an adversary’s operational architecture.”

To be sure, using what is essentially a ballistic missile to attack a distant aircraft is costly, difficult and risky.

The travel time of a ballistic missile — 20 minutes or more at maximum range — means that targeting information must be extremely precise. That long “kill chain” of getting distant sensor information to the missile makes operations easier to disrupt.

Ballistic missiles also can be mistaken for a nuclear attack. This so-called discrimination problem complicates the weapon’s use, especially during an all-out war, when it may inadvertently provoke a nuclear response.

China appeared to [ramp up missile production](http://bloomberg.com/graphics/2026-china-missiles-military-ambitions/) last year by the most since Xi Jinping became president in 2013, Bloomberg reported in May, mapping out the sector’s finances for the first time.

China’s long-range PL-15 air-to-air missile saw combat last year, when [India and Pakistan had a brief conflict](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-10-06/chinese-arms-did-exceptionally-against-india-pakistan-says). Pakistan said it used the weapons to shoot down several Indian aircraft.

The Pentagon estimates China had at least 3,150 ballistic missiles and 300 ground-launched cruise missiles as of 2024. Those represent increases of 147% and 50%, respectively, from 2015, when the US started disclosing such estimates.

Long-range air-to-air weapons are considered key to any future conflict. The US developed the AIM-174B from its [RTX Corp.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/RTX%20US%20Equity) SM-6 air defense missile; its exact range is classified but the ground-launched SM-6 has a range of 250 miles or more.

On Saturday, the US Navy said it was testing the [AIM-424 Malice air-to-air missile](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-23/us-navy-unveils-advanced-air-to-air-missile-with-300-mile-range) from RTX unit [Raytheon Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/RTN%20US%20Equity), which it said had a range of more than 300 miles.

The [Lockheed Martin Corp.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/LMT%20US%20Equity) [AIM-260 missile](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-07/pentagon-seeks-2-billion-boost-for-secretive-lockheed-missile), which is in production but not yet in service, is also expected to have extremely long range. [MBDA Missile Systems Services SAS](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/7431124Z:FP)’ Meteor, widely used in Europe, is ramjet-powered and can hit targets more than 100 miles away.
