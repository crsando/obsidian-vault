## 消息人士称，长鑫存储计划在北京建设第二家芯片工厂并洽谈其资金问题

![CXMT chipmaker production facility in Beijing](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3b181cc90b14811d9becee62520a771e/3b181cc9-0b14-81bb-b887-e865ce600781-0c93997e5da61e3b9219dc1901b13260.jpg)

中国顶级内存芯片制造商长鑫存储的公司标志出现在其位于中国北京的生产设施外墙上，2026年7月29日。路透社/Maxim Shemetov [购买许可权利，打开新标签页](https://www.reutersconnect.com/item/cxmt-chipmaker-production-facility-in-beijing/dGFnOnJldXRlcnMuY29tLDIwMjY6bmV3c21sX1JDMkhOTUE5TEVaUw%3D%3D/?utm_medium=rcom-article-media&utm_campaign=rcom-rcp-lead)

- 摘要
- 公司
- 消息人士称，第二座12英寸晶圆厂将建在北京亦庄地区
- 消息人士称，该工厂将建在长鑫存储已有DRAM工厂的地方
- 消息人士称，长鑫存储为北京工厂寻求至少6000万元人民币的支持
8月3日（路透社）——长鑫存储 [(688825.SS)，打开新标签页](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/688825.SS)，中国市值最大的芯片制造商，正考虑在北京建设第二家内存芯片工厂，并与当地政府支持的一个科技制造中心进行融资谈判，两位知情人士称。

此举正值长鑫存储在全球芯片短缺（由AI基础设施支出驱动）背景下寻求提高产量。这凸显了中国地方政府之间争夺长鑫存储的竞争加剧，这家内存芯片制造商在7月份进行[86亿美元首次公开募股](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/china-memory-chipmaker-cxmt-set-shanghai-debut-after-asias-biggest-ipo-2026-07-26/)后，正寻求大规模扩张，这是中国内地半导体行业有史以来规模最大的上市。

路透社此前[报道](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/chinas-cxmt-wins-3-billion-memory-supply-deal-with-tencent-sources-say-2026-06-29/)称，长鑫存储正在上海和合肥建设新工厂，并正与其它地方当局商讨另一座工厂事宜。这些项目全面投产后，可能使其产能翻番，达到每月超过60万片晶圆。
消息人士称，新的北京12英寸工厂将建在亦庄，位于北京市中心东南约20公里（12英里）处，长鑫存储已在当地运营一座生产动态随机存取存储器（DRAM）芯片的晶圆厂。由于计划尚未公开，他们拒绝透露身份。
[长鑫存储](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/what-is-cxmt-how-did-it-become-chinas-dram-champion-2026-07-27/)正在寻求从该开发区管理机构（又称北京经济技术开发区）获得至少6000万元人民币（约890万美元）的支持，其他国有科技公司也表示有意参与融资，他们表示。

消息人士称，谈判尚处于早期阶段，任何融资方案的规模和结构都可能发生变化。目前尚不清楚资金是直接来自开发区行政机构还是通过其投资平台提供。

长鑫存储和北京市政府均未回应置评请求。

拟建工厂的规划产能和总投资额尚未得知。建设一座能够生产先进DRAM芯片的晶圆厂通常耗资超过100亿美元。

上述人士称，长鑫存储在合肥运营两座12英寸DRAM晶圆厂，在北京运营一座，每座产能约为每月10万片晶圆。

“合肥模式”
这些讨论是在长鑫存储上周股市首次亮相之前开始的，此次上市为该公司提供了新的资本，以在由AI基础设施、数据中心和消费电子产品[需求](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/chinas-cxmt-wins-3-billion-memory-supply-deal-with-tencent-sources-say-2026-06-29/)驱动的存储芯片上行周期中推进扩张。自那以来，该股已上涨13%。

该公司已成为北京推动建设自主芯片产业、缩小与美国在AI等战略技术领域差距的关键支柱，当前两大超级大国之间的科技竞争十分激烈。

尽管长鑫存储是全球第四大DRAM生产商，但其规模仍远小于三星电子[(005930.KS), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/005930.KS)、SK海力士[(000660.KS), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/000660.KS)和美光[(MU.O), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/MU.O)，根据Counterpoint Research的数据，这三家公司第一季度合计全球市场份额接近90%。

然而，路透社上月报道，在中国国内，长鑫存储日益增强的主导地位使其能够提高对华为等客户的价格。

该公司的崛起与“合肥模式”紧密相关，即安徽省会利用国有资金培育战略性科技公司。

另一位直接知情人士表示，北京和上海也为长鑫存储提供了资金和其他支持，因为这些城市希望从该公司增长带来的经济与战略利益中分得更大份额。上海市政府未回应置评请求。

根据公司记录，长鑫存储位于北京的晶圆厂由长鑫集电运营，成立于2020年，并获得了亦庄开发区政府背景的投资机构北京亦庄国际投资发展有限公司及其关联方北京亦庄科技的投资。

该北京开发区是科技和芯片公司的制造基地，包括芯片代工企业中芯国际[(0981.HK), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/0981.HK)、芯片设备制造商北方华创[(002371.SZ), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/002371.SZ)和智能手机及电动汽车制造商小米[(1810.HK), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/1810.HK)。

该地区还致力于打造机器人和人工智能中心。去年，这里举办了主办方所称的全球首个人形机器人半程马拉松，旨在推广和测试具身人工智能技术。

## CXMT plans second chip plant in Beijing and is in talks on its funding, sources say

A logo of China's top memory chipmaker CXMT appears on the facade of company's production facility, in Beijing, China, July 29, 2026. REUTERS/Maxim Shemetov [Purchase Licensing Rights, opens new tab](https://www.reutersconnect.com/item/cxmt-chipmaker-production-facility-in-beijing/dGFnOnJldXRlcnMuY29tLDIwMjY6bmV3c21sX1JDMkhOTUE5TEVaUw%3D%3D/?utm_medium=rcom-article-media&utm_campaign=rcom-rcp-lead)

- Summary
- Companies
- Second 12-inch wafer fab would be built in Beijing's Yizhuang area, sources say
- Plant would be built where CXMT already operates DRAM fab, sources say
- CXMT seeks at least 60 million yuan in support for Beijing plant, sources say
Aug 3 (Reuters) - CXMT [(688825.SS), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/688825.SS), China's largest chipmaker by market value, is considering building a second memory-chip plant in Beijing and is in financing talks with a tech manufacturing hub backed by the local government, two sources familiar with the matter said.

The move ‌comes as CXMT seeks to boost production amid a global chip shortage driven by AI infrastructure spending. It highlights intensifying competition among Chinese local governments to attract CXMT, as the memory chipmaker pursues a major expansion following its [$8.6 billion IPO](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/china-memory-chipmaker-cxmt-set-shanghai-debut-after-asias-biggest-ipo-2026-07-26/) last month, the largest mainland Chinese semiconductor listing on record.

Reuters previously [reported](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/chinas-cxmt-wins-3-billion-memory-supply-deal-with-tencent-sources-say-2026-06-29/) that CXMT was building new plants in Shanghai and Hefei and was in discussions with authorities elsewhere about another facility. Those projects, when fully operational, could double its capacity to more than 600,000 wafers per month.
The new Beijing 12-inch plant would be built in Yizhuang, about 20 km (12 miles) southeast of central Beijing, where CXMT already operates a ⁠fab producing dynamic random access memory (DRAM) chips, the sources said. They declined to be identified as the plans are not public.
[CXMT](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/what-is-cxmt-how-did-it-become-chinas-dram-champion-2026-07-27/) is seeking at least 60 million yuan ($8.9 million) in support from the development zone's governing body, also known as the Beijing Economic-Technological Development Area, and other state-owned tech companies have also expressed interest in participating in the financing, they said.

The talks are at an early stage and the size and structure of any funding package could change, the sources said. It was not immediately clear whether the funding would come directly from the development zone's administrative authority or through its investment vehicles.

CXMT and the Beijing municipal government did not respond to requests for comment.

The planned capacity and total investment for the proposed fab were not immediately known. Building a fab that can produce leading-edge DRAM chips usually costs more than $10 billion.

CXMT operates two 12-inch DRAM fabs in Hefei and one in Beijing, each with capacity of about 100,000 wafers per month, the people said.

THE 'HEFEI MODEL'
The discussions began before CXMT's stock market debut last week, which provided ‌the company ⁠with fresh capital for an expansion drive during a memory-chip upcycle fueled by [demand](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/chinas-cxmt-wins-3-billion-memory-supply-deal-with-tencent-sources-say-2026-06-29/) from AI infrastructure, data centres and consumer electronics. The shares have since gained 13%.

The company has become a key pillar of Beijing’s drive to build a self-sufficient chip industry and narrow the gap with the U.S. in strategic technologies such as AI amid a fierce tech rivalry between the two superpowers.

Although CXMT is the world's fourth-largest DRAM producer, it remains far smaller than Samsung Electronics [(005930.KS), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/005930.KS), SK Hynix [(000660.KS), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/000660.KS) and Micron [(MU.O), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/MU.O), whose combined global market share approached 90% in the first quarter, according to data from ⁠Counterpoint Research.

Within China, however, CXMT's growing dominance has enabled it to raise prices for customers such as Huawei, Reuters [reported](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/chinas-memory-chip-makers-ride-ai-boom-new-power-us-scrutiny-2026-07-24/) last month.

The company's rise has been closely linked to the "[Hefei model](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/chipmaker-cxmt-debut-spotlights-chinas-state-funded-path-tech-power-2026-07-23/)," under which the capital of Anhui province has used state funding to nurture strategic technology companies.

Beijing and Shanghai have also provided CXMT with funding and other support, according to a separate person with direct knowledge of ⁠the matter, as the cities seek a larger share of the economic and strategic benefits generated by the company's growth. The Shanghai municipal government did not respond to a request for comment.

CXMT's Beijing-based fab, operated by Changxin Jidian, was founded in 2020 and received funding from E-Town Capital, a state-backed investment arm of the Yizhuang development zone, and its ⁠affiliate Beijing E-Town Technology, according to corporate records.

The Beijing development area is a manufacturing base for technology and chip companies, including contract chipmaker SMIC [(0981.HK), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/0981.HK), chip equipment maker Naura Technology [(002371.SZ), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/002371.SZ) and smartphone and electric-vehicle maker Xiaomi [(1810.HK), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/1810.HK).

The area is also positioning itself as a hub for robotics and AI. Last year, it hosted what organisers described as the world's first humanoid robot half-marathon, part of an effort to promote and test embodied-AI technologies.
