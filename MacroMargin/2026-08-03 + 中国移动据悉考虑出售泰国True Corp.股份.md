### 中文译文

## 中国移动据悉考虑出售泰国True Corp.股份

据知情人士透露，[中国移动有限公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/941%20HK%20Equity)正考虑出售其在泰国电信公司[True Corp.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/TRUE%20TB%20Equity)所持的少数股权。

上述因讨论私下事宜而要求匿名的知情人士称，这家中国移动和互联网公司正在与一位顾问合作，以试探潜在买家对其所持True股份的初步兴趣。

True股价在曼谷一度下跌4.9%，创一个多月来最大盘中跌幅，随后收复部分失地。该股今年已累计上涨28%，公司市值约为145亿美元。按此水平计算，中国移动所持股份价值约为11亿美元。

根据泰国集团最新[年报](https://investor.truecorp.co.th/misc/AR/true-ar2025-en.pdf)，中国移动持有True约7.8%的股份。

知情人士称，审议仍在进行中，不一定会达成交易。中国移动的代表未立即回应置评请求。

True投资者关系主管Naureen Quayum表示，中国移动已告知公司，打算长期担任这家泰国运营商的股东，无意退出。

True于2023年与挪威[Telenor ASA](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/TEL%20NO%20Equity)的泰国子公司[合并](https://www.telenor.com/media/newsroom/press-releases/telenor-announces-the-completion-of-the-amalgamation-of-dtac-and-true-in-thailand/)。今年早些时候，Telenor [同意](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-01-22/telenor-sells-stake-in-thailand-s-true-to-arise-for-3-9-billion)以39亿美元的总价将其在True的股份出售给Arise Digital Technology Co.

True拥有超过4800万移动用户，同时还经营家庭宽带和有线电视业务。

中国移动是全球用户数量最多的无线运营商，也是中国移动通信、互联网和数字基础设施服务的主要提供商。这家国有电信巨头拥有约[10亿](https://iconnect.cmi.chinamobile.com/en/product/i-connect-data)移动用户和数亿宽带用户。

### English Original

## China Mobile Is Said to Mull Stake Sale in Thailand’s True

[China Mobile Ltd.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/941%20HK%20Equity) is considering selling its minority stake in Thailand’s telecommunications company [True Corp.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/TRUE%20TB%20Equity), according to people familiar with the situation.

The Chinese mobile and internet firm is working with an adviser to sound out initial interest from prospective buyers in its stake in True, the people said, asking not to be identified discussing a private matter.

True shares fell as much as 4.9% in Bangkok, the biggest intraday drop in more than a month, before paring some losses. The stock has gained 28% this year, giving the company a market value of about $14.5 billion. At that level, China Mobile’s holding would be worth about $1.1 billion.

China Mobile has roughly a 7.8% stake in True, according to the Thai group’s latest [annual report](https://investor.truecorp.co.th/misc/AR/true-ar2025-en.pdf).

Deliberations are ongoing and may not lead to a transaction, the people said. A representative for China Mobile didn’t immediately respond to requests seeking comment.

Naureen Quayum, head of investor relations at True, said China Mobile has told the company it intends to be a long-term shareholder of the Thai carrier and has no intention of exiting its position.

True [merged](https://www.telenor.com/media/newsroom/press-releases/telenor-announces-the-completion-of-the-amalgamation-of-dtac-and-true-in-thailand/) with the Thai unit of Norway’s [Telenor ASA](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/TEL%20NO%20Equity) in 2023. Earlier this year, Telenor [agreed to sell](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-01-22/telenor-sells-stake-in-thailand-s-true-to-arise-for-3-9-billion) its stake in True to Arise Digital Technology Co. for a total value of $3.9 billion.

True has more than 48 million mobile subscribers and also operates home broadband and cable television.

China Mobile is the world’s largest wireless carrier by subscribers and the country’s primary provider of mobile, internet, and digital infrastructure services. The state-owned telecommunications giant has about [1 billion](https://iconnect.cmi.chinamobile.com/en/product/i-connect-data) mobile subscribers and hundreds of millions of broadband users.
