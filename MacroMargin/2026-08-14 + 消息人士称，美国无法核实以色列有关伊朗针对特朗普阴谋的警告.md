### 中文译文

## 消息人士称，美国无法核实以色列有关伊朗针对特朗普阴谋的警告

![U.S. President Donald Trump disembarks Air Force One at Joint Base Andrews](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3bc81cc90b1481b39072f0c1a1a61f5c/3bc81cc9-0b14-813c-b8c7-c75d03e9a1f1-33fb12fdc5e4025e99b2e15fde497e8c.jpg)

*2026年8月11日，美国总统唐纳德·特朗普在马里兰州美国安德鲁斯联合基地走下空军一号。路透社/凯莉·库珀*

### 摘要

- 消息人士称，以色列的提示包括据称针对特朗普的狙击手、刀具和肩射导弹威胁
- 消息人士称，特勤局在土耳其之行期间将特朗普从空军一号转移到另一架飞机
- 消息人士称，中央情报局对以色列有关伊朗威胁的部分情报评估为低可信度
华盛顿，8月13日（路透社）——（这篇8月13日的报道已重新提交，以修正第15段消息来源中的乱码）

据一位现任美国官员和两位前官员称，美国在过去一年中多次收到以色列的警告，称伊朗意图暗杀总统唐纳德·特朗普，包括在土耳其[秘密的空军一号策略](https://www.reuters.com/business/aerospace-defense/trump-flew-secretly-turkey-due-iranian-threat-washington-post-reports-2026-08-11/)之前，美国情报官员无法独立核实这些警告。

消息人士称，这些警告已传递给美国情报机构，在某些情况下由以色列官员直接转达给白宫高级官员，其中包括可能用狙击手射杀特朗普，或招募某人在大型公共活动中持刀袭击他的阴谋。消息人士称，这些提示始于2025年6月为期12天的战争前夕，并在美国决定2月对伊朗开战之前加强。

最详细的警告出现在7月份[在安卡拉举行的北约峰会](https://www.reuters.com/business/aerospace-defense/nato-leaders-meet-ankara-after-trump-rekindles-disputes-over-iran-greenland-2026-07-08/)之前，美国官员和另一位知情人士表示。以色列官员向白宫通报了情报，显示伊朗可能试图在特朗普[乘坐空军一号](https://www.reuters.com/business/aerospace-defense/credible-missile-threat-led-secret-aircraft-switch-trump-source-says-2026-08-12/)前往峰会时暗杀他，可能使用肩扛式导弹。

美国情报界长期以来评估认为，伊朗希望就特朗普2020年击杀伊朗军事指挥官[卡西姆·苏莱曼尼](https://www.reuters.com/article/world/soleimani-was-irans-celebrity-soldier-spearhead-in-middle-east-idUSKBN1Z20C3/)进行报复。但官员们表示，自去年初以来，中央情报局一直无法证实以色列情报部门分享的一些针对特朗普人身安全的具体威胁。

就北约事件而言，一名土耳其官员表示，该国情报机构也没有任何证据证实以色列关于特朗普人身安全受到威胁的说法，东道国已将此评估与美国分享。

尽管缺乏证实，白宫官员和特勤局出于极度谨慎，已采取措施减轻威胁，包括在土耳其之行期间进行的一次非同寻常的欺骗行动，涉及[将特朗普从空军一号转移到另一架飞机](https://www.reuters.com/business/aerospace-defense/trump-says-his-plane-faced-greater-risk-flight-change-2026-08-12/)以离开该国。

这一事件凸显出以色列情报部门正在如何影响特朗普政府关于伊朗的决策，而此时美国与德黑兰关系紧张，美以情报机构对伊朗整体威胁的评估也存在更广泛分歧。

今年早些时候，美国情报机构评估认为，伊朗并未积极[建造核武器](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/first-iaea-report-irans-nuclear-programme-since-february-shows-little-change-2026-06-04/)，尽管以色列坚称德黑兰构成紧迫威胁。尽管美国情报界提出了这些报告，特朗普还是在2月份推进了对伊朗的打击。路透社此前报道，特朗普在发动军事行动前，还考虑了以色列分享的情报，即德黑兰曾试图暗杀他。

以色列大使馆发言人对以色列利用情报影响美国对伊朗政策的说法予以否认。

“那些声称以色列试图通过所谓的情报共享影响美国政策的人，如果符合他们自己的议程，他们同样会指责以色列隐瞒关键信息，”发言人说。

白宫和中央情报局均拒绝对此置评。

特勤局发言人安东尼·古列尔米拒绝讨论具体情报事务，但表示，在最近发生暗杀未遂事件以及针对总统的威胁增加之际，该机构仍将重点放在特朗普的安全上。

他说：“美国特勤局持续分析广泛的信息，以实时指导我们的保护行动，并覆盖我们所保护的每一个地点。”

### 紧密联盟

长期以来，美国在中东事务上严重依赖以色列的情报，尤其是涉及伊朗的情报。

两名美国前官员表示，这些情报通常通过正式渠道流向中央情报局或国家安全局，随后进行分析。

这些官员表示，美国情报机构往往无法核实以色列的情报，但会利用其他形式的信息对其真实性和准确性作出判断。

据一位前官员和另一位知情人士透露，在美军对伊朗发动的两次军事进攻过程中，中央情报局曾多次评估认为，其对以色列有关伊朗所构成威胁（包括任何暗杀特朗普的计划）的情报“信心不足”。

目前尚不清楚这些评估在政府内部流传的范围有多广。

美国国家安全局（NSA）拒绝置评。

两名美国前官员向路透社描述了去年发生的几个实例：以色列高级官员和代表直接致电白宫（包括打到战情室），向特朗普政府高级官员通报伊朗威胁。

这种直接接触发生之际，特朗普政府内部的情报共享正陷入破裂。

上述前官员称，去年，在中央情报局与国家情报总监办公室之间紧张加剧的情况下，中情局停止了与国家情报委员会的联合评估工作。

该委员会是由国家情报总监办公室（ODNI）监管的美国情报界精英分析小组，负责产出反映更广泛情报界观点的报告，中央情报局是其评估的最大贡献者。

官员们和另一位知情人士补充说，国家情报委员会近期对伊朗的评估减少，部分原因是国家情报总监办公室最近裁员。

由 Erin Banco 在华盛顿报道；Humeyra Pamuk 补充报道；Colleen Jenkins 和 Nick Zieminski 编辑。

### English Original

## US could not verify Israeli warnings of Iran plots against Trump, sources say

*U.S. President Donald Trump disembarks Air Force One at Joint Base Andrews, Maryland, U.S., August 11, 2026. REUTERS/Kylie Cooper*

### Summary

- Israeli tips included alleged sniper, knife and shoulder-launched missile threats against Trump, sources say
- Secret Service moved Trump from Air Force One to another aircraft during Turkey trip, sources say
- CIA assessed low confidence in some Israeli intelligence on Iranian threats, sources say
WASHINGTON, Aug 13 (Reuters) - (This Aug 13 story has been refiled to fix the garble in sourcing in paragraph 15)

The U.S. has received several warnings from Israel over the past year that Iran intended to assassinate President Donald Trump, including before [a secret Air Force One ruse](https://www.reuters.com/business/aerospace-defense/trump-flew-secretly-turkey-due-iranian-threat-washington-post-reports-2026-08-11/) in Turkey, which U.S. intelligence officials could not independently verify, according to a current U.S. official and two former officials.

The warnings, passed to U.S. intelligence agencies and in some cases relayed directly by Israeli officials to senior White House officials, included possible plots to shoot Trump with a sniper or recruit someone to attack him with a knife at a large public event, the sources said. The tips began in the lead-up to the 12-day war in June 2025 and intensified prior to the U.S. decision to go to [war in Iran](https://www.reuters.com/world/iran/) in February, the sources said.

The most detailed warning came ahead of [the NATO summit in Ankara](https://www.reuters.com/business/aerospace-defense/nato-leaders-meet-ankara-after-trump-rekindles-disputes-over-iran-greenland-2026-07-08/) in July, the U.S. official and the other person familiar with the matter said. Israeli officials briefed the White House about intelligence that indicated Iran may try to kill Trump, potentially with a shoulder-launched missile, while [he traveled on Air Force One](https://www.reuters.com/business/aerospace-defense/credible-missile-threat-led-secret-aircraft-switch-trump-source-says-2026-08-12/) for the summit, the officials said.

The U.S. intelligence community has long assessed that Iran wants to retaliate for Trump’s killing of Iranian military commander [Qassem Soleimani](https://www.reuters.com/article/world/soleimani-was-irans-celebrity-soldier-spearhead-in-middle-east-idUSKBN1Z20C3/) in 2020. But the Central Intelligence Agency has not been able to corroborate some of the specific threats against Trump's life shared by Israeli intelligence since early last year, the officials said.

In the case of the NATO episode, a Turkish official said the country's intelligence agencies also did not have any evidence to corroborate the Israeli claim about the threat to Trump's life, an assessment the host country shared with Americans.

Despite the lack of verification, White House officials and the Secret Service have taken steps to mitigate the threats out of an abundance of caution, including an extraordinary deception operation during the Turkey trip that involved [moving Trump from Air Force One to a different aircraft](https://www.reuters.com/business/aerospace-defense/trump-says-his-plane-faced-greater-risk-flight-change-2026-08-12/) for his flight out of the country.

That episode underscores how Israeli intelligence is influencing the Trump administration's decision-making about Iran at a time of heightened tensions with Tehran — and broader disagreement between U.S. and Israeli intelligence assessments on Iran's threat overall.

Earlier this year, American intelligence agencies assessed that Iran was not actively [building a nuclear weapon](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/first-iaea-report-irans-nuclear-programme-since-february-shows-little-change-2026-06-04/), even as Israel argued Tehran posed an urgent threat. Despite the reports from the U.S. intelligence community, Trump moved forward with strikes in Iran in February. Trump also took into consideration intelligence shared by Israel that Tehran had tried to assassinate him before launching the military campaign, Reuters previously reported.

A spokesperson for the Israeli embassy disputed that Israel was using its intelligence to influence U.S. policy on Iran.

"The same people who claim that Israel is trying to influence U.S. policy through alleged intelligence sharing would just as soon accuse Israel of concealing critical information if it benefited their own agenda," the spokesperson said.

The White House and CIA both declined comment.

Secret Service spokesman Anthony Guglielmi declined to discuss specific intelligence matters but said the agency remains focused on Trump's security amid recent assassination attempts and an increase in threats against the president.

"The U.S. Secret Service continuously analyzes a wide range of information to guide our protective operations in real time and across every location we secure," he said.

### CLOSE ALLIANCE

The U.S. has long relied heavily on Israeli intelligence on Middle East matters, particularly as they relate to Iran.

The intelligence typically flows to the CIA or the National Security Agency through formal channels and is subsequently analyzed, two former U.S. officials said.

The U.S. intelligence agencies often cannot verify Israeli intelligence but will use other forms of information to make judgment calls on its authenticity and accuracy, the officials said.

In several instances over the course of the two U.S. military offensives in Iran, the CIA assessed it had "low confidence" in the Israeli intelligence about the threats posed by Iran, including any plans to assassinate Trump, according to one former official and another person familiar with the matter.

It is unclear how widely those assessments have been circulated inside the administration.

The NSA declined to comment.

Two of the former U.S. officials described to Reuters several instances in which senior Israeli officials and representatives over the last year called the White House, including in the Situation Room, to brief senior Trump officials on Iranian threats directly.

The direct engagement comes amid a breakdown in U.S. intelligence sharing inside the Trump administration.

Last year, amid increased tension with the Office of the Director of National Intelligence, the CIA stopped working on joint assessments with the National Intelligence Council, the former officials said.

The council is the U.S. intelligence community's elite analytical group overseen by ODNI to turn out reports reflecting the view of the broader intelligence community, with the CIA being the largest contributor to its assessments.

Fewer recent Iran assessments by the NIC have stemmed partly from recent layoffs at ODNI, the officials and another person familiar with the matter added.
