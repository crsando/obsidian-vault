### 中文译文

## 特朗普动摇美韩关系之际，中国最高特使将访问首尔

中国外交部长王毅将从周三起对韩国进行为期两天的访问，加强与中国首尔的接触，而此时美国总统唐纳德·特朗普正在对华盛顿在该地区的可靠性提出质疑。

韩国外交部周二在一份[声明](https://www.mofa.go.kr/www/brd/m_4080/view.do?seq=377501)中表示，王毅将会见韩国外长赵显，讨论朝鲜问题、双边及全球问题。此次访问是在首尔部署美国导弹防御系统（北京方面反对）引发多年摩擦后，恢复高层交流的后续。

此次访问是在特朗普命令五角大楼“大幅减少”与韩国的联合军演之后进行的，理由是军演成本以及他与朝鲜领导人金正恩的“非常良好的关系”。这一宣布似乎让首尔感到意外。

中国外交部周二在北京例行记者会上没有证实此次访问，仅表示，对于这次行程的提问，如果有相关信息将予以发布。

发言人林剑表示：“中国和韩国是重要的邻国，我们关系良好，我们也保持着各层级的正常沟通。”

据李在明总统办公室消息，王毅此次近五年来首次访韩，将会见总统李在明。韩国期待会谈有助于推进两国关系恢复，并为11月在中国南方城市深圳举行的亚太经合组织[峰会](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-11-01/china-picks-tech-hub-of-shenzhen-to-host-apec-summit-in-2026)前的高层交流做准备。

亚洲集团中国区总监韩林表示：“王毅到来对北京而言是一个异常有利的时机：华盛顿正对平壤展现灵活性，同时又在首尔制造不确定性。”

他说：“中国可以利用这一间隙增强外交影响力。”他还表示，韩国的技术和供应链也为北京提供了加强接触的强烈动力。

一项涉及约18000名韩国军队的为期10天的演习于周一开始，重点是应对拥核朝鲜的威胁。平壤将此类演习视为入侵预演，过去一周已发射多枚导弹。

中国此前表示，在特朗普发布社交媒体帖子后，中方将欢迎华盛顿与平壤恢复对话。

“我们注意到了相关报道。维护半岛和平稳定，推动半岛问题政治解决，符合各方的利益，”中国发言人林剑周一表示。

不过，彭博经济分析师亚当·法拉尔表示，首尔方面的优先事项（包括与金正恩恢复会谈）取得进展的可能性不大。

“一个关键问题是，北京是否愿意超越陈词滥调，采取切实措施试图重振两国关系，包括允许韩国文化内容重新进入中国市场，”他说。

中国对韩国娱乐业（从K-pop演唱会到电视剧和电影）的非正式禁令大约在十年前实施，此前韩国决定部署美国的导弹防御系统，北京视该系统为安全威胁。

## China’s Top Envoy to Visit Seoul as Trump Shakes US-Korea Ties

Chinese Foreign Minister Wang Yi will visit South Korea for two days from Wednesday, stepping up engagement with Seoul as President Donald Trump sows doubts about Washington’s reliability in the region.

Wang will meet South Korean Foreign Minister Cho Hyun to discuss North Korea and bilateral and global issues, South Korea’s Foreign Ministry said in a [statement](https://www.mofa.go.kr/www/brd/m_4080/view.do?seq=377501) Tuesday. The trip follows renewed high-level exchanges after years of friction over Seoul’s [deployment](https://www.bloomberg.com/politics/articles/2017-03-06/explaining-thaad-and-why-it-so-bothers-china-quicktake-q-a) of a US missile defense system opposed by Beijing.

The visit comes after Trump ordered the Pentagon to “substantially reduce” joint exercises with South Korea, citing their cost and his “very good relationship” with North Korean leader Kim Jong Un. The announcement appeared to catch Seoul by surprise.

China’s Foreign Ministry didn’t confirm the visit in a regular press briefing in Beijing on Tuesday, saying only that it will release relevant information if there’s any in response to a question about the trip.

“China and South Korea are important neighboring countries. We have good relations and we are also have normal communication at various levels,” spokesman Lin Jian said.

Wang’s first trip to South Korea in nearly five years will include meetings with President Lee Jae Myung, according to Lee’s office. South Korea expects the talks to advance efforts to restore relations and prepare for high-level exchanges ahead of November’s Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation [summit](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-11-01/china-picks-tech-hub-of-shenzhen-to-host-apec-summit-in-2026) in the southern Chinese city of Shenzhen.

“Wang arrives at an unusually favorable moment for Beijing: Washington is signaling flexibility toward Pyongyang while simultaneously creating uncertainty in Seoul,” said Han Lin, China country director at The Asia Group.

“China can use that gap to bolster diplomatic influence,” he said, adding that South Korea’s technology and supply chains also give Beijing a strong incentive to engage.

A 10-day exercise involving about 18,000 South Korean troops began Monday, focused on threats from nuclear-armed North Korea. Pyongyang, which regards such drills as invasion rehearsals, has launched multiple missiles over the past week.

China previously said it would welcome renewed dialogue between Washington and Pyongyang following Trump’s social post.

“We have noticed relevant reports. Maintaining peace and stability in the peninsula and promoting the political solution of the peninsula issue fits the interest of all sides,” Lin, the Chinese spokesman, said Monday.

Still, Bloomberg Economics analyst Adam Farrar said progress on Seoul’s priorities, including reviving talks with Kim, was unlikely.

“A key question will be whether Beijing is willing to move beyond platitudes and take tangible steps to try and renew the relationship including allowing South Korean cultural content to reenter the Chinese market,” he said.

China’s informal ban on Korean entertainment — from K-pop concerts to TV dramas and films — was imposed about a decade ago after South Korea’s decision to deploy the US missile defense system, which Beijing views as a security threat.
