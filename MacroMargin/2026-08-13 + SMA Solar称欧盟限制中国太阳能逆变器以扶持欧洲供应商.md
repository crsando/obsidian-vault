### 中文译文

## SMA Solar称欧盟限制中国太阳能逆变器以扶持欧洲供应商

路透社8月13日电 - SMA Solar首席执行官周四表示，欧盟禁止将公共资金用于从“高风险”国家采购公用事业级太阳能逆变器，此举将促使市场转向欧洲供应商。

这个由27国组成的欧盟于5月[实施](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/eu-curbs-chinese-inverters-risk-slowing-solar-rollout-over-security-fears-2026-06-11/)了这项禁令，主要影响中国制造的产品，理由是担心来自所谓“高风险”国家的联网逆变器可能被用来扰乱欧洲电网。

首席执行官于尔根·赖纳特（Jürgen Reinert）对路透社表示：“在所有大型项目中，大约五分之一由欧盟资助，在这些项目上，对中国、朝鲜、俄罗斯、伊朗的限制当然会对我们有所帮助。”他估计，SMA Solar可能在欧洲公用事业级市场获得约10%的份额。

近年来，以华为和阳光电源为首的中国制造商供应了欧洲约70%的逆变器，导致该地区对其电力供应中快速增长的部分依赖外国设备。

根据路透社的[计算](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/eu-curbs-chinese-inverters-risk-slowing-solar-rollout-over-security-fears-2026-06-11/)，基于目前的部署水平，这项全欧盟范围的禁令将影响至少14吉瓦的新增太阳能装机容量。

赖纳特表示：“对于欧洲融资项目，将会出现从中国企业向欧洲企业的转变，但这仍需要一些时间。”他预计，到2027年初，情况将变得更加明朗。

他补充道，这家德国光伏和电池储能设备供应商已经与之前曾从中国公司购买逆变器的新客户进行了“大量讨论”。

赖纳特表示，SMA Solar已减少对中国的依赖，目前其大型逆变器的零部件有2%至4%来自中国，并认为潜在报复性措施造成供应链中断的风险有限。

### English Original

## EU curbs on Chinese solar inverters to bolster European suppliers, SMA Solar says

Aug 13 (Reuters) - A European Union ban on public funding for utility-scale solar inverters from "high-risk" countries is set to shift the market towards European suppliers, SMA Solar's chief executive said on Thursday.

The 27-country bloc [imposed](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/eu-curbs-chinese-inverters-risk-slowing-solar-rollout-over-security-fears-2026-06-11/) the ban in May, mainly affecting Chinese-made products, citing fears that internet-connected inverters supplied by what it called "high-risk" countries could be used to disrupt Europe's power grid.

"Roughly a fifth of all bigger projects are EU-funded and on those, the restriction on China, North Korea, Russia, Iran, of course will help us," CEO Jürgen Reinert told Reuters, estimating that SMA Solar could gain around 10% share in the utility-scale market in Europe.

Chinese manufacturers, led by Huawei and Sungrow, supplied about 70% of Europe’s inverters in recent years, leaving the region reliant on foreign equipment for a fast-growing share of its electricity supply.

Based on current deployment levels, the EU-wide ban would affect at least 14 gigawatts of new solar capacity, according to Reuters [calculations](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/eu-curbs-chinese-inverters-risk-slowing-solar-rollout-over-security-fears-2026-06-11/).

"There will be a shift from Chinese players towards European players when it comes to European finance projects, but it will still take some time," said Reinert, predicting the situation would become more certain by the start of 2027.

But the German photovoltaic and battery storage equipment supplier has already held "lots of discussions" with new customers who previously purchased inverters from Chinese companies, he added.

SMA Solar has reduced its dependence on China, where it currently sources between 2% and 4% of components for its large-scale inverters, and sees limited risk of supply chain disruptions from potential retaliatory measures, Reinert said.
