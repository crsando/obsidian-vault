## 德国机械工业联合会呼吁欧洲在人类形机器人领域加码，因宇树科技异军突起

![2026 World Robot Conference in Beijing](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3c181cc90b14819b98dbcd7e4d0b95ec/3c181cc9-0b14-8178-bcf8-eb1ca70acd0d-ae8d992286067fa918c1dcc3c5b85f02.jpg)

2026年8月19日，中国北京，2026年世界机器人大会上，宇树科技的人形机器人进行群舞表演。路透社/王婷姝 [购买许可权利，新窗口打开](https://www.reutersconnect.com/item/2026-world-robot-conference-in-beijing/dGFnOnJldXRlcnMuY29tLDIwMjY6bmV3c21sX1JDMkgxTkFQWTI0MQ%3D%3D/?utm_medium=rcom-article-media&utm_campaign=rcom-rcp-lead)

8月19日（路透社）——德国机械工业联合会（VDMA）周三表示，人形机器人应被置于政治议程的首要位置，呼吁德国和欧洲加大力度，建立有韧性的国内供应链，以获取关键零部件。

- 中国最著名的人形机器人制造商宇树科技[(688836.SS)，新窗口打开](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/688836.SS)在上海上市首日股价[飙升](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/chinese-humanoid-robot-maker-unitree-set-jump-over-600-shanghai-debut-2026-08-19/)，这标志着中国机器人行业的一个重要时刻，该行业已成为中美科技战的一个战场。
- 今年上半年，全球人形机器人出货量几乎[翻了两番](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/china-robot-makers-flock-beijing-show-seek-path-mass-adoption-2026-08-19/)，达到19,100台，其中中国制造商占主导地位。
- VDMA表示，全球人形机器人领导地位的竞争已经开始，人形机器人和物理人工智能这一议题必须被置于政治议程的首位。
- VDMA表示，关键挑战在于规模化。
- “德国和欧洲必须建立一条综合价值链，实现关键零部件的本土生产，”VDMA副总干事哈特穆特·劳恩表示。
报道：马蒂亚斯·威廉姆斯；编辑：托马斯·塞塔尔

## Germany's VDMA lobby calls on Europe to step up on humanoid robotics as Unitree surges

Humanoid robots by Unitree Robotics demonstrate a group dance during the 2026 World Robot Conference, in Beijing, China August 19, 2026. REUTERS/Tingshu Wang [Purchase Licensing Rights, opens new tab](https://www.reutersconnect.com/item/2026-world-robot-conference-in-beijing/dGFnOnJldXRlcnMuY29tLDIwMjY6bmV3c21sX1JDMkgxTkFQWTI0MQ%3D%3D/?utm_medium=rcom-article-media&utm_campaign=rcom-rcp-lead)

Aug 19 (Reuters) - Germany's VDMA engineering association on Wednesday said humanoid robotics should move to the top of the political ‌agenda, calling on Germany and Europe to step up and create resilient domestic supply chains for sourcing critical parts.

- Shares in Unitree [(688836.SS), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/688836.SS), China's best-known humanoid robot ⁠maker, [soared](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/chinese-humanoid-robot-maker-unitree-set-jump-over-600-shanghai-debut-2026-08-19/) in their Shanghai trading debut, in a landmark moment for the country's robotics sector, which has become a battleground in the Sino-U.S. tech war.
- Global humanoid robot shipments nearly [quadrupled](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/china-robot-makers-flock-beijing-show-seek-path-mass-adoption-2026-08-19/) in the first half of this year to 19,100 units, with Chinese ‌manufacturers ⁠dominating.
- The VDMA said a global race for leadership in humanoid robotics has begun, and the topic of humanoid robotics and physical AI ⁠must be placed at the top of the political agenda.
- The VDMA said the crucial challenge ⁠lies in scaling.
- "Germany and Europe must establish a comprehensive value chain featuring domestic ⁠production of critical components," said Hartmut Rauen, Deputy Executive Director of the VDMA.
Reporting by Matthias Williams, editing by Thomas Seythal
