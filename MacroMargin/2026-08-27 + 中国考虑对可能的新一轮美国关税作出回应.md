### 中文译文

## 中国考虑对可能的新一轮美国关税作出回应

中国表示将关注美国针对所谓产能过剩的关税相关行动，并可能采取反制措施，这凸显了尽管存在贸易休战，但摩擦仍在持续。

中国商务部发言人黄凌重申北京反对对中国及其他经济体进行调查，称华盛顿以“产能过剩为借口”启动了这项调查。

“我们将继续密切关注并全面评估美方后续行动，并保留采取一切必要措施的权利，”黄凌在周四的例行新闻发布会上回答关于美国可能征收关税的问题时表示。

彭博新闻社早些时候报道，美国正在考虑在对所谓产能过剩进行调查后，对中国征收7.5%的关税。此举将标志着美国总统唐纳德·特朗普在最高法院否决其此前进口关税后，重启其保护主义贸易议程的最新一步。这也将使对华关税达到北京所称双方此前贸易谈判中同意的水平。

中国外交部声明称，外交部长王毅周三在[会见](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-27/china-s-wang-yi-points-to-risks-in-meeting-with-us-ambassador)美国大使戴维·珀杜时敦促美方将两国关系拉回“正确轨道”，并指责华盛顿“毫无根据地”采取一系列“消极措施”。

### English Original

## China Weighs Response to Possible Next Round of US Tariffs

China said it would monitor US actions related to tariffs for alleged overcapacity and may take countermeasures, underscoring lingering friction despite a trade truce.

Commerce Ministry spokesperson Huang Ling reiterated Beijing’s opposition to the investigation into China and other economies, saying Washington has launched the probe under the “pretext of overcapacity.”

“We will continue to closely monitor and comprehensively assess subsequent US actions and reserve the right to take all necessary measures,” Huang said at a regular press briefing on Thursday in response to a question about a possible US levy.

Bloomberg News reported earlier that the US is considering a 7.5% tariff on China following investigations into alleged excess capacity. The move would mark the latest step by US President Donald Trump to resurrect his protectionist trade agenda after the Supreme Court struck down his previous import taxes. It would also bring tariffs on China to a level Beijing said the two sides agreed to during previous trade talks.

Foreign Minister Wang Yi urged the US to put relations back on the “right track” during a [meeting](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-27/china-s-wang-yi-points-to-risks-in-meeting-with-us-ambassador) with Ambassador David Perdue on Wednesday, accusing Washington of introducing a series of “negative measures on unfounded grounds,” according to a Chinese statement.
