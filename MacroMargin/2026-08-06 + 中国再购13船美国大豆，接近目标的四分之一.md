### 中文译文

## 中国再购13船美国大豆，接近目标的四分之一

中国又预订了一批美国大豆船货，正朝着履行去年达成的贸易休战协议中的采购承诺取得进展。

知情人士透露，该国国有企业周三又购买了至少13船大豆——约80万吨。这些知情人士表示，这使得中国2026-27年度的总采购量达到约600万吨。由于未获授权向媒体发言，他们要求匿名。

这意味着，距离白宫所称中国将在2028年前每年购买至少[2500万吨](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2025/11/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-strikes-deal-on-economic-and-trade-relations-with-china/)美国大豆的目标，中国已接近四分之一。该目标是唐纳德·特朗普总统与中国国家主席习近平在10月底韩国峰会期间达成的贸易休战协议的一部分。

两位领导人于5月在北京再次会晤，之后美国宣布，除大豆采购承诺外，中国还将每年购买至少[170亿美元](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2026/05/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-secures-historic-deals-with-china-delivering-for-american-workers-farmers-and-industry/)的美国农产品，期限相同。白宫表示，2026年的金额将按比例计算。

据知情人士称，最新一批货物主要从9月起装运，将来自美国墨西哥湾和太平洋西北地区。

美国农业部周四通过每日报告系统宣布，仅向中国销售了12.2万吨大豆，标题为“美国作物出口：向中国出口12.2万吨大豆”。这还不包括周一宣布的中国在2026年以迄今最大单笔采购增购美国大豆——这是中国今年最大的一笔单日交易——以及周二宣布的另一笔[13.2万吨](https://www.fas.usda.gov/newsroom/export-sales-china-93)。该机构要求任何超过10万吨的交易必须在24小时内披露。

由于近期降雨提振了产量前景，芝加哥大豆期货价格徘徊在一个月低点附近。

这些对华销售恰逢[美国大豆出口协会](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/7649659Z:US)在芝加哥举办Soy Connext大会，来自60多个国家的代表与会。

中国在实现那些采购目标方面“取得了良好进展”，美国大豆出口协会（USSEC）总裁吉姆·萨特在活动中表示，他还指出美国对世界其他目的地的[豆粕](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/SCMLNOTL%20Index)等出口也在增加。“因此，看来我们的需求状况非常好。”

不过，即使农业采购仍在继续，美中紧张局势再次加剧，因为双方都在升级技术和贸易限制。彭博社还报道称，[美国方面担心](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-15/trump-s-aides-see-china-cheating-on-trade-but-shun-retaliation)中国对贸易协议的遵守情况，这凸显了当前缓和局面的脆弱性。

北京最近收紧了无人机出口管制，并[制裁了美国实体](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-05/china-hits-back-at-us-with-tighter-drone-export-curbs-sanctions)。此举是在美国联邦通信委员会禁止部分外国制造的机器人和电源逆变器，并将40多家被指控使用强迫劳动的中国公司列入黑名单之后采取的。

中国商务部发言人表示，北京愿意保持关系正常发展，特朗普此前曾表示，他预计在[9月下旬](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-06/trump-says-he-expects-to-host-china-s-xi-in-us-around-sept-24)再次与习近平会面。

## China Books 13 More US Soy Cargoes, Nearing Quarter of Target

China booked another flurry of US soybean cargoes, as the country makes headway toward fulfilling the purchase pledge made as part of a trade truce struck last year.

The country’s state-owned firms bought at least another 13 soybean cargoes — or roughly 800,000 tons — on Wednesday, according to people familiar with the matter. It brings the country’s total purchases for the 2026-27 season to about 6 million tons, said the people, asking not to be named as they were not authorized to talk to the media.

That would be close to a quarter of the way toward meeting the at least [25 million tons](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2025/11/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-strikes-deal-on-economic-and-trade-relations-with-china/) of US soybeans the White House has said China will buy annually through 2028. The target was announced as part of the trade truce struck by President Donald Trump and his Chinese counterpart Xi Jinping during their late October summit in South Korea.

The two leaders met again in Beijing in May, after which the US announced that China will also buy at least [$17 billion](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2026/05/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-secures-historic-deals-with-china-delivering-for-american-workers-farmers-and-industry/) of US agricultural products annually over the same period, on top of the soybean pledge. The amount will be prorated for 2026, the White House said.

The latest shipments are mainly for loading from September and will come from both the US Gulf and Pacific Northwest, the people said.

The US Department of Agriculture announced sales of only US CROP EXPORTS: 122,000 Tons of Soybeans to China of soybeans to China through its daily reporting system Thursday. That comes on top of China Adds to US Soybean Buys With Largest Purchase Yet in 2026 announced Monday, which marked the biggest daily transaction for China this year, and another [132,000 tons](https://www.fas.usda.gov/newsroom/export-sales-china-93) announced Tuesday. The agency requires any deals over 100,000 tons to be disclosed within 24 hours.

Futures for the beans hovered near a one-month low in Chicago as recent rainfall boosts yield prospects.

Sales to China are also coming as the [US Soybean Export Council](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/7649659Z:US) hosts the Soy Connext conference in Chicago, with representatives from more than 60 countries.

China is “making good progress toward those” purchasing targets,” USSEC President Jim Sutter said at the event, pointing also to increases in other exports such as US [soybean meal](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/SCMLNOTL%20Index) to other world destinations. “So it appears that we’re in a very good demand situation.”

Still, even as agricultural purchases continue, US-China tensions have been rising again as both sides escalate technology and trade restrictions. Bloomberg has also reported that there are [US concerns](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-15/trump-s-aides-see-china-cheating-on-trade-but-shun-retaliation) over China’s compliance with the trade deal, highlighting the fragility of the current détente.

Beijing recently stiffened drone export controls and [sanctioned US entities](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-05/china-hits-back-at-us-with-tighter-drone-export-curbs-sanctions). That move came after the Federal Communications Commission’s ban on some foreign-made robots and power inverters and the blacklisting of more than 40 Chinese companies accused of using forced labor.

A spokesperson for China’s Commerce Ministry signaled Beijing’s willingness to keep relations on track, and Trump has previously said that he expects to meet again with Xi in [late September](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-06/trump-says-he-expects-to-host-china-s-xi-in-us-around-sept-24).
