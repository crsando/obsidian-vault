### 中文译文

## Moderna与默克宣称黑色素瘤疫苗试验获得成功

一种由 [Moderna Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/MRNA%20US%20Equity) 和 [Merck & Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/MRK%20US%20Equity) 开发的个性化疫苗，在一项大型后期试验中帮助降低了黑色素瘤的复发率，提升了人们对于一种能够延长这种隐匿性癌症患者寿命的疗法的期望。

该试验达到了主要目标，证明疫苗与默克公司的免疫药物 Keytruda 联用比单独使用免疫疗法更能减少黑色素瘤复发。该研究还达到了减缓癌症扩散到身体新部位的关键次要目标。公司于周三表示，默克和 Moderna 宣布，III 期 INTerpath-001 试验评估了 Intismeran Autogene 联合 KEYTRUDA®，达到了无复发生存终点。

Moderna 股价在盘前交易中上涨超过一倍，而默克股价截至纽约时间上午 8:55 涨幅一度达到 9.5%。

这项研究是首个针对任何 mRNA 癌症疗法的阳性最终阶段试验，对两家公司来说都是令人欣喜的进展。它提供了迄今为止最有力的证据，表明这项在疫情期间让 Moderna 家喻户晓的技术将迎来成功的第二幕。对于默克而言，面对其最畅销药物 Keytruda 的专利到期，这为其未来增长提供了一个关键的新市场。

![图片](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3c181cc90b1481819346cf0d525304ee/3c181cc9-0b14-817c-868e-e9df78d8a348-dc7770b0469cb3db3932885277ff6635.png)

关于该疫苗在多大程度上改善无复发生存期——即患者存活且癌症未复发的时间——的细节尚未公布。该试验仍在进行中，以评估接种疫苗的患者能否活得更久。

黑色素瘤是最严重的皮肤癌类型。根据美国癌症协会的数据，美国每年约有 [112,000 人](https://www.cancer.org/cancer/types/melanoma-skin-cancer/about/key-statistics.html) 被诊断出患有黑色素瘤，约有 8,500 人因此死亡。

根据声明，两家公司将与监管机构讨论申请批准事宜，并将在即将举行的医学会议上展示数据。Moderna 首席执行官斯特凡·班塞尔（Stéphane Bancel）在采访中表示，根据监管审查的结果，该产品最早可能在 2027 年获得批准。

“这是 mRNA 科学的一个非凡里程碑，”班塞尔表示。

### **关键问题**

尽管如此，关于大规模生产这种定制癌症治疗方法的成本和可行性仍存在疑问。而且，两家公司还需要证明该疫苗在黑色素瘤以外的其他肿瘤类型中同样有效。

这两家公司已经在该疫苗背后的科学原理上研究了十多年，这种疫苗名为intismeran autogene（个体化新抗原疗法）。该疫苗利用为Moderna的新冠疫苗提供动力的信使RNA技术开发，两家公司根据患者特定的肿瘤突变定制每一剂疫苗。

制作这种疫苗大约需要六周时间。科学家从患者的肿瘤中取样，无论是现有组织还是新活检，并分析其突变。

默克公司肿瘤早期开发主管Jane Healy表示，一种算法会识别出最有可能引发免疫反应的突变，其中多达34种突变被用于生产个性化疫苗。在等待期间，患者继续接受Keytruda治疗。

该试验招募了超过1100名患者，在患者肿瘤被手术切除后，给予mRNA疫苗与默克公司的Keytruda联合治疗。研究中的对照组患者仅接受Keytruda治疗，这是一种用于预防高危患者复发的标准治疗方法。

目标是让这两种药物协同作用，训练人体的免疫系统攻击任何残留的癌细胞。

得益于这款癌症疫苗的前景，Moderna的股价从年初到周二收盘飙升了113%。黑色素瘤试验被视为检验这种方法效果的关键早期测试。默克和Moderna正在测试其在其他类型癌症（包括肺癌）中的效果，但黑色素瘤研究进展最为领先。

科学家们长期追求一种疫苗的理念，这种疫苗能将人体自身的防御机制转向对抗癌症，教会免疫系统识别肿瘤的分子指纹，并摧毁残留的癌细胞。但迄今为止，事实证明要让这些疫苗发挥作用非常困难。

希利在结果公布前几周接受采访时表示：“治疗性疫苗在癌症领域一直像是圣杯。你知道，人们以这样或那样的方式尝试这样做已有一百多年了。”她说，这种疫苗的一个潜在好处是延长生存期，而不会增加太多副作用。

两家公司[今年早些时候表示](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-01-20/moderna-merck-skin-cancer-treatment-shows-long-term-benefits)，在一项中期试验中，接受mRNA疫苗与Keytruda联合治疗的皮肤癌患者，五年内死亡或癌症复发的可能性比单独接受Keytruda的患者低49%。

### English Original

## Moderna and Merck Tout Success in Melanoma Vaccine Trial

A personalized vaccine developed by [Moderna Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/MRNA%20US%20Equity) and [Merck & Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/MRK%20US%20Equity)helped reduce the recurrence of melanoma in a large, late-stage trial, raising expectations for a potential life-extending treatment for an insidious form of cancer.

The trial met its main goal of showing that the vaccine, combined with Merck’s immune drug Keytruda, reduced melanoma recurrences more than the immunotherapy alone. The study also met a key secondary goal of slowing the cancer’s spread to new areas of the body, the companies said in a Merck and Moderna Announce Phase 3 INTerpath-001 Trial of Intismeran Autogene Plus KEYTRUDA® Met Endpoints of Recurrence-Free Wednesday.

Moderna shares more than doubled in pre-market trading, while Merck rose as much as 9.5% as of 8:55 a.m. in New York.

The study, the first positive final-stage trial for any mRNA-based cancer therapy, is a welcome development for both companies. It provides the strongest evidence yet that the technology that made Moderna a household name during the pandemic will have a successful second act. For Merck, facing a patent loss for its best-selling drug Keytruda, it offers a key new market for future growth.

Details on how much the shot improved recurrence-free survival — the amount of time a patient lives without their cancer coming back — weren’t released. The trial remains ongoing to assess whether patients who get the vaccine live longer.

Melanoma is the most serious form of skin cancer. In the US, roughly [112,000 people](https://www.cancer.org/cancer/types/melanoma-skin-cancer/about/key-statistics.html) are diagnosed with it each year and about 8,500 people die from it, according to the American Cancer Society.

The companies will discuss filing for approval with regulators and will present the data at an upcoming medical meeting, according to the statement. In an interview, Moderna Chief Executive Officer Stéphane Bancel said the product could be approved as soon as 2027, depending on the outcome of the regulatory review.

“It is an extraordinary milestone for mRNA science,” Bancel said.

### **Key Questions**

Still, questions remain about the cost and feasibility of producing such bespoke cancer treatments at scale. And the companies will need to prove that the vaccine works in other tumor types beyond melanoma.

The two companies have been working on the science behind the vaccine, called intismeran autogene, for more than a decade. Developed using the messenger RNA technology that powered Moderna’s Covid-19 shot, the companies customize each shot based on a patient’s specific tumor mutations.

It takes about six weeks to make the shot. Scientists take a sample of the patient’s tumor, either from existing tissue or a new biopsy, and analyze it for mutations.

An algorithm identifies which are most likely to provoke an immune response, and up to 34 of them are used to produced the personalized vaccine, said Jane Healy, head of oncology early development at Merck. During that waiting period, patients continue to receive Keytruda.

In the trial, which enrolled more than 1,100 patients, the mRNA vaccine was given in combination with Merck’s Keytruda to patients after their tumors were surgically removed. A control group of patients in the study were treated with Keytruda alone, a standard treatment used to prevent recurrence in patients at risk.

The goal is for the two drugs to work together to train the body’s immune system to attack any remaining cancer cells.

Moderna’s stock had shot up 113% from the beginning of the year through Tuesday’s close on the prospects of the cancer shot. The melanoma trial was seen as a crucial early test of how well the approach would work. Merck and Moderna are testing it in other types of cancer, including lung cancer, but the melanoma studies are the furthest along.

Scientists have long chased the idea of a vaccine that could turn the body’s own defenses against cancer, teaching the immune system to recognize a tumor’s molecular fingerprints and destroy the cells that remain. But, until now, it’s proven very difficult to get them to work.

“Therapeutic vaccines have been something of a holy grail in cancer. People have been trying to do this for, you know, over 100 years in one way or another,” Healy said in an interview several weeks before the results were released. She said one potential benefit of such a shot would be extending survival without adding many more side effects.

The companies [said earlier this year](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-01-20/moderna-merck-skin-cancer-treatment-shows-long-term-benefits) that skin cancer patients in a mid-stage trial who got the mRNA shot with Keytruda were 49% less likely to die or have their cancer return after five years than those who received Keytruda alone.
