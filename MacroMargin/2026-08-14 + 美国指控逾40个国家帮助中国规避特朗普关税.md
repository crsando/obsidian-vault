### 中文译文

## 美国指控逾40个国家帮助中国规避特朗普关税

白宫报告称，受到美国高关税打击的国家正在复制中国的“转运”模式 © Getty Images

[**德米特里·塞瓦斯托普洛**](https://www.ft.com/demetri-sevastopulo) 华盛顿报道

发布日期：2026年8月13日

白宫指责包括加拿大、墨西哥和日本在内的40多个国家帮助北京通过经由美国进口关税低于中国的国家转运贸易，非法规避美国关税。

白宫在一份题为“[《大转运骗局》](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/08/the-great-transshipment-scam/)”的报告中称，这些国家和欧盟使得价值600亿美元的贸易能够逃避关税。报告还援引了美国政府其他部门和私营部门的报告，估计转运贸易额在400亿美元至3030亿美元之间。

发布该报告的白宫贸易与制造业政策办公室主任彼得·纳瓦罗表示，自唐纳德·特朗普在2018年首次对中国加征关税以来，中国一直利用“极其复杂”的方式从事转运活动。

随着美国总统去年对各国加征的关税造成了国与国之间的税率差异，这种转运行为仍在继续，并激励了货物的改道运输。

纳瓦罗表示：“多年来，这场大型转运骗局让共产主义中国得以向40多个国家洗白其出口，掠夺我们财政部数百亿美元，并窃取美国工人的工资。”

### 视频 | 01:10

彼得·纳瓦罗在接受彭博电视采访时警告各国不要试图规避美国关税 © 彭博电视

该报告发布六个星期后，预计中国国家主席习近平将对华盛顿进行回访，此前他于5月[在北京欢迎特朗普](https://www.ft.com/content/7ae6a01d-df8d-4148-8424-27272e63939d?syn-25a6b1a6=1)。尽管两位领导人去年10月在韩国釜山达成了一年休战协议，但两国之间的贸易紧张局势依然严峻。

财政部长斯科特·贝森特最近表示，他和美国贸易代表贾米森·格里尔在与主要对应方副总理何立峰的通话中，对中国履行不[放缓向美国出口稀土](https://www.ft.com/content/541e9027-1e3b-49d8-bfdb-4b54fba1a97e?syn-25a6b1a6=1)的承诺表示关切。

中国对美国新的国家安全措施感到不满，包括联邦通信委员会[禁止进口中国机器人](https://www.ft.com/content/48f0cc68-dfae-48d7-8950-c8c72c2ebe93?syn-25a6b1a6=1)。

纳瓦罗表示，海关和边境保护局正开始使用一个名为“侦探边境”的人工智能系统，以更好地确定出口商品的成分，确保关税归类正确。他说，这项努力将为美国财政部带来数百亿美元的关税收入，并为美国人创造“数千个更多就业机会”。

自特朗普去年发动全球“解放日”关税以来，美国贸易代表办公室在与各国谈判的许多贸易协定中都加入了减少转运的条款。

但该贸易机构仍在努力定义“原产地规则”，以帮助评估产品在何处制造，从而确定适当的关税水平。

“特朗普总统的贸易议程包括各种协议和关税协定，这些协议的好处应归于同意这些协议的国家，”格里尔说。“转运是一种从特朗普总统的协议中搭便车的行为，是由不诚实的出口商延续的，而这些出口商往往正是我们一开始征收关税的原因。”

白宫报告称，其他被美国加征更高关税的国家正在效仿中国的转口模式。纳瓦罗表示：“随着我们对其他国家、印度、越南等加征更高关税，他们也会尝试这种转口贸易。”

## US accuses more than 40 countries of helping China avoid Donald Trump’s tariffs

A White House report says countries that have been hit with high US tariffs are copying the Chinese ‘transshipment’ model © Getty Images

[**Demetri Sevastopulo**](https://www.ft.com/demetri-sevastopulo) in Washington

PublishedAugust 13 2026

The White House has accused more than 40 countries, including Canada, Mexico and Japan, of helping Beijing illegally avoid US tariffs by rerouting trade through nations subject to lower American import duties than China. 

The nations and the EU were enabling the evasion of tariffs for $60bn in trade, the White House said in a report titled “[The Great Transshipment Scam](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/08/the-great-transshipment-scam/)”. It also cited other US government and private sector reports which estimated the range of transshipment volumes from $40bn to $303bn.

Peter Navarro, head of the White House office of trade and manufacturing policy that issued the report, said China had used “extremely sophisticated” ways to engage in transshipment since Donald Trump first imposed tariffs on the country in 2018.

The practice has continued as the levies that the US president put on countries last year have created differentials in rates between countries that incentivise rerouting.

“For years, the great transshipment scam has let communist China launder its exports to more than 40 countries, rob our Treasury of tens of billions of dollars and steal the pay cheques of American workers,” Navarro said.

Peter Navarro warns countries in an interview on Bloomberg TV against trying to avoid US tariffs © Bloomberg TV

The report comes six weeks before President Xi Jinping is expected to make a reciprocal visit to Washington after [welcoming Trump in Beijing](https://www.ft.com/content/7ae6a01d-df8d-4148-8424-27272e63939d?syn-25a6b1a6=1) in May. Trade tensions between the countries remain high despite the one-year truce the two leaders reached in Busan, South Korea, last October.

Treasury secretary Scott Bessent recently said he and US trade representative Jamieson Greer had raised concerns in a call with their main counterpart, vice-premier He Lifeng, about China upholding its commitment not to [slow the export of rare earths](https://www.ft.com/content/541e9027-1e3b-49d8-bfdb-4b54fba1a97e?syn-25a6b1a6=1) to the US.

China is frustrated at new US national security measures, including the Federal Communications Commission [banning the import of Chinese robots](https://www.ft.com/content/48f0cc68-dfae-48d7-8950-c8c72c2ebe93?syn-25a6b1a6=1).

Navarro said the Customs and Border Protection agency was starting to use an AI-powered system called “Detective Border” to better determine the composition of exports to ensure correct labelling for tariff purposes. He said the effort would bring in tens of billions of dollars in tariff revenue to the US Treasury and would create “thousands more jobs for Americans”.

The USTR has included provisions to reduce transshipment in many of the trade deals it has negotiated with countries since Trump unleashed his worldwide “liberation day” tariffs last year.

But the trade agency is still working on defining the “rules of origin” necessary to help evaluate where a product is manufactured to determine the appropriate tariff level.

“President Trump’s trade agenda includes deals and tariff agreements, and the benefits of those deals should go to the countries agreed to them,” Greer said. “Transshipment is a form of freeriding off President Trump’s deals and is perpetuated by bad-faith exporters who are often the very reason we have tariffs in the first place.”

The White House report said other countries that had been hit with higher US tariffs were copying the Chinese transshipment model. “As we impose higher tariffs on other countries, India, Vietnam, on down the line, they’re going to try this transshipment too,” Navarro said.
