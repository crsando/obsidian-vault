A surge in longer-maturity US Treasury yields is testing the resolve of global bond investors torn between the possibility of locking in the rates near the highest levels in decades and the risk of an even greater selloff.
长期美国国债收益率飙升，正考验着全球债券投资者的决心——他们既想抓住接近数十年高位的利率锁定收益，又担忧可能面临更严重的抛售风险。

With Treasury 30-year yields a whisker away from the highest since 2007, a team at Goldman Sachs Group Inc. sees some emerging measures of value but urges caution. Barclays Plc and Citigroup Inc. strategists are warning clients they may breach 5.5%, levels last seen in 2004. The head of BlackRock’s research unit is recommending investors reduce their exposure to developed-market government bonds — including Treasuries — in favor of equities.
随着 30 年期美债收益率距 2007 年以来最高点仅一步之遥，高盛集团团队虽看到部分估值指标初现价值，但仍建议保持谨慎。巴克莱银行与花旗集团策略师警告客户，收益率可能突破 5.5%关口，触及 2004 年以来水平。贝莱德研究部门主管则建议投资者减少包括美债在内的发达市场政府债券敞口，转而增持股票。

Such views are an indication of a market trying to price diverging outcomes, ranging from the persistent reemergence of inflation amid a resilient economy to a slowdown driven by higher energy prices. It also increases the pressure on incoming Federal Reserve Chair Kevin Warsh and US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent, who has committed to bringing down borrowing costs.
这些观点折射出市场正试图为分化走势定价：既包括经济韧性下通胀持续抬头的可能性，也涵盖能源价格飙升引发的经济放缓风险。这同时给即将上任的美联储主席凯文·沃什与承诺降低借贷成本的美国财政部长斯科特·贝森特带来更大压力。

“While I am attracted to the yields, I am cautious,” said Gregory Peters, co-chief investment officer at PGIM Credit. He said he’s underweight 30-year Treasuries based on his expectation that the term premium — the extra compensation investors demand to hold longer-dated debt — will keep rising. “The global bond market is in disarray as investors are losing confidence.”
"虽然我对收益率感兴趣，但我持谨慎态度，"PGIM 信贷联席首席投资官格雷戈里·彼得斯表示。他称，基于对期限溢价（投资者持有较长期债券所要求的额外补偿）将持续上升的预期，他目前低配 30 年期美国国债。"全球债券市场陷入混乱，投资者信心正在流失。"

The yield on 30-year Treasuries was higher by two basis points in early Tuesday trading in New York at 5.14% — just shy of their highest levels in almost two decades. Two-year and 10-year yields were up three basis points to 4.08% and 4.61%, respectively.
周二纽约早盘交易中，30 年期美国国债收益率上涨 2 个基点至 5.14%——距离近二十年来的最高水平仅一步之遥。两年期和 10 年期国债收益率分别上涨 3 个基点至 4.08%和 4.61%。

Those levels have Wall Street dealers flagging the prospect of even higher longer-dated yields as inflation concerns ripple across markets.
随着通胀担忧在市场中蔓延，华尔街交易商开始预警长期国债收益率可能进一步攀升。

Jim McCormick, Citi’s London-based macro rates strategist, said the market focus is likely to shift to a test of 5.5%, a level last registered in June 2004. A Bank of America Big Treasury Move to Drive 30-Year Yield Above 6%: BofA Survey of fund managers shows 62% think a big move in yields over the next year will drive 30-year government borrowing costs above 6%. The long bond last traded above that threshold in June 2000, when the Federal Reserve’s policy rate sat at 6.5%.
花旗银行驻伦敦宏观利率策略师吉姆·麦考密克表示，市场焦点可能转向测试 5.5%的收益率水平，该水平上次出现是在 2004 年 6 月。美国银行调查显示，62%的基金经理认为，未来一年债券收益率的大幅波动将推动 30 年期政府借贷成本突破 6%。长期国债上一次交易在 6%以上是在 2000 年 6 月，当时美联储的政策利率为 6.5%。

Vanguard Group Inc.’s global head of fixed income Sara Devereux, meanwhile, said yields in the $31 trillion market are [near the top](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-18/vanguard-favors-treasuries-as-10-year-yields-near-range-top) of the expected range for 10-year notes.
与此同时，先锋集团全球固定收益主管萨拉·德弗罗表示，这个 31 万亿美元市场中，10 年期国债收益率已接近预期区间的上限。

Global bond yields have surged in recent weeks as a jump in energy prices caused by the Iran war adds to inflationary pressures and compels central banks such as the Fed to consider raising interest rates. Add in worries over US budget deficits and signs that the world’s largest economy remains resilient, and the result is that investors have been seeking greater compensation to own longer-maturity debt.
全球债券收益率近几周大幅攀升，原因是伊朗战争导致的能源价格飙升加剧了通胀压力，并迫使美联储等央行考虑加息。再加上对美国预算赤字的担忧，以及全球最大经济体依然保持韧性的迹象，投资者要求持有较长期限债券获得更高补偿。

Traders remain on tenterhooks for a resolution to the Middle East conflict, which may yet open a path to a sustainable bond rally.
交易员们仍在紧张等待中东冲突的解决方案，这或许能为可持续的债券反弹开辟道路。

“Value arguments are very fragile right now,” said John Sidawi, a senior portfolio manager at Federated Hermes. It’s predicated on how things develop in the Middle East and if there’s an escalation, “you throw the value arguments out,” he said.
“价值论据目前非常脆弱，”Federated Hermes 高级投资组合经理 John Sidawi 表示。这取决于中东局势如何发展，如果局势升级，“价值论据就不复存在了，”他说道。

Fiscal Deterioration 财政状况恶化

Should yields continue to grind higher, one concern is that long-end rates will become untethered as the market adjusts to a new trading range. While some traders pointed to 4.5% in the 10-year note, and 5% in the 30-year bond as areas that would draw demand from investors seeking to lock in lofty yields, the latest selloff has seen the market push through both.
若收益率持续攀升，市场担忧长端利率将因投资者适应新交易区间而失去锚定。尽管部分交易员指出 10 年期国债收益率 4.5%和 30 年期国债收益率 5%将吸引寻求锁定高收益的投资者入场，但最新一轮抛售已使市场突破这两个关键点位。

“Yields might be at the highs of the year, but that by itself isn’t an argument in favor of duration,” said Ajay Rajadhyaksha, global chairman of research at Barclays, which Barclays Warns of Risk of Extended Long Bond Selloff: Roundup to stay away from long bonds. “The forces driving the selloff – fiscal deterioration, defense spending, sticky inflation, central bank paralysis – are not resolving in the next week.”
“收益率可能处于年内高位，但这本身并非看多长久期债券的理由，”巴克莱研究全球主席 Ajay Rajadhyaksha 表示——该机构在《巴克莱警告长债抛售风险：综述》中建议规避长期债券。“推动抛售的驱动因素——财政恶化、国防开支、通胀粘性、央行政策僵局——不会在一周内化解。”

The tension reflects what Goldman strategists term an “uneasy introduction of value.” While by several metrics longer-dated Treasuries are starting to look attractive, things may easily get worse before they get better.
这种紧张局势反映了高盛策略师所称的"令人不安的价值显现"。尽管从多项指标来看，长期美国国债正开始显现吸引力，但在情况好转之前，形势可能轻易恶化。

For the team led by George Cole, investors seeking to take a bullish stance should consider structures which limit the downside if rates continue to rise.
以乔治·科尔为首的团队认为，寻求看涨立场的投资者应考虑采用能限制利率持续上升时下行风险的结构。

“We would look either for deeper selloffs that more durably challenge the risk asset trend, or credible relief and a return of energy flows as catalysts to add to long duration exposure,” the strategists wrote.
策略师们写道："我们要么等待更深度抛售以更持久地挑战风险资产趋势，要么等待可信的缓解信号及能源流动回归，以此作为增加长期久期敞口的催化剂。"
