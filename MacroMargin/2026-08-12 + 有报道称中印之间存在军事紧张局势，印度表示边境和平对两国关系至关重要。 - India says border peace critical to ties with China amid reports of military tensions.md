NEW DELHI, Aug 12 (Reuters) - India's foreign ministry said peace along its disputed border with China is critical to ties between the Asian ‌neighbours, amid reports of renewed military tensions between the two countries' troops in a remote corner of the Himalayas.
新德里，8 月 12 日（路透社）——鉴于有报道称中印军队在喜马拉雅山偏远地区再度出现军事紧张局势，印度外交部表示，维持与中国的争议边境和平对于这两个亚洲邻国之间的关系至关重要。

New Delhi's comment is its [second in less than a week](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/india-says-talks-with-china-stressed-need-border-peace-boost-ties-2026-08-07/) and comes as the Asian giants seek to mend ties following deadly clashes in 2020. It also comes ahead of a summit of BRICS countries in New Delhi from September 12 to 13 that Chinese President Xi Jinping is expected to attend.
新德里方面的这番表态是不到一周内的第二次发声。当前，这两个亚洲大国正在努力修复 2020 年发生致命冲突后的关系，而这一表态也出现在 9 月 12 日至 13 日金砖国家峰会即将于新德里举行之际，预计中国国家主席习近平将会出席该峰会。

"In matters relating to the border areas between India and China, we have always emphasised in discussions with the Chinese side that ⁠we consider these issues as most serious," Indian foreign ministry spokesperson Randhir Jaiswal said on Tuesday at a regular media briefing.

"We have also stated that the state of the border affairs will reflect on the state of our larger bilateral ties," he added, responding to a question on new military tensions between the two sides.

### TWO SIDES AGREE TO KEEP TALKING

The Chinese foreign ministry said that currently the situation along the border was "generally stable", responding to a request for comment from Reuters.

The two countries had agreed to "maintain communications through diplomatic and military channels and (to) jointly safeguard peace and tranquility in the border areas", at the latest meeting of their joint border affairs consultation panel that was held in New Delhi last week, Beijing said on Wednesday.

Indian news website ThePrint reported after the foreign ministry briefing on Tuesday that Indian and Chinese army patrols came face to ‌face in ⁠late July near Taksing, a remote border village in Arunachal Pradesh's Upper Subansiri district bordering Tibet.

China claims Arunachal Pradesh, which it calls Zangnan, as part of southern Tibet. India says the state is an integral and inalienable part of its territory.

The incident reportedly took place 2.5 km (1.5 miles) inside territory controlled by India in the eastern Himalayas, and the Chinese later withdrew. But in early August, the Chinese returned to the area and pitched a couple of tents, the report ⁠said.

### INDIAN DEFENCE AUTHORITIES DISMISS REPORTS OF NEW TENSIONS

Separately, a July 26 letter to local authorities from a residents' welfare body in the area, seen by Reuters, said Chinese personnel were fast expanding their presence by "occupying new and important places of our land situated at border area".

An Indian defence spokesperson dismissed the reports.

"These (reports) are unsubstantiated ⁠and uncorroborated," said the defence spokesperson for Arunachal Pradesh.

It is not unusual for military patrols of the two countries to confront each other along the 3,488 km (2,167-mile) frontier, but neither is it a frequent occurrence.

Clashes between the two sides in the western Himalayas in 2020 resulted in the ⁠deaths of 20 Indian soldiers and four Chinese troops.

Both sides had amassed tens of thousands of troops on the frontier there until Xi and Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi held talks in 2024 and agreed to end the standoff.
