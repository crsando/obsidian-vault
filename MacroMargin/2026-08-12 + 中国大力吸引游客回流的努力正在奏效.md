### 中文译文

## 中国大力吸引游客回流的努力正在奏效

上海南京路上的游客。来华外国游客数量激增。*摄影师：Qilai Shen/彭博*

彭博新闻社 2026年8月12日 上午8:00（GMT+8）

### **要点**

- 中国入境旅游首次超过疫情前水平，2025年有数百万外国游客到访。
- 政府的线上推广活动和其他国家对中国的好感推动了旅游热潮，去年免签入境旅客占旅客总数的70%以上。
- 尽管旅游业复苏，中国在旅游总收入和人均消费方面仍落后，国家需要让游客更容易出行，包括改善语言支持和数字支付选项。
一座极简风格的别墅坐落在茂密的森林、雾气缭绕的喀斯特山峰和如电影般的天际线景观之中：正是这些惊艳的Instagram短视频最终说服了克莱尔·瑟姆（Claire Thum）第一次来到中国。

这位28岁的新加坡人是2025年访问中国的数百万外国游客之一，那一年入境人数和旅游消费首次超过疫情前水平。

Thum 原本只知道兵马俑，直到中国旅游内容开始频繁出现在她的信息流中。她说：“对我来说，探索中国更多地方的第一步就是先涉足上海。”

旅行亮点包括参观走红的香水品牌 To Summer 及其标志性庭院、体验滑雪模拟，以及在日本运动服饰店铺 Descente 挑选冬装。现在，她正计划再次前往这个亚洲国家，云南、新疆和哈尔滨这些遥远的地区在她的清单上位列前茅。

在实施多年的疫情限制后，中国于2023年启动了大规模行动以赢回游客，逐步对50个国家实行免签入境。这一行动起初进展缓慢，因为新冠管控措施的记忆损害了该国的声誉，且航线恢复尚需时日。但如今，游客似乎正在以快得多的速度回归。

![Tourists, including many international travelers, visit the Yuyuan Garden in Shanghai. Photographer: Qilai Shen/Bloomberg](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3ba81cc90b1481b4ba70ea95572e7ab8/3ba81cc9-0b14-8190-be1e-cc5d9cc59169-7acc3e9c4e6c1e8d8b318409c692fac7.png)

游客（包括许多国际旅行者）在上海豫园游览。摄影师：Qilai Shen/Bloomberg

这在一定程度上要归功于政府去年加强的在线推广活动，包括在中国被禁的西方平台上的推广。与此同时，皮尤研究中心的数据显示，随着唐纳德·特朗普重塑全球对美国的印象，2025年多个国家对这个世界第二大经济体的好感度达到历史新高。像“Chinamaxxing”这样的潮流——西方人开始采纳这个亚洲国家的生活习惯，比如喝热茶——也迅速走红。

“入境旅游热潮让中国获得了多年来一直追求的软实力提升。”总部位于上海的市场营销咨询公司 Wai Social 的创始人奥利维亚·普洛特尼克表示。“我不止一次听到最近来上海的游客告诉我，在他们眼中，上海就是新的东京。”

![图片](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3ba81cc90b1481b4ba70ea95572e7ab8/3ba81cc9-0b14-81cf-80c4-cb45816da52e-1033df4f6148711c4c05e9dfd75a3c50.png)

根据[国家统计局的数据](https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/zxfbhjd/202602/t20260228_1962662.html)，去年约有3500万外国游客入境，这使中国跻身亚洲最成熟的旅游目的地行列——超过泰国，略低于日本和马来西亚。国家移民管理局（NIA）表示，今年迄今引领旅游热潮的国家包括韩国、俄罗斯、马来西亚和泰国等享有免签入境政策的亚洲国家。

免签入境似乎尤其受欢迎，去年占游客总数的70%以上，较2024年增长50%。2026年上半年，来自免签国家的入境人数达1800万人次，使外国游客总人数增长了20%。在此期间，北京接待的越南和俄罗斯游客数量激增，而日本游客数量则因两国间的外交争端而下降。

![图片](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3ba81cc90b1481b4ba70ea95572e7ab8/3ba81cc9-0b14-8161-a074-d5326ddc69a8-fcf41ec70cb37af689b87af668a0ab5c.png)

在放宽签证的同时，地方政府向旅游部门提供资金，包括2026年为北京额外提供264万元人民币（39万美元），用于管理新员工人数并开展线上宣传项目——这在地方财政中属罕见支出。北京将部分资金用于奖励引进外语导游的旅行社。根据官方采购[文件](https://whlyj.sh.gov.cn/zbgg/index.html)，上海今年将在热门酒店预订平台 Expedia 和 Booking.com 上投入至少160万元人民币用于促销活动。

为了利用社交媒体吸引年轻游客，许多官员已跨过防火墙在 X 和 TikTok 等中国无法访问的社交媒体平台上开设账号。在山城重庆，霓虹闪烁的巨型城市中，旅游局副局长朱茂表示，他决心让这座城市在互联网上出名，依靠网红甚至美国篮球明星斯蒂芬·库里来提升人气。

“当国际游客不再局限于参观传统旅游景点，而是开始体验当地日常生活时，中国变得更加生动、多元且更具吸引力，”AI旅游指南Kora创始人Bobo Rok表示。“从那一刻起，他们开始看到一个真实的中国，而不仅仅是纸上的或想象中的那个。”

在华为和大疆体验高科技产品、在主题公园式泡泡玛特门店购物、在海底捞吃火锅，这些都位列游客待办清单的前列。

Photographer: Qilai Shen/Bloomberg](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3ba81cc90b1481b4ba70ea95572e7ab8/3ba81cc9-0b14-8192-8c78-cdf44bbe2e04-b2de71be452f68a4ad78d57bf5a416c6.png)

一名店员在上海海底捞火锅店内进行拉面表演。摄影师：沈绮来/彭博社

### 华为位于深圳的全球旗舰店。摄影师：沈绮来/彭博社

伊朗战争也重塑了人们的旅行习惯，推高了机票价格，并促使过境交通经由中国而非中东。自伊朗战争开始以来，中国最大的几家航空公司——国有中国东方航空、中国国际航空和中国南方航空——的航班客流量均有所增加。中国航空公司的票价低于外国航空公司，后者尚未将往返中国的航线恢复至疫情前水平。

尽管旅游业复苏，中国在旅游总收入和人均消费方面仍落后，入境旅游消费总额不足美国的三分之一，人均入境消费约为美国的40%，在中国约为2240美元，据携程网（Trip.com）数据。

中国媒体称，2025年国际旅游业占中国国内生产总值的比重不到0.5%，远低于西班牙和泰国等成熟旅游经济体8%的水平。

尽管如此，这给中国零售业带来了亟需的提振，该行业此前因国内消费疲软和房地产市场低迷而遭受打击。费用仅为美国五分之一的口腔清洁对美国游客很有吸引力，而欧洲人则喜欢在当地服装市场定制西装。

![图片](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3ba81cc90b1481b4ba70ea95572e7ab8/3ba81cc9-0b14-81d0-8c22-c88ec2e1635f-c09b3b3203828e8bdfcf384b345086b4.png)

在上海南京东路的名创优品旗舰店，外国游客在一天中的某些时段占客流量的70%，他们被与Chiikawa、三丽鸥、哈利·波特和迪士尼等热门角色的联名产品所吸引。在海外门店，这种对游客的吸引力也持续存在。名创优品报告称，在国际游客社交媒体曝光推动下，海外需求不断上升，海外销售额超过了中国大陆收入，同比增长20%。

泡泡玛特的收藏玩具在上海豫园集市颇具吸引力。摄影师：Qilai Shen/Bloomberg

Joy Group 是爆款美妆品牌 Judydoll 和 Joocyee 的母公司，去年海外零售额超过6亿元人民币，其中 Judydoll 在两年内实现超过十倍增长，尤其在越南和日本。

如果中国想让这种好奇心持续下去，就必须让这个国家更容易游览。主要城市几乎无现金化，这意味着对于没有当地银行账户、数字支付钱包或当地手机号码的游客来说，付款、点餐或购票都很困难。语言支持的缺乏，再加上无法使用谷歌、Instagram 和 WhatsApp 等主流全球应用（除非使用 VPN，而 VPN 在中国是非法的），令问题雪上加霜。

人工智能旅游向导 Kora 是多个缓解上述摩擦的新商业创意之一，它简化了叫车和餐厅预订，并提供聊天机器人，自今年早些时候推出以来已服务数万名游客，其创始人 Rok 表示。

维吉尔·凯巴伊里（Virgile Kebaïli），23岁，来自法国的历史系学生，第一次来中国旅行，花了两个多星期游览了成都、重庆、西安和北京。“这是一段很棒的旅程，非常精彩。就美食而言，它和印度并列前两名，”他说，并补充说，他带了几包中国香烟回家，这些香烟比法国便宜六倍。

有两件事让他印象深刻。一是在他旅行期间的部分时间里，其他国际游客相对较少。他很乐意与当地人合影，甚至在三星堆博物馆接受了一群学生关于他在中国旅行经历的采访。

还有监控。

“我没想到会有这样一个无处不在、无所不能的监控体系，”凯巴伊里说。“我没想到到处都是摄像头，到处都是警察。”他在参观天安门广场前接受的背包检查“来自另一个维度”。他朋友的扑克牌和纸张被没收了。

尽管如此，他还会再来：“我真的很享受这次旅行，以及我在那里发现的一切。”

## How China’s Big Push to Win Back Tourists Is Paying Off

Tourists on Nanjing Road in Shanghai. Foreign visitor numbers to China have been surging.*Photographer: Qilai Shen/Bloomberg*

By Bloomberg NewsAugust 12, 2026 at 8:00 AM GMT+8

### **Takeaways**

- China's inbound tourism has surpassed pre-pandemic levels for the first time, with millions of foreign tourists visiting the country in 2025.
- The government's online promotion campaign and favorable views of China in other countries have contributed to the tourism boom, with visa-free entry accounting for more than 70% of travelers last year.
- Despite the recovery of tourism, China still lags behind in total tourism receipts and per-capita spending, and the country needs to make itself an easier place to navigate for tourists, including improving language support and digital payment options.
A minimalist villa nestled among lush forests, misty limestone peaks and cinematic skyline views: striking Instagram reels like these finally convinced Claire Thum to make her first trip to China.

The 28-year-old Singaporean is one of millions of foreign tourists who visited the country in 2025, when arrivals and tourist spending surpassed pre-pandemic levels for the first time.

Thum knew only of terracotta warriors until Chinese travel content began regularly popping up on her feed. “To me, the first step to exploring more of China was to dip my toes into Shanghai,” she said.

Highlights included a visit to the viral perfumery To Summer with its signature garden, a snowboarding simulation experience and picking out winter clothes at Japanese sportswear store Descente. Now she’s planning another trip to the Asian nation, with the far-flung regions of Yunnan, Xinjiang and Harbin top of her list.

After years of pandemic restrictions, in 2023 China launched a massive push to win back tourists with the gradual introduction of visa-free travel for 50 countries. That campaign got off to a slow start, as memories of Covid curbs hurt the country's reputation and flight routes took time to come back. But now tourists appear to be returning much faster.

Tourists, including many international travelers, visit the Yuyuan Garden in Shanghai. Photographer: Qilai Shen/Bloomberg

That's partly thanks to an online promotion campaign the government ramped up last year, including on Western platforms banned in China. At the same time, favorable views of the world’s second-largest economy reached record highs in a number of countries in 2025, according to Pew Research Center, as Donald Trump reshaped global impressions of America. Trends like ‘Chinamaxxing’ — where Western citizens adopt habits from the Asian country, such as drinking hot tea — also went viral.

“The inbound tourism boom gives China the soft power boost it’s chased after for years,” said Olivia Plotnick, founder of Shanghai-based marketing consultancy Wai Social. “I’ve heard more than a few recent visitors to Shanghai tell me that in their eyes, Shanghai is the new Tokyo.”

Around 35 million foreign tourists arrived last year, according to [data from the National Bureau of Statistics](https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/zxfbhjd/202602/t20260228_1962662.html), putting China in the ranks of some of the most well-established travel destinations in Asia — more than Thailand and just shy of Japan and Malaysia. The NIA said nations leading the tourism surge so far this year include Asian countries with visa-free entry such as Korea, Russia, Malaysia and Thailand.

Visa-free entry appears to be particularly popular, accounting for more than 70% of travelers last year, up by 50% from 2024. In the first half of 2026, 18 million people arrived from visa-free countries, pushing overall foreign tourist arrivals up by 20%. Beijing has seen surging numbers of Vietnamese and Russians in that period, while Japanese figures are in decline following a diplomatic spat between the two countries.

Alongside relaxing visas, local governments provided cash to tourism offices, including an extra 2.64 million yuan ($390,000) for Beijing in 2026 to manage new headcount and launch online publicity projects — a rare spend in local finances. Beijing used some of the money to hand out rewards to travel agencies that introduced foreign-language tour guides. Shanghai is due to spend at least 1.6 million yuan on promotional campaigns on popular hotel booking platforms Expedia and Booking.com this year, according to official procurement [documents](https://whlyj.sh.gov.cn/zbgg/index.html).

To capitilize on the power of social media in luring young travelers, many officials have hopped over the Great Firewall to set up accounts on social media platforms like X and TikTok, unavailable in China. In the neon-lit mountainous mega-city of Chongqing, the deputy director general of tourism Zhu Mao said he was determined to make the city internet famous, leaning on influencers and even American basketball star Stephen Curry for boosts in popularity.

“China becomes more vivid, multi-dimensional and more appealing when international tourists are no longer limited to visiting traditional tourists attractions, but instead start to experience the everyday local life,” said Bobo Rok, founder of AI tourist guide Kora. “From that moment on, they start to see a real China, not just something from paper or imagination.”

Exploring high-tech products at Huawei and DJI, shopping at theme-park style Pop Mart stores or eating hot-pot at Haidilao feature high on to-do lists.

An employee performs a noodle making routine inside a Haidilao hotpot restaurant in Shanghai. Photographer: Qilai Shen/Bloomberg

### Huawei’s global flagship store in Shenzhen. Photographer: Qilai Shen/Bloomberg

The Iran war has also reshaped people’s travel habits by pushing up air ticket prices and driving transit traffic through China instead of the Middle East. China’s biggest airlines, state-run China Eastern, Air China and China Southern, all saw increasing flows of passengers filling its flights since the start of the Iran war. Chinese airlines offer cheaper prices than foreign carriers, which have not returned their routes to China back to pre-Covid levels.

Despite the recovery of tourism, China still lags behind in both total tourism receipts and per-capita spending, generating less than one third of the inbound tourism spend compared to the US, and about 40% the per-capita inbound spend, at around $2,240 in China, according to Trip.com.

The international tourism industry accounted for less than 0.5% of China’s national GDP in 2025, Chinese media said, much lower than some mature tourism economies such Spain and Thailand where it's 8%.

Still, it’s giving a much-needed boost to the Chinese retail sector which suffered from sluggish domestic consumption and a real estate market in the doldrums. Dental cleaning, which costs one fifth of US prices, is a draw for American tourists, while Europeans like to order bespoke suits at local garment markets.

At Miniso’s flagship store on Shanghai’s Nanjing East Road, foreign visitors account for up to 70% of foot traffic during some parts of the day, drawn to collaborations with popular characters from Chiikawa, Sanrio, Harry Potter and Disney. Popularity with tourists has also continued in branches abroad. Miniso reported rising overseas demand fueled by social media exposure from international visitors, while overseas sales surpassed its Mainland China revenue and grew 20% year-on-year.

Miniso’s flagship store in Shanghai, where foreign visitors can account for over 70% of foot traffic during some parts of the day. Photographer: Qilai Shen/Bloomberg

Pop Mart’s collectable toys are a draw at Shanghai’s Yuyuan Bazaar. Photographer:BloombergQilai Shen/

Joy Group, parent of viral beauty brand Judydoll and Joocyee, generated more than 600 million yuan in overseas retail sales last year, with Judydoll achieving over tenfold growth in two years, particularly in Vietnam and Japan.

If China wants the curiosity to last it has to make the country an easier place to navigate. Major cities are virtually cashless, meaning for tourists without local bank accounts, digital payment wallets or local phone numbers it can be challenging to make payments, order food or buy tickets. A lack of language support is compounded by no access to mainstream global applications like Google, Instagram and WhatsApp unless using a VPN, which is illegal in China.

AI tourist guide Kora is one of several new business ideas helping to ease the friction by simplifying ride-hailing and restaurant reservations and providing a chatbot that has served tens of thousands of visitors since its launch earlier this year, said Rok, its founder.

Virgile Kebaïli, a 23-year-old history student from France, spent more than two weeks visiting Chengdu, Chongqing, Xian and Beijing on his first trip to China. “It was a great journey, it was wonderful. It was in my top two best travels for food, along with India,” he said, adding that he went home with a few packs of Chinese cigarettes, which were six times cheaper than in France.

Two things stood out to him. One is the relative lack of other international tourists during parts of his visit. He was happy to pose for photos for locals and was even interviewed by a group of students at the Sanxingdui museum about his travel experience in China.

And then there was the surveillance.

A security camera reflected in a pane of glass at a subway station entrance in Shanghai’s Nanjing Road shopping area. Photographer: Qilai Shen/Bloomberg

“I was not expecting such an omnipresent and omnipotent regime,” said Kebaïli. “I was not expecting cameras everywhere, policemen everywhere.” The bag checks before he could visit Tiananmen square were “from another dimension.” His friend’s playing cards and paper sheets were confiscated.

Still, he would come back again: “I really enjoyed my trip and what I discovered there.”
