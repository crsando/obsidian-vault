> 📌 **核心摘要**
>
> 伊朗议员正就与阿曼的霍尔木兹海峡协议措辞辩论，特朗普称谈判“进展顺利”，但协议细节及最高领袖态度仍存变数。
> • 伊朗副外长称已与阿曼“原则上”达成共识，最终文本将“很快”公布。
> • 部分伊朗议员推动禁止美以船只通过、要求敌国赔偿等条款。
> • 伊朗最高领袖哈梅内伊是否批准尚不清楚。
> • 特朗普表示美国正参与谈判，并威胁若不通航将再袭。
> • 胡塞武装袭击沙特也门政府军，红海紧张局势加剧。
> • 布伦特原油约82美元/桶，前一交易日涨近4%。

**🔑 关键词**：霍尔木兹、伊朗、特朗普

Iranian lawmakers are debating the wording of a proposed deal with Oman on the Strait of Hormuz, with US President Donald Trump insisting an agreement is getting closer.
伊朗议员正就拟与阿曼达成的霍尔木兹海峡协议措辞展开辩论，美国总统唐纳德·特朗普坚称协议即将达成。

Trump said the talks are “moving along” and gave no additional comment when asked about progress by White House reporters late Thursday. Iran’s Deputy Foreign Minister Kazem Gharibabadi said earlier this week that an understanding with Oman had been reached “in principle,” and state TV cited one lawmaker on Friday as saying the final text and details of an agreement with Muscat will be announced “soon.”
特朗普表示谈判"进展顺利"，当周四晚些时候被白宫记者问及进展时未作进一步评论。伊朗副外长卡齐姆·加里巴巴迪本周早些时候表示，已与阿曼"原则上"达成共识。伊朗国家电视台周五援引一位议员的话称，与马斯喀特方面协议的最终文本和细节将"很快"公布。

Some Iranian politicians are pushing for clauses such as a ban on US and Israeli ships from the waterway, the semi-official Fars news agency reported, a condition that would defy Washington’s demand for free passage despite a lack of US- or Israeli-flagged commercial ships globally.
据半官方法尔斯通讯社报道，部分伊朗政界人士正推动加入禁止美国和以色列船只通过该水道的条款——这一条件将违背华盛顿方面对自由通行的要求，尽管全球商船中并无悬挂美以国旗的船只。

Other modifications reported by Fars include an Iranian demand for compensation from “hostile countries,” a Iran Says US, Israel Ships Barred from Hormuz Passage: Fars on cargo related to Israel, and a Iran, Oman Plan ‘Central Corridor’ for Strait Traffic: Fars covering services like insurance and environmental costs.
法尔斯通讯社报道的其他修改内容还包括：伊朗要求"敌对国家"就与以色列相关的货物运输进行赔偿，以及伊朗与阿曼计划为海峡交通设立"中央走廊"，涵盖保险和环境成本等服务。

It’s also unclear whether Iran’s Supreme Leader Mojtaba Khamenei has given his assent to the proposal, a condition for it to go ahead. Iranian officials have said that communicating with him takes time.
目前尚不清楚伊朗最高领袖莫杰塔巴·哈梅内伊是否已批准该提案，而这是提案得以推进的前提条件。伊朗官员表示，与他沟通需要时间。

A deal on the management of the strait has become key to expectations for a further pick up in energy flows, which have continued despite recent outbreaks of hostilities, albeit at lower levels than before the US and Israel attacked Iran at the end of February. A reopening of the crucial chokepoint would also likely pave the way for a return to broader negotiations between the US and Iran to end the war.
关于海峡管理协议的达成，已成为能源运输进一步恢复的关键。尽管近期爆发冲突，能源运输仍在继续，但流量低于 2 月底美以袭击伊朗之前的水平。这一关键咽喉要道的重新开放，也可能为美伊之间更广泛的谈判铺平道路，以结束战争。

Fars Iran Struck ‘Hostile Targets’ in Strait of Hormuz: Fars late Thursday that Iranian naval forces had struck “hostile targets” at the entrance to the strait, citing an unnamed source, an indication that tensions remain high.
法尔斯通讯社：伊朗在霍尔木兹海峡打击"敌对目标"。法尔斯通讯社周四晚间援引匿名消息源称，伊朗海军部队在海峡入口处打击了"敌对目标"，这表明紧张局势依然高涨。

Brent crude oil held steady at about $82 a barrel, after gaining almost 4% in the previous session.
布伦特原油价格稳定在每桶约 82 美元，此前一个交易日上涨近 4%。

Arab mediators discussing a deal with Iran for the Strait of Hormuz are concerned that the nation’s diplomats may not be able to guarantee compliance with any agreement reached, the Wall Street Journal Can Iran's Diminished Diplomats Deliver a Hormuz Deal? -- WSJ. Mediators cited by the paper say Tehran’s lead negotiators are under growing pressure from hardline officials to eke out more explicit references to Iran’s role in the strait and clearer benefits.
据《华尔街日报》报道，正在与伊朗就霍尔木兹海峡协议进行讨论的阿拉伯调解人担心，伊朗外交官可能无法确保任何达成的协议得到遵守。调解人表示，德黑兰的首席谈判代表正面临强硬派官员越来越大的压力，要求他们在协议中更明确地提及伊朗在海峡中的作用，并争取更清晰的利益。

One a few US- or Israeli-flagged commercial ships have sailed in and out of the Persian Gulf since the war started. It remains to be seen how broadly Iran might interpret a ban on them, as the complex nature of vessel ownership and management means some shipping companies with US or Israeli ownership or investment could be viewed as having links to those countries.
自战争开始以来，只有少数悬挂美国或以色列国旗的商船进出波斯湾。伊朗对禁止这些船只通行的解释范围尚待观察，因为船舶所有权和管理的复杂性意味着，一些拥有美国或以色列所有权或投资的航运公司可能被视为与这些国家存在关联。

Trump has insisted for months that navigation through Hormuz must be resumed, threatening fresh attacks on Iran if it fails to reopen the waterway. Any temporary routes through the strait won’t be subject to approvals, permissions, tolls or charges, a US official said Thursday in response to reports on the latest proposals.
特朗普数月来一直坚称，必须恢复通过霍尔木兹海峡的航行，并威胁称，如果伊朗不重新开放这条水道，将对其发动新的攻击。一名美国官员周四在回应有关最新提议的报道时表示，任何通过该海峡的临时航线都无需获得批准、许可、缴纳通行费或费用。

Tehran says a full reopening would require the lifting of the American maritime blockade, something the US hasn’t indicated whether it’s ready to do.
德黑兰方面表示，全面重新开放需要美国解除海上封锁，而美国尚未表明是否准备这样做。

Fars, citing an informed source at Iran’s Foreign Ministry, said the draft agreement with Oman foresees Iranian management over the entry of vessels into Hormuz, with the exit being overseen jointly by both countries. They envisage transit through a “central corridor,” with traffic via the two other routes that are currently in limited use to cease within a “specified timeframe,” the agency reported.
法尔斯通讯社援引伊朗外交部知情消息人士的话称，与阿曼达成的协议草案设想由伊朗管理船只进入霍尔木兹海峡，而船只驶出则由两国共同监督。该机构报道称，双方设想通过一条“中央走廊”实现通行，目前有限使用的另外两条航线的交通将在“规定时间内”停止。

Trump, who stepped back from his most recent threats to resume military attacks on the Islamic Republic, said Thursday that the US is involved in negotiations, but declined to say whether an agreement had been reached.
特朗普周四表示，美国正在参与谈判，但拒绝透露是否已达成协议。他此前曾收回最近一次威胁，即恢复对伊朗伊斯兰共和国的军事打击。

“We control it, but they can always shoot something,” Trump said of the vital waterway earlier Thursday.
“我们控制着它，但他们随时可能发动攻击，”特朗普周四早些时候谈及这条关键水道时表示。

Many previous claims of an imminent deal have proven unfounded, and it’s not clear if the Iran-Oman proposals will be enough to get traffic flowing again. On Thursday, Iran’s lead negotiator with the US, Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf, [mocked](https://x.com/mb_ghalibaf/status/2085433285602136467) the White House for repeatedly making threats and then climbing down, calling it “theater diplomacy on loop.”
此前许多关于协议即将达成的说法均被证实为不实，目前尚不清楚伊朗与阿曼的提议是否足以恢复航运。周四，伊朗与美国谈判的首席代表穆罕默德·巴吉尔·卡利巴夫嘲讽白宫反复发出威胁后又退缩，称其为“循环上演的戏剧外交”。

Several potential obstacles to a durable peace deal have emerged as the war spread across the region.
随着战火蔓延至整个地区，达成持久和平协议的多个潜在障碍已经显现。

Those include attacks on Saudi Arabia by the Houthis, an Iran-backed militia that controls much of Yemen. The Yemeni group said it launched a “large-scale” attack against Saudi-aligned troops in the country’s central region on Thursday, claiming to have killed and injured hundreds.
其中包括胡塞武装对沙特阿拉伯发动的袭击。这支伊朗支持的民兵组织控制着也门大部分地区。该也门组织周四表示，他们对沙特联军在也门中部地区的部队发动了“大规模”攻击，并声称造成数百人伤亡。

The Associated Press reported that at least 30 troops of Yemen’s internationally recognized government had been killed in the strikes, citing officials from the government. The Saudi-led coalition in Yemen also said the militant group fired projectiles across the Saudi border into the region of Najran, close to Yemen’s northern border.
美联社援引也门政府官员报道称，至少 30 名也门国际公认政府的士兵在袭击中丧生。在也门作战的沙特领导联盟也表示，该武装组织向沙特边境发射了炮弹，落入靠近也门北部边境的纳季兰地区。

The Houthis and the Saudi-led coalition URGENT: Houthi rebel attacks kill at least 30 Yemeni government forces, officials say a ceasefire since 2022, but hostilities have erupted in recent weeks. These included incidents in the Red Sea, from where the majority of Saudi Arabia’s oil exports have departed since the closure of the Strait of Hormuz.
胡塞武装与沙特领导联盟紧急消息：胡塞武装叛乱分子袭击导致至少 30 名也门政府军士兵死亡，官员称自 2022 年以来双方一直维持停火，但近几周敌对行动再度爆发。其中包括红海地区的事件——自霍尔木兹海峡关闭以来，沙特阿拉伯大部分石油出口均从该海域启运。

Visible traffic through the Strait of Hormuz remains limited, with only limited cargoes from Middle Eastern producers ferried through as part of a shuttle program involving help from the US military and vessels turning off their transponders.
