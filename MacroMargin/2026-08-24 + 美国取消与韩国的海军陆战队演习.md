## 美国取消与韩国的海军陆战队演习

## **美国取消与韩国的海军陆战队演习**

### 取消训练是美国因中东战争需求更大而在亚洲的最新一次收缩

Timothy W. Martin

2026年8月24日 美国东部时间上午7:36

![A US soldier guides a military vehicle during joint US-South Korean military exercises.](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3c681cc90b1481dc8d41e32c6ea70fe9/3c681cc9-0b14-81da-8bdd-ffd645392934-3bafdeee40dba9c5b9335ec7b01d663b.jpg)

上周在韩国举行的美韩联合军演。图片来源：YONHAP/AFP/Getty Images

- 美国取消了原定于下月与韩国举行的“双龙”两栖演习。
- 韩国军方表示，美国以中东需求为由，于6月通知韩方取消演习。
- 此次取消是在特朗普总统下令提前停止今年夏天的“乙支自由之盾”演习之后。
- 美国取消了原定于下月与韩国举行的“双龙”两栖演习。
首尔——美国取消了原定于下月与[韩国](https://www.wsj.com/topics/place/south-korea)举行的大规模两栖演习，这是伊朗战争后美国在亚洲的最新收缩。

所谓的“双龙”演习曾有数千名美国和韩国部队在韩国沿海城市实施抢滩登陆。过去，这项由两国海军陆战队参加的演习旨在让盟友相信华盛顿对该地区的承诺。

这一举措是美国在亚洲收缩的最新例证。今年3月，驻日美军逾2000名海军陆战队员被[调往中东](https://www.wsj.com/world/middle-east/us-strait-of-hormuz-ship-escort-95d6daf0?mod=article_inline)。韩国总统曾公开[反对](https://www.wsj.com/livecoverage/iran-war-us-israel-trump-2026/card/south-korea-opposes-u-s-moving-air-defense-assets-to-middle-east-am08UuKHkLsY8uS62zKf?mod=article_inline)今年春天美国从韩国撤走防空资产。以太平洋为基地的美国航母“乔治·华盛顿”号刚刚在中东[替换](https://www.wsj.com/politics/pentagon-rotates-aircraft-carriers-showing-toll-of-the-iran-war-4fe8a57e?mod=article_inline)了已部署超过250天的“亚伯拉罕·林肯”号。

周一，韩国军方表示，“双龙”演习将被取消。韩国军方还表示，美国在6月已通知韩国，理由是中东的需求。驻韩美军没有置评。

韩国刚刚看到今年夏天与美国的“乙支自由之盾”演习被突然缩短，此前[特朗普总统下令](https://www.wsj.com/politics/national-security/trump-orders-cuts-to-u-s-south-korea-military-drills-2bc63cec?mod=article_inline)提前停止演习。特朗普曾称此类联合演习成本高昂、不合时宜，且对朝鲜具有敌意，他表示朝鲜在他的第二个任期内表现“无威胁且尊重”态度。

朝鲜[冷落了这一举措](https://www.wsj.com/world/asia/after-trump-decree-u-s-south-korea-pare-back-drills-that-angered-kim-jong-un-149e3350?mod=article_inline)，称这[不会为两国带来任何善意](https://www.wsj.com/world/asia/trump-wants-a-summit-that-kim-jong-un-doesnt-need-b9342293?mod=article_inline)。

民主党和共和党都警告称，缩减“乙支自由之盾”演习可能损害与韩国的同盟关系，并呼吁恢复演习。这一训练原定持续11天，却提前六天于上周五结束。

双龙演习此前自2018年起暂停，当时特朗普曾提出类似缩减美韩联合演习的要求。演习于2023年恢复。

次年，联合演习出动了超过13,000名人员、20多艘海军舰艇、30架飞机和40辆两栖突击车。美国海军表示，演习还包括旨在“演练消灭敌军”的陆上行动。

## U.S. Cancels Marines Drills With South Korea

## **U.S. Cancels Marines Drills With South Korea**

### Dropped training is latest U.S. pullback in Asia owing to bigger demands from wars in the Middle East

By

Timothy W. Martin

Aug. 24, 2026 7:36 am ET

A joint U.S.-South Korean military exercise in South Korea last week. YONHAP/AFP/Getty Images

- The U.S. has canceled the Twin Dragons amphibious exercise with South Korea, which was scheduled for next month.
- Seoul’s military said the U.S. informed South Korea of the cancellation in June, citing demands from the Middle East.
- The cancellation follows an order from President Trump to halt this summer’s Ulchi Freedom Shield exercises early.
- The U.S. has canceled the Twin Dragons amphibious exercise with South Korea, which was scheduled for next month.
SEOUL—The U.S. has scrapped a large-scale amphibious exercise with [South Korea](https://www.wsj.com/topics/place/south-korea) set for next month, the latest American pullback in Asia in the wake of the war in Iran.

The so-called Twin Dragons exercises have featured thousands of American and South Korean forces carrying out a beachfront landing at a South Korean coastal city. In the past, the drill—involving Marines from both countries—sought to reassure allies of Washington’s commitment to the region.

Now it is the most recent example of Asian pullbacks from the U.S. More than 2,000 U.S. Marines stationed in Japan were [transferred to the Middle East](https://www.wsj.com/world/middle-east/us-strait-of-hormuz-ship-escort-95d6daf0?mod=article_inline) in March. South Korea’s president publicly [opposed the withdrawal](https://www.wsj.com/livecoverage/iran-war-us-israel-trump-2026/card/south-korea-opposes-u-s-moving-air-defense-assets-to-middle-east-am08UuKHkLsY8uS62zKf?mod=article_inline) of U.S. air defense assets this spring from his country. A Pacific-based American aircraft carrier, the USS George Washington, just [replaced in the Middle East](https://www.wsj.com/politics/pentagon-rotates-aircraft-carriers-showing-toll-of-the-iran-war-4fe8a57e?mod=article_inline) the USS Abraham Lincoln, which had been deployed for more than 250 days.

On Monday, Seoul’s military said the Twin Dragons drills would be canceled. The U.S. had informed South Korea in June, citing demands from the Middle East, it added. U.S. Forces Korea didn’t comment.

South Korea just saw an abrupt curtailment of this summer’s “Ulchi Freedom Shield” exercises with the U.S., following [an order from President Trump](https://www.wsj.com/politics/national-security/trump-orders-cuts-to-u-s-south-korea-military-drills-2bc63cec?mod=article_inline) to halt the drills early. Trump had called such combined exercises costly, inappropriate and hostile to North Korea, who he said had behaved in an “unthreatening and respectful” manner during his second term.

North Korea [snubbed the move](https://www.wsj.com/world/asia/after-trump-decree-u-s-south-korea-pare-back-drills-that-angered-kim-jong-un-149e3350?mod=article_inline), saying it [wouldn’t create any goodwill](https://www.wsj.com/world/asia/trump-wants-a-summit-that-kim-jong-un-doesnt-need-b9342293?mod=article_inline) between the two countries.

Both Democrats and Republicans have warned that the pullback on Ulchi Freedom Shield drills risked undermining the alliance with South Korea, calling for the exercises to be restored. The training, originally set for 11 days, ended six days early on Friday.

The Twin Dragons drills were previously paused starting in 2018, when Trump made similar calls to scale back U.S.-South Korea exercises. They resumed in 2023.

The following year, the joint drills featured more than 13,000 personnel, over 20 naval vessels, 30 aircrafts and 40 amphibious-assault vehicles. The exercises also included land-based operations designed “to rehearse eliminating enemy forces,” the U.S. Navy said.
