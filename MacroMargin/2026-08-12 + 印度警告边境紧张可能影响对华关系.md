## 印度警告边境紧张可能影响对华关系

## 印度警告边境紧张可能影响对华关系

*2023年，印度阿鲁纳恰尔邦金泽马内的一处边境哨所。摄影师：Arun Sankar/AFP/Getty Images*

作者：[Sudhi Ranjan Sen](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AUpqObttH3w/sudhi-ranjan-sen)
2026年8月11日晚上11:15 GMT+8
*更新于2026年8月12日晚上7:26 GMT+8*

印度政府表示，与中国的边境发生的任何事件都被视为“严重”，以回应社交媒体上关于两国军队在印度阿鲁纳恰尔邦可能发生对峙的猜测。

外交部发言人兰德希尔·贾斯瓦尔在周二的每两周一次的记者吹风会上没有直接回答有关该问题的提问。在随后的一份声明中，该部未确认是否发生了事件，但警告称稳定的边境对于两国更广泛的关系至关重要。

“关于印中边境地区的问题，我们始终在与中方的讨论中强调，我们认为这些问题最为严重，维护这些地区的和平与安宁至关重要，”声明称。“我们还表示，边境事务的状况将反映在我们更广泛双边关系的状况上。”

近日，阿鲁纳恰尔邦一些内容创作者在社交媒体上发布的视频称中国军队侵入印度领土，这些视频迅速走红，但无法得到独立核实。

阿鲁纳恰尔邦首席部长佩马·坎杜上周表示，政府将核实这些报道，同时对印度陆军沿边境的部署表示信心，据边境居民称阿鲁纳恰尔邦是“第一道防线力量”：首席部长。

印度国防部和印度陆军未回应寻求评论的消息。针对彭博新闻社的提问，中国外交部发言人周三表示，当前边境局势总体稳定。

中印两国共享一条长达3488公里（2167英里）的未划定边界，该边界近年来一直是紧张局势的根源。2020年6月的一场边境冲突导致双方多名士兵死亡，并使这两个拥有核武器的邻国之间的关系破裂。自一年前印度总理纳伦德拉·莫迪在天津会见中国国家主席习近平以来，两国关系稳步改善，两国间的直飞航班也已恢复。

中国称为藏南的阿鲁纳恰尔邦仍是两国间的争议领土。本周早些时候，北京[谴责](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-10/china-slams-india-for-naming-sites-in-disputed-border-region)印度正式公布争议边境地区27个地点的名称，包括山口、定居点和一个湖泊。

杰斯瓦尔周二表示，印度在最近一次边境事务联合军事和外交工作委员会会议上重申了其关于边境问题的立场。

根据印度外交部（MEA）的声明，“印度再次强调，边境地区的和平与安宁对于双边关系的全面发展至关重要。”

中国外交部表示，双方在联合委员会会议期间同意通过外交和军事渠道保持沟通，共同维护边境地区的和平与稳定。

这一紧张局势出现之际，距离习近平预计将于9月访问印度出席金砖国家峰会还有数周，此次峰会由新德里作为该集团轮值主席国主办。

## India Warns Border Strains Could Weigh on China Relations

## India Warns Border Strains Could Weigh on China Relations

*An Indian border post in Khinzemane, Arunachal Pradesh state, in 2023. Photographer: Arun Sankar/AFP/Getty Images*

By [Sudhi Ranjan Sen](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AUpqObttH3w/sudhi-ranjan-sen)
August 11, 2026 at 11:15 PM GMT+8
*Updated on August 12, 2026 at 7:26 PM GMT+8*

India’s government said any incident at its border with China was considered “serious,” in response to social media speculation of a possible standoff between the two militaries in India’s Arunachal Pradesh.

Randhir Jaiswal, spokesman for the Ministry of External Affairs, didn’t directly respond to a question on the matter during a biweekly briefing to reporters on Tuesday. In a subsequent statement, the ministry didn’t confirm whether an incident had occurred but warned that a stable border was important for broader ties between the two countries.

“In matters relating to the border areas between India and China, we have always emphasized in discussions with the Chinese side that we consider these issues as most serious and that the maintenance of peace and tranquility in these areas is of the utmost importance,” according to the statement. “We have also stated that the state of the border affairs will reflect on the state of our larger bilateral ties.”

Social media videos by some content creators in Arunachal Pradesh have gone viral in recent days alleging incursions by Chinese military into India’s territory. The videos couldn’t be independently verified.

Pema Khandu, chief minister of Arunachal Pradesh, said last week that the government would verify the reports, while expressing confidence in the Indian Army’s deployment along the border, according to Border residents of Arunachal 'first line of strength': CM.

India’s Ministry of Defense and the Indian Army didn’t respond to messages seeking comment. In response to a question from Bloomberg News, a spokesperson for China’s Ministry of Foreign Affairs said Wednesday the border situation is generally stable at present.

China and India share a 3,488 kilometer-long (2,167 mile) unmarked border that has been a source of tensions in recent years. A border clash in June 2020 led to the deaths of several soldiers on both sides and raptured ties between the two nuclear-armed neighbors. Relations have been steadily improving since Prime Minister Narendra Modi met Chinese President Xi Jinping in Tianjin a year ago, with direct flights between the two countries resuming.

Arunachal Pradesh, which China calls Zangnan, remains disputed territory between the two countries. Earlier this week, Beijing [condemned](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-10/china-slams-india-for-naming-sites-in-disputed-border-region) India for formally announcing names for 27 sites, including mountain passes, settlements and a lake, in the disputed border area.

Jaiswal said Tuesday that India reiterated its position about the border during a recent meeting of the joint military and diplomatic working committee on border affairs.

“India again underlined that peace and tranquillity in the border areas is essential for the overall development of bilateral ties,” according to the MEA’s statement.

China’s MOFA said the two sides had agreed during the joint committee meeting to maintain communication through diplomatic and military channels and jointly maintain peace and stability in the border areas.

The tension comes weeks before Xi’s expected visit to India for the BRICS summit in September, which New Delhi is hosting as the group’s rotating chair.
