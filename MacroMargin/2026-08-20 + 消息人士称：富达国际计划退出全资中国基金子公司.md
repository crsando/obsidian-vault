### 中文译文

## 消息人士称：富达国际计划退出全资中国基金子公司

2022年2月24日，中国上海城市天际线和黄浦江的景观。路透社/Aly Song/档案照片

香港，8月20日（路透社）——两位知情人士称，富达国际（FIL）计划退出其全资中国基金子公司，这标志着全球资产管理公司从世界第二大经济体的最大撤退之一。

据这两位因退出计划未公开而要求匿名的知情人士称，总部位于伦敦的FIL在全球管理着1.18万亿美元客户资产，目前正在考虑在其境内基金子公司成立三年后全面撤出。

上述人士称，激烈的本土竞争、频繁的领导层更迭以及长期难以扩大规模，最终使FIL全球高管认为，中国零售业务难以为继。

计划退出凸显了外国金融机构在中国面临的日益增大的逆风。自2020年北京允许外资全资子公司以来，利润率收窄阻碍了扩张，吸引了包括富达和贝莱德在内的六家全球资产管理公司设立新的在岸业务[(BLK.N), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/BLK.N)。

"中国仍然是富达国际的重要市场，我们继续相信它为我们的业务和投资者提供有吸引力的长期机会。目前没有任何关于我们战略或市场布局的变动需要报告，"FIL在给路透社的一份声明中表示。

总部位于波士顿的富达投资最初设立FIL作为其国际部门，后于1980年将其分拆为独立业务。FIL和富达投资均由美国女商人阿比盖尔·约翰逊担任董事长。

目前尚不清楚FIL计划如何重组或清算其14只中国零售基金产品，这些产品持有45亿元人民币（合6.7亿美元）资产。根据路透社查阅的一份2024年内部文件，这远低于2029年设定的目标。当时该公司认为，中国子公司至少需要140亿美元资产才能盈利。

最新发布的其中国产品报告显示，该子公司成立一年后，FIL的管理资产规模达到峰值60亿元人民币，随后截至6月底从峰值下降25%。

其中一位消息人士称，这家总部位于上海的子公司拥有近100名员工。

中国证监会向路透社表示，尚未收到FIL的正式退出申请。

近年来，北京方面[承诺](https://www.reuters.com/sustainability/boards-policy-regulation/chinas-securities-regulator-streamlines-scheme-foreign-investors-2025-10-27/)进一步向外国参与者开放金融市场，推出了放宽基金、银行和保险行业所有权壁垒的措施，简化许可程序并扩大投资范围。

### 其他资产管理公司撤退

FIL退出其中国基金子公司的任何计划都可能发生变化，并需要获得监管批准。然而，这一计划中的退出将代表过去十年外国资产管理公司从中国5.9万亿美元公募基金市场最显著的收缩之一。

工商登记记录显示，FIL迄今已向该子公司投入2.18亿美元，是所有外资全资基金公司中投入最多的，超过了贝莱德的2.15亿美元。

就在上个月，英国竞争对手施罗德 [(SDR.L), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/SDR.L) 成为首家退出其全资在岸基金子公司的外国资产管理公司——该子公司管理着2.5亿美元的本土资产——根据两家公司的公告，施罗德在规模扩张困难中将产品出售给了路博迈(Neuberger Berman)。

施罗德（Schroders）此举之前，其他同行也已撤退，包括 Legal & General [(LGEN.L)，打开新标签页](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/LGEN.L)，该公司叫停了在华扩张计划；还有先锋领航（Vanguard），关闭了其本地基金销售合资企业，并放弃了进入公募基金行业的计划。

任何富达国际（FIL）退出中国内地公募基金市场的举措，都将为其在中国更广泛、渐进的撤退画上句号。中国经济在今年下半年初因工业产出和消费疲软而[失去动力](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/chinas-industrial-output-slows-july-retail-sales-miss-forecasts-2026-08-17/)。

富达国际（FIL）于2024年底以数据问题为由[裁减](https://www.reuters.com/business/fidelity-international-cuts-500-jobs-china-sources-say-2024-10-21/)了其大连技术和运营中心约500个职位，而在此前一年早些时候，由于增长缓慢和成本压力，该公司已[裁减](https://www.reuters.com/business/finance/fidelity-international-plan-cut-16-china-fund-unit-jobs-sources-say-2024-03-19/)了其本地基金管理员工总数的16%。

这家中国基金管理业务自五年前成立以来，经历了快速的[领导层更替](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/fidelity-internationals-china-head-resigns-after-one-year-stint-2026-05-28/)，先后更换了四位董事长和三位首席执行官。

## Fidelity International plans to pull out of wholly owned China fund unit, sources say

A view of the city skyline and Huangpu River in Shanghai, China, on February 24, 2022. REUTERS/Aly Song/File Photo

HONG KONG, Aug 20 (Reuters) - Fidelity International (FIL) plans to exit its wholly owned China fund unit, two people familiar with the matter said, marking one of the biggest retreats by a ‌global asset manager from the world's second-largest economy.

London-headquartered FIL, which manages $1.18 trillion in client assets globally, is weighing a total retreat from its onshore fund unit three years after its launch, according to the two people who spoke on condition of anonymity because the exit plan is not public.

A combination of fierce local competition, frequent leadership turnover and chronic struggles to build scale ultimately convinced global FIL executives that the China retail venture was untenable, the people said.

The planned departure highlights the growing headwinds for foreign financial firms in China, with squeezed margins hampering expansion since Beijing allowed fully foreign-owned units in 2020, attracting six global asset managers to set ⁠up new onshore operations including Fidelity and BlackRock [(BLK.N), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/BLK.N).

"China remains an important market for Fidelity International and we continue to believe it offers attractive long-term opportunities both for our business and for investors. There is no change to report on our strategy or market presence," FIL told Reuters in a statement.

Boston-based Fidelity Investments originally set up FIL as its international unit, before spinning it off as an independent business in 1980. Both FIL and Fidelity Investments are chaired by American businesswoman Abigail Johnson.

It remains unclear how FIL plans to restructure or liquidate its 14 China retail fund products, which hold 4.5 billion yuan ($670 million) in assets. That's well below a target set for 2029, according to a 2024 internal document reviewed by Reuters. The firm believed then that the China unit needed at least $14 billion in assets to be profitable.

FIL assets under management peaked one year after the unit's launch at 6 billion yuan, before dropping 25% from there as of the end of June, the latest reports on its China products show.

The Shanghai-based unit employs ‌nearly 100 people, ⁠according to one of the sources.

The China Securities Regulatory Commission told Reuters it has not received any official withdrawal application from FIL.

Beijing has in recent years [pledged](https://www.reuters.com/sustainability/boards-policy-regulation/chinas-securities-regulator-streamlines-scheme-foreign-investors-2025-10-27/) to open its financial markets further to foreign players by rolling out measures to ease ownership barriers across funds, banks and insurance, simplifying licensing and expanding investment scopes.

### OTHER ASSET MANAGERS PULL BACK

Any FIL plan to exit its China fund unit would be subject to change and require regulatory approval. The planned exit would, however, represent one of the most prominent retrenchments from China's $5.9 trillion public fund market by a foreign ⁠asset manager over the last decade.

FIL has so far put $218 million into the unit, the largest among all foreign wholly owned fund houses, topping BlackRock's $215 million, business registration records showed.

Just last month, British rival Schroders [(SDR.L), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/SDR.L) became the first foreign manager to exit its wholly owned onshore fund unit — which managed $250 million in local assets — [offloading its products](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/schroders-seeks-permission-transfer-china-funds-us-asset-manager-neuberger-2026-05-20/) to Neuberger Berman amid scaling struggles, according to announcements from ⁠both firms.

The Schroders move followed earlier pullbacks by other peers, including Legal & General [(LGEN.L), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/LGEN.L), which halted its China expansion plans, and Vanguard, which closed its local fund sales joint venture and abandoned plans to enter the mutual fund sector.

Any FIL exit from China's onshore mutual fund market would cap a broader, gradual pullback in China, an economy which [lost momentum](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/chinas-industrial-output-slows-july-retail-sales-miss-forecasts-2026-08-17/) ⁠at the start of the second half of this year on weaker industrial output and consumption.

FIL [cut](https://www.reuters.com/business/fidelity-international-cuts-500-jobs-china-sources-say-2024-10-21/) about 500 positions at its Dalian technology and operations centre in late 2024 over data concerns, having already [cut](https://www.reuters.com/business/finance/fidelity-international-plan-cut-16-china-fund-unit-jobs-sources-say-2024-03-19/) 16% of the local fund management staff earlier that year amid sluggish growth and cost pressures.

The China fund management business has experienced rapid [leadership churn](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/fidelity-internationals-china-head-resigns-after-one-year-stint-2026-05-28/) since its inception five years ago, cycling through four board chairmen and three chief executives.
