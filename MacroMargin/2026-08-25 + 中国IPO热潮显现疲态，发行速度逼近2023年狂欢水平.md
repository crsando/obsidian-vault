### 中文译文

## 中国IPO热潮显现疲态，发行速度逼近2023年狂欢水平

中国火爆的首次公开募股市场正显示出其飞速节奏可能难以持续的迹象。

在围绕人工智能的热情推动下，企业正以2023年以来最快的速度融资，发行规模已接近当时降温措施出台前引发多年沉寂的水平。近期备受瞩目的交易难以守住初始涨幅，而庞大的IPO管道重新引发了流动性担忧。一些备受期待的交易上市可能比原先预期的更耗时。

自7月初以来，募资额已超过1190亿元人民币（合177亿美元），高于2023年第三季度的1140亿元人民币。三年前，这一里程碑标志着中国IPO市场的一个[转折点](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2023-08-03/china-s-ipo-market-seen-cooling-as-beijing-targets-liquidity)。随着北京承诺提振资本市场，监管机构也采取措施抑制IPO热潮，以应对流动性流失的担忧。这些措施实际上为此前放宽上市条件的[改革](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2023-04-10/china-s-first-ipos-under-new-relaxed-rules-surge-on-debuts)踩下了刹车。

迄今为止，投资者的热情仍足以吸纳大量发行。但随着科技板块动能开始显现中国IPO热潮掩盖市场疲态：市场承压，市场吸收新增供应的能力正受到考验。

“自6月人工智能交易回落以来，资金一直在从市场流出，”福建通恒投资基金经理杨庭武表示。“公募基金、量化基金和散户投资者既没有能力也没有意愿加仓，国家队资金在很大程度上已是净卖出。在没有增量资金流入的市场中，这种规模的IPO发行很难消化。”

![图片](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3c781cc90b1481e78b3bcc9000005b9d/3c781cc9-0b14-8160-a131-e6aba011f52a-3d996728c44e09da8e985186daed56e3.png)

中国股市连同香港市场，已成为人工智能建设融资的引擎，企业争相出售股票以扩大产能，并主要与美国竞争对手竞争。据彭博汇编的数据，随着长鑫存储公司（CXMT Corp.）[接近纪录的股票发售](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-26/china-memory-champion-cxmt-set-to-debut-after-9-8-billion-ipo)等交易，今年IPO募资额自2023年以来首次突破300亿美元。

当然，除了长鑫存储交易外，还有大型IPO进入市场，而该交易本身就占了总募资额的近三分之一。[宇树科技](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-18/unitree-robotics-set-to-debut-after-904-million-shanghai-ipo)上周在上海首秀，而[华润新能源](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-23/cr-new-energy-s-record-ipo-gets-strong-retail-demand-in-shenzhen)完成了深交所历史上最大的上市。

在备受关注的即将进行的交易中，[上海燧原科技股份有限公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1800178D:CH)正[推进IPO](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-15/tencent-backed-enflame-heads-to-ipo-as-china-ai-chip-wave-grows)，而[闪存制造商](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-19/cxmt-rival-ymtc-moves-closer-to-china-s-next-marquee-chip-ipo)[长江存储科技有限责任公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1577197D:CH)筹集330亿元人民币的申请已于周五获上交所受理并进入审核。

到目前为止，还没有官方沟通表明当局正准备收紧IPO。与2023年的交易浪潮相比，许多大型发行都与半导体和人工智能等资本需求巨大的战略行业相关。当局也已明确表示，将[继续](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-17/china-watchdog-urges-more-ipos-from-ai-hong-kong-listed-firms)支持包括本土大语言模型在内的高质量科技公司上市。

然而，疲劳迹象正在显现。本季度近三分之一的上市公司从上市后高点跌去了一半以上的价值。Unitree较其在[首日暴涨](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-18/unitree-robotics-set-to-debut-after-904-million-shanghai-ipo)交易中创下的盘中高点下跌了45%。

监管机构可能已经对活动节奏感到不安。[当地媒体报道](https://api3.cls.cn/b/2462580)称，交易所最近与券商开会讨论IPO申报文件的质量。当局迄今尚未对这些报道作出回应。

虽然与CXMT一样，YMTC被视为中国科技自主化进程中的战略关键参与者，但该公司并[未](https://www.sse.com.cn/listing/renewal/ipo/index_listing_detail.shtml?auditId=2251)像其规模更大的竞争对手那样走加速上市流程。目前没有交易走快速通道，迄今为止只有CXMT和Unitree使用过。

“保持这种节奏将严重损害市场的风险偏好，”福建通恒的杨表示。“幸运的是，监管机构仍控制着节奏，如果需要可以进行调整。”

## China’s IPO Rush Is Showing Strain With Pace Nearing 2023 Frenzy

China’s high-powered market for initial public offerings is showing signs that its breakneck pace could be difficult to sustain.

Fueled by enthusiasm for all things artificial intelligence, companies are raising money at the fastest rate since 2023, with volumes at levels just before cooling measures back then ushered in a multiyear lull. Recent high-profile deals have struggled to hold on to initial gains, while a robust IPO pipeline has reanimated liquidity concerns. And some highly anticipated deals may be taking longer to come to market than previously thought.

Since the beginning of July, proceeds have exceeded 119 billion yuan ($17.7 billion), more than the 114 billion yuan in the third quarter of 2023. Three years ago, that milestone marked a [turning point](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2023-08-03/china-s-ipo-market-seen-cooling-as-beijing-targets-liquidity) in China’s IPO market. As Beijing pledged to boost capital markets, regulators also moved to curb the IPO frenzy to address concerns about liquidity drain. The measures effectively applied brakes to earlier [reforms](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2023-04-10/china-s-first-ipos-under-new-relaxed-rules-surge-on-debuts) that had made it easier for companies to list.

Investor enthusiasm has so far been buoyant enough to absorb the barrage of offerings. But as momentum in tech begins to China’s IPO Boom Masks a Market Running on Empty: Taking Stock, the market’s ability to take on fresh supply is being tested.

“Since the AI trade rolled over in June, liquidity has been draining from the market,” said Yang Tingwu, fund manager at [Fujian Tongheng Investment](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/US%20Equity). “Mutual funds, quants and retail investors alike have little capacity nor appetite to add, and state-backed funds have largely been net sellers. In a market without incremental inflows, this level of IPO issuance is hard to digest.”

China’s stock market, along with Hong Kong, has emerged as the engine of fundraising for the AI buildout, with companies racing to sell shares as they look to expand capacity and compete with mostly US competitors. With deals such as CXMT Corp.’s [near-record share sale](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-26/china-memory-champion-cxmt-set-to-debut-after-9-8-billion-ipo), IPO proceeds this year have surpassed $30 billion for the first time since 2023, according to data compiled by Bloomberg.

To be sure, large IPOs are coming to the market beyond the CXMT deal, which alone accounts for almost a third of the total fundraising. [Unitree Robotics](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-18/unitree-robotics-set-to-debut-after-904-million-shanghai-ipo) made its Shanghai debut last week, while [China Resources New Energy](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-23/cr-new-energy-s-record-ipo-gets-strong-retail-demand-in-shenzhen) completed the largest listing in the history of the Shenzhen exchange.

And in closely watched upcoming deals, [Shanghai Enflame Technology Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1800178D:CH) is [proceeding with an IPO](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-15/tencent-backed-enflame-heads-to-ipo-as-china-ai-chip-wave-grows), while [flash memory maker](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-19/cxmt-rival-ymtc-moves-closer-to-china-s-next-marquee-chip-ipo) [Yangtze Memory Technologies Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1577197D:CH)’s application to raise 33 billion yuan has was accepted for review by the Shanghai Exchange on Friday.

So far, there’s been no official communication to indicate that authorities are preparing a clamp down on IPOs. And in contrast with the wave of 2023 deals, many of the largest offerings are tied to strategic sectors such as semiconductors and AI, which have heavy capital needs. Authorities have also made it clear that they will [continue to](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-17/china-watchdog-urges-more-ipos-from-ai-hong-kong-listed-firms) support high-quality tech firms including homegrown large language models to list.

Still, signs of fatigue are emerging. Nearly a third of this quarter’s listings have lost more than half their value from post-debut peaks. Unitree is down 45% from its intraday high recorded during its [blistering first day](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-18/unitree-robotics-set-to-debut-after-904-million-shanghai-ipo) of trading.

Regulators may already be signaling discomfort with the pace of activity. [Local media reported](https://api3.cls.cn/b/2462580) that exchanges recently met with brokerages to discuss the quality of IPO filings. Authorities have so far haven’t reacted to the reports.

And while YMTC is seen as a strategically crucial player in China’s tech self sufficiency drive, much like CXMT, the company [didn’t](https://www.sse.com.cn/listing/renewal/ipo/index_listing_detail.shtml?auditId=2251) go through an accelerated listing process like its larger rival. Currently, there are no deals taking the expedited route, which has only been used by CXMT and Unitree so far.

“Keeping up this pace would seriously damage the market’s risk appetite, said Yang at Fujian Tongheng. “Fortunately, regulators still control the tempo and can make adjustments if needed.”
