### 中文译文

## 阿根廷续签190亿美元中国货币互换协议，期限再延五年

阿根廷央行与中国人民银行续签了为期五年的货币互换协议，规模约190亿美元，这为该国在2027年总统选举前保障了关键的储备来源。

货币当局周三在一份[声明](https://www.bcra.gob.ar/noticias/el-banco-central-de-la-republica-argentina-y-el-banco-de-la-republica-popular-de-china-renuevan-su-acuerdo-de-swap-y-extienden-el-plazo-de-3-a-5-anos/)中表示，此次续签涵盖全部互换额度，自2023年以来阿根廷一直在使用的已激活部分仍约为50亿美元。新协议的期限延长了两年。

央行表示：“该协议加强了双方机构的密切合作，以及自2009年首次签署协议以来的17年间所保持的卓越关系。”

央行行长圣地亚哥·鲍西利（Santiago Bausili）在6月由[国际清算银行](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/BIZ%20SW%20Equity)于上海举办的研讨会上会见了中国人民银行行长潘功胜。会前，鲍西利表示双方正在进行讨论，以按以往相同条件续签互换协议。

“我们正在与他们商谈延长事宜，一如既往。”鲍西利在5月于布宜诺斯艾利斯的新闻发布会上表示。“这继续保持稳定，近乎永久。”

米莱的左翼前任在2023年激活了部分互换额度，以支付欠[国际货币基金组织](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/13347Z:US)的款项，并在大选前为进口融资。根据最新的[央行数据](https://www.bcra.gob.ar/archivos/Pdfs/PublicacionesEstadisticas/informes/estados-contables-2025.pdf)，米莱已偿还了这50亿美元中的绝大部分，仅剩约6.8亿美元。

中美之间的地缘政治紧张曾一度给互换额度的续签带来疑问，尤其是在特朗普政府一名官员称这是一种[敲诈性安排](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-04-08/china-us-spat-over-18-billion-fx-swap-line-puts-milei-in-a-bind)、应当终止之后。尽管财长斯科特·贝森特在去年向米莱提供200亿美元救命钱后表示，米莱“承诺让中国离开阿根廷”，但他从未明确表示互换协议应终止。不过，贝森特确实表示，米莱的经济改革应给阿根廷带来足够的[外汇流入](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-04-14/bessent-expects-argentina-to-be-able-to-pay-off-china-swap-line)，以偿还已激活的50亿美元部分。

经济部长路易斯·卡普托（Luis Caputo）和外长巴勃罗·基尔诺（Pablo Quirno）预计将在两国解决贸易谈判后很快访华，米莱将另行出访。基尔诺在最近一次电台采访中表示。

米莱面临2027年底的连任竞选，而与此同时，该国必须偿还超过250亿美元的到期债务。在阿根廷，大选年历来会给央行带来压力，因为对美元的需求激增。

### English Original

## Argentina Renews $19 Billion China Swap for Another Five Years

Argentina secured a key source of reserves through the 2027 presidential election after the central bank renewed its roughly $19 billion currency swap with the People’s Bank of China for five years.

The renewal covers the entire swap line and the activated portion that Argentina has been using since 2023 remains at about $5 billion, the monetary authority said in a [statement](https://www.bcra.gob.ar/noticias/el-banco-central-de-la-republica-argentina-y-el-banco-de-la-republica-popular-de-china-renuevan-su-acuerdo-de-swap-y-extienden-el-plazo-de-3-a-5-anos/) Wednesday. The duration of the new deal has been extended by two years.

“This agreement strengthens the close cooperation between the two institutions and the excellent relationship they have maintained over the 17 years since the first agreement was signed in 2009,” the bank said.

Central Bank President Santiago Bausili met with PBOC Governor Pan Gongsheng during a June symposium hosted by the [Bank for International Settlements](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/BIZ%20SW%20Equity) in Shanghai. Ahead of the meeting, Bausili signaled the two sides were engaged in discussions to renew their swap under the same conditions as in the past.

“We’re talking with them to extend it, as always,” Bausili said at a May press conference in Buenos Aires. “This continues to be stable and quasi-permanent.”

Milei’s leftist predecessor activated a portion of the swap line in 2023 to make payments owed to the [International Monetary Fund](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/13347Z:US) and finance imports ahead of the election. Milei has already paid down the vast majority of those $5 billion, with only about $680 million remaining, according the latest [central bank figures](https://www.bcra.gob.ar/archivos/Pdfs/PublicacionesEstadisticas/informes/estados-contables-2025.pdf).

Geopolitical strains between the US and China at one point cast doubt on the renewal of the swap line, especially after a Trump administration official said it was an [extortionary arrangement](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-04-08/china-us-spat-over-18-billion-fx-swap-line-puts-milei-in-a-bind) that should come to an end. While Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said Milei was “committed to getting China out of Argentina” after throwing him a $20 billion lifeline last year, he never specified the swap should come to an end. Bessent did, however, say Milei’s economic reforms should give Argentina enough [foreign exchange inflows](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-04-14/bessent-expects-argentina-to-be-able-to-pay-off-china-swap-line) to pay off the active $5 billion portion.

Economy Minister Luis Caputo, alongside Foreign Affairs Minister Pablo Quirno, are expected to travel to China soon after the two countries settle trade talks ahead of a separate trip by Milei, Quirno said in a recent radio interview.

Milei faces a reelection bid in late 2027, at the same time as the country must pay more than $25 billion in debt maturities. Election years in Argentina have historically strained the central bank as demand for dollars surges.
