### 中文译文

## A2牛奶公司在中国配方奶粉供应问题后复苏缓慢

新西兰婴儿配方奶粉供应商a2牛奶公司首席执行官戴维·博尔托卢西表示，公司将需要12个月才能从今年早些时候影响其中国销售的供应链中断中完全恢复。

在公司的全年业绩公布后接受彭博采访时，博尔托卢西表示，该业绩表明截至12月的六个月盈利利润率将表现疲软，a2将推出两款新的中文标签产品，以帮助提振其在市场上的复苏。公司还将致力于恢复其现有中文标签业务失去的销售额和用户基础。

他说：“基础业务的复苏在财务影响方面将更具实质性。”

周一早些时候，a2表示，中文标签配方奶粉的短缺对截至6月的三个月里的市场供应造成了实质性影响，并导致大量现有用户转向其他品牌。该公司表示，造成这一问题的因素——包括货运挑战和生产积压——现已解决，产品供应已显著改善。

在产品供应受到打击之际，a2今年早些时候曾表示，其有望在中国扩大市场份额。在雀巢发现一种可产生名为蜡样芽孢杆菌毒素（cereulide）的细菌后，一些竞争对手供应商已在60多个国家面临召回，该毒素可引发婴儿突发恶心和呕吐。

该股在惠灵顿收跌5.7%，创5月初以来最大单日跌幅。

花旗集团分析师Sam Teeger在报告中表示：“从供应链中断中复苏所需时间将比预期更长，且2027财年的营收增长指引与该股的交易倍数相比显得逊色。”

a2表示，预计配方奶粉销售额将与上年同期大致持平，中文标签销售额将在截至2027年6月的财年内逐步复苏。该公司表示，英文标签销售额的增长势头预计将在上半年恢复。

### **供应链**

同时，首席执行官表示，公司正在寻找“独立机会”以在其他市场实现增长，美国和越南增长强劲。“从我们的角度来看，我们希望东南亚有更多越南市场，”博尔托卢西（Bortolussi）说。

博尔托卢西在早些时候与分析师的电话会议上表示，供应链问题之一是Synlait Milk的生产积压，该公司为a2生产配方奶粉。他表示这个问题已经解决，他对Synlait的供应没有担忧。

博尔托卢西告诉彭博社，公司计划将其广受欢迎的a2 Platinum产品生产从Synlait转移到公司新收购的Pokeno工厂，该工厂位于新西兰中部怀卡托地区。

他发表上述言论之际，澳大利亚一家报纸报道称a2和恒天然合作社集团（Fonterra Cooperative Group）正在考虑接管Synlait。博尔托卢西拒绝对此猜测置评，并告诉投资者，a2和Synlait有着长期、牢固的关系，“我们打算与他们长期合作。”

另外，Synlait在一份提交的文件中表示，它没有参与与a2或恒天然的任何讨论。

在集团层面，a2预计全年收入增长中个位数百分比，上半年收入与去年同期大体一致。该公司表示，盈利利润率预计约为15%，但上半年将“大幅低于”去年同期。

在截至2026年6月的12个月中，持续经营业务收入增长12%至19.7亿新西兰元（合11亿美元），而净利润下降5.8%。集团盈利利润率为14.4%，低于上年同期的16.6%。

这些结果不包括出售Mataura Valley Milk部门的9600万新西兰元亏损。包括该亏损，净收入同比下降43%。

该公司今天表示，中国标签配方奶粉的净销售收入同比下降14%，市场份额略有下降。中国英文标签配方奶粉的销售也因竞争对手不得不召回产品而受到干扰，但此后已恢复。

### English Original

## A2 Milk Sees Slow Recovery From China Formula Supply Problem

New Zealand infant formula supplier a2 Milk will take 12 months to fully recover from supply chain disruptions that impacted its China sales earlier this year, Chief Executive Officer David Bortolussi said.

Speaking to Bloomberg after the company’s full-year results, which signaled the earnings margins will be soft in the six months through December, Bortolussi said** **a2 would be bringing two new China-label products to help boost their recovery in the market. The company would also work on recovering the lost sales and user base in its existing China-label business.

"The recovery of the base business will be more substantive in terms of the financial impact," he said.

Earlier Monday, a2 said the shortfall on Chinese-label formula materially impacted in-market availability in the three months through June and saw a large proportion of existing users switch to alternative brands. The contributing factors — which included freight challenges and a production backlog — have now been resolved with product availability significantly improved, it said.

The hit to product availability came as a2 was indicating earlier this year that it was on track to grow market share in China. Some rival suppliers have faced recalls across more than 60 countries after Nestlé discovered bacteria that can produce a toxin called cereulide, triggering sudden nausea and vomiting in infants.

The stock closed down 5.7% in Wellington, its biggest one day slide since early May.

"The recovery from the supply chain disruption will take longer than expected and the revenue growth FY27 guidance is underwhelming compared to the multiple that the stock trades on," Citigroup Inc. analyst Sam Teeger said in a note.

Formula sales are expected to be broadly similar to the year-earlier with China-label sales gradually recovering over the course of the financial year through June 2027, a2 said. Momentum in English-label sales is expected to return in the first half, it said.

### **Supply Chain**

At the same time the chief executive said that the company was looking for "independent opportunities" to grow in other markets, with strong growth in the US and Vietnam. "We wish there were more Vietnam markets in Southeast Asia from our point of view," Bortolussi said.

Speaking on an earlier call with analysts, Bortolussi said one of the issues for the supply chain had been a backlog of production at Synlait Milk, which makes formula for a2. He said that had been resolved and he has no concerns with supply from Synlait.

Bortolussi told Bloomberg that the company was planning to shift production of its popular a2 Platinum product away from Synlait and into the company’s newly-acquired Pokeno facility, located in the central Waikato region of New Zealand.

His comments came as the Australian newspaper reported a2 and Fonterra Cooperative Group were contemplating taking over Synlait. Bortolussi declined to comment on the speculation, telling investors a2 and Synlait have a long, strong relationship and "we intend to partner with them in the long term."

Separately, Synlait said in a filing that it is not involved in any discussions with a2 or Fonterra.

At a group level, a2 expects a mid single-digit percent lift in full-year revenue, with first-half revenue broadly in line with the year earlier. The earnings margin is expected to be around 15% but in the first-half will be "materially down" on the year-earlier, it said.

In the 12 months through June 2026, revenue from continuing operations grew 12% to NZ$1.97 billion ($1.1 billion) while net income dropped 5.8%. The group’s earnings margin was 14.4% down from 16.6% a year earlier.

The results exclude a NZ$96 million loss on sale of its Mataura Valley Milk unit. Including that loss, net income fell 43% year on year.

The company today said net sales revenue from China-label formula fell 14% from the year earlier, while market share declined slightly. Sales of English-label formula in China were also disrupted when rivals had to recall product, but have since recovered.
