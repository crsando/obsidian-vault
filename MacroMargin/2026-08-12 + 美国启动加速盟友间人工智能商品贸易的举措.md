### 中文译文

## 美国启动加速盟友间人工智能商品贸易的举措

### **彭博AI要点**

- 特朗普政府宣布一项新计划，以加速美国与其最紧密贸易伙伴之间对发展人工智能至关重要的货物的运输。
- 美国国务院将在巴拿马创建一个试点平台，以快速跟踪对人工智能至关重要的产品的贸易，利用人工智能技术加速物流流程，并投入高达5000万美元的外国援助资金。
- 该项目旨在使巴拿马和其他参与经济体能够跟踪和加快人工智能基础设施、半导体和关键矿产等高价值货物在整个人工智能供应链中的运输。
特朗普政府宣布了一项新计划，旨在加速美国与其最紧密贸易伙伴之间对发展人工智能至关重要的货物的运输。

美国国务院周三表示，将在巴拿马创建一个试点平台，旨在快速跟踪对人工智能至关重要的产品的贸易，作为“Pax Silica”的一部分，这是一项由美国主导、旨在加强盟友之间供应链的倡议。该机构在一份声明中表示，将承诺投入高达5000万美元的外国援助资金来开发和部署该平台，该平台将利用人工智能技术加速物流流程。

“该项目预计将加快物流和合规操作，”美国国务院经济事务副国务卿雅各布·赫尔伯格在一封电子邮件中表示。“该项目侧重于使巴拿马和其他参与经济体能够跟踪和加快来自可信供应商的高价值货物在人工智能基础设施、半导体和关键矿产方面的运输，涵盖整个人工智能供应链。”

该公告标志着美国为加强与其盟友的供应链、减少其在人工智能和其他先进技术所用关键材料方面对中国的依赖而采取的最新举措。拜登政府也发起了一些倡议，包括“矿产安全伙伴关系”，旨在削弱北京对这一关键领域的控制——尽管迄今为止，美国的努力尚未对中国的主导地位产生实质性影响。

今年早些时候，特朗普政府表示，将为一个财团出资高达2.5亿美元，用于资助旨在扩大能源和关键矿物获取渠道的项目——总统多次强调这两个领域是国家安全的关键关切。

托运人目前采用传统纸质记录与数字系统相结合的方式，在入境口岸追踪货物，这给美国政府及其外国盟友耗费了大量时间和金钱。随着对官员和专家担心可能落入对手之手的敏感技术和知识产权的监管需求日益增长，美国希望人工智能辅助技术能够开始取代传统追踪方式。

巴拿马是[全球重要航运动脉](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-03-04/why-does-trump-want-the-panama-canal-back-and-can-he-take-it)，全球约3%的海上贸易经由其连接大西洋和太平洋的运河。赫尔伯格（Helberg）表示，由于他在5月份的到访以及巴拿马政府的强烈兴趣，该国被选为试点项目所在地。

Pax Silica 项目约有二十多个参与方，包括欧盟、英国、印度和阿联酋。

## US Launches Effort to Speed Trade in AI Goods Between Allies

### **Takeaways **by Bloomberg AI

- The Trump administration announced a new program to accelerate shipments of goods critical to developing artificial intelligence between the US and its closest trading partners.
- The State Department will create a pilot platform in Panama to fast track trade in products essential to AI, using AI technology to speed the logistics process, with up to $50 million in foreign assistance funding.
- The project aims to enable Panama and other participating economies to track and expedite shipments of high-value cargo for AI infrastructure, semiconductors, and critical minerals across the entire AI supply chain.
The Trump administration announced a new program aimed at accelerating shipments between the US and its closest trading partners of goods considered critical to developing artificial intelligence.

The State Department said Wednesday that it will create a pilot platform in Panama intended to fast track trade in products essential to AI as part of [Pax Silica](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-23/us-to-commit-250-million-toward-supply-chain-investment-fund), a US-led initiative created to strengthen supply chains between allies. In a statement, the agency said it’s committing up to $50 million in foreign assistance funding to develop and deploy the platform, which will use AI technology to speed the logistics process.

“The project is expected to accelerate logistics and compliance operations,” Under Secretary of State for Economic Affairs Jacob Helberg said in an email. “This project is focused on enabling Panama and other participating economies to track and expedite shipments from trusted suppliers of high-value cargo for AI infrastructure, semiconductors, and critical minerals across the entire AI supply chain.”

The announcement marks the latest US move to strengthen its supply chains with allies and reduce their reliance on China for crucial materials used in AI and other advanced technologies. The Biden administration also launched initiatives, including the Minerals Security Partnership, aimed at loosening Beijing’s grip on the crucial sector — though no US effort so far has had a meaningful impact on China’s dominance.

Earlier this year, the Trump administration said that it would contribute as much as $250 million toward a consortium to help fund projects aimed at boosting access to energy and critical minerals — two sectors that the president has repeatedly highlighted as key national security concerns.

Shippers use a combination of traditional pen-and-paper record-keeping alongside digital systems to track goods at ports of entry, costing the US government and foreign allies significant time and money. With the rising need to closely monitor custody of sensitive technology and intellectual property that officials and experts worry could fall into adversaries’ hands, the US is hoping AI-assisted technology can begin to replace conventional tracking methods.

Panama is a [vital global shipping artery](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-03-04/why-does-trump-want-the-panama-canal-back-and-can-he-take-it), with roughly 3% of all global maritime trade passing through its canal connecting the Atlantic and Pacific Oceans. Helberg said that Panama was chosen as the site of the pilot project following his visit there in May and based on strong interest from the country’s government.

The Pax Silica program has roughly two-dozen participants, including the European Union, the UK, India and United Arab Emirates.
