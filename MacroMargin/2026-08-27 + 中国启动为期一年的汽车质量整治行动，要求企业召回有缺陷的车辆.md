### 中文译文

## 中国启动为期一年的汽车质量整治行动，要求企业召回有缺陷的车辆

![Staff member attends to a customer inside a Tesla store at a shopping mall in Beijing](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3c981cc90b1481319285e5ca397b952b/3c981cc9-0b14-8183-a9a4-db1fc6b9d9ea-5f9d163c766ae4c313ffb95eea3f26b9.jpg)

图1/2 2026年5月12日，中国北京，一名工作人员在一家购物中心的特斯拉门店内为顾客服务。路透社/Tingshu Wang/资料图片

北京，8月27日（路透社）——中国监管机构周四要求汽车制造商在自我审查中发现任何缺陷时主动提交召回计划，这是这个全球最大汽车市场为提升汽车质量和安全而开展的为期一年行动的一部分。

特斯拉（[TSLA.O](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/TSLA.O)）和八家中国电动汽车制造商于8月21日[宣布修复措施](https://www.reuters.com/world/tesla-fix-software-millions-china-made-imported-evs-china-2026-08-21/)，涉及总计430万辆汽车，这是中国有史以来规模最大的汽车召回，原因是紧急车门释放机制的安全隐患。

根据该行动，汽车制造商必须对其运营及关键供应商的产品质量、可靠性、耐久性以及新技术的测试和验证进行全面检查。根据市场监管总局与工业和信息化部、公安部、生态环境部发布的联合声明，他们必须在2026年底前向当地主管部门提交报告。

企业必须及时向市场监管总局提交召回计划，并对缺陷产品实施自愿召回。地方主管部门需要加强监管，并在引入新汽车技术之前确保严格的安全验证。

审计还将审查高级驾驶辅助系统和自动驾驶系统的安全性，以及汽车制造商在功能安全、网络安全、数据安全、软件更新、事件监控和应急响应等领域的能力。

最新措施表明中国对汽车安全监管的态度日益坚定。

在全球第二大经济体整体放缓导致国内需求疲软的背景下，中国汽车行业已将其重点从通过长期价格战捍卫市场份额，转向提升产品质量和长期竞争力。

在官方指令发布后不久，中国汽车工业协会在一项单独的行业倡议中警告称，“非理性竞争”可能削弱生产一致性和产品安全，损害该行业的高质量发展。

报道：李巧伊、Liz Lee；编辑：Sharon Singleton

## China launches year-long auto quality drive, mandates defect recalls

Item 1 of 2 A staff member attends to a customer inside a Tesla store at a shopping mall, in Beijing, China, May 12, 2026. REUTERS/Tingshu Wang/File Photo

BEIJING, Aug 27 (Reuters) - Chinese regulators on Thursday ordered automakers to file proactive recall plans for any defects uncovered in self-audits, as part of a year-long campaign to improve vehicle quality and safety ‌in the world's largest auto market.

Tesla ([TSLA.O](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/TSLA.O)) and eight Chinese electric vehicle makers on August 21 [announced fixes](https://www.reuters.com/world/tesla-fix-software-millions-china-made-imported-evs-china-2026-08-21/) affecting a combined 4.3 million vehicles in China's largest-ever automotive recall, prompted by safety concerns involving emergency door-release mechanisms.

Under the campaign, automakers are required to conduct comprehensive inspections of product ⁠quality, reliability, durability and the testing and validation of new technologies across their operations and key suppliers. They must submit reports to local authorities by the end of 2026, according to a joint statement issued by the market regulator and the ministries of industry, public security, and environment.

Companies must promptly file recall plans with the market regulator and carry out voluntary recalls of defective products. Local authorities need to tighten oversight and ensure ‌rigorous ⁠safety validation before introducing new vehicle technologies.

The audits will also scrutinise the safety of advanced driver-assistance and autonomous-driving systems, as well as automakers' capabilities in areas including functional safety, cybersecurity, data security, software updates, incident monitoring and emergency response.

The latest ⁠measures signal China's increasingly assertive approach to auto-safety regulation.

As domestic demand weakens amid a broader slowdown in the world's second-largest economy, China's auto industry has shifted its ⁠focus from defending market share through a prolonged price war to improving product quality and long-term competitiveness.

In a separate industry initiative issued shortly after ⁠the official mandate, the China Association of Automobile Manufacturers warned that "irrational competition" could erode production consistency and product safety, undermining the sector's high-quality development.
