### 中文译文

## 中国银行业以隔夜利率为债券定价，央行改革扩大

中国的银行已开始根据国内隔夜融资成本为债券定价，这表明该成本正日益成为金融体系的基准。

招商银行股份有限公司（China Merchants Bank Co.）本周正在[发行](https://www.shclearing.com.cn/xxpl/fxpl/jrz/202608/t20260821_1849747.html)一只以隔夜债券回购协议（即回购）成本为基准的三年期浮动利率[票据](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/DO841224%40BGN%20%20Corp)。据彭博汇编的数据，此次发行最多可能筹集20亿元人民币（合2.98亿美元），将是国内商业银行首次进行此类发行。

本月早些时候，中国进出口银行（Export-Import Bank of China）——中国三大政策性银行之一——成为[首家](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MzI4OTM1MTA2Mw%3D%3D&mid=2247551265&idx=3&sn=2aae86736badd982ebedf0d09529c87a&chksm=ed8d4b4b0c7dc21dbd1c9aefa91aa76ac8e97abe64fe738f373a799bf291a895d320c0506720&mpshare=1&scene=1&srcid=08245KB9gF3F4DSIgT3YikG4&sharer_shareinfo=4836c8f99f3385424e04f100c241994a&sharer_shareinfo_first=a0f9b9ac7e55796c763d935b2a47cefe&exportkey=n_ChQIAhIQtw6XEjdVz6G5LSk8kn05lBL1AQIE97dBBAEAAAAAAG7zOU9vnyYAAAAOpnltbLcz9gKNyK89dVj0lXz0U6xq6A6s6kfHggYCUtMfKDuzwbGQdspDE%2B9CGmgWvaaVBEyJU51EBMfbBTuVH%2Bw%2BfK%2FyGRio7YSBQ0eDLkJ35wMVsAZ4rke5KjhavAvgxP4oEyNRL0EX2K6mzfAq2CPMTQiYVgsBNDzU6b7aloT4KLKVteMyRqzEQAqYDIbgXWWcfIzvTgWyd7iobRWFChaqJMTEMci%2FWdVKVI1cZt1S9eJUeF5FkL3Nmj3ncAwqn9fe0KPNyGphop0D8ErUgZzzrtqJQbPPXTufIbYz&acctmode=0&pass_ticket=6Y0V3D6i%2B7%2FMAxHY0us%2FScmF7xeDIQjONjnMppFWVPV4zZEWIeFFjILANpwhXR1h&wx_header=0#rd)使用同一种货币市场利率发行债券的机构。

在这些交易达成之际，越来越多的中国银行也开始[采用](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-24/chinese-banks-break-with-lending-benchmark-in-pricing-new-credit)隔夜回购利率为贷款定价。中国央行正在推动使用这一基准来锚定其金融体系，这是更广泛[改革](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-17/pboc-hints-at-new-policy-framework-focused-on-overnight-rate)的关键一环，旨在使其货币政策框架与全球主要同行保持一致。

![图片](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3c881cc90b1481d5ba82efc6232cb94b/3c881cc9-0b14-8160-a7bf-db1d3450216c-b7907e955094cd7cfcacb4f6316f911e.png)

渣打银行（Standard Chartered Bank）大中华区策略主管刘洁（Becky Liu）表示，这些举措"支持向新利率框架过渡，未来可能会更广泛地采用隔夜回购利率作为资产和负债的基准。"

刘洁补充称，如果这一转变有助于保持银行的息差，中国人民银行（People’s Bank of China）在放松政策方面将有更大的灵活性。

在本月早些时候发布的第二季度货币政策报告中，中国人民银行表示，其短期利率的"[操作目标](https://www.pbc.gov.cn/goutongjiaoliu/113456/113469/2026081218034520348/index.html)"已逐渐从七天期回购转向隔夜回购。

与此同时，近几个月来，中国人民银行扩大了货币市场流动性操作，将隔夜期限纳入其中，这一变化可能逐渐帮助央行更精准地引导资金供给。

上海清算所在进出口银行债券发行后的一份[声明](https://www.shclearing.com.cn/en/news/202608/t20260818_1847618.html?xyz=0.08783445386132571)中表示，以隔夜利率为定价基准的债券（在中国金融市场上以代码DR001著称）有助于扩大从货币市场到债券的利率传导渠道。它补充道，这也符合“使用隔夜无风险利率作为定价基准的全球市场规范”，这将有助于进一步提升人民币资产定价的国际化。

招商联合消费金融首席经济学家董希淼表示，银行在债券融资和贷款发放中尝试采用相同的利率锚，也可能有助于提高资产配置效率，因为它们的息差徘徊在历史低点附近。

### English Original

## China Banks Use Overnight Rate for Bonds as PBOC Reform Widens

Chinese lenders have started pricing bonds off the nation’s overnight funding cost, an indication that it’s increasingly the benchmark for the financial system.

China Merchants Bank Co. is [selling](https://www.shclearing.com.cn/xxpl/fxpl/jrz/202608/t20260821_1849747.html)a three-year floating-rate [note](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/DO841224%40BGN%20%20Corp) benchmarked against the cost of overnight bond repurchase agreements, or repos, this week. The sale, which may raise up to 2 billion yuan ($298 million), will be the first of its kind by a local commercial lender, according to data compiled by Bloomberg.

Earlier this month, the Export-Import Bank of China — one of the nation’s three policy lenders — became the [first](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MzI4OTM1MTA2Mw%3D%3D&mid=2247551265&idx=3&sn=2aae86736badd982ebedf0d09529c87a&chksm=ed8d4b4b0c7dc21dbd1c9aefa91aa76ac8e97abe64fe738f373a799bf291a895d320c0506720&mpshare=1&scene=1&srcid=08245KB9gF3F4DSIgT3YikG4&sharer_shareinfo=4836c8f99f3385424e04f100c241994a&sharer_shareinfo_first=a0f9b9ac7e55796c763d935b2a47cefe&exportkey=n_ChQIAhIQtw6XEjdVz6G5LSk8kn05lBL1AQIE97dBBAEAAAAAAG7zOU9vnyYAAAAOpnltbLcz9gKNyK89dVj0lXz0U6xq6A6s6kfHggYCUtMfKDuzwbGQdspDE%2B9CGmgWvaaVBEyJU51EBMfbBTuVH%2Bw%2BfK%2FyGRio7YSBQ0eDLkJ35wMVsAZ4rke5KjhavAvgxP4oEyNRL0EX2K6mzfAq2CPMTQiYVgsBNDzU6b7aloT4KLKVteMyRqzEQAqYDIbgXWWcfIzvTgWyd7iobRWFChaqJMTEMci%2FWdVKVI1cZt1S9eJUeF5FkL3Nmj3ncAwqn9fe0KPNyGphop0D8ErUgZzzrtqJQbPPXTufIbYz&acctmode=0&pass_ticket=6Y0V3D6i%2B7%2FMAxHY0us%2FScmF7xeDIQjONjnMppFWVPV4zZEWIeFFjILANpwhXR1h&wx_header=0#rd) to use the same money market rate to issue debt.

The deals come as more Chinese lenders have also begun [adopting](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-24/chinese-banks-break-with-lending-benchmark-in-pricing-new-credit) the overnight repo rate to price loans. The country’s central bank is cultivating the use of the benchmark to anchor its financial system, a key plank of broader [reforms](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-17/pboc-hints-at-new-policy-framework-focused-on-overnight-rate)to make its monetary policy framework resemble that of major global peers.

These steps “support transition toward a new interest rate framework, and will likely be followed by more broad-based adoption of the overnight repo rate as a benchmark for both assets and liabilities ahead,” said Becky Liu, head of Greater China strategy at Standard Chartered Bank.

The People’s Bank of China would have more flexibility to ease policy if the transition helps preserve banks’ interest margins, Liu added.

In its second-quarter monetary policy report released earlier this month, the PBOC said it has been gradually shifting toward the overnight repo, away from its seven-day counterpart, as its “[operational target](https://www.pbc.gov.cn/goutongjiaoliu/113456/113469/2026081218034520348/index.html)” for short-term interest rates.

Meanwhile, the PBOC has expanded its money market liquidity operations to feature the overnight tenor in recent months, a change that may gradually help the central bank steer the supply of funds with greater precision.

Bonds priced against the overnight rate, known for its DR001 code in Chinese financial markets, help expand the interest-rate transmission channel from the money market to bonds, the Shanghai Clearing House said in a [statement](https://www.shclearing.com.cn/en/news/202608/t20260818_1847618.html?xyz=0.08783445386132571) following the Export-Import Bank debt sale. It’s also aligned with “the global market norm of using overnight risk-free rates as pricing benchmarks,” which will help further elevate the internationalization of yuan asset pricing, it added.

Banks’ experiment with adopting the same rate anchor in bond financing and loan issuance could also help them improve asset allocation efficiency, as their interest margins hover around record lows, said Dong Ximiao, chief economist at Merchants Union Consumer Finance.
