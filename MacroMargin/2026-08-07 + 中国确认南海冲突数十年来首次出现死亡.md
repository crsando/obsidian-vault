### 中文译文

## 中国确认南海冲突数十年来首次出现死亡

中国已追授两名去年在南海“维护海洋权益”时牺牲的海警官员荣誉，这似乎证实了自2001年与美国飞机相撞以来，中国首次在争议水域冲突中出现死亡。

据[官方网站](https://www.chinamartyrs.gov.cn/shengji_lsyml/shengji_dqlsyml/shengji_dqlsymlnamelist/shengji_dqlsymlnamedetail/index.html?id=5B7FA8DA3F7E11F10011FA163E4C80C5)，管理中国海警局的中国人民武装警察部队本周追认Yi Xinyu和Cheng Long为“烈士”。名单显示，两人均于2025年8月11日去世，年龄分别为21岁和25岁。

公告未说明他们的死亡细节。然而，同一天，一艘中国海警船和一艘中国人民解放军海军军舰在斯卡伯勒浅滩附近发生[碰撞](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-08-11/philippines-says-china-ship-targeted-vessel-with-water-cannon)，此前其中一艘船追赶了一艘菲律宾海岸警卫队船只。

据菲律宾海岸警卫队当时的一位发言人表示，碰撞对中国海警船的艏楼造成了“重大损坏”。菲律宾国家广播公司PTV发布的图片显示，中国船只的船头严重受损。事件发生后，北京方面未披露任何伤亡情况。

这些死亡似乎是自2001年以来，中国首次确认在南海行动或对抗中出现的死亡。2001年，中国人民解放军海军飞行员王伟的歼击机在上述水域上空与美国侦察机[相撞](https://www.globaltimes.cn/page/202503/1331246.shtml?id=11)后死亡。

北京和华盛顿就那次碰撞的责任归属争执不下，尽管美国最终发表了措辞谨慎的声明表示遗憾，而中国在几天后释放了其扣押在海南岛的24名美国机组人员。这一事件加深了北京对美国在该地区意图的怀疑，该地区自此成为两国之间的主要冲突点。

中国倾向于对军事行动中的伤亡保持低调。在2020年与印度发生致命边境冲突后，北京等了大约八个月才首次发布 [说明](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2021-02-19/china-unveils-new-details-of-deadliest-india-clash-in-decades) 关于阵亡中国士兵的信息。

### English Original

## China Confirms First Deaths in South China Sea Clash in Decades

China has posthumously honored two Coast Guard officers who died while “safeguarding maritime rights” in the South China Sea last year, appearing to confirm the first Chinese fatalities from a clash in the contested waters since a collision with a US aircraft in 2001.

The People’s Armed Police, which oversees the China Coast Guard, recognized Yi Xinyu and Cheng Long as “martyrs” this week, according to an [official website](https://www.chinamartyrs.gov.cn/shengji_lsyml/shengji_dqlsyml/shengji_dqlsymlnamelist/shengji_dqlsymlnamedetail/index.html?id=5B7FA8DA3F7E11F10011FA163E4C80C5). Both of them died on Aug 11, 2025, at age 21 and 25 respectively, the listing showed.

The announcement didn’t describe the circumstances of their deaths. On the same day, however, a China Coast Guard vessel and a People’s Liberation Army Navy warship [collided](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-08-11/philippines-says-china-ship-targeted-vessel-with-water-cannon) near Scarborough Shoal after one of them chased a Philippine Coast Guard ship.

The collision caused “substantial damage” to the Coast Guard vessel’s forecastle, according to a Philippine Coast Guard spokesperson at the time. Images published by the Philippine state broadcaster PTV showed the bow of the Chinese vessel badly damaged. Beijing didn’t disclose any casualties after the incident.

The deaths appear to be China’s first acknowledged fatalities from an operation or confrontation in the South China Sea since 2001, when PLA Navy pilot Wang Wei died after his fighter jet [collided](https://www.globaltimes.cn/page/202503/1331246.shtml?id=11) with a US reconnaissance aircraft over the waters.

Beijing and Washington disputed responsibility for that collision, although the US ultimately issued a carefully worded statement expressing regret, and China U.S. Navy Plane Crew Arrives in Guam as Standoff Ends (Update3) the 24 American crew members it had held on Hainan island days after. The episode deepened Beijing’s suspicions of US intentions in the region, which has since become a major flashpoint between the two powers.

China prefers to keep a low profile on casualties from its military operations. After a deadly border clash with India in 2020, Beijing waited about eight months before releasing its first [account](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2021-02-19/china-unveils-new-details-of-deadliest-india-clash-in-decades) of the Chinese troops who were killed.
