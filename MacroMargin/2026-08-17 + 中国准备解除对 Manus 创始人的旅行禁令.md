### 中文译文

## 中国准备解除对 Manus 创始人的旅行禁令

北京计划很快解除对 Manus 创始人的旅行禁令，这家 AI 智能体公司正在解除 Meta 对其 20 亿美元的收购。

据两位知情人士透露，[Manus](https://www.ft.com/stream/e412f2b1-dbd9-44c0-a6d1-97309d3319ce) 的首席执行官兼联合创始人肖宏（Xiao Hong）最近告诉员工，他计划很快返回新加坡，该智能体平台的总部设在新加坡。

肖宏和 Manus 管理层，包括首席科学家季一超（Ji Yichao），在 3 月被传唤到北京，接受有关这家初创公司向美国科技集团 Meta 出售交易的调查，以确定是否违反投资规则，之后被[限制离开](https://www.ft.com/content/d9123d9d-c807-41d6-8a17-80ff1111834a?syn-25a6b1a6=1)中国。

Manus 本周宣布，它将很快恢复为独立业务运营。该公司周二在其网站上表示：“这是我们与 Meta 分离的一部分；我们必须采取这一步，以遵守世界特定地区的监管要求。”

解除收购计划需要以国家发展和改革委员会（NDRC）为首的监管机构的最终批准，该委员会是中国强大的国家规划机构，在 4 月[阻止了 Meta 的收购](https://www.ft.com/content/1e4c269a-5258-406c-a308-e55c3d5d640f?syn-25a6b1a6=1)。

知情人士表示，北京监管机构已告知 Manus，一旦解除收购的交易以符合所有要求的方式达成，其业务运营不会受到影响。

北京的举措反映了中国在加强对科技和人工智能行业管控的同时，也允许企业和企业家在遵守规则的前提下发展业务的细致态度。

Manus 首席科学家季一超被禁止离开中国 © Manus

在这项撤销交易中，Manus 的大多数前投资者，包括[腾讯](https://www.ft.com/stream/26272ebc-a21c-450e-a743-be89c41d1eb8)、真格基金和私募股权公司 HSG，以及管理层，将以约 20 亿美元的相同估值从 Meta 回购公司，英国《金融时报》上月[报道](https://www.ft.com/content/0d04378d-d71b-4225-b31a-70504e358480?syn-25a6b1a6=1)。

美国风险投资基金 Benchmark 以及几家较小的投资者不会参与此次回购，它们的股份将主要由腾讯收购，腾讯将成为 Manus 的最大股东。

腾讯将只持有少数股权，Manus 将继续在新加坡独立运营，而不是并入这家中国科技公司。

据其中一位知情人士透露，在接受调查期间，Manus 继续积极更新其平台，并计划在撤销交易完成后不久按计划推出下一个 2.0 版本。与 Meta 分离后，其年度经常性收入预计将保持在 3 亿美元以上。

Meta 去年 12 月收购了 Manus，就在该公司将总部和核心工程师从中国迁至新加坡几个月后，Manus 最初在中国成立。这家美国科技集团迅速将 Manus 整合到其平台中，包括其广告系统。

今年 4 月，中国[责令 Meta 撤销其收购](https://www.ft.com/content/1e4c269a-5258-406c-a308-e55c3d5d640f?syn-25a6b1a6=1)，理由是违反投资规则。英国《金融时报》早些时候报道，Meta 不久后开始分离相关业务并停止数据共享。

命令撤销这笔交易是对其他中国科技公司的警告，提醒它们不要利用新加坡作为最终向海外买家出售的中转站。

为了堵住这一漏洞，北京还在考虑将代理式人工智能添加到其下一次更新的出口管制清单中，英国《金融时报》上月[报道](https://www.ft.com/content/6049a031-9e9b-464c-97bb-414da04d5a6a?syn-25a6b1a6=1)。

Manus、国家发改委和商务部均未回应置评请求。

### English Original

## China poised to lift travel ban on Manus founders

Beijing plans to soon lift a travel ban imposed on the founders of Manus as the AI agent company unwinds its $2bn acquisition by Meta.

Xiao Hong, chief executive and a co-founder of [Manus](https://www.ft.com/stream/e412f2b1-dbd9-44c0-a6d1-97309d3319ce), recently told staff that he planned to return soon to Singapore, where the agentic platform is based, according to two people with knowledge of the matter.

Xiao and Manus management, including chief scientist Ji Yichao, were [restricted from leaving](https://www.ft.com/content/d9123d9d-c807-41d6-8a17-80ff1111834a?syn-25a6b1a6=1) China after being summoned to Beijing in March for an investigation into the start-up’s sale to US tech group Meta to determine whether investment rules were breached.

Manus this week announced it would soon return to operating as an independent business. “This is part of our separation from Meta; we must take this step to comply with regulatory requirements in specific parts of the world,” the company said on its website on Tuesday.

Unwinding plans would require final approval from regulators led by the National Development and Reform Commission, China’s powerful state planner, which in April [blocked the Meta takeover](https://www.ft.com/content/1e4c269a-5258-406c-a308-e55c3d5d640f?syn-25a6b1a6=1).

Regulators in Beijing have told Manus that its business operations would not be affected once the unwinding deal is settled in a way that satisfies all requirements, the people said.

Beijing’s move reflects China’s nuanced approach to tightening control of its technology and AI industries while also allowing companies and entrepreneurs to develop their businesses if they abide by the rules.

Manus chief scientist Ji Yichao has been barred from leaving China © Manus

In the unwinding deal, most of Manus’s former investors including [Tencent](https://www.ft.com/stream/26272ebc-a21c-450e-a743-be89c41d1eb8), ZhenFund and private equity firm HSG, as well as management, will buy back the company from Meta for about the same valuation of $2bn, the FT [reported](https://www.ft.com/content/0d04378d-d71b-4225-b31a-70504e358480?syn-25a6b1a6=1) last month.

American venture capital fund Benchmark, as well as a few smaller investors, will not participate in the buyback and their shares will largely be acquired by Tencent, which is set to become the biggest shareholder in Manus.

Tencent will only have a minority stake and Manus will continue to operate independently from Singapore rather than being folded into the Chinese tech company.

While it was being investigated, Manus continued to update its platform actively and is on schedule to launch its next 2.0 version soon after the unwinding deal is completed, according to one of the people. Its annual recurring revenue is expected to remain above $300mn after separating from Meta.

Meta bought Manus in December last year, just months after the company moved its headquarters and core engineers to Singapore from China, where it was originally founded. The US tech group quickly folded Manus into its platform, including its advertising systems.

In April, China [ordered Meta to unwind its acquisition](https://www.ft.com/content/1e4c269a-5258-406c-a308-e55c3d5d640f?syn-25a6b1a6=1), citing breaches of investment rules. Meta started separating the operations and stopped data sharing shortly after, the FT earlier reported.

Ordering the deal to be unwound serves as a warning to other Chinese technology companies against using Singapore as a staging post for eventual sales to overseas buyers.

In order to address the loophole, Beijing was also considering adding agentic AI to its next updated export control list, the FT [reported](https://www.ft.com/content/6049a031-9e9b-464c-97bb-414da04d5a6a?syn-25a6b1a6=1) last month.

Manus, the NDRC and the commerce ministry did not respond to requests for comment.
