> 📌 **核心摘要**
>
> 克利夫兰联储行长贝丝·哈马克质疑通胀放缓持续性，并重申现在应加息。
> • 她表示对通胀持续回落至2%缺乏信心。
> • 她认为利率并未显著限制经济，可能需要多次调整。
> • 她对美联储上月维持利率不变持异议，倾向加息25个基点。
> • 最新核心CPI环比上涨0.2%，经济学家认为缓解加息压力。

Federal Reserve Bank of Cleveland President Beth Hammack questioned whether recent signs of a slowdown in inflation will continue and reiterated her call to raise interest rates now.

克利夫兰联邦储备银行行长贝丝·哈马克质疑近期通胀放缓的迹象能否持续，并重申了她现在加息的呼吁。

“I love to see that those numbers are coming in lower — that’s a good thing — but I don’t have confidence that we’re going to continue to see that, or that we’re going to see them low enough that it’s going to bring us back down to that 2% number,” Hammack said Thursday.

“我很高兴看到这些数字走低——这是好事——但我不确信我们会继续看到这种情况，也不确信它们会低到足以让我们回到2%的水平，”哈马克周四表示。

Speaking at an event with the Dayton Area Chamber of Commerce in Kettering, Ohio, she also said, “I think we need to act now” on interest rates.

她在俄亥俄州凯特林与代顿地区商会共同参加的一场活动上发表讲话时还说：“我认为我们现在就需要采取行动”来调整利率。

Hammack dissented against last month’s decision to hold rates steady, saying she would have preferred to raise the Fed’s benchmark rate by a quarter point. She told Yahoo Finance on Monday that rates weren’t “meaningfully restricting” the economy and it could take “[some number](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-10/fed-s-hammack-says-some-number-of-rate-hikes-may-be-needed)” of rate adjustments to bring inflation back to target, but she didn’t want to prejudge what the landing point should be.

哈马克对美联储上月维持利率不变的决定持异议，称她本倾向于将美联储基准利率上调25个基点。她周一告诉雅虎财经，利率并未“显著限制”经济，可能需要“[一定数量](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-10/fed-s-hammack-says-some-number-of-rate-hikes-may-be-needed)”的利率调整才能使通胀回到目标水平，但她不想预判最终利率应落在何处。

The core consumer price index — which excludes often-volatile food and energy categories — increased 0.2% from a month earlier, official data showed Wednesda. Economists viewed that as a [subdued](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-12/us-core-inflation-was-subdued-in-july-easing-pressure-on-fed) pace that eased pressure on policymakers to raise rates.

官方数据显示，核心消费者物价指数（不包括通常波动的食品和能源类别）环比上涨0.2%，周三。经济学家认为这是[温和](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-12/us-core-inflation-was-subdued-in-july-easing-pressure-on-fed)的涨速，缓解了政策制定者加息的压力。
