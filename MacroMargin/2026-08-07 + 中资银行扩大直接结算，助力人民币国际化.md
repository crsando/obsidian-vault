### 中文译文

## 中资银行扩大直接结算，助力人民币国际化

中国正在加速推进人民币国际化，国内银行新增多种可直接结算的外币，以在跨境贸易中绕开美元。

据公开公告和官方媒体报道，今年至少有六家中资商业银行新增了约十种货币用于人民币直接结算和清算，包括[泰铢](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/THB%20BGNT%20Curncy)、[巴西雷亚尔](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/BRL%20BGN%20Curncy)和[哈萨克斯坦坚戈](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/KZT%20BGN%20Curncy)。新增货币主要是全球交易量较低的新兴市场货币，以及与中国“一带一路”倡议相关经济体的货币。

随着中国企业足迹伴随中国贸易和对外投资的增长而扩大，可结算货币范围的扩大可能为其进一步减少对美元的依赖铺平道路。这也与习近平主席打造“[强大货币](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2024-11-16/china-s-dream-of-powerful-currency-runs-into-trump-s-return)”的[雄心](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-10-27/china-signals-shift-in-yuan-push-as-pboc-drops-cautious-tone)一致，并且此前已有一系列旨在提升人民币计价资产对海外投资者吸引力的举措。

“地缘政治格局，特别是中国、美国和欧元区之间的贸易紧张局势，促使中国探索新的机遇并实现贸易伙伴多元化，”香港东亚银行有限公司策略师Bosco Wu表示。“更紧密的贸易联系自然会增加对相应金融服务的需求，包括直接货币结算。”

![图片](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3b581cc90b148118b47ac3528b8feca6/3b581cc9-0b14-8150-8f5d-e8f6836332e5-19ae52da4c47004db6ca36f41c4275ab.png)

[兴业银行](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/601166%20CH%20Equity)是最新一家扩大可直接结算货币范围的中资银行。上个月，该行向其企业和零售客户提供泰铢和[阿联酋迪拉姆](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/AED%20BGN%20Curncy)的跨境支付、存款和融资选项，并宣传其支付效率和较低的交易成本。

[浙江稠州商业银行](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/ZJCUZZ%20CH%20Equity)总部位于中国东部制造业中心义乌，今年新增了包括巴西雷亚尔、[孟加拉塔卡](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/BDT%20BGN%20Curncy)和[土耳其里拉](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/TRY%20BGN%20Curncy)在内的五种货币，用于其所谓的“无边界服务”。该行还提供六种非洲货币的直接清算，中国对这些地区的出口增速已超过[20%](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CNCXAFCY%20Index)，尽管当地金融条件受到资本管制或美元短缺的严重[制约](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-18/absa-weighs-joining-yuan-platform-on-thriving-china-africa-trade)。

这些举措“揭示了人民币国际化如何日益嵌入中国企业的实际经营活动中，”上海方长信息发展有限公司创始人王志毅表示。

尽管关于这些中资银行为客户结算的小币种外汇交易量信息有限，但中国人民银行数据显示，2026年上半年经常项目下以人民币结算的跨境交易额增长18%，达到9.83万亿元人民币（约1.46万亿美元），其中截至6月的三个月内结算额达创纪录的5.31万亿元人民币。

中资银行能否成功提升直接人民币交易，取决于所提供的成本和汇率，ING Bank NV首席大中华区经济学家Lynn Song表示。

“货币结算选项似乎很可能在中资银行持续扩大，而双边贸易和投资水平较高的银行最有可能成为候选者，”Song表示。

### English Original

## Chinese Banks Expand Direct Settlement to Aid Yuan’s Global Role

China is accelerating its push to internationalize the yuan as domestic banks add new foreign currencies available for direct settlement to bypass the dollar in cross-border trade.

At least six Chinese commercial banks have added about a dozen currencies, including the [Thai baht](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/THB%20BGNT%20Curncy), [Brazilian real](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/BRL%20BGN%20Curncy), and [Kazakhstan tenge](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/KZT%20BGN%20Curncy), for direct yuan settlement and clearing this year, according to public announcements and state media reports. The additions consist mostly of emerging-market currencies with low global transaction volumes as well as those from economies linked to China’s Belt and Road Initiative.

The widening pool of currencies may pave the way for Chinese businesses to further reduce their reliance on the dollar as their footprints expand alongside the Asian nation’s growing trade and outbound investment. It also aligns with President Xi Jinping’s [ambition](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-10-27/china-signals-shift-in-yuan-push-as-pboc-drops-cautious-tone) to build a “[powerful currency](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2024-11-16/china-s-dream-of-powerful-currency-runs-into-trump-s-return),” and follows a raft of recent initiatives aimed at burnishing the appeal of yuan-denominated assets to overseas investors.

“The geopolitical landscape, specifically trade tensions between China, the US, and the euro zone, has pushed China to explore new opportunities and diversify its trading partners,” Bosco Wu, a strategist at Bank of East Asia Ltd. in Hong Kong. “Stronger trade ties naturally increase the demand for corresponding financial services, including direct currency settlement.”

[Industrial Bank Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/601166%20CH%20Equity) is the latest to widen its pool of currencies eligible for direct yuan settlement. Last month, it offered its corporate and retail clients the option of cross-border payments, deposits and financing in the Thai baht and [UAE dirhams](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/AED%20BGN%20Curncy), touting payment efficiency and lower transaction costs.

[Zhejiang Chouzhou Commercial Bank Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/ZJCUZZ%20CH%20Equity), based in the manufacturing hub of Yiwu in eastern China, added five more currencies this year, including the Brazilian real, [Bangladesh taka](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/BDT%20BGN%20Curncy) and [Turkish lira](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/TRY%20BGN%20Curncy), to what it calls the “border-less service.” It also offers direct clearing of six currencies in Africa, with which China’s exports have grown at a pace of [over 20%](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CNCXAFCY%20Index), even as the local finance condition are heavily [restrained](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-18/absa-weighs-joining-yuan-platform-on-thriving-china-africa-trade) by capital controls or dollar shortages.

The moves “reveal how yuan internationalization is increasingly being embedded in the real operating activities of Chinese companies,” said Wang Zhiyi, founder of Shanghai Fang Chang Information Development Co.

While there’s limited information about the volume of foreign exchange these Chinese banks have settled in smaller currencies for clients, cross-border deals settled in yuan under the current account rose 18% to 9.83 trillion yuan ($1.46 trillion) in the first half of 2026, including a record 5.31 trillion yuan in the three months through June, data from the People’s Bank of China show.

Whether the Chinese banks succeed in boosting direct yuan transactions depends on the cost and exchange rate offered, said Lynn Song, chief Greater China economist at ING Bank NV.

“It seems likely that currency settlement options will keep expanding at Chinese banks, and those with high levels of bilateral trade and investment are the likeliest candidates,” Song said.
