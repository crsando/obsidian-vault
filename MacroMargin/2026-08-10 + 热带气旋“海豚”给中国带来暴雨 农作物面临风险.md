周日袭击中国沿海的台风在登陆后正在稳步减弱，但预计本周将带来大范围强降雨，可能威胁农作物。

台风“海豚”在周日晚在中国东部[两次登陆](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-09/china-issues-red-alert-airports-cancel-flights-as-typhoon-nears)后已减弱为热带风暴，[据中国气象局](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/QS5X4B6YpG55MZKVyQVENA)消息。强风和暴雨已引发大范围交通中断，包括上海两大主要机场关闭。

该天气系统继续向北移动，构成“潜在的重大内陆降雨事件”，可能损害农田，[James Caron](https://www.linkedin.com/in/james-caron-3956689/)（Atmospheric G2 北美和亚洲气象运营总监）表示。他说，“在这个生长季阶段”的强降雨可能有害无益。

### 热带风暴“海豚”路径图

据[中国气象局](https://www.nmc.cn/publish/agro/ten-week/index.html)称，周一和周二，总雨量高达400毫米（15.7英寸）的暴雨、雷暴和强对流天气将威胁长江东部大片地区，包括浙江、江苏、安徽、湖北和河南等省。

该地区的玉米和大麦等主要作物于8月开始收割，而大豆作物从9月开始收割。

气象机构在周一上午发布的公报中表示：“强风和降雨将导致作物受淹和倒伏、水果裂果和落果，以及农业和渔业设施受损，阻碍成熟水果的采收。”

中国气象局补充说，热带气旋残余在进一步向北推进时将与冷空气相互作用，可能从周三到周末在农业密集的华北平原引发极端强降雨。它表示，低洼农田将面临较高的内涝风险，并可能损害水稻和玉米作物。

Caron说：“最终的农业影响将在很大程度上取决于残余环流及相关雨带的准确路径。该路径走廊的相对适度偏移可能会显著改变哪些产区获得最高降水量。”

## Tropical Cyclone Dolphin Brings Heavy Rains to China in Risk to Crops

Officials help evacuate local residents in Hangzhou, Zhejiang province on Aug. 9.*Source: AFP/Getty Images*

By [Mary Hui](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AXDwzJNHmiM/mary-hui)August 10, 2026 at 11:08 AM GMT+8

### **Takeaways**

- Typhoon Dolphin has weakened to a tropical storm after making landfall in eastern China, but is set to bring widespread heavy rain that could threaten crops.
- The weather system is continuing to move northwards, posing a “potentially significant inland rainfall event” that could damage farmlands, according to James Caron.
- Torrential rains and strong convective weather are set to menace large swathes of land around the eastern Yangtze, including Zhejiang, Jiangsu, Anhui, Hubei and Henan provinces, according to the China Meteorological Administration.
A typhoon that struck the Chinese coast on Sunday is steadily weakening after making landfall, but is set to bring widespread heavy rain through this week that could threaten crops.

Typhoon Dolphin has weakened to a tropical storm after making [two landfalls](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-09/china-issues-red-alert-airports-cancel-flights-as-typhoon-nears) in eastern China on Sunday evening, [according to](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/QS5X4B6YpG55MZKVyQVENA) the China Meteorological Administration. Powerful winds and intense rains had triggered widespread transport disruptions, including the closure of Shanghai’s two main airports.

The weather system is continuing to move northwards, posing a “potentially significant inland rainfall event” that could damage farmlands, said [James Caron](https://www.linkedin.com/in/james-caron-3956689/), director of meteorological operations for North America and Asia at Atmospheric G2. Heavy rain “at this point in the growing season” can be damaging rather than beneficial, he said.

### Tropical Storm Dolphin track map

Torrential rains totaling up to 400 millimeters (15.7 inches), thunderstorms and strong convective weather are set to menace large swathes of land around the eastern Yangtze on Monday and Tuesday, including Zhejiang, Jiangsu, Anhui, Hubei and Henan provinces, [according to](https://www.nmc.cn/publish/agro/ten-week/index.html) the CMA.

Key crops in the region like corn and barley begin their harvests in August, while soybean crops are harvested beginning September.

“The strong winds and rain will cause crop flooding and lodging, fruit cracking and drop, and damage to agricultural and fishery facilities, hindering the harvesting of ripe fruits,” the weather agency said in a bulletin published on Monday morning.

Remnants of the tropical cyclone will interact with colder air as it pushes further north, likely triggering extremely heavy rains around the agriculture-heavy North China Plain from Wednesday into the weekend, the CMA added. Low-lying farmland will face high risks of waterlogging, and potentially damage rice and corn crops, it said.

“The ultimate agricultural impact will depend heavily on exactly where the remnant circulation and associated rain bands track,” Caron said. “A relatively modest shift in that corridor could substantially change which production areas receive the highest totals.”
