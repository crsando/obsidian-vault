### 中文译文

## 英国在“中国恐慌”后加强对国防供应链的审查

*Kraken Technology公司的K3 Scout无人水面艇。*
*来源：Kraken Technology*

英国国防部在发现其部分海军无人机向中国传输信号后，正在加强对供应链的审查。

该部门本周早些时候透露，一项常规的网络漏洞评估发现了影响由[Kraken Technology Group](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/2625097D:LN)制造并被英国皇家海军使用的K3 Scout无人机的问题。

据知情人士透露，官员们正在寻找其国防工业中的其他漏洞，包括是否有其他承包商使用Kraken所采购的同一供应商制造的摄像头。这些人士因讨论私下谈话而要求匿名。

“我们的保障和测试流程旨在尽早识别和解决潜在漏洞，我们继续对我们的系统和设备开展安全活动，”该部在一份声明中表示。“这些包括审查我们供应基地内潜在敌对暴露风险并设计适当缓解措施的积极项目。”

《每日电讯报》本周早些时候报道，Kraken的监视无人机含有在中国制造的组件，这些组件秘密向该国一台设备传输信息。如果英国和法国继续推进在美伊和平协议后准备中的排雷任务，其中一些无人机可能会被派往霍尔木兹海峡。

国防部表示，其调查未发现政府数据或系统被访问、泄露或外部传输的证据。相反，据《每日电讯报》报道和国防官员证实，这些无人机的摄像头向中国一个IP地址发送了所谓的“心跳通信”，该地址可以识别其位置以及当时是否在使用。

Kraken的一位发言人表示，一些符合美国《国防授权法》的第三方摄像头有少量部件来自英国以外。该发言人表示，没有敏感信息被共享，任何潜在漏洞都已被识别并关闭。

这一发现暴露了国防供应链的脆弱性，尤其是在防止不知不觉使用来自某些国家的零部件方面，这些国家可能试图利用此类漏洞来监视英国的军事活动。

知情人士称，该部门正在持续审查其流程，以确保其能有效应对不断变化的威胁，但迄今为止尚未选择对整个行业进行全面审计。

一些官员告诉彭博社，风险在于英国在识别这一问题上只是侥幸。其中一位知情人士称，该部已警告供应商，他们应同时满足合同安全要求和国际公认的安全标准。

## UK Steps Up Scrutiny of Defense Supply Chain After China Scare

*Kraken Technology’s K3 Scout uncrewed surface vessel.*
*Source: Kraken Technology*

The UK Ministry of Defense is stepping-up scrutiny of its supply chain after discovering some of its naval drones transmitted signals to China.

The department revealed earlier this week that a routine cyber vulnerability assessment identified an issue affecting K3 Scout drones made by [Kraken Technology Group](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/2625097D:LN) and used by the Royal Navy.

Officials are looking for other vulnerabilities in its defense industry, including if any other contractors use cameras made by the same supplier that Kraken sourced from, according to people familiar with the matter who asked not to be identified discussing private conversations.

“Our assurance and testing processes are designed to identify and address potential vulnerabilities early, and we continue to undertake security activity across our systems and equipment,” the ministry said in a statement. “These include active program which review risks of potential adversarial exposure within our supply base and design appropriate mitigations.”

The Kraken surveillance drones had components made in China that were secretly transmitting information to a device in the country, the Telegraph reported earlier this week. Some of the drones could be sent to the Strait of Hormuz if the UK and France proceed with a de-mining mission that’s being prepared for after a US-Iran peace deal.

The ministry said its investigation found no evidence of the government’s data or systems being accessed, compromised or transmitted externally. Instead, the drones’ cameras sent what are known as “heartbeat communications” to an IP address in China that could identify their location and whether they were in use at the time, the Telegraph reported and defense officials confirmed.

A Kraken spokesperson said some third-party cameras, compliant with the US National Defense Authorization Act, had a small number of components originating from outside the UK. The spokesperson said no sensitive information was shared and any potential vulnerabilities were identified and closed.

The discovery exposes the fragility of defense supply chains, particularly in ensuring against components unknowingly being used from countries which may seek to exploit such vulnerabilities to spy on Britain’s military activities.

The department is keeping its processes under review to ensure their effectiveness against evolving threats, the people said, though have so far chosen not to conduct a full audit across the industry.

The risk is that the UK got lucky in identifying the issue, some officials told Bloomberg. The ministry has warned its suppliers that they should meet both contractual security requirements and internationally recognized security standards, one of the people said.
