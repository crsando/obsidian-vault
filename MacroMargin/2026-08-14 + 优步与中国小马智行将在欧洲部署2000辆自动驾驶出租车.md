### 中文译文

## 优步与中国小马智行将在欧洲部署2000辆自动驾驶出租车

一辆小马智行自动驾驶出租车在中国深圳。*摄影师：沈启来/彭博社*

彭博新闻社 2026年8月14日 上午9:00 GMT+8

### **要点** 由彭博AI提供

- 小马智行（Pony AI Inc.）计划与优步科技公司（Uber Technologies Inc.）合作，在欧洲部署超过2000辆自动驾驶出租车。
- 该举措将扩大两家公司今年早些时候在克罗地亚萨格勒布建立的合作，扩展到欧洲大陆的另外四个城市。
- 小马智行与优步的扩张还包括中东地区，更多推出细节将于稍后公布。
[小马智行（Pony AI Inc.）](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/PONY%20US%20Equity)表示，计划与[优步科技公司（Uber Technologies Inc.）](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/UBER%20US%20Equity)合作，在欧洲部署超过2000辆自动驾驶出租车，加速Waymo、优步及中国竞争对手在欧洲开启自动驾驶出租车竞赛：BNEF推动该地区自动驾驶商业化。

小马智行在周五的一份声明中表示，该举措将深化两家公司自今年年初以来在克罗地亚萨格勒布的现有合作，并将扩展到欧洲大陆的另外四个城市。声明还称，更多关于推出计划的细节（其中也将包括中东地区）将在稍后公布。

此次扩张紧随 Uber 与 Wayve Technologies Ltd. 达成合作，获得在伦敦运营受监督的 Robotaxi 服务的许可。这家美国公司还与百度公司合作，在英国首都开展一项独立的自动驾驶出行项目，与 Lyft 和 Uber 一起在伦敦开始测试 Robotaxi（1）。

近几个月来，随着 Waymo 在美国迅速扩张，并进军日本东京和英国伦敦，Uber 的无人驾驶汽车技术一直面临投资者的质疑。有消息称 Waymo 正在探索退出与 Uber 现有的专属 Robotaxi 合作关系的选项，这导致 Uber 股价暴跌，加剧了自去年 10 月创下历史新高以来 20% 的跌幅。

但这家网约车巨头已将重心转向全球，并与一系列其他合作伙伴签署了协议。其中一些合作伙伴正在伦敦等关键市场取得进展，而与 Pony AI 的合作则是首次在欧洲萨格勒布推出收费 Robotaxi 服务。

此次扩张也凸显了 Pony AI 的全球雄心，此前其在中国国内遭遇了长达数月的 Robotaxi 牌照暂停——这是由百度车辆故障引发的安全审查所致。虽然审批已重新启动，但恢复似乎是渐进式的，可能会减缓新车辆的部署。

Pony AI 及其同行则加速了海外部署，其中 WeRide 最近与 GreenMobility 达成合作，将于 2027 年在丹麦部署 Robotaxi，GreenMobility 将于 2027 年在这个北欧国家部署自动驾驶汽车。同样与 Uber 有合作关系的 Momenta Global Ltd 成为首家获得在德国测试 Robotaxi 许可的亚洲公司。

## Uber and China’s Pony AI to Deploy 2,000 Robotaxis in Europe

A Pony AI autonomous taxi in Shenzhen, China.*Photographer: Qilai Shen/Bloomberg*

By Bloomberg NewsAugust 14, 2026 at 9:00 AM GMT+8

### **Takeaways ** by Bloomberg AI

- Pony AI Inc. plans to deploy more than 2,000 robotaxis across Europe in partnership with Uber Technologies Inc.
- The initiative will expand an existing partnership between the two companies in Croatia's Zagreb to four additional cities in the continent.
- Pony AI and Uber's expansion also includes the Middle East, with more details about the rollout to be announced later.
[Pony AI Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/PONY%20US%20Equity) said it plans to deploy more than 2,000 robotaxis across Europe in partnership with [Uber Technologies Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/UBER%20US%20Equity), accelerating the Waymo, Uber, Chinese Rivals Kick Off Europe Robotaxi Race: BNEF to commercialize autonomous-mobility in the region.

The initiative will deepen an existing partnership between the two companies in Croatia’s Zagreb since early this year, and see them expand to four additional cities in the continent, Pony AI said in a statement Friday. It said more details about the rollout, which will also include the Middle East, will be announced later.

The expansion comes on the heels of Uber’s tie-up with Wayve Technologies Ltd. securing [permits](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-05/uber-and-wayve-win-licenses-for-supervised-robotaxis-in-london) to run a supervised robotaxi service in London. Uber in recent months has faced [doubts](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-04/uber-s-robotaxi-ambition-to-face-scrutiny-as-shares-languish) from investors over its driverless car technology as Waymo expands rapidly in the US and has also ventured into Tokyo in Japan and London in the UK. News of Waymo exploring options to exit the existing and exclusive robotaxi partnership with Uber sent its share price plummeting, adding to a 20% slump from a record high in October last year.

But the ride-hailing giant has inked a pact with a series of other partners as its focus turns global. Some of those are making progress in key markets like London, and its tie-up with Pony AI is the first to introduce fare-charging robotaxi service in Europe, in Zagreb.

The expansion also underscores Pony AI’s global ambition amid the months-long suspension of robotaxi licenses back home in China — the result of a [safety review](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-29/china-suspends-new-autonomous-driving-permits-after-baidu-outage) triggered by an outage of Baidu’s vehicles. While approval has restarted, the [resumption](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-23/china-resumes-issuing-robotaxi-licenses-after-months-long-freeze) seems to be gradual and could slow the deployment of new vehicles.

Momenta Global Ltd, which also has a partnership with Uber, became the first Asian company to obtain a license to test robotaxis in Germany.
