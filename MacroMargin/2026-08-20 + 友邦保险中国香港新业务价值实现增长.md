### 中文译文

## 友邦保险中国香港新业务价值实现增长

图为香港中环摩天轮和友邦金融中心上的友邦保险集团标识。*摄影师：林翊/彭博*

[友邦保险集团](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/1299%20HK%20Equity)今年上半年新业务价值增长13%，主要得益于包括中国香港和中国内地在内的关键市场的强劲销售。

新业务价值增长至32.1亿美元，低于彭博新闻社汇总的六位分析师预测中值32.6亿美元。若不计汇率影响，该保险公司的增长率本为10%。

该公司的扩张凸显了更广泛的行业趋势。在本国利率低迷的情况下，保险公司持续看到本地和内地客户寻求更高回报的需求。投资者正在评估中国近期针对离岸保险产品分红派息[征税](https://blinks.bloomberg.com/news/stories/TJBRTPR24U82)努力的力度及其对香港保险业的影响。

在香港这个友邦保险最大的市场，由于中国内地访客群体的持续需求，前六个月新业务价值增长10%至11.7亿美元。中国内地该项指标增长26%至9.37亿美元。在东南亚，新加坡的销售额增长14%至2.94亿美元，得益于富裕和高净值客户对财富和长期储蓄产品的需求。

集团首席财务官Garth Jones在接受彭博新闻社采访时表示，尽管北京方面加强了对离岸财富征税的举措，该保险公司仍看到来自中国内地游客的“良好需求”。

“根据我们的了解，中国的税法没有变化。”他说，“人们来香港购买我们的产品，主要不是出于税务原因。”

该公司将支付每股53.9港仙的中期股息，增幅为10%。

“在我们应对一个充满不确定性的长期时期，其特点是全球地缘政治紧张局势加剧、金融市场波动和通胀压力重现，友邦保险的核心优势对今天的客户而言，与以往任何时候都一样重要，”集团首席执行官兼总裁李源祥在周四的声明中表示。

友邦保险表示，已于6月完成3月份宣布的17亿美元股票回购计划。该公司还报告了创纪录的17.5%的年化经营股本回报率，高于2025年的15.5%。

琼斯表示，该公司近年来投资约8亿美元更新技术后，每年节省了2亿美元的开支。他说，技术发展有助于减少行政工时，使代理人能够专注于客户服务。

## AIA Posts Growth in New Business Value on Hong Kong, China

Signage for AIA Group Ltd. at the Hong Kong Observation Wheel and atop the AIA Central building in Hong Kong.*Photographer: Lam Yik/Bloomberg*

[AIA Group Ltd.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/1299%20HK%20Equity) reported 13% growth in new business value in the first half of this year, led by strong sales in key markets including Hong Kong and China.

The growth to $3.21 billion fell short of the $3.26 billion median estimate of six analysts compiled by Bloomberg News. Without the exchange rate impact, the insurer’s growth rate would have been 10%.

The firm’s expansion underscored the broader industry trend. Insurers have seen continued demand from both local and mainland clients seeking higher returns amid low interest rates at home. Investors are gauging the extent of China’s recent [tax collection](https://blinks.bloomberg.com/news/stories/TJBRTPR24U82) efforts for dividend payouts on offshore insurance products and the impact on Hong Kong’s insurance industry.

In Hong Kong, the largest market for AIA, new business value increased 10% to $1.17 billion for the first six months on continued demand from the Chinese mainland visitor segment. The metric for China grew 26% to $937 million. In Southeast Asia, Singapore’s sales climbed 14% to $294 million, driven by demand in wealth and long-term savings products from both affluent and high-net-worth clients.

Garth Jones, group chief financial officer, said in an interview with Bloomberg News that the insurer continues to see “good demand” from mainland China visitors despite Beijing’s increasing moves to tax offshore wealth.

“Based on our understanding, there’s no change in the the tax laws in China,” he said. “People don’t come to Hong Kong to buy our products for for tax reasons principally.”

The company will pay an interim dividend of 53.9 Hong Kong cents per share, an increase of 10%.

“As we navigate an extended period of uncertainty, marked by rising global geopolitical tensions, financial market volatility and renewed inflationary pressures, AIA’s core advantages remain as relevant to customers today as they have ever been,” Lee Yuan Siong, group chief executive and president, said in the statement on Thursday.

AIA said it completed in June the $1.7 billion share buyback plan that was announced in March. It also reported a record high annualized operating return on equity at 17.5%, up from 15.5% in 2025.

The insurer has made an annual expense saving of $200 million after investing some $800 million to update its technology in recent years, Jones said. The technology developments have helped cut down administrative hours and enabled agents to focus on client services, he said.
