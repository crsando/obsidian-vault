### 中文译文

## 宇树科技上海IPO获散户超额认购5526倍

备受期待的宇树科技（Yushu Technology Co.，即[Unitree Robotics](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/688836%20CH%20Equity)）首次公开发行吸引了散户投资者的巨大需求，这家中国领先的人形机器人制造商在上海开启簿记建档。

根据交易所周一发布的[公告](https://static.sse.com.cn/disclosure/listedinfo/announcement/c/new/2026-08-11/688836_20260811_HOO6.pdf)，IPO的散户部分获5526倍超额认购，个人投资者提交了980万份申购订单。

这家总部位于杭州的公司以每股150.8元人民币的价格发行了4040万股，筹集约61亿元人民币（合9.04亿美元），成为内地首家公开上市的人形机器人制造商。该公司预计本月在上海科创板上市。此次发行使宇树科技市值约为610亿元人民币。

高涨的关注度凸显了投资者对物理AI公司的兴趣，这是北京的战略重点之一。强劲的认购也可能得益于包括AI初创公司[DeepSeek](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/2544189D:CH)在内的战略投资者的支持，这增强了对宇树科技以及人工智能与机器人日益融合的信心。

约20%的发行股份已配售给战略投资者，包括DeepSeek、一个与[腾讯控股](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/700%20HK%20Equity)有限公司关联的投资工具，以及大型国有企业的投资部门，如[中国石油天然气集团](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CNPZ%20CH%20Equity)、[中国南方电网](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/SOPOWZ%20CH%20Equity)和[中国电信](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/728%20HK%20Equity)。

DeepSeek获得2.31%的股份配售，锁定期为三年。此次合作使宇树科技能够借助DeepSeek的AI实力为其人形机器人开发具身智能模型，这些机器人在亮相中国春晚后获得了广泛关注。

成功的上市也可能为其他寻求IPO的中国机器人公司积聚势头，包括乐居机器人和深之蓝机器人。另一家人形机器人开发商[上海智元新创技术](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/2267429D:CH)已于7月启动香港上市流程，据《证券日报》[报道](http://www.zqrb.cn/gscy/qiyexinxi/2026-07-24/A1784880058545.html)。

## Unitree’s Shanghai IPO 5,526 Times Subscribed by Retail Buyers

The highly anticipated initial public offering of Yushu Technology Co., known as [Unitree Robotics](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/688836%20CH%20Equity), drew huge demand from retail investors as China’s leading humanoid robot maker opened books in Shanghai.

The retail portion of the IPO was 5,526 times subscribed, with individual investors submitting 9.8 million orders, according to an exchange [filing](https://static.sse.com.cn/disclosure/listedinfo/announcement/c/new/2026-08-11/688836_20260811_HOO6.pdf) released on Monday.

The Hangzhou-based company sold 40.4 million shares at 150.8 yuan apiece, raising about 6.1 billion yuan ($904 million) to become mainland China’s first publicly traded humanoid robot maker. The company is expected to debut on Shanghai’s STAR Market this month. The offering gives Unitree a market value of approximately 61 billion yuan.

The high level of interest underscores the investor appetite for physical AI companies, one of Beijing’s strategic priorities. The strong subscription was also likely aided by backing from strategic investors including AI startup [DeepSeek](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/2544189D:CH), reinforcing confidence in Unitree and the growing convergence of artificial intelligence and robotics.

About 20% of the shares for sale have been allocated to strategic investors including DeepSeek, a [Tencent Holdings](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/700%20HK%20Equity) Ltd.-affiliated investment vehicle, and investment arms of major state-owned enterprises such as [China National Petroleum Corp.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/CNPZ%20CH%20Equity), [China Southern Power Grid Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/SOPOWZ%20CH%20Equity) and [China Telecom Corp.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/728%20HK%20Equity)

DeepSeek received a 2.31% stake allocation with a lockup period of three years. The collaboration allows Unitree to draw on DeepSeek’s AI strength to develop embodied-intelligence models for its humanoid robots, which gained broad public attention after appearing in a Chinese Spring Festival gala.

A successful listing could also build momentum for other Chinese robotics companies pursuing IPOs, including Leju Robotics and Deep Robotics. Another humanoid robot developer, [Shanghai AgiBot Innovation Technology](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/2267429D:CH), has started the process for a Hong Kong listing, Securities Daily [reported](http://www.zqrb.cn/gscy/qiyexinxi/2026-07-24/A1784880058545.html) in July.
