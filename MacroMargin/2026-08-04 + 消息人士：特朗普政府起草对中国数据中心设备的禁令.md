## 消息人士：特朗普政府起草对中国数据中心设备的禁令

无人机视角下的美国加利福尼亚州奥克兰Digital Realty数据中心，摄于2026年7月18日。路透社/Fred Greaves/资料图片 [购买许可版权，打开新窗口](https://www.reutersconnect.com/item/drone-view-of-a-data-center-in-oakland-california/dGFnOnJldXRlcnMuY29tLDIwMjY6bmV3c21sX1JDMkJHTUFWOFpHRw%3D%3D/?utm_medium=rcom-article-media&utm_campaign=rcom-rcp-lead)

- 中国表示将对损害其利益的行为作出回应
华盛顿，8月4日（路透社）——四位知情人士告诉路透社，特朗普政府正在起草一项禁止美国进口中国新型数据中心组件的禁令，以保护支撑人工智能繁荣的基础设施。

美国联邦通信委员会（FCC）负责监管美国电信行业，正在制定措施，禁止进口新的中国光收发器。光收发器能让数据在数据中心内的光纤电缆上以光速传输。官员们希望今年发布该措施，届时将生效。

这一此前未曾报道的举措旨在防止中国企业在美国数据中心窃取数据、安装恶意软件或破坏服务，这些数据中心存放着用于训练和运行人工智能模型的芯片。

消息人士强调，FCC仍可能修改或搁置这一限制，他们因讨论敏感问题而要求匿名。但这是特朗普政府试图限制中国技术侵入美国尖端产业、防止其嵌入供应链的最新例证。

“收发器确实构成风险，”华盛顿特区咨询公司Beacon Global Strategies的人工智能政策专家Divyansh Kaushik表示。“随着数据中心建设规模扩大，你希望从一开始就确保数据中心供应链的安全，”他补充道。

### 中国使馆：必要时将作出回应

白宫和FCC没有回应置评请求。中国驻华盛顿大使馆表示，北京敦促美国“听取两国商界客观理性的声音”，“停止抹黑中国公司并威胁制裁它们”。

“中国将对任何对其利益造成实质性损害的行动采取一切必要措施，”使馆补充道。

政府中的对华鹰派人士急于避免再次出现类似华为的情况，这家受到严厉制裁的中国公司制造的电信设备在美国基础设施中嵌入如此之深，以至于移除这些设备的努力进展缓慢、代价高昂且不完整。

美国联邦通信委员会（FCC）历来保持独立，但6月美国最高法院支持了唐纳德·特朗普总统解雇一名民主党联邦贸易委员会成员的决定，从而扩大了他对政府（包括某些监管机构）的权力。

美国对中国数据中心设备新机型的禁令可能会打击中国的中际旭创（Zhongji Innolight）[(300308.SZ), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/300308.SZ)，该公司是全球最大的收发器销售商之一，并于6月被列入五角大楼所谓的中国军方支持企业名单。这份名单可能预示着更严厉的行动。旭创未回应置评请求。

禁令还可能提高美国云服务公司（如亚马逊云服务（AWS）[(AMZN.O), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/AMZN.O)）的成本，因为这可能迫使它们转向其他生产商，如总部位于美国的Coherent（相干公司）[(COHR.N), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/COHR.N)和Lumentum（朗美通）[(LITE.O), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/LITE.O)，这些公司将从该措施中受益。

根据Counterpoint Research的数据，旭创在全球数据中心收发器市场占据领先的27%份额。根据美国创新基金会（Foundation for American Innovation）的一份报告，Coherent和Lumentum销售具有竞争力的技术，但缺乏替代中国供应商的规模。该报告还称，旭创90%的收入来自中国以外。

AWS、Coherent和Lumentum未回应置评请求。

据路透社率先报道，美国联邦通信委员会（FCC）已对中国[无人机](https://www.reuters.com/business/aerospace-defense/us-adds-dji-other-foreign-drones-national-security-list-2025-12-22/)、[路由器](https://www.reuters.com/sustainability/boards-policy-regulation/fcc-banning-imports-new-chinese-made-routers-citing-security-concerns-2026-03-23/)、[机器人](https://www.reuters.com/world/trump-administration-ban-new-chinese-robots-inverters-protecting-us-ai-buildout-2026-07-28/)和[逆变器](https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/us-working-ban-targeting-chinese-energy-inverters-sources-say-2026-06-30/)实施了类似限制。

据三位消息人士称，与这些限制一致，该机构将禁止所有新型收发器机型的进口，然后豁免许多非中国供应商。

特朗普在第一个任期内曾提请注意中国企业涉嫌窃取知识产权以及华为进行国家支持间谍活动的指控，华为否认了这些指控。

但在北京去年对稀土矿产实施出口管制后，特朗普在其第二任期内采取了不那么激进的做法。

[路透社2月报道](https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/us-china-trade-detente-fuels-mothballing-key-china-tech-curbs-2026-02-12/)称，美国商务部拥有监管科技供应链以应对来自中国的潜在威胁的工具，在去年10月贸易战缓和后，搁置了一组对中国的进口限制，其中包括一项针对中国数据中心设备的限制。

但美国联邦通信委员会(FCC)已介入，分别于去年12月和今年3月通过其所谓的“受保护清单”(Covered List)宣布对无人机和路由器的禁令。该清单由国会创建，以禁止那些产品构成国家安全风险的外国公司未来的设备销售。上周，针对中国逆变器和机器人的行动也随之而来。

## Trump administration drafting ban on Chinese data center devices, sources say

A drone view of the Digital Realty data center in Oakland, California, U.S., July 18, 2026. REUTERS/Fred Greaves/File Photo [Purchase Licensing Rights, opens new tab](https://www.reutersconnect.com/item/drone-view-of-a-data-center-in-oakland-california/dGFnOnJldXRlcnMuY29tLDIwMjY6bmV3c21sX1JDMkJHTUFWOFpHRw%3D%3D/?utm_medium=rcom-article-media&utm_campaign=rcom-rcp-lead)

- Move seeks to avert entrenchment in critical US infrastructure
- China says it will respond to measures that harm its interests
WASHINGTON, Aug 4 (Reuters) - The Trump administration is drafting a ban on U.S. imports of new models of Chinese data center components, four people familiar with the matter told Reuters, as it seeks to protect the infrastructure that undergirds the AI boom.

The Federal Communications Commission, which oversees the U.S. telecom ‌industry, is working on the measure to bar imports of new Chinese optical transceivers, which allow data to travel over fiber-optic cables at the speed of light within data centers. Officials hope to publish it this year, when it would take effect.

The move, not previously reported, aims to prevent Chinese firms from stealing data, installing malware or disrupting service at U.S. data centers, which house the chips to train and run AI models.

The FCC could still modify or shelve the restriction, the sources stressed, speaking on condition of anonymity to discuss sensitive matters. But it is the latest example of the Trump administration trying to limit Chinese technological incursions into cutting-edge U.S. industries before they become embedded in the ⁠supply chain.

"Transceivers definitely pose a risk," said Divyansh Kaushik, an AI policy expert at Washington, D.C., advisory firm Beacon Global Strategies. "As the data center buildout scales up, you want to make sure the data center supply chain is secure from the get-go," he added.

### CHINA WILL RESPOND IF NECESSARY: EMBASSY

The White House and the FCC did not respond to requests for comment. The Chinese embassy in Washington said Beijing urges the United States to "heed the objective and rational voices of the business communities in both countries" and "stop smearing Chinese companies and threatening them with sanctions."

"China will take all necessary measures in response to any action that causes material harm to its interests," it added.

China hawks in the administration are keen to avoid another situation like Huawei, where telecom equipment made by the heavily sanctioned Chinese firm was so deeply embedded in U.S. infrastructure that efforts to remove it were slow, expensive and incomplete.

The FCC has historically been independent, but in June the [U.S. Supreme Court](https://www.reuters.com/legal/us-supreme-court/) backed [President Donald Trump](https://www.reuters.com/world/us/donald-trump/)'s firing of a Democratic Federal Trade Commission member, expanding his powers over the government, including certain regulatory agencies.

A U.S. ban on new models of Chinese ‌data center ⁠devices would likely hit China’s Zhongji Innolight [(300308.SZ), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/300308.SZ), one of the biggest global sellers of transceivers, which was added to the Pentagon’s list of alleged Chinese military-backed companies in June. The list can be a harbinger of tougher action. Innolight did not respond to requests for comment.

A ban could also raise costs for American cloud firms such as Amazon Web Services [(AMZN.O), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/AMZN.O), as it may force them to transition to other producers such as U.S.-based Coherent [(COHR.N), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/COHR.N) and Lumentum [(LITE.O), opens new tab](https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/LITE.O), which stand to benefit from the measure.

Innolight has a leading 27% share of the global data center transceiver market, according to Counterpoint Research. Coherent and Lumentum sell competitive technology but ⁠lack the scale to replace Chinese vendors, according to a report by the Foundation for American Innovation. Innolight generates 90% of its revenue outside China, the report added.

AWS, Coherent and Lumentum did not respond to requests for comment.

The FCC has imposed similar curbs on Chinese [drones](https://www.reuters.com/business/aerospace-defense/us-adds-dji-other-foreign-drones-national-security-list-2025-12-22/), [routers](https://www.reuters.com/sustainability/boards-policy-regulation/fcc-banning-imports-new-chinese-made-routers-citing-security-concerns-2026-03-23/), [robots](https://www.reuters.com/world/trump-administration-ban-new-chinese-robots-inverters-protecting-us-ai-buildout-2026-07-28/) and [inverters](https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/us-working-ban-targeting-chinese-energy-inverters-sources-say-2026-06-30/), as first reported by Reuters.

In line with those restrictions, the agency would ban all imports of new transceiver models and then exempt many ⁠non-Chinese suppliers from the restrictions, three of the sources said.

Trump drew attention during his first term to alleged intellectual property theft by Chinese firms and state-sponsored spying by Huawei, which the company denies.

But Trump has taken a less aggressive approach during his second term after Beijing's use of export controls on rare earth minerals last year.

[Reuters reported in February](https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/us-china-trade-detente-fuels-mothballing-key-china-tech-curbs-2026-02-12/) that the Commerce Department, which ⁠has tools to police the tech supply chain for perceived threats from China, shelved a group of import restrictions on China — including one targeting Chinese data center equipment — following a trade war détente last October.

But the FCC has stepped in, announcing the drone and router bans in December and March, respectively, via its so-called Covered List, created by Congress to bar future equipment sales by foreign companies whose products pose national security risks. Moves against Chinese inverters and robots followed last week.
