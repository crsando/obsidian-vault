**🔑 关键词**：海警、台湾、台海局势

TAIPEI/BEIJING, July 31 (Reuters) - China's coast guard conducted patrols it described as routine law enforcement in waters east of Taiwan on Friday, angering Taipei, which condemned the move as a political operation aimed at expanding Beijing's control.
台北/北京，7 月 31 日（路透社）——中国海警周五在台湾以东海域进行了其称为例行执法巡逻的行动，此举激怒了台北方面，后者谴责这一行动是旨在扩大北京控制的政治操作。

The Chinese activities off ‌Taiwan's Pacific coast have triggered concern from some Western countries, which have [warned](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/uk-france-germany-raise-alarm-about-chinese-activities-off-eastern-taiwan-2026-06-24/) against any unilateral change to the status quo or actions that could raise tension in the Taiwan Strait.
中国在台湾太平洋沿岸附近的活动引发了一些西方国家的担忧，这些国家警告不要单方面改变现状或采取可能加剧台海紧张局势的行动。

In a statement, China's coast guard said the patrols east of Taiwan were led by one of its ships, Xiushan, and it would continue to strengthen such efforts.
中国海警在一份声明中表示，台湾以东的巡逻由其"秀山"号舰艇主导，并将继续加强此类行动。

"Since July, the Xiushan task group had been ⁠strengthening control over the relevant waters to effectively ensure orderly navigation and activities," its spokesperson, Jiang Lue, said.
"自 7 月以来，'秀山'任务组一直在加强对相关海域的管控，以有效确保航行和活动的有序进行，"其发言人蒋略表示。

The effort was safeguarding the legitimate and lawful rights and interests, as well as the lives and property of fishermen on both sides of the Strait, he added.
他补充说，此举旨在维护海峡两岸渔民的合法权益及生命财产安全。

Taiwan's Coast Guard Administration said a patrol ship, the Taipei, was among the vessels it sent to monitor the Chinese ships, and accused Beijing of engaging in "fake law enforcement, real expansion of power".
台湾海巡署表示，其派遣的巡逻船中包括"台北舰"，用于监控中国船只，并指责北京方面"名为执法，实为扩张势力"。

Two Chinese coast guard vessels, the Xiushan and Chongming, were detected on Friday, it said.
该机构称，周五发现了两艘中国海警船——"秀山舰"和"崇明舰"。

One drifted about 151 nautical miles east of Lanyu, also known as Orchid ‌Island, ⁠and the other about 43 nautical miles to the island's northeast, it said, adding that Taiwan vessels were monitoring them.
据通报，其中一艘在兰屿（亦称兰花岛）以东约 151 海里处漂流，另一艘位于该岛东北约 43 海里处，台湾方面船只正在对其进行监控。

It vowed to "take all necessary measures to defend national sovereignty" and ensure the security of Taiwan's waters.
它誓言将"采取一切必要措施捍卫国家主权"，并确保台湾海域的安全。

China claims democratically governed Taiwan as its own territory, despite Taipei's rejection of Beijing's sovereignty claims. Taiwan says only its people can ⁠decide their future.
中国宣称民主治理的台湾为其领土，尽管台北拒绝接受北京的主权主张。台湾表示只有其人民才能⁠决定自身未来。

China has stepped up military and coast guard activity around Taiwan, including operations to the island's east, on its Pacific side, an area Taiwan sees as vital for wartime [supply lines](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/taiwan-says-chinese-vessel-activity-jumps-raising-fears-pacific-supply-routes-2026-07-20/) from allies.
中国已加强在台湾周边的军事和海警活动，包括在岛屿东侧太平洋方向的行动——该区域被台湾视为盟友战时补给线的关键地带。

China started the eastern patrols [early ⁠in June](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/china-patrols-waters-east-taiwan-response-japan-philippine-maritime-border-talks-2026-06-01/), in response to maritime border talks between Japan and the Philippines, [drawing concern](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/uk-france-germany-raise-alarm-about-chinese-activities-off-eastern-taiwan-2026-06-24/) from countries such as Britain, France, Germany and the United States.
中国于 6 月初启动东部巡逻行动，以回应日本与菲律宾之间的海上边界谈判，此举引发英国、法国、德国及美国等国家的关切。

This week, the United States' de facto embassy in Taiwan ⁠made a [rare public disclosure](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/taiwan-drills-test-relocating-arms-production-under-chinese-attack-2026-07-28/) of coordination between their coast guards.
本周，美国在台湾的所谓“大使馆”罕见公开披露了双方海岸警卫队之间的协调行动。

It released a picture of a Taiwan coast guard vessel sailing in formation with the U.S. Coast Guard cutter Midgett at an unspecified location.

Reporting by Liz Lee in Beijing and Yimou Lee in Taipei; Editing by Tom Hogue and Clarence Fernandez
