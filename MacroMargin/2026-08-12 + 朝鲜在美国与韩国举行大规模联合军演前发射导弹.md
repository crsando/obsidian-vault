## 朝鲜在美国与韩国举行大规模联合军演前发射导弹

这张由朝鲜官方媒体朝鲜中央通讯社发布的照片显示，2026年6月25日，朝鲜在试验中用火箭发射器发射了一枚弹药。图片来自路透社/档案照片 [购买许可，打开新标签页](https://www.reutersconnect.com/item/north-korea-fires-ballistic-missile-ahead-of-major-us-south-korea-military-drills/dGFnOnJldXRlcnMuY29tLDIwMjY6bmV3c21sX1JDMkIxTUExRVpWSQ%3D%3D/?utm_medium=rcom-article-media&utm_campaign=rcom-rcp-lead)

- 摘要
- 据韩国和日本称，该导弹飞行了约 700 公里。
- 此次发射距从同一地区进行的上一次发射已有六天。
- 美韩军事演习定于 8 月 17 日至 27 日举行。
- 泽连斯基称俄罗斯在周二袭击中使用了朝鲜导弹。
首尔，8月12日（路透社）——韩国和日本当局称，朝鲜周三向朝鲜半岛东海岸外的海域发射了一枚弹道导弹，而此时距离首尔与华盛顿即将展开的联合军事演习仅剩数天，平壤一直强烈反对此类演习。

韩国联合参谋本部（JCS）称，该导弹于上午6时左右（格林尼治标准时间周二21:00）从朝鲜元山地区发射，距离朝鲜从同一地区发射另一枚弹道导弹已有[六天](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/north-korea-fires-unidentified-projectile-south-koreas-military-says-2026-08-06/)。

这次发射是在韩国和美国举行代号为“乙支自由之盾”的[军事演习](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/south-korea-us-conduct-military-drills-amid-evolving-north-korea-threats-2026-08-10/)之前进行的，该演习定于8月17日至8月27日举行，旨在应对平壤不断演进的核与武器能力。

首尔韩国统一研究院资深研究员洪敏表示，此次发射很可能是朝鲜现有武器开发计划的一部分，但时机也可能是为了在演习前传递信息。

洪敏说：“即使是对研发中武器的测试，选择何时进行也需要政治决定。”

朝鲜方面没有立即对元山发射事件发表声明，但平壤周三下午批评了韩国和日本的核野心。

韩国表示，朝鲜8月6日还从元山发射了一枚弹道导弹，但平壤没有在官方媒体上报道这次发射。洪敏表示，那次测试可能未能达到预期目的。

朝鲜今年的一系列测试以短程弹道导弹、火箭炮和其他战术武器为特点。

据韩国评估，周三的发射是2026年第11次疑似弹道导弹测试。

韩国和日本国防当局表示，该导弹飞行了约700公里（435英里）。

韩国联合参谋本部并未将其明确为短程弹道导弹，称当局将进行详细分析。

分析人士称，这一省略留下了关于导弹类型的问题。射程在300公里至1,000公里（186英里至620英里）之间的弹道导弹通常被归类为短程导弹。

洪表示，射程、轨迹和发射地点表明它可能是一种高超音速导弹，但潜艇发射的导弹也有可能。

### 谴责与紧张局势加剧

韩国和日本均谴责此次发射。

日本防卫大臣小野寺五典对记者表示：“日本通过在北京的日本大使馆通过外交渠道向朝鲜提出强烈抗议，并强烈谴责了这一行为。”

韩国国家安保室召开紧急会议，对朝鲜在8月6日发射后继续进行的弹道导弹发射表示担忧。它敦促平壤停止违反联合国安理会决议的挑衅行为。

韩国军方表示，在与美国的联合防御态势下，保持随时准备对任何挑衅做出“压倒性”回应的状态。

美国印太司令部表示正在与盟国和伙伴密切磋商，并补充说其初步评估认为此次发射对美国人员或领土及其盟友不构成直接威胁。

周二，乌克兰总统弗拉基米尔·泽连斯基表示，俄罗斯在袭击中使用了朝鲜的弹道[导弹](https://www.reuters.com/world/russian-attack-kyiv-triggers-fires-officials-say-2026-08-10/)，此次袭击导致东南部城市扎波罗热一家钢铁厂的七名工人死亡。

朝鲜外务省一名负责韩国事务的官员在声明中表示，首尔开发核动力潜艇的计划将加剧地区不稳定，对朝鲜的安全构成“严重挑战”。

声明还说，平壤需要具备以报复性打击对抗对手军事行动的能力，使对手“无法生存和恢复”。

在另一篇评论中，朝鲜官方通讯社朝中社指责日本以和平利用原子能为幌子谋求核武器，并援引日本的核电计划以及日本政界人士关于核武装的争论。

## North Korea fires missile ahead of major US-South Korea drills

A rocket launcher fires a munition during tests in North Korea, June 25, 2026, in this picture released by North Korea's official Korean Central News Agency. - Summary 摘要
- Missile flew around 700 km, say South Korea and Japan 据韩国和日本称，该导弹飞行了约 700 公里。
- Launch comes six days after another firing from the same area 此次发射发生在同一地区再次进行试射后的六天。
- US-South Korean military drills set for August 17 to 27 美韩军事演习定于 8 月 17 日至 27 日举行
- Zelenskiy says Russia used N.Korean missile in Tuesday attack 泽连斯基称俄罗斯在周二袭击中使用了朝鲜导弹
SEOUL, Aug 12 (Reuters) - North Korea fired a ballistic missile on Wednesday toward the sea off the Korean peninsula's east coast, South Korean and Japanese authorities said, days ahead of major ‌joint military exercises by Seoul and Washington long denounced by Pyongyang.
首尔，8 月 12 日（路透社）——韩国和日本当局称，朝鲜周三向朝鲜半岛东海岸外的海域发射了一枚弹道导弹，而此时距离首尔与华盛顿即将展开的联合军事演习仅剩数天，平壤一直强烈反对此类演习。

The missile was launched from the North's Wonsan area at about 6 a.m. (2100 GMT Tuesday), South Korea's Joint Chiefs of Staff (JCS) said, [six days](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/north-korea-fires-unidentified-projectile-south-koreas-military-says-2026-08-06/) after North Korea fired another ballistic missile from the same area.
韩国联合参谋本部称，该导弹于上午 6 点左右（格林尼治标准时间周二 21:00）从朝鲜元山地区发射，这距离朝鲜再次从同一地区发射弹道导弹仅过去了六天。

The launch came ahead of South Korean and U.S. [military drills](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/south-korea-us-conduct-military-drills-amid-evolving-north-korea-threats-2026-08-10/), dubbed Ulchi Freedom Shield, set to run from August 17 to August 27, aimed at countering Pyongyang's evolving nuclear and weapons capabilities.

Hong Min, a senior researcher at the Korea Institute for National Unification in Seoul, said the launch was likely part of North Korea's existing weapons development schedule, but the timing could also have been selected ⁠to send a message ahead of the exercises.

"Even if it was a test of a weapon under development, choosing when to conduct it requires a political decision," Hong said.

There was no immediate statement from North Korea on the Wonsan launch, but Pyongyang on Wednesday afternoon criticised the nuclear ambitions of South Korea and Japan.

South Korea said North Korea also fired a ballistic missile from Wonsan on August 6, but Pyongyang did not report that launch in state media. Hong said that test may have failed to achieve its intended objective.

North Korea's series of tests this year has featured short-range ballistic missiles, artillery rockets and other tactical weapons.

Wednesday's launch was its 11th suspected ballistic missile test in 2026, according to South Korean assessments.

South Korean and Japanese defence authorities said the missile flew about 700 km (435 miles).

Seoul's Joint Chiefs of Staff did not identify it as a short-range ballistic missile, saying authorities would conduct a detailed analysis.

The omission leaves open the question of what type of missile it was, analysts said. Ballistic missiles with ranges of 300 km to ‌1,000 km (186 ⁠miles to 620 miles) are generally classified as short-range.

Hong said the range, trajectory and launch site suggested it may have been a type of hypersonic missile, though a submarine-launched missile was also possible.

### CONDEMNATION AND HEIGHTENED TENSIONS

South Korea and Japan both condemned the launch.

"Japan lodged a strong protest with North Korea through diplomatic channels at the Japanese Embassy in Beijing and strongly condemned the action," Japanese Defence Minister Shinjiro Koizumi told reporters.

South Korea's Office of National Security convened an emergency meeting and expressed concern over North Korea's continued ballistic-missile launches following the August 6 firing. It urged Pyongyang ⁠to halt provocations that violate U.N. Security Council resolutions.

South Korea's military said it was maintaining readiness to respond "overwhelmingly" to any provocation under its combined defence posture with the United States.

The U.S. Indo-Pacific Command said it was consulting closely with allies and partners, adding that its initial assessment was that the launch posed no immediate threat to U.S. personnel or territory, or to its allies.

On Tuesday, ⁠Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskiy said Moscow used North Korean ballistic [missiles](https://www.reuters.com/world/russian-attack-kyiv-triggers-fires-officials-say-2026-08-10/) during a Russian attack that killed seven workers at a steel plant in the southeastern city of Zaporizhzhia.

In a statement, a North Korean Foreign Ministry official responsible for South Korean affairs said Seoul's plans to develop nuclear-powered submarines would heighten instability in the region, for a "serious challenge" to the ⁠North's security.

Pyongyang needed the capability to counter its adversaries' military moves with retaliatory strikes that would make their "survival and recovery impossible", the statement added.

In a separate commentary state news agency KCNA accused Japan of seeking nuclear weapons under the guise of peaceful use of atomic energy, citing its nuclear power programme and debate among Japanese politicians over nuclear armament.
