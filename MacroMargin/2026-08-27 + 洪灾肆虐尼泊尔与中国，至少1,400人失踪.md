### 中文译文

## 洪灾肆虐尼泊尔与中国，至少1,400人失踪

尼泊尔靠近中国边境的一处山谷突发山洪，毁灭性巨浪淹没村庄，冲毁建筑，已致至少359人死亡，超过1,400人失踪，其中包括数百名外国游客。

来自印度、马来西亚、澳大利亚、英国和美国等国的游客在灾害发生24小时后仍下落不明。这场灾害冲毁了尼泊尔山区拉苏瓦区的道路和桥梁，并引发大范围疏散警报。

根据地震和卫星数据的初步评估，此次洪水是由周三尼泊尔与中国西藏之间的喜马拉雅地区[冰川崩塌](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-27/what-fueled-the-deadly-flash-floods-on-the-nepal-china-border)引发的，该事件造成5.2级地震，将水、冰、岩石和碎屑冲向了下游。

据国际山地综合开发中心称，洪水波强度极大，在30分钟内达到了9米高。

尼泊尔国家减少灾害风险管理署表示，由于伦德河水流受阻，在尼中边境拉苏瓦加迪检查站上游约18公里处形成了一个湖泊。尼泊尔国家减少灾害风险管理署在X平台上的[一篇帖子](https://x.com/NDRRMA_Nepal/status/2092947083091427824)中写道：“该湖的天然堤坝仍存在溃决风险，可能引发另一场大洪水。”

“没人知道任何情况，”加德满都居民帕里贝什·奇卢瓦尔说。他的28岁妻子和2岁儿子在灾难后失踪。他怀孕八个月的妻子在尼泊尔拉苏瓦区提穆尔的当地卫生站工作。他将过去几个小时描述为“创伤性”。

记录这场灾难的图片和视频显示，水墙和岩石冲击了这座游客众多的山谷中的村庄。在一段广泛传播的视频中，一堵碎屑墙[吞没了西藏吉隆口岸的一处边境建筑群](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-27/tibet-mudslide-engulfs-complex-at-china-s-gateway-to-nepal)。吉隆口岸是中国通往尼泊尔的主要门户，洪水冲毁了建筑，人们纷纷逃生。

据报道，尼泊尔与中国边境发生猛烈山洪后，已有数百人死亡或失踪。来源：彭博

尼泊尔当局周四晚表示，已有359人遇难。截至周四下午，仍有910人失联，其中包括517名外国人。中国国家电视台中央电视台称，截至周四上午，边境中国一侧至少有3人死亡、558人失踪，其中包括260名外国人。

据中国官方媒体报道，中国已从遭受泥石流袭击的西藏地区疏散555人，报道称目前没有游客被困。

### **“全力救援”**

随着救援工作展开，死亡人数预计将进一步上升。对尼泊尔而言，这场灾难可能是自2015年地震以来最致命的一次，那次地震夷平了该国各地的村庄，造成近9000人死亡。

这也给这个喜马拉雅国家的[新政府](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-05/nepal-election-pits-ex-rapper-against-old-guard-vying-for-gen-zs)带来了迄今最大的挑战之一。新政府由巴伦德拉·沙阿领导，他曾是饶舌歌手和加德满都市长，去年在一场大规模Z世代抗议运动推翻前任领导层后就任总理。

这场运动由年轻人对腐败和失业的普遍不满所推动，该国是该地区最贫穷的国家之一。尼泊尔经济高度依赖海外汇款和旅游业，而旅游业自新冠疫情以来的几年里一直受到挤压。这场灾难可能会进一步加剧压力。

据尼泊尔媒体[报道](https://english.khabarhub.com/2026/27/565118/)，沙阿周四前往加德满都以北的河畔小镇比杜尔，视察受灾地区并会见受灾民众。受灾地区包括深受游客欢迎的公园和徒步路线。

在中国，国家主席习近平呼吁在西藏开展全力救援工作，此前这可能是该国今年最致命的自然灾害之一。据新华社报道，中国西部战区空军已派出指导组，在吉隆县组织联合救援行动。

周四黎明时分，灾难的规模变得更加明显，来自世界各地的游客失踪报告开始陆续传来。

来自新德里的Rishabh Jain表示，他的叔叔和婶婶在前往尼泊尔进行名为“冈仁波齐-玛旁雍错”的[宗教朝圣](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-27/india-pilgrims-missing-after-nepal-floods-ravage-himalayan-route)之旅后至今仍下落不明。Jain说，旅行社告知家人，该团的领队最后一次联系是在周三上午。此后，家人一直在“疯狂地”试图获取信息。

“此刻对我们来说，只要有确认，官方确认他们还活着、已有下落，就足够了，”Jain说，他还说，自从在网上救援画面中看到一位酷似他婶婶的女士后，希望有所增加。“并非所有希望都破灭了。”

位于加尔各答的Chalo Jaai旅行社的项目经理Yashojit Basu表示，30名孟加拉游客在朝圣途中与2名导游一同失踪。

估计该旅行团在泥石流发生的同时已抵达一个[移民局](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-27/tibet-mudslide-engulfs-complex-at-china-s-gateway-to-nepal)。视频画面显示，人们在一场洪流前逃命，洪流冲垮建筑并使碎片四飞。

Basu在埃及通过电话表示：“尚未取得联系。”他正在埃及带另一个旅行团。他说，几小时后，当地团队曾试图进行搜救行动，但由于极端条件，直升机无法降落。

Basu说：“他们告诉我们，根据破坏程度，生还的几率非常渺茫。”

尼泊尔灾害风险减少与管理局在[X](https://x.com/NDRRMA_Nepal/status/2092688918365737054?s=20)上发帖称，多达80座桥梁和40公里（25英里）的铺装公路被损坏或冲毁。该局随后补充说，有89名安全人员失踪，其中包括警察和武装部队成员。

马来西亚外交部周四表示，有55名公民下落不明，而澳大利亚总理安东尼·阿尔巴尼斯称失踪澳大利亚人人数为[34](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-26/australia-working-to-locate-citizens-missing-in-nepal-floods)。

美国国务院表示，正在与地方当局密切合作，监测尼泊尔发生致命洪水后的局势，并提供50万美元的紧急资金。

### English Original

## At Least 1,400 Missing as Flood Devastates Nepal and China

A flash flood in a Nepalese valley near the Chinese border unleashed waves of devastation that inundated villages and swept away buildings, killing** **at least 359 and leaving more than 1,400 missing, including hundreds of foreign tourists.

Visitors from countries including India, Malaysia, Australia, the UK and US are unaccounted for more than 24 hours after the disaster, which washed away roads and bridges in Nepal’s mountainous Rasuwa district and prompted widespread evacuation alerts.

Initial assessments based on seismic and satellite data indicate the flood was triggered by the [collapse of a glacier](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-27/what-fueled-the-deadly-flash-floods-on-the-nepal-china-border) in the Himalayan region between Nepal and Tibet on Wednesday, causing a 5.2-magnitude seismic event that sent water, ice, rock and debris downstream.

The flood wave was so intense it reached as high as nine meters in 30 minutes, according to the International Centre for Integrated Mountain Development.

Nepal National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Authority said a lake had formed roughly 18 kilometers above of the Rasuwagadhi checkpoint at the Nepal-China border as the flow of the Lhende River has been obstructed. “The risk still remains that the natural dam of that lake could burst and cause another major flood,” Nepal NDRRMA wrote in [a post](https://x.com/NDRRMA_Nepal/status/2092947083091427824) on X.

“Nobody knows anything,” said Paribesh Chiluwal, a Kathmandu resident whose 28-year-old wife and 2-year-old son were missing after the disaster. His wife, who is eight months pregnant, worked at the local health post in Timure, Nepal’s Rasuwa district. He described the last several hours as “traumatic.”

Images and video capturing the catastrophe showed walls of water and rock smashing into villages in the heavily visited mountain valley. In one widely circulating video, a wall of debris [overtook a border complex](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-27/tibet-mudslide-engulfs-complex-at-china-s-gateway-to-nepal) in Tibet at Gyirong Port, China’s main gateway to Nepal, washing away buildings and sending people fleeing for their lives.

Hundreds of people have been reported killed or missing after a violent flash flood at the border between Nepal and China. Source: Bloomberg

Nepalese authorities said Thursday evening that 359 had died. As of Thursday afternoon 910 people were out of contact, including 517 foreign nationals. China’s state broadcaster CCTV said as of Thursday morning there were at least three deaths and 558 missing — including 260 foreigners — on the Chinese side of the border.

China has now evacuated 555 people from the Tibet area hit by the mudslide, according to Chinese state media, which reported that no travelers are stranded anymore.

### **‘All-Out Rescue’**

The death count is widely expected to climb as rescue efforts get underway. For Nepal, the disaster may be the deadliest since the 2015 earthquake that flattened villages across the country and left nearly 9,000 dead.

It also presents one of the biggest challenges yet to the Himalayan country’s[ new government](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-05/nepal-election-pits-ex-rapper-against-old-guard-vying-for-gen-zs) led by Balendra Shah, a former rapper and Kathmandu mayor, who took over as prime minister last year following a massive Gen-Z protest movement that toppled the previous leadership.

That movement was fueled by widespread youth discontent over corruption and unemployment in a nation that is one of the region’s poorest. Nepal’s economy is highly dependent on foreign remittances and on tourism, which has been squeezed in the years since the Covid-19 pandemic. The disaster could add further strain.

Shah traveled to Bidur, a riverside town north of Kathmandu, on Thursday to inspect areas hit by the disaster and meet with those affected, Nepalese media [reported](https://english.khabarhub.com/2026/27/565118/). The disaster-hit area includes parks and trekking routes popular with tourists.

In China, President Xi Jinping called for an all-out rescue effort in Tibet after what could become one of the country’s deadliest natural disasters this year. China’s Western Theater Command Air Force has sent a guidance team to organize joint rescue operations in Gyirong county, according to Xinhua News Agency.

As dawn broke Thursday, the scale of the devastation became more apparent, and reports of missing tourists from countries around the world began to trickle in.

Rishabh Jain, who is from New Delhi, said his uncle and aunt remain unaccounted for after traveling to Nepal for a [religious pilgrimage](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-27/india-pilgrims-missing-after-nepal-floods-ravage-himalayan-route) known as Kailash-Mansarovar. Jain said the family was told by their travel agency that the group’s handler was last contacted Wednesday morning. Since then, the family has been “trying frantically” to obtain information.

“Just a confirmation, official confirmation that they are alive, they are accounted for, is OK for us at this point,” Jain said, adding that hopes have gone up since spotting a woman resembling his aunt in rescue footage online. “Not all hope is lost.”

Thirty Bengali tourists who were traveling on a pilgrimage were missing along with two guides, said Yashojit Basu, project manager at the Kolkata-based Chalo Jaai Travel Club.

The group is estimated to have reached an [immigration office](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-27/tibet-mudslide-engulfs-complex-at-china-s-gateway-to-nepal) at around the same time the mudslide hit. Video footage showed people running for their lives ahead of a deluge that toppled structures and sent debris flying.

“No contact has been made yet,” Basu said by phone from Egypt, where he is running a different tour. A local team attempted a search-and-rescue operation several hours later, he said, but was unable to land a helicopter due to the extreme conditions.

“What they told us is, based on the devastation, the chance for survival is very thin,” Basu said.

As many as 80 bridges and 40 kilometers (25 miles) of paved highway were damaged or swept away, Nepal’s Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Authority said in a [post on X](https://x.com/NDRRMA_Nepal/status/2092688918365737054?s=20). There are 89 security personnel missing, including members of the police and armed forces, it later added.

Malaysia’s foreign ministry said Thursday that 55 of its citizens were unaccounted for, while Prime Minister Anthony Albanese put the number of Australians missing at [34](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-26/australia-working-to-locate-citizens-missing-in-nepal-floods).

The US State Department said it’s working closely with local authorities to US Monitoring Situation in Nepal After Deadly Floods and is providing $500,000 in emergency funding.
