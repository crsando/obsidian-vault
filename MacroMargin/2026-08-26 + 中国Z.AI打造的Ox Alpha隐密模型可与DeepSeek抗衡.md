### 中文译文

## 中国Z.AI打造的Ox Alpha隐密模型可与DeepSeek抗衡

### **要点**

- Ox Alpha模型由中国Z.AI公司打造，已跃居AI市场OpenRouter排行榜首位。
- Z.AI公司将发布Ox Alpha的权重，允许开发者在该模型基础上构建其他产品，该模型仍可免费使用。
- 根据描述，Ox Alpha是一个推理模型，专为编码和智能体任务设计，可处理文本、图像和视频输入。
以零成本高性能席卷在线使用排行榜的新AI模型，由中国[Z.AI公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/2513%20HK%20Equity)（又称智谱）打造。

该公司周三证实了外界猜测，即Ox Alpha模型是其GLM系列的新迭代，并表示将于今晚发布其权重，以回应彭博新闻的询问。

Ox Alpha仍可免费使用，已跃居AI市场OpenRouter[排行榜](https://openrouter.ai/rankings#top-models)的榜首，使用量是DeepSeek的两倍多。公开开放权重的计划——即指导人工智能系统决策的参数——将允许开发者在该模型基础上构建其他产品。

Ox Alpha于周末作为未署名的发布出现在OpenRouter上——这是该市场历史上[规模最大](https://x.com/openrouter/status/2091912024922177562?s=46&t=AnkmPHt62Np5g-bRuMwkBg)的一次发布——并迅速在好奇的观察者和用户中引起关注。根据其描述，它是一个推理模型，专为编码和智能体任务设计，可处理文本、图像和视频输入。正在收购OpenRouter的[Stripe Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/0170016D:US)首席执行官Patrick Collison[称为](https://x.com/patrickc/status/2090833307730923528)这次秘密发布“非常令人印象深刻”。

![图片](https://pub-b62e8da959b74c1495a20ed03df4741e.r2.dev/notion-images/china/3c881cc90b1481cd8c8cca7beb0e85b2/3c881cc9-0b14-8118-a350-ca380bb7564a-9406878100bbb422ec746106ce3b7156.png)

Ox Alpha是中国公司今年夏天推出的[一系列](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-04/china-s-ai-blitz-creates-death-zone-for-rival-us-model-makers)强大模型之一，这些模型以远低于美国竞争对手的成本展现出在基准测试中领先的性能，加剧了世界两大经济体之间的AI竞赛。据Z.ai称，该模型的代号灵感来自最近在中国上映的一部热门电影，片名为*牛来*——即“牛来了”。

这家总部位于北京的公司沿用了[阿里巴巴集团控股有限公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/BABA%20US%20Equity)和[小米公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/1810%20HK%20Equity)今年使用的方式，发布新模型时[最初不公开承认](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-10/stealth-alibaba-video-ai-model-tops-global-ranking-on-debut)自己的功劳。通过在一开始不公开自己的所有权，科技公司可以测试新服务的市场反应，从而在试验其前沿产品时获得更多自由。

该公司将让所有人一周内免费使用Ox Alpha，此后费用尚未确定。该公司将于周一发布首份详细财报，涵盖自1月份上市以来的前六个月。

Ox Alpha的发布凸显了中国公司在推进技术时推出产品的惊人速度。本月早些时候，Z.ai[发布了](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-14/z-ai-aims-to-catch-anthropic-openai-in-coding-with-new-ai-model)其最新的旗舰模型GLM-5.3，该公司表示，该模型在一些基准测试中可以与Anthropic PBC的Fable 5相媲美。

### English Original

## China’s Z.AI Made Ox Alpha Stealth Model That Rivals DeepSeek

### **Takeaways**

- The Ox Alpha model was created by China's Z.AI Co. and has surged to the top spot on AI marketplace OpenRouter's leaderboard.
- Z.AI Co. will release the weights for Ox Alpha, allowing developers to build other products on top of the model, and the model is still free to use.
- Ox Alpha is a reasoning model designed for coding and agentic tasks, and can process text, image and video input, according to its description.
The new AI model that’s swept to the top of online usage charts with high performance at zero cost was created by China’s [Z.AI Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/2513%20HK%20Equity), also known as Zhipu.

The company on Wednesday confirmed speculation that the Ox Alpha model is a new iteration of its GLM series and said it will release the weights for it tonight, in response to queries by Bloomberg News.

Still free to use, Ox Alpha surged to the top spot on AI marketplace OpenRouter’s [leaderboard](https://openrouter.ai/rankings#top-models), more than doubling DeepSeek’s use. The plan to openly release its weights, the parameters that guide decision making in artificial intelligence systems, will allow developers to build other products on top of the model.

Ox Alpha emerged over the weekend as an uncredited release on OpenRouter — the [biggest](https://x.com/openrouter/status/2091912024922177562?s=46&t=AnkmPHt62Np5g-bRuMwkBg) launch in that marketplace’s history — and quickly gained traction among curious observers and users. It’s a reasoning model designed for coding and agentic tasks, and it can process text, image and video input, according to its description. Patrick Collison, the chief executive officer of [Stripe Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/0170016D:US), which is acquiring OpenRouter, [called](https://x.com/patrickc/status/2090833307730923528) the stealth-mode release “very impressive.”

Ox Alpha is [one of a series of](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-04/china-s-ai-blitz-creates-death-zone-for-rival-us-model-makers) powerful models launched by Chinese firms this summer that have shown benchmark-topping performance at a much lower cost than US rivals, intensifying the AI race between the world’s two largest economies. The code name for the model was inspired by a popular film recently released in China, titled *Niu Lai* — or “Ox Comes” — according to Z.ai.

The Beijing-based company reprised the practice used by [Alibaba Group Holding Ltd.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/BABA%20US%20Equity) and [Xiaomi Corp.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/1810%20HK%20Equity) this year of releasing a new model [without initially claiming](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-10/stealth-alibaba-video-ai-model-tops-global-ranking-on-debut) the credit. By not publicizing their ownership at the outset, tech companies can test the waters for new services, allowing more freedom to experiment with their frontier products.

The company is giving everyone a week to use Ox Alpha for free and hasn’t yet specified the cost thereafter. It will issue its first detailed earnings report on Monday, covering the first six months since its public listing in January.

The launch of Ox Alpha highlights the breakneck speed of Chinese product releases as companies advance their technology. Earlier this month, Z.ai [released](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-14/z-ai-aims-to-catch-anthropic-openai-in-coding-with-new-ai-model) its latest flagship model, GLM-5.3, which the company said can rival Anthropic PBC’s Fable 5 on some benchmarks.
