### 中文译文

## 中国央行在香港增持黄金以支持枢纽建设

据知情人士透露，中国央行正在香港储存更多黄金，此举很可能支持该市打造主要黄金交易中心的努力。

这些因讨论私人事务而要求匿名的知情人士表示，过去几个月，[中国人民银行](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/PBCZ%20CH%20Equity)在香港建立了库存。他们补充说，最新的增持正在加速一种长期趋势，即中国人民银行一直在将部分黄金储备从伦敦迁回国内。

知情人士表示，将黄金从伦敦转移至香港的行动将继续。

中国人民银行和国家外汇管理局（负责管理中国外汇储备）没有立即回复传真查询。香港财经事务及库务局发言人拒绝置评。

世界各地的许多央行选择将其部分黄金储备存放在伦敦——全球最主要的黄金市场，因为这样他们可以通过向商业银行贷出黄金来积极管理库存。持有大量黄金储备也提供了重要的流动性来源。

然而，近年来，包括印度和塞尔维亚在内的部分央行出于安全或政治原因将部分黄金[调回国内](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-10-29/india-central-bank-accelerates-repatriation-of-gold-held-abroad)。中国人民银行也是全球最大的官方部门买家之一，近几个月加大了购买力度。根据上周五发布的数据，该行7月份[增加了约20吨储备](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-07/china-s-central-bank-adds-20-tons-to-gold-reserves-in-july)。

通过将更多黄金调往香港，中国人民银行正在表明其对上个月开始试运行的[黄金清算系统](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-07/hong-kong-begins-trial-operation-of-new-gold-clearing-system)的支持。该机制包括一个[新基准](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/HAU%20BGN%20Curncy)，是加强香港在全球价格发现中作用的关键一步，也对现有中心以及地区竞争对手新加坡构成挑战，新加坡有自己的[计划](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-15/singapore-to-begin-gold-clearing-this-year-in-push-to-become-hub)来加强黄金交易。

中国人民银行行长潘功胜在7月的正式启动仪式上表示，央行将[持续增加](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-07/pboc-unveils-new-measures-to-cement-hong-kong-s-yuan-hub-status)国家外汇储备在香港的配置，重申了他2025年初做出的[承诺](https://news.cnr.cn/native/gd/20250114/t20250114_527040413.shtml)。

香港还[邀请](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-27/hong-kong-woos-central-banks-in-push-to-become-gold-trading-hub)其他央行参与清算系统，重点聚焦于参与北京“一带一路”倡议的国家。这是中国[持续努力](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-09-23/china-courts-foreign-gold-reserves-in-bid-to-boost-global-clout)成为外国黄金储备托管方的延伸，这一提议已获[柬埔寨采纳](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-11-05/china-s-offer-to-store-foreign-gold-gets-taken-up-by-cambodia)。

官方部门的购买是金价三年涨势的主要推动力，1月下旬金价曾逼近每盎司5600美元的历史高位。一个月后伊朗战争爆发加速了黄金的回调，能源驱动的通胀担忧提高了加息可能性，给无息黄金带来了阻力。

近几周，中国的购买在维持金价基本高于每盎司4000美元关键支撑位方面发挥了重要作用。除了中国人民银行增加的购买量外，机构投资者也在价格下跌时涌入，中国黄金支持的交易所交易基金录得自3月以来[最长的](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-04/chinese-dip-buying-bolsters-gold-as-prices-find-floor-at-4-000)资金流入连续纪录。

### English Original

## China’s Central Bank Adds Gold in Hong Kong to Support Hub

China’s central bank is stockpiling more gold in Hong Kong, according to people familiar with the matter, in a move that’s likely to support the city’s push to become a major bullion-trading hub.

The [People’s Bank of China](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/PBCZ%20CH%20Equity) has built up inventories in Hong Kong over the past few months, said the people, who asked not to be named discussing private matters. The latest additions are accelerating a longer-term trend whereby the PBOC has been moving some of its gold reserves back home from London, they added.

That relocation of metal from London to Hong Kong is set to continue, the people said.

PBOC and the State Administration of Foreign Exchange, which oversees China’s foreign-exchange reserves, didn’t immediately respond to faxed inquiries. A spokesperson for Hong Kong’s Financial Services and the Treasury Bureau declined to comment.

Many central banks around the world choose to store a portion of their bullion reserves in London, the world’s dominant bullion market, as it allows them to actively manage their inventories by lending out the metal to commercial banks. Large volumes of gold held in reserves also provide an important source of liquidity.

In recent years, however, some central banks, including India’s and Serbia’s, have [repatriated](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-10-29/india-central-bank-accelerates-repatriation-of-gold-held-abroad)some of their bullion for security or political reasons. The PBOC has also been among the world’s biggest official-sector buyers, and has stepped up purchases in recent months. It added about [20 tons to reserves](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-07/china-s-central-bank-adds-20-tons-to-gold-reserves-in-july) in July, according to data released on Friday.

By diverting more gold to Hong Kong, the PBOC is signaling its support for a [gold clearing system](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-07/hong-kong-begins-trial-operation-of-new-gold-clearing-system) that was launched on a trial basis last month. The mechanism, which includes a [new benchmark](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/HAU%20BGN%20Curncy), is a key step in strengthening Hong Kong’s role in global price discovery and presents a challenge to established centers as well as regional rival Singapore, which has [plans of its own](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-15/singapore-to-begin-gold-clearing-this-year-in-push-to-become-hub) to enhance gold trading.

PBOC Governor Pan Gongsheng said at the official launch ceremony in July that the central bank would [keep increasing](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-07/pboc-unveils-new-measures-to-cement-hong-kong-s-yuan-hub-status) the allocation of national foreign reserves to Hong Kong, reiterating a [pledge](https://news.cnr.cn/native/gd/20250114/t20250114_527040413.shtml) he made in early 2025.

The city has also [invited](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-27/hong-kong-woos-central-banks-in-push-to-become-gold-trading-hub) other central banks to participate in the clearing system, focusing on nations involved in Beijing’s Belt and Road Initiative. That’s an extension of China’s [ongoing efforts](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-09-23/china-courts-foreign-gold-reserves-in-bid-to-boost-global-clout) to become a custodian of foreign gold reserves, an offer already [taken up by Cambodia](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-11-05/china-s-offer-to-store-foreign-gold-gets-taken-up-by-cambodia).

Official-sector purchases were a major driver of gold’s three-year rally to a record high near $5,600 an ounce in late January. The start of the Iran war a month later accelerated the metal’s pullback, with energy-led inflation concerns raising the likelihood of higher interest rates and creating headwinds for non-yielding bullion.

In recent weeks, Chinese purchases have been instrumental in keeping gold largely above the key $4,000-an-ounce support threshold. As well as the increased volumes bought by the PBOC, institutional investors have swooped as prices dropped, with bullion-backed exchange-traded funds in China recording the [longest streak](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-04/chinese-dip-buying-bolsters-gold-as-prices-find-floor-at-4-000) of inflows since March.
