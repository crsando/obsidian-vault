### 中文译文

## 美国称中国关联黑客瞄准NASA、美联储和参议院

美国司法部表示，当局破坏了据称由中国国家支持的黑客用于攻击美国政府机构和关键基础设施的网络基础设施。

该机构在周三的一份[声明](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/justice-department-and-fbi-seize-platforms-operated-and-used-china-state-sponsored-hackers)中表示，这些被指控的入侵影响了包括NASA、美联储、能源部和美国参议院在内的实体。它指控一个名为QTFY的中国关联组织利用两个平台实施攻击，这两个平台已在与联邦调查局的联合行动中被禁用。

美国司法部称，QTFY受雇于总部位于中国的南京新久微网络技术有限公司（Nanjing Xinjiuwei Network Technology Company），并向包括中国国家安全部和中国人民解放军在内的付费客户提供黑客服务。据该部门称，这些被称为QScan和QTRouter的平台允许黑客通过中国境外的受感染设备路由恶意流量，使活动看起来来自其他地点。

该部门没有说明黑客据称在这些组织访问了哪些系统，也没有说明是否有任何信息被盗。

美国当局表示，此次行动是破坏针对敏感网络的中国网络活动的更广泛努力的一部分。根据联邦调查局和国家安全局发布的一份[网络安全公告](https://www.ic3.gov/CSA/2026/260826.pdf)，QTFY的活动至少可以追溯到2018年。

中国外交部指责美国在毫无证据的情况下抹黑中国。外交部发言人林剑在周四的例行记者会上批评华盛顿“以网络安全为借口向中国泼脏水”。南京新久微网络科技有限公司未能立即联系到置评。

### English Original

## US Says China-Linked Hackers Targeted NASA, Fed and Senate

The US Justice Department said authorities disrupted online infrastructure allegedly used by Chinese state-sponsored hackers to target American government agencies and critical infrastructure.

The alleged intrusions affected entities including NASA, the Federal Reserve, the Department of Energy and the US Senate, the agency said in a Wednesday [statement](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/justice-department-and-fbi-seize-platforms-operated-and-used-china-state-sponsored-hackers). It accused a China-linked group called QTFY of carrying out the attacks using two platforms that were disabled in a joint operation with the FBI.

The Justice Department said QTFY was employed by China-based Nanjing Xinjiuwei Network Technology Company and offered hacking services to paying customers, including the Chinese Ministry of State Security and People’s Liberation Army. The platforms, known as QScan and QTRouter, allowed hackers to route malicious traffic through compromised devices outside China, making the activity appear to originate elsewhere, according to the department.

The department did not say what systems the hackers allegedly accessed at those organizations or whether any information was stolen.

US authorities said the operation was part of a broader effort to disrupt Chinese cyber campaigns targeting sensitive networks. QTFY’s activities date back to at least 2018, according to a [cybersecurity advisory released](https://www.ic3.gov/CSA/2026/260826.pdf) by the Federal Bureau of Investigation and National Security Agency.

The Chinese Ministry of Foreign Affairs accused the US of smearing China without evidence. Lin Jian, a ministry spokesman, criticized Washington for “slinging mud at China under the pretext of cybersecurity” during a regular press briefing on Thursday. Nanjing Xinjiuwei Network Technology could not immediately be reached for comment.
