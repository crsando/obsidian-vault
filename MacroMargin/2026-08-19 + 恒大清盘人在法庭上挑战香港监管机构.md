### 中文译文

## 恒大清盘人在法庭上挑战香港监管机构

### **要点**

- 香港法院正在审理一项关于补偿中国恒大少数股东的交易是否有效的辩论。
- 该交易由证券及期货事务监察委员会与普华永道香港附属公司达成，以了结对该公司为这家开发商进行的审计工作的调查。
- 恒大的清盘人寻求宣布该交易“非法、无效和/或不可执行”，认为证监会缺乏与普华永道香港等非受监管实体达成和解的法定权力。
香港一家法院开始审理一项设立基金以补偿[中国恒大](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/3333%20HK%20Equity)少数股东的交易是否有效的辩论。

该市的证券及期货事务监察委员会于4月与普华永道香港附属公司达成了10亿港元（约合1.276亿美元）的[协议](https://apps.sfc.hk/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/news-and-announcements/news/doc?refNo=26PR62)，以了结对该事务所为这家开发商进行的审计工作的调查。这家开发商的倒闭是中国房地产危机的象征。证监会当时表示，该协议解决了此事，普华永道香港无需承认责任。

在周三的听证会上，普华永道和恒大清盘人的律师表示，该会计师事务所的香港附属公司已在单独的银行账户中为该基金预留了10亿港元。清盘人的律师还要求查看证监会与普华永道之间的协议条款，并询问了有资格获得补偿的股东详情。监管机构表示这些属于机密信息，并补充说，清盘人对普华永道的索赔能否成功仍不确定。

高等法院法官罗文汉表示，他将在10月30日前就该协议是否应公开作出裁决。之后，清盘人将能够提交进一步论据，并随后就该补偿协议的有效性作出最终裁决。

此案考验香港法院是否愿意在债权人提出法律异议后撤销监管机构的决定。它也凸显了清盘案件中关于优先顺序的争夺，并可能影响债权人最终能够追回多少资金。

清盘人表示，香港证监会（SFC）的股东赔偿协议将股东置于债权人之前。根据香港[法律](https://www.elegislation.gov.hk/hk/cap32)，清盘案件中股东最后获得偿付，且仅在所有债权人的债权得到满足后尚有剩余资产的情况下。

恒大清盘人早前已寻求对赔偿协议进行[司法审查](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-23/court-to-review-evergrande-liquidators-challenge-to-hk-watchdog)，称香港证监会缺乏与普华永道香港（PwC HK）等非受监管实体达成[和解](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-16/evergrande-liquidators-seek-review-of-pwc-hk-s-128-million-deal)的法定权力，并辩称责任在于该市的会计监管机构。他们试图让香港证监会的决定（包括该基金）被宣布为“非法、无效及/或失效”。

香港证监会6月表示，尽管面临法律挑战，它仍[推进](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-17/sfc-moving-ahead-with-hk-1-billion-evergrande-payout-plan)其计划。

根据清盘人的评估，恒大的负债将远超任何追回的资产，因此股东“不可能”获得任何赔款。他们表示，恒大的债务负担比先前估计的更大，[达到](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-08-12/evergrande-s-debt-hits-45-billion-with-restructuring-ruled-out)3500亿港元。

各方都在寻求从普华永道获取资金。由于对恒大的审计，该公司已经面临重大的财务和监管压力。其中国大陆关联公司此前因在审计这家中国开发商中的角色而被[罚款](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2024-09-13/china-fines-pwc-halts-business-for-six-months-over-evergrande)4.41亿元人民币。

恒大清盘人[寻求](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-18/evergrande-liquidators-pursue-claims-against-pwc-in-hk-court)在针对普华永道国际有限公司及其中国大陆和香港关联公司的诉讼中获得570亿元人民币（84亿美元）的赔偿，这是该市有史以来规模最大的企业索赔之一。

与此同时，普华永道本地关联公司的一些合伙人正在探索[保护](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-10/some-pwc-partners-seek-to-protect-assets-over-evergrande-risk)个人资产的策略，以防该公司面临的法律和监管挑战不断升级，最终转化为他们个人的财务或法律责任。

### English Original

## Evergrande Liquidators Challenge Hong Kong Watchdog in Court

### **Takeaways**

- A Hong Kong court is hearing arguments on whether a deal to compensate China Evergrande minority shareholders is valid.
- The deal was struck between the Securities and Futures Commission and PricewaterhouseCoopers' Hong Kong affiliate to settle investigations into the firm's auditing work for the developer.
- Evergrande's liquidators are seeking to have the deal declared "unlawful, void and/or invalid", arguing that the SFC lacks the statutory authority to enter into a settlement with a non-regulated entity like PwC HK.
A Hong Kong court began hearing arguments on whether a deal that set up a fund to compensate [China Evergrande](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/3333%20HK%20Equity) minority shareholders is valid.

The city’s Securities and Futures Commission struck the HK$1 billion ($127.6 million) [deal](https://apps.sfc.hk/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/news-and-announcements/news/doc?refNo=26PR62) with PricewaterhouseCoopers’ Hong Kong affiliate in April to settle investigations into the firm’s auditing work for the developer, whose downfall was emblematic of China’s property crisis. The agreement resolved the matter without an admission of liability by PwC HK, the SFC said at the time.

During the hearing Wednesday, lawyers for PwC and Evergrande’s liquidators said the auditor’s Hong Kong affiliate had already set aside HK$1 billion for the fund in a separate bank account. Lawyers for the liquidators also asked to see the terms of the agreement between the SFC and PwC, and sought details of shareholders who would be eligible for compensation. The watchdog said that was confidential information, adding that it is still uncertain that liquidators will prevail in their claims against PwC.

High Court Judge Russell Coleman said he would make a decision on whether the agreement should be disclosed by Oct. 30. After that, liquidators will be able to submit further arguments and a final decision on the validity of the compensation deal will be made later.

The case is a test of whether a Hong Kong court is willing to quash a regulator’s decision after legal pushback from creditors. It also underscores the battle over seniority in winding-up cases, and could affect how much money creditors are eventually able to recover.

Liquidators say the SFC’s deal to compensate shareholders prioritizes them over creditors. Under Hong Kong [law](https://www.elegislation.gov.hk/hk/cap32), shareholders are paid last in liquidation cases and only if there is any surplus after all creditors are satisfied.

Evergrande’s liquidators had earlier sought the judicial [review](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-23/court-to-review-evergrande-liquidators-challenge-to-hk-watchdog) of the compensation deal, saying that the SFC lacks the statutory authority to enter into a [settlement](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-16/evergrande-liquidators-seek-review-of-pwc-hk-s-128-million-deal) with a non-regulated entity like PwC HK, arguing that responsibility lies with the city’s accounting watchdog. They are seeking to have the SFC’s decisions, including the fund, declared “unlawful, void and/or invalid.”

The SFC said in June that it was [proceeding](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-17/sfc-moving-ahead-with-hk-1-billion-evergrande-payout-plan) with its plan, despite the legal challenge.

According to liquidators’ assessment, Evergrande’s liabilities will far exceed any assets recovered, so “there is no possibility” shareholders will receive any payout. Evergrande’s debt burden is larger than previously estimated, [reaching](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-08-12/evergrande-s-debt-hits-45-billion-with-restructuring-ruled-out) HK$350 billion, they have said.

Different parties are seeking to extract funds from PwC. The firm is already under significant financial and regulatory pressure due to its audits of Evergrande. Its mainland China affiliate was previously [fined](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2024-09-13/china-fines-pwc-halts-business-for-six-months-over-evergrande) 441 million yuan for its role in auditing the Chinese developer.

Evergrande liquidators are [seeking](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-18/evergrande-liquidators-pursue-claims-against-pwc-in-hk-court) 57 billion yuan ($8.4 billion) in a lawsuit against PricewaterhouseCoopers International Ltd. and its mainland China and Hong Kong affiliates, among the largest corporate claims ever sought in the city.

Meanwhile, some partners at PwC’s local affiliates are exploring strategies to [safeguard](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-10/some-pwc-partners-seek-to-protect-assets-over-evergrande-risk) their personal assets, in case the escalating legal and regulatory challenges facing the firm ultimately translate into financial or legal liabilities for them individually.
