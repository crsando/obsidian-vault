### 中文译文

## 伊朗加大控制霍尔木兹海峡力度，油轮在该海峡停下

### **要点**

- 一艘燃料油轮在伊朗格什姆岛附近进行了一系列不规则的转弯后停了下来，与此同时，伊朗正加大控制霍尔木兹海峡的力度。
- 法尔斯通讯社报道称，一艘属于阿联酋一家公司的油轮在海峡被扣留，并补充说，船只必须为通过霍尔木兹海峡支付费用。
- 英国海军报告称，另一艘船只在沿阿曼海岸驶出海湾时被一枚不明射弹击中。
一艘试图进入波斯湾的燃料油轮在伊朗格什姆岛附近进行了一系列不规则的转弯后停了下来，与此同时，伊朗正加大控制霍尔木兹海峡的力度。

船舶追踪数据显示，“Amara”号周一空载驶入海湾，应答器处于开启状态，随后至少进行了五次掉头。目前，该船停在该水道北部，这条水道一直是伊朗批准的过境通道。

与此同时，伊朗半官方的法尔斯通讯社周一报道称，一艘属于阿联酋一家公司（未具名）的油轮在海峡被扣留，并补充说，船只必须为通过霍尔木兹海峡的过境付费。

周一，“Amara”号在驶入波斯湾时被看到在霍尔木兹海峡掉头。来源：彭博

数小时后，英国海军报告称，一艘船只在霍尔木兹海峡被未知射弹击中，造成一人死亡，当时该船正沿阿曼海岸驶出海湾。

船东和租船人对船只要么关闭识别系统（暗航）要么开启应答器通过海峡越来越感到紧张，因为伊朗势力在过去一周加强了敌对行动。根据联合海事信息中心（一个海军安全监控组织）的数据，在最新一次袭击之前，本月已有七起针对穿越霍尔木兹海峡船只的袭击，其中三起发生在周五以来。

这一激增之际，所谓的[穿梭贸易](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-16/covert-mideast-oil-flows-are-keeping-global-prices-in-check)（即原油出波斯湾后，在阿曼湾转运到其他船只）日益流行，并有助于抑制油价上涨。阿联酋的阿布扎比国家石油公司一直是使用这种方法保持能源出口的主要用户，现在[沙特阿拉伯](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-17/saudis-offer-oil-from-near-oman-in-possible-sign-of-shuttling)似乎也在加强类似行动。

Amara 驻阿联酋的技术与安全经理没有立即回应通过电子邮件发送的置评请求。海事数据库 Equasis 上未列出其船东的联系方式。

## Tanker Stops in Hormuz as Iran Raises Efforts to Control Strait

### **Takeaways**

- A fuel tanker has made an erratic series of turns and come to a halt near Iran’s Qeshm Island, as the country steps up efforts to control the Strait of Hormuz.
- The Fars news agency reported that a tanker belonging to a United Arab Emirates company was detained in the strait, adding that ships must pay for transits through Hormuz.
- The UK Navy reported that another vessel had been struck by an unknown projectile as it was exiting the gulf along the Omani coast.
A fuel tanker trying to enter the Persian Gulf has made an erratic series of turns and come to a halt near Iran’s Qeshm Island, as the country steps up efforts to control the Strait of Hormuz.

The Amara sailed into the gulf empty on Monday with its transponders on, before making a series of at least five U-turns, ship-tracking data show. It’s now at a standstill in the northern part of the waterway that has served as a route for transits approved by Iran.

That came as the country’s semi-official Fars news agency reported Monday that a tanker belonging to a United Arab Emirates company, which it didn’t name, was [detained in the strait](https://x.com/FarsNews_Agency/status/2089383558645182889), adding that ships must pay for transits through Hormuz.

The Amara was seen U-turning in the Strait of Hormuz on Monday, as it was sailing into the Persian Gulf. Source: Bloomberg

Hours later, the UK Navy reported that UK Navy Says Vessel Struck by Projectile in Hormuz, One Deadhad been struck by an unknown projectile as it was exiting the gulf along the Omani coast.

Shipowners and charterers have become increasingly nervous about having vessels cross the strait either dark or with transponders on, as Iranian forces stepped up hostilities over the past week. Before the latest strike, there had been seven attacks on ships crossing Hormuz this month, with three of them taking place since Friday, according to the [Joint Maritime Information Center](https://combinedmaritimeforces.com/combined-task-forces/joint-maritime-information-center/), a naval security monitoring group.

The upsurge comes as the so-called [shuttle trade](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-16/covert-mideast-oil-flows-are-keeping-global-prices-in-check), where crude is ferried out of the gulf and then transferred to other ships in the Gulf of Oman, increases in popularity, and helps to keep a lid on oil price rises. The UAE’s Abu Dhabi National Oil Co. has been a major user of this method to keep exporting energy, and it now appears that [Saudi Arabia](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-17/saudis-offer-oil-from-near-oman-in-possible-sign-of-shuttling) may be ramping up similar efforts.

Amara’s UAE-based technical and safety manager didn’t immediately respond to an emailed request for comment. There were no contact details listed on maritime database Equasis for its owner.
