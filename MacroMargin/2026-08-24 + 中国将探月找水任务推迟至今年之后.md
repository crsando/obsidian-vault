### 中文译文

## 中国将探月找水任务推迟至今年之后

中国推迟了一项雄心勃勃的太空任务的发射，该任务旨在[搜索](https://www.chinadailyasia.com/article/638159)月球上的冰冻水，发射时间将延至今年之后。

中国国家航天局在周日晚间发布的一份简短[声明](https://www.cnsa.gov.cn/n6758823/n6758838/c10770822/content.html)中表示，由于发射条件未满足，嫦娥七号航天器无法在今年窗口期内发射。声明未详细说明原因或下次发射时间。

嫦娥七号不仅仅是中国的又一次探月之旅。该任务旨在搜索冰冻水并勘测月球南极，这一地区可能支持永久基地，也已成为NASA阿尔忒弥斯计划的[关注焦点](https://www.nasa.gov/moonbase/)。中国的目标是2030年前实现载人登月，而NASA[计划](https://www.nasa.gov/humans-in-space/artemis/)在2028年将宇航员送回月球表面，这使得两大航天强国在建立月球长期存在的竞赛中日益并驾齐驱。

近期，一家中国初创公司在缩小与美国剩余的重大技术差距方面迈出了一大步——这可能使太空旅行更加经济实惠。[蓝箭航天科技公司](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1678414D:CH)上周成为中国内地首家成功[回收](https://www.reuters.com/science/landspace-nails-rocket-booster-landing-first-chinas-private-launchers-2026-08-18/)轨道火箭助推器的私营公司，加入了埃隆·马斯克的[SpaceX](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/SPCX%20US%20Equity)和杰夫·贝佐斯的[蓝色起源](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/5043517Z:US)之列。蓝箭航天目前正在推进首次公开募股。

除嫦娥七号探测器外，中国还计划随后发射嫦娥八号，这两个任务旨在为未来的月球研究站奠定基础。

### English Original

## China Delays Mission to Find Water on Moon Until After This Year

China delayed the launch of an ambitious space mission to [search](https://www.chinadailyasia.com/article/638159) for frozen water on the moon until beyond this year.

The Chang’e-7 spacecraft couldn’t proceed with its blastoff within this year’s window because the launch requirements weren’t met, the China National Space Administration said in a short [statement](https://www.cnsa.gov.cn/n6758823/n6758838/c10770822/content.html) late Sunday. It didn’t elaborate on the reasons or when the next launch would be.

Chang’e-7 is more than just another Chinese trip to the moon. The mission is designed to search for frozen water and survey the lunar south pole, a region that could support permanent bases and has also [become a focus](https://www.nasa.gov/moonbase/) of NASA’s Artemis program. China is targeting a crewed lunar landing by 2030, while NASA [plans to return](https://www.nasa.gov/humans-in-space/artemis/) astronauts to the surface in 2028, putting the top space powers on increasingly parallel tracks in the race to establish a lasting presence on the moon.

Recently, a Chinese startup took a big step toward closing a major remaining technology gap with the US — one that could make space travel more affordable. [LandSpace Technology Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1678414D:CH) last week became the first private mainland company to [land](https://www.reuters.com/science/landspace-nails-rocket-booster-landing-first-chinas-private-launchers-2026-08-18/) an orbital rocket booster, joining Elon Musk’s [SpaceX](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/SPCX%20US%20Equity) and Jeff Bezos’ [Blue Origin](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/5043517Z:US). LandSpace is now pursuing an initial public offering.

Beyond the Chang’e-7 probe, China plans to follow it with Chang’e-8, with the two missions intended to lay the groundwork for a future lunar research station.
