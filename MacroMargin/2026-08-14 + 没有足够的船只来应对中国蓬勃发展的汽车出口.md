### 国内需求下滑之际，中国汽车正涌入其他市场，同时也在考验航运业

Aug. 13, 2026 10:00 pm ET

中国汽车制造商比亚迪拥有自己的船只，将汽车运往出口市场。AFP/Getty Images

- 中国汽车出口的激增正超出全球航运业的承载能力，专用汽车运输船的预订已排到数年后。
- 6月，大型汽车运输船的日均包租费率达到7万美元，高于去年年底的4.25万美元。
- 一些汽车制造商正转而采用集装箱运输车辆，而比亚迪则推出了自己的八艘汽车运输船队。
- 中国汽车出口的激增正超出全球航运业的承载能力，专用汽车运输船的预订已排到数年后。**查看更多**
中国的汽车工厂正在生产大量出口汽车，全球航运业应接不暇。

专用汽车运输船（本质上就是漂浮的停车库）的档期已经排到数年后，用于从中国工厂出口汽车。由于将汽车运出中国的需求激增，今年包船费率上涨了65%。

由于超过100个国内汽车品牌之间的[激烈竞争](https://www.wsj.com/business/autos/china-auto-industry-outlook-2025-1afec5a0?mod=article_inline)、产能过剩以及[经济低迷](https://www.wsj.com/business/autos/chinas-auto-market-weakens-further-on-subdued-domestic-demand-8bc84990?mod=article_inline)，中国汽车制造商正在涌入海外市场。如今，航运业正面临费率飙升和运力紧缺的局面。

一些汽车制造商急于将汽车运往欧洲、澳大利亚和拉丁美洲，不得不将它们塞进通常用于运输家具、服装和电子产品的集装箱里。

全球最大汽车运输船队Wallenius Wilhelmsen的首席执行官Lasse Kristoffersen在今年早些时候的财报电话会议上表示：“航运市场如此强劲令人惊叹，这要归因于中国出口的史无前例增长。”

近年来，航运公司掀起了创纪录的购船热潮以满足需求。Kristoffersen表示，全球汽车运输船队规模已增长约40%，但仍然无法满足中国的需求。

根据研究机构Mobility Global的数据，2019年中国出口了略低于60万辆汽车和厢式货车。该机构预计，今年中国可能出口多达1000万辆汽车。

根据欧洲汽车制造商协会的数据，2026年上半年，中国[上汽集团](https://www.wsj.com/market-data/quotes/CN/XSHG/600104) [600104 -0.69%下跌；下箭头](https://www.wsj.com/market-data/quotes/CN/XSHG/600104)的欧盟注册量跃升19%，[比亚迪](https://www.wsj.com/market-data/quotes/CN/XSHE/002594) [002594 -0.90%下跌；下箭头](https://www.wsj.com/market-data/quotes/CN/XSHE/002594)增加超过一倍。与此同时，传统竞争对手停滞不前：Stellantis仅增长6%，大众微升2.6%，雷诺下降4.2%。

挪威汽车运输船公司[Höegh Autoliners](https://www.wsj.com/market-data/quotes/NO/XOSL/HAUTO)的首席执行官安德烈亚斯·恩格尔表示：“中国在短短五年内从无足轻重变成了全球最大的汽车出口国。”

恩格尔表示，这一繁荣已将汽车海运价格推高至疫情前水平的两倍。

行业高管原本预计，由于大量新船投入使用，今年船舶租船费率将大幅下滑。2022年至2025年间，随着消费者购车支出激增以及中国出口开始快速增长，各公司订购了价值数十亿美元的汽车运输船。对舱位的需求使2023年末和2024年初的租船费率升至每日11.5万美元的峰值。

然而，由于承运商难以满足需求，费率再次飙升，很大程度上是因为[中国汽车出口](https://www.wsj.com/business/autos/everyone-loves-chinese-cars-except-the-chinese-b1533c82?mod=article_inline)。据船舶经纪公司克拉克森（Clarksons）称，6月份大型汽车运输船的年均包租费率达到每日7万美元，而去年年底为4.25万美元。

除少数例外，中国汽车不出口到美国，原因是高关税和与国家安全问题相关的软件限制。然而，来自比亚迪和上汽集团等品牌的更便宜、技术更先进的纯电动和混合动力汽车，不仅在中国超越了长期占据主导地位的西方品牌，而且越来越多地在英国、巴西和德国等国家也这样做。

咨询公司Sino Auto Insights的董事总经理杜乐（Tu Le）表示，随着国内销售放缓，中国汽车行业激进的出口计划相当于一个“泄压阀”。根据国际能源署的数据，2026年上半年，中国汽车销量比去年同期下降超过20%。

“当你去德国时，不会像在上海那样有20个其他中国汽车品牌挤兑你争取那一单生意。”Le说道。

法国基华物流（Ceva Logistics）成品车物流副总裁Eric Dessupoiu表示，汽车制造商更倾向于使用汽车运输船，因为与其他运输方式相比，车辆可直接开上开下，成本更低且车辆损坏风险更小。不过，Dessupoiu表示，别无选择的汽车制造商将使用集装箱运输车辆。

Wallenius Wilhelmsen的Kristoffersen在周二的财报发布会上表示，中国每年有多达400万辆汽车通过集装箱或其他替代汽车运输船的方式出口。

#### 汽车运输的三种主要方式

专用汽车运输船

这是将数千辆汽车运往海外的最高效方式。汽车可以快速、轻松地驶上驶下船舶，车辆存放在多层空间，就像一个浮动停车场。这能将运输成本降至最低，并降低损坏风险。

集装箱运输汽车

一种更昂贵且需要多个步骤的方法。承运人将两到四辆汽车装入一个集装箱，然后用起重机吊到船上。这种技术被认为更容易造成车辆损坏。

专用货架系统

最不常见的汽车运输方式。车辆被放置在金属货架上，由起重机吊起并储存在货船的货舱内。货架可以相互堆叠，使承运人能够用一艘船运输数百辆汽车。

用集装箱运输汽车并非新鲜事；只是通常不会大规模地运输数千辆汽车。这种情况在新冠疫情期间发生了变化，当时运输车辆的需求超过了船舶供应。迪拜货运代理公司DP World的成品车全球副总裁克里斯托夫·塞茨表示，一些西方汽车制造商对把汽车装入集装箱持怀疑态度，但中国汽车制造商毫不犹豫。“他们立刻表示，‘我们需要更多运力’”，塞茨说。

通过集装箱运输的汽车必须先运到港口附近的设施，在那里装入集装箱，然后由起重机吊上船。在目的港，这个过程必须反向重复。

塞茨表示，用集装箱运输汽车已变得如此普遍，以至于丹麦的马士基（A.P. Moller-Maersk）和瑞士的地中海航运公司（Mediterranean Shipping Co.）等大型集装箱航运公司已开始直接向汽车制造商销售服务。

出口供应量如今如此巨大，中国汽车制造商也在向航运领域扩张。比亚迪于2024年推出了首艘专用汽车运输船，目前拥有8艘船舶。该公司未回应置评请求。

## **There Aren’t Enough Ships to Handle China’s Booming Car Exports**

### As demand slumps at home, Chinese cars are flooding other markets—and testing the shipping industry

By

Aug. 13, 2026 10:00 pm ET

Chinese automaker BYD has its own vessel to ship its cars to export markets. AFP/Getty Images

- A surge in car exports from China is outstripping the capacity of the global shipping industry, booking out specialized car carriers years ahead.
- The average annual rate to charter a large car carrier reached $70,000 a day in June, up from $42,500 at the end of last year.
- Some automakers are resorting to shipping vehicles inside containers, while BYD has launched its own fleet of eight car carriers.
- A surge in car exports from China is outstripping the capacity of the global shipping industry, booking out specialized car carriers years ahead.**View more**
China’s auto factories are building so many cars for export that the global shipping industry can’t keep up.

Specialized car carriers, essentially floating parking garages, are booked out years ahead to export cars from Chinese factories. Rates to charter ships are up 65% this year on the surging demand to move vehicles out of China.

Chinese carmakers are flooding foreign markets because of [fierce competition](https://www.wsj.com/business/autos/china-auto-industry-outlook-2025-1afec5a0?mod=article_inline) between more than 100 domestic auto brands, overproduction and a [sluggish economy](https://www.wsj.com/business/autos/chinas-auto-market-weakens-further-on-subdued-domestic-demand-8bc84990?mod=article_inline). Now, the shipping industry is navigating skyrocketing rates and scarce capacity.

Some automakers are so desperate to get vehicles to Europe, Australia and Latin America they are squeezing them inside shipping containers more commonly used to transport furniture, clothes and electronics.

“The strength of the market in the shipping segment is amazing, and it is due to the unprecedented growth of exports out of China,” said Lasse Kristoffersen, chief executive of the world’s largest car-carrier fleet, Wallenius Wilhelmsen, on an earnings call earlier this year.

Carriers have gone on a record buying spree in recent years to meet demand. Kristoffersen said the global car-carrier fleet has grown by about 40%, but still can’t meet China’s needs.

China exported just under 600,000 cars and vans in 2019, according to research group Mobility Global. This year, the group forecasts China could ship up to 10 million vehicles.

Registrations in the European Union for China’s [SAIC Motor](https://www.wsj.com/market-data/quotes/CN/XSHG/600104) [600104 -0.69%decrease; down pointing triangle](https://www.wsj.com/market-data/quotes/CN/XSHG/600104) jumped 19% and [BYD’s](https://www.wsj.com/market-data/quotes/CN/XSHE/002594) [002594 -0.90%decrease; down pointing triangle](https://www.wsj.com/market-data/quotes/CN/XSHE/002594) more than doubled in the first half of 2026, according to the European Automobile Manufacturers’ Association. Meanwhile, legacy rivals stagnated: Stellantis gained just 6%, Volkswagen edged up 2.6%, and Renault fell 4.2%.

“You’ve got China moving from being insignificant to being the world’s largest vehicle exporter in only a five-year period,” said Andreas Enger, chief executive of Norwegian car carrier [Höegh Autoliners](https://www.wsj.com/market-data/quotes/NO/XOSL/HAUTO).

The boom has pushed ocean freight rates for cars to double their prepandemic levels, Enger said.

Industry executives had expected vessel charter rates to slump this year due to a big influx of new ships entering service. Companies ordered billions of dollars worth of car carriers between 2022 and 2025 as consumer spending on vehicles surged and Chinese exports started their rapid rise. Instead, rates are soaring again as carriers struggle to meet demand, largely because of [Chinese auto exports](https://www.wsj.com/business/autos/everyone-loves-chinese-cars-except-the-chinese-b1533c82?mod=article_inline). The average annual rate to charter a large car carrier hit $70,000 a day in June, according to shipbroker Clarksons, up from $42,500 at the end of last year.

With a handful of exceptions, Chinese cars aren’t exported to the U.S., thanks to high tariffs and software restrictions tied to national security concerns. Yet cheaper and more technologically advanced battery-electric and hybrid cars from brands like BYD and SAIC Motor haven’t only overtaken longtime Western brands in China, but they also are increasingly doing so in countries like the U.K., Brazil and Germany.

Tu Le, managing director of advisory firm Sino Auto Insights, said the Chinese auto industry’s aggressive export plans equate to a “pressure release valve” as sales at home slow. In the first half of 2026, car sales in China fell more than 20% from the same year-earlier period, according to data from the International Energy Agency.

“When you go to Germany, you don’t have 20 other Chinese car brands that are elbowing you to get that one sale, like you have in Shanghai,” Le said.

Automakers prefer to ship on car carriers because driving cars on and off vessels is cheaper than other shipping methods and carries less risk of vehicle damage, said Eric Dessupoiu, vice president of finished vehicle logistics at France’s Ceva Logistics. Still, automakers that have no other choice will move cars in containers, Dessupoiu said.

Wallenius Wilhelmsen’s Kristoffersen said during an earnings presentation on Tuesday that up to four million vehicles are exported from China each year in containers or other alternatives to car carriers.

#### Three main methods of shipping autos

Dedicated car carrier

The most efficient method for moving thousands of vehicles across the ocean. Cars are quickly and easily driven on and off the vessel where they are stored on multiple levels like a floating parking garage. This keeps shipping costs to a minimum and reduces the risk of damage.

Cars in containers

A more expensive method that requires multiple steps. Carriers fit two to four vehicles inside a container, which is lifted by crane onto a ship. This technique is considered more prone to vehicle damage.

Specialized racking system

The least common form of autos transport. Vehicles are placed on metal racks that are lifted by crane and stored in the hold of cargo ships. The racks can be stacked on top of each other, allowing carriers to move hundreds of vehicles using a single vessel.

Shipping cars in containers isn’t new; it just isn’t usually done at scale to move thousands of cars. That changed during the Covid-19 pandemic, when demand to move vehicles outstripped the supply of ships. Christoph Seitz, global vice president of finished vehicles for DP World, a Dubai-based freight forwarder, said some Western automakers were skeptical of putting cars in boxes, but Chinese automakers didn’t flinch. “They immediately went, ‘We need more capacity,’” Seitz said.

A car being shipped by container must first be taken to a facility near a port, where it is loaded into a box and then lifted by crane onto a ship. That process must be repeated, in reverse, at the destination port.

Seitz said shipping cars in containers has become so popular that major container-shipping companies such as Denmark’s A.P. Moller-Maersk and Switzerland’s Mediterranean Shipping Co. have started selling their services directly to automakers.

The export supply is now so immense that Chinese automakers are expanding into the shipping sector, too. BYD launched its first dedicated car carrier in 2024 and now has a fleet of eight vessels. The automaker didn’t respond to requests for comment.
