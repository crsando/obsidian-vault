### 中文译文

## 中国太阳能股受国内政策与美国关税夹击剧烈波动

国内旨在叫停低于成本销售的努力，加上美国再度发出的关税威胁，令中国太阳能股本周剧烈震荡。

这一波折凸显出中国太阳能产业面临的双重现实——国内努力遏制过剩产能，外部贸易壁垒则使制造商的全球布局复杂化。

该国最大的多晶硅生产商[通威股份](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/600438%20CH%20Equity)的股价就是一个很好的例子。在国家市场监督管理总局于7月31日召集光伏制造商开会讨论价格纪律后，该股周一触及10%的日涨幅上限。

然而，由于有报道称美国总统唐纳德·特朗普正准备对进口多晶硅及其衍生物（太阳能供应链的基石）征收关税并设定最低价格要求，这种乐观情绪周四消退。通威股价下跌3.8%。

周五，投资者重新将目光投向国内政策，在通威及中国其他七家最大多晶硅生产商——中国多晶硅制造商承诺终止低于成本销售——承诺不以低于生产成本的价格销售太阳能产品后，股价一度上涨8.9%。

不过，分析师们对推行价格纪律的长期影响看法不一。[上海有色网](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1687832D:CH)表示，此举应将低价多晶硅供应挤出市场，而杰富瑞（Jefferies）的Alan Lau则质疑“多晶硅价格的反弹能有多持久、反弹幅度能有多高”。

在海外方面，特朗普对进口多晶硅衍生物（包括硅片、光伏电池和太阳能组件）征收关税并设定最低进口价格，此举给国际供应链带来不确定性。尽管由于关税，中国不直接向美国销售太阳能电池板，但许多企业使用中国上游材料在第三国制造产品再出口到美国。

特朗普的最新举措表明，随着越来越多的国家寻求建立自己的太阳能供应链，中国企业的海外扩张面临更具挑战性的局面。上海有色网分析师陈嘉辉在一份[报告](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/jtxZRv3aozmbpQEmXsmEQQ)中表示，出海努力已经面临政策风险、劳动力成本和供应链问题的挑战。

“未来，简单的出口贸易模式将发生变化，企业赚取海外利润的方式将转向合资建厂，”陈说。

### 关键数据点：

- 根据该协会的一份[周报](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/ZbHRrVc7vM5g4UqygUksLw)，由于国内市场已进入观望状态，交易活动陷入停滞，中国硅业协会本周未提供多晶硅价格。
- 截至周四的一周，[硅片价格](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/N7KZ4Fz6ed-L4Rr0o9ZbKQ)保持平稳，该行业的高库存和疲弱需求没有实质性改善。
- 电池片价格维持在每瓦0.25-0.26元人民币；组件价格为每瓦0.67-0.68元人民币，与上周持平。
### 本周要闻：

## China’s Solar Stocks Whipsawed by Domestic Policies, US Tariffs

Chinese solar stocks were whipsawed this week by a combination of domestic efforts to end below-cost sales and renewed tariff threats from the US.

The bumpy ride underscored the dual realities facing China’s solar industry, with domestic efforts aiming to curb overcapacity and external trade barriers that complicate manufacturers’ global presence.

Shares of [Tongwei Co.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/600438%20CH%20Equity), the country’s top polysilicon producer, provide a good example. They hit the 10% daily upside limit on Monday after the State Administration for Market Regulation convened a meeting with solar manufacturers July 31 to discuss price discipline.

That optimism faded on Thursday, however, after reports emerged that President Donald Trump was preparing to impose tariffs and minimum-price requirements on imported polysilicon and its derivatives, the corner stones of the solar supply chain. Tongwei fell 3.8%.

Investors have shifted back to focusing on domestic policies Friday, with shares up as much as 8.9% after Tongwei and China’s seven other largest polysilicon producers China’s Polysilicon Makers Pledge to End Below-Cost Sales not to sell solar products below production costs.

Still, analysts are mixed on the long-term impacts of the push for price discipline. [Shanghai Metals Market](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1687832D:CH) said the move should take low-priced polysilicon supply out of the market, while Jefferies’ Alan Lau questioned “how sustainable and how high the rebound on polysilicon prices would be.”

On the overseas front, Trump’s move to place tariffs and minimum import prices on imported polysilicon derivatives, including silicon wafers, photovoltaic cells and solar modules is raising uncertainty across international supply chains. While China doesn’t directly sell solar panels to the US because of taxes, many firms use Chinese upstream materials to manufacture goods in third countries to export to the US.

Trump’s latest move signals a more challenging landscape for Chinese companies’ overseas expansion as more countries are now seeking to build up their own solar supply chains. Already, efforts to go abroad have been challenged by policy risks, labor costs and supply chain issues, Chen Jiahui, an analyst with Shanghai Metals Market, said in a [note](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/jtxZRv3aozmbpQEmXsmEQQ).

“In the future, the simple export trade model will change, and the way companies earn overseas profits will shift to joint venture factory construction,” Chen said.

### Key Data Points:

- Solar cell prices remained at 0.25-0.26 yuan per watt; module prices were 0.67-0.68 yuan per watt, also same as last week
