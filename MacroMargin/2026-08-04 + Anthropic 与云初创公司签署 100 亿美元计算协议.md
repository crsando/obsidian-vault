### 中文译文

## Anthropic 与云初创公司签署 100 亿美元计算协议

据知情人士透露，[Anthropic PBC](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1892140D:US) 已与一家成立仅数个月的基础设施初创公司达成100亿美元的计算能力交易，这标志着这家 Claude 开发商为跟上产品需求所做的最新努力。

这家 AI 开发商已签署合同，使用由 [Nvidia Corp.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/NVDA%20US%20Equity) 支持的云初创公司 Volta Infra Holdings Ltd. 管理的数据中心。上述知情人士因交易未公开而要求匿名。

Volta [周二早些时候表示](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-04/nvidia-dell-back-ai-cloud-startup-volta-at-2-4-billion-value)，已与一家未具名的人工智能实验室达成100亿美元交易，将与运营数据中心的比特币矿商 [Bitdeer Technologies Group](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/BTDR%20US%20Equity) 合作，使用挪威的一处场地交付。该公司表示，协议期限为六年。

Volta 首席执行官 Ricard Boada 拒绝透露客户身份。Anthropic 和 Bitdeer 的代表拒绝置评。Bitdeer 股价周二盘前交易中上涨近10%。

近几个月来，随着越来越多的企业和消费者使用 Anthropic 的工具来简化编码和其他任务，该公司积极加强其计算资源。它已与埃隆·马斯克的 [SpaceX](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/SPCX%20US%20Equity)、[Advanced Micro Devices Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/AMD:US) 和 [Akamai Technologies Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/AKAM:US) 签署了计算协议。据彭博新闻社报道，这家 AI 开发商还在[讨论](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-17/meta-in-talks-to-sell-computing-power-to-anthropic-nyt-reports)从 [Meta Platforms Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/META%20US%20Equity) 的数据中心租用计算能力。

Anthropic 今年早些时候在一轮融资中[筹集了650亿美元](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-28/anthropic-raises-at-965-billion-valuation-eclipsing-openai)，以帮助支付 AI 开发的巨额成本。该公司还在考虑最早于今年[在华尔街首次公开亮相](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-15/anthropic-is-said-to-plan-ipo-investor-meetings-as-listing-nears)，从公开市场融资。

Volta 表示，挪威数据中心场地提供133兆瓦的容量，并将配备 Nvidia 最新的 Vera Rubin 芯片。

Volta 由 [Brookfield Asset Management Ltd](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/BAM%20US%20Equity) 的前高管于今年1月创立，该公司租赁 AI 容量，并帮助客户为昂贵的芯片交易融资。这家初创公司周二宣布，已通过风险投资筹集3亿美元，估值达24亿美元。

Volta的合作伙伴Bitdeer是越来越多的比特币挖矿公司之一，随着比特币价格暴跌，这些公司正将部分数据中心从铸造加密货币转向支持人工智能应用。根据Bitdeer于7月21日发布的月度运营[更新](https://ir.bitdeer.com/news-releases/news-release-details/bitdeer-announces-june-2026-production-and-operations-update)，该公司计划将其在德克萨斯州、田纳西州和华盛顿州的一些加密货币站点进行转换。

Anthropic和竞争对手OpenAI依靠大型科技公司和较新的云计算初创公司来帮助建设数据中心容量，他们认为这些容量对于开发更好的AI系统和支持更广泛的采用是必要的。

OpenAI的Sam Altman此前曾表示，他预计该公司将在AI物理基础设施上花费“数万亿美元”。该公司目前正在[计划在乔治亚州建设一个新的数据中心](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-22/openai-plans-to-spend-over-30-billion-on-georgia-data-center)，其成本可能超过300亿美元。据彭博新闻社报道，英伟达也正在[洽谈](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-26/nvidia-in-talks-on-250-billion-backing-for-openai-hub-wsj-says)帮助OpenAI租用一个耗资5000亿美元、10吉瓦的数据中心枢纽，该枢纽由[软银集团](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/9984:JP)在俄亥俄州负责。

过去一年，涉及OpenAI和Anthropic的一些融资安排被[批评为具有循环性质](https://www.bloomberg.com/graphics/2026-ai-circular-deals/)。这些交易在技术供应商和AI开发者之间建立了日益相互依存的依赖网络，如果AI需求未能达到过高的预期，就可能放大损失。

### English Original

## Anthropic Inks $10 Billion Computing Deal With Cloud Startup

[Anthropic PBC](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1892140D:US) has struck a $10 billion deal for computing capacity from a months-old infrastructure startup, according to people familiar with the matter, marking the Claude maker’s latest effort to keep pace with demand for its products.

The AI developer has signed a contract to use a data center managed by [Nvidia Corp.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/NVDA%20US%20Equity)-backed cloud startup Volta Infra Holdings Ltd., said the people, who requested anonymity as the deal is not public.

Volta [said earlier Tuesday](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-04/nvidia-dell-back-ai-cloud-startup-volta-at-2-4-billion-value) that it had secured a $10 billion deal with an unnamed AI lab to be delivered in partnership with [Bitdeer Technologies Group](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/BTDR%20US%20Equity), a Bitcoin miner that operates data centers, using a site in Norway. The agreement runs for six years, it said.

Volta Chief Executive Officer Ricard Boada declined to name the client. Representatives for Anthropic and Bitdeer declined to comment. Shares of Bitdeer rose nearly 10% in premarket trading Tuesday.

Anthropic has moved aggressively in recent months to shore up its computing resources as more businesses and consumers turn to its tools to streamline coding and other tasks. It has inked computing agreements with Elon Musk’s [SpaceX](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/SPCX%20US%20Equity), [Advanced Micro Devices Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/AMD:US) and [Akamai Technologies Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/AKAM:US) The AI developer is also in [discussions to lease computing power](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-17/meta-in-talks-to-sell-computing-power-to-anthropic-nyt-reports) from [Meta Platforms Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/META%20US%20Equity)’s data centers, Bloomberg News has reported.

Anthropic [raised $65 billion](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-28/anthropic-raises-at-965-billion-valuation-eclipsing-openai) in a funding round earlier this year to help cover the immense cost of AI development. It’s also considering tapping the public markets for capital with [a Wall Street debut](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-15/anthropic-is-said-to-plan-ipo-investor-meetings-as-listing-nears) as soon as this year.

The data center site in Norway offers 133 megawatts of capacity and will be stocked with Nvidia’s newest Vera Rubin chips, Volta said.

Founded in January by former executives from [Brookfield Asset Management Ltd](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/BAM%20US%20Equity), Volta leases AI capacity and helps clients finance deals for costly chips. The startup announced Tuesday that it raised $300 million in venture funding, valuing it at $2.4 billion.

Volta’s partner, Bitdeer, is among a growing number of Bitcoin mining companies that are converting some of their data centers from minting the cryptocurrency to supporting AI applications as Bitcoin prices slump. Bitdeer plans to convert some of its crypto sites in Texas, Tennessee and Washington state, according to a monthly operational [update](https://ir.bitdeer.com/news-releases/news-release-details/bitdeer-announces-june-2026-production-and-operations-update) by the company on July 21.

Anthropic and rival OpenAI have relied on a mix of large tech firms and newer cloud-computing startups to help build out the data center capacity they see as necessary for developing better AI systems and supporting wider adoption.

OpenAI’s Sam Altman has previously said he expects the company to spend “trillions” on physical infrastructure for AI. The startup is currently [planning a new data center](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-22/openai-plans-to-spend-over-30-billion-on-georgia-data-center) in Georgia that may cost more than $30 billion. Nvidia is also [in discussions](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-26/nvidia-in-talks-on-250-billion-backing-for-openai-hub-wsj-says) to help OpenAI lease a $500 billion, 10-gigawatt hub that [SoftBank Group Corp.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/9984:JP) is overseeing in Ohio, Bloomberg News has reported.

Over the past year, a number of financing arrangements involving OpenAI and Anthropic have been [criticized for being circular](https://www.bloomberg.com/graphics/2026-ai-circular-deals/) in nature. These deals create an increasingly interconnected web of dependencies between technology suppliers and AI developers that risks magnifying losses if AI demand fails to live up to the lofty expectations.
