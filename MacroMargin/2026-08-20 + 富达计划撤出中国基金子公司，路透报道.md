### 中文译文

## 富达计划撤出中国基金子公司，路透报道

据路透社援引知情人士报道，富达国际计划在推出其全资中国基金子公司三年后撤出，原因是当地竞争激烈且难以扩大规模对该业务造成压力。

报告称，这家上海子公司仍远未达到目标规模，促使管理层考虑撤退。全面退出仍有待最终审议和监管批准。

“中国仍然是富达国际的重要市场，我们仍然相信，中国为我们的业务和投资者都提供了有吸引力的长期机遇。我们的战略或市场布局没有变化需要报告。”富达国际在给彭博新闻社的一份声明中表示。

2020年中国取消持股限制后，外国公司投入数亿美元建立全资业务，押注中国庞大的家庭储蓄池将提供新的增长引擎。然而，它们一直在与根深蒂固的国内竞争对手、薄弱的品牌知名度和分销网络，以及长期的房地产市场低迷作斗争。

据路透社报道，目前尚不清楚FIL将如何重组或清算其14只中国零售基金，这些基金管理着45亿元人民币（6.7亿美元），远低于其2029年的目标。路透社查阅的一份2024年内部文件显示，该公司估计该部门需要至少140亿美元的资产才能实现盈利。

富达是全球最大的养老金管理机构之一。去年1月，它在中国退休养老市场迈出早期一步，推出了首只面向养老金投资者的多资产公开基金中基金。该公司于2021年获批设立全资子公司。

近期，该公司经历了一些[领导层变动](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-28/fidelity-international-china-head-jame-sun-leaves-firm)。富达基金管理（中国）有限公司总经理James Sun在任职约一年后于5月离职。该公司还在2024年计划在其中国大连运营中心裁员约500人，这是全球成本削减计划的一部分。

### English Original

## Fidelity Plans China Fund Unit Pullout, Reuters Reports

Fidelity International plans to pull out of its wholly owned China fund unit three years after its launch, as fierce local competition and difficulty gaining scale weigh on the business, Reuters reported, citing people familiar with the matter.

The Shanghai subsidiary remains well below its target size, prompting management to consider a retreat. A full withdrawal remains subject to final deliberations and regulatory approval, the report said.

“China remains an important market for Fidelity International and we continue to believe it offers attractive long-term opportunities both for our business and for investors,” the company said in a statement to Bloomberg News. “There is no change to report on our strategy or market presence.”

Foreign firms poured hundreds of millions of dollars into building wholly-owned operations after China scrapped ownership limits in 2020, betting that the country’s vast pool of household savings would provide a new engine of growth. Yet they have struggled against entrenched domestic rivals, weak brand recognition and distribution networks, and a prolonged property market downturn.

It remains unclear how FIL would restructure or liquidate its 14 China retail funds, which manage 4.5 billion yuan ($670 million), far short of its 2029 target. A 2024 internal document reviewed by Reuters showed the firm estimated the unit needed at least $14 billion in assets to turn profitable, the report said.

Fidelity, one of the world’s biggest pension managers, took an early step into China’s retirement market in January last year, launching its first multi-asset public fund of funds aimed at pension investors. It won approval to set up a wholly-owned unit in 2021.

The firm has seen some [leadership change](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-28/fidelity-international-china-head-jame-sun-leaves-firm) recently. James Sun, general manager of FIL Fund Management (China) Co., left in May after about a year in the job. It also made plans in 2024 to cut about 500 jobs at its Dalian operations center in China, part of a global cost-cutting drive.
